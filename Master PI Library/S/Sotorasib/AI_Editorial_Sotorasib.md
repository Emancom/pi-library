# Editorial Product Information: Sotorasib

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following regulatory authority: **Australian (TGA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of sotorasib for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises clinical data, dosing recommendations, and safety information.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority's current approved PI for definitive guidance.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Sotorasib (KRAS G12C inhibitor).
- **Brand Name:** **LUMAKRAS** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** L01XX73.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Immediate-release, film-coated tablet containing 120 mg of sotorasib [Australian].
- **Appearance:** Yellow, oblong-shaped, debossed with “AMG” on one side and “120” on the reverse.
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Amgen Australia Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC):** Treatment of adult patients with *KRAS G12C*-mutated locally advanced or metastatic NSCLC who have received at least one prior systemic therapy for advanced disease [Australian].
- **Regulatory Note:** This indication has received provisional approval in Australia based on objective response rate (ORR) and duration of response (DOR). Continued approval is contingent upon verification of clinical benefit in confirmatory trials [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **Testing:** Confirm the presence of a *KRAS G12C* mutation using a validated test prior to initiating therapy.
- **Recommended Dose:** 960 mg (eight 120 mg tablets) administered orally once daily.
- **Duration:** Continue treatment until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity.
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Take at the same time each day, with or without food.
    - Swallow tablets whole; do not chew, crush, or split.
- **Administration for Swallowing Difficulties:**
    - Disperse tablets in 120 mL of room-temperature tap water without crushing. 
    - Stir until dispersed into small pieces (will not completely dissolve). 
    - Drink immediately or within two hours. 
    - Rinse the container with an additional 120 mL of water and drink immediately to ensure the full dose is taken.
- **Missed Dose:** If a dose is missed by more than 6 hours, it should be skipped and the next dose taken at the regularly scheduled time.
- **Vomiting:** If vomiting occurs after administration, do not take an additional dose. Resume the regular schedule the following day.
- **Dose Modifications:**
    - **Starting Dose:** 960 mg daily.
    - **First Reduction:** 480 mg daily.
    - **Second Reduction:** 240 mg daily.
    - **Discontinuation:** If 240 mg daily is not tolerated, permanently discontinue treatment.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to sotorasib or any of its excipients (including lactose) [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hepatotoxicity:** Sotorasib can cause drug-induced liver injury and hepatitis. 
    - **Incidence:** Hepatotoxicity occurred in 27% of patients (16% Grade ≥ 3) in pooled studies [Australian]. 
    - **Monitoring:** Perform liver function tests (ALT, AST, alkaline phosphatase, and total bilirubin) prior to initiation, every 3 weeks for the first 3 months, then monthly thereafter or as clinically indicated.
    - **Risk Factor:** Recent (≤ 3 months) immunotherapy is associated with a higher risk of hepatotoxicity (40% vs 17% in those never receiving immunotherapy) [Australian].
- **Interstitial Lung Disease (ILD)/Pneumonitis:** 
    - **Incidence:** Occurred in 2.2% of patients (1.1% Grade ≥ 3), including fatal cases [Australian].
    - **Management:** Immediately withhold treatment in patients with suspected ILD/pneumonitis. Permanently discontinue if no other cause is identified.
- **Lactose Intolerance:** Tablets contain lactose monohydrate. Patients with rare hereditary problems of galactose intolerance, total lactase deficiency, or glucose-galactose malabsorption should avoid this medicine [Australian].

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Pneumonia (8%), hepatotoxicity (3.4%), and diarrhoea (2%) [Australian].
- **Fatalities:** Fatal reactions (3.4%) included respiratory failure, pneumonitis, and cardiac failure [Australian].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Acid-Reducing Agents:** Sotorasib solubility is pH-dependent. Coadministration with PPIs or H2 receptor antagonists decreases sotorasib concentration. 
    - **Management:** Avoid coadministration. If necessary, take sotorasib 4 hours before or 10 hours after a local antacid [Australian].
- **Strong CYP3A4 Inducers:** (e.g., rifampin, carbamazepine, St John’s wort) Decrease sotorasib concentrations. Coadministration is not recommended [Australian].
- **CYP3A4 Substrates:** Sotorasib is a moderate CYP3A4 inducer. It may decrease concentrations of substrates (e.g., midazolam, rivaroxaban, fentanyl). Avoid use with narrow therapeutic index substrates.
- **BCRP Substrates:** Sotorasib is a BCRP inhibitor. It may increase concentrations of substrates (e.g., rosuvastatin). Monitor for adverse reactions and adjust substrate dosage as needed.
- **P-gp Substrates:** Sotorasib may increase concentrations of P-gp substrates (e.g., digoxin). Avoid coadministration with narrow therapeutic index P-gp substrates [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [Australian Category: B3]. There are no clinical data in pregnant women. Animal studies showed no teratogenicity, but reduced ossification and lower fetal weights were noted in rabbits at maternally toxic doses. Use is not recommended during pregnancy [Australian].
- **Breastfeeding:** Safety is not established. Discontinue breastfeeding during treatment and for 1 week after the final dose [Australian].
- **Fertility:** No human data. Animal studies showed no adverse effects on reproductive organs [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy have not been established [Australian].
- **Elderly Use:** No overall differences in safety or efficacy were observed in patients ≥ 65 years compared to younger patients. No dose adjustment required [Australian].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment for mild impairment (CrCl ≥ 60 mL/min). Not studied in moderate or severe renal impairment [Australian].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment for mild (Child-Pugh A) or moderate (Child-Pugh B) hepatic impairment. The effect of severe impairment (Child-Pugh C) is unknown; monitor these patients closely [Australian].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Sotorasib is a highly selective, covalent, and irreversible inhibitor of KRAS G12C. It locks the KRAS protein in an inactive GDP-bound state, blocking oncogenic signalling and inducing apoptosis in tumour cells [Australian].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Does not prolong the QT interval to a clinically relevant extent [Australian].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Median Tmax is 1 hour. A high-fat, high-calorie meal increases AUC by 38%.
    - **Distribution:** Plasma protein binding is 89%. Volume of distribution is 211 L.
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily non-enzymatic conjugation and oxidative metabolism by CYP3As.
    - **Elimination:** Primarily fecal (74%); 6% recovered in urine. Mean terminal half-life is 5 hours. Steady state is reached within 22 days [Australian].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **CodeBreaK 100:** A single-arm, open-label trial of 124 patients with *KRAS G12C*-mutated NSCLC previously treated with immunotherapy or platinum-based chemotherapy.
    - **Objective Response Rate (ORR):** 37.1% (95% CI: 28.6, 46.2).
    - **Median Duration of Response (DOR):** 10.0 months.
    - **Disease Control Rate (DCR):** 80.6%.
    - **Median Progression-Free Survival (PFS):** 6.8 months.
    - **Median Overall Survival (OS):** 12.5 months [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage:** Store below 30°C.
- **Packaging:** Supplied in PVC/PVDC/Aluminium or PVC/Aclar/Aluminium blister packs containing 240 tablets [Australian].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule:** Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine) [Australian].
- **Additional Monitoring:** This product is subject to additional monitoring in Australia to allow quick identification of new safety information (Black Triangle Scheme) [Australian].
