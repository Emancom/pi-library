# Editorial Product Information: sulfathiazole + malachite + nitrofurantoin + nitrofurazone + quinacrine

## 3. Approved Indications
- Treatment and prevention of prevalent bacterial, fungal, and parasitic diseases of ornamental freshwater fish (general statement of use for freshwater ornamental aquaria).
- Use when introducing new fish to an aquarium (stated as part of prophylactic use).
- Jurisdictional differences: All indication information in the available sources derives from materials intended for ornamental aquarium use; no human therapeutic indications are provided. If regional approvals or indications beyond ornamental freshwater aquarium use exist, they are not specified in available sources.

## 4. Dosage and Administration
- General dosing (adult target species: ornamental freshwater fish / aquarium use):
  - Use 1 level measure (provided with product) per 40 L (10 US gallons) of aquarium water.
  - Repeat dosing every 3 days for up to 2 weeks or until clinical signs resolve.
  - Use prophylactically when introducing new fish.
- Administration instructions:
  - Administer to aquarium according to the stated measure-to-volume ratio.
  - Remove product with activated carbon prior to turning on ultraviolet (UV) sterilization equipment.
- Titration schedules: Not specified in available sources.
- Missed dose instructions: Not specified in available sources.

## 5. Contraindications
