## 3. Approved Indications
- Relieves occasional constipation (irregularity). (Approval context described as OTC monograph marketing.)  
- No other indications are specified in the available sources.

## 4. Dosage and Administration
### General information
- Unit strength stated in the available product listing: 8.6 mg sennosides (sennosides A and B) per tablet.  
- Onset of action: generally causes a bowel movement in 6–12 hours (as stated for the product).

### Recommended dosing
- Adults and children 12 years and over:
  - Starting dosage: 2 tablets once a day.
  - Maximum dosage: 4 tablets twice a day.
- Children 6 to under 12 years:
  - Starting dosage: 1 tablet once a day.
  - Maximum dosage: 2 tablets twice a day.
- Children 2 to under 6 years:
  - Starting dosage: 1/2 tablet once a day.
  - Maximum dosage: 1 tablet twice a day.
- Children under 2 years: consult a doctor before use.

### Administration instructions
- Preferably administer at bedtime or as directed by a doctor.
- If a comfortable bowel movement is not achieved by the second day, increase the dose by one tablet (do not exceed the stated maximum dosage) or decrease dose until comfortable.

### Titration
- Incremental increase by one tablet after the second day if no comfortable bowel movement is achieved (do not exceed maximum dosage).

### Missed dose
- Not specified in available sources

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
- **Do not use laxative products for longer than 1 week unless directed by a doctor.**
- **Keep out of reach of children.**
- In case of overdose: get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.
- Ask a doctor before use if the patient has:
  - stomach pain
  - nausea
  - vomiting
  - a sudden change in bowel movements that continues over a period of 2 weeks
