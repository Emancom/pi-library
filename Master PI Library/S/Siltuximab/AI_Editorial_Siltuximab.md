# Editorial Product Information: Siltuximab

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial: **Australian (TGA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising regulatory data into a clinically focused reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI or prescribing information required in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult the current official PI for their region before prescribing.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Siltuximab (rmc).
- **Chemical Class:** Chimeric (human-murine) immunoglobulin G1κ (IgG1κ) monoclonal antibody against human interleukin-6 (IL-6).
- **Brand Name:** **SYLVANT** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** L04AC11.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 100 mg and 400 mg lyophilised powder for injection in single-use vials. After reconstitution, the solution contains 20 mg/mL siltuximab.
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Recordati Rare Diseases [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Multicentric Castleman’s Disease (MCD):** For the treatment of patients with MCD who are human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) negative and human herpesvirus-8 (HHV-8) negative [Australian-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Recommended Dose:** 11 mg/kg administered every 3 weeks [Australian].
- **Duration:** Continue until treatment failure or unacceptable toxicity.

### Treatment Criteria
Before administration, clinicians must verify the following laboratory parameters:

| Laboratory Parameter | Requirement before First Dose | Retreatment Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Absolute Neutrophil Count (ANC) | ≥ 1.0 × 10⁹/L | ≥ 1.0 × 10⁹/L |
| Platelet Count | ≥ 75 × 10⁹/L | ≥ 50 × 10⁹/L |
| Haemoglobin | < 170 g/L | < 170 g/L |

*Note: Siltuximab may naturally increase haemoglobin levels in MCD patients.*

### Administration Instructions
- **Preparation:** Use aseptic technique. Allow vials to reach room temperature (~30 minutes). Reconstitute the 100 mg vial with 5.2 mL and the 400 mg vial with 20.0 mL of Sterile Water for Injection to achieve a 20 mg/mL concentration.
- **Reconstitution Warning:** Gently swirl; **DO NOT SHAKE**, vortex, or swirl vigorously.
- **Dilution:** Add the calculated volume to a 250 mL infusion bag containing Dextrose 5%.
- **Infusion:** Administer over 1 hour via an administration set lined with PVC, PU, or PE, containing a 0.2-micron inline polyethersulfone (PES) filter. Do not infuse concomitantly with other agents.

### Dose Adjustments and Delays
- **Dose Reduction:** Not recommended.
- **Toxicity Management:** Withhold therapy if the patient has a severe infection or severe non-haematological toxicity. Resume at the same dose once recovered.
- **Discontinuation:** Consider permanent discontinuation if more than two dose delays occur due to treatment-related toxicities within the first 48 weeks.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Severe hypersensitivity to siltuximab or any excipient (histidine, polysorbate-80, sucrose).

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Serious Infections:** Clinicians must treat existing infections prior to therapy. Siltuximab can mask signs of acute inflammation (fever, C-reactive protein), potentially delaying the diagnosis of serious infections (e.g., pneumonia, sepsis, HBV reactivation).
- **Infusion Reactions/Anaphylaxis:** If a severe infusion-related reaction or cytokine release syndrome occurs, discontinue permanently. Mild-to-moderate reactions may be managed by slowing or stopping the infusion and using antihistamines or corticosteroids upon re-initiation.
- **Vaccinations:** Do not administer live, attenuated vaccines concurrently or within 4 weeks prior to starting therapy due to potential interference with the immune response.
- **Gastrointestinal (GI) Perforation:** Reported in other clinical trials; use with caution in patients at increased risk for GI perforation and evaluate symptoms promptly.
- **Lipid Parameters:** Elevations in triglycerides and cholesterol have been observed; manage according to hyperlipidaemia guidelines.

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Very Common (>10%):** Upper respiratory tract infection (42.5%), pruritus (32.2%), rash/maculo-papular rash, arthralgia, diarrhoea, fatigue, peripheral oedema, malaise, decreased appetite, dyspnoea, cough, and peripheral sensory neuropathy.
- **Common (1–10%):** Urinary tract infection, nasopharyngitis, hypertension, hypertriglyceridaemia, hyperuricaemia, thrombocytopenia, and neutropenia.
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Anaphylaxis is the most serious associated risk. Infusion-related reactions occurred in approximately 5.1% to 6.3% of patients [Australian].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **CYP450 Normalization:** IL-6 inhibits CYP450 enzymes. By binding IL-6, siltuximab may normalize CYP450 activity, potentially increasing the metabolism of co-administered CYP450 substrates.
- **Management:** Monitor drugs with a narrow therapeutic index (e.g., **warfarin**, **cyclosporine**, **theophylline**) when starting or stopping siltuximab. Dose adjustments of concomitant medications may be necessary.
- **Caution:** Exercise caution with CYP3A4 substrates where reduced efficacy is undesirable, such as oral contraceptives.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** **Category C** [Australian]. There are no data in pregnant women. IgG antibodies cross the placenta. Use only if the clinical benefit clearly outweighs the potential risk to the foetus. 
- **Contraception:** Women of childbearing potential must use effective contraception during and for at least 3 months after treatment.
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if siltuximab is excreted in human milk. A decision should be made to discontinue nursing or the drug, considering the importance of the medication to the mother.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy have not been established [Australian].
- **Elderly (>65 years):** No significant differences in pharmacokinetics (PK) or safety profile were observed; no dose adjustment is required.
- **Renal Impairment:** No formal studies conducted. Population PK analysis suggests no meaningful effect on siltuximab PK in patients with creatinine clearance ≥12 mL/min [Australian].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No formal studies conducted. Population PK analysis suggests no meaningful effect on PK in patients with mild-to-moderate impairment.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Siltuximab is an IL-6 antagonist. It binds to human IL-6, preventing its interaction with both soluble and membrane-bound IL-6 receptors (IL-6R). This inhibits the formation of the hexameric signalling complex with gp130, thereby suppressing the pro-inflammatory cascades associated with MCD.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Clearance:** 3.54 ± 0.44 mL/kg/day.
    - **Half-life:** Approximately 16.3 ± 4.2 days.
    - **Steady State:** Achieved by the sixth infusion (every 3 weeks). Accumulation index is approximately 1.7.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Dose-dependently inhibits CRP, serum amyloid A, and IgM production in vitro and in clinical settings.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Study CNTO328MCD2001 (Pivotal):** A Phase 2, randomised, placebo-controlled study (n=79) of siltuximab (11 mg/kg) vs. placebo. 
- **Outcomes:** The siltuximab arm showed a statistically significant improvement in durable tumour and symptomatic response (34% vs 0%, p=0.0012) sustained for ≥18 weeks. 
- **Subgroup Note:** Treatment effect favoured siltuximab in all histological subtypes (plasmacytic and mixed) except the hyaline vascular subgroup, where responses were less frequent.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vials:** Store in a refrigerator at 2°C to 8°C. Do not freeze. Keep in the original package to protect from light.
- **Reconstituted/Diluted Solution:** Chemical and physical stability demonstrated for 8 hours at room temperature. The infusion should ideally be completed within 6 hours of adding the concentrate to the infusion bag.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Australia:** Poison Schedule S4 (Prescription Only Medicine).
