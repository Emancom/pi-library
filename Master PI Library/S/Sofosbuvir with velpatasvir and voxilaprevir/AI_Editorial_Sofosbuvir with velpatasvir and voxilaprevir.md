# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine Injection

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Note:** While the user context mentioned Australian (TGA) documentation for Sofosbuvir/Velpatasvir/Voxilaprevir, the source document provided for analysis pertains exclusively to **Levocarnitine Injection, USP** from the **United States (FDA)**. Consequently, this editorial reflects the harmonised clinical data for Levocarnitine.
- **Purpose:** This is a harmonised editorial overview intended for use by healthcare professionals. It is a synthesis of available regulatory data and is not a substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN/Generic).
- **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule/amino acid derivative ((R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt).
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency in Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** For the acute and chronic management of patients with metabolic defects resulting in secondary deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD) on Hemodialysis:** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients undergoing chronic dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    - Recommended dose is **50 mg/kg** administered as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) or via infusion [US].
    - In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose may be followed by equivalent doses every 3 to 4 hours (never less than every 6 hours) [US].
    - Subsequent daily doses typically remain at 50 mg/kg, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilised [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - Recommended starting dose: **10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg** dry body weight [US].
    - Administration: Slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Inspect visually for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration [US].
- Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial [US].

### Monitoring and Titration
- **Metabolic:** Monitor plasma carnitine concentrations weekly/monthly; target free carnitine levels between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].
- **ESRD:** Initiation may be prompted by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levels <40 to 50 µmol/L. Dose adjustments (e.g., down to 5 mg/kg) may be made as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough levels [US].

### Missed Dose Instructions
- Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known** [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions can occur within minutes of injection. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Levocarnitine (both oral and IV) has been associated with seizures in patients with or without pre-existing seizure disorders. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been reported [US].
- **Oral Levocarnitine Limitation:** The safety and efficacy of *oral* levocarnitine have not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency; chronic high-dose oral use in ESRD may lead to accumulation of toxic metabolites (TMA and TMAO) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions are often confounded by the underlying pathology (ESRD or metabolic crisis).

| Frequency | System | Adverse Event |

| **Very Common (>10%)** | General | Injection site reaction (33–38%), Pain (30–35%), Headache (22%), Fever (12%), Chest pain (12%) |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Anaphylaxis, seizures, and laryngeal edema [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalised Ratio (INR) when used concomitantly. **Clinical Management:** Monitor INR levels at initiation and after dose adjustments of levocarnitine in patients on warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not assigned by letter, but label states "should be used during pregnancy only if clearly needed"]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine in milk. Risks of excess carnitine to the infant must be weighed against the benefits of supplementation to the mother [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impairment in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders and ESRD is weight-based (see Section 3); specific age-range safety profiles are not further differentiated in source data [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Primarily indicated for ESRD on dialysis. Dose adjustments are based on pre-dialysis trough carnitine levels [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not protein-bound. Distribution half-life is 0.585 hours [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (TMAO) and γ-butyrobetaine [US].
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% excreted in urine within 24 hours (post-IV dose). Mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Study:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients (20 mg/kg 3x/week) showed mean trough levels increased from 21.3 µmol/L to 190 µmol/L after 8 weeks [US].
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** Comparisons of 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses over 12 and 24 weeks demonstrated dose-dependent increases in trough carnitine concentrations [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat. Discard any unused portion as the formulation lacks preservatives [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations of 0.5 mg/mL to 8.0 mg/mL [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources [Australian].
