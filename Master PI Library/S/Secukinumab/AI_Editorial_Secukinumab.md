# Editorial Product Information: Secukinumab

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following regulatory authority:
    - **Australian (TGA):** "Product Information — Secukinumab" (Date of Revision: 5 March 2025).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonized editorial overview intended for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesizes clinical, pharmacological, and regulatory data into a single, structured reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult the current local PI before initiating therapy.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Secukinumab (rch); a recombinant fully human monoclonal anti-human interleukin-17A (IL-17A) antibody of the IgG1/kappa isotype.
- **Brand Name:** **Cosentyx** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** L04AC10.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths [Australian]:**
    - **Solution for Injection (Prefilled Syringe):** 75 mg/0.5 mL, 150 mg/1 mL, 300 mg/2 mL.
    - **Solution for Injection (Prefilled Pen/Auto-injector):** 150 mg/1 mL, 300 mg/2 mL.
    - **Powder for Injection (Vial):** 150 mg (lyophilised cake for reconstitution).
- **Route of Administration:** Subcutaneous (SC) injection.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Novartis Pharmaceuticals Australia Pty Limited [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
**Secukinumab** is indicated for the treatment of:

- **Plaque Psoriasis:** Moderate to severe disease in patients $\ge$ 6 years who are candidates for systemic therapy or phototherapy [Australian].
- **Psoriatic Arthritis (PsA):** Active disease in adults, including axial manifestations, when response to previous DMARD therapy is inadequate [Australian].
- **Ankylosing Spondylitis (AS):** Active disease in adults (axial spondyloarthritis with radiographic damage) [Australian].
- **Non-radiographic Axial Spondyloarthritis (nr-axSpA):** Active disease in adults with objective signs of inflammation (elevated CRP and/or MRI changes) who have had an inadequate response to, or are intolerant of, NSAIDs [Australian].
- **Juvenile Idiopathic Arthritis (JIA):**
    - **Enthesitis-Related Arthritis (ERA):** Active disease in patients $\ge$ 4 years with inadequate response to at least one NSAID and one DMARD [Australian].
    - **Juvenile Psoriatic Arthritis (JPsA):** Active disease in patients $\ge$ 2 years with inadequate response to at least one NSAID and one DMARD [Australian].
- **Hidradenitis Suppurativa (HS):** Active moderate to severe disease in adults with inadequate response to conventional systemic therapy [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults)
- **Plaque Psoriasis:** 300 mg via SC injection at weeks 0, 1, 2, 3, and 4, followed by 300 mg monthly maintenance. Patients $\ge$ 90 kg may benefit from 300 mg every 2 weeks [Australian].
- **Psoriatic Arthritis:** 150 mg at weeks 0, 1, 2, 3, and 4, then monthly. May increase to 300 mg based on response. For anti-TNF IR patients or those with concomitant moderate-to-severe psoriasis, 300 mg is the recommended dose [Australian].
- **Ankylosing Spondylitis:** 150 mg at weeks 0, 1, 2, 3, and 4, then monthly. May increase to 300 mg based on response [Australian].
- **Non-radiographic axSpA:** 150 mg monthly (with or without a weekly loading dose for the first month) [Australian].
- **Hidradenitis Suppurativa:** 300 mg at weeks 0, 1, 2, 3, and 4, then 300 mg monthly. Some patients may require dosing every 2 weeks for optimal response [Australian].

### Dosing in Paediatric Populations [Australian]
- **Plaque Psoriasis ($\ge$ 6 years):** Dosing is weight-based at weeks 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, then monthly.
    - < 25 kg: 75 mg.
    - 25 to < 50 kg: 75 mg (may increase to 150 mg).
    - $\ge$ 50 kg: 150 mg (may increase to 300 mg).
- **JIA (ERA $\ge$ 4 years; JPsA $\ge$ 2 years):** Dosing is weight-based at weeks 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, then monthly.
    - < 50 kg: 75 mg.
    - $\ge$ 50 kg: 150 mg.

### Administration Instructions
- **Preparation:** Allow the prefilled syringe or pen to reach room temperature (approx. 20 minutes) before injection. Do not shake.
- **Sites:** Avoid areas of active psoriasis. Recommended sites include the thighs or abdomen.
- **Self-Administration:** Patients $\ge$ 12 years may self-inject after training; caregivers may inject patients $\ge$ 6 years [Australian].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Severe hypersensitivity** to the active substance or any excipients [Australian].
- **Clinically important, active infections** (e.g., active tuberculosis) [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Infections:** Secukinumab increases the risk of infections, particularly mild-to-moderate mucocutaneous candidiasis. Exercise caution in patients with chronic or recurrent infections. Discontinue treatment until serious infections resolve [Australian].
- **Tuberculosis (TB):** Screen patients for TB before starting treatment. Do not initiate in patients with active TB. Treat latent TB before starting secukinumab [Australian].
- **Hepatitis B Reactivation:** Screening for HBV should be considered. Monitor patients with positive serology for reactivation; consider discontinuation if reactivation occurs [Australian].
- **Inflammatory Bowel Disease (IBD):** New-onset or exacerbations of Crohn’s disease and ulcerative colitis have been reported. Monitor patients closely [Australian].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Rare cases of anaphylaxis and angioedema. Discontinue immediately if a serious allergic reaction occurs.
- **Latex Sensitivity:** The removable caps of the 1 mL and 0.5 mL prefilled syringes/pens contain a natural rubber latex derivative [Australian].
- **Vaccinations:** Do not administer **live vaccines** concurrently. Patients may receive inactivated or non-live vaccines; paediatric patients should be up to date on immunisations prior to starting therapy [Australian].

## 6. Adverse Effects
The safety profile is generally consistent across indications.

| Frequency | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Nasopharyngitis |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Upper respiratory tract infection, rhinitis, pharyngitis, oral herpes, diarrhoea, urticaria, rhinorrhoea, dermatitis/eczema |
| **Uncommon (0.1–1%)** | Oral candidiasis, sinusitis, tinea pedis, tonsillitis, otitis externa, conjunctivitis, neutropenia, IBD |
| **Rare (<0.1%)** | Anaphylactic reactions, hypersensitivity vasculitis |

- **Neutropenia:** Cases were generally mild, transient, and reversible, with no dose-dependency and no strong temporal link to serious infections [Australian].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **CYP450 Substrates:** No clinically relevant interaction was observed with midazolam (CYP3A4 substrate). In patients treated with CYP substrates having a narrow therapeutic index, consider monitoring for effect or concentration after initiating or stopping secukinumab [Australian].
- **MTX/Corticosteroids:** No significant interaction noted when used concomitantly in arthritis studies [Australian].
- **Live Vaccines:** Avoid concurrent use [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** **[Australian Category C]**. Secukinumab crosses the placenta. Use only if the clinical benefit clearly outweighs the potential risk. Live vaccines should be avoided in infants for 16 weeks following the mother’s last dose of secukinumab [Australian].
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if secukinumab is excreted in human milk. Exercise caution; a decision to discontinue breastfeeding or the drug must consider the benefit of the drug to the mother [Australian].
- **Fertility:** Human data are not available; animal studies (mice) showed no effect on fertility using anti-murine IL-17A antibodies [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Approved for plaque psoriasis ($\ge$ 6 years), ERA ($\ge$ 4 years), and JPsA ($\ge$ 2 years). Safety in other JIA categories or for other indications below 18 years is not established [Australian].
- **Elderly ($\ge$ 65 years):** No dose adjustment required. Clearance is similar to younger adults [Australian].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specifically studied; no dose recommendations can be made [Australian].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Secukinumab is a human IgG1 monoclonal antibody that selectively binds to and neutralizes IL-17A, a pro-inflammatory cytokine. By blocking IL-17A interaction with its receptor, it inhibits the release of pro-inflammatory cytokines and chemokines, reducing inflammation in the skin and joints [Australian].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Reduces levels of C-reactive protein (CRP) and lesional epidermal neutrophils within 1-2 weeks of treatment [Australian].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Bioavailability:** 73% following SC administration.
    - **Distribution:** Mean volume of distribution (Vz) ranges from 7.1 to 8.6 L.
    - **Metabolism:** Expected to be degraded into small peptides and amino acids via catabolic pathways.
    - **Elimination:** Mean systemic clearance (CL) is 0.19 L/day; **mean elimination half-life is 27 days** (range 17–41 days) [Australian].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Psoriasis:** Pivotal trials (ERASURE, FIXTURE, FEATURE, JUNCTURE) showed significant PASI 75 and IGA "clear/almost clear" responses at Week 12. Superiority over etanercept (FIXTURE) and ustekinumab (CLEAR) was demonstrated [Australian].
- **Psoriatic Arthritis:** FUTURE studies (1, 2, and 5) demonstrated ACR 20/50/70 responses and inhibition of radiographic progression (mTSS) [Australian].
- **Axial Spondyloarthritis:** MEASURE trials (AS) and PREVENT trial (nr-axSpA) showed significant ASAS 20/40 responses [Australian].
- **JIA:** A randomized withdrawal study showed significant reduction in the risk of disease flare [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Refrigeration:** Store at 2°C to 8°C. Do not freeze.
- **Light Protection:** Keep in the original package.
- **Out-of-Fridge Rule:** Prefilled syringes and pens may be stored unrefrigerated (up to 30°C) for a single period of up to **4 days**. Discard if not used within this window; do not return to the refrigerator [Australian].

- **Poison Schedule:** **[Australian Schedule 4]** (Prescription Only Medicine).
