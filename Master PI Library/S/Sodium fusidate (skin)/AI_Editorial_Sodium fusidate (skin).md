# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of Levocarnitine Injection for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises clinical, pharmacological, and regulatory data into a single, professional reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI or local regulatory guidance required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP).
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Chemical Name:** (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection, 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Administration
**Levocarnitine** must be administered intravenously. For parenteral products, visual inspection for particulate matter and discoloration is required prior to administration [US].

### Metabolic Disorders
- **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg as a slow bolus injection (2–3 minutes) or by infusion [US].
- **Loading Dose:** Often administered in severe metabolic crises, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours [US].
- **Frequency:** Administer every 3 or 4 hours; frequency should never be less than every 6 hours [US].
- **Maximum Dose:** The highest reported administered dose is 300 mg/kg [US].
- **Monitoring:** Obtain baseline plasma carnitine levels. Weekly and monthly monitoring is recommended, including blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L) [US].

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
- **Administration:** Slow 2-to-3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].
- **Dose Adjustments:** Guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations (normal range: 40–50 µmol/L). Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known** [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Serious Warnings
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients on dialysis. Some reactions occur within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and initiate appropriate medical care [US].

### General Precautions
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with known seizure disorders, an increase in seizure frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Oral Levocarnitine in Renal Impairment:** The safety and efficacy of oral levocarnitine have not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in ESRD patients may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) normally excreted in urine [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Clinical Trial Experience (ESRD Patients)
Adverse events reported at a frequency ≥5% in double-blind, placebo-controlled trials (regardless of causality) include [US]:

| Body System | Adverse Event | Levocarnitine (All Doses) | Placebo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Body as a Whole** | Abdominal pain, Asthenia, Chest pain, Fever, Headache, Infection, Injection site reaction, Pain | Common (7–33%) | 5–59% |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension, Hypotension, Tachycardia | Common (6–20%) | 5–19% |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea, Nausea, Vomiting | Common (8–16%) | 10–19% |
| **Metabolic** | Hypercalcemia, Hyperkalemia | Common (6–9%) | 3–6% |

### Other Reported Effects
- **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea and vomiting; less frequent: body odor and gastritis [US].
- **Neurologic:** Seizures (see Section 5) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have occurred when used concomitantly with **levocarnitine**. It is recommended to monitor INR levels at the initiation of therapy and following dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not specified, but text states:] Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) revealed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. No adequate, well-controlled studies exist in pregnant women. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine concentrations in milk after administration. The risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant should be weighed against the benefits of maternal supplementation [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing recommendations for metabolic disorders and ESRD apply to paediatric patients; see Section 3 [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically detailed in available sources [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Safety of **oral** levocarnitine is not established in severe renal impairment due to metabolite accumulation (TMA/TMAO). **Intravenous** levocarnitine is indicated for use in ESRD patients on dialysis (see Section 3) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
**Levocarnitine** is a naturally occurring substance required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production [US].

### Pharmacodynamics
In patients with inborn errors of metabolism or organic acidemias, **levocarnitine** promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters, which are rapidly excreted. This process helps alleviate "autointoxication" caused by acyl-CoA accumulation [US].

### Pharmacokinetics
- **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
- **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
- **Elimination:** Following a single IV bolus (20 mg/kg), approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours. The mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours; the mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Efficacy:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients (20 mg/kg post-dialysis), mean trough concentrations increased from 21.3 ± 7.7 µmol/L at baseline to 190 ± 55 µmol/L after 8 weeks [US].
- **Dose Ranging:** A study of ~30 patients per group (10, 20, or 40 mg/kg) showed dose-proportional increases in trough levocarnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks. Note: Effects on clinical outcomes (signs/symptoms of deficiency) in the ESRD population have not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat.
- **Stability:** Compatible and stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer's (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8.0 mg/mL) [US].
- **Preservation:** Does not contain preservatives; discard any unused portion of opened vials [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources [US].
