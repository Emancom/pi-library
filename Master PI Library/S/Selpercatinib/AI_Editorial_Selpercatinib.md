# Editorial Product Information: Selpercatinib

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical data and regulatory guidance into a single, structured reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis intended for professional reference and is not a substitute for the official, legally binding Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Selpercatinib (a receptor tyrosine kinase inhibitor).
- **Brand Name:** **Retevmo** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** L01EX22.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - 40 mg immediate-release hard capsules (grey, opaque, imprinted with "Lilly 3977" and "40 mg").
    - 80 mg immediate-release hard capsules (blue, opaque, imprinted with "Lilly 2980" and "80 mg").
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Eli Lilly Australia Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC):** Treatment of adult patients with locally advanced or metastatic *RET* fusion-positive NSCLC [Australian].
- **Medullary Thyroid Cancer (MTC):** Treatment of adult and adolescent patients (12 years of age and older) with advanced or metastatic *RET*-mutant MTC who require systemic therapy [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Adolescents $\ge$ 12 years)
Dosing is determined by patient body weight:
- **Less than 50 kg:** 120 mg orally twice daily.
- **50 kg or greater:** 160 mg orally twice daily.

### Administration Instructions
- **General:** Capsules must be swallowed whole; do not open, crush, or chew. May be taken with or without food.
- **Gastric pH Modifiers:** 
    - **Proton Pump Inhibitors (PPIs):** Take **Retevmo** with a meal if used concomitantly with a PPI.
    - **H2 Receptor Antagonists:** Administer **Retevmo** 2 hours before or 10 hours after the H2 antagonist.
    - **Local Antacids:** Administer 2 hours before or 2 hours after **Retevmo**.
- **Duration:** Continue treatment until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity.

### Missed Dose
If a dose is missed or vomiting occurs after a dose, the patient should skip that dose and take the next one at the scheduled time. Do not take a double dose.

### Dose Modifications for Toxicity
Reductions should occur in 40 mg decrements. 
- **$\ge$ 50 kg:** 160 mg $\rightarrow$ 120 mg $\rightarrow$ 80 mg $\rightarrow$ 40 mg.
- **< 50 kg:** 120 mg $\rightarrow$ 80 mg $\rightarrow$ 40 mg $\rightarrow$ (Discontinue).

## 4. Contraindications
- Hypersensitivity to selpercatinib or any excipients (microcrystalline cellulose, colloidal anhydrous silica, gelatin, titanium dioxide, iron oxide black, and/or Brilliant Blue FCF).

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hepatotoxicity:** Grade $\ge$ 3 increases in ALT and AST are common. Monitor liver function tests (LFTs) prior to initiation, every 2 weeks for the first 3 months, then monthly for the next 3 months.
- **Hypertension:** Blood pressure must be controlled prior to initiation and monitored during therapy. Permanent discontinuation is required if medically significant hypertension cannot be controlled.
- **QT Interval Prolongation:** Can cause concentration-dependent QTc prolongation. Correct hypokalaemia, hypomagnesaemia, and hypocalcaemia before and during treatment. Monitor ECGs at baseline, 1 week after initiation, and monthly for the first 6 months.
- **Haemorrhage:** Serious and fatal events have been reported. Discontinue permanently for Grade 3 or 4 haemorrhage.
- **Interstitial Lung Disease (ILD)/Pneumonitis:** Monitor for new or worsening pulmonary symptoms.
- **Hypersensitivity:** Often presents with fever, rash, and arthralgia/myalgia, particularly in NSCLC patients previously treated with anti-PD-1/PD-L1 immunotherapy.
- **Hypothyroidism:** Monitor thyroid function at baseline and periodically. Selpercatinib may inhibit the conversion of T4 to T3; some patients may require liothyronine (T3) supplementation.
- **Impaired Wound Healing:** Withhold treatment for at least 7 days before elective surgery and at least 2 weeks after major surgery.
- **Tumour Lysis Syndrome (TLS):** Monitor patients with high tumour burden or renal insufficiency.
- **Pediatric Specific (SCFE/SUFE):** Slipped Capital Femoral Epiphysis (SCFE) or Slipped Upper Femoral Epiphysis (SUFE) has been reported in adolescents. Monitor for limb pain or gait changes.

### Serious Adverse Effects
- Most common serious adverse events ($\ge$ 1%): Pneumonia, haemorrhage, abdominal pain, hyponatraemia, and hypersensitivity.
- Post-marketing reports include rare cases of **Stevens-Johnson Syndrome** and fulminant hepatitis.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Strong CYP3A inhibitors:** Avoid use. If unavoidable, reduce the **Retevmo** dose by 50%.
- **Strong CYP3A inducers:** Avoid use (e.g., rifampicin, carbamazepine, St. John’s Wort) as they significantly decrease selpercatinib exposure (AUC decreased by 87%).
- **CYP2C8 and CYP3A substrates:** Selpercatinib increases concentrations of sensitive substrates (e.g., repaglinide, midazolam). Avoid co-administration.
- **Transporter-mediated Interactions:** Selpercatinib inhibits MATE1 (may increase serum creatinine), P-gp, and BCRP. Caution is advised with sensitive P-gp substrates with a narrow therapeutic index (e.g., digoxin).

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [Australian Category D]. Selpercatinib can cause foetal harm. Animal studies showed 96.4% post-implantation loss and major malformations at exposures ~1.4x human therapeutic levels.
- **Contraception:** Females of childbearing potential must use highly effective contraception during and for 1 week after the last dose. Males with female partners of childbearing potential must also use effective contraception for 1 week after the final dose.
- **Breastfeeding:** Discontinue breastfeeding during treatment and for at least 1 week after the last dose.
- **Fertility:** May compromise male and female fertility based on animal findings (e.g., germ cell depletion, testicular degeneration).

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety not established in children < 18 years for NSCLC. Approved for MTC in patients $\ge$ 12 years. Growth plate monitoring is required in adolescents.
- **Elderly Use:** No dose adjustment required based on age ($\ge$ 65 years).
- **Renal Impairment:** No adjustment for mild, moderate, or severe impairment. Data are lacking for end-stage renal disease (eGFR < 15 mL/min) or dialysis.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** 
    - Mild/Moderate: No adjustment.
    - **Severe (Child-Pugh C):** Reduce dose to 80 mg twice daily.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Highly selective inhibitor of the *RET* (Rearranged during Transfection) receptor tyrosine kinase. It inhibits various *RET* fusions and mutations (e.g., *RET-V804L*, *RET-M918T*), preventing downstream signalling that drives tumour cell proliferation.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** $T_{max}$ ~2 hours; absolute bioavailability ~73%.
    - **Distribution:** 96% protein bound; $V_d$ ~203 L.
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily via **CYP3A4**.
    - **Elimination:** Half-life ~26.5 hours. Excretion: 69% in faeces, 24% in urine.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **LIBRETTO-001:** Phase 1/2 trial demonstrating objective response rates (ORR) of 61.5% in treatment-experienced NSCLC and 82.6% in treatment-naïve NSCLC. In MTC, ORR reached 77.6%–82.5%.
- **LIBRETTO-431:** Phase 3 trial in treatment-naïve *RET* fusion-positive NSCLC. Selpercatinib significantly improved Progression-Free Survival (PFS) (Median 24.8 months vs 11.2 months for chemotherapy).
- **LIBRETTO-531:** Phase 3 trial in kinase inhibitor-naïve *RET*-mutant MTC. Selpercatinib showed superior PFS compared to physician's choice of cabozantinib or vandetanib (30-month PFS rate 76.4% vs 24.8%).
