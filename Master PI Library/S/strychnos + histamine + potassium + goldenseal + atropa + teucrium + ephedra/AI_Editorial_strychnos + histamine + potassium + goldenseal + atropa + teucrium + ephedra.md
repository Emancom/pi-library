## 3. Approved Indications
- Temporarily relieves symptoms of non-apneic snoring. Individual response may vary. [US]
- Note: The product label indicates that it does not treat sleep apnea and advises consultation with a health professional for suspected sleep apnea. [US]

## 4. Dosage and Administration
### General dosing (adult)
- Adults and adolescents aged 12 years and older: spray twice under the tongue and twice at the back of the throat at bedtime.
- Additional use as needed: up to 4 times per day.

### Dosing by indication
- Indication-specific dosing beyond the above (e.g., for conditions other than non-apneic snoring): Not specified in available sources.

### Administration instructions
- Use 30 minutes apart from any food, water, or medicine.
- Initially, depress pump until primed.
- Allow 3–5 days for initial treatment effect (as labelled).
- Consult a physician for use in children under 12 years of age.
