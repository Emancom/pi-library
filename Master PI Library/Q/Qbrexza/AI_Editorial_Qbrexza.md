# Editorial Product Information: Glycopyrronium (Topical)

## Editorial Note
- Contributing Jurisdictions: United States (FDA)
- Purpose: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of the clinical and regulatory profile of topical glycopyrronium for use by healthcare professionals.
- Note: This is an editorial synthesis based on available regulatory documentation and is not a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Glycopyrronium tosylate (an anticholinergic quaternary amine).
- **Brand Name(s):** **Qbrexza** [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Single-use cloth pre-moistened with 2.4% glycopyrronium solution [US]. Each pouch contains 105 mg glycopyrronium tosylate, equivalent to 66 mg of glycopyrronium [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical (axillary).
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Journey Medical Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Primary Axillary Hyperhidrosis:** Indicated for the topical treatment of excessive underarm sweating in adults and paediatric patients aged 9 years and older [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing:** Apply once daily to both axillae using a single cloth [US].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - For topical use in the underarm area only; do not apply to other body sites or broken skin [US].
    - Using one cloth, wipe across one entire underarm once, then use the same cloth to wipe the other underarm once [US].
    - **Hand Hygiene:** Wash hands immediately with soap and water after application and disposal of the cloth [US].
    - **Avoid Occlusion:** Do not use with occlusive dressings [US].
- **Frequency:** Should not be used more than once every 24 hours [US].
- **Missed Dose:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
Glycopyrronium topical is contraindicated in patients with medical conditions that may be exacerbated by anticholinergic effects [US]:
- Glaucoma.
- Paralytic ileus.
- Unstable cardiovascular status in acute haemorrhage.
- Severe ulcerative colitis or toxic megacolon complicating ulcerative colitis.
- Myasthenia gravis.
- Sjögren’s syndrome.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Urinary Retention:** New or worsening urinary retention (e.g., difficulty passing urine, distended bladder) has been reported. Caution is advised in patients with prostatic hypertrophy or bladder-neck obstruction. Discontinue use immediately if symptoms occur [US].
- **Thermoregulation (Heat Illness):** Anticholinergics may impair sweating, leading to heat illness (hyperpyrexia and heat stroke) in high ambient temperatures. Patients should monitor for a generalised lack of sweating and avoid use if this occurs [US].
- **Operating Machinery:** Transient blurred vision may occur. Patients should not drive or operate machinery until vision is clear [US].
- **Accidental Exposure:** Contact with the eyes may cause temporary pupillary dilation (mydriasis), anisocoria, and blurred vision. Strict adherence to handwashing is required. Avoid transfer to the periocular area [US].
- **Flammability:** The product contains alcohol and is flammable. Avoid use near heat or open flame [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Anticholinergic Medications:** Coadministration with other anticholinergic-containing drugs may result in additive interactions, increasing the risk and severity of anticholinergic adverse effects. Avoid concurrent use [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** No human data are available to inform drug-associated risks. Animal studies (rats/rabbits) using oral or IV glycopyrrolate during organogenesis did not show increased incidence of gross external or visceral defects at standard doses. Reduced fetal weight and minor skeletal effects were noted in rats only at maternally toxic doses [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** There are no data on the presence of glycopyrronium in human milk or its effects on milk production/breastfed infants. The developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding should be weighed against the clinical need for the drug [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies in rats showed no treatment-related effects on fertility or reproductive parameters [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy are established for patients aged 9 years and older. Safety has not been established in patients under 9 years of age [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical trials did not include sufficient numbers of subjects aged 65 and over to determine if they respond differently than younger subjects [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Elimination of glycopyrronium is severely impaired in patients with renal failure. The pharmacokinetics of the topical formulation have not been specifically studied in this population [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not evaluated in this population [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Glycopyrronium is a competitive inhibitor of acetylcholine receptors located on peripheral tissues, specifically the eccrine sweat glands. By blocking this action, it reduces sweat production [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** The specific systemic pharmacodynamics of the topical cloth are not fully characterised [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Low systemic absorption. In adults, mean Cmax was 0.08 ± 0.04 ng/mL. No evidence of systemic accumulation was observed over 5 days of daily application [US].
    - **Distribution:** Following IV administration, the volume of distribution is approximately 1.3 to 1.8 L/kg in children and 0.42 L/kg in the elderly [US].
    - **Metabolism:** A small proportion is metabolised, but the pathway is not characterised [US].
    - **Excretion:** Primarily excreted in urine (~85%) and bile (<5%) following IV administration, mostly as unchanged drug [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pivotal Trials (Trial 1 and 2):** Two randomized, vehicle-controlled, 4-week multicenter trials enrolled 697 subjects (age ≥9) with primary axillary hyperhidrosis. 
- **Efficacy Measures:** Success was defined by a ≥4-point improvement on the Axillary Sweating Daily Diary (ASDD) and reduction in gravimetrically measured sweat production.
- **Results:** At Week 4, 53%–66% of Qbrexza-treated patients achieved the ASDD response compared to 27%–28% in the vehicle group. Median sweat production was reduced by approximately 79–81 mg per 5 minutes in the active group [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at room temperature 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
- **Handling:** **Flammable.** Keep away from heat or open flame [US]. Discard used cloths in household trash out of reach of children [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Rx-only (Prescription only) [US].
