## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); a carrier molecule for long-chain fatty acid transport.
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium.
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - Sterile solution for intravenous injection: 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only [US].
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** For the acute and chronic management of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance hemodialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    - **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg administered as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) or via infusion [US].
    - **Loading Dose:** Often utilised in severe metabolic crises, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours [US].
    - **Frequency:** Dosing should occur every 3 to 4 hours (q3h or q4h) and never less frequently than every 6 hours (q6h) [US].
    - **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses typically range around 50 mg/kg, though up to 300 mg/kg has been administered if clinically required [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - **Recommended Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight) [US].
    - **Administration:** Administered as a slow 2 to 3 minute bolus into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected for particulate matter or discoloration prior to use [US].
- Discard any unused portions as the formulation lacks preservatives [US].

### Monitoring and Titration
- **Metabolic Patients:** Obtain baseline plasma carnitine levels. Monitor weekly/monthly, including blood chemistry and vital signs. Target plasma free carnitine concentrations: 35 to 60 µmol/L [US].
- **ESRD Patients:** Initiation may be prompted by trough (pre-dialysis) levels <40 to 50 µmol/L. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be made as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough levels [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None [US].
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions can occur within minutes of administration [US].
- **Seizures:** Cases of seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity may occur [US].
- **Oral Levocarnitine Caution:** The safety/efficacy of oral levocarnitine has not been evaluated in renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in ESRD may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Common (≥5% frequency in dialysis patients):**
    - **General:** Chest pain, fever, flu syndrome, headache, infection, injection site reaction, pain [US].
    - **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension, hypotension, tachycardia [US].
    - **Digestive:** Diarrhea, abdominal pain, nausea, vomiting [US].
    - **Metabolic:** Hypercalcemia, hypervolemia [US].
    - **Respiratory:** Cough increase, pharyngitis, dyspnea, rhinitis [US].
    - **Neurological:** Dizziness [US].
- **Less Frequent/Other:** Transient nausea and vomiting; body odor; gastritis [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalised Ratio (INR) have occurred when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin. Monitoring of INR is recommended upon initiation or dose adjustment [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not explicitly lettered. Animal studies (rats/rabbits) at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk carnitine after exogenous administration. Benefits to the mother should be weighed against potential risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing is weight-based (see Section 3). Secondary deficiency in newborns is defined as concentrations below age-related normals [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Used specifically in ESRD patients on dialysis (see Section 3). Caution is advised regarding the accumulation of metabolites if oral formulations are used [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also facilitates the excretion of excess organic/fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
    - **Elimination:** Terminal half-life of 17.4 hours. Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours (uncorrected for endogenous levels) [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (feces) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Study:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients on hemodialysis receiving 20 mg/kg three times weekly showed a significant increase in pre-dialysis trough levels from ~21 µmol/L at baseline to ~190 µmol/L at 8 weeks [US].
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** 12- and 24-week data for 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses in ESRD patients confirmed dose-dependent increases in trough carnitine concentrations [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Protection:** Avoid excessive heat and protect from freezing [US].
- **Stability (Admixture):** Compatible and stable in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s (0.5 mg/mL to 8.0 mg/mL) for up to 24 hours at room temperature in PVC bags [US].
