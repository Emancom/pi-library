# Editorial Product Information: Viloxazine Hydrochloride

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, consolidating regulatory information into a structured, clinically relevant format.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory documents for legal prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Viloxazine hydrochloride
- **Chemical Class:** Selective norepinephrine reuptake inhibitor (SNRI)
- **Brand Name(s):** **Qelbree** [US]
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available source.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Extended-release capsules: 100 mg, 150 mg, and 200 mg [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Supernus Pharmaceuticals, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Attention-Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD):** Indicated for the treatment of ADHD in adults and pediatric patients aged 6 years and older [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration

### General Dosing [US]
- **Pediatric Patients (6 to 11 years):** Initially 100 mg once daily. Dosage may be increased in 100 mg weekly increments to a maximum of 400 mg once daily.
- **Pediatric Patients (12 to 17 years):** Initially 200 mg once daily. After one week, dosage may be increased by 200 mg to a maximum of 400 mg once daily.
- **Adults:** Initially 200 mg once daily. Dosage may be increased in 200 mg weekly increments to a maximum of 600 mg once daily.

### Administration Instructions
- Capsules may be administered with or without food.
- **Swallowing:** Capsules must not be cut, crushed, or chewed.
- **Sprinkling:** Capsules may be opened and the entire contents sprinkled onto a teaspoonful or tablespoonful of applesauce or pudding. 
    - The mixture must be consumed within 15 minutes for pudding or 2 hours for applesauce.
    - The mixture should be swallowed whole without chewing.

### Missed Dose
- Not specified in available source.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors (MAOIs):** Concomitant use with an MAOI, or within 14 days of discontinuing an MAOI, due to the risk of hypertensive crisis [US].
- **CYP1A2 Substrates:** Concomitant administration of sensitive CYP1A2 substrates or CYP1A2 substrates with a narrow therapeutic range [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions

### Boxed Warning: Suicidal Thoughts and Behaviors [US]
Higher rates of suicidal thoughts and behaviors were reported in pediatric and adult patients treated with **Qelbree** compared to placebo. Close monitoring is required for clinical worsening and the emergence of suicidal impulses, particularly during the first few months of therapy and at times of dosage adjustment.

### General Warnings
- **Blood Pressure and Heart Rate Increases:** Viloxazine can increase heart rate and diastolic blood pressure. Heart rate and blood pressure must be assessed prior to treatment, after dose increases, and periodically during therapy [US].
- **Activation of Mania/Hypomania:** Treatment with noradrenergic drugs may induce manic or mixed episodes in patients with bipolar disorder. Patients should be screened for bipolar disorder, including personal/family history of suicide, depression, and mania [US].
- **Somnolence and Fatigue:** Potential for significant somnolence (including sedation and lethargy). Patients should be cautioned against operating hazardous machinery or driving until they understand the drug's effects [US].

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Suicidal ideation and behavior, significant tachycardia, and hypertensive crisis (when used with contraindicated agents).
- **Weight Effects:** Pediatric patients may experience reduced weight gain compared to age-matched placebo groups. Monitoring of weight is recommended [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions

### Pharmacokinetic Interactions [US]
- **CYP1A2 (Strong Inhibitor):** Viloxazine significantly increases the total exposure of CYP1A2 substrates. 
    - **Contraindicated:** Sensitive substrates or those with a narrow therapeutic range (e.g., alosetron, duloxetine, ramelteon, tasimelteon, tizanidine, theophylline).
    - **Moderate sensitive substrates:** Coadministration is not recommended; dose reduction of the substrate may be required if coadministered.
- **CYP2D6 (Weak Inhibitor):** Increases exposure of CYP2D6 substrates (e.g., dextromethorphan). Monitor for adverse reactions and adjust doses as needed.
- **CYP3A4 (Weak Inhibitor):** Increases exposure of CYP3A4 substrates (e.g., midazolam). Monitor and adjust dosages as clinically indicated.

### Pharmacodynamic Interactions
- **MAOIs:** Risk of life-threatening hypertensive crisis [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Category:** Not specified by letter category in available source [US].
    - **Recommendations:** May cause maternal and fetal harm based on animal data (delayed fetal development and toxicities at doses equal to the MRHD). Discontinue **Qelbree** when pregnancy is recognized unless benefits outweigh risks.
    - **Registry:** A National Pregnancy Registry for Psychiatric Medications is available for monitoring outcomes [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Viloxazine and its metabolite are present in human milk. The relative infant dose (RID) is approximately 1%. Data suggest transfer into breastmilk is low; however, the effects on the breastfed infant or milk production are unknown [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies showed no impact on male or female fertility parameters at doses approximately equal to the MRHD [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy not established in patients younger than 6 years [US].
- **Geriatric Use:** Clinical trials did not include sufficient numbers of patients aged 65 and older to determine if they respond differently than younger patients [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    - **Mild to Moderate (eGFR 30–89 mL/min/1.73m²):** No dosage adjustment required [US].
    - **Severe (eGFR < 30 mL/min/1.73m²):** Initial dose 100 mg once daily; titrate in weekly increments of 50–100 mg to a maximum of 200 mg once daily [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Exposure is increased in patients with hepatic impairment, though specific dosing adjustments are not detailed in the primary administration section [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** The exact mechanism in ADHD is unclear but is thought to be mediated through the inhibition of norepinephrine reuptake [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** 
    - Inhibits the norepinephrine transporter (Ki = 0.13 μM).
    - Partial agonist at serotonin 5-HT2C receptors (Ki = 0.66 μM) [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Relative bioavailability is ~88%. Tmax is approximately 5 hours. High-fat meals decrease Cmax and AUC by ~9% [US].
    - **Distribution:** 76–82% plasma protein bound [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily metabolized by CYP2D6, UGT1A9, and UGT2B15.
    - **Elimination:** Mean half-life is 7.02 hours. 90% of the dose is recovered in urine within 24 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials [US]
- **Pediatric Trials (Studies 1, 2, and 3):** Three short-term, randomized, placebo-controlled trials in patients aged 6–17 years. The primary endpoint was the change from baseline in the ADHD Rating Scale (ADHD-RS-5). Viloxazine showed statistically significant reductions in ADHD symptoms compared to placebo.
- **Adult Trial (Study 4):** A 6-week flexible-dose trial (200–600 mg). Viloxazine demonstrated statistically significant improvement in the ADHD Investigator Symptom Rating Scale (AISRS) compared to placebo.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Excursions:** Permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Keep out of reach of children.
