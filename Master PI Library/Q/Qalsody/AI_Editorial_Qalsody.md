# Editorial Product Information: Qalsody (tofersen)

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is derived from the official Product Information (PI) documents of the Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA, Australia) and the Food and Drug Administration (FDA, United States).
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised, clinically accurate overview for healthcare professionals. 
- This document is an editorial synthesis for informational purposes and does not replace the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Always consult the local approved labeling before prescribing.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Tofersen, an antisense oligonucleotide (ASO).
- **Brand Name:** **Qalsody** [TGA, FDA].
- **ATC Code:** N07XX22.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Sterile, preservative-free solution for injection; 100 mg/15 mL (6.7 mg/mL) in a single-dose vial.
- **Route of Administration:** Intrathecal.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Biogen Australia Pty Ltd [TGA]; Biogen MA Inc. [FDA].

## 2. Approved Indications
**Qalsody** is indicated for the treatment of amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS) in adults who have a mutation in the superoxide dismutase 1 (*SOD1*) gene.

- **Regulatory Status:** This indication is approved under "provisional approval" [TGA] and "accelerated approval" [FDA] based on the reduction of plasma neurofilament light chain (NfL), a biomarker of axonal injury and neurodegeneration. Continued approval for this indication may be contingent upon verification of clinical benefit in confirmatory trials.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Recommended Dose:** 100 mg (15 mL) per administration.
- **Loading Regimen:** Three loading doses administered at 14-day intervals (Day 1, Day 15, and Day 29).
- **Maintenance Regimen:** One dose administered every 28 days thereafter.

### Administration Instructions
- **Route:** Intrathecal injection via lumbar puncture, performed by or under the direction of healthcare professionals experienced in the procedure.
- **CSF Removal:** It is recommended to remove approximately 10 mL of cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) prior to the injection of **Qalsody**.
- **Injection Technique:** Administer as an intrathecal bolus over 1 to 3 minutes.
- **Preparation:** 
    - Allow the vial to warm to room temperature (25°C) naturally without external heat sources.
    - Inspect visually for particles or discoloration; the solution should be clear and colourless to slightly yellow.
    - Do not shake or dilute.
    - Once drawn into a syringe, it must be used immediately (within 4 hours if kept at room temperature); otherwise, discard.

### Missed Dose Instructions
- **Second Loading Dose:** Administer as soon as possible, with the third dose 14 days later.
- **Third Loading Dose:** Administer as soon as possible, with the first maintenance dose 28 days later.
- **Maintenance Dose:** Administer as soon as possible, then resume dosing every 28 days from the last dose.

## 4. Contraindications
- **FDA:** None listed in the official PI.
- **TGA:** Hypersensitivity to the active substance or any of the excipients (sodium phosphate, sodium chloride, potassium chloride, calcium chloride dihydrate, magnesium chloride hexahydrate).

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Serious Neurological Events
- **Myelitis and/or Radiculitis:** Serious events, including transverse myelitis and radiculopathy, have been reported. Clinicians should monitor for new-onset sensory or motor symptoms. Diagnostic workup and treatment should be initiated per standard of care.
- **Increased Intracranial Pressure (ICP) and Papilloedema:** Serious cases have occurred. Monitoring for symptoms (e.g., headache, visual disturbances, nausea) is required.
- **Aseptic Meningitis:** Also referred to as chemical or drug-induced meningitis. Symptoms should be investigated promptly.

### Oligonucleotide Class-Specific Precautions [TGA]
While not specifically observed with intrathecal tofersen in pivotal trials, these effects have occurred with other systemic antisense oligonucleotides:
- **Thrombocytopenia and Coagulation Abnormalities:** If clinically indicated, platelet and coagulation testing is recommended prior to administration.
- **Renal Toxicity:** Monitoring for urine protein (preferably first morning specimen) is recommended if clinically indicated.

## 6. Adverse Effects
The safety profile is based on clinical trials (Study 101 and 102) involving 147 patients with *SOD1*-ALS.

| Adverse Reaction | Frequency Category |

| Fatigue | Very Common (≥10%) |
| Arthralgia | Very Common (≥10%) |
| CSF white blood cell increased | Very Common (≥10%) |
| CSF protein increased | Very Common (≥10%) |
| Myalgia | Very Common (≥10%) |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Myelitis, radiculitis, aseptic meningitis, and increased ICP are the primary serious risks associated with therapy.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** No clinical interaction studies have been performed. Tofersen is not an inducer or inhibitor of CYP450 enzymes and is not a substrate for major transporters.
- **Intrathecal Co-administration:** The safety of co-administering other intrathecal medications with tofersen is unknown [TGA].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
### Pregnancy
- **TGA Category:** Category B2.
- **FDA Classification:** No letter category; narrative risk summary provided. 
- **Summary:** There are no adequate data on the use of tofersen in pregnant women. Animal studies (mice/rabbits) showed no evidence of embryo-fetal lethality or teratogenicity. **Qalsody** is not recommended during pregnancy or for women of childbearing potential not using contraception [TGA].

### Breastfeeding
- It is unknown if tofersen or its metabolites are excreted in human milk. Tofersen was detected in the milk of lactating mice. The developmental benefits of breastfeeding should be weighed against the clinical need for the mother and potential risks to the infant.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness in patients under 18 years have not been established.
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical studies included patients up to 78 years. No overall differences in safety or effectiveness were observed, and no dose adjustments are recommended.
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Tofersen has not been studied in patients with renal or hepatic impairment. It is not expected to be metabolised by hepatic enzymes or undergo significant renal clearance of the intact drug.

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
Tofersen is a 20-mer antisense oligonucleotide. It binds to the mRNA of the *SOD1* gene, leading to RNase-H-mediated degradation. This reduces the synthesis of the SOD1 protein, which in *SOD1*-ALS patients accumulates in toxic forms.

### Pharmacokinetics
- **Absorption:** Rapid transfer from CSF to systemic circulation (Plasma $T_{max}$ 2–6 hours).
- **Distribution:** Extensively distributed into CNS tissues (spinal cord and brain). Steady state is reached after the loading phase.
- **Metabolism:** Metabolised by exonucleases; not a substrate for CYP450.
- **Elimination:** Effective half-life in CSF is approximately 4 weeks. Primary route of systemic elimination is expected to be urinary excretion of metabolites.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pivotal Study (VALOR/Study 101 Part C):** A 28-week, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled study (n=108).
- **Primary Endpoint:** Change from baseline in the ALSFRS-R total score at Week 28. While the result numerically favoured tofersen, it did not reach statistical significance ($p=0.0904$).
- **Biomarker Results:** Statistically significant reductions in plasma NfL (55% reduction vs. 12% increase in placebo) and CSF SOD1 protein (35% reduction vs. 2% in placebo) were observed at Week 28.
- **Long-term Extension (Study 102):** Earlier initiation of tofersen was associated with a trend toward reduced risk of death or permanent ventilation compared to delayed initiation.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C. Do not freeze.
- **Light:** Store in the original carton to protect from light.
- **Stability at Room Temperature:** Unopened vials can be kept at room temperature (up to 30°C) for up to 14 days. If removed and returned to the refrigerator, the total time out of refrigeration must not exceed 6 hours per day for a maximum of 6 days [TGA, FDA].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Australia:** Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine) [TGA].
- **United States:** Rx-only [FDA].
