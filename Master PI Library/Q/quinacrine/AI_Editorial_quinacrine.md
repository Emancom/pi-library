# Editorial Product Information: PolyGuard (Sulfathiazole, Malachite Green, Nitrofurantoin, Quinacrine, and Nitrofurazone)

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial is based on Product Information provided by the **United States (FDA)**. 
- This document serves as a harmonized editorial overview for healthcare professionals, specifically those in veterinary pharmacy or aquarium management. 
- **Important Regulatory Note:** This product is classified in the United States as an "Unapproved Drug Other." It has not been evaluated by the FDA for safety or effectiveness. 
- This synthesis is an editorial summary and does not substitute for the official Product Information (PI) or labeling required for legal prescribing or dispensing in any specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:**
    - Sulfathiazole (364 mg/g; 36%) [US]
    - Malachite Green (19 mg/g; 1.9%) [US]
    - Nitrofurantoin (1 mg/g; 0.14%) [US]
    - Quinacrine dihydrochloride (2 mg/g; 0.27%) [US]
    - Nitrofurazone (Nitrofural) (1 mg/g; 0.14%) [US]
- **Brand Name:** PolyGuard™ [US]
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form:** Oral/Immersion Powder [US]
- **Route of Administration:** Added to aquarium water (Oral/Topical immersion for fish) [US]
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Seachem Laboratories Inc. [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Freshwater Fish Disease Treatment:** Indicated for the treatment of bacterial, fungal, and parasitic diseases in exotic and freshwater fish [US-approved].
- **Prophylaxis:** Indicated as a safeguard when introducing new fish to an environment [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Freshwater Aquariums):** Use one (1) level measure (included with the product) for every 40 Liters (10 US gallons) of water [US].
- **Dosing Schedule:** Repeat the dose every 3 days for a duration of up to 2 weeks, or until clinical symptoms resolve [US].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Remove chemical filtration (e.g., carbon) from the system before use, as these media may remove the active ingredients [US].
    - Turn off Ultraviolet (UV) sterilizers/lamps during treatment [US].
- **Missed Dose:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Human Consumption:** Strictly contraindicated for human consumption [US].
- **Target Species:** Not for use in fish intended for human food [US].
- **Environment:** Specified for use in ornamental aquariums only [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None [US].
- **Regulatory Status Warning:** This drug has not been found by the FDA to be safe and effective, and the labeling has not been approved by the FDA [US].
- **General Precautions:**
    - For use in ornamental aquariums only [US].
    - Ensure chemical filtration is removed prior to administration to maintain therapeutic concentrations [US].
- **Monitoring Requirements:** Not specified in available sources; however, visual monitoring of fish symptoms is implied [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Frequency Data:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Not specified in available sources.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:**
    - **Activated Carbon:** Will adsorb and remove active ingredients from the water, rendering the treatment ineffective [US].
- **Pharmacodynamic Interactions:**
    - **Ultraviolet (UV) Light:** UV radiation may degrade or interfere with the efficacy of the active ingredients (particularly malachite green and nitrofurans) [US].
- **Management:** Ensure carbon is removed and UV filters are turned off during the 2-week treatment cycle [US].
