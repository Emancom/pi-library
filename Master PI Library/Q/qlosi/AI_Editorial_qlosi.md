# Editorial Product Information: Qlosi (pilocarpine hydrochloride)

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdiction: **United States** (Food and Drug Administration).
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. 
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Pilocarpine hydrochloride (cholinergic agonist).
- **Brand Name:** Qlosi [United States].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Ophthalmic solution containing 0.4% (4 mg/mL) pilocarpine hydrochloride (equivalent to 0.3% [3.4 mg] pilocarpine free-base) [United States].
- **Route of Administration:** Topical ophthalmic.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Orasis Pharmaceuticals, Inc. [United States].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Presbyopia:** Indicated for the treatment of presbyopia in adults [United States].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing:** Instill one drop of **Qlosi** into each eye.
- **Dosing Frequency:** May be administered once daily or as needed. A second dose may be repeated 2 to 3 hours after the initial dose to extend the effect for up to 8 hours. The maximum frequency is twice daily [United States].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - For topical ophthalmic use only.
    - If using multiple topical ophthalmic medications, administer them at least 5 minutes apart.
    - Discard the single-patient-use vial immediately after use.
    - To prevent contamination or eye injury, avoid contact between the vial tip and the eye or any other surface.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to the active ingredient (pilocarpine hydrochloride) or any of the excipients [United States].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Blurred Vision:** Miotics may cause accommodative spasm. Patients should be advised not to drive or operate machinery if vision is not clear.
- **Night Driving and Poor Illumination:** Caution is advised during night driving or other hazardous occupations performed in poor light, as miotics may cause temporary dim or dark vision.
- **Risk of Retinal Detachment:** Rare cases of retinal detachment have been reported with the use of miotics, particularly in susceptible individuals or those with pre-existing retinal disease. A thorough retinal examination is advised for all patients prior to starting therapy. Patients should seek immediate medical attention if they experience sudden flashes of light, floaters, or vision loss.
- **Iritis:** Use is not recommended in patients with active iritis due to the risk of adhesion (synechiae) formation between the iris and the lens.
- **Contact Lens Wear:** Contact lenses should be removed prior to instillation. Patients should wait at least 10 minutes after dosing before reinserting lenses.

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions reported in clinical trials were generally mild, transient, and self-resolving.

| Frequency | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Common (5–8%)** | Instillation site pain, headache |
| **Common (2–5%)** | Blurred vision |

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Management:** If using more than one topical ophthalmic drug, separate administration by at least 5 minutes [United States].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not assigned under current labeling rules; however, there are no adequate and well-controlled studies in pregnant women. 
    - **Animal Data:** Oral administration in rats at doses ≥1200 times the maximum recommended human ophthalmic dose (MRHOD) resulted in maternal toxicity, skeletal anomalies, and reduced fetal weight. No adverse effects were noted at clinically relevant doses [United States].
- **Breastfeeding:** There is no data regarding the presence of pilocarpine in human milk or its effects on the breastfed infant. While systemic levels following topical application are low, the health benefits of breastfeeding should be weighed against the mother's clinical need for the drug [United States].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies (oral administration) showed impaired reproductive function, including reduced fertility and decreased sperm motility in rats at doses approximately 240 times the MRHOD [United States].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Presbyopia is an age-related condition that does not occur in children; safety and efficacy have not been established in the paediatric population [United States].
- **Elderly Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established in patients over the age of 65. Clinical trials did not include sufficient numbers of subjects in this age group to determine if they respond differently than younger subjects [United States].
- **Renal Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Pilocarpine is a cholinergic muscarinic agonist. It activates muscarinic receptors on the iris sphincter muscle (causing pupillary constriction/miosis) and the ciliary muscle. This miosis increases the depth of focus, improving near visual acuity while maintaining some pupillary response to light [United States].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** 
    - **Absorption:** Following twice-daily administration for 8 days, the median $T_{max}$ was 2.20 hours. 
    - **Elimination:** The mean plasma half-life ($T_{1/2}$) is approximately 3.96 hours [United States].

## 11. Clinical Trials
The efficacy of **Qlosi** was evaluated in two Phase 3, randomized, double-masked, vehicle-controlled studies (NEAR-1 and NEAR-2) involving 613 participants aged 45 to 64.
- **Primary Outcome:** Responders were defined as those achieving a gain of 3 lines or more in near Best Distance Corrected Visual Acuity (BDCVA) at 40 cm without losing 1 line (≥5 letters) of distance BDCVA.
- **Results:** The percentage of responders was significantly higher in the **Qlosi** group compared to vehicle at all assessment points (e.g., 39-52% vs 15-21% at 1 hour post-dose). The effect was observed as early as 20 minutes post-dose and lasted up to 8 hours (5 hours after the second dose) [United States].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage Temperature:** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F). 
- **Pouch Opening:** Once a foil pouch is opened, the single-patient-use vials may be stored at room temperature up to 25°C (77°F) for up to 30 days.
- **Handling:** Protect from light. Discard opened vials immediately after use [United States].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Rx-only (Prescription Only Medicine).
