# Editorial Product Information: Quizartinib

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) document from the following jurisdiction: **United States (Food and Drug Administration)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of quizartinib for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises clinical, regulatory, and pharmacological data into a single reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for the official PI or prescribing information required for clinical practice in any specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Quizartinib (as dihydrochloride).
- **Chemical Class:** Kinase inhibitor (specifically targeting the FLT3 receptor).
- **Brand Name:** **VANFLYTA** [US].
- **ATC Code:** L01EX11 [Not explicitly stated in source, but standard for quizartinib].
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - **Oral tablets:** 17.7 mg and 26.5 mg [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Daiichi Sankyo Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Newly Diagnosed FLT3-ITD Positive AML:** Indicated in combination with standard cytarabine and anthracycline induction and cytarabine consolidation, and as maintenance monotherapy following consolidation, for the treatment of adult patients with newly diagnosed acute myeloid leukemia (AML) that is FLT3 internal tandem duplication (ITD)-positive [US].
- **Limitations of Use:** Quizartinib is not indicated as maintenance monotherapy following allogeneic hematopoietic stem cell transplantation (HSCT), as overall survival benefits have not been demonstrated in this specific setting [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults)
- **Administration:** Tablets must be swallowed whole; do not cut, crush, or chew. Take once daily at approximately the same time, with or without food [US].
- **Selection:** Use of quizartinib must be based on the presence of the FLT3-ITD mutation as detected by a validated diagnostic test [US].

### Dosing by Phase [US]
- **Induction:** 35.4 mg once daily on Days 8 through 21 of a "7 + 3" regimen (cytarabine and anthracycline). Up to two cycles may be administered.
- **Consolidation:** 35.4 mg once daily on Days 6 through 19 of high-dose cytarabine consolidation. Up to four cycles.
- **Maintenance:** 
    - **Days 1–14 (Cycle 1):** 26.5 mg once daily.
    - **Day 15 onwards:** Increase to 53 mg once daily if the QT interval corrected by Fridericia’s formula (QTcF) is ≤ 450 ms.
    - **Duration:** Continue for up to 36 cycles or until disease progression/unacceptable toxicity.

### Missed or Vomited Doses
- **Missed Dose:** If a dose is missed or not taken at the usual time, administer it as soon as possible on the same day and return to the usual schedule the next day. Do not take two doses on the same day [US].
- **Vomited Dose:** If a dose is vomited, do not take a replacement dose; wait until the next scheduled dose [US].

## 4. Contraindications
Quizartinib is contraindicated in patients with [US]:
- Severe hypokalemia.
- Severe hypomagnesemia.
- Long QT syndrome.
- A history of ventricular arrhythmias or torsades de pointes.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warning: QT Prolongation, Torsades de Pointes, and Cardiac Arrest [US]
- **Cardiac Risk:** Quizartinib prolongs the QT interval in a dose- and concentration-dependent manner. Torsades de pointes and cardiac arrest have occurred.
- **Screening:** Perform ECGs and monitor electrolytes (potassium, magnesium) prior to initiation and periodically.
- **Thresholds:** Do not initiate or escalate the dose if the QTcF is > 450 ms. 
- **Restricted Access:** Available only through the **VANFLYTA REMS** program [US].

### Monitoring Requirements
- **ECG Monitoring:** During induction and consolidation, perform ECGs prior to initiation and once weekly during treatment. During maintenance, monitor prior to initiation, weekly for the first month, and periodically thereafter [US].
- **Electrolytes:** Correct hypokalemia and hypomagnesemia to the normal range prior to and during treatment. Monitor more frequently in patients with diarrhea or vomiting [US].

### Embryo-Fetal Toxicity
- Quizartinib can cause fetal harm. Verify pregnancy status within seven days of starting treatment. Advise patients of reproductive potential of the risk to the fetus and the need for effective contraception [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Serious Adverse Effects
- **Common Serious Reactions (≥5%):** Febrile neutropenia (11%) and sepsis (5%) [US].
- **Fatal Reactions:** Occurred in 10% of patients, including sepsis, fungal infections, cardiac arrest, and cerebral infarction [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
### Pharmacokinetic Interactions
- **Strong CYP3A Inhibitors:** Concomitant use increases quizartinib exposure. Reduce the quizartinib dose (e.g., reduce 53 mg to 26.5 mg; 35.4 mg to 17.7 mg; 26.5 mg to 17.7 mg). If the current dose is 17.7 mg, interrupt treatment [US].
- **Strong or Moderate CYP3A Inducers:** Avoid concomitant use as these decrease quizartinib exposure and may reduce efficacy [US].
- **BCRP Substrates:** Quizartinib may increase the concentration of Breast Cancer Resistance Protein (BCRP) substrates. Avoid co-administration where possible; if unavoidable, monitor for substrate-related toxicities and consider dose reductions for the substrate [US].

### Pharmacodynamic Interactions
- **QT-Prolonging Drugs:** Co-administration increases the risk of serious arrhythmias. Monitor ECGs more frequently if use of these agents is required [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Not classified by lettered category. Based on animal data, quizartinib is likely to cause structural abnormalities (anasarca, skeletal ossification) and fetal growth alterations at 3 times the human exposure [US].
- **Contraception:**
    - **Females:** Effective contraception during treatment and for 7 months after the final dose [US].
    - **Males:** Effective contraception for 4 months after the final dose if the partner is of reproductive potential [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** There are no data on the presence of quizartinib in human milk. Due to potential serious adverse reactions in the infant, advise women not to breastfeed during treatment and for 1 month after the final dose [US].
- **Fertility:** Based on animal studies, quizartinib may impair both male and female fertility; effects were reversible after one month of recovery in most cases [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established in patients under 18 years of age [US].
- **Elderly Use:** No overall differences in safety or efficacy were observed between patients ≥ 65 years and younger adults [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment for mild (CLcr 60–89 mL/min) or moderate (CLcr 30–59 mL/min) impairment. Data are unavailable for severe renal impairment [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment for mild (Child-Pugh A) or moderate (Child-Pugh B) hepatic impairment. Data are unavailable for severe hepatic impairment [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Quizartinib is a small-molecule inhibitor of the FLT3 receptor tyrosine kinase. It inhibits FLT3 kinase activity and downstream signaling, blocking the proliferation of cells dependent on FLT3-ITD [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Prolongs the QT interval by inhibiting the slow delayed rectifier potassium current ($I_{Ks}$) [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Absolute bioavailability is ~71%. Median $T_{max}$ is 4 hours. No significant food effect [US].
    - **Distribution:** Plasma protein binding is ≥ 99%. Volume of distribution is 275 L [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily metabolised via oxidation by CYP3A4/5. The major active metabolite is AC886 [US].
    - **Elimination:** Effective half-life is ~81 hours. Total body clearance is 2.23 L/h. Excretion is primarily via faeces (76.3%) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **QuANTUM-First Trial:** A randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled study (n=539) of quizartinib plus chemotherapy vs. placebo plus chemotherapy.
- **Primary Outcome:** Statistically significant improvement in overall survival (OS) for the quizartinib arm (Hazard Ratio 0.78; 95% CI: 0.62, 0.98; p=0.0324).
- **Secondary Outcomes:** The Complete Remission (CR) rate was 55% in both arms; however, the median duration of CR was 38.6 months for quizartinib vs. 12.4 months for placebo [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Keep out of reach of children.
