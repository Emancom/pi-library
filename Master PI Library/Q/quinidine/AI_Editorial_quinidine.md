# Editorial Product Information: Dextromethorphan hydrobromide and Quinidine sulfate

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial product information was derived from official Product Information (PI) documentation provided by the **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical and regulatory data into a single reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific PI required for prescribing in any particular region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** Dextromethorphan hydrobromide (uncompetitive NMDA receptor antagonist and sigma-1 agonist) and Quinidine sulfate (CYP450 2D6 inhibitor).
- **Brand Name:** **NUEDEXTA** [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Capsules containing 20 mg dextromethorphan hydrobromide and 10 mg quinidine sulfate [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Otsuka America Pharmaceutical, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Pseudobulbar Affect (PBA):** Indicated for the treatment of PBA secondary to various underlying neurological conditions (e.g., Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (ALS), Multiple Sclerosis (MS)) [US]. 
- **Note:** PBA is characterized by involuntary, sudden, and frequent episodes of laughing and/or crying that are incongruent with the patient's underlying emotional state.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **Initial Dosing:** One capsule daily for the first 7 days of treatment [US].
- **Maintenance Dosing:** Starting on day 8, the dose is increased to one capsule every 12 hours [US].
- **Maximum Dose:** Not to exceed two capsules in a 24-hour period [US].
- **Administration Instructions:** May be taken with or without food.
- **Dose Reassessment:** Periodically reassess the need for continued treatment, as spontaneous improvement of PBA symptoms may occur in some patients.
- **Missed Dose:** Patients should not take a double dose to make up for a missed dose.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Concomitant Medications:** 
    - Use with quinidine, quinine, or mefloquine [US].
    - Use with Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors (MAOIs) or within 14 days of stopping an MAOI. Allow 14 days after stopping this drug before starting an MAOI [US].
    - Use with drugs that both prolong the QT interval and are metabolized by CYP2D6 (e.g., thioridazine, pimozide) [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** History of hypersensitivity to dextromethorphan; or history of quinidine, quinine, or mefloquine-induced thrombocytopenia, hepatitis, bone marrow depression, or lupus-like syndrome [US].
- **Cardiovascular Conditions:** 
    - Congenital long QT syndrome, history of prolonged QT interval, or history suggestive of torsades de pointes [US].
    - Heart failure [US].
    - Complete atrioventricular (AV) block without an implanted pacemaker, or patients at high risk of complete AV block [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Thrombocytopenia and Hypersensitivity:** Quinidine can cause severe or fatal immune-mediated thrombocytopenia. Discontinue immediately if this occurs and do not restart. Be alert for symptoms like lightheadedness, chills, fever, nausea, or vomiting [US].
- **Hepatotoxicity:** Hepatitis, including granulomatous hepatitis, has occurred (usually in the first few weeks). Discontinue if hepatitis occurs [US].
- **Cardiac Effects (QT Prolongation):** Causes dose-dependent QTc prolongation. 
    - **Monitoring:** For patients at risk (e.g., those with left ventricular hypertrophy, left ventricular dysfunction, or those taking moderate/strong CYP3A4 inhibitors), conduct an ECG at baseline and 3–4 hours after the first dose [US].
    - **Electrolytes:** Correct hypokalemia and hypomagnesemia prior to initiation and monitor during treatment.
- **CYP2D6 Interactions:** This product is a strong inhibitor of CYP2D6. Concomitant use with CYP2D6 substrates may increase substrate levels, potentially requiring dose adjustments of the substrate [US].
- **Dizziness and Falls:** Increased risk of dizziness may lead to falls; take precautions, especially in patients with motor impairment [US].
- **Serotonin Syndrome:** Risk increases when used with SSRIs or tricyclic antidepressants. Symptoms include mental status changes, hyperthermia, and hyperreflexia [US].
- **Anticholinergic Effects:** Monitor for worsening symptoms in patients with myasthenia gravis or other conditions sensitive to anticholinergic effects [US].
- **CYP2D6 Poor Metabolizers:** In these patients, quinidine does not contribute to effectiveness but may still cause toxicity. Genotyping should be considered [US].

### Serious and Other Adverse Effects
- **Cinchonism:** Characterized by nausea, vomiting, tinnitus, deafness, vertigo, and blurred vision (associated with quinidine component) [US].
- **Lupus-like Syndrome:** Associated with quinidine [US].
- **Falls:** Reported in clinical trials, often associated with dizziness [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **CYP2D6 Substrates:** Exposure to drugs like desipramine (8-fold increase) and paroxetine (2-fold increase) is significantly elevated. Reduce initial doses of such drugs and titrate based on response [US].
- **CYP3A4 Inhibitors:** Strong/moderate inhibitors (e.g., ritonavir, clarithromycin, grapefruit juice) increase quinidine levels and the risk of QT prolongation.
- **Digoxin:** Quinidine inhibits P-glycoprotein, potentially doubling serum digoxin levels. Monitor levels and reduce digoxin dose as necessary [US].
- **Memantine:** Possible additive NMDA antagonism; quinidine levels may increase by 20–30% [US].
- **Alcohol:** Use caution due to additive CNS effects.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not specified as a letter, but data from animal studies (rats/rabbits) showed developmental toxicity, including teratogenicity and embryo lethality [US]. No adequate data in pregnant women. Use only if potential benefit justifies the risk to the fetus.
- **Breastfeeding:** Quinidine is excreted in human milk. It is unknown if dextromethorphan is excreted. Consider the developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding alongside the clinical need for the drug and potential adverse effects on the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** No effects on fertility were observed in rat studies at doses up to 12/50 times the maximum recommended human dose [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness in patients under 18 years have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical studies included 14% of patients ≥65 years. In general, dose selection should be cautious, starting at the low end of the range, due to decreased organ function and comorbid conditions [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment required for mild to moderate impairment. Not evaluated in severe renal impairment [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment required for mild to moderate impairment. Not evaluated in severe hepatic impairment [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:**
    - **Dextromethorphan:** Sigma-1 receptor agonist and uncompetitive NMDA receptor antagonist. The exact mechanism for treating PBA is unknown.
    - **Quinidine:** Inhibits CYP2D6, thereby significantly increasing and prolonging the plasma concentrations of dextromethorphan.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** DM Cmax reached in 3–4 hours; quinidine Cmax in 1–2 hours. Quinidine increases DM exposure by ~20-fold [US].
    - **Distribution:** DM is 60–70% protein bound; Quinidine is 80–89% protein bound [US].
    - **Metabolism:** DM via CYP2D6; Quinidine via CYP3A4.
    - **Elimination:** DM half-life is ~13 hours; Quinidine half-life is ~7 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pivotal Trial:** A 12-week, placebo-controlled study in patients with ALS or MS.
- **Outcomes:** Significant reduction in the rate of laughing and crying episodes compared to placebo. Improved scores on the Center for Neurologic Studies Lability Scale (CNS-LS) were also observed [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at controlled room temperature, 25°C (77°F).
- **Excursions:** Permitted between 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
- **Packaging:** Keep out of reach of children.
