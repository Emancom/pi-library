# Editorial Product Information: QIVIGY (Immune Globulin Intravenous [Human-kthm] 10%)

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the following jurisdiction's Product Information: United States (FDA).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, consolidating clinical and regulatory data into a single reference. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult the current local PI for full prescribing details and legal requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Immune globulin intravenous (human-kthm), 10% liquid solution.
- **Brand Name:** **QIVIGY** [US].
- **ATC Code:** J06BA02 (Immune globulins, normal human, for intravascular administration).
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Sterile liquid solution for infusion containing 100 mg/mL (10%) of IgG.
    - 5 g in a 50 mL single-dose vial [US].
    - 10 g in a 100 mL single-dose vial [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Kedrion S.p.A. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Primary Humoral Immunodeficiency (PI):** Treatment of adults with PI, including but not limited to:
    - Common Variable Immunodeficiency (CVID).
    - Congenital agammaglobulinemia.
    - X-linked agammaglobulinemia.
    - Wiskott-Aldrich syndrome.
    - Severe Combined Immunodeficiencies (SCID) [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Dose Range:** 300 mg/kg to 800 mg/kg administered every 3 to 4 weeks.
- **Titration:** Adjust dosage and frequency over time based on clinical response and target serum IgG trough levels.

### Infusion Rates
| Infusion Number | Initial Rate | Maintenance Rate (as tolerated) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1st Infusion** | 1 mg/kg/min (0.01 mL/kg/min) for 30 min | Increase every 30 min to a maximum of 8 mg/kg/min (0.08 mL/kg/min) |
| **Subsequent Infusions** | 2 mg/kg/min (0.02 mL/kg/min) for 15 min | Increase every 15 min to a maximum of 8 mg/kg/min (0.08 mL/kg/min) |

### Measles Prophylaxis
- **Post-exposure:** 400 mg/kg administered as soon as possible after exposure.
- **Pre-exposure:** If a patient routinely receives <400 mg/kg every 3–4 weeks and is at risk of exposure (e.g., travel to endemic areas), administer at least 400 mg/kg just prior to expected exposure.

### Administration Instructions
- **Hydration:** Ensure adequate hydration before administration.
- **Preparation:** Bring to room temperature (up to 25°C) before use. Inspect for particulate matter; do not use if cloudy or contains deposits. Do not shake.
- **Incompatibilities:** Do not mix with other IGIV products or medications (including normal saline).
- **Post-infusion:** Flush infusion line with Saline or Dextrose 5% (D5W).

### Missed Dose
- Administer as soon as possible, then resume the regular 3- or 4-week schedule.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Severe Hypersensitivity:** History of anaphylactic or severe systemic reactions to human immune globulin [US].
- **IgA Deficiency:** Patients with known antibodies against IgA and a history of hypersensitivity [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warning [US]
- **Thrombosis:** May occur regardless of risk factors. Risk is higher with advanced age, prolonged immobilisation, hypercoagulable states, history of thrombosis, oestrogen use, or cardiovascular risk factors. 
- **Renal Dysfunction and Acute Renal Failure:** May occur in predisposed patients. Higher risk with pre-existing renal insufficiency, diabetes, age >65, volume depletion, or nephrotoxic drugs.
- **Management:** Use the minimum dose and infusion rate practicable in at-risk patients. Ensure adequate hydration and monitor for symptoms.

### General Warnings
- **Hypersensitivity:** Severe reactions/anaphylaxis can occur. Discontinue immediately if suspected. Epinephrine must be available.
- **Hyperproteinemia/Hyponatremia:** IGIV may cause hyperproteinemia, increased serum viscosity, and pseudohyponatremia. True hyponatremia must be distinguished from pseudohyponatremia to avoid inappropriate water restriction.
- **Aseptic Meningitis Syndrome (AMS):** May occur, usually within hours to 2 days post-infusion. More common with high doses (2 g/kg) or rapid infusion.
- **Hemolysis:** Hemolytic anemia may develop due to blood group antibodies (hemolysins). Monitor for clinical signs and drop in haemoglobin.
- **Transfusion-Related Acute Lung Injury (TRALI):** Characterised by severe respiratory distress and pulmonary oedema within 1–6 hours of infusion.
- **Transmissible Infectious Agents:** As a human plasma derivative, a risk of transmitting infectious agents (viruses, vCJD) exists, though minimised by donor screening and inactivation steps.

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions (ARs) were defined as events occurring during or within 72 hours of infusion.

| Adverse Reaction | Frequency (Patients) | Frequency (Infusions) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Headache** | 30% | 4% |
| **Fatigue** | 15% | 2% |
| **Nausea** | 13% | <1% |
| **Infusion-related Reaction** | 11% | 1% |
| **Coombs Direct Test Positive** | 11% | 1% |
| **Sinusitis** | 6% | <1% |
| **Dizziness** | 6% | <1% |
| **Diarrhoea** | 6% | <1% |

### Serious/Post-marketing Adverse Effects
The following have been reported with IGIV products: 
- **Respiratory:** ARDS, apnea, pulmonary embolism.
- **Cardiovascular:** Myocardial infarction, cardiac arrest, thromboembolism.
- **Neurological:** Seizures, coma, encephalopathy.
- **Renal:** Acute tubular necrosis, osmotic nephropathy.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Live Virus Vaccines:** Passively acquired antibodies may interfere with the immune response to live vaccines (measles, mumps, rubella, varicella). Delay vaccination for at least 3 months post-IGIV.
- **Loop Diuretics:** Avoid concomitant use as it may increase blood viscosity and the risk of thromboembolism [US].
- **Serological Testing:** Passive transfer of antibodies can result in misleading positive results in serological tests (e.g., Coombs' test).

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not specified; follow general IGIV guidance]. No human data available. IgG is known to cross the placenta, increasing in the third trimester. Use only if clearly needed.
- **Breastfeeding:** Immune globulins are excreted in human milk. Consider the clinical need of the mother and the potential effects on the infant.
- **Fertility:** Animal studies have not been conducted to evaluate effects on fertility.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established in paediatric patients [US].
- **Geriatric Use:** Seven patients aged ≥65 were included in clinical trials. Do not exceed the recommended dose and use the minimum infusion rate practicable [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Use with extreme caution. Discontinue if renal function deteriorates.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Post-marketing reports include hepatic dysfunction and increased liver enzymes.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** IgG replacement therapy. Provides a broad spectrum of opsonizing and neutralizing IgG antibodies against bacterial and viral agents to restore abnormally low IgG levels.
- **Pharmacokinetics [US]:**
    - **Half-life:** Approximately 24.5 days (3-week dosing) to 37.3 days (4-week dosing).
    - **Cmax:** 2680 mg/dL (3-week) vs 2300 mg/dL (4-week).
    - **Metabolism:** IgG and IgG-complexes are degraded by cells of the reticuloendothelial system.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Study Design:** Open-label, prospective study in 47 adults with PI (NCT03961009) [US].
- **Primary Outcome:** Incidence of acute serious bacterial infections (SBIs). 
- **Results:** The annualized rate of acute SBIs was 0 per person-year. The annualized rate of other infections was 2.1 per patient-year. No hospitalizations due to infection were reported during the 12-month study.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F).
- **Shelf Life:** Up to 36 months [US].
- **Handling:** Do not freeze. Protect from light by keeping the vial in the outer carton. Do not use after the expiration date.
- **Stability:** Once a vial is opened, it must be used immediately.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as Rx-only. Initial approval 2025.
