# Editorial Product Information: Quadracel

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on Product Information (PI) documents from the Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA), Australia, and the Food and Drug Administration (FDA), United States.
- **Purpose:** To provide a harmonised, clinically accurate overview for healthcare professionals by integrating regulatory data from multiple jurisdictions.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and should not be used as a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific Product Information when prescribing or administering this vaccine.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** Diphtheria toxoid, Tetanus toxoid, Acellular Pertussis antigens (Pertussis toxoid [PT], Filamentous haemagglutinin [FHA], Pertactin [PRN], and Fimbriae types 2 and 3 [FIM]), and Inactivated Poliovirus (IPV) Types 1, 2, and 3.
- **Brand Name:** **Quadracel**
- **ATC Code:** J07CA02
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Sterile, uniform, cloudy white to off-white suspension for injection. Each 0.5 mL dose contains:
    - Diphtheria Toxoid: 15 Lf (reported as $\ge$ 30 IU [TGA])
    - Tetanus Toxoid: 5 Lf (reported as $\ge$ 40 IU [TGA])
    - Pertussis Toxoid (PT): 20 mcg
    - Pertussis Filamentous Haemagglutinin (FHA): 20 mcg
    - Pertactin (PRN): 3 mcg
    - Pertussis Fimbriae 2 + 3 (FIM): 5 mcg
    - Poliovirus Type 1 (Mahoney): 29 D-antigen units
    - Poliovirus Type 2 (MEF-1): 7 D-antigen units
    - Poliovirus Type 3 (Saukett): 26 D-antigen units
    - Adjuvant: Aluminium phosphate (0.33 mg aluminium)
- **Route of Administration:** Intramuscular (IM) injection only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Sanofi (Sanofi-aventis Australia Pty Ltd [TGA]; Sanofi Vaccines US Inc. [FDA]).

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Primary Immunisation:** Indicated for the primary vaccination series in infants from 2 months to 12 months of age against diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis, and poliomyelitis [TGA-approved; not FDA-approved for this age].
- **Booster Vaccination:**
    - Indicated as a fourth dose in children from 15 months to 6 years of age who received three prior doses of DTPa and polio vaccines [TGA].
    - Indicated as a fifth dose in the DTaP series and a fourth or fifth dose in the IPV series in children 4 through 6 years of age whose previous DTaP doses were Pentacel, Daptacel, and/or Vaxelis [FDA].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing:** A single 0.5 mL dose administered intramuscularly.
- **Immunisation Schedule:**
    - **Infant Series:** Three doses at 2, 4, and 6 months of age [TGA].
    - **Preschool Booster:** A single dose administered between 15 months and 6 years [TGA] or 4 through 6 years [FDA].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Shake the vial or pre-filled syringe well to maintain a uniform suspension.
    - **Injection Site:** Anterolateral thigh (preferred for infants <12 months) or deltoid muscle (older children) [TGA]. The FDA specifically recommends the deltoid muscle for the 4–6 year age group.
    - **Do not** administer intravascularly or subcutaneously.
- **Concomitant Administration:** If multiple vaccines are administered, use separate syringes and different injection sites. **Quadracel** may be used to reconstitute Act-HIB (Haemophilus influenzae Type b vaccine) where indicated [TGA].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Severe allergic reaction (e.g., anaphylaxis) after a previous dose of any DTPa or IPV-containing vaccine, or to any component of **Quadracel** (including trace residuals: neomycin, streptomycin sulfate, polymyxin B sulfate, glutaraldehyde, and formaldehyde).
- **Encephalopathy:** Occurring within 7 days of a previous pertussis-containing vaccine not attributable to another identifiable cause.
- **Neurologic Disorder:** Progressive neurological disorders (e.g., infantile spasms, uncontrolled epilepsy, or progressive encephalopathy). Vaccination should be deferred until the condition has stabilised.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Prior Pertussis Vaccine Events:** Clinical judgment is required if the following occurred within the specified timeframes after a prior pertussis vaccine:
    - Temperature $\ge$ 40.5°C within 48 hours (not due to identifiable cause).
    - Collapse or shock-like state (Hypotonic-Hyporesponsive Episode, HHE) within 48 hours.
    - Persistent, inconsolable crying lasting $\ge$ 3 hours within 48 hours.
    - Seizures (with or without fever) within 3 days.
- **Guillain-Barré Syndrome (GBS) and Brachial Neuritis:** If GBS occurred within 6 weeks of a prior tetanus-toxoid vaccine, the decision to give **Quadracel** must weigh potential benefits against risks.
- **Altered Immune Status:** Immunogenicity may be reduced in patients receiving immunosuppressive therapy or those with immunodeficiency.
- **Bleeding Disorders:** Administer with caution to individuals with thrombocytopenia or coagulation disorders due to the risk of bleeding following IM injection.
- **Syncope:** Can occur in association with administration of injectable vaccines; procedures should be in place to prevent falling injuries.
- **Apnoea in Premature Infants:** The potential risk of apnoea and the need for respiratory monitoring for 48–72 hours should be considered for very premature infants (born $\le$ 28 weeks gestation) [TGA].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions are most frequent within 24 hours of vaccination.

### Frequency of Solicited Reactions (Children 4–6 years) [FDA]
| Reaction | Frequency (%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Injection Site** | |
| Pain | >75% |
| Increase in arm circumference | >65% |
| Erythema | >55% |
| Swelling | >40% |
| **Systemic** | |
| Myalgia | >50% |
| Malaise | >35% |
| Headache | >15% |
| Fever ($\ge$ 38.0°C) | 6% |

### Pediatric Series Reactions (2, 4, 6, and 18 months) [TGA]
- **Very Common (>10%):** Redness, swelling, tenderness, fever, fussiness, crying, decreased activity, decreased eating.
- **Common (1–10%):** Vomiting, diarrhoea.

### Serious Adverse Effects (Post-Marketing)
- Anaphylactic reactions, convulsions (including febrile), HHE, somnolence, and large injection site reactions (>50 mm), including limb swelling extending beyond joints.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Immunosuppressants:** Therapies including radiation, antimetabolites, alkylating agents, cytotoxic drugs, and corticosteroids (in higher than physiologic doses) may reduce the immune response to the vaccine.
- **Concomitant Vaccines:** **Quadracel** was evaluated and found compatible with MMR and Varicella vaccines in children 4–6 years [FDA]. In Australia, it may be used to reconstitute Act-HIB [TGA].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Not applicable. The vaccine is not intended for administration to women of child-bearing age. It has not been evaluated for effects on fertility or fetal harm.
- **Breastfeeding:** Not applicable.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** 
    - Safety and effectiveness are not established in infants < 2 months or children 7–16 years of age.
    - **Premature Infants:** Should be vaccinated according to their chronological age from birth if their clinical condition is satisfactory [TGA].
- **Elderly/Adult Use:** Not indicated for use in adults.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Induces active immunity by stimulating the production of specific antibodies against diphtheria toxin, tetanus toxin, *Bordetella pertussis* antigens, and poliovirus types 1, 2, and 3.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** 
    - **Diphtheria:** Protective antitoxin level is $\ge$ 0.01 IU/mL.
    - **Tetanus:** Protective antitoxin level is $\ge$ 0.01 IU/mL (neutralization assay) or $\ge$ 0.1 IU/mL (ELISA).
    - **Poliomyelitis:** Neutralising antibody titres $\ge$ 1:8 are correlated with protection.
    - **Pertussis:** No well-established serological correlate of protection exists; efficacy is based on antibody responses to PT, FHA, PRN, and FIM.
- **Pharmacokinetics:** As a vaccine, standard pharmacokinetic studies (ADME) are not performed.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Protective Efficacy:** Efficacy against pertussis was supported by studies of a similar acellular pertussis vaccine (Tripacel) in Sweden, showing an absolute vaccine efficacy of 85% (95% CI: 81%–89%) against WHO-defined pertussis.
- **Immunogenicity (Study M5I02):** In US children 4–6 years, **Quadracel** was non-inferior to separate administration of Daptacel and IPOL regarding booster response rates and antibody Geometric Mean Concentrations (GMCs) for all components.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 2°C to 8°C (35°F to 46°F).
- **Handling:** **REFRIGERATE. DO NOT FREEZE.** Discard if the vaccine has been frozen. Protect from light.
- **Shelf Life:** Refer to the expiry date on the packaging. Use immediately after opening/reconstitution.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Australia:** Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine) [TGA].
- **United States:** Rx-only [FDA].
- **Note:** Vials and pre-filled syringes are not made with natural rubber latex [FDA].
