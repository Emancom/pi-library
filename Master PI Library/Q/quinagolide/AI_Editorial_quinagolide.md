# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical data and regulatory requirements into a single reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI or the statutory requirements for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory documents for specific legal and prescribing mandates.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP).
- **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule/amino acid derivative; (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection (generic) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - Sterile injection: 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency (Metabolic):** Indicated for the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism which result in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
- **General Dose:** 50 mg/kg administered as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion.
- **Loading Dose:** In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose is often followed by an equivalent dose divided over the next 24 hours.
- **Frequency:** Administer every 3 to 4 hours (q3h/q4h) and never less frequently than every 6 hours (q6h) [US].
- **Subsequent Dosing:** Daily maintenance doses are typically 50 mg/kg, though higher doses up to 300 mg/kg have been used.

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight.
- **Administration:** Administer as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line after each dialysis session [US].
- **Dose Adjustments:** Guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Adjustments (e.g., down to 5 mg/kg) can be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].

### Monitoring and Administration Instructions
- Obtain baseline plasma carnitine levels prior to therapy.
- Monitor weekly and monthly; target free carnitine concentrations should be between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].
- Visually inspect for particulate matter or discoloration before use.

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known** [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions may occur within minutes of IV administration. Discontinue immediately if a reaction occurs [US].
- **Seizures:** Levocarnitine may cause seizures in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with a known history, an increase in seizure frequency or severity has been reported [US].
- **Oral Formulation Cautions:** Chronic administration of high-dose *oral* levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide), which are normally excreted in urine [US].
- **Monitoring:** Periodic blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations are recommended [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects in ESRD patients are often confounded by underlying pathology. 

### Clinical Trial Experience (ESRD patients at ≥5% frequency)
| Body System | Adverse Event |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Chest pain, fever, headache, injection site reaction (33%), pain (30%), flu syndrome [US] |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension (20%), hypotension (14%), tachycardia [US] |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea (16%), vomiting (15%), nausea (8%), abdominal pain [US] |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness (13%), paresthesia (12% at high dose), insomnia, depression [US] |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis (23%), cough increase, dyspnea, rhinitis [US] |
| **Other** | Hypercalcemia (9%), anemia, pruritus, leg cramps [US] |

### Postmarketing Experience
- **Serious:** Anaphylaxis, seizures, laryngeal edema, bronchospasm [US].
- **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea and vomiting; body odor and gastritis occur less frequently [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have been observed when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels in patients on warfarin therapy after initiating levocarnitine or following dose adjustments [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not explicitly assigned in modern label; use only if clearly needed]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine concentration in milk following administration. Risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant should be weighed against the benefits of supplementation for the mother [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing recommendations for metabolic disorders apply to paediatric patients. Specific age-range safety studies are not detailed, but use is established in this population for inborn errors of metabolism [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in provided sources [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Primarily indicated for ESRD on dialysis. Safety and efficacy of *oral* levocarnitine have not been evaluated in renal insufficiency [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring carrier molecule required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent ATP production [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In patients with inborn errors of metabolism or ESRD, levocarnitine promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids (as acylcarnitine esters), alleviating metabolic autointoxication and acidosis [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours (excluding endogenous carnitine). Terminal elimination half-life is approximately 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Trials:** Two double-blind, placebo-controlled trials in chronic hemodialysis patients (n=130 on drug) demonstrated that IV levocarnitine (10, 20, or 40 mg/kg) significantly increased plasma carnitine concentrations. Trough levels remained stable or increased over 24 weeks of therapy. Clinical outcomes regarding signs and symptoms of deficiency in this population have not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C [US].
- **Precautions:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat.
- **Handling:** Single-dose vials; discard any unused portion as the formulation contains no preservative [US].
- **Stability:** Compatible with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer's (0.5 mg/mL to 8.0 mg/mL) for up to 24 hours at room temperature in PVC bags [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Rx-only. NDC 36000-368-10 (10 x 5 mL vials) [US].
