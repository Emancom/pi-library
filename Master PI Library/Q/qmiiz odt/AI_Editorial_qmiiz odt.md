# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial has been synthesised from the following official Product Information (PI) source: United States (FDA).
- **Purpose:** This document is a harmonised editorial overview intended for use by healthcare professionals. It provides a clinically focused synthesis of the available regulatory data.
- **Note:** This editorial is not a substitute for the official PI and should not be used as the sole basis for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians must refer to the current approved local PI for their respective region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (generic name); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection, USP (Baxter Healthcare Corporation) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Clear, colorless sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) single-dose vial [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Indicated for the acute and chronic management of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that lead to secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with end-stage renal disease who are undergoing maintenance dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    - The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg.
    - Administration: Slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or intravenous infusion.
    - Loading Dose: In severe metabolic crisis, a loading dose may be given, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours.
    - Frequency: Dosing should be administered every 3 or 4 hours (q3h or q4h) and never less than every 6 hours (q6h).
    - Maximum Dose: The highest administered dose reported is 300 mg/kg.
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - Recommended starting dose: 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight.
    - Administration: Slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line after each dialysis session.
    - Titration: Initiation of therapy may be prompted by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma concentrations <40 µmol/L to 50 µmol/L. 
    - Adjustments: Dose adjustments (e.g., reduction to 5 mg/kg) may be made as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough levocarnitine levels.

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products should be inspected for particulate matter and discoloration prior to use.
- **Compatibility:** Stable and compatible with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer's for up to 24 hours at room temperature in PVC bags, in concentrations ranging from 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in patients with ESRD on dialysis. Reactions may occur within minutes of administration. If severe reactions occur, discontinue the drug immediately and do not re-administer without careful risk-benefit assessment [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Accumulation of Metabolites:** In patients with severely compromised renal function or ESRD on dialysis, chronic high-dose administration of *oral* levocarnitine (not specified for IV) may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide). The safety of the oral formulation has not been evaluated in renal insufficiency [US].
- **Monitoring:** 
    - In metabolic disorders, monitor plasma free carnitine concentrations (target: 35–60 µmol/L), blood chemistries, and vital signs weekly and monthly.
    - In ESRD, monitor trough (pre-dialysis) plasma concentrations.

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are often confounded by the underlying pathology of ESRD or metabolic disorders.

### Clinical Trial Experience (ESRD Patients)
- **Very Common (>10%):** 
    - *General:* Injection site reaction (33%), Pain (30%), Flu syndrome (25%), Headache (22%), Chest pain (12%), Accidental injury (10%).
    - *Cardiovascular:* Hypertension (20%), Hypotension (14%).
    - *Digestive:* Diarrhea (16%), Vomiting (15%).
    - *Respiratory:* Pharyngitis (23%).
    - *Nervous:* Dizziness (13%).
- **Common (1–10%):** 
    - *General:* Abdominal pain (9%), Asthenia (9%), Back pain (8%), Fever (7%), Infection (15%), Allergic reaction (2%).
    - *Cardiovascular:* Tachycardia (6%), Palpitations (5%), Arrhythmia (2%), Atrial fibrillation (2%).
    - *Digestive:* Nausea (8%), Anorexia (5%), Dyspepsia (5%), Constipation (3%), Melena (2%).
    - *Metabolic:* Hypercalcemia (9%), Hyperkalemia (6%), Hypervolemia (5%), Peripheral edema (5%), Weight increase/decrease (2-5%).
    - *Respiratory:* Cough increase (9%), Dyspnea (7%), Rhinitis (9%), Sinusitis (2%), Bronchitis (3%).
    - *Neurological:* Insomnia (4%), Paresthesia (5%), Vertigo (2%), Hypertonia (1%).
    - *Other:* Pruritus (5%), Rash (3%), Anemia (6%), Urinary tract infection (2%), Kidney failure (6%).

### Postmarketing Experience
- **Neurologic:** Seizures (in patients with or without pre-existing conditions) [US].
- **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea and vomiting, body odor, and gastritis have been observed with chronic use [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have been observed when levocarnitine is used concomitantly with warfarin. Monitor INR levels at initiation and after dose adjustments [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not specified by letter, but reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine concentrations in milk after administration. Risks of excess intake to the child must be weighed against maternal benefits. Consideration may be given to discontinuing nursing or the drug [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing recommendations for metabolic disorders apply to paediatric patients (50 mg/kg) [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Specific data not provided in the source document.
- **Renal Impairment:** See "Dosage and Administration" for ESRD-specific protocols. Oral levocarnitine safety/efficacy has not been evaluated in renal insufficiency [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring carrier molecule required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent ATP production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin. Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites are trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces).
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours. Mean apparent terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours.
    - **Bioavailability:** Absolute bioavailability of oral formulations is approximately 15–16% [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients, 20 mg/kg IV three times weekly post-dialysis increased mean pre-dialysis plasma levocarnitine from 20 µmol/L to 190 µmol/L after 8 weeks. Similar increases were noted in dose-ranging studies (10, 20, and 40 mg/kg) over 12 and 24 weeks. While carnitine concentrations increased, the effect on clinical outcomes in the ESRD population has not been determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat. Discard any unused portion as the formulation is preservative-free [US].
