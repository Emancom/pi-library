## 3. Approved Indications
- Prevention and treatment of vitamin and fluoride deficiencies in patients at high risk for dental caries and with inadequate dietary intake of essential vitamins.
- Clinical dietary management intended to support normal growth and development, promote bone and dental health, and help maintain metabolic and immune function.
- Pediatric supplementation context: It is presented for use in children where water fluoride is suboptimal; the product is identified for children in the 6–16 year age range in areas where drinking-water fluoride is low in the source text. [US]

## 4. Dosage and Administration
- General dosing (adult and pediatric): One tablet daily.
- Dosing by indication:
  - Preventive supplementation for vitamin and fluoride deficiencies: one tablet daily.
  - Pediatric supplementation: product text describes use for children (see Section 10). [US]
- Administration instructions:
  - Tablet should be chewed or allowed to dissolve in the mouth prior to swallowing.
  - Do not exceed the recommended/suggested dose; excessive fluoride intake can cause dental fluorosis.
  - Do not eat or drink dairy products within one hour after fluoride administration.
  - Chewable tablet formulations are not recommended for children under 4 years of age because of the risk of choking.
- Titration schedules: Not specified in available sources

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
- **KEEP OUT OF THE REACH OF CHILDREN. In case of accidental overdose, seek professional assistance or contact a Poison Control Center immediately.** [US]
- Do not exceed the suggested dose; prolonged ingestion of large amounts of fluoride may cause dental fluorosis.
- Dental hygiene and monitoring:
  - Children using long-term fluoride supplementation should receive semiannual dental examinations.
  - Before prescribing or recommending supplementation, determine the fluoride content of drinking water from all major sources.
  - Ensure the child is not receiving significant additional fluoride from other sources (for example, other supplements, medications, or swallowed toothpaste).
  - Periodically assess for dental fluorosis during use.
- Administration precautions:
  - Chewable tablets are not recommended for children under 4 years due to choking risk.
  - Avoid intake of dairy products within one hour of fluoride administration (product-specific instruction).
- Folate-specific precaution:
  - Folate in doses above 0.1 mg daily may mask hematological signs of pernicious anemia while neurologic manifestations continue to progress; this can delay diagnosis and result in severe neurologic damage.
  - Adequate vitamin B12 replacement can prevent, halt, or improve neurologic changes caused by pernicious anemia; consider a patient’s B12 status before or during folate therapy.
- Phenylalanine content:
  - Product contains phenylalanine (amount specified in source); consider this in patients with phenylketonuria or related disorders.
- Drug/herb/supplement considerations:
  - Consider the patient’s concurrent medications, herbal products, and dietary supplements when prescribing or dispensing this product.
- Monitoring requirements:
  - Dental examinations (semiannually) to monitor for dental fluorosis and oral health status.
  - Monitor hematologic and neurologic status if folate therapy is used in patients at risk of B12 deficiency.

## 11. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of action
- Fluoride: The fluoride ion interacts with tooth hydroxyapatite to form fluorapatite, a more caries-resistant crystalline form. Three stages of fluoride deposition in enamel are described:
  1. Small amounts are incorporated into enamel crystals during their formation.
  2. After enamel formation, fluoride continues to deposit in the surface enamel, with limited inward diffusion.
  3. After tooth eruption, surface enamel acquires fluoride from water, food, supplementary fluoride, and saliva, providing topical benefit.
- Vitamins: Specific mechanisms for each included vitamin are not described in detail in the available sources; the product is intended to supplement dietary vitamin intake and support metabolic functions related to growth, bone, dental, metabolic, and immune health.
