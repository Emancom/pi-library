## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (Generic name); chemical name: (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
*   **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
*   **Brand Names:** **Levocarnitine Injection, USP** [US].
*   **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    *   Intravenous injection: 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) single-dose vials [US].
*   **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation (Manufactured by Baxter Pharmaceuticals India Private Ltd) [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency (Metabolic):** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
*   **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
*   **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg.
    *   **Administration:** Delivered as a slow 2- to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion [US].
    *   **Loading Dose:** Often administered in patients with severe metabolic crisis, followed by an equivalent dose over the next 24 hours [US].
    *   **Frequency:** Should be administered every 3 or 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours [US].
    *   **Maximum Dose:** The highest administered dose reported is 300 mg/kg [US].
*   **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:** 
    *   **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
    *   **Administration:** Slow 2- to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].

### Titration and Monitoring
*   **Monitoring:** Weekly and monthly monitoring is recommended. Blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations should be tracked [US].
*   **Target Concentration:** Plasma free carnitine levels should ideally be maintained between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].
*   **Dose Adjustments (ESRD):** Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levels [US].

### Administration Instructions
*   Inspect visually for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration.
*   **Compatibility:** Compatible and stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer's (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
*   None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warnings
*   None [US].

### General Warnings and Precautions
*   **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm have occurred, primarily in ESRD patients. Some reactions occur within minutes of injection. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and monitor for recurrence if re-administration is attempted [US].
*   **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with and without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with known seizure disorders, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
*   **Metabolite Accumulation:** Chronic high doses of **oral** levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function or ESRD may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) [US].

### Monitoring Requirements
*   **Warfarin Therapy:** Reports of INR increase have been observed. Monitor INR levels after initiating levocarnitine or following dose adjustments in patients on warfarin [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **Warfarin:** Pharmacodynamic interaction resulting in increased INR and potential bleeding risk. **Management:** Monitor INR closely [US].
*   **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin; therefore, displacement interactions are unlikely [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy:** 
    *   **Category:** Not explicitly categorized by letter, but reproductive studies in rats/rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm [US].
    *   **Recommendation:** Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
*   **Breastfeeding:** Not specifically studied in humans. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine concentrations in milk following administration. Risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant must be weighed against maternal benefits; consider discontinuing nursing or the drug [US].
*   **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use:** Defined in the context of inborn errors of metabolism and secondary deficiency in premature infants and newborns (deficiency defined as concentrations below age-related normals) [US].
*   **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Renal Impairment:** 
    *   **ESRD:** Specific dosing provided for dialysis patients (10–20 mg/kg) [US].
    *   **Precautions:** Safety of oral levocarnitine not evaluated in renal insufficiency [US].
*   **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into cellular mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production [US].

### Pharmacokinetics
*   **Distribution:** Not protein bound. Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours [US].
*   **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
*   **Elimination:** Following IV bolus, approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours. Terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   **ESRD Studies:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients undergoing hemodialysis showed that 20 mg/kg doses administered three times weekly post-dialysis increased mean pre-dialysis plasma levocarnitine concentrations from ~20 µmol/L to 190 ± 55 µmol/L by week 8 [US].
*   **Dose-Ranging Data:** Studies using 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses showed dose-dependent increases in trough concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
*   **Precautions:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat.
*   **Handling:** Vials are single-dose only; discard any unused portion as the formulation contains no preservatives [US].
