# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- **Jurisdictions contributing to this editorial:** United States (FDA).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of levocarnitine injection for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical data, regulatory requirements, and administration guidelines.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should refer to local regulatory guidelines for specific legal requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN/USP); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule/Amino acid derivative.
- **Brand Name:** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Clear, colorless sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Pediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
  - Recommended dose is 50 mg/kg administered as a slow 2- to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion [US].
  - Severe metabolic crisis: A loading dose is often followed by equivalent doses every 3 to 4 hours (never less than every 6 hours) via infusion or IV injection over the subsequent 24 hours [US].
  - Subsequent daily doses: Typically 50 mg/kg/day, though higher doses up to 300 mg/kg/day may be required [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
  - Recommended starting dose: 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
  - Administration: Slow 2- to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line after each dialysis session [US].
  - Titration: Dose adjustments (e.g., reduction to 5 mg/kg) may be guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations and should be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].

### Administration and Monitoring
- **Monitoring:** Obtain baseline plasma carnitine levels. Perform weekly and monthly monitoring including blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations [US].
- **Target Concentration:** Maintain plasma free carnitine concentrations between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer's (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) [US].
- **Visual Inspection:** Inspect for particulate matter and discoloration before use [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients on dialysis. Discontinue immediately if reactions occur [US].
- **Seizures:** Reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In patients with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Renal Impairment (Oral Formulation Note):** The safety of *oral* levocarnitine in renal insufficiency is unestablished. Chronic high-dose oral administration in ESRD may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) normally cleared by the kidneys [US].

### Serious/Post-marketing Adverse Effects
- **Gastrointestinal:** Gastritis, transient nausea, and vomiting.
- **Miscellaneous:** Body odor (infrequent) [US].
- **Neurological:** Seizures (see Warnings) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have occurred when used concomitantly with levocarnitine. Monitor INR levels at initiation and after dose adjustments in patients on warfarin [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not specified, but data provided]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (based on surface area) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine is a normal component of human milk. Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk concentrations after exogenous administration. Weigh the benefits of maternal supplementation against potential risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing guidelines are provided for both metabolic disorders and ESRD (see Section 3) [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Specifically indicated for use in ESRD patients on hemodialysis. Monitor for metabolite accumulation if oral forms are used [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is essential for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane for oxidation and energy production. In patients with inborn errors, it promotes the excretion of toxic organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
  - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
  - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (feces) [US].
  - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. Terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Efficacy:** Studies confirm that IV levocarnitine increases plasma carnitine concentrations in dialysis patients. In a dose-ranging study (10, 20, 40 mg/kg), trough concentrations increased significantly over 12 and 24 weeks. However, the impact on specific clinical outcomes in the ESRD population has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C–30°C [US].
- **Precautions:** Avoid excessive heat. Protect from freezing [US].
- **Stability:** Single-dose vial; contains no preservative. Discard any unused portion [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Prescription Only (Rx-only) [US].
