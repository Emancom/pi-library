# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating regulatory data into a single, clinically focused reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI or local prescribing guidelines required for clinical practice in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine [(R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt].
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection, USP (Baxter Healthcare Corporation) [United States].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [United States].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [United States].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency (Inborn Errors of Metabolism):** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [United States].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD) / Dialysis:** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with end-stage renal disease who are undergoing dialysis [United States].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Metabolic Disorders)
- **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg administered as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) or via infusion [United States].
- **Severe Metabolic Crisis:** A loading dose is often administered, followed by equivalent doses every 3 or 4 hours. Doses should not be spaced further than every 6 hours.
- **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses typically range from 50 mg/kg or as clinically required.
- **Maximum Reported Dose:** 300 mg/kg [United States].

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [United States].
- **Administration Timing:** Administer as a slow 2 to 3 minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
- **Dose Adjustments:** Guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy if levels are supratherapeutic [United States].

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration.
- **Compatibility:** Stable for up to 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations ranging from 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL [United States].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Known Hypersensitivity:** Not specified as a formal contraindication in available sources, but serious hypersensitivity reactions have been reported [United States].
- **Other:** None known [United States].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions can occur within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue immediately [United States].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with known seizure disorders, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [United States].
- **Monitoring Requirements:**
    - **Metabolic Disorders:** Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine: 35 to 60 µmol/L) [United States].
    - **ESRD:** Trough (pre-dialysis) plasma concentrations should be monitored to guide dosing.
- **Renal/Oral Caution:** Safety of oral levocarnitine in patients with renal impairment or ESRD has not been evaluated; chronic high-dose oral use may lead to accumulation of toxic metabolites (TMA and TMAO) [United States].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Very Common (>10%)
- **General:** Injection site reaction (33–38%), pain (21–35%), flu syndrome (15–29%), headache (3–37%), chest pain (6–15%).
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (18–21%), hypotension (3–19%).
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea (9–35%), vomiting (9–21%).
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (15–27%), cough increase (10–18%), rhinitis (6–11%), dyspnea (3–11%).
- **Other:** Dizziness (10–18%).

### Common (1–10%)
- **Digestive:** Abdominal pain, nausea, gastritis, anorexia, dyspepsia, melena.
- **Metabolic:** Hypercalcemia, hyperkalemia, peripheral edema, weight changes.
- **Neurologic:** Paresthesia, insomnia, depression, anxiety, hypertonia.
- **Special Senses:** Amblyopia, eye disorder, taste perversion.
- **Other:** Body odor, anemia, fever, asthenia, back pain.

### Postmarketing Experience
- **Serious:** Anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, bronchospasm, seizures [United States].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalised Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Clinical Management:** Monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after any dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin [United States].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [United States: Not formally categorised by letter]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [United States].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine is a normal component of human milk. Supplementation has not been specifically studied in nursing mothers. In dairy cows, milk concentrations increase after exogenous administration. Weigh the risks to the infant against the benefits to the mother [United States].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [United States].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders is based on body weight (mg/kg). See Section 3 for specific instructions [United States].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Dosing adjustments for ESRD patients are required based on pre-dialysis plasma concentrations (see Section 3). High-dose oral levocarnitine is cautioned in severe renal impairment due to metabolite accumulation [United States].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into cellular mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production. It also facilitates the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [United States].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not protein-bound; mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours [United States].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (urinary) and γ-butyrobetaine (fecal).
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. Apparent terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [United States].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Increases plasma levocarnitine concentrations in ESRD and metabolic deficiency states. Normal plasma free carnitine is approximately 40 to 50 µmol/L [United States].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients (20 mg/kg 3x weekly), trough concentrations rose from 21.3 µmol/L (baseline) to 190 µmol/L at 8 weeks.
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** Evaluated 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses over 24 weeks. Results showed dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine levels. While carnitine levels increased, the impact on specific clinical outcomes in the ESRD population has not been definitively determined [United States].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Protection:** Avoid excessive heat and protect from freezing.
- **Stability:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as it contains no preservative [United States].
