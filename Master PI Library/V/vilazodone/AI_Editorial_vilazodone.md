# Editorial Product Information: Vilazodone

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (Food and Drug Administration)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, consolidating regulatory guidance into a single, clinically focused reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory documents for legal compliance.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Vilazodone hydrochloride.
- **Chemical Class:** Selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor (SSRI) and 5-HT1A receptor partial agonist [US].
- **Brand Names:** **VIIBRYD** [US].
- **ATC Code:** N06AB12 (Not explicitly stated in the source but corresponds to this class).
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:**
    - **Film-coated tablets:** 10 mg (pink, oval), 20 mg (orange, oval), and 40 mg (blue, oval) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** AbbVie Inc. (Distributed by); Allergan Sales, LLC (Sponsor) [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Major Depressive Disorder (MDD):** Treatment of MDD in adult patients [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Target Dosage:** 20 mg to 40 mg once daily [US].
- **Administration Instruction:** Must be administered **with food**. Administration in a fasted state decreases absorption (AUC) by approximately 50% and peak concentrations (Cmax) by 60%, which may result in inadequate clinical efficacy [US].

### Titration Schedule
- **Initial Dose:** 10 mg once daily for 7 days [US].
- **Titration:** Increase to 20 mg once daily for at least 7 days [US].
- **Maximum Dose:** May be increased up to 40 mg once daily [US].

### Specific Instructions
- **Screening:** Prior to initiation, patients must be screened for bipolar disorder, mania, or hypomania [US].
- **Discontinuation:** Avoid abrupt cessation. A gradual taper is recommended. 
    - *Example 40 mg taper:* Reduce to 20 mg daily for 4 days, then 10 mg daily for 3 days [US].
    - *Example 20 mg taper:* Reduce to 10 mg daily for 7 days [US].
- **Missed Dose:** Take as soon as remembered. If it is nearly time for the next dose, skip the missed dose. Do not double the dose [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors (MAOIs):** Concomitant use with MAOIs is contraindicated. At least 14 days must elapse between the discontinuation of an MAOI and the initiation of vilazodone. Conversely, at least 14 days must elapse after stopping vilazodone before starting an MAOI [US].
- **Linezolid and Methylene Blue:** Initiation of vilazodone is contraindicated in patients being treated with linezolid or intravenous methylene blue due to the risk of serotonin syndrome [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warning: Suicidality [US]
- **Suicidal Thoughts and Behaviors:** Antidepressants increase the risk of suicidal thoughts and behaviors in pediatric and young adult patients (age 24 and younger). 
- **Monitoring:** Closely monitor all patients for clinical worsening and the emergence of suicidality, particularly during the initial months of therapy and during dose adjustments.
- **Pediatric Use:** Vilazodone is not approved for use in pediatric patients.

### General Warnings
- **Serotonin Syndrome:** Potentially life-threatening; risk is increased with concomitant use of other serotonergic agents (e.g., triptans, tricyclic antidepressants, fentanyl, lithium, tramadol, St. John's Wort) or drugs that impair serotonin metabolism.
- **Increased Risk of Bleeding:** Use of drugs that interfere with serotonin reuptake may increase the risk of bleeding events (e.g., GI bleeds). Caution is advised with concomitant NSAIDs, aspirin, or anticoagulants (e.g., warfarin).
- **Activation of Mania/Hypomania:** May occur in patients with bipolar disorder.
- **Seizures:** Use with caution in patients with a history of seizure disorders.
- **Angle-Closure Glaucoma:** Avoid use in patients with untreated anatomically narrow angles.
- **Hyponatremia/SIADH:** May occur, particularly in elderly patients or those taking diuretics.
- **Sexual Dysfunction:** May cause symptoms such as decreased libido or ejaculatory delay.

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Frequency Profile [US]
The most common adverse reactions (incidence ≥ 5% and at least twice the rate of placebo) are:
- **Gastrointestinal:** Diarrhea (26–29%), Nausea (22–24%), Vomiting (4–5%), Dry mouth (7–8%).
- **Psychiatric/Nervous System:** Insomnia (6–7%), Dizziness (6–8%).

### Other Reported Reactions
- **Common (1–10%):** Abdominal pain, dyspepsia, flatulence, somnolence, fatigue, palpitations, increased appetite, and increased weight.
- **Uncommon/Infrequent (0.1–1%):** Panic attack, migraine, tremor, sedation, blurred vision, night sweats, and ventricular extrasystoles.
- **Rare (<0.1%):** Cataracts.
- **Serious Postmarketing Reports:** Acute pancreatitis, sleep paralysis, hallucinations, suicide attempt, and severe skin reactions (e.g., Stevens-Johnson Syndrome/rash).

## 7. Drug Interactions
### Pharmacokinetic Interactions
- **Strong CYP3A4 Inhibitors (e.g., ketoconazole, clarithromycin):** Increase vilazodone exposure. The dose **should not exceed 20 mg daily** when co-administered [US].
- **Strong CYP3A4 Inducers (e.g., carbamazepine, rifampin):** Decrease vilazodone exposure. Consider increasing the vilazodone dose 2-fold (up to 80 mg daily) if co-administered for more than 14 days [US].
- **Digoxin:** Vilazodone may increase digoxin concentrations. Monitor serum digoxin levels and adjust the dose as necessary [US].

### Pharmacodynamic Interactions
- **Serotonergic Drugs:** Increased risk of serotonin syndrome.
- **Antiplatelet/Anticoagulants:** Increased risk of bleeding. Monitor INR carefully in patients on warfarin [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Classification:** Not specified by letter category in the current source, but includes a "Risk Summary."
    - **Clinical Considerations:** Exposure in the third trimester increases the risk of neonatal complications (respiratory distress, seizures, temperature instability) and Persistent Pulmonary Hypertension of the Newborn (PPHN).
    - **Maternal Risk:** Exposure in the month before delivery may increase the risk of postpartum hemorrhage [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** There are no data on the presence of vilazodone in human milk. The developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding should be considered alongside the mother's clinical need [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies in rats showed impaired male fertility at 30 times the Maximum Recommended Human Dose (MRHD); no effect on female fertility [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness not established in patients aged 7 to 17 years. Efficacy was not demonstrated in two 8-week trials [US].
- **Elderly Use:** No general dosage adjustment is recommended based on age, but dose selection should be conservative. There is a higher risk of hyponatremia in the elderly [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dosage adjustment is necessary for mild to severe renal impairment (GFR 15-90 mL/min) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dosage adjustment is necessary for mild to severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh score 5-15) [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Selective inhibition of serotonin reuptake and partial agonism at serotonergic 5-HT1A receptors. The specific contribution of 5-HT1A activity to the antidepressant effect is unknown [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Peak levels (Tmax) in 4–5 hours. Absolute bioavailability is 72% with food.
    - **Distribution:** Highly protein-bound (96–99%).
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily hepatic via CYP3A4, with minor contributions from CYP2C19 and CYP2D6.
    - **Elimination:** Terminal half-life is approximately 25 hours. Only 1% of the dose is recovered unchanged in urine [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- The efficacy of vilazodone was established in four multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled studies (8 to 10 weeks) in adults with MDD.
- **Primary Endpoint:** Change from baseline in the Montgomery-Asberg Depression Rating Scale (MADRS) total score.
- **Results:** Vilazodone (20 mg and 40 mg) demonstrated statistically significant improvement compared to placebo [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C – 30°C (59°F – 86°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Dispense in a tightly closed container.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Rx-only. Not a controlled substance [US].
- **Australian/European/Other:** Not specified in available sources.
