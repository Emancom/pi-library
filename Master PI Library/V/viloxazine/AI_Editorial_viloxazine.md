# Editorial Product Information: Viloxazine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (Food and Drug Administration)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, intended to consolidate clinical and regulatory data into a single, structured reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official PI for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should refer to the local approved labelling when prescribing.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Viloxazine (as viloxazine hydrochloride).
- **Chemical Class:** Selective norepinephrine reuptake inhibitor (SNRI).
- **Brand Name(s):** **Qelbree** [US].
- **ATC Code:** N06AX09 (Not explicitly stated in the source but is the standard classification).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Extended-release (ER) capsules: 100 mg, 150 mg, and 200 mg [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Supernus Pharmaceuticals, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Attention-Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD):** Indicated for the treatment of ADHD in adults and pediatric patients aged 6 years and older [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Pediatric Patients 6 to 17 Years)
- **Ages 6 to 11 years:** Start at 100 mg once daily. May titrate in increments of 100 mg per week. Maximum recommended dosage is 400 mg once daily [US].
- **Ages 12 to 17 years:** Start at 200 mg once daily. After one week, may titrate by an increment of 200 mg. Maximum recommended dosage is 400 mg once daily [US].

### General Dosing (Adults)
- **Adult patients:** Recommended starting dosage is 200 mg once daily. May titrate in increments of 200 mg weekly. Maximum recommended dosage is 600 mg once daily [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Administer once daily with or without food.
- Capsules must not be cut, crushed, or chewed.
- **Sprinkle method:** Capsules may be opened and the entire contents sprinkled onto a teaspoonful or tablespoonful of applesauce or pudding. Consume the mixture in its entirety without chewing. For pudding, consume within 15 minutes; for applesauce, consume within 2 hours. Do not store for future use [US].

### Renal Impairment
- **Severe Renal Impairment (eGFR <30 mL/min/1.73m²):** Initial dosage is 100 mg once daily. Titrate in weekly increments of 50 mg to 100 mg. Maximum recommended dosage is 200 mg once daily [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors (MAOIs):** Concomitant use or use within 14 days of discontinuing an MAOI is contraindicated due to the risk of hypertensive crisis [US].
- **CYP1A2 Substrates:** Concomitant administration with sensitive CYP1A2 substrates or those with a narrow therapeutic range (e.g., alosetron, duloxetine, ramelteon, tasimelteon, tizanidine, theophylline) is contraindicated [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warning: Suicidal Thoughts and Behaviors [US]
Clinical trials showed higher rates of suicidal ideation and behavior in patients (pediatric and adult) treated with viloxazine compared to placebo. 
- Closely monitor all patients for clinical worsening or emergence of suicidal thoughts, especially during the first few months of therapy and during dosage adjustments. 
- Families and caregivers should be advised to report any sudden changes in mood or behavior immediately.

### General Warnings
- **Blood Pressure and Heart Rate:** Viloxazine can increase heart rate and diastolic blood pressure. Assess cardiovascular status prior to initiation, following dose increases, and periodically during treatment [US].
- **Activation of Mania/Hypomania:** Use with caution in patients with bipolar disorder. Screen for personal or family history of suicide, bipolar disorder, and depression prior to initiation [US].
- **Somnolence and Fatigue:** Sedation and lethargy are common. Patients should be cautioned against operating hazardous machinery or driving until they know how the drug affects them [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are categorized based on frequency from clinical trial data.

### Very Common (>10%)
- **Pediatric:** Somnolence (16%), Headache (11%).
- **Adult:** Insomnia (23%), Headache (17%), Fatigue (12%), Nausea (12%), Decreased appetite (10%), Dry mouth (10%).

### Common (1–10%)
- **Pediatric:** Decreased appetite (7%), Upper respiratory tract infection (7%), Fatigue (6%), Abdominal pain (5%), Nausea (5%), Vomiting (4%), Insomnia (4%), Irritability (3%), Pyrexia (2%).
- **Adult:** Constipation (6%), Somnolence (6%), Dizziness (4%), Tachycardia (4%), Irritability (4%), Vomiting (4%), Gastroesophageal reflux disease (2%).

### Serious Adverse Effects
- Suicidal ideation and behavior.
- Clinically significant increases in blood pressure and heart rate.
- Activation of mania/hypomania.

## 7. Drug Interactions
### Pharmacokinetic Interactions
- **CYP1A2:** Viloxazine is a strong CYP1A2 inhibitor. Coadministration with sensitive CYP1A2 substrates is contraindicated [US].
- **CYP2D6:** Weak inhibitor; monitor for increased exposure of CYP2D6 substrates [US].
- **CYP3A4:** Weak inhibitor; monitor for increased exposure of CYP3A4 substrates [US].

### Management of Interactions
- **MAOIs:** Absolute contraindication [US].
- **Sensitive CYP1A2 Substrates:** Contraindicated for narrow therapeutic range drugs; not recommended for others. Dose reductions may be necessary if coadministration is unavoidable [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Pregnancy Category not assigned under current labelling rules; refers to animal data]. Animal studies suggest potential for maternal harm and fetal toxicities at doses near the maximum recommended human dose (MRHD). Discontinue viloxazine when pregnancy is recognized unless the benefits outweigh the risks [US]. A pregnancy registry is available for monitoring outcomes.
- **Breastfeeding:** Viloxazine and its metabolite (5-HVLX-gluc) are present in human milk. The relative infant dose (RID) is approximately 1%. Impact on milk production or the breastfed infant is unknown [US].
- **Fertility:** No impact on male or female fertility was observed in animal models at doses up to the MRHD [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy established for ages 6 to 17. Use in patients under 6 years has not been established [US]. Monitor for weight changes (potential weight loss in adolescents; reduced weight gain in younger children) [US].
- **Geriatric Use:** Clinical trials did not include sufficient numbers of patients aged 65 and older to determine if they respond differently than younger adults [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** No adjustment needed for mild-to-moderate impairment. Dose reduction required for severe impairment (eGFR <30 mL/min/1.73m²) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Exposure increases with severity of hepatic impairment. Information on specific dosing adjustments is not specified in the primary text, though caution is implied [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
The exact mechanism in ADHD is unclear but is primarily attributed to the inhibition of norepinephrine reuptake [US].

### Pharmacodynamics
- Inhibits the norepinephrine transporter (Ki = 0.13 µM).
- Exhibits partial agonist activity at serotonin 5-HT2C receptors (Ki = 0.66 µM) [US].
- Does not prolong the QT interval at 3x the maximum dose [US].

### Pharmacokinetics
- **Absorption:** Relative bioavailability is ~88%. Tmax is ~5 hours.
- **Food Effect:** High-fat meals decrease Cmax and AUC by ~9%, while sprinkling on applesauce decreases Cmax by ~10%.
- **Distribution:** 76–82% plasma protein bound.
- **Metabolism:** Primarily metabolized by CYP2D6, UGT1A9, and UGT2B15.
- **Elimination:** Primarily renal (90% in urine within 24 hours). Mean half-life is ~7 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pediatric (Studies 1, 2, & 3):** Three randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trials in patients 6–17 years demonstrated statistically significant improvements in ADHD Rating Scale (ADHD-RS-5) scores compared to placebo [US].
- **Adult (Study 4):** A flexible-dose study (200–600 mg) in adults 18–65 years showed significant reduction in ADHD Investigator Symptom Rating Scale (AISRS) scores compared to placebo [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F). Excursions are permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Keep out of reach of children.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources (US does not use the Australian S-schedule system).
