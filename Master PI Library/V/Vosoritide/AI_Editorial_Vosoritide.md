# Editorial Product Information: Vosoritide

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis was prepared using official Product Information from the following jurisdiction: **Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of vosoritide for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises clinical data and regulatory requirements into a single reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority's approved labelling for definitive prescribing requirements. Information regarding other jurisdictions (e.g., US, European, Canadian, Japanese) was not available in the provided source material and is noted as "Not specified in available sources" where applicable.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Vosoritide (a modified recombinant human type C natriuretic peptide [CNP]).
- **Chemical Class:** Natriuretic peptide receptor-B (NPR-B) agonist.
- **Brand Name(s):** **Voxzogo** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Powder and diluent for injection in three strengths: 0.4 mg, 0.56 mg, and 1.2 mg per vial [Australian].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Subcutaneous injection.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** BioMarin Pharmaceutical Australia Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Achondroplasia:** Indicated for the treatment of achondroplasia in paediatric patients whose epiphyses are not closed. Diagnosis must be confirmed by appropriate genetic testing [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Standard Dose:** 15 µg/kg body weight administered subcutaneously once daily.
- **Weight-Based Dosing:** Dosing is determined by the patient's actual body weight. The volume to be administered varies based on weight brackets to maintain consistent exposure (ranging from 0.12 mL to 0.50 mL depending on the vial strength used).
- **Duration of Treatment:** Therapy should be discontinued upon confirmation of closure of epiphyses or when growth velocity falls below 1.5 cm/year [Australian].

### Administration Instructions
- **Preparation:** Reconstitute only with the provided diluent (Water for Injections). Do not shake; gently swirl until dissolved.
- **Timing:** Inject at approximately the same time each day. 
- **Hydration:** Patients should be well-hydrated. It is recommended to consume a light snack and adequate fluid (e.g., water, milk, juice) approximately 30 minutes prior to injection to mitigate potential hypotensive effects.
- **Injection Sites:** Rotate sites (front middle thighs, lower abdomen [excluding 5 cm around the navel], top of buttocks, or back of upper arms). Do not inject into red, swollen, or tender areas.

### Missed Dose
- If a dose is missed, it may be administered within 12 hours of the scheduled time. If more than 12 hours have passed, the dose should be skipped, and the patient should resume the next scheduled dose the following day [Australian].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to the active substance (vosoritide) or any of the excipients (citric acid monohydrate, sodium citrate dihydrate, trehalose dihydrate, mannitol, methionine, and polysorbate 80) [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Blood Pressure Effects:** Vosoritide may cause transient decreases in blood pressure. In clinical trials, these events were typically mild and resolved without intervention. Patients with significant cardiac or vascular disease or those on anti-hypertensive medications were excluded from trials. Pre-injection hydration and snacks are recommended to reduce this risk [Australian].
- **Monitoring:** Monitor body weight, growth, and physical development every 3–6 months. Adjust dosage based on changes in body weight [Australian].
- **Instruction:** Caregivers and patients must be trained to recognise signs of decreased blood pressure (dizziness, fatigue, nausea) and instructed on appropriate management.

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Frequency Data
Adverse reactions observed in clinical trials are categorised by frequency below:

| Frequency | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Injection site reactions (85%), vomiting (27%), hypotension (asymptomatic and symptomatic) (13%), increased alkaline phosphatase |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Syncope, pre-syncope, dizziness, nausea, fatigue, femur fracture |
| **Post-marketing** | Hypertrichosis (abnormal hair growth) |

### Serious Adverse Effects
- **Hypersensitivity:** Potential for systemic reactions.
- **Femur Fracture:** Reported at a rate of 0.6 per 100 patient-years in extension studies, typically associated with trauma/falls [Australian].
- **Hypotension:** While usually transient, symptomatic episodes (dizziness/vomiting) occurred in 2% of patients [Australian].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Cytochrome P450 (CYP) Enzymes:** In vitro data indicate vosoritide does not inhibit or induce major CYP isoforms (1A2, 2B6, 2C8, 2C9, 2C19, 2D6, or 3A4/5) at clinical concentrations.
- **Transporters:** Low potential for interaction with major drug transporters (OAT1, OAT3, OCT1, OCT2, OATP1B1, OATP1B3, MATE1, MATE2-K, BCRP, P-gp, and BSEP).
- **General Assessment:** As a recombinant human protein, vosoritide is an unlikely candidate for traditional drug-drug interactions [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Australian Category B2:** Animal studies do not indicate direct reproductive toxicity. However, as a precaution, use should be avoided during pregnancy due to limited human data [Australian].
- **Breastfeeding:** Vosoritide is excreted in rat milk. Because a risk to newborns/infants cannot be excluded, use is not recommended during breastfeeding [Australian].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies in rats showed no effect on mating, fertility, or litter characteristics at doses up to 15 times the maximum recommended human dose [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy are supported in patients aged 0.3 to 15 years. The safety profile is similar across the paediatric age spectrum. 
- **Elderly Use:** Not indicated for use in the elderly as the drug requires open epiphyses for efficacy [Australian].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Safety and efficacy have not been evaluated. Based on the catabolic elimination mechanism, impairment is not expected to significantly alter pharmacokinetics [Australian].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Vosoritide binds to natriuretic peptide receptor-B (NPR-B). This binding antagonises the fibroblast growth factor receptor 3 (FGFR3) downstream signalling pathway (MAPK/ERK) at the level of RAF-1. By inhibiting this pathway, vosoritide promotes chondrocyte proliferation and differentiation, stimulating endochondral bone growth [Australian].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Rapidly absorbed; median $T_{max}$ of 15 minutes.
    - **Distribution:** Apparent volume of distribution ($V_d$) is 2,910 mL/kg.
    - **Metabolism:** Catabolic pathways; degraded into small peptide fragments and amino acids.
    - **Elimination:** Apparent clearance ($CL/F$) of 79.4 mL/min/kg. Mean half-life is approximately 28 minutes [Australian].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pivotal Study (ACH 111-301):** A 52-week, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled study in 121 patients (ages 5.1 to 14.9 years). 
- **Outcomes:** The vosoritide group (15 µg/kg/day) demonstrated a statistically significant improvement in annualised growth velocity (AGV). The LS mean difference in AGV compared to placebo was 1.57 cm/year ($p < 0.0001$).
- **Long-term Data:** Improvements in AGV were maintained for up to 5 years in extension studies, with a cumulative height gain of approximately 9.08 cm over historical controls [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Unopened Vials:** Store in a refrigerator at 2°C to 8°C. Do not freeze. Store in original package to protect from light.
- **Room Temperature Excursion:** Vials may be stored below 30°C for a single period of up to 90 days. Do not return to the refrigerator after storage at room temperature.
- **Reconstituted Solution:** Use immediately if possible. Chemical and physical stability is demonstrated for 3 hours at 25°C [Australian].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison/Schedule Classification:** 
    - **Australian:** Schedule 4 (S4) – Prescription Only Medicine.
- **Additional Monitoring:** This product is subject to additional monitoring in Australia to allow quick identification of new safety information [Australian].
