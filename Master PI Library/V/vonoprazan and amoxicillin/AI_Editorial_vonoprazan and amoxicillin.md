# Editorial Product Information: Vonoprazan, Amoxicillin, and Clarithromycin

## Editorial Note
- This editorial harmonisation is based on official Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical data into a single reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. At the time of this publication, only the United States PI was provided for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** 
    - **Vonoprazan** (as fumarate): Potassium-competitive acid blocker (PCAB).
    - **Amoxicillin** (as trihydrate): Penicillin-class antibacterial.
    - **Clarithromycin**: Macrolide antimicrobial.
- **Brand Names:** **VOQUEZNA TRIPLE PAK** (Vonoprazan/Amoxicillin/Clarithromycin); **VOQUEZNA DUAL PAK** (Vonoprazan/Amoxicillin) [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths [US]:**
    - **Vonoprazan:** 20 mg film-coated tablets.
    - **Amoxicillin:** 500 mg capsules.
    - **Clarithromycin:** 500 mg film-coated tablets.
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Phathom Pharmaceuticals, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Helicobacter pylori Infection:** Indicated for the treatment of *H. pylori* infection in adults [US].
- **Usage Note:** To prevent the development of drug-resistant bacteria, these products should only be used to treat infections proven or strongly suspected to be caused by susceptible bacteria [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Adult Dosing [US]
Both regimens are administered for **14 days**, with or without food.

- **VOQUEZNA TRIPLE PAK:** 
    - Vonoprazan 20 mg + Amoxicillin 1,000 mg + Clarithromycin 500 mg.
    - **Frequency:** Twice daily (morning and evening, approximately 12 hours apart).
- **VOQUEZNA DUAL PAK:**
    - **Vonoprazan:** 20 mg twice daily (morning and evening).
    - **Amoxicillin:** 1,000 mg **three times daily** (morning, mid-day, and evening).

### Missed Doses [US]
- If a dose is missed, it should be taken as soon as possible within 4 hours. 
- If more than 4 hours have passed, the missed dose should be skipped, and the next dose taken at the regularly scheduled time. 

## 4. Contraindications
### Applicable to Both Regimens [US]
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to **vonoprazan**, **amoxicillin** (or other $\beta$-lactams), or any component of the formulation.
- **Drug Interactions:** Concomitant use with **rilpivirine**-containing products.

### Additional Contraindications for TRIPLE PAK (Clarithromycin component) [US]
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to **clarithromycin** or other macrolides (e.g., erythromycin).
- **Serious Drug Interactions:** Concomitant use with:
    - **Pimozide** (risk of fatal arrhythmias).
    - **Lurasidone**.
    - **Statins:** **Lomitapide**, **lovastatin**, or **simvastatin**.
    - **Ergot Alkaloids:** Ergotamine or dihydroergotamine.
    - **Colchicine:** In patients with renal or hepatic impairment.
- **History:** Previous history of cholestatic jaundice or hepatic dysfunction associated with **clarithromycin** use.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious and occasionally fatal anaphylactic reactions have been reported with components of these products. Therapy must be discontinued immediately if an allergic reaction occurs [US].
- **Severe Cutaneous Adverse Reactions (SCAR):** Includes Stevens-Johnson syndrome (SJS) and toxic epidermal necrolysis (TEN). Discontinue at the first sign of a skin rash or other hypersensitivity symptoms [US].
- **Acute Tubulointerstitial Nephritis (TIN):** Reported with **vonoprazan**. If suspected, discontinue treatment and evaluate [US].
- **Clostridioides difficile-Associated Diarrhea (CDAD):** Reported with nearly all antibacterial agents and acid-suppressing therapies; severity may range from mild diarrhea to fatal colitis [US].
- **Drug-Induced Enterocolitis Syndrome (DIES):** Reported with **amoxicillin**, primarily in paediatric patients, though these products are approved only for adults [US].
- **Rash in Mononucleosis:** Amoxicillin should be avoided in patients with mononucleosis due to a high risk of erythematous skin rash [US].

### Triple Pak Specific (Clarithromycin) [US]
- **QT Prolongation:** Avoid in patients with known QT prolongation, ventricular arrhythmias (including Torsades de Pointes), or those on Class IA or III antiarrhythmics.
- **Hepatotoxicity:** Discontinue immediately if signs of hepatitis (jaundice, dark urine, pruritus) occur.
- **Embryo-Fetal Toxicity:** Not recommended for use in pregnancy except where no alternative therapy is appropriate.

- **Serious Reactions:** Anemia, leukopenia, neutropenia, thrombocytopenia, hepatic failure, and QT prolongation have been identified in postmarketing reports [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **CYP3A4 Interactions:** **Clarithromycin** is a strong CYP3A inhibitor; **vonoprazan** is a weak CYP3A inhibitor. Concomitant use with CYP3A substrates (e.g., **quetiapine**, **alprazolam**, **midazolam**) requires caution or dose adjustment [US].
- **Gastric pH-Dependent Drugs:** **Vonoprazan** increases gastric pH, which may significantly reduce the absorption of drugs like **atazanavir**, **nelfinavir**, **iron salts**, **erlotinib**, and **ketoconazole** [US].
- **CYP2C19 Substrates:** **Vonoprazan** is a CYP2C19 inhibitor and may decrease the efficacy of **clopidogrel** by reducing its active metabolite [US].
- **Anticoagulants:** Prolongation of prothrombin time/increased INR has been reported in patients receiving **amoxicillin** and oral anticoagulants concurrently [US].
- **Diagnostic Interference:** Treatment with **vonoprazan** increases Serum Chromogranin A (CgA) levels; testing for neuroendocrine tumors should be suspended for at least 4 weeks after stopping treatment [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Category:** Not specified by letter, but a Pregnancy Exposure Registry is available [US]. 
    - **Triple Pak:** Not recommended due to clarithromycin’s potential for embryo-fetal toxicity [US].
    - **Animal Data:** High doses of **vonoprazan** in rats resulted in fetal abnormalities (ventricular septal defects) and liver discoloration in offspring [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** 
    - **Vonoprazan** is present in human milk at <0.03% of the maternal dose. 
    - **Amoxicillin** and **Clarithromycin** are present in human milk. 
    - Monitor breastfed infants for diarrhea, vomiting, or rash [US].
- **Fertility:** **Clarithromycin** may impair male fertility based on animal findings (testicular atrophy) [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established [US].
- **Geriatric Use:** No overall differences in safety/efficacy observed for **vonoprazan**. However, elderly patients may be more susceptible to **clarithromycin**-induced QT prolongation [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    - Mild to Moderate (eGFR 30–89 mL/min): No dose adjustment.
    - Severe (eGFR <30 mL/min): **Use is not recommended** [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:**
    - Mild (Child-Pugh A): No dose adjustment.
    - Moderate to Severe (Child-Pugh B or C): **Use is not recommended** [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** 
    - **Vonoprazan:** Inhibits $H^+, K^+$-ATPase (the proton pump) in a potassium-competitive, non-covalent manner. It does not require acid activation [US].
    - **Amoxicillin/Clarithromycin:** Provide bactericidal/bacteriostatic activity against *H. pylori* [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics [US]:**
    - **Absorption:** **Vonoprazan** reaches steady state by Day 3–4. Food has no clinically significant effect.
    - **Metabolism:** **Vonoprazan** is metabolised via multiple CYP enzymes (predominantly 3A4/5, 2C19, 2D6, and 2B6).
    - **Excretion:** **Vonoprazan** is excreted in urine (67%) and feces (31%).

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pivotal Study (NCT04167670):** A randomised, double-blind triple therapy/open-label dual therapy study vs. lansoprazole/amoxicillin/clarithromycin (LAC) for 14 days in *H. pylori*-positive adults [US].
- **Outcomes:** 
    - Both **VOQUEZNA** regimens were non-inferior to LAC in patients without resistant strains.
    - Both regimens were **superior** to LAC in the overall population and in patients with clarithromycin-resistant strains [US].
    - Eradication rate for Triple Pak: 80.8% vs. 68.5% for LAC [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage Temperature:** 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from light [US].
