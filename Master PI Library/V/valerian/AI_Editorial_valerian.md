## 2. Approved Indications
The following indications are based on traditional homeopathic practice and have not been evaluated by the FDA [US]:
- Relief of symptoms related to stress, tension, and separation anxiety in animals (including dogs, cats, rabbits, and birds).
- Management of behavioral symptoms associated with:
    - Travel and boarding.
    - Veterinary visits.
    - Sensitivity to loud noises (e.g., thunderstorms, fireworks).
- Specific symptom targets:
    - Anxiety, restlessness, and sleep disturbances (*Chamomilla*).
    - Irritability and aggressiveness (*Hyoscyamus niger*).
    - Sensitivity and moodiness (*Ignatia amara*).
    - Sadness or depressive moods following loss or separation (*Natrum muriaticum*).
    - Nervousness and mental restlessness (*Passiflora incarnata*).
    - General irritability (*Sepia*).
    - Hyperexcitation (*Valeriana officinalis*).

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Veterinary)
- **Standard Dose:** 10 drops administered 3 times daily.
- **Administration:** Doses should be given approximately 30 minutes before meals. Drops may be administered directly into a water bowl or placed on a treat.

### Acute Dosing
- **Acute Cases:** 10 drops every 10 minutes.
- **Maximum Frequency:** Up to 6 consecutive intakes per day.

### Titration and Maintenance
- As symptoms improve, frequency should be reduced to twice daily, then once daily.
- Discontinue use once symptoms have fully disappeared.
- Treatment may be recommenced at original doses if symptoms reappear.
