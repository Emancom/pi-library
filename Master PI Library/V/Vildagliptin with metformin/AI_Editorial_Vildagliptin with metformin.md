# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine Injection

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised, clinically focused overview of **levocarnitine** injection for use by healthcare professionals.
- **Editorial Disclaimer:** This document is an expert synthesis and does not replace the official PI. Clinicians must refer to the approved PI in their specific jurisdiction before prescribing or administering this medication.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP).
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter Healthcare Corporation) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Acute and chronic management of patients with inborn metabolic errors resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US-approved].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing hemodialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg administered as a slow bolus injection (2–3 minutes) or by infusion.
    - **Acute Crisis:** A loading dose is often employed, followed by an equivalent dose divided over the subsequent 24 hours.
    - **Maintenance:** Administer every 3 or 4 hours; intervals should never be less than every 6 hours.
    - **Maximum Dose:** Doses up to 300 mg/kg have been administered in clinical practice [US].
- **ESRD on Hemodialysis:** The recommended starting dose is 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight).
    - **Timing:** Administer as a slow bolus (2–3 minutes) into the venous return line following each dialysis session.

### Administration Instructions
- Inspect for particulate matter or discoloration prior to use.
- Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial.

### Titration and Monitoring
- **Monitoring:** Obtain baseline plasma carnitine levels. Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma carnitine levels (target range: 35–60 µmol/L) is recommended [US].
- **Dose Adjustment (ESRD):** Adjustments may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy, guided by pre-dialysis (trough) plasma carnitine concentrations.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Known Hypersensitivity:** No specific contraindications are listed in the provided source; however, see Warnings for hypersensitivity reactions.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions can occur within minutes of IV administration.
- **Seizures:** Levocarnitine may increase the frequency or severity of seizures in patients with pre-existing seizure disorders. Seizures have also been reported in patients without a prior history of epilepsy [US].
- **Metabolite Accumulation:** Chronic high-dose oral administration (not specifically injection) in patients with severe renal impairment may lead to the accumulation of trimethylamine (TMA) and trimethylamine-N-oxide (TMAO), which are potentially toxic.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels regularly after initiating levocarnitine or following dose adjustments in patients receiving anticoagulant therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Not categorized by letter in provided text, but data available]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly required.
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine is a normal component of human milk. Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant must be weighed against the benefits of supplementation for the mother [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders is weight-based (50 mg/kg), which applies to paediatric populations [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically detailed in provided sources; however, dose selection should generally be cautious, reflecting the greater frequency of decreased hepatic, renal, or cardiac function.
- **Renal Impairment:** **Levocarnitine injection** is specifically indicated for use in ESRD patients on hemodialysis. Safety of high-dose **oral** levocarnitine in renal failure is not established due to metabolite accumulation [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not protein-bound.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (feces).
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. Terminal elimination half-life is approximately 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients, 20 mg/kg IV three times weekly post-dialysis increased mean trough carnitine levels from 21.3 µmol/L to 190 µmol/L at 8 weeks.
- **Dose-Ranging:** Studies comparing 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses showed dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Precautions:** Avoid excessive heat and protect from freezing.
- **Compatibility:** Stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when diluted to concentrations of 0.5 mg/mL to 8.0 mg/mL in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s [US].

- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources.
