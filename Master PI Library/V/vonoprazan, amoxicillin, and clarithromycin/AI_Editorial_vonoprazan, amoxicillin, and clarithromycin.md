# Editorial Product Information: Vonoprazan, Amoxicillin, and Clarithromycin

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating the clinical profiles of vonoprazan, amoxicillin, and clarithromycin when co-packaged for therapeutic use.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** 
    - **Vonoprazan** (as fumarate): A potassium-competitive acid blocker (PCAB).
    - **Amoxicillin**: A penicillin-class antibacterial.
    - **Clarithromycin**: A macrolide antimicrobial.
- **Brand Names:** **VOQUEZNA TRIPLE PAK** (Vonoprazan/Amoxicillin/Clarithromycin) and **VOQUEZNA DUAL PAK** (Vonoprazan/Amoxicillin) [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths [US]:**
    - **Vonoprazan:** 20 mg film-coated tablets (pale red, oval, debossed "V20").
    - **Amoxicillin:** 500 mg capsules (yellow, opaque, imprinted "AMOX 500" and "GG 849").
    - **Clarithromycin:** 500 mg film-coated tablets (white, oval, debossed "GG C9").
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Phathom Pharmaceuticals, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Helicobacter pylori Infection:** Indicated for the treatment of *H. pylori* infection in adult patients [US].
- **Usage Note:** To mitigate the development of drug-resistant bacteria, these products should only be used to treat infections proven or strongly suspected to be caused by susceptible bacteria [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Adult Dosing
- **Triple Therapy (VOQUEZNA TRIPLE PAK):** Vonoprazan 20 mg plus amoxicillin 1,000 mg plus clarithromycin 500 mg. Each component is administered twice daily (morning and evening, 12 hours apart) for 14 days [US].
- **Dual Therapy (VOQUEZNA DUAL PAK):**
    - **Vonoprazan:** 20 mg twice daily (morning and evening).
    - **Amoxicillin:** 1,000 mg three times daily (morning, mid-day, and evening).
    - **Duration:** 14 days [US].

### Administration Instructions
- May be taken with or without food [US].
- **Missed Dose:** If a dose is missed, it should be taken as soon as possible within 4 hours. If more than 4 hours have elapsed, the dose should be skipped, and the next dose taken at the scheduled time. Do not double doses [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **General Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to vonoprazan, amoxicillin (or any $\beta$-lactam), clarithromycin (or any macrolide), or any component of the formulations [US].
- **Drug-Specific Contraindications [US]:**
    - **Rilpivirine-containing products:** Contraindicated due to vonoprazan's effect on gastric pH.
    - **Pimozide:** Risk of serious cardiac arrhythmias.
    - **Statins:** Lovastatin and simvastatin (clarithromycin component).
    - **Ergot Alkaloids:** Ergotamine or dihydroergotamine.
    - **Lurasidone:** Risk of serious adverse reactions.
    - **Colchicine:** Contraindicated in patients with renal or hepatic impairment.
    - **Hepatic Dysfunction:** History of cholestatic jaundice or hepatic dysfunction associated with prior clarithromycin use.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious and potentially fatal reactions (anaphylaxis, SCAR, DIES) have been reported. Therapy must be discontinued immediately at the first sign of a rash or hypersensitivity [US].
- **Acute Tubulointerstitial Nephritis (TIN):** May occur at any point during therapy; requires discontinuation and evaluation [US].
- **Clostridioides difficile-associated diarrhea (CDAD):** Reported with nearly all antibacterial agents; severity ranges from mild diarrhea to fatal colitis [US].
- **Clarithromycin-Specific Precautions [US]:**
    - **QT Prolongation:** Avoid in patients with known prolongation, ventricular arrhythmia, or significant bradycardia.
    - **Hepatotoxicity:** Monitor for signs of hepatitis (jaundice, dark urine, pruritus).
    - **Embryo-Fetal Toxicity:** Not recommended in pregnancy except where no alternative exists.
    - **Myasthenia Gravis:** Potential for exacerbation of symptoms.
- **Diagnostic Interference:** Increased Chromogranin A (CgA) levels due to gastric acid suppression may cause false-positive results in neuroendocrine tumor investigations. Stop treatment at least 4 weeks before CgA testing [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Vonoprazan (pH-Dependent):** Reduces absorption of drugs dependent on low gastric pH (e.g., iron salts, erlotinib, ketoconazole, certain antiretrovirals like atazanavir and nelfinavir) [US].
- **Clarithromycin (CYP3A4 Inhibition):** A strong CYP3A inhibitor. Contraindicated or requires caution with CYP3A substrates (e.g., quetiapine, warfarin, benzodiazepines, calcium channel blockers). It may significantly increase exposure to these agents, leading to toxicity [US].
- **Vonoprazan (CYP2C19 Inhibition):** May reduce the efficacy of clopidogrel by decreasing the formation of its active metabolite [US].
- **Probenecid:** Increases amoxicillin exposure by inhibiting renal tubular secretion [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Triple Therapy:** Not recommended due to clarithromycin. Animal data show fetal malformations and increased pregnancy loss [US].
    - **Dual Therapy:** Available human data on amoxicillin and vonoprazan have not established a clear drug-associated risk for major birth defects [US].
- **Breastfeeding:**
    - Vonoprazan, amoxicillin, and clarithromycin are present in human milk. 
    - **Clinical Consideration:** Monitor infants for diarrhea, vomiting, or rash. The developmental benefits should be weighed against the mother's clinical need [US].
- **Fertility:** Clarithromycin may impair male fertility based on animal findings [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** No overall differences in safety/efficacy observed for vonoprazan. Elderly patients may be more sensitive to clarithromycin-induced QT prolongation and amoxicillin-related renal risks [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    - **Mild to Moderate (eGFR 30–89 mL/min):** No dosage adjustment required.
    - **Severe (eGFR <30 mL/min):** Use is not recommended [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** 
    - **Mild (Child-Pugh A):** No dosage adjustment required.
    - **Moderate to Severe (Child-Pugh B or C):** Use is not recommended [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** 
    - **Vonoprazan:** Inhibits the H+, K+-ATPase enzyme system in a potassium-competitive, non-covalent, and reversible manner, suppressing both basal and stimulated gastric acid secretion.
    - **Amoxicillin:** Bactericidal; inhibits cell wall biosynthesis.
    - **Clarithromycin:** Bacteriostatic/Bactericidal; binds to the 50S ribosomal subunit to inhibit protein synthesis.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Vonoprazan onset is within 2–3 hours. Steady-state intragastric pH (mean 6.0) is typically reached by Day 4 [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Vonoprazan is metabolized primarily by CYP3A4/5 and is roughly 85–88% protein-bound. Amoxicillin and clarithromycin are largely excreted renally [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pivotal Study (NCT04167670):** A randomized, controlled, double-blind study (Triple therapy) and open-label (Dual therapy) conducted in the US and Europe.
- **Outcomes:** Vonoprazan-based triple and dual therapies were non-inferior to lansoprazole-amoxicillin-clarithromycin (LAC) in patients without resistant strains and superior to LAC in patients with clarithromycin-resistant strains and the overall population [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from light [US].
