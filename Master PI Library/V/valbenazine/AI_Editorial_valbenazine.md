# Editorial Product Information: Valbenazine

## Editorial Note
- Contributing Jurisdictions: United States (FDA)
- Purpose: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of valbenazine product information for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises regulatory data to ensure clinical accuracy and clarity.
- Disclaimer: This is an editorial synthesis intended for informational purposes and is not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient**: Valbenazine (present as valbenazine tosylate)
- **Chemical Class**: Vesicular monoamine transporter 2 (VMAT2) inhibitor
- **Brand Names**: **INGREZZA**, **INGREZZA SPRINKLE** [US]
- **ATC Code**: Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths**:
    - Oral Capsules: 40 mg, 60 mg, and 80 mg [US]
    - Oral Sprinkle Capsules: 40 mg, 60 mg, and 80 mg (granules for sprinkling on soft food or swallowing whole) [US]
- **Route of Administration**: Oral
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor**: Neurocrine Biosciences, Inc. [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Tardive Dyskinesia**: Treatment of adults with tardive dyskinesia [US-approved].
- **Huntington’s Disease Chorea**: Treatment of chorea associated with Huntington’s disease in adults [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults)
- **Tardive Dyskinesia**: Initial dose is 40 mg once daily. After one week, increase to the recommended dosage of 80 mg once daily. Maintenance doses of 40 mg or 60 mg once daily may be considered based on patient response and tolerability [US].
- **Chorea associated with Huntington’s Disease**: Initial dose is 40 mg once daily. Titrate in 20 mg increments every two weeks to the recommended dosage of 80 mg once daily. Maintenance doses of 40 mg or 60 mg once daily may be considered based on response and tolerability [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Administer once daily with or without food [US].
- **Standard Capsules**: Swallow whole with water.
- **Sprinkle Capsules**: May be swallowed whole with water or opened and the entire contents sprinkled onto one tablespoon of soft food (e.g., applesauce, yogurt, pudding). Consume the mixture immediately or within 2 hours of preparation if stored at room temperature. Follow with a glass of water. Do not sprinkle into milk or drinking water; do not crush or chew granules.
- **Enteral Tubes**: Do not administer via nasogastric or gastrostomy tubes as granules may cause obstruction [US].

### Missed Dose
- Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity**: Known hypersensitivity to valbenazine or any components of the formulation. Reported reactions include rash, urticaria, and angioedema [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warning [US]
- **Depression and Suicidal Ideation/Behavior in Huntington’s Disease**: VMAT2 inhibitors increase the risk of depression and suicidal thoughts/behavior in patients with Huntington’s disease. Clinicians must balance this risk against the clinical need for chorea treatment. Monitor patients closely for new or worsening depression or suicidal ideation. Inform caregivers of these risks.

### General Warnings
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions**: Angioedema involving the larynx, glottis, lips, and eyelids may occur. This can be fatal if laryngeal edema is present. Discontinue valbenazine immediately if hypersensitivity occurs [US].
- **Somnolence and Sedation**: May impair the ability to perform tasks requiring mental alertness (e.g., driving). Alcohol and other sedatives may potentiate this effect [US].
- **QT Prolongation**: Valbenazine may prolong the QT interval. Avoid use in patients with congenital long QT syndrome or arrhythmias associated with prolonged QT. Assessment is required for patients at increased risk prior to dose escalation [US].
- **Neuroleptic Malignant Syndrome (NMS)**: A potentially fatal complex involving hyperpyrexia, muscle rigidity, and autonomic instability has been reported. Discontinue treatment immediately and provide intensive symptomatic monitoring [US].
- **Parkinsonism**: Cases of drug-induced parkinsonism, including severe cases requiring hospitalisation, have been reported. Symptoms typically appear within the first two weeks of starting or increasing the dose. Reduce the dose or discontinue if clinically significant signs occur [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors (MAOIs)**: Avoid concomitant use. MAOIs may increase synaptic monoamine levels, increasing the risk of serotonin syndrome or reducing the efficacy of valbenazine. Allow a 14-day washout period [US].
- **Strong CYP3A4 Inhibitors**: (e.g., itraconazole, clarithromycin) Increase valbenazine exposure. Limit dose to 40 mg once daily [US].
- **Strong CYP2D6 Inhibitors**: (e.g., paroxetine, fluoxetine) Increase exposure of the active metabolite. Limit dose to 40 mg once daily [US].
- **Strong CYP3A4 Inducers**: (e.g., rifampin, carbamazepine) Significantly decrease exposure. Concomitant use is not recommended [US].
- **Digoxin**: Valbenazine inhibits intestinal P-glycoprotein (P-gp). Monitor digoxin levels; a dose adjustment of digoxin may be required [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy**: [US Category: Not assigned under current labelling rules]. Limited human data are available. Animal studies (rats) showed increased stillbirths and postnatal pup mortality at doses less than the maximum recommended human dose (MRHD). Use only if potential benefits outweigh risks to the fetus [US].
- **Breastfeeding**: Due to the potential for serious adverse reactions in nursing infants based on animal data, breastfeeding is not recommended during treatment and for 5 days after the final dose [US].
- **Fertility**: Animal studies showed delayed mating and disrupted estrous cyclicity in rats at 1.2 times the MRHD, though no effects on sperm parameters were noted [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use**: Safety and effectiveness have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use**: No dose adjustment is required. Clinical trials showed similar safety and efficacy profiles in patients ≥65 years compared to younger adults [US].
- **Renal Impairment**: No dose adjustment is necessary for patients with mild, moderate, or severe renal impairment. Valbenazine is not primarily cleared renally [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment**: For patients with moderate or severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh score 7 to 15), the recommended dosage is 40 mg once daily [US].
- **CYP2D6 Poor Metabolizers**: The recommended dosage is 40 mg once daily due to increased exposure of the active metabolite [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action**: Reversible inhibition of VMAT2, which regulates monoamine (dopamine, norepinephrine, serotonin) uptake from the cytoplasm into synaptic vesicles. This reduces monoamine release into the synaptic cleft [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics**: Valbenazine has high affinity for VMAT2 but no appreciable affinity for VMAT1 or other receptors (dopaminergic D2, serotonergic, adrenergic, histaminergic) [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics**:
    - **Absorption**: Absolute bioavailability is ~49%. Median Tmax is 0.5 to 1.0 hour.
    - **Distribution**: Plasma protein binding >99%. Volume of distribution is 92 L.
    - **Metabolism**: Extensively metabolised via hydrolysis (to active metabolite [+]-α-HTBZ) and oxidative metabolism primarily by CYP3A4/5. [+]-α-HTBZ is further metabolised by CYP2D6.
    - **Elimination**: Mean half-life is 15–22 hours. Excreted via urine (60%) and faeces (30%) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Tardive Dyskinesia**: A 6-week, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial used the Abnormal Involuntary Movement Scale (AIMS). Valbenazine 80 mg showed a statistically significant reduction in AIMS score (-3.2) compared to placebo (-0.1) [US].
- **Huntington’s Chorea**: A 12-week trial evaluated the Total Maximal Chorea (TMC) score. Valbenazine showed a mean improvement of 4.6 units vs 1.4 units for placebo (p<0.0001). Significant improvements were also noted in clinician and patient global impression scales [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Capsules**: Store at 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
- **Sprinkle Capsules**: Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F). Protect from moisture [US].
