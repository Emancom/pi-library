# Editorial Product Information: Velmanase alfa

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration) and US (Food and Drug Administration).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of **velmanase alfa** Product Information for healthcare professionals, synthesising data from the aforementioned regulatory authorities.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory approved labeling for definitive guidance.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Velmanase alfa (a recombinant form of human lysosomal alpha-mannosidase).
- **Chemical Class:** Enzyme replacement therapy (ERT).
- **Brand Name(s):** **Lamzede**.
- **ATC Code:** A16AB15.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Lyophilized powder for injection. Each single-dose vial contains 10 mg of velmanase alfa. After reconstitution with 5 mL of Sterile Water for Injection, the solution contains 2 mg/mL.
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Chiesi Farmaceutici S.p.A. (Italy); Sponsor: Chiesi Australia Pty Ltd [Australian]; Chiesi USA, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Alpha-mannosidosis:** Treatment of non-central nervous system manifestations in adult and paediatric patients.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Recommended Dose:** 1 mg/kg (actual body weight) administered once every week via intravenous infusion.

### Administration Instructions
- **Supervision:** Treatment should be supervised by a physician experienced in managing alpha-mannosidosis or the administration of ERT for lysosomal storage disorders.
- **Pre-medication:** Consider pre-treating with antihistamines, antipyretics, and/or corticosteroids to reduce the risk of infusion-associated reactions (IARs).
- **Reconstitution:**
  - Remove vials from refrigeration approximately 30 minutes prior to use to reach room temperature.
  - Reconstitute each 10 mg vial with 5 mL Sterile Water for Injection (USP) by slow drop-wise addition down the inside wall of the vial. 
  - Do not eject water forcefully; avoid foaming. 
  - Allow to stand for 5–15 minutes, then tilt and roll gently for 15–20 seconds. **Do not shake, swirl, or invert.**
  - The final concentration is 2 mg/mL. The solution should be clear to slightly opalescent; thin white strands or translucent fibers may occasionally be present and will be removed by an in-line filter.
- **Infusion:**
  - Use an infusion set equipped with a pump and an in-line, low protein-binding 0.2 μm filter.
  - **Infusion Rates:**
    - [US: Minimum 60 minutes for patients weighing up to 49 kg; maximum 25 mL/hour for patients $\ge$50 kg].
    - [Australian: Minimum 50 minutes for patients weighing up to 42 kg; maximum 25 mL/hour for patients $\ge$42 kg].
  - Once the dose is administered, flush the line with 10 mL of 0.9% Sodium Chloride.
- **Monitoring:** Observe the patient for IARs for at least one hour [Australian] or as clinically indicated [US] following the infusion.

### Missed Dose Instructions
- [US]: Restart treatment as soon as possible if at least 3 days remain before the next scheduled dose. If the missed dose is within 3 days of the next schedule, give only the next dose as per the regular schedule.
- [Australian]: Not explicitly specified in clinical overview; contact treating specialist.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Severe hypersensitivity:** Known severe allergic reaction to velmanase alfa or any excipient [Australian].
- [US]: The US labeling lists "None" under the Contraindications section, but includes a Boxed Warning regarding severe hypersensitivity.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning [US]:** **Severe Hypersensitivity Reactions.** Patients have experienced anaphylaxis. Appropriate medical support, including CPR equipment, must be available. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue immediately and initiate medical treatment.
- **Infusion-Associated Reactions (IARs):** Highly prevalent (up to 50% of patients). Symptoms include pyrexia, chills, erythema, vomiting, cough, and urticaria.
  - **Management:** If a severe IAR occurs, discontinue immediately. For mild-to-moderate reactions, consider slowing the infusion rate (e.g., to 25–50% of the recommended rate) or temporarily withholding the dose [US].
- **Embryo-Fetal Toxicity:** Based on animal data, may cause fetal harm. Verify pregnancy status in females of reproductive potential prior to treatment. 
- **Traceability [Australian]:** The name and batch number of the biological product must be clearly recorded to improve traceability.
- **Irreversible Complications:** Velmanase alfa does not cross the blood-brain barrier and does not affect irreversible complications such as skeletal deformities or impaired cognitive function.

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions are classified by frequency: Very common ($\ge$10%), Common (1–10%).

| System Organ Class | Frequency | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Infections** | Very Common | Nasopharyngitis, rhinitis [US] |
| **Immune System** | Very Common | **Hypersensitivity** (including **anaphylaxis**), IARs |
| **Nervous System** | Very Common | Headache; Common: Syncope, dizziness, tremor, somnolence |
| **Gastrointestinal**| Very Common | Diarrhoea, vomiting, abdominal pain |
| **Musculoskeletal** | Very Common | Arthralgia, back pain, pain in extremity |
| **General** | Very Common | Pyrexia, chills, weight increase |
| **Renal** | Common | **Acute renal failure** |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:**
  - **Anaphylaxis:** Reported in 5% of trial patients [US].
  - **Acute Renal Failure:** Reported in one patient (3-7% of study populations) who was also receiving high-dose ibuprofen.
  - **Seizure:** One episode reported in a patient without prior history; relationship to treatment could not be excluded [US].
  - **IgA Vasculitis:** One case (Henoch-Schönlein Purpura) reported in a patient with high anti-drug antibody levels [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** No formal drug-drug interaction studies have been performed. As a recombinant protein, CYP450-mediated interactions are not expected.
- **Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Use caution with nephrotoxic agents; one case of renal failure occurred during concomitant high-dose ibuprofen use [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:**
  - **Australian Category D:** Recombinant proteins may cause skeletal and visceral malformations based on animal studies.
  - **US Risk Summary:** Advise pregnant females of the potential risk to the fetus. The decision to continue treatment should weigh the maternal need against fetal risks.
  - **Contraception:** Females of reproductive potential should use effective contraception during treatment and for **14 days** after the final dose.
- **Breastfeeding:** There are no data on the presence of velmanase alfa in human milk. The absorption of ingested enzyme by a breastfed child is considered minimal [Australian].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies in rats showed no adverse effects on male or female fertility at doses significantly higher than the human clinical exposure.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness established. Pediatric patients reported a higher incidence of hypersensitivity reactions compared to adults (58% vs 36% [US]). No dose adjustment is required.
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical trials did not include patients $\ge$65 years old. As alpha-mannosidosis is primarily a disease of children and young adults, use in the elderly is not well-characterised.
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment is necessary. Velmanase alfa is a protein and is expected to undergo metabolic degradation into small peptides rather than renal or hepatic elimination.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Velmanase alfa supplements or replaces natural alpha-mannosidase, which is deficient in patients with alpha-mannosidosis. It catalyzes the degradation of mannose-rich oligosaccharides in the lysosome.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
  - **Steady State:** Achieved after weekly infusions.
  - **Distribution:** Low volume of distribution (mean 276 mL/kg), indicating confinement to plasma.
  - **Elimination:** Total body clearance is ~5.7–6.7 mL/h/kg. The mean terminal half-life is approximately 30–33.6 hours.
- **Immunogenicity:** Anti-drug antibodies (ADA) are common. High ADA levels are associated with lower plasma concentrations and may reduce the pharmacodynamic response (reduction of serum oligosaccharides).

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pivotal Study (rhLAMAN-05):** A 52-week, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial (n=25) in adults and children.
  - **Outcomes:** Treated patients showed a statistically significant reduction in serum oligosaccharide concentrations (mean difference -3.50 μmol/L vs placebo; p<0.001). 
  - **Functional tests:** Numerical improvements were seen in the 3-minute stair climbing test (3MSCT), 6-minute walking test (6MWT), and forced vital capacity (FVC% predicted), particularly in patients <18 years of age.
- **Long-term Data (rhLAMAN-10):** Integrated analysis (n=33) for up to 209 weeks showed sustained improvements in serum oligosaccharides and functional domains.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Unopened Vials:** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C. Do not freeze. Protect from light.
- **Reconstituted Solution:** 
  - Chemical and physical stability demonstrated for 24 hours at 2°C to 8°C.
  - [US]: Reconstituted vials must be infused within 10 hours after removal from the refrigerator (inclusive of total infusion time).
  - [Australian]: If not used immediately, store at 2°C to 8°C for up to 24 hours.
