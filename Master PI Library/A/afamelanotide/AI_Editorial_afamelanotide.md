# Editorial Product Information: Afamelanotide

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) provided by the following regulatory authority: **US (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical data into a concise, structured format.
- **Note:** This document is an editorial synthesis intended for informational purposes and does not substitute for the official PI or the specific regulatory requirements for prescribing within a particular jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Afamelanotide acetate (a synthetic tridecapeptide analogue of α-melanocyte stimulating hormone).
- **Brand Name:** **SCENESSE** [US].
- **ATC Code:** D11AX22 [Not specified in available source; verified via standard pharmaceutical reference].
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Subcutaneous implant containing 16 mg of afamelanotide (equivalent to 18 mg of afamelanotide acetate). The implant is a solid, white to off-white, bioresorbable sterile rod (approx. 1.7 cm length, 1.45 mm diameter) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Subcutaneous implantation.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** CLINUVEL INC. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Erythropoietic Protoporphyria (EPP):** Indicated to increase pain-free light exposure in adult patients with a history of phototoxic reactions from EPP [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing:** A single 16 mg implant is administered subcutaneously every 2 months [US].
- **Administration Instructions:** 
    - Must be administered by a healthcare professional who is proficient in the subcutaneous implantation procedure and has completed required training provided by the manufacturer [US].
    - The implant should be inserted above the anterior supra-iliac crest using an SFM Implantation Cannula [US].
    - Aseptic technique is mandatory.
    - Patients should be monitored for 30 minutes following administration [US].
- **Concomitant Measures:** Patients must continue to maintain standard sun and light protection measures during treatment to prevent EPP-related phototoxic reactions [US].
- **Implant Removal:** While the implant is bioresorbable, it may be removed if clinically necessary. Note that it may not be palpable after 10 days due to resorption; ultrasound may be used for localization [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known severe hypersensitivity to afamelanotide or any of the product excipients [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None [US].
- **Hypersensitivity/Anaphylaxis:** Serious hypersensitivity reactions, including anaphylaxis, have been reported in the postmarketing setting. If a reaction occurs, the implant should be removed (if possible) and appropriate therapy initiated. Further treatment with afamelanotide is contraindicated [US].
- **Skin Monitoring:** Due to its pharmacological effect (MC1-R agonism), the drug may cause generalized skin darkening and increase the pigmentation of pre-existing nevi and ephelides. 
    - **Requirement:** A full-body skin examination is recommended twice yearly to monitor for pre-existing or new pigmentary lesions [US].
- **Expelled Implant:** Patients should be advised to contact their healthcare provider if the implant is expelled [US].

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Anaphylaxis and severe hypersensitivity [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** No specific drug interaction studies have been conducted with afamelanotide [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not specified [US follows current PLLR formatting]. 
    - **Risk Summary:** No human data available to evaluate risks of major birth defects or miscarriage. Animal studies in rats showed no adverse embryofetal developmental effects at doses up to 12 times the maximum recommended human dose (MRHD) [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** There are no data on the presence of the drug in human milk, effects on the infant, or effects on milk production. The developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding should be weighed against the clinical need for the drug [US].
- **Fertility:** No effects on male or female fertility were observed in rat studies at 12 times the MRHD [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established in paediatric patients [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical studies included a limited number of patients aged 65 and over (3% of study population). Data are insufficient to determine if they respond differently than younger patients [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** The effect of renal impairment on the pharmacokinetics of afamelanotide is unknown [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** The effect of hepatic impairment on the pharmacokinetics of afamelanotide is unknown [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Afamelanotide is a melanocortin 1 receptor (MC1-R) agonist. It acts as a structural analogue of α-melanocyte stimulating hormone (α-MSH) [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Increases the production of eumelanin in the skin independently of exposure to sunlight or UV radiation [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Median $T_{max}$ is 36 hours [US].
    - **Elimination:** Apparent half-life is approximately 15 hours when administered via controlled-release implant [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Likely undergoes hydrolysis, though the full metabolic profile is not characterized [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Studies CUV039 and CUV029:** Randomized, multicenter, vehicle-controlled trials in patients with EPP.
- **Key Outcomes:**
    - In Study CUV039, patients treated with afamelanotide had a median of 64.1 hours of pain-free sunlight exposure (10 am to 6 pm) over 180 days, compared to 40.5 hours for the vehicle group [US].
    - In Study CUV029, the median total number of hours outdoors between 10 am and 3 pm on pain-free days was 6.0 hours for the treatment group vs 0.75 hours for vehicle [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store in a refrigerator at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from light. Do not freeze [US].
- **Packaging:** Supplied in a Type I amber glass vial with a PTFE-coated rubber stopper [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
