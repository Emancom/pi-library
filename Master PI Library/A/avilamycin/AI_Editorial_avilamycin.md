# Editorial Product Information: avilamycin

## 1. Product Identification
- Active ingredient (INN/generic name): **avilamycin**
- Chemical class: orthosomycin class antibiotic
- ATC code: Not specified in available sources
- Route(s) of administration: Oral (administered in medicated feed)

## 2. Regulatory History
- First marketing/approval date (as stated in available source): 05/08/2015 [US]
- Regulatory/label revision date (as stated in available source): Revised: 11/2025 [US]
- Regulatory identifier provided: NADA #141-438 [US]
- Withdrawal, suspension, or restriction history: Not specified in available sources
- If registration dates or other jurisdictions' approval dates are not present in the
  sources: Not specified in available sources

## 3. Approved Indications
- For reduction in the incidence and overall severity of diarrhea in the presence of
  pathogenic Escherichia coli in groups of weaned pigs. [US-approved; not specified
  for other jurisdictions]
- Intended for prophylactic group treatment in pigs at risk of developing diarrhea;
  not for use in pigs already showing clinical signs at the time medication begins.

## 4. Dosage and Administration
### General dosing
- Incorporate at a rate delivering 73 grams of avilamycin per ton of complete Type C
  medicated swine feed, equivalent to 80 ppm, offered as the sole ration.

### Duration
- Standard treatment duration is 21 consecutive days.
- Based on clinical assessment, the attending veterinarian may extend treatment to a
  total of 42 consecutive days. The lifetime cumulative total of medicated feed
  containing avilamycin must not exceed 42 days per pig. [US]

### Administration instructions
- Avilamycin Type A medicated article is intended for incorporation into swine feed
  only; the undiluted product must never be fed directly to animals.
- Mixing procedure:
  - The Type A medicated article contains 90.7 g avilamycin per pound (200 g/kg).
  - To prepare Type C medicated feed containing 73 g avilamycin per ton, add 0.8 lb
    of Type A medicated article per ton of complete feed.
  - Begin by preparing an intermediate pre-blend: thoroughly mix the required
    quantity of Type A medicated article with a convenient portion of feed
    ingredients. Then incorporate this pre-blend into the remaining feed ingredients
    to produce the finished complete feed.
  - The product must be thoroughly and uniformly mixed into the complete feed before
    it is offered to animals.

### Special administration notes
- Administer only to pigs that are at risk of developing, but have not yet begun
  showing clinical signs of, diarrhea in the presence of pathogenic Escherichia coli.
- Efficacy has not been established in pigs that are already exhibiting clinical signs
  of diarrhea at the time medication is initiated.

### Missed dose / interrupted treatment
- Not specified in available sources

### Titration
- Not specified in available sources

## 5. Contraindications
- Do not administer to pigs 14 weeks of age or older. [US]
- Not for human consumption.
- Any other formal contraindications: Not specified in available sources

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed warnings
- None specified in available sources.

### General warnings and precautions
- Veterinary Feed Directive (VFD) requirements apply: medicated feed containing
  avilamycin is restricted to use by or on the order of a licensed veterinarian. The
  VFD expiration date may not exceed 90 days from the date of issuance, and VFDs for
  avilamycin may not be refilled. [US]
- The total lifetime exposure to medicated feed containing avilamycin must not exceed
  42 days per pig. [US]
- Avilamycin has not been demonstrated to be effective in pigs that are already
  showing clinical signs of diarrhea before medication begins.
- Safety in swine intended for breeding purposes has not been established; use in
  breeding animals is therefore not recommended. [US]

### Human/user safety precautions (occupational)
- Eye and skin contact with avilamycin may cause irritation; hypersensitive
  individuals may experience allergic reactions.
- Inhalation, oral exposure, and direct skin or eye contact should be avoided during
  mixing and handling.
- Personnel handling the product should wear protective clothing, chemical-resistant
  gloves, safety goggles, and an approved dust mask at all times during mixing and
  handling.
- Hands should be washed thoroughly with soap and water after any handling.
- In case of accidental eye contact: flush the eyes thoroughly with water and obtain
  medical attention promptly. If contact lenses are worn, rinse the eyes first, then
  remove the lenses and continue rinsing before seeking medical care.
- In case of accidental skin contact: wash all exposed skin thoroughly with soap and
  water; seek medical attention if irritation develops.
- In case of accidental inhalation: seek medical attention if any breathing difficulty
  arises.
- Not for human consumption; if accidental ingestion occurs, contact a physician or
  poison control centre immediately and do not induce vomiting.
- For reporting of adverse effects in users, for additional product information, or to
  obtain occupational safety data, contact the manufacturer at the phone number
  indicated on the product labelling.

### Specific clinical precautions
- Thorough, uniform mixing of the product into complete feed is essential to ensure
  appropriate dose delivery to each animal.
- Use only as directed by a veterinarian under VFD conditions. [US]
