# Editorial Product Information: Aripiprazole Lauroxil

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**. 
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. 
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official PI or the local regulatory guidance required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Aripiprazole lauroxil (an atypical antipsychotic).
- **Brand Names:** **ARISTADA**; **ARISTADA INITIO** (used for treatment initiation) [US].
- **ATC Code:** N05AX12 (Aripiprazole).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Extended-release injectable suspension (white to off-white) provided in single-dose pre-filled syringes:
    - 441 mg / 1.6 mL
    - 662 mg / 2.4 mL
    - 882 mg / 3.2 mL
    - 1064 mg / 3.9 mL
- **Route of Administration:** Intramuscular (IM) injection only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Alkermes, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Schizophrenia:** For the treatment of schizophrenia in adults [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Maintenance:** Can be administered at doses of 441 mg, 662 mg, or 882 mg monthly; 882 mg every 6 weeks; or 1064 mg every 2 months [US].

### Initiation Schedules
Establish tolerability with oral aripiprazole prior to starting **ARISTADA** in aripiprazole-naïve patients. There are two initiation options [US]:
1. **Option #1:** One IM injection of **ARISTADA INITIO** (675 mg) plus a single 30 mg dose of oral aripiprazole.
2. **Option #2:** 21 consecutive days of oral aripiprazole in conjunction with the first **ARISTADA** injection.

### Administration Instructions
- **Site of Injection:** 
    - 441 mg: Deltoid or Gluteal muscle.
    - 662 mg, 882 mg, 1064 mg: Gluteal muscle only [US].
- **Technique:** Requires rapid and continuous IM injection using the provided safety needles. Do not administer by any other route.
- **Preparation:** Syringe must be tapped at least 10 times and shaken vigorously for 30 seconds to ensure a uniform suspension.

### Missed Dose Instructions [US]
Supplementation depends on the time since the last injection:
- **441 mg dose:** If >7 weeks since last dose, re-initiate with **ARISTADA INITIO** + 30 mg oral aripiprazole (or 21 days oral).
- **662 mg / 882 mg doses:** If >12 weeks since last dose, re-initiate as above.
- **1064 mg dose:** If >12 weeks since last dose, re-initiate as above.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to aripiprazole. Reactions have ranged from pruritus/urticaria to anaphylaxis [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning [US]: Increased Mortality in Elderly Patients with Dementia-Related Psychosis.** Elderly patients with dementia-related psychosis treated with antipsychotic drugs are at an increased risk of death. **ARISTADA** is not approved for this population.
- **Cerebrovascular Adverse Reactions:** Increased incidence of stroke and transient ischaemic attacks, including fatalities, in elderly patients with dementia-related psychosis [US].
- **Neuroleptic Malignant Syndrome (NMS):** Potentially fatal; manage with immediate discontinuation and close monitoring.
- **Tardive Dyskinesia:** Risk increases with duration of treatment; discontinue if clinically appropriate.
- **Metabolic Changes:** Monitor for hyperglycaemia/diabetes mellitus, dyslipidaemia, and weight gain.
- **Pathological Gambling/Compulsive Behaviors:** Patients may experience intense urges (e.g., gambling, sexual, shopping). Consider dose reduction or discontinuation if these occur.
- **Orthostatic Hypotension:** Use with caution in patients with known cardiovascular or cerebrovascular disease.
- **Leukopenia, Neutropenia, and Agranulocytosis:** Perform CBC in patients with a history of low white blood cell counts; consider discontinuation if a significant decline occurs.
- **Seizures:** Use cautiously in patients with a history of seizures or conditions that lower the seizure threshold.

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Very Common (>10%):** Akathisia [US].
- **Common (1–10%):** Injection site pain, increased weight, headache, insomnia, restlessness, and increased blood creatine phosphokinase [US].
- **Uncommon (0.1–1%):** Orthostatic hypotension (0.2–0.5% in trials) [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Stroke (in elderly), NMS, tardive dyskinesia, severe neutropenia, and hypersensitivity reactions (anaphylaxis).

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Strong CYP3A4 Inhibitors / CYP2D6 Inhibitors:** Concomitant use for >2 weeks requires a dose reduction to the next lower strength. For patients on 441 mg, no adjustment is necessary if tolerated [US].
- **Strong CYP3A4 Inducers:** Concomitant use for >2 weeks requires a dose increase for the 441 mg strength (to 662 mg). No adjustment recommended for higher strengths [US].
- **Antihypertensive Drugs:** Aripiprazole may enhance the effect of certain antihypertensives due to alpha-1 adrenergic antagonism.
- **Benzodiazepines (e.g., lorazepam):** Increased intensity of sedation and higher risk of orthostatic hypotension compared to aripiprazole alone [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy [US]:** Neonates exposed to antipsychotics during the third trimester are at risk for extrapyramidal and/or withdrawal symptoms. Data from published studies have not established a clear drug-associated risk for major birth defects. A pregnancy exposure registry is available.
- **Breastfeeding:** Aripiprazole is present in human milk. It may decrease milk supply. Monitor the infant for dehydration and appropriate weight gain [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal data show persistent diestrus and decreased fertility indices in females and disturbances in spermatogenesis in males at high doses [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness in patients <18 years have not been evaluated [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Safety and effectiveness in patients >65 years have not been evaluated. High risk in dementia-related psychosis (see Warnings).
- **Renal Impairment:** No dosage adjustment required for mild to severe renal impairment (GFR 15–90 mL/minute) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dosage adjustment required for mild to severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh score 5–15) [US].
- **CYP2D6 Poor Metabolizers:** Dose adjustments are required due to higher aripiprazole concentrations (approx. 3–8% of the population) [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Aripiprazole lauroxil is a prodrug of aripiprazole. Its efficacy in schizophrenia may be mediated through partial agonist activity at dopamine D2 and serotonin 5-HT1A receptors, and antagonist activity at 5-HT2A receptors [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Appearance in systemic circulation starts 5–6 days post-injection. Steady-state is reached after 4 months.
    - **Distribution:** Extensive extravascular distribution (Vd 268 L). Protein binding >99%.
    - **Metabolism:** Converted by enzyme-mediated hydrolysis to N-hydroxymethyl-aripiprazole, then to aripiprazole. Final metabolism via CYP3A4 and CYP2D6.
    - **Elimination:** Terminal half-life is approximately 53.9 to 57.2 days [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Efficacy Study [US]:** A 12-week, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled study (n=622) in adults with schizophrenia demonstrated that both 441 mg and 882 mg monthly doses of **ARISTADA** were statistically superior to placebo in improving PANSS (Positive and Negative Syndrome Scale) total scores.
- **Pharmacokinetic Bridging:** The 662 mg monthly, 882 mg every 6 weeks, and 1064 mg every 2 months regimens were approved based on PK bridging to the 441 mg and 882 mg monthly doses [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage:** Store at room temperature 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F). Excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C [US].
- **Handling:** Keep the pre-filled syringe in the carton until use to protect from light.

- **Legal Classification [US]:** Rx-only (Prescription only).
