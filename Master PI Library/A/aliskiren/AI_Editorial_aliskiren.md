# Editorial Product Information: Aliskiren

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical and regulatory information into a single reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI or summary of product characteristics (SmPC) required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulations and the most current official PI for legal prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Aliskiren (as hemifumarate).
- **Chemical Class:** Direct Renin Inhibitor (DRI).
- **Brand Names:** Aliskiren (Generic) [US]. 
- **ATC Code:** C09XA02.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:**
    - **Oral Tablets:** 150 mg (Pink, biconvex round, film-coated) and 300 mg (Brown, biconvex ovaloid, film-coated) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Par Health USA, LLC [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Hypertension:** For the treatment of hypertension in adults and paediatric patients (aged 6 years and older weighing at least 50 kg) to reduce blood pressure [US]. 
- **Note on Cardiovascular Outcomes:** While blood pressure reduction is established, no controlled trials have demonstrated a reduction in cardiovascular risk (e.g., myocardial infarction, stroke) specifically with aliskiren [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):**
    - **Initial Dose:** 150 mg once daily.
    - **Titration:** If blood pressure remains inadequately controlled, the dose may be increased to 300 mg once daily. Doses exceeding 300 mg do not provide additional antihypertensive benefit but increase the incidence of adverse effects (specifically diarrhoea) [US].
- **Paediatric Dosing (≥6 years and ≥50 kg):** 
    - **Initial Dose:** 150 mg once daily.
    - **Titration:** May be increased to 300 mg once daily if required for blood pressure control [US].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Patients should establish a consistent routine regarding food (e.g., always taking it with a meal or always on an empty stomach).
    - High-fat meals significantly reduce absorption [US].
- **Onset of Effect:** The majority of the antihypertensive effect (85% to 90%) is achieved within 2 weeks of initiating a specific dose [US].
- **Missed Dose:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Dual RAAS Blockade in Diabetes:** Concomitant use with Angiotensin-Converting Enzyme Inhibitors (ACEIs) or Angiotensin Receptor Blockers (ARBs) in patients with diabetes mellitus [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to aliskiren or any component of the formulation [US].
- **Paediatric Age:** Contraindicated in children less than 2 years of age due to the risk of high systemic exposure and potential toxicity observed in juvenile animal studies [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning: Fetal Toxicity [US]**
    - Drugs that act directly on the renin-angiotensin system (RAS) can cause injury and death to the developing fetus.
    - Discontinue aliskiren immediately if pregnancy is detected.
- **Dual Blockade of the RAAS:** Avoid concomitant use with ACEIs or ARBs, particularly in patients with renal impairment (CrCl <60 mL/min), due to increased risks of hypotension, hyperkalemia, and renal dysfunction [US].
- **Anaphylaxis and Angioedema:** Rare but serious reactions involving the face, extremities, lips, tongue, or larynx have occurred. Airway obstruction may be fatal. Discontinue therapy immediately and provide emergency support if these occur [US].
- **Hypotension:** Symptomatic hypotension may occur in patients with volume or salt depletion (e.g., those on high-dose diuretics). These conditions should be corrected prior to starting aliskiren [US].
- **Impaired Renal Function:** Monitor serum creatinine periodically. Patients whose renal function depends on the RAAS (e.g., renal artery stenosis, severe heart failure) are at increased risk for acute renal failure [US].
- **Hyperkalemia:** Monitor serum potassium levels regularly. Risk factors include renal insufficiency, diabetes, and concomitant use of potassium-sparing diuretics, potassium supplements, or other RAAS blockers [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Common (1–10%):**
    - Diarrhoea (2.3% at 300 mg; higher in women and elderly patients) [US].
    - Cough (1.1%) [US].
    - Rash (1%) [US].
- **Uncommon to Rare (<1%):**
    - Angioedema (0.06%) [US].
    - Hyperkalemia (0.9% as monotherapy) [US].
    - Increased serum uric acid (0.4%), Gout (0.2%), and Renal stones (0.2%) [US].
    - Elevated Creatine Kinase (>300% increase in ~1% of patients) [US].
- **Postmarketing/Serious Effects (Frequency Not Known):**
    - Anaphylactic reactions, Stevens-Johnson syndrome, Toxic Epidermal Necrolysis (TEN), peripheral oedema, hepatic enzyme elevation, and hyponatremia [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Cyclosporine:** Co-administration significantly increases aliskiren blood concentrations; avoid concomitant use [US].
- **Itraconazole:** Co-administration significantly increases aliskiren blood concentrations; avoid concomitant use [US].
- **NSAIDs/COX-2 Inhibitors:** May lead to deterioration of renal function (including acute renal failure) and may attenuate the antihypertensive effect of aliskiren, particularly in elderly or volume-depleted patients [US].
- **Furosemide:** Aliskiren may reduce the oral exposure (AUC and Cmax) of furosemide; monitor diuretic effects [US].
- **P-glycoprotein (P-gp) Substrates:** Aliskiren is a substrate of P-gp; potent inhibitors increase aliskiren levels [US].
- **Dual RAAS Blockade:** (See Section 4 and 5) [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Category:** US PI does not use the lettered system but provides a "Fetal Toxicity" warning. Use in the second and third trimesters reduces fetal renal function and increases morbidity and mortality (e.g., skull hypoplasia, oligohydramnios) [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if aliskiren is excreted in human milk. Due to the potential for serious adverse reactions in the infant, breastfeeding is not recommended during treatment [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies in rats showed no effect on fertility at doses up to 250 mg/kg/day (8 times the maximum recommended human dose) [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:**
    - Approved for patients ≥6 years weighing ≥50 kg [US].
    - Avoid use in children 2 to <6 years due to lack of metabolic data [US].
    - Contraindicated in children <2 years [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Exposure (AUC) is increased in patients ≥65 years. No specific starting dose adjustment is required, but monitoring is advised due to potential increased sensitivity [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    - No initial dose adjustment required for mild to moderate impairment.
    - Safety and efficacy not established in patients with severe renal impairment (CrCl <30 mL/min) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment required for patients with mild to severe liver disease [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** A direct renin inhibitor that binds to the active site of renin, preventing the conversion of angiotensinogen to Angiotensin I. This reduces plasma renin activity (PRA) and subsequent levels of Angiotensin I and II [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Poorly absorbed; bioavailability ~2.5%. Tmax reached in 1–3 hours.
    - **Distribution:** Steady state reached in 7–8 days.
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily metabolised by CYP3A4, though the extent of metabolism is unknown. Aliskiren does not inhibit or induce CYP450 enzymes.
    - **Elimination:** Accumulation half-life of ~24 hours. Approximately 25% of the absorbed dose is excreted unchanged in the urine; the remainder is excreted via the bile [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Adult Monotherapy:** Six randomized, 8-week trials demonstrated dose-dependent reductions in blood pressure at 150 mg and 300 mg [US].
- **ALTITUDE Study:** A study of 8,606 patients with diabetes and renal disease was terminated early due to lack of efficacy and increased risks of renal impairment (5.7% vs 4.3% placebo), hypotension (19.9% vs 16.3%), and hyperkalemia (38.9% vs 28.8%) when adding aliskiren to standard ACEI/ARB therapy [US].
- **Paediatric Hypertension:** An 8-week trial in patients 6–17 years showed weight-based dose-dependent reductions in systolic blood pressure [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from moisture. Dispense in the original container and do not remove the desiccant [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Prescription-only (Rx-only) [US].
- **Other Jurisdictions:** Not specified in available sources.
