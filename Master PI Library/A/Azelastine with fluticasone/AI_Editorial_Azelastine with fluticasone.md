## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP).
- **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule/amino acid derivative ((R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium).
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01 (Not explicitly in source, but standard for Levocarnitine).
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL).
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US-approved].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Administration
- Administer by intravenous injection only.
- Inspect parenterals visually for particulate matter or discoloration before use.

### Metabolic Disorders
- **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg.
- **Method:** Administered as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion.
- **Acute Management:** In severe metabolic crisis, a loading dose may be given, followed by an equivalent dose over the next 24 hours.
- **Frequency:** Administer every 3 or 4 hours; never less than every 6 hours.
- **Monitoring:** Obtain plasma carnitine concentrations before starting therapy. Weekly and monthly monitoring (including blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma carnitine levels) is recommended. Target free carnitine levels should be between 35 and 60 µmol/L.

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight.
- **Method:** Administered as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line after each dialysis session.
- **Adjustment:** Dose adjustments may be initiated by the third or fourth week of therapy, guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations (normal range: 40 to 50 µmol/L).

## 4. Contraindications
- None known at the time of publication [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions can occur within minutes of administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue use and consider the risks/benefits before re-administration [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In those with pre-existing conditions, an increase in seizure frequency or severity has been noted [US].
- **Oral Formulation Cautions:** The safety and efficacy of **oral** levocarnitine have not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in ESRD may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse events reported in clinical trials for ESRD patients (frequency ≥5% regardless of causality):

| Body System | Adverse Effect |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Chest pain, flu syndrome, headache, infection, injection site reaction, pain, fever, abdominal pain. |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension, hypotension, tachycardia, arrhythmia. |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea, nausea, vomiting, dyspepsia, anorexia. |
| **Metabolic** | Hypercalcemia, hypervolemia, hyperkalemia, weight changes. |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness, insomnia, paresthesia, depression. |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis, cough increase, dyspnea, rhinitis. |

- **Other Reactions:** Body odor and gastritis have been observed [US].
- **Serious Effects:** Anaphylaxis and seizures [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have occurred when used concurrently with levocarnitine.
- **Management:** It is recommended to monitor INR levels after initiating levocarnitine or following dose adjustments in patients on warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not specified, but text indicates]: Reproductive studies in rats/rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed.
- **Breastfeeding:** Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk carnitine concentrations after administration. Risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant must be weighed against maternal benefit. Discontinuation of nursing or the drug may be considered [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing is based on mg/kg body weight (see Section 3).
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Specifically indicated for ESRD patients on dialysis via the IV route. High-dose **oral** levocarnitine is cautioned against in severe renal impairment [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces).
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. Mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of ESRD patients (n=12), IV administration of 20 mg/kg after dialysis increased mean plasma levocarnitine from 20 µmol/L (pre-dialysis) to trough levels between 54 and 180 µmol/L after one week of therapy.
- **Dose-Ranging:** Studies comparing 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg demonstrated dose-dependent increases in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Handling:** Avoid excessive heat; protect from freezing.
- **Stability:** Once opened, discard any unused portion as the formulation contains no preservative.
- **Compatibility:** Stable for up to 24 hours in PVC bags at room temperature when mixed with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) [US].
