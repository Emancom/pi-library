### Dosing by strength (reconstitution volumes provided in product materials)
- Product strengths and labeled reconstitution volumes as supplied:
  - 1000 mg in vial — reconstitute with 20 mL sterile water for injection (reconstituted solution composition provided)
  - 4000 mg in vial — reconstitute with 76 mL sterile water for injection (reconstituted solution composition provided)
  - 5000 mg in vial — reconstitute with 95 mL sterile water for injection (reconstituted solution composition provided)

### Administration instructions
- Route: Intravenous administration only.
- Reconstitution: Reconstitute the lyophilized drug with the accompanying volume of Sterile Water for Injection using an appropriate transfer set (transfer set supplied in kit).
- Use/handling of reconstituted product:
  - Administer at room temperature within 3 hours of reconstitution.
  - Product contains no preservative; vials are single-dose.
  - Hydrochloric acid and/or sodium hydroxide may have been added to adjust pH.
- Reconstituted solution ionic composition (ranges reported per reconstitution volume):
  - All strengths (ranges common across volumes): sodium 73–89 mM; phosphate 15–20 mM; mannitol 121–168 mM.
  - Chloride ranges by reconstitution volume:
    - 1000 mg (20 mL reconstitution): 33–42 mM chloride
    - 4000 mg (76 mL reconstitution): 30–39 mM chloride
    - 5000 mg (95 mL reconstitution): 30–39 mM chloride
