# Editorial Product Information: Anthrax Vaccine Adsorbed, Adjuvanted (**CYFENDUS**)

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of the product information for **CYFENDUS** (Anthrax Vaccine Adsorbed, Adjuvanted) for use by healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This synthesis is an editorial summary based on the provided source and is not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. At the time of compilation, only the United States (FDA) PI was provided for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Anthrax Vaccine Adsorbed (AVA), derived from cell-free filtrates of an avirulent, nonencapsulated strain of *Bacillus anthracis* (V770-NP1-R). 
- **Chemical Class:** Adjuvanted bacterial vaccine.
- **Brand Name:** **CYFENDUS** [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Injectable suspension. Each 0.5 mL dose contains 100 mcg total adsorbed cell-free filtrate, 1.3 mg aluminum adjuvant (as aluminum hydroxide), and 0.5 mg CPG 7909 (Agatolimod sodium) adjuvant [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intramuscular (IM) injection.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Emergent Product Development Gaithersburg Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Post-Exposure Prophylaxis (PEP):** Indicated for the prevention of disease following suspected or confirmed exposure to *Bacillus anthracis* in individuals aged 18 through 65 years. It must be administered in conjunction with recommended antibacterial drugs [US].
- **Note:** Efficacy for PEP is based solely on animal models of inhalational anthrax [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adults 18–65 years):** Administer as a two-dose series (0.5 mL each).
- **Schedule:** 
    - First dose: Week 0 (as soon as possible after exposure).
    - Second dose: Week 2.
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - For intramuscular use only [US].
    - Gently swirl or roll the vial to obtain a homogeneous milky-white suspension. **DO NOT SHAKE** to avoid foaming [US].
    - Inspect visually for particulate matter or discoloration; discard if present.
    - If using a multiple-dose vial, once the stopper is pierced, the vial must be used or discarded within 28 days [US].
- **Missed Doses:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** History of a severe allergic reaction (e.g., anaphylaxis) following a previous dose of **CYFENDUS**, **BioThrax** (a related anthrax vaccine), or any component of the vaccine [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Acute Allergic Reactions:** Appropriate medical treatment and supervision must be available to manage potential anaphylactic reactions immediately following administration [US].
- **Altered Immunocompetence:** Individuals who are immunocompromised (due to disease or immunosuppressive therapy) may exhibit a diminished immune response to the vaccine [US].
- **Pregnancy Warning:** **CYFENDUS** can cause fetal harm. In observational studies of **BioThrax** (which shares the same active ingredient), increased birth defects were noted in infants born to individuals vaccinated during the first trimester compared to those vaccinated post-pregnancy or never vaccinated [US].
- **Clinical Monitoring:** Patients should be monitored for at least 7 days post-vaccination for local and systemic reactogenicity.

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions are reported based on a pooled safety population (N=3,276).

### Very Common (>10%)
- **Local (Injection Site):** Tenderness (88.1%), Pain (86.3%), Arm motion limitation (63.7%), Warmth (51.2%), Induration (37.5%), Itching (21.9%), Swelling (19.7%), Erythema/Redness (17.9%), Bruising (17.2%) [US].
- **Systemic:** Muscle aches (75.2%), Tiredness (67.1%), Headache (58.0%) [US].

### Common (1–10%)
- **Systemic:** Fever (6.8%) [US].

### Serious Adverse Effects and Postmarketing Experience
- **Serious Adverse Events (SAEs):** In clinical trials, 1.9% of participants reported SAEs, though none were determined to be definitively related to **CYFENDUS** [US].
- **Potential Immune-mediated Events:** Rare cases of ulcerative colitis, chronic idiopathic urticaria, and diffuse alopecia were reported and considered possibly related by adjudicators [US].
- **Postmarketing (based on BioThrax experience):** Lymphadenopathy, nausea, anaphylaxis, angioedema, Stevens-Johnson syndrome, syncope, tremor, rhabdomyolysis, and flu-like symptoms [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Antibacterials:** Concomitant administration with oral ciprofloxacin or doxycycline does not have a clinically relevant impact on the pharmacokinetics of the antibiotics or the immunogenicity of **CYFENDUS** [US].
- **Immunosuppressants:** May reduce the vaccine's immune response (see Section 5).

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not explicitly assigned a lettered category under current labeling, but the PI states **CYFENDUS** can cause fetal harm. 
    - Data from the **BioThrax** registry showed a birth defect rate of 4.68% for first-trimester exposure vs 3.85% for post-pregnancy vaccination [US].
    - Animal studies (rats) using full human doses showed no evidence of harm to the fetus or reproductive performance [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if vaccine components are excreted in human milk. The developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding should be weighed against the clinical need for the vaccine and potential risks to the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** No effect on female rat fertility was observed. Male fertility has not been evaluated [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness in individuals younger than 18 years have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Safety and effectiveness in individuals older than 65 years have not been established [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Dosing adjustments are not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** **CYFENDUS** induces antibodies against the 83-kDa protective antigen (PA) protein of *B. anthracis*. These antibodies neutralize the activity of cytotoxic lethal toxin and edema toxin, which are primary virulence factors of the bacterium [US].
- **Adjuvant Effect:** The addition of CPG 7909 (a synthetic DNA molecule) to the aluminum hydroxide adjuvant is designed to enhance the immune response [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** As a vaccine, standard PK studies (absorption/distribution/metabolism/elimination) are not applicable.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Efficacy Bridging:** Because human efficacy trials with anthrax are unethical, efficacy was bridged from animal models (rabbits and non-human primates). Survival in these models correlated with toxin-neutralizing antibody (TNA) levels [US].
- **Immunogenicity (Study 4):** A randomized, double-blind trial in 3,151 adults compared **CYFENDUS** (2 doses IM) to **BioThrax** (3 doses SC). **CYFENDUS** demonstrated non-inferiority to **BioThrax** at Day 64, with 86.6% of recipients achieving the target TNA threshold compared to 61.4% in the **BioThrax** group [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) [US].
- **Handling:** 
    - Protect from light.
    - **DO NOT FREEZE.** Discard if the product has been frozen [US].
    - Do not use after the expiration date.
- **Punctured Vials:** Multiple-dose vials must be discarded 28 days after the initial stopper puncture. The date of discard should be written on the vial label [US].

- **Legal Classification:**
