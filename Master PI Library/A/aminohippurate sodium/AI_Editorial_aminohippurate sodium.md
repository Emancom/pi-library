# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA)
- **Purpose:** This document is a harmonised editorial synthesis of the official Product Information (PI) for **levocarnitine** injection. It is designed to provide healthcare professionals with a coherent, clinically accurate overview.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not replace the official, legally approved Product Information required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority’s approved PI for full prescribing details. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule/amino acid derivative.
- **Brand Names:** Not specified in available sources (Generic: **Levocarnitine Injection, USP**).
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency (Inborn Errors of Metabolism):** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that result in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    - The recommended dose is **50 mg/kg** administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion [US].
    - In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose is often followed by an equivalent dose over the next 24 hours.
    - Frequency: Every 3 to 4 hours (q3h or q4h), and never less than every 6 hours (q6h) [US].
    - All subsequent daily doses should be in the range of 50 mg/kg or as clinically required.
    - Maximum recorded dose: 300 mg/kg [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - Recommended starting dose: **10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg** dry body weight [US].
    - Administration: Slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line after each dialysis session [US].
    - Dose adjustments (e.g., down to 5 mg/kg) may be made as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Inspect for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration.
- **Compatibility:** Stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer's at concentrations ranging from 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL [US].
- Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial.

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients on dialysis. These may occur within minutes of intravenous administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and initiate medical management [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with a known history, an increase in seizure frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Monitoring:** 
    - Obtain baseline plasma carnitine levels before starting therapy. 
    - Monitor plasma free carnitine concentrations (target: 35–60 µmol/L), blood chemistry, and vital signs weekly and monthly [US].
    - In dialysis patients, monitor trough (pre-dialysis) concentrations (normal range: 40–50 µmol/L) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Clinical Trial Experience (ESRD Patients)
Adverse events reported at a frequency **≥5%** (regardless of causality) in double-blind, placebo-controlled trials include:
- **General:** Injection site reaction (33%), pain (30%), headache (22%), flu syndrome (25%), infection (15%), chest pain (12%), accidental injury (10%), asthenia (9%), abdominal pain (9%) [US].
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (20%), hypotension (14%), tachycardia (6%), palpitations (5%) [US].
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea (16%), vomiting (15%), nausea (8%), anorexia (5%) [US].
- **Metabolic/Nutritional:** Hypercalcemia (9%), hypervolemia (5%), peripheral edema (5%), weight increase/decrease (5%) [US].
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (23%), cough increase (9%), dyspnea (7%), rhinitis (9%) [US].
- **Nervous:** Dizziness (13%), paresthesia (5%), insomnia (4%), depression (5%) [US].
- **Skin:** Pruritus (5%), rash (3%) [US].

### Postmarketing Experience
- **Serious:** Anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, bronchospasm, and seizures [US].
- **Other:** Body odor, gastritis, and transient nausea/vomiting have been noted [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have occurred when levocarnitine is used concomitantly with warfarin. It is recommended to monitor INR levels after initiation or dose adjustments of levocarnitine [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category C:** Animal studies (rats/rabbits) using up to 3.8 times the human dose showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. However, there are no adequate, well-controlled studies in pregnant women. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine concentrations in milk after administration. The benefits to the mother must be weighed against risks of excess carnitine intake for the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** Mutagenicity tests were negative; however, long-term carcinogenicity studies have not been performed [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders and ESRD is based on mg/kg body weight, applicable across age groups as clinically indicated [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    - **Oral Levocarnitine Warning:** Safety and efficacy of *oral* levocarnitine have not been evaluated in renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in ESRD patients may result in the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) [US].
    - **Intravenous Use:** Approved for ESRD patients on dialysis [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring carrier molecule required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane for beta-oxidation and energy production [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In patients with organic acidemias, levocarnitine promotes the excretion of excess organic/fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters, which are quickly eliminated [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. The mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours; the mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].
    - **Bioavailability:** Absolute bioavailability of oral formulations is low (~15-16%) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Study:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients on dialysis (20 mg/kg 3x weekly) showed pre-dialysis plasma concentrations increased from ~20 µmol/L at baseline to ~190 µmol/L at 8 weeks. While levocarnitine effectively increases plasma carnitine levels, clinical outcomes regarding symptoms of deficiency in the ESRD population have not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Precautions:** Avoid excessive heat. Protect from freezing [US].
- **Preservation:** The formulation contains no preservatives; discard any unused portion [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources.
