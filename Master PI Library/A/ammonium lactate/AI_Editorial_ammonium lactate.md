# Editorial Product Information: Ammonium Lactate

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial has been synthesized from official Product Information (PI) documents provided by the following regulatory authority:
    - United States (Food and Drug Administration)
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonized editorial overview for healthcare professionals.
- **Note:** This synthesis is an editorial summary and does not replace the official, jurisdiction-specific PI required for prescribing and dispensing in any particular region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Ammonium lactate (equivalent to 12% lactic acid)
- **Chemical Class:** Alpha-hydroxy acid
- **Brand Name(s):** Ammonium Lactate Cream 12% (repackaged by RemedyRepack; original manufacturer Taro Pharma) [US]
- **ATC Code:** D02AF (Pharmacotherapeutic group: Keratolytics) [Inferred; not specified in source]
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Topical cream containing 120 mg of ammonium lactate per gram (12%) [US]
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical (dermatological) use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** RemedyRepack Inc. (Repackager); Taro Pharma Inc. (Original Manufacturer) [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Ichthyosis vulgaris:** Treatment of skin thickening and scaling [US].
- **Xerosis:** Treatment of dry skin [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adults and Pediatrics ≥2 years):**
    - Apply a thin layer to the affected skin areas.
    - Frequency: Twice daily [US].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - The cream must be rubbed in thoroughly.
    - Avoid contact with eyes, lips, and mucous membranes.
    - For external use only; not for ophthalmic, oral, or intravaginal use.
- **Titration and Missed Dose:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known history of hypersensitivity to ammonium lactate, lactic acid, or any other component of the formulation (e.g., parabens, propylene glycol) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None [US].
- **Photosensitivity:** 
    - Clinical studies in mice suggest that topical 12% ammonium lactate may enhance the rate of skin tumor formation induced by ultraviolet (UV) light.
    - Patients must minimize or avoid exposure to natural or artificial sunlight (including tanning beds) on treated areas.
    - Protective clothing should be worn if sun exposure is unavoidable [US].
- **Local Irritation:** 
    - Transient stinging or burning may occur, particularly when applied to skin with fissures, erosions, or abrasions (e.g., recently shaved legs).
    - Caution is advised when applying to the face due to the potential for irritation [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Treatment should be discontinued if signs of hypersensitivity or severe irritation occur [US].
- **Pigmentation:** The potential for post-inflammatory hypo- or hyperpigmentation following use has not been formally studied [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effect frequencies vary by indication based on controlled clinical trials:

| Adverse Effect | Frequency in Ichthyosis Vulgaris | Frequency in Xerosis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Rash (including erythema/irritation)** | 10–15% | ~2% |
| **Burning / Stinging** | 10–15% | ~3% |
| **Pruritus (Itching)** | ~5% | Not specified |
| **Dry Skin** | Not specified | ~2% |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** None identified in available sources, though severe irritation or hypersensitivity requires discontinuation of therapy.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Management Notes:** No clinically significant drug-drug interactions were reported in the provided documentation.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Classification:** Not formally assigned a letter category in this document, but states "use during pregnancy only if clearly needed" [US].
    - **Animal Data:** Reproduction studies in rats and rabbits at doses up to 0.7 and 1.5 times the human dose, respectively, revealed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** 
    - While lactic acid is a normal constituent of human blood and tissue, it is unknown if topical ammonium lactate affects lactic acid levels in human milk. 
    - Exercise caution when administering to nursing women [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies in rats at doses of 300 mg/kg/day (0.4 times the human dose) showed no effects on fertility or pre-/post-natal development [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** 
    - Safety and efficacy have been established in pediatric patients as young as **2 years of age** [US].
- **Elderly Use (Geriatrics):** 
    - Clinical trials did not include sufficient numbers of subjects aged 65 and older to determine if they respond differently than younger subjects.
    - Dose selection should be cautious, generally starting at the low end of the dosing range [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** 
    - **Ammonium lactate** (an alpha-hydroxy acid salt) acts as a humectant, facilitating hydration of the stratum corneum.
    - It may also decrease corneocyte cohesion, though the exact mechanism for this keratolytic-like effect is unknown [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** In vitro studies using human cadaver skin indicate approximately 6.1% systemic absorption of the cream after 68 hours [US].
    - **Distribution/Metabolism/Elimination:** Not specified in available sources.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Ichthyosis Vulgaris:** Controlled trials demonstrated efficacy, with the most common adverse events being local irritation (rash and stinging) in 10-15% of patients [US].
- **Xerosis:** Controlled trials showed the drug was well-tolerated, with transient burning being the most frequent side effect (3%) [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Excursions:** Brief excursions are permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F and 86°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Keep out of reach of children. Ensure the container is tightly closed when not in use.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** 
    - **United States:** Rx-only (Prescription Only) [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources.
