# Editorial Product Information: Alitretinoin

## Editorial Note
*   **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA).
*   **Purpose:** This document is a harmonised editorial synthesis of the Product Information (PI) for alitretinoin, intended for use by healthcare professionals. It integrates regulatory data to provide a comprehensive clinical overview.
*   **Disclaimer:** This editorial is not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific PI. Clinicians must consult the local approved labelling for prescribing and legal compliance.

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredient:** Alitretinoin (9-cis-retinoic acid).
*   **Chemical Class:** Retinoid (Vitamin A derivative).
*   **Brand Names:** **Panretin** [US].
*   **ATC Code:** L01XF02 (Antineoplastic agents, other antineoplastic agents, retinoids).
*   **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    *   Topical Gel: 0.1% (1 mg/g alitretinoin) in 60-gram tubes [US].
*   **Route of Administration:** Topical.
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Advanz Pharma (US) Corp [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **AIDS-Related Kaposi’s Sarcoma (KS):** Indicated for the topical treatment of cutaneous lesions in adult patients [US-approved].
*   **Limitations of Use:** Not indicated when systemic therapy for Kaposi’s sarcoma is required (e.g., >10 new lesions in the prior month, symptomatic lymphedema, pulmonary involvement, or visceral disease) [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
*   **General Adult Dosing:** Apply twice daily to cover the entirety of each cutaneous Kaposi’s sarcoma lesion [US].
*   **Titration:** Depending on individual tolerance, the frequency of application may be gradually increased to a maximum of four times daily [US].
*   **Administration Instructions:**
    *   **Application:** Apply only to lesions; avoid contact with healthy surrounding skin and mucosal surfaces (eyes, nose, mouth, lips, vagina, tip of the penis, rectum, or anus) [US].
    *   **Drying:** Allow the gel to dry for three to five minutes before covering treated areas with clothing [US].
    *   **Occlusion:** Do not use occlusive dressings [US].
    *   **Hygiene:** Wash hands immediately after application unless the lesions being treated are located on the hands [US].
*   **Toxicity Management:** If severe skin irritation occurs, reduce application frequency. If necessary, interrupt treatment until symptoms improve, then resume at a reduced frequency [US].
*   **Duration:** Treatment should continue as long as the patient derives clinical benefit [US].

## 4. Contraindications
*   **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to alitretinoin, other retinoids, or any excipient in the formulation (e.g., butylated hydroxytoluene, polyethylene glycol 400) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
*   **Embryo-Fetal Toxicity [US Boxed Warning Equivalent]:** Alitretinoin is a known teratogen and can cause fetal harm. Topical application may result in systemic exposure. Pregnant women must be advised of the risk to the fetus. Females of reproductive potential must use effective contraception [US].
*   **Photosensitivity:** Retinoids increase sensitivity to ultraviolet light. Patients should minimize exposure to sunlight and artificial UV sources (sunlamps) and use protective clothing over treated areas [US].
*   **DEET Toxicity:** Animal studies indicate that alitretinoin may increase the toxicity of DEET (N,N-diethyl-m-toluamide). Patients should avoid concurrent use of DEET-containing products (e.g., insect repellents) [US].
*   **Application Site Reactions:** Localized toxicity (erythema, edema, vesiculation) is common. Management via dose adjustment is recommended (see Section 3).

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions are primarily localized to the site of application.
*   **Very Common (>10%):**
    *   Rash (includes erythema, scaling, irritation, redness, dermatitis) (25%–77%) [US]
    *   Pain (burning, pain) (0%–34%) [US]
    *   Pruritus (8%–11%) [US]
    *   Paresthesia (stinging, tingling) (3%–22%) [US]
*   **Common (1–10%):**
    *   Exfoliative dermatitis (flaking, peeling, desquamation) (3%–9%) [US]
    *   Edema (swelling, inflammation) (3%–8%) [US]
    *   Skin disorder (excoriation, cracking, scab, crusting, drainage, fissure) (0%–8%) [US]
*   **Serious Adverse Effects:** Severe local skin reactions (erythema/edema with/without vesiculation) occurred in up to 10% of patients in clinical trials, occasionally leading to treatment withdrawal [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **DEET (N,N-diethyl-m-toluamide):** Increased DEET toxicity observed in animal models; concurrent use is not recommended [US].
*   **Metabolic Interactions:** Alitretinoin is metabolized by CYP2C9, 3A4, 1A1, and 1A2. While plasma concentrations after topical use are generally similar to endogenous levels, caution is advised with potent inhibitors of these enzymes [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy:** 
    *   **Risk Summary:** Teratogenic and embryo-lethal in animal studies at 5 times the human topical dose. There are no adequate data in pregnant women [US]. 
    *   **Contraception:** Females of reproductive potential must use effective contraception starting before therapy, during treatment, and for 1 week following the final dose. Males with female partners of reproductive potential should also use effective contraception during and for 1 week after treatment [US].
    *   **Pregnancy Testing:** Verify pregnancy status in females of reproductive potential prior to initiation [US].
*   **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if alitretinoin is excreted in human milk. Due to the potential for serious adverse reactions in the infant, breastfeeding is not recommended during treatment and for 1 week after the last dose [US].
*   **Fertility:** Carcinogenicity studies have not been conducted; however, retinoids as a class may impact fertility [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established in paediatric patients [US].
*   **Elderly Use:** Clinical trials did not include sufficient numbers of subjects aged 65 and over to determine if they respond differently than younger adults [US].
*   **Renal Impairment:** Data not specified in available sources.
*   **Hepatic Impairment:** Data not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
*   **Mechanism of Action:** Alitretinoin (9-cis-retinoic acid) is an endogenous retinoid that binds to and activates all known intracellular retinoid receptor subtypes (RARα, RARβ, RARγ, RXRα, RXRβ, and RXRγ). These receptors act as transcription factors regulating gene expression related to cell differentiation and proliferation. It inhibits the growth of KS cells in vitro [US].
*   **Pharmacodynamics:** The relationship between exposure and response for topical application is not fully established [US].
*   **Pharmacokinetics:**
    *   **Absorption:** Plasma concentrations of 9-cis-retinoic acid following multiple daily topical applications for up to 60 weeks remained within the range of naturally occurring endogenous levels found in untreated healthy participants [US].
    *   **Metabolism:** Metabolized by CYP2C9, 3A4, 1A1, and 1A2 to 4-hydroxy-9-cis-retinoic acid and 4-oxo-9-cis-retinoic acid [US].
    *   **Elimination:** Not specified in available sources.

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   **Pivotal Trials (Trial 1 and 2):** Two multicenter, randomized, double-blind, vehicle-controlled studies in adults with cutaneous AIDS-related KS.
*   **Trial 1:** (US and Canada) N=268. Dosing 3–4 times daily. Modified ACTG response rate was 35% for alitretinoin vs 16% for vehicle (p=0.0012) [US].
*   **Trial 2:** (International) N=82 (early stop for efficacy). Dosing 2 times daily. Modified ACTG response rate was 36% for alitretinoin vs 7% for vehicle [US].
*   **Outcomes:** Responses were typically partial (only 1% complete response in Trial 1). The median time to response was roughly 8 weeks (28% of patients responding by this point) [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
*   **Handling:** The gel is flammable; keep away from heat, open flames, and fire. Avoid smoking during and immediately after application [US].
*   **Safety:** Keep the tube tightly closed and out of reach of children.
