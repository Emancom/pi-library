# Editorial Product Information: Acetaminophen and Dextromethorphan Hydrobromide (Oral Powder)

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (Drug Facts) provided by the **United States (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical data into a single, coherent reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official Product Information for prescribing or dispensing in any specific jurisdiction. The information provided is based on a single jurisdiction (US); clinical practice in other regions may vary.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** Acetaminophen (1000 mg) and Dextromethorphan Hydrobromide (30 mg).
- **Chemical Class:** Acetaminophen: Non-salicylate analgesic and antipyretic. Dextromethorphan HBr: Antitussive (morphinan derivative).
- **Brand Names:** Leader Multi-Symptom Flu Relief Daytime Max Strength [US].
- **ATC Code:** R05DA20 (Dextromethorphan, combinations).
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Powder for oral solution. Each packet contains Acetaminophen 1000 mg and Dextromethorphan HBr 30 mg [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Cardinal Health 110, LLC. DBA Leader [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
This product is indicated for the temporary relief of symptoms associated with the common cold or influenza [US]:
- Headache
- Minor aches and pains
- Cough due to minor throat and bronchial irritation
- Minor sore throat pain
- Reduction of fever

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Children 12 years and over)
- **Standard Dose:** One packet every 6 hours while symptoms persist [US].
- **Maximum Dose:** Do not exceed 3 packets in any 24-hour period unless directed by a physician [US].

### Administration Instructions
- **Hot Water Method:** Dissolve contents of one packet into 8 oz. of hot water. Sip while hot and consume the entire beverage within 10 to 15 minutes [US].
- **Microwave Method:** Add contents of one packet to 8 oz. of cool water. Stir briskly before and after heating. Do not overheat [US].

### Missed Dose Instructions
- Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Age:** Use in children under 12 years of age is contraindicated [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known allergy to acetaminophen or any inactive ingredient in the formulation [US].
- **Concurrent Acetaminophen Use:** Contraindicated with any other drug containing acetaminophen (prescription or non-prescription) [US].
- **Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitor (MAOI) Interaction:** Contraindicated if the patient is currently taking a prescription MAOI (for depression, psychiatric/emotional conditions, or Parkinson’s disease) or for 2 weeks after stopping the MAOI [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warnings
- Not specified in available sources [US].

### General Warnings and Precautions
- **Hepatotoxicity:** This product contains acetaminophen. Severe liver damage may occur if:
    - More than 4,000 mg of acetaminophen is taken in 24 hours.
    - Taken with other drugs containing acetaminophen.
    - Three or more alcoholic drinks are consumed daily while using the product [US].
- **Severe Skin Reactions:** Acetaminophen may cause serious skin reactions, including reddening, blisters, or rash. If a skin reaction occurs, discontinue use and seek immediate medical attention [US].
- **Sore Throat Warning:** If sore throat is severe, persists for more than 2 days, or is accompanied by fever, headache, nausea, or vomiting, consult a physician promptly [US].

### Specific Clinical Precautions
- Patients should consult a physician before use if they have:
    - Liver disease.
    - Respiratory conditions (e.g., emphysema, chronic bronchitis, asthma).
    - Cough accompanied by excessive phlegm (mucus).
    - Chronic or persistent cough (e.g., from smoking) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Dermatologic:** Skin reddening, blisters, rash [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Hepatotoxicity (associated with overdose or alcohol use) and severe cutaneous adverse reactions (SCARs) [US].
- **Frequency Data:** Specific frequencies for common or uncommon adverse effects are not specified in the available OTC source documentation.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors (MAOIs):** Concurrent use or use within 14 days of cessation is contraindicated due to the risk of serotonin syndrome or hypertensive crisis associated with dextromethorphan [US].
- **Anticoagulants:** Patients taking the blood-thinning drug **warfarin** should consult a doctor or pharmacist before use, as acetaminophen may enhance the anticoagulant effect [US].
- **Other Acetaminophen Products:** Increased risk of toxicity when combined with other APAP-containing medications [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Healthcare professional consultation is required before use [US]. Specific lettered categories are not assigned in the provided OTC labeling.
- **Breastfeeding:** Healthcare professional consultation is required before use [US].
- **Fertility:** Not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Approved for children 12 years and over. Do not use in children under 12 years of age [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources; general adult dosing applies unless otherwise directed by a physician.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Caution is advised; patients must consult a doctor before use due to the risk of acetaminophen-induced hepatotoxicity [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** 
    - **Acetaminophen:** Acts as a pain reliever and fever reducer, likely through central inhibition of prostaglandin synthesis.
    - **Dextromethorphan HBr:** A cough suppressant that acts centrally on the cough center in the medulla [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics/Pharmacodynamics:** Not specified in the available source documentation.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- Not specified in available sources.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at room temperature [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from excessive heat and moisture. Do not use if the inner sealed packet is torn or broken (Tamper Evident) [US].
- **Inactive Ingredients:** Anhydrous citric acid, caramel, flavors, maltodextrin, potassium chloride, silica, sucralose, sucrose.
- **Electrolytes:** Each packet contains 6 mg of potassium [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as a Human OTC Drug. Regulated under OTC Monograph Drug M012 [US].
