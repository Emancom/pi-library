## 6. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed / Prominent warnings
- **Ingestion of more than 3 grams of omega‑3 fatty acids (such as DHA) per day has been associated with antithrombotic effects, including increased bleeding time and International Normalized Ratio (INR). Administration of omega‑3 fatty acids should be avoided in patients taking anticoagulants and in those known to have an inherited or acquired predisposition to bleeding.** [US]
- **Accidental overdose of iron-containing products is a leading cause of fatal poisoning in children under 6 years of age. KEEP THIS PRODUCT OUT OF REACH OF CHILDREN. In case of accidental overdose, call a doctor or poison control center immediately.** [US]

### General warnings and precautions
- Folic acid recommendations and caution:
  - Folic acid alone is not appropriate therapy for pernicious anemia and other megaloblastic anemias where vitamin B12 is deficient.
  - Daily folic acid doses above 1.0 mg may mask hematologic signs of pernicious anemia, permitting hematologic remission while neurologic manifestations progress.
- Biotin:
  - Biotin at levels higher than the recommended daily allowance may interfere with certain laboratory tests (including cardiovascular diagnostic tests such as troponin and various hormone assays) and may lead to incorrect test results. Patients should be advised to inform healthcare providers about all supplements containing biotin.
- Colorant/allergen caution:
  - Product contains FD&C Yellow No. 5 (tartrazine), which can cause allergic-type reactions (including bronchial asthma) in certain susceptible individuals; sensitivity is more frequently observed in persons with aspirin hypersensitivity.
- Allergen content:
  - Product contains fish oil and soy derivatives (soy lecithin); avoid or exercise caution in patients with fish or soy allergy.
- Allergic sensitization:
  - Allergic sensitization has been reported following administration of folic acid (see Adverse Effects).
- Monitoring requirements: Not specified in available sources (no explicit laboratory or clinical monitoring schedule provided)

## 8. Drug Interactions
- Pharmacodynamic interactions:
  - Concomitant anticoagulant therapy: Omega‑3 fatty acid component may increase bleeding risk and affect INR; concomitant use should be avoided in patients taking anticoagulants and in those with inherited or acquired bleeding predisposition. [US]
- Laboratory interactions:
  - Biotin may interfere with certain immunoassays and other laboratory tests (including troponin and hormone assays), potentially producing inaccurate results. Patients should inform healthcare providers about biotin-containing supplement use.
- Allergen-related interactions:
  - FD&C Yellow No. 5 (tartrazine) sensitivity is more frequent in individuals with aspirin hypersensitivity; this can precipitate allergic-type reactions.
- Other interactions:
  - Product contains fish oil and soy; consider these when assessing potential allergic responses or interactions with dietary restrictions.
- Management notes:
  - Avoid use of omega‑3 fatty acid–containing product in patients on anticoagulants or with bleeding disorders. [US]
  - Advise patients to disclose supplement use (particularly biotin) before laboratory testing; specific laboratory management steps (such as withholding biotin before testing) are not specified in the available sources.
