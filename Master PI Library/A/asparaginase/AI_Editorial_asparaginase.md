# Editorial Product Information: Rylaze (asparaginase erwinia chrysanthemi (recombinant)-rywn)

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the **United States (FDA)**. 
- At the time of this publication, only the United States PI was available for review. Consequently, jurisdictional comparisons for other regions (e.g., Australian, European, Canadian) are not included. 
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. It is not a substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Asparaginase erwinia chrysanthemi (recombinant)-rywn.
- **Chemical Class:** Asparagine-specific enzyme (L-asparaginase).
- **Brand Name:** **RYLAZE** [US].
- **ATC Code:** L01XX02 (Asparaginase).
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Injection: 10 mg/0.5 mL clear to opalescent, colorless to slightly yellow, preservative-free solution in a single-dose vial [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intramuscular (IM) injection only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Jazz Pharmaceuticals, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **RYLAZE** is indicated as a component of a multi-agent chemotherapeutic regimen for the treatment of acute lymphoblastic leukemia (ALL) and lymphoblastic lymphoma (LBL) in adult and pediatric patients (1 month or older) who have developed hypersensitivity to *E. coli*-derived asparaginase [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Pediatric ≥ 1 month)
There are two approved regimens to replace long-acting asparaginase products [US]:

1.  **48-hour Regimen:** 25 mg/m² administered intramuscularly every 48 hours.
2.  **Monday/Wednesday/Friday Regimen:** 
    *   25 mg/m² IM on Monday morning.
    *   25 mg/m² IM on Wednesday morning.
    *   50 mg/m² IM on Friday afternoon (administer 53 to 58 hours after the Wednesday dose).

### Recommended Premedication
Premedicate 30–60 minutes prior to administration to reduce the risk of hypersensitivity:
*   Acetaminophen.
*   H-1 receptor blocker (e.g., diphenhydramine).
*   H-2 receptor blocker (e.g., famotidine).

### Preparation and Administration Instructions
*   **Visual Inspection:** Check for particulate matter or discoloration. Discard if present.
*   **Administration:** Intramuscular use only. Do not shake the vial.
*   **Volume Limits:** Limit injection volume to 2 mL per site. If the total dose exceeds 2 mL, divide the dose equally into multiple syringes and use separate injection sites.
*   **Site Rotation:** Rotate injection sites; avoid scar tissue, or areas that are inflamed/swollen.

### Missed Dose Instructions
Not specified in available sources for the 48-hour regimen; however, for hepatotoxicity (>10x ULN bilirubin), instructions specify not to make up missed doses [US].

## 4. Contraindications
**RYLAZE** is contraindicated in patients with [US]:
*   History of serious hypersensitivity reactions to **RYLAZE**, including anaphylaxis.
*   History of serious pancreatitis during previous asparaginase therapy.
*   History of serious thrombosis during previous asparaginase therapy.
*   History of serious hemorrhagic events during previous asparaginase therapy.
*   Severe hepatic impairment.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
*   **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Occurred in 29% of patients (6% severe). Anaphylaxis occurred in 2% [US]. Monitor patients closely. Discontinue permanently for Grade 3 or 4 reactions.
*   **Pancreatitis:** Reported in 20% of patients (8% severe). Monitor for abdominal pain and amylase/lipase levels. Discontinue permanently for necrotizing or hemorrhagic pancreatitis.
*   **Thrombosis:** Serious events (e.g., sagittal sinus thrombosis, pulmonary embolism) occurred in 1%. Discontinue for severe or life-threatening thrombosis.
*   **Hemorrhage:** Reported in 25% of patients (2% severe). Monitor coagulation parameters (PT, PTT, fibrinogen).
*   **Hepatotoxicity:** Elevated transaminases or bilirubin occurred in 75% of patients. Monitor liver function tests (LFTs) prior to each cycle and weekly during treatment. Monitor for hepatic veno-occlusive disease (VOD), characterized by rapid weight gain, ascites, and hepatomegaly.

### Monitoring Requirements
Monitor bilirubin, transaminases, and glucose every 2–3 weeks and as clinically indicated [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions observed when used in multi-agent chemotherapeutic regimens:

**Very Common (>10%)**
*   **Gastrointestinal:** Nausea (47%), Stomatitis (27%), Abdominal pain (25%), Diarrhea (25%), Pancreatitis (22%), Decreased appetite (27%).
*   **Systemic:** Fatigue (36%), Pyrexia (30%), Infection (36%), Headache (36%), Insomnia (15%).
*   **Laboratory/Metabolic:** Abnormal LFTs (75%), Hyperglycemia (21%), Hypokalemia (22%), Dehydration (15%), Febrile neutropenia (30%).
*   **Musculoskeletal:** Musculoskeletal pain (45%).
*   **Vascular/Heme:** Hemorrhage (27%).
*   **Other:** Drug hypersensitivity (24%), Tachycardia (18%), Cough (15%).

**Serious Adverse Effects**
Serious reactions occurred in 60% of patients. The most frequent non-hematological serious reactions (≥5%) were febrile neutropenia, infection, drug hypersensitivity, pyrexia, nausea, dehydration, stomatitis, acute kidney injury, pancreatitis, and diarrhea [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Not studied/specified in available sources.
*   **Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** As part of multi-agent chemotherapy, additive toxicities (e.g., hepatotoxicity, coagulopathy) are expected.
*   **Management:** Monitor parameters as outlined in Section 5.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not assigned, but "Risk Summary" provided]. Based on animal studies, **RYLAZE** can cause fetal harm. Pregnancy testing is recommended for females of reproductive potential prior to treatment. 
*   **Contraception:** Females should use effective non-hormonal contraception during treatment and for 3 months after the final dose [US].
*   **Breastfeeding:** No data on presence in human milk. Due to potential adverse reactions in the infant, breastfeeding is not recommended during treatment and for 1 week after the final dose [US].
*   **Fertility:** Animal data showed decreased sperm count in males but no impact on fertility. No impact on female rat fertility was observed [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy established in patients 1 month to <17 years. No clinically meaningful PK differences were observed across pediatric age groups [US].
*   **Geriatric Use:** Insufficient data in patients ≥65 years to determine if they respond differently than younger patients [US].
*   **Renal Impairment:** Not studied [US].
*   **Hepatic Impairment:** Not studied in patients with baseline impairment. **RYLAZE** is contraindicated in patients with severe hepatic impairment [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
*   **Mechanism of Action:** **RYLAZE** is an enzyme that converts L-asparagine into aspartic acid and ammonia. Leukemic cells, which often lack asparagine synthetase, are unable to synthesize asparagine de novo and die due to depletion of exogenous asparagine.
*   **Pharmacokinetics:**
    *   **Absorption:** Median $T_{max}$ is 12 hours. Absolute bioavailability is 37% [US].
    *   **Distribution:** Volume of distribution is 1.37 L/m² [US].
    *   **Metabolism:** Expected to be catabolized into small peptides.
    *   **Elimination:** Clearance is 0.17 L/hour/m²; apparent half-life is 15.9 hours [US].
*   **Pharmacodynamics:** Exposure-response relationships and the time course of response are currently unknown.

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   **Study JZP458-201:** An open-label, multi-cohort trial in 225 evaluable patients (median age 10 years) who had hypersensitivity or silent inactivation to *E. coli*-derived asparaginase. 
*   **Efficacy Criterion:** Achievement and maintenance of nadir serum asparaginase activity (NSAA) ≥ 0.1 U/mL.
*   **Outcomes:** Simulations indicated that 96% of patients on the 48-hour regimen and over 91% on the Mon/Wed/Fri regimen achieved the target NSAA threshold [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   **Temperature:** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F).
*   **Handling:** Keep in the original carton to protect from light. Do not shake or freeze.
*   **Stability (Prepared Dose):** If not used immediately, stored in a syringe for up to 8 hours at room temperature (15°C to 25°C) or up to 24 hours under refrigeration [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
*   **United States:** Rx-only (Prescription).
