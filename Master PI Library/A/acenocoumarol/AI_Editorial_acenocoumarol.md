# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial synthesis is derived from the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical and regulatory data into a single, concise reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and should not be used as a substitute for the official, current Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN/USP); a carrier molecule for long-chain fatty acid transport.
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative [(R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt].
- **Brand Names:** **Levocarnitine Injection** [US: Baxter Healthcare Corporation].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01 (Alimentary tract and metabolism).
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency (Inborn Errors of Metabolism):** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that result in secondary carnitine deficiency [US-approved].
- **Carnitine Deficiency in End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance hemodialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
  - Recommended dose: 50 mg/kg as a slow (2 to 3 minute) bolus injection or by infusion. 
  - In severe metabolic crisis, a loading dose is often followed by an equivalent dose over the next 24 hours.
  - Frequency: Every 3 to 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours.
  - Maintenance: Subsequent daily doses are generally 50 mg/kg, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been administered.
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
  - Starting dose: 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight.
  - Administration: Administer as a slow (2 to 3 minute) bolus into the venous return line after each dialysis session.
  - Adjustments: Guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy.

### Administration Instructions
- Inspect parenteral products for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration.
- Discard any unused portion of a single-dose vial; the formulation contains no preservatives.

### Monitoring
- **Metabolic Disorders:** Obtain baseline plasma carnitine levels. Monitor weekly/monthly, including blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma concentrations (target range: 35 to 60 µmol/L).
- **ESRD:** Monitor trough (pre-dialysis) concentrations. Normal range is 40 µmol/L to 50 µmol/L.

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported [US]. These may occur within minutes of intravenous administration, particularly in ESRD patients. Discontinue immediately if a reaction occurs.
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with known seizure disorders, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed.
- **Thromboembolic Risk/Warfarin Interaction:** Increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) has been reported when levocarnitine is used concomitantly with warfarin.

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Clinical Trials Experience (ESRD Patients)
In double-blind, placebo-controlled trials, the following adverse events occurred at a frequency of ≥5% (regardless of causality):

| Body System | Adverse Event (Incidence ≥5%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Abdominal pain, accidental injury, asthenia, chest pain, fever, flu syndrome, headache, infection, injection site reaction, pain |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension, hypotension, tachycardia, arrhythmia, atrial fibrillation |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea, nausea, vomiting, anorexia, constipation, dyspepsia |
| **Metabolic** | Hypercalcemia, hyperkalemia, hypervolemia, peripheral edema, weight changes |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness, insomnia, paresthesia, depression, anxiety |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis, rhinitis, cough, dyspnea |
| **Skin** | Pruritus, rash |

### Specific Clinical Adverse Effects
- **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea and vomiting are common. Gastritis occurs less frequently.
- **Body Odor:** Noted as a less frequent but specific adverse effect.
- **Neurologic:** Seizures [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Concomitant use may result in an increased INR. Monitor INR levels after initiating **levocarnitine** or following dose adjustments [US].
- **Note:** Pharmacokinetic interactions for the intravenous form have not been extensively detailed beyond the warfarin interaction in provided sources.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Pregnancy Category not assigned in current labeling style, but noted as follows]: Reproductive studies in rats/rabbits (3.8x human dose) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed.
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk concentrations after administration. Weigh the risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant against the benefits of maternal supplementation [US].
- **Fertility:** Mutagenicity tests (Salmonella, etc.) were negative. Long-term carcinogenicity studies have not been performed [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders is weight-based (50 mg/kg), applicable to pediatric patients [US].
- **Elderly:** Specific dose adjustments for the elderly are not explicitly detailed in the source, though general caution is advised regarding organ function.
- **Renal Impairment:** 
  - Intravenous **levocarnitine** is specifically indicated for ESRD patients on hemodialysis.
  - **Precaution:** Safety/efficacy of *oral* levocarnitine has not been evaluated in renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in renal failure may lead to accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine/TMA and TMAO) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic/fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
  - **Distribution:** Not protein-bound. Distribution half-life is 0.585 hours [US].
  - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites are trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (feces).
  - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours (uncorrected for endogenous levels). Apparent terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Corrects carnitine deficiency (defined as <20 µmol/L) and normalizes the acylcarnitine/levocarnitine ratio.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients (20 mg/kg 3x weekly post-dialysis), trough levels increased from ~20 µmol/L to ~190 µmol/L after 8 weeks.
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** Evaluated 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses in ESRD patients over 24 weeks; trough concentrations remained dose-proportional and stable [US].
- **Clinical Outcomes Note:** While **levocarnitine** increases carnitine concentrations in ESRD, the impact on specific clinical outcomes (e.g., malaise, arrhythmias) has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Conditions:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat.
- **Compatibility:** Stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations of 0.5 mg/mL to 8.0 mg/mL [US].
