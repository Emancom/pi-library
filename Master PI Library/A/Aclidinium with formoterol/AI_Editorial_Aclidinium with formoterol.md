# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) provided from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**. 
- While the user context mentioned an Australian source for a different medication, the provided technical documentation pertains exclusively to **Levocarnitine Injection** (Baxter Healthcare Corporation). Consequently, this document reflects US-approved labeling data.
- **Purpose:** This document serves as a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. It is an editorial synthesis and must not be used as a substitute for the official PI when prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN/USP).
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection, USP [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01 (Not specified in provided source, but standard clinical classification).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US]; Manufactured by Baxter Pharmaceuticals India Private Ltd.

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency (Metabolic):** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that result in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing hemodialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Pediatrics)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    - The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg administered as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion. 
    - In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose is often followed by equivalent doses every 3 or 4 hours (never less than every 6 hours) over the first 24 hours. 
    - Maximum reported dose: 300 mg/kg [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:** 
    - Recommended starting dose: 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight) administered as a slow (2 to 3 minute) bolus into the venous return line after each dialysis session [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Inspect visually for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration.
- Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as it contains no preservatives [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable for up to 24 hours in PVC plastic bags at room temperature (25°C) when diluted in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations between 0.5 mg/mL and 8.0 mg/mL [US].

### Titration and Monitoring
- In ESRD, dose adjustments should be guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].
- Target plasma free carnitine concentrations should ideally be maintained between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known at the time of publication [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. These may occur within minutes of administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and consider the risks/benefits before re-administration [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with known seizure disorders, an increase in the frequency or severity of seizures has been observed [US].
- **Metabolite Accumulation (Oral vs. IV):** The safety of *oral* levocarnitine has not been evaluated in renal impairment. Chronic high doses of oral levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function or ESRD may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites, trimethylamine (TMA) and trimethylamine-N-oxide (TMAO) [US].
- **Monitoring:** Periodic monitoring of plasma carnitine concentrations, blood chemistry, and vital signs is recommended [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Very Common (>10%)
- **General:** Injection site reaction (up to 38%), pain (up to 35%), flu syndrome [US].
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (up to 21%), hypotension (up to 19%) [US].
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea (up to 35%), vomiting (up to 21%), nausea (up to 12%) [US].
- **Nervous:** Headache (up to 37%), dizziness (up to 18%), paresthesia (up to 12%) [US].
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (up to 27%), cough increase (up to 18%), dyspnea (up to 11%) [US].

### Common (1–10%)
- **Metabolic:** Hypercalcemia (up to 15%), hypervolemia (up to 12%), peripheral edema, weight changes [US].
- **Gastrointestinal:** Gastritis, abdominal pain, constipation, dyspepsia [US].
- **General:** Body odor, fever, asthenia, chest pain [US].
- **Musculoskeletal:** Leg cramps [US].

### Serious Adverse Effects
- **Anaphylaxis and Angioedema:** Reported in post-marketing surveillance [US].
- **Seizures:** New onset or exacerbation of existing condition [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have occurred when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin. It is recommended to monitor INR levels at the start of treatment or following dose adjustments [US].
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Levocarnitine is not protein-bound, reducing the likelihood of displacement-based interactions [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not explicitly assigned lettered category in current labeling, but reproductive studies in rats/rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** 
    - Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine concentrations in milk after exogenous administration. Benefits to the mother must be weighed against risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing is weight-based (50 mg/kg for metabolic disorders); specific age-related safety data beyond general dosing instructions are not detailed in the source [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** IV administration is indicated for ESRD patients on dialysis. Dose adjustments are required based on trough plasma carnitine levels [US]. (See Section 3 and 5).
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane for beta-oxidation. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and $\gamma$-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours. Mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients (20 mg/kg 3x weekly), mean pre-dialysis trough levels increased from ~20 µmol/L to 190 ± 55 µmol/L by week 8.
- **Dose-Ranging Data:** Studies using 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses over 12 and 24 weeks demonstrated dose-dependent increases in trough carnitine concentrations. However, the impact on specific clinical outcomes in this population has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat.
- **Shelf Life:** Discard unused portion of opened vials immediately [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as Rx-only (Human Prescription Drug) [US].
