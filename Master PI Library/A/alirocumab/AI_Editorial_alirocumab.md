# Editorial Product Information: Alirocumab

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial information is derived solely from the official Product Information provided by the **United States (FDA)** for **Praluent (alirocumab)**. 
- At the time of this writing, other jurisdictional documents (e.g., Australian, European, Canadian, Japanese) were not provided for synthesis. Consequently, this document reflects US-approved indications, dosing, and regulatory classifications.
- This document serves as a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals and is not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient**: Alirocumab (a human monoclonal antibody of the IgG1 isotype).
- **Chemical Class**: Proprotein convertase subtilisin kexin type 9 (PCSK9) inhibitor.
- **Brand Name**: **Praluent** [US].
- **ATC Code**: C10AX14.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths**:
    - **Single-dose pre-filled pen**: 75 mg/mL or 150 mg/mL [US].
    - **Single-dose pre-filled syringe**: 75 mg/mL or 150 mg/mL [US].
- **Route of Administration**: Subcutaneous injection.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor**: Sanofi-Aventis U.S. LLC [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Cardiovascular Risk Reduction**: To decrease the risk of major adverse cardiovascular events (including myocardial infarction, stroke, unstable angina requiring hospitalisation, and coronary heart disease death) in adults with established cardiovascular disease [US].
- **Primary Hyperlipidaemia**: As an adjunct to diet and exercise to reduce low-density lipoprotein cholesterol (LDL-C) in adults with primary hyperlipidaemia (including heterozygous familial hypercholesterolaemia [HeFH]) [US].
- **Heterozygous Familial Hypercholesterolaemia (HeFH) - Pediatric**: As an adjunct to diet and other LDL-C lowering therapies in pediatric patients aged 8 years and older [US].
- **Homozygous Familial Hypercholesterolaemia (HoFH)**: As an adjunct to other LDL-C lowering therapies in adults [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Adult Dosing
- **General Hyperlipidaemia/HeFH**: Starting dose is 75 mg subcutaneously every 2 weeks or 300 mg every 4 weeks. If the LDL-C response is insufficient, the dose may be increased to a maximum of 150 mg every 2 weeks [US].
- **Adults with HoFH or HeFH undergoing LDL apheresis**: The recommended dose is 150 mg every 2 weeks. The timing of administration can be independent of the apheresis session [US].

### Pediatric Dosing (HeFH, age 8 years and older)
- **Weight < 50 kg**: 150 mg every 4 weeks. May be adjusted to 75 mg every 2 weeks if response is inadequate [US].
- **Weight ≥ 50 kg**: 300 mg every 4 weeks. May be adjusted to 150 mg every 2 weeks if response is inadequate [US].

### Administration Instructions
- **Site Selection**: Inject into the thigh, abdomen, or upper arm. Rotate injection sites with each dose.
- **Preparation**: Allow the pen or syringe to reach room temperature (30–40 minutes) before use. Visually inspect for particles or discoloration; the solution should be clear and colourless to pale yellow [US].
- **300 mg Dose**: Administer as two consecutive 150 mg injections at two different sites [US].

### Missed Dose Instructions
- **Within 7 days of missed dose**: Administer the dose immediately and resume the original schedule.
- **More than 7 days (q2w schedule)**: Skip the missed dose and wait until the next scheduled dose.
- **More than 7 days (q4w schedule)**: Administer the dose immediately and start a new schedule based on that date [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- History of a serious hypersensitivity reaction to alirocumab or any excipients in the formulation [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions**: Serious reactions, including angioedema, hypersensitivity vasculitis, and reactions requiring hospitalisation, have been reported. If signs of serious hypersensitivity occur, discontinue treatment immediately and manage according to the standard of care [US].
- **Monitoring**: LDL-C levels should be measured when clinically appropriate. The lipid-lowering effect is generally measurable 4 weeks after initiation [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Common (>5%)**:
    - **Adults (Hyperlipidaemia)**: Injection site reactions (7%), influenza (6%).
    - **Adults (Established CV Disease)**: Myalgia (6%).
- **Other Reported Reactions (2–5% and >placebo)**: Diarrhoea, muscle spasms, and contusion [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects**: Hypersensitivity (including pruritus and rare cases of vasculitis), and elevations in serum transaminases (>3x ULN in 1.7% of patients) [US].
- **Immunogenicity**: In clinical trials, approximately 4.8% to 5.5% of adults developed anti-drug antibodies (ADA). Persistent or neutralizing antibodies may lead to attenuated LDL-C efficacy [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Statins**: Co-administration with a statin reduces the apparent half-life of alirocumab to approximately 12 days; however, this is not considered clinically meaningful.
- **Cytochrome P450**: Alirocumab is a protein and is not expected to affect CYP450 enzymes or transporter proteins (e.g., P-gp, OATP) [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy**: [US: No specific category provided under current labelling rules; formerly interpreted similarly to Category B/C]. Human IgG1 is known to cross the placental barrier. Animal studies in rats showed no embryo-fetal effects, but studies in monkeys showed suppression of the humoral immune response in infants. Use during pregnancy only if the potential benefit justifies the potential risk to the fetus [US].
- **Breastfeeding**: It is unknown if alirocumab is excreted in human milk. Monoclonal antibodies are generally degraded in the gastrointestinal tract and are not expected to enter neonatal circulation in substantial amounts. The developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding should be weighed against the clinical need for the drug [US].
- **Fertility**: No adverse effects on surrogate markers of fertility were observed in monkey studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use**: Safety and efficacy established for HeFH in patients aged 8 years and older. Safety not established in pediatric patients under 8 years or those with other types of hypercholesterolaemia [US].
- **Elderly Use**: No overall differences in safety or effectiveness were observed between patients ≥65 years and younger patients, though greater sensitivity in some older individuals cannot be ruled out [US].
- **Renal Impairment**: No dose adjustment is required for patients with mild to moderate renal impairment. Data are unavailable for patients with severe renal impairment [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment**: No dose adjustment is required for patients with mild to moderate hepatic impairment. Data are unavailable for patients with severe hepatic impairment [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action**: Alirocumab binds to PCSK9, preventing it from binding to LDL receptors (LDLR) on hepatocytes. By inhibiting PCSK9, which normally mediates LDLR degradation, alirocumab increases the number of available LDLRs to clear LDL from the circulation [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics**: Free PCSK9 is reduced in a concentration-dependent manner, with maximal suppression occurring within 4–8 hours of a single dose [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics**:
    - **Absorption**: Absolute bioavailability is approximately 85%. T-max is 3–7 days.
    - **Distribution**: Primarily in the circulatory system (Volume of distribution: 0.04 to 0.05 L/kg).
    - **Elimination**: Degraded into small peptides and amino acids. Steady state is reached after 2–3 doses. The median apparent half-life is 17–20 days at steady state [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ODYSSEY OUTCOMES**: A trial of 18,924 patients with established CV disease. Alirocumab significantly reduced the risk of the primary composite endpoint (CHD death, non-fatal MI, fatal/non-fatal stroke, or unstable angina requiring hospitalisation) with a Hazard Ratio of 0.85 (95% CI: 0.78, 0.93) [US].
- **ODYSSEY LONG TERM**: In patients with primary hyperlipidaemia, alirocumab 150 mg every 2 weeks resulted in a 58% greater reduction in LDL-C compared to placebo at week 24 [US].
- **ODYSSEY HoFH**: Adult patients with HoFH showed a 36% mean LDL-C reduction compared to placebo at week 12. Patients with "receptor-negative" alleles showed minimal response [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- Store in a refrigerator at **2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F)**.
- Keep in the original carton to protect from light.
- **Do not freeze. Do not shake.**
- **Alternative storage**: Once removed from the refrigerator, the product may be kept at room temperature (up to 25°C/77°F) for a maximum of **30 days**. Discard if not used within this period [US].
