# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial product information was synthesized from the official Product Information (PI) provided by the **United States (FDA)**. 
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonized editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical data and regulatory requirements into a single, concise reference. 
- This document is an editorial synthesis and is not intended to replace the official PI for prescribing purposes in any specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule/Amino acid derivative.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection, USP [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Sterile solution for injection: 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism which result in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg. 
    - **Administration:** Administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion [US].
    - **Initial Dosing:** In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose is often administered, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours.
    - **Frequency:** Dosing should occur every 3 to 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours.
    - **Maximum Dose:** The highest reported dose administered is 300 mg/kg [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - **Recommended Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
    - **Administration:** Slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
    - **Titration:** Dose adjustments (e.g., downward to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter and discoloration before administration.
- **Compatibility:** Stable and compatible when mixed in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations ranging from 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL for up to 24 hours in PVC bags at room temperature (25°C) [US].
- **Monitoring:** It is recommended to obtain plasma carnitine concentrations before initiating therapy. Weekly and monthly monitoring should include blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma free carnitine levels (targeted range: 35 to 60 µmol/L) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have occurred, primarily in ESRD patients on dialysis. Some reactions manifest within minutes of administration. Treatment must be discontinued immediately if a severe reaction occurs [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in both patients with and without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with a known seizure disorder, levocarnitine may increase the frequency or severity of seizures [US].
- **Monitoring Requirements:** Periodic monitoring of plasma carnitine levels is essential to guide dosing and ensure therapeutic levels are maintained [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Clinical Trial Experience (ESRD Patients)
In double-blind, placebo-controlled trials, the following adverse events were reported with a frequency ≥5% (regardless of causality) [US]:

| Body System | Adverse Event (≥5%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Body as a Whole** | Abdominal pain, accidental injury, asthenia, back pain, chest pain, fever, flu syndrome, headache, infection, injection site reaction, pain |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension, hypotension, tachycardia, palpitations |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea, nausea, vomiting, dyspepsia, anorexia |
| **Metabolic/Nutritional** | Hypercalcemia, hyperkalemia, hypervolemia, peripheral edema, weight change |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness, insomnia, paresthesia, depression |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis, cough increase, dyspnea, rhinitis |
| **Skin/Appendages** | Pruritus |

### Serious and Other Adverse Effects
- **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea and vomiting; less frequently, gastritis and body odor [US].
- **Neurologic:** Seizures (post-marketing report) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is used concomitantly with warfarin. 
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after any dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not explicitly assigned in source, but reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm.
    - **Recommendation:** Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk carnitine concentrations after administration. The risks to the infant from excess carnitine must be weighed against the benefits of supplementation to the mother [US].
- **Fertility:** Mutagenicity tests (Salmonella, S. cerevisiae, S. pombe) were negative. Long-term carcinogenicity studies have not been performed [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders is calculated by weight (50 mg/kg); safety and efficacy for ESRD indications follow adult protocols as prompted by clinical need [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Oral levocarnitine has not been evaluated in renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in patients with severely compromised renal function or ESRD may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide), which are normally excreted in urine [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane for beta-oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites are trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Following IV bolus (20 mg/kg), approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours (uncorrected for endogenous levels). The mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].
    - **Bioavailability:** Absolute bioavailability of oral formulations is approximately 15–16% [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients (20 mg/kg three times weekly), mean pre-dialysis (trough) levels increased from ~20 µmol/L to 190 ± 55 µmol/L at 8 weeks. While increases in carnitine levels are demonstrated, the effect on clinical outcomes in the ESRD population has not been determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Cautions:** Avoid excessive heat; protect from freezing.
- **Handling:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as it contains no preservative [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources (refer to local jurisdiction) [US].
