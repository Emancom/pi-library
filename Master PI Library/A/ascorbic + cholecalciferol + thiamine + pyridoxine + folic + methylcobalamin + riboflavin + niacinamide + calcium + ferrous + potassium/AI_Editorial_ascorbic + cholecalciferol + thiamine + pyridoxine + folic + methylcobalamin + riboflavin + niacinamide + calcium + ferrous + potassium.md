## 4. Dosage and Administration
### General dosing (adult)
- Usual adult dose: one tablet taken orally once to twice daily, or as prescribed by a licensed medical practitioner. [US]

- Riboflavin (Vitamin B2): 2.5 mg
- Niacinamide (Vitamin B3): 16 mg

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed warnings
- None explicitly boxed in the provided source. The source includes a highlighted warning regarding accidental iron overdose in children (see below).

### General warnings and precautions
- **WARNING regarding iron toxicity in children:** Iron-containing products have been identified as a leading cause of fatal poisoning from accidental overdose in children under the age of 6. This product must be kept out of the reach of children. In case of accidental ingestion, a doctor or poison control center should be contacted immediately.
- Use with caution in patients who have a medical condition, are pregnant, lactating, trying to conceive, are under the age of 18, or are taking other medications.
- This product is contraindicated in patients with known hypersensitivity to any ingredient.
- Hypersensitivity reactions have been reported with oral folic acid administration; if adverse side effects occur, a physician should be consulted immediately.
