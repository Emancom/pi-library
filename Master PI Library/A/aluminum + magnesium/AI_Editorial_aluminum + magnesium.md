## 3. Approved Indications
- Relief of:
  - acid indigestion
  - heartburn
  - sour stomach
  - upset stomach associated with these symptoms
- Note: These indications are presented as stated in the available source [US].

## 4. Dosage and Administration
### General adult dosing
- Chew 2 to 4 tablets completely four times daily, or as directed by a physician.
- Administer after meals and at bedtime, or as needed for symptom relief.
- For best results, follow administration with approximately a half glass of water or other liquid.
- **Do not swallow whole**; tablets are intended to be chewed completely.

### Maximums, duration and special instructions
- Maximum usual daily dose (based on dosing schedule above): up to 16 tablets in 24 hours (4 doses × up to 4 tablets per dose).
- If on a sodium‑restricted diet, do not take more than 16 tablets in 24 hours [US].
- Do not use the maximum dosage for more than 2 weeks without physician direction [US].
