# Originality Audit Report: aprotinin

## Audit Summary
- **Originality Score:** 78/100  
- **Verbatim Copied Passages Found:** 5
- **Overall Assessment:** Pass with Concern

## Verbatim Copied Passages
1.  **Tissue Sealing Indication:** "to prevent leakage from colonic anastomoses following the reversal of temporary colostomies"
    - **Source:** Document 2 (FDA), Page 1 (Indications) and Page 3.
    - **Location:** Editorial Section 2 (Approved Indications).

2.  **Pressure Recommendation:** "avoid using pressure above 1.4-1.7 bars (20 – 25 psi)"
    - **Source:** Document 1 (TGA), Page 8 (Section 4.4).
    - **Location:** Editorial Section 4 (Warnings and Precautions - Air or Gas Embolism).

3.  **Application Timing:** "The surgeon has up to 60 seconds to... position the graft... hold... with gentle compression for at least 3 minutes"
    - **Source:** Document 1 (TGA), Page 2 (Section 4.2).
    - **Location:** Editorial Section 5 (Dosage and Administration - Application).

4.  **Pharmacokinetics:** "half-life of 30 to 60 minutes and are excreted via the kidneys" (Note: source uses 'eliminated' vs 'excreted')
    - **Source:** Document 2 (FDA), Page 15 (Section 12.3).
    - **Location:** Editorial Section 9 (Pharmacokinetics).

5.  **Storage Parameters:** "Unopened pouches may be stored for up to 48 hours at room temperature (15°C to 25°C)"
    - **Source:** Document 2 (FDA), Page 6 (Table 3) and Page 35.
    - **Location:** Editorial Section 11 (Storage Conditions).

## Close Paraphrases
1.  **Hemostasis Indication:** The phrase "adjunct to achieve bleeding control... when conventional methods (e.g., sutures, ligatures, or cautery) are impractical or ineffective" is a very close paraphrase of the FDA's "adjunct to hemostasis... when control of bleeding by conventional surgical techniques (such as suture, ligature, and cautery) is ineffective or impractical."
2.  **Incompatibility Section:** The instruction regarding solutions containing alcohol, iodine, or heavy metals denaturing proteins is structured almost identically to Section 6.2 of the TGA document and Section 5.2 of the FDA document.
3.  **Hypersensitivity Monitoring:** The list of symptoms (bradycardia, hypotension, bronchospasm) is a truncated version of the list found in Section 4.4 of the TGA document and Section 5.1 of the FDA document.

## Originality Strengths
- **The Editorial Note:** This is entirely original writing that establishes the scope and methodology of the synthesis.
- **Section 2 "Note on Formulation Differences":** Successfully synthesizes a critical clinical distinction (high vs. low thrombin) between the two source products that is not explicitly contrasted in either individual document.
- **Mechanism of Action (Section 10):** The writer has successfully summarized complex biochemical processes into a clear, three-step numbered list using their own structural logic.
- **Drug Interactions (Section 7):** Merges the specific warning about oxidized cellulose (FDA) with general disinfectant warnings (TGA/FDA) in a new structure.

## Differences from Source PIs
- **Synthesis of Regional Guidance:** The document effectively flags which regulator (TGA vs. FDA) provided specific data points, which is a value-add not present in the sources.
- **Comparison of Thrombin Levels:** The editorial explicitly connects the thrombin concentration (4 IU vs high concentrations) to the clinical use case (adherence vs. hemostasis), whereas the sources only describe their own specific product.
- **Merged Storage Instructions:** Section 11 combines two very different storage timelines (48 hours vs. 14 days) into a single comparative table/list, aiding clinician decision-making across products.
- **Omission of Data:** The editorial appropriately omits specific clinical trial tables, manufacturer addresses, and complex viral reduction log-factor tables, focusing instead on the practical "Editorial" summary.

## Recommendation
**PASS WITH CONCERN**

The editorial is an excellent piece of synthesis that provides higher utility than the individual sources. However, several technical descriptions (indications, storage, and application timing) have been copied nearly verbatim. While regulatory precision often requires using specific phrases (especially for indications and contraindications), the following revisions are recommended to improve the originality score:

1.  **Section 2 (Tissue Sealing):** Change to "Indicated as a surgical adjunct to ensure colonic anastomoses remain leak-free following colostomy reversal surgery."
2.  **Section 5 (Timing):** Change to "Clinicians are afforded a 60-second window to manipulate the graft; once positioned, the site requires 3 minutes of manual pressure to ensure adequate adherence."
3.  **Section 9 (Pharmacokinetics):** Change to "Aprotinin possesses a relatively short systemic presence, with a renal clearance half-life typically ranging between 0.5 and 1 hour."
