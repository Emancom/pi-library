## 3. Approved Indications
- Indicated for the treatment of iron deficiency anemia, which may occur due to an increased
  need for iron, a deficient intake of iron, or an excessive loss of iron [US].

## 4. Dosage and Administration
### General dosing (adult)
- Adults and adolescents (persons over 12 years of age): one (1) capsule daily [US].

### Dosing by indication
- Treatment of iron deficiency anemia: one capsule daily (as above) [US].

### Administration instructions
