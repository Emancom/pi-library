# Editorial Product Information: Aztreonam

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions’ Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial: **Australian (TGA)**. 
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview of **aztreonam** for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical data and regulatory requirements.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis intended for informational purposes and is not a substitute for the official Product Information approved for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Aztreonam (a synthetic monocyclic beta-lactam antibiotic; monobactam).
- **Brand Names:** **Azactam** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** J01DF01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Powder for injection, 1 g (sodium-free, containing approximately 750 mg L-arginine per gram of aztreonam) [Australian].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) or Intramuscular (IM).
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Arrotex Pharmaceuticals Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
**Aztreonam** is indicated for the treatment of infections caused by susceptible gram-negative aerobes [Australian]:
- **Urinary Tract Infections:** Including complicated and uncomplicated cases.
- **Gonorrhoea:** Acute uncomplicated cases.
- **Cystitis:** Acute uncomplicated cases.
- **Serious Systemic Infections:** Used in combination with other suitable antibiotics when "problem organisms" are suspected or known to be susceptible.
- **Meningitis:** Caused by *Haemophilus influenzae* or *Neisseria meningitidis*. **Aztreonam** must not be used as monotherapy for meningitis; it must be used in combination with other appropriate antibiotics [Australian].
- **Pseudomonas Infections:** Serious *P. aeruginosa* infections may benefit from concurrent use with an aminoglycoside due to potential synergy [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
Dosage depends on the severity and site of infection. The maximum recommended dose is 8 g per day [Australian].

| Type of Infection | Dose (g) | Frequency (hr) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Urinary tract infection | 0.5 or 1 | 8 or 12 |
| Moderately severe systemic infections | 1 or 2 | 8 or 12 |
| Severe/life-threatening infections | 2 | 6 or 8 |

### Administration Instructions
- **Intramuscular:** Reconstitute with at least 3 mL of diluent per gram. Administer via deep injection into a large muscle mass (e.g., gluteus maximus) [Australian].
- **Intravenous Bolus:** Reconstitute with 6–10 mL Water for Injection. Inject slowly over 3 to 5 minutes [Australian].
- **Intravenous Infusion:** Reconstitute to a concentration not exceeding 2% w/v (at least 50 mL solution per gram). Infuse over 30 minutes [Australian].

### Meningitis Duration [Australian]
- *N. meningitidis*: 7–10 days.
- *H. influenzae*: 10–14 days.

## 4. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to **aztreonam** or any component of the formulation (including L-arginine) [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity:** Inquire regarding histories of reactions to other beta-lactams (penicillins/cephalosporins). While cross-reactivity is low due to the monobactam structure, caution is required. Discontinue immediately if an allergic reaction occurs [Australian].
- ***Clostridium difficile*-Associated Diarrhoea (CDAD):** Reported with nearly all antibiotics, including **aztreonam**. Severity ranges from mild diarrhoea to fatal pseudomembranous colitis. Avoid drugs that delay peristalsis [Australian].
- **Encephalopathy:** Use of beta-lactams can cause metabolic encephalopathy (confusion, seizures, movement disorders), especially in patients with renal impairment or following overdose [Australian].
- **Severe Cutaneous Adverse Reactions (SCAR):** Including SJS, TEN, DRESS, and AGEP have been reported with beta-lactams. Discontinue if SCAR is suspected [Australian].
- **L-Arginine Content:** Exposure to L-arginine in neonates (particularly low birth weight) may lead to increased serum arginine, insulin, and indirect bilirubin [Australian].
- **Overgrowth:** Prolonged use may result in the overgrowth of non-susceptible organisms, including fungi [Australian].

## 6. Adverse Effects
**Aztreonam** is generally well-tolerated; fewer than 2% of patients discontinued therapy in clinical trials [Australian].

| System Organ Class | Adverse Reactions |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Hypersensitivity** | Anaphylaxis, angioedema, bronchospasm. |
| **Dermatological** | Rash, pruritus, urticaria, purpura, erythema multiforme, exfoliative dermatitis, SJS, TEN, DRESS. |
| **Gastrointestinal** | Diarrhoea, nausea/vomiting, abdominal cramps, mouth ulcers, altered taste. |
| **Hepatobiliary** | Elevated transaminases and alkaline phosphatase (usually reversible); jaundice and hepatitis (rare). |
| **Haematological** | Eosinophilia, thrombocytosis, leukocytosis, anaemia, neutropenia, pancytopenia (rare). |
| **Local** | Phlebitis/thrombophlebitis at IV site; mild discomfort at IM site. |
| **Neurological** | Confusion, dizziness, vertigo, seizures, paraesthesia, encephalopathy. |

- **Laboratory:** A positive direct or indirect Coombs test may develop [Australian].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Aminoglycosides:** Synergistic activity against *P. aeruginosa* is possible but not always predictable. Monitor renal function if used concurrently [Australian].
- **Probenecid and Frusemide:** Concomitant administration causes clinically insignificant increases in **aztreonam** serum levels [Australian].
- **Beta-lactamase Inducers:** Antibiotics such as cefoxitin or imipenem may cause antagonism for certain gram-negative aerobes (*Enterobacter* and *Pseudomonas* species) [Australian].
- **Alcohol:** No reports of disulfiram-like reactions have been noted [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [Australian Category B1]. **Aztreonam** crosses the placenta. Human data are limited; use only if clearly needed [Australian].
- **Breastfeeding:** **Aztreonam** is excreted in breast milk at concentrations <1% of maternal serum levels. Consider temporary discontinuation of nursing during treatment [Australian].
- **Fertility:** No data available [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for infants >1 week is 30 mg/kg Q8H. For severe infections in children ≥2 years, 50 mg/kg Q6–8H is recommended. Data for neonates <1 week are limited [Australian].
- **Elderly Use:** Renal status is the primary determinant of dose. Estimate Creatinine Clearance (Clcr) as serum creatinine alone may be inaccurate [Australian].
- **Renal Impairment:** After an initial loading dose (1–2 g), reduce maintenance doses based on Clcr [Australian]:
    - Clcr 30–79 mL/min: 1/2 of normal dose.
    - Clcr 10–29 mL/min: 1/3 of normal dose.
    - Clcr <10 mL/min: 1/4 of normal dose.
    - **Haemodialysis:** For serious infections, give 1/8 of the initial dose after each dialysis session [Australian].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Dose reduction is recommended for long-term treatment of alcoholic cirrhosis, particularly if renal function is also impaired [Australian].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Bactericidal activity via binding to Penicillin Binding Protein 3 (PBP3), which inhibits bacterial cell wall synthesis. It is highly resistant to hydrolysis by many beta-lactamases (penicillinases and cephalosporinases) [Australian].
- **Microbiology:** Spectrum is limited to gram-negative aerobes, including *E. coli*, *Klebsiella* spp., *P. mirabilis*, *S. marcescens*, *H. influenzae*, and *P. aeruginosa*. It has no activity against anaerobes or gram-positive organisms [Australian].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not absorbed GI; peak serum levels occur ~1 hour after IM injection. Average half-life is 1.7 hours in adults with normal renal function. Approximately 60–70% is excreted unchanged in the urine [Australian].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- Pivotal trial data in the source document focus on pharmacokinetic parameters across different age groups and renal functions, demonstrating that serum half-life increases significantly with declining renal function (up to 8.5 hours in the elderly with Clcr 40 mL/min) [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Powder:** Store below 30°C.
- **Reconstituted Solution:** IM solutions must be used within 48 hours if kept below 25°C. IV solutions should be used immediately [Australian].
