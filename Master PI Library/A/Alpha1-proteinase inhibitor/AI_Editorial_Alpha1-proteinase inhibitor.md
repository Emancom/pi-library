# Editorial Product Information: Alpha1-Proteinase Inhibitor (Human) [Prolastin-C Liquid]

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesises information from the following official source: **US (FDA)**.
- **Note:** Only the United States Product Information was available for the current synthesis. Clinicians in other regions (e.g., Australia, Europe, Canada) should consult their local regulatory documentation for specific jurisdictional requirements.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. It is not a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Alpha1-Proteinase Inhibitor (Human) [Alpha1-PI].
- **Brand Name:** **Prolastin-C Liquid** [US].
- **ATC Code:** B02AB02 (Alpha1-antitrypsin).
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Solution for intravenous injection available in single-dose vials of approximately 500 mg (10 mL), 1,000 mg (20 mL), and 4,000 mg (80 mL) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Grifols Therapeutics LLC [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Chronic Augmentation and Maintenance Therapy:** Indicated for adults with clinical evidence of emphysema resulting from severe hereditary deficiency of Alpha1-PI (Alpha1-antitrypsin deficiency) [US].
- **Limitations of Use:**
    - Clinical data have not conclusively demonstrated an effect on pulmonary exacerbations or the progression of emphysema in randomized trials [US].
    - Long-term clinical data for the liquid formulation are unavailable [US].
    - Not indicated for lung disease where severe Alpha1-PI deficiency has not been confirmed [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **Standard Adult Dose:** 60 mg/kg body weight administered intravenously once per week [US].
- **Preparation:**
    - Allow vials to reach room temperature before use.
    - Inspect for particulate matter; the solution should be clear, colorless to pale yellow/green. Do not use if cloudy or discolored.
    - Multiple vials may be pooled into a sterile container using aseptic technique [US].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - **Filter Required:** Must be administered through an intravenous set equipped with a 5 to 15-micron infusion filter (not supplied) [US].
    - **Infusion Rate:** Administer at a rate of 0.08 mL/kg/min, adjusted based on patient comfort and response. A 60 mg/kg dose typically takes approximately 15 minutes to infuse [US].
    - **Compatibility:** Administer separately; do not mix with other agents or diluting solutions [US].
- **Stability:** Pooled solution must be administered within three hours of preparation if stored at room temperature [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **IgA Deficiency:** Patients with selective or severe Immunoglobulin A (IgA) deficiency who have known antibodies against IgA, due to the high risk of severe hypersensitivity or anaphylaxis [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** History of anaphylaxis or severe systemic reactions to Alpha1-PI products [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Acute reactions, including anaphylaxis, may occur. Monitor vital signs and observe the patient closely during infusion. If symptoms (e.g., dyspnea, hypotension, generalized urticaria, syncope) occur, discontinue the infusion immediately and initiate emergency treatment. Epinephrine should be readily available [US].
- **Transmissible Infectious Agents:** As this product is derived from human plasma, it carries a theoretical risk of transmitting infectious agents, including viruses (e.g., HIV, Hepatitis), the variant Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease (vCJD) agent, and other unknown pathogens. Risk is mitigated by donor screening and manufacturing processes (solvent/detergent treatment and 15 nm nanofiltration) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Common Adverse Effects (>5%):**
    - Diarrhea (6%) [US].
    - Fatigue (6%) [US].
- **Other Reported Effects (Clinical Trials):** Upper respiratory tract infection, urinary tract infection, nausea, headache, dizziness, dyspnea, and musculoskeletal pain (back pain, arthralgia) [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:**
    - Severe hypersensitivity and anaphylactic reactions.
    - In clinical trials, one subject experienced a serious abdominal and extremity rash that resolved with treatment but led to withdrawal upon rechallenge [US].
- **Postmarketing Experience:** Tachycardia, blood pressure increase, malaise, influenza-like illness, and vomiting have been reported [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources [US].
- **Clinical Management:** No specific drug-drug interaction studies have been conducted.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** There are no data on use in pregnant women. Animal reproduction studies have not been conducted. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Presence in human milk and effects on the breastfed infant or milk production are unknown. Consider developmental/health benefits of breastfeeding alongside the mother's clinical need and potential infant risks [US].
- **Fertility:** Data not specified in available sources [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness in the paediatric population have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical trials did not include sufficient numbers of subjects aged 65 and over to determine if they respond differently than younger subjects. Dosing should be cautious and appropriate for the patient's overall clinical situation [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Specific dose adjustments for renal or hepatic impairment are not specified in available sources [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Alpha1-PI is the primary anti-protease in the lower respiratory tract. It inhibits neutrophil elastase (NE), an enzyme capable of degrading alveolar walls. Augmentation therapy corrects the protease-antiprotease imbalance, protecting lung tissue from inappropriate proteolysis [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Bioequivalence:** Prolastin-C Liquid is bioequivalent to the predecessor lyophilized Prolastin-C [US].
    - **Half-life:** Approximately 156 hours (antigenic content) to 126 hours (functional activity) [US].
    - **Steady State:** Trough levels maintained at approximately 17.7 µM following weekly dosing of 60 mg/kg [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pharmacokinetic Comparability:** A randomized, double-blind crossover study in 32 subjects demonstrated bioequivalence between the liquid and lyophilized formulations.
- **Safety Study:** A 20-week open-label study in 38 subjects showed that weekly infusions of 60 mg/kg were generally well-tolerated and maintained Alpha1-PI blood levels above the historical target of 11 µM [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Refrigeration:** Store at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F). Do not freeze [US].
- **Room Temperature:** May be stored at temperatures not exceeding 25°C (77°F) for up to one month. Once stored at room temperature, it must be used or discarded; do not return to refrigeration [US].
- **Shelf Life:** Use by the expiration date printed on the label [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as Rx Only (Prescription Only) [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified for other jurisdictions in available sources.
