# Editorial Product Information: Avacopan

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial: **Australian (TGA)**. 
- Purpose: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of **avacopan** for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical and regulatory data into a single reference.
- Disclaimer: This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory documents for definitive prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Avacopan (a selective antagonist of the human complement 5a receptor).
- **Brand Name(s):** **Tavneos** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** L04AJ05.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 10 mg hard capsules. The capsules feature a yellow body and light orange cap, imprinted with "CCX168" in black ink [Australian].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Seqirus Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **ANCA-associated Vasculitis:** Indicated for the treatment of adults with granulomatosis with polyangiitis (GPA) and microscopic polyangiitis (MPA) in combination with a rituximab or cyclophosphamide-based regimen [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Adult Dosing:** 30 mg (three 10 mg capsules) taken twice daily, in the morning and evening [Australian].
- **Administration Instructions:** 
    - Must be taken with food [Australian].
    - Capsules must be swallowed whole with water; do not crush, chew, or open [Australian].
    - Grapefruit and grapefruit juice should be avoided during treatment as they may increase drug concentrations [Australian].
- **Dose Management (Hepatic):** 
    - Re-assess/temporarily stop if ALT or AST >3 × ULN.
    - Temporarily stop if ALT or AST >5 × ULN.
    - Permanently discontinue if ALT or AST >8 × ULN, or if transaminase elevations are accompanied by bilirubin >2 × ULN, INR >1.5, or symptoms of hepatic injury (fatigue, nausea, right upper quadrant pain) [Australian].
- **Missed Dose Instructions:** If a dose is missed, it should be taken as soon as possible. However, if the next scheduled dose is less than three hours away, the missed dose should be skipped [Australian].

## 4. Contraindications
- Hypersensitivity to **avacopan** or any of the excipients (e.g., PEG-40 hydrogenated castor oil) [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hepatotoxicity:** Serious adverse reactions involving elevated transaminases and total bilirubin have occurred. Vanishing bile duct syndrome has been reported post-marketing. Baseline LFTs are required, with periodic monitoring as clinically indicated. Avoid use in patients with active liver disease (AST/ALT or bilirubin >3 × ULN) [Australian].
- **Hematologic Effects:** Monitor white blood cell (WBC) count before and during treatment. Do not initiate if WBC <3.5 x 10⁹/L, neutrophils <1.5 x 10⁹/L, or lymphocytes <0.5 x 10⁹/L [Australian].
- **Serious Infections:** Serious infections, including pneumonia and opportunistic infections, have been reported. **Avacopan** has not been studied in patients with HBV, HCV, or HIV; caution is advised in these populations [Australian].
- **Neisseria meningitidis:** While **avacopan** does not decrease the formation of the membrane attack complex, clinicians should monitor for signs of Neisseria infections according to standard practice [Australian].
- **Pneumocystis jirovecii pneumonia (PJP):** Prophylaxis is recommended for adults with GPA/MPA during treatment as per local guidelines [Australian].
- **Angioedema:** Cases have been reported. Treatment must be withheld if angioedema develops [Australian].
- **Interactions:** Avoid concomitant use with strong CYP3A4 inducers. Exercise caution with strong CYP3A4 inhibitors [Australian].
- **Cardiac Disorders:** Increased risk of myocardial infarction and cardiac failure may exist, particularly in regimens using cyclophosphamide followed by azathioprine [Australian].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Very Common (>10%):** Nausea (23.5%), peripheral oedema (21.1%), headache (20.5%), arthralgia (18.7%), hypertension (18.1%), cough (15.7%), nasopharyngitis (15.1%), diarrhoea (15.1%), vomiting (15.1%), upper respiratory tract infection (14.5%), and rash (11.4%) [Australian].
- **Common (1–10%):** Muscle spasms, pyrexia, fatigue, back pain, myalgia, epistaxis, dizziness, insomnia, and hypercholesterolaemia [Australian].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Liver function abnormalities (5.4%), pneumonia (4.8%), angioedema (1.2%), and rare cases of agranulocytosis [Australian].
- **Post-Marketing:** Vanishing bile duct syndrome [Australian].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:**
    - **CYP3A4 Substrate:** **Avacopan** is a substrate of CYP3A4. Strong inducers (e.g., rifampicin) reduce exposure by ~93% and must be avoided. Strong inhibitors (e.g., itraconazole) increase exposure ~2.2-fold; use with caution and monitor for side effects [Australian].
    - **CYP3A4 Inhibitor:** **Avacopan** is a moderate inhibitor of CYP3A4. It can increase the exposure of substrates with a narrow therapeutic index (e.g., alfentanil, ciclosporin, sirolimus). Co-administration with simvastatin increased its AUC 2.57-fold [Australian].
- **P-glycoprotein (P-gp):** The excipient PEG-40 hydrogenated castor oil may increase the exposure of low-bioavailability P-gp substrates (e.g., dabigatran etexilate); exercise caution [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** **Category D** [Australian]. There are no data in pregnant women. Animal studies showed increased abortions in rabbits and skeletal variations in hamsters at sub-clinical or clinical exposures. **Avacopan** is not recommended during pregnancy or in women of childbearing potential not using contraception [Australian].
- **Breastfeeding:** Animal data suggest secretion into milk (pup-to-maternal plasma ratio 0.37). A risk to newborns/infants cannot be excluded. A decision must be made to either discontinue nursing or the drug [Australian].
- **Fertility:** No human data available. No effects on male or female fertility were observed in hamsters [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Not recommended for use in adolescents or children under 17 years of age. Safety and efficacy have not been established in these populations [Australian].
- **Elderly Use:** No dose adjustment required. Data are limited for patients over 75 years of age [Australian].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment needed. Not studied in patients with eGFR <15 mL/min/1.73 m² or those requiring dialysis/plasma exchange [Australian].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment for mild or moderate impairment. Not recommended for severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh Class C) [Australian].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** A selective antagonist of the C5a receptor (C5aR1/CD88). It competitively inhibits the interaction between C5aR1 and C5a, reducing neutrophil activation, migration, and adherence to inflamed small blood vessels. It does not inhibit C5b-9 (terminal complement complex) formation [Australian].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Tmax is ~2 hours (fasted). Food increases AUC by ~72% [Australian].
    - **Distribution:** Plasma protein binding is >99.9%. Large volume of distribution (3,000–11,000 L) [Australian].
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily CYP3A4-mediated. Major active metabolite is M1 (mono-hydroxylated product), which is roughly equipotent to the parent drug [Australian].
    - **Elimination:** Terminal half-life is ~21 days. Primarily eliminated via faeces (77%) [Australian].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ADVOCATE Study:** A pivotal Phase 3, double-blind trial (n=330) compared **avacopan** (30 mg BID for 52 weeks) to a tapered prednisone regimen. All patients received rituximab or cyclophosphamide.
    - **Primary Endpoints:** **Avacopan** was non-inferior to prednisone for clinical remission at Week 26 (72.3% vs 70.1%) and superior for sustained remission at Week 52 (65.7% vs 54.9%) [Australian].
    - **Secondary Outcomes:** Significant reductions in glucocorticoid-related toxicity (via the Glucocorticoid Toxicity Index) and improved eGFR recovery were observed in the **avacopan** group [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- Store below 30°C [Australian].
- Keep in the original bottle to protect from light [Australian].
- The bottle is high-density polyethylene (HDPE) with a child-resistant closure [Australian].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule:** **Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine)** [Australian].
