# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction:
    - **United States** (Food and Drug Administration)
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of **levocarnitine** for use by healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an expert editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult the current approved PI in their specific region before prescribing.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule/Amino acid derivative.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Sterile injection, 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that result in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Metabolic Disorders)
- **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion [US].
- **Loading Dose:** Often administered in patients with severe metabolic crisis, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours [US].
- **Frequency:** Doses should be given every 3 to 4 hours (q3h or q4h), and never less than every 6 hours (q6h) [US].
- **Maximum Dose:** The highest administered dose reported is 300 mg/kg [US].

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
- **Administration:** Slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].
- **Dose Adjustment:** Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations [US].

### Administration Instructions
- **Visual Inspection:** Inspect for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration [US].
- **Compatibility:** Compatible and stable for up to 24 hours in PVC bags at room temperature (25°C) when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer's (concentration range 0.5 mg/mL to 8.0 mg/mL) [US].
- **Discarding:** The formulation contains no preservatives; discard any unused portion of opened vials [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Known Contraindications:** None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warnings
- None specified in available sources.

### General Warnings
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. These may occur within minutes of administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and initiate appropriate medical care [US].
- **Seizures:** Levocarnitine has been associated with seizures in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In patients with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been reported [US].

### Precautions and Monitoring
- **Renal Insufficiency (Oral Formulation):** The safety of oral levocarnitine has not been evaluated in renal impairment. Chronic high doses of oral levocarnitine in ESRD patients may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites, trimethylamine (TMA) and trimethylamine-N-oxide (TMAO) [US].
- **Monitoring Requirements:** Obtain baseline plasma carnitine concentrations. Weekly and monthly monitoring should include blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma carnitine levels (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common Adverse Effects (≥5%)
The following were reported in ESRD clinical trials regardless of causality [US]:
- **Body as a Whole:** Abdominal pain (5–21%), headache (3–37%), infection (10–24%), injection site reaction (27–38%), pain (21–35%), fever (5–12%), flu syndrome (15–29%).
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (18–21%), hypotension (3–19%), tachycardia (5–9%), chest pain (6–15%).
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea (9–35%), nausea (5–12%), vomiting (9–21%).
- **Metabolic/Nutritional:** Hypercalcemia (6–15%), hypervolemia (3–12%).
- **Respiratory:** Cough increase (10–18%), dyspnea (3–11%), pharyngitis (15–27%).
- **Nervous System:** Dizziness (10–18%), paresthesia (3–12%).

### Serious and Other Adverse Effects
- **Neurologic:** Seizures (post-marketing reports) [US].
- **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea/vomiting; less frequent reactions include body odor and gastritis [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Anaphylaxis, bronchospasm, and laryngeal edema [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have been observed when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after any dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin anticoagulation [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not explicitly lettered, but indicates that reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at doses up to 3.8 times the human dose revealed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine milk concentrations. The risks of excess intake to the infant should be weighed against the benefits of supplementation to the mother [US].
- **Fertility:** Mutagenicity tests (Salmonella, S. cerevisiae, S. pombe) were negative. No long-term animal studies for carcinogenic potential have been performed [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Refer to Dosage and Administration for metabolic disorder dosing, which is weight-based (50 mg/kg) [US].
- **Geriatric Use:** Not specifically addressed in the source document [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Indicated for carnitine deficiency in ESRD patients on dialysis. Note the specific dosing and the caution regarding oral metabolite accumulation (TMA/TMAO) in this population [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring carrier molecule required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Following IV administration, approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours. The mean apparent terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In ESRD patients, IV administration increases plasma carnitine concentrations. Trough levels typically stabilise or increase after one week of therapy (3 doses) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients (20 mg/kg 3x/week post-dialysis), mean pre-dialysis carnitine levels rose from ~20 µmol/L to 190 ± 55 µmol/L at 8 weeks.
- **Dose-Ranging Data:** A study comparing 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg 3x/week in ESRD patients showed dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine concentrations at 12 and 24 weeks. While carnitine levels increased, the effect on clinical outcomes in the ESRD population has not been determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
- **Precautions:** Avoid excessive heat. Protect from freezing [US].
- **Shelf Life:** Refer to the expiry date on the packaging. Use within 24 hours if diluted [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Rx-only (Prescription only) [US].
