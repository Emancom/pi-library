# Editorial Product Information: Andexanet alfa

## Editorial Note
*   **Contributing Jurisdictions:** US (FDA). Note: While the source document was presented as Australian, the content provided is the official US Prescribing Information for **ANDEXXA**.
*   **Purpose:** This document is a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising regulatory information into a clinically relevant format.
*   **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredient:** Andexanet alfa (coagulation factor Xa (recombinant), inactivated-zhzo).
*   **Chemical Class:** Recombinant modified human factor Xa (FXa) protein.
*   **Brand Name(s):** **ANDEXXA** [US].
*   **ATC Code:** V03AB38.
*   **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Lyophilized powder for solution for intravenous injection. Available in single-dose vials containing 200 mg of andexanet alfa.
*   **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) use only.
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** AstraZeneca Pharmaceuticals LP [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Reversal of FXa Inhibitor Anticoagulation:** Indicated for patients treated with **rivaroxaban** or **apixaban**, when reversal of anticoagulation is needed due to life-threatening or uncontrolled bleeding [US-approved].
*   **Limitations of Use:** **ANDEXXA** has not been shown to be effective for, and is not indicated for, the treatment of bleeding related to any FXa inhibitors other than apixaban or rivaroxaban [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
The dosage is based on the specific FXa inhibitor, the dose of the inhibitor, and the time since the patient's last dose. There are two regimens: **Low Dose** and **High Dose**.

**Table 1: Dosing Regimen Definitions**
| Dose | Initial IV Bolus | Follow-On IV Infusion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Low Dose** | 400 mg (30 mg/min) | 4 mg/min for 120 minutes (480 mg) |
| **High Dose** | 800 mg (30 mg/min) | 8 mg/min for 120 minutes (960 mg) |

**Table 2: Selection of Regimen based on FXa Inhibitor**
| FXa Inhibitor | Last Dose | < 8 Hours since last dose or Unknown | ≥ 8 Hours since last dose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Rivaroxaban** | ≤ 10 mg | Low Dose | Low Dose |
| | > 10 mg or Unknown | High Dose | Low Dose |
| **Apixaban** | ≤ 5 mg | Low Dose | Low Dose |
| | > 5 mg or Unknown | High Dose | Low Dose |

### Administration Instructions
*   **Preparation:** Reconstitute each 200 mg vial with 20 mL Sterile Water for Injection (SWFI) to achieve a 10 mg/mL concentration. Gently swirl; do not shake.
*   **Filtration:** Must be administered through a 0.2 or 0.22 micron in-line polyethersulfone (or equivalent) low protein-binding filter.
*   **Timing:** Start the continuous infusion within two minutes following the completion of the bolus dose.
*   **Compatibility:** Transfer reconstituted solution to polyolefin or polyvinyl chloride (PVC) bags (250 mL or less).

### Missed Dose Instructions
Not applicable due to acute, single-use administration in emergency settings.

## 4. Contraindications
*   None listed in available sources [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warning [US]
*   **Thromboembolic and Ischemic Risks:** Treatment has been associated with serious and life-threatening arterial and venous thromboembolic events, ischemic events (myocardial infarction, stroke), cardiac arrest, and sudden deaths. 
*   **Monitoring:** Monitor for symptoms of thromboembolism and signs preceding cardiac arrest. 
*   **Anticoagulation:** Resume anticoagulant therapy as soon as medically appropriate to reduce the risk of the patient's underlying thrombotic disease.

### General Warnings and Precautions
*   **Unresponsiveness to Unfractionated Heparin (UFH):** **ANDEXXA** interferes with the action of UFH. Do not use **ANDEXXA** prior to heparinization, as it may cause unresponsiveness to heparin. If anticoagulation is needed, use an alternative to heparin [US].
*   **Re-elevation of Anti-FXa Activity:** Anti-FXa activity may return to placebo levels approximately two hours after the end of infusion. A rapid decrease occurs during the bolus, followed by a sustained decrease during infusion [US].

### Monitoring Requirements
*   Monitor for signs of thrombosis (e.g., DVT, PE, MI, Stroke).
*   Assess for infusion-related reactions (observed in 0.5% of patients) including rigors, chills, and hypertension.

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common Adverse Reactions (≥ 5%)
*   Urinary tract infections.
*   Pneumonia.

### Serious Adverse Effects
*   **Thromboembolic Events:** Occurred in 10.7% of patients in clinical trials (median time to event: 10 days). 
*   **Deaths:** In the ANNEXA-4 study, 18% of patients died within 30 days, primarily due to cardiovascular causes related to the initial bleeding event [US].
*   **Infusion-Related Reactions:** Reported in 0.5% of patients; typically transient and mild to moderate in severity.

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **Unfractionated Heparin (UFH):** Pharmacodynamic interaction leading to UFH unresponsiveness. Characterized by a lack of prolongation in activated clotting time (ACT) [US].
*   **Anti-FXa Activity Assays:** **ANDEXXA** interferes with commercial anti-FXa activity tests, leading to erroneously elevated levels and underestimation of reversal activity. These assays are unsuitable for monitoring treatment [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy:** No adequate or well-controlled studies in pregnant women. Animal reproductive studies have not been conducted. The background risk of major birth defects and miscarriage in the US population remains the clinical benchmark [US].
*   **Breastfeeding:** Presence in human milk and effects on the breastfed child are unknown. Consider the developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding alongside the mother’s clinical need for the drug.
*   **Fertility:** Data not available; no animal studies performed [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy have not been established [US].
*   **Elderly Use:** No overall differences in safety or efficacy were observed between patients ≥ 65 years (91% of trial population) and younger patients, though greater sensitivity in some older individuals cannot be ruled out [US].
*   **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources; however, **ANDEXXA** is not expected to be cleared by these routes given its protein structure.

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
**ANDEXXA** is a recombinant form of human FXa protein that has been modified to be catalytically inactive (serine substituted with alanine) and unable to assemble into the prothrombinase complex (Gla domain removed). It acts as a decoy by binding and sequestering FXa inhibitors (apixaban, rivaroxaban), thereby restoring thrombin generation. It also binds and inhibits Tissue Factor Pathway Inhibitor (TFPI).

### Pharmacokinetics
*   **Distribution:** Volume of distribution ($V_{ss}$) is approximately 3.0 L (High Dose) to 4.4 L (Low Dose).
*   **Elimination:** Clearance is approximately 3.1 to 4.4 L/hr.
*   **Half-life:** Terminal half-life ($t_{1/2}$) is approximately 2.7 to 3.3 hours.
*   **Metabolism:** Not affected by apixaban or rivaroxaban.

### Pharmacodynamics
Rapid decrease in anti-FXa activity (within 2 minutes post-bolus) maintained during infusion. TF-initiated thrombin generation is elevated for at least 22 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   **ANNEXA-A & ANNEXA-R:** Prospective, randomized, placebo-controlled studies in healthy volunteers. Demonstrated significant reduction in anti-FXa activity (p < 0.0001) for apixaban (-92.3%) and rivaroxaban (-96.7%).
*   **ANNEXA-4:** Multinational, prospective, open-label study in patients with acute major bleeding. Confirmed median anti-FXa activity reduction of 93-94%. Hemostatic efficacy was adjudicated by an independent committee.

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   **Prior to Reconstitution:** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F). Do not freeze.
*   **After Reconstitution:** Stable in vials at room temperature for up to 8 hours or refrigerated for up to 24 hours.
*   **In IV Bags:** Stable at room temperature for up to 8 hours.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
*   **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
*   **Approval Status:** Approved under accelerated approval based on anti-FXa activity changes; continued approval may be contingent upon verification of clinical benefit in post-marketing studies [US].
