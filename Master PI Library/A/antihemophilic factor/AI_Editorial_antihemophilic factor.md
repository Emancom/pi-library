# Editorial Product Information: Adynovate (Antihemophilic Factor [Recombinant], PEGylated)

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial: US (FDA).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical and regulatory data.
- **Disclaimer:** This synthesis is an editorial summary and does not replace the official PI required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Antihemophilic Factor (Recombinant), PEGylated.
- **Chemical Class:** Recombinant full-length human coagulation factor VIII (2,332 amino acids, ~280 kDa) covalently conjugated to polyethylene glycol (20 kDa) [US].
- **Brand Name(s):** **ADYNOVATE** [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Lyophilized powder for reconstitution in single-dose vials: 250, 500, 750, 1000, 1500, 2000, and 3000 International Units (IU) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous injection.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Takeda Pharmaceuticals America, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Hemophilia A (Congenital Factor VIII Deficiency):**
    - On-demand treatment and control of bleeding episodes [US-approved].
    - Perioperative management (surgical prophylaxis) [US-approved].
    - Routine prophylaxis to reduce the frequency of bleeding episodes [US-approved].
- **Limitations of Use:** Not indicated for the treatment of von Willebrand disease [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
Dosing is based on the empirical finding that 1 IU of **ADYNOVATE** per kg body weight raises the plasma factor VIII (FVIII) level by 2 IU/dL.

### Dosing by Indication
- **On-Demand and Perioperative Management:**
    - **Dose Calculation:** $Dose (IU) = Body Weight (kg) \times Desired FVIII Rise (IU/dL) \times 0.5$.
    - **Minor Bleeds/Surgery:** Target FVIII levels 20–40% (bleeds) or 60–100% (surgery).
    - **Moderate Bleeds:** Target FVIII levels 30–60%.
    - **Major Bleeds/Surgery:** Target FVIII levels 60–100% (bleeds) or 80–120% (surgery).
- **Routine Prophylaxis:**
    - **Adults and Adolescents (≥12 years):** 40–50 IU/kg twice weekly [US].
    - **Pediatrics (<12 years):** 55 IU/kg twice weekly, up to a maximum of 70 IU/kg [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Administer via intravenous bolus injection over ≤5 minutes (maximum rate 10 mL/min) [US].
- Reconstitute using the provided Sterile Water for Injection (sWFI) and the **BAXJECT** system.
- Use the reconstituted solution within 3 hours; do not refrigerate after reconstitution [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- Prior anaphylactic reactions to **ADYNOVATE**, the parent molecule (**ADVATE**), mouse or hamster proteins, or any excipients (e.g., Tris, mannitol, trehalose, glutathione, polysorbate 80) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Allergic-type reactions, including anaphylaxis, are possible. Symptoms may include angioedema, chest tightness, wheezing, and urticaria. Discontinue immediately if symptoms occur [US].
- **Neutralizing Antibodies (Inhibitors):** The formation of FVIII inhibitors may occur. Patients should be monitored via clinical observation and Bethesda assays if expected plasma FVIII levels are not reached or if bleeding is not controlled [US].
- **Monitoring:** Perform validated one-stage clotting assays to confirm FVIII levels. Monitor for inhibitors regularly [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common Adverse Effects (≥1%)
The following reactions were reported in the pooled safety database (N=478) [US]:
- **Respiratory:** Cough (10%)
- **Nervous System:** Headache (9%), Dizziness (1%)
- **Gastrointestinal:** Diarrhea (8%), Vomiting (5%), Nausea (2%)
- **Skin:** Rash (3%), Urticaria (2%)

### Less Common and Serious Effects
- **Uncommon (<1%):** Ocular hyperaemia, flushing, infusion-related reactions, and increased eosinophil count [US].
- **Postmarketing Experience:** Anaphylactic reactions have been reported [US].
- **Pancreatitis:** Two cases of acute pancreatitis were reported in adult extension studies [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** No data are available regarding use in pregnant women. Animal reproduction studies have not been conducted. Use only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if **ADYNOVATE** is excreted in human milk. Caution should be exercised, considering the mother’s clinical need and potential effects on the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** Long-term animal studies to evaluate effects on fertility have not been performed [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy are established for all pediatric age groups. Patients <12 years exhibit higher clearance and a shorter half-life, requiring higher or more frequent dosing (55 IU/kg twice weekly) compared to adults [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical trials did not include a sufficient number of patients aged 65 and over to determine if they respond differently from younger subjects [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** A PEGylated recombinant FVIII that temporarily replaces the missing coagulation factor. PEGylation extends the half-life by reducing binding to the LRP1 clearance receptor [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Normalizes the activated partial thromboplastin time (aPTT) and increases plasma FVIII activity levels [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Terminal Half-life:** ~14.7 hours in adults; ~11.8 to 12.4 hours in children <12 years [US].
    - **Clearance:** 2.27 mL/(kg·h) in adults; 3.11–3.53 mL/(kg·h) in children <12 years [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Study 1 (Adults/Adolescents):** A multicenter trial compared twice-weekly prophylaxis (40–50 IU/kg) to on-demand treatment. Prophylaxis resulted in a significant reduction in the Annualized Bleed Rate (ABR), with a median ABR of 1.9 compared to 41.5 in the on-demand group [US].
- **Study 2 (Pediatrics):** Pediatric patients (<12 years) on twice-weekly prophylaxis (40–60 IU/kg) achieved a median overall ABR of 2.0. Hemostatic efficacy was rated "excellent" or "good" in 90% of bleeding episodes [US].
- **Study 4 (Perioperative):** Evaluated in 21 patients undergoing 26 surgeries. Hemostatic efficacy was rated as "excellent" for all 24 procedures with available assessments [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store in the original box at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) [US].
- **Room Temperature Excursion:** May be stored at room temperature (up to 30°C/86°F) for a single period of up to 3 months. Do not return to the refrigerator after room temperature storage [US].
- **Handling:** Do not freeze. Protect from extreme exposure to light [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Prescription Only Medicine (Rx-only) [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources.
