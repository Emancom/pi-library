# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine Injection

## Editorial Note
*   **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA)
*   **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of Levocarnitine injection for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises clinical data, dosing protocols, and safety information.
*   **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. At the time of writing, only the United States (FDA) source documentation was provided for review.

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt. 
*   **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for mitochondrial fatty acid transport.
*   **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter Healthcare Corporation) [US].
*   **ATC Code:** A16AA01
*   **Dosage Form and Strength:** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
*   **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Acute and chronic treatment of secondary carnitine deficiency resulting from congenital metabolic defects [US].
*   **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
*   **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg.
    *   **Administration:** Administered as a slow bolus injection (2–3 minutes) or by infusion.
    *   **Frequency:** In severe metabolic crisis, a loading dose may be given, followed by equivalent doses every 3 to 4 hours (never less than every 6 hours) [US].
    *   **Maximum Dose:** The highest recorded dose administered is 300 mg/kg [US].

*   **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:** 
    *   **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight) [US].
    *   **Administration:** Administered as a slow bolus (2–3 minutes) into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
    *   **Dose Adjustment:** Adjustments should be guided by pre-dialysis (trough) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Reductions (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on levels [US].

### Administration Instructions
*   Parenteral products must be inspected for particulate matter and discoloration before use.
*   **Compatibility:** Compatible with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations of 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL. Stable for up to 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags [US].

### Monitoring
*   Baseline plasma carnitine levels should be obtained prior to treatment.
*   Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L) is recommended [US].

## 4. Contraindications
*   None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
*   **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have occurred—primarily in patients with ESRD on dialysis. Reactions may occur within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and monitor the patient closely if re-administration is attempted [US].
*   **Seizure Risk:** Levocarnitine (oral or IV) has been associated with seizures in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of epilepsy, an increase in seizure frequency or severity has been reported [US].
*   **General Precaution (Oral vs. IV):** The safety of high-dose **oral** levocarnitine has not been established in renal impairment due to the potential accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and TMAO) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are often confounded by the underlying pathology of ESRD or metabolic disorders.

### Clinical Trial Experience (ESRD Patients)
Adverse events occurring at a frequency ≥5% in trials, regardless of causality:
*   **Common (>10%):** Injection site reaction (33–38%), chest pain, hypertension (20%), hypotension, headache (22%), nausea/vomiting (15–21%), pharyngitis (23%), fever, abdominal pain, diarrhea (16%), and dizziness (13%) [US].
*   **Other reported effects (5–10%):** Asthenia, back pain, infection, palpitation, tachycardia, hypercalcemia, and pruritus [US].

### Serious/Other Notable Effects
*   **Neurologic:** Seizures (see Warnings and Precautions).
*   **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea and vomiting; gastritis.
*   **General:** Body odor (infrequent) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
    *   **Management:** Monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after any dose adjustment in patients on warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy:** 
    *   **US Classification:** Not explicitly assigned a letter category in the current text, but reproductive studies in rats and rabbits (up to 3.8 times the human dose) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm [US].
    *   **Recommendation:** Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed, as animal studies are not always predictive of human response [US].
*   **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. While studies in dairy cows show increased milk concentrations of carnitine following exogenous administration, the risks to the infant must be weighed against the benefits of treatment to the mother [US].
*   **Fertility:** No impairment of fertility was observed in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy for metabolic disorders are established; dosing is consistent with adult weight-based protocols (50 mg/kg) [US].
*   **Elderly Use:** Not specifically detailed; however, dose selection should be cautious, reflecting the greater frequency of decreased hepatic, renal, or cardiac function [US].
*   **Renal Impairment:** Primarily indicated for carnitine deficiency in ESRD. Levocarnitine is removed by dialysis. High-dose oral formulations are generally avoided in severe renal impairment to prevent metabolite accumulation (TMA/TMAO) [US].
*   **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for energy metabolism. It facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into the mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent ATP production. It also assists in the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].

### Pharmacokinetics
*   **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
*   **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
*   **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an intravenous dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. The mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours, and the terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients, 20 mg/kg administered three times weekly post-dialysis increased mean pre-dialysis plasma levocarnitine from 20 µmol/L to 190 µmol/L after 8 weeks.
*   **Dose-Ranging Data:** Studies using 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses showed dose-dependent increases in trough carnitine levels. While carnitine concentrations increased significantly, clinical outcomes (impact on malaise or muscle weakness) were not determined in these specific pharmacokinetic trials [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
*   **Precautions:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat.
*   **Vial Handling:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial; the formulation contains no preservatives [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
*   **United States:** Rx-only [US].
*   **Other Jurisdictions:** Not specified in available sources.
