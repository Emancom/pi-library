## 3. Approved Indications
- The available product labeling lists **achillea millefolium** as an active
  ingredient and describes its labeled purpose as an astringent for topical use.
- Uses attributed to the overall topical products that include **achillea
  millefolium** (product-level labeling): temporary protection of minor cuts,
  scrapes, abrasions, bruises, blisters, and burns; prevention and relief of
  chapped or cracked skin; protection from the drying effects of wind and cold
  weather; temporary relief of minor skin irritation and itching associated with
  insect bites, poison ivy, sunburns, and rashes caused by soaps, detergents,
  cosmetics, or jewelry; temporary relief of minor pain associated with
  abrasions, bruises, blisters, cuts, minor burns, sunburns, and scrapes.
  - Note: The specific labeled purpose for **achillea millefolium** in the
    available materials is "astringent." The broader product indications above
    are the labeled uses of topical products that contain **achillea millefolium**
    together with other active ingredients.

## 4. Dosage and Administration
- General dosing (adult): The topical product labels containing **achillea
  millefolium** direct users to apply to the affected area as often as required.
- Dosing by indication: Not specified in available sources for **achillea
  millefolium** as a single-agent indication-specific dose.
- Administration instructions:
  - Intended for external use on the skin only.
  - Contact with the eyes should be avoided; should eye contact occur, the
    affected eye should be rinsed thoroughly with water.
  - Product literature includes a warning that these preparations must be kept
    away from children.
- Titration schedules: Not specified in available sources.
- Missed dose instructions: Not specified in available sources.

## 5. Contraindications
- Listed contraindications and warnings applicable to topical products
  containing **achillea millefolium**:
  - For external use only; not intended for internal use.
  - Should not be applied to deep or penetrating wounds.
  - Should not be applied to animal bite wounds.
  - Should not be applied to serious or severe burns.
  - Contact with the eyes should be avoided.
  - Must be kept out of reach of children.
- Any additional absolute contraindications specific to **achillea millefolium**
  alone: Not specified in available sources.

## 6. Warnings and Precautions

### Boxed warnings
- None reported in available sources.

### General warnings and precautions
- **For external use only.** Not intended for internal administration.
- Should not be applied to deep wounds, animal bites, or serious burns.
- Eye contact should be avoided; if contact with the eyes occurs, the eye
  should be rinsed with water.
- Users should discontinue use and seek medical advice if:
  - The condition deteriorates or fails to improve as expected.
  - Symptoms persist beyond seven days of use.
  - Symptoms resolve and then recur within a few days.
- Must be maintained out of reach of children.

### Monitoring requirements
- Specific monitoring requirements for **achillea millefolium**: Not specified
  in available sources.

### Specific clinical precautions
- Product labeling recommends stopping treatment and seeking medical
  assessment if there is worsening, prolonged, or recurring symptoms
  (see above).
- No additional organ-specific or systemic precautions are described for
  **achillea millefolium** in the available sources.
