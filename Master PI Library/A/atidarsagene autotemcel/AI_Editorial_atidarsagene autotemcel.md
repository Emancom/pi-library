# Editorial Product Information: Atidarsagene Autotemcel

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) provided by the following regulatory authority: **US (FDA)**. 
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical data and regulatory requirements into a single, structured reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for the official PI. Clinicians must consult the specific, current PI approved in their jurisdiction before prescribing or administering this therapy.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Atidarsagene autotemcel (autologous hematopoietic stem cell-based gene therapy).
- **Chemical Class:** Autologous CD34+ cells transduced with a lentiviral vector (LVV) encoding the human arylsulfatase A (*ARSA*) gene.
- **Brand Name(s):** **LENMELDY** [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Single-dose cell suspension for intravenous infusion. Composed of one to eight infusion bags containing 2 to 11.8 x 10⁶ cells/mL (1.8 to 11.8 x 10⁶ CD34+ cells/mL) suspended in a cryopreservation solution [US]. Each bag contains 10 to 20 mL.
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous infusion.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Orchard Therapeutics (Europe) Ltd [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Metachromatic Leukodystrophy (MLD):** Treatment of children with pre-symptomatic late infantile (PSLI), pre-symptomatic early juvenile (PSEJ), or early symptomatic early juvenile (ESEJ) MLD [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Autologous Use Only:** For one-time single-dose intravenous use only.
- **Dose Calculation:** Dosing is based on the number of CD34+ cells per kg of body weight at the time of infusion. The number of bags (1 to 8) depends on the individual patient's requirements.

### Dosing by MLD Subtype [US]
| MLD Subtype | Minimum Recommended Dose (CD34+ cells/kg) | Maximum Recommended Dose (CD34+ cells/kg) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **PSLI** | 4.2 x 10⁶ | 30 x 10⁶ |
| **PSEJ** | 9.0 x 10⁶ | 30 x 10⁶ |
| **ESEJ** | 6.6 x 10⁶ | 30 x 10⁶ |

### Preparation and Administration
- **Pre-treatment Requirements:** Patients must undergo hematopoietic stem cell (HSC) mobilization followed by apheresis to obtain cells for manufacturing.
- **Back-up Collection:** A minimum of 2.0 x 10⁶ unmanipulated CD34+ cells/kg must be collected and cryopreserved as a rescue source before myeloablative conditioning.
- **Conditioning:** Myeloablative conditioning (e.g., busulfan) must be completed before infusion. A minimum 24-hour washout period is required between conditioning and infusion.
- **Thawing:** Thaw at 37°C (98.6°F) in a controlled device. Once thawed, do not wash, spin, or resuspend.
- **Administration:** Infuse via a central venous catheter within 2 hours of thawing. Each bag should be infused within 30 minutes. If multiple bags are used, administer no more than one bag per hour.
- **Filters:** Do **NOT** use a leukodepleting filter.
- **Post-infusion:** Flush tubing with 0.9% sodium chloride. Irradiate any required blood products for the first 3 months post-infusion.

## 4. Contraindications
- None listed in available sources [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Thrombosis and Thromboembolic Events:** Risk of serious events, including cerebral infarction. Evaluate risk factors and consider anti-thrombotic prophylaxis prior to treatment [US].
- **Encephalitis:** Potential for serious neurological deterioration. Monitor for symptoms (weakness, hypotonia, cognitive/behavioral changes) post-treatment [US].
- **Serious Infection:** Severe (Grade 3) infections occur in approximately 39% of patients within the first year. Monitor for signs of sepsis and respiratory infections, especially following myeloablative conditioning [US].
- **Veno-occlusive Disease (VOD):** Reported in 8% of clinical trial subjects. Monitor liver function tests and weight during the first month post-infusion; consider VOD prophylaxis [US].
- **Delayed Platelet Engraftment:** Increased bleeding risk. Monitor counts until recovery; 10% of trial patients had engraftment after Day 60 [US].
- **Neutrophil Engraftment Failure:** Defined as failure to reach ANC ≥ 500 cells/µL by Day 60. If failure occurs, administer rescue back-up cells [US].
- **Insertional Oncogenesis:** Potential risk of LVV-mediated malignancy. Perform annual complete blood counts and lifelong monitoring (integration site analysis for at least 15 years) [US].
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Risks associated with the cryopreservative Dimethyl Sulfoxide (DMSO), including anaphylaxis.
- **Anti-retroviral Use:** May interfere with manufacturing. Stop anti-retrovirals 1 month before mobilization [US].

### Laboratory Abnormalities

- Elevated liver enzymes (23%)

### Serious Adverse Effects
- Cerebral infarction [US]
- Encephalitis [US]
- Veno-occlusive disease [US]
- Ovarian failure (reported in 50% of female trial subjects, likely related to conditioning) [US]

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Anti-retrovirals:** Must be discontinued at least one month prior to mobilization and for the duration of time needed for elimination, as they may interfere with the lentiviral vector and manufacturing process [US].
- **Vaccines:** Safety not studied. Avoid vaccination during the 6 weeks prior to conditioning and until hematological recovery [US].
- **Cytochrome P-450:** No formal studies; however, the product is not expected to interact with CYP450 enzymes or drug transporters [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** No clinical data available. **LENMELDY** must not be administered during pregnancy due to the risks of myeloablative conditioning. [US: Not categorized by letter; note risks to fetus from conditioning].
- **Breastfeeding:** No data on presence in milk. Discontinue breastfeeding during conditioning due to potential risks to the infant [US].
- **Pregnancy Testing:** Negative serum pregnancy tests are required prior to mobilization, conditioning, and infusion for females of childbearing potential.
- **Contraception:** Use effective methods from the start of mobilization through at least 6 months post-infusion.
- **Fertility:** Risk of infertility from conditioning agents. Advise on cryopreservation of semen or ova prior to treatment [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy established for PSLI, PSEJ, and ESEJ subtypes. Safety/efficacy not established in the late juvenile form [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources (indication is paediatric-specific).
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not studied in these populations. Assess patients to ensure suitability for HSC transplantation [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Transduces autologous CD34+ HSCs with a lentiviral vector carrying the *ARSA* gene. After engraftment, the progeny of these cells produce functional ARSA enzyme, which breaks down or prevents the accumulation of sulfatides that cause MLD [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In PSLI and PSEJ populations, median ARSA activity in peripheral blood mononuclear cells (PBMC) reached supranormal levels by 3 months post-treatment and was sustained during follow-up [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Conventional ADME studies are not applicable to cell-based gene therapies [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Study Design:** Integrated analysis of 39 children (20 PSLI, 7 PSEJ, 10 ESEJ, and 2 advanced disease) across two single-arm trials and an expanded access program.
- **Efficacy Outcomes:**
    - **PSLI:** 100% of treated patients survived and were free of severe motor impairment at age 5, compared to 0% in a natural history cohort.
    - **Cognitive Function:** 19 of 20 PSLI patients maintained performance standard scores >55 (threshold for severe impairment) at last assessment, significantly outperforming natural history data.
    - **ESEJ:** Favorable cognitive outcomes were noted in 4 of 10 patients despite motor decline, which is atypical for untreated MLD [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store in the vapor phase of liquid nitrogen at less than -130°C (-202°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Do not re-freeze, sample, alter, or irradiate the product.
- **Thawing:** Infuse within 2 hours of thawing; keep at room temperature until infusion.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only (Prescription Only) [US].
