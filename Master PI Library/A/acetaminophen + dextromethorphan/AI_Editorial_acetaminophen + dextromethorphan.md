## 3. Approved Indications
- Temporary relief of symptoms associated with the common cold or influenza, including:
  - Headache
  - Minor aches and pains
  - Cough due to minor throat and bronchial irritation
  - Minor sore throat pain
  - Temporary reduction of fever
- Pharmacologic purposes listed: Pain reliever / Fever reducer (acetaminophen); Cough suppressant (dextromethorphan)

## 4. Dosage and Administration
### General dosing (adult)
- Adults and children 12 years of age and over: one packet every 6 hours while symptoms persist.
- Do not take more than 3 packets in 24 hours unless directed by a doctor.

### Pediatric dosing
- Children under 12 years of age: do not use.

### Administration instructions
- Dissolve contents of one packet into 8 oz (approximately 240 mL) of hot water; sip while hot and consume the entire drink within 10–15 minutes.
- If using a microwave: add contents of one packet to 8 oz of cool water; heat in microwave per normal microwave use and sip briskly before and after heating; do not overheat.
- Do not exceed the recommended dosage.

### Missed dose instructions

## 5. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to acetaminophen or to any component of the product.
- Concomitant use of this product with any other drug containing acetaminophen (prescription or nonprescription) due to the risk of acetaminophen overdose.
- Use in children under 12 years of age (do not use).
- Other absolute contraindications: Not specified in available sources

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed/Prominent warnings
- **Liver warning:** Severe liver damage may occur if more than 4,000 mg of acetaminophen are taken in 24 hours, particularly when combined with other acetaminophen-containing products or with regular heavy alcohol consumption.

### General warnings and precautions
- **Allergy alert:** Acetaminophen may cause severe skin reactions. Symptoms may include skin reddening, blisters, and rash. If a skin reaction occurs, stop use and seek medical help immediately.
- Sore throat warning: If sore throat is severe, persists for more than 2 days, or is accompanied or followed by fever, headache, rash, nausea, or vomiting, consult a doctor.
- Do not exceed recommended dosage.
- Alcohol: Avoid consuming 3 or more alcoholic drinks every day while using this product (increased risk of liver damage).
- Stop use and ask a doctor if:
  - Pain or cough worsens or lasts for more than 7 days
  - Fever worsens or lasts more than 3 days
  - Redness or swelling is present
  - New symptoms occur
  - Cough returns or occurs with rash or persistent headache

### Conditions requiring medical advice before use
- Ask a doctor before use if the patient has:
  - Liver disease
  - A breathing problem such as emphysema or chronic bronchitis
  - Cough that occurs with excessive phlegm (mucus)
  - A chronic or long-lasting cough such as occurs with smoking, asthma, or emphysema
  - Is taking the blood-thinning drug warfarin
  - Is taking a prescription monoamine oxidase inhibitor (MAOI) — consult a doctor or pharmacist if currently taking an MAOI or within 2 weeks after stopping an MAOI

### Pregnancy and lactation precautions
- If pregnant or breastfeeding, ask a health professional before use.

### Monitoring requirements
- Monitor for signs of hepatotoxicity (e.g., jaundice, right upper quadrant abdominal pain) and severe skin reactions; specific monitoring parameters and intervals: Not specified in available sources
- In case of suspected overdose, obtain immediate medical attention or contact a Poison Control Center. Prompt medical attention is critical for adults as well as children.

## 8. Drug Interactions
- Concomitant use with other acetaminophen-containing products: risk of additive acetaminophen exposure and hepatotoxicity; avoid duplication of acetaminophen-containing products.
- Alcohol: Regular heavy alcohol use (e.g., 3 or more alcoholic drinks every day) increases risk of liver damage with acetaminophen.
- Monoamine oxidase inhibitors (MAOI): Patients currently taking a prescription MAOI or within 2 weeks after stopping an MAOI should consult a doctor or pharmacist before using this product. (Interaction noted; specific clinical management not specified in available sources.)
- Warfarin: Patients taking the blood-thinning drug warfarin should consult a doctor before use. (Interaction noted; specific management or monitoring instructions not specified in available sources.)
