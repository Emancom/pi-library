## 3. Approved Indications
- Intended use (as stated in the source):
  - This combination product is intended to support the clinical dietary management of
    patients with suboptimal nutritional status for whom advanced folate supplementation
    is indicated, as well as to provide nutritional support during physiologically stressful
    conditions in order to maintain good health.
- Dispensing status / regulatory note:
  - Described in the source as a prescription-dispensed folate-containing dietary supplement
    because elevated folate levels carry a risk of masking vitamin B12 deficiency (pernicious
    anemia); dispensing and monitoring under the care of a licensed medical practitioner is
    therefore required [US].
- Jurisdictional approvals (specific regional approvals or differences): Not further specified
  in available sources.

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed warnings
- None specified in available sources.

### General warnings and precautions
- **Do not use in patients with a known hypersensitivity to any of the ingredients.**
- **Not intended for use in pregnant or lactating patients.**
- The source advises caution in patients who have an underlying medical condition, are
  pregnant, lactating, or trying to conceive, are younger than 18 years of age, or are
  currently taking other medications.
- Use and dispensing under the supervision of a licensed medical practitioner is advised
  because elevated folate supplementation can mask vitamin B12 deficiency (pernicious
  anemia), necessitating appropriate safety monitoring [US].
- Keep out of the reach of children (stated in source product information).

### Monitoring requirements

  (pernicious anemia) [US].

### Specific clinical precautions
- Masking of vitamin B12 deficiency by folic acid at elevated folate doses is explicitly noted;
  this risk underlies the recommendation for practitioner supervision and safety monitoring [US].
