# Editorial Product Information: Alvimopan

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA).
- **Purpose:** This document is a harmonised editorial synthesis of the available Product Information for **alvimopan**. It is designed as a concise reference for healthcare professionals to aid in clinical decision-making.
- **Disclaimer:** This editorial synthesis is not a substitute for the official Product Information approved in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority's approved labelling for prescribing purposes.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Alvimopan (amide hydrolysis compound)
- **Chemical Class:** Peripherally acting mu-opioid receptor antagonist (PAMORA)
- **Brand Names:** Entereg [US]
- **ATC Code:** A06AH02
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** 12 mg oral capsules [US]
- **Route of Administration:** Oral
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Par Health USA, LLC [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Postoperative Gastrointestinal Recovery:** Indicated to accelerate the time to upper and lower gastrointestinal recovery following surgeries involving partial bowel resection with primary anastomosis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adults):** 12 mg administered orally [US].
- **Dosing Schedule:** 
    - **Preoperative:** One 12 mg dose administered between 30 minutes and 5 hours prior to the start of surgery [US].
    - **Postoperative:** 12 mg twice daily starting the day after surgery [US].
- **Duration of Treatment:** Continued until hospital discharge for a maximum of 7 days. Total dosing must not exceed 15 doses [US].
- **Administration Instructions:** Can be taken with or without food [US].
- **Setting:** Restricted to hospital use only [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Recent Opioid Use:** Contraindicated in patients who have received therapeutic doses of opioids for more than 7 consecutive days immediately prior to starting alvimopan [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning: Risk of Myocardial Infarction (MI) and Restricted Distribution [US]:**
    - A higher incidence of MI was observed in a 12-month study of alvimopan 0.5 mg twice daily in patients treated for chronic non-cancer pain. A causal relationship has not been established, and this risk was not observed in short-term surgical trials.
    - Due to this potential risk, alvimopan is available only through a restricted program called the **Alvimopan REMS Program**. 
    - Hospitals must be enrolled in the REMS to prescribe/dispense the drug. 
    - Use is strictly limited to short-term, inpatient use (max 15 doses).
- **Opioid Tolerance:** Patients recently exposed to opioids may be hypersensitive to the effects of mu-opioid antagonists. Monitor for gastrointestinal symptoms such as abdominal pain, nausea, vomiting, and diarrhoea [US].
- **Severe Hepatic Impairment:** Use is not recommended due to significantly increased plasma concentrations (up to 10-fold) [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Use is not recommended as this population has not been studied [US].
- **Complete Gastrointestinal Obstruction:** Use is not recommended in patients with complete bowel obstruction or those undergoing surgery for its correction [US].
- **Gastric/Pancreatic Anastomoses:** Use is not recommended due to a lack of clinical data in this population [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Common (1–10%):** 
    - Dyspepsia (1.5% vs 0.8% placebo) [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** 
    - Myocardial Infarction (associated with long-term use in non-approved indications) [US].
- **Note:** In short-term surgical trials (up to 7 days), the incidence of adverse events was generally similar to placebo [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Intravenous Morphine:** Coadministration does not significantly alter the pharmacokinetics of morphine or its metabolite (morphine-6-glucuronide); no dose adjustment for morphine is required [US].
- **Acid Blockers and Antibiotics:** Pharmacokinetics are not significantly affected by PPIs, H2-receptor antagonists, or antibiotics [US].
- **P-glycoprotein (p-gp) Inhibitors:** Alvimopan is a p-gp substrate. While mild inhibitors do not appear to affect levels, no studies have been conducted with strong p-gp inhibitors (e.g., verapamil, cyclosporine) [US].
- **Cytochrome P450 Enzymes:** Alvimopan is primarily metabolized via non-CYP pathways and is unlikely to interact with CYP inducers or inhibitors [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not specified [Note: US has transitioned from letter categories].
    - Data in pregnant women are limited and insufficient to inform risk. Animal studies (rats/rabbits) showed no fetal harm at doses significantly higher than the human oral dose [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** There are no data on the presence of alvimopan in human milk or its effects on the infant. It was detected in the milk of lactating rats. Benefits of breastfeeding should be weighed against the clinical need for the drug [US].
- **Fertility:** No adverse effects on fertility or reproductive performance were found in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established in patients under 18 years of age [US].
- **Elderly Use:** No dosage adjustment is required. While no overall differences in safety/efficacy were observed, greater sensitivity in some older individuals cannot be ruled out [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment is required for mild-to-severe impairment. Use is **not recommended** in End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD). Monitor for GI adverse effects in patients with severe impairment [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment is required for mild-to-moderate impairment (Child-Pugh A and B), but close monitoring for GI adverse effects is necessary. Use is **not recommended** in severe hepatic impairment [US].
- **Race/Ethnicity:** No dose adjustment is required for Japanese patients, despite an observed 2-fold increase in plasma concentrations compared to Caucasian subjects; close monitoring for adverse reactions is advised [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Alvimopan is a selective, competitive antagonist of the human mu-opioid receptor. It binds to peripheral mu-receptors in the GI tract, antagonising the inhibitory effects of opioids on GI motility and secretion without reversing central analgesia [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Reduces the delay in bowel transit induced by codeine but does not affect gastric emptying. At 24 mg twice daily, no clinically relevant QTc prolongation was observed [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Absolute bioavailability is approximately 6%. Peak plasma concentration (Cmax) occurs at ~2 hours [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Metabolised by intestinal microflora into an active "metabolite." It is not a CYP substrate [US].
    - **Distribution:** Steady-state volume of distribution is ~30 L. Protein binding is 80–94% [US].
    - **Elimination:** Primarily via biliary secretion. Renal excretion accounts for ~35% of total clearance. Mean terminal half-life is 10–17 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Efficacy Summary:** Six multicenter, randomized, double-blind trials (5 in the US, 1 non-US) evaluated alvimopan in patients undergoing bowel resection. 
- **Primary Outcomes:** Alvimopan significantly accelerated the time to recovery of GI function (composite endpoint of toleration of solid food and first bowel movement) compared to placebo across all studies.
- **Hospital Discharge:** Alvimopan reduced the median time to discharge order by 6 to 22 hours compared to placebo in various studies [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage:** Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
- **Packaging:** Supplied in unit-dose blisters of 30 capsules [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only; restricted via REMS program [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources.
