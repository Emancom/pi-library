## 3. Approved Indications
- Indications reported in the provided product information:
  - Temporary relief of difficulty falling asleep
  - Helps relaxation and sleep (attributed to multiple ingredients)
  - Emotional stress (attributed to Helleborus niger root and Ignatia amara / Strychnos ignatii seed)
  - Mood support (attributed to Pulsatilla vulgaris and Serotonin)
  - Restlessness (attributed to Valeriana officinalis)
  - Supports hormonal balance (attributed to Sus scrofa pineal gland)
- Jurisdictional approval status: Not specified in available sources whether indications are jurisdiction-specific approvals; indications are presented in the provided product information without regional differentiation.

## 4. Dosage and Administration
- General dosing (adult)
  - Adults and children 12 years and older: 20 drops in a little water, two times per day
- Paediatric dosing
  - Children between 6 years and under 12 years of age: 10 drops in a little water, two times per day
  - Children under 6 years: consult a physician
- Dosing by indication: Not specified in available sources beyond the general dosing above and the statement that product provides temporary relief of difficulty falling asleep.
- Administration instructions

  - Administer drops in a little water
