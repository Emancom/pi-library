# Editorial Product Information: Albuterol Sulfate

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of albuterol sulfate inhalation aerosol, synthesised for healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. At the time of this writing, only the United States (FDA) source documentation was provided for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Albuterol sulfate (USP). In accordance with World Health Organization (WHO) nomenclature, this agent is also known as **salbutamol sulfate**.
- **Chemical Class:** Relatively selective beta$_2$-adrenergic bronchodilator.
- **Brand Names:** Repackaged by Hawaii Repack, Inc. (Original manufacturer: Sandoz Inc.).
- **ATC Code:** R03AC02.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Pressurized metered-dose aerosol unit (MDI). Each actuation delivers 120 mcg albuterol sulfate from the valve and 108 mcg albuterol sulfate from the mouthpiece (equivalent to 90 mcg of albuterol base) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral inhalation only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Hawaii Repack, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Treatment or prevention of bronchospasm:** Indicated for adults and children (4 years of age and older) with reversible obstructive airway disease [US].
- **Exercise-Induced Bronchospasm (EIB):** Prevention of bronchospasm triggered by exercise in adults and children (4 years of age and older) [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Pediatrics $\ge$ 4 years)
- **Acute Episodes/Prevention of Symptoms:** Two inhalations repeated every 4 to 6 hours. 
- **Alternative Dosing:** In some patients, one inhalation every 4 hours may be sufficient [US].
- **Maximum Frequency:** More frequent administration or a higher number of inhalations than recommended is not advised.

### Exercise-Induced Bronchospasm (EIB)
- **Dosing:** Two inhalations administered 15 to 30 minutes before exercise [US].

### Administration Instructions
- **Priming:** The inhaler must be primed before the first use and if the unit has not been used for more than 2 weeks. Prime by releasing four "test sprays" into the air, away from the face.
- **Device Maintenance:** The plastic mouthpiece must be washed with warm water and air-dried at least once a week to prevent medication buildup and blockage.
- **Dose Indicator:** The canister includes a counter; the unit should be discarded when the indicator reaches zero, as the correct delivery of medication cannot be assured thereafter [US].

### Missed Dose
- Not specified in available sources; however, patients are cautioned not to increase the dose or frequency without medical consultation.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Contraindicated in patients with a history of hypersensitivity to albuterol or any other component of the formulation [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Paradoxical Bronchospasm:** Inhaled albuterol can produce life-threatening paradoxical bronchospasm. If this occurs, discontinue use immediately and initiate alternative therapy [US].
- **Deterioration of Asthma:** Increased use of albuterol may signal destabilization of asthma. Patients requiring more frequent dosing need urgent clinical re-evaluation and consideration of anti-inflammatory therapy (e.g., corticosteroids).
- **Cardiovascular Effects:** Like all sympathomimetic amines, albuterol can produce clinically significant cardiovascular effects, including increased pulse rate and blood pressure. Use with caution in patients with coronary insufficiency, cardiac arrhythmias, or hypertension [US].
- **Hypokalemia:** Beta-agonists may induce significant hypokalemia via intracellular shunting. This effect is usually transient.
- **Immediate Hypersensitivity:** Rare cases of urticaria, angioedema, rash, and oropharyngeal edema have been reported.
- **Co-morbid Conditions:** Use with caution in patients with convulsive disorders, hyperthyroidism, or diabetes mellitus.

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse event data is derived from a 12-week clinical trial (N=193) [US].

| Frequency | Adverse Event |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Upper respiratory tract infection (21%), Rhinitis (16%) |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Nausea (10%), Tremor (7%), Tachycardia (7%), Nervousness (7%), Bronchospasm (unspecified) (6%), Fever (6%), Inhalation site sensation (6%), Back pain (4%), Vomiting (7%), Urinary tract infection (3%) |
| **Uncommon (<3%)** | Dysphonia, xerostomia (dry mouth), chest pain, palpitations, dizziness, leg cramps, hyperkinesia, anxiety. |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Paradoxical bronchospasm, cardiac arrhythmias, and metabolic acidosis [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Beta-Blockers:** Beta-adrenergic receptor blocking agents antagonize the pulmonary effect of albuterol and may produce severe bronchospasm in asthmatic patients. Avoid use unless mandatory (e.g., post-MI).
- **Diuretics:** The ECG changes or hypokalemia associated with non-potassium-sparing diuretics (loop or thiazide) may be acutely worsened by beta-agonists.
- **Digoxin:** Albuterol may decrease serum digoxin levels (observed 16–22% decrease in studies). Monitor digoxin levels in patients receiving both medications [US].
- **MAOIs and TCAs:** Use with extreme caution in patients treated with Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors or Tricyclic Antidepressants (or within 2 weeks of discontinuation), as they may potentiate the cardiovascular effects of albuterol [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category C]. There are no adequate and well-controlled studies in pregnant women. Albuterol should be used during pregnancy only if the potential benefit justifies the potential risk to the fetus.
- **Teratogenicity:** Animal studies (mice/rabbits) have demonstrated cleft palate and cranioschisis at high doses. However, no consistent pattern of defects has been established in human marketing experience [US].
- **Labor and Delivery:** Use should be restricted to patients where benefits clearly outweigh the risk of beta-agonist interference with uterine contractility.
- **Breastfeeding:** It is not known whether albuterol is excreted in human milk. Due to potential tumorigenicity observed in animal studies, a decision should be made whether to discontinue nursing or the drug.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established in children under 4 years of age [US].
- **Geriatric Use:** Clinical studies did not include sufficient numbers of subjects aged 65 and over. Caution is advised in elderly patients with concomitant cardiovascular disease [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Albuterol stimulates beta$_2$-adrenergic receptors, leading to the activation of adenyl cyclase and an increase in intracellular cyclic AMP. This activates protein kinase A, which inhibits myosin phosphorylation and lowers intracellular calcium, resulting in smooth muscle relaxation throughout the airways (trachea to terminal bronchioles) [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Onset of a 15% increase in FEV$_1$ occurs within 6 minutes, with peak effect between 50 to 55 minutes. Duration of action is typically 3 to 6 hours [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Plasma levels after inhaled therapeutic doses are very low.
    - **Metabolism/Elimination:** Not detailed in the provided inhalation-specific source, though animal data suggests rapid elimination of the propellant (HFA-134a) with a half-life of 5 to 7 minutes in humans [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Adult Efficacy:** In a 12-week study of 565 asthma patients, two inhalations of albuterol HFA significantly improved pulmonary function compared to placebo and were clinically comparable to CFC-propelled albuterol [US].
- **Pediatric Efficacy:** A 4-week study in children (4–11 years) demonstrated outcomes comparable to CFC-propelled formulations [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Handling:** Store with the mouthpiece down. Protect from extreme heat and cold. Do not puncture or incinerate the canister.
- **Shelf Life:** Discard after 200 actuations [US].
