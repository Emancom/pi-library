# Originality Audit Report: abrocitinib

## Audit Summary
- **Originality Score:** 68/100
- **Verbatim Copied Passages Found:** 6
- **Overall Assessment:** Concern

## Verbatim Copied Passages

1.  **Indications (Adults):** 
    - **Editorial Text:** "indicated for the treatment of moderate-to-severe atopic dermatitis in adults who are candidates for systemic therapy"
    - **Source Match:** Document 1 (TGA) p. 2 / Document 2 (FDA) p. 1.
    - **Location:** Section 2, Approved Indications.

2.  **Indications (Pediatrics):**
    - **Editorial Text:** "whose disease is not adequately controlled with other systemic medicinal products (including biologics) or when use of those therapies is inadvisable"
    - **Source Match:** Document 2 (FDA) p. 1 & 5 (Uses "drug products" instead of "medicinal products").
    - **Location:** Section 2, Approved Indications.

3.  **Limitations of Use:**
    - **Editorial Text:** "Use in combination with other JAK inhibitors, biological immunomodulators, or other potent immunosuppressants is not recommended"
    - **Source Match:** Document 1 (TGA) p. 9 / Document 2 (FDA) p. 5.
    - **Location:** Section 2, Approved Indications.

4.  **Contraindications (Antiplatelet):**
    - **Editorial Text:** "antiplatelet therapies (except low-dose aspirin ≤81 mg daily) during the initial 3 months of treatment"
    - **Source Match:** Document 1 (TGA) p. 5 / Document 2 (FDA) p. 2 & 8.
    - **Location:** Section 3, Contraindications.

5.  **Laboratory Initiation Thresholds:**
    - **Editorial Text:** "Do not initiate if platelets <150,000/mm³, ALC <500/mm³, ANC <1,000/mm³, or Hemoglobin <8 g/dL."
    - **Source Match:** Document 1 (TGA) p. 3 / Document 2 (FDA) p. 6.
    - **Location:** Section 4, Laboratory Abnormalities.

6.  **Mechanism of Action:**
    - **Editorial Text:** "Abrocitinib reversibly inhibits JAK1 by blocking the adenosine triphosphate (ATP) binding site."
    - **Source Match:** Document 2 (FDA) p. 20.
    - **Location:** Section 10, Mechanism of Action.

## Close Paraphrases

1.  **Contraindications (Infections/TB):** The phrase "Active serious systemic infections, including tuberculosis (TB)" in Section 3 is a direct lift of the TGA contraindication (p. 5) with only minor structural changes.
2.  **Administration Instructions:** The instruction "Tablets must be swallowed whole and should not be crushed or split" in Section 5 is nearly identical to TGA p. 4 and FDA p. 8 ("Swallow CIBINQO tablets whole... Do not crush, split, or chew").
3.  **Metabolism:** Section 9 ("metabolism of abrocitinib is mediated by multiple CYP enzymes, CYP2C19 (~53%) and CYP2C9 (~30%)") closely follows the technical breakdown in TGA p. 27 and FDA p. 21.

## Originality Strengths
- **Editorial Synthesis:** The "Editorial Note" and the overarching structure are original and provide a clear framework for a comparative regulatory document.
- **Harmonization:** The editorial successfully identifies and labels specific differences (e.g., TGA’s strict contraindication of pregnancy vs. FDA’s precautionary guidance).
- **Adverse Effects:** Section 6 (Adverse Effects) is well-synthesized, summarizing complex tables from the source PIs into a readable narrative format using the author's own categories.
- **Pharmacokinetics/Mechanism:** While the opening sentence of Section 10 is a direct lift, the subsequent explanation of cytokine modulation (IL-31, IFN-gamma) is a good summary of very dense technical data.

## Differences from Source PIs
- **Merged Storage Data:** The editorial combines TGA's 30°C limit with FDA's "excursions permitted" range to create a single storage section.
- **Tapering Guidance:** The editorial highlights TGA-specific advice regarding dose tapering back to 100 mg upon disease control, which is less emphasized in the FDA "Highlights" section.
- **Consolidation:** The editorial removes extensive clinical trial data and statistical tables (e.g., EASI-75 scores), focusing only on the regulatory clinical takeaways.

## Recommendation
The editorial needs **Revision** before it can be considered a fully original synthesis.

**Required Changes:**
1.  **Reword Indications:** The indications are currently a verbatim copy. Change to: "CIBINQO is approved for adults requiring systemic treatment for moderate-to-severe atopic dermatitis. It is also authorized for patients 12 and older when prior systemic or biologic therapies have failed or are contraindicated."
2.  **Reword Mechanism of Action:** Modify the first sentence of Section 10. For example: "Abrocitinib functions as a selective JAK1 inhibitor by competing at the ATP binding site, thereby preventing phosphorylation."
3.  **Paraphrase Contraindications:** Instead of listing the antiplatelet therapy restriction verbatim, rephrase: "During the first 90 days of therapy, patients should avoid antiplatelet drugs, though low-dose aspirin (81 mg or less) is permitted."
4.  **Formatting Lab Thresholds:** While the numbers must remain the same for safety, the sentence structure "Do not initiate if..." should be varied to avoid matching the source tables exactly.
