## 6. Warnings and Precautions

### Boxed warnings
- None specified in available sources.

### General warnings and precautions
The following warnings are specified in the product labeling:

- **Intended for external topical use only** — not to be used internally.
- **Avoid application to broken or damaged skin** — the product should not be applied
  to skin that is broken or irritated prior to use.
- **Discontinue use if skin reactions develop** — users should stop using the product
  if a rash or irritation arises during use.
- **Medical consultation recommended for kidney disease** — individuals with kidney
  disease should seek advice from a healthcare provider before using this product.
- **Accidental ingestion requires prompt medical attention** — in the event of
  swallowing, users should seek medical help or contact a Poison Control Center
  immediately.
- **Store away from children** — the product must be kept out of the reach of children
  at all times.
- For topical underarm application only.

### Monitoring requirements

### Specific clinical precautions
- Caution is advised in individuals with kidney disease; consultation with a physician
  prior to use is recommended (no dosing adjustment details are provided in the
  available source).
