# Originality Audit Report: Acalabrutinib

## Audit Summary
- **Originality Score:** 82/100  
- **Verbatim Copied Passages Found:** 4
- **Overall Assessment:** Pass

The editorial document is a high-quality clinical synthesis that effectively harmonizes data from both the EMA and FDA sources. It successfully navigates the technical differences between product formulations (capsule vs. tablet) and provides clear attribution for agency-specific guidance. While certain technical safety instructions and mechanistic descriptions are near-verbatim, this is largely due to the requirement for medical precision in regulatory writing.

## Verbatim Copied Passages
1. **Passage:** "Withhold Calquence for 3 to 7 days pre- and post-surgery based on the type of procedure and bleeding risk."
   - **Source:** Document 2 (FDA), Page 8.
   - **Location:** Section 4, "Hemorrhage" bullet point.
   - **Match:** FDA text: "Consider the benefit-risk of withholding CALQUENCE for 3 to 7 days pre- and post-surgery depending upon the type of surgery and the risk of bleeding."

2. **Passage:** "forms a covalent bond with a cysteine residue... in the BTK active site, leading to irreversible inactivation."
   - **Source:** Document 1 (EMA), Page 20.
   - **Location:** Section 10, Mechanism of Action.
   - **Match:** EMA text: "...form a covalent bond with a cysteine residue in the BTK active site, leading to irreversible inactivation of BTK."

3. **Passage:** "If a dose is missed by more than 3 hours, it should be skipped, and the next dose should be taken at the regularly scheduled time. Do not double the dose."
   - **Source:** Document 2 (FDA), Page 3.
   - **Location:** Section 5, Missed Dose.
   - **Match:** FDA text: "If a dose of CALQUENCE is missed by more than 3 hours, it should be skipped and the next dose should be taken at its regularly scheduled time. Extra capsules of CALQUENCE should not be taken to make up for a missed dose."

4. **Passage:** "Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F), with excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C."
   - **Source:** Document 2 (FDA), Page 33.
   - **Location:** Section 11, Storage Conditions.
   - **Match:** FDA text: "Store at 20°C-25°C (68°F-77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C-30°C (59°F-86°F)."

## Close Paraphrases
- **Contraindications:** The phrase "Known hypersensitivity to the active substance or any of the product excipients" (Editorial Section 3) is a standard regulatory phrase found in Document 1 (EMA) p. 7.
- **Hepatotoxicity:** The requirement to evaluate bilirubin and transaminases (Section 4) follows the exact sequence and logic of Document 2 (FDA) p. 8.
- **Dose Modifications:** The criteria for Grade 3/4 toxicities (Section 5) mirror the specific logic tables found in Document 1 (EMA) p. 4 and Document 2 (FDA) p. 4.
- **Pharmacokinetics:** The terminal half-life figures for the parent drug and metabolite (Section 9) are extracted and phrased almost identically to the "Elimination" section in Document 1 (EMA) p. 35.

## Originality Strengths
- **Harmonization of Formulations:** The editorial correctly identifies and synthesizes the difference between the capsule (acalabrutinib base) and tablet (acalabrutinib maleate) versions, specifically regarding pH-dependent solubility—a detail that requires cross-referencing EMA's technical data (EMA p. 72).
- **Indications Summary:** The writer did not copy the lengthy indication paragraphs; instead, they consolidated the CLL/SLL and MCL requirements into a clean, hierarchical list.
- **Safety Data Merging:** Section 6 (Adverse Effects) successfully pools data from the "pooled safety population" of over 2,000 patients mentioned across both documents, rather than just copying a single table from one source.
- **Regulatory Comparison:** The document adds value by explicitly stating where one agency is more specific (e.g., FDA’s surgery window vs. EMA’s broader language).

## Differences from Source PIs
- **Synthesis of Dosage:** The editorial merges monotherapy and combination therapy durations into a concise summary, whereas the sources spread these across multiple sections and tables.
- **Interaction Nuance:** The interaction section (Section 7) is uniquely structured to contrast the capsule/tablet differences regarding gastric acid reducing agents, which is a merged clinical insight not found as a single block in either source.
- **Omission of Trial Details:** The editorial omits massive sections of clinical trial data (ELEVATE-TN, ASCEND, etc.) to focus exclusively on prescribing information.
- **Structure:** The MOA and PK sections are placed toward the end, reflecting a clinical "Product Information" flow rather than the formal "SmPC" or "Prescribing Information" structural templates.

## Recommendation
**PASS**

The editorial passes the audit. While certain safety-critical instructions (missed doses, surgery withholding, storage) remain close to the source text, this is necessary for regulatory compliance and patient safety. The document demonstrates high originality in how it synthesizes the competing technical information regarding capsules vs. tablets and harmonizes different agency recommendations into a single, cohesive clinical guide. No revisions are required.
