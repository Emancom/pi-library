# Editorial Product Information: Acetylcholine Chloride

**Editorial Note:** This unified Product Information (PI) document has been synthesized from official regulatory filings from the Australian Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA) and the United States Food and Drug Administration (FDA). It is important to note that these sources describe significantly different preparations: a sterile powder for intraocular irrigation used in surgical settings [TGA] and a homeopathic oral liquid preparation [FDA]. The following information combines these distinct clinical profiles into a single reference for healthcare professionals.

---

## 1. Product Name and Active Ingredient
**Product Name:** Acetylcholine chloride
**Active Ingredient:** Acetylcholine chloride

The medication is available as:
*   **Powder for Intraocular Irrigation:** 20 mg of acetylcholine chloride per vial, which, when reconstituted with 2 mL of diluent, provides a 10 mg/mL (1%) solution [TGA].
*   **Oral Liquid (Homeopathic):** 6X dilution in a solution of demineralized water and 25% ethanol [FDA].

## 2. Approved Indications
**Intraocular Administration:**
Acetylcholine chloride is indicated for the induction of rapid and complete miosis of the iris during ophthalmic procedures, including:
*   Cataract surgery (following the placement of an intraocular lens).
*   Iridectomy.
*   Penetrating keratoplasty.
*   Other anterior segment surgeries where immediate pupillary constriction is required [TGA].

**Oral Administration (Homeopathic Use):**
The homeopathic preparation is marketed for the temporary relief of symptoms including sneezing, rhinorrhea, chest congestion, indigestion, headache, and general fatigue. These claims are based on traditional homeopathic practice and have not been evaluated by the FDA for safety or clinical efficacy [FDA].

## 3. Contraindications
Acetylcholine chloride is contraindicated in individuals with a known hypersensitivity to the active ingredient or any of the excipients (e.g., mannitol in the ophthalmic powder) [TGA, FDA].

## 4. Warnings and Precautions
**Surgical Use (Ophthalmic):**
*   **Sterility:** Ensure the blister pack is intact before use. Reconstitution must occur under aseptic conditions. The product must not be re-sterilized or gas-sterilized [TGA].
*   **Stability:** Aqueous solutions of the drug are highly unstable. The solution must be prepared immediately before use, and any unused portion must be discarded [TGA].
*   **Surgical Timing:** In cataract procedures, the drug should only be applied after the intraocular lens is securely in place [TGA].

**Homeopathic Use (Oral):**
*   **Evaluation:** This product has not been evaluated by the FDA for efficacy; there is no accepted medical evidence to support its use for the relief of respiratory or digestive symptoms [FDA].
*   **Tamper Evidence:** Do not use if the seal is damaged or missing [FDA].

**General Precautions:**
*   **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness in children have not been established. For homeopathic use, a healthcare professional should be consulted for children under 12 years of age [TGA, FDA].
*   **Elderly Use:** Specific data for the geriatric population are not available [TGA].

## 5. Dosage and Administration
**Intraocular Irrigation (Surgical):**
*   **Dose:** Generally, 0.5 mL to 2 mL is sufficient to achieve miosis.
*   **Administration:** Instill the reconstituted solution gently into the anterior chamber using a sterile irrigation cannula. The application should be parallel to the iris and tangential to the pupillary border.
*   **Preparation:** Reconstitute with the provided diluent. If storage is required, the reconstituted solution should be held at 2–8°C for no more than 6 hours [TGA].

**Oral Administration (Homeopathic):**
*   **Dose:** 10 drops orally, 3 times per day, or as directed by a healthcare professional [FDA].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions are primarily associated with systemic absorption during intraocular use.

**Ocular Effects:**
*   **Uncommon (≥1/1,000 to <1/100):** Corneal clouding, corneal oedema, and corneal decompensation [TGA].

**Systemic Effects (Rare: ≥1/10,000 to <1/1,000):**
*   **Cardiovascular:** Bradycardia and hypotension.
*   **Respiratory:** Dyspnoea/breathing difficulties.
*   **Other:** Flushing, sweating [TGA].

## 7. Drug Interactions
There are no known drug interactions reported for acetylcholine chloride in the provided regulatory documentation [TGA].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
**Pregnancy:**
*   **Ophthalmic (Category B2):** Animal reproduction studies have not been performed. It is unknown if intraocular administration causes fetal harm. Use only if the clinical necessity clearly outweighs potential risks [TGA].
*   **Homeopathic:** Consult a healthcare professional before use [FDA].

**Breastfeeding:**
It is unknown whether acetylcholine chloride is excreted in human milk. Exercise caution and professional judgment when administering to nursing women [TGA, FDA].

## 9. Pharmacokinetics
**Ophthalmic:** Pharmacokinetic data for topical application are not applicable as the drug is intended for local action within the eye. Acetylcholine is rapidly hydrolyzed into acetic acid and choline by the enzyme acetylcholinesterase, preventing significant systemic accumulation under normal conditions [TGA].

## 10. Mechanism of Action
**Pharmacodynamics:**
Acetylcholine is a naturally occurring neurohormone and the primary neurotransmitter of the parasympathetic nervous system. It facilitates nerve impulse transmission at cholinergic sites.

**Ocular Action:**
When applied directly to the iris, it triggers rapid miosis by stimulating the contraction of the sphincter pupillae muscle. Because it is rapidly destroyed by cholinesterase, the duration of action is short [TGA].

**Homeopathic Action:**
The mechanism of the 6X homeopathic preparation is based on traditional "like cures like" principles and is not established by standard pharmacological models [FDA].

## 11. Storage Conditions
*   **Ophthalmic Preparation:** Store below 25°C. Do not freeze [TGA].
*   **Homeopathic Preparation:** Store in a cool, dry place [FDA].
*   **General:** Keep out of reach of children [FDA].
