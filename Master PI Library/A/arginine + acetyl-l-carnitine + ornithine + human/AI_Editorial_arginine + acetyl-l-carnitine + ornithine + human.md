## 4. Dosage and Administration
- General dosing (adult):
  - The recommended adult dose is either 5 drops taken four times daily or 3 drops taken
    eight times daily, administered sublingually (held under the tongue).
- Dosing by indication:
  - No separate, indication-specific dosing regimens are provided; the above adult regimen
    applies to both stated uses (detoxification, appetite control).
- Administration instructions:
  - Drops should be held under the tongue after each administration.
  - To maximise absorption, no food or fluids should be consumed for 15 minutes following
    each dose.
