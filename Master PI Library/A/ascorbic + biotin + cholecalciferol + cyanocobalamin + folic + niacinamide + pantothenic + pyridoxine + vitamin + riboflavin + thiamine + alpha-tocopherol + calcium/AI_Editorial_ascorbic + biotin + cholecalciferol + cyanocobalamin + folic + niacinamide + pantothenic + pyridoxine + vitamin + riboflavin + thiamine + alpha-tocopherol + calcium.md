## 3. Approved Indications
- Indication (as stated in available source): Oral nutritional supplementation intended to deliver significant amounts of vitamins B6, B12, C, D3, E, riboflavin, niacinamide, thiamine mononitrate, and folate, to supplement the diet and help prevent the development of nutritional deficiencies of those specific vitamins.
- Dispensing remark: The product is classified as a dietary supplement dispensed by prescription and intended for use only under the supervision of a licensed medical practitioner (as stated in available source).

## 4. Dosage and Administration
### General dosing (adult)
- Recommended labelled serving size: 2 tablets daily.
- Stated dosing instruction: Two tablets administered once or twice daily, or at the direction of a licensed medical practitioner.

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
- Boxed warnings: None specified in available sources.
- General warnings and precautions:
  - **Hypersensitivity:** The product is contraindicated in any patient with a known hypersensitivity to any of its ingredients (as stated in available source).
  - The product should be used only under the direction and supervision of a qualified licensed medical practitioner (as stated in available source).
  - Caution is warranted in patients with pre-existing medical conditions, those who are pregnant, breastfeeding, attempting to conceive, younger than 18 years of age, or concurrently taking other medications (as stated in available source).
  - Keep out of reach of children (as stated in available source).
  - Hypersensitivity reactions, including allergic sensitization, have been reported in association with oral folic acid administration; patients experiencing adverse reactions should be advised to seek prompt medical attention (as stated in available source).
- Monitoring requirements: Specific laboratory or clinical monitoring recommendations are not specified in available sources.
- Specific clinical precautions: Individualised assessment by a licensed medical practitioner is recommended prior to use in pregnancy, lactation, paediatric patients, or patients taking concomitant medications (as stated in available source).
