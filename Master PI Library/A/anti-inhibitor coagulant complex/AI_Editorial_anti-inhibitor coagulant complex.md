# Editorial Product Information: Anti-Inhibitor Coagulant Complex (FEIBA)

## Editorial Note
*   **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA).
*   **Purpose:** This document is a harmonised editorial synthesis of the official Product Information for **FEIBA**. It is intended for use by healthcare professionals as a concise clinical reference.
*   **Disclaimer:** This editorial is a synthesis of available regulatory documents and is not a substitute for the official, full Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredient:** Anti-inhibitor coagulant complex (human).
*   **Brand Name:** **FEIBA** [US].
*   **Chemical Class:** Human plasma fraction with Factor VIII inhibitor bypassing activity.
*   **ATC Code:** B02BD03.
*   **Dosage Form and Strength:** Lyophilized powder for solution in single-dose glass vials, nominally containing 500, 1000, or 2500 units [US].
*   **Route of Administration:** Intravenous injection or infusion.
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Takeda Pharmaceuticals America, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
**FEIBA** is indicated for use in patients with Hemophilia A and B who have developed inhibitors for:
*   Control and prevention of bleeding episodes [US].
*   Perioperative management [US].
*   Routine prophylaxis to reduce or prevent the frequency of bleeding episodes [US].

**Limitations of Use:** **FEIBA** is not indicated for the treatment of bleeding episodes caused by coagulation factor deficiencies in the absence of inhibitors to Factor VIII or Factor IX [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
*   **Maximum Dose:** Do not exceed a single dose of 100 units/kg or a daily dose of 200 units/kg [US].
*   **Maximum Rate:** 10 units/kg per minute [US].
*   **Calculation:** Dosing is based on units of Factor VIII inhibitor bypassing activity.

### Dosing by Indication [US]
| Type of Bleeding | Dose (units/kg) | Frequency | Duration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Joint Hemorrhage** | 50 – 100 | Every 12 hours | Until pain and disability improve |
| **Mucous Membrane Bleeding** | 50 – 100 | Every 6 hours | At least 1 day or until resolved |
| **Soft Tissue (e.g., retroperitoneal)** | 100 | Every 12 hours | Until resolution |
| **Other Severe (e.g., CNS)** | 100 | Every 6 – 12 hours | Until resolution |
| **Perioperative (Pre-op)** | 50 – 100 | Single dose | Immediately prior to surgery |
| **Perioperative (Post-op)** | 50 – 100 | Every 6 – 12 hours | Until healing is achieved |
| **Routine Prophylaxis** | 85 | Every other day | Long-term |

### Administration Instructions
*   **Reconstitution:** Use only the provided Sterile Water for Injection and the BAXJECT II Hi-Flow device. Warm to room temperature before reconstitution. Gently swirl; do not shake [US].
*   **Timing:** Administer immediately and complete within 3 hours of reconstitution [US].
*   **Equipment:** Use plastic Luer lock syringes (protein may adhere to glass) [US].
*   **Co-administration:** Flush venous access with isotonic saline before and after. Do not mix with other medicinal products [US].

## 4. Contraindications
*   Known anaphylactic or severe hypersensitivity to the product or its components (including factors of the kinin-generating system) [US].
*   Disseminated intravascular coagulation (DIC) [US].
*   Acute thrombosis or embolism, including myocardial infarction [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warning: Embolic and Thrombotic Events [US]
*   Thromboembolic events (including VTE, pulmonary embolism, myocardial infarction, and stroke) have been reported, particularly following high doses (>200 units/kg/day) or in patients with existing thrombotic risk factors.
*   Monitor patients closely for clinical signs of thrombosis or DIC.

### General Warnings
*   **Emicizumab Interaction:** Cases of thrombotic microangiopathy (TMA) have been reported when **FEIBA** was used for breakthrough bleeding in patients receiving emicizumab. Monitor closely if co-administration is necessary [US].
*   **Hypersensitivity:** Severe anaphylactoid reactions can occur. Discontinue immediately if symptoms (urticaria, angioedema, hypotension) appear [US].
*   **Infectious Agents:** As a plasma-derived product, a risk of transmitting infectious agents (viruses, vCJD) exists, despite inactivation steps (nanofiltration and vapor heat treatment) [US].
*   **Isohemagglutinins:** Contains blood group isohemagglutinins (anti-A, anti-B). Passive transmission may interfere with serological tests (e.g., Coombs test) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common Adverse Effects (>5%) [US]
*   Anemia
*   Diarrhea
*   Hemarthrosis
*   Hepatitis B surface antibody positive
*   Nausea and Vomiting
*   Headache

### Serious Adverse Effects
*   **Thromboembolic Events:** Stroke, pulmonary embolism, deep vein thrombosis, and myocardial infarction [US].
*   **Hematologic:** Disseminated intravascular coagulation (DIC), anamnestic response (rise in inhibitor titers) [US].
*   **Systemic:** Anaphylaxis and severe infusion-related reactions (chills, pyrexia, hypotension) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **Antifibrinolytics:** Use of systemic antifibrinolytics (e.g., tranexamic acid, aminocaproic acid) is not recommended within 6 to 12 hours of **FEIBA** administration due to increased thrombotic risk [US].
*   **Recombinant Factor VIIa:** Concomitant use may increase thrombotic risk due to circulating tissue factor; potential benefits must be weighed against risks [US].
*   **Emicizumab:** Potential for serious thrombotic microangiopathy (TMA) [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy:** There are no adequate data on use in pregnant women. Animal reproduction studies have not been conducted. It is unknown if the drug causes fetal harm [US].
*   **Breastfeeding:** Presence in human milk and effects on the breastfed child are unknown. Consider the clinical need of the mother and potential adverse effects on the infant [US].
*   **Fertility:** Genotoxicity and fertility studies have not been performed [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Pediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy have been evaluated in children ≥7 years in prophylaxis trials. Dosing is weight-based. Safety/efficacy in neonates has not been evaluated [US].
*   **Elderly Use:** Clinical trials did not include sufficient numbers of subjects ≥65 years to determine if they respond differently than younger subjects [US].
*   **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
*   **Mechanism of Action:** The exact mechanism is under discussion. The complex contains non-activated factors II, IX, X and activated factor VII. These components interact with plasma coagulation factors and platelets to increase thrombin generation in the absence of Factor VIII/IX, thereby bypassing the inhibitor [US].
*   **Pharmacodynamics:** Shortens the activated partial thromboplastin time (aPTT) and increases thrombin generation [US].
*   **Pharmacokinetics:** Composed of multiple factors with varying half-lives. Formal pharmacokinetic studies have not been conducted in humans [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   **Bleeding Control:** In a multicenter randomized trial, **FEIBA** was effective/partly effective in 66% of bleeding episodes compared to 46% for non-activated prothrombin complex concentrate [US].
*   **Prophylaxis:** A prospective trial showed a 72% reduction in median Annual Bleed Rate (ABR) for subjects on prophylaxis (85 U/kg every other day) compared to on-demand treatment (median ABR 7.9 vs 28.7) [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   **Temperature:** Store at 2°C to 25°C (36°F to 77°F) [US].
*   **Protection:** Store in original package to protect from light [US].
*   **Restrictions:** Do not freeze. Do not refrigerate after reconstitution [US].
