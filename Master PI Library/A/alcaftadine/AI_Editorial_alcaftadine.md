# Editorial Product Information: Alcaftadine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdiction:
    - **United States (FDA)**: LASTACAFT (alcaftadine ophthalmic solution) 0.25%.
- **Purpose**: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising available regulatory data into a single clinical reference.
- **Disclaimer**: This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific PI. Clinicians should consult the local approved labelling for prescribing purposes in their specific region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient**: Alcaftadine; an $H_1$ histamine receptor antagonist.
- **Brand Names**: LASTACAFT [US].
- **ATC Code**: S01GX11.
- **Dosage Form and Strength**: Ophthalmic solution 0.25% (2.5 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route of Administration**: Topical ophthalmic.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor**: Allergan, Inc. (an AbbVie company) [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Allergic Conjunctivitis**: Indicated for the prevention of itching associated with allergic conjunctivitis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adults and Children $\ge$ 2 years)**: Instill one drop into each affected eye once daily [US].
- **Administration Instructions**:
    - For topical ophthalmic use only.
    - If using multiple topical ophthalmic medicinal products, administer each medication at least five minutes apart [US].
- **Missed Dose**: Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity**: Known hypersensitivity to alcaftadine or any component of the formulation [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Potential for Eye Injury and Contamination**: Patients should be instructed to avoid touching the dropper tip to the eyelids, surrounding areas, or any other surface to prevent contamination of the solution and potential eye injury. The bottle should be kept tightly closed when not in use [US].
- **Contact Lens Wear**: 
    - **LASTACAFT** should not be used to treat irritation related to contact lens wear.
    - Patients should not wear contact lenses if their eye is red.
    - The formulation contains benzalkonium chloride, which may be absorbed by soft contact lenses. 
    - Lenses must be removed prior to instillation and may be reinserted 10 minutes following administration [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Clinical Trial Experience
The following adverse effects were reported in clinical trials (less than 4% of treated eyes):
- **Ocular**: Eye irritation, burning and/or stinging upon instillation, eye redness (hyperemia), and eye pruritus [US].
- **Non-Ocular**: Nasopharyngitis and headache (occurring in less than 3% of subjects) [US].

### Postmarketing Experience
The following reactions have been identified during post-approval use. Because these are reported voluntarily from a population of uncertain size, frequency cannot be reliably estimated:
- **Ocular**: Eye discharge, eye swelling, erythema of the eyelid, eyelid edema, increased lacrimation, and blurred vision [US].
- **Systemic**: Hypersensitivity reactions (including facial swelling), allergic dermatitis, and somnolence [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions**: In vitro studies indicate that neither alcaftadine nor its primary carboxylic acid metabolite substantially inhibit reactions catalyzed by major CYP450 enzymes [US].
- **Pharmacodynamic Interactions**: Not specified in available sources.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy**:
    - **Risk Summary**: There are no adequate and well-controlled studies in pregnant women. Animal data in rats and rabbits using oral doses significantly higher than the maximum recommended human ophthalmic dose (MRHOD) showed no maternal or embryofetal toxicity during organogenesis. However, lower pup weights were observed in rats when administered during late pregnancy and lactation at high doses [US].
    - **Recommendation**: Should be used during pregnancy only if the potential benefit justifies the potential risk to the fetus [US].
- **Breastfeeding**: It is unknown if alcaftadine is excreted in human milk. The developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding should be considered alongside the mother’s clinical need and any potential adverse effects on the infant [US].
- **Fertility**: Oral doses up to 20 mg/kg/day in rats (230 times the MRHOD) showed no effect on male or female fertility [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use**: Safety and efficacy have not been established in paediatric patients under the age of 2 years [US].
- **Elderly Use**: No overall differences in safety or effectiveness have been observed between elderly and younger subjects [US].
- **Renal Impairment**: Not specified in available sources.
- **Hepatic Impairment**: Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action**: Alcaftadine is an $H_1$ histamine receptor antagonist and an inhibitor of histamine release from mast cells. It also demonstrates decreased chemotaxis and inhibition of eosinophil activation [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics**:
    - **Absorption**: Following topical ocular administration, the mean $C_{max}$ was approximately 60 pg/mL (at 15 minutes). The active carboxylic acid metabolite reached a $C_{max}$ of 3 ng/mL at 1 hour. Systemic accumulation does not occur with daily dosing [US].
    - **Distribution**: Protein binding is 39.2% for alcaftadine and 62.7% for the active metabolite [US].
    - **Metabolism**: Mediated by non-CYP450 cytosolic enzymes to an active carboxylic acid metabolite [US].
    - **Elimination**: The elimination half-life of the metabolite is approximately 2 hours. Elimination is primarily via the urine [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Efficacy**: Clinical efficacy was established using conjunctival allergen challenge (CAC) studies. Alcaftadine was significantly more effective than its vehicle in preventing ocular itching at 3 minutes and 16 hours post-dosing [US].
- **Safety**: Safety was evaluated in a randomized study of 909 subjects over a 6-week period [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature**: Store between 15°C to 25°C (59°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Shelf Life**: Once opened, the product can be used until the expiration date marked on the bottle [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States**: Classified as Rx-only (Prescription drug) [US].
