# Editorial Product Information: Arformoterol

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, consolidating clinical and regulatory data into a structured format.
- **Note:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. At the time of publication, only United States source data was available for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Arformoterol tartrate (the (R,R)-enantiomer of formoterol).
- **Chemical Class:** Selective long-acting beta$_2$-adrenergic agonist (LABA).
- **Brand Names:** Not specified in provided source [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** Inhalation solution for nebulization; 15 mcg of arformoterol (equivalent to 22 mcg of arformoterol tartrate) in 2 mL unit-dose vials [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral inhalation via nebulization.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Fosun Pharma USA Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD):** Long-term, twice-daily maintenance treatment of bronchoconstriction in patients with COPD, including chronic bronchitis and emphysema [US].

**Clinical Limitations of Use:**
- Not indicated for the treatment of acute deteriorations of COPD [US].
- Not indicated for the treatment of asthma; safety and efficacy in asthma patients have not been established [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Adult Dose:** 15 mcg administered twice daily (morning and evening) [US].
- **Maximum Dose:** The total daily dose should not exceed 30 mcg (15 mcg twice daily) [US].
- **Administration Instructions:** 
    - Administer exclusively via oral inhalation using a standard jet nebulizer connected to an air compressor. 
    - Should be used with a face mask or mouthpiece. 
    - The solution must not be swallowed or injected [US].
    - Requires no dilution prior to use.
- **Missed Dose:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Maintenance:** If the standard regimen fails to provide the expected response, it often indicates COPD destabilisation, requiring immediate medical re-evaluation [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known history of hypersensitivity to arformoterol, racemic formoterol, or any components of the formulation [US].
- **Asthma Monotherapy:** Use of a LABA without a concomitant inhaled corticosteroid (ICS) is contraindicated in patients with asthma [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Serious Asthma-Related Events [US Boxed Warning equivalent]:** Use of LABAs as monotherapy for asthma increases the risk of asthma-related death. Data also suggest an increased risk of asthma-related hospitalisations in paediatric and adolescent populations. Arformoterol is not indicated for asthma [US].
- **Acute Deterioration:** Do not initiate therapy in patients with acutely deteriorating or life-threatening COPD. It is not a rescue medication for acute bronchospasm [US].
- **Excessive Use:** Fatalities have been reported with the excessive use of inhaled sympathomimetic drugs. Do not use in conjunction with other LABAs [US].
- **Paradoxical Bronchospasm:** Like other inhaled agents, arformoterol can produce life-threatening paradoxical bronchospasm. If this occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and institute alternative therapy [US].
- **Cardiovascular Effects:** May produce clinically significant increases in pulse rate and blood pressure, or ECG changes (T-wave flattening, QTc prolongation, ST-segment depression). Use with caution in patients with cardiovascular disorders, especially coronary insufficiency, cardiac arrhythmias, and hypertension [US].
- **Coexisting Conditions:** Use with caution in patients with convulsive disorders, thyrotoxicosis, or those highly sensitive to sympathomimetic amines [US].
- **Metabolic Effects:** Beta-agonists may induce significant hypokalemia (via intracellular shunting) and transient hyperglycemia. Use with caution in patients with diabetes mellitus [US].

**Other Noted Effects (<2%):**
- **Cardiovascular:** Atrial flutter, AV block, myocardial infarction, QTc prolongation, supraventricular tachycardia [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Anaphylaxis, urticaria, angioedema, and rash [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Adrenergic Drugs:** Concomitant use of other adrenergics (by any route) may potentiate the sympathetic effects of arformoterol; use with caution [US].
- **Xanthine Derivatives and Steroids:** May potentiate hypokalemia induced by beta-agonists. Caution is advised [US].
- **Diuretics:** Non-potassium-sparing diuretics (loop or thiazide) may have their hypokalemic and ECG-lowering effects acutely worsened by beta-agonists [US].
- **MAOIs, TCAs, and QTc Prolonging Drugs:** Use with extreme caution as these agents may potentiate cardiovascular effects. Drugs known to prolong the QTc interval increase the risk of ventricular arrhythmias [US].
- **Beta-Blockers:** May inhibit the therapeutic effect of arformoterol and potentially induce severe bronchospasm in COPD patients. If necessary, cardioselective beta-blockers are preferred but must be used with caution [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Classification:** Not specified by letter category in the current text, but use is advised only if benefits outweigh potential fetal risks [US]. 
    - **Data:** Animal studies (rats/rabbits) showed teratogenicity (omphalocele, malpositioned kidneys) and embryofetal toxicity at exposures significantly higher (370x to 8,400x) than the maximum recommended human daily inhalation dose (MRHDID). 
    - **Labour and Delivery:** Beta-agonists can interfere with uterine contractility; use during labour should be restricted [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** No data exist on arformoterol in human milk. It is excreted in the milk of lactating rats. Use should consider the developmental/health benefits of breastfeeding alongside the mother's clinical need [US].
- **Fertility:** No impairment of fertility was observed in rats at oral doses up to 3,200 times the MRHDID [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established. COPD does not typically occur in children [US].
- **Elderly Use:** No overall differences in safety or effectiveness were observed between geriatric (≥65 years) and younger subjects, though greater sensitivity in some older individuals cannot be ruled out [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment is required [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Systemic exposure (AUC and C$_{max}$) increased 1.3 to 2.4-fold in patients with hepatic impairment. Use with caution and monitor closely due to prolonged clearance [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Arformoterol is a selective long-acting beta$_2$-adrenergic agonist. It stimulates intracellular adenyl cyclase, increasing cyclic AMP levels, which leads to relaxation of bronchial smooth muscle and inhibition of mediator release from mast cells [US].
- **Potency:** Arformoterol is the (R,R)-enantiomer of formoterol and is approximately 1000-fold more potent than the (S,S)-enantiomer and twice as potent as racemic formoterol [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Peak plasma levels occur approximately 0.5 hours post-dose. Systemic exposure increases linearly with dose [US].
    - **Distribution:** Plasma protein binding is 52-65% [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily via direct conjugation (glucuronidation) and secondarily via O-demethylation (CYP2D6 and CYP2C19) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Primarily renal (67% in urine) and 22% in feces. Terminal half-life is approximately 26 hours in COPD patients [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **COPD Maintenance:** Two 12-week, double-blind trials (n=1,456) compared arformoterol 15 mcg BID to placebo and salmeterol. Arformoterol demonstrated a statistically significant ~11% improvement in mean FEV$_1$ over the dosing interval compared to placebo [US].
- **Onset of Action:** Median time to onset of bronchodilation (15% FEV$_1$ increase) was 6.7 minutes. Peak effect was typically seen within 1–3 hours [US].
- **Long-term Safety:** A 52-week trial (n=841) demonstrated that arformoterol was not associated with an increased risk of respiratory death or COPD exacerbation-related hospitalisations compared to placebo [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Refrigerated:** Store in the protective foil pouch between 2°C and 8°C (36°F-46°F). Protect from light and heat [US].
- **Room Temperature:** Unopened foil pouches may be stored at 20°C to 25°C (68°F-77°F) for up to 6 weeks (42 days). Discard if not used within this period or if past the expiration date [US].
- **Vial Use:** Once the foil pouch is opened, vials must be used within the specified timeframes. Opened individual vials should be used immediately. The solution should be colorless; discard if discolored [US].
