# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
*   This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) provided by the following regulatory jurisdiction:
    *   **United States**: United States Food and Drug Administration (FDA) – Source: Baxter Healthcare Corporation.
*   **Purpose**: This document provides a harmonized editorial overview for healthcare professionals.
*   **Disclaimer**: This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. The information below reflects the data available in the provided source document.

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredient**: Levocarnitine (INN); chemical class: carrier molecule/amino acid derivative.
*   **Chemical Name**: (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
*   **Brand Name(s)**: Levocarnitine Injection, USP [US].
*   **ATC Code**: A16AA01.
*   **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s)**: Sterile solution for injection, 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) [US].
*   **Route(s) of Administration**: Intravenous (IV) use only.
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor**: Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency (Inborn Errors)**: For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
*   **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD)**: For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Pediatric)
*   **Metabolic Disorders**: The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg.
    *   **Administration**: Administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion.
    *   **Loading Dose**: In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose may be given, followed by an equivalent dose divided over the next 24 hours.
    *   **Frequency**: Dosing should occur every 3 or 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours.
    *   **Maximum Dose**: Doses up to 300 mg/kg have been administered.
*   **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis**:
    *   **Starting Dose**: 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight.
    *   **Administration**: Administered as a slow 2 to 3 minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
    *   **Dose Adjustment**: Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be made as early as the third or fourth week of therapy, guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma carnitine concentrations.

### Monitoring
*   **Baseline**: Obtain plasma carnitine concentrations prior to starting parenteral therapy.
*   **Maintenance**: Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine level: 35 to 60 µmol/L) is recommended.

### Administration Instructions
*   Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration.
*   **Compatibility**: Stable for 24 hours in PVC bags at room temperature (25°C) when mixed in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations between 0.5 mg/mL and 8 mg/mL.

## 4. Contraindications
*   None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
*   **Boxed Warnings**: None.
*   **Hypersensitivity Reactions**: Serious reactions including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm have occurred, particularly in ESRD patients on dialysis. Reactions may occur within minutes of IV administration. If severe reactions occur, discontinue treatment immediately and evaluate risks before considering re-administration.
*   **Seizure Activity**: Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In patients with a known seizure disorder, an increase in seizure frequency or severity has been observed.
*   **Oral Levocarnitine Limitation**: The safety and efficacy of *oral* levocarnitine have not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in these patients may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide).

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common and Very Common Effects
In clinical trials of ESRD patients on hemodialysis, the following events occurred at a frequency ≥5% (regardless of causality):
*   **General**: Injection site reaction (33%), pain (30%), headache (22%), flu syndrome (25%), asthenia (9%), chest pain (12%).
*   **Cardiovascular**: Hypertension (20%), hypotension (14%), tachycardia (6%), palpitations (5%).
*   **Gastrointestinal**: Diarrhea (16%), vomiting (15%), nausea (8%), abdominal pain (9%).
*   **Respiratory**: Pharyngitis (23%), cough increase (9%), dyspnea (7%), rhinitis (9%).
*   **Neurological**: Dizziness (13%), paresthesia (5%).
*   **Metabolic**: Hypercalcemia (9%), hyperkalemia (6%), hypervolemia (5%).

### Serious and Postmarketing Effects
*   **Hypersensitivity**: Anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, bronchospasm.
*   **Neurological**: Seizures.
*   **Gastrointestinal**: Transient nausea/vomiting, gastritis, and body odor have been observed with chronic use.

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **Warfarin**: Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have been observed when co-administered with levocarnitine.
*   **Management**: Monitor INR levels in patients on warfarin therapy after initiating levocarnitine or following dose adjustments.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy**: [US Category: Not formally assigned, but text indicates "Category C" style logic]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (based on surface area) revealed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
*   **Breastfeeding**: Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk carnitine concentrations after exogenous administration. Weigh the risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant against the benefits of supplementation to the mother.
*   **Fertility**: No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use**: Dosing based on mg/kg is applicable; see Section 3.
*   **Elderly Use**: Not specifically detailed in provided source.
*   **Renal Impairment**: Main target population for ESRD indication; however, safety of the *oral* formulation is not established in this group due to metabolite accumulation (TMA/TMAO).
*   **Hepatic Impairment**: Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane for oxidation and energy production. In patients with metabolic defects, it promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters that are rapidly eliminated.

### Pharmacokinetics
*   **Distribution**: Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
*   **Metabolism**: Major metabolites are trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and [3H]-γ-butyrobetaine (feces).
*   **Elimination**: Following IV bolus (20 mg/kg), the mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours and the terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours. Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours.
*   **Bioavailability**: Absolute bioavailability of oral formulations is approximately 15–16% [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   **ESRD Studies**: A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients showed that 20 mg/kg doses three times weekly post-dialysis increased mean pre-dialysis plasma levocarnitine concentrations from ~20 µmol/L to 190 ± 55 µmol/L.
*   **Dose-Ranging Study**: Studies comparing 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses confirmed a dose-dependent increase in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks.
*   **Clinical Outcome Note**: While levocarnitine increases carnitine concentrations in ESRD, the effect on clinical outcomes (malaise, muscle weakness, etc.) has not been definitively determined.

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   **Temperature**: Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C.
*   **Conditions**: Protect from freezing and excessive heat.
*   **Handling**: Discard any unused portion of opened vials as the formulation contains no preservatives.
