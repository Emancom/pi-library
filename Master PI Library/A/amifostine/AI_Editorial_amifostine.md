# Editorial Product Information: Amifostine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of amifostine for healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific PI. Clinicians should refer to the local approved labelling for prescribing purposes in their specific region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Amifostine (an organic thiophosphate).
- **Brand Names:** Ethyol [US].
- **ATC Code:** V03AF05.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Sterile lyophilised white powder for injection, 500 mg (on an anhydrous basis) in a 10 mL single-dose vial [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous infusion.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Legacy Pharma USA Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Reduction of Cumulative Renal Toxicity:** Indicated to reduce cumulative renal toxicity associated with repeated cisplatin administration in patients with advanced ovarian cancer [US].
- **Reduction of Xerostomia:** Indicated to reduce the incidence of moderate to severe xerostomia in patients undergoing post-operative radiation for head and neck cancer, specifically where the radiation port involves a significant portion of the parotid glands [US].

**Limitations of Use [US]:**
- Should be avoided in clinical settings where chemotherapy is intended to produce a significant survival benefit or cure (e.g., germ cell malignancies).
- Should be avoided in patients receiving definitive radiotherapy, except within the context of a clinical trial, due to insufficient data regarding tumor-protective effects.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Instructions
- **Hydration:** Verify adequate hydration and correct dehydration prior to infusion [US].
- **Premedication:** 
    *   For the 910 mg/m² dose: Administer antiemetics (e.g., dexamethasone 20 mg IV plus a 5-HT3 receptor antagonist) prior to amifostine [US].
    *   For the 200 mg/m² dose: Administer antiemetics (e.g., oral 5-HT3 antagonists) prior to amifostine [US].
- **Positioning:** Patients must remain in a supine position during the infusion [US].

### Dosing by Indication
- **Reduction of Cumulative Renal Toxicity (Chemotherapy):**
    *   Recommended starting dose: 910 mg/m² [US].
    *   Administration: 15-minute IV infusion, starting 30 minutes prior to chemotherapy [US].
    *   **Crucial Note:** Do not exceed a 15-minute infusion time as longer durations are linked to higher adverse reaction rates [US].
- **Reduction of Xerostomia (Radiotherapy):**
    *   Recommended dose: 200 mg/m² [US].
    *   Administration: 3-minute IV infusion, starting 15–30 minutes prior to standard fraction radiation (1.8–2.0 Gy) [US].

### Dose Modifications
- **Infusion Interruption (Hypotension):** If systolic blood pressure (SBP) drops significantly (see Table 1 below), interrupt the infusion. If SBP returns to normal within 5 minutes and the patient is asymptomatic, the infusion may be restarted [US].
- **Dose Reduction:** For subsequent chemotherapy cycles, if the full dose of amifostine could not be administered previously, reduce the dose to 740 mg/m² [US].
- **Discontinuation:** Permanently discontinue if severe infusion-related or cutaneous reactions occur [US].

**Table 1: SBP Thresholds for Interruption [US]**
| Baseline SBP (mmHg) | <100 | 100–119 | 120–139 | 140–179 | ≥180 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Decrease during infusion (mmHg)** | 20 | 25 | 30 | 40 | 50 |

## 4. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to aminothiol compounds [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypotension and Cardiovascular Events:** Significant hypotension is common. Monitor blood pressure every 5 minutes during 910 mg/m² infusions and before/after 200 mg/m² infusions. Amifostine should be avoided in patients who are hypotensive or dehydrated [US]. 
- **Antihypertensive Management:** Interrupt antihypertensive therapy 24 hours prior to amifostine administration if possible. If therapy cannot be stopped, amifostine should not be administered [US].
- **Severe Cutaneous Reactions:** Fatal cases of Stevens-Johnson syndrome (SJS), toxic epidermal necrolysis (TEN), and DRESS have occurred. Monitor patients for lesions appearing outside the radiation port or on palms/soles [US].
- **Hypocalcemia:** Monitor serum calcium in at-risk patients (e.g., nephrotic syndrome). Supplement as needed [US].
- **Nausea and Vomiting:** Frequent and potentially severe; aggressive antiemetic prophylaxis is required [US].
- **Embryo-Fetal Toxicity:** Based on animal data, amifostine may cause fetal harm [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common Adverse Effects
- **Very Common (>10%):** Hypotension (62% at high dose; 15% at low dose), nausea, and vomiting (up to 96% at high dose) [US].
- **Common (1–10%):** Hypocalcemia (1% in head/neck cancer trials) [US].

### Infusion-Related and Other Reactions
- **Infusion-related:** Flushing, chills, pyrexia, dizziness, somnolence, hiccups, sneezing, and blurred vision [US].
- **Injection site:** Rash, urticaria, inflammation, and local swelling [US].

### Serious Adverse Effects (Post-Marketing)
- **Cardiovascular:** Myocardial ischemia/infarction, cardiac arrest, arrhythmias (atrial fibrillation/flutter, tachycardia, bradycardia) [US].
- **Neurological:** Seizures [US].
- **Respiratory:** Apnea, hypoxia, respiratory arrest [US].
- **Renal:** Renal failure [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Antihypertensive agents:** Amifostine may potentiate the hypotensive effects of these medications. Close monitoring is required [US].
- **General:** Compatibility with solutions other than 0.9% Sodium Chloride has not been established; other additives are not recommended [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not specified by letter, but risk of fetal harm is noted]. Advise patients of potential risk to the fetus. Verify pregnancy status in females of reproductive potential before initiation [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** There are no data on presence in human milk. Due to potential serious reactions, breastfeeding is not recommended during treatment [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies indicate potential impairment of male fertility (bilateral testicular degeneration) [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established [US].
- **Geriatric Use:** Clinical trials did not include enough subjects ≥65 years to determine if they respond differently. Use caution and start at the lower end of the dosing range, reflecting the higher frequency of decreased organ function [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Specific dose adjustments are not provided in source documents, but caution is advised in the elderly regarding renal/hepatic function [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Amifostine is a prodrug dephosphorylated by alkaline phosphatase (more active in normal tissues due to higher pH and vascularity) into an active thiol metabolite. This metabolite scavenges reactive oxygen species and detoxifies cisplatin metabolites, protecting normal tissue [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** 
    *   **Distribution:** Rapidly cleared from plasma; distribution half-life <1 minute [US].
    *   **Metabolism:** Rapidly converted to active thiol; a less active disulfide metabolite follows [US].
    *   **Elimination:** Elimination half-life is approximately 8 minutes. Less than 10% remains in plasma 6 minutes post-administration [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **WR-1 (Ovarian Cancer):** A randomized trial (n=242) showed that 910 mg/m² amifostine significantly reduced renal toxicity (30% vs 13% incidence of ≥40% CrCl reduction). No negative impact on the antitumor efficacy of cisplatin was observed [US].
- **WR-38 (Head and Neck Cancer):** A randomized trial (n=315) demonstrated that 200 mg/m² amifostine reduced the incidence of Grade 2 or higher acute xerostomia (51% vs 78%) and late xerostomia at one year (35% vs 57%) [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Lyophilised Powder:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C [US].
- **Reconstituted Solution:** Chemically stable for 5 hours at room temperature (~25°C) or 24 hours under refrigeration (2°C to 8°C) [US].
- **Diluted Solution (5–40 mg/mL):** Stable for 5 hours at room temperature or 24 hours under refrigeration [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources.
