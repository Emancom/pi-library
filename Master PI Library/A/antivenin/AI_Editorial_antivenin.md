# Editorial Product Information: Antivenin (Latrodectus mactans)

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial is based on the official Product Information provided by the **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical data into a structured format for quick reference. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. At the time of writing, only the United States (FDA) documentation was available for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Black Widow Spider (*Latrodectus mactans*) Immune Globulin Antivenin (Equine).
- **Brand Name(s):** Antivenin (Latrodectus mactans) [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Lyophilized powder for reconstitution. Each single-dose vial contains a minimum of 6000 Antivenin units [US]. 
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intramuscular (IM) or Intravenous (IV).
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Merck Sharp & Dohme LLC [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Black Widow Spider Envenomation:** Indicated for the treatment of patients exhibiting symptomatic bites from the black widow spider (*Latrodectus mactans*). Early administration is emphasised to achieve prompt symptomatic relief [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Adults and Children:** The standard dose is the entire contents of one reconstituted vial (2.5 mL).
- **Repeat Dosing:** While one dose is typically sufficient, a second dose may be required in certain clinical scenarios [US].

### Administration Instructions
- **Reconstitution:** Using a sterile syringe, inject 2.5 mL of Sterile Water for Injection into the vial. Shake the vial with the needle still in the stopper to ensure complete dissolution.
- **Intramuscular Route:** Administer into the anterolateral thigh. This site allows for the application of a tourniquet if a systemic reaction occurs.
- **Intravenous Route:** Preferred for severe cases, patients under 12 years of age, or those in shock. Dilute the reconstituted antivenin in 10 to 50 mL of saline solution and infuse over 15 minutes [US].

### Supportive Measures
- Hospitalization is recommended. 
- **Pain Management:** 10 mL of 10% calcium gluconate IV may be used to control muscle pain (repeated as necessary). Morphine or barbiturates may be used but require extreme caution due to the risk of respiratory paralysis from the neurotoxic venom [US].
- **Other:** Warm baths and adrenocorticosteroids have been used with varying success [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- Not specified in available sources. However, extreme caution is required in patients with known sensitivity to horse serum (see Section 5).

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Horse Serum Sensitivity
- **Testing Requirement:** A skin or conjunctival test must be performed prior to administration. History of prior horse serum exposure or allergies must be reviewed [US].
- **Skin Test:** Inject 0.02 mL of a 1:10 dilution of normal horse serum intradermally. A positive result is an urticarial wheal with erythema within 10 minutes [US].
- **Conjunctival Test:** Instill one drop of 1:10 dilution (adults) or 1:100 dilution (children) into the conjunctival sac. Itching and reddening indicate a positive reaction [US].
- **Anaphylaxis:** Severe reactions can occur even after a negative sensitivity test [US].

### Desensitisation
- Only attempted if the antivenin is deemed life-saving. Epinephrine must be immediately available. The process involves serial subcutaneous injections of 1:100 and 1:10 dilutions at 15–30 minute intervals [US].

### General Precautions
- **Asthma:** Patients with a history of asthma are at increased risk of anaphylactic reactions and death [US].
- **Serum Sickness:** Patients should be monitored for 8 to 12 days post-administration for signs of serum sickness [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
The following adverse effects have been reported [US]:
- **Hypersensitivity:** Anaphylaxis and serum sickness (Common, 8–12 days post-treatment).
- **Musculoskeletal:** Muscle cramps.
- **General:** Potential for death in highly sensitive patients.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Supportive Therapy Caution:** Use of morphine or barbiturates may potentiate the respiratory paralysis caused by the venom's neurotoxins [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Not specified (Pregnancy Category not assigned). Use only if clearly needed. It is unknown if the antivenin affects reproductive capacity or causes fetal harm [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Use with caution; it is unknown if the drug is excreted in human milk [US].
- **Fertility:** No long-term animal studies available to evaluate impairment of fertility [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** No controlled clinical studies available. However, the IV route is the preferred method of administration for children under 12 years of age [US].
- **Elderly Use (>60 years):** Antivenin is recommended as preferred initial therapy for patients over 60 years due to the increased risk of complications from envenomation in this age group [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** The antivenin contains specific venom-neutralizing globulins that bind to and neutralize the venom of *Latrodectus mactans*. The precise pharmacological mode of action is unknown [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** No human metabolic or pharmacokinetic data are available [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** The venom is a neurotoxin acting on myoneural junctions and nerve endings, leading to ascending motor paralysis or destruction of peripheral nerve endings [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- Pivotal clinical trial data was not detailed in the provided source document. Clinical use is based on established practice and biological assay standardization (neutralization of 6000 mouse LD50 per dose) [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store and ship at 2–8°C (36–46°F).
- **Freezing:** Do not freeze.
- **Stability:** Reconstituted color ranges from light straw to dark "iced tea"; color depth does not affect potency [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Preservative:** Contains Thimerosal (mercury derivative) 1:10,000 [US].
