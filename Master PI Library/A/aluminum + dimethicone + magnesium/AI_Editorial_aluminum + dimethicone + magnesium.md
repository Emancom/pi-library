# Editorial Product Information: **aluminum + dimethicone + magnesium**

## 1. Product Identification
- Active ingredient (INN/generic name): **aluminum + dimethicone + magnesium**
  - Aluminum (as colloidal aluminum phosphate) 12.38 g per 20 g sachet
  - Magnesium (as magnesium hydroxide) 400 mg per 20 g sachet
  - Dimethicone (simethicone) 40 mg per 20 g sachet
- Chemical class: Not specified in available sources
- ATC code: Not specified in available sources
- Route(s) of administration: Oral
- Note: For oral use only.

## 3. Approved Indications
- Antacid activity and symptomatic relief of:
  - Sour stomach, acid indigestion, upset stomach associated with these symptoms
  - Heartburn
  - Gastritis (including gastroduodenal gastritis) and related gastric hyperacidity
  - Dyspepsia
  - Nausea and vomiting associated with hyperacidity
  - Gastric pain
  - Gastric flatus (flatulence)
- Indications presented above are those listed in the available source(s). Jurisdictional differences in approved indications are not specified in the available sources.

## 4. Dosage and Administration
- General adult dosing:
  - One 20 g sachet orally three times daily.
  - Administer between meals (at mealtime and between meals as described in the source).
  - Allow approximately 4 hours between doses.
- Dosing by indication: Not specified in available sources beyond the general antacid/antigas dosing above.
