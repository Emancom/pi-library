# Originality Audit Report: ascorbic acid

## Audit Summary
- **Originality Score:** 78/100
- **Verbatim Copied Passages Found:** 4
- **Overall Assessment:** Pass

## Verbatim Copied Passages
1. **Source:** Doc 2 (FDA), Page 2, Warnings.
   - **Editorial Text:** "Accidental overdose of iron-containing products is a leading cause of fatal poisoning in children under six. Products containing iron must be kept out of reach of children."
   - **Source Text:** "WARNING: Accidental overdose of iron-containing products is a leading cause of fatal poisoning in children under 6. KEEP THIS PRODUCT OUT OF REACH OF CHILDREN."
   - **Location:** Section 4, Pediatric Safety (Oral).

2. **Source:** Doc 1 (TGA), Page 2, Section 4.1.
   - **Editorial Text:** "...to prevent or correct vitamin deficiencies when oral nutrition is contraindicated, insufficient, or impossible."
   - **Source Text:** "...to correct or prevent vitamin deficiencies when oral administration is contraindicated, impossible or insufficient."
   - **Location:** Section 2, Parenteral Administration.

3. **Source:** Doc 2 (FDA), Page 3, Storage.
   - **Editorial Text:** "...prevent surface discoloration of the tablets."
   - **Source Text:** "...may produce surface discoloration of the tablet."
   - **Location:** Section 11, Oral Storage.

4. **Source:** Doc 1 (TGA), Page 3, Section 4.2.
   - **Editorial Text:** "...stored at 2°C to 8°C for no more than 24 hours."
   - **Source Text:** "...stored at 2 °C to 8 °C for no more than 24 hours."
   - **Location:** Section 11, Parenteral Storage.

## Close Paraphrases
- **B12 Masking (Section 4):** The editorial states folic acid dosages above 0.1 mg may "improve hematological parameters... while allowing neurological damage to progress." This closely follows the FDA Precautions (p. 2) which states "hematologic remission can occur while neurological manifestations remain progressive."
- **Pregnancy Category D (Section 8):** The description of teratogenic effects at doses exceeding 10,000 IU/day and the classification as Category D is a very close paraphrase of TGA Section 4.6 (p. 9).
- **Laboratory Interference (Section 7):** The mention of "streptavidin-based immunoassays (e.g., thyroid or troponin tests)" is a direct lift of the examples provided in TGA Section 4.4 (p. 7), though slightly reworded.

## Originality Strengths
- **Structural Synthesis:** The editorial successfully creates a unified framework for two radically different delivery methods (IV Cernevit and Oral Altriva). This "Unified PI" structure is not present in the sources.
- **Dose Integration:** Section 1 (Product Name and Active Ingredient) identifies the variation in Ascorbic Acid content (120mg vs 125mg) and presents it as a range, which is an original synthesis of the two disparate data sheets.
- **Mechanism of Action (Section 10):** This section provides a concise summary of the physiological roles of the vitamins, which is rewritten in a more accessible editorial voice compared to the clinical metabolism descriptions in the TGA document.

## Differences from Source PIs
- **Harmonization:** The editorial explicitly distinguishes which warnings apply to which route (e.g., specifying Iron warnings for FDA/Oral and precipitates for TGA/IV).
- **Omissions:** The auditor notes that the editorial omitted the specific excipient list of Cernevit (Glycine, Glycocholic acid, etc.) to maintain a high-level summary.
- **Terminology:** The editorial uses the term "Refeeding Syndrome" to categorize a set of electrolyte shift warnings that are described but not explicitly named in the "Warnings" header of the TGA source (though named in the sub-header).

## Recommendation
**PASS.**

The editorial is an original synthesis. While it contains four short strings of near-verbatim text, these are largely regulatory "black box" warnings (Iron overdose) or technical storage specifications where deviation from the source language could lead to clinical inaccuracy. The writer has successfully integrated two distinct regulatory documents into a coherent, original editorial piece. No major revisions are required for originality.
