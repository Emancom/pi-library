# Editorial Product Information: Alprostadil

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following regulatory authority: **Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical and regulatory data into a single reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official PI used for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory documents for regional legal compliance.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Alprostadil (Prostaglandin E1; PGE1).
- **Chemical Class:** Naturally occurring acidic lipid; prostaglandin.
- **Brand Name(s):** Caverject Impulse [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** G04BE01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - **Powder for intracavernosal injection:** Provided in a dual-chamber syringe containing alprostadil (as a lyophilised powder) and diluent (bacteriostatic Water for Injections) [Australian]. 
    - **Strengths:** 10 micrograms and 20 micrograms [Australian].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intracavernosal injection.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Pfizer Australia Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Erectile Dysfunction:** Treatment of erectile dysfunction in adult males [Australian].
- **Diagnostic Adjunct:** Useful adjunct to other diagnostic tests in the diagnosis of erectile dysfunction [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
Treatment should be initiated and titrated under the supervision of a specialist practitioner trained in the management of erectile dysfunction. The goal is to produce an erection suitable for intercourse that does not exceed one hour in duration.
- **Frequency:** Maximum of once in a 24-hour period and no more than three times weekly.
- **Usual Response Range:** 10–20 micrograms.

### Dosing by Etiology [Australian]
- **Neurogenic or Psychogenic:** Initial dose of 2.5 micrograms; titrate upward in increments of 2.5 micrograms.
- **Arteriogenic or Other Organic Causes:** Initial dose of 5 micrograms; titrate upward in increments of 5 micrograms.
- **Diagnostic Adjunct:** Initial dose of 2.5 micrograms; titrate upward in increments of 2.5 micrograms.

### Administration Instructions
- **Reconstitution:** The dual-chamber syringe automatically mixes the powder and diluent when the plunger is turned. The solution must be clear and free of particles.
- **Injection Site:** Direct intracavernosal injection into the corpora cavernosa (at a 90-degree angle). Avoid visible veins, the midline, and the underside of the penis. 
- **Site Rotation:** Alternate injections between the left and right cavernosa and vary the site along the penile shaft.
- **Post-Injection:** Apply pressure to the injection site for approximately 5 minutes or until bleeding stops. Massage the penis to help spread the drug.

## 4. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to alprostadil or any excipients.
- Conditions predisposing to **priapism** (e.g., sickle cell anaemia or trait, multiple myeloma, leukaemia).
- Anatomical deformation of the penis (e.g., angulation, cavernosal fibrosis, Peyronie's disease).
- Pre-existing penile fibrosis.
- Men for whom sexual activity is inadvisable or contraindicated.
- Use in women, children, or newborns.
- Presence of penile implants.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Priapism/Prolonged Erection:** Known to occur following administration. Patients must report any erection lasting **4 hours or longer** to a physician immediately to prevent tissue hypoxia and necrosis.
- **Penile Fibrosis:** Includes Peyronie's disease, angulation, and fibrotic nodules. Regular physician examination of the penis is required. Treatment must cease if fibrotic complications develop.
- **Bleeding Risk:** Increased propensity for bleeding in patients on anticoagulants (e.g., warfarin, heparin).
- **STI Transmission:** Intracavernosal alprostadil does not protect against sexually transmitted diseases (including HIV). Use may cause minor bleeding at the injection site, potentially increasing transmission risk.
- **Needle Breakage:** Potential for superfine needles to break; patients must be trained in proper handling and instructed not to use bent needles.
- **Benzyl Alcohol Toxicity:** The diluent contains benzyl alcohol, associated with "gasping syndrome" in paediatric patients; use is contraindicated in children/newborns.

## 6. Adverse Effects
| Frequency | Adverse Event |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Penile pain (burning sensation or tension) |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Penile fibrosis (4.8%), prolonged erection 4–6 hours (4%), haematoma, ecchymosis |
| **Uncommon (0.1–1%)** | Priapism (0.4%), testicular pain/swelling, scrotal oedema, haematuria, injection site haemorrhage/inflammation/pruritus, hypotension, dizziness, headache, nausea, dry mouth |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Priapism (requires emergency intervention), penile fibrosis, and systemic vascular reactions (hypotension, vagal shock).

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **ED Therapies:** Alprostadil is not intended for co-administration with any other agent for the treatment of erectile dysfunction. 
- **Vasoactive Agents:** Safety and efficacy of combinations with other vasoactive drugs have not been systematically studied.
- **Systemic Medications:** Clinical trials indicated no effect on safety or efficacy when used with antihypertensives, diuretics, antidiabetic agents (including insulin), or NSAIDs.
- **Anticoagulants:** Increased risk of bleeding/haematoma at the injection site [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Not for use in women. Alprostadil is an **abortifacient** and stimulates uterine smooth muscle. Although PGE1 occurs naturally in semen, it is not significantly increased by intracavernosal injection; however, partners of pregnant women should consult an obstetrician regarding use [Australian].
- **Breastfeeding:** Not applicable (not for use in women).
- **Fertility:** In rat studies, subcutaneous doses up to 0.2 mg/kg/day did not adversely affect spermatogenesis.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Contraindicated. Not for use in children or newborns.
- **Elderly Use:** No specific data available in the provided source [Australian].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Specific dosing adjustments are not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Alprostadil (PGE1) induces erection by relaxing trabecular smooth muscle and dilating cavernosal arteries. This leads to the expansion of lacunar spaces and entrapment of blood via the corporal veno-occlusive mechanism.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Erection typically begins 5–20 minutes after injection; duration is dose-dependent.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Metabolism:** Rapidly transformed to inactive metabolites. Following IV administration, 70–90% is extracted in a single pass through the lungs (half-life <1 minute).
    - **Distribution:** After intracavernosal injection, levels of alprostadil and its primary metabolite (15-oxo-13,14-dihydro-PGE1) rise in the cavernosa.
    - **Excretion:** No intact alprostadil is detected in peripheral circulation following intracavernosal use.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Unreconstituted Product:** Store below 25°C. Protect from moisture.
- **Reconstituted Solution:** Use immediately. If necessary, store at 2–8°C (Refrigerate. Do not freeze) for a maximum of 24 hours.
- **Handling:** For single use only; discard the syringe and any remaining solution after one dose.
