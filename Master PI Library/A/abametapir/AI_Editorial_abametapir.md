# Editorial Product Information: Abametapir

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA)
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of abametapir, synthesising available regulatory information into a single reference for healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory documents for definitive prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Abametapir (5,5'-dimethyl-2,2'-bipyridinyl)
- **Chemical Class:** Metalloproteinase inhibitor
- **Brand Name:** XEGLYZE [US]
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Topical lotion, 0.74% w/w (viscous white to off-white oil-in-water emulsion) [US]
- **Route of Administration:** Topical (scalp hair and scalp only)
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories Inc. [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Head Lice Infestation:** Indicated for the topical treatment of head lice (*Pediculus humanus capitis*) infestation in patients aged 6 months and older [US-approved].
- **Clinical Context:** Use should be part of a comprehensive lice management program, including laundering of bedding and clothing, and the use of a fine-tooth comb for nit removal [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric):** Treatment consists of a single topical application [US].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - For topical use only; not for oral, ophthalmic, or intravaginal administration.
    - Shake the bottle well before use.
    - Apply to **dry hair** in a quantity sufficient to thoroughly coat the entire hair and scalp (up to one full 200 g bottle).
    - Massage the lotion into the scalp and through the hair.
    - Leave the medication on the hair and scalp for **10 minutes**.
    - Rinse thoroughly with warm water.
    - Wash hands immediately after application.
    - Hair may be shampooed at any time following the 10-minute treatment.
- **Special Considerations:** Discard any unused product; do not flush down the sink or toilet [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None listed in available sources [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Neonatal Benzyl Alcohol Toxicity:** XEGLYZE contains 2% benzyl alcohol. Systemic exposure to benzyl alcohol has been linked to "gasping syndrome" (respiratory distress, CNS depression, metabolic acidosis) and death in neonates and low-birth-weight infants. Due to the risk of increased systemic absorption in infants with immature skin barriers, use is **not recommended** in patients under 6 months of age [US].
- **Accidental Ingestion Risk:** Ingestion of benzyl alcohol in large quantities can cause serious adverse effects, including seizures, respiratory depression, and death. Application in children must occur under direct adult supervision to prevent accidental oral exposure [US].
- **Local Irritation:** Avoid contact with eyes; if contact occurs, rinse immediately with water [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions reported in clinical trials (incidence ≥1%) include:

| Adverse Reaction | XEGLYZE (n=349) | Vehicle (n=350) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Erythema** | 4.0% | 2.0% |
| **Rash** | 3.2% | 2.3% |
| **Skin burning sensation** | 2.6% | 0.0% |
| **Contact dermatitis** | 1.7% | 1.1% |
| **Vomiting** | 1.7% | 0.6% |
| **Eye irritation** | 1.2% | 0.6% |
| **Pruritus** | 1.4% | 0.7% |
| **Hair color changes** | 1.0% | 0.0% |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** While not reported in topical clinical trials, systemic benzyl alcohol toxicity (via ingestion or neonatal exposure) can lead to cardiovascular collapse and multi-organ failure [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Cytochrome P450 Inhibition:** In vitro studies indicate that abametapir and its primary metabolite (abametapir carboxyl) may inhibit CYP3A4, CYP2B6, and CYP1A2 [US].
- **Management Note:** Because the metabolite abametapir carboxyl has a long half-life, patients should **avoid** taking systemic drugs that are substrates of **CYP3A4, CYP2B6, or CYP1A2** for **2 weeks** after the application of XEGLYZE [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not assigned (PLR format). There are no available data on use in pregnant women to assess for drug-associated risks of birth defects or miscarriage [US].
    - **Animal Data:** No evidence of fetal harm or malformations was observed in rats (up to 50 times the maximum recommended human dose) or rabbits [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** There is no information regarding the presence of abametapir in human milk or its effects on milk production or the breastfed infant. The clinical need for treatment should be weighed against potential risks [US].
- **Fertility:** No effects on fertility were observed in rat studies at doses up to 50 times the human equivalent dose [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy are established for patients 6 months of age and older. Use is not recommended for those under 6 months due to benzyl alcohol toxicity risks [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical trials did not include sufficient numbers of subjects aged 65 and over to determine if they respond differently than younger subjects [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Dosing adjustments not specified in available sources [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Dosing adjustments not specified in available sources [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Abametapir is a metalloproteinase inhibitor. These enzymes are essential for the physiological processes involved in the development of louse eggs and the survival of the lice [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Rapid systemic absorption occurs; median $T_{max}$ is 0.57 to 1.54 hours. Systemic exposure increases as the age of the patient decreases [US].
    - **Distribution:** Highly protein-bound (Abametapir: 91–92%; Abametapir carboxyl: 96–97%) [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Extensively metabolised via CYP1A2 to abametapir hydroxyl and subsequently to abametapir carboxyl.
    - **Elimination:** Abametapir carboxyl is cleared slowly; its elimination half-life is approximately 71 ± 40 hours or longer in adults [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pivotal Studies:** Two identical multi-center, randomized, double-blind, vehicle-controlled trials were conducted in 704 subjects [US].
- **Outcome:** Efficacy was measured as the proportion of subjects free of live lice at Days 1, 7, and 14 following a single 10-minute application.
- **Results:** Treatment success was significantly higher with abametapir (81.1% and 81.8% across two trials) compared to vehicle (50.9% and 47.2%) [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store upright at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions are permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Do not refrigerate or freeze [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Prescription-only (Rx only) [US].
