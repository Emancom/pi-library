# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
*   The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial: US (FDA).
*   **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of levocarnitine injection for use by healthcare professionals.
*   **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
*   **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
*   **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection, USP [US].
*   **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
*   **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) sterile solution for injection [US].
*   **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency (Inborn Errors of Metabolism):** For the treatment of acute and chronic secondary carnitine deficiency resulting from inborn errors of metabolism [US-approved].
*   **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
*   **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg.
    *   **Method:** Administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion.
    *   **Severe Metabolic Crisis:** A loading dose is often administered, followed by an equivalent dose divided over the subsequent 24 hours.
    *   **Frequency:** Dosing should occur every 3 or 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours.
    *   **Maximum Dose:** The highest reported administered dose is 300 mg/kg [US].
*   **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    *   **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight.
    *   **Method:** Administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
    *   **Monitoring and Adjustment:** Therapy initiation may be prompted by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine levels <40 to 50 µmol/L. Downward dose adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be made as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough concentrations.

### Administration Instructions
*   Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration.
*   **Compatibility:** Stable for up to 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations ranging from 0.5 mg/mL to 8.0 mg/mL [US].

## 4. Contraindications
*   None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### General Warnings
*   **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Some reactions occur within minutes of IV administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately.
*   **Seizures:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with known seizure disorders, an increase in frequency or severity of seizures has been observed following oral or intravenous levocarnitine.

### Specific Clinical Precautions
*   **Oral Formulation in Renal Impairment:** The safety and efficacy of **oral** levocarnitine have not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency. Chronic high doses of the oral formulation in patients with severely compromised renal function or ESRD may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites: trimethylamine (TMA) and trimethylamine-N-oxide (TMAO) [US].
*   **Clinical Outcomes in ESRD:** While levocarnitine increases carnitine concentrations in dialysis patients, the effect of supplementation on specific clinical outcomes or signs/symptoms of carnitine deficiency in this population has not been determined [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### General Experience
*   **Common:** Transient nausea and vomiting.
*   **Uncommon/Rare:** Body odor, gastritis.

### Clinical Trial Experience (ESRD Patients)
In two double-blind, placebo-controlled trials of dialysis patients, the following events occurred at a frequency of ≥5% (regardless of causality):

| Body System | Adverse Event (≥5%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Body as Whole** | Abdominal pain, accidental injury, asthenia, back pain, chest pain, fever, flu syndrome, headache, infection, injection site reaction, pain |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension, hypotension, tachycardia, palpitations |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea, dyspepsia, nausea, vomiting |
| **Metabolic/Nutritional** | Hypercalcemia, hypervolemia, peripheral edema |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness, insomnia, paresthesia, depression |
| **Respiratory** | Cough increased, dyspnea, pharyngitis, rhinitis |
| **Skin** | Pruritus |

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalised Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
*   **Management:** Monitor INR levels in patients on warfarin therapy following the initiation of levocarnitine or after any dose adjustments [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
### Pregnancy
*   **Risk Summary:** Not specified in available sources (US lettered category system not explicitly cited in the provided text).
*   **Animal Data:** Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at doses up to 3.8 times the human dose (based on surface area) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm.
*   **Clinical Consideration:** There are no adequate/controlled human studies; use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].

### Breastfeeding
*   **Safety Information:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk concentrations of levocarnitine after exogenous administration.
*   **Recommendation:** Weigh the risks of excess carnitine intake for the child against the benefits of supplementation for the mother. Consideration should be given to discontinuing nursing or the drug.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use:** Dosing recommendations for metabolic disorders are inclusive of paediatric patients (see Section 3).
*   **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Renal Impairment:** Levocarnitine is cleared via dialysis. See Section 3 for specific ESRD dosing and Section 5 regarding oral formulation precautions.
*   **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into cellular mitochondria, providing the substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters, which are rapidly excreted.

### Pharmacokinetics
*   **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
*   **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
*   **Elimination:** 
    *   **Half-life:** Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours; mean apparent terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours.
    *   **Excretion:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours (excluding endogenous carnitine).
*   **Bioavailability (Oral):** Approximately 15–16% [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   **ESRD Trough Levels:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients receiving 20 mg/kg three times weekly after dialysis showed that trough (pre-dialysis) concentrations increased from a baseline of ~21 µmol/L to ~190 µmol/L after 8 weeks of therapy.
*   **Dose-Ranging Study:** In dialysis patients receiving 10, 20, or 40 mg/kg, a dose-dependent increase in trough concentrations was observed over 12 and 24 weeks.

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
*   **Cautions:** Avoid excessive heat; protect from freezing.
*   **Handling:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as the formulation contains no preservatives.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
*   **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
*   **Poison/Schedule:** Not specified in available sources.
