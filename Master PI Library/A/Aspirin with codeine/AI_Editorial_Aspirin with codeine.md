# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine Injection

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA)
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonized editorial overview of Levocarnitine clinical information for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesizes data regarding the intravenous formulation of the drug.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); a carrier molecule involved in the transport of long-chain fatty acids.
- **Chemical Class:** (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt. 
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection, USP (Baxter Healthcare Corporation) [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency in Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Indicated for the acute and chronic management of patients with metabolic defects resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD) on Hemodialysis:** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients undergoing chronic hemodialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Pediatrics)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    - Recommended dose is 50 mg/kg administered as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion. 
    - In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose may be given, followed by equivalent doses every 3 or 4 hours (never less than every 6 hours) over the first 24 hours.
    - Subsequent daily maintenance doses generally remain at 50 mg/kg, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilized.
- **ESRD on Hemodialysis:**
    - Recommended starting dose: 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight) administered as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line following each dialysis session.

### Administration and Monitoring
- **Monitoring:** Clinical response should be guided by plasma carnitine concentrations (trough levels). In metabolic disorders, weekly and monthly monitoring is advised. Target plasma free carnitine concentrations should ideally be between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].
- **Titration:** In dialysis patients, downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough levels [US].
- **Visual Inspection:** Parenteral products must be inspected for particulate matter and discoloration before use.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Known Hypersensitivity:** Not specified as an absolute contraindication in available sources, though serious reactions are warned against.
- **General:** None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None [US].
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions can occur within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, the drug must be discontinued immediately and appropriate treatment initiated [US].
- **Metabolite Accumulation:** Chronic high-dose oral administration (not specifically injection) in patients with severely compromised renal function or ESRD may lead to the accumulation of trimethylamine (TMA) and trimethylamine-N-oxide (TMAO), which are potentially toxic [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Levocarnitine has been associated with reports of seizures in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with known seizure disorders, an increase in the frequency or severity of seizures has been observed [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Clinical Trial Experience (ESRD Population)
Adverse events occurring at a frequency ≥5% in double-blind, placebo-controlled trials (regardless of causality) include:
- **General:** Abdominal pain (5-21%), accidental injury (8-12%), asthenia (8-12%), chest pain (6-15%), fever (5-12%), flu syndrome (15-29%), headache (3-37%), infection (10-24%), injection site reaction (27-38%), and generalized pain (21-35%) [US].
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (18-21%), hypotension (3-19%), tachycardia (5-9%), and arrhythmia (2-3%) [US].
- **Gastrointestinal:** Diarrhea (9-35%), nausea (5-12%), vomiting (9-21%), and dyspepsia (5-9%) [US].
- **Nervous System/Other:** Dizziness (10-18%), pharyngitis (15-27%), and cough increase (10-18%) [US].

### Postmarketing and Serious Effects
- **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea and vomiting; less frequent reports of body odor and gastritis.
- **Neurological:** Seizures (see Section 5).
- **Hypersensitivity:** Anaphylaxis and laryngeal edema.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have occurred when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin. It is recommended to monitor INR levels upon initiation or dose adjustment of levocarnitine in patients on anticoagulant therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Category:** Not specified by letter category in available sources. 
    - **Data:** Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. However, there are no adequate/well-controlled studies in pregnant women. Use only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Animal data (cows) show increased milk concentrations after administration. The risk of excess carnitine intake in the infant must be weighed against maternal benefit. Discontinuation of nursing or the drug should be considered [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies revealed no evidence of impaired fertility [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing guidelines for metabolic disorders apply to pediatric patients (see Section 3). Specific age-range safety profiles are not further detailed in the source.
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically detailed in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** The safety and efficacy of high-dose oral levocarnitine have not been evaluated in renal insufficiency, and caution regarding metabolite accumulation (TMA/TMAO) is advised [US]. For ESRD patients on dialysis, see Section 3 for IV dosing.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. The mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours, and the terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Pharmacokinetic Studies:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients receiving 20 mg/kg IV three times weekly after dialysis showed mean pre-dialysis (trough) levels increased from ~20 µmol/L at baseline to 190 ± 55 µmol/L at 8 weeks.
- **Dose-Ranging Studies:** Trials comparing 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses in ESRD patients (N≈30 per group) demonstrated dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine levels over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat.
- **Stability:** Compatible with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations of 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL for up to 24 hours at room temperature in PVC bags [US].
- **Vial Management:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as the formulation contains no preservatives.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources.
