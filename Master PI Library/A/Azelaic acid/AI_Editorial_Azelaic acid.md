## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient**: Azelaic Acid
- **Chemical Class**: Dicarboxylic acid
- **Brand Name(s)**: Séfralls [US]
- **ATC Code**: D10AX03
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s)**: 
    - Topical Cream: 10% (10 g/100 g) [US]
    - Topical Cream: 14% (14 g/100 g) [US]
- **Route of Administration**: Topical
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor**: Guangzhou Ariel Medical Technology Co., Ltd. [US-Labeler]; Ariel Biotech Inc [US-Distributor].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Acne Vulgaris**: For the treatment of acne, reduction of blemishes, and evening of skin tone/fading of spots [US-Approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Adolescents)
- **Frequency**: Apply twice daily, once in the morning and once in the evening [US].
- **Application Method**:
    1. Cleanse the affected area to remove dirt and impurities.
    2. Gently pat the skin dry.
    3. Dispense cream onto hands and apply directly to acne-prone areas.
- **Administration Instructions**: For external use only. Wash hands after application.

### Missed Dose
- Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity**: Known allergy or hypersensitivity to azelaic acid or any inactive ingredients in the formulation [US].
- **Broken Skin**: Do not apply to broken or severely irritated skin [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **General Precautions**: For external use only [US].
- **Ocular Exposure**: Avoid contact with eyes. If contact occurs, rinse eyes thoroughly with water [US].
- **Hypersensitivity/Skin Irritation**: Patients should discontinue use and consult a healthcare professional if they experience significant facial swelling, large areas of red rash, or intense itching or burning sensations [US].
- **Monitoring**: Healthcare professionals should monitor for signs of local irritation, especially during the initial weeks of treatment.
