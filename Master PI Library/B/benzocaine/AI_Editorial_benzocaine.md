# Editorial Product Information: Benzocaine and Menthol

## Editorial Note
*   **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA).
*   **Purpose:** This document is a harmonized editorial overview intended for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesizes information from available regulatory sources into a single clinical reference.
*   **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing or dispensing in a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredients:** **Benzocaine** (ester-type local anesthetic) and **Menthol** (counter-irritant/analgesic).
*   **Brand Names:** Orajel 2x Medicated for Toothache and Gum [US].
*   **ATC Code:** N01BA05 (Benzocaine); N01BA (Menthol - combination codes vary).
*   **Dosage Form and Strength:** Topical liquid containing Benzocaine 20% (200 mg/mL) and Menthol 0.1% (1 mg/mL) [US].
*   **Route of Administration:** Topical (Oral mucosa).
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Church & Dwight Co., Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Oral Pain Relief [US]:** Temporary relief of pain associated with:
    *   Canker sores.
    *   Minor injury of the mouth and gums.
    *   Minor irritation of the mouth and gums caused by dentures or orthodontic appliances.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Children ≥2 years)
*   **Application:** Apply the liquid to the affected area using the provided applicator brush [US].
*   **Frequency:** Up to 4 times daily, or as directed by a healthcare professional (dentist or doctor) [US].

### Administration Instructions
*   Remove the safety seal from the bottle cap before use [US].
*   Do not use if the safety seal is broken or missing [US].
*   Avoid smoking during application due to flammability [US].

### Treatment Duration
*   Usage should not exceed 7 days unless directed by a dentist or doctor [US].

## 4. Contraindications
*   **Hypersensitivity:** History of allergy to local anesthetics, specifically those in the "caine" family (e.g., procaine, butacaine, benzocaine) [US].
*   **Age Restriction:** Contraindicated in children under 2 years of age [US].
*   **Specific Condition:** Do not use for the treatment of teething [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warnings
*   Not specified in available sources.

### Methemoglobinemia Warning
*   **Risk:** Use of benzocaine may cause methemoglobinemia, a serious condition that reduces the blood's oxygen-carrying capacity. This can occur even in patients who have previously used the product without incident [US].
*   **Action:** Immediate medical attention is required if symptoms develop, including pale, gray, or blue-colored skin (cyanosis), headache, tachycardia, dyspnea, dizziness, lightheadedness, or fatigue [US].

### General Precautions
*   **Flammability:** The liquid is flammable; keep away from fire or open flames [US].
*   **Excessive Use:** Do not use more than directed [US].
*   **Ingestion:** If an amount exceeding the dose for pain relief is accidentally swallowed, contact a Poison Control Center or seek medical help immediately [US].

### Monitoring Requirements
*   Discontinue use and consult a doctor if:
    *   Swelling, rash, or fever develops [US].
    *   Irritation, pain, or redness persists or worsens [US].
    *   Symptoms do not improve within 7 days [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
The following adverse effects have been identified from clinical warnings; frequency data is not specified in the available source:
*   **Serious/Life-Threatening:** Methemoglobinemia (characterized by cyanosis, rapid heart rate, and respiratory distress) [US].
*   **Hypersensitivity/Local Reactions:** Swelling, rash, fever, and localized irritation or redness [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
*   **Mechanism of Action:** 
    *   **Benzocaine:** A local anesthetic that prevents the generation and conduction of nerve impulses by decreasing the permeability of the neuronal membrane to sodium ions [Implied clinical class].
    *   **Menthol:** Provides a cooling sensation and acts as a mild local analgesic/counter-irritant [US].
*   **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Pharmacodynamics:** Provides immediate pain relief that lasts up to 2 hours [US].
