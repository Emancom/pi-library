## 6. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed / Prominent Warning
- **WARNING: Accidental overdose of iron-containing products is a leading cause of fatal poisoning in children under 6 years of age. KEEP THIS PRODUCT OUT OF REACH OF CHILDREN. In case of accidental overdose, call a doctor or poison control center immediately.** [US]

### General warnings and precautions
- Folic acid considerations:
  - Folic acid alone is an improper therapy for pernicious anemia and other megaloblastic anemias when vitamin B12 is deficient.
  - Folic acid in doses above 0.1 mg (100 mcg) daily may obscure pernicious anemia because hematologic remission can occur while neurologic manifestations remain progressive.
  - Allergic sensitization has been reported following both oral and parenteral administration of folic acid.
- Patients’ concurrent medical conditions and the use of other drugs, herbal products, and dietary supplements should be reviewed prior to use.
- Iron-related precautions:
  - Iron, even at usual recommended levels, has been associated with gastrointestinal intolerance in some patients.
  - See boxed warning regarding pediatric fatal overdose risk.
- Drug–disease interactions and special-risk situations: Not otherwise specified; clinicians should consider individual patient comorbidities prior to use.
- Monitoring requirements: Not specified in available sources beyond the general cautions above.

## 8. Drug Interactions
- Pharmacodynamic interactions:
  - Pyridoxine (vitamin B6) antagonizes the action of levodopa. The product is **not recommended for and should not be given** to patients receiving levodopa (interaction described in source).
  - There is a possibility of increased bleeding due to pyridoxine interaction with anticoagulants or antiplatelet agents (examples cited: aspirin, heparin, clopidogrel). Clinicians should monitor bleeding risk when concomitant therapy is used.
- Interactions related to folic acid:
  - Folic acid can mask hematologic indicators of vitamin B12 deficiency; this is a clinical interaction that may delay diagnosis and appropriate treatment of B12 deficiency.
- Pharmacokinetic interactions: Not specified in available sources.
- Management notes:
  - Evaluate necessity of concomitant pyridoxine in patients on levodopa; avoid concomitant use as recommended.
  - Assess bleeding risk and monitor coagulation/clinical bleeding signs when used with anticoagulant or antiplatelet therapy.
  - Consider evaluation of vitamin B12 status before or during folic acid supplementation in patients at risk for B12 deficiency.
- Region-specific interactions: Not specified beyond the information above.
