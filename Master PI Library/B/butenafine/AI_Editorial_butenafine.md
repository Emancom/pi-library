# Editorial Product Information: Butenafine

## Editorial Note
- The following information is derived from the official Product Information (PI) provided by the **United States (FDA)** for **butenafine hydrochloride** 1% cream.
- This document serves as a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals and is not a substitute for the official PI or the "Drug Facts" label required for prescribing or dispensing in a specific jurisdiction. 
- At the time of this editorial, only the **United States (FDA)** documentation was provided for synthesis.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient**: Butenafine hydrochloride (a synthetic benzylamine antifungal).
- **Brand Name(s)**: Welmate Butenafine [US].
- **ATC Code**: D01AE23.
- **Dosage Form and Strength**: Topical cream containing 10 mg of butenafine hydrochloride per 1 g (1%).
- **Route of Administration**: Topical.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor**: Distributed by Wellspring (Airmont, NY); Manufactured by Sun Pharma Canada Inc. for YYBA Corp [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Tinea Pedis**: Treatment of interdigital athlete’s foot (effectiveness on the bottom or sides of the foot is not specified/unknown) [US].
- **Tinea Cruris**: Treatment of jock itch [US].
- **Tinea Corporis**: Treatment of ringworm [US].
- Relief of associated symptoms including itching, burning, cracking, and scaling [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Adult and Paediatric (12 years and older)
- **Tinea Pedis (Interdigital)**: 
    - Apply to the affected skin between and around the toes twice daily for 1 week; OR
    - Apply once daily for 4 weeks [US].
- **Tinea Cruris and Tinea Corporis**:
    - Apply to the affected skin once daily for 2 weeks [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Wash the affected area with soap and water and dry completely before application.
- Apply cream to the affected skin and immediate surrounding area.
- Wash hands thoroughly after each use.
- For tinea pedis, clinicians should advise patients to wear well-fitting, ventilated shoes and change socks at least once daily [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to butenafine hydrochloride or any of the inactive ingredients [Implicit].
- Not for use on the nails or scalp [US].
- Not for use in or near the mouth or eyes [US].
- Not for use for vaginal yeast infections [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **External Use Only**: For topical dermatological use; not for ophthalmic, oral, or intravaginal administration [US].
- **Local Irritation**: Discontinue use and consult a healthcare provider if significant irritation occurs or if symptoms worsen [US].
- **Ocular Exposure**: Avoid contact with eyes. If accidental contact occurs, rinse eyes thoroughly with water [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Frequency Not Specified**: The following local reactions may occur:
    - Skin irritation
    - Burning or stinging
    - Redness
    - Itching
- If excessive irritation develops, treatment should be discontinued [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- Not specified in available sources. Topical administration of butenafine results in low systemic absorption, potentially limiting the risk of systemic drug-drug interactions.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy**: Not specified in available sources. 
- **Breastfeeding**: Not specified in available sources. 
- **Fertility**: Not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use**: Safety and efficacy have been established for children 12 years and older. For children under 12 years, use should be directed by a physician [US].
- **Elderly Use**: Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment**: Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action**: Butenafine is a benzylamine derivative with a mode of action similar to allylamine antifungals. It acts by inhibiting the enzyme squalene epoxidase, which blocks the synthesis of ergosterol, a key component of fungal cell membranes. This leads to the accumulation of squalene, resulting in fungal cell death.
- **Pharmacokinetics**: [US] source indicates low systemic absorption from topical application, though specific parameters (Cmax, Tmax, Half-life) are not provided in the "Drug Facts" format.
- **Pharmacodynamics**: Exhibits fungicidal activity against dermatophytes.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Summary**: The product is described as "clinically proven" to cure most cases of athlete's foot between the toes, jock itch, and ringworm when used as directed [US]. Specific trial data (e.g., mycological cure rates) were not provided in the source documentation.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature**: Store between 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Integrity**: Do not use if the seal on the tube is broken or not visible.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification**: 
    - [US]: Over-the-counter (OTC) Human Drug.
- **Product Identification Number**: NDC 73581-210 [US].
