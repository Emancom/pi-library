# Editorial Product Information: bacitracin + neomycin + polymyxin + pramoxine

## 1. Product Identification
- Active ingredient (INN/generic name): **bacitracin + neomycin + polymyxin + pramoxine**
  - Bacitracin zinc 500 USP units per 1000 mg
  - Neomycin sulfate 3.5 mg per 1000 mg
  - Polymyxin B sulfate 10,000 USP units per 1000 mg
  - Pramoxine hydrochloride 10 mg per 1000 mg
- Inactive ingredient (major): petrolatum
- Chemical class: Not specified in available sources
- ATC code: Not specified in available sources
- Route(s) of administration: topical (external)

- Withdrawal, suspension, or restriction history: Not specified in available sources
- Other jurisdictions' registration/approval dates: Not specified in available sources

## 3. Approved Indications
- First aid to help prevent infection in minor cuts, scrapes, and burns. [US]

## 4. Dosage and Administration
- General dosing (adult):
  - Clean the affected area prior to application.
  - Apply a small quantity of the product — no more than the amount that would cover the
    surface of a fingertip — to the affected area one to three times each day.
  - The treated area may be covered with a sterile bandage if desired.
- Dosing by indication:
  - Indication-specific dosing beyond the general first-aid directions above: Not specified
    in available sources.
- Administration instructions:
  - For external use only.
  - Do not apply in the eyes.
  - Do not apply over large areas of the body.
  - Do not use for longer than 1 week unless directed by a doctor.
- Titration schedules: Not specified in available sources
- Missed dose instructions: Not specified in available sources

## 5. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity/allergy to bacitracin, neomycin, polymyxin B, pramoxine, or any
  component of the formulation.
- Application to the eyes.
- Application over large areas of the body. (Instruction stated as "Do Not Use"; consider
  as contraindication per source wording.)
- Use for longer than 1 week without direction from a physician. (Instruction stated as
  "Do Not Use"; consider as contraindication per source wording.)

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
- **For external use only.**
- **Do not use** if allergic to any of the ingredients.
- **Do not use in the eyes.**
- **Do not use over large areas of the body.**
- **Do not use longer than 1 week unless directed by a doctor.**
- Ask a doctor before use if the patient has:
  - deep or puncture wounds,
  - animal bites,
  - serious burns.
- Stop use and ask a doctor if:
  - the condition persists or gets worse,
  - a rash or other allergic reaction develops.
- Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison
  Control Center right away.
- Monitoring requirements: Not specified in available sources.
- Specific clinical precautions (e.g., avoidance in particular comorbidities,
  cross-sensitivity with other agents): Not specified in available sources.
