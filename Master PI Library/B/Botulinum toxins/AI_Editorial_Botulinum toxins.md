# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine Injection

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial: **US (FDA)**. 
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical and regulatory data into a single reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific PI. Clinicians should consult the approved PI in their specific region for prescribing purposes.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN/USP).
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Chemical Name:** (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Clear, colorless sterile solution for injection, 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency:** Indicated for the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that result in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD undergoing maintenance dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    - **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg, administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion [US].
    - **Acute Crisis:** A loading dose is often administered, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours. Dosing frequency should be every 3 to 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours [US].
    - **Titration:** Subsequent daily doses are generally maintained at 50 mg/kg, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been administered if clinically required [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
    - **Administration:** Administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].
    - **Monitoring and Adjustment:** Therapy initiation may be prompted by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine levels <40 µmol/L to 50 µmol/L. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be made as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough concentrations [US].
- **General Instructions:** Parenteral products should be inspected for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration. The formulation contains no preservatives; discard unused portions [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known at the time of publication [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None specified in available sources.
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have occurred, primarily in ESRD patients. Some reactions manifest within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and evaluate the risks/benefits before considering re-administration [US].
- **Seizure Risk:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Monitoring Requirements:** In metabolic disorders, weekly and monthly monitoring is recommended, including blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine: 35 to 60 µmol/L) [US].
- **Oral Levocarnitine Caution:** Safety and efficacy of oral levocarnitine have not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency. High doses of the oral form in ESRD may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions in ESRD patients undergoing chronic hemodialysis (occurring at ≥5% regardless of causality) [US]:

| Body System | Adverse Event (≥5%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Injection site reaction (33%), pain (30%), headache (22%), chest pain (12%), fever (7%), abdominal pain (9%) |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension (20%), hypotension (14%), tachycardia (6%), palpitations (5%) |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea (16%), vomiting (15%), nausea (8%), dyspepsia (5%), anorexia (5%) |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness (13%), insomnia (4%), depression (5%), paresthesia (5%) |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis (23%), cough (9%), rhinitis (9%), dyspnea (7%) |
| **Metabolic** | Hypercalcemia (9%), hyperkalemia (6%), hypervolemia (5%) |
| **Other** | Anemia (6%), pruritus (5%), leg cramps (4%) |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm [US].
- **Post-marketing Experience:** Seizures and body odor have been reported [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have been observed when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels in patients on warfarin therapy following the initiation of levocarnitine or after dose adjustments [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Category:** Not specified by letter [US]. 
    - **Data:** Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. While studies in dairy cows show increased milk carnitine following administration, the risks to the infant must be weighed against the benefits of maternal supplementation [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders (50 mg/kg) is applicable to pediatric patients [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Specifically indicated for ESRD patients on dialysis. However, caution is advised regarding the use of oral levocarnitine in this population due to metabolite accumulation [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production in all tissues except the brain. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin. Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. Mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Bioavailability Study:** In healthy volunteers, levocarnitine tablets and oral solution were bioequivalent. Absolute bioavailability from oral forms (corrected for endogenous levels) was approximately 15% [US].
- **ESRD Studies:** A 9-week study of ESRD patients (20 mg/kg three times weekly after dialysis) showed that trough concentrations reached 190 ± 55 µmol/L by week 8, compared to a baseline of 21.3 ± 7.7 µmol/L. Efficacy in increasing carnitine concentrations is established, though effects on clinical outcomes in ESRD have not been determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F). Avoid excessive heat [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing. Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial [US].
- **Stability:** Compatible and stable for up to 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations of 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **US:** Rx-only. Poison Schedule classification not applicable (Prescription Drug) [US].
