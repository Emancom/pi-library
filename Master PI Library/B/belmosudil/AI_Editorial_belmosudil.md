# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising regulatory data into a single, clinically focused reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI or local prescribing guidelines in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority's approved PI before prescribing.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP).
- **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule/amino acid derivative.
- **Brand Names:** Generic **Levocarnitine** Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Injection, solution: 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency:** Indicated for the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that result in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
- **General Dose:** 50 mg/kg administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion [US].
- **Loading Dose:** Often required in patients with severe metabolic crisis, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours [US].
- **Maintenance Frequency:** Doses should be administered every 3 or 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours [US].
- **Highest Administered Dose:** 300 mg/kg [US].

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Recommended Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
- **Administration:** Administer as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line after each dialysis session [US].
- **Monitoring/Adjustment:** Dose adjustments may be prompted by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma **levocarnitine** concentrations below 40–50 µmol/L. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].

### Administration Instructions
- **Visual Inspection:** Inspect parenteral products for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable and compatible when mixed in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations of 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL. Stable for up to 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known at the time of publication [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in dialysis patients. Reactions may occur within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately [US].
- **Neurologic Effects (Seizures):** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In patients with known seizure disorders, an increase in seizure frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Renal Impairment (Oral Formulations):** Safety and efficacy of high-dose *oral* **levocarnitine** have not been evaluated in renal insufficiency; chronic oral administration in ESRD may lead to accumulation of toxic metabolites (TMA and TMAO) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects listed were reported in double-blind, placebo-controlled trials in chronic hemodialysis patients at a frequency of ≥5%, regardless of causality [US].

| Body System | Adverse Effects (Frequency ≥5%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Chest pain (12%), Flu syndrome (25%), Headache (22%), Infection (15%), Injection site reaction (33%), Pain (30%), Fever (7%), Abdominal pain (9%) |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension (20%), Hypotension (14%), Tachycardia (6%), Palpitations (5%) |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea (16%), Vomiting (15%), Nausea (8%), Anorexia (5%) |
| **Metabolic/Nutritional** | Hypercalcemia (9%), Hyperkalemia (6%), Hypervolemia (5%) |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness (13%), Paresthesia (5%), Depression (5%), Insomnia (4%) |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis (23%), Cough increase (9%), Dyspnea (7%), Rhinitis (9%) |
| **Special Senses** | Taste perversion (3%), Eye disorder (3%) |

**Serious Adverse Effects:** Anaphylaxis, bronchospasm, and laryngeal edema have been reported postmarketing [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalised Ratio (INR) have occurred with concomitant use. **Management:** Monitor INR levels upon initiation of **levocarnitine** or after dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Not formally categorised by US letter system in this PI, but reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows indicate increased carnitine concentrations in milk following administration. Risks of excess intake to the child must be weighed against maternal benefit [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosage for metabolic disorders is weight-based (50 mg/kg). Specific safety/efficacy studies for other paediatric indications are not detailed in the available source [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Specifically indicated for use in ESRD patients on dialysis. High-dose oral administration is cautioned against in severe renal impairment due to metabolite accumulation [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring carrier molecule essential for mammalian energy metabolism. it facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids in patients with metabolic defects by forming acylcarnitine esters, which are rapidly excreted [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not protein-bound (including albumin) [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours (uncorrected for endogenous levels). Mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients receiving 20 mg/kg IV three times weekly post-dialysis showed that trough (pre-dialysis) concentrations increased from 21.3 µmol/L at baseline to 190 µmol/L after 8 weeks. Normal levels are 40–50 µmol/L [US].
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** Studies of 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg IV doses demonstrated dose-dependent increases in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Special Precautions:** Avoid excessive heat. Protect from freezing.
- **Handling:** Discard any unused portion of an opened vial as the formulation is preservative-free [US].
