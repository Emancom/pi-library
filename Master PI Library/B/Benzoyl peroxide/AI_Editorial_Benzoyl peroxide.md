# Editorial Product Information: Benzoyl Peroxide and Clindamycin Phosphate

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions’ Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial: **Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of the combination of benzoyl peroxide and clindamycin phosphate, intended for use by healthcare professionals.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis of available regulatory data and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing and dispensing in a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** Benzoyl peroxide and Clindamycin phosphate.
- **Chemical Class:** Benzoyl peroxide (oxidising agent/keratolytic); Clindamycin phosphate (lincosamide antibiotic).
- **Brand Name(s):** **Duac Once Daily Gel** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Topical gel containing clindamycin 1% w/w (as phosphate) and benzoyl peroxide 5% w/w [Australian].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical (external) use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** GlaxoSmithKline Australia Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Acne Vulgaris:** Indicated for the topical treatment of comedo, papular, and pustular acne vulgaris [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adults and Adolescents ≥12 years):** Apply a thin film once daily in the evening to the entire affected area.
- **Preparation:** The skin should be thoroughly washed, rinsed with warm water, and gently patted dry before application [Australian].
- **Treatment Duration:** Recommended maximum duration of 11 weeks [Australian].
- **Titration:** If excessive dryness or peeling occurs, application frequency should be reduced or temporarily interrupted. Efficacy for less-than-daily dosing has not been established [Australian].
- **Missed Dose:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Administration Instructions:** Patients should wash hands after application. Excessive application does not improve efficacy but increases the risk of skin irritation [Australian].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to clindamycin, lincomycin, benzoyl peroxide, or any excipients in the formulation [Australian].
- **Gastrointestinal History:** Patients with a history of regional enteritis, ulcerative colitis, or antibiotic-associated colitis (including pseudomembranous colitis) [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None specified in available sources.
- **Gastrointestinal Effects:** Although systemic absorption is low, oral and parenteral clindamycin are associated with severe diarrhoea and pseudomembranous colitis. If significant diarrhoea or abdominal cramps occur, treatment should be discontinued immediately and the patient investigated for *Clostridium difficile* [Australian].
- **Local Irritation:** Avoid contact with the mouth, eyes, lips, and mucous membranes. Use with caution on sensitive, irritated, or broken skin. Irritation potential increases if applied under occlusion [Australian].
- **Photosensitivity:** Benzoyl peroxide may increase sensitivity to UV light. Patients should minimise sun exposure, avoid sunlamps, and use protective clothing and sunscreen [Australian].
- **Bleaching:** The gel may bleach hair and dyed fabrics; contact with hair, clothing, and carpets should be avoided [Australian].
- **Antibiotic Resistance:** Prolonged use may lead to the selection of resistant organisms. There is a potential for cross-resistance with other lincosamides and macrolides [Australian].
- **Severe Acne:** The product may not be sufficient for the treatment of severe nodulocystic acne [Australian].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions are primarily dermatological and occur at the site of application.

| Frequency | Adverse Event [Australian] |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Erythema, peeling, dryness |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Burning sensation, pruritus, application site photosensitivity |
| **Uncommon (0.1–1%)** | Dermatitis, paraesthesia, erythematous rash, worsening of acne |
| **Rare (<0.1%)** | Allergic reactions (hypersensitivity, anaphylaxis), urticaria, application site discoloration, colitis (including pseudomembranous colitis), diarrhoea, abdominal pain |

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Erythromycin:** Should not be used in combination with clindamycin due to observed in vitro antagonism [Australian].
- **Neuromuscular Blocking Agents:** Clindamycin has neuromuscular blocking properties that may enhance the action of other blocking agents; use with caution in patients receiving these medications [Australian].
- **Concomitant Topical Therapies:** Use with tretinoin, isotretinoin, or tazarotene may increase irritation and reduce efficacy. If required, apply at different times of the day (e.g., morning and evening) [Australian].
- **Sulphonamides:** Concomitant use with topical benzoyl peroxide may cause temporary yellow/orange discoloration of the skin and facial hair [Australian].
- **Abrasive/Drying Agents:** Medicated soaps, abrasive cleansers, or products with high alcohol concentrations may have a cumulative irritant effect [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
  - **Australian Category A:** Drugs which have been taken by a large number of pregnant women and women of childbearing age without any proven increase in the frequency of malformations or other direct or indirect harmful effects on the fetus having been observed.
  - **Clinical Recommendation:** Limited data exists. Use only if the potential benefit justifies the potential risk to the fetus [Australian].
- **Breastfeeding:** Use during lactation has not been studied. Systemic absorption is low, but clindamycin has been detected in breast milk following oral/parenteral use. To prevent accidental ingestion by the infant, do not apply the gel to the breast area [Australian].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies showed no impairment of fertility at standard doses, though reduced testes/epididymis weights were noted in rats at very high oral doses (1000 mg/kg/day) [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy have not been established in children under 12 years of age [Australian].
- **Elderly Use:** No specific data available [Australian].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources (systemic absorption is minimal).

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** 
  - **Clindamycin:** A lincosamide antibiotic that binds to the 23S subunit of the bacterial ribosome, inhibiting protein synthesis. It is primarily bacteriostatic [Australian].
  - **Benzoyl Peroxide:** A keratolytic agent with bactericidal activity against *Propionibacterium acnes*. It is also sebostatic, counteracting excessive sebum production [Australian].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Clindamycin reduces free fatty acids on the skin. Benzoyl peroxide reduces the potential for the emergence of clindamycin-resistant organisms [Australian].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** 
  - **Absorption:** Clindamycin absorption is negligible (0.043% of the dose). Benzoyl peroxide is absorbed by the skin and metabolised to benzoic acid; approximately 1.9% of the dose enters systemic circulation as benzoic acid [Australian].
  - **Elimination:** Benzoic acid is excreted via the kidneys as hippuric acid [Australian].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pivotal Data:** In five randomised double-blind studies involving 1,319 patients, the combination was significantly more effective than its individual components or vehicle in reducing inflammatory lesions.
- **Efficacy:** In Study 158, the combination showed a 60% mean reduction in inflammatory lesions versus 46% for benzoyl peroxide alone and 37% for clindamycin alone [Australian].
- **Onset:** Therapeutic effect may require 4 to 6 weeks of consistent treatment [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Pharmacist Storage:** Store at 2°C to 8°C (Refrigerate. Do not freeze). Shelf life is 2 years under these conditions [Australian].
- **Patient Storage:** Store at 2°C to 8°C (Refrigerate. Do not freeze). Discard after 2 months [Australian].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule:** Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine) [Australian].
