# Editorial Product Information: Brolucizumab

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following regulatory authority: **Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview of brolucizumab for use by healthcare professionals.
- This document is an editorial synthesis intended for informational purposes and is not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific Product Information required for prescribing.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Brolucizumab (rbe).
- **Chemical Class:** Humanised monoclonal single-chain Fv (scFv) antibody fragment, produced in *Escherichia coli* via recombinant DNA technology.
- **Brand Name:** **Beovu** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** S01LA06.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - Solution for injection: 120 mg/mL.
    - Available as a single-use pre-filled syringe (19.8 mg in 0.165 mL) or a single-use vial (27.6 mg in 0.230 mL). Both presentations are designed to deliver a single 0.05 mL dose containing 6 mg of brolucizumab [Australian].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravitreal injection.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Novartis Pharmaceuticals Australia Pty Limited [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Neovascular (wet) age-related macular degeneration (nAMD)** [Australian-approved].
- **Diabetic macular oedema (DME)** [Australian-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
Brolucizumab must be administered by a qualified ophthalmologist experienced in intravitreal injections.

### Dosing by Indication
- **Neovascular (wet) AMD:**
    - **Loading Phase:** 6 mg (0.05 mL) every 4 weeks for the first three doses.
    - **Maintenance Phase:** After the loading phase, the clinician may individualise treatment based on disease activity. In patients without activity, intervals of every 12 weeks should be considered. In patients with disease activity, intervals of every 8 weeks should be considered.
    - **Note:** The maintenance interval between doses must not be less than every 8 weeks [Australian].
- **Diabetic Macular Oedema (DME):**
    - **Loading Phase:** 6 mg (0.05 mL) every 6 weeks for the first five doses.
    - **Maintenance Phase:** After 12 months of treatment, patients without disease activity may have intervals extended up to every 16 weeks. Patients with disease activity should be considered for 8-weekly dosing.
    - **Note:** The maintenance interval between doses must not be less than every 8 weeks [Australian].

### Administration Instructions
- **Aseptic Technique:** Must be performed under sterile conditions (disinfected hands, sterile gloves/drape, eyelid speculum).
- **Preparation:** The solution should be inspected for particulates or cloudiness before use. Excess volume in the syringe must be discarded to align the plunger with the 0.05 mL dose mark.
- **Injection Site:** The needle should be inserted 3.5 to 4.0 mm posterior to the limbus, avoiding the horizontal meridian and aiming toward the centre of the globe.

### Missed Dose Instructions
Not specified in available sources; however, the PI notes that treatment should be discontinued if the patient is not benefiting from visual or anatomical outcomes.

## 4. Contraindications
- Hypersensitivity to brolucizumab or any listed excipients (sodium citrate, sucrose, polysorbate 80).
- Active or suspected ocular or periocular infections.
- Active intraocular inflammation [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Endophthalmitis and Retinal Detachment:** Intravitreal injections are associated with risks of endophthalmitis and retinal detachment. Proper aseptic technique is mandatory.
- **Intraocular Inflammation (IOI), Retinal Vasculitis (RV), and Retinal Vascular Occlusion (RVO):** These immune-mediated events may occur after the first injection and are more frequent at the start of treatment. Treatment must be discontinued in patients who develop RV or RVO. Patients with a history of IOI/RVO within 12 months of their first dose are at higher risk.
- **Maintenance Interval Warning:** Maintenance intervals shorter than 8 weeks (e.g., 4-weekly maintenance) are associated with a higher incidence of IOI, RV, and RVO [Australian].
- **Intraocular Pressure (IOP):** Transient and sustained increases in IOP have been reported. Both IOP and optic nerve head perfusion must be monitored.
- **Arterial Thromboembolic Events (ATE):** There is a theoretical risk of ATE (e.g., myocardial infarction or stroke) following intravitreal VEGF inhibitor use.
- **Bilateral Treatment:** Safety and efficacy for concurrent bilateral administration have not been established.

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common (1–10%)
- **Ocular:** Reduced visual acuity, retinal haemorrhage, uveitis, iritis, iridocyclitis, retinal vascular occlusion, vitreous haemorrhage, vitreous detachment, retinal tear, cataract, conjunctival haemorrhage, eye pain, increased IOP, vision blurred, corneal abrasion, punctate keratitis.
- **Non-Ocular:** Hypersensitivity (urticaria, rash, pruritus).

### Uncommon (0.1–1%)
- Blindness, endophthalmitis, retinal detachment, retinal vasculitis, vitritis, corneal oedema, anterior chamber inflammation/flare.

### Serious Adverse Effects
- Retinal vasculitis and retinal vascular occlusion (often in the presence of intraocular inflammation).
- Endophthalmitis and blindness.
- Arterial thromboembolic events (4.5% in AMD studies) [Australian].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** No formal interaction studies have been performed.
- **Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Brolucizumab should not be administered concurrently with other anti-VEGF products (systemic or ocular) [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** **Category D** [Australian]. Based on the anti-VEGF mechanism, brolucizumab is potentially teratogenic and embryo-fetotoxic. It should not be used unless the benefit outweighs the risk.
- **Contraception:** Females of childbearing potential should use effective contraception during treatment and for at least one month after the final dose.
- **Breastfeeding:** Not recommended during treatment and for at least one month after the last dose, as it is unknown if brolucizumab is excreted in human milk.
- **Fertility:** No specific human studies; however, VEGF inhibition may affect corpus luteum function and follicular development.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy in children under 18 years have not been established [Australian].
- **Elderly (≥65 years):** No dose adjustment required.
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment required. Systemic exposure is low and driven by distribution from the eye rather than renal elimination. Data on severe renal impairment are limited.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment required. Metabolism occurs via proteolysis and is independent of hepatic function.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Brolucizumab is a high-affinity inhibitor of VEGF-A isoforms (e.g., VEGF110, VEGF121, VEGF165). By preventing VEGF-A from binding to receptors VEGFR-1 and VEGFR-2, it suppresses vascular permeability, endothelial cell proliferation, and pathological neovascularisation.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Following 6 mg intravitreal delivery, the mean plasma $C_{max}$ of free brolucizumab is 49.0 ng/mL, reached within 1 day.
    - **Distribution/Elimination:** Systemic half-life is approximately 4.3 ± 1.9 days. It is eliminated via target-mediated binding to VEGF, renal elimination, and proteolysis.
- **Immunogenicity:** Pre-treatment anti-drug antibodies (ADAs) were found in 35–52% of nAMD patients and 64% of DME patients. Treatment-emergent ADAs are associated with a higher incidence of intraocular inflammation.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **nAMD (HAWK and HARRIER):** Two Phase III studies (n=1,817) demonstrated that brolucizumab (6 mg) was non-inferior to aflibercept (2 mg) regarding BCVA change at week 48. Visual gains were maintained through week 96. Approximately 50% of patients were maintained on a 12-weekly dosing interval.
- **DME (KESTREL and KITE):** Two Phase III studies (n=926) demonstrated non-inferiority to aflibercept (2 mg). At week 100, brolucizumab showed significant reductions in central subfield thickness (CST) and fluid presence compared to baseline.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store in a refrigerator (2°C – 8°C).
- **Handling:** Do not freeze. Store in the original carton/blister to protect from light.
- **Stability:** Prior to use, an unopened vial or syringe may be kept at room temperature (25°C) for up to 24 hours.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule:** **Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine)** [Australian].
