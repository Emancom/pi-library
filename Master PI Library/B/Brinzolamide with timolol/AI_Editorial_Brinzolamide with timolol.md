# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**. 
- Information regarding other jurisdictions (e.g., Australian, European, Canadian, Japanese) was not available in the provided source material and is therefore noted as "Not specified" or omitted where regional comparisons are required.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. It is a clinical synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP), a carrier molecule for long-chain fatty acid transport.
- **Chemical Class:** (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection [United States].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL) [United States].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [United States].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency:** Indicated for the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that result in secondary carnitine deficiency [United States].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [United States].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
- **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion.
- **Loading Dose:** Often required in severe metabolic crises, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours.
- **Frequency:** Dosing should occur every 3 or 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours.
- **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses are typically 50 mg/kg, though higher doses up to 300 mg/kg have been used [United States].

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight.
- **Administration:** Administer as a slow 2 to 3 minute bolus into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
- **Dose Adjustments:** Should be guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Adjustments (e.g., down to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [United States].

### Administration Instructions
- Inspect visually for particulate matter or discoloration prior to use.
- **Compatibility:** Stable when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s in concentrations from 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL.
- **Stability:** Once mixed in PVC bags, the solution is stable for up to 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) [United States].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known at the time of publication [United States].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in patients with ESRD on dialysis. Reactions may occur within minutes of administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue immediately and consider the risks/benefits before re-administration [United States].
- **Laboratory Monitoring:** 
    - In metabolic disorders, monitor blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L).
    - In ESRD, monitor trough plasma levels (normal range: 40–50 µmol/L) [United States].
- **Renal Impairment (Oral Formulations):** Safety of oral levocarnitine has not been evaluated in renal insufficiency. Chronic high doses of oral levocarnitine in ESRD may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [United States].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common Adverse Effects (≥5% in ESRD Trials)
Adverse events reported in clinical trials for ESRD patients, regardless of causality, include:
- **Body as a Whole:** Abdominal pain (5–21%), headache (3–37%), injection site reaction (27–38%), pain (21–35%), fever (5–12%), asthenia (8–12%).
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (18–21%), hypotension (3–19%), tachycardia (5–9%), chest pain (6–15%).
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea (9–35%), vomiting (9–21%), nausea (5–12%).
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (15–27%), cough increase (10–18%), dyspnea (3–11%).
- **Nervous System:** Dizziness (10–18%), paresthesia (3–12%).

### Post-marketing Experience
- **Neurologic:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed.
- **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea and vomiting; body odor and gastritis occur less frequently.
- **Hypersensitivity:** Anaphylaxis, bronchospasm, and laryngeal edema [United States].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalised Ratio (INR) when used concomitantly with levocarnitine.
- **Management:** It is recommended to monitor INR levels after initiating levocarnitine or following dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin [United States].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [United States: Category not explicitly assigned, but follows former Category C logic]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed.
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk carnitine levels. Risks to the infant from excess carnitine should be weighed against the benefits of maternal supplementation [United States].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [United States].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders (50 mg/kg) applies to paediatric patients [United States].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in the source document.
- **Renal Impairment:** Specifically indicated for ESRD patients on dialysis. High-dose oral levocarnitine should be avoided in severe renal impairment due to metabolite accumulation [United States].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into cellular mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production. It also facilitates the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In ESRD, it may alleviate symptoms such as malaise, muscle weakness, and arrhythmias related to abnormal carnitine metabolism.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin. Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (feces).
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. Mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [United States].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pharmacokinetic Studies:** Healthy volunteer studies showed an absolute bioavailability of approximately 15% for oral formulations. 
- **ESRD Studies:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients receiving 20 mg/kg IV three times weekly showed a significant increase in mean plasma levocarnitine concentrations (from ~20 µmol/L to 190 µmol/L trough at 8 weeks). Clinical outcomes regarding symptoms of deficiency in this population remain to be determined [United States].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Precautions:** Avoid excessive heat; protect from freezing.
- **Handling:** Single-dose vials; discard any unused portion as the formulation contains no preservatives [United States].

- **United States:** Classified as Rx-only [United States].
- **Other Jurisdictions:** Not specified in available sources.
