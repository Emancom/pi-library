# Editorial Product Information: Baricitinib

## Editorial Note
- Contributing Jurisdictions: Australian (TGA)
- Purpose: This document provides a harmonised editorial synthesis of the Product Information for baricitinib, designed as a concise clinical reference for healthcare professionals. 
- Disclaimer: This is an editorial synthesis and does not replace the official Product Information required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory documents for full prescribing details.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Baricitinib; a selective and reversible Janus kinase (JAK) 1 and 2 inhibitor.
- **Brand Name(s):** **Olumiant** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Film-coated tablets in 2 mg (light pink, oblong) and 4 mg (medium pink, round) [Australian].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Eli Lilly [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Rheumatoid Arthritis (RA):** Treatment of moderate to severe active RA in adults who have had an inadequate response to, or are intolerant of, one or more disease-modifying anti-rheumatic drugs (DMARDs). May be used as monotherapy or with conventional synthetic DMARDs (e.g., methotrexate) [Australian].
- **Atopic Dermatitis (AD):** Treatment of moderate to severe AD in adults who are candidates for systemic therapy [Australian].
- **Alopecia Areata (AA):** Treatment of severe AA in adults where other treatments have failed or are inappropriate, and no spontaneous improvement is observed [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Administration
- Administered once daily, with or without food [Australian].

### Rheumatoid Arthritis
- **Recommended Dose:** 4 mg once daily.
- **Maintenance/Tapering:** 2 mg once daily may be considered for patients who achieve sustained control with 4 mg and are eligible for tapering.
- **Specific Subgroups:** 2 mg once daily may be acceptable for patients with moderate disease severity, limited risk of progressive damage, or moderate physical impairment who responded poorly to DMARDs [Australian].

### Atopic Dermatitis
- **Recommended Dose:** 2 mg once daily.
- **Dose Escalation:** 4 mg once daily may be considered if sustained control is not reached with 2 mg.
- **Discontinuation:** Stop treatment if no benefit is seen after 8 weeks of 4 mg dosing [Australian].

### Alopecia Areata
- **Recommended Dose:** 4 mg once daily.
- **Alternative Starting Dose:** 2 mg once daily based on patient factors.
- **Risk-Based Dosing:** 2 mg once daily is recommended for patients at higher risk of VTE, MACE, malignancy, those aged ≥65 years, or with chronic/recurrent infections.
- **Discontinuation:** Consider stopping if no benefit is seen after 36 weeks [Australian].

### Dose Modifications
- **Renal Impairment:** Reduce dose to 2 mg once daily in patients with eGFR 30–60 mL/min/1.73m².
- **Drug Interactions:** Reduce dose to 2 mg once daily when co-administered with strong OAT3 inhibitors (e.g., probenecid) [Australian].
- **Missed Dose:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to baricitinib or any excipients.
- Combination use with biological DMARDs (bDMARDs) [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warnings [Australian]
- **Safety Risks for JAK Inhibitors:** Based on data from other JAK inhibitors, baricitinib should only be used if no suitable alternatives exist in patients:
    - Aged 65 years and older.
    - With a history of atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease or other cardiovascular risk factors (e.g., current/past long-term smokers).
    - With malignancy risk factors (current or history of malignancy).

### General Warnings
- **Mortality:** Increased all-cause mortality, including sudden cardiovascular death, has been observed with other JAK inhibitors in patients over 50 with CV risk factors.
- **Thrombosis:** Serious and sometimes fatal VTE (DVT and PE) and arterial thrombosis have been reported. Discontinue immediately if VTE is suspected.
- **Major Adverse Cardiovascular Events (MACE):** Higher rates of non-fatal MI and stroke have been reported compared to TNF inhibitors.
- **Serious Infections:** Increased risk of serious fungal, bacterial, or viral infections (e.g., Herpes Zoster, cellulitis, tuberculosis). Screen for TB before initiation.
- **Gastrointestinal Perforation:** Use with caution in patients with a history of diverticulitis or those taking NSAIDs, corticosteroids, or opioids.
- **Laboratory Abnormalities:** Monitor for neutropenia (ANC <1000 cells/mm³), lymphopenia (ALC <500 cells/mm³), and anaemia (Hb <8 g/dL); treatment should be interrupted if these thresholds are met. Monitor lipid profiles 12 weeks after initiation.

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Frequency Distribution [Australian]
- **Very Common (>10%):** Hypercholesterolaemia, upper respiratory tract infections.
- **Common (1–10%):** Nausea, abdominal pain, Herpes Simplex, Herpes Zoster, urinary tract infections, headache, acne, CPK elevations (>5x ULN).
- **Uncommon (0.1–1%):** Neutropenia, lymphopenia, thrombocytosis, ALT/AST elevations (≥3x ULN), weight gain, diverticulitis (post-marketing).

### Serious Adverse Effects
- Venous thromboembolism (VTE), MACE, serious opportunistic infections, and malignancies (including lymphoma and non-melanoma skin cancer).

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **OAT3 Inhibitors:** Probenecid increases baricitinib AUC approximately 2-fold. Dose reduction to 2 mg daily is required [Australian].
- **Transporters:** Baricitinib is a substrate for P-gp, BCRP, OAT3, and MATE2-K.
- **CYP Enzymes:** No clinically meaningful interactions with CYP3A4 inhibitors (ketoconazole) or inducers (rifampicin).
- **Vaccinations:** Avoid use of live, attenuated vaccines during or immediately prior to therapy.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [Australian Category D]. Baricitinib has shown embryofoetal toxicity (malformations) in animal studies at maternotoxic doses. It is not recommended during pregnancy. Women of childbearing potential should use effective contraception during and for 1 week after treatment.
- **Breastfeeding:** Discontinue breastfeeding during treatment. It is unknown if baricitinib is excreted in human milk; it was detected in the milk of lactating rats.
- **Fertility:** Animal data suggest potential for reduced female fertility and impaired early embryonic development at high exposures.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy not established in patients <18 years [Australian].
- **Elderly (≥65 years):** Increased risk of MACE, malignancy, and serious infections. Use only if no suitable treatment alternatives exist.
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    - Moderate (eGFR 30–60 mL/min/1.73m²): 2 mg once daily.
    - Severe (eGFR <30 mL/min/1.73m²): Not recommended [Australian].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** 
    - Mild to Moderate: No dose adjustment required.
    - Severe: Not recommended [Australian].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Selective and reversible inhibitor of JAK1 and JAK2. These enzymes transduce signals from cytokine/growth factor receptors (e.g., IL-6, IFN) to STATs, which regulate gene expression.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Rapid; median $t_{max}$ ~1 hour; bioavailability ~80%.
    - **Distribution:** ~50% plasma protein bound.
    - **Metabolism:** Minimal (~6%); mediated by CYP3A4.
    - **Elimination:** Primarily renal (75% in urine, mostly unchanged); half-life in RA is approximately 12.5 hours.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Rheumatoid Arthritis:** Evaluated in RA-BEGIN (MTX-naïve), RA-BEAM (MTX-IR), RA-BUILD (cDMARD-IR), and RA-BEACON (TNFi-IR). 4 mg dose showed superior ACR20/50/70 responses compared to placebo/MTX.
- **Atopic Dermatitis:** BREEZE-AD1, -AD2, and -AD7 demonstrated significant improvements in IGA 0/1 and EASI75 scores at 16 weeks.
- **Alopecia Areata:** BRAVE-AA1 and BRAVE-AA2 showed significantly higher proportions of patients achieving SALT ≤20 (≥80% scalp coverage) at week 36 with 2 mg and 4 mg doses compared to placebo.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- Store below 30°C in the original package to protect from moisture [Australian].
