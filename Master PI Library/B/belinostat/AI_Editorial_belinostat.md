# Editorial Product Information: Belinostat

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) provided by the following regulatory authority: **US (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical data into a single reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific PI. Clinicians must consult the local approved PI before prescribing or administering this medication in their specific region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Belinostat (histone deacetylase inhibitor).
- **Brand Name:** **Beleodaq** [US].
- **ATC Code:** L01XH03 (Not specified in source, but clinically standard).
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** For injection; 500 mg of lyophilised powder in a single-dose vial for reconstitution [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous infusion.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Acrotech Biopharma Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Peripheral T-cell Lymphoma (PTCL):** Treatment of adult patients with relapsed or refractory PTCL [US-approved].
  - *Note:* In the United States, this indication was granted under accelerated approval based on tumor response rate and duration. Continued approval may depend on verification of clinical benefit in confirmatory trials [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Adult Dosing
- **Standard Regimen:** 1,000 mg/m² administered via intravenous infusion over 30 minutes once daily on Days 1 through 5 of a 21-day cycle [US].
- **Duration:** Treatment continues until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity [US].

### Dosage Modifications for Toxicity
- **Hematologic Toxicities:** 
  - If nadir absolute neutrophil count (ANC) is <0.5 x 10⁹/L (any platelet count) or nadir platelet count is <25 x 10⁹/L (any ANC), reduce the dose by 25% (to 750 mg/m²) for subsequent cycles [US].
  - Prior to each cycle, ANC must be ≥1.0 x 10⁹/L and platelets must be ≥50 x 10⁹/L [US].
- **Non-Hematologic Toxicities:**
  - For any CTCAE Grade 3 or 4 adverse reaction, reduce the dose by 25% (750 mg/m²) [US].
  - Recurrence of Grade 3 or 4 reactions after two dose reductions requires permanent discontinuation [US].
  - *Note:* Dose modifications may not be necessary for nausea, vomiting, or diarrhea if managed by supportive care and lasting <7 days [US].

### Administration Instructions
- **Reconstitution:** Reconstitute each 500 mg vial with 9 mL of Sterile Water for Injection to achieve a concentration of 50 mg/mL [US].
- **Dilution:** Withdraw the required volume and add to 250 mL of 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection [US].
- **In-line Filter:** A 0.22-micron in-line filter must be used during administration [US].
- **Infusion Time:** 30 minutes. If infusion site pain occurs, the time may be extended to 45 minutes [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None specified in available sources [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hematologic Toxicity:** Belinostat can cause severe thrombocytopenia, leukopenia (neutropenia and lymphopenia), and anemia. Full blood counts (FBC) should be monitored at baseline and weekly during treatment [US].
- **Infection:** Serious and potentially fatal infections, including pneumonia and sepsis, have been reported. Treatment should not be initiated in patients with an active infection [US].
- **Hepatotoxicity:** Fatal hepatic failure and liver function test (LFT) abnormalities have occurred. LFTs must be monitored at baseline and before the start of each cycle [US].
- **Tumor Lysis Syndrome (TLS):** Reported in patients with advanced disease or high tumor burden. Patients should be monitored closely and appropriate precautions taken [US].
- **Gastrointestinal Toxicity:** Nausea, vomiting, and diarrhea are common and may require antiemetics and antidiarrheals [US].
- **Embryo-Fetal Toxicity:** Belinostat is genotoxic and can cause fetal harm [US].

### Serious Adverse Effects
- **Common (>2%):** Pneumonia, pyrexia, infection, anemia, increased creatinine, thrombocytopenia, and multi-organ failure [US].
- **Other:** One treatment-related death from hepatic failure and one from ventricular fibrillation (in a separate trial) have been reported [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **UGT1A1 Inhibitors:** Belinostat is primarily metabolised by UGT1A1. Concomitant use with strong UGT1A1 inhibitors (e.g., atazanavir) increases belinostat exposure. Avoid co-administration; if unavoidable, reduce the belinostat dose by 25% (e.g., from 1,000 mg/m² to 750 mg/m²) [US].
- **Warfarin:** No clinically significant pharmacokinetic interactions have been observed with R- or S-warfarin [US].
- **CYP Enzyme Inhibition:** In vitro data suggests belinostat and its metabolites inhibit CYP2C8 and CYP2C9 [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
  - **Category:** Not assigned a letter category in current US labelling, but classified as having embryo-fetal toxicity risk [US]. 
  - **Recommendation:** Advise pregnant women of the risk to the fetus. Females of reproductive potential must use effective contraception during treatment and for 6 months after the final dose [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** There are no data on the presence of belinostat in human milk. Breastfeeding is not recommended during treatment and for 2 weeks following the last dose [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies indicate belinostat may impair male fertility. Males with female partners of reproductive potential should use effective contraception during and for 3 months after treatment [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Response rates were higher in patients ≥65 years (36%) compared to those <65 years (16%) in clinical trials. No significant differences in safety were observed based on age [US].
- **Renal Impairment:**
  - **Mild (CLcr 60 to <90 mL/min):** No dosage adjustment required [US].
  - **Moderate (CLcr 30 to <60 mL/min):** Reduce starting dose to 500 mg/m² [US].
  - **Severe (CLcr <30 mL/min):** Avoid use due to lack of data [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:**
  - **Mild (Total bilirubin ≤1.5 x ULN):** No dosage adjustment required [US].
  - **Moderate (Total bilirubin >1.5 to 3 x ULN):** Reduce starting dose to 500 mg/m² [US].
  - **Severe (Total bilirubin >3 x ULN):** Avoid use [US].
- **UGT1A1*28 Polymorphism:** Patients known to be homozygous for the UGT1A1*28 allele should have the starting dose reduced to 750 mg/m² [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Histone deacetylase (HDAC) inhibitor. It inhibits the enzymatic activity of HDACs at nanomolar concentrations, leading to the accumulation of acetylated histones, induction of cell cycle arrest, and apoptosis in transformed cells [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
  - **Distribution:** Mean volume of distribution approaches total body water; protein binding is 92.9% to 95.8% [US].
  - **Metabolism:** Primarily metabolised by hepatic UGT1A1; minor metabolism via CYP2A6, CYP2C9, and CYP3A4 [US].
  - **Elimination:** Elimination half-life is approximately 1.1 hours. 84.8% of the dose is excreted in urine (mostly as metabolites, <2% unchanged) and 9.7% in feces [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pivotal Study:** An open-label, single-arm trial of 129 patients with relapsed or refractory PTCL.
- **Outcome:** The overall response rate (ORR) was 25.8% (n=31). The median duration of response was 8.4 months. 7.5% of patients were able to proceed to stem cell transplant following treatment [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vials:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F). Keep in the original package to protect from light [US].
- **Reconstituted Solution:** Stable for up to 12 hours at room temperature (15°C to 25°C) [US].
- **Diluted Infusion:** Stable for up to 36 hours at room temperature, including infusion time [US].
- **Handling:** Belinostat is a hazardous drug. Follow applicable special handling and disposal procedures [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources [US].
