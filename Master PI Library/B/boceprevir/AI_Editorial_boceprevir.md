# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
*   **Jurisdictions Contributed:** US (FDA).
*   **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of **levocarnitine** injection for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical data and regulatory guidance.
*   **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
*   **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule for fatty acid transport; amino acid derivative.
*   **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
*   **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
*   **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) [US].
*   **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency (Inborn Errors of Metabolism):** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
*   **Carnitine Deficiency in End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
*   **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg. It should be administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion. A loading dose is often required in severe metabolic crises, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours. Dosing frequency should be every 3 to 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours. Subsequent daily doses typically remain in the 50 mg/kg range but may be adjusted based on clinical requirements. The maximum reported dose is 300 mg/kg [US].
*   **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:** The recommended starting dose is 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight. It should be administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].

### Monitoring and Adjustments
*   **Metabolic Disorders:** It is recommended to obtain plasma carnitine concentrations prior to treatment. Weekly and monthly monitoring should include blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine levels: 35 to 60 µmol/L) [US].
*   **ESRD:** Therapy initiation may be prompted by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine levels below the normal range (40 to 50 µmol/L). Dose adjustments should be guided by trough levels; downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be performed as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].

### Administration Instructions
*   **Visual Inspection:** Inspect for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration.
*   **Compatibility:** Stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when diluted to concentrations between 0.5 mg/mL and 8.0 mg/mL in Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s [US].
*   **Missed Dose:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
*   None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
*   **Boxed Warnings:** None [US].
*   **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions may occur within minutes of injection. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue immediately and initiate medical treatment. Assess risks/benefits before re-administration [US].
*   **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
*   **Oral Levocarnitine in Renal Impairment:** The safety and efficacy of **oral** levocarnitine have not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in ESRD patients may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse events reported in clinical trials of ESRD patients on hemodialysis (incidence ≥5%, regardless of causality):

| Body System | Adverse Event (Incidence ≥5%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Chest pain, headache, injection site reaction, pain, fever, flu syndrome, asthenia, abdominal pain, accidental injury |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension, hypotension, tachycardia, palpitations, vascular disorder |
| **Digestive** | Nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, dyspepsia, anorexia, gastrointestinal disorder |
| **Metabolic/Nutritional**| Hypercalcaemia, hyperkalaemia, hypervolaemia, peripheral edema, weight changes |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness, insomnia, paresthesia, depression |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis, cough increase, dyspnea, rhinitis, bronchitis |
| **Musculoskeletal** | Leg cramps |
| **Skin** | Pruritus, rash |
| **Special Senses** | Amblyopia, eye disorder, taste perversion |
| **Urogenital** | Kidney failure, urinary tract infection |

*   **Other Noted Effects:** Body odor and gastritis have been reported less frequently. Seizures and hypersensitivity (anaphylaxis) are noted in post-marketing experience [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalised Ratio (INR) have occurred when levocarnitine is used concomitantly with warfarin. It is recommended to monitor INR levels at treatment initiation or after dose adjustments [US].
*   **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Levocarnitine is not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy:** [US: Pregnancy Category not explicitly lettered, but indicates "should be used during pregnancy only if clearly needed"]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at doses up to 3.8 times the human dose (based on surface area) revealed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. There are no adequate and well-controlled studies in pregnant women [US].
*   **Breastfeeding:** Not specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk carnitine concentrations following exogenous administration. Risks to the infant from excess carnitine must be weighed against maternal benefits. Consideration may be given to discontinuing nursing or the drug [US].
*   **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders is weight-based (50 mg/kg) [US].
*   **Elderly Use:** No specific dose adjustments provided; clinical studies did not determine if elderly patients respond differently from younger subjects [US].
*   **Renal Impairment:** Used specifically in ESRD patients on hemodialysis (see Section 3). High-dose oral formulations are cautioned against due to metabolite accumulation [US].
*   **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
*   **Mechanism of Action:** A naturally occurring carrier molecule required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria for oxidation and energy production. In patients with metabolic defects, it promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters that are rapidly excreted [US].
*   **Pharmacokinetics:**
    *   **Distribution:** Not protein-bound; distribution half-life of 0.585 hours [US].
    *   **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    *   **Elimination:** Following IV bolus (20 mg/kg), ~76% of the dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours. The mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of ESRD patients (n=12), 20 mg/kg IV three times weekly post-dialysis increased mean trough plasma concentrations from 21.3 µmol/L at baseline to 190 µmol/L at 8 weeks. Similar increases were seen in 12- and 24-week studies.
*   **Clinical Outcomes:** While IV levocarnitine increases carnitine concentrations in ESRD patients, the effect on clinical outcomes (e.g., malaise, muscle weakness) has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
*   **Handling:** Protect from freezing. Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as it contains no preservatives [US].
