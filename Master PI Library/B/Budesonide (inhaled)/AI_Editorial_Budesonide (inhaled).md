# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine Injection

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**, specifically the labeling for **Levocarnitine Injection, USP** manufactured by Baxter Healthcare Corporation.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, consolidating clinical and regulatory data into a single reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific PI. Clinicians must consult local regulations and current official labeling before prescribing or administering this medication in their specific region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP).
- **Chemical Class:** (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt; a naturally occurring carrier molecule required for mammalian energy metabolism.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01 (Levocarnitine).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL) [US]. 
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency (Inborn Errors):** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that result in secondary carnitine deficiency [US-approved].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing hemodialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Administration:** Administer as a slow bolus injection over 2 to 3 minutes or by infusion [US].

### Indication-Specific Dosing
- **Metabolic Disorders:**
    *   **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg [US].
    *   **Loading Dose:** Often required in severe metabolic crises, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours.
    *   **Frequency:** Administer every 3 or 4 hours; frequency should never be less than every 6 hours.
    *   **Maximum Dose:** The highest administered dose reported is 300 mg/kg [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    *   **Recommended Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight.
    *   **Timing:** Administer into the venous return line after each dialysis session [US].
    *   **Dose Adjustments:** Guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Reductions (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].

### Monitoring and Titration
- Obtain baseline plasma carnitine concentrations before initiating parenteral therapy.
- Weekly and monthly monitoring is recommended, including blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma carnitine levels (target free carnitine concentration: 35 to 60 µmol/L) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. These may occur within minutes of administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and initiate medical management [US].
- **Seizures:** Levocarnitine (oral or IV) has been associated with seizures in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been reported [US].
- **Laboratory Monitoring:** Monitor International Normalized Ratio (INR) in patients receiving concomitant warfarin therapy [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Very Common (>10%):**
    *   Injection site reactions (33–38%) [US].
    *   Hypertension (18–21%) [US].
    *   Nausea and vomiting (incidence difficult to estimate due to underlying pathology) [US].
    *   Dizziness (10–18%) [US].
    *   Headache (up to 37% in 20 mg/kg cohorts) [US].
- **Common (1–10%):**
    *   Body odor, gastritis, abdominal pain, diarrhea [US].
    *   Cardiovascular: Arrhythmia, palpitations, tachycardia, atrial fibrillation [US].
    *   Metabolic: Hypercalcemia, hyperkalemia, hypervolemia [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** 
    *   Seizures [US].
    *   Anaphylaxis and severe respiratory distress (bronchospasm/laryngeal edema) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in INR when used with levocarnitine. **Management:** Monitor INR levels after initiating treatment or following dose adjustments [US].
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    *   **US Classification:** Not explicitly assigned a lettered category in provided text, but reproductive studies in rats/rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm.
    *   **Recommendation:** Should be used during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows showed increased carnitine concentrations in milk after exogenous administration. **Management:** Weigh the risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant against the maternal benefit [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility was found in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders and ESRD is based on mg/kg weight, applicable to pediatric populations [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    *   **IV Administration:** Indicated for use in ESRD patients on dialysis.
    *   **Oral Note:** The safety and efficacy of *oral* levocarnitine have not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency. High doses of oral formulations in ESRD may lead to accumulation of toxic metabolites (TMA and TMAO) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids in patients with metabolic defects by forming acylcarnitine esters which are rapidly excreted [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    *   **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin. Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours [US].
    *   **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and $\gamma$-butyrobetaine (feces) [US].
    *   **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. Terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Study:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients (20 mg/kg 3x weekly post-dialysis), trough levels increased from a baseline of ~21 µmol/L to 190 µmol/L at 8 weeks [US].
- **Dose-Ranging Studies:** Trials using 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg showed dose-proportional increases in carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks. While carnitine levels increased, the effect on clinical outcomes in the ESRD population has not been determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Avoid excessive heat; protect from freezing.
- **Stability:** Once opened, discard any unused portion as the formulation contains no preservative. Compatible with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s for up to 24 hours at room temperature in PVC bags [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as Rx-only (Human Prescription Drug) [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources for other jurisdictions.
