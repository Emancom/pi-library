## 3. Approved Indications
- Temporarily relieves itching. [US]

## 4. Dosage and Administration

### General dosing (adult and older children)
- Adults and children 12 years of age and older: a small fingertip-sized amount,
  equivalent to approximately one inch in length, should be applied to the affected
  area up to three to four times per day. [US]

### Paediatric dosing
- Children under 12 years of age: a doctor should be consulted before use. [US]

### Administration instructions
- For topical application to affected external skin areas only.
- Eye contact must be avoided; should contact occur, the affected eye(s) should be
  rinsed thoroughly and immediately with water. [US]
- After each application, the tube nozzle should be wiped clean before the cap is
  replaced, and the cap should be kept firmly closed between uses. [US]

## 5. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity or allergy to local anesthetics of the caine type — for
  example, procaine, butacaine, benzocaine, or other -caine anesthetics: do not use.
  [US]
- Other absolute contraindications: Not specified in available sources

## 6. Warnings and Precautions

### Boxed warnings
- None specified in available sources

### General warnings and precautions
- For external use only. [US]
- Allergy alert: this product should not be used by individuals with a history of
  allergy to local anesthetics such as procaine, butacaine, benzocaine, or other
  -caine anesthetics. [US]
- Eye contact should be avoided; if contact occurs, the eye(s) must be rinsed
  thoroughly and immediately with water. [US]
- Discontinue use and consult a doctor if the condition deteriorates, if symptoms
  continue beyond 7 days, or if symptoms resolve but recur within a few days. [US]
- Keep out of reach of children. In the event of ingestion, medical help should be
  sought or a Poison Control Center contacted immediately. [US]

### Monitoring requirements
- No specific monitoring parameters are specified in available sources beyond clinical
  reassessment if symptoms persist or worsen. [US]

### Specific clinical precautions
- Ocular exposure must be avoided. [US]
- Use by individuals with a known sensitivity to local anesthetics of the caine class
  should be avoided, as described in Section 5 (Contraindications). [US]
