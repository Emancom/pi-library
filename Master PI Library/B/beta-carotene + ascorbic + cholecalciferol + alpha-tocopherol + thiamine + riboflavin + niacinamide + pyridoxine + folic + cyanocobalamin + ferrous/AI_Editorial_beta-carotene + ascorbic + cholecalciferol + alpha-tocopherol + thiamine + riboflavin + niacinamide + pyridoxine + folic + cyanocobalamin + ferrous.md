## 6. Warnings and Precautions
### Prominent safety warning
- Iron-containing products have been identified as a leading cause of fatal 
  accidental poisoning in children younger than 6 years of age. This product 
  must be kept out of the reach of children at all times. In the event of 
  accidental ingestion or suspected overdose, a doctor or poison control center 
  should be contacted immediately.

### General warnings and precautions
- Folic acid by itself is not an appropriate therapeutic approach for the 
  management of pernicious anemia or other megaloblastic anemias arising from 
  vitamin B12 deficiency.
- When folic acid is administered at doses exceeding 0.1 mg per day, it may 
  mask the underlying diagnosis of pernicious anemia by producing hematologic 
  improvement, even as neurological deterioration continues.
- Use with caution in patients who have underlying medical conditions; in 
  patients who are pregnant, lactating, or trying to conceive; in patients under 
  18 years of age; and in patients taking other medications.
- This product is contraindicated in patients with known hypersensitivity to 
  any of the ingredients.

### Monitoring requirements
- Specific laboratory or clinical monitoring recommendations are not detailed 
  in the available sources. Clinicians should be alert to the potential for 
  folic acid supplementation to obscure vitamin B12 deficiency and should 
  monitor for signs of iron overdose in children.

### Specific clinical precautions
- Patients or caregivers should consult a physician immediately if any adverse 
  side effects occur.
- The product must be kept out of the reach of children to prevent accidental 
  iron ingestion.
