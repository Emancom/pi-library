# Editorial Product Information: Bremelanotide

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of bremelanotide, synthesised from official Product Information (PI) for use by healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This synthesis is an editorial reference and does not replace the official PI required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. At the time of this publication, only the United States (FDA) PI was provided for review. Information regarding other jurisdictions (e.g., Australian, European, Canadian) was not specified in the available source.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Bremelanotide (as bremelanotide acetate).
- **Chemical Class:** Melanocortin receptor (MCR) agonist; synthetic cyclic heptapeptide.
- **Brand Name(s):** **Vyleesi** [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - **Subcutaneous Injection:** 1.75 mg/0.3 mL clear solution in a single-dose prefilled autoinjector [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Subcutaneous.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Cosette Pharmaceuticals, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Hypoactive Sexual Desire Disorder (HSDD):** Indicated for the treatment of premenopausal women with acquired, generalized HSDD characterized by low sexual desire causing significant distress or interpersonal conflict.
    - **Diagnostic Criteria:** The low desire must not be due to a co-existing psychiatric/medical condition, relationship issues, or the effects of other medications/substances [US].
- **Limitations of Use [US]:**
    - Not indicated for postmenopausal women or men.
    - Not indicated for the enhancement of sexual performance.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult Premenopausal Women):** 1.75 mg administered subcutaneously as needed [US].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Inject into the abdomen or thigh at least 45 minutes before anticipated sexual activity [US].
    - Optimal timing may vary based on individual experience regarding duration of effect and onset of adverse reactions (e.g., nausea) [US].
- **Dosing Limits [US]:**
    - **Maximum Frequency:** Do not exceed one dose within a 24-hour period.
    - **Monthly Maximum:** No more than 8 doses per month is recommended.
- **Discontinuation:** Therapy should be ceased after 8 weeks if the patient reports no improvement in symptoms [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Uncontrolled Hypertension:** [US].
- **Known Cardiovascular Disease:** [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Transient Cardiovascular Effects:** Each dose induces a transient increase in blood pressure (average increase 6 mmHg systolic / 3 mmHg diastolic) and a decrease in heart rate (up to 5 bpm). These effects typically peak 2–4 hours post-dose and resolve within 12 hours. Cardiovascular risk should be assessed prior to initiation and periodically during treatment [US].
- **Focal Hyperpigmentation:** Reported in 1% of patients (up to 8 doses/month), involving the face, gingiva, and breasts. Risk is higher in patients with darker skin and increases with daily use. Discontinuation should be considered if pigmentation develops, as it may not be reversible [US].
- **Nausea:** Very common (40% of patients). While usually transient and resolving within 2 hours, it may require anti-emetic therapy or lead to discontinuation. Nausea is most common with the first dose [US].
- **Gastric Emptying Delay:** Bremelanotide may slow gastric motility, potentially affecting the absorption of concomitant oral medications [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions are based on Phase 3 clinical trial data (n=627) [US].

| Frequency | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Nausea (40%), Flushing (20.3%), Injection site reactions (13.2%), Headache (11.3%) |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Vomiting (4.8%), Cough (3.3%), Fatigue (3.2%), Hot flush (2.7%), Paraesthesia (2.6%), Dizziness (2.2%), Nasal congestion (2.1%) |
| **Uncommon (<2%)** | Upper abdominal pain, diarrhea, myalgia, arthralgia, restless leg syndrome, rhinorrhea, increased creatine phosphokinase, focal hyperpigmentation |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:**
    - **Acute Hepatitis:** A single case was reported in an open-label study; while causality was not definitively established, transaminases exceeded 40x ULN [US].
    - **Intractable Headache:** Leading to hospitalization in rare cases [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Naltrexone (Oral):** **Contraindicated Interaction.** Bremelanotide significantly decreases systemic exposure of oral naltrexone. Avoid use in patients taking naltrexone for alcohol or opioid addiction due to the risk of treatment failure [US].
- **Slowed Gastric Emptying:** May reduce the rate and extent of absorption of oral drugs. 
    - **Clinical Management:** Avoid use when taking oral drugs dependent on threshold concentrations for efficacy (e.g., antibiotics). Use caution with drugs requiring rapid onset (e.g., indomethacin for pain relief) [US].
- **Alcohol:** No clinically significant pharmacokinetic interaction or potentiation of orthostatic hypotension was observed in specific interaction studies [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Risk Summary:** Insufficient human data to determine drug-associated risks. Animal studies (dogs) showed fetal harm at 16x human exposure.
    - **Recommendation:** Advise patients to use effective contraception and discontinue bremelanotide immediately if pregnancy is suspected [US]. 
    - **Pregnancy Registry:** A registry is available in the US to monitor outcomes.
- **Breastfeeding:** No data available regarding the presence of the drug in human milk, effects on the infant, or effects on milk production. The developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding should be weighed against the clinical need for the drug [US].
- **Fertility:** No effects on fertility were observed in animal studies at high multiples of human exposure [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established in geriatric patients [US].
- **Renal Impairment:**
    - **Mild to Moderate (eGFR 30–89 mL/min/1.73 m²):** No dosage adjustment required.
    - **Severe (eGFR <30 mL/min/1.73 m²):** Increase in AUC (2-fold) and Cmax observed. Use with caution due to increased risk of adverse reactions (nausea/vomiting) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:**
    - **Mild to Moderate (Child-Pugh A and B):** No dosage adjustment required.
    - **Severe (Child-Pugh C):** Not studied. Use with caution as exposure may be increased, potentially raising the incidence of adverse effects [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Non-selective agonist of melanocortin receptors (MCR). Potency order: MC1R > MC4R > MC3R > MC5R > MC2R. MC4R is involved in CNS sexual response pathways; MC1R activation on melanocytes mediates hyperpigmentation [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics [US]:**
    - **Absorption:** Absolute bioavailability ~100%; Median $T_{max}$ ~1 hour.
    - **Distribution:** 21% bound to human serum protein; $V_d$ is 25.0 ± 5.8 L.
    - **Metabolism:** Hydrolysis of the peptide amide bonds.
    - **Elimination:** Mean terminal half-life ~2.7 hours; Clearance 6.5 ± 1.0 L/hr.
    - **Excretion:** 64.8% in urine; 22.8% in feces.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pivotal Studies:** Two identical 24-week, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trials (n=1247) in premenopausal women with HSDD [US].
- **Primary Outcomes:**
    - Significant increase in the FSFI (Female Sexual Function Index) Desire Domain score compared to placebo.
    - Significant reduction in the FSDS-DAO (Female Sexual Distress Scale) Question 13 score (bothered by low desire) compared to placebo.
- **Secondary Outcomes:** No significant difference compared to placebo in the number of satisfying sexual events (SSEs) [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at or below 25°C (77°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Do not freeze. Protect from light [US].
- **Shelf Life:** Not specified in available sources, refer to expiration date on packaging.
