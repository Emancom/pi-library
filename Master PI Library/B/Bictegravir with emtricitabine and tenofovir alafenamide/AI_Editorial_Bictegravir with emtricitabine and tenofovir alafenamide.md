# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine Injection

## Editorial Note
*   The following jurisdictions’ Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial:
    *   United States (FDA): Levocarnitine Injection, USP (Baxter Healthcare Corporation).
*   **Note on Document Discrepancy:** While the user instructions referenced a document for Bictegravir/Emtricitabine/Tenofovir Alafenamide, the actual file provided and analyzed for this editorial is the official US PI for **Levocarnitine Injection**.
*   **Purpose:** This document is a harmonized editorial overview intended for use by healthcare professionals.
*   **Disclaimer:** This text is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
*   **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
*   **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection, USP [US].
*   **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
*   **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    *   Sterile solution for injection: 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) single-dose vials [US].
*   **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) use only.
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency (Metabolic):** For the management of acute and chronic treatment in patients with inborn errors of metabolism that result in secondary carnitine deficiency [US-approved].
*   **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance dialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Pediatric)
*   **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg. 
    *   **Method:** Administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion.
    *   **Severe Crisis:** A loading dose is often administered, followed by equivalent doses every 3 to 4 hours (never less than every 6 hours) over the first 24 hours. 
    *   **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses are typically 50 mg/kg, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilized based on clinical requirements [US].
*   **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:** 
    *   **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight.
    *   **Method:** Administered as a slow 2 to 3 minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
    *   **Adjustments:** Dose reductions (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy, guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations [US].

### Administration Instructions
*   Parenteral products must be visually inspected for particulate matter and discoloration prior to use.
*   **Compatibility:** Compatible and stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s in concentrations ranging from 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL [US].

### Monitoring
*   Plasma carnitine concentrations should be obtained before starting therapy.
*   Weekly and monthly monitoring is recommended, including blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma concentrations (target free carnitine level: 35 to 60 µmol/L) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
*   None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
*   **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Some reactions occur within minutes of IV administration. If a severe reaction occurs, the drug must be discontinued and appropriate medical management initiated [US].
*   **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without a history of seizure disorders. In patients with pre-existing seizure activity, an increase in the frequency or severity of seizures has been observed [US].
*   **Oral Levocarnitine Caution:** The safety/efficacy of *oral* levocarnitine has not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency. High doses of oral formulations in ESRD patients may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions are often confounded by the underlying pathology of metabolic disorders or ESRD.
*   **Common (>10%):** Transient nausea and vomiting. 
*   **ESRD-Specific (reported at ≥5% regardless of causality) [US]:**
    *   **General:** Injection site reaction (33%), pain (30%), headache (22%), flu syndrome (25%), infection (15%), chest pain (12%).
    *   **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (20%), hypotension (14%), tachycardia (6%).
    *   **Digestive:** Diarrhea (16%), vomiting (15%), nausea (8%), abdominal pain (9%).
    *   **Metabolic:** Hypercalcemia (9%), hyperkalemia (6%).
    *   **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (23%), cough increase (9%), dyspnea (7%).
*   **Post-marketing Experience:** Body odor and gastritis have been reported [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have occurred when levocarnitine is used concomitantly with warfarin. 
*   **Management:** Monitor INR levels closely after initiating levocarnitine or following dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin anticoagulation [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy:** 
    *   **US Category:** Not specified by letter, but reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (based on surface area) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. 
    *   **Recommendation:** Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed, as animal studies are not always predictive of human response [US].
*   **Breastfeeding:** 
    *   Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. 
    *   Studies in dairy cows indicate increased carnitine concentrations in milk following exogenous administration. 
    *   **Recommendation:** Weigh the risks to the infant of excess carnitine intake against the benefits of supplementation to the mother [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders is calculated by weight (50 mg/kg); see Section 3 [US].
*   **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in provided sources; clinical monitoring is advised as per standard protocols.
*   **Renal Impairment:** Dosing for ESRD on hemodialysis is specific (10-20 mg/kg post-dialysis). Caution is advised regarding the accumulation of metabolites if oral formulations are used in patients with severe renal compromise [US].
*   **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
*   **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring carrier molecule required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
*   **Pharmacokinetics:**
    *   **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
    *   **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    *   **Elimination:** Primarily renal; approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. Terminal elimination half-life is approximately 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   **ESRD Pharmacokinetic Study:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients, IV levocarnitine (20 mg/kg three times weekly after dialysis) increased mean trough plasma concentrations from 21.3 µmol/L at baseline to 190 µmol/L after 8 weeks.
*   **Clinical Outcomes Note:** While efficacy in increasing carnitine plasma concentrations has been demonstrated, the effects of supplemental carnitine on clinical outcomes or specific signs/symptoms of carnitine deficiency in ESRD patients have not been determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
*   **Precautions:** Avoid excessive heat and protect from freezing.
*   **Handling:** Vials are single-dose and contain no preservatives; discard any unused portion [US].

*   **United States:** Classified as Rx-only.
*   **Other Jurisdictions:** Not specified in available sources.
