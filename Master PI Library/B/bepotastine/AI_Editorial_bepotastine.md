# Editorial Product Information: Bepotastine Besilate

## Editorial Note
*   **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA).
*   **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of bepotastine besilate ophthalmic solution for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises clinical and regulatory information from the contributing jurisdictions listed above.
*   **Disclaimer:** This editorial synthesis is intended for information purposes and is not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredient:** Bepotastine besilate, a histamine H1 receptor antagonist.
*   **Brand Name(s):** Not specifically named in the source document (Mylan generic).
*   **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    *   Ophthalmic solution: 1.5% (15 mg/mL) [US].
*   **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical ophthalmic.
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Mylan Pharmaceuticals Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Allergic Conjunctivitis:** Treatment of ocular itching associated with the signs and symptoms of allergic conjunctivitis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
*   **General Dosing (Adult):** Instill one drop into each affected eye two times per day [US].
*   **Administration Instructions:**
    *   Remove contact lenses before instilling the solution [US].
    *   Avoid touching the dropper tip to the eyelids, surrounding areas, or any other surface to prevent contamination [US].
    *   Maintain the bottle tightly closed when not in use [US].
*   **Missed Dose Instructions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
*   **Hypersensitivity:** History of hypersensitivity to bepotastine or any component of the formulation [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
*   **Boxed Warnings:** None [US].
*   **Contamination of Tip and Solution:** Patients must be instructed to avoid contact between the dropper tip and any surface, including the eye or eyelids, to maintain sterility [US].
*   **Contact Lens Wear:**
    *   This solution should not be used to treat irritation related to contact lens wear [US].
    *   The preservative benzalkonium chloride may be absorbed by soft contact lenses. Lenses may be reinserted 10 minutes after administration, provided the eye is not red [US].
*   **Monitoring Requirements:** Not specified in available sources.

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reaction data is primarily derived from clinical trials involving 861 subjects over 6 weeks and two conjunctival allergen challenge (CAC) studies.

| Frequency | Adverse Effect |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Mild taste following instillation (approx. 25%) |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Eye irritation, headache, nasopharyngitis |
| **Rare (<0.1%) / Post-marketing** | Hypersensitivity reactions (itching, body rash, swelling of lips, tongue, and/or throat) |

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Bepotastine is minimally metabolised by CYP450 isozymes. In vitro studies indicate that bepotastine does not inhibit CYP3A4, CYP2C9, or CYP2C19. It has a low potential for drug interactions via these pathways [US].
*   **Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Management Notes:** The effect of bepotastine on substrates of CYP1A2, CYP2C8, and CYP2D6 has not been studied [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy:** 
    *   **US Classification:** There are no available human data regarding the use of bepotastine besilate in pregnant women to inform drug-associated risks [US].
    *   **Animal Data:** Oral administration in rats and rabbits during organogenesis showed no teratogenic effects at doses up to 3300 times (rats) and 5400 times (rabbits) the maximum recommended human ophthalmic dose (RHOD). However, skeletal anomalies were observed in rats at 5400 times the RHOD, a dose that also caused maternal toxicity [US].
*   **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if bepotastine is excreted in human milk following topical ocular administration. Animal data showed that after oral administration, bepotastine concentrations in rat milk were higher than maternal plasma concentrations. The developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding should be weighed against the clinical need and potential adverse effects on the infant [US].
*   **Fertility:** In animal studies, high oral doses (5400 times RHOD) resulted in a reduced fertility index in both male and female rats [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use:** 
    *   Safety and efficacy have not been established in children under 2 years of age [US].
    *   Efficacy in children aged 2 to 10 years is extrapolated from data in older children and adults [US].
*   **Elderly Use:** No overall differences in safety or effectiveness have been observed between geriatric and younger patients [US].
*   **Renal Impairment:** Not specified in available sources (though bepotastine is primarily excreted unchanged in the urine) [US].
*   **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
*   **Mechanism of Action:** Bepotastine is a topically active, direct H1-receptor antagonist and an inhibitor of histamine release from mast cells [US].
*   **Pharmacokinetics:** 
    *   **Absorption:** Peak plasma concentrations occur 1 to 2 hours post-instillation. Systemic exposure is low; after QID dosing for seven days, plasma levels were generally below the limit of quantitation (2 ng/mL) at 24 hours [US].
    *   **Distribution:** Approximately 55% protein bound [US].
    *   **Metabolism:** Minimal metabolism by CYP450 isozymes [US].
    *   **Elimination:** Primarily renal, with 75% to 90% excreted unchanged in the urine [US].
*   **Pharmacodynamics:** Not specified in available sources beyond H1-receptor antagonism.

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   **Efficacy:** Evaluated in two conjunctival allergen challenge (CAC) studies (n=237). Bepotastine 1.5% was significantly more effective than vehicle in relieving ocular itching at 15 minutes and 8 hours post-dose [US].
*   **Safety:** Evaluated in a 6-week randomised study of 861 subjects [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   **Temperature:** Store at 15° to 25°C (59° to 77°F) [US].
*   **Shelf Life:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Handling:** Product should be dispensed in the original unopened container [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
*   **Legal Classification:** 
    *   **US:** Rx-only (Prescription Only).
