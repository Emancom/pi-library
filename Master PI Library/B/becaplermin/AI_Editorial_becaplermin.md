# Editorial Product Information: Becaplermin

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA)
- **Purpose:** This document is a harmonised editorial overview of becaplermin product information, synthesized for use by healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This synthesis is an editorial summary and does not replace the official Product Information required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. The information provided is based solely on the source material listed in the references.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Becaplermin.
- **Chemical Class:** Recombinant human platelet-derived growth factor (rhPDGF-BB).
- **Brand Name(s):** **REGRANEX** [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Topical gel, 0.01% (each gram contains 100 mcg of becaplermin) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** LRM Therapeutics, LLC [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Diabetic Neuropathic Ulcers:** Indicated for the treatment of lower extremity neuropathic ulcers in patients with diabetes mellitus. Specifically, ulcers must extend into the subcutaneous tissue or deeper (Stage III or IV) and possess an adequate blood supply [US].
- **Clinical Context:** This agent is intended as an adjunct to, and not a substitute for, standard ulcer care, including sharp debridement, pressure relief (off-loading), and infection control [US].

**Limitations of Use [US]:**
- Efficacy is not established for pressure ulcers or venous stasis ulcers.
- Safety and efficacy have not been evaluated for wounds closing by primary intention.
- Data are unavailable regarding application to exposed bone, tendons, ligaments, or joints.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
Applied once daily to the ulcer site. The gel should be applied and left in place for approximately 12 hours, then rinsed off with saline or water. The wound should be covered with a moist dressing for the remainder of the day [US].

### Calculation of Dose [US]
The length of gel to be applied is determined by the size of the ulcer area. The amount should be recalculated weekly or biweekly by a healthcare provider.
- **Using Inches:** Ulcer length (in) $\times$ ulcer width (in) $\times$ 0.6 = length of gel (in) from a 15 g tube.
- **Using Centimeters:** Ulcer length (cm) $\times$ ulcer width (cm) $\div$ 4 = length of gel (cm) from a 15 g tube.
- **Application Thickness:** The gel should be spread in a thin, continuous layer approximately 1/16th of an inch thick.

### Treatment Duration
Treatment should continue until complete healing. If the ulcer does not decrease in size by approximately 30% after 10 weeks, or if complete healing is not achieved by 20 weeks, the continuation of therapy should be reassessed [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Known Malignancy:** Contraindicated in patients with known neoplasms at the site(s) of application [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Risk of Cancer:** Becaplermin is a growth factor that promotes cellular proliferation and angiogenesis. Malignancies distant from the application site have been reported in clinical and post-marketing settings. Prescribers should carefully weigh the benefits and risks when considering treatment for patients with any known malignancy [US].
- **Application Site Reactions:** If sensitization or irritation occurs, clinicians should consider the possibility of a reaction to the preservatives (parabens or m-cresol). Interruption or discontinuation may be necessary [US].
- **Administration Cautions:** Not for ophthalmic, oral, or intravaginal use [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common (1–10%)
- **Skin and Subcutaneous Tissue:** Erythematous rashes occurred in 2% of patients in clinical trials [US].

### Post-marketing and Other Reports
- **Application Site:** Burning sensation and erythema [US].
- **Systemic Malignancy:** An increased rate of death from systemic malignancies was observed in a retrospective study among patients who used 3 or more tubes of the gel [US].
- **Immunogenicity:** In clinical trials, subjects did not develop neutralizing antibodies against becaplermin [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Topical Medications:** It is unknown if becaplermin interacts with other topical medications. The concurrent use of becaplermin with other topical drugs at the ulcer site has not been studied [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Data are unavailable regarding use in pregnant women to inform drug-associated risks. Animal reproduction studies have not been conducted [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** There are no data on the presence of becaplermin in human milk or its effects on milk production/the breastfed infant. The developmental benefits of breastfeeding should be considered alongside the clinical need for the drug [US].
- **Fertility:** Carcinogenesis and reproductive toxicity studies have not been conducted [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness in patients under 16 years of age have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical studies included 150 subjects aged 65 and older. No overall differences in safety or efficacy were observed between these patients and younger subjects, though data for those aged 75 and older are limited [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Becaplermin is a recombinant human platelet-derived growth factor. It possesses biological activity similar to endogenous PDGF, promoting the chemotactic recruitment and proliferation of cells essential for wound repair and the formation of granulation tissue [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Systemic absorption is minimal. In a study of patients with Stage III or IV ulcers receiving daily applications for 14 days, PDGF levels remained non-quantifiable or did not increase substantially above baseline in most subjects [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Efficacy in Diabetic Ulcers:** Efficacy was established in four randomized controlled trials. In Study 2, **REGRANEX** 0.01% achieved a 50% incidence of complete ulcer closure within 20 weeks, compared to 35% for the placebo gel (p=0.01) [US].
- **Lack of Efficacy in Other Ulcers:** Studies in pressure ulcers and venous stasis ulcers failed to demonstrate a statistically significant difference in healing rates compared to vehicle/standard care [US].
- **Long-term Safety:** Retrospective observational studies have examined the relationship between becaplermin use and cancer development. One study indicated a five-fold increased risk of cancer mortality in patients using three or more tubes, although other larger database studies (e.g., Veterans Affairs) did not confirm an increased hazard ratio for cancer mortality [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Do not freeze. Do not use after the expiration date [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Prescription Only (Rx-only) [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources.
