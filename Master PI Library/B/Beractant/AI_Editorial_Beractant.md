# Editorial Product Information: Beractant

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following regulatory authority: **Australian (TGA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of beractant for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises clinical data, administration protocols, and safety information into a single reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official, current Product Information required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory guidelines before administration.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Beractant (a natural bovine lung extract).
- **Chemical Class:** Pulmonary surfactant.
- **Brand Name:** **Survanta** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** R07AA02.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Intratracheal suspension. Each mL contains 25 mg of phospholipids (200 mg total phospholipids per 8 mL vial), suspended in 0.9% sodium chloride [Australian].
- **Composition:** Natural bovine lung extract containing phospholipids, neutral lipids, fatty acids, and surfactant-associated proteins (SP-B and SP-C). It is fortified with colfosceril palmitate, palmitic acid, and tripalmitin to standardise surface-tension-lowering properties [Australian].
- **Route of Administration:** Intratracheal use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** AbbVie Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Prevention of Respiratory Distress Syndrome (RDS):** For use in premature infants with a birth weight less than 1250 g, or those with evidence of surfactant deficiency. Administration should occur as soon as possible, preferably within 15 minutes of birth [Australian].
- **Rescue Treatment of RDS:** For use in infants with RDS confirmed by X-ray who require mechanical ventilation. Administration should occur as soon as possible, preferably by 8 hours of age [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Dose:** 100 mg of phospholipids per kg of birth weight (equivalent to 4 mL/kg) [Australian].
- **Maximum Frequency:** Up to four doses may be administered within the first 48 hours of life, given no more frequently than every 6 hours [Australian].
- **Redosing Criteria:** Required if the infant remains intubated and requires at least 30% inspired oxygen to maintain a $PaO_2 \le 80$ torr. Confirm RDS via X-ray before redosing infants who received a prevention dose [Australian].

### Preparation
- **Inspection:** Inspect for discolouration (normal colour is off-white to light brown).
- **Redispersion:** If settling occurs, swirl the vial gently; **DO NOT SHAKE**. Foaming is inherent to the product [Australian].
- **Warming:** Before use, warm the vial by standing at room temperature for at least 20 minutes or in the hand for at least 8 minutes. **Do not use artificial warming methods** [Australian].
- **Handling:** Single-use only. Discard unused portions. Unopened vials warmed to room temperature once may be returned to the refrigerator within 8 hours [Australian].

### Administration Instructions
Beractant must be administered by clinicians experienced in neonatal intubation and ventilator management.
- **Method A (Disconnection from Ventilator):** Instillation via a 5 French end-hole catheter inserted into the endotracheal tube (ETT). The dose is divided into four quarter-doses, each administered with the infant in a different position (head/body inclined down/up and turned right/left) [Australian].
- **Method B (Continuous Ventilation):** Instillation via a neonatal suction valve or secondary lumen of a double-lumen ETT. The dose can be given in four quarter-doses or two half-doses (head/body turned 45° right, then left) without disconnecting the ventilator [Australian].
- **Method C (Brief Disconnection):** Instillation via a 5 French catheter with the ETT briefly disconnected, using two half-dose positions [Australian].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known at the time of publication [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Specialist Setting:** Use must be restricted to highly supervised clinical settings with immediate availability of clinicians experienced in neonatal resuscitation and ventilation [Australian].
- **Rapid Clinical Change:** Beractant can rapidly improve oxygenation and lung compliance. Frequent monitoring of systemic oxygen and carbon dioxide is essential to avoid hyperoxia [Australian].
- **Dosing-Related Events:** Transient bradycardia and oxygen desaturation have been reported during administration. If these occur, stop the procedure until the infant is stabilised [Australian].
- **Airway Obstruction:** Rales and moist breath sounds are common post-administration. Endotracheal suctioning is not recommended for 1 hour post-dose unless signs of airway obstruction occur [Australian].
- **Infection:** An increased probability of post-treatment nosocomial sepsis was observed in clinical trials, though not associated with increased mortality [Australian].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Procedure-Related Effects
- **Very Common (>10%):** Transient bradycardia (11.9% of doses) [Australian].
- **Common (1–10%):** Oxygen desaturation (9.8% of doses) [Australian].
- **Rare (<1%):** Endotracheal tube reflux, pallor, vasoconstriction, hypotension, ETT blockage, hypertension, hypocarbia, hypercarbia, and apnoea [Australian].

### Concurrent Illnesses in Premature Infants
The following were observed in clinical trials at rates similar to control groups (unless noted):
- **Patent ductus arteriosus:** ~47%
- **Intracranial haemorrhage:** ~48% (Note: higher rates were seen in specific single-dose and multiple-dose sub-studies) [Australian].
- **Pulmonary air leaks:** ~11% (Significantly lower than control 24.7%) [Australian].
- **Post-treatment sepsis:** 20.7% (Control 16.1%, p=0.019) [Australian].
- **Apnoea:** ~65%
- **Pulmonary haemorrhage:** 7.2%

## 7. Drug Interactions
- No data available on specific drug-drug interactions.
- No effect was observed on common laboratory tests (WBC count, sodium, potassium, bilirubin, creatinine) in clinical trials [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Not applicable; indicated for use in premature infants. No data available on use in pregnant women [Australian].
- **Breastfeeding:** Not applicable; no data available on use in breastfeeding women [Australian].
- **Fertility:** Animal reproduction studies have not been conducted [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy have been evaluated in infants with birth weights ranging from 600 g to 1750 g. Use in infants <600 g or >1750 g has not been evaluated in controlled trials [Australian].
- **Elderly:** Not indicated for this population; no data available [Australian].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Beractant replenishes surfactant in premature infants with RDS. It lowers surface tension on alveolar surfaces during respiration and stabilises the alveoli against collapse at resting transpulmonary pressures [Australian].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In vitro, it lowers surface tension to <8 dynes/cm. In vivo, it improves lung compliance and oxygenation in animal models [Australian].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Human metabolic data is unavailable. Animal models (sheep/rabbit) indicate that components enter endogenous surfactant metabolic pathways and are recycled. Clearance from the alveolar space occurs in two phases, with approximately 80% of the dose cleared or recycled within 24 hours [Australian].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- Data from six single-dose and four multiple-dose randomised, multicentre trials involving approximately 1700 infants demonstrated efficacy.
- **Prevention Studies:** Infants 600–1250 g birth weight (23–29 weeks gestation) showed benefit when treated within 15 minutes of birth.
- **Rescue Studies:** Infants 600–1750 g birth weight requiring ventilation showed benefit when treated before 8 hours of age [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C.
- **Light:** Protect from light; store vials in the original carton until use.
- **Shelf Life:** Refer to the expiry date on the packaging [Australian].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Australian Classification:** Prescription Only Medicine - Schedule 4 (S4) [Australian].
- **CAS Number:** 108778-82-1.
