# Editorial Product Information: Benzonatate

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **US (Food and Drug Administration)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonized editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical data into a concise, professional reference.
- **Note:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult the current local PI for full prescribing details and legal requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Benzonatate; a non-narcotic oral antitussive agent. It is a polyglycol derivative (2, 5, 8, 11, 14, 17, 20, 23, 26-nonaoxaoctacosan-28-yl p(butylamino) benzoate).
- **Brand Names:** Benzonatate (generic) [US].
- **ATC Code:** R05DB01 (Benzonatate) [Not specified in available source, inferred from standard classification].
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** Soft gelatin capsules, 100 mg and 200 mg [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Bryant Ranch Prepack (Repackager) [US]; Bionpharma (Source manufacturer) [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Symptomatic Relief of Cough:** Approved for the relief of cough symptoms [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Adult Dosing:** The usual dose is 100 mg or 200 mg three times daily (TID) as needed for cough [US].
- **Maximum Dose:** The total daily dosage should not exceed 600 mg [US].
- **Administration Instructions:** 
    - Capsules must be swallowed whole. 
    - **Do NOT** break, chew, dissolve, cut, or crush the capsules. Release of the medication in the mouth can cause rapid, temporary local anesthesia of the oral mucosa, which may lead to choking or airway compromise [US].
- **Missed Dose:** If a dose is missed, skip that dose and take the next dose at the scheduled time. Do not double the dose [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to benzonatate or related compounds of the para-amino-benzoic acid (PABA) class, such as procaine and tetracaine [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Accidental Ingestion and Death in Children:** Accidental ingestion resulting in death has been reported in children under age 10. Signs and symptoms of overdose (restlessness, tremors, convulsions, coma, and cardiac arrest) can occur within 15–20 minutes, with death reported within one hour of ingestion. Benzonatate must be kept strictly out of the reach of children [US].
- **Severe Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Severe reactions, including bronchospasm, laryngospasm, and cardiovascular collapse, have been reported. These are often associated with local anesthesia resulting from sucking or chewing the capsule. Such reactions may require vasopressors and intensive supportive care [US].
- **Psychiatric Effects:** Isolated reports of bizarre behavior, mental confusion, and visual hallucinations have occurred, typically in patients using benzonatate in combination with other prescribed medications [US].
- **Oropharyngeal Anesthesia:** If the capsule is released in the mouth, numbness of the tongue, mouth, and throat may occur. Patients should refrain from eating or drinking until numbness resolves. If symptoms persist or worsen, medical attention should be sought [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
*Note: Frequencies of adverse effects are not provided in the source documentation.*

- **Hypersensitivity:** Bronchospasm, laryngospasm, cardiovascular collapse [US].
- **Central Nervous System (CNS):** Sedation, headache, dizziness, mental confusion, visual hallucinations [US].
- **Gastrointestinal (GI):** Constipation, nausea, general GI upset [US].
- **Dermatologic:** Pruritus (itching), skin eruptions [US].
- **Other:** Nasal congestion, burning sensation in the eyes, vague "chilly" sensation, numbness of the chest [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** CNS effects may be exacerbated by concomitant medications, though specific interacting agents are not listed in the source material [US].
- **PABA Derivatives:** Because benzonatate is chemically related to anesthetic agents of the para-amino-benzoic acid class (e.g., procaine, tetracaine), clinicians should be alert to potential cross-sensitivity or interactions with these agents [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** US Pregnancy Category C. Animal reproduction studies have not been conducted. It is unknown if benzonatate can cause fetal harm or affect reproductive capacity. Use in pregnant women only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** It is not known whether benzonatate is excreted in human milk. Exercise caution when administering to nursing women [US].
- **Fertility:** Reproduction studies have not been conducted to evaluate the effect on fertility [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** 
    - Safety and effectiveness in children below the age of 10 have not been established [US].
    - Extreme caution is required due to the risk of fatal accidental ingestion in this age group [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available source.
- **Renal Impairment:** Not specified in available source.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available source.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Benzonatate acts peripherally by anesthetizing the stretch receptors (stretch-sensitive vagal afferent fibers) located in the respiratory passages, lungs, and pleura. This dampens their activity and reduces the cough reflex at its source [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** 
    - Unlike narcotic antitussives, benzonatate has no inhibitory effect on the respiratory center at recommended dosages [US].
    - Onset of action: 15 to 20 minutes [US].
    - Duration of action: 3 to 8 hours [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** 
    - The drug is generally well-absorbed after ingestion. 
    - Further pharmacokinetic details (metabolism, distribution, elimination) are not specified in the source documentation.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage Temperature:** Store at controlled room temperature, 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [US].
- **General Conditions:** Protect from light, moisture, and humidity. Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container [US].
