# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, consolidating clinical and regulatory data into a single, concise reference.
- **Note:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory documents for legal prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN/generic name). 
- **Chemical Class:** A naturally occurring carrier molecule required for mammalian energy metabolism; chemically described as (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection, USP [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Clear, colorless sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Indicated for both acute and chronic management of patients with inborn metabolic errors that lead to secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance hemodialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    - The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg.
    - Administration should be via slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion.
    - **Acute Crisis:** A loading dose is often administered, followed by equivalent doses divided every 3 or 4 hours (q3h or q4h). Dosing intervals should never be less than every 6 hours (q6h).
    - **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses are typically in the range of 50 mg/kg, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been administered based on clinical requirements.
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - Recommended starting dose: 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight).
    - Administration: Slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
    - **Titration:** Dose adjustments should be guided by pre-dialysis (trough) plasma levocarnitine concentrations (normal range: 40 µmol/L to 50 µmol/L). Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy.

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected visually for discoloration or particulate matter prior to use.
- **Monitoring:** For metabolic disorders, it is recommended to obtain plasma carnitine levels prior to therapy. Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma carnitine (target free carnitine: 35 to 60 µmol/L) is advised [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- No contraindications are known at this time [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported. These reactions have occurred within minutes of intravenous administration, primarily in patients with ESRD on hemodialysis. Treatment must be discontinued immediately upon signs of hypersensitivity [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In those with an existing seizure disorder, an increase in the frequency or severity of seizures has been observed [US].
- **Monitoring Requirements:** In ESRD patients, safety and efficacy of high-dose oral levocarnitine have not been established; chronic use may lead to accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) normally excreted in urine [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Common (>1–10%):** Transient nausea and vomiting. 
- **Less Frequent/Other:** Body odor, gastritis, and abdominal pain.
- **Clinical Trial Data (ESRD Patients):** In placebo-controlled trials, the following events occurred at a frequency of ≥5% regardless of causality:
    - **General:** Chest pain, headache, infection, injection site reaction, pain, fever, flu syndrome.
    - **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension, hypotension, tachycardia.
    - **Digestive:** Diarrhea, abdominal pain, vomiting.
    - **Metabolic:** Hypercalcemia, hypervolemia.
    - **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis, cough increase, dyspnea, rhinitis.
    - **Neurologic:** Dizziness, paresthesia [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in the International Normalized Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is used concomitantly with warfarin. **Management:** Monitor INR levels regularly after initiating levocarnitine or following dose adjustments in patients on anticoagulant therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not specifically assigned a lettered category in the provided source, but reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at doses up to 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Animal data (dairy cows) show increased carnitine concentrations in milk following exogenous administration. The risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant must be weighed against the benefits to the mother [US].
- **Fertility:** No impairment of fertility was observed in animal reproductive studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders is weight-based (50 mg/kg), which applies to the paediatric population [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically detailed in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    - **ESRD on Dialysis:** Specific dosing provided (10–20 mg/kg).
    - **Non-dialysis Renal Insufficiency:** The safety and efficacy of oral levocarnitine have not been evaluated in patients with renal impairment [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In patients with inborn errors of metabolism or ESRD, levocarnitine clears toxic acyl-CoA compounds and restores plasma carnitine levels.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not protein-bound.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces).
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours (uncorrected for endogenous levels). The mean terminal elimination half-life is approximately 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pharmacokinetic Studies:** Studies in ESRD patients showed that a 20 mg/kg dose three times weekly after dialysis increased mean pre-dialysis trough concentrations from ~20 µmol/L to ~190 µmol/L after 8 weeks.
- **Dose-Ranging Studies:** Trials comparing 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses in ESRD patients demonstrated dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C.
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat.
- **Stability:** Compatible and stable for up to 24 hours in PVC bags when mixed with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations between 0.5 mg/mL and 8.0 mg/mL [US].
- **Shelf Life:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as it contains no preservative [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Prescription only (Rx-only). NDC 36000-368-10 (carton of 10 vials).
