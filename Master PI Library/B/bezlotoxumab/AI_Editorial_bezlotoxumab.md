# Editorial Product Information: Bezlotoxumab

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) document from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised, clinically accurate editorial overview for healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis intended for informational purposes and does not substitute for the official PI or prescribing information required for clinical practice in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Bezlotoxumab (a human monoclonal antibody; IgG$_1$ immunoglobulin).
- **Brand Name:** **Zinplava** [US].
- **ATC Code:** J06BC03.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Injection (solution). Supplied as 1,000 mg/40 mL (25 mg/mL) in a single-dose vial [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Merck Sharp & Dohme LLC [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Reduction of Recurrence in *Clostridioides difficile* Infection (CDI):** Indicated to reduce the recurrence of CDI in adult and paediatric patients (1 year of age and older) who are currently receiving antibacterial treatment for CDI and are at high risk for recurrence [US].
- **Limitation of Use:** Bezlotoxumab is not an antibacterial drug and is not indicated for the treatment of active CDI. It must only be used in conjunction with standard-of-care (SoC) antibacterial therapy for CDI [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing:** A single dose of 10 mg/kg administered via intravenous infusion [US].
- **Administration Schedule:** The dose should be administered during the course of antibacterial therapy for CDI [US].
- **Preparation Instructions:**
    - Dilute before administration into an IV bag containing either 0.9% Sodium Chloride or 5% Dextrose to a final concentration of 1 mg/mL to 10 mg/mL.
    - Mix by gentle inversion; do not shake.
    - Inspect for particulates or discoloration (solution should be clear to moderately opalescent and colorless to pale yellow).
- **Administration:** Infuse over 60 minutes through a sterile, non-pyrogenic, low-protein binding 0.2 micron to 5 micron in-line or add-on filter.
- **Compatibility:** Do not co-administer other drugs through the same infusion line. Can be infused via central or peripheral line; do not administer as a bolus [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **None:** No contraindications are listed in the available source [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Heart Failure:** In clinical trials, heart failure was reported more frequently in patients treated with bezlotoxumab compared to placebo. This was observed primarily in patients with a pre-existing history of congestive heart failure (CHF). In this specific population, 12.7% of bezlotoxumab-treated patients experienced heart failure compared to 4.8% in the placebo group. Furthermore, higher mortality rates were noted in patients with a history of CHF receiving bezlotoxumab. 
    - **Clinical Recommendation:** Bezlotoxumab should be reserved for use in patients with a history of CHF only when the potential benefit outweighs the risk [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Adult Patients:** Common adverse reactions (incidence $\ge 4\%$ and greater than placebo) reported within 4 weeks of infusion [US]:
    - Nausea (7%)
    - Pyrexia (5%)
    - Headache (4%)
- **Paediatric Patients:** Common adverse reactions (incidence $> 10\%$) [US]:
    - Pyrexia (18%)
    - Headache (14%)
- **Infusion-Related Reactions:** Reported in approximately 10% of patients on the day of or day after infusion. Specific symptoms (incidence $\ge 0.5\%$) included nausea, fatigue, pyrexia, dizziness, headache, dyspnea, and hypertension [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Heart failure (2.3% in adults). One instance of ventricular tachyarrhythmia was reported during clinical trials [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** As bezlotoxumab is eliminated via catabolism, no metabolic drug-drug interactions are expected [US].
- **Concomitant Antibacterials:** Clinical trials were conducted with patients receiving SoC antibacterials (vancomycin, metronidazole, or fidaxomicin); no interference with antibacterial efficacy was noted [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** There are no adequate or well-controlled studies in pregnant women. Animal reproductive studies have not been conducted. The background risk for the indicated population is unknown [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Information regarding the presence of bezlotoxumab in human milk or its effects on the breastfed infant is unavailable. The developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding should be weighed against the mother's clinical need and potential adverse effects on the child [US].
- **Fertility:** No fertility data is available [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Approved for patients 1 year of age and older. Safety and efficacy in children under 1 year of age have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** No dosage adjustment is required for patients $\ge 65$ years of age. In trials, 50% of patients were $\ge 65$ years, and 27% were $\ge 75$ years; no overall differences in safety or efficacy were observed between these subjects and younger patients [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** No clinically meaningful differences in pharmacokinetics were found in patients with mild, moderate, or severe renal impairment, including end-stage renal disease, compared to those with normal renal function [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No clinically meaningful differences in pharmacokinetics were found in patients with hepatic impairment compared to those with normal hepatic function [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** A human monoclonal antibody that binds with high affinity to *C. difficile* toxin B. It neutralizes the toxin, preventing its binding to mammalian cells and thereby inhibiting its toxic effects. It does not bind to toxin A [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Bezlotoxumab acts by preventing the intestinal inflammation and damage caused by toxin B, which is a key driver of CDI recurrence.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Mean volume of distribution is 7.33 L.
    - **Metabolism/Elimination:** Eliminated by catabolism. The elimination half-life is approximately 19 days.
    - **Clearance:** Geometric mean clearance is 0.317 L/day [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Adult Data (MODIFY I and MODIFY II):** Two Phase 3, randomized, double-blind trials (n=1554) compared bezlotoxumab (10 mg/kg single dose) to placebo, both added to SoC antibacterial therapy.
    - **Primary Outcome:** Sustained clinical response (clinical cure of the baseline episode and no recurrence through 12 weeks).
    - **Results:** Bezlotoxumab showed significantly lower rates of CDI recurrence compared to placebo (Trial 1: 17.4% vs 27.6%; Trial 2: 15.7% vs 25.7%).
- **Paediatric Data:** Safety and PK were evaluated in a Phase 3 trial (n=143) in patients 1 to <18 years, showing comparable results to the adult population [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vials:** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) in the original carton to protect from light. Do not freeze or shake [US].
- **Diluted Solution:** 
    - Store at room temperature for up to 16 hours.
    - Store under refrigeration at 2°C to 8°C for up to 24 hours.
    - Total time from dilution to completion of infusion should not exceed these limits [US].

- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources (refer to regional jurisdictional schedules for non-US regions).
