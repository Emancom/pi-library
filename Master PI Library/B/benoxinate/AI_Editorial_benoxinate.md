# Editorial Product Information: Fluorescein Sodium and Benoxinate Hydrochloride

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdiction:
    - **United States (FDA)**: Fluorescein Sodium and Benoxinate Hydrochloride Ophthalmic Solution, 0.3%/0.4% (Bausch & Lomb Incorporated).
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. 
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. At the time of writing, only the United States (FDA) documentation was available for inclusion.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** 
    - **Fluorescein Sodium**: A disclosing agent (3',6' Dihydroxy-3H-spiro[isobenzofuran-1,9-xanthen]-3-one disodium salt).
    - **Benoxinate Hydrochloride**: A short-acting local ester anesthetic (2-(Diethylamino) ethyl 4-amino-3-butoxybenzoate hydrochloride).
- **Brand Names:** Not specified in available sources (Genericised as Fluorescein Sodium and Benoxinate Hydrochloride Ophthalmic Solution).
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** 
    - Ophthalmic solution containing fluorescein sodium 2.6 mg/mL (0.3%) and benoxinate hydrochloride 4.4 mg/mL (0.4%) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Topical ophthalmic.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Bausch & Lomb Incorporated [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Ophthalmic Procedures:** Indicated for use in adult and pediatric patients requiring a disclosing agent in combination with a topical ophthalmic anesthetic agent [US]. These procedures include applanation tonometry, tear fluid dynamics evaluation, and short conjunctival or corneal procedures [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing:** 
    - **Adult and Pediatric:** Instill 1 to 2 drops topically in the eye as needed to achieve adequate anesthesia [US].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - For topical ophthalmic use only.
    - Avoid touching the tip of the dispensing container to any surface to maintain sterility.
- **Titration:** The anesthetic effect may be extended by subsequent administration 10 to 20 minutes after the initial dose [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to any component of the product, including benoxinate hydrochloride (ester-type anesthetic), fluorescein sodium, or the preservative chlorobutanol [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Corneal Toxicity:** Prolonged use or abuse of topical ocular anesthetics may lead to corneal epithelial toxicity. This may manifest as epithelial defects, which can progress to permanent corneal damage and subsequent visual loss [US].
- **Corneal Injury Due to Insensitivity:** Patients must be cautioned not to touch or rub the eye for approximately 20 minutes after application. Accidental injury may occur due to the temporary lack of sensation in the eye [US].
- **Monitoring:** Clinical monitoring for corneal integrity is recommended if multiple doses are administered.

## 6. Adverse Effects
The following adverse effects have been identified during post-marketing use of this combination product. Because these are reported voluntarily, frequency estimates are not available from the source documentation [US].

| System Organ Class | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ocular** | Stinging, burning, conjunctival redness (ocular hyperemia), eye irritation, blurred vision, punctate keratitis. |
| **Serious Reactions** | Corneal toxicity and corneal injury due to insensitivity [US]. |

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not assigned under current labeling rules; however, there are no available data on use in pregnant women to inform drug-associated risk. Animal reproduction studies have not been conducted. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** 
    - There are no data regarding the presence of fluorescein sodium or benoxinate hydrochloride in human milk, effects on the breastfed infant, or effects on milk production. The developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding should be considered alongside the mother's clinical need and potential adverse effects on the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** Studies to evaluate the impairment of fertility have not been conducted [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have been established for pediatric patients. Support is derived from adequate and well-controlled studies in both adults and children [US].
- **Elderly Use:** No overall differences in safety or effectiveness have been observed between elderly and younger patients [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** 
    - **Benoxinate hydrochloride** is a local anesthetic of the ester type which blocks sodium channels to prevent the generation and conduction of nerve impulses.
    - **Fluorescein sodium** is a hydroxyxanthene dye that responds to electromagnetic radiation between 465 and 490 nm, resulting in fluorescence that allows for the visualization of corneal defects and tear film [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** 
    - **Onset:** Maximal corneal anesthesia typically occurs within 5 to 45 seconds following a single administration [US].
    - **Duration:** Anesthesia lasts for approximately 20 minutes [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified in available sources.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Summary:** Controlled clinical studies in adult and pediatric patients have demonstrated that the topical administration of this solution enables visualization and corneal anesthesia sufficient for applanation tonometry, tear fluid dynamics evaluation, and short conjunctival/corneal procedures [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store in a refrigerator at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) [US].
- **Light Protection:** Protect from light [US].
- **Post-Opening Storage:** After opening, the product may be stored for up to one month at room temperature. If stored under refrigerated conditions, it may be used until the expiration date on the bottle [US].
- **Handling:** Keep the bottle tightly closed when not in use.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** 
    - **United States:** Prescription Only (Rx only) [US].
- **Packaging:** 5 mL fill in a 6 mL amber glass bottle with a sterilized dropper bulb and glass pipette [US].
