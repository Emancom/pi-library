# Editorial Product Information: Blinatumomab

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdiction: **Australian (TGA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, consolidating clinical, pharmacological, and regulatory data.
- **Disclaimer:** This synthesis is an editorial reference and does not replace the official PI required for prescribing and dispensing in any specific jurisdiction. At the time of writing, only the Australian PI was provided for synthesis.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Blinatumomab
- **Chemical Class:** Bispecific T-cell engager (BiTE®) molecule
- **Brand Name:** **Blincyto®** [Australian]
- **ATC Code:** L01FX07
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Lyophilised powder for injection (38.5 micrograms per single-use vial). Supplied with a specific IV solution stabiliser.
- **Route of Administration:** Continuous intravenous infusion via a programmable, lockable, non-elastomeric infusion pump.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Amgen Australia Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
[All indications listed below are Australian-approved]:
- Treatment of relapsed or refractory B-cell precursor acute lymphoblastic leukaemia (ALL).
- Treatment of minimal residual disease (MRD) positive B-cell precursor ALL in patients experiencing complete haematological remission.
- Treatment of B-cell precursor ALL during the consolidation phase:
    - In combination with chemotherapy for Philadelphia chromosome-negative disease.
    - In combination with a tyrosine kinase inhibitor (TKI) for Philadelphia chromosome-positive disease in patients unable to receive chemotherapy.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults)
- **Relapsed/Refractory ALL:** 
    - **Cycle 1:** 9 mcg/day (Days 1–7), then 28 mcg/day (Days 8–28).
    - **Subsequent Cycles:** 28 mcg/day (Days 1–28).
    - **Interval:** 28 days of infusion followed by a 14-day treatment-free interval.
- **MRD-Positive/Consolidation ALL:** 28 mcg/day fixed dose (for patients ≥ 45 kg) for 28 days, followed by a 14-day treatment-free interval.

### Dosing by Weight (Paediatric and Low-Weight Adults)
- **Patients < 45 kg:** Dosing is Body Surface Area (BSA) based.
    - **Relapsed/Refractory:** 5 mcg/m²/day (max 9 mcg/day) for the first 7 days, then 15 mcg/m²/day (max 28 mcg/day).
    - **MRD-Positive/Consolidation:** 15 mcg/m²/day (max 28 mcg/day).

### Administration Instructions
- **Hospitalisation:** Recommended for the first 9 days of Cycle 1 and the first 2 days of Cycle 2 in relapsed/refractory ALL. For MRD-positive or consolidation ALL, hospitalisation is recommended for the first 3 days of Cycle 1 and the first 2 days of Cycle 2.
- **Infusion Bags:** Must be changed every 24 to 96 hours depending on the chosen infusion rate and weight. 
- **Preparation:** Aseptic technique is mandatory. The IV solution stabiliser must be used to coat the infusion bag to prevent drug adhesion. **Do not use the stabiliser for reconstitution.**
- **Priming:** The IV line must be primed **only** with the final prepared solution. Do not prime with saline.

### Premedication
- **Adults:** Dexamethasone 20 mg IV 1 hour prior to the first dose of each cycle. 
- **Paediatrics:** Dexamethasone 5 mg/m² (max 20 mg) prior to the first dose of Cycle 1, step-doses, or restarts after interruptions > 4 hours.

## 4. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to blinatumomab, Chinese Hamster Ovary (CHO) cell-derived proteins, or any excipients [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warning [Australian]
- **Cytokine Release Syndrome (CRS):** May be life-threatening or fatal. 
- **Neurological Toxicities:** Including ICANS; may be severe or fatal. 
- **JC Virus Reactivation:** Potential for progressive multifocal leukoencephalopathy (PML).

### General Warnings
- **Infections:** Increased risk of serious, life-threatening bacterial, fungal, or viral infections (including sepsis and pneumonia).
- **Haemophagocytic Lymphohistiocytosis (HLH)/IEC-HS:** Reported in the setting of CRS; requires close monitoring for fever and transaminitis.
- **Tumour Lysis Syndrome (TLS):** Prophylactic hydration is recommended.
- **Medication Errors:** Strict adherence to preparation and administration volumes is required to avoid bolus administration or underdosing.
- **Pancreatitis:** Reported in clinical trials; often associated with high-dose steroid therapy.
- **Leukoencephalopathy:** MRI changes observed; clinical significance is currently unknown.
- **CD19-Negative Relapse:** Not recommended for patients with CD19-negative disease.

### Monitoring Requirements
- Clinical monitoring for signs of CRS and neurologic events.
- Liver enzymes (ALT, AST, GGT) and total bilirubin prior to and during treatment.
- Laboratory parameters for TLS and neutropenia (WBC, ANC).

## 6. Adverse Effects
*Frequencies based on adult relapsed/refractory ALL clinical studies (N=267)*

| Frequency | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Pyrexia (60%), Infusion-related reactions (34%), Headache (29%), Anaemia (27%), Neutropenia (23%), Thrombocytopenia (24%), Increased liver enzymes (17%), Oedema (17%), Bacterial/Fungal/Viral infections. |
| **Common (1–10%)** | HLH, Leukopenia, Lymphadenopathy, Hypersensitivity, TLS, Confusional state, Seizure, Encephalopathy, Aphasia, Tachycardia, Hypotension. |
| **Uncommon (0.1–1%)** | Cytokine storm, Speech disorder, Pancreatitis. |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** The most frequent serious reactions were infections (unspecified pathogen), febrile neutropenia, and CRS.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **CYP450 Enzymes:** No formal studies. Transient cytokine elevation may suppress CYP450 activity for less than a week; clinical impact is expected to be low.
- **Immunisation:** Live viral vaccines are not recommended for at least 2 weeks before treatment, during treatment, and until B-lymphocyte recovery.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** **Category C** [Australian]. Blinatumomab may cross the placenta and cause fetal B-cell depletion. Use only if benefits outweigh risks. Women of childbearing potential must use contraception during and for at least 48 hours after treatment.
- **Breastfeeding:** Discontinue nursing during and for at least 48 hours after treatment due to unknown excretion in human milk.
- **Fertility:** No specific human data; no effects seen in animal reproductive organs.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Established for patients ≥ 28 days old. Dosing is BSA-based for those < 45 kg.
- **Elderly Use:** No dose adjustment required for patients ≥ 65 years. Increased susceptibility to neurologic events (e.g., confusion, encephalopathy) noted.
- **Renal Impairment:** No adjustment for mild to moderate dysfunction. Not studied in severe renal impairment.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No adjustment for mild to moderate dysfunction. Not studied in severe hepatic impairment.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** A bispecific T-cell engager (BiTE®). It binds CD19 on B-lineage cells and CD3 on T-cells, facilitating a cytolytic synapse. This leads to T-cell activation, production of cytolytic proteins, and the subsequent elimination of CD19+ malignant and benign B-cells.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Vz of 5.27 L.
    - **Metabolism:** Likely catabolic pathway into small peptides and amino acids.
    - **Elimination:** Systemic clearance of 3.10 L/hour; mean half-life of 2.20 hours. Minimal urinary excretion.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Study 1 (Adults R/R):** Randomised Phase 3 trial. Blinatumomab showed a median overall survival (OS) of 7.7 months vs. 4.0 months for standard chemotherapy.
- **Study 5 (Adults MRD+):** Single-arm trial showing 78% of patients achieved a complete MRD response within one cycle.
- **Study 6 (Paediatrics):** Phase 3 trial in high-risk first relapsed ALL. Blinatumomab significantly improved event-free survival (EFS) compared to consolidation chemotherapy (Hazard Ratio 0.35).

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Unopened Vials:** Store at 2°C to 8°C. Do not freeze. Protect from light.
- **Reconstituted Solution:** Stable for 24 hours at 2°C to 8°C or 4 hours at room temperature.
- **Diluted Solution (IV Bag):** Combined storage/infusion time is 10 days at 2°C to 8°C or 96 hours at room temperature (< 25°C).

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule:** **Schedule 4 (Prescription Medicine)** [Australian].
