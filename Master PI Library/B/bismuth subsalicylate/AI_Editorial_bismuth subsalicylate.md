# Editorial Product Information: Bismuth Subsalicylate

## Editorial Note

- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical data into a concise, structured format.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Bismuth subsalicylate.
- **Chemical Class:** Gastrointestinal agent; salicylate derivative.
- **Brand Names:** Stomach Relief Ultra (Equate/Wal-Mart Stores, Inc.) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A07BB03 (Bismuth subsalicylate).
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - **Oral Liquid (Ultra Strength):** 525 mg bismuth subsalicylate per 15 mL [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Wal-Mart Stores, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Diarrhea:** Relief of non-specific diarrhea and traveler's diarrhea [US].
- **Upset Stomach:** Relief of symptoms associated with overindulgence in food and drink, including heartburn, indigestion, nausea, gas, fullness, and belching [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Children 12 years and over)
- **Diarrhea and Traveler’s Diarrhea:** 15 mL (525 mg) every 30 minutes OR 30 mL (1050 mg) every hour as needed [US].
- **Upset Stomach (Overindulgence):** 15 mL (525 mg) every 30 minutes as needed [US].
- **Maximum Daily Dose:** Do not exceed 8 doses (120 mL) in a 24-hour period [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Shake the liquid suspension well before use [US].
- Use only the provided dosing cup to ensure accurate measurement [US].
- **Hydration:** Patients should consume plenty of clear fluids to prevent dehydration associated with diarrhea [US].
- **Duration of Use:** Discontinue use and consult a physician if symptoms worsen or if diarrhea persists for more than 2 days [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Salicylate Hypersensitivity:** Known allergy to salicylates, including aspirin [US].
- **Active Gastrointestinal Pathology:** Presence of an ulcer or a bleeding problem [US].
- **Abnormal Stools:** Use is contraindicated if the patient has bloody or black stools [US].
- **Concomitant Salicylates:** Use in patients already taking other salicylate-containing products [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Reye’s Syndrome (Boxed Warning):** Children and teenagers who have or are recovering from chickenpox or flu-like symptoms should not use this product. If changes in behavior with nausea and vomiting occur, a doctor should be consulted immediately, as these symptoms may be an early sign of Reye’s syndrome, a rare but serious illness [US].
- **Pre-existing Symptoms:** Consult a physician before use if the patient has a fever or mucus in the stool [US].
- **Ototoxicity:** Discontinue use and consult a healthcare provider if ringing in the ears (tinnitus) or loss of hearing occurs [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Common/Expected:** 
    - Darkening of the tongue (temporary and harmless) [US].
    - Darkening of the stool (temporary and harmless) [US].
- **Serious/Ototoxic:** 
    - Ringing in the ears or hearing loss (frequency not specified) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Anticoagulants:** Use with caution in patients taking blood-thinning agents (e.g., warfarin), as salicylates may increase bleeding risk [US].
- **Antidiabetic Agents:** Potential for pharmacodynamic interaction; consult a professional before use [US].
- **Gout and Arthritis Medications:** Salicylates may interfere with the efficacy of certain uricosuric or anti-inflammatory agents [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Patients are advised to ask a health professional before use. Specific jurisdictional categories (e.g., Australian Category or US lettered categories) were not specified in the available source [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Patients are advised to ask a health professional before use [US].
- **Fertility:** Not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** 
    - **Aged 12 years and over:** Refer to adult dosing [US].
    - **Under 12 years:** Consult a physician before use [US].
    - **Warning:** See Reye's Syndrome in Section 5.
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Use with caution (implied by salicylate content); specific dosing adjustments not specified in available sources.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Bismuth subsalicylate acts as an antidiarrheal and mucosal protective agent. It also possesses mild antimicrobial properties and inhibits prostaglandin synthesis (via the salicylate moiety) to reduce intestinal inflammation and hypermotility [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not detailed in the provided source document.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** The product contains 206 mg of salicylate per 15 mL dose [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at room temperature 20°C–25°C (68°F–77°F) [US].
- **Precautions:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat exceeding 40°C (104°F) [US].
- **Packaging:** Keep container tightly closed [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction

- **Sodium Content:** Each 15 mL dose contains 5 mg of sodium (classified as low sodium) [US].
