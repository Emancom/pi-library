# Originality Audit Report: burosumab

## Audit Summary
- **Originality Score:** 72/100
- **Verbatim Copied Passages Found:** 4
- **Overall Assessment:** Concern

## Verbatim Copied Passages
1.  **Section 1: Product Name and Active Ingredient**
    *   *Editorial:* "Burosumab is a recombinant human monoclonal IgG1 antibody directed against fibroblast growth factor 23 (FGF23). It is produced using recombinant DNA technology in Chinese hamster ovary (CHO) mammalian cell culture."
    *   *Source (Document 1 - TGA, Page 1):* "Burosumab is a recombinant human monoclonal IgG1 antibody for FGF23 and is produced by recombinant DNA technology using Chinese hamster ovary mammalian cell culture."
    *   *Source (Document 2 - FDA, Page 18):* "Burosumab-twza is a human immunoglobulin G subclass 1 (IgG1), anti-human fibroblast growth factor 23 (FGF23) antibody produced by recombinant DNA technology using Chinese hamster ovary cells."

    *   *Source (Document 1 - TGA, Page 6):* "...monitoring for signs and symptoms of nephrocalcinosis, e.g. by renal ultrasonography, is recommended at the start of treatment and every 6 months for the first 12 months of treatment, and annually thereafter."

    *   *Source (Document 2 - FDA, Page 16):* "Adverse effects such as late fetal loss and preterm birth were observed... accompanied by maternal hyperphosphatemia and placental mineralization."

    *   *Source (Document 2 - FDA, Page 19/20):* "The half-life of burosumab-twza is approximately 19 days. Clearance and volume of distribution of burosumab-twza increases with body weight."

## Close Paraphrases
*   **Approved Indications:** The TIO indication description ("FGF23-related hypophosphatemia associated with phosphaturic mesenchymal tumors that cannot be curatively resected or localized") follows the TGA and FDA regulatory language almost exactly.
*   **Contraindications:** The list provided in the editorial mirrors the phrasing in TGA Section 4.3 and FDA Section 4, specifically regarding "within or above the normal reference range for the patient's age."
*   **Mechanism of Action:** The editorial's description of FGF23 suppressing the kidney's ability to reabsorb phosphate and inhibiting Vitamin D production is a very close structural paraphrase of TGA Section 5.1 and FDA Section 12.1.

## Originality Strengths
*   **The Editorial Note:** The opening paragraph is entirely original and provides clear context for the harmonization effort.
*   **Dosage Harmonization:** Section 5 is a strong synthesis. It successfully identifies the differences between FDA and TGA (e.g., the 6-month age indication in the US vs. 1-year in Australia) and presents them in a unified, easy-to-read format.
*   **Adverse Effects Merging:** The writer has combined data points from the US "Highlights" and Australian "Table 4/6" to create a more concise summary rather than just copying one specific table.

## Differences from Source PIs
*   **Consolidation:** The editorial significantly condenses the lengthy clinical trial data (Section 5.1 of TGA / Section 14 of FDA) into a few sentences within the Mechanism of Action and Indications.
*   **Regulatory Tagging:** The document uses brackets (e.g., [FDA], [TGA]) to highlight specific geographic differences in approval and dosing, which does not exist in either source document.
*   **Terminology Harmonization:** The editorial uses "Vitamin D analogues" (TGA) and "Vitamin D analogs" (FDA) interchangeably or chooses a standard spelling to maintain consistency.
*   **Omission of Commercial Details:** The editorial removes specific US NDC numbers and Australian sponsor address details, focusing purely on clinical/product information.

## Recommendation
The editorial **PASSES** originality review with a requirement for **Minor Revision**.

While the synthesis of the two documents is excellent and provides high value, several clinical descriptions (Mechanism of Action, Active Ingredient definition, and Pregnancy risk summary) rely too heavily on the exact sentence structures of the source PIs. 

**Required Changes:**
1.  Rewrite the **Active Ingredient** description in Section 1 to avoid the "recombinant human monoclonal IgG1 antibody... produced using recombinant DNA technology..." sequence.
2.  Reword the **Ectopic Mineralization** monitoring sentence to avoid the exact "start of treatment, every 6 months... annually thereafter" phrasing used by the TGA.
3.  Adjust the **Pregnancy** section to describe the monkey study findings in more varied prose (e.g., "Animal models demonstrated higher rates of premature birth and fetal expiration at high exposure levels").
