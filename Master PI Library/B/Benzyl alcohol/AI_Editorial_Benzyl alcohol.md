## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** A complex homeopathic preparation containing multiple components including:
    - **Primary Dilutions:** Glycerinum 3X, *Taraxacum officinale* 3X, *Phytolacca decandra* 6X.
    - **Homeopathic Dilutions (12X):** *Arsenicum album*, *Bovista*, *Carbo vegetabilis*, *Carboneum sulphuratum*, *Coffea cruda*, *Nitricum acidum*, *Nux vomica*, Petroleum, Phosphorus, *Phosphoricum acidum*, Sulphur.
    - **Chemical Isodes & Other Components (12X, 30X, 200X):** *Acetylsalicylicum acidum* (Aspirin), *Formalinum*, *Glonoinum* (Nitroglycerin), *Insulinum* (Human), *Lithium carbonicum*, *Thyroidinum* (Suis), *Terebinthina*, *Adrenocorticotrophin* (15C), *Aceticum acidum*, *Benzoicum acidum*, Benzyl alcohol, *Boricum acidum*, *Chlorinum*, *Cortisone aceticum*, Eugenol, *Folliculinum*, Isopropyl palmitate, *Lacticum acidum*, Petroleum jelly, Phenylbutazone, *Plumbum metallicum* (Lead), Potassium sorbate, Resorcinol, *Salicylicum acidum*, Sorbitol, Stearyl alcohol, Xylitol.
- **Brand Name:** **CHEMSTAT** [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not available.
- **Dosage Form:** Oral liquid (drops).
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Energique, Inc. (Woodbine, IA) [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
**CHEMSTAT** is used within the framework of traditional homeopathic practice for the temporary relief of the following symptoms [US]:
- Weakness and exhaustion.
- Minor abdominal cramps.
- Bloating.
- Vomiting.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Adults:** 5 to 10 drops taken orally, 3 times daily, or as directed by a healthcare professional [US].
- **Children (Age 12 and older):** 5 to 10 drops taken orally, 3 times daily [US].

### Administration Instructions
- If symptoms persist for more than 7 days, patients are advised to consult a healthcare professional [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Tamper Evident Seal:** Do not use if the tamper-evident seal is broken or missing [US].
- No further specific clinical contraindications are provided in the available source [US].
