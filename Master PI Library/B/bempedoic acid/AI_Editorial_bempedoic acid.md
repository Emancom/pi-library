# Editorial Product Information: Bempedoic Acid and Ezetimibe

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the following jurisdiction's Product Information:
    - **United States (FDA)**: NEXLIZET (bempedoic acid and ezetimibe) tablets.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. 
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. At the time of writing, only the United States PI was available for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** Bempedoic acid (an adenosine triphosphate citrate lyase [ACL] inhibitor) and Ezetimibe (a dietary cholesterol absorption inhibitor).
- **Brand Names:** NEXLIZET [US].
- **ATC Code:** C10BA10 (Bempedoic acid and ezetimibe).
- **Dosage Form and Strength:**
    - **Film-coated tablets:** 180 mg bempedoic acid and 10 mg ezetimibe [US].
    - **Physical description:** Blue, oval-shaped, debossed with "818" on one side and "ESP" on the other [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Esperion Therapeutics, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Hypercholesterolemia:** Indicated as an adjunct to diet and exercise to reduce low-density lipoprotein cholesterol (LDL-C) in adults with hypercholesterolemia, including heterozygous familial hypercholesterolemia (HeFH) [US].
- **Cardiovascular Risk Reduction (Bempedoic acid component):** Indicated to reduce the risk of major adverse cardiovascular events (MACE), including cardiovascular death, myocardial infarction, stroke, or coronary revascularization, in adults at increased risk for these events who are unable to take recommended statin therapy [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adults):** One tablet (180 mg/10 mg) administered orally once daily [US].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - May be taken with or without food [US].
    - Tablets must be swallowed whole; do not cut, chew, or crush [US].
- **Concomitant Therapy with Bile Acid Sequestrants:** Administer the dose at least 2 hours before or at least 4 hours after taking a bile acid sequestrant (e.g., cholestyramine) to avoid decreased ezetimibe absorption [US].
- **Missed Dose:** If a dose is missed, it should be taken as soon as remembered. Patients should not double the next dose [US].
- **Monitoring:** Analyze lipid levels within 8 to 12 weeks after initiating therapy [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to bempedoic acid, ezetimibe, or any excipients in the formulation. Reported reactions include anaphylaxis, angioedema, rash, and urticaria [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hyperuricemia:** Bempedoic acid inhibits renal tubular organic anion transporter 2 (OAT2), which may increase serum uric acid levels. 
    - Increases typically occur within the first 4 weeks of treatment and persist until discontinuation [US].
    - **Monitoring:** Assess uric acid levels periodically as clinically indicated. Monitor for signs of gout and initiate urate-lowering therapy if appropriate [US].
- **Tendon Rupture:** Bempedoic acid is associated with an increased risk of tendon rupture or injury (e.g., Achilles, rotator cuff, or biceps).
    - Rupture may occur within weeks to months of starting the drug [US].
    - **Risk Factors:** Age >60 years, concurrent use of corticosteroids or fluoroquinolones, renal failure, or a history of tendon disorders [US].
    - **Management:** Discontinue treatment immediately if tendon rupture occurs. Consider discontinuation if the patient experiences joint pain, swelling, or inflammation [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reaction frequencies are based on clinical trial data [US]:

| Frequency | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Hyperuricemia (16% in CV outcomes trial), Renal impairment (11% in CV outcomes trial) |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Upper respiratory tract infection, muscle spasms, back pain, abdominal pain/discomfort, bronchitis, pain in extremity, anemia, elevated liver enzymes, diarrhea, arthralgia, sinusitis, fatigue, influenza, gout, cholelithiasis |
| **Uncommon (0.1–1%)** | Not specifically categorised in available sources |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Tendon rupture, clinically significant hyperuricemia/gout, and cholelithiasis (gallstones) [US].
- **Laboratory Changes:** Increases in serum creatinine and blood urea nitrogen (BUN), decreases in hemoglobin and leukocytes, and asymptomatic increases in platelet counts [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Simvastatin:** Avoid concomitant use with simvastatin doses greater than 20 mg due to increased simvastatin concentration and risk of myopathy [US].
- **Pravastatin:** Avoid concomitant use with pravastatin doses greater than 40 mg [US].
- **Cyclosporine:** Concomitant use increases concentrations of both ezetimibe and cyclosporine. Monitor cyclosporine levels closely [US].
- **Fibrates:** Coadministration with fibrates (other than fenofibrate) is not recommended. Concomitant use may increase the risk of cholelithiasis and may result in increased triglycerides and decreased HDL-C [US].
- **Bile Acid Sequestrants:** May decrease ezetimibe efficacy; separate dosing (see Section 3) [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Classification:** Based on the mechanism of action (reduction of cholesterol synthesis required for fetal development), the drug may cause fetal harm. 
    - NEXLIZET should be discontinued when pregnancy is recognized unless the benefits clearly outweigh the potential risks to the fetus [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Both active ingredients are excreted in human milk. Due to the potential for serious adverse reactions in the breastfed infant, breastfeeding is not recommended during treatment [US].
- **Fertility:** No adverse effects on female fertility were observed in animal studies. In male rats, decreased sperm counts were observed at 9 times the maximum recommended human dose (MRHD) [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established in patients under 18 years of age [US].
- **Elderly Use:** No overall differences in safety or effectiveness were observed between patients ≥65 years and younger patients [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dosage adjustment is necessary for patients with mild, moderate, or severe renal impairment [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** 
    - **Mild (Child-Pugh A):** No dosage adjustment required [US].
    - **Moderate to Severe (Child-Pugh B or C):** Not recommended due to unknown effects of increased ezetimibe exposure [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** 
    - **Bempedoic acid:** A prodrug activated in the liver that inhibits adenosine triphosphate-citrate lyase (ACL), an enzyme upstream of HMG-CoA reductase. This inhibits cholesterol synthesis and upregulates LDL receptors.
    - **Ezetimibe:** Inhibits the sterol transporter Niemann-Pick C1-Like 1 (NPC1L1) at the brush border of the small intestine, reducing the absorption of dietary and biliary cholesterol [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Bempedoic acid $T_{max}$ is 3 hours; ezetimibe $T_{max}$ is 5 hours. Bioavailability is not affected by food to a clinically meaningful extent [US].
    - **Distribution:** Both are highly protein-bound (>90%) [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Bempedoic acid is metabolised via glucuronidation and converted to an active metabolite (ESP15228). Ezetimibe is rapidly conjugated to ezetimibe-glucuronide in the intestine and liver [US].
    - **Elimination:** Bempedoic acid is primarily excreted in urine (70%); ezetimibe is primarily excreted in feces (78%). The half-life for bempedoic acid is approximately 21 hours and 22 hours for ezetimibe [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Trial 1 (HeFH and ASCVD):** A 12-week trial (n=301) showed that NEXLIZET reduced LDL-C by an additional 38% compared to placebo when added to maximally tolerated statin therapy [US].
- **CLEAR Outcomes Trial (Trial 4):** A cardiovascular outcomes trial (n=13,970) demonstrated that bempedoic acid significantly reduced the risk of MACE-4 (CV death, nonfatal MI, nonfatal stroke, or coronary revascularization) by 13% (HR 0.87) over a median follow-up of 3.4 years [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F and 86°F) [US].
- **Special Handling:** Store and dispense in the original package to protect from heat and moisture. Do not discard the desiccant [US].
