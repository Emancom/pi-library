## 6. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed warning
- **WARNING:** Accidental overdose of iron-containing products is a leading cause of fatal poisoning in children under 6 years of age. **Keep this product out of the reach of children.** In case of accidental overdose, contact a physician or poison control center immediately.

### General warnings and precautions
- Contraindicated in patients with known hypersensitivity to any of the ingredients.
- Use with caution in patients who have a medical condition, are pregnant, lactating, trying to conceive, under age 18, or taking other medications (as stated in the source).
- Folic acid specific precaution: Folic acid alone is an improper therapy for pernicious anemia and other megaloblastic anemias where vitamin B12 is deficient. Folic acid at doses above 0.1 mg (100 mcg) daily may mask pernicious anemia — hematologic remission can occur while neurological manifestations progress.

### Monitoring requirements
- Monitor for signs of iron overdose, particularly in children who may have accidental access.
- Monitor for allergic reactions (see Adverse Effects).
- For patients receiving folic acid, be vigilant for neurological signs of B12 deficiency if folic acid is given without assessment/treatment of vitamin B12 deficiency.

### Specific clinical precautions
- Avoid relying on folic acid alone to treat megaloblastic anemia without assessing vitamin B12 status.
- Observe standard precautions when prescribing to women who are pregnant, breastfeeding, or attempting conception — use only under medical supervision.
