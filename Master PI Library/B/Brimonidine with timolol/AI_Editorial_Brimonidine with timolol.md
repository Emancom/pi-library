# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial: **US (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising regulatory data into a single clinically relevant reference. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory documents for specific legal and prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP).
- **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule/amino acid derivative ((R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt).
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection, USP [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency in Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Indicated for both acute and chronic treatment of patients with metabolic defects resulting in secondary deficiency [US].
- **Carnitine Deficiency in End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
  - The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg.
  - Administration: Slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion.
  - **Severe Metabolic Crisis:** A loading dose is often administered, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours. Dosing should occur every 3 or 4 hours (and not less than every 6 hours).
  - Subsequent daily doses range from 50 mg/kg up to a maximum reported dose of 300 mg/kg [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
  - Recommended starting dose: 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight).
  - Administration: Slow bolus injection into the venous return line after each dialysis session.
  - **Dose Adjustments:** Initiation may be prompted by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levels below 40–50 µmol/L. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be made as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Inspect visually for particulate matter or discoloration before use.
- Use only single-dose vials; discard any unused portion as the formulation lacks preservatives [US].

### Monitoring
- **Metabolic Patients:** Obtain baseline plasma levels. Weekly and monthly monitoring should include blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### General Warnings
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported. These often occur within minutes of administration, primarily in ESRD patients. If a reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and evaluate risks before considering re-administration [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with a known history, an increase in seizure frequency or severity has been observed [US].

### Specific Clinical Precautions
- **Renal Impairment (Oral Levocarnitine):** The safety of *oral* levocarnitine has not been evaluated in renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in ESRD patients may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) normally excreted in urine [US].

### Other Adverse Effects
- **Common (>1% to 10%):** Body odor, gastritis, abdominal pain, fever, and flu syndrome [US].
- **Serious Post-marketing Reports:** Seizures, anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when used concomitantly with levocarnitine. Monitoring of INR is recommended after initiating treatment or following dose adjustments [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category C]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. However, adequate human studies are lacking. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Specific studies on levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers have not been conducted. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk carnitine concentrations after exogenous administration. Weigh the risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant against the benefits of supplementation to the mother; discontinuation of nursing or treatment may be considered [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing is based on mg/kg body weight; see Section 3 [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** See Section 5. Levocarnitine is removed by dialysis [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring carrier molecule required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids in patients with metabolic defects by forming acylcarnitine esters, which are rapidly excreted [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
  - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
  - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites are trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (feces) [US].
  - **Elimination:** Following IV bolus (20 mg/kg), approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours. Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours; mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients (20 mg/kg 3x weekly post-dialysis), trough levels increased from a baseline of ~21 µmol/L to ~190 µmol/L at 8 weeks. While levocarnitine increases carnitine concentrations, the impact on clinical outcomes in the ESRD population has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Precautions:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat [US].
- **Stability:** Compatible and stable for 24 hours in PVC bags at room temperature (25°C) when mixed with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources.
