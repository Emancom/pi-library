# Editorial Product Information: Belimumab

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial synthesis of the Product Information for belimumab. It is designed as a high-level reference for healthcare professionals to support clinical decision-making.
- **Disclaimer:** This editorial is a synthesis of available regulatory data and does not replace the official Product Information mandated for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory guidelines for specific legal and prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Belimumab (rmc).
- **Chemical Class:** Human IgG1λ monoclonal antibody.
- **Brand Name:** **Benlysta** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** L04AA26 (Selective immunosuppressant).
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:**
    - **Powder for Intravenous (IV) Infusion:** 120 mg or 400 mg lyophilised powder in single-use vials. After reconstitution, the solution contains 80 mg/mL belimumab [Australian].
    - **Solution for Subcutaneous (SC) Injection:** 200 mg/mL in a 1 mL pre-filled syringe or pre-filled pen [Australian].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous infusion or Subcutaneous injection.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** GlaxoSmithKline Australia Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Systemic Lupus Erythematosus (SLE):** Add-on therapy for adult patients with active, autoantibody-positive SLE who have a high degree of disease activity (e.g., positive ANA or anti-dsDNA) despite receiving standard therapy [Australian].
- **Lupus Nephritis (LN):** Treatment of adult patients with active lupus nephritis who are receiving standard therapy [Australian].
- **Limitations of Use:** Safety and efficacy have not been established in patients with severe active central nervous system lupus [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults)
**Intravenous Infusion (SLE and Lupus Nephritis):**
- **Dose:** 10 mg/kg.
- **Schedule:** Days 0, 14, and 28, then every 4 weeks thereafter [Australian].

**Subcutaneous Injection:**
- **SLE:** 200 mg once weekly [Australian].
- **Lupus Nephritis:** 
    - *Initiation:* 400 mg (two 200 mg injections) once weekly for 4 doses.
    - *Maintenance:* 200 mg once weekly thereafter [Australian].

### Administration Instructions
- **IV Infusion:** Must be reconstituted and diluted. Administer over a 1-hour period. Do **NOT** administer as a bolus or IV push. 
- **SC Injection:** Administer in the abdomen or thigh. Rotate injection sites. If a 400 mg dose is required (for LN initiation), give as two separate 200 mg injections at least 5 cm apart [Australian].
- **Transitioning:**
    - *IV to SC (SLE):* Administer the first SC dose 1–4 weeks after the last IV dose.
    - *IV to SC (LN):* Administer the first 200 mg SC dose 1–2 weeks after the second IV dose has been completed [Australian].

### Missed Doses
- **SC:** Administer the missed dose as soon as possible. Patients may then resume their usual weekly schedule or start a new weekly cycle from the day the missed dose was taken. Do not administer two doses on the same day [Australian].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Demonstrated anaphylaxis to belimumab or any excipient in the formulation [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Infusion/Injection-Related Reactions:** Acute hypersensitivity and anaphylaxis may occur, sometimes several days after administration. Treatment should be initiated in an environment with full resuscitation facilities [Australian].
- **Serious Infections:** Belimumab increases the risk of serious and fatal infections (e.g., pneumonia, sepsis). Do not initiate in patients with active serious infections [Australian].
- **Progressive Multifocal Leukoencephalopathy (PML):** Rare, fatal cases reported. Monitor for new or worsening neurological symptoms; if suspected, suspend therapy immediately [Australian].
- **Depression and Suicidality:** Increased reports of psychiatric events (depression, suicidal ideation/behaviour) in clinical trials. Assess psychiatric history before initiation and monitor during treatment [Australian].
- **Malignancy:** As an immunomodulator, belimumab may increase the risk of malignancies [Australian].
- **Immunisation:** Live vaccines should not be administered concurrently or within 30 days of belimumab [Australian].
- **Concomitant Therapy:** Use with other B-cell targeted therapies (e.g., rituximab) is not recommended due to insufficient data [Australian].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Frequency Table
| Frequency | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Bacterial infections (e.g., bronchitis, UTI), Diarrhoea, Nausea, Headache, Nasopharyngitis |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Viral gastroenteritis, Pharyngitis, Leucopenia, Hypersensitivity reactions, Depression, Migraine, Pyrexia, Injection site reactions (SC only), Pain in extremity |
| **Uncommon (0.1–1%)** | Anaphylactic reaction, Angioedema, Suicidal ideation/behaviour |
| **Rare (<0.1%)** | Delayed-type, non-acute hypersensitivity reactions (rash, nausea, fatigue) |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Mortality imbalance was noted in early IV trials (0.8% belimumab vs 0.4% placebo), primarily due to infection, cardiovascular events, or suicide. Recent SC trials showed a more balanced profile (0.5% vs 0.7%) [Australian].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** No formal *in vivo* studies performed. Belimumab is unlikely to affect CYP450 enzymes directly, though an indirect effect via cytokine modulation cannot be ruled out [Australian].
- **Clinical Significance:** No meaningful effect on belimumab pharmacokinetics was observed when co-administered with corticosteroids, antimalarials, NSAIDs, or immunosuppressants (azathioprine, methotrexate, mycophenolate) [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [Australian Category C]. There are limited data in humans. Animal studies in monkeys showed B-cell depletion in infants. Belimumab should not be used unless the benefit clearly outweighs the risk. Women of childbearing potential must use effective contraception during and for at least 4 months after treatment [Australian].
- **Breastfeeding:** Safety is not established. Use should consider the benefits of breastfeeding for the infant against the mother's clinical need for the drug. Excretion into human milk is unknown, though IgG is known to be excreted [Australian].
- **Fertility:** No human data available [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy in children under 18 years have not been established; use is not approved [Australian].
- **Elderly (>65 years):** Limited data. No dose adjustment is recommended, but caution is advised due to the small sample size in clinical trials [Australian].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment is required for mild, moderate, or severe impairment. Caution is recommended in severe impairment due to limited data [Australian].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No formal studies conducted. As a monoclonal antibody, belimumab is catabolised by proteolytic enzymes, making hepatic-mediated clearance changes unlikely [Australian].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Belimumab is a BLyS-specific inhibitor. It binds to soluble B Lymphocyte Stimulator (BLyS) protein, preventing it from binding to its receptors on B cells. This inhibits B-cell survival and reduces the differentiation of B cells into immunoglobulin-producing plasma cells [Australian].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Significant reductions in circulating B cells (naïve, transitional, and plasma cells) and autoantibodies (anti-dsDNA) are observed, along with increases in complement levels (C3, C4) [Australian].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Bioavailability (SC):** ~74%.
    - **Distribution:** Volume of distribution ~5 L.
    - **Terminal Half-life:** ~18 days (IV) to ~18.3 days (SC) [Australian].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **SLE (BLISS-52 & BLISS-76):** Pivotal trials in 1,684 patients demonstrated a significantly higher SLE Responder Index (SRI) at Week 52 in the belimumab 10 mg/kg group compared to placebo. Primary endpoint components included ≥4-point reduction in SELENA-SLEDAI, no new BILAG A/B organ scores, and no worsening in Physician’s Global Assessment [Australian].
- **Lupus Nephritis (BEL114054):** A 104-week study in 448 patients showed that belimumab plus standard therapy (mycophenolate or cyclophosphamide/azathioprine) significantly increased the Primary Efficacy Renal Response (PERR) at Week 104 (43% vs 32.3%, p=0.0311) [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vials/Pre-filled Pens:** Store at 2°C to 8°C (Refrigerate. Do not freeze). Protect from light by storing in the original carton [Australian].
- **Reconstituted/Diluted Solution (IV):** Total time from reconstitution to completion of infusion must not exceed 8 hours. If not used immediately, store at 2°C to 8°C or room temperature [Australian].
- **SC Devices:** Once removed from the refrigerator, must be administered within 12 hours if the pack is opened [Australian].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule:** Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine) [Australian].
