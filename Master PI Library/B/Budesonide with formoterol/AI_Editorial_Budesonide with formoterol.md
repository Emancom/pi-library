# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
*   This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **US (FDA)**.
*   The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical data into a concise, professional reference.
*   **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information approved for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory guidelines for specific legal and prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN/USP)
*   **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
*   **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
*   **ATC Code:** A16AA01
*   **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
*   **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency in Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Indicated for both acute and chronic management of patients with metabolic defects resulting in secondary deficiency [US].
*   **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients undergoing chronic hemodialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
*   **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg. This may be administered as a slow bolus injection (2–3 minutes) or via infusion.
    *   **Loading Dose:** In cases of severe metabolic crisis, a loading dose is often administered, followed by an equivalent dose divided over the subsequent 24 hours.
    *   **Frequency:** Dosing should occur every 3 or 4 hours, and never less frequently than every 6 hours.
    *   **Maximum Dose:** While daily doses are typically 50 mg/kg, higher doses may be required; the maximum studied dose is 300 mg/kg [US].
*   **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    *   **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight).
    *   **Administration Timing:** Administer as a slow (2–3 minute) bolus into the venous return line immediately following each dialysis session.
    *   **Titration:** Adjustments should be guided by pre-dialysis (trough) plasma levocarnitine levels. Dose reductions (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].

### Administration Instructions
*   Parenteral products must be inspected for particulate matter and discoloration before use.
*   **Compatibility:** Stable for up to 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) when diluted in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations between 0.5 mg/mL and 8.0 mg/mL in PVC bags [US].

## 4. Contraindications
*   None known at the time of publication [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
*   **Boxed Warnings:** None [US].
*   **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, particularly in ESRD patients. These may occur within minutes of administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue immediately and do not re-administer unless benefits clearly outweigh risks, under close monitoring [US].
*   **Seizures:** Levocarnitine may increase the frequency or severity of seizures in patients with pre-existing seizure disorders. Seizures have also been reported in patients without a prior history of epilepsy [US].
*   **Monitoring:** 
    *   Establish baseline plasma carnitine levels before starting therapy.
    *   Monitor weekly and monthly, including blood chemistry and vital signs.
    *   **Target Levels:** Plasma free carnitine should ideally be maintained between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are often confounded by the underlying pathology (ESRD or metabolic disease).

*   **Common (>10%) [US]:**
    *   Gastrointestinal: Transient nausea, vomiting, and diarrhea.
    *   Nervous System: Dizziness, headache.
    *   Respiratory: Pharyngitis, cough, dyspnea.
    *   Cardiovascular: Hypertension, hypotension.
    *   General: Pain, injection site reactions, flu-like symptoms.
*   **Uncommon/Rare (<1%) [US]:**
    *   Body odor (specifically a "fishy" odor).
    *   Gastritis.
    *   Seizures.
*   **Serious Adverse Effects:** Anaphylaxis, bronchospasm, and increased seizure activity [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
    *   **Management:** Monitor INR levels closely upon initiation of levocarnitine or following any dose adjustments in patients receiving oral anticoagulants [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy:** 
    *   **US Classification:** Not specified as a letter category in the current label, but reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (BSA-based) showed no evidence of fetal harm or impaired fertility. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
*   **Breastfeeding:** Not specifically studied in humans. While studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine in milk following administration, the risks to the infant must be weighed against the benefits to the mother. Discontinuation of nursing or the drug may be considered [US].
*   **Fertility:** Animal studies showed no impairment [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders follows the mg/kg weight-based guidelines provided for adults [US].
*   **Elderly Use:** Clinical documents do not specify age-related dose adjustments; however, cautious dosing is generally advised for geriatric patients due to potential comorbidities.
*   **Renal Impairment:** 
    *   **Oral Levocarnitine Caution:** Safety of *oral* levocarnitine has not been evaluated in renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in ESRD patients may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites (trimethylamine/TMA and trimethylamine-N-oxide/TMAO) normally excreted in urine [US].
    *   **Hemodialysis:** Intravenous levocarnitine is effectively removed by dialysis [US].
*   **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
*   **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring carrier molecule. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also aids in the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
*   **Pharmacokinetics:**
    *   **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
    *   **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    *   **Elimination:** Following IV bolus (20 mg/kg), approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. The terminal elimination half-life is approximately 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients, IV levocarnitine (20 mg/kg post-dialysis) increased mean pre-dialysis plasma concentrations from ~20 µmol/L to ~190 µmol/L at 8 weeks. 
*   **Dose-Ranging:** Trough concentrations remained stable or increased over 12–24 weeks of therapy across 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg dose groups.
*   **Clinical Outcomes:** While IV levocarnitine increases plasma concentrations in ESRD patients, its effect on specific clinical outcomes or signs/symptoms of carnitine deficiency in this population has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   **Temperature:** Store between 20°C and 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
*   **Handling:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat.
*   **Stability:** Single-dose vials; contains no preservative. Discard any unused portion [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
*   **Legal Classification:** Rx only [US].
*   **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in provided US sources.
