# Editorial Product Information: baptisia tinctoria

## 1. Product Identification
- Active ingredient (INN/generic name): **baptisia tinctoria**
- Chemical class: Homeopathic plant-derived preparation (wild indigo root) formulated as a 
  homeopathic nasal spray
- ATC code: Not specified in available sources
- Route(s) of administration: Nasal

## 2. Regulatory History
- Marketing/registration status: Marketed as an unapproved homeopathic nasal product in the 
  United States. [US]
- Date of marketing start (as stated in source): 01/21/2019. [US]
- Date of last revision/update of the source PI (as stated): Revised 1/2019. [US]
- Withdrawal, suspension, or restriction history: Not specified in available sources
- Additional regulatory notes: This product is classified as an unapproved homeopathic 
  product. The product labeling includes a regulatory disclaimer stating that it has not 
  undergone FDA evaluation for safety or efficacy, and that the FDA does not recognise 
  scientific evidence supporting homeopathy as an effective treatment modality. Product 
  claims are based on traditional homeopathic practice and are not accepted as medical 
  evidence. [US]

## 3. Approved Indications
- Indications as stated on product labeling (claims based on traditional homeopathic 
  practice; not evaluated for efficacy):
  Temporary relief of symptoms associated with upper respiratory and sinonasal conditions, 
  including:
  - Difficulty breathing
  - Mucous blockage and accumulation of fluid
  - Swelling of mucous membranes
  - Running nose
  - Sinus pressure and congestion
  - Blocked nose
  - Temporary relief of colds and sinus irritations
  - These labeled indications are presented on the product labeling as marketed. [US]

## 4. Dosage and Administration
- General dosing (adult):
  - Adults and children 13 years and older: 1–3 sprays into each nostril every 2–4 hours 
    as needed. [US]
- Pediatric dosing:
  - Children 3–12 years: half the adult dose, administered every 2–4 hours as needed. [US]
  - Children under 3 years: a healthcare professional should be consulted prior to use. 
    Note: one labeling panel references "children under 2 years" for this precaution; 
    the more conservative threshold of under 3 years appears in the primary Drug Facts 
    labeling. [US]
- Administration instructions:
  - The protective cap should be removed before use.
  - With the head held in an upright position, the nozzle is inserted into the nostril 
    and the pump is fully depressed while inhaling through the nose.
  - Upon completion, the nozzle should be wiped clean and the cap replaced. [US]
- Titration schedules: Not specified in available sources
- Missed dose instructions: Not specified in available sources
- Maximum daily dose or duration limits: Not specified in available sources; label 
  instructs not to exceed the recommended dosage. [US]

## 5. Contraindications
- Formal contraindications: Not specified in available sources.
- Product/packaging-related contraindication (label instruction): The product should not 
  be used if the full-body seal over the bottle is broken or missing, as this indicates 
  a potential product integrity concern. [US]

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
- Boxed warnings: None specified in available sources.
- General warnings and precautions:
  - The recommended dosage should not be exceeded. [US]
  - Transient local discomfort may occur following nasal administration, such as a 
    burning sensation, stinging, sneezing, or a temporary increase in nasal secretions. [US]
  - Sharing the spray container between individuals carries a risk of transmitting 
    infection. [US]
  - Individuals who are pregnant or breastfeeding should seek advice from a qualified 
    healthcare professional prior to use. [US]
  - This product must be kept out of reach of children. In the event of accidental 
    ingestion, immediate medical assistance or contact with a Poison Control Center is 
    advised. [US]
  - The product must not be used if the tamper-evident seal is absent or compromised. [US]
  - All labeled indications are based on traditional homeopathic practice and have not 
    been evaluated for safety or efficacy by regulatory authorities. [US]
- Monitoring requirements: Not specified in available sources
- Specific clinical precautions:
  - To minimise the risk of cross-infection, the spray device should not be shared 
    between individuals. [US]
