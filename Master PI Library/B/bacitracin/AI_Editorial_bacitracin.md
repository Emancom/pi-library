# Editorial Product Information: Bacitracin Zinc

## Editorial Note
*   This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (Drug Facts) provided by the **US (FDA)** for Bacitracin Zinc Ointment.
*   The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising available regulatory data into a single, clinically relevant reference.
*   **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis intended for informational use by healthcare professionals and does not substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing or dispensing in any specific jurisdiction. At the time of writing, only documentation from the United States was provided for review.

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredient:** Bacitracin Zinc (Polypeptide antibiotic).
*   **Chemical Class:** Polypeptide.
*   **Brand Name(s):** Globe Bacitracin Zinc Ointment USP [US].
*   **ATC Code:** R02AB04 (Throat preparations), D06AX05 (Topical antibiotics); not explicitly listed in the provided source document.
*   **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    *   Topical Ointment: 500 Units of Bacitracin Zinc per gram [US].
*   **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical (External use only).
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Distributed by Trifecta Pharmaceuticals USA; Relabeled by Preferred Pharmaceuticals Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **First Aid Antibiotic:** Indicated for the prevention of infection in minor cuts, scrapes, and burns [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Children)
*   **Preparation:** Clean the affected area prior to application.
*   **Application:** Apply a small amount of the product (equivalent to the surface area of the tip of a finger) to the affected area 1 to 3 times daily [US].
*   **Dressing:** The treated area may be covered with a sterile bandage if necessary [US].

## 4. Contraindications
*   **Hypersensitivity:** Do not use if there is a known allergy to any of the ingredients (Bacitracin Zinc, aloe vera gel, light mineral oil, or white petrolatum) [US].
*   **Ophthalmic Use:** Contraindicated for use in the eyes [US].
*   **Extensive Application:** Do not apply over large areas of the body [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### General Warnings
*   **For External Use Only.**
*   **Duration of Use:** Limit treatment to no longer than one week unless directed by a physician [US].
*   **Professional Consultation Required:** Patients should consult a physician before use in the following circumstances:
    *   Deep puncture wounds.
    *   Animal bites.
    *   Serious burns [US].

### Monitoring Requirements
*   Treatment should be discontinued, and a healthcare provider consulted if:
    *   The skin condition persists or worsens.
    *   A rash or symptoms of an allergic reaction develop [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
*   **Frequency Not Specified:** The available source does not provide statistical frequency data for adverse effects.
*   **Dermatological/Immunological:** Rash and allergic reactions (hypersensitivity) are the primary reported adverse effects requiring cessation of therapy [US].
*   **Serious Adverse Effects:** Systemic toxicity is not typical for topical application as directed, but hypersensitivity reactions may occur.

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Management:** Clinicians should monitor for local reactions if co-administered with other topical agents.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Breastfeeding:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Fertility:** Not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use:** Use as directed for minor wounds; however, the product must be kept out of reach of children. If accidental ingestion occurs, medical help or Poison Control Center contact is required immediately [US].
*   **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Renal Impairment:** Not specified in available sources (systemic absorption is generally minimal with topical use).
*   **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
*   **Mechanism of Action:** Identified as a "First Aid Antibiotic" for infection prevention. (Bacitracin generally inhibits bacterial cell wall synthesis by preventing the dephosphorylation of the C55-isoprenyl pyrophosphate, though this specific detail is not in the provided label) [US].
*   **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Pharmacodynamics:** Not specified in available sources.

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   Key pivotal trial summaries and specific outcomes are not described in the provided regulatory label documentation.

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   **Temperature:** Store between 15°C and 25°C (59°F to 77°F) [US].
*   **Handling:** Ensure the tube is tightly closed when not in use. Refer to the tube crimp or box for lot number and expiration date.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
