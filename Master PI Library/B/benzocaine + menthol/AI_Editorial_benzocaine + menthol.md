# Editorial Product Information: benzocaine + menthol

## 1. Product Identification
- Active ingredient (INN/generic name): **benzocaine + menthol**
- Chemical class: Not specified in available sources
- ATC code: Not specified in available sources
- Route(s) of administration: Topical (oral mucosal application)
- Strength (as stated in available sources): benzocaine 200 mg/mL (20%); menthol 1 mg/mL (0.1%)

## 2. Regulatory History
- Date of first registration/approval by jurisdiction: 08/07/2020 [US] (marketing start date provided in source documents)
- Date of last revision/update of each source PI: Revised: 6/2026 [US]
- Any withdrawal, suspension, or restriction history: Not specified in available sources
- Additional regulatory/marketing classification noted in source: OTC monograph listing (monograph identifier provided in source) [US]

## 3. Approved Indications
- Temporary relief of oral pain due to canker sores (aphthous ulcers).
- Temporary relief of pain from minor injury of the mouth and gums.
- Temporary relief of minor irritation of the mouth and gums caused by dentures or orthodontic appliances.
- Region-specific note: Indications presented above are those included in the available source PI [US]. Indication status in other jurisdictions: Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Dosage and Administration
### General dosing (adult and paediatric where specified)
- Strengths: benzocaine 200 mg/mL and menthol 1 mg/mL (as provided).
- Adults and children 2 years of age and over: apply product to the affected area with an applicator brush up to 4 times daily or as directed by a dentist or doctor [US].
- Children between 2 and 12 years of age: should be supervised in the use of this product [US].
- Children under 2 years of age: do not use; contraindicated (see Section 5) [US].

### Administration instructions
- Remove safety seal from bottle cap prior to first use [US].
- Apply with applicator brush directly to the affected oral mucosal area [US].
- Keep away from fire or flame during and after application due to alcohol content (flammability) [US].

### Duration of use / treatment limits
- Do not use for more than 7 days unless directed by a dentist or doctor [US].

### Titration schedules

### Missed dose instructions

## 5. Contraindications
List of contraindications and explicit "do not use" statements as provided in the source PI:
- Known allergy or hypersensitivity to local anesthetics of the "caine" type, including but not limited to procaine, butacaine, benzocaine, or other "caine" anesthetics (Allergy Alert) [US].
- Do not use in children under 2 years of age [US].
- Do not use for teething [US].
- Do not use more than directed [US].
- Do not use for more than 7 days unless directed by a dentist or doctor [US].
- Do not use if the safety seal on the bottle cap is broken or missing prior to opening [US].
- Note: the source PI also advises immediate medical attention for accidental ingestion/overdose (see Section 6 and 7).

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed warning / prominent safety alert
- **Methemoglobinemia warning:** Use of this product may cause methemoglobinemia, a serious condition that reduces the oxygen-carrying capacity of the blood and requires prompt treatment. This can occur even in patients who have used the product previously. Seek immediate medical attention if symptoms develop (see signs and symptoms below). [US]

### General warnings and precautions
- Allergy Alert: Do not use if there is a history of allergy to local anesthetics of the "caine" class (procaine, butacaine, benzocaine, others) [US].
- Flammable: product contains alcohol (reported ~54.5% by volume); keep away from fire or flame and avoid smoking during application [US].
- Supervision: children between 2 and 12 years should be supervised when using the product [US].
- Do not use more than directed and do not use for more than 7 days unless under dental/medical direction [US].
- Stop use and seek medical advice if swelling, rash, or fever develops; if irritation, pain, or redness persists or worsens; or if sore mouth symptoms do not improve in 7 days [US].
- Keep out of reach of children. If more than is used for pain relief is accidentally swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center immediately [US].

### Monitoring requirements
- Monitor for signs/symptoms of methemoglobinemia (see below) and for allergic skin or systemic reactions.
- In the event of ingestion beyond recommended use, arrange urgent medical assessment or Poison Control Center consultation [US].

### Specific clinical precautions
- Avoid use in situations where ingestion risk is increased (e.g., very small children or inability to limit swallowing) — children under 2 are explicitly not to be treated [US].
- Given flammability, avoid exposure to ignition sources during and immediately after application [US].

## 7. Adverse Effects
- All adverse effects listed in the available source PI are included below. Frequency estimates were not provided in the source and are therefore indicated as "Not specified in available sources."

- Serious adverse effects to highlight:
  - **Methemoglobinemia** — serious and potentially life-threatening (see Warning).  
  - Allergic reactions (including hypersensitivity to "caine" local anesthetics).

### Adverse effects table (by System Organ Class)
| System organ class | Adverse effect | Frequency |
|---|---:|---|
| Blood and lymphatic system disorders | Methemoglobinemia (reduced oxygen-carrying capacity of blood) | Not specified in available sources |
| Respiratory, thoracic and mediastinal disorders | Shortness of breath (can be a symptom of methemoglobinemia) | Not specified in available sources |
| Cardiac disorders | Rapid heart rate (tachycardia) (can be associated with methemoglobinemia) | Not specified in available sources |
| Nervous system disorders | Headache; dizziness; lightheadedness; fatigue or lack of energy | Not specified in available sources |
| Skin and subcutaneous tissue disorders | Rash; swelling; irritation; redness | Not specified in available sources |
| Immune system disorders | Allergic hypersensitivity reactions to local anesthetics ("caine" allergy) | Not specified in available sources |
| General disorders and administration site conditions | Pain or irritation at application site (if persists or worsens, stop use) | Not specified in available sources |

- Note: The source PI instructs to stop use and seek medical care for pale, gray, or blue colored skin (cyanosis), headache, rapid heart rate, shortness of breath, dizziness or lightheadedness, fatigue or lack of energy — these are presented above as signs/symptoms and adverse events associated with methemoglobinemia [US].
