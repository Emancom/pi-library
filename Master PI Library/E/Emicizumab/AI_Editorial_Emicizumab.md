# Editorial Product Information: Emicizumab (Hemlibra)

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) document from the following jurisdiction: **Australian (TGA)**.
- **Purpose:** To provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, specifically pharmacists and prescribers, regarding the clinical use of emicizumab.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and should not be used as a substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Emicizumab (INN); a humanised, modified monoclonal IgG4 bispecific antibody.
- **Chemical Class:** Recombinant bispecific monoclonal antibody produced in Chinese hamster ovary (CHO) cells.
- **Brand Name:** **Hemlibra** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** B02BX06.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:**
    - Solution for subcutaneous injection in single-use glass vials.
    - 30 mg/1 mL (30 mg/mL concentration).
    - 60 mg/0.4 mL (150 mg/mL concentration).
    - 105 mg/0.7 mL (150 mg/mL concentration).
    - 150 mg/1 mL (150 mg/mL concentration).
- **Route of Administration:** Subcutaneous.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Roche Products Pty Limited [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Haemophilia A Prophylaxis:** Indicated for routine prophylaxis to prevent or reduce the frequency of bleeding episodes in adult and paediatric patients with congenital factor VIII deficiency (Haemophilia A).
- **Inhibitor Status:** Approved for patients with or without factor VIII inhibitors [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Loading Dose:** 3 mg/kg administered via subcutaneous injection once weekly for the first 4 weeks.
- **Maintenance Dose (starting from Week 5):** 
    - 1.5 mg/kg once weekly, OR
    - 3 mg/kg every two weeks, OR
    - 6 mg/kg every four weeks.
- The choice of maintenance regimen should be based on clinician/patient preference to support adherence.

### Administration Instructions
- For subcutaneous use only. Do not administer intravenously or intramuscularly.
- **Injection Sites:** Abdomen, upper outer arms, or thighs. The upper outer arm should only be used by a caregiver or healthcare professional.
- **Technique:** Rotate injection sites. Do not inject into moles, scars, or areas where the skin is bruised, red, tender, or hard.
- **Preparation:** Vials are ready to use and do not require dilution. Do not shake.
- **Combining Vials:** Multiple vials may be combined in a single syringe to deliver the prescribed dose. However, different concentrations (30 mg/mL and 150 mg/mL) must **not** be combined in the same syringe [Australian].

### Missed Dose Instructions
- If a dose is missed, it should be administered as soon as possible, provided it is before the day of the next scheduled dose.
- Resume the usual schedule for the next dose. Do not administer two doses on the same day to compensate for a missed dose.

## 4. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to emicizumab, any of the excipients (arginine, histidine, poloxamer, aspartic acid), or to hamster-derived proteins [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warning [Australian]
**Thrombotic Microangiopathy and Thromboembolism:** Cases of thrombotic microangiopathy (TMA) and thrombotic events (TE) have been reported when patients receiving **Hemlibra** prophylaxis were administered a cumulative amount of > 100 U/kg/24 hours of activated prothrombin complex concentrate (aPCC) for 24 hours or more. Monitor patients closely for TMA and TE if aPCC is administered. Discontinue aPCC and suspend **Hemlibra** dosing if clinical or laboratory symptoms occur.

### Guidance on Bypassing Agents
- **Discontinuation:** Bypassing agents must be discontinued the day before starting **Hemlibra**. 
- **aPCC Usage:** Avoid aPCC unless no other options exist. If required, the initial dose should not exceed 50 U/kg, and the total dose in the first 24 hours should not exceed 100 U/kg.
- **rFVIIa:** Clinical trials showed no TMA or TE with the use of activated recombinant human factor VII (rFVIIa) alone.

### Laboratory Test Interference
- **Intrinsic Pathway Assays:** Emicizumab restores the tenase cofactor activity of factor VIIIa. Therefore, any test based on the intrinsic clotting pathway (e.g., aPTT, ACT, one-stage FVIII activity assays) will yield **overly shortened** clotting times and invalid results.
- **Monitoring:** Do not use aPTT-based tests to monitor **Hemlibra** activity, FVIII levels, or inhibitor titres.
- **Affected Tests:** aPTT, Bethesda assays (clotting-based), ACT, and aPTT-based APC-R.
- **Unaffected Tests:** Thrombin time (TT), bovine chromogenic FVIII activity assays (insensitive to emicizumab), and chromogenic Bethesda assays (bovine-based).

### Immunogenicity
- Anti-emicizumab antibodies occur in a small percentage of patients (approx. 5.1%). Neutralising antibodies may lead to a loss of efficacy, manifest as increased breakthrough bleeding.

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common Adverse Effects
| System Organ Class | Adverse Reaction | Frequency |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| General | Injection site reactions | Very Common (21%) |
| Nervous System | Headache | Very Common (14%) |
| Musculoskeletal | Arthralgia | Very Common (16%) |
| General | Pyrexia | Common (6%) |
| Gastrointestinal | Diarrhoea | Common (5%) |
| Musculoskeletal | Myalgia | Common (4%) |
| Skin | Urticaria, Rash | Common |

### Serious Adverse Effects
- **Thrombotic Microangiopathy (TMA):** Reported in <1% of the total study population but in 9.7% of those receiving high-dose aPCC [Australian].
- **Thrombotic Events (TE):** Including cavernous sinus thrombosis and superficial vein thrombosis with skin necrosis (<1% overall).
- **Hypersensitivity:** Including angioedema (uncommon).

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Activated Prothrombin Complex Concentrate (aPCC):** Major interaction; risk of hypercoagulability/TMA/TE (see Section 5).
- **rFVIIa and FVIII:** Potential for hypercoagulability based on preclinical data; lower doses of these factors may be required to achieve haemostasis while on **Hemlibra**.
- No formal drug-drug interaction studies have been conducted.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - [Australian Category B2].
    - No clinical studies in pregnant women. Animal studies are limited.
    - Placental transfer is expected as it is an IgG antibody.
    - Use only if the potential benefit to the mother outweighs the potential risk to the fetus. 
- **Breastfeeding:** Safety is not established. It is unknown if emicizumab is excreted in human milk. Endogenous IgG is present in milk.
- **Fertility:** No adverse effects on reproductive organs observed in animal studies (cynomolgus monkeys).

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy established in children of all ages. No dose adjustments are required. Lower concentrations may occur in infants < 6 months old due to pharmacokinetic differences [Australian].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical studies included limited patients ≥ 65 years. No specific dose adjustment is recommended, though bioavailability may decrease slightly with age.
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment needed for mild or moderate renal impairment. Data for severe renal impairment are unavailable.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment needed for mild or moderate hepatic impairment. Not studied in severe hepatic impairment.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Emicizumab is a bispecific monoclonal antibody that bridges activated factor IX and factor X. This restores the function of the missing activated factor VIII, which is a required cofactor in the coagulation cascade.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Half-life of 1.6 days. Bioavailability is 80–93% depending on the site (thigh vs. arm).
    - **Distribution:** Apparent volume of distribution is 10.4 L.
    - **Metabolism:** Expected to be catabolised by lysosomal proteolysis.
    - **Elimination:** Terminal half-life is approximately 4 to 5 weeks (approx. 27 days).
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Prophylaxis shortens aPTT and increases reported FVIII activity in chromogenic assays (though these do not reflect the true in vivo haemostatic effect).

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **HAVEN 1:** Randomised, open-label trial in adults/adolescents with FVIII inhibitors. Demonstrated 87% reduction in treated bleeds vs. no prophylaxis.
- **HAVEN 2:** Single-arm trial in paediatric patients (<12 years or <40kg) with inhibitors. Demonstrated high efficacy (ABR of 0.3).
- **HAVEN 3:** Randomised trial in adults/adolescents without FVIII inhibitors. 1.5 mg/kg weekly and 3 mg/kg fortnightly maintenance both showed >96% reduction in bleeds vs. no prophylaxis.
- **HAVEN 4:** Single-arm trial evaluating 6 mg/kg every 4 weeks. Demonstrated sustained bleed control (ABR 2.4).

## 12. Storage Conditions
- Store at 2°C to 8°C (Refrigerate. Do not freeze).
- Keep the vial in the outer carton to protect from light.
- **Room Temperature Stability:** Unopened vials can be kept at room temperature (up to 30°C) for a cumulative period of 7 days. Vials may be returned to the refrigerator if not used.
- Once transferred to a syringe, the solution should be used immediately.
