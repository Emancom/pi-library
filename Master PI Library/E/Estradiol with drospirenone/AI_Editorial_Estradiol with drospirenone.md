# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the **United States (FDA)** via Baxter Healthcare Corporation. 
- At the time of this editorial preparation, official PI documents for other jurisdictions (e.g., Australian, European, Canadian, Japanese) were not provided for comparison. 
- This document serves as a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals and is not a substitute for the official PI required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN/USP), a carrier molecule for long-chain fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection, USP [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01 (not explicitly stated in source, but standard for levocarnitine).
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** 
    - Sterile injection solution [US].
    - 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) single-dose vials [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US]; Manufactured by Baxter Pharmaceuticals India Private Ltd [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency in Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Indicated for both acute and chronic treatment of patients with metabolic defects resulting in secondary deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD) on Dialysis:** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
- **General Dosing:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg [US].
- **Administration:** Delivered as a slow bolus injection (2–3 minutes) or via infusion. 
- **Loading Dose:** In cases of severe metabolic crisis, a loading dose is often administered, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours.
- **Dosing Interval:** Should be administered every 3 or 4 hours (q3h or q4h); frequency should never be less than every 6 hours (q6h).
- **Maximum Dose:** The highest administered dose reported is 300 mg/kg [US].

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
- **Administration:** Administered as a slow 2–3 minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
- **Adjustments:** Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations (normal range: 40–50 µmol/L) [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter and discoloration before use.
- **Compatibility:** Stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations between 0.5 mg/mL and 8 mg/mL [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Known Hypersensitivity:** While the source lists "None known" in the contraindications section, serious hypersensitivity reactions are noted in the Warnings section.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions can occur within minutes of administration [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity may occur [US].
- **Monitoring:** For ESRD patients, weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L) is recommended [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Common (>10%):** Transient nausea and vomiting [US].
- **Other Reported Effects:** Body odor, gastritis, and various systemic reactions. In ESRD clinical trials, many events were confounded by the underlying pathology.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have occurred when levocarnitine is used concomitantly with warfarin. It is recommended to monitor INR levels at treatment initiation and after dose adjustments [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not explicitly lettered, but the PI states reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine is a normal component of human milk. Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Consider the risk of excess carnitine intake in the infant versus the benefit of treatment to the mother [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders and ESRD is weight-based (mg/kg) as described in Section 3 [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Safety and efficacy of *oral* levocarnitine have not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in ESRD patients may lead to accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [US]. 
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into cellular mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (feces).
    - **Excretion:** Following IV administration, approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. Terminal elimination half-life is approximately 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Efficacy:** Studies showed that 20 mg/kg IV three times weekly after dialysis significantly increased plasma levocarnitine levels from a baseline of ~20 µmol/L to trough levels of ~190 µmol/L after 8 weeks. 
- **Note:** While efficacy in increasing carnitine concentrations is proven, the impact on clinical outcomes in ESRD patients has not been determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C–30°C [US].
- **Protection:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat [US].
- **Handling:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as it contains no preservative [US].
