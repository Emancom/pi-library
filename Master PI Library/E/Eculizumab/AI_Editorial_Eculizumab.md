# Editorial Product Information: Eculizumab

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the **Australian (TGA)**. 
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of eculizumab for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises clinical, regulatory, and pharmacological data into a single reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI or the specific regulatory requirements for prescribing within a particular jurisdiction. For legal prescribing information, clinicians should consult the current approved PI in their respective country.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Eculizumab rmc (a genetically-engineered humanised monoclonal antibody; terminal complement inhibitor).
- **Brand Name:** **Soliris** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** L04AA25 (Selective immunosuppressants).
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Concentrated solution for intravenous (IV) infusion; 300 mg single-use glass vial containing 30 mL (10 mg/mL).
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous infusion only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Alexion Pharmaceuticals Australasia Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
**Soliris** is indicated for the treatment of:
- **Paroxysmal Nocturnal Haemoglobinuria (PNH):** To reduce haemolysis.
- **Atypical Haemolytic Uraemic Syndrome (aHUS).**
- **Neuromyelitis Optica Spectrum Disorder (NMOSD):** Specifically in adult patients who are anti-aquaporin-4 (AQP4) antibody-positive. It is not intended for the acute treatment of an NMOSD relapse [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults ≥18 years)
- **PNH Regimen:**
    - *Initial Phase:* 600 mg via IV infusion weekly for the first 4 weeks.
    - *Maintenance Phase:* 900 mg on week 5, followed by 900 mg every 14 ± 2 days.
- **aHUS and NMOSD Regimen:**
    - *Initial Phase:* 900 mg via IV infusion weekly for the first 4 weeks.
    - *Maintenance Phase:* 1200 mg on week 5, followed by 1200 mg every 14 ± 2 days.

### Paediatric Dosing (PNH and aHUS)
For patients <18 years, dosing is weight-based.
- **≥ 40 kg:** Use adult dosing.
- **30 to < 40 kg:** 600 mg (wks 1-2) initial; 900 mg (wk 3), then 900 mg q2w maintenance.
- **20 to < 30 kg:** 600 mg (wks 1-2) initial; 600 mg (wk 3), then 600 mg q2w maintenance.
- **10 to < 20 kg:** 600 mg (wk 1) initial; 300 mg (wk 2), then 300 mg q2w maintenance.
- **5 to < 10 kg:** 300 mg (wk 1) initial; 300 mg (wk 2), then 300 mg q3w maintenance.
*Note: Safety/efficacy in paediatric NMOSD is not established.*

### Supplemental Dosing (Plasma Interventions)
Supplemental doses are required during plasmapheresis (PP), plasma exchange (PE), or plasma infusion (PI):
- **PP/PE:** 300–600 mg (based on most recent dose) within 60 minutes after each session.
- **PI:** 300 mg 60 minutes prior to each session.

### Administration Instructions
- **Dilution:** Must be diluted to a final concentration of 5 mg/mL (e.g., 300 mg in 30 mL diluent). Compatible diluents include 0.9% NaCl, 0.45% NaCl, 5% Dextrose, or Ringer's solution.
- **Infusion Time:** 25–45 minutes in adults; 1–4 hours in paediatric patients. Do not administer as a bolus or IV push.
- **Monitoring:** Monitor for at least 1 hour post-infusion for signs of infusion reactions.

## 4. Contraindications
- Hypersensitivity to eculizumab, murine proteins, or any excipients.
- Unresolved *Neisseria meningitidis* infection.
- Patients not currently vaccinated against *Neisseria meningitidis* (unless receiving 2 weeks of prophylactic antibiotics) [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warning
**Serious Meningococcal Infection:** Eculizumab increases the risk of life-threatening or fatal meningococcal infections. Vaccination (A, B, C, Y, and W135) is mandatory at least 2 weeks prior to therapy unless the risk of delay outweighs the infection risk (in which case 2 weeks of antibiotics are required). Patients must be monitored for early symptoms (e.g., fever, headache with stiff neck).

### General Precautions
- **Other Infections:** Increased susceptibility to encapsulated bacteria and *Neisseria* species (including disseminated gonococcal infections).
- **Infusion Reactions:** May cause anaphylaxis. If severe, interrupt infusion and initiate appropriate therapy.
- **Discontinuation Risks:** 
    - *PNH:* Monitor for serious intravascular haemolysis (LDH elevation) for at least 8 weeks post-discontinuation.
    - *aHUS:* Risk of severe thrombotic microangiopathy (TMA). Discontinuation is not recommended unless clinically justified; close monitoring is required.
- **Sodium Content:** Contains 5 mmol sodium per vial; consider for patients on controlled sodium diets.

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Very Common (≥10%):** Headache (most common in initial phase), nasopharyngitis, upper respiratory tract infection, fatigue, diarrhoea, back pain, dizziness.
- **Common (1–10%):** Pneumonia, bronchitis, urinary tract infection, oral herpes, insomnia, dysgeusia, hypertension, cough, oropharyngeal pain, abdominal pain, vomiting, nausea, alopecia, pruritus, rash, arthralgia, myalgia, pyrexia, influenza-like illness.
- **Uncommon (0.1–1%):** Meningococcal sepsis, fungal infection, thrombocytopenia, anaphylactic reaction, vision blurred, tinnitus, palpitation, renal impairment.
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Meningococcal sepsis (most serious), TMA complications (following missed doses or discontinuation), and serious systemic infections.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **IVIg:** Chronic IVIg may interfere with FcRn recycling, potentially decreasing eculizumab serum concentrations.
- **FcRn Blockers:** Concomitant use may lower eculizumab exposure; monitor for reduced effectiveness.
- **Plasma Interventions:** PP, PE, and PI significantly reduce serum levels, requiring supplemental dosing.
- **Laboratory Tests:** No known drug-laboratory interactions [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** **Category B2** [Australian]. Limited data (under 300 outcomes) suggest no increased risk of major malformations. Eculizumab crosses the placenta. Use only if benefits justify risks to the mother and fetus. Women should use contraception during and for 5 months after treatment.
- **Breastfeeding:** IgG is present in human milk, but detectable levels of eculizumab are not reported. Benefits of breastfeeding should be weighed against the mother's clinical need and potential infant risks.
- **Fertility:** Animal studies using surrogate antibodies showed no adverse effects on fertility.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Approved for PNH and aHUS (weight-based dosing). Not established for NMOSD in patients <18 years.
- **Elderly (≥65 years):** No evidence suggesting special precautions, though clinical experience is limited.
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment required.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Safety and efficacy have not been studied.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** A terminal complement inhibitor that binds with high affinity to C5, preventing its cleavage to C5a and C5b. This inhibits the formation of the terminal complement complex C5b-9 (membrane attack complex). It preserves early complement components necessary for opsonisation.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Complete blockade of terminal complement is associated with free C5 concentrations <0.5 µg/mL.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - *Distribution:* Mean volume of distribution (Vd) ~110 mL/kg [PNH].
    - *Metabolism:* Endocytotic digestion into small peptides and amino acids.
    - *Elimination:* Half-life ~11.3 days (PNH) and ~12.4 days (aHUS). Steady state is reached in 49–56 days. Clearance is increased by 50% during plasma exchange.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **PNH (TRIUMPH and SHEPHERD):** Demonstrated significant reduction in LDH, improved haemoglobin stabilisation, and reduced need for blood transfusions compared to placebo.
- **aHUS:** Prospective studies showed eculizumab led to platelet count normalisation and improved renal function (eGFR), even in patients who were dialysis-dependent at baseline.
- **NMOSD (ECU-NMO-301):** A 94.2% reduction in the risk of adjudicated relapse was observed in AQP4-antibody positive patients treated with eculizumab compared to placebo.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vials:** Store at 2°C to 8°C (Refrigerate. Do not freeze). Protect from light.
- **Excursion:** Unopened vials in the original package may be kept at up to 25°C for a single period of up to 3 days.
- **Diluted Solution:** Stable for 24 hours. If not used immediately, store at 2°C to 8°C for no more than 24 hours.
- **Handling:** Do not shake vials.
