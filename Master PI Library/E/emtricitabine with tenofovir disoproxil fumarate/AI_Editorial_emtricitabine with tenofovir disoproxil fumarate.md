# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- Data regarding other jurisdictions (e.g., Australian, European, Canadian, Japanese) was not provided in the source documents and is therefore not included in this harmonised document.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. It is a clinical synthesis and must not be used as a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific PI when prescribing or dispensing in a specific region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP), a carrier molecule for long-chain fatty acids.
- **Chemical Class:** (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Brand Names:** Noted generically as **Levocarnitine Injection**.
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Injection, 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) single-dose vial [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency in Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Indicated for both acute and chronic treatment of patients with metabolic defects that result in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **Carnitine Deficiency in End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
- **General Dosing:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg.
- **Administration:** Administer as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion.
- **Severe Crisis:** A loading dose is often given, followed by an equivalent dose divided over the next 24 hours.
- **Maintenance:** Administer every 3 or 4 hours; intervals should never be less than every 6 hours. Subsequent daily doses typically remain at 50 mg/kg, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been used.
- **Monitoring:** Obtain plasma carnitine concentrations before therapy. Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma concentrations (target: 35 to 60 µmol/L) is recommended.

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Recommended Dosing:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg of dry body weight.
- **Administration:** Administer as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
- **Adjustments:** Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine levels (target: 40 to 50 µmol/L).

### Administration Instructions
- Visually inspect for particulate matter or discoloration prior to use.
- Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as it contains no preservatives.

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in patients with ESRD on dialysis. Some reactions occur within minutes of intravenous administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue the drug immediately and initiate medical treatment [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with a known history, an increase in seizure frequency or severity may occur [US].
- **Renal Impairment (Oral Formulation Caution):** While the IV formulation is indicated for ESRD, the safety and efficacy of **oral** levocarnitine have not been evaluated in renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in these patients may result in the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) [US].
- **Monitoring Requirements:** Periodic monitoring of plasma carnitine levels is essential to guide dosing.

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Very Common (>10%) and Common (1–10%)
Based on clinical trials in ESRD patients, the following events occurred at a frequency ≥5% (regardless of causality) [US]:
- **General:** Injection site reaction (33%), pain (30%), chest pain (12%), flu syndrome (25%), headache (22%), infection (15%), abdominal pain (9%), fever (7%).
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (20%), hypotension (14%), tachycardia (6%), palpitations (5%).
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea (16%), vomiting (15%), nausea (8%), anorexia (5%), dyspepsia (5%).
- **Metabolic:** Hypercalcemia (9%), hyperkalemia (6%), hypervolemia (5%).
- **Nervous System:** Dizziness (13%), insomnia (4%), paresthesia (5%), depression (5%).
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (23%), cough (9%), rhinitis (9%), dyspnea (7%).

### Other Reported Reactions
- **Less Frequent:** Body odor, gastritis.
- **Serious:** Anaphylaxis, seizures [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is used concomitantly with warfarin. It is recommended to monitor INR levels at the start of therapy and following dose adjustments [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US-specific data]: Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at doses up to 3.8 times the human dose (based on surface area) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. However, there are no adequate/well-controlled human studies. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed.
- **Breastfeeding:** Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine concentrations in milk following exogenous administration. The risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant must be weighed against the benefits to the mother [US].
- **Fertility:** Mutagenicity tests (Salmonella, Saccharomyces, Schizosaccharomyces) were negative. Long-term animal carcinogenicity studies have not been performed [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders is weight-based (50 mg/kg), which applies to the paediatric population [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically detailed in provided sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** IV administration is indicated for carnitine deficiency in ESRD patients on dialysis. Dosing is adjusted based on post-dialysis dry body weight and plasma trough levels (see Section 3) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production. It also facilitates the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In ESRD patients, dialysis causes carnitine loss. Levocarnitine injection increases plasma carnitine concentrations.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
  - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
  - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces).
  - **Elimination:** Following a single IV bolus (20 mg/kg), approximately 76% is excreted in urine within 24 hours. The mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours, and the terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients receiving 20 mg/kg three times weekly after dialysis showed mean pre-dialysis (trough) levels increased from ~20 µmol/L to ~190 µmol/L by week 8.
- **Dose-Ranging Studies:** Trials comparing 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses confirmed dose-proportional increases in trough levocarnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Protection:** Avoid excessive heat; protect from freezing.
- **Stability:** Stable for up to 24 hours when mixed in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations between 0.5 mg/mL and 8.0 mg/mL, stored at 25°C in PVC bags [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Rx-only. NDC 36000-368-10 (10 x 5 mL vials).
