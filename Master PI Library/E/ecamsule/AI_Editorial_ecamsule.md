# Editorial Product Information: Ecamsule (Multi-Ingredient Formulation)

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial: United States (FDA). 
- The primary source document is the "Drug Facts" and labeling for Kiehl’s Dermatologist Solutions Ultra Light Daily UV Defense Sunscreen.
- This document serves as a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. It is an editorial synthesis and must not be used as a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific PI when prescribing or recommending therapy in a specific region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** 
    - **Ecamsule** (Terephthalylidene Dicamphor Sulfonic Acid) [US]
    - **Drometrizole Trisiloxane** [US]
    - **Titanium Dioxide** [US]
    - **Bemotrizinol** [US]
    - **Diethylamino Hydroxybenzoyl Hexyl Benzoate** [US]
    - **Octinoxate** (Ethylhexyl Methoxycinnamate) [US]
- **Chemical Class:** Combined organic (chemical) and inorganic (physical) UV filters.
- **Brand Name(s):** Kiehl's Dermatologist Solutions Ultra Light Daily UV Defense Sunscreen SPF 50 [US].
- **ATC Code:** D02BA (Sunscreen for topical use) [Generic classification].
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - **Topical Lotion [US]:** Each 1 mL contains:
        - Ecamsule: 120 mg
        - Octinoxate: 67.5 mg
        - Drometrizole Trisiloxane: 40 mg
        - Titanium Dioxide: 40 mg
        - Bemotrizinol: 5 mg
        - Diethylamino Hydroxybenzoyl Hexyl Benzoate: 5 mg
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** L'Oreal USA Products, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Broad-spectrum Sun Protection:** High protection (SPF 50 / PA++++) against UVA and UVB radiation [US].
- **Anti-Pollution:** Formulated to defend skin against environmental aggressors [US].
- **Photo-aging Prevention:** Helps prevent the first signs of skin aging caused by sun exposure [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** Apply to the face and neck daily.
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Apply in the morning as the final step of a daily skincare routine [US].
    - Avoid the immediate eye area [US].
    - In case of contact with eyes, rinse immediately and thoroughly with water [US].
- **Re-application:** To maintain protection, the product should be re-applied frequently and generously, particularly after swimming, perspiring, or toweling [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Not specified in available sources, though standard clinical practice contraindicates use in patients with known hypersensitivity to any active or inactive ingredient (see Section 1 for full list).
- **Broken Skin:** Not specified in available sources.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None [US].
- **Sun Overexposure:** Healthcare professionals should advise patients that overexposure to the sun is a serious health threat. Sunscreen does not provide 100% protection [US].
- **General Precautions:**
    - Avoid contact with eyes [US].
    - Do not stay too long in the sun, even when using a sunscreen product [US].
- **Specific Clinical Precautions:** The formula is labeled as non-comedogenic, paraben-free, and fragrance-free, suitable for sensitive skin [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Frequency and Severity:** Not specified in available source documents.
- **Local Reactions:** As with any topical sunscreen, potential for contact dermatitis or local skin irritation exists, though not explicitly listed in the provided US Drug Facts.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Not specified in available sources. No US FDA pregnancy category is assigned in the provided OTC documentation.
- **Breastfeeding:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Fertility:** Not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Use with caution. The labeling explicitly advises keeping babies and young children out of direct sunlight [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified; however, systemic absorption of these topical agents is generally minimal.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** 
    - **Ecamsule:** A photostable organic filter that provides strong UVA protection.
    - **Titanium Dioxide:** A physical (inorganic) blocker that reflects and scatters UV radiation.
    - **Octinoxate/Bemotrizinol/Drometrizole Trisiloxane:** Chemical filters that absorb UV radiation and dissipate it as heat.
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** The product provides a Sun Protection Factor (SPF) of 50 and a UVA protection rating of PA++++ [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Summary:** Pivotal trial data was not detailed in the provided source document. The product is marketed based on established UV filter efficacy and SPF testing standards.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Shelf Life:** 12 months after opening (indicated by the 12M symbol) [US].
- **Handling:** Keep in a cool, dry place. Ensure the cap is tightly closed when not in use.
