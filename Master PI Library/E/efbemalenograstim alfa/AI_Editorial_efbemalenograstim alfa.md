# Editorial Product Information: efbemalenograstim alfa

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdiction:
    - **United States** (Food and Drug Administration)
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising available regulatory data into a single clinically relevant resource.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory approved labelling for legal and prescribing compliance.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active ingredient:** Efbemalenograstim alfa-vuxw [US]. It is a 413 amino acid recombinant fusion protein consisting of human G-CSF, a 16 amino-acid linker, and the Fc portion of human IgG2.
- **Chemical Class:** Leukocyte growth factor; recombinant human granulocyte colony-stimulating factor (rhG-CSF) Fc-fusion protein.
- **Brand Name:** **RYZNEUTA** [US].
- **ATC Code:** L03AA (Proprietary leukocyte growth factor).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - **Injection:** 20 mg/mL solution in a single-dose prefilled syringe [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Subcutaneous injection.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** 
    - **United States:** Distributed by Acrotech Biopharma Inc.; Manufactured by Evive Biotechnology Singapore Pte. Ltd.

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Febrile Neutropenia Prophylaxis:** Indicated to decrease the incidence of infection, manifested by febrile neutropenia, in adult patients with non-myeloid malignancies receiving myelosuppressive anti-cancer drugs associated with a clinically significant incidence of febrile neutropenia [US].
- **Limitations of Use:**
    - Not indicated for the mobilization of peripheral blood progenitor cells for hematopoietic stem cell transplantation [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adults):** The recommended dose is 20 mg administered once per chemotherapy cycle [US].
- **Timing of Administration:** 
    - Administer approximately 24 hours after cytotoxic chemotherapy. 
    - **Restricted Period:** Do not administer in the period between 14 days before and 24 hours after the administration of cytotoxic chemotherapy [US].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Administered by a healthcare professional [US].
    - Allow the prefilled syringe to reach room temperature for at least 30 minutes before injection (keep inside the carton to protect from light) [US].
    - Inspect visually for particulates or discoloration; do not use if observed.
    - Inject into the abdomen, thighs, or back/side of the upper arms. Rotate injection sites. Avoid scar tissue or areas that are inflamed or bruised.
- **Latex Caution:** The needle cap contains natural rubber latex, which may trigger allergic reactions in sensitive individuals [US].
- **Missed Dose:** If a dose is missed, the patient should contact their healthcare provider to determine the next appropriate dose timing [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Contraindicated in patients with a history of serious allergic reactions to granulocyte colony-stimulating factors (G-CSFs) such as efbemalenograstim alfa, pegfilgrastim, or filgrastim products [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Splenic Rupture:** May occur, including fatal cases. Evaluate patients presenting with left upper abdominal or shoulder pain [US].
- **Acute Respiratory Distress Syndrome (ARDS):** Evaluate patients developing fever, lung infiltrates, or respiratory distress. Discontinue treatment if ARDS occurs [US].
- **Serious Allergic Reactions:** Including anaphylaxis. Permanently discontinue if a serious reaction occurs [US].
- **Sickle Cell Crisis:** Severe and sometimes fatal crises can occur in patients with sickle cell disorders. Discontinue if a crisis occurs [US].
- **Glomerulonephritis:** Has been reported. If suspected, evaluate for causality and consider dose reduction or interruption [US].
- **Leukocytosis:** White blood cell (WBC) counts of $\ge 100 \times 10^9\text{/L}$ have been observed. Monitor CBC and discontinue if counts exceed this threshold [US].
- **Thrombocytopenia:** Reported in 11% of patients. Monitor platelet counts [US].
- **Capillary Leak Syndrome (CLS):** Characterized by hypotension, hypoalbuminemia, edema, and hemoconcentration. Requires close monitoring and may require intensive care [US].
- **MDS and AML:** Use in breast and lung cancer patients receiving chemotherapy/radiotherapy has been associated with an increased risk of Myelodysplastic Syndrome and Acute Myeloid Leukemia. Monitor for signs and symptoms [US].
- **Aortitis:** Reported with G-CSFs. Symptoms include fever, abdominal pain, malaise, and back pain [US].
- **Nuclear Imaging:** Increased bone marrow hematopoietic activity may cause transient positive bone imaging changes; consider this when interpreting results [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Very Common ($\ge 10\%$):**
    - Nausea (51%)
    - Anemia (15%)
    - Thrombocytopenia (12%) [US]
- **Common (1–10%):**
    - Febrile neutropenia (4.8% in RYZNEUTA arm vs 25.6% in placebo arm in Study GC-627-04) [US].
- **Other Notable Reactions:** 
    - Fatigue and bone pain were reported by $\ge 20\%$ of patients in specific active-controlled trials [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Splenic rupture, ARDS, anaphylaxis, sickle cell crisis, and capillary leak syndrome [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Cytotoxic Chemotherapy:** Due to the potential sensitivity of rapidly dividing myeloid cells to G-CSF, do not administer efbemalenograstim alfa within 14 days before or 24 hours after cytotoxic chemotherapy [US].
- **Other Interactions:** Not specifically studied. No other clinically significant interactions specified in available sources [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Data in pregnant women are insufficient to establish drug-associated risks. 
    - **Animal Data:** In rats and rabbits, no evidence of structural malformations or fetal loss was observed at doses approximately 2.6 and 0.7 times the human dose, respectively. Maternal toxicity (weight loss/death) was observed at higher doses in rabbits [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** There are no data on the presence of the drug in human milk, effects on the breastfed child, or effects on milk production. The benefits of breastfeeding should be weighed against the mother's clinical need for the drug [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies in rats showed no effect on reproductive performance or fertility at doses 2.2 times the human dose [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical studies did not identify differences in responses between elderly and younger patients, though the sample size for those $\ge 65$ years was limited [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** The impact of renal impairment on pharmacokinetics is unknown [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** The impact of hepatic impairment on pharmacokinetics is unknown [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** A colony-stimulating factor that binds to specific cell surface receptors on hematopoietic cells, stimulating proliferation, differentiation, commitment, and end-cell functional activation of neutrophils [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Neutrophils increase in a dose-dependent manner over the range of 30 to 360 $\mu\text{g/kg}$ [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Median $T_{\text{max}}$ ranges from 24 to 48 hours [US].
    - **Distribution:** Apparent volume of distribution is approximately 18.8 L (Cycle 1) to 40.7 L (Cycle 3) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Non-linear and time-dependent. Apparent clearance increases between Cycle 1 (0.36 L/h) and Cycle 3 (0.76 L/h). The geometric mean elimination half-life is approximately 36 hours [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Expected to be catabolized into small peptides via standard catabolic pathways [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Study GC-627-04:** A placebo-controlled study in 122 female patients with breast cancer. Efbemalenograstim alfa significantly reduced the mean duration of severe (Grade 4) neutropenia (1.4 days vs 4.3 days for placebo; $p < 0.001$) and the incidence of febrile neutropenia (4.8% vs 25.6%) [US].
- **Study GC-627-05:** An active-controlled study in 393 female patients with breast cancer comparing RYZNEUTA to pegfilgrastim. The mean duration of Grade 4 neutropenia was 0.2 days in both arms, demonstrating non-inferiority to pegfilgrastim [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store refrigerated at $2^\circ\text{C}$ to $8^\circ\text{C}$ ($36^\circ\text{F}$ to $46^\circ\text{F}$) [US].
- **Special Handling:**
    - Store in original carton to protect from light.
    - Do not shake.
    - Do not freeze; discard if frozen.
- **Shelf Life/Stability:** Discard any product left at room temperature for more than 48 hours [US].
