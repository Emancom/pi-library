# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) provided by the **United States (FDA)**. 
- At the time of this editorial preparation, documentation from other major jurisdictions (e.g., Australian, European, Canadian, Japanese) was not provided for comparison. Consequently, the clinical recommendations and regulatory statuses reflect the **United States** perspective.
- This document serves as a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals and is not a substitute for the official PI required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN/USP); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Metabolic Disorders:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients presenting with an inborn error of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Pediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    - The recommended dose is **50 mg/kg** administered as a slow bolus (2–3 minutes) or by infusion [US].
    - In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose may be followed by equivalent doses every 3 to 4 hours (never less than every 6 hours) [US].
    - Subsequent daily doses are typically 50 mg/kg, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilised [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - Recommended starting dose: **10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg** (dry body weight) [US].
    - Administration should occur as a slow bolus (2–3 minutes) into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].

### Monitoring and Titration
- **Plasma Concentrations:** Monitoring of plasma carnitine levels is recommended weekly or monthly. Target free carnitine concentrations should ideally be between **35 and 60 µmol/L** [US].
- **Dose Adjustments in ESRD:** Adjustments may be made as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough (pre-dialysis) concentrations [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Inspect visually for particulate matter or discoloration prior to use [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when diluted in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations ranging from 0.5 mg/mL to 8.0 mg/mL [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions can occur within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, the drug must be discontinued immediately and appropriate therapy initiated [US].
- **Oral Formulation Caution:** The safety and efficacy of **oral** levocarnitine have not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in patients with severely compromised renal function may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites: trimethylamine (TMA) and trimethylamine-N-oxide (TMAO) [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Levocarnitine has been associated with reports of seizures in patients with or without pre-existing seizure disorders. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse events reported in clinical trials for ESRD patients (occurring at ≥5% regardless of causality) include:

| Body System | Adverse Event |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Abdominal pain, asthenia, chest pain, fever, flu syndrome, headache, infection, injection site reaction, pain. |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension, hypotension, tachycardia, arrhythmia [US]. |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea, nausea, vomiting, anorexia, constipation, dyspepsia [US]. |
| **Metabolic/Nutritional** | Hypercalcemia, hypervolemia, peripheral edema, weight changes [US]. |
| **Nervous System** | Dizziness, insomnia, paresthesia, depression [US]. |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis, cough increase, dyspnea, rhinitis [US]. |
| **Other** | Body odor (transient), anemia, pruritus, eye disorders, taste perversion [US]. |

**Serious Adverse Effects:** Anaphylaxis and seizures are the most clinically significant serious adverse events reported [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalised Ratio (INR) have occurred when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** It is recommended that INR levels be monitored upon initiation of levocarnitine or following any dose adjustment in patients receiving warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not specified [US].
    - Animal reproductive studies (rats and rabbits) at 3.8 times the human dose showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. There are no adequate/well-controlled studies in pregnant women. Use only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. In dairy cows, carnitine concentrations in milk increase following administration. Clinicians must weigh the risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant against the benefits of maternal supplementation [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility was found in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing recommendations for metabolic disorders apply to paediatric patients. Safety and efficacy in this population are established for the treatment of carnitine deficiency [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Specific data not provided in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Intravenous levocarnitine is specifically indicated for use in ESRD patients on dialysis. However, **oral** levocarnitine should be avoided due to metabolite (TMA/TMAO) accumulation [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring carrier molecule required for the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane. This process provides substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production in tissues (except the brain), especially skeletal and cardiac muscle [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an intravenous dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. The mean apparent terminal elimination half-life is **17.4 hours** [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Levocarnitine can promote the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids in patients with metabolic defects by forming acylcarnitine complexes that are quickly excreted [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Efficacy:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients, 20 mg/kg doses three times weekly post-dialysis increased mean trough concentrations from 21.3 µmol/L to 190 µmol/L after 8 weeks [US].
- **Dose-Ranging Data:** Studies comparing 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses showed dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [US].
- **Clinical Outcome Note:** While levocarnitine increases plasma concentrations in ESRD, its effect on specific clinical outcomes (e.g., muscle weakness, cardiomyopathy) in this population has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C [US].
- **Handling:** Avoid excessive heat. Protect from freezing.
- **Integrity:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as the formulation contains no preservative [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as Rx-only (Human Prescription Drug) [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources for other jurisdictions.
