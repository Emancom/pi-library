# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial has been synthesised from official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document is a harmonised editorial overview intended for use by healthcare professionals. It aims to provide a clear, concise, and clinically accurate summary of the drug's profile across the provided regulatory source.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific PI required for prescribing. Clinicians should consult the local approved PI for definitive guidance.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN).
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Sterile solution for injection: 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency:** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that result in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion. 
    - In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose is often administered, followed by an equivalent dose divided over the next 24 hours.
    - Dosing frequency should be every 3 or 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours.
    - Subsequent daily doses typically range around 50 mg/kg, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilised [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:** The recommended starting dose is 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight) administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration.
- **Compatibility:** Stable for up to 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations between 0.5 mg/mL and 8.0 mg/mL [US].

### Monitoring and Titration
- **Plasma Monitoring:** It is recommended to obtain plasma carnitine levels prior to starting parenteral therapy. Weekly and monthly monitoring should follow.
- **Target Levels:** Plasma free carnitine concentrations should ideally be maintained between 35 and 60 µmol/L. 
- **Dose Adjustments (ESRD):** Adjustments should be guided by pre-dialysis (trough) plasma carnitine levels. Downward titration (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients on dialysis. Some reactions occur within minutes of administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue use and initiate appropriate medical treatment [US].
- **Seizures:** Levocarnitine (oral or IV) has been associated with seizures in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been reported [US].
- **Oral Formulation Caution:** The safety and efficacy of **oral** levocarnitine have not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency. Chronic high doses of oral levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function or ESRD may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions reported in clinical trials for ESRD patients (frequency $\geq$5%) regardless of causality:
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (20%), Hypotension (14%), Tachycardia (6%), Chest pain (12%).
- **Gastrointestinal:** Diarrhea (16%), Vomiting (15%), Nausea (8%), Abdominal pain (9%).
- **Neurological:** Headache (22%), Dizziness (13%), Paresthesia (5%), Insomnia (4%).
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (23%), Cough increase (9%), Dyspnea (7%).
- **Body as a Whole:** Injection site reaction (33%), Pain (30%), Flu syndrome (25%), Asthenia (9%), Fever (7%).
- **Metabolic:** Hypercalcemia (9%), Hyperkalemia (6%).

**Serious Adverse Effects:**
- Anaphylaxis and severe hypersensitivity.
- Seizures.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalised Ratio (INR) have been observed when levocarnitine is used concomitantly with warfarin. 
- **Management:** It is recommended to monitor INR levels frequently after initiating levocarnitine or following dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not explicitly assigned a letter grade in current labeling, but reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) revealed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. 
    - **Recommendation:** Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Animal data (cows) indicate carnitine concentrations in milk increase following exogenous administration. Risks to the infant from excess carnitine must be weighed against the benefits of maternal supplementation [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** See Section 3 for dosing in metabolic disorders.
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in the available source; clinical studies did not include sufficient numbers of subjects aged 65 and over to determine if they respond differently than younger subjects [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Specifically indicated for ESRD patients on dialysis. However, accumulation of metabolites (TMA/TMAO) is a concern with chronic **oral** administration in renal failure [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In patients with inborn errors of metabolism or ESRD, levocarnitine helps clear accumulated toxic acyl-CoA compounds by forming acylcarnitine, which is rapidly excreted.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and $\gamma$-butyrobetaine (primarily in faeces).
    - **Elimination:** Following IV bolus (20 mg/kg), approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. The mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients on hemodialysis receiving 20 mg/kg IV three times weekly, mean pre-dialysis (trough) levocarnitine levels rose from a baseline of $21.3 \pm 7.7$ µmol/L to $190 \pm 55$ µmol/L after 8 weeks.
- **Note:** While levocarnitine increases carnitine concentrations in ESRD patients, the effects on specific clinical outcomes in this population have not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Conditions:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat.
- **Handling:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as the formulation contains no preservatives [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Prescription only (Rx-only).
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources for other jurisdictions.
