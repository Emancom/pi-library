# Editorial Product Information: Epcoritamab

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial:
    - **Australian (TGA)**: EPKINLY (epcoritamab) solution for injection.
- **Purpose**: This document is a harmonised editorial synthesis of epcoritamab information intended for healthcare professionals. It provides a clinically focused overview of the drug's profile, administration, and safety.
- **Note**: This editorial is for informational purposes and does not replace the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Always consult the local approved PI for full prescribing details.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient**: Epcoritamab (humanised bispecific IgG1 antibody).
- **Brand Name(s)**: **EPKINLY** [Australian].
- **ATC Code**: L01FX27.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s)**:
    - Concentrate solution for injection (requires dilution): 4 mg/0.8 mL single-dose vial [Australian].
    - Solution for injection (ready-to-use): 48 mg/0.8 mL single-dose vial [Australian].
- **Route of Administration**: Subcutaneous (SC) injection only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor**: AbbVie Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Relapsed or Refractory Diffuse Large B-cell Lymphoma (DLBCL)**: Indicated for the treatment of adult patients after two or more lines of systemic therapy.
    - [Australian: Provisional approval based on overall response rate and duration of response in an uncontrolled study; continued approval is dependent on verification of clinical benefit in confirmatory trials].
- **Limitations of Use**: Not indicated for the treatment of patients with primary central nervous system lymphoma [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
Epcoritamab is administered in 28-day cycles until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity. A step-up dosing schedule is required in Cycle 1 to minimize the risk of Cytokine Release Syndrome (CRS).

### Step-up Dosing Schedule (Cycle 1)
- **Day 1 (Priming dose)**: 0.16 mg
- **Day 8 (Intermediate dose)**: 0.8 mg
- **Day 15 (First full dose)**: 48 mg
- **Day 22 (Full dose)**: 48 mg

### Maintenance Schedule
- **Cycles 2–3**: 48 mg weekly (Days 1, 8, 15, and 22).
- **Cycles 4–9**: 48 mg every two weeks (Days 1 and 15).
- **Cycles 10 and beyond**: 48 mg every four weeks (Day 1).

### Pre-medication and Prophylaxis
- **Hydration**: Patients must be adequately hydrated.
- **Pre-medication (Cycle 1)**: Required for all patients. Administer prednisolone (100 mg oral) or dexamethasone (16 mg oral/15 mg IV), diphenhydramine (50 mg oral), and paracetamol (1,000 mg oral) 30–120 minutes before each dose. Corticosteroids should be continued for three days following each Cycle 1 dose.
- **PCP and Herpesvirus Prophylaxis**: Strongly recommended, particularly during concurrent steroid use.

### Administration Instructions
- Inject subcutaneously, preferably into the lower abdomen or thigh. 
- Rotate injection sites, especially during the weekly dosing phase.

### Missed or Delayed Dose
A **re-priming cycle** (identical to Cycle 1 step-up dosing) is required if:
- >8 days between the 0.16 mg and 0.8 mg doses.
- >14 days between the 0.8 mg and the first 48 mg dose.
- >6 weeks between 48 mg doses.

## 4. Contraindications
- Hypersensitivity to epcoritamab or any of its excipients (including sorbitol, sodium acetate trihydrate, glacial acetic acid, and polysorbate 80) [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warnings
- **Cytokine Release Syndrome (CRS) and Neurological Toxicity**: CRS and Immune Effector Cell-Associated Neurotoxicity Syndrome (ICANS), including fatal reactions, can occur. Treatment must be initiated with step-up dosing. Patients should be monitored closely, especially for 24 hours after the first 48 mg dose [Australian].

### General Warnings and Precautions
- **Cytokine Release Syndrome (CRS)**: Occurred in 50% of patients, primarily in Cycle 1. Median time to onset was 2 days from the most recent dose. Management includes withholding the drug and administering tocilizumab and/or corticosteroids based on severity.
- **ICANS**: Manifestations include aphasia, altered consciousness, and seizures. Median onset was 16.5 days from treatment initiation. 
- **Serious Infections**: Increased risk of fatal bacterial, viral, or fungal infections. 
- **Progressive Multifocal Leukoencephalopathy (PML)**: Reported in patients receiving epcoritamab. Monitor for new/worsening neurological symptoms; if PML is suspected, withhold the drug.
- **Tumour Lysis Syndrome (TLS)**: Monitor blood chemistry in patients with high tumour burden or renal impairment. 
- **Tumour Flare**: Localised pain and swelling at tumour sites. Exercise caution if tumours are near airways or vital organs.
- **Haemophagocytic Lymphohistiocytosis (HLH)**: Consider if CRS is atypical or unresponsive.

### Monitoring Requirements
- Monitor for signs of CRS/ICANS for at least 24 hours after the first 48 mg dose. 
- Regular monitoring of CBC, LFTs, and immunoglobulin (Ig) levels.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions**: No formal drug interaction studies have been conducted.
- **CYP450 Substrates**: Epcoritamab-induced cytokine release may suppress CYP450 enzyme activity. For patients on CYP450 substrates with a narrow therapeutic index, therapeutic drug monitoring is recommended upon initiation of epcoritamab.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy**: [Australian: Category C]. Epcoritamab may cause fetal harm, including B-cell lymphocytopenia and increased risk of opportunistic infections in the neonate. IgG1 antibodies are known to cross the placenta.
- **Contraception**: Females of reproductive potential should use effective contraception during treatment and for **4 months** after the final dose.
- **Breastfeeding**: Safety is unknown. Due to the presence of IgGs in human milk, breastfeeding should be discontinued during treatment and for **4 months** after the final dose.
- **Fertility**: No animal data available; effects on human fertility are unknown.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use**: Safety and efficacy have not been established in patients <18 years of age.
- **Elderly Use ($\ge$ 65 years)**: No clinically meaningful differences in safety or efficacy were observed compared to younger adults.
- **Renal Impairment**: No dose adjustment for mild-to-moderate impairment (CrCl $\ge$ 30 mL/min). No data available for severe renal impairment or end-stage renal disease.
- **Hepatic Impairment**: No dose adjustment for mild impairment. Not studied in patients with moderate-to-severe hepatic impairment.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action**: Epcoritamab is a bispecific antibody that simultaneously binds CD3 on T cells and CD20 on B cells. This leads to T-cell activation and subsequent T-cell-mediated lysis of CD20-expressing malignant B cells.
- **Pharmacodynamics**: Induces rapid depletion of circulating B cells and transient elevations in pro-inflammatory cytokines (IFN-$\gamma$, TNF$\alpha$, IL-6) which typically peak 1–4 days after the first 48 mg dose.
- **Pharmacokinetics**:
    - **Absorption**: Peak concentrations ($T_{max}$) occur around 3–4 days post-dose.
    - **Distribution**: Central volume of distribution is approximately 8.27 L.
    - **Metabolism**: Expected to be degraded into small peptides/amino acids via catabolic pathways.
    - **Elimination**: Half-life is approximately 22–25 days at the 48 mg dose.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **EPCORE NHL-1 Study**: A single-arm, open-label trial in patients with R/R LBCL (N=139 in pivotal cohort).
- **Primary Outcomes**:
    - **Overall Response Rate (ORR)**: 62% (95% CI: 53.3, 70).
    - **Complete Response (CR) Rate**: 39% (95% CI: 30.7, 47.5).
    - **Median Duration of Response (DOR)**: 12 months.
    - **Median Time to CR**: 2.7 months.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vials**: Store at 2°C to 8°C. Do not freeze.
- **Protection**: Keep the vial in the original carton to protect from light. Do not shake.
- **Diluted/Prepared Solution**: Use immediately. If necessary, store at 2°C to 8°C for up to 24 hours. Within this window, the solution may be held at room temperature for up to 12 hours (from preparation to administration).
