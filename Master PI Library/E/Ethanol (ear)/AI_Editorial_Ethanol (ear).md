## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** A complex homeopathic preparation containing multiple components including:
    - **Cytokines/Biologics:** Interleukin-12 (human), Interferon gamma-1a.
    - **Glandular/Organotherapy (Sus scrofa/Bos taurus):** Adrenal gland, lymph vessel, spleen, thymus.
    - **Botanicals:** *Apis mellifera*, *Fumaria officinalis*, *Pinus sylvestris*, *Piper nigrum*, *Prunus spinosa*, *Tilia tomentosa*.
    - **Allersodes (Spring Pollens):** *Fraxinus americana*, *Betula nigra*, *Juglans nigra*, *Ulmus americana*, *Quercus rubra*, *Fagus grandifolia*, *Populus deltoides*, *Carya alba*, *Platanus racemosa*, *Salix repens*, *Acer saccharum*.
    - **Minerals/Chemicals:** Calcium sulfide, Gallic acid, Histamine, Potassium carbonate, Manganese, Quercetin, Rutin, Salsolinol, Silicon dioxide, Sulfuric acid.
    - **Other:** Ethylicum (Ethanol), *Hepar sulphuris calcareum*, *Kali carbonicum*, *Silicea*.
- **Brand Name:** Pollens Early [US]
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Oral spray. Potencies range from 6X, 12X, and 10X to 30K and 200K.
- **Route of Administration:** Oral (mucosal spray).
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Viatrexx Bio Incorporated [US].
