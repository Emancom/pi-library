# Editorial Product Information: Evinacumab

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial: US (FDA). 
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of **evinacumab** for healthcare professionals, synthesising regulatory data into a single clinical reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and should not be used as a substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Evinacumab-dgnb; a recombinant human monoclonal antibody (IgG4 isotype).
- **Chemical Class:** Angiopoietin-like protein 3 (ANGPTL3) inhibitor.
- **Brand Name(s):** **Evkeeza** [US].
- **ATC Code:** C10AX17.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Injection: 345 mg/2.3 mL (150 mg/mL) solution in a single-dose vial [US].
    - Injection: 1,200 mg/8 mL (150 mg/mL) solution in a single-dose vial [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Regeneron Pharmaceuticals, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Homozygous Familial Hypercholesterolemia (HoFH):** Indicated as an adjunct to diet, exercise, and other low-density lipoprotein-cholesterol (LDL-C) lowering therapies for the reduction of LDL-C in adults and pediatric patients aged 1 year and older [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric $\ge$ 1 year):** 15 mg/kg administered via intravenous infusion once every 4 weeks (monthly) [US].
- **Preparation Instructions:**
    - Calculate dose based on current body weight.
    - Withdraw required volume and transfer to an IV bag containing 0.9% Sodium Chloride or 5% Dextrose.
    - **Final Concentration:** 0.5 mg/mL to 20 mg/mL [US].
    - Mix by gentle inversion; do not shake.
- **Maximum Diluent Volume by Weight [US]:**
    - 3 kg to < 26 kg: 5 mL/kg.
    - 26 kg to < 45 kg: 150 mL.
    - $\ge$ 45 kg: 250 mL.
- **Administration:** 
    - Infuse over 60 minutes.
    - Must use an IV line containing a sterile, in-line or add-on, 0.2-micron to 5-micron filter.
    - Do not co-administer other medications through the same infusion line.
- **Missed Dose:** Administer as soon as possible. Thereafter, schedule doses monthly from the date of the last administered dose [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** History of serious hypersensitivity reactions to **evinacumab** or any of its excipients [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Serious Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Anaphylaxis and other serious reactions have occurred. If signs of a reaction occur, discontinue the infusion immediately and initiate appropriate standard-of-care. **Evinacumab** is contraindicated in those with a history of serious hypersensitivity to the drug [US].
- **Embryo-Fetal Toxicity:** Based on animal data (rabbits), **evinacumab** may cause fetal harm. Advise patients of reproductive potential of the risk to the fetus [US].
- **Monitoring:** Assess LDL-C levels as early as 2 weeks after treatment initiation to monitor efficacy [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions observed in clinical trials (24-week pooled data) [US]:

| Adverse Reaction | Frequency (%) in **Evinacumab** (N=81) | Frequency (%) in Placebo (N=54) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Nasopharyngitis | 16% | 13% |
| Influenza-like illness | 7% | 6% |
| Dizziness | 6% | 0% |
| Rhinorrhea | 5% | 0% |
| Nausea | 5% | 2% |
| Pain in extremity | 4% | 0% |
| Asthenia | 4% | 0% |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Anaphylaxis was reported in 1% of patients treated with **evinacumab** [US].
- **Infusion Reactions:** Reported in 7% of patients; symptoms included infusion site pruritus, pyrexia, muscular weakness, nausea, and nasal congestion [US].
- **Pediatric Specifics:** Fatigue was more common in pediatric patients aged 5 to 11 years (15%) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** No formal drug interaction studies have been conducted. **Evinacumab** does not meaningfully alter concentrations of concomitant statins (atorvastatin, rosuvastatin, simvastatin) [US].
- **Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** **Evinacumab** concentrations were comparable in patients with HoFH whether or not they were taking background lipid-lowering therapies [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not assigned a letter grade; however, animal studies showed fetal malformations (hydrocephalus, domed head, flexed limbs) at doses below the human exposure at the MRHD.
    - **Contraception:** Females of reproductive potential should use effective contraception during treatment and for at least 5 months after the final dose [US].
    - **Testing:** Consider pregnancy testing prior to initiating treatment.
- **Breastfeeding:** There is no data on the presence of **evinacumab** in human milk. Human IgG is known to be present in milk; the risk to the infant should be weighed against the clinical benefit to the mother [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies in monkeys and rats showed no adverse effects on surrogate markers of fertility or reproductive organs [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy are established in patients aged 1 to 17 years. Safety and efficacy in patients < 1 year have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical trials did not include enough patients $\ge$ 65 years to determine if they respond differently than younger subjects [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Trough concentrations were comparable in patients with mild or moderate renal impairment. Data for severe renal impairment are unavailable [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No data are available for patients with hepatic impairment [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** **Evinacumab** is a recombinant human monoclonal antibody that inhibits ANGPTL3. ANGPTL3 normally inhibits lipoprotein lipase (LPL) and endothelial lipase (EL). By inhibiting ANGPTL3, **evinacumab** increases LPL and EL activity, leading to reduced LDL-C, TG, and HDL-C. Crucially, it lowers LDL-C independent of the LDL receptor (LDLR) by promoting VLDL processing and clearance upstream of LDL formation [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Results in reductions in LDL-C, total cholesterol, apolipoprotein B, and triglycerides [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Steady State:** Reached after 4 doses; accumulation ratio is 2.
    - **Distribution:** Volume of distribution is approximately 4.7 L in adults.
    - **Elimination:** Parallel linear (proteolytic) and non-linear (target-mediated) pathways. Half-life is not constant and depends on serum concentrations.
    - **Washout:** Approximately 20 weeks for concentrations to fall below the limit of quantitation after the last dose [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ELIPSE-HoFH (Trial 1):** A 24-week, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial (N=65). Patients received **evinacumab** 15 mg/kg IV every 4 weeks as an adjunct to other lipid therapies. 
- **Primary Outcome:** **Evinacumab** demonstrated a least-squares mean difference in LDL-C reduction of -49% compared to placebo (95% CI: -65% to -33%; p < 0.0001) [US].
- **Trial 3 (Pediatrics 5–11 years):** Open-label trial (N=14) showed a -48% mean change in LDL-C from baseline at week 24 [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vials:** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F). Keep in the original carton to protect from light. Do not freeze or shake [US].
- **Diluted Solution:** If not used immediately, store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C for no more than 24 hours, OR at room temperature (up to 25°C/77°F) for no more than 6 hours from the time of preparation to the end of infusion [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources (e.g., Australian S4 classification not listed in US PI).
