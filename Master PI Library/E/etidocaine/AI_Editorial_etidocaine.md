# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial product information has been synthesized from the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document is a harmonized editorial overview intended for use by healthcare professionals. It provides a single, coherent reference by synthesizing regulatory data.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and serves as a clinical resource. It is not a substitute for the official, current Product Information approved in a specific jurisdiction for the purposes of prescribing. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (the (R)-isomer of carnitine).
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection, 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Indicated for the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism which result in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg. This may be administered as a slow bolus injection (over 2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion [US].
- **ESRD on Hemodialysis:** The recommended starting dose is 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight) [US].

### Dosing by Indication
- **Acute Metabolic Crisis:** A loading dose is often administered, followed by an equivalent dose divided over the subsequent 24 hours. Dosing frequency should be every 3 or 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours [US].
- **ESRD Maintenance:** Administer as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line after each dialysis session. Dose adjustments (e.g., down to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough plasma levocarnitine levels [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration.
- **Compatibility:** Stable for up to 24 hours in PVC bags at room temperature (25°C) when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations ranging from 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL [US].

### Monitoring
- **Plasma Concentrations:** Trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations should be monitored. Normal levels are generally between 40 µmol/L and 50 µmol/L. The target therapeutic range for plasma free carnitine is 35 to 60 µmol/L [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known based on available clinical data [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in patients with ESRD on dialysis. Reactions can occur within minutes of administration [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in both patients with and without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with a known seizure disorder, an increase in seizure frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Renal Impairment (Oral Levocarnitine Note):** While this editorial concerns the injection, clinicians should note that high-dose *oral* levocarnitine is not evaluated in renal insufficiency and may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and TMAO) normally excreted in urine [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse events reported in clinical trials of ESRD patients on hemodialysis (incidence ≥5%) include:

| Body System | Adverse Event (≥5%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Abdominal pain, accidental injury, asthenia, chest pain, fever, flu syndrome, headache, infection, injection site reaction, pain. |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension, hypotension, tachycardia, arrhythmia [US]. |
| **Gastrointestinal** | Diarrhea, nausea, vomiting, dyspepsia, anorexia [US]. |
| **Metabolic** | Hypercalcemia, hypervolemia, hyperkalemia, weight changes [US]. |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis, cough increase, dyspnea, rhinitis [US]. |
| **Neurological** | Dizziness, paresthesia, insomnia [US]. |

- **Other Reactions:** Transient nausea/vomiting and body odor have been observed.
- **Serious Effects:** Anaphylaxis and seizures [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after any dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category C (prior system classification)]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (based on surface area) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. No adequate, well-controlled studies exist in pregnant women; use only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk carnitine concentrations after administration. The benefits of maternal therapy must be weighed against the potential risk of excess carnitine intake in the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility was found in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing is based on mg/kg body weight (refer to Section 3). Specific age-range safety profiles beyond metabolic/ESRD indications are not detailed [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically detailed in available sources; however, monitoring of renal function is advised in populations where age-related decline is expected [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Highly relevant in ESRD; see Section 3 for dialysis-specific dosing. Levocarnitine is dialyzable [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria for oxidation and subsequent energy production [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In patients with inborn errors of metabolism, levocarnitine promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters, which are quickly eliminated, thereby reducing "autointoxication" and acidosis [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces).
    - **Elimination:** Following a 20 mg/kg IV bolus, approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. The mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours and the terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients (20 mg/kg three times weekly after dialysis), trough concentrations increased from a baseline of ~21 µmol/L to ~190 µmol/L at 8 weeks.
- **Dose-Ranging Data:** Studies using 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg demonstrated a dose-dependent increase in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks.
- **Note:** While levocarnitine effectively increases plasma carnitine concentrations in ESRD, the impact on specific clinical outcomes in this population has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing. Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as the formulation is preservative-free [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources.
