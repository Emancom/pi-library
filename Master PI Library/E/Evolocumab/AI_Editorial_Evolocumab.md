# Editorial Product Information: Evolocumab

## Editorial Note
- This document is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration)**.
- **Note on Jurisdictional Scope:** At the time of this editorial synthesis, only the Australian PI was available for review. Consequently, comparison with US, European, or other regulatory authorities was not possible. For jurisdictions outside of Australia, clinicians must consult their local official PI.
- **Purpose:** This is a harmonised editorial overview intended for use by healthcare professionals. It is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official, legally binding PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Evolocumab; a fully human immunoglobulin G2 (IgG2) monoclonal antibody.
- **Chemical Class:** Proprotein convertase subtilisin/kexin type 9 (PCSK9) inhibitor.
- **Brand Name:** **Repatha** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** C10AX13.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:**
    - 140 mg/mL solution in a single-use pre-filled syringe (not available in Australia).
    - 140 mg/mL solution in a single-use pre-filled pen (SureClick).
    - 420 mg/3.5 mL (120 mg/mL) solution in a single-use pre-filled cartridge co-packaged with an automated mini-doser (AMD).
- **Route of Administration:** Subcutaneous injection.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Amgen Australia Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
**Repatha** is indicated as an adjunct to diet and exercise in:
- **Prevention of Cardiovascular Events:** To reduce the risk of myocardial infarction, stroke, and coronary revascularisation in adults with established cardiovascular disease, in combination with optimally dosed statins and/or other lipid-lowering therapies.
- **Primary Hypercholesterolaemia:** In adults with primary hypercholesterolaemia (including heterozygous familial and non-familial hypercholesterolaemia) to reduce LDL-C:
    - In combination with a statin or other lipid-lowering therapies.
    - Alone or in combination with other therapies in patients who are statin-intolerant.
- **Homozygous Familial Hypercholesterolaemia (HoFH):** In adults and adolescents aged 12 years and over in combination with other lipid-lowering therapies.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults)
- **Primary Hypercholesterolaemia and CV Prevention:** 140 mg every 2 weeks OR 420 mg once monthly. Both regimens are clinically equivalent.
- **Homozygous Familial Hypercholesterolaemia (HoFH):** Initial dose is 420 mg once monthly. May be increased to 420 mg every 2 weeks if a meaningful response is not achieved within 12 weeks. Patients on apheresis may initiate 420 mg every 2 weeks to match their schedule.

### Administration Instructions
- **Sites:** Upper arm, thigh, or abdomen. Rotate injection sites.
- **Preparation:** Allow the product to reach room temperature (30 minutes for pre-filled pen; 45 minutes for AMD) before injection. Do not warm by other means.
- **Handling:** Do not shake. Visually inspect for particles or discolouration (should be clear to opalescent, colourless to yellowish).
- **420 mg Dose Delivery:** Administered via one automated mini-doser (AMD) OR three 140 mg pre-filled pens consecutively within 30 minutes.

### Missed Dose
- Not explicitly detailed in the core clinical sections of the source; clinicians should refer to patient-specific instructions.

## 4. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to evolocumab or any of the excipients (proline, glacial acetic acid, polysorbate 80, water for injections, and sodium hydroxide).

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Allergic Reactions:** Hypersensitivity reactions (e.g., rash, urticaria) have occurred. If serious reactions occur, treatment must be discontinued and appropriate therapy initiated.
- **Latex Sensitivity:** The needle cover of the pre-filled syringe and pen contains dry natural rubber (a latex derivative), which may cause allergic reactions in latex-sensitive individuals.
- **Immunogenicity:** Anti-evolocumab binding antibodies occurred in 0.3% of patients in clinical trials. No neutralising antibodies have been detected, and there was no observed impact on PK, safety, or efficacy.
- **Concomitant Therapy:** When used with statins or other lipid-lowering agents, refer to those products’ PIs for specific contraindications and precautions.

## 6. Adverse Effects
The safety profile is based on exposure in over 35,000 patients, with a similar incidence of adverse events between evolocumab and control groups.

| Frequency | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | None specified as >10% in available source tables. |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Nasopharyngitis (7.4%), Diabetes mellitus (6.7%), Hypertension (6.4%), Upper respiratory tract infection (4.6%), Back pain (4.4%), Arthralgia (3.9%), Influenza, Nausea, Rash, Injection site reactions (pain, erythema, bruising, swelling). |
| **Uncommon (0.1–1%)** | Urticaria, Abdominal pain upper, Gout, Eczema, Epistaxis, Palpitations, Insomnia, Peripheral arterial occlusive disease. |

**Serious Adverse Effects/Post-Marketing:**
- Angioedema.
- Influenza-like illness.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Formal Studies:** No formal drug-drug interaction studies have been conducted.
- **Statins:** Co-administration with statins results in an approximate 20% increase in evolocumab clearance. This is mediated by statin-induced increases in PCSK9 concentrations but does not adversely impact the pharmacodynamic effect on lipids. No dose adjustments for statins or evolocumab are required.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** **Australian Category B1.** Animal studies (monkeys and hamsters) showed no embryo-foetal or postnatal development issues. Use in pregnancy only if the potential benefit justifies the potential risk to the foetus.
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if evolocumab is excreted in human milk. A decision should be made to discontinue nursing or the drug, weighing the importance of the drug to the mother and the infant.
- **Fertility:** No human data available. Animal studies showed no effect on male or female fertility.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy not established in children with primary hypercholesterolaemia. Approved for HoFH in patients $\ge$ 12 years of age [Australian].
- **Elderly:** No dose adjustment required for patients $\ge$ 65 years. No overall differences in safety or efficacy were observed.
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment necessary in patients with mild to moderate renal impairment, or even stage 4/5 Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD).
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment required for mild to moderate impairment (Child-Pugh A or B). Evolocumab has not been studied in severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh C).

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Evolocumab binds selectively to PCSK9 and prevents it from binding to the LDL receptor (LDLR) on the liver surface. This prevents PCSK9-mediated LDLR degradation, increasing LDLR density and resulting in lower serum LDL-C levels.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Absolute bioavailability is 72%. Peak concentrations occur in 3–4 days.
    - **Distribution:** Steady-state volume of distribution is approximately 3.3 L.
    - **Metabolism:** Degraded into small peptides and amino acids via catabolic pathways.
    - **Elimination:** Effective half-life is 11 to 17 days. Clearance is non-linear at lower concentrations but becomes linear at higher doses once PCSK9 binding is saturated.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **FOURIER Study:** A landmark CV outcomes study ($N=27,564$) showed that evolocumab reduced the risk of the primary composite endpoint (CV death, MI, stroke, hospitalisation for unstable angina, or coronary revascularisation) by 15% and the key secondary endpoint (CV death, MI, stroke) by 20% over a mean of 2.2 years.
- **GLAGOV Study:** Demonstrated that evolocumab induced regression of coronary atherosclerosis as measured by intravascular ultrasound (IVUS).
- **LAPLACE-2:** Showed LDL-C reductions of 63% to 75% when combined with various statin regimens.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C.
- **Alternative:** If removed from the refrigerator, may be kept at room temperature (up to 25°C) in the original carton but must be used within 30 days.
- **Handling:** Protect from direct light. Do not freeze. Do not shake.
