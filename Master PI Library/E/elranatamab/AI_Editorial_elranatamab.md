# Editorial Product Information: Elranatamab

**Editorial Note:** This unified Product Information (PI) document has been synthesized from official regulatory documents provided by the Australian Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA) and the United States Food and Drug Administration (FDA). Where clinical requirements or safety directives differ between agencies, both are noted to provide a comprehensive global perspective for healthcare professionals.

---

## 1. Product Name and Active Ingredient
**Product Name:** ELREXFIO  
**Active Ingredient:** Elranatamab (also referred to as elranatamab-bcmm [FDA])  
**Formulation:** Solution for subcutaneous injection (40 mg/mL). Available in 44 mg/1.1 mL and 76 mg/1.9 mL single-dose vials.

## 2. Approved Indications
ELREXFIO is indicated for the treatment of adult patients with relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma who have received at least:
*   **Three prior lines of therapy** [TGA], including a proteasome inhibitor, an immunomodulatory agent, and an anti-CD38 monoclonal antibody, and have demonstrated disease progression on the last therapy.
*   **Four prior lines of therapy** [FDA], including a proteasome inhibitor, an immunomodulatory agent, and an anti-CD38 monoclonal antibody.

*Note: These indications are approved under provisional [TGA] or accelerated [FDA] pathways based on response rates. Continued approval may be contingent upon verification of clinical benefit in confirmatory trials.*

## 3. Contraindications
*   **Hypersensitivity:** ELREXFIO is contraindicated in patients with a known history of hypersensitivity to the active substance or any of the excipients (histidine, L-histidine hydrochloride monohydrate, disodium edetate, polysorbate 80, and sucrose) [TGA]. 
*   *Note: The FDA label does not list any specific contraindications.*

## 4. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warning: CRS and Neurological Toxicity
ELREXFIO can cause severe, life-threatening, or fatal Cytokine Release Syndrome (CRS) and neurological toxicities, including Immune Effector Cell-Associated Neurotoxicity Syndrome (ICANS). 
*   **Monitoring:** Patients must be closely monitored for signs and symptoms for 48 hours following the first step-up dose and 24 hours after the second step-up dose.
*   **REMS Program:** In the United States, ELREXFIO is available only through a restricted Risk Evaluation and Mitigation Strategy (REMS) program [FDA].

### Critical Safety Considerations
*   **Infections:** Severe, life-threatening, or fatal bacterial and viral infections (including opportunistic infections) have occurred. Treatment should not be initiated in patients with active infections.
*   **Neutropenia:** High rates of Grade 3 or 4 neutropenia and febrile neutropenia have been observed. Monitor complete blood counts at baseline and periodically during treatment.
*   **Hepatotoxicity:** Liver enzyme elevations (ALT/AST) and bilirubin increases may occur with or without concurrent CRS. Monitor hepatic function at baseline and as clinically indicated.
*   **Progressive Multifocal Leukoencephalopathy (PML):** Fatal cases of PML have been reported during therapy. Monitor for new or worsening neurological/cognitive signs [TGA].
*   **Embryo-Fetal Toxicity:** Based on its mechanism of action, the drug may cause fetal harm.

## 5. Dosage and Administration
ELREXFIO is for **subcutaneous injection only**, preferably in the abdomen or thigh.

### Recommended Dosing Schedule
Treatment follows a step-up transition to reduce the risk of CRS/ICANS:
1.  **Step-up Dose 1 (Day 1):** 12 mg
2.  **Step-up Dose 2 (Day 4):** 32 mg
3.  **First Full Treatment Dose (Day 8/Week 2):** 76 mg
4.  **Weekly Dosing:** 76 mg weekly from Week 2 through Week 24.
5.  **Dosing Interval Transition:** For patients maintaining a response for at least 24 weeks, the interval transitions to 76 mg every two weeks. After a further 24 weeks of bi-weekly dosing (at Week 49), responding patients may transition to 76 mg every four weeks.

### Pre-treatment Medications
Administer the following approximately 1 hour prior to the first three doses (step-up 1, step-up 2, and the first full dose):
*   Paracetamol/Acetaminophen: 500–650 mg orally.
*   Dexamethasone: 20 mg orally or intravenously.
*   Diphenhydramine: 25 mg orally (or equivalent).

### Dose Delays
Dose reductions are not recommended. If a dose is delayed, the schedule must be adjusted based on the duration of the delay and the last dose administered to determine if the step-up sequence must be restarted.

## 6. Adverse Effects
The safety profile is based on the MagnetisMM-3 study. The most common adverse reactions (≥20%) include:
*   **Systemic:** CRS (58%), fatigue (43%), pyrexia (21%).
*   **Gastrointestinal:** Diarrhea (36%), nausea (21%).
*   **Respiratory:** Upper respiratory tract infection (36%), pneumonia (32%), dyspnea (15%), cough (24%).
*   **Local:** Injection site reactions (37%).
*   **Musculoskeletal:** Pain (34%) and arthralgia.

**Laboratory Abnormalities (Grade 3/4):** Decreased lymphocytes (84%), neutrophils (51%), hemoglobin (43%), and platelets (32%).

## 7. Drug Interactions
No formal drug-drug interaction studies have been conducted. However, the release of cytokines during ELREXFIO treatment can suppress Cytochrome P450 (CYP) enzymes. 
*   **Clinical Impact:** This may increase exposure to CYP substrates, particularly those with a narrow therapeutic index (e.g., cyclosporine or warfarin). 
*   **Monitoring:** Monitor for toxicity or drug concentrations of sensitive CYP substrates for up to 14 days after the first step-up dose and during/after episodes of CRS.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy:** There are no data on use in pregnant women. Animal data suggest T-cell activation and cytokine release may compromise pregnancy maintenance and cause fetal B-cell depletion. ELREXFIO is not recommended during pregnancy.
*   **Contraception:** Females of reproductive potential should have their pregnancy status verified before treatment and must use effective contraception during therapy and for **4 months** after the final dose.
*   **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if elranatamab is excreted in human milk. Due to the potential for serious adverse reactions in the infant, breastfeeding is not recommended during treatment and for 4 months after the last dose.

## 9. Pharmacokinetics
*   **Absorption:** Mean bioavailability is 56.2%. Median time to maximum concentration (Tmax) is approximately 7 days.
*   **Distribution:** The steady-state volume of distribution is approximately 7.76 L.
*   **Metabolism:** Elranatamab is expected to be degraded into small peptides via catabolic pathways, typical of immunoglobulin G (IgG) molecules.
*   **Elimination:** The predicted geometric mean half-life is 22 days at steady state. Clearance is approximately 0.324 L/day.
*   **Special Populations:** No clinically significant differences in PK were observed based on age (36–89 years), sex, race, or mild-to-moderate renal/hepatic impairment.

## 10. Mechanism of Action
Elranatamab is a bispecific IgG2-kappa antibody. It is designed to bridge T-cells and myeloma cells by binding simultaneously to:
1.  **BCMA (B-cell maturation antigen):** Expressed on the surface of multiple myeloma cells.
2.  **CD3:** Expressed on the surface of T-cells.
This dual-binding triggers T-cell activation and proliferation, leading to the release of pro-inflammatory cytokines and the subsequent lysis of BCMA-expressing malignant cells.

## 11. Storage Conditions
*   **Unopened Vials:** Store in a refrigerator at 2°C to 8°C. Do not freeze or shake. Keep the vial in the original carton to protect from light.
*   **Prepared Syringes:** 
    *   TGA guidelines: Stable for 4 days at 2°C to 8°C or 36 hours at up to 25°C.
    *   FDA guidelines: Stable for up to 72 hours refrigerated (2°C to 8°C) or 24 hours at room temperature (8°C to 25°C). 
    *   *Recommendation: Use immediately; if not, follow the more conservative refrigeration limits of the providing facility.*
