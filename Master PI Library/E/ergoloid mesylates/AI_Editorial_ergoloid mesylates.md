# Editorial Product Information: Ergoloid Mesylates

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising available regulatory data into a single clinical reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI or summary of product characteristics (SmPC) required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. At the time of preparation, only the United States PI was available for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Ergoloid mesylates; a mixture consisting of the methanesulfonate salts of hydrogenated ergot alkaloids: dihydroergocornine, dihydroergocristine, and dihydroergocryptine (the latter existing as a 2:1 ratio of alpha and beta isomers).
- **Brand Names:** Not specified as a primary brand in source; distributed as a generic product [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - **Oral Tablet:** 1 mg [US].
    - Each 1 mg tablet contains: Dihydroergocristine mesylate (0.333 mg), Dihydroergocornine mesylate (0.333 mg), and Dihydroergocryptine mesylate (0.333 mg).
- **Route of Administration:** Oral (Note: Bioequivalence has been established between oral and sublingual formulations) [US].
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Distributed by Sun Pharmaceutical Industries, Inc.; Repackaged by Carilion Materials Management [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Symptomatic Decline in Mental Capacity:** Treatment of individuals over the age of sixty who manifest signs and symptoms of idiopathic decline in mental capacity (e.g., impairments in cognitive and interpersonal skills, mood, self-care, and motivation) [US].
- **Dementia-Related Conditions:** Approved for use in patients suffering from age-related decline or underlying dementing conditions, including primary progressive dementia, Alzheimer's dementia, senile onset, and multi-infarct dementia [US].

**Important Clinical Caveats:**
- Ergoloid mesylates are **not** indicated for the treatment of acute or chronic psychosis, regardless of the underlying etiology [US].
- Potential benefit must be weighed against the necessity of excluding reversible or treatable conditions such as delirium, systemic disease, primary neurological disease, or primary mood disturbances [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **Standard Adult Dose:** 1 mg administered three times daily [US].
- **Administration Instructions:** Tablets are for oral use.
- **Onset of Effect:** Clinical alleviation of symptoms is typically gradual. Initial results may not be observable for 3 to 4 weeks following the initiation of therapy [US].
- **Ongoing Review:** The decision to continue therapy should be reviewed periodically, as the underlying clinical picture may evolve, necessitating a different diagnosis or alternative treatment [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to ergoloid mesylates or any component of the formulation [US].
- **Psychosis:** Contraindicated in patients with acute or chronic psychosis, regardless of etiology [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Diagnostic Rigour:** Before prescribing, clinicians must attempt a careful diagnosis to exclude reversible conditions. Particular care is required to differentiate idiopathic decline from delirium or secondary dementiform illnesses [US].
- **Continued Evaluation:** Regular clinical evaluation is required to determine if initial benefits persist over time [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **General Profile:** Ergoloid mesylates are generally well-tolerated and have not been associated with serious side effects [US].
- **Gastrointestinal:** 
    - Transient nausea (Frequency not specified) [US].
    - Gastric disturbances (Frequency not specified) [US].
- **Vascular Safety:** Unlike natural ergot alkaloids, ergoloid mesylates do not possess vasoconstrictor properties [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** The exact mechanism by which ergoloid mesylates produce mental effects is not clearly established. There is no conclusive evidence that the drug specifically affects cerebral arteriosclerosis or cerebrovascular insufficiency [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Approximately 25% of an oral dose is absorbed. Rapid absorption occurs from the gastrointestinal tract, with peak levels reached within 0.6–1.3 hours [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Undergoes significant first-pass hepatic metabolism; less than 50% of the therapeutic moiety reaches systemic circulation [US].
    - **Elimination:** Elimination is biphasic, with half-lives of 4 and 13 hours. The mean half-life of unchanged ergoloid in plasma is approximately 2.6–5.1 hours. No drug is detectable in plasma by 24 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Efficacy Assessment:** Evaluated using the Sandoz Clinical Assessment-Geriatric (SCAG) scale. 
- **Trial Outcomes:** Statistically significant changes were observed over 12 weeks in several parameters, including mental alertness, orientation, recent memory, confusion, emotional lability, sociability, and overall clinical status [US].
