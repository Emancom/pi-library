# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following regulatory authority: **US (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document is a harmonised editorial overview intended for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises clinical data and regulatory requirements into a single reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official, full Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult the current local PI for legal and regulatory compliance.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP).
- **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule for long-chain fatty acids (amino acid derivative).
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):**
    - **Intravenous Injection:** 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) sterile solution in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism leading to secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance haemodialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    - Recommended dose is 50 mg/kg administered as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion [US].
    - In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose is often followed by equivalent doses every 3 to 4 hours (never less than every 6 hours) over the first 24 hours [US].
    - Subsequent daily doses are typically 50 mg/kg, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been administered [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Haemodialysis:** 
    - Recommended starting dose: 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
    - Administration: Slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line after each dialysis session [US].
    - Monitoring: Therapy initiation may be prompted by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma concentrations <40–50 µmol/L. Dose adjustments should be guided by trough levels; downward titration (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may occur as early as the third or fourth week of treatment [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Inspect visually for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration [US].
- Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial [US].

### Compatibility and Stability
- Compatible with Sodium Chloride 0.9% and Lactated Ringer’s in concentrations from 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL. Stable for up to 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known at the time of publication [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal oedema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients on dialysis. These may occur within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately [US].
- **Seizures:** Reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Oral Levocarnitine in Renal Impairment:** The safety/efficacy of *oral* levocarnitine has not been evaluated in renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in these patients may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites: trimethylamine (TMA) and trimethylamine-N-oxide (TMAO) [US].
- **Monitoring:** For metabolic disorders, weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target 35–60 µmol/L) is recommended [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common Adverse Effects (≥5%) 
Based on clinical trials in ESRD patients, the following events occurred at a frequency of ≥5% (regardless of causality):
- **Body as a Whole:** Abdominal pain (9%), accidental injury (10%), asthenia (9%), back pain (8%), chest pain (12%), fever (7%), flu syndrome (25%), headache (22%), infection (15%), injection site reaction (33%), pain (30%) [US].
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (20%), hypotension (14%), tachycardia (6%) [US].
- **Digestive:** Diarrhoea (16%), nausea (8%), vomiting (15%) [US].
- **Metabolic:** Hypercalcaemia (9%), hyperkalaemia (6%), hypervolaemia (5%) [US].
- **Nervous:** Dizziness (13%), paresthesia (5%), depression (5%) [US].
- **Respiratory:** Cough increase (9%), dyspnea (7%), pharyngitis (23%), rhinitis (9%) [US].

### Less Common/Postmarketing Experience
- **Gastrointestinal:** Gastritis, body odor (due to metabolite accumulation) [US].
- **Neurologic:** Seizures [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have occurred when levocarnitine is used concomitantly with warfarin. It is recommended to monitor INR levels after initiating treatment or following dose adjustments [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm [US].
    - **Recommendation:** Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed, as animal studies are not always predictive of human response [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk carnitine levels following administration. Risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant must be weighed against the benefits of maternal supplementation [US].
- **Fertility:** No mutagenic effects seen in standard tests (Salmonella, Saccharomyces, Schizosaccharomyces). Long-term animal carcinogenicity studies have not been performed [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing is weight-based (50 mg/kg) as in adults for metabolic disorders [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Specifically indicated for ESRD on dialysis (IV only). *Oral* levocarnitine is cautioned in severe renal impairment due to toxic metabolite accumulation [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In patients with inborn errors of metabolism or ESRD, levocarnitine helps clear accumulated toxic acyl-CoA compounds by forming acylcarnitine, which is rapidly excreted [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not protein or albumin bound [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in faeces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Following IV bolus (20 mg/kg), the mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours and terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours. Approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients (20 mg/kg three times weekly), mean pre-dialysis carnitine levels rose from ~20 µmol/L to ~190 µmol/L after 8 weeks. Dose-ranging studies (10, 20, 40 mg/kg) confirmed a dose-dependent increase in trough concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [US].
- **Note:** While levocarnitine increases carnitine concentrations in ESRD, the effect on specific clinical outcomes in this population has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
- **Precautions:** Avoid excessive heat. Protect from freezing [US].
- **Vial Handling:** Single-dose vial; discard any unused portion as the formulation contains no preservative [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Prescription Only (Rx only) [US].
