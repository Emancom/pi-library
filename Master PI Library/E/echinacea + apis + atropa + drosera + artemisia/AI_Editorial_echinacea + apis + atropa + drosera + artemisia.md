## 3. Approved Indications
- For temporary relief of a sore throat without fever. [US]

## 4. Dosage and Administration
- General dosing (adult and adolescents):
  - Adults and children over 12 years: take 1–2 tablets, dissolve under the tongue or chew, three times daily until symptoms reduce; thereafter, once daily to maintain control, or as directed by a health professional. [US]
- Dosing by indication:
  - Indication listed is temporary relief of sore throat without fever; dosing as above. [US]
- Administration instructions:
  - Tablets to be dissolved sublingually or chewed as above. [US]
- Titration schedules: Not specified in available sources.
- Missed dose instructions: Not specified in available sources.
- Duration and follow-up:
  - If symptoms persist for more than 10 days, consult a health professional. [US]
