*   **Occupational Exposure/Inhalation:** Harmful if inhaled. Prolonged or repeated exposure via inhalation may cause damage to organs, specifically the respiratory tract [US].
*   **Handling Precautions:**

    *   Use only outdoors or in well-ventilated environments [US].

*   **Environmental Cautions:** Harmful to aquatic life with long-lasting effects. Avoid release into the environment [US].
