# Editorial Product Information: Eszopiclone

## Editorial Note
- This document is an editorial synthesis based on Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdiction: United States (FDA). [Note: Only one source document was provided for this synthesis.]
- **Purpose:** This harmonised editorial overview is intended for use by healthcare professionals to provide a clear, clinically accurate summary of the drug's profile.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI or local regulatory guidance required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Eszopiclone (a nonbenzodiazepine hypnotic of the cyclopyrrolone class).
- **Brand Names:** Available as generic formulations; distributed by Glenmark Pharmaceuticals and St Mary's Medical Park Pharmacy [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - Film-coated tablets: 1 mg, 2 mg, and 3 mg [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Glenmark Pharmaceuticals Inc., USA; St Mary's Medical Park Pharmacy [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Insomnia:** Indicated for the treatment of insomnia in adults. Clinical studies have demonstrated efficacy in decreasing sleep latency and improving sleep maintenance [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** 
    - The recommended starting dose is 1 mg immediately before bedtime.
    - Dosing may be increased to 2 mg or 3 mg if clinically indicated.
    - **Maximum Dose:** 3 mg once daily [US].
- **Administration Instructions:** 
    - Administer immediately prior to retiring.
    - Ensure at least 7 to 8 hours remain for sleep before the planned time of awakening.
    - **Food Effect:** Do not take with or immediately after a heavy, high-fat meal, as this slows absorption and reduces the effect on sleep latency [US].
- **Dose Adjustments:** See Section 9 (Use in Special Populations).

## 4. Contraindications
- **Complex Sleep Behaviors:** Contraindicated in patients with a history of complex sleep behaviors (e.g., sleep-walking, sleep-driving) after taking eszopiclone [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Contraindicated in patients with known hypersensitivity to eszopiclone. Reported reactions include angioedema and anaphylaxis [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning: Complex Sleep Behaviors [US]:** 
    - Use may result in complex sleep behaviors, including sleep-walking, sleep-driving, and engaging in other activities while not fully awake.
    - These events can result in serious injury or death. 
    - **Management:** Discontinue eszopiclone immediately if a patient experiences any complex sleep behavior.
- **CNS Depressant Effects:** Can cause next-day impairment of functions requiring mental alertness (e.g., driving). Risk increases with the 3 mg dose, co-administration with other CNS depressants, or insufficient sleep (less than 7–8 hours) [US].
- **Comorbid Diagnoses:** Insomnia may be a symptom of an underlying physical or psychiatric disorder. Re-evaluate if insomnia persists after 7 to 10 days of treatment [US].
- **Severe Anaphylactic/Anaphylactoid Reactions:** Rare cases of angioedema involving the tongue, glottis, or larynx. Do not rechallenge if such reactions occur [US].
- **Abnormal Thinking and Behavioral Changes:** Includes decreased inhibition, bizarre behavior, agitation, and depersonalization. Amnesia may occur [US].
- **Depression and Suicidality:** May worsen depression or suicidal thinking. Use with caution in patients with depressive symptoms; prescribe the minimum feasible quantity of tablets [US].
- **Withdrawal Effects:** Rapid dose reduction or abrupt discontinuation may cause symptoms similar to other CNS-depressant withdrawal [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions are generally dose-related. The most frequent reactions observed in clinical trials (≥2%) include:

| Organ System | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Special Senses** | Unpleasant taste (Common 8–34%) |
| **Nervous System** | Headache, somnolence, dizziness, nervousness, anxiety, confusion, hallucinations |
| **Digestive System** | Dry mouth, dyspepsia, nausea, vomiting, diarrhea |
| **Respiratory System** | Respiratory infection, viral infection |
| **General** | Rash, accidental injury, pain |

- **Pediatric Specifics:** In clinical trials for ADHD-associated insomnia (not an approved indication), reported effects included dysgeusia, dizziness, hallucinations, and suicidal ideation [US].
- **Post-marketing Experience:** Dysosmia (olfactory dysfunction) has been reported [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **CNS Depressants:** Additive psychomotor impairment when used with alcohol, benzodiazepines, opioids, or tricyclic antidepressants. Dose adjustments may be necessary [US].
- **CYP3A4 Inhibitors (e.g., Ketoconazole):** Significantly increase eszopiclone exposure (e.g., 2.2-fold increase with ketoconazole). Eszopiclone dose should not exceed 2 mg when co-administered with potent CYP3A4 inhibitors [US].
- **CYP3A4 Inducers (e.g., Rifampicin):** May significantly decrease exposure (up to 80%) and efficacy of eszopiclone [US].
- **Olanzapine:** Produces a pharmacodynamic interaction (decreased DSST scores) without affecting pharmacokinetics [US].
- **Pharmacokinetic Neutrality:** No clinically significant interactions were found with digoxin, warfarin, paroxetine, or lorazepam (though lorazepam/eszopiclone may mutually decrease Cmax by 22%) [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Risk Summary:** Insufficient human data to identify drug-associated risks. [US].
    - **Animal Data:** No teratogenicity in rats/rabbits, but reduced fetal weight and skeletal variations occurred at doses ≥100 times the maximum human dose. Rats showed increased post-implantation loss and decreased pup survival at high doses [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** No data available on the presence of eszopiclone in human milk or its effects on the breastfed infant. Weigh developmental/health benefits against the mother's clinical need [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies in rats showed decreased fertility and abnormal estrus cycles in females, and decreased sperm motility/morphology in males at high doses [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness not established. In a 12-week study of children (6–17 years) with ADHD-associated insomnia, eszopiclone failed to show efficacy compared to placebo [US].
- **Elderly Use (≥65 years):** 
    - Increased exposure (41% increase in AUC) and prolonged half-life (~9 hours) compared to younger adults. 
    - **Dose adjustment:** Total dose should not exceed 2 mg at bedtime [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** 
    - **Mild-to-moderate:** No dose adjustment required.
    - **Severe:** Exposure is doubled. The dose should not exceed 2 mg. Use with caution [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment necessary as less than 10% is excreted unchanged in urine [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Precise mechanism unknown; believed to interact with GABA-receptor complexes at binding domains coupled to benzodiazepine receptors [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Rapid; Peak plasma concentration (Tmax) in ~1 hour.
    - **Distribution:** Weakly protein-bound (52–59%).
    - **Metabolism:** Extensively metabolized via oxidation and demethylation (primary metabolites: (S)-zopiclone-N-oxide and (S)-N-desmethyl zopiclone). Primary enzymes: CYP3A4 and CYP2E1.
    - **Elimination:** Terminal half-life (t1/2) is approximately 6 hours. Primarily excreted in urine as metabolites [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Chronic Insomnia:** Efficacy established in five controlled studies (up to 6 months' duration) involving 2,100 subjects. Eszopiclone significantly decreased sleep latency and improved maintenance (measured objectively and subjectively) [US].
- **Transient Insomnia:** In a single-night laboratory study (n=436), 3 mg eszopiclone was superior to placebo in reducing latency and improving maintenance [US].
- **Next-Day Residual Effects:** A study in healthy adults showed psychomotor and memory impairment 7.5 hours after a 3 mg dose. While impairment was still present at 11.5 hours, patients' subjective perception of impairment did not always match objective results [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Excursions:** Permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F and 86°F) [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as a Schedule IV (CIV) controlled substance under the Controlled Substances Act due to the potential for abuse and dependence [US].
- **Prescription Status:** Rx-only [US].
