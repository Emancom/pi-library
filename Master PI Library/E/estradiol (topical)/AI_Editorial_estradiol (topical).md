# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- Contributing Jurisdictions: United States (FDA)
- Purpose: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of levocarnitine injection for healthcare professionals, synthesising regulatory information into a clinically applicable format.
- Disclaimer: This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory documents for specific legal and prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter Healthcare Corporation) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01 (Not specified in available source; standard reference).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
  - Recommended dose is 50 mg/kg administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus or by infusion [US].
  - Severe metabolic crisis: A loading dose is often followed by equivalent doses every 3 or 4 hours (never less than every 6 hours) for 24 hours.
  - Subsequent daily doses are typically 50 mg/kg or as clinically required. Doses up to 300 mg/kg have been administered [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
  - Recommended starting dose: 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight).
  - Administration: Slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Inspect for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration.
- Discard any unused portion as the formulation contains no preservatives [US].

### Titration and Monitoring
- Initiation in ESRD may be prompted by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine levels <40–50 µmol/L [US].
- Dose adjustments (e.g., down to 5 mg/kg) may be made as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough concentrations.
- **Target Range:** Maintain plasma free carnitine between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None [US].
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have occurred—often within minutes of administration. Most cases involve ESRD patients on dialysis. If a reaction occurs, discontinue immediately and initiate appropriate medical management [US].
- **Neurological Effects:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Renal Insufficiency (Oral Formulation Precaution):** While this PI covers the injection, it notes that chronic high-dose oral levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) [US].
- **Monitoring:** Periodic monitoring of blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations is recommended [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Clinical Trials Experience (ESRD Patients)
Adverse events reported at a frequency ≥5% (regardless of causality):
- **Body as a Whole:** Abdominal pain (5–21%), headache (3–37%), infection (10–24%), injection site reaction (27–38%), pain (21–35%), fever (5–12%), asthenia (8–12%).
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (18–21%), hypotension (3–19%), chest pain (6–15%), tachycardia (5–9%).
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea (9–35%), vomiting (9–21%), nausea (5–12%).
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (15–27%), cough increase (10–18%), dyspnea (3–11%).
- **Nervous:** Dizziness (10–18%), paresthesia (3–12%).
- **Metabolic/Nutritional:** Hypercalcemia (6–15%), hypervolemia (3–12%).

### Postmarketing Experience
- **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea and vomiting; body odor and gastritis (less frequent).
- **Neurologic:** Seizures [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, bronchospasm [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalised Ratio (INR) have been observed when used concomitantly.
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after dose adjustments in patients on warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Not formally categorised by letter in available source [US]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine is a normal component of human milk; supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk carnitine following exogenous administration. Weigh the benefits to the mother against potential risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders is calculated by mg/kg body weight (see Section 3) [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically detailed in available source, though ESRD is common in this demographic.
- **Renal Impairment:** Primarily indicated for use in ESRD patients on dialysis. Safety/efficacy of the *oral* form has not been evaluated in renal insufficiency [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production. It also facilitates the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Corrects carnitine deficiency (defined as <20 µmol/L at one week post-term). It alleviates metabolic acidosis caused by the accumulation of acylCoA compounds [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
  - **Distribution:** Not protein-bound. Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours [US].
  - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
  - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. Terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Bioavailability Studies:** Oral tablets and solution were found to be bioequivalent. Absolute bioavailability is approximately 15% after correction for endogenous levels [US].
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of ESRD patients (n=12), 20 mg/kg IV three times weekly post-dialysis increased mean pre-dialysis plasma carnitine from 21.3 µmol/L to 190 µmol/L after 8 weeks [US].
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** Doses of 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg demonstrated dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Conditions:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat [US].
- **Compatibility:** Compatible and stable for 24 hours in PVC bags at room temperature when mixed with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8.0 mg/mL) [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as Rx-only [US].
- **Poison/Schedule Classification:** Not specified for other regions in available source.
