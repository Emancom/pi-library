# Editorial Product Information: Eflornithine

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) from Australia (Therapeutic Goods Administration - TGA).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of eflornithine for healthcare professionals, consolidating clinical, regulatory, and pharmacological data into a single reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should refer to their local regulatory authority's current approved documentation for definitive prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Eflornithine (as hydrochloride); also known as difluoromethylornithine (DFMO).
- **Chemical Class:** Irreversible ornithine decarboxylase (ODC) inhibitor.
- **Brand Names:** **Ifinwil** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** L01XX79.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Oral tablet containing 192 mg eflornithine.
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Norgine Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **High-Risk Neuroblastoma (HRNB):** Maintenance treatment for adult and paediatric patients with high-risk neuroblastoma who have achieved at least a partial response to prior multiagent, multimodality therapy [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
Dosage is determined by body surface area (BSA) and administered twice daily. Treatment duration is 2 years or until disease recurrence or unacceptable toxicity occurs. BSA should be recalculated every 3 months during therapy [Australian].

**Standard Dosage Schedule:**
| Body Surface Area (BSA) | Twice Daily Dose | Total Daily Tablets |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| >1.5 m² | 768 mg (4 tablets) | 8 tablets |
| 0.75 to 1.5 m² | 576 mg (3 tablets) | 6 tablets |
| 0.5 to < 0.75 m² | 384 mg (2 tablets) | 4 tablets |
| 0.25 to < 0.5 m² | 192 mg (1 tablet) | 2 tablets |

### Administration Instructions
- Tablets may be taken with or without food.
- Tablets may be swallowed whole, chewed, or crushed.
- **Crushed Administration:** Mix with approximately 30 mL (2 tablespoons) of soft food or liquid. If particles remain, mix with an additional 30 mL. The preparation must be consumed within one hour [Australian].

### Missed Dose
- If a dose is missed, it should be taken as soon as possible.
- If the next scheduled dose is due within 7 hours, skip the missed dose.
- If vomiting occurs after administration, do not take an additional dose; proceed with the next scheduled dose.

### Dose Modifications for Toxicity
Dose reductions should follow a tiered approach until a minimum dose of 192 mg once daily is reached. If the patient cannot tolerate 192 mg daily, the drug must be permanently discontinued. Specific modification criteria exist for:
- **Myelosuppression:** Withhold for ANC <500/mm³ or Platelets <25,000/mm³ until recovery.
- **Hepatotoxicity:** Withhold for ALT or AST ≥10 × ULN until <10 × ULN.
- **Hearing Loss:** Clinically concerning changes require an audiogram in 3 weeks; persistent changes may require holding dose for 30 days or dose reduction [Australian].

## 4. Contraindications
- Hypersensitivity to eflornithine or any included excipients (silicified microcrystalline cellulose, pregelatinised maize starch, silicon dioxide, and magnesium stearate) [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Myelosuppression:** Eflornithine can cause significant neutropenia (4.2% Grade 3/4), thrombocytopenia (1.4%), and anaemia (3.3%). Perform full blood counts (FBC) at baseline and periodically during treatment.
- **Hepatotoxicity:** Grade 3/4 ALT elevations occurred in 11% of patients. Monitor liver function tests (ALT, AST, total bilirubin) at baseline, monthly for the first 6 months, and every 3 months thereafter.
- **Hearing Loss / Hypoacusis:** New or worsening hearing loss occurred in 13% of patients. High baseline abnormality rates (81%) were noted. Perform audiograms at baseline and every 6 months during treatment [Australian].
- **Monitoring Requirements:** FBC, LFTs, and audiometry are required before initiation and throughout the 2-year maintenance period.

## 6. Adverse Effects
The following frequency data is derived from a pooled safety population (n=360) and Study 3b (n=85) [Australian].

| Frequency | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Otitis media (32%), Diarrhoea (15%), Cough (15%), Sinusitis (13%), Pneumonia (12%), Upper respiratory tract infection (11%), Conjunctivitis (11%), Vomiting (11%), Pyrexia (11%), Allergic rhinitis (11%), Hearing loss (11% pooled/7% Study 3b) |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Skin infection (7%), Urinary tract infection (6%) |
| **Serious Reactions** | Skin infection (most common serious reaction), hearing loss (leading to discontinuation in 1.4%) |

**Laboratory Abnormalities (Grade 3 or 4):**
- Increased ALT (11%)
- Increased AST (6%)
- Decreased neutrophils (4.2%)
- Decreased haemoglobin (3.3%)

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** No in vivo studies have been performed. In vitro studies indicate eflornithine is **not** a substrate or inhibitor of major CYP450 isoforms (1A2, 2B6, 2C8, 2C9, 2C19, 2D6, 3A4). It is not a substrate or inhibitor of major transporters (OAT1, OAT3, OCT2, OATP1B1/3, MATE1/2-K, BCRP, P-gp).
- **Food Interactions:** Bioavailability is not affected by food [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** **Category B3** [Australian]. No clinical data available. Animal studies demonstrated embryofetal toxicity (decreased fetal weights, skeletal variations, and resorptions) at doses equivalent to human exposure. Use is not recommended during pregnancy.
- **Contraception:** Female patients of reproductive potential and male patients with partners of reproductive potential must use effective contraception during treatment and for 1 week after the final dose.
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if eflornithine is excreted in human milk. Breastfeeding is contraindicated during treatment and for 1 week after the last dose.
- **Fertility:** Human data is unavailable. Animal studies showed a lower fertility index in offspring exposed in utero [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Approved for patients aged 1–17 years. Safety and efficacy in infants under 1 year have not been established.
- **Elderly Use:** No data available [Australian].
- **Renal Impairment:** Eflornithine is primarily renally excreted (80%).
    - **Moderate (eGFR 30–59 mL/min/1.73 m²):** Use with caution; monitor closely for toxicities.
    - **Severe (eGFR ≤29 mL/min/1.73 m²):** Reduce dose by 50% according to specific BSA-based renal tables [Australian].
- **Hepatic Impairment:**
    - **Mild:** No dose adjustment required.
    - **Moderate to Severe:** Not studied [Australian].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Irreversible inhibitor of ornithine decarboxylase (ODC). By inhibiting ODC, it depletes polyamines necessary for the differentiation and proliferation of neoplastic cells. It restores the LIN28/Let-7 metabolic pathway balance and decreases the expression of MYCN and LIN28B oncogenic drivers.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** No significant QTc interval prolongation (>20 ms) was observed at recommended doses.
- **Pharmacokinetics:** 
    - **Absorption:** Tmax is approximately 3.5 hours.
    - **Distribution:** Not protein-bound; Volume of distribution (Vz/F) is 24.3 L.
    - **Elimination:** Terminal half-life is 3.5 hours; Clearance (Cl/F) is 5.3 L/h [Australian].

## 11. Clinical Trials
Efficacy was established in an externally controlled comparison (Study 3b vs. ANBL0032).
- **Study 3b (n=105):** Multi-center, open-label trial in HRNB patients.
- **Primary Endpoint:** Event-free survival (EFS).
- **Outcomes:** The EFS hazard ratio (HR) was 0.48 (95% CI: 0.27, 0.85) and the Overall Survival (OS) HR was 0.32 (95% CI: 0.15, 0.70) when compared to the external control group. The primary analysis was conducted using propensity score matching [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage Temperature:** Store below 25°C.
- **Special Conditions:** Keep the container tightly closed and airtight.
- **Packaging:** White HDPE bottles containing 100 tablets with a silica gel desiccant [Australian].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Australia:** Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine). Subject to additional monitoring (Black Triangle Scheme) [Australian].
- **Other Jurisdictions:** Not specified in provided sources.
