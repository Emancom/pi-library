## 4. Dosage and Administration
- General dosing (adults and adolescents):
  - Adults and children 12 years and over: take 15 drops orally (placed under the tongue) every 3–4 hours.
- Special dosing instructions:
  - Sensitive persons and asthma sufferers: initial dosing of 1–3 drops, then wait 3 hours; if tolerated, continue with the regular dosage (15 drops every 3–4 hours) thereafter.
  - If allergy or asthma symptoms develop during use, discontinue the product.
- Dosing by indication: Not specified beyond symptomatic relief of allergy symptoms as listed in Section 3.
