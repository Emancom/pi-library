# Editorial Product Information: ethanol

## 1. Product Identification
- Active ingredient (INN/generic name): **ethanol** (ethyl alcohol)
- Chemical class: Not specified in available sources
- ATC code: Not specified in available sources
- Route(s) of administration: Topical (external use only)
- Strength: 70% v/v (70 mL in 100 mL)

## 2. Regulatory History
- Marketing start date: 08/28/2023 [US]
- Date of last revision/update of source PI: Revised: 7/2026 [US]
- Withdrawals, suspensions, restrictions: Not specified in available sources

## 3. Approved Indications
- Indicated as an antimicrobial hand sanitizer for decreasing bacteria on the skin;
  recommended for repeated use (topical hand antisepsis).

## 4. Dosage and Administration
- General dosing (adult):
  - Apply sufficient product to wet hands thoroughly.
  - Briskly rub hands together until the product has dried.
- Dosing by indication:
  - For hand antisepsis to decrease bacteria on the skin (no quantitative dosing
    frequency specified in available sources).
- Administration instructions:
  - For external use only.
  - Do not use in the eyes; if ocular contact occurs, rinse eyes thoroughly with water.
  - Supervise children under 6 years of age during use.
- Titration schedules: Not specified in available sources
- Missed dose instructions: Not specified in available sources

## 5. Contraindications
- Use in or around the eyes — do not apply to the eyes.
- Other specific contraindications: Not specified in available sources

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
- Boxed warnings: Not specified in available sources
- General warnings and precautions:
  - **Flammable** — keep away from heat and flame.
  - For external use only.
  - Do not use in the eyes; if ocular contact occurs, rinse eyes thoroughly with water.
  - Discontinue use and consult a healthcare provider if skin irritation and redness
    develop and persist for more than 72 hours.
  - Keep out of reach of children. If accidentally ingested, seek medical help or
    contact a Poison Control Center immediately.
- Monitoring requirements: Not specified in available sources
- Specific clinical precautions:
  - Supervise use in young children (under 6 years of age).
