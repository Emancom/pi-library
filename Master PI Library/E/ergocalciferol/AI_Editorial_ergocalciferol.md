# PRODUCT INFORMATION: ERGOCALCIFEROL (VITAMIN D2)

## 1. Product Name and Active Ingredient
**Active Ingredient:** Ergocalciferol (Vitamin D2)

**Formulations:** 
*   **Oral:** Soft gelatin capsules containing 1.25 mg ergocalciferol, equivalent to 50,000 USP Units [FDA].
*   **Intravenous:** Concentrated emulsion for injection (as part of a multi-vitamin complex) providing 5 µg (200 IU) for adults or 10 µg (400 IU) for infants per 10 mL ampoule [TGA].

## 2. Approved Indications
Ergocalciferol is indicated for the following clinical uses:
*   **Treatment of Metabolic Disorders:** Management of hypoparathyroidism, refractory rickets (vitamin D resistant rickets), and familial hypophosphatemia [FDA].
*   **Nutritional Supplementation:** As a component of complete parenteral nutrition to meet daily requirements for fat-soluble vitamins (A, D2, E, and K1) in adult and pediatric patients [TGA].

## 3. Contraindications
Administration is contraindicated in the following circumstances:
*   Known hypersensitivity to ergocalciferol or any excipients (e.g., soy oil, egg lecithin, or tartrazine).
*   Hypercalcemia or pre-existing hypervitaminosis D [FDA, TGA].
*   Malabsorption syndrome [FDA].
*   **IV-Specific:** Patients in acute shock or with severe lipid metabolism disturbances (e.g., pathologic hyperlipaemia) due to the lipid emulsion carrier [TGA].

## 4. Warnings and Precautions
**Narrow Therapeutic Index:** In treating resistant rickets, the range between therapeutic and toxic doses is narrow. Dosage must be strictly individualized, and clinical improvement should be followed by prompt dose readjustment [FDA].

**Monitoring:** Patients on prolonged parenteral nutrition or high-dose oral therapy require periodic monitoring of blood vitamin levels, serum calcium, and phosphorus. In pediatric patients, daily requirements must be calculated precisely to prevent overdosage and toxic effects [TGA, FDA].

**Bone Health:** While vitamin D is essential for bone mineralization, fractures have been reported in patients on long-term parenteral nutrition. This syndrome has been observed to regress following the withdrawal of vitamin D supplements [TGA].

**Specific Excipient Warnings:**
*   **Tartrazine (FD&C Yellow No. 5):** Some oral formulations contain tartrazine, which may cause allergic-type reactions (including bronchial asthma) in susceptible individuals, particularly those with aspirin hypersensitivity [FDA].
*   **Soy and Egg:** Intravenous emulsions containing soya oil and egg lecithin may rarely cause allergic reactions; cross-reactivity between soybean and peanut has been observed [TGA].

## 5. Dosage and Administration
### Oral Administration (Therapeutic)
Dosage must be individualized under close medical supervision. 
*   **Vitamin D Resistant Rickets:** 12,000 to 500,000 USP Units daily.
*   **Hypoparathyroidism:** 50,000 to 200,000 USP Units daily, often administered alongside calcium lactate [FDA].

### Intravenous Administration (Supplemental)
Must be diluted before use and added aseptically to compatible infusion solutions (e.g., lipid emulsions or TPN admixtures).
*   **Adults and Children (≥11 years):** One 10 mL ampoule per day.
*   **Infants and Children (<11 years):** 1 mL/kg body weight per day, not to exceed a total daily dose of 10 mL [TGA].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are primarily associated with **Hypervitaminosis D**, which affects multiple organ systems:
*   **Renal:** Impairment of function, polyuria, nocturia, polydipsia, hypercalciuria, nephrocalcinosis, and potentially irreversible renal insufficiency.
*   **CNS:** Mental retardation and fatigue.
*   **Soft Tissue:** Widespread calcification of the heart, blood vessels, renal tubules, and lungs.
*   **Gastrointestinal:** Anorexia, nausea, vomiting, constipation, and weight loss.
*   **Skeletal:** Bone demineralization (osteoporosis) in adults; decreased linear growth and vague bone pain in children [FDA, TGA].

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **Mineral Oil:** May interfere with the intestinal absorption of oral vitamin D preparations [FDA].
*   **Thiazide Diuretics:** Concurrent use in hypoparathyroid patients may induce hypercalcemia [FDA].
*   **Anticoagulants:** If ergocalciferol is administered as part of a multi-vitamin IV complex containing Vitamin K1, it may interact with coumarin-type anticoagulants [TGA].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
**Pregnancy:** (Category C). High-dose vitamin D (exceeding 400 USP Units daily) should be avoided during pregnancy unless the potential benefits outweigh the significant risks to the fetus. Animal studies have linked hypervitaminosis D to fetal abnormalities similar to supravalvular aortic stenosis syndrome [FDA].

**Breastfeeding:** Vitamin D metabolites are excreted in human milk. High maternal doses may lead to infant hypercalcemia; therefore, monitoring of the infant's serum calcium is required if high-dose therapy is used [FDA]. Supplemental IV doses in TPN may be insufficient for the increased requirements of lactation [TGA].

## 9. Pharmacokinetics
*   **Absorption:** Oral ergocalciferol requires the presence of bile for absorption in the small intestine.
*   **Activation:** Ergocalciferol undergoes a two-step hydroxylation process: first in the liver to 25-hydroxyvitamin D, and second in the kidneys to the biologically active 1, 25-dihydroxyvitamin D.
*   **Onset:** There is a time lag of 10 to 24 hours between administration and the initiation of biological action due to the necessity of synthesis of active metabolites [FDA].

## 10. Mechanism of Action
Ergocalciferol is a synthetic calcium regulator. It facilitates the active absorption of calcium and phosphorus from the small intestine and increases the renal tubular reabsorption of phosphate. Together with parathyroid hormone, it also mobilizes calcium and phosphate from bone to maintain systemic mineral homeostasis [FDA].

## 11. Storage Conditions
*   **Oral Capsules:** Store between 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F). Protect from light [FDA].
*   **IV Ampoules:** Store unopened below 25°C. Protect from light and do not freeze. Once diluted or mixed into an infusion bag, the product should be used within 24 hours [TGA].
