# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial is derived from the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: United States (FDA).
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical data into a concise reference.
- This document is an editorial synthesis and serves as a supplement to, not a substitute for, the official Product Information required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - **Injection:** 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) single-dose vial [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    - The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg. 
    - **Administration:** Administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion. 
    - **Acute Crisis:** A loading dose is often given, followed by an equivalent dose over the next 24 hours. Dosing should occur every 3 to 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours.
    - **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses are typically 50 mg/kg, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilized.
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight.
    - **Administration:** Administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
    - **Dose Adjustment:** Adjustments (e.g., down to 5 mg/kg) should be guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations, which may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy.

### Monitoring
- Plasma carnitine concentrations should be obtained prior to initiating parenteral therapy.
- Weekly and monthly monitoring is recommended, including blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine levels between 35 and 60 µmol/L) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm have occurred, primarily in ESRD patients on dialysis. Some reactions manifest within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue the drug immediately and initiate appropriate medical management [US].
- **Seizures:** Levocarnitine administration (both oral and intravenous) has been associated with reports of seizures. In patients with pre-existing seizure disorders, an increase in the frequency or severity of seizures has been reported [US].
- **General Precautions:** The safety and efficacy of oral levocarnitine have not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in patients with severely compromised renal function or ESRD may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin. It is recommended to monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after dose adjustments [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not assigned letter category, but clinical data is limited. 
    - Animal reproductive studies (rats and rabbits) at 3.8 times the human dose (based on surface area) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine concentrations in milk following administration. The benefits of treatment to the mother must be weighed against potential risks of excess carnitine intake for the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders is weight-based (50 mg/kg) and applies to paediatric patients [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Specific dosing is provided for ESRD patients on hemodialysis (see Section 3). High-dose oral therapy is cautioned in patients with renal insufficiency due to metabolite accumulation [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into the mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters which are then eliminated [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Following intravenous administration, approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. The mean apparent terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Administration to ESRD patients on hemodialysis results in increased plasma levocarnitine concentrations [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients, 20 mg/kg administered three times weekly post-dialysis increased mean pre-dialysis trough concentrations from 21.3 ± 7.7 µmol/L to 190 ± 55 µmol/L after 8 weeks. While carnitine levels increased, the impact on clinical outcomes in this population has not been determined [US].
- **Bioavailability:** Absolute bioavailability from oral formulations (tablets and solution) is approximately 15-16% [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Conditions:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat.
- **Handling:** Discard any unused portion of a single-dose vial as the formulation contains no preservatives [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable for up to 24 hours at room temperature when mixed in PVC bags with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8.0 mg/mL) [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Rx-only (Prescription only).
- **Other Jurisdictions:** Not specified in available sources.
