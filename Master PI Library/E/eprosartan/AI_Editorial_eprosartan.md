# Editorial Product Information: Eprosartan Mesylate and Hydrochlorothiazide

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial product information is derived from the official labeling provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**. 
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonized editorial overview of eprosartan mesylate and hydrochlorothiazide for use by healthcare professionals.
- **Disclaimer:** This synthesis is an editorial reference and does not replace the official Product Information required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** Eprosartan mesylate (non-biphenyl non-tetrazole angiotensin II receptor antagonist) and Hydrochlorothiazide (thiazide diuretic).
- **Brand Names:** **TEVETEN HCT** [United States].
- **ATC Code:** C09DA02.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
  - Film-coated, non-scored, capsule-shaped tablets:
    - 600 mg eprosartan / 12.5 mg hydrochlorothiazide (Butterscotch-colored).
    - 600 mg eprosartan / 25 mg hydrochlorothiazide (Brick-red). [US]
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Abbott Laboratories / Physicians Total Care, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Hypertension:** Indicated for the management of hypertension. This fixed-dose combination may be used alone or in conjunction with other antihypertensive agents (e.g., calcium channel blockers). [US]
- **Note on Initial Therapy:** This fixed-dose combination is not indicated for the initial treatment of hypertension. [US]

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing:** The standard starting dose of eprosartan as monotherapy is 600 mg once daily for patients who are not volume-depleted.
- **Combination Therapy:** **TEVETEN HCT** 600 mg/12.5 mg once daily may be substituted for the individual components.
- **Titration:** If the blood pressure response is inadequate after 2 to 3 weeks at the 600 mg/12.5 mg strength, the dose may be increased to 600 mg/25 mg once daily. [US]
- **Maximum Dose:** Doses beyond 600 mg/25 mg once daily have not been studied in combination. [US]
- **Administration:** May be taken with or without food.
- **Missed Dose:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- Hypersensitivity to eprosartan mesylate or any component of the formulation. [US]
- **Anuria:** Contraindicated in patients with anuria due to the hydrochlorothiazide component. [US]
- **Sulfonamide Hypersensitivity:** Contraindicated in patients with known hypersensitivity to other sulfonamide-derived drugs. [US]

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning: Fetal Toxicity [US]:** 
  - Drugs acting on the renin-angiotensin system (RAS) can cause injury or death to the developing fetus when used in the second and third trimesters. 
  - **TEVETEN HCT** must be discontinued immediately upon detection of pregnancy.
- **Hypotension in Volume-/Salt-Depleted Patients:** Symptomatic hypotension may occur in patients with an activated RAS (e.g., those on high-dose diuretics). These conditions should be corrected prior to administration or initiated under close medical supervision. [US]
- **Renal Impairment:** Changes in renal function, including acute renal failure, may occur in susceptible individuals (e.g., those with severe heart failure or renal artery stenosis). Thiazides may precipitate azotemia in patients with renal disease. [US]
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Thiazides should be used with caution in patients with impaired hepatic function, as minor electrolyte imbalances may precipitate hepatic coma. [US]
- **Electrolyte Imbalance:** Monitor for clinical signs of fluid or electrolyte imbalance (hyponatremia, hypochloremic alkalosis, hypokalemia, and hypomagnesemia). [US]
- **Acute Myopia and Secondary Angle-Closure Glaucoma:** Hydrochlorothiazide can cause an idiosyncratic reaction resulting in acute transient myopia and acute angle-closure glaucoma. Discontinue treatment immediately if symptoms (decreased visual acuity, ocular pain) occur. [US]
- **Systemic Lupus Erythematosus:** Thiazide diuretics may cause exacerbation or activation of SLE. [US]

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse events are generally mild and transient. Discontinuation due to clinical adverse experiences was required in approximately 3% of patients in controlled trials. [US]

| Adverse Event | Eprosartan 600mg/HCTZ 12.5mg (n=268) | Placebo (n=246) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| Dizziness | 4.1% | 1.6% |
| Headache | 3.4% | 8.9% |
| Back Pain | 2.6% | 2.4% |
| Fatigue | 1.9% | 2.4% |

**Potentially Important Adverse Events (<1% frequency or monotherapy-related):**
- **Cardiovascular:** Angina pectoris, bradycardia, palpitation, orthostatic hypotension, tachycardia.
- **Gastrointestinal:** Pancreatitis (HCTZ-linked), diarrhea, vomiting, dry mouth, dyspepsia.
- **Hematologic:** Anemia, leukopenia, thrombocytopenia, agranulocytosis (HCTZ-linked).
- **Metabolic:** Hyperuricemia, hyperglycemia, hyperkalemia, gout.
- **Skin:** Stevens-Johnson syndrome, toxic epidermal necrolysis, photosensitivity, rash.
- **Serious Reactions:** Angioedema (rare). [US]

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **NSAIDs and COX-2 Inhibitors:** Co-administration with ARBs like eprosartan may lead to further deterioration of renal function, including possible acute renal failure, particularly in elderly or volume-depleted patients. The antihypertensive effect of **TEVETEN HCT** may also be attenuated. [US]
- **Lithium:** Thiazides reduce the renal clearance of lithium, significantly increasing the risk of lithium toxicity. Generally, should not be co-administered. [US]
- **Potassium Supplements/Sparing Diuretics:** Concomitant use may increase serum potassium. [US]
- **Digoxin/Warfarin/Glyburide:** No clinically significant pharmacokinetic or pharmacodynamic interactions were observed with eprosartan. [US]
- **Cholestyramine/Colestipol Resins:** These resins bind hydrochlorothiazide and reduce its absorption from the GI tract by 43% to 85%. [US]
- **Alcohol/Barbiturates/Narcotics:** May potentiate orthostatic hypotension when used with thiazides. [US]

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
  - **Category C [US] (First Trimester):** Limited data; use should be avoided.
  - **Category D [US] (Second and Third Trimesters):** Proven risk of fetal/neonatal injury (hypotension, neonatal skull hypoplasia, anuria, renal failure, and death).
- **Breastfeeding:** Hydrochlorothiazide is excreted in human milk; it is unknown if eprosartan is excreted. Due to the potential for serious adverse reactions in the infant, a decision should be made to discontinue nursing or the drug. [US]
- **Fertility:** Eprosartan had no adverse effects on the reproductive performance of rats at doses up to 1000 mg/kg/day. [US]

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness in patients under 18 years of age have not been established. [US]
- **Elderly Use:** No initial dosage adjustment is generally necessary. Clinical trials showed a slightly smaller antihypertensive response in patients over 65 compared to younger patients. [US]
- **Renal Impairment:** No initial dosage adjustment is required for moderate to severe impairment, provided the maximum daily dose of eprosartan does not exceed 600 mg. Thiazides are ineffective in severe renal impairment (CrCl <30 mL/min). [US]
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No initial dosage adjustment is necessary for eprosartan. However, caution is advised with the hydrochlorothiazide component. [US]

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:**
  - **Eprosartan:** Selectively blocks the binding of Angiotensin II to the $AT_1$ receptor, inhibiting vasoconstriction and aldosterone secretion. It does not inhibit ACE (kininase II) and thus does not affect bradykinin degradation.
  - **Hydrochlorothiazide:** A thiazide diuretic that inhibits sodium and chloride reabsorption in the distal convoluted tubule, increasing the excretion of sodium, chloride, and water.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
  - **Absorption:** Eprosartan absolute bioavailability is ~13%; peak plasma levels occur in 1 to 2 hours.
  - **Distribution:** Eprosartan is 98% protein bound. HCTZ crosses the placenta but not the blood-brain barrier.
  - **Metabolism:** Eprosartan is not metabolized by CYP450 enzymes. HCTZ is not metabolized.
  - **Elimination:** Eprosartan is eliminated via biliary and renal routes (90% in feces, 7% in urine). HCTZ is eliminated unchanged by the kidney (>61% within 24 hours). [US]

## 11. Clinical Trials
- In a 2x2 factorial study involving 1457 patients with mild-to-moderate hypertension, the combination of eprosartan 600 mg and HCTZ 12.5 mg resulted in a mean baseline- and placebo-subtracted reduction in blood pressure of 10.0/5.0 mmHg at 8 weeks. 
- Peak antihypertensive effects occur within 1 to 3 hours, and maximal blood pressure reduction is achieved in 2 to 3 weeks. [US]

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at controlled room temperature, 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F). [US]
- **Handling:** Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container. [US]

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources (refer to local jurisdiction).
