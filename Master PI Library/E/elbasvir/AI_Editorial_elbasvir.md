# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial: US (Food and Drug Administration).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of levocarnitine injection for use by healthcare professionals.
- **Note:** This synthesis is an editorial summary intended for informational purposes and does not serve as a substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP).
- **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule / Amino acid derivative ((R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt).
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - Sterile injection: 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency (Inborn Errors of Metabolism):** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg.
    - **Administration:** Delivered as a slow 2-to-3-minute bolus injection or by infusion.
    - **Loading Dose:** Often utilised in patients with severe metabolic crisis, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours.
    - **Frequency:** Administer every 3 or 4 hours; intervals must never be less than every 6 hours.
    - **Maximum Dose:** Doses up to 300 mg/kg have been administered.
- **ESRD on Hemodialysis:**
    - **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight.
    - **Administration:** Administered as a slow 2-to-3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
    - **Adjustment:** Initiation may be prompted by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma concentrations below 40 µmol/L to 50 µmol/L. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be made as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough levels.

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration.
- **Compatibility:** Stable for up to 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations between 0.5 mg/mL and 8 mg/mL [US].

### Missed Dose
- Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known:** There are no listed contraindications for levocarnitine injection in the available source [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None.
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm have occurred, primarily in ESRD patients on dialysis. Reactions can occur within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and consider the risks/benefits before re-administration [US].
- **Seizures:** Reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Monitoring Requirements:**
    - Monitor plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L).
    - Regular monitoring of blood chemistries, vital signs, and overall clinical condition is recommended [US].
- **Metabolite Accumulation:** In patients with severely compromised renal function or ESRD on dialysis, chronic administration of high oral doses may lead to accumulation of trimethylamine (TMA) and trimethylamine-N-oxide (TMAO), which are potentially toxic [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common and Very Common Adverse Effects
The following frequencies are derived from clinical trials in ESRD patients (≥5% occurrence):
- **Body as a Whole:** Injection site reaction (27–38%), pain (21–35%), flu syndrome (15–29%), headache (3–37%), chest pain (6–15%), infection (10–24%), abdominal pain (5–21%) [US].
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (18–21%), hypotension (3–19%), tachycardia (5–9%) [US].
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea (9–35%), vomiting (9–21%), nausea (5–12%), anorexia (3–6%) [US].
- **Nervous System:** Dizziness (10–18%), paresthesia (3–12%), insomnia (3–6%) [US].
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (15–27%), cough increase (10–18%), dyspnea (3–11%) [US].
- **Metabolic:** Hypercalcemia (6–15%), hypervolemia (3–12%) [US].

### Less Common and Postmarketing Experience
- **Serious:** Anaphylaxis, seizures.
- **General:** Body odor, gastritis, drug dependence, and taste perversion [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when used concomitantly with levocarnitine.
    - **Management:** Monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not explicitly lettered, but indicates "use only if clearly needed"]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. There are no adequate/controlled studies in pregnant women [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Safety has not been specifically studied in humans. Studies in dairy cows indicate increased carnitine concentrations in milk following administration. The benefits of supplementation to the mother should be weighed against potential risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility was found in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy are supported by use in metabolic disorders (dosing is mg/kg based). Specific age range limits are not defined in the source [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Dosing is specifically defined for ESRD patients on hemodialysis. The safety/efficacy of oral levocarnitine has not been evaluated in renal insufficiency [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring carrier molecule required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent ATP production [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Levocarnitine may promote the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters, which are rapidly excreted. This helps alleviate autointoxication and metabolic acidosis in certain organic acidopathies [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin. Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours (uncorrected for endogenous levels). Mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients (20 mg/kg three times weekly post-dialysis), mean trough levocarnitine levels increased from ~20 µmol/L (baseline) to 190 ± 55 µmol/L at 8 weeks.
- **Dose-Ranging Studies:** Trials comparing 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses over 12 and 24 weeks confirmed a dose-dependent increase in trough carnitine concentrations. However, the effect of this increase on specific clinical outcomes in ESRD patients has not been determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Conditions:** Protect from freezing and avoid excessive heat.
- **Handling:** Single-dose vials; discard any unused portion as the formulation contains no preservative [US].
