# Editorial Product Information: Elivaldogene Autotemcel

## Editorial Note
- Contributing Jurisdictions: United States (FDA).
- Purpose: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of elivaldogene autotemcel for healthcare professionals, synthesising regulatory information from the provided source.
- Note: This is an editorial synthesis and does not replace the official Product Information required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. At the time of writing, only the United States Product Information was available for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Elivaldogene autotemcel (an autologous hematopoietic stem cell-based gene therapy).
- **Chemical Class:** Autologous CD34+ cells transduced ex vivo with a Lenti-D lentiviral vector (LVV) encoding the human adenosine triphosphate binding cassette, sub family D, member 1 (*ABCD1*) complementary DNA (cDNA).
- **Brand Name:** **SKYSONA** [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Cell suspension for intravenous infusion. A single dose contains a minimum of 5.0 × 10⁶ CD34+ cells/kg of body weight, suspended in a solution containing 5% dimethyl sulfoxide (DMSO) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous infusion.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Genetix Biotherapeutics Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Cerebral Adrenoleukodystrophy (CALD):** Indicated to slow the progression of neurologic dysfunction in boys aged 4–17 years with early, active CALD who do not have an available human leukocyte antigen (HLA)-matched donor for allogeneic hematopoietic stem cell transplant (HSCT) [US].
    - *Definition of Early, Active CALD:* Asymptomatic or mildly symptomatic (Neurologic Function Score [NFS] ≤ 1) with gadolinium enhancement on brain MRI and Loes scores of 0.5–9 [US].
    - *Regulatory Status:* Approved under accelerated approval based on 24-month Major Functional Disability (MFD)-free survival. Continued approval may depend on verification of clinical benefit in confirmatory trials [US].
- **Limitations of Use:**
    - Does not treat or prevent adrenal insufficiency [US].
    - Not studied in CALD secondary to head trauma [US].
    - Efficacy may be rapidly lost in patients with full deletions of the *ABCD1* gene due to potential immune responses [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing:** Dosing is weight-based, determined by the number of CD34+ cells in the infusion bag(s). The minimum recommended dose is 5.0 × 10⁶ CD34+ cells/kg [US].
- **Pre-treatment Requirements:**
    - **Mobilization and Apheresis:** Patients must undergo HSC mobilization and apheresis to collect a minimum of 12 × 10⁶ CD34+ cells/kg for manufacturing [US].
    - **Back-up Collection:** A back-up collection of CD34+ cells (≥ 1.5 × 10⁶ CD34+ cells/kg) or bone marrow (≥ 1.0 × 10⁸ TNC/kg) must be cryopreserved before starting conditioning [US].
    - **Conditioning:** Full myeloablative and lymphodepleting conditioning must be completed before infusion. Ensure a minimum 48-hour washout period between the completion of conditioning and SKYSONA infusion [US].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - For autologous use only.
    - Confirm patient identity matches the infusion bag label exactly [US].
    - **Thawing:** Thaw at 37°C (98.6°F) for approximately 2–4 minutes. Do not leave unattended or submerge ports [US].
    - **Infusion:** Administer via gravity drip (not an infusion pump) within 4 hours of thawing. Use a period of less than 60 minutes. Do **not** use an in-line blood filter [US].
    - **Post-infusion:** Flush tubing with at least 50 mL of 0.9% sodium chloride [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None listed in available sources [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning: Hematologic Malignancy [US]:** Life-threatening hematologic malignancies, including myelodysplastic syndrome (MDS) and acute myeloid leukemia (AML), have occurred (incidence approx. 15% in clinical studies). These appear to be the result of SKYSONA treatment (insertional oncogenesis). 
    - *Monitoring:* Lifelong monitoring is required. Perform CBC with differential at least every 3 months. Assess for clonal expansion at least twice in the first year and then annually for at least 15 years [US].
- **Serious Infections:** Life-threatening bacterial, viral, and opportunistic infections (e.g., BK cystitis, CMV reactivation, adenovirus) may occur. Avoid administration in patients with active infections [US].
- **Prolonged Cytopenias:** Cytopenias (including pancytopenia) may persist for >1 year post-treatment. Monitor blood counts until normalization [US].
- **Delayed Platelet Engraftment:** Increased risk of bleeding until platelet recovery. Monitor counts frequently [US].
- **Neutrophil Engraftment Failure:** Defined as failure to achieve ANC ≥ 0.5 × 10⁹/L by Day 43. If failure occurs, administer the back-up rescue cells [US].
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Allergic reactions may occur due to DMSO.
- **Laboratory Test Interference:** SKYSONA interferes with PCR-based HIV assays, potentially causing false-positive results [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Very Common (>10%):** Mucositis (92%), nausea (84%), vomiting (76%), febrile neutropenia (73%), alopecia (72%), decreased appetite (64%), abdominal pain (45%), pyrexia (36%), headache (28%), constipation (42%), diarrhea (28%), rash (21%), epistaxis (19%), tachycardia (15%), anxiety (15%), transfusion reaction (12%), and hypertension (12%) [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:**
    - Hematologic malignancies (MDS, AML) [US].
    - Febrile neutropenia (18%), pyrexia (18%), and seizure (7%) [US].
    - **Grade 3/4 Laboratory Abnormalities (≥40%):** Leukopenia, lymphopenia, thrombocytopenia, neutropenia, anemia, and hypokalemia (all 100% except neutropenia 96%, anemia 84%, and hypokalemia 42%) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Anti-retrovirals:** Must be discontinued at least one month prior to mobilization and not resumed until all apheresis cycles are complete. These agents may interfere with SKYSONA manufacturing [US].
- **Vaccines:** Safety/efficacy of vaccination during or after treatment has not been studied. Avoid vaccination 6 weeks before conditioning and until hematological recovery [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** No human data available. No animal reproductive studies have been conducted. Risks associated with the required myeloablative conditioning agents must be considered [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** No information available regarding the presence of SKYSONA in human milk or its effects on the infant or milk production [US].
- **Fertility:** No data available for SKYSONA. However, myeloablative conditioning is associated with a risk of infertility. Advise patients of semen cryopreservation options [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy established in boys aged 4–17 years. Use in children <4 years has not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** CALD is a pediatric disease; SKYSONA is not intended for geriatric use [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not studied in these populations. Patients should be assessed for organ function to ensure they can tolerate HSCT procedures [US].
- **HIV Seropositive:** Not studied. Patients with positive HIV, HBV, HCV, or HTLV tests are ineligible for SKYSONA manufacturing [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** SKYSONA adds functional copies of the *ABCD1* cDNA into the patient's HSCs. After infusion and engraftment, these cells differentiate into monocytes (CD14+) that produce functional adrenoleukodystrophy protein (ALDP). ALDP facilitates the degradation of very long chain fatty acids (VLCFAs), potentially slowing neuroinflammation and demyelination [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** ALDP expression was quantifiable in CD14+ cells by Month 1 and remained stable through Month 24 [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Conventional PK studies (absorption, distribution, metabolism, elimination) are not applicable to this autologous cell therapy [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pivotal Evidence:** Safety and efficacy were evaluated in two single-arm studies (Study 1, N=32; Study 2, N=35) in boys with early, active CALD.
- **Outcomes:** Compared to an external natural history control, SKYSONA-treated symptomatic patients showed slower progression to MFD or death. The 24-month MFD-free survival was estimated at 72% for the SKYSONA group vs. 43% for the natural history population [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage:** Must be stored in the vapor phase of liquid nitrogen at ≤ -140°C (-220°F) [US].
- **Special Handling:**
    - Do **not** irradiate (inactivates the product).
    - Do **not** refreeze after thawing.
    - Standard biohazard precautions for handling human-derived gene therapy products apply [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as Rx-only. Approved under a Biologics License Application (BLA) [US].
