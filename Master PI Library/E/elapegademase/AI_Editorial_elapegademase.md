# Editorial Product Information: Elapegademase

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial synthesis is derived from the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States** (Food and Drug Administration).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of elapegademase for use by healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This synthesis is an editorial summary and does not replace the official PI required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority's current approved labeling for full prescribing details.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Elapegademase-lvlr.
- **Chemical Class:** Recombinant adenosine deaminase (rADA) based on a bovine amino acid sequence, conjugated to monomethoxypolyethylene glycol (mPEG) via a succinimidyl carbamate linker (SC-PEG rADA).
- **Brand Name:** **Revcovi** [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Injection: 2.4 mg/1.5 mL (1.6 mg/mL) clear, colorless, preservative-free solution in a single-dose vial [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intramuscular (IM) injection only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Chiesi USA, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Adenosine Deaminase Severe Combined Immune Deficiency (ADA-SCID):** Indicated for the treatment of ADA-SCID in both pediatric and adult patients [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Pediatric)
- **Transitioning from Adagen (pegademase bovine):**
  - If the previous weekly Adagen dose is unknown or $\le$ 30 U/kg: Start **Revcovi** at 0.2 mg/kg weekly, administered IM [US].
  - If the previous weekly Adagen dose was $>$ 30 U/kg: Calculate the weekly **Revcovi** dose (mg/kg) using the following formula:
    > $\text{Revcovi dose (mg/kg)} = \frac{\text{Adagen dose (U/kg)}}{150}$
- **Adagen-Naïve Patients:**
  - Starting dose: 0.4 mg/kg weekly, based on the greater of ideal body weight or actual weight [US].
  - The weekly dose should be divided into two injections (0.2 mg/kg twice weekly) for at least 12 to 24 weeks until immune reconstitution is confirmed [US].

### Titration and Maintenance
- Doses may be increased by increments of 0.033 mg/kg weekly if:
  - Trough plasma ADA activity is $< 30$ mmol/hr/L.
  - Trough erythrocyte dAXP levels are $> 0.02$ mmol/L.
  - Immune reconstitution is deemed inadequate by clinical assessment [US].
- The total weekly dose may be divided into multiple IM administrations during the week.

### Therapeutic Monitoring Schedule
- **Plasma ADA Activity:** Target trough levels $\ge 30$ mmol/hr/L. Monitor every 2 weeks (Adagen-naïve) or every 4 weeks (Adagen-transitioned) during the first 8–12 weeks of therapy, then every 3–6 months [US]. 
- **Erythrocyte dAXP:** Maintain trough levels below 0.02 mmol/L. Monitor two months after starting treatment, then at least twice annually [US].
- **Immune Function:** Monitor total and subset lymphocyte counts periodically (every 4–8 weeks for the first year in naïve patients; every 3–6 months for others) [US].

### Administration Instructions
- For IM injection only. Rotate injection sites periodically.
- **Preparation:** Do not dilute or mix with other drugs. Do not freeze or shake. Allow the vial to reach room temperature for 30 minutes before use.
- **Equipment:** Use polypropylene syringes. Draw with a 25-gauge needle or larger, then switch to a needle size appropriate for the patient's anatomy.
- **Timing:** Administer immediately after preparation and discard any unused portion [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None specified in the provided US documentation [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None [US].
- **Injection Site Bleeding (Thrombocytopenia):** Exercise caution in patients with low platelet counts; elapegademase should not be used if thrombocytopenia is severe due to the risk of local bleeding from IM administration [US].
- **Delay in Immune Improvement:** Clinical improvement in immune function may take months. Maintain infection prophylaxis and precautions until immune reconstitution is achieved [US].
- **Immunogenicity:** Development of anti-drug, anti-PEG, or neutralizing antibodies may lead to a persistent fall in trough plasma ADA activity (specifically if levels drop below 15 mmol/hr/L). If antibodies are suspected, testing should be performed and dosage adjustments or tolerance induction may be required [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Very Common (>10%):** Cough (50%), vomiting (33%) [US].
- **Other Reported Reactions (Clinical Trials):** Abdominal pain, arthralgia, asthenia, conjunctivitis, convulsion, diarrhea, epistaxis, fatigue, fungal skin infection, influenza, injection site discomfort, lymphadenopathy, migraine, nausea, and various respiratory tract infections [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:**
  - **Neutropenia** was reported in one patient in clinical trials [US].
  - **Death** due to CMV pneumonitis and respiratory failure occurred in one infant with pre-existing pulmonary hemorrhage [US].
- **Class Effect (based on Adagen experience):** Hemolytic anemia, autoimmune hemolytic anemia, thrombocythemia, thrombocytopenia, injection site erythema, urticaria, and lymphomas [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** The drug interaction potential of elapegademase is currently unknown [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:**
  - **US Category:** Not specified [US].
  - No adequate or well-controlled studies in pregnant women. Animal reproduction studies have not been conducted.
  - While no reports of pregnancy exist for elapegademase, successful pregnancies have been reported with the same class of enzyme replacement therapy (Adagen) with no teratogenic effects [US].
  - **Recommendation:** Monitor the health status of both mother and offspring closely during pregnancy [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** There are no data on the presence of elapegademase in human milk or its effects on the breastfed infant. The clinical need for the drug should be weighed against the benefits of breastfeeding [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies evaluating the impairment of fertility have not been performed [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy have been established in pediatric patients [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical studies did not include patients aged 65 years and older; therefore, safety and efficacy in this population are not established [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** ADA-SCID is caused by a deficiency in the ADA enzyme, leading to toxic accumulation of deoxyadenosine and adenosine, which causes T-lymphopenia. Elapegademase provides an exogenous source of the ADA enzyme to catalyze the deamination of these toxins, thereby supporting immune cell function and survival [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** The effect on the QT interval is unknown [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** 
  - **Steady State:** Reached after approximately 7 weeks of once-weekly IM dosing.
  - **Tmax:** Ranges from 27.2 to 72.0 hours.
  - **C-trough:** In clinical studies, mean trough ADA activity at a 0.2 mg/kg/week dose was 34.3 ± 6.6 mmol/hr/L [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Study 1 (US):** An open-label, crossover study of 6 patients (ages 8–37) transitioning from Adagen. Most patients maintained metabolic detoxification (dAXP $\le 0.02$ mmol/L) and achieved higher trough ADA activity compared to previous therapy. Lymphocyte counts increased 1.5- to 3-fold in several patients [US].
- **Study 2 (Japan):** An open-label study of 4 patients (ages 3.4 months to 25 years). All achieved and maintained metabolic detoxification. Three patients showed increased lymphocyte counts by Day 15 of treatment [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store in a refrigerator between 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Keep the vial in the original carton to protect from light. **Do not freeze or shake.** Discard if the product has been frozen [US].
- **Vial Stopper:** Not made with natural rubber latex [US].
