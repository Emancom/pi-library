## 3. Approved Indications

## 4. Dosage and Administration
### General dosing (adult)
- Adults and children 12 years of age and older: apply topically to affected area; use at minimum four times daily and then as needed. Best efficacy reported when used at first sign of an outbreak [US].

### Dosing by indication
- Symptom relief for genital herpes manifestations (itching, burning, pain, tingling): follow the general dosing above [US].

### Administration instructions
- Wash hands before and after applying the product.
- Apply generously to affected areas; apply only to affected areas.
- For external use only; do not use in or near the eyes.
- Do not share the product with others (may spread infection) [US].

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
- **For external use only** — do not apply internally or in/near the eyes [US].
- **This product is not a cure for herpes and will not prevent future outbreaks**; it will not stop viral transmission to others [US].
- Does not prevent other sexually transmitted infections (STIs); practice safer sex measures. The source also states the product may damage condoms and diaphragms and cause them to fail; therefore counselling regarding barrier method reliability is required when topical products are applied to genital areas [US].
- Consult a healthcare professional before using this product if this is the first occurrence of genital sores or blisters (first presentation may require diagnostic evaluation) [US].
- Do not share this product with others (to avoid potential spread of infection) [US].
- Stop use and consult a doctor if symptoms worsen [US].
- If pregnant or breastfeeding, ask a healthcare professional before use [US].
- Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, seek medical help or contact Poison Control immediately [US].

### Specific clinical precautions
