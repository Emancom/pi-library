# Editorial Product Information: Etravirine

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** Australian (TGA).
- **Purpose:** This document is a harmonised editorial overview of etravirine, synthesised from official Product Information for use by healthcare professionals.
- **Disclaimer:** This synthesis serves as a clinical reference and is not a substitute for the official Product Information mandated for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Etravirine.
- **Chemical Class:** Substituted diarylpyrimidine (DAPY) derivative; Non-nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitor (NNRTI).
- **Brand Names:** **Intelence** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** J05AG04.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - 100 mg tablets (White to off-white, oval, debossed with "T125" and "100") [Australian].
    - 200 mg tablets (White to off-white, biconvex, oblong, debossed with "T200") [Australian].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Janssen-Cilag Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **HIV-1 Infection:** Indicated for the treatment of HIV-1 infection in antiretroviral treatment-experienced adults with evidence of viral replication and resistance to NNRTIs and other antiretroviral agents. Must be used in combination with other antiretroviral medicinal products [Australian].
- **Note:** Use should be guided by treatment history and genotypic/phenotypic testing. The risks and benefits have not been established in treatment-naïve patients [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** 200 mg (one 200 mg tablet or two 100 mg tablets) taken orally twice daily (b.i.d.) [Australian].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Must be taken **following a meal** to ensure optimal absorption [Australian].
    - Tablets should ideally be swallowed whole with water.
    - **Dispersion Option:** For patients unable to swallow tablets whole, they may be dispersed in 5 mL (1 teaspoon) of water. Stir for 1 minute until milky. Additional water, orange juice, or milk (up to 30 mL) may be added. The dose must be consumed immediately, and the glass rinsed several times to ensure the full dose is taken. Warm (>40°C) or carbonated beverages must be avoided [Australian].
- **Missed Dose:**
    - If within 6 hours of the usual time: Take as soon as possible following a meal and resume the normal schedule.
    - If more than 6 hours after the usual time: Skip the missed dose and resume the normal dosing schedule. Do not double the dose [Australian].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to etravirine or any of the product's excipients (e.g., lactose monohydrate in 100 mg tablets) [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Severe Skin and Hypersensitivity Reactions:** 
    - Severe, potentially life-threatening, and fatal skin reactions (including Stevens-Johnson Syndrome and Toxic Epidermal Necrolysis) have been reported (<0.1%) [Australian]. 
    - Hypersensitivity reactions (e.g., DRESS) may present as rash, fever, malaise, fatigue, organ dysfunction, or hepatitis. 
    - **Clinical Action:** Discontinue **Intelence** immediately if severe rash or hypersensitivity symptoms develop. Delaying discontinuation may result in a life-threatening reaction [Australian].
- **Immune Reconstitution Inflammatory Syndrome (IRIS):** May occur during the initial phase of combination antiretroviral therapy (CART), leading to inflammatory responses to indolent or residual opportunistic infections (e.g., CMV retinitis, mycobacterial infections). Autoimmune disorders (e.g., Graves’ disease) have also been reported [Australian].
- **Fat Redistribution:** CART is associated with redistribution of body fat (lipodystrophy), including central obesity, buffalo hump, and peripheral wasting [Australian].
- **Transmission:** Patients must be advised that therapy does not prevent the risk of transmitting HIV to others through sexual contact or blood contamination [Australian].

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** SJS/TEN (<0.1%), DRESS, and rhabdomyolysis (post-marketing) [Australian].
- **Laboratory Abnormalities:** Grade 3 or 4 elevations in pancreatic amylase (8.9%), total cholesterol (8.1%), and triglycerides (9.2%) [Australian].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Profile:** Etravirine is a substrate of CYP3A4, CYP2C9, and CYP2C19. It is an **inducer** of CYP3A4 and an **inhibitor** of CYP2C9 and CYP2C19. It is a weak inhibitor of P-glycoprotein [Australian].
- **Contraindicated/Not Recommended Combinations:**
    - **Other NNRTIs:** Not recommended to co-administer (e.g., efavirenz, nevirapine).
    - **Unboosted PIs:** Not recommended (e.g., atazanavir, fosamprenavir).
    - **Boosted PIs:** Specific combinations like tipranavir/ritonavir or atazanavir/cobicistat are not recommended.
    - **Anticonvulsants:** Carbamazepine, phenobarbital, phenytoin (significantly decrease etravirine levels).
    - **Antimycobacterials:** Rifampin, rifapentine (significantly decrease etravirine levels).
    - **Herbal Products:** St. John’s wort.
- **Clinically Significant Interactions:**
    - **Maraviroc:** Etravirine decreases maraviroc levels; dose adjustment of maraviroc is required unless a boosted PI is also present.
    - **Clopidogrel:** Etravirine may decrease the activation of clopidogrel; consider alternatives.
    - **Warfarin:** Monitor INR frequently as etravirine may affect warfarin concentrations.
    - **Dexamethasone (systemic):** Use with caution; may decrease etravirine levels [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** **Category B1** [Australian]. There are no adequate, well-controlled studies in pregnant women. Animal studies show no developmental toxicity. Etravirine exposure is generally higher during pregnancy than postpartum. Use only if potential benefits outweigh risks [Australian].
- **Breastfeeding:** Mothers should be instructed **not to breastfeed** due to the potential for HIV transmission and adverse reactions in the infant. Etravirine is excreted in human milk [Australian].
- **Fertility:** No human data; no effects observed in animal models at human-equivalent exposures [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy have not been established in children or adolescents under 18 years of age [Australian].
- **Elderly Use:** Limited data. Type and incidence of adverse events in patients >55 years were similar to younger patients. Dose selection should be cautious [Australian].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment required. Etravirine is not significantly cleared by the kidneys (<1.2%) or by hemodialysis [Australian].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment required for mild to moderate (Child-Pugh A or B) impairment. Not studied in severe (Child-Pugh C) impairment [Australian].
- **Hepatitis B/C Co-infection:** No dose adjustment necessary, but standard monitoring for liver toxicity is required due to higher risk of AST/ALT elevations [Australian].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** NNRTI that binds directly to HIV-1 reverse transcriptase (RT) and blocks DNA polymerase activities. Its DAPY structure allows for torsional flexibility, permitting binding despite common NNRTI mutations.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** $T_{max}$ is approximately 4 hours. Absolute bioavailability is unknown. Absorption is reduced by 51% in the fasted state.
    - **Distribution:** 99.9% protein bound (primarily albumin and $\alpha_1$-acid glycoprotein).
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily oxidative metabolism via CYP3A4, 2C9, and 2C19, followed by glucuronidation.
    - **Elimination:** Terminal half-life is 30–40 hours. Excreted primarily in feces (93.7%) [Australian].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **DUET-1 and DUET-2:** Two identical Phase III trials in treatment-experienced adults with NNRTI resistance. At 48 weeks, 60.6% of the etravirine group achieved undetectable viral loads (<50 copies/mL) compared to 39.7% in the placebo group (pooled analysis). Etravirine also showed superior CD4+ cell count increases [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store below 30°C.
- **Condition:** Store in the original bottle and keep tightly closed to protect from moisture.
- **Handling:** Do not remove the desiccant pouches [Australian].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Australian Classification:** **Schedule 4 (S4)** – Prescription Only Medicine.
