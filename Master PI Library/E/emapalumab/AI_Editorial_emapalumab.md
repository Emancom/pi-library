# Editorial Product Information: Emapalumab

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial synthesis is derived from the following jurisdiction's Product Information:
    - US (Food and Drug Administration)
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising regulatory data into a single clinical reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority's approved labelling for definitive guidance.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Emapalumab-lzsg
- **Chemical Class:** Interferon gamma (IFNγ) neutralizing antibody; monoclonal immunoglobulin G1 (IgG1)
- **Brand Name(s):** **Gamifant** [US]
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):**
    - **Injection (5 mg/mL):** 10 mg/2 mL, 50 mg/10 mL, and 100 mg/20 mL single-dose vials [US].
    - **Injection (25 mg/mL):** 50 mg/2 mL, 100 mg/4 mL, 250 mg/10 mL, and 500 mg/20 mL single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Swedish Orphan Biovitrum AB (publ); Distributed by Sobi Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Primary Hemophagocytic Lymphohistiocytosis (HLH):** Treatment of adult and paediatric patients with refractory, recurrent, or progressive disease, or those who are intolerant to conventional HLH therapy [US-approved].
- **HLH/Macrophage Activation Syndrome (MAS) in Still’s Disease:** Treatment of adult and paediatric patients with known or suspected Still’s disease (including systemic Juvenile Idiopathic Arthritis [sJIA]) who have had an inadequate response or intolerance to glucocorticoids, or who have recurrent MAS [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
Dosing is based on actual body weight. The maximum titrated dose for either indication is **10 mg/kg** [US].

### Indication-Specific Dosing
- **Primary HLH:**
    - **Starting Dose:** 1 mg/kg administered via intravenous infusion over 1 hour, twice weekly (every 3 to 4 days) [US].
- **HLH/MAS in Still’s Disease:**
    - **Day 1:** 6 mg/kg [US].
    - **Days 4 to 16:** 3 mg/kg every 3 days for 5 doses [US].
    - **Day 19 onwards:** 3 mg/kg twice weekly (every 3 to 4 days) [US].

### Titration Schedules
Doses may be titrated upward if the clinical response is unsatisfactory based on clinical and laboratory criteria (e.g., persistent fever, inadequate platelet/neutrophil recovery, worsening ferritin, splenomegaly, or coagulopathy) [US].
- **Primary HLH Titration:** Incremental steps to 3 mg/kg, 6 mg/kg, and a maximum of 10 mg/kg [US].
- **HLH/MAS Titration:** If response is unsatisfactory, the dose may be increased to a maximum cumulative dose of 10 mg/kg over 3 days, and frequency may be increased to every 2 days or once daily [US].

### Administration Instructions
- **Preparation:** Dilute with 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection, USP.
    - 5 mg/mL vials: Dilute to 0.25–2.5 mg/mL [US].
    - 25 mg/mL vials: Dilute to 1.25–12.5 mg/mL [US].
- **Equipment:** Use a non-PVC polyolefin infusion bag and a sterile, non-pyrogenic, low-protein binding 0.2-micron in-line filter [US].
- **Infusion:** Administer over 1 hour. Do not infuse concomitantly with other agents [US].

### Concomitant Medication
- **Primary HLH:** Use in combination with dexamethasone. Dexamethasone (at least 5–10 mg/m²) should be started the day before **Gamifant** treatment begins [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None reported in provided sources [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Infections (Serious and Fatal):** Neutralization of IFNγ may increase susceptibility to pathogens such as mycobacteria, herpes zoster, and *Histoplasma capsulatum*. Gamifant should not be initiated in patients with these active infections until treated [US].
- **Tuberculosis (TB) Monitoring:** Patients must be evaluated for TB risk factors and tested for latent infection (PPD or IFNγ release assay) prior to initiation. Prophylaxis should be administered to at risk or PPD-positive patients [US].
- **Prophylaxis:** Consider prophylaxis for herpes zoster, *Pneumocystis jirovecii*, and fungal infections [US].
- **Live Vaccines:** Do not administer live or live-attenuated vaccines during treatment and for at least 4 weeks after the final dose [US].
- **Infusion-Related Reactions (IRRs):** Reported in 27% (Primary HLH) and 13% (HLH/MAS) of patients. Symptoms include rash, pyrexia, headache, and tachycardia. If severe, interrupt the infusion and manage medically; resume at a slower rate if appropriate [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Cytochrome P450 Substrates:** Chronic inflammation and high levels of cytokines (like IFNγ) can suppress CYP450 enzyme formation. By neutralizing IFNγ, **Gamifant** may normalize CYP450 activity, potentially increasing the metabolism and reducing the efficacy of drugs that are CYP450 substrates. Monitor for reduced efficacy of concomitant CYP substrates upon initiation or discontinuation of **Gamifant** [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:**
    - **US Category:** No lettered category provided; "Risk Summary" approach used. There are no available data on human use. Animal studies (mice) using a surrogate antibody showed placental transfer but no fetal harm or teratogenicity [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** No data exist regarding the presence of emapalumab-lzsg in human milk. Endogenous IgG is known to be present in milk, but systemic absorption in the infant is likely minimal. The benefits of breastfeeding should be weighed against the mother's clinical need [US].
- **Fertility:** No adverse effects on male or female reproductive organs were observed in repeat-dose toxicity studies in monkeys [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness are established in patients from birth to 17 years for the approved indications [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical studies did not include sufficient numbers of subjects aged 65 and over to determine if they respond differently than younger patients [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** No clinically significant differences in pharmacokinetics were observed in patients with renal impairment, including those on dialysis [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No clinically significant differences in pharmacokinetics were observed across mild, moderate, and severe hepatic impairment [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Emapalumab-lzsg is a monoclonal antibody that binds to and neutralizes interferon gamma (IFNγ). HLH is characterized by hypersecretion of IFNγ, which plays a pivotal role in the disease pathogenesis [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Treatment reduces plasma concentrations of CXCL9, a chemokine induced by IFNγ [US]. No clinically relevant QT interval prolongation was observed at 3 mg/kg [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Central and peripheral volumes of distribution are 2.8 L and 4.4 L (for a 70 kg adult) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Exhibits target-mediated clearance. The half-life is ~22 days in healthy subjects but varies significantly in HLH patients (2.5 to 18.9 days) depending on IFNγ levels; higher IFNγ concentrations shorten the half-life [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Expected to be degraded into small peptides and amino acids via catabolic pathways [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Primary HLH (Study NI-0501-04):** A single-arm trial of 27 paediatric patients. The overall response rate (ORR) at the end of treatment was 63% (95% CI: 0.42, 0.81), with 70% of patients proceeding to hematopoietic stem cell transplantation (HSCT) [US].
- **HLH/MAS in Still's Disease (Pooled Analysis):** Evaluation of 39 patients (median age 12 years). The complete response (CR) rate at Week 8 was 53.8% (95% CI: 37.2, 69.9), based on clinical MAS remission and laboratory parameters [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vials:** Store under refrigeration at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) in the original carton to protect from light. Do not freeze or shake [US].
- **Diluted Solution:** If not used immediately, store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C for no more than 4 hours. Allow to reach room temperature before administration [US].
