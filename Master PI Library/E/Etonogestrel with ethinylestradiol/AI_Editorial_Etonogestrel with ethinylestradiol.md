## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt. 
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection, USP (Baxter Healthcare Corporation) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Sterile injection, 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US]; Manufactured by Baxter Pharmaceuticals India Private Ltd [US/India].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency (Inborn Errors of Metabolism):** Indicated for the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that result in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing hemodialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg [US].
- **ESRD on Hemodialysis:** The recommended starting dose is 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].

### Administration Instructions
- **Metabolic Disorders:** Administer as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion. In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose is often given, followed by equivalent doses every 3 to 4 hours (never less than every 6 hours) over the subsequent 24 hours [US].
- **ESRD on Hemodialysis:** Administer as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line after each dialysis session [US].
- **Parenteral Inspection:** Visually inspect for particulate matter and discoloration before administration [US].

### Titration and Monitoring
- **Monitoring:** Obtain plasma carnitine concentrations before starting therapy. Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L) is recommended [US].
- **Dose Adjustments (ESRD):** Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be made based on trough (pre-dialysis) levocarnitine concentrations, typically by the third or fourth week of treatment [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known:** No specific contraindications are listed in available source documentation [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None [US].
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions can occur within minutes of IV administration. If severe, discontinue treatment and do not re-administer without a careful risk-benefit analysis and close monitoring [US].
- **Neurologic Effects (Seizures):** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Oral Formulation Caution:** The safety and efficacy of oral levocarnitine have not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral use in ESRD may lead to accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine/TMA and trimethylamine-N-oxide/TMAO) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have been observed when used concomitantly with levocarnitine. Monitor INR levels at therapy initiation and after dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not specified by letter, but reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (based on surface area) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm [US]. 
    - **Recommendation:** No adequate, well-controlled human studies exist; use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk carnitine concentrations after exogenous administration. Weigh the risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant against the benefits to the mother [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Use is indicated for both acute and chronic treatment of inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency; see dosage and administration for weight-based dosing [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** **Levocarnitine Injection** is indicated for ESRD patients on hemodialysis. However, safety/efficacy of *oral* levocarnitine is not established in renal insufficiency [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** **Levocarnitine** is a naturally occurring substance required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not protein-bound. Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours (uncorrected for endogenous levels). Mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].
    - **Bioavailability (Oral):** Approximately 15–16% [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In ESRD, it increases plasma carnitine levels and may alleviate metabolic abnormalities associated with inborn errors (e.g., glutaric aciduria II, methylmalonic aciduria) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Pharmacokinetic Studies:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients receiving 20 mg/kg post-dialysis showed mean pre-dialysis trough levels increased from ~20 µmol/L to 190 ± 55 µmol/L after 8 weeks of therapy [US].
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** Studies of 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg (n≈30 per group) demonstrated dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [US].
- **Clinical Outcomes Note:** While **levocarnitine** effectively increases plasma concentrations in ESRD patients, its effect on specific clinical outcomes (signs/symptoms of deficiency) in this population has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C–30°C [US].
- **Conditions:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat.
- **Handling:** Single-dose vials; discard any unused portion as the formulation contains no preservative [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable for up to 24 hours in PVC bags at room temperature when mixed with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) [US].
