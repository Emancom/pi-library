# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) provided by the following regulatory authority: **United States (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical and regulatory data into a concise reference.
- **Note:** This document is an editorial synthesis intended for informational purposes. It is not a substitute for the official, current Product Information approved by the relevant national regulatory authority in the jurisdiction where prescribing occurs.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine ((R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt).
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection: 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency (Inborn Errors):** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that result in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance haemodialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Administration
- Administer as an intravenous injection only.
- For metabolic disorders, may be given as a slow (2 to 3 minute) bolus or by infusion.
- For ESRD, administer as a slow (2 to 3 minute) bolus into the venous return line after each dialysis session.

### Metabolic Disorders
- **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg.
- **Loading Dose:** Often required in severe metabolic crisis, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours.
- **Frequency:** Administer every 3 or 4 hours; frequency should never be less than every 6 hours.
- **Dose Range:** Subsequent daily doses are typically 50 mg/kg, though therapy may require adjustment based on clinical response. The maximum recorded dose is 300 mg/kg [US].

### ESRD on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
- **Dose Adjustment:** Adjustments should be guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Downward titration (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy if levels are supratherapeutic.
- **Monitoring:** Target trough plasma free carnitine concentrations between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known at the time of publication [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal oedema, and bronchospasm, have been reported. These can occur within minutes of administration, particularly in ESRD patients on dialysis. If a reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In those with a known seizure disorder, an increase in frequency or severity of seizures may occur [US].
- **Monitoring Requirements:** Obtain plasma carnitine concentrations prior to starting therapy. Weekly and monthly monitoring is recommended, alongside blood chemistries, vital signs, and overall clinical assessment [US].
- **Oral Formulation Caution:** While this PI pertains to the injection, it is noted that chronic high-dose oral levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Frequency ≥5% (ESRD Clinical Trials)
Based on placebo-controlled trials in dialysis patients, the following events occurred at ≥5% regardless of causality [US]:
- **Body as a Whole:** Abdominal pain (9%), headache (22%), injection site reaction (33%), chest pain (12%), fever (7%), infection (15%).
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (20%), hypotension (14%), tachycardia (6%).
- **Digestive:** Diarrhoea (16%), nausea (8%), vomiting (15%).
- **Metabolic:** Hypercalcaemia (9%), hypervolaemia (5%).
- **Nervous:** Dizziness (13%), paraesthesia (5%), depression (5%).
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (23%), cough increase (9%), dyspnea (7%).

### Other Significant Effects
- **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea, vomiting, and body odour have been observed [US].
- **Neurologic:** Seizures (post-marketing report) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalised Ratio (INR) have occurred when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels after initiating levocarnitine or following dose adjustments in patients receiving anticoagulant therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not specified; text-based guidance provided]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Specific studies in humans are unavailable. Studies in dairy cows indicate increased carnitine concentrations in milk following administration. Weigh the benefits of maternal treatment against potential risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies showed no evidence of impaired fertility [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders is calculated by weight (50 mg/kg) [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Specific data not provided in available source.
- **Renal Impairment:** In ESRD patients on dialysis, plasma concentrations are significantly altered. Adjust dose based on pre-dialysis trough levels (Target 35–60 µmol/L) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In patients with inborn errors of metabolism or ESRD, it alleviates metabolic abnormalities by promoting the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids through acylcarnitine formation [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not protein-bound. Distribution half-life is 0.585 hours.
    - **Elimination:** Terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours. Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours (uncorrected for endogenous levels) [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites (TMA and TMAO) are formed via gut microflora (primarily relevant to oral administration).

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Dose-Ranging Studies:** Trials using 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses three times weekly after dialysis demonstrated dose-proportional increases in trough levocarnitine concentrations. At 24 weeks, mean trough levels were 148 ± 50 µmol/L (10 mg/kg), 240 ± 60 µmol/L (20 mg/kg), and 456 ± 162 µmol/L (40 mg/kg) [US].
- **Metabolic Clinical Impact:** While the injection effectively increases carnitine concentrations in ESRD patients, the definitive impact on specific clinical outcomes in this population has not been fully determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Protection:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat.
- **Compatibility:** Compatible and stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations of 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL [US].
- **Handling:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as it contains no preservatives [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Rx-only (Prescription only).
