# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine Injection

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**. 
- Documents from other jurisdictions (e.g., Australian (TGA), European (EMA), Canadian (Health Canada), or Japanese (PMDA)) were not available for comparison at the time of this writing.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical data into a concise reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI or local regulatory guidance required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP).
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available source [A16AA01].
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Sterile solution for injection: 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency in Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with genetic metabolic defects resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD undergoing maintenance haemodialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    - **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion [US].
    - **Loading Dose:** Often required in severe metabolic crises, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours.
    - **Frequency:** Administer every 3 to 4 hours; never less than every 6 hours [US].
    - **Maximum Dose:** Up to 300 mg/kg has been administered in clinical practice.
- **ESRD Patients on Haemodialysis:**
    - **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
    - **Administration:** Slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
    - **Dose Adjustment:** Guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations (target 40–50 µmol/L). Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Inspect visually for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration.
- Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Known Hypersensitivity:** No specific contraindications are listed in the available source; however, serious hypersensitivity reactions have been reported during use (see Warnings and Precautions).

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal oedema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Some reactions occur within minutes of administration. Treatment must be discontinued immediately if a reaction occurs [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with known seizure disorders, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Monitoring Requirements:** 
    - **Metabolic Disorders:** Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L) is recommended [US].
    - **ESRD:** Monitor trough plasma levels and adjust doses accordingly.
- **Oral Levocarnitine in Renal Impairment:** The safety/efficacy of oral levocarnitine in patients with severe renal impairment or ESRD has not been evaluated. Chronic high-dose oral administration in these patients may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse events reported in clinical trials for ESRD patients (frequency $\ge$ 5% regardless of causality):

| Body System | Adverse Event |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Injection site reaction (33%), Pain (30%), Flu syndrome (25%), Headache (22%), Abdominal pain (9%) |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension (20%), Hypotension (14%), Tachycardia (6%), Chest pain (12%) |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhoea (16%), Vomiting (15%), Nausea (8%), Anorexia (5%) |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis (23%), Cough increase (9%), Dyspnea (7%) |
| **Neurological** | Dizziness (13%), Insomnia (4%), Paresthesia (5%) |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Anaphylaxis, bronchospasm, and seizures [US].
- **Other:** Transient nausea and vomiting; body odour and gastritis have been reported less frequently [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalised Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after any dose adjustment in patients receiving warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Category:** Not assigned a letter category in the available source, but reproductive studies in rats and rabbits (up to 3.8 times the human dose) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm [US].
    - **Recommendation:** Should be used during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine in milk following administration. Risks of excess intake by the infant must be weighed against the benefits of supplementation to the mother [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility was found in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy are established for the treatment of inborn errors of metabolism (see Section 3 for dosing) [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically detailed in the available source.
- **Renal Impairment:** Used specifically in ESRD (see Section 3). Caution is advised regarding the accumulation of metabolites if oral forms are used in this population [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria for oxidation and energy production [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not protein-bound. 
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and $\gamma$-butyrobetaine (faeces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. Terminal elimination half-life is approximately 17.4 hours [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In ESRD, it alleviates the metabolic consequences of carnitine loss during dialysis and reduced renal synthesis.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients receiving 20 mg/kg after dialysis showed that trough levels increased from 21.3 $\mu$mol/L at baseline to 190 $\pm$ 55 $\mu$mol/L after 8 weeks [US].
- **Clinical Outcomes:** While levocarnitine increases plasma concentrations, the direct effect on clinical outcomes (e.g., muscle weakness, malaise) in ESRD patients has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C–30°C [US].
- **Precautions:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat.
- **Stability:** Compatible with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s (0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) for up to 24 hours at room temperature in PVC bags [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources (refer to local schedules for Australia S4, etc.).
