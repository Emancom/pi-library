# Editorial Product Information: Econazole Nitrate

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA). 
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial synthesis of the product information for econazole nitrate 1% topical cream. It is designed as a clinical reference for healthcare professionals.
- **Disclaimer:** This editorial is a professional synthesis and does not replace the official Product Information (PI) or Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. The provided source material identifies this product as a US-regulated pharmaceutical.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Econazole nitrate (an imidazole derivative antifungal).
- **Brand Names:** Generic formulations (e.g., Encube Ethicals) [US].
- **ATC Code:** D01AC03.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Topical cream containing 1% (10 mg/g) econazole nitrate [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical use only; strictly not for ophthalmic or intravaginal use [US].
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Encube Ethicals, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
Econazole nitrate cream 1% is indicated for the topical treatment of the following fungal skin infections:
- **Tinea pedis** (athlete's foot) [US].
- **Tinea cruris** (jock itch) [US].
- **Tinea corporis** (ringworm) [US].
- **Cutaneous candidiasis** [US].
- **Tinea versicolor** (pityriasis versicolor) [US].

The indicated pathogens include *Trichophyton rubrum*, *Trichophyton mentagrophytes*, *Trichophyton tonsurans*, *Microsporum canis*, *Microsporum audouini*, *Microsporum gypseum*, *Epidermophyton floccosum*, *Candida albicans*, and *Malassezia furfur* [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Tinea pedis, Tinea cruris, Tinea corporis, and Tinea versicolor:** Apply a sufficient quantity to cover the affected areas once daily [US].
- **Cutaneous candidiasis:** Apply a sufficient quantity to cover the affected areas twice daily (morning and evening) [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Gently rub the cream into the affected skin. 
- **Tinea versicolor:** Clinical and mycological clearing is typically observed after two weeks of therapy [US].
- **Tinea cruris, Tinea corporis, and Candidal infections:** Treatment should generally continue for two weeks to minimize recurrence [US].
- **Tinea pedis:** Treatment should continue for one month to reduce the risk of recurrence [US].

### Clinical Monitoring
If no clinical improvement is observed after the standard treatment period, the diagnosis should be reassessed [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** This product is contraindicated in any patient with a known history of hypersensitivity to econazole nitrate or any of the excipients in the formulation (including pegoxol 7 stearate, peglicol 5 oleate, mineral oil, benzoic acid, or butylated hydroxyanisole) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Ophthalmic Use:** Econazole nitrate cream is for external use only and must not be introduced into the eyes [US].
- **Sensitivity Reactions:** If a patient develops a chemical irritation or a localized sensitivity reaction, therapy should be discontinued immediately [US].
- **Occlusion/Large Areas:** Application to large surface areas, use under occlusive dressings, or application to the genital area may increase systemic absorption and potential for drug interactions [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
During clinical trials, approximately 3% of patients experienced adverse effects. Only 1% of patients reported effects deemed possibly related to the medication [US].

| Frequency | Adverse Effect |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Burning, itching, stinging, and erythema (total incidence ~3%) [US] |
| **Rare (<0.1%)** | Pruritic rash [US] |

**Serious Adverse Effects:** None identified in the provided clinical trial data for topical application [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
### Pharmacodynamic Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Concomitant use of topical econazole and oral warfarin has been reported to enhance the anticoagulant effect of warfarin. This interaction is primarily associated with application to large surface areas, genital application, or use under occlusion, which may facilitate higher systemic absorption [US].

### Management
- **INR Monitoring:** Clinicians should consider monitoring the International Normalized Ratio (INR) or prothrombin time more frequently in patients using warfarin who require extensive or occluded application of econazole nitrate [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
### Pregnancy
- **Risk Summary:** Econazole nitrate is not teratogenic in mice, rabbits, or rats when administered orally. However, fetotoxic or embryotoxic effects were noted in animal studies (rats) at oral doses 10 to 40 times the human dermal dose [US]. 
- **Recommendations:** Use during the **first trimester** only if the physician deems it essential to the patient's welfare. Use during the **second and third trimesters** only if clearly needed [US].

### Breastfeeding
- **Risk Summary:** It is unknown if econazole is excreted in human milk. Animal data shows excretion in the milk of lactating rats. Reduced postpartum viability and survival to weaning were seen in rat pups at very high maternal oral doses (40–80x human dermal dose) [US].
- **Recommendations:** Exercise caution when prescribing to a nursing woman [US].

### Fertility
- Long-term animal studies on carcinogenic potential or impairment of fertility in humans have not been performed [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness in paediatric populations are not specifically detailed in the available documentation [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical studies did not include enough subjects aged 65 and older to determine if they respond differently than younger subjects. In general, dose selection for an elderly patient should be cautious, typically starting at the lower end of the dosing range due to the higher frequency of decreased organ function and comorbid conditions [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Specific dose adjustments are not provided in the source documentation, though systemic absorption is minimal (<1%) [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
Econazole nitrate is an imidazole antifungal that interferes with the synthesis of ergosterol, a vital component of fungal cell membranes, leading to increased permeability and cell death [US].

### Pharmacokinetics
- **Absorption:** Systemic absorption following topical application to healthy skin is extremely low (<1%). Most of the drug remains on the skin surface [US].
- **Distribution:** Concentrations in the stratum corneum significantly exceed the minimum inhibitory concentrations for dermatophytes. The drug penetrates the epidermis and reaches as deep as the middle region of the dermis [US].
- **Elimination:** Less than 1% of an applied dose is recovered in the urine and faeces [US].

### Pharmacodynamics
Econazole exhibits broad-spectrum activity in vitro against dermatophytes (e.g., *Trichophyton*, *Microsporum*) and yeasts (e.g., *Candida*, *Malassezia furfur*) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage Temperature:** Store at controlled room temperature between 20°C and 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Ensure the cap is secured after use. Do not use if the seal is punctured or missing [US].
