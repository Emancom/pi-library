# Editorial Product Information: Ethanolamine Oleate

## Editorial Note

- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising regulatory information into a concise clinical reference.

Clinicians should refer to their local regulatory authority's current approved PI for the most up-to-date local requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Ethanolamine oleate (a mild sclerosing agent).
- **Brand Name(s):** Ethamolin [US].
- **ATC Code:** B05BB04 (Note: Not explicitly listed in source, derived from standard classification).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 5% Injection (50 mg ethanolamine oleate per mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Local intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** QOL Medical, LLC [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Esophageal Varices:** Indicated for the treatment of patients with esophageal varices that have recently bled, specifically to prevent rebleeding [US].
- **Important Limitations of Use:** 
    - Not indicated for patients with esophageal varices that have NOT bled; there is no evidence of efficacy in decreasing the likelihood of initial bleeding [US].
    - Not recommended for the treatment of varicosities of the leg [US].
    - No beneficial effect upon portal hypertension [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** 
    - **Per Varix:** 1.5 mL to 5.0 mL intravenously per varix [US].
    - **Maximum Dose:** Should not exceed 20 mL per treatment session [US].
- **Administration Instructions:** 
    - Must be administered by physicians familiar with acceptable injection techniques [US].
    - Submucosal injections are NOT recommended as they increase the risk of local ulceration [US].
    - Inspect visually for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration [US].
- **Titration/Schedule:** Injections may be performed at the time of the acute bleeding episode, then subsequently after one week, six weeks, three months, and six months as clinically indicated [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to ethanolamine, oleic acid, or ethanolamine oleate [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None specified in available sources [US].
- **Anaphylaxis:** Fatal anaphylactic shock has been reported. Physicians must be prepared to treat allergic reactions immediately. Recommended emergency management: 0.25 mL of a 1:1,000 intravenous solution of epinephrine (0.25 mg) and antihistamines [US].
- **Tissue Necrosis:** Severe injection site necrosis may occur, particularly if excessive volumes are used or if the drug is injected incorrectly. Esophageal necrosis and death have been reported [US].
- **Cardiorespiratory Disease:** Patients with concomitant cardiorespiratory disease require careful monitoring and minimisation of the total dose per session [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Acute renal failure has been reported following injections of 15–20 mL [US].
- **Esophageal Complications:** Risks include ulceration, stricture, and perforation. Patients with Child Class C liver disease are at higher risk for esophageal ulceration [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
The overall frequency of complications/adverse events is approximately 13% per injection session [US].

| Adverse Effect | Frequency |
| :--- | :--- |
| Pleural effusion/infiltration | 2.1% |
| Esophageal ulcer | 2.1% |
| Pyrexia | 1.8% |
| Retrosternal pain | 1.6% |
| Esophageal stricture | 1.3% |
| Pneumonia | 1.2% |

**Other Adverse Effects (Frequency 0.1% to 0.4%):**
- **Local Esophageal Reactions:** Esophagitis, tearing of the esophagus, mucosal sloughing, necrosis, periesophageal abscess, and perforation [US].
- **Systemic Reactions:** Bacteremia, anaphylactic shock, acute renal failure, and disseminated intravascular coagulation (DIC) [US].
- **Neurological:** Spinal cord paralysis (occlusion of the anterior spinal artery) has been reported in a paediatric case [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category C:** Animal reproduction studies have not been conducted. It is unknown if the drug causes fetal harm. Use in pregnant women only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** It is not known if ethanolamine oleate is excreted in human milk. Exercise caution when administering to nursing women [US].
- **Fertility:** Data not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established [US]. One case of spinal cord paralysis was reported in a child following sclerotherapy [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Special precautions are required to prevent fatal aspiration pneumonia, which has occurred in elderly patients during the procedure [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Spontaneous recovery from acute renal failure has been reported; dose-related risks may apply [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Patients with Child Class C liver dysfunction should receive doses lower than the recommended maximum, as they are more susceptible to complications like ulceration [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Acts as an irritant to the intimal endothelium of the vein, producing a dose-related inflammatory response. This leads to fibrosis and eventual occlusion of the vein.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** The oleic acid component triggers inflammation and may activate coagulation via tissue factor/Hageman factor release. The ethanolamine component may paradoxically inhibit fibrin clot formation by chelating calcium [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** 
    - **Distribution:** Disappears from the injection site within five minutes via the portal vein. 
    - **Pulmonary Impact:** If volumes >20 mL are used, the drug may flow into the azygos vein and potentially reach the lungs, leading to pulmonary complications [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Procedural Outcomes:** Autopsy studies show neutrophil infiltration (within 4 days), hemorrhage (within 6 days), granulation tissue (10 days), red thrombi (20 days), and complete sclerosis/obliteration (2.5 months) [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at controlled room temperature, 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from light [US].
