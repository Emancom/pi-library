# Editorial Product Information: tetrahydrozoline + polyethylene + zinc

## 1. Product Identification
- Active ingredient (INN/generic name): **tetrahydrozoline hydrochloride + polyethylene glycol 400 + zinc sulfate**
  - Strengths (as stated in available source): tetrahydrozoline hydrochloride 0.05% (0.5 mg/mL); polyethylene glycol 400 (PEG 400) 1% (10 mg/mL); zinc sulfate 0.25% (2.5 mg/mL).
- Chemical class: Not specified in available sources.
- ATC code: Not specified in available sources.
- Route(s) of administration: Ophthalmic (eye drops).
- Inactive ingredients (as stated): benzalkonium chloride, boric acid, edetate disodium, glycerin, hypromellose, purified water, sodium chloride, sodium citrate.

## 3. Approved Indications
- Temporary relief of discomfort and redness of the eye due to minor eye irritations.
- Relief of dryness of the eye.
- Temporary relief of burning and irritation due to exposure to wind or sun.
- Protection against further irritation.
- Jurisdictional qualifiers: Indications above are presented as stated in the available source; no region-specific differences were provided in the available sources.

## 4. Dosage and Administration
### General dosing (adult and paediatric)
- Adults and children 6 years of age and over: instil 1–2 drops in the affected eye(s) up to 4 times daily.
- Children under 6 years of age: consult a doctor before use.
- Children under 2 years of age: do not use.

### Administration instructions
- For ophthalmic use only.
- Remove contact lenses before using.
- Do not touch the tip of the container to any surface to avoid contamination.
- Do not use if the solution changes colour or becomes cloudy.
- Replace cap after each use.

### Titration schedules

### Missed dose instructions

## 5. Contraindications
- No explicit contraindications are listed in the available source documents.
- Precautionary statement: ask a doctor before use if the patient has narrow-angle glaucoma (see Warnings and Precautions).

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
- **For external ophthalmic use only.**
- Ask a doctor before use if the patient has narrow-angle glaucoma.
- During use:
  - Some users may experience a brief tingling sensation on instillation.
  - Pupils may become temporarily enlarged.
  - Overuse may cause increased eye redness (rebound hyperemia).
  - Remove contact lenses before use.
  - Do not use if the solution becomes discoloured or cloudy.
  - Avoid contamination of the dropper tip.
- Stop use and seek medical advice if:
  - Eye pain develops.
  - Changes in vision occur.
  - Redness or irritation of the eye lasts, worsens, or persists for more than 72 hours.
- If swallowed: obtain medical help or contact a Poison Control Center immediately.
- If pregnant or breastfeeding: consult a health professional before use.
- Keep out of reach of children.
