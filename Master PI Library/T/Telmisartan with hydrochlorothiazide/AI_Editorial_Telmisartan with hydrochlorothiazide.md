# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical data into a coherent reference. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official PI or summary of product characteristics (SmPC) required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory documents for specific legal and prescribing requirements. Information regarding other jurisdictions (e.g., Australian, European, Canadian, Japanese) was not provided in the source materials and is therefore marked as not specified.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP).
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Acute and chronic management of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg. It should be administered as a slow bolus injection (2–3 minutes) or via infusion [US].
- **ESRD on Hemodialysis:** The recommended starting dose is 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight). This should be administered as a slow bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].

### Dosing by Indication
- **Acute Metabolic Crisis:** A loading dose is often administered, followed by equivalent doses every 3 to 4 hours (and never less than every 6 hours) over the subsequent 24 hours [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Administer intravenously only.
- Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations ranging from 0.5 mg/mL to 8.0 mg/mL [US].

### Titration and Monitoring
- **Monitoring:** Obtain baseline plasma carnitine levels. Weekly and monthly monitoring of plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L), blood chemistries, and vital signs is recommended [US].
- **Dose Adjustment (ESRD):** Adjustments may be made as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough (pre-dialysis) carnitine levels (e.g., reduction to 5 mg/kg) [US].

### Missed Dose Instructions
- Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Known Hypersensitivity:** There are no known contraindications listed in the provided source, though hypersensitivity to the drug is a general clinical contraindication [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have occurred, occasionally within minutes of IV administration. Most reports involve patients with ESRD on dialysis. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue immediately [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure disorders. In patients with known seizure activity, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Laboratory Monitoring:** Plasma carnitine levels must be monitored to ensure therapeutic concentrations and avoid potential toxicity from metabolite accumulation in renal impairment [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects observed in clinical trials of ESRD patients (frequency ≥5%) regardless of causality:

| Body System | Adverse Event (Incidence ≥5%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Injection site reaction (33%), Pain (30%), Flu syndrome (25%), Headache (22%), Abdominal pain (9%), Fever (7%) |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension (20%), Hypotension (14%), Tachycardia (6%), Chest pain (12%) |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea (16%), Vomiting (15%), Nausea (8%), Anorexia (5%) |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis (23%), Cough increase (9%), Dyspnea (7%), Rhinitis (9%) |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness (13%), Paresthesia (5%), Depression (5%), Insomnia (4%) |
| **Metabolic** | Hypercalcemia (9%), Hyperkalemia (6%), Hypervolemia (5%) |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm [US].
- **Other:** Transient nausea and vomiting; body odor and gastritis have been reported less frequently [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after any dose adjustments in patients receiving anticoagulant therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not assigned in text]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits using 3.8 times the human dose (based on surface area) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if levocarnitine is excreted in human milk; however, studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine concentrations in milk after administration. Risks of excess intake to the infant should be weighed against the benefits of supplementation for the mother [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders is weight-based (50 mg/kg) and applicable to pediatric patients [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical studies did not include sufficient numbers of subjects aged 65 and over to determine if they respond differently than younger subjects [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    - **Oral Levocarnitine:** Not evaluated in renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in patients with severely compromised renal function or ESRD may lead to accumulation of toxic metabolites (TMA and TMAO) [US].
    - **IV Levocarnitine:** Indicated for use in ESRD patients on dialysis [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria for oxidation and subsequent energy production [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In patients with inborn errors of metabolism or ESRD, levocarnitine helps clear accumulated acyl-CoA compounds by forming acylcarnitine, which is rapidly excreted [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not protein-bound.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (TMAO) and γ-butyrobetaine.
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of a single IV dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours. The mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of ESRD patients, IV levocarnitine (20 mg/kg thrice weekly after dialysis) increased mean plasma carnitine concentrations from 20 µmol/L (pre-dialysis) to troughs of 190 µmol/L after 8 weeks. Similar results were seen in 12- and 24-week dose-ranging studies (10, 20, and 40 mg/kg) [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Precautions:** Avoid excessive heat. Protect from freezing.
- **Handling:** Discard any unused portion of a single-dose vial as it contains no preservatives [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Rx-only; Single-dose vials (NDC 36000-368-10) [US].
- **Other Jurisdictions:** Poison/Schedule classification not specified in available sources.
