# Editorial Product Information: Tolcapone

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**. 
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. 
- This document is an editorial synthesis and serves as a clinical reference; it is not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific Product Information required for prescribing decisions in a particular region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Tolcapone, a selective and reversible inhibitor of catechol-O-methyltransferase (COMT).
- **Brand Name:** Tasmar [US].
- **ATC Code:** N04BD01 (Not explicitly stated in source, but standard pharmaceutical classification).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 100 mg film-coated tablets. The tablets are hexagonal, biconvex, and beige to yellowish-beige in color [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Oceanside Pharmaceuticals (a division of Bausch Health US, LLC) [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Parkinson's Disease:** Indicated as an adjunct to levodopa and carbidopa for the treatment of signs and symptoms of idiopathic Parkinson’s disease. Due to the risk of potentially fatal, acute fulminant liver failure, it should be reserved for patients experiencing symptom fluctuations who are not responding satisfactorily to, or are not appropriate candidates for, other adjunctive therapies [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** The recommended dose is 100 mg three times daily (TID) [US]. 
- **Maximum Dose:** In clinical trials, 200 mg TID was used; however, elevations in ALT occurred more frequently at this dose. 200 mg TID should only be used if the anticipated incremental clinical benefit justifies the risk [US].
- **Administration Instructions:** Always take in conjunction with levodopa/carbidopa. The first daily dose should be taken with the first daily dose of levodopa/carbidopa. Subsequent doses are taken approximately 6 and 12 hours later. May be taken with or without food [US].
- **Concomitant Levodopa Adjustment:** Most patients require a reduction in their daily levodopa dose (average reduction ~30%) if their daily dose is >600 mg or if they have pre-existing moderate-to-severe dyskinesia [US].
- **Clinical Benefit Assessment:** Therapy should be discontinued if the patient fails to show substantial clinical benefit within 3 weeks of initiation [US].
- **Withdrawal:** Avoid abrupt withdrawal. Tapering is recommended to prevent symptoms resembling neuroleptic malignant syndrome (e.g., hyperpyrexia and confusion) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Liver Disease:** Patients with clinical evidence of liver disease [US].
- **Prior Tolcapone Injury:** Patients withdrawn from tolcapone due to evidence of drug-induced hepatocellular injury [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to tolcapone or its inactive ingredients [US].
- **Neuroleptic Malignant Syndrome/Rhabdomyolysis:** History of nontraumatic rhabdomyolysis or hyperpyrexia and confusion possibly related to medication [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning (Hepatotoxicity):** **Tolcapone is associated with a risk of potentially fatal acute fulminant liver failure.** Therapy must not be initiated in patients with liver disease or AST/ALT values > upper limit of normal (ULN). Prescribing requires written patient acknowledgement of risks [US].
- **Liver Monitoring:** 
    - Baseline: Determine ALT and AST.
    - Ongoing: Every 2 to 4 weeks for the first 6 months. Thereafter, monitor at clinically relevant intervals.
    - Dose increase: If increasing to 200 mg TID, repeat the 2-to-4-week monitoring cycle for an additional 6 months [US].
- **Discontinuation Criteria:** Immediately stop treatment if ALT/AST exceeds 2x ULN or if clinical signs of hepatic dysfunction (jaundice, fatigue, dark urine, etc.) occur [US].
- **Somnolence and Sudden Sleep Onset:** Patients may fall asleep during activities of daily living without prior warning. Caution is required when driving or operating machinery [US].
- **Hypotension:** May exacerbate orthostatic hypotension associated with dopaminergic therapy [US].
- **Hallucinations and Psychosis:** May cause or worsen psychotic-like behavior and hallucinations, typically within the first 2 weeks of therapy [US].
- **Impulse Control:** Intense urges to gamble, spend money, or increased sexual urges have been reported [US].
- **Dyskinesia:** May potentiate levodopa-induced dyskinesia [US].
- **Diarrhea:** Commonly occurs (16-18%); usually presents 6 to 12 weeks after initiation and can be severe [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects

| **Dyskinesia** | 20% | 42% | 51% |
| **Nausea** | 18% | 30% | 35% |
| **Sleep Disorder** | 18% | 24% | 25% |
| **Anorexia** | 13% | 19% | 23% |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Fulminant liver failure, rhabdomyolysis, and Neuroleptic Malignant Syndrome-like complex (associated with withdrawal) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Monoamine Oxidase (MAO) Inhibitors:** Non-selective MAOIs (e.g., phenelzine, tranylcypromine) should not be used concomitantly due to the risk of inhibiting the majority of catecholamine metabolism pathways. Selective MAO-B inhibitors (e.g., selegiline) can be used [US].
- **COMT-metabolised Drugs:** Caution and dose reduction should be considered for drugs like $\alpha$-methyldopa, dobutamine, and apomorphine when co-administered [US].
- **Warfarin:** Limited clinical data; monitor coagulation parameters as tolcapone may interfere with CYP2C9-mediated metabolism in vitro, although in vivo studies with tolbutamide showed no effect [US].
- **Desipramine:** Caution is advised; the frequency of adverse events increased slightly when combined with tolcapone and levodopa [US].
- **Protein Binding:** Tolcapone is highly protein-bound (>99.9%) but does not displace warfarin, phenytoin, or tolbutamide at therapeutic concentrations in vitro [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category C]. There are no adequate and well-controlled studies in pregnant women. Animal studies showed increased rates of abortion and fetal malformations when given with levodopa/carbidopa (which are known teratogens in rabbits). Use only if potential benefit justifies potential risk to the fetus [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Tolcapone is excreted in rat milk. It is unknown if it is excreted in human milk. Exercise caution [US].
- **Fertility:** No effect on fertility or general reproductive performance was observed in rats at doses up to 300 mg/kg/day [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** No identified potential use in paediatric patients [US].
- **Elderly Use:** No overall differences in safety or efficacy between patients >65 and younger patients, though those >75 may have a higher incidence of hallucinations [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment needed for mild-to-moderate impairment. Exercise caution in patients with severe renal impairment (CrCl <25 mL/min); this population has not been adequately studied [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Contraindicated in patients with active liver disease or elevated baseline transaminases. Clearance of unbound tolcapone is reduced by 50% in moderate cirrhotic liver disease (Child-Pugh Class B) [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Selective and reversible COMT inhibitor. In the presence of a decarboxylase inhibitor, COMT becomes the primary metabolising enzyme for levodopa. Tolcapone increases and sustains levodopa plasma levels, allowing for more constant dopaminergic stimulation in the brain [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Rapid; $T_{max}$ ~2 hours. Bioavailability ~65%. Food decreases bioavailability by 10-20% [US].
    - **Distribution:** Small volume of distribution (9 L). Protein binding >99.9% (primarily to albumin) [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Almost completely metabolised via glucuronidation to an inactive conjugate [US].
    - **Elimination:** Elimination half-life is 2–3 hours. 60% excreted in urine and 40% in feces [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Single 200 mg dose inhibits erythrocyte COMT activity by >80% at peak [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Fluctuating Patients:** Two 3-month trials in patients with "wearing-off" phenomena showed that tolcapone (100 mg and 200 mg TID) significantly increased "On" time and decreased "Off" time compared to placebo. Daily levodopa doses were also significantly reduced [US].
- **Non-fluctuating Patients:** A 6-month trial in stable patients showed significant improvements in Activities of Daily Living (UPDRS Subscale II) and reduced levodopa requirements [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- Store in tight containers at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as Rx-only. Requires a signed "Patient Acknowledgement of Risks" form prior to initiation [US].
