# Editorial Product Information: Teprotumumab

## Editorial Note
*   **Contributing Jurisdictions:** This editorial synthesis incorporates Product Information (PI) from the Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration) and the United States (Food and Drug Administration).
*   **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview intended for healthcare professionals. It synthesises clinical data and regulatory requirements from the provided sources into a single reference.
*   **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official, legally binding Product Information required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority's approved PI for full prescribing details.

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredient:** Teprotumumab (referred to as teprotumumab-trbw in the [US]).
*   **Chemical Class:** Insulin-like growth factor-1 receptor (IGF-1R) inhibitor; fully human IgG1 monoclonal antibody produced by recombinant DNA technology in Chinese Hamster Ovary (CHO-DG44) cells.
*   **Brand Name:** **TEPEZZA**
*   **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Dosage Form and Strength:** Lyophilised powder for injection; 500 mg single-dose vial. When reconstituted with 10 mL of sterile water, the solution contains 47.6 mg/mL.
*   **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion.
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Amgen Australia Pty Ltd [Australian]; Horizon Therapeutics USA, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Thyroid Eye Disease (TED):** 
    *   Indicated for the treatment of moderate to severe Thyroid Eye Disease [Australian].
    *   Indicated for the treatment of Thyroid Eye Disease regardless of disease activity or duration [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
The recommended treatment course consists of 8 intravenous infusions administered every 3 weeks:
*   **Initial Dose:** 10 mg/kg body weight.
*   **Subsequent Doses:** 20 mg/kg body weight for the remaining 7 infusions.

### Reconstitution and Preparation
1.  **Calculate Dose:** Determine the required mg dose and number of vials based on patient weight.
2.  **Reconstitute:** Using aseptic technique, reconstitute each 500 mg vial with 10 mL of Sterile Water for Injection. Gently swirl (do not shake) until the cake is dissolved.
3.  **Dilute:** The reconstituted solution (47.6 mg/mL) must be further diluted in a 0.9% Sodium Chloride infusion bag.
    *   For doses < 1800 mg: Use a 100 mL bag.
    *   For doses ≥ 1800 mg: Use a 250 mL bag.
4.  **Volume Maintenance:** To maintain constant volume, remove a volume of sodium chloride from the bag equal to the volume of reconstituted **TEPEZZA** to be added.
5.  **Final Mix:** Mix by gentle inversion; do not shake.

### Administration Instructions
*   **Route:** IV infusion only. Do not administer as a bolus or push.
*   **Infusion Rate:** 
    *   First two infusions: Administer over at least 90 minutes. 
    *   Subsequent infusions: If well-tolerated, the rate may be increased to a minimum of 60 minutes. 
    *   If not well-tolerated, maintain the 90-minute rate or reduce the rate further.
*   **Concomitant Administration:** Do not infuse with other agents through the same line.

## 4. Contraindications
*   **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to teprotumumab or any of the excipients (histidine, histidine hydrochloride monohydrate, polysorbate 20, trehalose dihydrate).
*   **Pregnancy:** **TEPEZZA** is contraindicated in pregnancy based on the risk of fetal loss and malformations [Australian-specific contraindication; the US source warns against use and highlights fetal risk but does not list it under the formal 'Contraindications' section].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
*   **Hearing Impairment Including Hearing Loss:** **TEPEZZA** may cause severe hearing impairment, which may be permanent in some cases. Events include deafness, sensorineural hearing loss, tinnitus, and eustachian tube dysfunction. 
    *   *Monitoring:* Assess hearing before, during, and after treatment. Consider audiograms where clinically warranted.
    *   *Management:* Perform a benefit-risk assessment for patients with pre-existing hearing loss. Advise patients to avoid smoking and high-intensity noise.
*   **Infusion Reactions:** Reported in approximately 4% of patients. Symptoms include hypertension, tachycardia, dyspnoea, headache, and muscular pain.
    *   *Management:* If a reaction occurs, interrupt or slow the infusion. For subsequent doses, consider pre-medication with antihistamines, antipyretics, or corticosteroids. Discontinue permanently if an anaphylactic reaction occurs.
*   **Exacerbation of Pre-existing Inflammatory Bowel Disease (IBD):** **TEPEZZA** may cause flares in patients with IBD. 
    *   *Monitoring:* Monitor for symptoms (diarrhoea, blood in stool, abdominal pain). Discontinue treatment if IBD worsens.
*   **Hyperglycaemia:** Increased blood glucose occurred in 10% of patients in clinical trials, two-thirds of whom had pre-existing diabetes or impaired glucose tolerance.
    *   *Management:* Assess glycaemic status prior to and during treatment. Ensure optimal control of diabetes before starting therapy.

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions are based on a safety profile of 285 patients [Australian] or 170 patients in premarketing trials [US].

### Frequency Data (Very Common $\ge$ 10%)
*   **Musculoskeletal:** Muscle spasms (25%–28%)
*   **Gastrointestinal:** Nausea (10%–17%), Diarrhoea (12%–15%)
*   **Dermatological:** Alopecia (13%), Dry skin (8%–10%)
*   **Metabolic:** Hyperglycaemia (10%–13%)
*   **Ear/Labyrinth:** Hearing impairment (10%–14%)
*   **Neurological:** Headache (8%–11%), Dysgeusia (8%–9%)
*   **General:** Fatigue (12%–13%)
*   **Reproductive:** Menstrual disorders (amenorrhoea, dysmenorrhoea) in ~23% of menstruating women [US].

### Common (1% to 10%)
*   Nail disorders (5%–6%)
*   Weight decreased (5%–6%) [US]
*   Infusion-related reactions (4%)
*   Ear discomfort (7%) [Australian]
*   Dry skin (10%) [Australian]

### Serious Adverse Effects
*   Bowel perforation [US post-marketing]
*   Diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA) or hyperosmolar hyperglycaemic state (HHS)
*   Permanent hearing loss
*   Severe IBD exacerbation

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **Formal Studies:** No clinical drug-drug interaction studies have been conducted.
*   **Ototoxic Medications:** Caution is advised when co-administering **TEPEZZA** with known ototoxic agents (e.g., aminoglycosides, vancomycin, loop diuretics, platinum-based chemotherapy) due to potential additive hearing impairment [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy:** 
    *   **Australia:** Category X. Contraindicated.
    *   **United States:** Fetal harm may occur. Fetal loss and multiple malformations (e.g., misshapen cranium, micrognathia, ossification abnormalities) were observed in animal studies. 
    *   **Recommendation:** Verify negative pregnancy status before initiation. Advise monthly testing during treatment and for 6 months after the final dose.
*   **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if teprotumumab is excreted in human milk. Because maternal IgG is present in breast milk, a risk to the infant cannot be excluded. A decision should be made to either discontinue breastfeeding or the drug.
*   **Fertility:** Animal studies showed no changes in reproductive organ weights or histology, but formal fertility studies in humans have not been performed.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established in patients under 18 years of age.
*   **Elderly Use:** No overall differences in efficacy or safety were observed between patients $\ge$ 65 years and younger patients.
*   **Renal Impairment:** No clinically significant PK differences were observed in mild to moderate renal impairment. Data for severe impairment are not available.
*   **Hepatic Impairment:** The effect of hepatic impairment on the PK of teprotumumab is unknown. No clinically significant PK differences were noted in patients with mildly elevated bilirubin or transaminases in clinical trials.

## 10. Pharmacology
*   **Mechanism of Action:** Teprotumumab is an IGF-1R antagonist. By binding to IGF-1R, it blocks the activation of the receptor by its ligands and may inhibit stimulatory autoantibodies in TED that cause orbital fibroblast proliferation and inflammation.
*   **Pharmacokinetics:**
    *   **Distribution:** Central volume of distribution (Vc) is ~3.0 L to 3.3 L; peripheral volume (Vp) is ~3.8 L to 4.3 L.
    *   **Metabolism:** Expected to undergo proteolysis into small peptides and amino acids.
    *   **Excretion:** Clearance is ~0.27 L/day. 
    *   **Half-life:** The elimination half-life is approximately 20 days.

## 11. Clinical Trials
Efficacy was primarily evaluated in randomized, double-masked, placebo-controlled trials (Studies 1, 2, 3 in Acute TED; Study 4 in Chronic TED).
*   **Primary Outcome:** Proptosis responder rate ($\ge$ 2 mm reduction in proptosis without deterioration in the fellow eye).
*   **Key Results:** 
    *   **Acute TED:** Responder rates were significantly higher for teprotumumab (71%–89%) compared to placebo (10%–20%) at week 24.
    *   **Chronic TED:** Significant improvement in proptosis was also demonstrated (62% vs 25% for placebo) [Australian].
    *   **Secondary Outcomes:** Significant improvements were noted in Clinical Activity Score (CAS) and diplopia (double vision) responder rates.

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   **Unopened Vials:** Refrigerate at 2°C to 8°C. Store in the original carton to protect from light. **Do not freeze.**
*   **Reconstituted/Diluted Solution:** The product contains no preservatives. Combined storage (vial and bag) should not exceed:
    *   4 hours at room temperature (20°C to 25°C).
    *   48 hours under refrigeration (2°C to 8°C).
*   If refrigerated, allow the diluted solution to reach room temperature before infusion.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
*   **Australia:** Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine).
*   **United States:** Rx-only.
