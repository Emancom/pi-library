# Editorial Product Information: Tivozanib

## Editorial Note
- This editorial was developed using the official Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising regulatory data into a single clinically relevant resource.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an expert editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI or local prescribing guidelines required for clinical practice in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Tivozanib (as tivozanib hydrochloride).
- **Chemical Class:** Tyrosine kinase inhibitor (TKI).
- **Brand Name:** **FOTIVDA** [US].
- **ATC Code:** L01EK03.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Oral capsules available in 1.34 mg and 0.89 mg strengths [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** AVEO Pharmaceuticals, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Advanced Renal Cell Carcinoma (RCC):** Indicated for the treatment of adult patients with relapsed or refractory advanced RCC who have received two or more prior systemic therapies [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** The recommended dose is 1.34 mg taken orally once daily for 21 consecutive days, followed by a 7-day rest period to complete a 28-day cycle [US].
- **Duration of Therapy:** Treatment should continue until disease progression or the development of unacceptable toxicity.
- **Administration Instructions:** 
    - Capsules should be swallowed whole with a glass of water. 
    - Do not open or crush capsules. 
    - May be taken with or without food.
- **Hepatic Impairment Adjustment:** 
    - **Moderate Hepatic Impairment:** Reduce dose to 0.89 mg daily for 21 days on, 7 days off [US].
    - **Severe Hepatic Impairment:** Avoid use; safety has not been established.
- **Missed Dose Instructions:** If a dose is missed, the patient should take the next dose at the regularly scheduled time. Two doses should not be taken simultaneously to make up for a missed dose [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **None:** There are no contraindications listed in the current available source [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypertension and Hypertensive Crisis:** Severe hypertension occurred in 45% of patients. Blood pressure (BP) must be controlled before starting therapy. Monitor BP after 2 weeks and at least monthly thereafter. In cases of persistent hypertension despite anti-hypertensive therapy, dose reduction or interruption is required [US].
- **Cardiac Toxicity:** May cause serious or fatal cardiac failure, cardiac ischemia, and arterial thromboembolic events (including MI and stroke). Patients with a recent history (within 6 months) of these events were excluded from clinical trials. Discontinue therapy for any severe arterial thromboembolic event [US].
- **Venous Thromboembolic Events (VTE):** Reported in 2.4% of patients. Monitor for symptoms of DVT and PE. Discontinue for severe or life-threatening VTE [US].
- **Hemorrhagic Events:** Serious and fatal bleeding events occurred in 11% of patients. Closely monitor those at risk. Discontinue for Grade 3 or 4 bleeding [US].
- **Proteinuria:** Reported in 8% of patients. Monitor urinalysis periodically. Withhold treatment if proteinuria is ≥ 2 grams/24 hours and resume at a reduced dose when it drops to ≤ 2 grams/24 hours. Discontinue for nephrotic syndrome [US].
- **Gastrointestinal Perforation and Fistula:** Periodically monitor for signs of GI perforation or fistula. Discontinue permanently if these occur [US].
- **Thyroid Dysfunction:** Hypothyroidism occurred in 8% of patients. Monitor thyroid function before initiation and periodically throughout treatment [US].
- **Impaired Wound Healing:** Withhold tivozanib for at least 24 days prior to elective surgery. Do not resume until at least 2 weeks after major surgery and once adequate wound healing is confirmed [US].
- **Reversible Posterior Leukoencephalopathy Syndrome (RPLS):** Evaluate any patient presenting with seizures, headache, visual changes, or confusion. Discontinue tivozanib if RPLS is confirmed [US].
- **Allergic Reactions (Tartrazine):** The 0.89 mg strength contains FD&C Yellow No. 5 (tartrazine), which may cause allergic-type reactions in susceptible individuals, particularly those with aspirin hypersensitivity [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Very Common (>10%):** Fatigue (67%), hypertension (44%), diarrhea (43%), decreased appetite (39%), nausea (30%), dysphonia (27%), hypothyroidism (24%), cough (22%), stomatitis (21%), and back pain (19%) [US].
- **Common (1–10%):** Weight decreased (17%), bleeding (17%), rash (18%), vomiting (18%), and dyspnea (15%) [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Bleeding (3.5%), VTE (3.5%), arterial thromboembolism (2.9%), acute kidney injury (2.3%), and hepatobiliary disorders (2.3%) [US].
- **Laboratory Abnormalities (Grade 3/4 ≥5%):** Sodium decreased (9%), lipase increased (9%), and phosphate decreased (5%) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Strong CYP3A4 Inducers:** Concomitant use with strong inducers (e.g., rifampin) decreases tivozanib exposure by approximately 52%, potentially reducing efficacy. Avoid co-administration [US].
- **Strong CYP3A4 Inhibitors:** No clinically significant change in tivozanib exposure was observed when co-administered with ketoconazole [US].
- **Transporter Systems:** Tivozanib inhibits BCRP in vitro; the clinical significance is not fully established [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not lettered under PLLR]. Based on animal studies, tivozanib can cause fetal harm and embryofetal lethality. Verify pregnancy status in females of reproductive potential before starting treatment.
- **Contraception:**
    - **Females:** Use effective contraception during treatment and for 1 month after the final dose [US].
    - **Males:** Use effective contraception during treatment and for 1 month after the final dose if they have female partners of reproductive potential [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** There are no data on the presence of tivozanib in human milk. Advise patients not to breastfeed during treatment and for 1 month after the final dose [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal data indicate tivozanib may impair fertility in both males and females [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy have not been established in patients under 18 years of age [US]. Animal studies showed dental and growth plate abnormalities.
- **Elderly Use:** In clinical studies, no overall differences in safety or effectiveness were observed between patients ≥ 65 years and younger patients [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment is recommended for patients with mild to severe renal impairment (CrCl 15-89 mL/min). Data are not available for patients with end-stage renal disease [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:**
    - **Mild:** No dose adjustment required [US].
    - **Moderate:** Reduce dose to 0.89 mg [US].
    - **Severe:** Not recommended; not studied [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Tivozanib is a potent and selective tyrosine kinase inhibitor of VEGFR-1, -2, and -3. It inhibits angiogenesis, vascular permeability, and tumor growth.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Median Tmax is 10 hours. Food does not significantly affect exposure [US].
    - **Distribution:** Highly protein-bound (≥ 99%), primarily to albumin.
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily metabolized by CYP3A4. Unchanged tivozanib is the major circulating component (90%) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Mean half-life is approximately 111 hours. Excreted mainly in feces (79%) and urine (12%) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **TIVO-3 Trial:** A randomized, open-label trial comparing tivozanib (n=175) to sorafenib (n=175) in patients with relapsed/refractory advanced RCC.
    - **Primary Outcome:** Median Progression-Free Survival (PFS) was 5.6 months for tivozanib vs. 3.9 months for sorafenib (HR 0.73; p=0.016) [US].
    - **Objective Response Rate (ORR):** 18% for tivozanib vs. 8% for sorafenib [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Keep out of reach of children.
