# Editorial Product Information: Treprostinil (Inhalation)

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following regulatory authority: **US (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonized, clinically accurate editorial overview of treprostinil inhalation solution for use by healthcare professionals.
- **Note:** This document is an editorial synthesis for informational purposes and does not substitute for the official PI or summary of product characteristics (SmPC) required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. Always consult your local regulatory guidelines and the specific manufacturer’s labeling for clinical practice.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Treprostinil
- **Chemical Class:** Prostacyclin mimetic (tricyclic benzindene analogue of prostacyclin)
- **Brand Name(s):** **Tyvaso** [US]
- **ATC Code:** B01AC21
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Sterile solution for oral inhalation. Available in 2.9 mL ampules containing 1.74 mg treprostinil (0.6 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral inhalation only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** United Therapeutics Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension (PAH; WHO Group 1):** To improve exercise ability. Efficacy established primarily in patients with NYHA Functional Class III symptoms with idiopathic/heritable PAH or PAH associated with connective tissue diseases [US].
- **Pulmonary Hypertension associated with Interstitial Lung Disease (PH-ILD; WHO Group 3):** To improve exercise ability. Efficacy established in patients with idiopathic interstitial pneumonia (including IPF), combined pulmonary fibrosis and emphysema (CPFE), and WHO Group 3 connective tissue disease [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Frequency:** Administered in four separate treatment sessions daily, approximately four hours apart, during waking hours [US].
- **Initial Dosage:** 3 breaths (approximately 18 mcg of treprostinil) per treatment session. if 3 breaths are not tolerated, reduce to 1 or 2 breaths [US].
- **Titration:** Increase dosage by an additional 3 breaths per session at intervals of 1 to 2 weeks, as tolerated [US].
- **Target Maintenance Dose:** 9 to 12 breaths (54–72 mcg) per treatment session, four times daily [US].

### Administration Instructions
- **Device Requirement:** Must be used exclusively with the **Tyvaso Inhalation System** [US].
- **Preparation:** One ampule contains enough medication for all four daily sessions. The entire contents should be emptied into the medicine cup daily. The device must be cleaned at the end of each day, and any remaining medication discarded [US].
- **Safety Precautions:** Avoid skin or eye contact with the solution; do not ingest orally [US].

### Missed Dose
- If a session is missed or interrupted, therapy should be resumed as soon as possible at the usual dose [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None specified in available sources [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Symptomatic Hypotension:** As a systemic vasodilator, treprostinil may produce symptomatic hypotension, particularly in patients with low baseline systemic arterial pressure [US].
- **Risk of Bleeding:** Treprostinil inhibits platelet aggregation. Co-administration with anticoagulants or antiplatelet agents may increase the risk of bleeding [US].
- **Bronchospasm:** Inhalation of treprostinil may induce acute bronchospasm. Patients with reactive airway disease (asthma, COPD) are at higher risk and should be optimally treated for their underlying condition before and during therapy [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Treprostinil clearance is significantly reduced in hepatic insufficiency, increasing the risk of dose-dependent adverse effects. Titrate slowly in mild-to-moderate impairment [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are generally related to the delivery route (local irritation) or the pharmacodynamic effects of prostacyclin (vasodilation).

| Adverse Reaction | Frequency (PAH Study) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Cough** | Very Common (>50%) |
| **Headache** | Very Common (41%) |
| **Throat Irritation / Pharyngolaryngeal Pain** | Very Common (25%) |
| **Nausea** | Very Common (19%) |
| **Flushing** | Very Common (15%) |
| **Syncope** | Common (6%) |

- **Post-Marketing Experience:** Angioedema has been reported (frequency not estimable) [US].
- **Other Reactions:** Diarrhea, dizziness, nasal discomfort, and muscle/bone/jaw pain [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **CYP2C8 Inhibitors:** (e.g., gemfibrozil) Co-administration increases systemic exposure (Cmax and AUC) to treprostinil. Dosage adjustments may be required [US].
- **CYP2C8 Inducers:** (e.g., rifampin) Co-administration decreases systemic exposure, potentially reducing clinical effectiveness [US].
- **Antihypertensives/Vasodilators:** Potential additive blood pressure-lowering effects [US].
- **Anticoagulants/Antiplatelets:** Increased risk of bleeding due to treprostinil's effect on platelet function [US].
- **Pharmacokinetic Neutrality:** No significant interactions were observed with bosentan, sildenafil, warfarin, acetaminophen, or fluconazole [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - [US]: Category Not Assigned (current labeling style). Limited human data are insufficient to inform drug-associated risks. Animal studies showed fetal skeletal malformations in rabbits at high doses (exposure ~145 times human AUC), while no harm was seen in rats at exposure ~1479 times human AUC.
    - Clinical Consideration: PAH is associated with high maternal and fetal mortality [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** No data exist regarding the presence of treprostinil in human milk, effects on the breastfed infant, or effects on milk production [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies in rats showed no effect on male or female fertility or mating performance [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness in patients under 18 years of age have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical trials included 47.8% of patients aged 65 and over. No overall differences in safety or efficacy were observed, but dose selection should be cautious due to a higher frequency of hepatic, renal, or cardiac dysfunction [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Clearance was reduced by up to 80% in mild-to-moderate hepatic insufficiency. Titrate slowly. Use in severe hepatic impairment has not been studied [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment is required. The drug is not cleared by dialysis [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** A prostacyclin analogue that causes direct vasodilation of pulmonary and systemic arterial vascular beds and inhibits platelet aggregation [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Single doses of 54 mcg and 84 mcg have been shown to prolong the QTc interval by approximately 10 ms, dissipating rapidly as concentrations decrease [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Absolute systemic bioavailability is approximately 64% (18 mcg) to 72% (36 mcg) after inhalation [US].
    - **Distribution:** Approximately 91% bound to human plasma proteins [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Substantially metabolized by the liver, primarily via **CYP2C8** [US].
    - **Elimination:** Biphasic; terminal half-life is approximately 4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **TRIUMPH I (PAH):** A 12-week study (n=235) in patients on background bosentan or sildenafil. Treatment with treprostinil resulted in a placebo-corrected median increase in 6-Minute Walk Distance (6MWD) of 20 meters at peak exposure (p<0.001) [US].
- **INCREASE (PH-ILD):** A 16-week study (n=326). Treatment resulted in a placebo-corrected median change in 6MWD of 21 meters (p=0.004) and a 39% reduction in the risk of clinical worsening events (HR 0.61; 95% CI: 0.40, 0.92) [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage:** Store at 20-25°C (68-77°F); excursions permitted to 15-30°C [US].
- **Light Sensitivity:** Ampules should be stored in the unopened foil pouch to protect from light [US].
- **Shelf Life After Opening:** Once the foil pouch is opened, ampules should be used within 7 days. Once an ampule is opened and transferred to the device, the solution must be used within 24 hours [US].
