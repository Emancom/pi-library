# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
*   This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following regulatory authority: **United States (FDA)**.
*   The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical and regulatory data into a single reference.
*   **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulations and the current approved PI for their region.

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine ((R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt).
*   **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
*   **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection, USP (Baxter Healthcare Corporation) [US].
*   **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
*   **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    *   Sterile solution for intravenous injection: 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL) [US].
*   **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Metabolic Disorders:** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
*   **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with end-stage renal disease undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
*   **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion [US].
*   **Loading Dose:** Often required in patients with severe metabolic crisis, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours [US].
*   **Frequency:** Should be administered every 3 or 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours [US].
*   **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses typically range around 50 mg/kg, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been administered [US].

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
*   **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
*   **Administration:** Slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].
*   **Dose Adjustments:** Guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy if levels are adequate [US].

### General Instructions
*   **Monitoring:** Obtain baseline plasma carnitine levels. Monitor weekly and monthly, including blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma concentrations (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L) [US].
*   **Visual Inspection:** Inspect for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration [US].

## 4. Contraindications
*   None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
*   **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions may occur within minutes of administration. Discontinue immediately if a reaction occurs [US].
*   **Seizures:** Levocarnitine (oral or IV) has been associated with seizures in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been reported [US].
*   **TMA/TMAO Accumulation:** While specific to chronic high-dose *oral* therapy, clinicians should be aware that in patients with severely compromised renal function, metabolites such as trimethylamine (TMA) and trimethylamine-N-oxide (TMAO) may accumulate [US].
*   **Monitoring:** Regular monitoring of plasma carnitine levels is essential to prevent over-supplementation and ensure therapeutic efficacy.

## 6. Adverse Effects
*   **Common Reactions:** Transient nausea and vomiting. Less frequent effects include body odor and gastritis [US].
*   **ESRD Clinical Trial Data (Frequency ≥5%):**
    *   **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (18–21%), hypotension (3–19%), tachycardia (5–9%) [US].
    *   **Gastrointestinal:** Diarrhea (9–35%), abdominal pain (5–21%), vomiting (9–21%) [US].
    *   **Neurological:** Headache (3–37%), dizziness (10–18%), paresthesia (3–12%) [US].
    *   **General:** Injection site reaction (27–38%), chest pain (6–15%), fever (5–12%), asthenia (8–12%) [US].
    *   **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (15–27%), cough increase (10–18%) [US].
*   **Postmarketing Experience:** Seizures and serious hypersensitivity reactions (anaphylaxis) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalised Ratio (INR) have occurred with concurrent use. **Clinical Management:** Monitor INR levels after initiating levocarnitine or following dose adjustments in patients on warfarin therapy [US].
*   **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Levocarnitine is not bound to plasma proteins, reducing the likelihood of displacement-based interactions [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy:** 
    *   **US Classification:** Not explicitly lettered, but indicates "use only if clearly needed" [US].
    *   **Data:** Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) revealed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. There are no adequate/controlled studies in pregnant women [US].
*   **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk carnitine after exogenous administration. Benefits to the mother should be weighed against potential risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant [US].
*   **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy for metabolic disorders and ESRD are established as per the "Dosage and Administration" section [US].
*   **Elderly Use:** Not specifically detailed in available sources.
*   **Renal Impairment:** The injection is indicated for use in ESRD patients on dialysis. High-dose oral therapy is cautioned against in severe renal impairment due to metabolite accumulation (TMA/TMAO) [US].
*   **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
*   **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria for beta-oxidation. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
*   **Pharmacodynamics:** In ESRD, it replenishes carnitine lost during dialysis and corrects the increased acylcarnitine/carnitine ratio [US].
*   **Pharmacokinetics:**
    *   **Distribution:** Not protein-bound; distribution half-life is approximately 0.585 hours [US].
    *   **Metabolism:** Major metabolites are trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (faeces) [US].
    *   **Elimination:** Terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours. Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours (uncorrected for endogenous levels) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   **ESRD Studies:** Double-blind, placebo-controlled trials in chronic haemodialysis patients demonstrated that levocarnitine injection (10, 20, or 40 mg/kg) significantly increased plasma carnitine concentrations. Pre-dialysis (trough) levels reached steady state (approx. 190–243 µmol/L) within 8 to 12 weeks of therapy [US].
*   **Efficacy Note:** While the injection effectively increases carnitine concentrations in ESRD patients, the impact on specific clinical outcomes (e.g., muscle weakness, cardiomyopathy) has not been definitively determined in this population [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
*   **Conditions:** Avoid excessive heat; protect from freezing [US].
*   **Shelf Life:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial; contains no preservative [US].
*   **Compatibility:** Compatible and stable for 24 hours at 25°C when mixed in Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s in PVC bags (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) [US].
