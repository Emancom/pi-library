# Editorial Product Information: Trolamine Salicylate

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) provided by the **United States (FDA)**. 
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. 
- This document is an editorial synthesis intended for reference only and is not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific PI required for prescribing or dispensing in a specific region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Trolamine salicylate (also known as Triethanolamine salicylate).
- **Chemical Class:** Salicylate derivative; topical analgesic.
- **Brand Name(s):** Equate Odor-Free Therapy Arthritis Cream [US].
- **ATC Code:** M02AC (Topical products for joint and muscular pain, salicylic acid and derivatives).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Topical cream containing 10% (100 mg/g) trolamine salicylate [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Distributed by Walmart Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
Trolamine salicylate is indicated for the temporary relief of minor pain associated with:
- Arthritis [US]
- Simple backache [US]
- Muscle strains [US]
- Muscle sprains [US]
- Bruises [US]

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Children $\ge$ 12 years)
- Apply the cream to the affected area no more than 3 to 4 times daily [US].

### Administration Instructions
- For external use only [US].
- Clean the affected area before application.
- Do not apply to wounds or damaged skin [US].
- Do not bandage the treated area tightly [US].
- Do not use in conjunction with heating pads or other external heating devices [US].
- Avoid contact with eyes and mucous membranes [US].

### Missed Dose
- Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known allergy or prone to allergic reactions from aspirin or other salicylates [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Allergy Alert:** Patients with a history of salicylate or aspirin sensitivity should consult a healthcare professional prior to use [US].
- **External Use Only:** Avoid ingestion and contact with ocular or mucosal surfaces [US].
- **Concomitant Topical Therapy:** Do not apply simultaneously with other topical analgesics to the same site [US].
- **Monitoring/Discontinuation:** Patients should stop use and consult a physician if:
    - The condition worsens [US].
    - Symptoms persist for more than 7 days [US].
    - Symptoms clear up and then recur within a few days [US].
    - Significant skin redness or irritation develops [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Available regulatory data for the OTC topical 10% preparation does not provide specific frequency categories (e.g., "common," "rare"). Reported adverse effects include:
- **Dermatologic:** Local irritation, skin redness [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Allergic reactions in susceptible individuals [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** If swallowed, immediate medical attention or contact with a Poison Control Center is required [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Topical Analgesics:** Do not use at the same time as other topical analgesics on the same application site [US].
- **Systemic Interactions:** While systemic absorption is lower than oral salicylates, potential interactions with systemic anticoagulants (e.g., warfarin) or NSAIDs should be considered by the clinician if used over large areas or for prolonged periods (Note: specific systemic interaction data is not detailed in the provided OTC source).

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Seek the advice of a healthcare professional before use [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Seek the advice of a healthcare professional before use [US].
- **Fertility:** Not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** 
    - Approved for use in children 12 years of age and older [US].
    - For children under 12 years of age: Use is not recommended unless directed by a doctor [US].
- **Elderly Use:** No specific dose adjustments provided; use according to adult directions [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources for topical administration.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Trolamine salicylate is a salicylate ester that acts as a topical analgesic. It is thought to work by inhibiting cyclooxygenase (COX) enzymes locally, thereby reducing the production of pro-inflammatory prostaglandins in the underlying tissues.
- **Pharmacokinetics:** 
    - **Absorption:** Absorbed through the skin.
    - **Distribution/Metabolism/Elimination:** Not specified in provided sources for the topical route.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Provides local analgesic and anti-inflammatory effects without the strong methyl salicylate odour [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage Temperature:** Store at room temperature [US].
- **Handling:** Keep container tightly closed. Keep out of reach of children [US].
