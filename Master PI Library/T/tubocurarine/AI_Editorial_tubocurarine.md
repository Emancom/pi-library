## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Tubocurarine chloride (INN/Generic).
- **Chemical Class:** Neuromuscular blocking agent (derived from curare); however, presented here in a homeopathic dilution.
- **Brand Names:** Curare 200CK [US].
- **ATC Code:** M03AA02 (Note: This code applies to the therapeutic substance; homeopathic preparations may not be explicitly listed under this code in all clinical databases).
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Oral pellets. Each pellet is reported to contain 0.443 mg of active ingredient in a 200CK homeopathic dilution [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral (Sublingual).
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Boiron, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Symptomatic Relief:** Temporarily relieves tingling pain in the limbs resulting from overuse [US-approved/Homeopathic].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Children)
- **Standard Dose:** Dissolve 5 pellets under the tongue 3 times daily [US].
- **Timing:** Administration should begin at the onset of symptoms and continue until symptoms are relieved [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Pellets should be dispensed into the cap of the container and then poured directly under the tongue to avoid contamination [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Although not explicitly listed in the source, standard clinical practice suggests contraindication in patients with known hypersensitivity to tubocurarine chloride, lactose, or sucrose.
- No other specific contraindications are identified in available sources.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warnings
- None [US].

### General Warnings and Precautions
- **Symptom Duration:** Patients should be advised to discontinue use and consult a physician if symptoms persist for more than 3 days or worsen during treatment [US].
- **Homeopathic Disclaimer:** The FDA notes that this product is not evaluated for safety or efficacy and there is no scientific evidence to support homeopathy as effective [US].
- **Excipients:** This product contains lactose and sucrose; use with caution in patients with carbohydrate metabolism disorders or relevant intolerances.

### Monitoring Requirements
