# Editorial Product Information: Talazoparib

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** Australian (TGA).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of talazoparib product information for healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and should not be used as a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) mandated in a specific jurisdiction for prescribing purposes.
- **Note on Sources:** At the time of compilation, only the Australian (TGA) PI was provided for review. Information regarding other jurisdictions (e.g., US, European, Japanese) is marked as "Not specified in available sources" where unique regional data is typically expected.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Talazoparib (present as talazoparib tosilate).
- **Chemical Class:** Poly (ADP-ribose) polymerase (PARP) inhibitor.
- **Brand Name(s):** **TALZENNA** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** L01XK04.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Hard capsules containing talazoparib tosilate equivalent to 0.1 mg, 0.25 mg, 0.35 mg, 0.5 mg, and 1 mg of talazoparib free base [Australian].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Pfizer Australia Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Breast Cancer:** Monotherapy for the treatment of adult patients with a deleterious or suspected deleterious germline breast cancer susceptibility gene (**BRCA**) mutation, who have human epidermal growth factor receptor 2 (**HER2**)-negative, locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer [Australian].
- **Prostate Cancer:** In combination with enzalutamide for the treatment of adult patients with homologous recombination repair (**HRR**) gene-mutated metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (**mCRPC**) [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Breast Cancer (Monotherapy):** 1 mg orally once daily.
- **Prostate Cancer (Combination Therapy):** 0.5 mg orally once daily in combination with enzalutamide 160 mg once daily. Patients should also receive a concurrent LHRH analogue or have had a bilateral orchiectomy.

### Administration Instructions
- Capsules must be swallowed whole; do not open or dissolve.
- May be taken with or without food.

### Missed Dose Instructions
- If a patient misses a dose or vomits, an additional dose should not be taken. The next prescribed dose should be taken at the usual time.

### Dosage Adjustment for Adverse Reactions
Treatment should be interrupted or dose-reduced based on severity. Discontinue if more than three dose reductions are required.
- **Breast Cancer Reduction Levels:** 1 mg (Starting) → 0.75 mg → 0.5 mg → 0.25 mg.
- **Prostate Cancer Reduction Levels:** 0.5 mg (Starting) → 0.35 mg → 0.25 mg → 0.1 mg.

**Haematological Criteria for Dose Resumption:**
- Haemoglobin: Withhold if <80 g/L; resume at reduced dose when ≥90 g/L.
- Platelets: Withhold if <50 x 10⁹/L; resume at reduced dose when ≥75 x 10⁹/L.
- Neutrophils: Withhold if <1 x 10⁹/L; resume at reduced dose when ≥1.5 x 10⁹/L.

## 4. Contraindications
- Hypersensitivity to talazoparib tosilate or any of the excipients (e.g., silicified microcrystalline cellulose, hypromellose, titanium dioxide).

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Myelodysplastic Syndrome/Acute Myeloid Leukaemia (MDS/AML):** Reported in <1% of patients treated with talazoparib. Monitor for prolonged haematological toxicity; if MDS/AML is confirmed, discontinue treatment.
- **Myelosuppression:** Anaemia, neutropenia, and thrombocytopenia are very common. Full blood counts (FBC) must be obtained prior to initiation and monitored monthly. Do not start treatment until haematological toxicity from previous therapy has recovered to ≤ Grade 1.
- **Embryo-fetal Toxicity:** Based on its mechanism of action, talazoparib can cause fetal harm. Pregnancy testing is required before initiation in females of reproductive potential.
- **Diagnostic Test Selection:** Identification of *BRCA* or *HRR* mutations must be performed by an experienced laboratory using a validated test method to minimize false results.

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions are categorized by frequency.

| Frequency | Adverse Reactions |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Anaemia, fatigue/asthenia, nausea, neutropenia, thrombocytopenia, headache, vomiting, alopecia, diarrhoea, decreased appetite, dizziness, abdominal pain, leucopenia. |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Dysgeusia, dyspepsia, stomatitis, lymphopenia, fractures (in prostate cancer combination), venous thromboembolism. |
| **Uncommon (0.1–1%)** | MDS/AML. |

**Serious Adverse Effects:**
- **Anaemia:** Grade ≥3 anaemia occurred in 39% (monotherapy) and 45% (combination therapy) of patients. Transfusions may be required.
- **MDS/AML:** Rare but life-threatening.

## 7. Drug Interactions
### Pharmacokinetic Interactions
- **P-glycoprotein (P-gp) Inhibitors:** Strong P-gp inhibitors (e.g., itraconazole, clarithromycin, amiodarone, verapamil) increase talazoparib exposure. Avoid co-administration. If unavoidable, reduce the talazoparib dose to the next lower level.
- **P-gp Inducers:** (e.g., rifampicin) increase Cmax but do not significantly affect total exposure (AUC); no dose adjustment required.
- **BCRP Inhibitors:** Co-administration may increase exposure; avoid strong BCRP inhibitors (e.g., curcumin, ciclosporin).
- **Acid-Reducing Agents:** PPIs and H2RAs have no significant impact on talazoparib absorption.

### Pharmacodynamic Interactions
- **Enzalutamide:** Co-administration in mCRPC increases talazoparib exposure approximately 2-fold. This is accounted for in the 0.5 mg recommended starting dose for this indication.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** **Category D** [Australian]. Talazoparib is teratogenic and can cause embryo-fetal death. Effective contraception is required:
    - **Females:** During treatment and for 7 months after the final dose.
    - **Males:** Use condoms during treatment and for 4 months after the final dose if the partner is pregnant or of reproductive potential.
- **Breastfeeding:** Breastfeeding is not recommended during treatment and for at least 1 month after the final dose.
- **Fertility:** Based on animal data, male and female fertility may be compromised.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy in children <18 years have not been established.
- **Elderly Use:** No dose adjustment required for patients ≥65 years, though greater sensitivity in some individuals cannot be ruled out.
- **Renal Impairment:**
    - **Mild (CrCL 60–89 mL/min):** No adjustment required.
    - **Moderate (CrCL 30–59 mL/min):** 0.75 mg daily (Breast Cancer); 0.35 mg daily (Prostate Cancer).
    - **Severe (CrCL 15–29 mL/min):** 0.5 mg daily (Breast Cancer); 0.25 mg daily (Prostate Cancer).
    - **Haemodialysis:** Not studied.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No adjustment required for mild, moderate, or severe hepatic impairment [Australian].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Talazoparib inhibits PARP1 and PARP2. It traps PARP proteins on DNA damage sites, preventing repair and leading to double-strand breaks and apoptosis, particularly in cells with HRR deficiencies (e.g., *BRCA* mutations).
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Tmax is 1–2 hours. High-fat food reduces Cmax by 46% but does not affect AUC.
    - **Distribution:** 74% protein bound.
    - **Metabolism:** Minimal hepatic metabolism; primary pathways include mono-oxidation and glucuronidation.
    - **Elimination:** Terminal half-life is ~90 hours. Major route is renal (69% of dose, 55% as unchanged drug).

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **EMBRACA (Breast Cancer):** A Phase 3 study comparing talazoparib (1 mg) to physician’s choice chemotherapy. Talazoparib significantly improved median progression-free survival (PFS): 8.6 months vs. 5.6 months (HR 0.54; p<0.0001).
- **TALAPRO-2 (Prostate Cancer):** A Phase 3 study in HRRm mCRPC comparing talazoparib (0.5 mg) + enzalutamide to placebo + enzalutamide. Combination therapy significantly improved radiographic PFS (rPFS): Median Not Reached vs. 13.8 months (HR 0.447; p<0.0001).

## 12. Storage Conditions
- Store below 30°C.
- Protect from light.
- **Shelf Life:** Expiry date is located on the packaging [Australian].
