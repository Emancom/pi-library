# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis was developed using official Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of levocarnitine injection for use by healthcare professionals.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI or local clinical guidelines required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. At the time of writing, only the United States PI was available for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN/USP).
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; a carrier molecule for long-chain fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter Healthcare Corporation) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Sterile injection: 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency (Inborn Errors):** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that lead to secondary carnitine deficiency [US-approved].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
- **Adult and Paediatric Dose:** 50 mg/kg given as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion [US].
- **Loading Dose:** In severe metabolic crisis, a loading dose is often administered, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours [US].
- **Frequency:** Doses should be administered every 3 or 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours [US].
- **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses are typically in the range of 50 mg/kg, though higher doses up to 300 mg/kg have been used [US].

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight) administered as a slow bolus injection into the venous return line after each dialysis session [US].
- **Dose Adjustment:** Adjustments should be guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations (normal range: 40 µmol/L to 50 µmol/L). Downward titration (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected for particulate matter and discoloration prior to use [US].
- Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known** [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None [US].
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Some reactions occur within minutes of administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and monitor if re-administration is attempted [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with known seizure disorders, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Monitoring Requirements:**
    - Obtain baseline plasma carnitine levels [US].
    - Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine: 35 to 60 µmol/L) [US].
- **Oral Formulation Caution:** The safety and efficacy of the *oral* formulation have not been evaluated in renal insufficiency; chronic high-dose oral administration in ESRD may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have been observed when levocarnitine is used concurrently with warfarin. It is recommended to monitor INR levels after initiating treatment or following dose adjustments [US].
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not assigned (narrative description used).
    - Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at doses up to 3.8 times the human dose (based on surface area) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine is a normal component of human milk. Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant must be weighed against the benefits to the mother [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing guidelines are provided for metabolic disorders (50 mg/kg). Pharmacokinetic data in premature infants and newborns define secondary deficiency as plasma levels below age-related norms [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Specifically indicated for ESRD on dialysis. Dose adjustments required based on trough plasma levels [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Corrects carnitine deficiency, which is biochemically defined as free carnitine <20 µmol/L [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not protein bound. Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and $\gamma$-butyrobetaine (feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. Terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Study:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients on hemodialysis receiving 20 mg/kg three times weekly showed that mean pre-dialysis trough levels increased from 21.3 µmol/L at baseline to 190 µmol/L after 8 weeks [US].
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** Studies of 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg showed dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C [US].
- **Protection:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable for up to 24 hours in PVC bags at room temperature when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer's (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) [US].
