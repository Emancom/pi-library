# Editorial Product Information: Teriparatide

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration)
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial synthesis of the approved Product Information for teriparatide, specifically the brand **Ritosa**, intended for use by healthcare professionals.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial overview and does not replace the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority’s current approved labeling for definitive guidance.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Teriparatide (recombinant human parathyroid hormone [1-34]). It is a synthetic peptide containing the first 34 N-terminal amino acids of the natural 84-amino acid human parathyroid hormone.
- **Brand Names:** **Ritosa** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** H05AA02.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Solution for injection: 250 micrograms/mL.
    - Presentation: 2.4 mL pre-filled pen containing 600 micrograms of teriparatide. Each daily dose delivers 20 micrograms in 80 microliters [Australian].
- **Route of Administration:** Subcutaneous.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Sun Pharma ANZ Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Postmenopausal Osteoporosis:** Treatment of osteoporosis in postmenopausal women [Australian].
- **Male Osteoporosis:** Treatment of primary osteoporosis in men when other therapies are considered unsuitable and there is a high risk of fractures [Australian].
- **Glucocorticoid-Induced Osteoporosis:** Treatment of osteoporosis associated with sustained systemic glucocorticoid therapy in men and women at high risk for fracture [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **Standard Adult Dose:** 20 micrograms administered once daily [Australian].
- **Route:** Subcutaneous injection into the thigh or abdominal wall [Australian].
- **Duration of Treatment:** The maximum lifetime duration of treatment is limited to 24 months. Prescribers should obtain informed consent regarding this lifetime limit [Australian].
- **Supportive Therapy:** Calcium and vitamin D supplementation is advised if dietary intake is inadequate [Australian].
- **Administration Instructions:** 
    - The pen is for single-patient use only.
    - It must be stored in the refrigerator between uses. 
    - Patients must be trained on proper injection technique using the specific pen device [Australian].
- **Missed Dose:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to teriparatide or any excipients [Australian].
- **Pregnancy and Breastfeeding:** Teriparatide is contraindicated in women who are pregnant or nursing [Australian].
- **Metabolic Bone Disorders:** Pre-existing hypercalcemia, hyperparathyroidism, or Paget’s disease of the bone [Australian].
- **Malignancy:** Patients with skeletal malignancies or bone metastases [Australian].
- **Radiation Therapy:** Prior external beam or implant radiation therapy to the skeleton [Australian].
- **Renal Function:** Severe renal impairment [Australian].
- **Other:** Unexplained elevations of alkaline phosphatase [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Risk of Osteosarcoma:** In rodent studies, teriparatide caused a dose-dependent increase in osteosarcoma. While monkey studies and post-marketing human data have not identified an increased risk, treatment is restricted to 24 months to minimize potential risk. Use is avoided in patients with high baseline risk for osteosarcoma (e.g., Paget’s disease, open epiphyses, or prior skeletal radiation) [Australian].
- **Hypercalcemia:** Teriparatide may cause transient increases in serum calcium, typically peaking 4 to 6 hours post-dose and returning to baseline by 16 to 24 hours. Persistent hypercalcemia requires treatment discontinuation and investigation [Australian].
- **Urolithiasis:** Caution is advised in patients with active or recent urolithiasis due to the potential for exacerbation [Australian].
- **Orthostatic Hypotension:** Isolated episodes of transient orthostatic hypotension may occur, typically within 4 hours of the first few doses. These episodes usually resolve spontaneously when the patient reclines [Australian].
- **Monitoring:** Routine calcium monitoring is generally not required for normocalcemic patients, but may be appropriate for non-ambulant patients [Australian].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Digoxin:** Teriparatide transiently increases serum calcium, which may predispose patients to digitalis toxicity. Use with caution in patients taking digoxin [Australian].
- **Hydrochlorothiazide:** Co-administration may slightly reduce urinary calcium excretion, but no significant clinical interaction with teriparatide’s calcemic response was observed [Australian].
- **Furosemide:** Co-administration resulted in a small, clinically insignificant increase in serum calcium (2%) [Australian].
- **Raloxifene:** No evidence of an interaction affecting serum or urinary calcium was observed [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** **Category B3** [Australian]. Contraindicated. Use in women of childbearing potential requires effective contraception. Teriparatide must be discontinued if pregnancy occurs [Australian].
- **Breastfeeding:** Contraindicated. It is unknown if teriparatide is excreted in human milk [Australian].
- **Fertility:** No adverse effects on fertility were observed in adult rat studies, but testicular degeneration was noted in juvenile rats [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy have not been established. Contraindicated in children and young adults with open epiphyses [Australian].
- **Elderly Use:** No dose adjustment required. Pharmacokinetics do not differ significantly based on age (31–85 years) [Australian].
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    - Mild/Moderate: Use with caution. 
    - Severe: Contraindicated. 
    - Note: Patients with renal impairment may show reduced calcemic/calciuric responses [Australian].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Safety and efficacy have not been evaluated. However, as the liver is not a primary site of metabolism for the PTH (1-34) fragment, a significant effect on exposure is unlikely [Australian].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Teriparatide is an anabolic agent. Once-daily administration stimulates new bone formation on trabecular and cortical surfaces by preferentially stimulating osteoblastic activity over osteoclastic activity [Australian].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Increases bone mass and improves bone microarchitecture. It affects calcium and phosphorus metabolism in a manner consistent with endogenous PTH [Australian].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Absolute bioavailability is approximately 95% following SC injection. Rapid absorption with peak concentrations reached within 30 minutes [Australian].
    - **Distribution:** Volume of distribution is ~1.7 L/kg [Australian].
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily cleared through hepatic and extra-hepatic pathways (mechanisms similar to endogenous PTH) [Australian].
    - **Elimination:** Half-life is approximately 1 hour after SC injection [Australian].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Postmenopausal Women:** A pivotal study (median 19 months) of 1637 women showed that 20 mcg/day teriparatide reduced the risk of new vertebral fractures by 65% and non-vertebral fragility fractures by 53% compared to placebo [Australian].
- **Male Osteoporosis:** A study of 437 men showed a 5% increase in lumbar spine BMD after 11 months [Australian].
- **Glucocorticoid-Induced Osteoporosis:** In a 36-month study of 428 patients, teriparatide increased lumbar spine BMD significantly more than alendronate (7.2% vs 3.4% at 18 months) [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 2°C to 8°C (Refrigerate) at all times [Australian].
- **Handling:** Do not freeze. Discard the pen if it has been frozen.
- **In-use Shelf Life:** The pen may be used for up to 28 days after the first injection if stored correctly. Minimize time outside the refrigerator [Australian].
- **Administration:** Do not store the pen with the needle attached [Australian].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule:** Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine) [Australian].
- **Legal Classification:** Not specified for other jurisdictions in available sources.
