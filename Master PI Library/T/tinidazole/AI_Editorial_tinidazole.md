# Editorial Product Information: Tinidazole

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) provided from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, consolidating regulatory information into a single reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific PI. Clinicians must refer to the local approved PI before prescribing or administering this medication.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Tinidazole (a second-generation 2-methyl-5-nitroimidazole)
- **Chemical Class:** Nitroimidazole antimicrobial/antiprotozoal
- **Brand Names:** Tinidazole Tablets [US]
- **ATC Code:** P01AB02 [Not explicitly in source, but standard pharmaceutical reference]
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - Oral Tablets: 250 mg (yellow, round, scored, debossed "207") [US]
    - Oral Tablets: 500 mg (yellow, oval, scored, debossed "208") [US]
- **Routes of Administration:** Oral
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Edenbridge Pharmaceuticals, LLC [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Trichomoniasis:** Treatment of infections caused by *Trichomonas vaginalis*. As a sexually transmitted disease, simultaneous treatment of sexual partners is indicated [US].
- **Giardiasis:** Treatment of infections caused by *Giardia duodenalis* (also known as *G. lamblia*) in adults and pediatric patients aged 3 years and older [US].
- **Amebiasis:** Treatment of intestinal amebiasis and amebic liver abscess caused by *Entamoeba histolytica* in adults and pediatric patients aged 3 years and older. (Note: Not indicated for asymptomatic cyst passage) [US].
- **Bacterial Vaginosis (BV):** Treatment in non-pregnant, adult women. Other pathogens (e.g., *Chlamydia*, *N. gonorrhoeae*) should be ruled out [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Instructions
- It is advisable to take tinidazole with food to minimize gastrointestinal side effects [US].
- Tablets may be crushed in artificial cherry syrup for patients unable to swallow whole tablets (stable for 7 days at room temperature) [US].

### Adult Dosing
- **Trichomoniasis:** 2 g single oral dose [US].
- **Giardiasis:** 2 g single oral dose [US].
- **Intestinal Amebiasis:** 2 g daily for 3 days [US].
- **Amebic Liver Abscess:** 2 g daily for 3 to 5 days [US].
- **Bacterial Vaginosis:** 2 g once daily for 2 days, OR 1 g once daily for 5 days [US].

### Administration Notes
- **Missed Dose:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Alcohol Restriction:** Abstain from alcoholic beverages and ethanol-containing preparations during therapy and for 3 days following the last dose [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known history of hypersensitivity to tinidazole or other nitroimidazole derivatives (reactions may range from urticaria to Stevens-Johnson syndrome) [US].
- **Cockayne Syndrome:** Contraindicated due to reports of severe, irreversible hepatotoxicity and fatal acute liver failure with related nitroimidazoles (e.g., metronidazole) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning: Potential Risk for Carcinogenicity [US]:** Carcinogenicity has been observed in rodents treated chronically with metronidazole. Although not reported for tinidazole, the drugs are structurally related. Use should be restricted to approved indications; avoid chronic use.
- **Neurological Effects:** Convulsive seizures and peripheral neuropathy (characterised by numbness or paresthesia) have been reported. Discontinue therapy immediately if abnormal neurologic signs develop [US].
- **Vaginal Candidiasis:** Use may result in fungal overgrowth (candidavaginitis), requiring antifungal treatment [US].
- **Blood Dyscrasias:** Use with caution in patients with a history of blood dyscrasias; transient leukopenia and neutropenia may occur [US].
- **Antimicrobial Resistance:** Prescribing in the absence of a proven/strongly suspected infection increases the risk of developing drug-resistant bacteria [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions are generally more frequent with multi-day dosing regimens.

| Adverse Reaction | 2 g Single Dose Incidence | Multi-day Dose Incidence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Metallic/Bitter Taste** | 3.7% | 6.3% |
| **Nausea** | 3.2% | 4.5% |
| **Anorexia** | 1.5% | 2.5% |
| **Dyspepsia/Cramps** | 1.8% | 1.4% |
| **Vomiting** | 1.5% | 0.9% |
| **Headache** | 1.3% | 0.7% |
| **Dizziness** | 1.1% | 0.5% |
| **Constipation** | 0.4% | 1.4% |

**Other Reported Effects:**
- **CNS:** Vertigo, ataxia, insomnia, drowsiness.
- **Gastrointestinal:** Tongue discoloration, stomatitis, diarrhea.
- **Hypersensitivity:** Urticaria, pruritus, rash, flushing, sweating, angioedema.
- **Serious Reactions:** Convulsions, Stevens-Johnson syndrome, erythema multiforme, and reversible thrombocytopenia [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
Interactions observed with metronidazole are expected to occur with tinidazole:
- **Alcohol:** Disulfiram-like reaction (abdominal cramps, nausea, vomiting, headaches, flushing) [US].
- **Warfarin/Coumarins:** May enhance anticoagulant effects; monitor prothrombin time and adjust dose during and up to 8 days post-therapy [US].
- **Lithium:** May elevate serum lithium levels; monitor lithium and creatinine levels [US].
- **Phenytoin/Fosphenytoin:** Concomitant use may prolong phenytoin half-life or decrease tinidazole levels [US].
- **Cyclosporine/Tacrolimus:** Potential for increased toxicity of these immunosuppressants [US].
- **Fluorouracil:** May decrease clearance, increasing toxicity risk [US].
- **CYP3A4 Inducers (e.g., Rifampin, Phenobarbital):** May accelerate tinidazole elimination [US].
- **CYP3A4 Inhibitors (e.g., Cimetidine, Ketoconazole):** May increase tinidazole plasma levels [US].
- **Cholestyramine:** May decrease oral bioavailability; separate dosing is advised [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - [US]: Data are insufficient to identify a drug-associated risk for major birth defects or miscarriage. Animal studies showed a slight increase in fetal mortality in rats at 2.5 times the human dose.
- **Breastfeeding:** Tinidazole is present in human milk. Breastfeeding is not recommended during treatment and for 72 hours after the last dose due to potential for serious adverse reactions [US].
- **Fertility:** Based on rodent data, may impair male fertility; reversibility is unknown [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness for giardiasis and amebiasis established in children aged $\ge$ 3 years. Not established for other indications in children [US].
    - *Pediatric Dose:* 50 mg/kg (up to 2 g) as a single dose (giardiasis) or daily for 3–5 days (amebiasis) [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical studies did not include sufficient numbers of subjects aged 65+. Use caution due to higher frequency of decreased hepatic/renal/cardiac function [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment necessary in patients with severe renal impairment (CrCL < 22 mL/min).
    - *Hemodialysis:* Administer an additional 1/2 dose if tinidazole is given prior to a session [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No specific data available; administer cautiously in patients with hepatic dysfunction [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** An antiprotozoal/antibacterial agent. The nitro group is reduced into free radicals which cause DNA strand breakage and damage bacterial/protozoal cells [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Rapid and complete; peak plasma levels reached in ~1.6 hours (fasted). Food delays peak by ~2 hours but does not affect total exposure (AUC) [US].
    - **Distribution:** Crosses the blood-brain barrier; 12% plasma protein binding. Volume of distribution is ~50 L [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily via CYP3A4 [US].
    - **Elimination:** Half-life is 12–14 hours. Excreted via liver and kidneys (20-25% unchanged in urine; 12% in feces) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Trichomoniasis:** Cure rates in published comparative studies (2 g single dose) ranged from 80% to 100% [US].
- **Giardiasis:** In eight controlled studies (adults and pediatrics), cure rates for 2 g single dose (or 50 mg/kg) ranged from 80% to 100% [US].
- **Bacterial Vaginosis:** In a double-blind, placebo-controlled trial (n=235), therapeutic cure rates were significantly higher for tinidazole (2 g x 2 days or 1 g x 5 days) compared to placebo [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C [US].
- **Conditions:** Protect contents from light [US].
