## 3. Approved Indications
- Topical skin cleansing and care.
- Skin lightening / brightening of the complexion and reduction of dark spots (as described in product functions).
- Daily cleaning and cosmetic skin care.
- Jurisdictional distinctions: Not specified in available sources beyond the single source; indications stated above are those provided in the available source document.

## 4. Dosage and Administration
- General dosing (adult):
  - Product concentrations as listed: each active ingredient present at 0.1 g per 100 g of product.
  - No specific frequency (number of uses per day) or duration of use provided in the available sources.
- Dosing by indication: Not specified in available sources.
- Administration instructions (from product directions):
  - Rub the product between wet palms until a rich foam forms.
  - Apply the foam to the face and body; massage gently to aid skin care.
  - Rinse thoroughly with clean water.
  - Users are advised to clean their hands before use to ensure optimal results.
