# Editorial Product Information: Tisotumab vedotin

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA)
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of tisotumab vedotin for healthcare professionals. It synthesises the available official Product Information (PI) into a single clinical reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not replace the official PI. Clinicians must consult the current approved PI in their specific jurisdiction before prescribing. Information regarding Australian, European, Canadian, or Japanese approvals was not specified in the provided source material.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Tisotumab vedotin-tftv; a tissue factor (TF)-directed antibody-drug conjugate (ADC).
- **Brand Name:** **TIVDAK** [US]
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Lyophilized cake or powder (40 mg) in a single-dose vial for reconstitution and further dilution [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Seagen Inc. [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Cervical Cancer:** Indicated for the treatment of adult patients with recurrent or metastatic cervical cancer who have experienced disease progression on or after chemotherapy [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### 3.1 General Dosing
- **Recommended Dose:** 2 mg/kg (up to a maximum of 200 mg for patients weighing $\ge$100 kg) administered as an IV infusion over 30 minutes every 3 weeks.
- **Duration:** Continue until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity.

### 3.2 Required Eye Care and Premedication
To mitigate the risk of ocular toxicity, the following regimen is mandatory for all patients:
- **Ophthalmic Exams:** Conduct baseline exam, then prior to every cycle for the first nine cycles, and as clinically indicated.
- **Topical Corticosteroids:** 1 drop in each eye prior to infusion, continuing three times daily for 72 hours post-infusion.
- **Topical Vasoconstrictors:** 1 drop in each eye immediately prior to infusion.
- **Cold Packs:** Apply cooling eye pads during the infusion.
- **Lubricating Eye Drops:** Use for the duration of therapy and for 30 days following the final dose.
- **Contact Lenses:** Patients should avoid wearing contact lenses unless advised by an eye care provider.

### 3.3 Dose Modifications
- **First Reduction:** 1.3 mg/kg (max 130 mg).
- **Second Reduction:** 0.9 mg/kg (max 90 mg).
- **Discontinuation:** Permanently discontinue if the patient cannot tolerate the 0.9 mg/kg dose.
- **Specific Adverse Reactions:** Detailed withholding and discontinuation protocols exist for Keratitis, Conjunctival/Corneal scarring, Peripheral Neuropathy (Grade 2-4), Hemorrhage (Grade 2-4), and Pneumonitis [US].

### 3.4 Administration Instructions
- Do not administer as an IV push or bolus.
- Do not mix with other medicinal products.
- Use a 0.2 $\mu$m in-line filter.

## 4. Contraindications
- None specified in available sources [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### 5.1 Boxed Warning: Ocular Toxicity [US]
**Tisotumab vedotin can cause severe ocular toxicities, including corneal ulceration and permanent vision loss. Adherence to the prescribed eye care regimen (premedication and post-infusion drops) and regular ophthalmic monitoring is essential.**

### 5.2 Peripheral Neuropathy
Monitor for new or worsening neuropathy (numbness, tingling, muscle weakness). Peripheral neuropathy occurred in 39% of patients in clinical trials. Withhold, reduce, or discontinue therapy based on severity.

### 5.3 Hemorrhage
Serious and fatal bleeding events have been reported. Monitor for signs of bleeding. For pulmonary or CNS hemorrhage, permanently discontinue the drug.

### 5.4 Pneumonitis
Severe, life-threatening, or fatal pneumonitis may occur. Monitor for pulmonary symptoms (hypoxia, cough, dyspnea). Permanently discontinue for Grade 3 or 4 events.

### 5.5 Severe Cutaneous Adverse Reactions (SCARs)
Fatal or life-threatening Stevens-Johnson syndrome (SJS) has been reported. Withhold for suspected SCARs and discontinue permanently for confirmed Grade 3 or 4 reactions.

### 5.6 Embryo-Fetal Toxicity
The drug can cause fetal harm. Verify pregnancy status before initiation. Advise patients of reproductive potential to use effective contraception.

## 6. Adverse Effects
### 6.1 Frequency Summary
| Frequency | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Decreased hemoglobin (45%), peripheral neuropathy (39%), conjunctival adverse reactions (38%), nausea (37%), fatigue (36%), increased AST (33%), epistaxis (33%), alopecia (31%), increased ALT (30%), hemorrhage (28%), decreased sodium, constipation, diarrhea, decreased appetite, rash, and urinary tract infection [US]. |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Keratopathy, dry eye, periorbital adverse reactions, and intestinal obstruction. |

### 6.2 Serious Adverse Effects
The most common serious adverse reactions ($\ge$2%) include urinary tract infection, small intestinal obstruction, sepsis, abdominal pain, and hemorrhage. Fatalities have occurred due to SJS, septic shock, and pneumonitis.

## 7. Drug Interactions
### 7.1 Pharmacokinetic Interactions
- **Strong CYP3A4 Inhibitors:** Monomethyl auristatin E (MMAE), the cytotoxic component, is a CYP3A4 substrate. Concomitant use with strong CYP3A4 inhibitors (e.g., ketoconazole) may increase unconjugated MMAE exposure. Monitor patients closely for increased adverse reactions [US].
- **Strong CYP3A4 Inducers:** Concomitant use may decrease MMAE exposure, potentially reducing efficacy.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not assigned under current labeling rules, but "can cause fetal harm"]. Advise females of reproductive potential to use contraception during treatment and for 2 months after the final dose.
- **Breastfeeding:** There are no data on presence in human milk. Due to potential serious reactions in the infant, advise women not to breastfeed during treatment and for 3 weeks after the last dose [US].
- **Fertility:** Based on animal studies, tisotumab vedotin may impair both male and female fertility. Male patients with female partners should use effective contraception during treatment and for 4 months after the final dose [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical trials included 14% of patients $\ge$65 years. No overall differences in effectiveness were observed, though higher rates of discontinuation due to adverse reactions were noted in older patients (25% vs 13%).
- **Renal Impairment:** No dosage adjustment is required for mild to moderate renal impairment (CLcr 30–90 mL/min). The effect of severe renal impairment is unknown [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:**
    - **Mild:** No starting dose adjustment required; monitor closely.
    - **Moderate or Severe:** Avoid use. Exposure to MMAE is significantly increased [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
### 10.1 Mechanism of Action
Tisotumab vedotin is an ADC. The antibody is a human IgG1 directed against Tissue Factor (TF). Upon binding and internalization, the protease-cleavable linker releases MMAE. MMAE binds to tubulin, disrupting the microtubule network and leading to cell cycle arrest and apoptosis.

### 10.2 Pharmacokinetics
- **Distribution:** Steady-state volume of distribution is approximately 7.83 L.
- **Metabolism:** The antibody component is catabolised into small peptides; MMAE is primarily metabolised by CYP3A4.
- **Elimination:** Terminal half-life is approximately 4 days for the ADC and 2.6 days for unconjugated MMAE.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **innovaTV 301:** A randomized, open-label trial (n=502) comparing tisotumab vedotin to investigator's choice chemotherapy. Tisotumab vedotin demonstrated significant improvements in Overall Survival (OS) (11.5 months vs 9.5 months) and Progression-Free Survival (PFS) (4.2 months vs 2.9 months) [US].
- **innovaTV 204:** A single-arm trial (n=101) showing a Confirmed Objective Response Rate (ORR) of 24%, with a median Duration of Response (DOR) of 8.3 months.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vial Storage:** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C in the original carton to protect from light. Do not freeze or shake [US].
- **Reconstituted Solution:** Use immediately or store up to 24 hours at 2°C to 8°C (or 8 hours at room temperature).
- **Diluted Infusion Bag:** Refrigeration limits depend on the diluent: 24 hours for 5% Dextrose, 18 hours for 0.9% NaCl, and 12 hours for Lactated Ringer's.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** US: Prescription-only (Rx Only).
- **Hazardous Agent:** Classified as a hazardous drug; follow applicable special handling and disposal procedures [US].
