# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
*   **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA).
*   **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of levocarnitine injection for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises clinical, regulatory, and pharmacological data to support informed therapeutic decision-making.
*   **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) or Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt. 
*   **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative (carrier molecule for fatty acid transport).
*   **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection, USP [US].
*   **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
*   **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    *   **Injection:** 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) single-dose vial [US].
*   **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV).
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency in Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US-approved].
*   **Carnitine Deficiency in End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
*   **General Dosing:** Recommended dose is 50 mg/kg given as a slow (2 to 3 minute) bolus injection or by infusion [US].
*   **Severe Metabolic Crisis:** A loading dose is often administered, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours. Dosing intervals should be every 3 or 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours [US].
*   **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses typically remain in the range of 50 mg/kg, though higher doses up to 300 mg/kg have been used [US].

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
*   **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
*   **Administration Timing:** Administered as a slow (2 to 3 minute) bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].
*   **Dose Adjustments:** Guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy if concentrations are supranormal [US].

### Administration Instructions
*   Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration.
*   **Compatibility:** Stable when mixed in Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations ranging from 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL. Stable for up to 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags [US].

## 4. Contraindications
*   None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Serious Hypersensitivity Reactions
*   Serious reactions including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients on dialysis. Some reactions occurred within minutes of IV administration.
*   **Management:** Discontinue treatment immediately if a reaction occurs. If re-administration is considered, the patient must be monitored closely for recurrence of symptoms [US].

### Seizures
*   Seizures have been reported in patients receiving levocarnitine, regardless of pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a known seizure disorder, an increase in seizure frequency or severity has been observed [US].

### Monitoring
*   **Plasma Concentrations:** For metabolic disorders, weekly and monthly monitoring is recommended. Plasma free carnitine concentrations should ideally be maintained between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].
*   **General Parameters:** Monitoring should include blood chemistries, vital signs, and overall clinical condition [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common Adverse Effects (ESRD Clinical Trials)
The following events were reported at a frequency ≥5% in double-blind, placebo-controlled trials of ESRD patients on chronic hemodialysis [US]:

| Body System | Adverse Event (Incidence ≥5%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Injection site reaction (33%), Pain (30%), Flu syndrome (25%), Headache (22%), Infection (15%), Abdominal pain (9%), Accidental injury (10%), Fever (7%) |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension (20%), Hypotension (14%), Tachycardia (6%), Palpitations (5%) |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea (16%), Vomiting (15%), Nausea (8%), Anorexia (5%), Dyspepsia (5%) |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis (23%), Cough increase (9%), Rhinitis (9%), Dyspnea (7%) |
| **Nervous System** | Dizziness (13%), Paresthesia (5%), Insomnia (4%) |
| **Metabolic/Nutr.** | Hypercalcemia (9%), Hyperkalemia (6%), Hypervolemia (5%), Peripheral edema (5%) |

### Other Reported Effects
*   **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea and vomiting; body odor and gastritis occur less frequently [US].
*   **Postmarketing:** Seizures and hypersensitivity reactions (anaphylaxis) have been reported during post-approval use [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when used concomitantly with levocarnitine. 
*   **Management:** Monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not specified in available sources, but reflects former Category B/C profile]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at doses up to 3.8 times the human dose (based on surface area) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
*   **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine is a normal component of human milk. Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant should be weighed against the benefits of maternal supplementation [US].
*   **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility was found in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for paediatric patients is weight-based (see Section 3).
*   **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Renal Impairment:** This IV formulation is indicated for use in ESRD patients. However, chronic high-dose *oral* levocarnitine is cautioned in patients with severely compromised renal function due to the potential accumulation of metabolites trimethylamine (TMA) and trimethylamine-N-oxide (TMAO) [US].
*   **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring carrier molecule required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production [US].

### Pharmacokinetics
*   **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
*   **Elimination:** Following a single IV bolus (20 mg/kg), approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. The mean apparent terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].
*   **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients (20 mg/kg three times weekly post-dialysis), trough levels increased from a baseline of ~21 µmol/L to 190 µmol/L after 8 weeks.
*   **Dose-Ranging Studies:** Trials using 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg three times weekly demonstrated dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [US].
*   **Note:** While efficacy in increasing carnitine concentrations is established, the specific impact on clinical outcomes (signs/symptoms) in the ESRD population has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
*   **Handling:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat.
*   **Vials:** Single-dose vials; discard any unused portion as the formulation contains no preservatives [US].
