# Editorial Product Information: Teplizumab

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial synthesis: Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration) and United States (Food and Drug Administration).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical and regulatory data from multiple regions.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI or prescribing requirements in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should refer to local official documentation for jurisdiction-specific prescribing.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Teplizumab (also referred to as teplizumab-mzwv in the [United States]).
- **Chemical Class:** CD3-directed humanised IgG1 kappa monoclonal antibody produced in Chinese hamster ovary (CHO) cell lines.
- **Brand Name:** **TZIELD**.
- **ATC Code:** A10XX01.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Concentrated injection supplied as a clear, colourless solution in a 2 mg/2 mL (1 mg/mL) single-dose vial.
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Sanofi (Provention Bio, Inc.).

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Stage 2 Type 1 Diabetes (T1D):** Indicated to delay the onset of Stage 3 T1D in adult and paediatric patients aged 8 years and older [Australian]. In the [United States], this indication extends to paediatric patients aged 1 year and older.
- **Stage 3 Type 1 Diabetes (T1D):** Indicated to delay the decline in endogenous insulin production in paediatric patients aged 8 to 17 years who were recently diagnosed (within 6 weeks) with Stage 3 T1D [US-approved; not Australian-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
Dosing is based on Body Surface Area (BSA), calculated using the Mosteller formula.

### Dosing for Stage 2 T1D (Adult and Paediatric)
Administered once daily for 14 consecutive days.
- **Day 1:** 65 mcg/m²
- **Day 2:** 125 mcg/m²
- **Day 3:** 250 mcg/m²
- **Day 4:** 500 mcg/m²
- **Days 5–14:** 1,030 mcg/m²

### Dosing for Stage 3 T1D (Paediatric 8–17 years) [United States]
Administered in two treatment courses separated by 6 months (range 6–12 months). Each course consists of 12 consecutive days:
- **Day 1:** 106 mcg/m²
- **Day 2:** 425 mcg/m²
- **Days 3–12:** 850 mcg/m²

### Administration Instructions
- **Infusion Duration:** Minimum 30 minutes for adults and children $\ge$ 8 years. For children 1 to < 8 years, the infusion must be administered over at least 2 hours [United States].
- **Premedication:** Mandatory for the first 5 days of treatment with an antipyretic (NSAID or paracetamol/acetaminophen) and an antihistamine. An antiemetic may also be considered.
- **Preparation:** Requires a two-step dilution process to a final concentration between 15 mcg/mL and 60 mcg/mL.

### Missed Dose and Interruption
- If a dose is missed, resume dosing as soon as possible and complete all remaining doses on consecutive days. Do not administer two doses on the same day.
- Interrupt treatment for severe laboratory abnormalities (e.g., significant drop in platelets or haemoglobin). Interruptions should not exceed 3 days; otherwise, the course may need adjustment or discontinuation.

## 4. Contraindications
- Hypersensitivity to teplizumab or any of its excipients.
- Presence of active viral infection, such as Epstein-Barr Virus (EBV) or Cytomegalovirus (CMV) [United States].
- Immunocompromised status [United States].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warning [United States]
- **Viral Reactivation:** Serious, life-threatening cases of EBV and CMV reactivation have been reported. Patients must be tested for active infection before initiation. Treatment is contraindicated in those with active viral infection or who are immunocompromised.

### General Warnings
- **Cytokine Release Syndrome (CRS):** Very common within the first 5 days of treatment. Symptoms include fever, nausea, fatigue, headache, and myalgia. Monitor liver enzymes and bilirubin. Pause treatment if ALT/AST > 5x ULN or bilirubin > 3x ULN.
- **Serious Infections:** Bacterial and viral infections (e.g., pneumonia, sepsis) have occurred. Use is not recommended in patients with active serious or chronic infections.
- **Lymphopenia:** Occurs in the majority of patients (approx. 75–78%). Nadir typically occurs on Day 4 or 5. Recovery usually occurs within 2–6 weeks. Discontinue permanently if severe lymphopenia (< 500 cells/mcL) lasts 1 week or longer.
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Acute reactions including serum sickness, angioedema, and anaphylaxis have been reported.

### Monitoring Requirements
- Baseline and regular monitoring of Full Blood Count (FBC) and Liver Function Tests (LFTs).
- Assessment for active EBV and CMV infection (viral load) prior to start and monitoring for signs of reactivation for 2 months post-infusion.

## 6. Adverse Effects
| Frequency | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Lymphopenia, leukopenia, rash (maculopapular/pruritic), neutropenia, headache, pyrexia, nausea, fatigue. |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Cytokine Release Syndrome (CRS), vomiting, diarrhoea, abdominal pain, alanine aminotransferase (ALT) increased, aspartate aminotransferase (AST) increased, chills, hypotension [United States]. |
| **Uncommon (0.1–1%)** | Hypersensitivity, serious infections, serum sickness [United States]. |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** CRS (approx. 1% serious), serious infections (cellulitis, sepsis), and severe hypersensitivity.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- No formal drug interaction studies have been conducted.
- **Therapeutic Caution:** Use with caution alongside other immune modulators or drugs associated with cytopenias or liver abnormalities.
- **Vaccinations:** Teplizumab may interfere with immune response to vaccines.
- **Cytochrome P450:** Not expected to have relevant CYP450-mediated interactions.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:**
    - [Australian]: **Category B3**. Animal studies showed post-implantation loss and reduced adaptive immune response in offspring.
    - [United States]: May cause fetal harm. Monoclonal antibodies are actively transported across the placenta. Avoid use during pregnancy and for at least 30 days prior to planned pregnancy.
- **Breastfeeding:** Safety is not established. It is unknown if teplizumab is excreted in human milk. Recommendations suggest discontinuing breastfeeding during treatment and for 20 days [United States] to 30 days [Australian] after the final dose.
- **Fertility:** Animal studies using surrogate antibodies showed no impact on female or male fertility, though a trend toward reduced fertility was noted in some offspring [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Approved for Stage 2 T1D from age 8 [Australian] or age 1 [United States]. Approved for Stage 3 T1D from age 8 to 17 [United States].
- **Elderly Use:** Safety and efficacy have not been established in patients $\ge$ 65 years.
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** No specific studies have been conducted in these populations. BSA-based dosing is used to normalise exposure.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Binds to the CD3 epsilon chain on T-lymphocytes. It acts as a partial agonist to deactivate autoreactive T-cells that attack pancreatic beta cells, while increasing the proportion of regulatory T-cells and exhausted CD8+ T-cells.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Induces transient lymphopenia (nadir at Day 5). Internalises the CD3 complex from the T-cell surface.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Central volume of distribution (Vd) is approximately 2.27 L. No protein binding studies conducted (monoclonal antibody).
    - **Metabolism:** Catabolic pathways into small peptides.
    - **Elimination:** Clearance is approximately 2.66 L/day. Apparent elimination half-life is approximately 3 days.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Study TN-10 (Stage 2 T1D):** A randomised, double-blind study (N=76) showed a median delay to Stage 3 T1D diagnosis of 50 months in the teplizumab group versus 25 months in the placebo group (Hazard Ratio 0.41).
- **PROTECT Study (Stage 3 T1D) [United States]:** A randomised study in patients aged 8–17 years (N=328) showed a statistically significant difference in the decline of C-peptide AUC from baseline at Week 78 compared to placebo, indicating preservation of beta-cell function.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C.
- Do not freeze or shake.
- Keep vials in the original carton to protect from light.
- **Diluted Solution Stability:** 
    - IV bags: 6 hours at room temperature (15°C to 25°C).
    - Syringe-based: 12 hours refrigerated (2°C to 8°C) followed by 6 hours at room temperature.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule [Australian]:** Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine).
- **Legal Classification [United States]:** Rx-only.
