## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None listed in the available source.
- **Occupational Hazards:** 
    - **Eye Irritation:** Causes serious eye irritation. If in eyes, rinse cautiously with water for several minutes. Remove contact lenses if present and easy to do. If irritation persists, seek medical advice [US].
    - **Reproductive Toxicity:** Suspected of damaging fertility or the unborn child [US].
- **Handling Precautions:** 
    - Obtain special instructions before use. Do not handle until all safety precautions have been read and understood [US].
    - Wear protective gloves, protective clothing, eye protection, and face protection [US].
    - Wash skin thoroughly after handling [US].
