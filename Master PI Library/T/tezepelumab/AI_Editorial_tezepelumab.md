# Editorial Product Information: Tezepelumab

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration), US (Food and Drug Administration).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of tezepelumab product information, synthesised from multiple regulatory sources for use by healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Always consult local regulatory documents for legal prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Tezepelumab (Note: Designated as **tezepelumab-ekko** in the [US]).
- **Chemical Class:** Human monoclonal antibody (IgG2λ) directed against thymic stromal lymphopoietin (TSLP).
- **Brand Name:** **TEZSPIRE**
- **ATC Code:** R03DX11
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - **Solution for injection:** 210 mg/1.91 mL (110 mg/mL) available in single-dose pre-filled syringes and pre-filled pens.
    - **Single-dose glass vial:** [US]
- **Route of Administration:** Subcutaneous (SC) injection.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** AstraZeneca Pty Ltd [Australian]; Amgen Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Severe Asthma:** Indicated as add-on maintenance treatment in adult and paediatric patients aged 12 years and older with severe asthma. 
    - [Australian-specific detail]: Specifically for patients inadequately controlled despite optimal therapy including medium or high dose inhaled corticosteroids (ICS) plus another non-steroidal maintenance product.
    - [US-specific detail]: Not restricted by specific prior ICS dosing levels in the indication statement.
    - **Limitations of Use:** Not indicated for the relief of acute bronchospasm or status asthmaticus.
- **Chronic Rhinosinusitis with Nasal Polyps (CRSwNP):** [US-approved; not specified in Australian source]: Indicated as add-on maintenance treatment in adult and paediatric patients aged 12 years and older with inadequately controlled CRSwNP.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adults and Adolescents $\ge$ 12 years):** 210 mg administered via subcutaneous injection once every 4 weeks.
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Administer into the thigh or abdomen. If administered by a healthcare professional or caregiver, the upper arm may also be used.
    - Do not inject into the 5 cm (2 inches) area around the navel.
    - Avoid areas where the skin is tender, bruised, erythematous, or hardened.
    - Rotate injection sites with each dose.
    - **Preparation:** Allow the device to reach room temperature (approximately 60 minutes) prior to injection. Visually inspect for particulate matter or discolouration (solution should be clear to opalescent, colourless to light yellow). Do not shake or freeze.
- **Missed Dose:** Administer the dose as soon as possible. The patient may then resume the regular dosing schedule. If the next dose is already due, administer the missed dose as planned [Australian] or skip the missed dose and inject the next at the scheduled time [US]. Do not administer more than one dose in a day [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to tezepelumab (or tezepelumab-ekko) or any of its excipients.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Acute Asthma Symptoms:** Tezepelumab should not be used to treat acute exacerbations, acute symptoms, or acute bronchospasm.
- **Corticosteroid Reduction:** Do not discontinue systemic or inhaled corticosteroids abruptly upon starting tezepelumab. Reductions should be gradual and performed under medical supervision.
- **Serious Cardiac Events:** [Australian-highlighted]: In long-term studies, a numerical increase in serious cardiac adverse events was observed (Incidence Rate [IR] 1.33 vs 0.0 in placebo during the on-treatment period). Events were heterogeneous and occurred in patients with pre-existing risk factors. While no causal relationship is established, clinicians should monitor for signs of cardiac distress (e.g., chest pain, dyspnoea).
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Anaphylaxis and rash may occur within hours or days of administration. If a serious reaction occurs, discontinue treatment and initiate appropriate therapy.
- **Infections:** TSLP inhibition may theoretically increase infection risk. Treat patients with pre-existing serious infections before initiating therapy. If an infection develops during treatment and does not resolve, consider withholding tezepelumab.
- **Parasitic (Helminth) Infection:** TSLP may be involved in the immune response to helminths. Treat patients with pre-existing infections before initiation. If a patient becomes infected during therapy and does not respond to anti-helminth treatment, discontinue tezepelumab until the infection resolves.
- **Vaccination:** Avoid the use of live attenuated vaccines in patients receiving tezepelumab.

### Serious and Post-marketing Effects
- **Anaphylaxis:** Reported in post-marketing settings (frequency 'not known').
- **Serious Cardiac Events:** Numerical imbalance noted in long-term extension trials [Australian].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** No formal drug interaction studies have been performed. Monoclonal antibodies are not metabolised by hepatic enzymes; therefore, interactions with cytochrome P450 substrates are not expected.
- **Vaccines:** The effect of tezepelumab on the antibody response to seasonal influenza vaccination was evaluated in a small study (n=70) and appeared unaffected [Australian]. However, avoid live attenuated vaccines.
- **Asthma Medications:** Common co-administered medications (leukotriene antagonists, theophylline, OCS) had no effect on tezepelumab pharmacokinetics.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Australian Category B1:** Limited data in humans. Animal studies (cynomolgus monkeys) showed no evidence of harm at doses 168 times the maximum recommended human dose (MRHD).
    - **US Guidance:** No available data on drug-associated risks of birth defects or miscarriage. Human IgG is known to cross the placental barrier, with potential effects likely higher in the third trimester.
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if tezepelumab is excreted in human milk. IgG is present in milk in small amounts. In monkeys, concentrations in milk were <0.5% of maternal serum levels. Use should consider the developmental/health benefits of breastfeeding alongside the mother's clinical need.
- **Fertility:** No human data available. Animal studies showed no impairment of male or female fertility.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Approved for ages 12 years and older. Safety and efficacy in children < 12 years have not been established.
- **Elderly Use:** No dosage adjustment required for patients $\ge$ 65 years.
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment required for mild to moderate impairment. Not studied in severe renal impairment, though renal clearance of monoclonal antibodies is negligible.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment required. As an IgG monoclonal antibody, tezepelumab is not primarily cleared via the hepatic pathway.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Tezepelumab is an anti-TSLP human monoclonal antibody. By binding to TSLP, it prevents interaction with the TSLP receptor complex. TSLP is an upstream epithelial cytokine that triggers multiple inflammatory cascades (eosinophilic and non-eosinophilic) and induces airway hyper-responsiveness.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Reduces blood eosinophils, FeNO, IgE, IL-5, and IL-13. Onset of effect is typically within 2 weeks.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Absolute bioavailability approximately 77%. $T_{max}$ is 3 to 10 days.
    - **Distribution:** Central volume 3.9 L; peripheral volume 2.2 L (for 70 kg individual).
    - **Metabolism:** Degraded by proteolytic enzymes; not metabolised by hepatic enzymes.
    - **Elimination:** Elimination half-life is approximately 26 days.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **PATHWAY & NAVIGATOR:** 52-week trials in severe asthma patients ($\ge$ 12 years). Tezepelumab significantly reduced the annualised asthma exacerbation rate (AAER) by 71% (PATHWAY) and 56% (NAVIGATOR) compared to placebo. Improvements in FEV1 and patient-reported outcomes (ACQ-6, AQLQ) were also observed.
- **SOURCE [Australian]:** A 48-week OCS reduction trial. While tezepelumab showed higher rates of OCS reduction than placebo, the difference was not statistically significant in this specific study.
- **WAYPOINT [US]:** A 52-week trial in CRSwNP. Tezepelumab significantly improved the Total Nasal Polyp Score (NPS) and Nasal Congestion Score (NCS) compared to placebo.
- **DESTINATION:** A long-term extension trial (up to 104 weeks) confirming sustained safety and efficacy.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Refrigeration:** Store at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F). 
- **Room Temperature:** May be kept at room temperature (up to 25°C/77°F) for a maximum of 30 days. Once it reaches room temperature, do not return to the refrigerator. Discard if not used within 30 days.
- **General Handling:** Store in original carton to protect from light. Do not freeze, shake, or expose to heat.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Australia:** Prescription Only Medicine (**Schedule 4**). Subject to additional monitoring ($\blacktriangledown$).
- **United States:** Prescription only (**Rx-only**).
