# Editorial Product Information: Xanomeline and Trospium Chloride

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of product information for healthcare professionals, synthesised from official regulatory sources.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. At the time of this publication, only the United States (FDA) PI was provided for review. Information regarding other jurisdictions (e.g., Australian, European, Canadian, Japanese) is not specified in available sources.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** Xanomeline (as tartrate) and Trospium chloride.
- **Chemical Class:** Xanomeline is a muscarinic agonist; Trospium chloride is a muscarinic antagonist (quaternary ammonium compound).
- **Brand Name:** **COBENFY** [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - Oral capsules (xanomeline/trospium chloride): 50 mg/20 mg, 100 mg/20 mg, and 125 mg/30 mg [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Bristol-Myers Squibb Company [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Schizophrenia:** Treatment of schizophrenia in adults [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Initiation:** 50 mg/20 mg orally twice daily for the first two days [US].
- **Titration:** Increase to 100 mg/20 mg twice daily for at least five days (Days 3–7) [US].
- **Maintenance/Maximum:** Based on tolerability and clinical response, the dosage may be increased to a maximum of 125 mg/30 mg twice daily starting on Day 8 [US].
- **Maximum Recommended Dosage:** 125 mg/30 mg twice daily [US].

### Administration Instructions
- **Food Effect:** Administer at least 1 hour before a meal or at least 2 hours after a meal (empty stomach) [US].
- **Capsule Integrity:** Capsules must be swallowed whole; do not open [US].

### Pre-treatment Assessment
- **Liver Function:** Assess liver enzymes and bilirubin prior to initiation and as clinically indicated during treatment [US].
- **Cardiovascular:** Assess heart rate at baseline and as clinically indicated [US].

## 4. Contraindications
**COBENFY** is contraindicated in patients with:
- Pre-existing urinary retention [US].
- Gastric retention [US].
- Untreated narrow-angle glaucoma [US].
- Moderate (Child-Pugh Class B) or severe (Child-Pugh Class C) hepatic impairment [US].
- History of hypersensitivity to xanomeline, trospium chloride, or any excipients [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Urinary Retention:** Risk is increased in geriatric patients and those with bladder outlet obstruction (e.g., BPH). Monitor for hesitancy, weak stream, or dysuria. Consider dose reduction or discontinuation if symptoms occur [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment and Biliary Disease:** Not recommended in mild hepatic impairment; contraindicated in moderate/severe. Transient liver enzyme elevations have been observed. Discontinue if alanine aminotransferase (ALT) exceeds 5x the upper limit of normal (ULN) or if jaundice occurs [US].
- **Decreased Gastrointestinal Motility:** Use with caution in patients with GI obstructive disorders, ulcerative colitis, intestinal atony, or myasthenia gravis [US].
- **Angioedema:** Reports of swelling of the face, lips, tongue, and larynx. Discontinue immediately if airway involvement is suspected [US].
- **Narrow-angle Glaucoma:** Pupillary dilation may trigger an acute attack. Use only if benefits outweigh risks in patients with narrow angles [US].
- **Increases in Heart Rate:** Mean increases of ~10 bpm have been observed. Monitor heart rate, particularly during titration [US].
- **Central Nervous System (CNS) Effects:** Trospium (anticholinergic) may cause dizziness, confusion, or hallucinations. Advise patients against operating heavy machinery until effect is known [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions are predominantly related to the muscarinic effects of xanomeline or the anticholinergic effects of trospium.

| Frequency | Adverse Reaction (Schizophrenia Trials) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Nausea (19%), Dyspepsia (18%), Constipation (17%), Vomiting (15%), Hypertension (11%) [US] |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Abdominal pain (8%), Diarrhea (6%), Tachycardia (5%), Dizziness (5%), GERD (5%), Dry mouth (4%), Somnolence (3%), Blurred vision (3%), Salivary hypersecretion (2%), Orthostatic hypotension (2%) [US] |

**Serious Adverse Effects:**
- **Angioedema:** Potential for life-threatening airway obstruction [US].
- **Biliary Injury:** Substantial liver injury characterized by jaundice or severe ALT elevations [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Strong CYP2D6 Inhibitors:** May increase xanomeline exposure. Monitor for increased frequency/severity of adverse reactions [US].
- **Drugs Eliminated by Active Tubular Secretion:** Trospium is eliminated via this pathway. Concomitant use (e.g., with metformin) may increase levels of either drug due to competition [US].
- **CYP3A4 and P-gp Substrates:** Xanomeline transiently inhibits these in the gut (locally). Monitor for increased effects of sensitive substrates (e.g., narrow therapeutic index P-gp substrates) [US].
- **Antimuscarinic Drugs:** Concomitant use with other anticholinergics may potentiate adverse effects (dry mouth, constipation, urinary retention) [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Lettered categories are no longer used in current US labeling.
    - **Risk Summary:** No human data available. Animal studies (rats/rabbits) showed maternal toxicity and associated embryofetal developmental delays at high doses, but no malformations [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** No data on the presence of the drug in human milk or effects on milk production. Xanomeline and trospium are present in animal milk [US].
- **Fertility:** No adverse effects on fertility were observed in animal studies at doses significantly higher than the maximum recommended human dose (MRHD) [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established in patients <18 years [US].
- **Geriatric Use:** Geriatric patients (≥65 years) have higher trospium exposures. Recommended starting dose is 50 mg/20 mg BID with a slower titration; maximum recommended dose is 100 mg/20 mg twice daily [US].
- **Renal Impairment:**
    - **Mild (eGFR 60 to <90 mL/min):** No dosage adjustment required [US].
    - **Moderate/Severe (eGFR <60 mL/min):** Not recommended due to increased risk of anticholinergic adverse reactions from trospium [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:**
    - **Mild:** Not recommended [US].
    - **Moderate/Severe:** Contraindicated [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** 
    - **Xanomeline:** Muscarinic acetylcholine receptor agonist with selectivity for M1 and M4 receptors in the CNS [US]. 
    - **Trospium Chloride:** Muscarinic antagonist that works primarily in peripheral tissues to offset the peripheral cholinergic side effects of xanomeline [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Median Tmax is 2 hours (xanomeline) and 1 hour (trospium). Food significantly reduces trospium absorption (70-80% reduction) [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Xanomeline is metabolised via CYP2D6, 2B6, 1A2, 2C9, 2C19, and FMOs. Trospium is primarily eliminated unchanged [US].
    - **Elimination:** Xanomeline t1/2 is ~5 hours. Trospium t1/2 is ~6 hours, with 85-90% of a systemic dose excreted in urine via active tubular secretion [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pivotal Studies:** Two 5-week, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trials (Studies 1 and 2) in adults with schizophrenia [US].
- **Primary Outcome:** Both trials showed a statistically significant reduction in the Positive and Negative Syndrome Scale (PANSS) total score compared to placebo at Week 5 (Placebo-subtracted difference: -9.6 and -8.4 respectively) [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
- **Shelf Life:** Not specified in available sources.
