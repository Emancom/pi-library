# Editorial Product Information: Telmisartan

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical data and regulatory requirements. 
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis intended for professional reference and does not replace the official Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. At the time of writing, only the Australian PI was provided for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Telmisartan, a non-peptide sartan and specific angiotensin II receptor (type $AT_1$) antagonist.
- **Brand Names:** ALKEM TELMISARTAN [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** C09CA07.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - **Tablets:** 40 mg and 80 mg [Australian].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Pharmacor Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Hypertension:** Management of essential hypertension in adult patients [Australian].
- **Cardiovascular (CV) Risk Reduction:** Prevention of cardiovascular morbidity and mortality in adults aged 55 years or older who are at high risk of CV events, specifically those with coronary artery disease, peripheral artery disease, a history of stroke or transient ischaemic attack, or high-risk diabetes mellitus with evidence of end-organ damage [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults)
- **Hypertension:** The standard starting dose is 40 mg once daily. If target blood pressure is not reached, the dose may be increased to a maximum of 80 mg once daily.
- **Cardiovascular Prevention:** The recommended dose is 80 mg once daily. The efficacy of doses lower than 80 mg for this indication has not been established [Australian].

### Administration Instructions
- Tablets should be taken once daily and may be administered with or without food. 
- Due to hygroscopic properties, tablets should not be removed from blister packs until immediately before administration [Australian].

### Titration and Response
- While an initial reduction in blood pressure is observed after the first dose, the maximum antihypertensive effect is typically achieved 4 to 8 weeks after initiating therapy.
- Telmisartan may be combined with thiazide diuretics (e.g., hydrochlorothiazide) or calcium-channel blockers (e.g., amlodipine) for additive effects [Australian].

## 4. Contraindications
- Hypersensitivity to telmisartan or any excipients (e.g., lactose).
- **Pregnancy:** Use during the second and third trimesters is strictly contraindicated [Australian].
- **Lactation:** Contraindicated during breastfeeding [Australian].
- **Biliary Disorders:** Biliary obstructive disorders.
- **Hepatic Function:** Severe hepatic impairment [Australian].
- **Dual RAAS Blockade:** Concomitant use with aliskiren in patients with diabetes mellitus or renal impairment ($GFR < 60$ mL/min/1.73 $m^2$) [Australian].
- **Hereditary Conditions:** Incompatibility with certain excipients (e.g., rare hereditary galactose intolerance) [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Renal Artery Stenosis:** Increased risk of severe hypotension and renal insufficiency in patients with bilateral renal artery stenosis or stenosis of the artery to a single functioning kidney.
- **Dual Blockade of the RAAS:** Combining telmisartan with ACE inhibitors or aliskiren is generally not recommended due to increased risks of hyperkalaemia, hypotension, and renal failure.
- **Triple Therapy Risk:** The concurrent use of an ARB (like telmisartan), an ACE inhibitor or NSAID/COX-2 inhibitor, and a thiazide diuretic significantly increases the risk of renal impairment, especially in the elderly.
- **Hyperkalaemia:** Risk is increased in patients with renal impairment, heart failure, or those using potassium-sparing diuretics/supplements. Monitoring of serum potassium is recommended.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Most telmisartan is eliminated via bile; clearance is reduced in patients with hepatic insufficiency.
- **Diabetes Mellitus:** Treatment may reduce coronary perfusion in patients with undiagnosed coronary heart disease; specific diagnostics should be performed prior to initiation.
- **Aortic/Mitral Stenosis:** Use with caution in patients with obstructive hypertrophic cardiomyopathy or valvular stenosis.
- **Ethnic Differences:** Telmisartan (and other RAAS blockers) may be less effective in lowering blood pressure in black populations compared to non-black populations [Australian].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Digoxin:** Co-administration may increase digoxin peak ($C_{max}$) by 50% and AUC by 22%. Monitor digoxin levels during initiation or adjustment of telmisartan.
- **Lithium:** Reversible increases in serum lithium concentrations and toxicity have been reported; monitor lithium levels closely.
- **NSAIDs (including Aspirin $\ge$ 3g/day and COX-2 inhibitors):** May reduce the antihypertensive effect and increase the risk of acute renal failure in dehydrated or elderly patients.
- **Ramipril:** Combination results in a 2.5-fold increase in ramiprilat exposure; not recommended.
- **Other Antihypertensives:** Potentiate the hypotensive effect of telmisartan.
- **Potassium-Sparing Agents:** Use with potassium supplements or potassium-sparing diuretics (e.g., spironolactone) may lead to significant hyperkalaemia [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Category D [Australian]:** Contraindicated in the 2nd and 3rd trimesters. Exposure during these trimesters is fetotoxic (reduced renal function, oligohydramnios, skull ossification retardation) and neonatotoxic (renal failure, hypotension, hyperkalaemia). 
    - If pregnancy is detected, telmisartan must be discontinued immediately.
- **Breastfeeding:** Contraindicated. It is unknown if telmisartan is excreted in human milk; animal studies have confirmed excretion in milk and adverse effects on offspring growth [Australian].
- **Fertility:** No human data; animal studies showed no effect on male or female fertility at 100 mg/kg/day [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy in patients under 18 years of age have not been established.
- **Elderly:** No dose adjustment is generally required.
- **Renal Impairment:** No adjustment needed, including patients on haemodialysis. Telmisartan is not dialysable.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** In patients with mild to moderate impairment, the dose should not exceed 40 mg once daily. Contraindicated in severe impairment [Australian].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Telmisartan selectively and with high affinity binds to the $AT_1$ receptor subtype. It displaces angiotensin II without acting as an agonist. This inhibits vasoconstriction and the release of aldosterone, lowering systemic vascular resistance. It does not inhibit ACE (kininase II) and thus does not affect bradykinin levels.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** An 80 mg dose almost completely inhibits angiotensin II-evoked blood pressure increases for 24 hours.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Rapid but variable; absolute bioavailability is dose-dependent (~40% at 40 mg; ~60% at 160 mg).
    - **Distribution:** Highly protein bound (>99.5%).
    - **Metabolism:** Metabolised by conjugation to an inactive acylglucuronide; not metabolised by the CYP450 system.
    - **Elimination:** Terminal half-life of 18–23 hours. Excreted almost exclusively in faeces as unchanged drug. Urinary excretion is <1% [Australian].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Hypertension:** Pivotal trials demonstrated that telmisartan 40–80 mg is as effective as amlodipine, atenolol, enalapril, and lisinopril in reducing blood pressure.
- **ONTARGET:** Compared telmisartan 80 mg, ramipril 10 mg, and the combination. Telmisartan was non-inferior to ramipril in reducing CV death, MI, and stroke. The combination group showed no added benefit but higher rates of adverse events (renal failure, hyperkalaemia).
- **TRANSCEND:** In ACE-inhibitor intolerant patients, telmisartan 80 mg showed a non-significant trend toward reducing the primary CV composite endpoint compared to placebo, though it significantly reduced the secondary endpoint of CV death, MI, and stroke [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- Store below 25°C.
- Protect from light and moisture.
- Tablets are sensitive to moisture and should remain in the original Al/Al blister pack until use [Australian].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule:** Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine) [Australian].
