# Editorial Product Information: Tavaborole

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (Food and Drug Administration)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of tavaborole for healthcare professionals. It synthesises available regulatory data into a single, clinically focused reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority's current approved labeling for definitive prescribing instructions.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Tavaborole (an oxaborole antifungal).
- **Brand Names:** Tavaborole [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - Topical solution, 5% w/w (43.5 mg of tavaborole per mL) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Topical (for use on toenails only).
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Amneal Pharmaceuticals LLC [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Onychomycosis of the Toenails:** Indicated for the topical treatment of onychomycosis of the toenails caused by *Trichophyton rubrum* or *Trichophyton mentagrophytes* [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** Apply once daily for 48 weeks [US].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Apply to the entire surface of the affected toenail and underneath the tip of the toenail [US].
    - Ensure the toenail is clean and dry before application [US].
    - Wash hands with soap and water following application [US].
    - For topical use only; not for oral, ophthalmic, or intravaginal administration [US].
- **Missed Dose:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Special Instructions:** The product is supplied with a separate dropper assembly. The screw cap should be replaced with the dropper at the start of use. The product should be discarded three months after the dropper is inserted [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None identified in the provided source [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **General Warnings:** For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes, mouth, or vagina [US].
- **Flammability:** The solution is alcohol-based and flammable. Keep away from heat and open flames [US].
- **Local Irritation:** May cause irritation at or near the application site. Patients should be advised to contact a healthcare professional if persistent irritation (redness, itching, or swelling) occurs [US].
- **Cosmetic Products:** The safety and efficacy of tavaborole used in conjunction with nail polish or other cosmetic nail products have not been evaluated [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions reported in clinical trials were generally localized to the application site.

| Adverse Reaction | Tavaborole 5% (N=791) | Vehicle (N=395) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Common (1–10%)** | | |
| Application site exfoliation | 2.7% | 0.3% |
| Ingrown toenail | 2.5% | 0.3% |
| Application site erythema | 1.6% | 0% |
| Application site dermatitis | 1.3% | 0% |

- **Postmarketing Experience:** Hypersensitivity and contact allergy have been reported during voluntary post-approval use. Because these are reported from a population of uncertain size, frequency cannot be reliably estimated [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** In vitro studies indicate that at therapeutic concentrations, tavaborole does not inhibit or induce Cytochrome P450 (CYP450) enzymes [US].
- **Management Notes:** Clinically significant systemic drug interactions are unlikely due to low systemic absorption following topical application.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not specified as a letter grade (follows Pregnancy and Lactation Labeling Rule). There are no available human data to inform drug-associated risks.
    - **Animal Data:** Oral administration in rats during organogenesis resulted in embryofetal toxicity and skeletal malformations at 570 times the Maximum Recommended Human Dose (MRHD). In rabbits, oral administration resulted in embryofetal toxicity at 155 times the MRHD [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** There is no information regarding the presence of tavaborole in human milk or its effects on the breastfed infant. The developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding should be weighed against the mother's clinical need and potential adverse effects on the child [US].
- **Fertility:** No effects on fertility were observed in rat studies at oral doses up to 300 mg/kg/day (107 times the MRHD) [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy have been established in patients aged 6 years and older. Use in this population is supported by adult trials and additional pharmacokinetic data from adolescents (12 to <17 years) [US].
- **Geriatric Use:** No overall differences in safety or effectiveness were observed between subjects aged 65 and older and younger subjects, though increased sensitivity in some older individuals cannot be ruled out [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Tavaborole is an oxaborole antifungal. It inhibits fungal protein synthesis by inhibiting an aminoacyl-transfer ribonucleic acid (tRNA) synthetase (AARS), specifically leucyl-tRNA synthetase [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** At therapeutic doses, tavaborole is not expected to prolong the QTc interval to a clinically relevant extent [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Systemic absorption is low. In adults with onychomycosis, steady-state was reached after 14 days of daily application. Mean peak concentration (Cmax) at steady state was 5.2 ± 3.5 ng/mL [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Undergoes extensive metabolism [US].
    - **Elimination:** Metabolites are primarily excreted in the urine [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
The efficacy of tavaborole was evaluated in two multicenter, double-blind, randomized, vehicle-controlled trials involving 1,194 subjects. Tavaborole or vehicle was applied once daily for 48 weeks.
- **Primary Endpoint:** Complete Cure at Week 52 (defined as 0% clinical involvement of the target toenail plus negative KOH and negative culture).
- **Results:**
    - **Trial 1:** Complete Cure was 6.5% for tavaborole vs 0.5% for vehicle.
    - **Trial 2:** Complete Cure was 9.1% for tavaborole vs 1.5% for vehicle [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F and 86°F) [US].
- **Shelf Life:** Discard the product within 3 months after insertion of the dropper [US].
- **Special Handling:** Keep bottle tightly closed and away from heat or open flames. Keep out of reach of children. The dropper assembly contains Dry Natural Rubber [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison/Schedule Classification:**
    - **United States:** Prescription only (Rx only).
