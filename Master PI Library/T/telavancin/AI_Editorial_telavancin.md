# Editorial Product Information: Telavancin

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial has been synthesised from the following official Product Information (PI) source: **US (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of telavancin for use by healthcare professionals. It serves as a clinically accurate synthesis of regulatory data across jurisdictions (currently based on available US documentation).
- **Disclaimer:** This editorial synthesis is for informational purposes only and is not a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Telavancin hydrochloride, a semi-synthetic lipoglycopeptide antibacterial derived from vancomycin.
- **Brand Name(s):** **Vibativ** [US].
- **ATC Code:** J01XA03.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Lyophilized powder for injection in single-dose vials containing 750 mg of telavancin (as hydrochloride) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Cumberland Pharmaceuticals Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Complicated Skin and Skin Structure Infections (cSSSI):** Treatment of adult patients with infections caused by susceptible isolates of Gram-positive bacteria, including *Staphylococcus aureus* (methicillin-susceptible and -resistant), *Streptococcus pyogenes*, *Streptococcus agalactiae*, *Streptococcus anginosus* group, and vancomycin-susceptible *Enterococcus faecalis* [US].
- **Hospital-Acquired and Ventilator-Associated Bacterial Pneumonia (HABP/VABP):** Treatment of adult patients with infections caused by susceptible isolates of *S. aureus* (methicillin-susceptible and -resistant). Telavancin should be reserved for cases where alternative treatments are unsuitable [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults ≥18 years)
- The standard dose is **10 mg/kg** administered via IV infusion over 60 minutes once every 24 hours [US].
- **Duration of therapy:** 
  - **cSSSI:** 7 to 14 days [US].
  - **HABP/VABP:** 7 to 21 days [US].

### Renal Impairment Dosage Adjustments
Dosage is based on Creatinine Clearance (CrCl) calculated using the Cockcroft-Gault formula and ideal body weight (IBW). Use actual body weight if it is less than IBW [US].

| Creatinine Clearance (CrCl) | Dosage Regimen |
| :--- | :--- |
| >50 mL/min | 10 mg/kg every 24 hours |
| 30–50 mL/min | 7.5 mg/kg every 24 hours |
| 10–<30 mL/min | 10 mg/kg every 48 hours |

- **CrCl <10 mL/min or Hemodialysis:** Insufficient data are available to provide dosing recommendations [US].

### Preparation and Administration Instructions
- **Reconstitution:** Reconstitute the 750 mg vial with 45 mL of 5% Dextrose Injection, Sterile Water for Injection, or 0.9% Sodium Chloride. The resulting concentration is 15 mg/mL.
- **Dilution:** Must be further diluted in a 100 to 250 mL infusion bag (5% Dextrose, 0.9% Sodium Chloride, or Lactated Ringer's).
- **Administration:** Infuse over a minimum of 60 minutes. Do not administer as a bolus or rapid infusion due to the risk of infusion-related reactions. 
- **Compatibility:** Do not add other medications to the vial or infuse simultaneously through the same IV line. If using a common line for sequential drugs, flush with an approved diluent before and after administration [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Intravenous Unfractionated Heparin Sodium:** Contraindicated due to telavancin’s interference with coagulation assays (aPTT) [US].
- **Known Hypersensitivity:** Prohibited in patients with a history of hypersensitivity to telavancin [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warnings [US]
- **Increased Mortality in HABP/VABP:** Patients with pre-existing moderate to severe renal impairment (CrCl ≤50 mL/min) treated for HABP/VABP showed higher mortality compared to those treated with vancomycin. Use only if benefits outweigh risks in this population.
- **Nephrotoxicity:** New onset or worsening renal impairment has been observed.
- **Embryo-Fetal Toxicity:** Animal studies suggest potential for fetal harm. Verify pregnancy status prior to initiation.

### General Warnings
- **Decreased Efficacy in cSSSI:** Reduced clinical response observed in cSSSI patients with baseline CrCl ≤50 mL/min [US].
- **Coagulation Test Interference:** Telavancin binds to artificial phospholipid surfaces used in tests, artificially prolonging PT, INR, aPTT, and ACT for up to 18 hours post-dose. It does **not** cause clinical bleeding. Use non-phospholipid-dependent tests (e.g., Chromogenic Factor Xa assay) if monitoring is required [US].
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious, potentially fatal anaphylactic reactions may occur. Use caution in patients with known vancomycin allergy (cross-reactivity is unknown) [US].
- **Infusion-Related Reactions:** Rapid infusion may cause "Red-man Syndrome" (flushing, pruritus, rash). Ensure a minimum 60-minute infusion time [US].
- **CDAD:** *Clostridium difficile*-associated diarrhea has been reported, ranging from mild to fatal colitis [US].
- **QTc Prolongation:** Avoid in patients with congenital long QT syndrome, uncompensated heart failure, or severe LV hypertrophy. Use with caution alongside other QTc-prolonging drugs [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Very Common (>10%):** Taste disturbance (often metallic or soapy), nausea, vomiting, and foamy urine [US].
- **Common (1–10%):** Diarrhea, rigors, decreased appetite, and acute renal failure (specifically in HABP/VABP trials) [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Nephrotoxicity (increased serum creatinine), serious hypersensitivity/anaphylaxis, and QTc prolongation [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Heparin:** Telavancin interferes with aPTT laboratory tests, making the results uninterpretable [US].
- **QTc Prolonging Drugs:** Potential pharmacodynamic interaction; monitor ECG if co-administered [US].
- **Nephrotoxic Agents:** Increased risk of renal impairment when used with NSAIDs, ACE inhibitors, or diuretics [US].
- **CYP450 System:** No clinically significant interactions; telavancin does not inhibit or induce major CYP450 isoforms in humans [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: No category letter assigned under PLLR, but animal data indicates risk]. Adverse developmental outcomes (limb and skeletal malformations) were seen in rats, rabbits, and minipigs at 1-2 times the human exposure. Women of reproductive potential must use effective contraception during treatment and for 2 days after the final dose [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** No data available on the presence of telavancin in human milk or its effects on the infant. Consider the benefits of breastfeeding against the mother's clinical need for the drug [US].
- **Fertility:** Reversible sperm parameter changes and histopathological testicular changes were seen in male rats. No effect on female rat fertility was observed [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness not established. Concern for poor outcomes in infants <1 year due to immature renal function [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Higher incidence of renal adverse events and lower clinical cure rates in cSSSI trials were observed in patients ≥65 years. Dosing should be carefully selected based on renal function [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Requires dose adjustment for CrCl ≤50 mL/min. Accumulation of the solubilizer hydroxypropyl-beta-cyclodextrin can occur in renal dysfunction [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No adjustment required for mild to moderate (Child-Pugh B) impairment. Not studied in severe (Child-Pugh C) impairment [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Bactericidal lipoglycopeptide. It inhibits bacterial cell wall synthesis by binding to late-stage peptidoglycan precursors (including Lipid II) and disrupts bacterial membrane barrier function (depolarization) [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
  - **Distribution:** 90% protein bound (primarily albumin); achieves significant concentrations in pulmonary epithelial lining fluid and alveolar macrophages [US].
  - **Metabolism:** No significant metabolism detected via CYP450 pathways [US].
  - **Elimination:** Primarily renal (76% recovered in urine); terminal half-life is approximately 8 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **cSSSI Trials:** Two Phase 3 trials showed non-inferiority to vancomycin, though cure rates were lower in patients with baseline renal impairment [US].
- **HABP/VABP Trials:** Two Phase 3 trials demonstrated similar clinical response rates to vancomycin. However, a protocol-specified analysis showed increased mortality in patients with pre-existing moderate-to-severe renal impairment [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vials:** Store original packages refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (35°F to 46°F). Brief excursions to room temperature (up to 25°C) are acceptable [US].
- **Reconstituted Solution:** Stable for 12 hours at room temperature or 7 days under refrigeration [US].
- **Diluted Infusion Bag:** Combined storage time (vial + bag) must not exceed 12 hours (room temperature) or 7 days (refrigerated). Can be stored frozen (-30°C to -10°C) for up to 32 days [US].
