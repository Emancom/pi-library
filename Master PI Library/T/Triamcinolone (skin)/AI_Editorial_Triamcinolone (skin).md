# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine Injection

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)** via Baxter Healthcare Corporation.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of Levocarnitine injection for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises clinical data, dosing, and safety information into a single reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis intended for informational purposes. It does not replace the official PI required for prescribing and dispensing in any specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP); a carrier molecule for long-chain fatty acid transport.
- **Chemical Class:** (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt (a naturally occurring quaternary ammonium compound).
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection (Generic).
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Acute and chronic treatment of secondary carnitine deficiency resulting from genetic metabolic defects [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients undergoing chronic haemodialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
- **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg.
- **Administration:** Delivered as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) or via infusion [US].
- **Loading Dose:** In cases of severe metabolic crisis, a loading dose may be given, followed by equivalent dosing over the subsequent 24 hours.
- **Frequency:** Administered every 3 to 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours.
- **Maximum Dose:** Doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilised in clinical practice [US].

### ESRD Patients on Haemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight).
- **Administration:** Delivered as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].
- **Dose Adjustments:** Trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levels should guide adjustments. Downward titration (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of treatment if levels are adequate [US].

### Monitoring
- **Baseline:** Obtain plasma carnitine levels prior to therapy.
- **Routine Monitoring:** Weekly and monthly monitoring is recommended, including blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine range: 35 to 60 µmol/L) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **None Known:** There are no listed contraindications for levocarnitine injection in the provided source material [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal oedema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. These may occur within minutes of administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and initiate medical management. Re-administration should only be considered after a strict risk-benefit analysis [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure disorders. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **General Precautions:** The safety of high-dose oral levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function has not been established, as it may lead to the accumulation of metabolites (TMA and TMAO) [US].

- Body odour.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** It is recommended to monitor INR levels frequently after initiating levocarnitine or following dose adjustments in patients on anticoagulant therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not specified by letter, but noted as use only if clearly needed]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. No adequate, well-controlled human studies exist [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk carnitine concentrations after administration. The risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant must be weighed against the benefit of supplementation for the mother [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies revealed no evidence of impaired fertility [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Refer to metabolic disorder dosing; levocarnitine is indicated for paediatric patients with inborn errors of metabolism [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Highly relevant in ESRD; however, the safety of chronic high-dose *oral* levocarnitine in severe renal impairment is cautioned due to metabolite accumulation (TMA/TMAO) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specifically addressed in the available source [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into the mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also facilitates the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in faeces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. Terminal half-life is approximately 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** Efficacy was demonstrated in a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients receiving 20 mg/kg three times weekly. Trough levocarnitine levels increased from a baseline of ~21 µmol/L to ~190 µmol/L by week 8 [US].
- **Dose-Ranging:** Studies at 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg demonstrated dose-dependent increases in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Protection:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat [US].
- **Stability:** Compatible with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s (0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL concentration) for up to 24 hours in PVC bags at room temperature [US].
- **Preservative:** None; discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources [US].
