# Editorial Product Information: Tioconazole

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial has been synthesized from the official Product Information (PI) and "Drug Facts" labeling provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of tioconazole for healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This synthesis is an editorial resource and does not replace the official, jurisdiction-specific PI required for prescribing or dispensing. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory guidelines for definitive instruction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Tioconazole (an imidazole antifungal)
- **Brand Name(s):** Tioconazole 1, Signature SELECT [US]
- **ATC Code:** G01AF08 (Not explicitly stated in source, but internationally recognised)
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Vaginal ointment, 6.5% (300 mg tioconazole per 4.6 g applicator) [US]
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Vaginal
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Distributed by Perrigo; Labeler: Safeway [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Vaginal Candidiasis:** Indicated for the treatment of vulvovaginal yeast infections [US].
- **Recurrent Infections:** Indicated for the treatment of recurrent vaginal yeast infections in patients previously diagnosed by a medical professional [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adults and Children ≥12 years):** A single-dose treatment consisting of one pre-filled applicator (300 mg tioconazole) inserted intravaginally [US].
- **Administration Instructions:** 
    - The dose should ideally be administered at bedtime.
    - The applicator is pre-filled. Patients should be instructed to remove the cap, insert the applicator high into the vagina, and depress the plunger to dispense the entire contents.
    - Use of sanitary napkins is recommended rather than tampons during treatment [US].
- **Clinical Course:** Patients may experience some symptom improvement within 24 hours, but complete resolution may take up to 7 days [US].
- **Missed Dose:** Not applicable for this single-dose regimen.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Undiagnosed Symptoms:** Contraindicated for use in patients who have never had a vaginal yeast infection diagnosed by a physician [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to tioconazole or other imidazole antifungals [General clinical standard; noted implicitly in safety warnings].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **General Warnings:** For vaginal use only. Not for ophthalmic or oral use [US].
- **Medical Consultation Required:** Patients should consult a healthcare provider before use if they experience:
    - First-time vaginal itching or discomfort.
    - Systemic symptoms: Fever, chills, lower abdominal, back, or shoulder pain, nausea, or vomiting.
    - Foul-smelling vaginal discharge (may indicate bacterial vaginosis or trichomoniasis).
- **Special Populations/Risk Factors:** Patients with frequent yeast infections (e.g., once a month or three in six months), diabetes, or a weakened immune system (including HIV/AIDS) should seek medical advice prior to use [US].
- **Mechanical Contraceptive Failure:** This product contains petrolatum, which can weaken latex condoms and diaphragms, leading to failure. Patients should wait at least 3 days after treatment before using these methods [US].
- **Hygiene and Concomitant Products:** 
    - Do not use tampons, douches, or spermicides during treatment.
    - Avoid vaginal intercourse during the treatment period [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions are generally localized and transient.
- **Common (1–10%):** 
    - Temporary increase in vaginal burning, itching, or irritation upon insertion.
- **Unspecified Frequency:** 
    - Vaginal swelling or redness.
    - Dysuria (difficulty or burning on urination).
    - Systemic symptoms: Headache, abdominal pain/cramping, and upper respiratory infection.
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Rash or hives (discontinue use and consult a physician).

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Vaginal Products:** Avoid concurrent use of other vaginal preparations (e.g., douches, spermicides, feminine hygiene sprays) as they may interfere with the efficacy of tioconazole [US].
- **Physical Interactions:** The ointment base (white petrolatum) degrades latex-based contraceptives [US].
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US]: Consult a healthcare professional before use. While imidazole antifungals are commonly used topically, systemic absorption should be considered.
- **Breastfeeding:** [US]: Consult a healthcare professional before use.
- **Fertility:** Not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** 
    - **Children ≥12 years:** Use same as adult dosing [US].
    - **Children <12 years:** Consult a physician prior to use [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Not specified in available sources; systemic absorption is minimal.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Tioconazole is a broad-spectrum imidazole antifungal that inhibits the synthesis of ergosterol, a vital component of fungal cell membranes, thereby altering membrane permeability and leading to the death of *Candida* species [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** 
    - **Absorption:** Primarily acts locally; minimal systemic absorption occurs following a single vaginal dose.
    - **Distribution/Metabolism/Elimination:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Exhibits fungicidal activity against *Candida albicans* and other *Candida* species.
