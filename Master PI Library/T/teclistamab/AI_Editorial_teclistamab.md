# Editorial Product Information: Teclistamab

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesizes official Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdictions: **Australia** (Therapeutic Goods Administration) and the **United States** (Food and Drug Administration).
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised, clinically accurate overview for healthcare professionals.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI or local clinical guidelines required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should refer to their local regulatory authority's current approved documentation.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Teclistamab (teclistamab-cqyv [US])
- **Chemical Class:** Bispecific B-cell maturation antigen (BCMA)-directed CD3 T-cell engager; humanised IgG4-PAA antibody.
- **Brand Name:** **TECVAYLI**
- **ATC Code:** L01FX24
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:**
  - 30 mg/3 mL (10 mg/mL) single-dose vial.
  - 153 mg/1.7 mL (90 mg/mL) single-dose vial.
- **Route of Administration:** Subcutaneous injection only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Janssen-Cilag Pty Ltd [Australian]; Janssen Biotech, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
**TECVAYLI** is indicated for the treatment of adult patients with relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma (RRMM) under the following conditions:
- **Monotherapy:** In patients who have received at least three prior therapies [Australian] OR at least four prior lines of therapy [US], including a proteasome inhibitor (PI), an immunomodulatory agent (IMiD), and an anti-CD38 monoclonal antibody.
- **Combination Therapy:** [US-approved; not Australian-approved] In combination with daratumumab and hyaluronidase-fihj in patients who have received at least one prior line of therapy, including a PI and an IMiD.

*Note: Monotherapy approval in both jurisdictions was granted under accelerated/provisional pathways based on response rates; continued approval may be contingent upon verification of clinical benefit in confirmatory trials.*

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults)
Teclistamab is administered via a step-up dosing schedule to mitigate the risk of cytokine release syndrome (CRS).

#### Monotherapy Step-Up Schedule
- **Step-up dose 1 (0.06 mg/kg):** Day 1.
- **Step-up dose 2 (0.3 mg/kg):** [Australian: Day 3; US: Day 4].
- **First treatment dose (1.5 mg/kg):** [Australian: Day 5; US: Day 7].
- **Maintenance:** 1.5 mg/kg once weekly thereafter.

#### Combination Therapy Schedule [US only]
- **Step-up dose 1 (0.06 mg/kg):** Day 1 (at least 20 hours after daratumumab/hyaluronidase-fihj dose).
- **Step-up dose 2 (0.3 mg/kg):** Day 3.
- **First treatment dose (1.5 mg/kg):** Day 7.
- **Maintenance:** 1.5 mg/kg once weekly (Weeks 2-8); 3 mg/kg every two weeks (Weeks 9-24); 3 mg/kg every four weeks (Week 25 onwards).

### Dose Modifications for Response
In patients who achieve and maintain a complete response (CR) or better for $\ge$ 6 months, clinicians may consider reducing the dosing frequency to 1.5 mg/kg every two weeks [Australian/US monotherapy].

### Administration Instructions
- **Subcutaneous only:** Do not administer intravenously. Inject into the abdomen (preferred) or thigh.
- **Monitoring:** Patients should be hospitalised for 48 hours after each step-up dose [Australian/US] and monitored daily for 48 hours after the first treatment dose [Australian].
- **Pretreatment Medications:** Administer 1–3 hours before each step-up dose:
  - Corticosteroid (Dexamethasone 16 mg).
  - Antihistamine (Diphenhydramine 50 mg or equivalent).
  - Antipyretic (Acetaminophen/Paracetamol 650–1000 mg).

## 4. Contraindications
- **United States:** None listed.
- **Australia:** Hypersensitivity to the active substance or any excipients (e.g., EDTA, glacial acetic acid, polysorbate 20, sodium acetate trihydrate, sucrose).

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warnings [Australian/US]
- **Cytokine Release Syndrome (CRS):** Can be severe, life-threatening, or fatal.
- **Neurologic Toxicity:** Includes Immune Effector Cell-Associated Neurotoxicity Syndrome (ICANS). Can be severe or fatal. 
- **Restricted Distribution [US only]:** Available only through the TECVAYLI and TALVEY REMS program.

### General Warnings
- **Hepatotoxicity:** [US-emphasised] Teclistamab can cause fatal hepatic failure. Monitor liver enzymes and bilirubin at baseline and during treatment.
- **Infections:** Severe, life-threatening, or fatal bacterial and viral infections (including opportunistic infections) have occurred. Monitor for signs of infection and treat promptly.
- **Neutropenia:** Highly prevalent; monitor CBC at baseline and periodically.
- **Hypogammaglobulinaemia:** Monitor immunoglobulin levels. Administer IVIG as clinically indicated to maintain levels >400 mg/dL [US].
- **PML:** Monitor for new/worsening neurological signs; discontinue if Progressive Multifocal Leukoencephalopathy is confirmed [Australian].
- **Embryo-Fetal Toxicity:** May cause fetal harm. Verify pregnancy status before initiation.

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Frequency Category: Very Common ($\ge$ 10%)
- **General:** Pyrexia (76%), Fatigue (33-41%), Edema, Pain.
- **Immune:** CRS (72% monotherapy; 60% combination), Hypogammaglobulinaemia (75%).
- **Hematologic:** Neutropenia (71-88%), Anaemia (55%), Lymphopenia (35%), Thrombocytopenia (40%).
- **Infections:** Upper respiratory tract infection, Pneumonia, Urinary tract infection, COVID-19.
- **Gastrointestinal:** Nausea, Diarrhoea, Constipation.
- **Neurologic:** Headache (25%), Encephalopathy, Neuropathy peripheral.
- **Musculoskeletal:** Musculoskeletal pain (44-52%).
- **Local:** Injection site reactions (37-38%).

### Serious Adverse Effects
- **Monotherapy:** Pneumonia (16%), COVID-19 (15%), CRS (8%), Sepsis (7%).
- **Combination Therapy [US]:** Pneumonia (33%), URTI (15%), CRS (10%).

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interaction:** No formal studies conducted.
- **CYP450 Suppression:** The release of cytokines during the initiation of treatment can suppress CYP450 enzymes. 
- **Management:** Monitor for toxicity or drug concentrations of concomitant CYP450 substrates with a narrow therapeutic index (e.g., cyclosporine) from the start of step-up dosing until 7 days after the first treatment dose or during a CRS event.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
  - [Australian: Category C]. 
  - [US: Based on mechanism, may cause fetal harm].
  - Use is not recommended during pregnancy. Teclistamab causes T-cell activation and cytokine release, which may compromise pregnancy maintenance.
- **Contraception:** Females of reproductive potential should use effective contraception during and for 5 months after the last dose. [Australian: Male patients with female partners should use effective contraception for 3 months after the last dose].
- **Breastfeeding:** Discontinue breastfeeding during treatment and for 5 months after the last dose due to the risk of serious adverse reactions in the infant.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy not established in patients <18 years [US] or $\le$ 17 years [Australian].
- **Elderly Use:** No overall differences in safety/efficacy observed in patients $\ge$ 65 years. However, patients $\ge$ 75 years may experience a higher incidence of serious adverse reactions (83% vs 63% in younger patients) [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment for mild or moderate impairment. Not studied in severe impairment.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment for mild impairment. Not studied in moderate or severe impairment.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** A bispecific antibody that binds to the CD3 receptor on T-cells and the BCMA on the surface of myeloma cells. This facilitates T-cell mediated lysis of BCMA-expressing cells via the release of proinflammatory cytokines.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
  - **Bioavailability:** 72% (subcutaneous).
  - **$T_{max}$:** Median 139 hours after the first treatment dose.
  - **Volume of Distribution:** 5.63 L.
  - **Elimination:** Clearance decreases over time (maximal reduction of 40.8% at steady state).
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Increased concentrations of IL-6, IL-10, TNF-$\alpha$, and IFN-$\gamma$ are observed within the first month of treatment.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **MajesTEC-1:** A single-arm, open-label study of teclistamab monotherapy. 
  - **N=165 [Australian]; N=110 [US subset].**
  - **ORR:** 63% [Australian cohort]; 61.8% [US cohort].
  - **Duration of Response:** Median 24 months [Australian]; not reached at early cut-off [US].
- **MajesTEC-3 [US description]:** A randomised, open-label trial comparing teclistamab + daratumumab/hyaluronidase-fihj vs. investigator's choice (DPd or DVd).
  - **N=587.**
  - **PFS:** Median Not Reached for the teclistamab arm vs. 18.1 months for the control arm (HR 0.17).

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vials:** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C. Do not freeze.
- **Protection:** Store in the original carton to protect from light.
- **Prepared Syringes:** May be stored at 2°C to 8°C or at ambient temperature (15°C to 30°C) for a maximum of 20 hours. Discard after 20 hours.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Australia:** Poison Schedule S4 (Prescription Only Medicine).
- **United States:** Prescription Only (Rx-only). Restricted through REMS program.
