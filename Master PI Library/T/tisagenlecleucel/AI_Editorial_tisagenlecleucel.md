# Editorial Product Information: Tisagenlecleucel

## Editorial Note
- Contributing Jurisdictions: Australian (TGA), US (FDA).
- Purpose: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of the product information for tisagenlecleucel, synthesised from multiple regulatory sources for use by healthcare professionals.
- Disclaimer: This is an editorial synthesis intended for informational purposes and does not substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Tisagenlecleucel (autologous T-cells genetically modified ex vivo using a lentiviral vector encoding an anti-CD19 chimeric antigen receptor [CAR]).
- **Brand Name:** **Kymriah**
- **ATC Code:** L01XL04
- **Dosage Form and Strength:**
    - Cell suspension for intravenous infusion.
    - A single dose contains a specific range of CAR-positive viable T-cells, depending on the indication and patient weight:
        - **B-cell ALL (≤50 kg):** 0.2 to 5.0 x 10⁶ CAR-positive viable T-cells/kg body weight.
        - **B-cell ALL (>50 kg):** 0.1 to 2.5 x 10⁸ CAR-positive viable T-cells.
        - **DLBCL and FL:** 0.6 to 6.0 x 10⁸ CAR-positive viable T-cells.
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous infusion.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Novartis Pharmaceuticals.

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Pediatric and Young Adult B-cell Acute Lymphoblastic Leukaemia (ALL):** Patients up to 25 years of age with B-cell precursor ALL that is refractory, in relapse post-transplant, or in second or later relapse.
- **Adult Relapsed or Refractory (r/r) Diffuse Large B-cell Lymphoma (DLBCL):** Treatment of adult patients after two or more lines of systemic therapy. [US: Includes DLBCL not otherwise specified, high-grade B-cell lymphoma, and DLBCL arising from follicular lymphoma; Australian: Not indicated for primary central nervous system lymphoma].
- **Adult Relapsed or Refractory Follicular Lymphoma (FL):** Treatment of adult patients after two or more lines of systemic therapy. [US: Approved under accelerated approval based on response rate; continued approval may be contingent upon verification of clinical benefit in confirmatory trials].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
**Kymriah** is a single-dose, one-time treatment for autologous use only. 

### Pre-treatment Conditioning (Lymphodepleting Chemotherapy)
- **Confirmation:** Availability of **Kymriah** must be confirmed prior to starting lymphodepletion.
- **B-cell ALL Regimen:** Fludarabine (30 mg/m² IV daily for 4 days) and cyclophosphamide (500 mg/m² IV daily for 2 days). 
- **DLBCL and FL Regimen:** Fludarabine (25 mg/m² IV daily for 3 days) and cyclophosphamide (250 mg/m² IV daily for 3 days).
- **Alternative Regimen:** Bendamustine (90 mg/m² IV daily for 2 days) if the patient experienced Grade 4 haemorrhagic cystitis with cyclophosphamide or is chemorefractory to cyclophosphamide-containing regimens.
- **Timing:** Infusion should occur 2 to 14 days after completion of lymphodepletion for ALL/DLBCL [Australian: 2 to 6 days for FL; US: 2 to 11 days for DLBCL].

### Administration Instructions
- **Premedication:** Paracetamol (acetaminophen) and an H1-antihistamine (e.g., diphenhydramine) approximately 30 to 60 minutes prior to infusion. Avoid systemic corticosteroids unless necessary for life-threatening emergencies.
- **Thawing:** Thaw at 37°C until no visible ice remains. Infuse within 30 minutes of thawing.
- **Infusion:** Use gravity flow (approx. 10 to 20 mL per minute) through latex-free tubing without a leukocyte-depleting filter. Rinse the bag with 10 to 30 mL of 0.9% sodium chloride by back-priming to ensure total cell delivery.

## 4. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to tisagenlecleucel or any component of the formulation, including dimethyl sulfoxide (DMSO) or dextran 40 [Australian]. 
- [US: None listed].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warnings
- **Cytokine Release Syndrome (CRS):** May be fatal or life-threatening. Do not administer to patients with active infection or inflammatory disorders. Ensure a minimum of two doses of tocilizumab are available on-site prior to infusion.
- **Neurological Toxicities (ICANS):** May be fatal or life-threatening. Occurs concurrently with CRS, after resolution, or in the absence of CRS.
- **Secondary Haematological Malignancies:** T-cell malignancies have occurred following treatment with CD19-directed CAR-T therapies.

### General Warnings and Precautions
- **Infections and Febrile Neutropenia:** Serious, sometimes fatal, infections may occur. Monitor for signs/symptoms and treat aggressively.
- **Prolonged Cytopenias:** Grade 3 or higher cytopenias may persist for several weeks.
- **Hypogammaglobulinemia:** Monitor immunoglobulin levels; replacement therapy may be required.
- **Hypersensitivity:** Monitor for reactions during infusion (DMSO/Dextran 40 content).
- **Tumour Lysis Syndrome (TLS):** Monitor for TLS and manage per standard guidelines.
- **Laboratory Test Interference:** Use of **Kymriah** may result in false-positive HIV nucleic acid tests (NAT).

### Monitoring Requirements
- Monitor patients 2-3 times per week for the first week [Australian] or daily during the first week [US] for signs of CRS/ICANS.
- Instruct patients to remain within proximity (within 2 hours travel) of the treatment facility for at least 4 weeks [Australian] or at least 2 weeks [US] following infusion.

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Very Common (>10%)
- **General:** CRS (up to 77%), pyrexia/fever, fatigue, oedema.
- **Infections:** Pathogen-unspecified, viral, bacterial, and fungal infections.
- **Haematological:** Anaemia, neutropenia, febrile neutropenia, thrombocytopenia, leukopenia, lymphopenia.
- **Gastrointestinal:** Diarrhoea, nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain, constipation.
- **Nervous System:** Encephalopathy (including ICANS), headache, dizziness.
- **Metabolic:** Hypogammaglobulinemia, decreased appetite, hypokalaemia, hypophosphataemia, hypocalcaemia.
- **Vascular/Respiratory:** Hypotension, hypoxia, dyspnoea, cough.

### Serious Adverse Effects
- Fatal CRS and ICANS.
- Secondary malignancies of T-cell origin.
- Progressive multifocal leukoencephalopathy (PML) [post-marketing].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Live Vaccines:** Safety not studied. Do not administer for at least 6 weeks prior to lymphodepletion, during treatment, and until immune recovery.
- **Corticosteroids/Immunosuppressants:** Prophylactic use may interfere with CAR-T activity.
- **Interactions:** No formal drug interaction studies have been performed.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - [Australian: Category C]. 
    - [US: No available data in pregnant women]. 
    - Not recommended during pregnancy or in women of childbearing potential not using contraception. Potential for B-cell lymphocytopenia in the foetus. 
- **Breastfeeding:** Safety not established. A risk to the breastfed infant cannot be excluded. Discontinue breastfeeding or discuss risks/benefits with the patient.
- **Fertility:** No animal or human data available.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** 
    - **B-cell ALL:** Safety and efficacy established for patients >2 years [US] or >3 years [Australian]. 
    - **DLBCL/FL:** Safety and efficacy not established in patients <18 years.
- **Geriatric Use:** 
    - **B-cell ALL:** Safety and efficacy not established.
    - **DLBCL/FL:** No dose adjustment required for patients >65 years.
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** No formal studies performed. Based on the mechanism of action, impairment is not expected to significantly impact cellular kinetics.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Reprogrammed autologous T-cells expressing a CAR that targets CD19-positive cells. Binding triggers T-cell expansion and elimination of target B-cells.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Expect a period of B-cell aplasia due to on-target effects.
- **Pharmacokinetics/Cellular Kinetics:** 
    - Exhibits initial rapid expansion (Cmax) followed by a bi-exponential decline.
    - **Tmax (median):** Approx. 10 days across indications.
    - **Persistence:** Transgene detected up to 2 years in ALL and DLBCL responders.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ALL (Study B2202):** 75 patients infused; ORR 81% (all MRD negative). Median duration of remission was not reached at the time of initial data-cut.
- **DLBCL (Study C2201):** 115 patients infused; ORR 50% (CR 32%, PR 18%).
- **FL (Study E2202/ELARA):** 94 patients evaluable; ORR 86% (CR 69%).

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage:** Must be stored in the vapour phase of liquid nitrogen in a temperature-monitored system at **≤-120°C**. 
- **Handling:** Use closed, break-proof, and leak-proof containers for transport within the facility.
- **Incompatibilities:** Must not be mixed with other medicinal products.
