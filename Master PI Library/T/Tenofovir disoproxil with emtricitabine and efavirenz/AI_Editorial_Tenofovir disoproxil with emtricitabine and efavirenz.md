# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **US (FDA)**. 
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonized, clinically focused overview of **Levocarnitine Injection** for use by healthcare professionals. 
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. At the time of writing, only the United States regulatory documentation was provided for analysis; clinical guidance should be cross-referenced with local regulations where applicable.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection, USP (Baxter).
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for intravenous injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US-approved].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD undergoing dialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    - Recommended dose is 50 mg/kg.
    - Administration: Slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion.
    - Acute Management: A loading dose is often given in severe metabolic crisis, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours.
    - Frequency: Dosing should be every 3 or 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours.
    - Maximum Dose: The highest reported dose administered is 300 mg/kg.
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - Recommended starting dose: 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight.
    - Administration: Slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line after each dialysis session.
    - Adjustment: Dose adjustments (e.g., down to 5 mg/kg) may be made as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough (pre-dialysis) plasma carnitine levels.

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter and discoloration before use.
- Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial.

### Monitoring
- **Metabolic Disorders:** Weekly and monthly monitoring is recommended. Monitoring should include blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine: 35 to 60 µmol/L).
- **ESRD:** Initiation of therapy may be prompted by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma concentrations below 40 to 50 µmol/L.

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known** [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions can occur within minutes of administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately [US].
- **Seizures:** Levocarnitine may increase the frequency or severity of seizures in patients with pre-existing seizure activity. Seizures have also been reported in patients without a prior history of epilepsy [US].
- **Risk of Oral Formulation in Renal Impairment:** While this document concerns the IV formulation, it is noted that chronic high doses of *oral* levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function or ESRD may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Very Common (>10%)
- **Body as a Whole:** Chest pain (12%), Flu syndrome (25%), Headache (22%), Infection (15%), Injection site reaction (33%), Pain (30%).
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (20%), Hypotension (14%).
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea (16%), Vomiting (15%).
- **Nervous:** Dizziness (13%).
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (23%).

### Common (1–10%)
- **Body as a Whole:** Abdominal pain (9%), Accidental injury (10%), Asthenia (9%), Back pain (8%), Fever (7%).
- **Cardiovascular:** Arrhythmia (2%), Atrial fibrillation (2%), Palpitations (5%), Tachycardia (6%).
- **Digestive:** Anorexia (5%), Constipation (3%), Dyspepsia (5%), Nausea (8%).
- **Metabolic:** Hypercalcemia (9%), Hyperkalemia (6%), Hypervolemia (5%), Peripheral edema (5%), Weight decrease/increase (2–5%).
- **Nervous:** Insomnia (4%), Paresthesia (5%).
- **Respiratory:** Cough increase (9%), Dyspnea (7%), Rhinitis (9%).
- **Skin:** Pruritus (5%), Rash (3%).

### Other Reported Effects
- **Transient effects:** Nausea and vomiting.
- **Less frequent:** Body odor and gastritis.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels after initiating levocarnitine or after dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not assigned under current labeling, but reproductive studies in rats/rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm]. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed.
- **Breastfeeding:** Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk carnitine concentrations. The risk of excess carnitine intake in the infant must be weighed against the benefits of maternal supplementation [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing is weight-based (50 mg/kg) as per the metabolic disorder guidelines.
- **Renal Impairment:** IV levocarnitine is indicated for ESRD patients on dialysis. However, the safety and efficacy of the *oral* formulation have not been evaluated in renal insufficiency [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also facilitates the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not protein-bound; mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites are trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces).
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine over 24 hours (uncorrected for endogenous levels). Mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours.
    - **Dialysis:** Levocarnitine is easily removed from plasma by hemodialysis.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Pharmacokinetic Study:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients, IV levocarnitine (20 mg/kg three times weekly post-dialysis) increased mean pre-dialysis plasma concentrations from 20 µmol/L to 190 µmol/L after 8 weeks.
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** Studies using 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg showed dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks.
- **Note:** While IV levocarnitine effectively increases carnitine concentrations in ESRD patients, the specific impact on clinical outcomes in this population has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Conditions:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat.
- **Stability:** Compatible with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s (0.5 mg/mL to 8.0 mg/mL concentrations) for up to 24 hours at room temperature in PVC bags.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources.
