# Editorial Product Information: Temsirolimus

## Editorial Note
- Contributing Jurisdictions: United States (FDA). 
- **Purpose**: This document is a harmonised editorial overview of temsirolimus, synthesized for use by healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer**: This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information (PI) or Prescribing Information required for clinical practice in any specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient**: Temsirolimus (a kinase inhibitor; mTOR inhibitor).
- **Chemical Class**: Rapamycin derivative.
- **Brand Name(s)**: **Torisel** [US].
- **ATC Code**: L01EG01 (Not specified in available source).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s)**: Injection kit containing:
    - 25 mg/mL concentrated solution (1.2 mL total, containing 0.2 mL overfill) [US].
    - Dedicated diluent (1.8 mL deliverable volume) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration**: Intravenous infusion.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor**: Wyeth Pharmaceuticals LLC (subsidiary of Pfizer Inc.) [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Advanced Renal Cell Carcinoma**: Indicated for the treatment of patients with advanced renal cell carcinoma [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing**: 25 mg administered once weekly until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity occurs [US].
- **Pre-medication**: Prophylactic intravenous diphenhydramine (25 to 50 mg) or a similar antihistamine should be administered approximately 30 minutes before every dose [US].
- **Dosage Interruption and Adjustment**:
    - Treatment should be withheld if absolute neutrophil count (ANC) is <1,000/mm³, platelet count is <75,000/mm³, or for NCI CTCAE Grade 3 or greater adverse reactions.
    - Upon recovery to Grade 2 or less, the drug may be restarted with a dose reduction of 5 mg/week (minimum dose 15 mg/week) [US].
- **Preparation Instructions (Two-Step Dilution)**:
    - **Step 1**: Inject 1.8 mL of the provided diluent into the 25 mg/mL vial. The resulting concentration is 10 mg/mL.
    - **Step 2**: Withdraw the required dose from the 10 mg/mL mixture and further dilute into 250 mL of 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection [US].
- **Administration**:
    - Infuse over 30 to 60 minutes via an infusion pump.
    - To minimize exposure to the plasticizer DEHP, use glass, polyolefin, or polyethylene containers and administration sets.
    - If PVC sets must be used, they must not contain DEHP. An in-line polyethersulfone filter (pore size ≤5 microns) is recommended [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hepatic Impairment**: Temsirolimus is contraindicated in patients with bilirubin levels >1.5× the Upper Limit of Normal (ULN) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity/Infusion Reactions**: Can include flushing, chest pain, dyspnea, hypotension, and anaphylaxis. Reactions may occur during the first infusion or subsequent doses. Monitor patients throughout the infusion. If a severe reaction occurs, stop the infusion and perform a benefit-risk assessment before restarting at a slower rate [US].
- **Hyperglycemia and Hyperlipidemia**: Significant increases in serum glucose, triglycerides, and cholesterol are likely. Monitor glucose and lipid profiles before and during treatment. Medical management (e.g., insulin, statins) may be required [US].
- **Infections**: Temsirolimus is immunosuppressive. Monitor for opportunistic infections, including *Pneumocystis jiroveci* pneumonia (PJP). Prophylaxis should be considered if concomitant corticosteroids are used [US].
- **Interstitial Lung Disease (ILD)**: Cases, some fatal, have occurred. Monitor for new/worsening respiratory symptoms. Baseline and periodic radiographic assessment (CT or CXR) is recommended. Discontinue if ILD is suspected [US].
- **Bowel Perforation**: Fatal cases have been reported. Evaluate symptoms of "acute abdomen" (fever, abdominal pain, bloody stools) promptly [US].
- **Renal Failure**: Rapidly progressive and sometimes fatal renal failure has occurred. Monitor renal function at baseline and throughout therapy [US].
- **Wound Healing**: Use with caution in the perioperative period due to risks of abnormal wound healing [US].
- **Intracerebral Hemorrhage**: Increased risk in patients with CNS tumors or those receiving anticoagulation [US].
- **Proteinuria/Nephrotic Syndrome**: Monitor urine protein at baseline and periodically. Discontinue if nephrotic syndrome develops [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Very Common (>10%)**: Rash, asthenia, mucositis, nausea, edema, anorexia, anemia, hyperglycemia, hyperlipidemia, hypertriglyceridemia, elevated alkaline phosphatase, elevated creatinine, lymphopenia, hypophosphatemia, thrombocytopenia, elevated AST, leukopenia [US].
- **Common (1–10%)**: Diarrhea, abdominal pain, constipation, vomiting, dyspnea, cough, epistaxis, pruritus, nail disorders, dry skin, dysgeusia, insomnia, pneumonia, hypertension, venous thromboembolism [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects**: Bowel perforation, ILD, fatal infections, renal failure, intracerebral hemorrhage, and angioedema (especially when combined with ACE inhibitors) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Strong CYP3A4 Inhibitors**: (e.g., ketoconazole, clarithromycin, ritonavir, grapefruit juice). These increase sirolimus (the primary metabolite) concentrations. Avoid concomitant use; if unavoidable, reduce temsirolimus dose to 12.5 mg/week [US].
- **Strong CYP3A4 Inducers**: (e.g., rifampin, carbamazepine, St. John’s Wort). These decrease sirolimus concentrations. Avoid concomitant use; if unavoidable, consider increasing temsirolimus dose up to 50 mg/week [US].
- **ACE Inhibitors**: Concomitant use increases the risk of angioedema-type reactions [US].
- **Sunitinib**: Combination therapy resulted in dose-limiting toxicities (Grade 3/4 rash, gout/cellulitis) [US].
- **P-glycoprotein (P-gp)**: Temsirolimus is a substrate of P-gp in vitro. Monitor for increased toxicity when co-administered with P-gp inhibitors [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy**: [US Category: Not specified by letter, but stated as "Can cause fetal harm"]. Animal studies show embryo-fetal toxicity at sub-therapeutic exposures. Females of reproductive potential must use effective contraception during treatment and for 3 months after the final dose [US]. 
- **Males**: Advise males with female partners of reproductive potential to use effective contraception for 3 months after the final dose [US].
- **Breastfeeding**: Do not breastfeed during treatment and for 3 weeks after the last dose [US].
- **Fertility**: Based on animal data, male and female fertility may be compromised [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use**: Effectiveness not established. Studied in patients aged 1–21 years. At a dose of 75 mg/m², systemic exposure was significantly higher (6-fold) than in adults receiving 25 mg [US].
- **Elderly Use**: Patients ≥65 years may be more susceptible to certain adverse effects, including diarrhea, edema, and pneumonia [US].
- **Renal Impairment**: No dosage adjustment is recommended. Temsirolimus has not been studied in patients undergoing hemodialysis [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment**:
    - **Mild** (Bilirubin >1–1.5×ULN or AST >ULN): Reduce dose to 15 mg/week.
    - **Moderate/Severe**: Contraindicated [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action**: Temsirolimus binds to the intracellular protein FKBP-12. This complex inhibits the activity of mTOR (mammalian target of rapamycin), which regulates cell division. Inhibition results in G1 growth arrest and reduced levels of angiogenic factors such as VEGF [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics**:
    - **Metabolism**: Extensively metabolized via CYP3A4. Sirolimus is the primary active metabolite.
    - **Excretion**: Primarily via feces (82%); minimal renal excretion (4.6%).
    - **Half-life**: Temsirolimus (~17.3 hours); Sirolimus (~54.6 hours) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pivotal Study**: A Phase 3, multi-center, randomized, open-label trial compared temsirolimus (25 mg weekly), interferon-alpha (IFN-α), and the combination of both in untreated patients with advanced renal cell carcinoma and poor prognostic factors.
- **Outcomes**: Temsirolimus monotherapy demonstrated a statistically significant improvement in Overall Survival (OS) compared to IFN-α (10.9 months vs. 7.3 months; Hazard Ratio 0.73). The combination arm did not show significant improvement over IFN-α alone [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage**: Store the kit under refrigeration at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F). Protect from light.
- **Stability**: The Step 1 concentrate-diluent mixture is stable for up to 24 hours at temperatures below 25°C. The final Step 2 infusion solution should be administered within 6 hours of preparation [US].
