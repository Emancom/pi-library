# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of **levocarnitine** for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises clinical data into a concise, structured format.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis intended for informational purposes. It does not replace the official PI or local regulatory guidelines required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. At the time of writing, only the United States PI was available for review; information for other regions is noted as "Not specified in available sources."

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP).
- **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule/Amino acid derivative ((R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium).
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter Healthcare Corporation) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Sterile injection, solution: 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation (Manufactured by Baxter Pharmaceuticals India Private Ltd) [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Indicated for both acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:**
    - **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion [US].
    - **Acute Crisis:** A loading dose is often given, followed by an equivalent dose divided over the subsequent 24 hours. Administration should occur every 3 or 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours [US].
    - **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses are typically 50 mg/kg, though higher doses up to 300 mg/kg may be required [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - **Recommended Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight) [US].
    - **Administration:** Administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line after each dialysis session [US].
    - **Dose Adjustment:** Adjustments should be guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Downward titration (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter or discolouration before administration [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable and compatible when mixed in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations of 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL. Mixed solutions in PVC bags are stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) [US].

### Monitoring
- It is recommended to obtain baseline plasma carnitine levels.
- **Target Levels:** Plasma free carnitine concentrations should ideally be maintained between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].
- Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma carnitine levels is recommended [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known** [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal oedema, and bronchospasm, have been reported. These typically occur in ESRD patients on dialysis and may manifest within minutes of administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with a known history, there may be an increase in the frequency or severity of seizures [US].
- **Oral Levocarnitine in Renal Impairment:** The safety/efficacy of *oral* levocarnitine has not been evaluated in renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in ESRD patients may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Clinical Trial Experience (ESRD Patients)
Adverse events reported in double-blind, placebo-controlled trials at a frequency of ≥5% (regardless of causality) include:
- **General:** Injection site reaction (33%), chest pain (12%), flu syndrome (25%), headache (22%), infection (15%), and abdominal pain (9%) [US].
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (20%), hypotension (14%), tachycardia (6%) [US].
- **Digestive:** Diarrhoea (16%), vomiting (15%), nausea (8%) [US].
- **Metabolic:** Hypercalcaemia (9%), hyperkalaemia (6%) [US].
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (23%), cough increase (9%), dyspnoea (7%) [US].
- **Nervous System:** Dizziness (13%), insomnia (4%), paresthesia (5%) [US].

### Postmarketing Experience
- **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea and vomiting; body odour; gastritis [US].
- **Neurologic:** Seizures [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalised Ratio (INR) have been observed when levocarnitine is used concomitantly with warfarin. **Clinical Note:** Monitor INR levels frequently after initiating levocarnitine or after dose adjustments in patients on anticoagulant therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not explicitly assigned in source; however, reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Risks to the infant from excess intake must be weighed against maternal benefits. Consideration may be given to discontinuing nursing or the drug [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing is weight-based (50 mg/kg for metabolic disorders). See Section 3 [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically detailed in the provided source.
- **Renal Impairment:** Specifically indicated for ESRD patients on dialysis. Dose adjustments are based on pre-dialysis trough levels. Oral levocarnitine is not recommended for chronic use in severe renal impairment [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane for beta-oxidation and energy production [US]. 
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In patients with metabolic errors, it promotes the excretion of excess organic acids by forming acylcarnitine esters, which are rapidly cleared [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not protein-bound. Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in faeces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. The mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients, 20 mg/kg doses three times weekly post-dialysis increased mean pre-dialysis trough concentrations from ~20 µmol/L to 190 ± 55 µmol/L by week 8 [US].
- **Dose-Ranging Data:** Trough levels correlate with dosage; 40 mg/kg doses resulted in mean troughs of 456 ± 162 µmol/L after 24 weeks [US].
- **Note:** While levocarnitine increases carnitine concentrations in ESRD patients, the effect on clinical outcomes (e.g., muscle weakness, cardiomyopathy) in this population has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C–30°C [US].
- **Precautions:** Avoid excessive heat. Protect from freezing [US].
- **Handling:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial, as the formulation contains no preservatives [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as Rx-only [US].
- **Other Jurisdictions:** Not specified in available sources.
