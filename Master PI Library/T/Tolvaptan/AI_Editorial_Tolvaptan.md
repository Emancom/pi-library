# Editorial Product Information: Tolvaptan

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the **Australian (TGA)**. 
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview of tolvaptan for healthcare professionals, consolidating regulatory data into a clinically relevant format.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI or local regulatory guidelines required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Tolvaptan (selective vasopressin V2-receptor antagonist).
- **Brand Name:** **JINARC** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** C03XA01.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:**
    - Tablets: 15 mg, 30 mg, 45 mg, 60 mg, and 90 mg [Australian].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Otsuka Australia Pharmaceutical Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Autosomal Dominant Polycystic Kidney Disease (ADPKD):** Indicated to slow the progression of renal insufficiency and cyst development in adults with Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) stages 1 to 3 at the start of treatment, with evidence of rapidly progressing disease [Australian-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Regimen:** Administered twice daily in split doses. Total daily doses range from 60 mg to 120 mg [Australian].
- **Split-Dose Ratios:**
    - 60 mg/day: 45 mg + 15 mg
    - 90 mg/day: 60 mg + 30 mg
    - 120 mg/day: 90 mg + 30 mg

### Titration Schedule
- **Initial Dose:** 60 mg/day (45 mg upon waking; 15 mg eight hours later) [Australian].
- **Titration:** Increase to 90 mg/day, then to a target of 120 mg/day at weekly intervals as tolerated.
- **Objective:** Maintain the highest tolerable dose to consistently block V2 receptors.

### Administration Instructions
- **Morning Dose:** Take at least 30 minutes before the morning meal.
- **Second Dose:** May be taken with or without food.
- **Water Intake:** Patients must be instructed to drink sufficient water to avoid dehydration and replenish fluids overnight during nocturia [Australian].
- **Contraindicated Fluid:** Do not take with grapefruit juice.

### Dose Adjustments for CYP3A Inhibitors [Australian]
- **Strong CYP3A Inhibitors:** Reduce dose to 30 mg (or 15 mg) once daily.
- **Moderate CYP3A Inhibitors:** Reduce split doses (e.g., 90+30 mg becomes 45+15 mg).

## 4. Contraindications
- Hypersensitivity to tolvaptan, benzazepine derivatives, or any excipients (e.g., lactose) [Australian].
- Pre-existing elevated liver enzymes or signs of liver injury meeting permanent discontinuation criteria.
- Volume depletion or anuria [Australian].
- Hypernatremia [Australian].
- Conditions impairing thirst perception or response.
- Pregnancy and Breastfeeding [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warning: Idiosyncratic Hepatic Toxicity [Australian]
- **Risk:** Tolvaptan is associated with idiosyncratic elevations of ALT and AST, which can rarely lead to serious liver injury or acute liver failure requiring transplantation.
- **Mandatory Monitoring:** Liver function tests (ALT, AST, Bilirubin) are required:
    - Prior to initiation.
    - Monthly for the first 18 months.
    - Every 3 months thereafter [Australian].
- **Restricted Access:** In Australia, prescribing is tied to the **IMADJIN Program**, requiring prescriber certification and patient education.

### General Warnings
- **Dehydration:** Tolvaptan induces aquaresis; volume status must be monitored. Therapy must be interrupted if water access is limited.
- **Electrolyte Imbalance:** Serum sodium must be corrected before initiation; electrolytes should be monitored at least every 3 months during long-term therapy [Australian].
- **Urinary Outflow Obstruction:** Patients with partial obstruction (e.g., prostatic hypertrophy) are at increased risk of acute retention.
- **Uric Acid:** Tolvaptan reduces uric acid clearance; monitoring is required, especially in patients with a history of gout.
- **Anaphylaxis:** Rare post-marketing reports of anaphylactic shock; treatment must be discontinued permanently if this occurs.

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Frequency Data
| Frequency | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Thirst (55%), polyuria (38%), nocturia (29%), pollakiuria (23%), headache, fatigue, dry mouth, dizziness, diarrhoea, and nausea. |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Constipation, dyspepsia, abdominal pain, hypernatremia, hyperuricaemia, gout, insomnia, and palpitations. |
| **Uncommon (0.1–1%)** | Hepatic cyst, renal cyst infection. |

### Serious Adverse Effects
- **Hepatotoxicity:** Significant ALT/AST elevations (>3x ULN) were observed in clinical trials (4.4% vs 1.0% placebo) [Australian].
- **Severe Dehydration:** May lead to acute renal dysfunction.

## 7. Drug Interactions
### Pharmacokinetic Interactions
- **CYP3A Inhibitors:** Strong (e.g., ketoconazole) and moderate (e.g., diltiazem) inhibitors significantly increase tolvaptan exposure (up to 440%). Dose reduction is mandatory [Australian].
- **CYP3A Inducers:** Potent inducers (e.g., rifampicin) reduce tolvaptan exposure by ~85%; avoid concomitant use.
- **P-gp Substrates:** Tolvaptan is a P-gp inhibitor; increase monitoring for drugs like digoxin and dabigatran [Australian].
- **Transporter Substrates:** May inhibit OATP1B1/3, OAT3, and BCRP; exercise caution with statins, methotrexate, and metformin.

### Pharmacodynamic Interactions
- **Vasopressin Analogues:** Tolvaptan may attenuate the effect of analogues like desmopressin used for bleeding control; co-administration is not recommended.
- **Diuretics:** Potential for additive dehydration and renal dysfunction; monitor volume status closely.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** **Category D** [Australian]. Tolvaptan is contraindicated. Animal studies showed teratogenicity (cleft palate, skeletal malformations) and embryo-fetal death at maternally toxic doses. Effective contraception is required for women of childbearing potential.
- **Breastfeeding:** Contraindicated [Australian]. Excretion in human milk is unknown, though animal data confirms excretion into milk.
- **Fertility:** Rat studies showed altered oestrus cycles at high doses but no impact on male/female fertility [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy in children/adolescents not established; use is not recommended [Australian].
- **Elderly Use:** No significant PK changes with age; however, efficacy data in patients >55 years is limited.
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment required; however, treatment should be discontinued if renal function progresses to CKD stage 5 [Australian].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** 
    - **Mild/Moderate (Child-Pugh A/B):** No dose adjustment required.
    - **Severe (Child-Pugh C):** Benefits must be carefully weighed; limited information available. Monitoring must be intensified.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Selective vasopressin V2-receptor antagonist. By blocking vasopressin binding in the distal nephron, it reduces intracellular cAMP, which is a key driver of cyst growth and fluid secretion in ADPKD.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Rapid; peak plasma concentration (~2 hours). Bioavailability ~56% [Australian].
    - **Distribution:** Highly protein-bound (>98%).
    - **Metabolism:** Extensively hepatic via CYP3A; metabolites have negligible activity.
    - **Elimination:** Terminal half-life ~8 hours. <1% excreted unchanged in urine.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **TEMPO 3:4 Trial:** A 36-month pivotal trial in 1,445 patients (CKD stages 1-3). Tolvaptan reduced the rate of Total Kidney Volume (TKV) increase by 49.2% compared to placebo and decreased the relative rate of ADPKD-related clinical events by 13.5% [Australian].
- **REPRISE Trial:** A 12-month trial in 1,370 patients (CKD stages 2-4). Tolvaptan demonstrated a 35% reduction in the loss of kidney function (eGFR) compared to placebo [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store below 25 °C [Australian].
- **Conditions:** Protect from light and moisture.
- **Shelf Life:** Refer to the product packaging for the specific expiry date.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Australia:** **Schedule 4 (S4)** – Prescription Only Medicine. Requires enrollment in the IMADJIN risk management program for liver safety.
