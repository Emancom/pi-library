# Editorial Product Information: Tucatinib

## Editorial Note
- Contributing jurisdictions: Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration) and US (Food and Drug Administration).
- Purpose: This document provides a harmonised editorial synthesis of the Product Information for healthcare professionals. 
- Disclaimer: This is an editorial compilation and not a substitute for the official, legally binding Product Information used for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Tucatinib (kinase inhibitor)
- **Brand Name:** **TUKYSA**
- **ATC Code:** L01EH03
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - 50 mg film-coated tablets: Round, yellow, debossed with "TUC" and "50"
    - 150 mg film-coated tablets: Oval, yellow, debossed with "TUC" and "150"
- **Route of Administration:** Oral
- **Sponsor/Manufacturer:** Pfizer Australia Pty Ltd [Australian]; Seagen Inc. [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
- **HER2-Positive Metastatic Breast Cancer:** In combination with trastuzumab and capecitabine for the treatment of adult patients with advanced unresectable or metastatic HER2-positive breast cancer (including those with brain metastases) who have received one or more prior anti-HER2-based regimens in the metastatic setting.
- **HER2-Positive Metastatic Colorectal Cancer:** In combination with trastuzumab for the treatment of adult patients with RAS wild-type HER2-positive unresectable or metastatic colorectal cancer that has progressed following treatment with fluoropyrimidine, oxaliplatin, and irinotecan-based chemotherapy. 
    - *Note:* This indication was approved under the provisional approval pathway [Australian] or accelerated approval [US] based on response rates; continued approval may be contingent upon verification of clinical benefit in confirmatory trials.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing:** 300 mg taken orally twice daily.
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Swallow tablets whole; do not chew, crush, or split.
    - Tablets may be taken with or without food [US] or with/without a meal [Australian].
    - Doses should be taken approximately 12 hours apart at the same time each day.
- **Combination Therapy Dosing:**
    - **Capecitabine:** 1000 mg/m² orally twice daily, taken within 30 minutes after a meal.
    - **Trastuzumab:** Refer to the respective jurisdiction's PI for specific loading and maintenance dose instructions.
- **Dose Modifications for Adverse Reactions:**
    - **First reduction:** 250 mg twice daily.
    - **Second reduction:** 200 mg twice daily.
    - **Third reduction:** 150 mg twice daily.
    - **Permanent discontinuation:** Required if 150 mg twice daily is not tolerated.
- **Missed Dose:** In the event of a missed dose or vomiting, the patient should not take an additional dose but should take the next dose at the regularly scheduled time.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Australian:** Hypersensitivity to tucatinib or any of the excipients.
- **US:** None.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Diarrhea:** Tucatinib can cause severe diarrhea (including Grade 3 or 4) which may lead to dehydration, hypotension, acute kidney injury, and death. Prophylactic anti-diarrheals were not required in clinical trials, but treatment should be initiated at the first sign of loose stools.
- **Hepatotoxicity:** Severe hepatotoxicity has occurred. Monitor ALT, AST, and bilirubin prior to initiation, every 3 weeks during treatment, and as clinically indicated. More frequent monitoring may be required during the first two months.
- **Embryo-Fetal Toxicity:** Based on animal studies, tucatinib can cause fetal harm. Verify pregnancy status in females of reproductive potential prior to starting treatment.
- **Monitoring Requirements:** In addition to liver function tests, monitor for symptoms of diarrhea and clinical signs of hepatotoxicity. [Australian] notes that the incidence and severity of transaminase elevations were higher in Japanese patients.

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common Adverse Reactions ($\ge$20%)
- **Breast Cancer (with trastuzumab/capecitabine):** Diarrhea, palmar-plantar erythrodysaesthesia (PPE), nausea, hepatotoxicity, vomiting, stomatitis, decreased appetite, anaemia, and rash.
- **Colorectal Cancer (with trastuzumab):** Diarrhea, fatigue, rash, nausea, abdominal pain, infusion-related reactions, and pyrexia.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Strong CYP2C8 Inhibitors (e.g., gemfibrozil):** Avoid concomitant use. If unavoidable, reduce tucatinib dose to 100 mg twice daily.
- **Moderate CYP2C8 Inhibitors:** Increase monitoring for tucatinib toxicity.
- **Strong CYP3A / Moderate CYP2C8 Inducers (e.g., rifampin):** Avoid concomitant use, as these may reduce tucatinib efficacy.
- **CYP3A Substrates:** Avoid concomitant use with CYP3A substrates where minimal concentration changes may lead to serious or life-threatening toxicities.
- **P-gp Substrates (e.g., digoxin):** Use with caution and consider dose reduction of the substrate.
- **MATE1/MATE2-K Substrates (e.g., metformin):** Tucatinib increases the plasma exposure of these substrates; monitor for toxicity.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Australian Category D:** Tucatinib is suspected to cause or may be expected to cause an increased incidence of human fetal malformations or irreversible damage.
    - **General Recommendation:** Advise females of reproductive potential to use effective contraception during treatment and for 1 week after the final dose.
- **Breastfeeding:** Due to the potential for serious adverse reactions in the breastfed child, breastfeeding is not recommended during treatment and for 1 week after the last dose.
- **Fertility:** Based on animal studies, tucatinib may impair male and female fertility.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established in patients under 18 years of age.
- **Elderly Use ($\ge$65 years):** No overall differences in safety or effectiveness were observed between these patients and younger patients. No dose adjustment is required.
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    - **Mild to Moderate (CLcr 30–89 mL/min):** No dose adjustment required.
    - **Severe (CLcr <30 mL/min):** Not specified/recommended in combination with capecitabine, as capecitabine is contraindicated in severe renal impairment.
- **Hepatic Impairment:**
    - **Mild (Child-Pugh A) or Moderate (Child-Pugh B):** No dose adjustment required.
    - **Severe (Child-Pugh C):** Reduce dose to 200 mg orally twice daily.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Tucatinib is a potent, reversible, and highly selective tyrosine kinase inhibitor (TKI) of the HER2 protein. It inhibits the phosphorylation of HER2 and HER3, resulting in the inhibition of downstream MAPK and AKT signalling and cellular proliferation.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** $T_{max}$ is approximately 2 hours. A high-fat meal increases AUC by 1.5-fold but is not considered clinically meaningful.
    - **Distribution:** Apparent volume of distribution is approx. 829–995 L. Plasma protein binding is 97.1%.
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily metabolised by CYP2C8 and to a lesser extent by CYP3A.
    - **Elimination:** Effective half-life is approximately 12–16 hours. Excretion is primarily via faeces (86%).

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **HER2CLIMB (Breast Cancer):** A randomised (2:1), double-blind, placebo-controlled trial. Tucatinib + trastuzumab + capecitabine significantly improved Progression-Free Survival (PFS) (7.8 vs 5.6 months; HR 0.54) and Overall Survival (OS) (21.9 vs 17.4 months; HR 0.66) compared to placebo + trastuzumab + capecitabine.
- **MOUNTAINEER (Colorectal Cancer):** A multicenter, open-label trial. In patients receiving tucatinib + trastuzumab, the confirmed Objective Response Rate (ORR) was 38.1% (95% CI: 27.7, 49.3) with a median Duration of Response (DOR) of 12.4 months.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store below 25°C [Australian]. Store at controlled room temperature 20°C to 25°C (excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C) [US].
- **Handling:** Store in the original container to protect from moisture. Do not discard the desiccant.
- **Shelf Life:** Discard any unused tablets 3 months after opening the bottle.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Australian:** Prescription Only Medicine (Schedule 4).
- **US:** Rx-only.
