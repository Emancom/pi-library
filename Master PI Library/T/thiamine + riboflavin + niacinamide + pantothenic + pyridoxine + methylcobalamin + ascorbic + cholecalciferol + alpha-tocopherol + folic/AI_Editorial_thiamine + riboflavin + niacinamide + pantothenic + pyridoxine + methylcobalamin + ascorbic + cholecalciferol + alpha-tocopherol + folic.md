## 3. Approved Indications
- Intended use: to provide substantial amounts of vitamins B5 (pantothenic acid), B6 (pyridoxine), B12 (methylcobalamin), C (ascorbic acid), D3 (cholecalciferol), E (alpha-tocopherol), riboflavin, niacinamide (vitamin B3), thiamine (vitamin B1), and folate to supplement the diet and help prevent the development of nutritional deficiencies.
- Regulatory statement: not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease [US].
- Dispensing/administration: to be used under the supervision of a licensed medical practitioner; dispensed by prescription in the source jurisdiction [US].

## 4. Dosage and Administration
### General adult dosing
- Usual adult dose: 1 capsule orally once daily; may be administered up to twice daily or as prescribed by a licensed medical practitioner.
- Active ingredient strengths per capsule (as provided in source):
  - Ascorbic acid (vitamin C): 75 mg
  - Thiamine mononitrate (vitamin B1): 1.5 mg
  - Riboflavin (vitamin B2): 1.7 mg
  - Niacinamide (vitamin B3): 15 mg
  - Pantothenic acid (vitamin B5): 10 mg
  - Pyridoxine hydrochloride (vitamin B6): 2.5 mg
  - Folic acid (folate): 1 mg
  - Methylcobalamin (vitamin B12): 6 µg
  - Cholecalciferol (vitamin D3): 20 µg
  - Alpha-tocopherol (vitamin E): 10 mg

## 5. Contraindications

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity**: contraindicated in patients with known hypersensitivity to any ingredient.
- **Allergic reactions**: allergic sensitizations have been reported following oral administration of folic acid; patients should be instructed to seek medical attention if signs of an allergic reaction occur.
- **Use with caution**: recommended caution in patients who have medical conditions, who are pregnant, lactating, trying to conceive, are under 18 years of age, or who are taking other medications.
- **Supervision**: product should be administered under the direction and supervision of a licensed medical practitioner [US].
- **Regulatory statement**: the product information includes the statement that the product's claims have not been evaluated by the regulatory authority and that it is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease [US].
- **Storage and safety**: keep out of the reach of children. (Administration/storage details beyond this are not specified in available sources.)
