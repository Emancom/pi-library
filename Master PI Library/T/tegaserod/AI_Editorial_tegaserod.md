# Editorial Product Information: Tegaserod

## Editorial Note
*   **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA).
*   **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of tegaserod, synthesized for healthcare professionals.
*   **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority's approved labeling for definitive guidance.

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredient:** Tegaserod (as tegaserod maleate).
*   **Chemical Class:** Serotonin-4 (5-HT₄) receptor agonist.
*   **Brand Name(s):** **Zelnorm** [US].
*   **ATC Code:** A03AE02.
*   **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 6 mg oral tablets (whitish to yellowish, round, beveled edge, engraved with "ZEL" and "6") [US].
*   **Route of Administration:** Oral.
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Alfasigma USA, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Irritable Bowel Syndrome with Constipation (IBS-C):** Indicated for the treatment of adult women less than 65 years of age [US].
*   **Limitations of Use:** Safety and effectiveness in men with IBS-C have not been established [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
*   **General Adult Dosing (Women <65 years):** 6 mg taken twice daily [US].
*   **Administration Instructions:** Administer orally at least 30 minutes before meals [US].
*   **Treatment Duration:** Discontinue therapy if the patient has not achieved adequate symptom control after 4 to 6 weeks of treatment [US].
*   **Missed Dose:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
Tegaserod is contraindicated in patients with:
*   A history of myocardial infarction, stroke, transient ischemic attack (TIA), or angina [US].
*   A history of ischemic colitis or other forms of intestinal ischemia [US].
*   Severe renal impairment (eGFR <15 mL/min/1.73 m²) or end-stage renal disease [US].
*   Moderate or severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh B or C) [US].
*   A history of bowel obstruction, symptomatic gallbladder disease, suspected sphincter of Oddi dysfunction, or abdominal adhesions [US].
*   Hypersensitivity to tegaserod [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
*   **Cardiovascular Ischemic Events:** Major Adverse Cardiovascular Events (MACE), including stroke, MI, and cardiovascular death, have been reported. Tegaserod should be discontinued immediately if a patient experiences an MI, stroke, TIA, or angina. Cardiovascular risk factors must be assessed prior to initiation [US].
*   **Ischemic Colitis:** Postmarketing reports include ischemic colitis, mesenteric ischemia, and gangrenous bowel. Discontinue use immediately if symptoms such as rectal bleeding, bloody diarrhea, or new/worsening abdominal pain occur [US].
*   **Volume Depletion Associated with Diarrhea:** Severe diarrhea can lead to hypovolemia, hypotension, and syncope. Avoid use in patients currently experiencing frequent diarrhea. If severe diarrhea occurs, the drug should be discontinued [US].
*   **Suicidal Ideation and Behavior:** Clinical trials showed a higher frequency of suicidal ideation and behavior compared to placebo. Patients should be monitored for worsening depression or emergent suicidal thoughts, particularly in the first few months of therapy [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common Adverse Effects
The following reactions occurred in >2% of patients and at a higher rate than placebo in clinical trials:

| Adverse Reaction | Tegaserod 6 mg BID (N=1,184) | Placebo (N=1,159) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Headache** | 14% | 10% |
| **Abdominal Pain** | 11% | 10% |
| **Nausea** | 8% | 7% |
| **Diarrhea** | 8% | 3% |
| **Flatulence** | 6% | 5% |
| **Dyspepsia** | 4% | 3% |
| **Dizziness** | 4% | 3% |

### Serious and Less Common Adverse Effects
*   **Serious:** MACE, Ischemic Colitis, Suicidal Ideation, and severe diarrhea leading to hospitalization for rehydration [US].
*   **Less Common (≤2%):** Anemia, vertigo, rectal hemorrhage, asthenia, increased creatine phosphokinase, increased appetite, arthropathy, tendonitis, and migraine [US].
*   **Postmarketing Experience:** Alopecia, hypersensitivity reactions (including anaphylaxis), sphincter of Oddi spasm, cholecystitis, and elevated transaminases/bilirubin [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **P-glycoprotein (P-gp) Inhibitors:** Co-administration with quinidine (a P-gp inhibitor) increased tegaserod AUC by 71% and Cmax by 63%. Other P-gp inhibitors (e.g., ritonavir, clarithromycin, itraconazole) may similarly increase exposure, though clinical relevance is unclear [US].
*   **Digoxin:** Tegaserod reduced digoxin Cmax and AUC by approximately 15%; however, this is not considered clinically relevant [US].
*   **Omeprazole:** Increased tegaserod exposure (AUC/Cmax) by 15-17%; not considered clinically significant [US].
*   **No Significant Interactions:** No clinically significant effects were observed with theophylline, dextromethorphan, warfarin, or oral contraceptives (ethinyl estradiol/levonorgestrel) [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy:** 
    *   **US Category:** Not specified (current labeling uses Risk Summary format) [US]. 
    *   Available case reports have not identified a drug-associated risk of major birth defects or miscarriage. Animal studies showed decreased pup survival and developmental delays at doses 45–71 times the recommended human dose [US].
*   **Breastfeeding:** Not recommended. While human data are absent, tegaserod is excreted in rat milk with a high milk-to-plasma ratio (33:1). Due to potential serious reactions in infants, including tumorigenicity observed in animal studies, breastfeeding should be avoided during treatment [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established [US].
*   **Elderly Use:** Not indicated for patients aged 65 years and older [US].
*   **Renal Impairment:** 
    *   **Mild to Moderate:** No dosage adjustment required.
    *   **Severe (eGFR <15 mL/min/1.73 m²) or ESRD:** Contraindicated due to significantly increased exposure to the M29 metabolite [US].
*   **Hepatic Impairment:**
    *   **Mild (Child-Pugh A):** No dosage adjustment required.
    *   **Moderate to Severe (Child-Pugh B or C):** Contraindicated [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
*   **Mechanism of Action:** A 5-HT₄ receptor agonist that stimulates the peristaltic reflex and intestinal secretion, while inhibiting visceral sensitivity. It also acts as a 5-HT₂B receptor antagonist [US].
*   **Pharmacokinetics:**
    *   **Absorption:** Absolute bioavailability is ~10% (fasted). Food reduces AUC by 40-65% [US].
    *   **Distribution:** 98% protein bound [US].
    *   **Metabolism:** Hydrolysis in the stomach and direct glucuronidation. The main metabolite is M29 [US].
    *   **Elimination:** ~2/3 excreted in feces (unchanged); 1/3 in urine as metabolites. Terminal half-life is 4.6 to 8.1 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   **Efficacy in Women:** Three multicenter, double-blind trials involved 2,470 women with IBS-C. Tegaserod 6 mg BID showed statistically significant improvement in symptoms (relief of abdominal pain, bloating, and constipation) at Month 1 compared to placebo. Response rates were maintained at Month 3 in two of the three studies [US].
*   **Efficacy in Men:** Two trials involving 288 males found efficacy response rates were similar between tegaserod and placebo [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   **Temperature:** Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
*   **Handling:** Protect from moisture [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
*   **Legal Classification:** Prescription Only (Rx-only) [US].
*   **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources.
