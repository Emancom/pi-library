# Editorial Product Information: Talimogene laherparepvec

## Editorial Note
- **Jurisdictions contributing to this editorial:** United States (FDA).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of talimogene laherparepvec for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises available regulatory data into a single clinical reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. At the time of writing, only the United States (FDA) documentation was available for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Talimogene laherparepvec (a genetically modified oncolytic herpes simplex virus type 1).
- **Brand Name:** **IMLYGIC** [US].
- **ATC Code:** L01XX46.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Sterile frozen suspension for intralesional injection. Provided in 1 mL single-dose vials in two strengths [US]:
    - $10^6$ (1 million) plaque-forming units (PFU) per mL (light green cap).
    - $10^8$ (100 million) plaque-forming units (PFU) per mL (royal blue cap).
- **Route of Administration:** Intralesional injection only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** BioVex LLC, a subsidiary of Amgen Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Melanoma:** Local treatment of unresectable cutaneous, subcutaneous, and nodal lesions in patients with melanoma that has recurred after initial surgery [US].
- **Limitations of Use:** **IMLYGIC** has not been shown to improve overall survival or have a clinical effect on visceral metastases [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### 3.1 General Dosing Schedule [US]
Treatment involves an initial injection followed by a series of subsequent doses:
- **Initial Dose:** $10^6$ (1 million) PFU/mL.
- **Second Dose:** $10^8$ (100 million) PFU/mL, administered 3 weeks after the initial dose.
- **Subsequent Doses:** $10^8$ (100 million) PFU/mL, administered every 2 weeks.
- **Duration:** Treatment should continue for at least 6 months unless there are no injectable lesions remaining or other treatment is required.

### 3.2 Dose Volume and Lesion Prioritization
The total volume injected per visit across all lesions must not exceed **4 mL**.
- **Volume per Lesion:** Determined by the longest dimension of the lesion:
    - $> 5$ cm: up to 4 mL
    - $> 2.5$ cm to 5 cm: up to 2 mL
    - $> 1.5$ cm to 2.5 cm: up to 1 mL
    - $> 0.5$ cm to 1.5 cm: up to 0.5 mL
    - $\le 0.5$ cm: up to 0.1 mL
- **Prioritization:** Inject the largest lesion(s) first. At subsequent visits, prioritize any new lesions that have appeared since the last treatment.

### 3.3 Administration Instructions
- **Preparation:** Thaw vials at room temperature (20°C to 25°C) until liquid (approx. 30–70 minutes). Swirl gently; do not shake.
- **Injection Technique:** Use a single insertion point when possible. Inject along multiple tracks within the lesion to ensure even dispersion. Withdraw the needle slowly to prevent leakage at the insertion site.
- **PPE:** Healthcare professionals must wear protective gowns, safety glasses/face shields, and gloves. Immunocompromised or pregnant HCPs must not prepare or administer the product [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Immunocompromised Patients:** Patients with a history of primary or acquired immunodeficiency states, leukemia, lymphoma, AIDS, or those receiving immunosuppressive therapy [US].
- **Pregnancy:** **IMLYGIC** is contraindicated in pregnant patients [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Accidental Exposure and Transmission:** Accidental needle sticks or splashback can transmit the virus. Avoid direct contact with injected lesions, dressings, or body fluids of treated patients.
- **Herpetic Infection:** **IMLYGIC** is sensitive to acyclovir; however, antiviral agents may decrease the efficacy of the therapy. Patients developing herpetic lesions should follow strict hygiene to prevent transmission.
- **Injection Site Complications:** Necrosis, ulceration, and impaired healing may occur. Caution is advised in patients with risk factors like previous radiation or poorly vascularized areas.
- **Immune-Mediated Events:** Includes risks of glomerulonephritis, vasculitis, pneumonitis, and worsening of pre-existing autoimmune diseases.
- **Plasmacytoma:** Reported in proximity to injection sites in patients with multiple myeloma.
- **Obstructive Airway Disorder:** Use caution when injecting lesions located near major airways.
- **Hepatic Hemorrhage:** **IMLYGIC** is NOT indicated for transcutaneous intrahepatic injection; such use has resulted in fatal hemorrhage in clinical trials [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Most reactions are mild to moderate and resolve within 72 hours.
- **Very Common (>10%):** Fatigue (50.3%), Chills (48.6%), Pyrexia (42.8%), Nausea (35.6%), Influenza-like illness (30.5%), Injection site pain (27.7%), Vomiting (21.2%), Diarrhea (18.8%), Myalgia (17.5%), Arthralgia (17.1%), Headache (18.8%).
- **Common (1–10%):** Constipation, Abdominal pain, Dizziness, Weight decreased, Oropharyngeal pain.
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Cellulitis (most common Grade 3 or higher reaction), deep vein thrombosis, and disseminated herpetic infection (especially in immunocompromised individuals).

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Antiviral Agents:** Acyclovir and other antiherpetic viral agents may interfere with the replication and effectiveness of **IMLYGIC**, as the drug is sensitive to these agents [US].
- **Immunosuppressants:** May increase the risk of disseminated viral infection (see Contraindications).
- **Formal Studies:** No formal drug interaction studies have been conducted [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Not assigned a specific letter category, but contraindicated]. There is potential for the virus to cross the placental barrier. Wild-type HSV-1 is associated with serious fetal/neonatal risks including multi-organ failure and death.
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if **IMLYGIC** is excreted in human milk. A decision should be made to either discontinue nursing or the drug, taking into account the mother's clinical need [US].
- **Contraception:** Women of childbearing potential should use effective contraception during treatment [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** No overall differences in safety or efficacy were observed in patients $\ge 65$ years [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** No specific clinical studies have evaluated the pharmacokinetics in these populations [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** A genetically modified oncolytic HSV-1. It is designed to replicate within tumours and produce the immune-stimulatory protein human GM-CSF. This causes local tumour cell lysis and releases tumour-derived antigens, which, combined with GM-CSF, is intended to trigger a systemic anti-tumour immune response.
- **Pharmacokinetics/Biodistribution:** Viral DNA is detectable in the blood and urine, peaking during the second cycle of treatment. Injected lesions show the highest DNA concentrations. Viral shedding can occur from the injection site and occlusive dressings; however, no infectious virus has been detected in samples after the second treatment cycle [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pivotal Study (OPTiM):** An open-label, randomized trial (n=436) compared **IMLYGIC** to subcutaneously administered GM-CSF.
- **Primary Outcome:** Durable Response Rate (DRR), defined as a complete or partial response maintained for at least 6 months.
- **Results:** DRR was 16.3% for **IMLYGIC** vs 2.1% for GM-CSF ($p < 0.0001$). Median time to response was 4.1 months.
- **Overall Survival (OS):** No statistically significant difference in OS was observed (22.9 months for **IMLYGIC** vs 19.0 months for GM-CSF, $p = 0.116$) [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Frozen Storage:** Store and transport at -90°C to -70°C (-130°F to -94°F).
- **Light Protection:** Store in original carton until use.
- **Post-Thaw Stability [US]:**
    - **$10^6$ PFU/mL:** Stable for 24 hours at 2°C to 8°C or 12 hours up to 25°C.
    - **$10^8$ PFU/mL:** Stable for 1 week at 2°C to 8°C or 24 hours up to 25°C.
- **Refreezing:** Do not refreeze once thawed.
