# Editorial Product Information: Tramazoline Hydrochloride

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial has been synthesized from the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following regulatory authority: **Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical data and regulatory requirements.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and should not be used as a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific Product Information when prescribing or dispensing in a specific region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Tramazoline hydrochloride (an alpha-sympathomimetic).
- **Brand Names:** Spray-Tish [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** R01AA09.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - **Nasal Mist:** 1.18 mg/mL solution (each metered dose delivers 82 micrograms of tramazoline hydrochloride) [Australian].
- **Route of Administration:** Intranasal.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Sanofi Consumer Healthcare [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- Symptomatic relief of nasal congestion associated with:
    - Common cold [Australian].
    - Hayfever [Australian].
    - Rhinitis [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Children $\ge$ 6 years)
- **Standard Dose:** 1 to 2 sprays into each nostril.
- **Frequency:** Up to 4 times daily as required [Australian].
- **Duration of Therapy:** Use should not exceed 3 consecutive days without medical consultation [Australian].

### Administration Instructions
1. Blow the nose thoroughly before application.
2. Prime the pump before initial use (or after infrequent use) by depressing the pump until a fine mist is released.
3. Insert the adapter into the nostril and depress while breathing in gently.
4. Clean the spray adapter and replace the protective cap after each use [Australian].

## 4. Contraindications
- Patients with dry nasal mucosa involving crusting or scabbing (rhinitis sicca) [Australian].
- Post-cranial surgery via the nasal cavity [Australian].
- Patients with narrow-angle glaucoma [Australian].
- Hypersensitivity to tramazoline hydrochloride, benzalkonium chloride, or any listed excipients [Australian].
- Children under six years of age (safety data not established) [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None [Australian].
- **Systemic Absorption Risks:** Use with caution and only under medical advice in patients with:
    - Arterial hypertension or heart disease.
    - Hyperthyroidism.
    - Diabetes mellitus.
    - Prostate hypertrophy.
    - Phaeochromocytoma.
    - Porphyria [Australian].
- **Chronic Use:** Frequent or prolonged use may lead to rebound congestion (marked mucosal swelling upon cessation), chronic inflammation, and eventual atrophy of the nasal mucous membrane [Australian].
- **Ocular Safety:** Avoid contact with eyes to prevent irritation [Australian].
- **Excipient Caution:** Contains benzalkonium chloride, which may cause local irritation of the nasal mucosa [Australian].

## 6. Adverse Effects
*Frequencies not formally defined in source documentation; effects listed by system organ class.*

- **Nervous System:** Headache, somnolence, sedation, dizziness, dysgeusia [Australian].
- **Psychiatric:** Hallucinations, insomnia, restlessness [Australian].
- **Cardiac:** Palpitations, tachycardia, arrhythmias [Australian].
- **Respiratory:** Rhinorrhea, epistaxis, nasal dryness, discomfort, sneezing, nasal oedema [Australian].
- **Gastrointestinal:** Nausea [Australian].
- **Immune System:** Hypersensitivity reactions (e.g., rash, pruritus, skin/mucosal oedema) [Australian].
- **General:** Fatigue, increased blood pressure [Australian].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **MAO Inhibitors and Tricyclic Antidepressants (TCAs):** Concurrent administration may lead to cardiovascular effects, including increased blood pressure. Combined use with TCAs may specifically increase the risk of arrhythmias [Australian].
- **Vasopressors:** Simultaneous use may potentiate hypertensive effects [Australian].
- **Antihypertensives:** Interactions with agents affecting the sympathetic nervous system are complex and may result in various cardiovascular effects [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Safety has not been established. 
    - **First Trimester:** Contraindicated [Australian].
    - **Second and Third Trimesters:** Use only under medical advice if the potential benefit to the mother outweighs the risk to the fetus [Australian].
- **Breastfeeding:** Safety has not been established. It is unknown if tramazoline is excreted in human milk. Use only under medical advice after a benefit-risk assessment [Australian].
- **Fertility:** No data available [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Contraindicated in children under 6 years of age [Australian].
- **Elderly:** No specific data available [Australian].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Tramazoline is an alpha-sympathomimetic that induces local vasoconstriction, thereby reducing swelling of the nasal mucosa and providing decongestion [Australian].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** 
    - **Onset of Action:** Local vasoconstriction usually occurs within 5 minutes.
    - **Duration of Action:** Up to 8 hours [Australian].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Specific data regarding absorption, distribution, metabolism, and excretion were not identified in the source documentation [Australian].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- No clinical trial data provided in the source documentation [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store below 25°C [Australian].
- **Packaging:** 10 mL Amber glass vial with a metered pump and polypropylene nasal adapter [Australian].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison/Schedule Classification:** Schedule 2 (Pharmacy Only Medicine) [Australian].
