# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine Injection

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) provided by the **US (FDA)** through Baxter Healthcare Corporation. 
- Information regarding other jurisdictions (e.g., Australian (TGA), European (EMA), Canadian (Health Canada), Japanese (PMDA)) was not provided in the source documents and is therefore listed as "Not specified in available sources" where appropriate.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. This synthesis is not a substitute for the official PI used for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP).
- **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule for long-chain fatty acid transport; quaternary ammonium compound.
- **Brand Names:** Not specified in available sources [US: Generically marketed as Levocarnitine Injection].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US-approved].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg.
    - **Administration:** Given as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion.
    - **Acute Crisis:** A loading dose is often administered, followed by an equivalent dose over the next 24 hours. Dosing should occur every 3 or 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours.
    - **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses are typically in the range of 50 mg/kg, though higher doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilised based on clinical requirement [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight.
    - **Administration:** Administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line after each dialysis session.
    - **Adjustments:** Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be made as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations [US].

### Monitoring
- It is recommended to obtain plasma carnitine concentrations prior to therapy.
- Weekly and monthly monitoring should include blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine level: 35 to 60 µmol/L) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known** [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm have been reported, particularly in ESRD patients. Some reactions occur within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, the drug must be discontinued and appropriate medical treatment initiated [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with known seizure disorders, an increase in frequency and/or severity of seizures has been observed [US].
- **Oral Levocarnitine Caution in Renal Impairment:** While this document concerns the IV formulation, the source notes that chronic high doses of *oral* levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions observed in double-blind, placebo-controlled trials in ESRD patients (frequency ≥5% regardless of causality) include:

| Body System | Adverse Event (Frequency ≥5%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Body as a Whole** | Abdominal pain (9%), accidental injury (10%), asthenia (9%), chest pain (12%), fever (7%), flu syndrome (25%), headache (22%), infection (15%), injection site reaction (33%), pain (30%) |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension (20%), hypotension (14%), tachycardia (6%) |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea (16%), nausea (8%), vomiting (15%) |
| **Metabolic/Nutritional** | Hypercalcemia (9%), hyperkalemia (6%), hypervolemia (5%) |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness (13%), insomnia (4%), paresthesia (5%) |
| **Respiratory** | Cough increase (9%), dyspnea (7%), pharyngitis (23%), rhinitis (9%) |

- **Serious/Postmarketing Effects:** Seizures, anaphylaxis, and transient nausea/vomiting. Less frequent reactions include body odor and gastritis [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalised Ratio (INR) have occurred when levocarnitine is used concomitantly with warfarin. **Clinical Management:** Monitor INR levels after initiating levocarnitine or following dose adjustments in patients on warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Classification:** [Category not explicitly lettered in provided text]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) revealed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. There are no adequate and well-controlled studies in pregnant women. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows indicate increased carnitine concentrations in milk following administration. Risks of excess carnitine intake to the infant must be weighed against maternal benefits [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility was found in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy have been established for use in the paediatric population for metabolic disorders and ESRD [US]. See section 3 for weight-based dosing.
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** IV levocarnitine is indicated for carnitine deficiency in ESRD patients on dialysis. However, accumulation of metabolites from *oral* formulations is a concern in this population [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into cellular mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine compounds [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin. Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites are trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours. Mean apparent terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients (20 mg/kg three times weekly after dialysis), mean trough levocarnitine concentrations increased from ~20 µmol/L (pre-therapy) to 190 ± 55 µmol/L at 8 weeks.
- **Dose-Ranging Data:** Studies using 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg showed dose-dependent increases in trough carnitine levels over 12 and 24 weeks.
- **Note:** While levocarnitine increases carnitine concentrations in ESRD, the effects on clinical outcomes in this specific population have not been determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat.
- **Stability:** Compatible with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer's (0.5 mg/mL to 8.0 mg/mL) for up to 24 hours at room temperature in PVC bags [US].
- **Shelf Life:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as it contains no preservative [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources [Australian].
