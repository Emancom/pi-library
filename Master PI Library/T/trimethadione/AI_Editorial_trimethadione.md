# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the **United States (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical data and regulatory requirements into a single reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI or local clinical guidelines required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule.
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - **Intravenous Injection:** 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** For the acute and chronic management of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg.
    - Administration: Given as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion [US].
    - Loading Dose: In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose is often administered, followed by equivalent doses every 3 to 4 hours (never less than every 6 hours) over the subsequent 24 hours [US].
    - Maintenance: Subsequent daily doses are typically 50 mg/kg, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilised [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - Recommended starting dose: 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight) [US].
    - Administration: Delivered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].
    - Adjustment: Downward titration (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough (pre-dialysis) plasma carnitine levels [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected for particulate matter and discoloration prior to use [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when diluted in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer's at concentrations between 0.5 mg/mL and 8.0 mg/mL [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### General Warnings
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. These may occur within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, therapy must be discontinued immediately [US].
- **Seizure Risk:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In patients with a known seizure history, an increase in the frequency or severity of seizures may occur [US].

### Monitoring Requirements
- **Plasma Carnitine Levels:** It is recommended to establish a baseline concentration before starting therapy. Regular monitoring (weekly and monthly) should include plasma free carnitine levels (target range: 35 to 60 µmol/L), blood chemistry, and vital signs [US].

### Specific Clinical Precautions
- **Renal Impairment:** The safety and efficacy of **oral** levocarnitine have not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in patients with severely compromised renal function or ESRD may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are categorised by frequency based on clinical trials in ESRD patients on hemodialysis.

- **Very Common (>10%):**
    - **General:** Injection site reaction (27–38%), pain (21–35%), headache (3–37%), chest pain (6–15%), fever (5–12%), abdominal pain (5–21%), flu syndrome (15–29%).
    - **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (18–21%), hypotension (3–19%).
    - **Digestive:** Diarrhea (9–35%), vomiting (9–21%), nausea (5–12%).
    - **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (15–27%), cough increase (10–18%), dyspnea (3–11%), rhinitis (6–11%).
    - **Metabolic:** Hypercalcemia (6–15%), hypervolemia (3–12%).
    - **Nervous:** Dizziness (10–18%), paresthesia (3–12%).

- **Common (1–10%):**
    - **General:** Asthenia, back pain, infection, allergic reaction.
    - **Cardiovascular:** Arrhythmia, atrial fibrillation, palpitations, tachycardia.
    - **Digestive:** Constipation, dyspepsia, anorexia, melena.
    - **Metabolic/Nutritional:** Hyperkalemia, peripheral edema, weight change.
    - **Nervous:** Anxiety, depression, insomnia.
    - **Special Senses/Other:** Amblyopia, eye disorder, taste perversion, pruritus, rash, leg cramps, urinary tract infection [US].

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Anaphylaxis, bronchospasm, and seizures [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after any dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not assigned/Current labeling states use only if clearly needed]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. No adequate, well-controlled studies exist in pregnant women [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk carnitine concentrations after administration. The benefits of maternal therapy should be weighed against potential risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant; discontinuation of either nursing or the drug may be considered [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility was found in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders is established based on body weight (see Section 3).
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically detailed in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Intravenous levocarnitine is indicated for ESRD patients on dialysis. However, safety for chronic high-dose **oral** levocarnitine is not established in this population due to metabolite accumulation [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In patients with inborn errors of metabolism or ESRD, levocarnitine helps clear accumulated acyl-CoA compounds by forming acylcarnitines, which are rapidly excreted.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not protein or albumin-bound [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Following IV bolus (20 mg/kg), approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. The mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours, and the terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Pharmacokinetic Studies:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients receiving 20 mg/kg IV after dialysis showed that trough levels (normally 40–50 µmol/L) increased from a baseline of ~21 µmol/L to ~190 µmol/L after 8 weeks of therapy [US].
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** In a study of ~90 ESRD patients, doses of 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg increased trough carnitine concentrations in a dose-proportional manner over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Precautions:** Avoid excessive heat. Protect from freezing.
- **Handling:** Single-dose vials; discard any unused portion as the formulation contains no preservatives [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources [US].
