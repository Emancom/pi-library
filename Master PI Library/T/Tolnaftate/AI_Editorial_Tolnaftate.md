# Editorial Product Information: Tolnaftate

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information and Drug Facts documentation from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of tolnaftate (1% topical soap) for use by healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing or dispensing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory databases for the most current regional labeling.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Tolnaftate (Chemical Class: Thiocarbamate antifungal)
- **Brand Name(s):** OAKDOLCHE Tea Tree Oil Antifungal Soap [US]
- **ATC Code:** D01AE18
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Topical soap containing Tolnaftate 1% (1 g per 100 g) [US]
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Nanjing Qiaotun Technology Co., Ltd. [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Tinea Pedis (Athlete's foot):** For the treatment/cure of most cases and relief of associated itching, burning, cracking, and scaling [US].
- **Tinea Cruris (Jock itch):** For the treatment/cure of most cases and relief of associated itching, burning, cracking, and scaling [US].
- **Tinea Corporis (Ringworm):** For the treatment/cure of most cases and relief of associated itching, burning, cracking, and scaling [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Children ≥ 2 years)
- **Application:** Wet the affected area and lather with the medicated soap. Wash the area and dry thoroughly [US].
- **Frequency:** Apply twice daily (morning and night) or as directed by a physician [US].
- **Duration of Treatment:** 
    - **Athlete's foot and Ringworm:** Use daily for 4 weeks [US].
    - **Jock itch:** Use daily for 2 weeks [US].

### Administration Instructions
- For athlete's foot, healthcare providers should advise patients to pay special attention to the spaces between the toes [US].
- Patients should be advised to wear well-fitting, ventilated shoes and to change shoes and socks at least once daily to prevent reinfection [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Pediatrics:** Not for use in children under 2 years of age unless specifically directed by a physician [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to tolnaftate or any of the inactive soap components (e.g., tea tree oil, shea butter, botanicals) [Editorial Note: Standard clinical precaution].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Route Restriction:** For external use only. Not for ophthalmic, oral, or intravaginal use [US].
- **Ocular Exposure:** Avoid contact with the eyes [US].
- **Treatment Cessation:** Patients should stop use and consult a healthcare professional if:
    - Local irritation occurs [US].
    - No clinical improvement is seen within 4 weeks (for athlete's foot or ringworm) or 2 weeks (for jock itch) [US].
- **Accidental Ingestion:** Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, medical assistance or contact with a Poison Control Center is required immediately [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Dermatologic:** Local irritation may occur (frequency not specified) [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Not specified in available sources. Systemic toxicity is generally low due to minimal percutaneous absorption.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources. Topical tolnaftate is not known to have significant systemic drug-drug interactions due to limited systemic bioavailability.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Not specified in available sources. 
- **Breastfeeding:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Fertility:** Not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Approved for use in children aged 2 years and older. For children under 2 years of age, consult a doctor [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Dose adjustment is not typically required for topical antifungal therapy due to minimal systemic absorption.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Dose adjustment is not typically required.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Tolnaftate is a fungicidal agent that inhibits the enzyme squalene epoxidase, a key enzyme in the biosynthesis of ergosterol. This leads to a deficiency in ergosterol, which is essential for fungal cell membrane integrity, and an accumulation of squalene, which is toxic to the fungal cell.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Systemic absorption following topical application is negligible.
    - **Distribution/Metabolism/Elimination:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Exhibits activity against various dermatophytes, including *Trichophyton*, *Microsporum*, and *Epidermophyton* species.
