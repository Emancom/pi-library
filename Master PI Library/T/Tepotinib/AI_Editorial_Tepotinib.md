# Editorial Product Information: Tepotinib

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) documents from the following jurisdiction: **Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration - TGA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, consolidating clinical and regulatory data into a structured reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI or clinical guidelines required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. At the time of this publication, only the Australian PI was available for review; therefore, regional variations for other jurisdictions (e.g., US, Europe, Canada) are not specified.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Tepotinib (present as tepotinib hydrochloride monohydrate).
- **Chemical Class:** Small molecule, Type I adenosine triphosphate (ATP)-competitive inhibitor of the mesenchymal-epithelial transition (MET) receptor.
- **Brand Name(s):** TEPMETKO [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** L01EX21.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 225 mg film-coated tablets (equivalent to 250 mg tepotinib hydrochloride monohydrate) [Australian].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Merck Healthcare Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **NSCLC with METex14 skipping:** Indicated for the treatment of adult patients with locally advanced or metastatic non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) harbouring mesenchymal-epithelial transition (MET) exon 14 skipping alterations [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing:** The recommended adult dose is 450 mg (two 225 mg tablets) taken orally once daily [Australian].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Tablets must be taken with food and swallowed whole.
    - Tablets should not be broken, crushed, or chewed.
    - **Swallowing Difficulties:** Tablets may be dispersed in 30 mL of non-carbonated water. Stir until small pieces are formed (will not fully dissolve) and swallow within 1 hour. Rinse the glass with another 30 mL of water and drink immediately to ensure the full dose is received [Australian].
- **Testing Requirements:** METex14 skipping status must be confirmed using a validated and sensitive test (tumour or plasma specimens) prior to treatment initiation [Australian].
- **Missed Dose:** If a dose is missed, it should be taken as soon as remembered on the same day, unless the next scheduled dose is due within 8 hours [Australian].
- **Dose Adjustments for Adverse Reactions:**
    - **First Reduction:** 225 mg once daily.
    - **Interstitial Lung Disease (ILD):** Withhold if suspected; permanently discontinue if confirmed.
    - **Hepatotoxicity (Grade 3):** Withhold until recovery to baseline. Resume at the same dose if recovery occurs within 7 days; otherwise, resume at a reduced dose (225 mg).
    - **Hepatotoxicity (Grade 4 or ALT/AST >3x ULN with Bilirubin >2x ULN):** Permanently discontinue.
    - **Other Grade 3+ Reactions:** Reduce to 225 mg until recovery to ≤ Grade 2. Interruption for up to 21 days is permissible [Australian].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to tepotinib or any excipients in the formulation [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None specified in available sources.
- **Interstitial Lung Disease (ILD):** ILD or ILD-like reactions (including fatal cases) have been reported. Patients must be monitored for new or worsening pulmonary symptoms (e.g., dyspnoea, cough, fever). Immediate withholding and investigation are required for suspected cases [Australian].
- **Hepatotoxicity:** Increases in ALT, AST, and bilirubin have been observed.
    - **Monitoring:** Liver function tests (LFTs) should be performed prior to initiation, every 2 weeks for the first 3 months of treatment, and then monthly or as clinically indicated [Australian].
- **Embryo-Fetal Toxicity:** Based on its mechanism of action and animal data, tepotinib can cause fetal harm. Teratogenicity (limb and skeletal malformations) was observed in animal studies at exposures significantly lower than human clinical exposure [Australian].
- **Laboratory Assessment (Creatinine):** Tepotinib inhibits renal transporters (OCT2, MATE2K), which can increase serum creatinine without affecting actual glomerular filtration. Use caution when interpreting creatinine-based renal function estimates [Australian].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:**
    - **P-gp Substrates:** Tepotinib inhibits P-gp. Co-administration with dabigatran etexilate increased AUC by ~50%. Monitor drugs with a narrow therapeutic index (e.g., digoxin) [Australian].
    - **BCRP Substrates:** Tepotinib inhibits BCRP in vitro; monitoring of BCRP substrates is recommended [Australian].
    - **OCT2/MATE2K Substrates:** Tepotinib may increase the AUC of metformin through renal transporter inhibition; monitoring is recommended [Australian].
    - **CYP3A4/P-gp Inducers/Inhibitors:** Strong inducers (e.g., carbamazepine) or dual inhibitors (e.g., itraconazole) do not have a clinically relevant effect on tepotinib exposure [Australian].
    - **Acid-Reducing Agents:** Co-administration with omeprazole under fed conditions does not markedly affect tepotinib pharmacokinetics [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Australian Category D:** Tepotinib is contraindicated during pregnancy. 
    - **Precautions:** Pregnancy testing is recommended before starting treatment.
    - **Contraception:** Females of childbearing potential must use effective contraception during and for 1 week after the final dose. Males with female partners of childbearing potential must use barrier contraception for the same duration [Australian].
- **Breastfeeding:** There are no data on the presence of tepotinib in human milk. Breastfeeding should be discontinued during treatment [Australian].
- **Fertility:** No human data available. Animal studies showed no morphological changes in reproductive organs, though reduced secretion in seminal vesicles was noted in rats [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy in patients <18 years of age have not been established [Australian].
- **Elderly Use:** No dose adjustment is required for patients ≥65 years. In clinical trials, no clinically important differences in safety or efficacy were observed between older and younger patients [Australian].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment is required for mild to moderate impairment (CLcr 30–89 mL/min). Data are unavailable for severe renal impairment (CLcr <30 mL/min) [Australian].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment is required for mild (Child-Pugh A) or moderate (Child-Pugh B) impairment. Data are unavailable for severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh C) [Australian].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Tepotinib is a potent, highly selective MET inhibitor. It blocks MET phosphorylation and downstream signalling pathways (PI3K/AKT and MAPK/ERK) that promote tumour cell proliferation, survival, and metastasis [Australian].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Absolute bioavailability is 71.6% (fed). Median Tmax is 8 hours. Food increases AUC by 1.6-fold [Australian].
    - **Distribution:** 98% protein bound. Volume of distribution is 574 L [Australian].
    - **Metabolism:** Minimal; no single pathway accounts for >25% of elimination [Australian].
    - **Elimination:** Primarily faecal (85%); renal (7%). Half-life is approximately 32 hours [Australian].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **VISION Study:** A single-arm, open-label, multicentre trial (N=313) in adult patients with advanced NSCLC harbouring METex14 skipping alterations.
    - **Efficacy Outcomes (IRC Assessment):**
        - **Overall Objective Response Rate (ORR):** 51.4% (95% CI: 45.8, 57.1).
        - **Treatment-naïve ORR:** 57.3%.
        - **Previously treated ORR:** 45.0%.
        - **Median Duration of Response (DOR):** 18.0 months.
        - **Median Progression-Free Survival (PFS):** 11.2 months.
        - **Median Overall Survival (OS):** 19.6 months [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store below 30°C [Australian].
- **Conditions:** Keep in the original package to protect from moisture [Australian].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule:** Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine) [Australian].
