# Editorial Product Information: Lifitegrast

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial has been synthesized from the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following regulatory authority: **US (Food and Drug Administration)**.
- **Purpose:** This document is a harmonised editorial overview designed for use by healthcare professionals to provide a clear, clinically accurate synthesis of lifitegrast pharmacology and administration.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific PI. Prescribers must consult the current official PI approved in their specific region before initiating treatment.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Lifitegrast (a lymphocyte function-associated antigen-1 [LFA-1] antagonist).
- **Brand Name:** **Xiidra** [US].
- **ATC Code:** S01XA25.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Ophthalmic solution 5% (50 mg/mL).
- **Route of Administration:** Topical ophthalmic use.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Novartis Pharmaceuticals Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Dry Eye Disease (DED):** Indicated for the treatment of both the signs and symptoms of dry eye disease [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** Instill one drop in each eye twice daily (approximately 12 hours apart) [US].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Use a single-use container for both eyes immediately after opening.
    - Discard the single-use container and any remaining solution immediately following administration.
    - **Contact Lenses:** Remove contact lenses prior to administration. Lenses may be reinserted 15 minutes after the dose is applied [US].
- **Missed Dose:** If a drop misses the eye, the dose should be repeated [US].
- **Titration:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to lifitegrast or any of the inactive ingredients in the formulation [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None [US].
- **General Warnings:**
    - **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Rare but serious cases of hypersensitivity, including anaphylaxis, have been reported in postmarketing use. If a serious reaction occurs, discontinue the medication and initiate appropriate treatment [US].
    - **Contamination Prevention:** To avoid eye injury or contamination of the solution, patients should be advised not to touch the tip of the single-use container to the eye or any other surface [US].
- **Monitoring:** Clinical response should be monitored via assessment of DED signs (e.g., corneal staining) and symptoms (e.g., dryness scores).

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions were identified during clinical trials (five studies involving 1,401 patients) and post-approval use.

| Frequency | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Instillation-site irritation, dysgeusia (unusual taste sensation), reduced visual acuity [US]. |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Blurred vision, conjunctival hyperemia (eye redness), eye irritation, headache, increased lacrimation, eye discharge, eye discomfort, eye pruritus, sinusitis [US]. |
| **Rare (<0.1%) / Postmarketing** | Anaphylactic reaction, bronchospasm, respiratory distress, pharyngeal edema, swollen tongue, urticaria, allergic conjunctivitis, dyspnea, angioedema, allergic dermatitis, eye swelling, and rash [US]. |

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** No specific drug interaction studies have been reported in the available source [US]. 
- **Note:** Due to the low systemic exposure of lifitegrast following topical ocular administration (trough levels <0.5 ng/mL in 81% of patients), systemic interactions are considered unlikely [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not explicitly letter-coded in the source, but no human data is available to inform drug-associated risks. 
    - **Animal Data:** Intravenous administration in rats showed no teratogenicity at 460 times the Recommended Human Ophthalmic Dose (RHOD). In rabbits, an increased incidence of omphalocele was observed at 400 times the RHOD. The clinical relevance to humans is unclear given the very low systemic absorption of topical lifitegrast [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** There are no data regarding the presence of lifitegrast in human milk or its effects on the breastfed infant. The developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding should be weighed against the mother’s clinical need and potential risks [US].
- **Fertility:** No effects on fertility or reproductive performance were observed in rat studies at intravenous doses up to 5,400 times the RHOD [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy in patients below the age of 17 years have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** No overall differences in safety or effectiveness have been observed between geriatric and younger adult patients [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Lifitegrast is an LFA-1 antagonist. It binds to the integrin LFA-1 on the surface of leukocytes, blocking its interaction with intercellular adhesion molecule-1 (ICAM-1). This prevents the formation of the immunological synapse, thereby inhibiting T-cell activation and migration into corneal and conjunctival tissues, which are often overexpressed in DED [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Systemic exposure is minimal. In a subset of 47 patients, 19% had trough plasma concentrations above the limit of quantitation (0.5 ng/mL), ranging from 0.55 to 3.74 ng/mL [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In vitro, lifitegrast inhibits T-cell adhesion to ICAM-1 and the secretion of inflammatory cytokines in human peripheral blood mononuclear cells [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
The efficacy of lifitegrast 5% was evaluated in four 12-week, randomized, double-masked, vehicle-controlled studies (N=1,181).
- **Primary Outcomes:**
    - **Symptoms:** Assessed via Eye Dryness Score (EDS). Lifitegrast showed a significantly larger reduction in EDS compared to vehicle at Day 42 and Day 84 across all studies [US].
    - **Signs:** Assessed via Inferior Fluorescein Corneal Staining Score (ICSS). A larger reduction in ICSS favoring lifitegrast was observed in three of the four studies at Day 84 [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Single-use containers should be stored in the original foil pouch to protect them from light and excessive moisture. Do not open the pouch until ready to use [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:**
    - **US:** Prescription Only (Rx Only).
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources for other jurisdictions.
