# Editorial Product Information: Levocetirizine

## Editorial Note

- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals by synthesising available regulatory data into a clinically relevant format.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing or dispensing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory documents for definitive guidance.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocetirizine dihydrochloride (Chemical class: H1-receptor antagonist; antihistamine).
- **Brand Names:** GoodSense Levocetirizine [US]; Xyzal Allergy 24HR (Reference product) [US].
- **ATC Code:** R06AE09 (Not explicitly stated in source, but standard for this class).
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Film-coated tablet, 5 mg [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** L. Perrigo Company [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Respiratory Allergies:** Temporary relief of symptoms due to hay fever or other upper respiratory allergies, including:
    - Runny nose
    - Sneezing
    - Itchy, watery eyes
    - Itching of the nose or throat [US]

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Children 12–64 years)
- **Standard Dose:** 5 mg (1 tablet) once daily in the evening.
- **Dose Adjustment:** 2.5 mg (1/2 tablet) once daily in the evening may be appropriate for patients with less severe symptoms.
- **Maximum Dose:** Do not exceed 5 mg (1 tablet) in a 24-hour period [US].

### Paediatric Dosing (Children 6–11 years)
- **Standard Dose:** 2.5 mg (1/2 tablet) once daily in the evening.
- **Maximum Dose:** Do not exceed 2.5 mg (1/2 tablet) in a 24-hour period [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Tablets should be taken in the evening [US].
- Tablets are scored to facilitate 2.5 mg dosing [US].

### Missed Dose Instructions
- Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Renal Impairment:** Contraindicated in patients with kidney disease [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to levocetirizine, any of its inactive ingredients, or any antihistamine containing cetirizine [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **CNS Depression:** Drowsiness may occur. Use caution when driving a motor vehicle or operating machinery [US].
- **Urinary Retention:** Use with caution in patients with a history of trouble urinating or emptying the bladder. Treatment should be discontinued if these symptoms occur [US].
- **Concomitant Substances:** Avoid consumption of alcoholic drinks. Concurrent use with alcohol, sedatives, or tranquilizers may increase the risk of drowsiness [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Available source documentation for the OTC product does not provide frequency-specific data (e.g., percentages).
- **Common Effects:** Drowsiness [US].
- **Serious Effects:** Allergic reactions (incidence not specified); patients are advised to seek immediate medical help if an allergic reaction occurs [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Alcohol:** Potentiates CNS depression and increases drowsiness [US].
- **Sedatives and Tranquilizers:** Additive effect on CNS depression, increasing the risk of drowsiness [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Healthcare professionals should be consulted before use. [US: Pregnancy category not specified in provided OTC label].
- **Breastfeeding:** Use is not recommended during breastfeeding [US].
- **Fertility:** Not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:**
    - Approved for children aged 6–11 years at reduced dosing.
    - **Contraindicated** in children under 6 years of age [US].
- **Elderly Use (65 years and older):** Patients should consult a physician before use [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** **Contraindicated** for use in consumers with kidney disease [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Antihistamine [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Provides 24-hour relief of allergy symptoms; clinically proven relief within 60 minutes [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- The provided source notes that the product provides clinically proven relief in 60 minutes and 24-hour relief for indoor and outdoor allergies. Specific trial designs and primary outcomes are not detailed in the available OTC documentation [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store between 20°C and 25°C (68°F and 77°F) [US].
- **Integrity:** Do not use if the printed foil under the cap is broken or missing [US].
