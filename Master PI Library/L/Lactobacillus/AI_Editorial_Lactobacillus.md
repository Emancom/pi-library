# Editorial Product Information: Lactobacillus acidophilus (AcidoBalance)

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial synthesis is derived from the official product documentation provided for the United States (FDA-regulated dietary supplement/prescription probiotic formulation).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating regulatory and clinical data into a concise reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information (PI) or prescribing information required for clinical practice in any specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** *Lactobacillus acidophilus* (UNII: 1PRR1V42V5).
- **Chemical Class:** Probiotic micro-organism.
- **Brand Name(s):** **AcidoBalance** [US].
- **ATC Code:** A07FA01 (Lactic acid producing organisms).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - **Oral Tablet:** 0.5 mg per tablet. A serving size of 4 tablets provides 400 million Colony Forming Units (CFU) in a 2 mg proprietary blend [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Blue Heron Pharmaceuticals, LLC [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- Clinical dietary management of suboptimal nutritional status in patients requiring advanced supplementation [US].
- Nutritional supplementation in physiologically stressful conditions for the maintenance of good health [US].
- Improvement of digestive health [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** The usual dosage is 4 tablets administered once or twice daily [US].
- **Administration Instructions:** 
    - Should be administered under the supervision of a licensed medical practitioner [US].
    - Dosage may be adjusted based on the specific recommendations of a healthcare professional [US].
- **Titration Schedules:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Missed Dose Instructions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to any of the ingredients, including **milk products** [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None [US].
- **General Warnings and Precautions:**
    - **Medical Supervision:** This product is intended for use only under the direction and supervision of a licensed medical practitioner [US].
    - **Underlying Conditions:** Use with caution in patients with pre-existing medical conditions [US].
    - **Age Restrictions:** Use with caution in individuals under the age of 18 [US].
- **Monitoring Requirements:** Patients should consult a physician immediately if adverse side effects occur [US].
- **Specific Clinical Precautions:** Keep out of reach of children [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Allergic Reactions:** Allergic sensitizations have been reported [US].
- **Specific Note:** The source document notes reports of allergic sensitizations following oral administration of folic acid (though folic acid is not explicitly listed in the primary ingredient blend for this specific formulation) [US].
- **Frequency Data:** Specific frequency data (e.g., common vs. rare) are not specified in the available source.
