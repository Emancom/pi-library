# Editorial Product Information: Lifileucel

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on Product Information (PI) documents from the Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA, Australia) and the Food and Drug Administration (FDA, United States).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, consolidating clinical, regulatory, and safety data.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official PI or local regulatory guidance required for prescribing and administration in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Lifileucel
- **Chemical Class:** Autologous T cell immunotherapy (Tumour Infiltrating Lymphocytes, TIL)
- **Brand Name:** **AMTAGVI** [TGA, FDA]
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** 
    - **Dispersion/Suspension for Infusion:** A patient-specific single dose consisting of 1 to 4 infusion bags.
    - **Cell Concentration:** 
        - [TGA]: 1.8 to 100 $\times$ 10⁹ viable cells.
        - [FDA]: 7.5 to 72 $\times$ 10⁹ viable cells.
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** 
    - [TGA]: Iovance Australia Pty Ltd.
    - [FDA]: Iovance Biotherapeutics, Inc.

## 2. Approved Indications
**AMTAGVI** is indicated for the treatment of adult patients with unresectable or metastatic melanoma who have previously been treated with a PD-1 blocking antibody, and if BRAF V600 mutation positive, a BRAF inhibitor with or without a MEK inhibitor.

- [FDA]: This indication is approved under accelerated approval based on objective response rate (ORR) and duration of response (DOR). Continued approval may be contingent upon verification of clinical benefit in confirmatory trials.
- [TGA]: A survival benefit has not yet been demonstrated.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults $\ge$ 18 years)
Treatment consists of a single IV infusion. **AMTAGVI** is for autologous use only. The regimen involves three distinct phases:
1.  **Lymphodepletion (Preparative Regimen):** Cyclophosphamide 60 mg/kg IV with mesna daily for 2 days, followed by fludarabine 25 mg/m² IV daily for 5 days.
2.  **AMTAGVI Infusion:** Administered 24 hours to 4 days after the final dose of fludarabine.
3.  **IL-2 Support (Aldesleukin):** Beginning 3 to 24 hours after **AMTAGVI** infusion, administer aldesleukin 600,000 IU/kg IV every 8 to 12 hours for up to 6 doses.

### Administration Instructions
- **Setting:** Must be administered in an authorised treatment centre [TGA]. [FDA] specifies an inpatient hospital setting with an intensive care facility and specialists skilled in cardiopulmonary or intensive care medicine available.
- **Premedication:** Administer paracetamol (acetaminophen) and diphenhydramine (or other H1-antihistamine) 30 to 60 minutes before infusion to reduce allergic risk.
- **Avoid Prophylactic Steroids:** Systemic corticosteroids may interfere with the activity of **AMTAGVI**.
- **Thawing:** Thaw one bag at a time at $37^\circ\text{C}$ ($\pm 2^\circ\text{C}$). Infusion must begin immediately and complete within 3 hours of thawing.
- **Filtration:** Use a Y-type blood filter with a 150-260 micron pore size. **Do NOT use a leukocyte-depleting filter.**
- **Infusion Rate:** Approximately 1 mL/minute for the first 5 minutes, then increase to 5–10 mL/minute.

## 4. Contraindications
- [FDA]: None.
- [TGA]: Hypersensitivity to the active substance or excipients (including dimethyl sulfoxide (DMSO), and potential traces of gentamicin and streptomycin).
- **Editorial Note:** Clinicians must also consider contraindications for the lymphodepleting agents (cyclophosphamide, fludarabine) and aldesleukin.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warnings [TGA, FDA]
**AMTAGVI** is associated with significant risks including:
- **Treatment-Related Mortality:** A rate of 7.5% was observed in clinical trials, with deaths occurring during lymphodepletion and up to 150 days post-infusion.
- **Prolonged Severe Cytopenia:** Grade 3 or higher cytopenias may persist for weeks.
- **Internal Organ Haemorrhage:** Fatal intra-abdominal and intracranial haemorrhages have occurred.
- **Severe Infection:** Sepsis and other life-threatening infections have been reported.
- **Cardiopulmonary and Renal Impairment:** Severe cardiac dysfunction, arrhythmia, respiratory failure, and acute kidney injury (AKI) have occurred.

### General Warnings and Precautions
- **Cytokine Release Syndrome (CRS):** Median onset is 3 days. Monitor for fever, hypotension, and hypoxia.
- **Capillary Leak Syndrome (CLS):** Associated with aldesleukin; monitor for hypotension, oedema, and decreased urine output.
- **Neurological Toxicity:** Including ICANS and encephalopathy (median onset 10.5 days). Monitor for mental status changes or seizures. [TGA] notes median onset range 4 to 125 days.
- **Uveitis:** [TGA] highlights potential for severe uveitis; median onset 24 days. Rule out active uveitis before treatment.
- **Hypersensitivity:** Reactions may occur due to DMSO or residual antibiotics (gentamicin, streptomycin).

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions reflect the combined regimen of lymphodepletion, **AMTAGVI**, and aldesleukin.

| Frequency | Adverse Reaction (MedDRA) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Thrombocytopenia (88%), Leukopenia (85%), Anaemia (83%), Chills (77%), Nausea (74%), Electrolyte imbalance (71%), Pyrexia (69%), Fatigue (62%), Arrhythmia (58%), Oedema (58%), Diarrhoea (54%), Rash (51%), Febrile neutropenia (47%), Dyspnoea (44%), Hypotension (42%), Musculoskeletal pain (40%), Encephalopathy (23%), Acute Kidney Injury (24%). |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Sepsis (7%), Visual impairment (9%), Uveitis (5%), Capillary Leak Syndrome (13.5% [TGA], 4.5% [FDA]), Infusion-related reactions (6%), Cytokine Release Syndrome (3%). |

**Serious Adverse Effects:** Infections (unspecified pathogen), febrile neutropenia, haemorrhage, AKI, and arrhythmia.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Live Vaccines:** Safety not established. Avoid vaccination 28 days prior to lymphodepletion, during treatment, and for 3 months after.
- **Corticosteroids:** Avoid systemic use 21 days before and 60 days after infusion, except for life-threatening conditions.
- **General Note:** No formal interaction studies have been performed. Consider interactions related to cyclophosphamide, fludarabine, and aldesleukin.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - [TGA]: Category C.
    - [FDA]: No available data.
    - **Recommendation:** Not recommended during pregnancy. Verify pregnancy status in females of reproductive potential before treatment. Advise use of effective contraception for 12 months post-treatment.
- **Breastfeeding:** Presence in human milk is unknown. Breastfeeding should be discontinued during treatment due to the potential for serious adverse reactions in infants from the total regimen.
- **Fertility:** No data available.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy have not been established in patients $\le$ 17 years.
- **Elderly Use ($\ge$ 65 years):** No overall differences in safety or effectiveness were observed between elderly and younger patients. No dose adjustment is required.
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not studied. The regimen is generally not expected to be used in significant impairment due to the toxicity profiles of fludarabine and aldesleukin.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Billions of autologous TILs are infused, migrate to the tumour site, and recognise patient-specific neoantigens via T-cell receptor (TCR) engagement with HLA. This induces T-cell activation and tumour cell lysis.
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Conventional studies are not possible. Persistence is tracked via unique TCR clonotypes in peripheral blood. TCR clonotypes peaked at Day 4 (83%) and typically remained between 37% and 41% at 12 months.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Peak levels of IL-15, CXCL10, and IFN-$\gamma$ occur between Days 1–4 post-infusion.

## 11. Clinical Trials
**Study C-144-01:** A global, multicentre, single-arm trial in 156 patients (pooled safety set).
- **Primary Efficacy Cohort (n=87) [TGA]:** ORR was 28.7% (95% CI: 19.5, 39.4). Median DOR was 10.4 months.
- **FDA Efficacy Set (n=73):** Focused on those receiving the recommended dose range (7.5 to 72 $\times$ 10⁹ cells). ORR was 31.5% (95% CI: 21.1, 43.4). Median DOR was not reached.
- **Pooled Analysis (n=153):** ORR was 31.4% with a median time to response of 1.4–1.5 months.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Frozen Storage:** Store and transport in the vapour phase of liquid nitrogen ($\le -150^\circ\text{C}$).
- **Shelf Life:** [TGA]: 1 year at $\le -150^\circ\text{C}$.
- **Post-Thaw:** Must be used within 3 hours if kept at room temperature ($18^\circ\text{C}$ to $25^\circ\text{C}$). Do not refreeze or refrigerate.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Australia [TGA]:** Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine).
- **United States [FDA]:** Rx-only.
