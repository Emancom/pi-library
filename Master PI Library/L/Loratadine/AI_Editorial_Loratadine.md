# Editorial Product Information: Loratadine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided for **Loratadine**, specifically focusing on the documentation from the **United States (FDA)** for over-the-counter (OTC) pediatric formulations. 
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, consolidating clinical guidance into a single reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official PI or local regulatory guidance required for prescribing or dispensing in a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** **Loratadine** (5 mg per 5 mL).
- **Chemical Class:** Antihistamine (Second-generation H1-receptor antagonist).
- **Brand Names:** Children's Loratadine, Children's Claritin (reference brand) [US].
- **ATC Code:** R06AX13 (Not explicitly stated in source, but standard for the active moiety).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Oral solution (syrup), 5 mg/5 mL [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Sun Pharmaceutical Industries, Inc.; Distributed by Taro Pharmaceuticals U.S.A., Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
**Loratadine** is indicated for the temporary relief of symptoms associated with hay fever or other upper respiratory allergies, including:
- Runny nose [US].
- Itchy, watery eyes [US].
- Sneezing [US].
- Itching of the nose or throat [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
Dosage is administered once every 24 hours. The provided dosing cup should be used for accurate measurement [US].

### Dosing by Age Group
- **Adults and Children 6 years and over:** 10 mg (2 teaspoonfuls) daily; do not exceed 10 mg in 24 hours [US].
- **Children 2 to under 6 years of age:** 5 mg (1 teaspoonful) daily; do not exceed 5 mg in 24 hours [US].
- **Children under 2 years of age:** Consult a physician for appropriate dosing [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Use only with the enclosed dosing cup to ensure accurate delivery of the dose [US].
- May be taken without regard to food [Not explicitly specified, though "24 hour" relief is noted].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known allergic reaction to **loratadine** or any of the inactive ingredients in the formulation (e.g., sodium metabisulfite) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None specified in provided source.
- **Drowsiness:** While labelled as non-drowsy when taken as directed, taking more than the recommended dose may cause somnolence [US].
- **Liver and Kidney Disease:** Patients with pre-existing hepatic or renal impairment should consult a healthcare professional prior to use, as a dose adjustment may be required [US].
- **Allergic Reactions:** If an allergic reaction occurs, patients should discontinue use and seek immediate medical attention [US].
- **Excipients:** This product contains sodium metabisulfite, which may cause allergic-type reactions in sensitive individuals [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Frequencies are not specifically quantified in the provided OTC labeling. Based on the source:
- **Common/General:** Drowsiness (specifically if the recommended dose is exceeded) [US].
- **Serious:** Hypersensitivity/allergic reactions [US].
- **Other:** Not specified in available sources.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in the available source document. 
- **Note:** Healthcare professionals should be aware that while not listed on the OTC label, **loratadine** is metabolised via CYP3A4 and CYP2D6.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Healthcare professionals should be consulted before use during pregnancy [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Healthcare professionals should be consulted before use during breastfeeding [US].
- **Fertility:** Not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Approved for use in children aged 2 years and older for OTC use. Use in children under 2 years requires medical supervision [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in the provided source.
- **Renal Impairment:** Patients should seek medical advice before use; dose adjustment may be necessary [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Patients should seek medical advice before use; dose adjustment may be necessary [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Identified as an antihistamine [US]. It acts as a selective, long-acting peripheral H1-receptor antagonist.
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not detailed in the provided OTC label.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Provides 24-hour relief of allergy symptoms [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store between 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Safety Packaging:** Do not use if the carton is opened or if the imprinted safety seal on the bottle is broken or missing [US].
- **Shelf Life:** Expiration date is located on the bottom panel of the packaging [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as a Human OTC Drug (ANDA076805) [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not applicable in the US OTC context; however, standard precautions for "Keep out of reach of children" apply. In case of overdose, contact a Poison Control Center immediately [US].
