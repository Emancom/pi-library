# Editorial Product Information: Lumasiran

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the Product Information (PI) document provided by the following regulatory authority:
    - **Australian** (Therapeutic Goods Administration - TGA)
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, intended to consolidate clinical and regulatory data into a single reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for the official Product Information. Clinicians should consult the specific regulatory documentation approved in their jurisdiction before prescribing.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Lumasiran (as lumasiran sodium).
- **Chemical Class:** Double-stranded small interfering ribonucleic acid (siRNA).
- **Brand Name:** **Oxlumo** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** A16AX18.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Solution for injection in a single-dose glass vial. Each vial contains 0.5 mL of solution equivalent to 94.5 mg of lumasiran (concentration: 189 mg/mL).
- **Route of Administration:** Subcutaneous injection.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Medison Pharma Australia Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Primary Hyperoxaluria Type 1 (PH1):** Indicated for the treatment of PH1 in patients of all age groups [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### 3.1 General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
Therapy must be initiated and supervised by a physician experienced in hyperoxaluria management. Dosing is weight-based and consists of a loading phase (monthly for 3 doses) followed by a maintenance phase.

**Table 1: Weight-Based Dosing Regimen**
| Body Weight | Loading Dose | Maintenance Dose (Starting 1 month after last loading dose) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Less than 10 kg** | 6 mg/kg once monthly for 3 doses | 3 mg/kg once monthly |
| **10 kg to <20 kg** | 6 mg/kg once monthly for 3 doses | 6 mg/kg once every 3 months (quarterly) |
| **20 kg and above** | 3 mg/kg once monthly for 3 doses | 3 mg/kg once every 3 months (quarterly) |

### 3.2 Administration Instructions
- **HCP Only:** Must be administered by a healthcare professional.
- **Preparation:** Use an 18-gauge (G) needle to withdraw the solution from the vial. For administration, use a sterile 25-G to 31-G needle (13 mm or 16 mm length). For volumes <0.3 mL, a sterile 0.3 mL syringe is recommended.
- **Injection Sites:** Abdomen, upper arms, or thighs. Rotate sites. Avoid injecting into scar tissue, inflamed, or reddened areas. If injecting into the abdomen, avoid a 2.0 cm radius around the navel.
- **Technique:** Inject at a 90-degree angle (45 degrees if subcutaneous tissue is minimal). Do not aspirate.

### 3.3 Special Considerations
- **Haemodialysis:** On dialysis days, administer **Oxlumo** *after* the haemodialysis session.
- **Missed Dose:** If a dose is missed, administer as soon as possible. Resume the prescribed monthly or quarterly schedule from the date of the most recently administered dose.

## 4. Contraindications
- Severe hypersensitivity to lumasiran or any of its excipients (sodium hydroxide, phosphoric acid, and water for injections).

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Renal Impairment & Metabolic Acidosis:** Lumasiran increases plasma glycolate levels. In patients with severe or end-stage renal disease (ESRD), this may increase the risk of metabolic acidosis or exacerbate pre-existing conditions. Monitor these patients for signs and symptoms of metabolic acidosis.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Patients with moderate or severe hepatic impairment may experience decreased efficacy due to reduced hepatic uptake of the siRNA. Clinical monitoring for efficacy is required in this population.

## 6. Adverse Effects
### 6.1 Summary Profile
The most frequent adverse reaction is injection site reaction.

**Table 2: Frequency of Adverse Reactions**
| Frequency | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (≥10%)** | Injection site reaction (35%) (erythema, pain, swelling, pruritus, haematoma, discolouration, rash), Abdominal pain (including upper/lower pain, discomfort, tenderness) |
| **Not Known** | Hypersensitivity [Australian post-marketing data] |

### 6.2 Immunogenicity
Anti-drug antibodies (ADA) were detected in 5.8% of patients in clinical trials. Titres were generally low and transient, with no observed impact on safety, efficacy, or pharmacokinetics.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** No clinical drug-drug interaction studies have been conducted. In vitro data indicate lumasiran is not a substrate, inhibitor, or inducer of major Cytochrome P450 (CYP) enzymes or drug transporters.
- **Pyridoxine:** Concomitant use of pyridoxine (Vitamin B6) does not meaningfully influence the pharmacodynamics or pharmacokinetics of lumasiran.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Category B1** [Australian]. 
    - No human data are available. Animal studies (rats/rabbits) showed no adverse effects at doses significantly higher than the human maintenance dose, though skeletal abnormalities were noted at maternally toxic doses in some animal models. Use during pregnancy should consider the benefit-risk ratio.
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if lumasiran is excreted in human milk. A decision must be made between discontinuing breastfeeding or abstaining from therapy, weighing the benefits of breastfeeding for the child against the treatment benefit for the mother.
- **Fertility:** Animal studies showed no adverse effects on male or female fertility. No human data are available.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatrics:** Approved for use in all age groups, including infants. Data are limited for patients under 1 year of age; use caution.
- **Elderly (≥65 years):** No dose adjustment is required.
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment is required for patients with any degree of renal impairment, including ESRD or those on dialysis. However, caution and monitoring for metabolic acidosis are recommended in severe renal disease.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment is required for patients with mild hepatic impairment (total bilirubin >1.0 to 1.5 × ULN). Lumasiran has not been studied in moderate or severe hepatic impairment; caution is advised due to potential for reduced efficacy.

## 10. Pharmacology
### 10.1 Mechanism of Action
Lumasiran is a double-stranded siRNA conjugated to N-acetylgalactosamine (GalNAc), which facilitates targeted delivery to hepatocytes. It reduces levels of the glycolate oxidase (GO) enzyme by targeting the *HAO1* messenger RNA. By reducing GO, lumasiran depletes the glyoxylate pool, the substrate for oxalate production. This reduces urinary and plasma oxalate levels.

### 10.2 Pharmacokinetics
- **Absorption:** Rapidly absorbed after subcutaneous injection (median $t_{max}$ of 4 hours).
- **Distribution:** Primarily distributes to the liver. Protein binding is moderate to high (77–85%).
- **Metabolism:** Degraded by endo- and exonucleases into shorter oligonucleotides.
- **Elimination:** Primarily cleared via hepatic uptake. Plasma half-life is approximately 5.2 hours. Renal clearance is minor (2.0 to 3.4 L/h).

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ILLUMINATE-A:** A 6-month, randomised, placebo-controlled study in patients ≥6 years (eGFR ≥30 mL/min/1.73 $m^2$). Lumasiran achieved a 65.4% reduction in 24-hour urinary oxalate compared to 11.8% in the placebo group. 52% of treated patients achieved normal urinary oxalate levels at month 6.
- **ILLUMINATE-B:** A single-arm study in patients <6 years. Lumasiran demonstrated a 72% reduction in spot urinary oxalate:creatinine ratio from baseline to month 6.
- **ILLUMINATE-C:** Conducted in patients with advanced renal disease (eGFR ≤45 mL/min/1.73 $m^2$), including those on haemodialysis. Both cohorts (dialysis and non-dialysis) showed significant reductions in plasma oxalate.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage:** Store below 30°C.
- **Protection:** Keep the vial in the outer carton to protect it from light.
- **Shelf Life:** Refer to the expiry date on the packaging. The product contains no preservatives and is intended for single use only.
