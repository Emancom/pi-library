# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- Contributing Jurisdictions: United States (FDA)
- Purpose: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of levocarnitine injection for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises the clinical and regulatory data available from the provided source.
- Disclaimer: This is an editorial synthesis intended for informational purposes. It is not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN/USP); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt. 
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection, USP [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection, 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Indicated for the acute and chronic management of patients with metabolic disorders resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US-approved].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance haemodialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
- **General Dosing:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg [US].
- **Administration:** Administer as a slow bolus injection (over 2 to 3 minutes) or via infusion.
- **Crisis Management:** In patients with severe metabolic crisis, a loading dose may be given, followed by equivalent doses every 3 to 4 hours (and no less than every 6 hours) over the subsequent 24 hours.
- **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses are typically 50 mg/kg, though higher doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilised.
- **Monitoring:** It is recommended to obtain plasma carnitine concentrations prior to therapy. Weekly and monthly monitoring should target a free carnitine concentration between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].

### ESRD Patients on Haemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
- **Timing:** Administer as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line after each dialysis session.
- **Titration:** Therapy initiation may be prompted by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma carnitine levels <40 to 50 µmol/L. 
- **Dose Adjustment:** Adjustments should be guided by trough levels. Downward titration (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of treatment [US].

### General Administration Instructions
- Inspect for particulate matter or discoloration prior to use.
- **Compatibility:** Stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when diluted in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations ranging from 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known at the time of publication [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal oedema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, particularly in ESRD patients on dialysis. Some reactions occur within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, therapy must be discontinued immediately and appropriate medical treatment initiated [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients receiving levocarnitine, including those with no prior history of seizures. In patients with pre-existing seizure disorders, an increase in seizure frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Accumulation of Metabolites:** In patients with severe renal impairment or ESRD on dialysis, chronic high-dose oral administration (not specific to IV) may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites, trimethylamine (TMA) and trimethylamine-N-oxide (TMAO), which are normally excreted renally [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are often confounded by the underlying pathology of ESRD or metabolic disorders.

| System Organ Class | Frequency ≥5% (regardless of causality) [US] |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Injection site reaction (33%), pain (30%), headache (22%), abdominal pain (9%), fever (7%) |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension (20%), hypotension (14%), tachycardia (6%), palpitations (5%) |
| **Gastrointestinal** | Diarrhea (16%), vomiting (15%), nausea (8%), anorexia (5%) |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis (23%), cough increase (9%), dyspnea (7%), rhinitis (9%) |
| **Metabolic** | Hypercalcemia (9%), hyperkalemia (6%), hypervolemia (5%) |
| **Neurological** | Dizziness (13%), paresthesia (5%) |

**Other Adverse Effects:**
- **Rare/Less Common:** Gastritis, body odour, and transient nausea/vomiting [US].
- **Serious:** Anaphylaxis, bronchospasm, and seizures [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalised Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin. It is recommended to monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after dose adjustments [US].
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Not formally categorised by letter, but reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm]. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk carnitine levels following administration. The benefits of maternal treatment should be weighed against potential risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Refer to the weight-based dosing in Section 3 for metabolic disorders [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Specific studies in the elderly were not detailed in the source document [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Safety and efficacy of oral levocarnitine have not been evaluated in renal insufficiency; however, the IV formulation is specifically indicated for use in ESRD patients undergoing dialysis [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent ATP production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In ESRD patients, levocarnitine corrects the increased ratio of acylcarnitine/carnitine and restores depleted plasma levels [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Following IV administration, approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours. The mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours, and the mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients on haemodialysis, a dose of 20 mg/kg post-dialysis increased mean pre-dialysis (trough) carnitine levels from 21.3 ± 7.7 µmol/L at baseline to 190 ± 55 µmol/L after 8 weeks. While the increase in concentrations is proven, the effects on specific clinical outcomes in this population have not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat.
- **Shelf Life:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as it contains no preservative [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Prescription only (Rx only); NDC 36000-368-10 (carton of 10 vials) [US].
