# Editorial Product Information: Levamisole

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA)
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of Levamisole Product Information, synthesised for healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis intended for informational purposes and is not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing or dispensing in a specific jurisdiction. The source material provided pertains specifically to veterinary use in food-producing animals. **Levamisole in this formulation is strictly not for human use.**

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levamisole hydrochloride
- **Chemical Class:** Anthelmintic (Imidazothiazole derivative)
- **Brand Name(s):** **Prohibit** [US]
- **ATC Code:** QP52AE01 (Veterinary)
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Soluble Drench Powder: Each packet contains 46.8 grams of levamisole hydrochloride activity (total packet weight 52 g) [US].
    - Soluble Drench Powder: Each bottle contains 544.5 grams of levamisole hydrochloride activity (total bottle weight 605 g) [US].
    - Final concentration upon reconstitution: 0.9 g of levamisole per 1 g of powder [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral (Drench)
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Huvepharma, Inc. [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
Levamisole is indicated as a broad-spectrum anthelmintic for the treatment of nematode infections in the following species [US]:

- **Sheep:**
    - **Stomach Worms:** *Haemonchus contortus*, *Trichostrongylus axei*, *Teladorsagia circumcincta*.
    - **Intestinal Worms:** *Trichostrongylus colubriformis*, *Cooperia curticei*, *Nematodirus spathiger*, *Bunostomum trigonocephalum*, *Oesophagostomum columbianum*, *Chabertia ovina*.
    - **Lungworms:** *Dictyocaulus filaria*.
- **Cattle:**
    - **Stomach Worms:** *Haemonchus placei*, *Trichostrongylus axei*, *Ostertagia ostertagi*.
    - **Intestinal Worms:** *Trichostrongylus longispicularis*, *Cooperia oncophora*, *Cooperia punctata*, *Nematodirus spathiger*, *Bunostomum phlebotomum*, *Oesophagostomum radiatum*.
    - **Lungworms:** *Dictyocaulus viviparus*.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Instructions
- **Weight Estimation:** Accurate animal weight estimates are essential for proper dosing and to prevent underdosing, which may lead to treatment failure and parasite resistance [US].
- **Re-treatment:** Animals maintained in conditions of constant helminth exposure may require repeat treatment within two to four weeks of the initial dose [US].

### Dosing by Species [US]
| Species | Preparation Method (Standard) | Reconstituted Dose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cattle** | Dissolve 52 g packet in 1 quart (32 fl. oz.) water | 1/2 fl. oz. per 200 lb body weight |
| **Sheep** | Dissolve 52 g packet in 1 gallon (128 fl. oz.) water | 1/2 fl. oz. per 50 lb body weight |

### Concentrated Drench Solution (Automatic Syringe) [US]
- **Cattle:** Dissolve 52 g packet in 8.75 fl. oz. water. Administer 2 mL per 100 lb body weight.
- **Sheep:** Dissolve 52 g packet in 17.5 fl. oz. water. Administer 2 mL per 50 lb body weight.
- **Bulk (605 g jug):** Reconstitute to 3 liters. Administer 2 mL per 100 lb (Cattle) or 1 mL per 50 lb (Sheep).

## 4. Contraindications
- **Human Use:** Strictly **not for use in humans** [US].
- **Dairy Animals:** Do not administer to dairy animals of breeding age to prevent residues in milk [US].
- **Slaughter Withdrawal:** Do not administer to cattle within 48 hours of slaughter or sheep within 72 hours of slaughter for food [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None specified in provided source.
- **Parasite Resistance:** Resistance may develop to any dewormer. Effectiveness should be monitored via fecal egg count reduction tests or other diagnostic methods [US].
- **Animal Condition:** Consult a veterinarian before administering to severely debilitated animals [US].
- **Safety Precautions:** Keep out of reach of children [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Frequency (Unspecified):**
    - **Muzzle Foam:** Transient foaming at the muzzle may be observed. This reaction typically resolves within a few hours. If the condition persists, veterinary consultation is advised [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Not specified in available sources.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store powder between 20°C and 25°C (68°F–77°F) [US].
- **Shelf Life (Reconstituted):**
    - **Packet (52 g):** Reconstituted solution may be stored in clean, closed containers for up to 90 days [US].
    - **Jug (605 g):** Reconstituted solution may be stored for up to 3 months in the tightly capped bottle; shake well before subsequent use [US].
