# Unified Product Information: JOENJA (Leniolisib)

## 1. Product Name and Active Ingredient
**Product Name:** JOENJA®  
**Active Ingredient:** Leniolisib (as leniolisib phosphate).  
**Formulation:** Each film-coated tablet contains leniolisib phosphate equivalent to 70 mg of leniolisib.

## 2. Approved Indications
JOENJA is a kinase inhibitor indicated for the treatment of activated phosphoinositide 3-kinase delta (PI3Kδ) syndrome (APDS) in adult and pediatric patients aged 12 years and older [FDA, TGA].

**Clinical Requirement:** Initiation of treatment should be reserved for patients with a confirmed pathogenic or likely pathogenic variant in an APDS-associated gene (such as *PIK3CD* or *PIK3R1*) identified through an accredited laboratory [TGA].

## 3. Contraindications
*   **Hypersensitivity:** JOENJA is contraindicated in patients with a known hypersensitivity to leniolisib or any of the inactive excipients in the formulation, which includes lactose monohydrate [TGA]. 
*   *Note: While the FDA label lists no specific contraindications, the TGA maintains a standard contraindication for hypersensitivity to the active substance or excipients.*

## 4. Warnings and Precautions
### Embryo-Fetal Toxicity
Leniolisib can cause fetal harm. Animal studies in rats and rabbits demonstrated teratogenicity and embryo-fetal toxicity (including malformations) at exposures 2 to 6 times the recommended human dose. Pregnancy status must be verified in females of reproductive potential prior to starting therapy [FDA, TGA].

### Hypersensitivity Reactions and Anaphylaxis
Serious hypersensitivity reactions, including anaphylaxis, have been reported in the post-marketing environment. If a clinically significant reaction occurs, JOENJA should be discontinued immediately, and appropriate medical management instituted [FDA].

### Immune-Related Adverse Events
While not observed specifically with JOENJA in APDS clinical trials, other PI3Kδ inhibitors used for oncological indications have been associated with fatal immune-related events, including colitis, pneumonitis, and hepatotoxicity. Caution is advised as long-term safety data for JOENJA is currently limited [TGA].

### Vaccinations
The use of live, attenuated vaccines may be less effective during treatment with JOENJA. Healthcare providers should review the patient's vaccination status prior to initiating therapy [FDA, TGA].

### Hepatic and Renal Impairment
*   **Hepatic:** Leniolisib is extensively metabolized by the liver. Its use is not recommended in patients with moderate to severe hepatic impairment [FDA, TGA].
*   **Renal:** Leniolisib has not been formally studied in patients with renal impairment [TGA].

### Excipient Warnings
Each tablet contains approximately 241 mg of lactose monohydrate. It should not be used in patients with rare hereditary conditions such as galactose intolerance, total lactase deficiency, or glucose-galactose malabsorption [TGA].

## 5. Dosage and Administration
### Recommended Dosage
The standard dose for adults and pediatric patients (12 years and older) weighing **45 kg or more** is **70 mg twice daily**, administered approximately 12 hours apart [FDA, TGA]. 
*   *Note: There is no recommended dosage for patients weighing less than 45 kg [FDA].*

### Administration Instructions
*   Tablets may be taken with or without food [FDA, TGA].
*   **Missed Dose:** If a dose is missed by more than 6 hours, the patient should skip the missed dose and resume the normal schedule. They should not double the dose [FDA, TGA].
*   **Vomiting:** If vomiting occurs within 1 hour of administration, a replacement dose should be taken. If it occurs after 1 hour, no additional dose is required [TGA].

## 6. Adverse Effects
The most common adverse reactions (incidence > 10%) reported in clinical trials include:
*   Headache
*   Sinusitis
*   Atopic dermatitis (including eczema)

### Laboratory Abnormalities
A decrease in absolute neutrophil count (ANC) was observed in 33% of patients. These cases were generally transient and not associated with an increase in clinical infections [TGA].

### Other Noted Reactions
Other reported effects include tachycardia, diarrhea, fatigue, pyrexia, back pain, and alopecia. Long-term extension studies also noted weight gain in approximately 14% of participants [FDA, TGA].

## 7. Drug Interactions
### Effects of Other Drugs on JOENJA
*   **Strong CYP3A4 Inhibitors:** Co-administration (e.g., with itraconazole) can double leniolisib exposure. Concurrent use should be avoided [FDA, TGA].
*   **CYP3A4 Inducers:** Strong and moderate inducers (e.g., rifampin, St. John’s Wort) may significantly reduce JOENJA efficacy and should be avoided [FDA, TGA].
*   **Gastric Acid Reducing Agents:** Leniolisib solubility is pH-dependent. While some data suggests no clinical impact, it is generally recommended to space antacid administration by at least 2 hours before or after the JOENJA dose [TGA].

### Effects of JOENJA on Other Drugs
*   **Transporter Substrates:** JOENJA inhibits BCRP, OATP1B1, and OATP1B3. Avoid co-administration with drugs that are sole substrates of these transporters (e.g., certain statins like rosuvastatin) [FDA, TGA].
*   **CYP1A2 Substrates with Narrow Therapeutic Index (NTI):** Leniolisib is a time-dependent inhibitor of CYP1A2. Avoid use with NTI substrates like tizanidine or duloxetine [TGA].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
### Pregnancy
JOENJA is not recommended during pregnancy. Females of reproductive potential must use highly effective contraception during treatment and for at least **one week** following the final dose [FDA, TGA]. (Pregnancy Category D [TGA]).

### Breastfeeding
It is unknown if leniolisib is excreted in human milk, though it has been detected in animal milk. Due to the potential for serious adverse reactions in the infant, breastfeeding should be discontinued during treatment and for **one week** after the last dose [FDA, TGA].

## 9. Pharmacokinetics
*   **Absorption:** Rapidly absorbed with a median Tmax of approximately 1 hour in a fasted state. A high-fat meal delays Tmax but does not significantly alter overall exposure (AUC) [TGA].
*   **Distribution:** Highly protein-bound (94.5%). The volume of distribution is approximately 28.5 L in APDS patients [FDA].
*   **Metabolism:** Primarily cleared via the liver (60%), dominated by CYP3A4 (95.4%) [TGA].
*   **Elimination:** The effective half-life is approximately 7 hours. Excretion is primarily fecal (67%) and urinary (25.5%) [TGA].

## 10. Mechanism of Action
Leniolisib is a selective inhibitor of the delta isoform of phosphoinositide 3-kinase (PI3Kδ). In APDS, gain-of-function mutations in *PIK3CD* or loss-of-function mutations in *PIK3R1* lead to hyperactive PI3Kδ signaling. This causes increased production of PIP3 and overactivation of the downstream mTOR/Akt pathway, resulting in B and T-cell dysregulation. Leniolisib blocks the active binding site of PI3Kδ, thereby normalizing pathway activity and reducing lymphoproliferation [TGA, FDA].

## 11. Storage Conditions
Store JOENJA at room temperature, ideally between 20°C to 25°C [FDA]. Keep the container tightly closed and store in a dry place below 30°C. Do not refrigerate [TGA]. Keep out of reach of children.
