# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
*   **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA).
*   **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of Levocarnitine Injection for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises the clinical and regulatory data available from the provided source.
*   **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis intended for informational purposes and is not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) or Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN/USP); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
*   **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative (carrier molecule).
*   **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
*   **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
*   **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) single-dose vials [US].
*   **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US-approved].
*   **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD undergoing dialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
*   **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion.
    *   **Loading Dose:** In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose is often followed by an equivalent dose divided over the subsequent 24 hours.
    *   **Frequency:** Dosing should occur every 3 or 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours.
    *   **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses typically remain in the 50 mg/kg range, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilised.
*   **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:** The recommended starting dose is 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight.
    *   **Administration:** Administered as a slow 2 to 3 minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session.

### Monitoring and Titration
*   **Plasma Levels:** It is recommended to obtain plasma carnitine concentrations prior to initiating parenteral therapy.
*   **Ongoing Monitoring:** Weekly and monthly monitoring should include blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine: 35 to 60 µmol/L) [US].
*   **ESRD Adjustments:** Therapy initiation may be prompted by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levels below 40–50 µmol/L. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be made as early as the third or fourth week of treatment based on trough levels.

### Administration Instructions
*   **Visual Inspection:** Inspect for particulate matter and discoloration prior to use.
*   **Compatibility:** Compatible with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations from 0.5 mg/mL to 8.0 mg/mL. Stable for up to 24 hours at room temperature in PVC bags [US].

## 4. Contraindications
*   None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
*   **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions may occur within minutes of administration. Treatment must be discontinued immediately if a severe reaction occurs [US].
*   **Seizures:** Seizures have been reported in patients with and without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity may occur [US].
*   **Renal Impairment (Oral formulation precaution):** While the injection is used in ESRD, chronic high doses of *oral* levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects observed in clinical trials for ESRD patients (occurring at ≥5% frequency) are listed below. An incidence for metabolic disorder patients is difficult to estimate due to underlying pathology.

| Body System | Adverse Event (Frequency ≥5%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Chest pain, fever, flu syndrome, headache, infection, injection site reaction, pain, abdominal pain, asthenia |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension, hypotension, tachycardia, arrhythmia, atrial fibrillation |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea, vomiting, nausea, anorexia, constipation, dyspepsia |
| **Metabolic/Nutritional** | Hypercalcemia, hyperkalemia, hypervolemia, peripheral edema, weight changes |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness, insomnia, paresthesia, anxiety, depression |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis, cough increase, dyspnea, rhinitis |
| **Skin** | Pruritus, rash |
| **Urogenital** | Kidney failure, Urinary Tract Infection |

*   **Serious Adverse Effects:** Anaphylaxis and other severe hypersensitivity reactions; seizures; laryngeal edema.
*   **Other Effects:** Body odor and gastritis have been reported less frequently [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalised Ratio (INR) have occurred when levocarnitine is used concomitantly with warfarin. 
*   **Management:** Monitor INR levels in patients on warfarin therapy after initiating levocarnitine or following dose adjustments [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not specified]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (based on surface area) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
*   **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine in milk. The risks to the child from excess carnitine must be weighed against the benefits of supplementation to the mother [US].
*   **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility was found in animal reproductive studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders follows the mg/kg guidelines provided in Section 3.
*   **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Renal Impairment:** Used specifically in ESRD patients on hemodialysis. Note that the safety of the *oral* form has not been evaluated in renal insufficiency [US].
*   **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
*   **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria for β-oxidation and subsequent energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
*   **Pharmacodynamics:** In patients with inborn errors of metabolism or ESRD, levocarnitine clears toxic acyl-CoA compounds and increases plasma carnitine levels.
*   **Pharmacokinetics:**
    *   **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin. 
    *   **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces).
    *   **Elimination:** Following IV bolus (20 mg/kg), the mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours and the terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours. Approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   **ESRD Study:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients on hemodialysis receiving 20 mg/kg three times per week showed mean pre-dialysis (trough) levels increased from 21.3 µmol/L at baseline to 190 µmol/L at 8 weeks.
*   **Dose-Ranging Study:** In studies involving 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses, trough concentrations increased proportionally with the dose over 12 and 24 weeks.
*   **Clinical Outcomes:** While levocarnitine increases carnitine concentrations in ESRD patients, the effects on specific clinical outcomes in this population have not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C.
*   **Protection:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat.
*   **Handling:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as the formulation contains no preservatives [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
*   **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
*   **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources.
