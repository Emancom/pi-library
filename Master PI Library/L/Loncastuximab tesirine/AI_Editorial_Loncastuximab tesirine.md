# Editorial Product Information: Loncastuximab Tesirine

**Editorial Note:** This unified Product Information (PI) document has been compiled by integrating clinical and regulatory data from the Australian Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA) and the United States Food and Drug Administration (FDA). While both agencies have approved this agent under accelerated/provisional pathways, this document synthesizes their specific warnings, dosing modifications, and safety data to provide a comprehensive reference for healthcare professionals.

---

## 1. Product Name and Active Ingredient
**Product Name:** ZYNLONTA®  
**Active Ingredient:** Loncastuximab tesirine (referred to as loncastuximab tesirine-lpyl in the United States [FDA]).

Loncastuximab tesirine is a CD19-directed antibody-drug conjugate (ADC). It consists of a humanized IgG1 kappa monoclonal antibody conjugated to SG3199, a pyrrolobenzodiazepine (PBD) dimer cytotoxic alkylating agent, via a protease-cleavable valine-alanine linker.

## 2. Approved Indications
ZYNLONTA is indicated as monotherapy for the treatment of adult patients with relapsed or refractory (R/R) large B-cell lymphoma after two or more lines of systemic therapy. This includes:
*   Diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL) not otherwise specified.
*   DLBCL arising from low-grade lymphoma.
*   High-grade B-cell lymphoma [FDA].

**Limitations of Use:**
*   The drug is not indicated for the treatment of patients with primary central nervous system lymphoma [TGA].
*   Continued approval for these indications may be contingent upon verification of clinical benefit in confirmatory trials.

## 3. Contraindications
*   **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to loncastuximab tesirine or any of its excipients [TGA].
*   **Other:** The FDA label lists no specific contraindications.

## 4. Warnings and Precautions
### Effusion and Edema (including Capillary Leak Syndrome)
Serious and sometimes fatal effusion and edema have occurred. Manifestations include pleural effusion, pericardial effusion, ascites, peripheral edema, and general edema. Capillary leak syndrome (CLS) has also been reported [FDA]. Patients with pre-existing cardiac or renal disease may be at increased risk [TGA].
*   **Monitoring:** Evaluate for new or worsening edema/effusion. Perform diagnostic imaging if symptoms (e.g., dyspnea, chest pain, abdominal swelling) develop.
*   **Management:** Institute appropriate medical management. Withhold or discontinue ZYNLONTA based on severity.

### Myelosuppression
Treatment can cause severe cytopenias, including neutropenia, thrombocytopenia, and anemia. 
*   **Monitoring:** Monitor complete blood counts (CBC) prior to each dose.
*   **Management:** Cytopenias may require dose interruption, reduction, or discontinuation. Consider prophylactic granulocyte colony-stimulating factor (G-CSF).

### Infections
Fatal and serious infections, including opportunistic infections and sepsis, have been reported. Monitor patients for new or worsening signs of infection and treat promptly.

### Hepatotoxicity
Serious hepatotoxicity, including drug-induced liver injury (DILI) and elevations in liver function tests (LFTs), has been observed. 
*   **Monitoring:** Monitor LFTs (ALT, AST, bilirubin) at baseline and throughout treatment.
*   **Management:** Withhold dosing if Grade 3 or higher elevations occur. Discontinue permanently if DILI is confirmed [FDA].

### Cutaneous Reactions
Photosensitivity and serious cutaneous reactions (e.g., rash, erythema) have been reported. 
*   **Advice:** Patients should minimize sun exposure, wear protective clothing, and use sunscreen.
*   **Management:** Withhold ZYNLONTA for Grade 3 or higher reactions until resolution.

### Embryo-Fetal Toxicity
Due to its genotoxic component (SG3199), ZYNLONTA can cause fetal harm. Verify pregnancy status in females of reproductive potential prior to initiation.

## 5. Dosage and Administration
### Recommended Dosage
ZYNLONTA is administered as an intravenous infusion over 30 minutes every 3 weeks (21-day cycle):
*   **Cycles 1 and 2:** 0.15 mg/kg.
*   **Subsequent Cycles:** 0.075 mg/kg.

### Body Weight Adjustment
For patients with a Body Mass Index (BMI) ≥35 kg/m², the dose should be calculated based on an adjusted body weight (ABW):  
*   $ABW (kg) = 35 kg/m^2 \times (height\ in\ meters)^2$

### Premedication
Unless contraindicated, administer **dexamethasone 4 mg** orally or intravenously twice daily for 3 days, beginning the day before infusion, to mitigate PBD-related toxicities. If not started the day before, begin dexamethasone at least 2 hours prior to ZYNLONTA.

### Dose Modifications for Adverse Reactions
*   **Hematologic (Neutrophil <1 x 10⁹/L or Platelets <50,000/mcL):** Withhold until recovery to baseline or specific thresholds.
*   **Edema/Effusion (Grade 2 or higher):** Withhold until resolved to Grade 1 or less.
*   **Hepatotoxicity (Grade 3 or higher):** Withhold until resolved to Grade 1 or less.
*   **Dose Reduction:** If a dose is delayed by more than 3 weeks due to toxicity, reduce the subsequent dose by 50%. If toxicity recurs after two dose reductions, consider permanent discontinuation.

## 6. Adverse Effects
The most common adverse reactions (≥20%) include:
*   **Laboratory Abnormalities:** Increased gamma-glutamyltransferase (GGT), neutropenia, thrombocytopenia, anemia, increased glucose, and increased transaminases.
*   **Clinical Reactions:** Fatigue, nausea, rash, musculoskeletal pain, and edema.

Serious adverse reactions occurring in ≥2% of patients include febrile neutropenia, pneumonia, sepsis, and pleural effusion. Hypercalcemia was also noted as a frequent serious reaction in some cohorts [TGA].

## 7. Drug Interactions
No formal drug-drug interaction studies have been conducted in humans. 
*   **In Vitro Data:** The cytotoxic moiety, SG3199, is a substrate of CYP3A4/5. Co-administration with strong CYP3A inhibitors or inducers may affect the exposure of SG3199.
*   **Transporters:** SG3199 is a substrate of P-glycoprotein (P-gp) but does not inhibit major transporters (BCRP, OATP1B1, OCT1) at clinically relevant concentrations.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
### Pregnancy
**Category D [TGA].** ZYNLONTA is not recommended during pregnancy. It contains a genotoxic agent that affects actively dividing cells. Females of reproductive potential must use effective contraception during treatment and for **10 months** after the final dose. 

### Fertility
Based on animal studies, ZYNLONTA may impair male fertility. Men with female partners of reproductive potential should use effective contraception during treatment and for **7 months** after the last dose. Male patients should consider sperm preservation prior to treatment.

### Breastfeeding
It is unknown if loncastuximab tesirine is excreted in human milk. Because of the potential for serious adverse reactions in breastfed children, breastfeeding is advised against during treatment and for **3 months** after the final dose.

## 9. Pharmacokinetics
*   **Distribution:** The mean volume of distribution is approximately 7.11 L [FDA].
*   **Elimination:** The mean half-life is approximately 20.8 days at steady state. Clearance decreases over time as steady state is reached (approximately 15 weeks).
*   **Metabolism:** The antibody portion is catabolized into small peptides. The small molecule cytotoxin (SG3199) is metabolized by CYP3A4/5 in vitro.
*   **Special Populations:** No clinically significant PK differences were observed based on age (20–94 years), sex, or race. No dose adjustment is required for mild-to-moderate renal impairment or mild hepatic impairment. It has not been studied in patients with severe renal or moderate-to-severe hepatic impairment.

## 10. Mechanism of Action
Loncastuximab tesirine is an ADC consisting of a humanized monoclonal antibody targeting CD19, which is highly expressed on B-lineage cells. Upon binding to CD19, the ADC is internalized. The cytotoxic PBD dimer, SG3199, is released via intracellular protease cleavage. SG3199 binds to the DNA minor groove, forming highly cytotoxic interstrand cross-links that inhibit DNA replication, ultimately leading to cell death.

## 11. Storage Conditions
*   **Vial Storage:** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C in the original carton to protect from light. Do not freeze or shake.
*   **Reconstituted Solution:** Use immediately. If necessary, store for up to 4 hours at room temperature (20°C–25°C) or refrigerated (2°C–8°C).
*   **Diluted Infusion Bag:** If not used immediately, the solution (in 5% glucose/dextrose) can be stored refrigerated (2°C–8°C) for up to 24 hours or at room temperature (20°C–25°C) for up to 8 hours. 

**Note:** ZYNLONTA is a cytotoxic/hazardous drug. Follow applicable handling and disposal procedures for antineoplastic agents.
