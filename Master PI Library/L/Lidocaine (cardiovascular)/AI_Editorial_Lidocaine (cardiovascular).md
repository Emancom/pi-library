# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **US (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of levocarnitine injection for healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult the current local PI for legal and regulatory compliance.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); chemical name: (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative (carrier molecule).
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection, USP [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Clear, colourless sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** For the acute and chronic management of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Administration
- Administer intravenously.
- Inspect parenteral products for particulate matter or discoloration prior to use.
- The solution is compatible with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer's (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) and remains stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags [US].

### Metabolic Disorders
- **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg as a slow (2 to 3 minute) bolus injection or by infusion.
- **Acute Management:** In cases of severe metabolic crisis, a loading dose may be given, followed by an equivalent dose split over the next 24 hours. Dosing frequency should be every 3 to 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours.
- **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses are typically 50 mg/kg, though higher doses may be required.
- **Maximum Dose:** The highest recorded dose administered is 300 mg/kg.
- **Monitoring:** Obtain baseline plasma carnitine concentrations. Weekly and monthly monitoring is advised, targeting a plasma free carnitine concentration between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Recommended Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight) administered as a slow (2 to 3 minute) bolus into the venous return line after each dialysis session.
- **Dose Adjustment:** Adjustments may be prompted by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine levels falling below 40–50 µmol/L. Downward titration (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known** [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have occurred, often within minutes of administration. Most cases involve ESRD patients. If a reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and monitor for recurrence if re-administration is attempted [US].
- **Oral Levocarnitine Caution:** Safety/efficacy of oral levocarnitine has not been established in patients with renal impairment. Chronic high doses in ESRD may lead to accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) normally excreted in urine [US].
- **Neurological Events:** Seizures have been reported in both patients with and without pre-existing seizure history. In those with a history, an increase in seizure frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Monitoring:** Routine monitoring of blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine levels is recommended [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Clinical Trial Experience (ESRD)
The following adverse events were reported at a frequency of ≥5% in double-blind, placebo-controlled trials of ESRD patients on hemodialysis, regardless of causality [US]:

| Body System | Adverse Event (Frequency ≥5%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Chest pain (12%), Headache (22%), Infection (15%), Injection site reaction (33%), Pain (30%), Fever (7%), Abdominal pain (9%) |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension (20%), Hypotension (14%), Tachycardia (6%), Palpitations (5%) |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea (16%), Vomiting (15%), Nausea (8%), Anorexia (5%), Constipation (3%) |
| **Metabolic/Nutritional** | Hypercalcemia (9%), Hyperkalemia (6%), Hypervolemia (5%), Peripheral edema (5%), Weight decrease (5%) |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness (13%), Insomnia (4%), Depression (5%), Paresthesia (5%) |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis (23%), Cough increase (9%), Rhinitis (9%), Dyspnea (7%) |
| **Other** | Asthenia (9%), Back pain (8%), Pruritus (5%), Eye disorder (3%) |

### Serious and Other Effects
- **Serious:** Anaphylaxis and other severe hypersensitivity reactions [US].
- **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea and vomiting; less frequent reports of body odor and gastritis [US].
- **Neurological:** Seizures [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when used concomitantly with levocarnitine. Monitoring of INR is recommended upon initiation or after dose adjustments [US].
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not assigned - narrative provided]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Safety has not been specifically studied in nursing mothers. Levocarnitine is known to be concentrated in cow milk. The benefits of maternal supplementation must be weighed against potential risks to the infant; consideration may be given to discontinuing nursing or the drug [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility was found in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders applies to paediatric patients; consult specific weight-based dosing in Section 3 [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically detailed in source documents; generally, dose selection should be cautious given higher frequency of hepatic, renal, or cardiac dysfunction in this population.
- **Renal Impairment:** Primarily indicated for use in ESRD patients on dialysis. Note that oral levocarnitine is not recommended for chronic use in severe renal impairment due to metabolite accumulation (TMA/TMAO) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring carrier molecule essential for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane for beta-oxidation and energy production [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** It promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids in patients with metabolic defects by forming acylcarnitine esters, which are rapidly excreted. This action helps alleviate acidosis and autointoxication [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Terminal elimination half-life is approximately 17.4 hours. About 76% of an intravenous dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pharmacokinetic Study (ESRD):** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients (20 mg/kg post-dialysis), mean pre-dialysis (trough) levels increased from 21.3 µmol/L at baseline to 190 µmol/L after 8 weeks.
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** ESRD patients received 10, 20, or 40 mg/kg three times weekly. At 24 weeks, mean trough concentrations were 148, 240, and 456 µmol/L, respectively.
- **Note:** While efficacy in increasing carnitine concentrations is proven, the direct effect on clinical outcomes in ESRD patients has not been determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat.
- **Integrity:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as the formulation contains no preservatives [US].
