# Editorial Product Information: Lactase

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' PI documents contributed to this editorial: US (FDA).
- Purpose: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of lactase enzyme supplementation for use by healthcare professionals.
- Note: This is an editorial synthesis intended for professional reference and does not substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing or dispensing in any specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Lactase (derived from *Aspergillus oryzae* or similar sources; FCC Units)
- **Brand Name(s):** Lactase Enzyme (Nationwide Pharmaceutical) [US]
- **ATC Code:** A09AA04
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Oral Tablet: 3000 FCC Units [US]
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Nationwide Pharmaceutical LLC [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Dairy Digestion Support:** Indicated as a digestive aid to assist in the breakdown of lactose in dairy foods for individuals with lactose intolerance or sensitivity [US-approved dietary supplement].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** 1 tablet (3000 FCC Units) [US].
- **Administration Instructions:** The dose should be taken orally with the first bite of dairy-containing food [US].
- **Titration:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Missed Dose:** Not specified in available sources; however, the product is intended for use concurrently with dairy consumption.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Product Integrity:** Do not use if the printed inner seal under the cap is missing or damaged [US].
- No other specific contraindications were identified in the provided source material.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **General Warnings:** Patients should be advised to keep this product out of the reach of children [US].
- **Clinical Monitoring:** Patients should consult a healthcare practitioner if symptoms of dairy intolerance continue after use or if symptoms are unusual and appear unrelated to dairy consumption [US].
- **Pre-existing Conditions:** Individuals with a pre-existing medical condition or those currently taking other medications should consult a healthcare professional prior to use [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- Not specified in available sources.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **General Interaction Caution:** Patients taking concurrent medications should consult a healthcare professional before initiating lactase supplementation [US].
- **Specific Interactions:** No specific pharmacokinetic or pharmacodynamic interactions were detailed in the source material.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Consult a healthcare practitioner before use if pregnant [US]. No specific regulatory pregnancy category (e.g., US Lettered or Australian Category) was specified in the provided source.
- **Breastfeeding:** Consult a healthcare practitioner before use if nursing [US].
- **Fertility:** Not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Use in children should be under the guidance of a healthcare provider; keep out of reach of children [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Lactase is an enzyme that hydrolyses the disaccharide lactose into the monosaccharides glucose and galactose, facilitating absorption in individuals who lack sufficient endogenous lactase.
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Specifically targets the digestion of dairy-based foods [US].
