# Editorial Product Information: Lofexidine

## Editorial Note
- Contributing Jurisdictions: United States (FDA).
- Purpose: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of lofexidine product information for use by healthcare professionals.
- Disclaimer: This synthesis is an editorial reference and does not replace the official Product Information required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Lofexidine hydrochloride (Central alpha-2 adrenergic agonist).
- **Brand Names:** Lucemyra [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 0.18 mg film-coated tablets (equivalent to 0.2 mg lofexidine hydrochloride) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** US WorldMeds, LLC [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Opioid Withdrawal Mitigation:** Indicated for the mitigation of opioid withdrawal symptoms to facilitate the abrupt discontinuation of opioids in adults [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults)
- **Starting Dosage:** Three 0.18 mg tablets (0.54 mg total) taken orally 4 times daily.
- **Dosing Interval:** 5 to 6 hours between doses.
- **Duration:** Treatment may continue for up to 14 days, with dosing guided by the severity of symptoms.
- **Maximum Daily Dose:** 2.88 mg (16 tablets) per day.
- **Maximum Single Dose:** 0.72 mg (4 tablets).

### Administration Instructions
- Tablets may be administered with or without food [US].
- Dosing should be most intensive during the peak withdrawal period (typically the first 5 to 7 days after the last opioid use).

### Discontinuation and Tapering
- **Gradual Reduction:** Do not discontinue abruptly. Reduce the dose gradually over 2 to 4 days (e.g., reducing by 1 tablet per dose every 1 to 2 days) to mitigate withdrawal symptoms and prevent a marked rise in blood pressure [US].

### Missed Dose
- Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- None identified in the available source [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Cardiovascular Effects (Hypotension, Bradycardia, Syncope):** Lofexidine causes decreases in blood pressure and heart rate. Monitor vital signs prior to dosing. Use with caution in outpatients; ensure they can self-monitor for orthostasis. Avoid use in patients with severe coronary insufficiency, recent MI, cerebrovascular disease, chronic renal failure, or marked bradycardia [US].
- **QT Prolongation:** Lofexidine prolongs the QT interval. Avoid use in patients with congenital long QT syndrome. Monitor ECG in patients with congestive heart failure, bradyarrhythmias, hepatic/renal impairment, or those taking other QTc-prolonging drugs (e.g., methadone) [US]. Correct electrolyte abnormalities (hypokalemia, hypomagnesemia) before initiation.
- **CNS Depression:** Lofexidine potentiates the effects of benzodiazepines and may increase the depressant effects of alcohol, barbiturates, and other sedatives. Patients should avoid driving or operating heavy machinery until they know how the drug affects them [US].
- **Opioid Overdose Risk:** Patients completing opioid discontinuation have reduced tolerance and are at an increased risk of fatal overdose if opioid use is resumed. Lofexidine is not a treatment for opioid use disorder (OUD) and should be used as part of a comprehensive management program [US].
- **Discontinuation Symptoms:** Abrupt cessation can cause a marked increase in blood pressure and symptoms such as diarrhea, insomnia, anxiety, and chills [US].

### Serious and Other Notable Effects
- **Syncope:** Occurred in 0.9% to 1.4% of patients [US].
- **Tinnitus:** Reported in up to 3.2% of patients receiving the highest dose [US].
- **Rebound Hypertension:** Following discontinuation, systolic blood pressure ≥140 mmHg and a ≥20 mmHg increase from baseline was observed in nearly 40% of patients [US].
- **Sex-Specific Findings:** Females may experience a higher incidence of serious cardiovascular adverse events (4% vs 1% in males) and higher rates of dose-holds due to bradycardia [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Methadone:** Both agents prolong the QT interval; enhanced ECG monitoring is recommended when used concomitantly [US].
- **Oral Naltrexone:** Concomitant use may reduce the efficacy of oral naltrexone; this interaction is not expected with non-oral naltrexone routes [US].
- **CNS Depressants:** Potentiation of depressive effects of benzodiazepines, alcohol, and barbiturates [US].
- **CYP2D6 Inhibitors (e.g., Paroxetine):** Concomitant use increases lofexidine plasma levels (approx. 28% increase in AUC). Monitor for orthostatic hypotension and bradycardia [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not assigned]. Safety has not been established in humans. Animal studies in rats and rabbits showed reduced fetal weights, increased resorptions, and litter loss at exposures below the maximum recommended human dose (MRHD). Use only if the potential benefit justifies the risk [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** No data are available regarding the presence of lofexidine in human milk or its effects on the breastfed infant. Caution is advised; consider the developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding alongside the mother's clinical need [US].
- **Fertility:** In animal studies, lofexidine decreased breeding rates and increased resorptions at exposures below human levels [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness in patients under 18 years of age have not been established [US].
- **Geriatric Use:** No specific PK studies performed. Use caution in patients >65 years; consider renal-appropriate dosing adjustments [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Clearance is reduced. Dosage adjustments are required based on Child-Pugh score [US]:
    - **Mild (5-6):** 2.16 mg/day (3 tablets 4x daily).
    - **Moderate (7-9):** 1.44 mg/day (2 tablets 4x daily).
    - **Severe (>9):** 0.72 mg/day (1 tablet 4x daily).
- **Renal Impairment:** Clearance is reduced. Dosage adjustments are required based on eGFR [US]:
    - **Moderate (30-89.9):** 1.44 mg/day (2 tablets 4x daily).
    - **Severe/ESRD (<30) or Dialysis:** 0.72 mg/day (1 tablet 4x daily).
    - Dialysis: Only a negligible fraction is removed; no additional dose is needed after a session [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Central alpha-2 adrenergic agonist that binds to receptors on adrenergic neurons. This reduces norepinephrine release and decreases sympathetic tone [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Prolongs the QTcF interval in a dose-dependent manner.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Well absorbed; peak plasma concentrations achieved in 3 to 5 hours. Absolute bioavailability is approximately 72%.
    - **Distribution:** Extensive distribution into body tissue (apparent volume of distribution ~480 L). Protein binding is ~55%.
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily metabolised by CYP2D6; CYP1A2 and CYP2C19 also contribute. Approximately 30% undergoes first-pass metabolism to inactive metabolites.
    - **Elimination:** Terminal half-life is 11 to 13 hours after the first dose; 17 to 22 hours at steady state. Primary excretion is renal (93.5%).

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Efficacy Data:** Supported by two randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trials in patients with opioid dependence undergoing abrupt withdrawal.
- **Outcome Measures:** Primary endpoints included the Short Opiate Withdrawal Scale of Gossop (SOWS-Gossop) and the proportion of patients completing treatment.
- **Results:** Patients treated with lofexidine (2.16 mg and 2.88 mg daily) had significantly lower SOWS-Gossop scores and significantly higher treatment completion rates compared to placebo [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at controlled room temperature, 25°C (77°F), with excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Store in the original container. Protect from excess heat and moisture. Do not remove desiccant packs from the bottle until all tablets are used [US].

- **United States:** Prescription-only (Rx-only).
