# Editorial Product Information: Lenalidomide

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** This editorial is based on the official Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdiction: **Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration - TGA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial synthesis of lenalidomide product information for healthcare professionals.
- **Prescribing Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and should not be used as a substitute for the official, current PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Lenalidomide (as a racemic mixture)
- **Chemical Class:** Thalidomide analogue; Immunomodulating agent
- **Brand Name(s):** Cipla Lenalidomide [Australian]
- **ATC Code:** L04AX04
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Hard gelatin capsules: 5 mg, 10 mg, 15 mg, 20 mg, and 25 mg. [Australian]
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Cipla Australia Pty Ltd [Australian]

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Multiple Myeloma (MM):** 
    - Initial therapy in combination with bortezomib and dexamethasone (Rbd) for patients ineligible for autologous stem cell transplant (ASCT).
    - Induction therapy prior to autologous hematopoietic stem cell transplantation.
    - Maintenance therapy following adequate haematologic recovery after ASCT.
    - Treatment in combination with dexamethasone for previously treated patients.
- **Myelodysplastic Syndromes (MDS):** Treatment of transfusion-dependent anaemia in patients with low- or intermediate-1 risk MDS associated with a deletion 5q cytogenetic abnormality (with or without additional abnormalities). [Australian]
- **Mantle Cell Lymphoma (MCL):** Treatment of relapsed and/or refractory disease. [Australian]

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Supervision:** Therapy must be initiated and managed by a Specialist Physician experienced in managing haematological and oncological malignancies. [Australian]
- **Administration Instructions:** 
    - Administer at approximately the same time each day. 
    - Capsules must be swallowed whole; do not open, break, or chew.
    - Preferably taken with water, either one hour before or two hours after food. [Australian]
- **Multiple Myeloma Dosing:**
    - **Initial/Induction (Rbd):** 25 mg daily on Days 1–14 (21-day cycle) or Days 1–21 (28-day cycle).
    - **Maintenance (Post-ASCT):** 10 mg daily continuously (Days 1–28). Dose may be increased to 15 mg after 3 months if tolerated. [Australian]
    - **Previously Treated:** 25 mg daily on Days 1–21 (28-day cycle) in combination with dexamethasone.
- **MDS Dosing:** 10 mg daily on Days 1–21 (28-day cycle).
- **MCL Dosing:** 25 mg daily on Days 1–21 (28-day cycle).
- **Missed Dose:** If <12 hours since scheduled time, the dose may be taken. If >12 hours, skip the dose and resume the next day at the normal time. [Australian]

## 4. Contraindications
- **Pregnancy:** Lenalidomide is strictly contraindicated in pregnant women due to structural relation to thalidomide, a known human teratogen. [Australian]
- **Women of Childbearing Potential:** Contraindicated unless all requirements of the mandatory Pregnancy Prevention Program are met. [Australian]
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to lenalidomide or any excipients.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning (Teratogenicity):** **Lenalidomide is structurally related to thalidomide. Exposure during pregnancy may cause severe, life-threatening birth defects or fetal death.** Access is restricted through a mandatory Pregnancy Prevention Program (PPP). [Australian]
- **Venous and Arterial Thromboembolism (VTE/ATE):** Increased risk of VTE (DVT, PE) and ATE (Myocardial Infarction, CVA), particularly in combination with dexamethasone. Prophylactic anticoagulation is recommended for at-risk patients. [Australian]
- **Haematological Toxicity:** Significant neutropenia and thrombocytopenia are common and dose-limiting. CBC monitoring is required weekly for the first 8–12 weeks depending on indication. [Australian]
- **Second Primary Malignancies (SPM):** Increased incidence of AML, MDS, and solid tumours has been observed in clinical trials, particularly in newly diagnosed MM. [Australian]
- **Tumour Lysis Syndrome (TLS) and Tumour Flare Reaction (TFR):** Reported in MM, MDS, and notably in CLL (where lenalidomide is not recommended). Monitor patients with high tumour burden. [Australian]
- **Hepatotoxicity:** Cases of hepatic failure, including fatal outcomes, have been reported. Monitor liver function tests (LFTs) if abnormalities occur. [Australian]
- **Other Precautions:** 
    - Progressive Multifocal Leukoencephalopathy (PML).
    - Serious Skin Reactions (Stevens-Johnson Syndrome, TEN, DRESS).
    - Thyroid dysfunction (Hypo/Hyperthyroidism). [Australian]

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Very Common (>10%):**
    - **Haematological:** Neutropenia, thrombocytopenia, anaemia, leukopenia, lymphopenia.
    - **Gastrointestinal:** Diarrhoea, constipation, nausea, abdominal pain, vomiting.
    - **General:** Fatigue, peripheral oedema, pyrexia, asthenia.
    - **Infections:** Pneumonia, nasopharyngitis, bronchitis, upper respiratory tract infection.
    - **Metabolic:** Hypokalaemia, hyperglycaemia, hypocalcaemia, decreased appetite.
    - **Nervous System:** Peripheral neuropathy, dizziness, headache, dysgeusia, paraesthesia.
    - **Skin:** Rash, pruritus, dry skin.
- **Common (1–10%):** Atrial fibrillation, myocardial infarction, congestive heart failure, pulmonary hypertension, renal failure, sepsis, and basal/squamous cell carcinoma. [Australian]
- **Post-marketing:** Rhabdomyolysis (risk may be additive with statins), viral reactivation (HBV, Herpes Zoster), and solid organ transplant rejection. [Australian]

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Digoxin:** Lenalidomide may increase plasma exposure of digoxin; monitor serum levels. [Australian]
- **Warfarin:** No PK interaction observed, but monitor INR closely due to increased VTE risk and potential PD interaction with dexamethasone. [Australian]
- **Erythropoietic Agents:** May increase the risk of thrombosis; use with caution. [Australian]
- **Metabolism:** Lenalidomide is not a substrate, inhibitor, or inducer of P450 enzymes. It is a weak substrate of P-gp but not an inhibitor. [Australian]

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy Category:** **Category X [Australian]**. Use is prohibited unless the Pregnancy Prevention Program is strictly followed. Females must use effective contraception for 4 weeks before, during (including dose interruptions), and 4 weeks after therapy. 
- **Male Patients:** Lenalidomide is present in semen. Men must use condoms if their partner is of childbearing potential. Sperm donation is prohibited during and for 7 days after therapy. [Australian]
- **Breastfeeding:** Unknown if excreted in human milk. Discontinuation of nursing is recommended. [Australian]
- **Fertility:** Animal studies showed no adverse effects on fertility at doses >70-fold human exposure. [Australian]

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** No experience in patients aged 0–18 years; use is not recommended. [Australian]
- **Elderly Use:** No adjustment required for age alone, though renal function must be monitored. Patients >75 years with MM may require a reduced starting dose of dexamethasone. [Australian]
- **Renal Impairment:** Dose adjustments are mandatory based on Creatinine Clearance (CLcr):
    - **Moderate (30 ≤ CLcr < 60 mL/min):** Adjust to 10 mg daily (for 25 mg baseline) or 5 mg daily (for 10 mg baseline).
    - **Severe (CLcr < 30 mL/min, not on dialysis):** Adjust to 15 mg every other day or 5 mg every other day.
    - **ESRD (on dialysis):** 5 mg daily or 5 mg three times weekly (post-dialysis). [Australian]
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not formally studied; no specific dose recommendations. Monitor for hepatotoxicity. [Australian]

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Pleiotropic effects including immunomodulatory, anti-neoplastic, anti-angiogenic, and pro-erythropoietic properties. It inhibits proliferation of malignant cells (MM, del 5q MDS), enhances T-cell and NK-cell mediated immunity, and inhibits pro-inflammatory cytokines (TNF-α, IL-6). [Australian]
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Rapidly absorbed (Tmax 0.6–1.5h). High-fat meals reduce Cmax by 50%.
    - **Distribution:** Low plasma protein binding (~23–29%).
    - **Elimination:** Primarily renal (65–85% excreted unchanged in urine). Terminal half-life is approximately 3–4 hours (increases with dose and renal impairment). [Australian]

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **SWOG-S0777 (Newly Diagnosed MM):** Rbd combination showed superior PFS (42.5 vs 29.9 months) and OS compared to Rd alone.
- **MM-020 (Transplant Ineligible MM):** Continuous Rd showed significantly prolonged PFS compared to MPT (melphalan, prednisone, thalidomide).
- **CALGB 100104 & IFM 2005-02 (MM Maintenance):** Maintenance therapy post-ASCT significantly improved PFS and OS compared to placebo.
- **MDS-004:** In del 5q MDS, lenalidomide achieved transfusion independence in 60.9% of patients compared to 7.5% for placebo. [Australian]

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store below 25°C. [Australian]
- **Handling:** Keep in the original package. Dispose of unused capsules via a pharmacy.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule:** **Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine) [Australian]**.
- **Special Access:** Restricted availability via the Cipla Lenalidomide-Pregnancy Prevention Program. [Australian]
