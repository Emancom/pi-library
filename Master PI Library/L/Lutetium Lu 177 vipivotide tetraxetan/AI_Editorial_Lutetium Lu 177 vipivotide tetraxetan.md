# Editorial Product Information: Pluvicto (Lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan)

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA). 
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of the product information for **Pluvicto**, synthesised for use by healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not replace official, jurisdiction-specific Product Information documents required for prescribing. At the time of writing, only the United States (FDA) source documentation was provided for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan.
- **Chemical Class:** Radioligand therapeutic agent; a prostate-specific membrane antigen (PSMA)-binding ligand radiolabeled with the beta-emitting radioisotope lutetium-177.
- **Brand Name:** **Pluvicto** [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Injection (solution). 1,000 MBq/mL (27 mCi/mL) of lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan in a single-dose vial [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Novartis Pharmaceuticals Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
**Pluvicto** is indicated for the treatment of adult patients with PSMA-positive metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (mCRPC) who have been treated with androgen receptor pathway inhibitor (ARPI) therapy and:
- Are considered appropriate to delay taxane-based chemotherapy [US-approved], or
- Have received prior taxane-based chemotherapy [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Patient Selection
Treatment eligibility must be confirmed by PSMA expression in tumors using an approved PSMA positron emission tomography (PET) imaging agent (e.g., LOCAMETZ) [US].

### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Recommended Dose:** 7.4 GBq (200 mCi) intravenously every 6 weeks.
- **Treatment Duration:** Up to 6 doses total, or until disease progression/unacceptable toxicity.

### Administration Instructions
- **Preparation:** Use aseptic technique and radiation shielding (e.g., lead shielding, tongs). Verify the dose in a calibrated dose calibrator.
- **Methods:** Administration may occur via:
    1. **Syringe Method:** Slow IV push (1–10 minutes) via an exclusive catheter.
    2. **Gravity Method:** Infusion over approximately 30 minutes.
    3. **Peristaltic Pump Method:** Infusion at approximately 25 mL/h.
- **Pre-administration:** Flush the dedicated IV line with ≥ 10 mL of 0.9% sodium chloride to ensure patency and minimize extravasation risk.

### Dosage Modifications for Adverse Reactions
- **Dose Reduction:** If a reduction is required, the dose is reduced by 20% to 5.9 GBq (160 mCi) once. Dose re-escalation is not permitted.
- **Dose Interruption:** Withhold treatment if treatment-related toxicity persists. If a delay exceeds 4 weeks, treatment should generally be discontinued.
- **Permanent Discontinuation:** Required for recurrent Grade 3/4 myelosuppression, Grade ≥3 renal toxicity, or other intolerable Grade 3/4 toxicities despite dose reduction [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None specified in available sources [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Radiation Exposure:** **Pluvicto** contributes to long-term cumulative radiation exposure, which is associated with an increased risk of cancer. To minimize exposure to medical personnel and household contacts:
    - Increase oral fluid intake and frequent voiding.
    - Limit close contact (<3 feet) with others for 2 days; with children/pregnant women for 7 days.
    - Sleep in a separate room from others for 3 days; from children for 7 days; from pregnant women for 15 days [US].
- **Myelosuppression:** Severe and life-threatening cases (anemia, thrombocytopenia, leukopenia, neutropenia) have occurred, including fatalities due to bone marrow failure. Monitor complete blood counts (CBC) at baseline and during treatment.
- **Renal Toxicity:** Severe renal toxicity (including acute kidney injury) has been reported. Advise hydration and monitor serum creatinine and creatinine clearance (CLcr) regularly.
- **Embryo-Fetal Toxicity:** Based on its radioactive mechanism, **Pluvicto** can cause fetal harm. 
- **Infertility:** Radiation absorbed by the testes may cause temporary or permanent male infertility [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reaction data is primarily derived from the VISION and PSMAfore trials.

### Very Common (>10%)
- **General:** Fatigue (48–53%), peripheral edema (10%), pyrexia.
- **Gastrointestinal:** Dry mouth (39–61%), nausea (32–36%), constipation (20–22%), diarrhea (17–19%), vomiting (11–19%), abdominal pain (12%), decreased appetite (21–22%).
- **Musculoskeletal:** Back pain (14–24%), arthralgia (20–22%), bone pain (11%).
- **Renal:** Urinary tract infection (12%), decreased eGFR (23–43%).
- **Hematology (Laboratory):** Decreased lymphocytes (78–85%), decreased hemoglobin (64–67%), decreased platelets (30–45%), decreased neutrophils (28–38%).

### Serious Adverse Effects
- **Pancytopenia:** Reported in 1.1% of patients in the VISION study, including fatal cases [US].
- **Hemorrhage:** Intracranial and subdural hematoma associated with thrombocytopenia.
- **Sepsis:** Occurred in both major trials, sometimes with fatal outcomes.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **CYP450 Interactions:** Lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan is not a substrate, inducer, or inhibitor of major CYP450 enzymes (1A2, 2B6, 3A4, 2C8, 2C9, 2C19, 2D6) *in vitro* [US].
- **Transporters:** The drug does not inhibit BCRP, P-gp, MATE1, MATE2-K, OAT1, OAT3, OATP1B1, OATP1B3, or OCT1/2 *in vitro* [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Safety and efficacy have not been established in females. Radiopharmaceuticals can cause fetal harm.
- **Breastfeeding:** No data available. Safety in females is not established as the drug is indicated for male patients with prostate cancer.
- **Contraception (Males):** Males with female partners of reproductive potential should use effective contraception during treatment and for **14 weeks** after the final dose [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established in pediatric patients [US].
- **Geriatric Use:** No overall differences in effectiveness were observed between patients ≥75 years and younger patients. However, Grade ≥3 adverse reactions were more frequent in the elderly (56% vs 53% in the VISION study) [US].
- **Renal Impairment:**
    - **Mild to Moderate (CLcr 30–89 mL/min):** No dose adjustment recommended; however, exposure increases and patients may be at higher risk of toxicity.
    - **Severe/ESRD (CLcr <30 mL/min):** Not studied; the potential for increased exposure is significant [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not studied. The drug does not undergo hepatic metabolism [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** The agent binds to PSMA-expressing cells. Upon internalization, the $Lu^{177}$ isotope releases beta-minus emission, delivering radiation to the target cell and surrounding microenvironment, causing DNA damage and cell death.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** At the recommended dose, the drug does not cause clinically significant QTc interval prolongation (>20 ms).
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** $V_d$ is 123 L. It distributes to the GI tract, liver, lungs, kidneys, and salivary glands within 2.5 hours.
    - **Plasma Protein Binding:** 60% to 70%.
    - **Metabolism:** Not metabolised by the liver or kidneys.
    - **Elimination:** Primarily renal. Terminal half-life is 41.6 hours. Clearance is 2.04 L/h.

## 11. Clinical Trials
### PSMAfore (mCRPC, Pre-taxane)
A randomized (1:1) trial of **Pluvicto** vs. ARPI change in 468 patients.
- **Outcome:** Significant improvement in radiographic progression-free survival (rPFS): 9.3 months vs. 5.6 months (HR 0.41, p < 0.0001) [US].

### VISION (mCRPC, Post-taxane)
A randomized (2:1) trial of **Pluvicto** + Best Standard of Care (BSoC) vs. BSoC alone in 831 patients.
- **Outcome:** Median overall survival (OS) was 15.3 months vs. 11.3 months (HR 0.62, p < 0.001). Median rPFS was also significantly improved [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store below $30^{\circ}C$ ($86^{\circ}F$). Do not freeze.
- **Shelf Life:** 120 hours (5 days) from the date and time of calibration [US].
- **Handling:** Store in the original lead-shielded package. Disposal must comply with local/federal regulations for radioactive materials.

- **Legal Classification:** Prescription Only (Rx Only) [US].
- **Poison/Schedule Classification:** Not specified in the provided US documentation (typically handled under nuclear regulatory licensure).
