# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial document has been synthesized from official Product Information (PI) source material provided by the following jurisdiction: **US (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, consolidating clinical and regulatory data into a single reference.
- **Note:** This document is an editorial synthesis for informational purposes and is not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific Product Information required for prescribing in any specific region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (Generic name); chemical name: $(R)$-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-$N,N,N$-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Sterile solution for injection: 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only [US].
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** For the acute and chronic treatment of secondary carnitine deficiency resulting from metabolic errors [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
- **General Dosing:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg.
- **Administration:** Administer as a slow 2-3 minute bolus injection or by infusion.
- **Severe Crisis:** A loading dose is often administered, followed by equivalent doses every 3 to 4 hours (never less than every 6 hours) over the subsequent 24 hours.
- **Maintenance:** Daily maintenance doses are typically 50 mg/kg, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilised based on clinical requirement [US].

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight.
- **Administration:** Administer as a slow 2-3 minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
- **Dose Adjustment:** Adjustments (e.g., down to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations [US].

### Administration Instructions and Monitoring
- **Monitoring:** Obtain plasma carnitine concentrations prior to therapy. Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma concentrations (target: 35 to 60 µmol/L) is recommended [US].
- **Visual Inspection:** Parenteral products must be inspected for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Known Contraindications:** None known at the time of publication [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warnings
- None listed in available sources [US].

### General Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions may occur within minutes of injection. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and initiate appropriate medical care [US].
- **Seizures:** Levocarnitine has been associated with seizures in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been reported [US].
- **Renal Insufficiency (Oral Formulation):** The safety of *oral* levocarnitine has not been evaluated in renal impairment. Chronic high-dose oral administration in ESRD patients may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites, trimethylamine (TMA) and trimethylamine-N-oxide (TMAO) [US].

### Monitoring Requirements
- Trough plasma levocarnitine levels (target 40–50 µmol/L).
- International Normalised Ratio (INR) in patients co-administered with warfarin [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common and Less Frequent Reactions
- **General:** Transient nausea, vomiting, body odor, and gastritis [US].

### ESRD Clinical Trial Data (Frequency ≥5%)
The following table summarises adverse events reported in double-blind, placebo-controlled trials for ESRD patients, regardless of causality [US]:

| Body System | Adverse Event | Frequency (%) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Body as Whole** | Injection site reaction, Pain, Headache, Infection, Chest pain, Flu syndrome | 22% – 33% |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension, Hypotension, Tachycardia | 6% – 20% |

### Serious Adverse Effects
- **Anaphylaxis and Angioedema:** Potentially life-threatening hypersensitivity [US].
- **Seizures:** Risk of new-onset or exacerbated seizure activity [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in INR when used concurrently with levocarnitine. **Management:** Monitor INR levels frequently after initiating levocarnitine or following dose adjustments in patients on warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not specified; animal studies used 3.8x human dose]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits revealed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. However, there are no adequate, well-controlled studies in pregnant women. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk concentrations following administration. The benefits to the mother should be weighed against the potential risk of excess carnitine intake in the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** Mutagenicity tests were negative; however, long-term animal carcinogenicity studies have not been performed [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders and ESRD is based on mg/kg body weight (refer to Section 3) [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically detailed in source documents [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Used specifically for ESRD patients on dialysis. Note the warning regarding the accumulation of metabolites (TMA/TMAO) if using oral formulations in this population [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring carrier molecule required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria for oxidation and energy production [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In patients with metabolic errors or ESRD, levocarnitine promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters, which are rapidly eliminated [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and $\gamma$-butyrobetaine (feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Following IV administration, approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. The mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours; the mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Efficacy:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients showed that 20 mg/kg doses three times weekly post-dialysis increased mean pre-dialysis plasma levocarnitine from 20 µmol/L to 190 µmol/L.
- **Dose-Ranging:** A study comparing 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses confirmed dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks.
- **Outcome Limitation:** While levocarnitine increases carnitine plasma concentrations, the effects on specific clinical outcomes in the ESRD population have not been determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Avoid excessive heat and protect from freezing.
- **Stability:** Compatible and stable for 24 hours in PVC bags when mixed with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) [US].
- **Preservative Status:** Does not contain preservatives; discard any unused portion of opened vials [US].
