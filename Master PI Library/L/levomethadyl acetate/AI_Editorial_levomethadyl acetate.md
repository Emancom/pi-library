# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- Contributing Jurisdictions: United States (FDA)
- Purpose: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of levocarnitine for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises clinical and regulatory data from the official Product Information provided.
- Note: This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP). It is a carrier molecule for long-chain fatty acid transport across the inner mitochondrial membrane.
- **Chemical Class:** (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency (Metabolic):** Acute and chronic management of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that result in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg. It may be administered as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) or via infusion. 
- **ESRD on Hemodialysis:** The suggested starting dose is 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight).

### Administration Instructions
- **Metabolic Crisis:** A loading dose is frequently administered in patients experiencing severe metabolic crisis, followed by equivalent doses over the subsequent 24 hours. Dosing intervals should be every 3 to 4 hours, and no less frequent than every 6 hours [US].
- **Hemodialysis Administration:** Administer as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].
- **Visual Inspection:** Parenteral products must be inspected for particulate matter and discoloration before administration [US].

### Titration and Monitoring
- **Metabolic Disorders:** Subsequent daily doses typically range from 50 mg/kg to as high as 300 mg/kg as required by the clinical condition.
- **ESRD Titration:** Dose adjustments should be guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Reductions (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be implemented as early as the third or fourth week of therapy if levels are adequate [US].
- **Monitoring:** Obtain baseline plasma carnitine levels. Monitor weekly and monthly, including blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma concentrations (target free carnitine: 35 to 60 µmol/L) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known at the time of publication [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warnings
- Not specified in available sources.

### General Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have occurred—primarily in ESRD patients. Some reactions manifest within minutes of intravenous administration [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure disorders. In those with a known seizure history, the frequency and/or severity of seizures may increase [US].

### Monitoring Requirements
- **INR Monitoring:** Increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) has been reported when levocarnitine is used concomitantly with warfarin. Monitor INR following the initiation of levocarnitine or after dose adjustments in patients on warfarin therapy [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Serious Adverse Effects
- **Anaphylaxis and Severe Hypersensitivity:** Immediate medical intervention is required if these occur.
- **Seizures:** Potentially increased frequency in predisposed patients.

### Common Adverse Effects (Clinical Trial Experience in ESRD)
Adverse events reported at a frequency of ≥5% regardless of causality in dialysis patients [US]:
- **Body as a Whole:** Abdominal pain (5–21%), headache (3–37%), injection site reaction (27–38%), pain (21–35%), fever (5–12%), flu syndrome (15–29%).
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (18–21%), hypotension (3–19%), chest pain (6–15%), tachycardia (5–9%).
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea (9–35%), nausea (5–12%), vomiting (9–21%).
- **Metabolic/Nutritional:** Hypercalcemia (6–15%), hypervolemia (3–12%).
- **Nervous System:** Dizziness (10–18%), paresthesia (3–12%).
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (15–27%), cough increase (10–18%), dyspnea (3–11%).

### Other Reported Effects
- **Transient Gastrointestinal:** Nausea and vomiting.
- **Body Odor:** Reported as a less frequent reaction.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Concomitant use may increase INR. Management: Monitor INR levels closely [US].
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not explicitly assigned, but equivalent to Category C]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. There are no adequate/controlled studies in pregnant women; use only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine concentrations in milk after exogenous administration. Risks to the child of excess intake should be weighed against the benefits of maternal supplementation [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility was found in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing and administration guidelines for metabolic disorders apply to pediatric patients [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Safety/efficacy of *oral* levocarnitine has not been evaluated in renal insufficiency; chronic high doses of oral formulations in ESRD may lead to accumulation of toxic metabolites (TMA and TMAO). This concern is primarily associated with oral rather than intravenous administration [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
Levocarnitine facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters, which are rapidly cleared [US].

### Pharmacokinetics
- **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin. Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours [US].
- **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
- **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an intravenous dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. The mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Pharmacokinetic Study:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients receiving 20 mg/kg three times weekly after dialysis showed that trough levocarnitine concentrations increased from a baseline of ~21 µmol/L to ~190 µmol/L after 8 weeks [US].
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** Studies of 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses in ESRD patients (N≈30 per group) demonstrated dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine levels over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Precautions:** Avoid excessive heat and protect from freezing [US].
- **Stability:** Compatible with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer's (0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) for up to 24 hours at room temperature in PVC bags [US].
- **Handling:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as it contains no preservative [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison/Schedule:** Not specified in available sources.
