# Editorial Product Information: Levomilnacipran

## Editorial Note
- Contributing Jurisdictions: United States (FDA)
- Purpose: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of levomilnacipran product information for use by healthcare professionals. 
- Disclaimer: This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. At the time of this publication, only the United States PI was available for synthesis.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient**: Levomilnacipran hydrochloride, a selective serotonin and norepinephrine reuptake inhibitor (SNRI). It is the 1S,2R-enantiomer of milnacipran.
- **Brand Name(s)**: **FETZIMA** [US]
- **ATC Code**: Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s)**: Extended-release capsules: 20 mg, 40 mg, 80 mg, and 120 mg [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration**: Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor**: AbbVie, Inc. (Licensed from Pierre Fabre Medicament) [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Major Depressive Disorder (MDD)**: Indicated for the treatment of MDD in adult patients [US].
- **Limitations of Use**: Levomilnacipran is not approved for the management of fibromyalgia; its safety and efficacy for this condition have not been established [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult)**: The recommended maintenance dosage is 40 mg to 120 mg once daily [US].
- **Titration Schedule**: 
    - **Initial**: 20 mg once daily for 2 days.
    - **Step-up**: Increase to 40 mg once daily.
    - **Escalation**: Based on clinical response and tolerability, dose may be increased in increments of 40 mg at intervals of 2 or more days [US].
- **Maximum Dose**: 120 mg once daily [US].
- **Administration Instructions**: 
    - Take at approximately the same time each day with or without food.
    - Capsules must be swallowed whole; do not open, chew, or crush [US].
- **Missed Dose**: Take the missed dose as soon as remembered. If it is almost time for the next dose, skip the missed dose and resume the regular schedule. Do not double doses [US].
- **Discontinuation**: Avoid abrupt cessation. Gradually reduce the dose to mitigate discontinuation symptoms [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity**: Known hypersensitivity to levomilnacipran, milnacipran HCl, or any excipients in the formulation [US].
- **Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors (MAOIs)**:
    - Concomitant use with MAOIs intended to treat psychiatric disorders.
    - Use within 14 days of stopping an MAOI intended to treat psychiatric disorders.
    - Initiation of an MAOI within 7 days of stopping levomilnacipran [US].
- **Linezolid/Methylene Blue**: Starting levomilnacipran in patients being treated with linezolid or intravenous methylene blue is contraindicated due to an increased risk of serotonin syndrome [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning: Suicidal Thoughts and Behaviors**: Antidepressants increase the risk of suicidal thoughts and behaviors in pediatric and young adult patients. Levomilnacipran is not approved for use in pediatric patients. Closely monitor for clinical worsening or emergent suicidality, especially during the first few months of therapy or at dose changes [US].
- **Serotonin Syndrome**: Potentially life-threatening condition. Risk is increased with other serotonergic agents (e.g., triptans, TCAs, fentanyl, lithium, St. John's Wort). Discontinue immediately if symptoms (e.g., autonomic instability, neuromuscular hyperactivity, mental status changes) occur [US].
- **Elevated Blood Pressure and Heart Rate**: SNRIs can increase blood pressure (mean increase ~3 mmHg) and heart rate (mean increase ~7.4 bpm). Pre-existing hypertension must be controlled before starting therapy. Monitor BP and HR regularly [US].
- **Increased Risk of Bleeding**: Concomitant use of NSAIDs, aspirin, or anticoagulants may increase the risk of bleeding events, including gastrointestinal bleeding [US].
- **Angle-Closure Glaucoma**: May trigger an angle-closure attack in patients with untreated anatomically narrow angles [US].
- **Urinary Hesitation or Retention**: Noradrenergic effects may affect urethral resistance. Caution in patients prone to obstructive urinary disorders [US].
- **Activation of Mania/Hypomania**: Screen patients for bipolar disorder prior to initiation [US].
- **Seizures**: Use with caution in patients with a history of seizure disorders [US].
- **Hyponatremia**: May occur in association with SIADH, particularly in the elderly or volume-depleted patients [US].
- **Sexual Dysfunction**: May cause symptoms such as erectile dysfunction, ejaculation disorder, or decreased libido [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions observed in clinical trials (incidence ≥ 5% and at least twice the rate of placebo) [US]:

| Adverse Reaction | Levomilnacipran (%) | Placebo (%) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| Nausea | 17 | 6 |
| Constipation | 9 | 3 |
| Hyperhidrosis | 9 | 2 |
| Heart rate increased | 6 | 1 |
| Erectile dysfunction (males) | 6 | 1 |
| Ejaculation disorder (males) | 5 | <1 |
| Tachycardia | 6 | 2 |
| Vomiting | 5 | 1 |
| Palpitations | 5 | 1 |

- **Serious Adverse Effects**: Suicidality, serotonin syndrome, hypertensive crisis, and severe hyponatremia [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Strong CYP3A4 Inhibitors**: (e.g., ketoconazole, clarithromycin) Increase levomilnacipran exposure. The dose of levomilnacipran should not exceed **80 mg once daily** [US].
- **CYP3A4 Inducers**: May reduce levomilnacipran levels; however, no specific dose adjustment is provided [US].
- **Serotonergic Drugs**: Increased risk of serotonin syndrome.
- **Hemostasis-affecting Drugs**: (e.g., NSAIDs, warfarin) Increased risk of bleeding.
- **Alcohol**: *In vitro* studies show 40% alcohol can cause rapid release ("dose dumping") of the entire dose in 4 hours. **Avoid concomitant use of alcohol** [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy**: 
    - **Jurisdiction Category**: Not specified (PLLR format used in source) [US].
    - **Clinical Considerations**: Exposure in the month before delivery increases the risk of postpartum hemorrhage. Third-trimester exposure may lead to neonatal complications requiring prolonged hospitalisation (e.g., respiratory distress, temperature instability, irritability) [US].
- **Breastfeeding**: Levomilnacipran is likely present in human milk (based on racemic milnacipran data). Monitor breastfed infants for agitation, irritability, and poor weight gain [US].
- **Fertility**: No effects on fertility were observed in animal studies at doses up to 8 times the maximum recommended human dose (MRHD) [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use**: Safety and effectiveness have not been established. Trials in patients aged 7–17 years failed to show superiority over placebo [US].
- **Elderly Use**: No dose adjustment based on age alone. However, elderly patients are at higher risk for hyponatremia and may have reduced renal clearance [US].
- **Renal Impairment**:
    - **Mild (CrCl 60-89 mL/min)**: No adjustment.
    - **Moderate (CrCl 30-59 mL/min)**: Maximum 80 mg once daily.
    - **Severe (CrCl 15-29 mL/min)**: Maximum 40 mg once daily.
    - **ESRD**: Not recommended [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment**: No dose adjustment recommended for mild, moderate, or severe hepatic impairment [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action**: Potentiation of serotonin and norepinephrine in the CNS through potent and selective reuptake inhibition at the transporters (SNRI) [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics**: High affinity for human serotonin (Ki = 11 nM) and norepinephrine (Ki = 91 nM) transporters. Does not prolong QTc to a relevant extent at 2.5x the maximum dose [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics**:
    - **Absorption**: Bioavailability 92%; Tmax 6–8 hours. Not affected by food.
    - **Distribution**: Plasma protein binding 22%.
    - **Metabolism**: Primarily via CYP3A4 (desethylation).
    - **Elimination**: Half-life ~12 hours. Primarily renal excretion (58% as unchanged drug) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Short-term Efficacy**: Three 8-week, randomised, placebo-controlled studies in adults demonstrated superiority over placebo on the Montgomery-Asberg Depression Rating Scale (MADRS) and Sheehan Disability Scale (SDS) [US].
- **Maintenance**: A 26-week, double-blind maintenance study showed a statistically significantly longer time to relapse for patients continuing levomilnacipran compared to those switched to placebo [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature**: Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
- **Shelf Life**: Not specified in available sources.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification**: 
    - **United States**: Rx-only; not a controlled substance.
