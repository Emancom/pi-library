# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine Injection

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following regulatory authority: **US (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, consolidating clinical, pharmacological, and regulatory data.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not intended to substitute for the official PI or local prescribing guidelines in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority's approved labeling for definitive prescribing information.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP). It is the (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium inner salt.
- **Chemical Class:** A naturally occurring carrier molecule required for mammalian energy metabolism (fatty acid transporter).
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01 (Not explicitly stated in source, but standard classification for levocarnitine).
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Clear, colorless sterile solution for intravenous use, containing 1 g of levocarnitine per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency in Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients where an inborn metabolic error results in carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD) on Hemodialysis:** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Metabolic Disorders)
- **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg given as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion [US].
- **Severe Metabolic Crisis:** A loading dose may be given, followed by equivalent doses administered every 3 or 4 hours (q3h/q4h). Doses should never be spaced less than every 6 hours (q6h) [US].
- **Maximum Dose:** The highest reported dose administered is 300 mg/kg [US].

### Dosing in ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
- **Administration Timing:** Administer as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].
- **Dose Adjustment:** Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be made as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine levels [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter and discoloration before administration [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable and compatible for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer's at concentrations from 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Known Hypersensitivity:** No specific contraindications are listed in the provided source, but hypersensitivity reactions are addressed in the warnings [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm have been reported. These can occur within minutes of IV administration, particularly in ESRD patients on dialysis. Discontinue immediately if a reaction occurs [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with known seizure disorders, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Renal Insufficiency (Oral Formulation Precaution):** The safety and efficacy of *oral* levocarnitine have not been evaluated in renal failure. Chronic high-dose oral administration in ESRD may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) [US].
- **Monitoring:** 
    - Obtain baseline plasma carnitine levels.
    - Monitor plasma free carnitine concentrations (target range: 35 to 60 µmol/L).
    - Monitor blood chemistries, vital signs, and overall clinical condition [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions reported in clinical trials for ESRD patients on hemodialysis (incidence ≥5%):

| Body System | Adverse Effect |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Chest pain, fever, headache, infection, injection site reaction, abdominal pain, asthenia |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension, hypotension, tachycardia, arrhythmia |
| **Gastrointestinal** | Diarrhea, nausea, vomiting, dyspepsia |
| **Metabolic** | Hypercalcemia, hypervolemia, hyperkalemia |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis, cough, dyspnea, rhinitis |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness, insomnia, paresthesia, depression |
| **Musculoskeletal** | Leg cramps |

- **Postmarketing Experience:** Seizures and serious hypersensitivity reactions (anaphylaxis) have been identified during post-approval use [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have occurred when levocarnitine is used with warfarin. It is recommended to monitor INR levels after initiating treatment or adjusting the dose [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not specified by letter, but reproductive studies in rats/rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed] [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Specific studies in nursing mothers have not been conducted. Levocarnitine concentrations increase in dairy cow milk following exogenous administration. Risks to the child must be weighed against maternal benefits [US].
- **Fertility:** Mutagenicity tests (Salmonella, etc.) were negative. No long-term animal studies exist for carcinogenic potential [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Use is supported by dosage recommendations for metabolic disorders (mg/kg basis); however, clinical outcomes specifically in the ESRD paediatric population are not detailed in the available source [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Primarily indicated for ESRD on dialysis. Dose adjustments are based on trough concentrations post-dialysis [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production. It also facilitates the excretion of excess organic/fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
    - **Elimination:** Terminal half-life is approximately 17.4 hours. About 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In ESRD, dialysis causes carnitine loss; IV administration increases plasma levocarnitine concentrations [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients, 20 mg/kg IV post-dialysis three times weekly increased mean pre-dialysis trough concentrations from ~20 µmol/L to 190 ± 55 µmol/L after 8 weeks [US].
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** Studies comparing 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses showed a dose-proportional increase in trough levocarnitine levels over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat. Vials are single-dose and contain no preservatives; discard any unused portion [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Prescription Only (Rx-only) [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available US source.
