# Editorial Product Information: levocetirizine dihydrochloride

## 1. Product Identification
- Active ingredient (INN/generic name): **levocetirizine dihydrochloride**
- Chemical class: Antihistamine
- ATC code: Not specified in available sources
- Route(s) of administration: Oral

- Withdrawal, suspension, or restriction history: Not specified in available sources.

## 3. Approved Indications
- Temporarily relieves symptoms due to hay fever or other respiratory allergies, including:
  - runny nose
  - sneezing
  - itchy, watery eyes
  - itching of the nose or throat

## 4. Dosage and Administration
### General dosing (adult and paediatric)
- Adults and children 12–64 years: 5 mL or 10 mL once daily in the evening depending upon severity of symptoms. Do not exceed 10 mL in 24 hours.
- Adults 65 years and older: ask a doctor before use.
- Children 6–11 years: 5 mL once daily in the evening. Do not exceed 5 mL in 24 hours.
- Children 2–5 years: 2.5 mL once daily in the evening. Do not exceed 2.5 mL in 24 hours.
- Children under 2 years: do not use.

### Administration instructions
- Use only with the enclosed dosing cup (or equivalent measured device as provided).
- Take once daily in the evening.
- In case of overdose, seek medical help or contact a Poison Control Center immediately.

### Titration schedules
- Not specified in available sources.

### Missed dose instructions
- Not specified in available sources.

## 5. Contraindications
- **Do not use in patients with kidney disease.**
- Known hypersensitivity to this product or any of its ingredients.
- Known hypersensitivity to an antihistamine containing cetirizine.

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed warnings
- None reported in the provided source.

### General warnings and precautions
- Drowsiness/somnolence may occur.
- Avoid alcoholic beverages while using this product.
- Alcohol, sedatives, and tranquilizers may increase drowsiness.
- Be cautious when driving a motor vehicle or operating machinery while taking this product.
- Ask a doctor before use if the patient has ever had trouble urinating or emptying the bladder.
- In rare cases, itching (pruritus) may occur after stopping levocetirizine; consult a healthcare provider if this occurs.
- Stop use and ask a doctor if you have trouble urinating or if an allergic reaction to this product occurs. Seek medical help right away for suspected severe allergic reactions.

### Monitoring requirements
- Specific monitoring parameters and intervals: Not specified in available sources.

### Specific clinical precautions
- **Renal impairment:** Do not use in patients with kidney disease (see Contraindications).
- **Elderly:** Adults ≥65 years should consult a physician prior to use.

## 8. Drug Interactions
### Pharmacodynamic interactions
- Alcohol may increase drowsiness produced by levocetirizine.
- Concomitant use of sedatives and tranquilizers may increase drowsiness; use caution and consider avoiding concurrent CNS depressants.

### Pharmacokinetic interactions
- No pharmacokinetic interaction data are provided in the source: Not specified in available sources.

### Clinically significant interactions and management
- Avoid alcohol and exercise caution when combining with other CNS depressant agents (sedatives, tranquilizers). Monitor for increased sedation and impaired ability to perform tasks requiring alertness (e.g., driving).
