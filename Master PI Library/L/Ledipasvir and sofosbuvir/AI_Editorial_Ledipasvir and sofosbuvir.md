# Editorial Product Information: Ledipasvir and Sofosbuvir

**Editorial Note:** This unified Product Information (PI) document has been compiled by an expert editorial team. It synthesizes regulatory data and clinical guidance from two primary sources: the Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA) of Australia (current as of March 2024) and the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) of the United States (revised November 2019). This document is intended for use by healthcare professionals and combines the most comprehensive and conservative guidance from both agencies to ensure a global standard of care.

---

## 1. Product Name and Active Ingredient
**Product Name:** HARVONI
**Active Ingredients:** Ledipasvir (90 mg) and Sofosbuvir (400 mg).

HARVONI is a fixed-dose combination tablet. Each tablet contains 90 mg of ledipasvir and 400 mg of sofosbuvir. 
*   **Physical Appearance:** The tablets are film-coated and diamond-shaped, debossed with "GSI" on one side and "7985" on the other. 
*   **Color:** Tablets are described as orange [TGA] or white [FDA].

## 2. Approved Indications
HARVONI is indicated for the treatment of chronic hepatitis C (CHC) infection in the following populations:

*   **Adults:** For genotypes 1, 4, 5, or 6 infection, including patients without cirrhosis or those with compensated cirrhosis.
*   **Decompensated Cirrhosis:** In patients with genotype 1 infection, used in combination with ribavirin [FDA, TGA].
*   **Liver Transplant Recipients:** In patients with genotype 1 or 4 infection without cirrhosis or with compensated cirrhosis, used in combination with ribavirin [FDA, TGA].
*   **Pediatrics:** Indicated for children 12 years of age and older, or weighing at least 35 kg, with genotype 1, 4, 5, or 6 infection without cirrhosis or with compensated cirrhosis [FDA].

## 3. Contraindications
*   **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to ledipasvir, sofosbuvir, or any excipient (e.g., lactose).
*   **Co-administration:** Should not be administered with other medicinal products containing ledipasvir or sofosbuvir.
*   **Combination Therapy:** When used in combination with ribavirin, all contraindications for ribavirin apply, including pregnancy and partners of pregnant women.

## 4. Warnings and Precautions

### Hepatitis B Virus (HBV) Reactivation
**Boxed Warning:** All patients must be screened for evidence of current or prior HBV infection (HBsAg and anti-HBc) before starting HARVONI. HBV reactivation, including fatal cases, has occurred during or after direct-acting antiviral (DAA) treatment in patients not receiving HBV antiviral therapy. Monitor for clinical and laboratory signs of hepatitis flare or reactivation during treatment and post-treatment follow-up.

### Serious Symptomatic Bradycardia
Severe bradycardia and cases requiring pacemaker intervention have been reported when amiodarone is co-administered with HARVONI. This effect typically occurs within hours to days of initiation. Co-administration is not recommended. If no alternative exists, continuous cardiac monitoring in an inpatient setting is required for the first 48 hours, followed by daily monitoring for at least two weeks.

### Drug Interaction Potential
*   **P-gp Inducers:** Potent P-gp inducers (e.g., rifampin, St. John’s Wort) may significantly reduce plasma concentrations of ledipasvir and sofosbuvir, leading to loss of therapeutic effect. These should not be used with HARVONI.
*   **Diabetes and Dysglycaemia:** Rapid reduction in HCV viral load may improve glucose metabolism, potentially resulting in symptomatic hypoglycaemia in diabetic patients. Monitor blood glucose levels closely during the first three months of therapy and adjust diabetic medications as necessary [TGA].

## 5. Dosage and Administration
The recommended dose is **one tablet once daily**, taken orally with or without food.

### Recommended Durations (Adults)
| Patient Population | Regimen | Duration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Genotype 1, 4, 5, 6:** Treatment-naïve (no cirrhosis or compensated cirrhosis) | HARVONI | 12 weeks* |
| **Genotype 1:** Treatment-experienced (no cirrhosis) | HARVONI | 12 weeks |
| **Genotype 1:** Treatment-experienced (compensated cirrhosis) | HARVONI | 24 weeks** |
| **Genotype 1:** Decompensated cirrhosis (regardless of transplant status) | HARVONI + ribavirin | 12 weeks |
| **Genotype 1 or 4:** Liver transplant recipients (no or compensated cirrhosis) | HARVONI + ribavirin | 12 weeks |

*\*8 weeks can be considered for treatment-naïve genotype 1 patients without cirrhosis who have pre-treatment HCV RNA < 6 million IU/mL.*
*\*\*12 weeks of HARVONI + ribavirin may be considered for treatment-experienced genotype 1 patients with compensated cirrhosis [FDA].*

### Renal Impairment
*   **Mild to Moderate:** No dose adjustment required.
*   **Severe (eGFR < 30 mL/min/1.73m²) or ESRD:** No dosage adjustment is recommended, including patients on dialysis [FDA]. Note: The TGA indicates that no specific dose recommendation can be made for this subgroup due to high metabolite exposure [TGA].

## 6. Adverse Effects
The most common adverse reactions (incidence ≥ 10%) reported in clinical trials were **fatigue, headache, and nausea**.

*   **In patients with cirrhosis:** Common effects included asthenia, irritability, and dyspnea.
*   **Post-marketing Surveillance:** Rare but serious reports include skin rashes (sometimes with blisters), angioedema, and Stevens-Johnson syndrome.

## 7. Drug Interactions
Ledipasvir increases the solubility of medications dependent on gastric pH; therefore, separation of doses is required for acid-reducing agents:
*   **Antacids:** Separate by 4 hours.
*   **H2-receptor antagonists:** Take simultaneously or 12 hours apart (dose ≤ famotidine 40 mg BID).
*   **PPIs:** Simultaneous administration with doses ≤ omeprazole 20 mg is permissible under fasted conditions [FDA].

**Other notable interactions:**
*   **Digoxin:** May increase digoxin levels; therapeutic monitoring is recommended.
*   **Statins:** Significant increase in rosuvastatin concentration (risk of myopathy/rhabdomyolysis). Co-administration is not recommended.
*   **HIV Antiretrovirals:** HARVONI increases tenofovir exposure. Monitor for tenofovir-associated toxicity, especially in patients with renal risk factors.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy (Category B1):** Data in pregnant women are insufficient to inform drug-associated risks. Use only if the potential benefit justifies the risk. If used with ribavirin, the combination is contraindicated in pregnancy due to ribavirin's teratogenic risk.
*   **Lactation:** It is unknown if HARVONI components are excreted in human milk. In animal studies, ledipasvir was detected in the plasma of suckling rats without adverse effects. The developmental benefits of breastfeeding should be weighed against the mother's clinical need for the drug.

## 9. Pharmacokinetics
*   **Absorption:** Ledipasvir reaches peak concentrations (Tmax) at 4–4.5 hours. Sofosbuvir is rapidly absorbed with a Tmax of 0.8–1 hour; its primary metabolite, GS-331007, reaches Tmax at 3.5–4 hours.
*   **Distribution:** Ledipasvir is >99.8% protein-bound. Sofosbuvir is approximately 61–65% protein-bound.
*   **Elimination:** Ledipasvir is primarily excreted in feces (86%). Sofosbuvir is mainly eliminated via renal excretion (80%), primarily as the GS-331007 metabolite.

## 10. Mechanism of Action
HARVONI is a combination of two direct-acting antiviral agents:
1.  **Ledipasvir:** An inhibitor of the HCV NS5A protein, which is essential for both viral RNA replication and the assembly of HCV virions.
2.  **Sofosbuvir:** A pangenotypic inhibitor of the NS5B RNA-dependent RNA polymerase. It acts as a chain terminator during viral replication.

## 11. Storage Conditions
*   Store below 30°C (86°F).
*   Dispense only in the original container and keep the bottle tightly closed to protect from moisture.
*   Do not use if the seal over the bottle opening is broken or missing.
