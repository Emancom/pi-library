# Originality Audit Report: Ledipasvir and sofosbuvir

## Audit Summary
- **Originality Score:** 68/100  
- **Verbatim Copied Passages Found:** 6  
- **Overall Assessment:** Concern

## Verbatim Copied Passages
The following passages are copied nearly word-for-word from the source documentation:

1.  **Physical Appearance (Section 1):** 
    - **Editorial:** "...diamond-shaped, debossed with 'GSI' on one side and '7985' on the other."
    - **Source:** Doc 1 (TGA), Page 1: "...diamond shaped, debossed with 'GSI' on one side and the number '7985' on the other side."
2.  **Bradycardia Warning (Section 4):**
    - **Editorial:** "...cases requiring pacemaker intervention have been reported when amiodarone is co-administered with HARVONI."
    - **Source:** Doc 1 (TGA), Page 3: "...cases requiring pacemaker intervention, have been reported when amiodarone is coadministered with a sofosbuvir-containing regimen."
3.  **8-Week Treatment Footnote (Section 5):**
    - **Editorial:** "8 weeks can be considered for treatment-naïve genotype 1 patients without cirrhosis who have pre-treatment HCV RNA < 6 million IU/mL."
    - **Source:** Doc 1 (TGA), Page 2: "HARVONI for 8 weeks can be considered in treatment-naïve genotype 1 patients without cirrhosis who have pre-treatment HCV RNA less than 6 million IU/mL."
4.  **Adverse Reactions (Section 6):**
    - **Editorial:** "The most common adverse reactions (incidence ≥ 10%) reported in clinical trials were fatigue, headache, and nausea."
    - **Source:** Doc 1 (TGA), Page 10: "...fatigue, headache and nausea were the most common (incidence ≥ 10%) treatment emergent adverse events reported..."
5.  **Pharmacokinetics - Absorption (Section 9):**
    - **Editorial:** "Ledipasvir reaches peak concentrations (Tmax) at 4–4.5 hours. Sofosbuvir is rapidly absorbed with a Tmax of 0.8–1 hour..."
    - **Source:** Doc 1 (TGA), Page 28: "...ledipasvir median peak concentrations were observed 4.0 to 4.5 hours post-dose. Sofosbuvir was absorbed quickly and the peak median plasma concentration was observed ~ 0.8 to 1 hour post-dose."
6.  **Mechanism of Action - Ledipasvir (Section 10):**
    - **Editorial:** "...inhibitor of the HCV NS5A protein, which is essential for both viral RNA replication and the assembly of HCV virions."
    - **Source:** Doc 1 (TGA), Page 13: "...HCV inhibitor targeting the HCV NS5A protein, which is essential for both RNA replication and the assembly of HCV virions."

## Close Paraphrases
- **HBV Screening (Section 4):** The instruction to screen for "HBsAg and anti-HBc" before starting treatment closely follows the phrasing in Section 4.4 of the TGA PI and Section 2.1 of the FDA PI.
- **Diabetes Warning (Section 4):** The wording regarding "Rapid reduction in HCV viral load may improve glucose metabolism" is a very close paraphrase of the "Potential for Dysglycaemia" section in the TGA PI (Page 4).
- **Lactation (Section 8):** The description of animal studies where "ledipasvir was detected in the plasma of suckling rats without adverse effects" mirrors the TGA PI (Page 10) nearly exactly, though it condenses the sentences.

## Originality Strengths
- **Section 1 (Tablet Color):** The editorial successfully notes a discrepancy between the Australian (orange) and US (white) versions of the product, which requires active comparison and synthesis.
- **Section 5 (Renal Impairment):** The editorial provides a high-quality "Note" highlighting the difference between the FDA's liberal stance on ESRD (no adjustment) and the TGA's more conservative stance (no recommendation), demonstrating genuine expert synthesis.
- **Section 7 (Drug Interactions):** The editorial does a good job of merging instruction sets for acid-reducing agents from two different tabular formats into a concise list.

## Differences from Source PIs
- **Harmonization:** The editorial explicitly cites which agency [FDA] or [TGA] provided specific guidance when they differ (e.g., pediatric age limits and renal impairment).
- **Consolidation:** The editorial removes extensive clinical trial data (SVR rates, etc.) to focus on a "standard of care" summary.
- **Editorial Voice:** The introduction and "Editorial Note" are entirely original.
- **Color Correction:** Correctly identifies that the TGA PI (March 2024) specifies orange tablets while the FDA PI (Nov 2019) specifies white tablets.

## Recommendation
**REVISION NEEDED**

While the editorial is an excellent piece of regulatory synthesis, it relies too heavily on verbatim technical descriptions from the Australian PI. To pass a strict originality audit:
1.  **Rewrite Section 10 (Mechanism of Action):** Use original phrasing to describe how NS5A and NS5B inhibitors work rather than using the standard clinical definition found in all Gilead PIs.
2.  **Paraphrase Section 4 (Bradycardia):** Re-word the "Cases requiring pacemaker intervention" sentence to avoid the exact sequence of the TGA text.
3.  **Re-word Section 6 (Adverse Effects):** Change the phrasing of the "incidence ≥ 10%" sentence to be more descriptive and less of a direct data-dump.
4.  **Footnote:** While medical data must remain accurate, the 8-week duration footnote should be integrated into the text or phrased more distinctively.
