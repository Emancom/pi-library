# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial: **US (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of Levocarnitine Injection for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises available regulatory data into a single, clinically focused reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official PI or summary of product characteristics (SmPC) required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN/USP)
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Chemical Name:** (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection, USP (Baxter Healthcare Corporation) [US].
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** 
    - Sterile solution for injection: 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL).
    - pH adjusted to 6.0–6.5 with hydrochloric acid or sodium hydroxide [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency in Metabolic Disorders:** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Metabolic Disorders)
- **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg.
- **Administration:** Administered as a slow bolus injection (2–3 minutes) or by infusion [US].
- **Acute Management:** In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose is often followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours. Dosing frequency should be every 3 to 4 hours, and no less than every 6 hours [US].
- **Maximum Dose:** The highest reported dose administered is 300 mg/kg [US].

### Dosing in ESRD on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
- **Administration:** Administered as a slow bolus (2–3 minutes) into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].
- **Dose Adjustment:** Adjustments (e.g., downward to 5 mg/kg) should be guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Reductions may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].

### Monitoring
- **Metabolic Disorders:** Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine range: 35–60 µmol/L) is recommended [US].
- **ESRD:** Initiation is typically prompted by trough plasma concentrations below 40–50 µmol/L [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions may occur within minutes of administration. Discontinue immediately if a reaction occurs [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure disorders. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Metabolite Accumulation:** In patients with severely compromised renal function or ESRD, chronic high-dose oral administration (not specifically noted for IV) may lead to the accumulation of trimethylamine (TMA) and trimethylamine-N-oxide (TMAO), which are potentially toxic and normally excreted renally [US].
- **Visual Inspection:** Parenteral products should be inspected for particulate matter and discoloration before use [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are often confounded by the underlying pathology of ESRD or metabolic disorders.

### Frequency ≥5% (Reported in ESRD Clinical Trials)
| Body System | Adverse Event |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Body as a Whole** | Abdominal pain (9%), Asthenia (9%), Chest pain (12%), Fever (7%), Flu syndrome (25%), Headache (22%), Infection (15%), Injection site reaction (33%), Pain (30%) |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension (20%), Hypotension (14%), Tachycardia (6%) |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea (16%), Nausea (8%), Vomiting (15%) |
| **Metabolic/Nutritional** | Hypercalcemia (9%), Hypervolemia (5%) |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness (13%), Insomnia (4%), Paresthesia (5%) |
| **Respiratory** | Cough increase (9%), Dyspnea (7%), Pharyngitis (23%), Rhinitis (9%) |

### Serious or Notable Effects
- **Neurologic:** Seizures (both new-onset and exacerbation).
- **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea and vomiting; body odor and gastritis (less frequent) [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Anaphylaxis and laryngeal edema [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when used concomitantly with levocarnitine. Monitoring of INR is recommended upon initiation or dose adjustment [US].
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Levocarnitine is not protein-bound; therefore, it is unlikely to interact with drugs via protein-binding displacement [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not assigned by letter, but noted as use only if clearly needed]. Reproductive studies in rats/rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (based on surface area) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. There are no adequate/controlled studies in pregnant women [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Specific studies in humans are unavailable. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine concentrations in milk after supplementation. The risk of excess carnitine intake in the infant must be weighed against the benefit of supplementation for the mother [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies revealed no evidence of impaired fertility [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy for the approved indications are established; dosing is weight-based (see Section 3) [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** IV levocarnitine is specifically indicated for use in ESRD patients on dialysis. Safety and efficacy of *oral* levocarnitine have not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Carnitine deficiency is defined biochemically by plasma concentrations <20 µmol/L (at one week post-term) or a ratio of acylcarnitine/levocarnitine >0.4 [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites are trimethylamine N-oxide (TMAO) and γ-butyrobetaine.
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours (uncorrected for endogenous levels). Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours; mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients, 20 mg/kg IV post-dialysis increased mean pre-dialysis plasma levocarnitine from 20 µmol/L to 190 µmol/L after 8 weeks. 
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** Studies using 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg three times weekly showed dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine concentrations at 12 and 24 weeks [US].
- **Note:** While IV levocarnitine increases carnitine concentrations in ESRD patients, the effect on specific clinical outcomes in this population has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Conditions:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat.
- **Handling:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial; the formulation contains no preservative [US].
