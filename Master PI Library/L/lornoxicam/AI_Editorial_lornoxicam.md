# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA)
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of levocarnitine injection for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises the available clinical and regulatory data into a single reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. At the time of preparation, only the United States (FDA) PI was provided for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule/amino acid derivative.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection, USP (Generic; Baxter Healthcare Corporation) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection, 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Metabolic Disorders:** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US-approved].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Pediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg. This may be administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or via infusion [US].
    - **Loading Dose:** Often required for patients in severe metabolic crisis, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours.
    - **Frequency:** Doses should be administered every 3 or 4 hours, and never less frequently than every 6 hours.
    - **Dose Range:** Subsequent daily doses typically range around 50 mg/kg, though doses as high as 300 mg/kg have been administered.
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
    - **Administration:** Administered as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
    - **Adjustment:** Therapy initiation may be prompted by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma concentrations <40–50 µmol/L. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be made as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough concentrations.

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration.
- **Compatibility:** Compatible and stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations between 0.5 mg/mL and 8.0 mg/mL [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Some reactions occur within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **INR Monitoring:** Reports indicate that levocarnitine may increase the International Normalized Ratio (INR) when used concomitantly with warfarin. Monitor INR levels after initiating treatment or adjusting the dose [US].
- **Monitoring:** For metabolic disorders, obtain a baseline plasma carnitine concentration. Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma carnitine (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L) is recommended [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Clinical Trial Experience (ESRD Patients)
Adverse events occurring at a frequency ≥5% in double-blind, placebo-controlled trials (regardless of causality) include [US]:
- **Body as a Whole:** Abdominal pain (5–21%), headache (3–37%), injection site reaction (27–38%), infection (10–24%), pain (21–35%), fever (5–12%), flu syndrome (15–29%).
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (18–21%), hypotension (3–19%), chest pain (6–15%), tachycardia (5–9%).
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea (9–35%), vomiting (9–21%), nausea (5–12%), dyspepsia (5–9%).
- **Metabolic/Nutritional:** Hypercalcemia (6–15%), hypervolemia (3–12%).
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (15–27%), cough increase (10–18%), dyspnea (3–11%), rhinitis (6–11%).
- **Nervous System:** Dizziness (10–18%), paresthesia (3–12%).

### Post-marketing Experience
- **Neurologic:** Seizures.
- **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea and vomiting; less frequent: body odor and gastritis.
- **Immune System:** Anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, bronchospasm.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Concomitant use may lead to an increased INR. Close monitoring of anticoagulation status is required [US].
- **Pharmacokinetic Note:** Chronic high doses of *oral* levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]), which are normally excreted in urine [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not explicitly assigned, but follows general safety guidelines]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk carnitine concentrations. Benefits to the mother must be weighed against potential risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders is calculated by weight (50 mg/kg) [US]. Safety and efficacy in other contexts follow adult data.
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically detailed in the provided source; generally, dose selection should be cautious, reflecting the greater frequency of decreased hepatic, renal, or cardiac function.
- **Renal Impairment:** Safety and efficacy of *oral* levocarnitine have not been evaluated in renal insufficiency. Intravenous levocarnitine is specifically indicated for use in ESRD patients on hemodialysis [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring carrier molecule essential for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In patients with inborn errors of metabolism or ESRD, levocarnitine alleviates metabolic abnormalities by promoting the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids and clearing toxic acyl-CoA compounds through the formation of acylcarnitine [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (feces).
    - **Elimination:** Following IV bolus (20 mg/kg), approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours. Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours; mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of ESRD patients (n=12), 20 mg/kg IV after dialysis increased pre-dialysis plasma concentrations from ~20 µmol/L to ~190 µmol/L at 8 weeks. Dose-ranging studies (10, 20, 40 mg/kg) demonstrated dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [US].
- **Note:** While levocarnitine increases carnitine concentrations in ESRD, the effect on specific clinical outcomes in this population has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Protection:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat.
- **Handling:** Discard any unused portion of an opened vial; the formulation contains no preservative [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not applicable in the US jurisdiction; refer to local schedules for other regions.
