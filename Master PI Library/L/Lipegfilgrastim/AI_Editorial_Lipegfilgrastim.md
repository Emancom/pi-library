# Editorial Product Information: Lipegfilgrastim

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the following jurisdictional Product Information (PI) source: **Australian (TGA)**. 
- **Note:** At the time of this editorial’s preparation, only the Australian PI was provided for review. Consequently, jurisdictional comparisons are limited to the information available within that source.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. It is a clinical synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Lipegfilgrastim (a glycoPEGylated recombinant human granulocyte colony-stimulating factor [G-CSF]).
- **Chemical Class:** Colony-stimulating factor; long-acting filgrastim analogue.
- **Brand Name:** **Lonquex** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** L03AA14.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Solution for injection in a pre-filled syringe. Each 0.6 mL syringe contains 6 mg of lipegfilgrastim (10 mg/mL concentration based on protein content).
- **Route of Administration:** Subcutaneous (SC) injection.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Teva Pharma Australia Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Reduction of Neutropenia:** Indicated to reduce the duration of neutropenia and the incidence of febrile neutropenia in adult patients receiving cytotoxic chemotherapy for malignancy.
- **Exclusions:** Use is specifically excluded for patients with chronic myeloid leukaemia (CML) and myelodysplastic syndromes (MDS) [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Adult Dose:** A single 6 mg subcutaneous dose administered once per chemotherapy cycle.
- **Timing:** Administer approximately 24 hours after the completion of cytotoxic chemotherapy.
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Inject into the abdomen, upper arm, or thigh.
    - Avoid areas that are tender, red, bruised, scarred, or have stretch marks.
    - Allow the syringe to reach room temperature (15°C–25°C) before use.
    - **Do not shake**; vigorous shaking may render the protein biologically inactive.
    - Inspect for particulate matter or discoloration; use only clear, colourless solutions.
- **Missed Dose:** Not specified in available sources (dosing is cycle-dependent).
- **Monitoring:** Obtain a complete blood count (CBC) and platelet count prior to chemotherapy.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to lipegfilgrastim, other G-CSF products (e.g., filgrastim, pegfilgrastim, lenograstim), or any excipient (including sorbitol) [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Splenic Adverse Reactions:** Frequent but often asymptomatic splenomegaly and infrequent cases of splenic rupture (some fatal) have been reported with G-CSF derivatives. Monitor spleen size; investigate reports of left upper abdominal or shoulder tip pain.
- **Pulmonary Effects:** Interstitial pneumonia, pulmonary oedema, and infiltrates have occurred. The onset of cough, fever, and dyspnoea associated with radiological signs may be preliminary signs of Adult Respiratory Distress Syndrome (ARDS). Discontinue therapy if ARDS is suspected.
- **Vascular Effects:**
    - **Capillary Leak Syndrome (CLS):** Characterised by hypotension, hypoalbuminaemia, oedema, and haemoconcentration. Requires close monitoring and symptomatic treatment.
    - **Aortitis:** Reported in both healthy subjects and cancer patients. Symptoms include fever, abdominal pain, and increased inflammatory markers.
- **Haematological Precautions:**
    - **Leukocytosis:** If White Blood Cell (WBC) counts exceed 50 x 10⁹/L after the expected nadir, lipegfilgrastim should be discontinued immediately.
    - **MDS/AML Risk:** While not directly established for lipegfilgrastim, other G-CSFs have been associated with MDS/AML in breast and lung cancer patients. Monitor these populations for signs of myeloid transformation.
- **Sickle Cell Crisis:** G-CSF use in patients with sickle cell anaemia has been associated with severe sickle cell crises.
- **Excipient Warning:** Contains 30 mg of sorbitol per syringe. Avoid in patients with rare hereditary fructose intolerance [Australian].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Frequency Table (based on clinical trial data)
| Frequency | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Musculoskeletal pains (bone pain, myalgia) |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Thrombocytopenia, hypokalaemia, headache, skin reactions (erythema, rash), chest pain |
| **Uncommon (0.1–1%)** | Leukocytosis, splenomegaly, hypersensitivity reactions, pulmonary adverse reactions, injection site reactions (induration, pain), increased alkaline phosphatase or LDH |
| **Not Known** | Capillary leak syndrome, splenic rupture, aortitis |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Splenic rupture, ARDS, and anaphylaxis are rare but serious. Musculoskeletal pain is generally mild-to-moderate and manageable with standard analgesics.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Timing with Chemotherapy:** Due to the sensitivity of rapidly dividing myeloid cells, lipegfilgrastim should not be administered in the period 24 hours before until 24 hours after cytotoxic chemotherapy.
- **5-Fluorouracil (5-FU):** Animal models suggest concomitant administration with antimetabolites may potentiate myelosuppression.
- **Lithium:** Caution is advised as lithium promotes neutrophil release and may potentiate the effects of lipegfilgrastim.
- **Metabolism:** In vitro data indicate no significant effect on CYP1A2, 2B6, 2C8, 2C9, 2C19, or 3A4/5.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **[Australian Category B3]:** Animal studies show reproductive toxicity (increased pregnancy loss in rabbits). There are very limited data in humans; use is not recommended during pregnancy.
- **Breastfeeding:** Not studied in lactating women. Use is not recommended while breastfeeding.
- **Fertility:** Animal studies do not indicate harmful effects on fertility.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy in children and adolescents under 18 years have not been established. No data available.
- **Elderly Use:** No relevant age-related differences in safety or efficacy; no dose adjustment required for patients ≥65 years.
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment required for mild impairment. No specific data for moderate or severe impairment, but neutrophil-mediated clearance suggests renal function is not a primary factor in elimination.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specifically studied; however, no dose adjustment is expected to be necessary.
- **Body Weight:** Exposure in patients >80 kg is approximately 30% of that in patients <60 kg. A decrease in efficacy cannot be excluded in patients with high body weight.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Lipegfilgrastim is a G-CSF receptor agonist. It regulates the production and release of functional neutrophils from the bone marrow and enhances their antibacterial activity (phagocytosis). 
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Induces a marked increase in peripheral blood neutrophil counts within 24 hours of administration.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Lag time of ~1 hour; Tmax reached at ~30–36 hours in healthy volunteers. Bioavailability is lower when injected in the thigh vs. abdomen or arm.
    - **Distribution:** Small volume of distribution (~70 mL/kg), limited to the lymphatic/vascular system.
    - **Metabolism:** Degraded by proteolytic enzymes (intracellularly via G-CSF receptors and extracellularly).
    - **Elimination:** Dual clearance pathways. At low neutrophil counts, linear (proteolytic) clearance dominates. As neutrophil counts rise, non-linear (neutrophil-mediated) clearance becomes the primary, self-regulating mechanism. Terminal half-life is ~33 hours.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Breast Cancer (Study XM22-03):** An active-controlled study (n=202) comparing 6 mg lipegfilgrastim to 6 mg pegfilgrastim. Lipegfilgrastim demonstrated non-inferiority regarding the duration of severe neutropenia (DSN) in Cycle 1 (0.7 days vs. 0.9 days).
- **Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (Study XM22-04):** A placebo-controlled study (n=375) where lipegfilgrastim significantly reduced the DSN (0.6 days vs. 2.3 days for placebo) and reduced the incidence of severe neutropenia (32.1% vs. 59.2%).

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store in a refrigerator at 2°C to 8°C. **Do not freeze.**
- **Light Protection:** Keep the syringe in the outer carton to protect from light.
- **Excursions:** May be removed from the refrigerator and stored at room temperature (up to 25°C) for a single period of up to **7 days**. If not used within 7 days, the product must be discarded.
