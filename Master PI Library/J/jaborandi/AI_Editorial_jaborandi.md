# Editorial Product Information: BM Hair Support Drops

- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised clinical overview of **BM Hair Support Drops** for healthcare professionals.

- **Active Ingredients:** A homeopathic preparation containing:
    - **Phosphorus** 7X
    - **Sulphur** 6X
    - **Arnica montana** (Whole plant) 7X
    - **Graphite** 12X
    - **Lycopodium clavatum** (Spore) 6X

    - **Phosphoric acid** (*Acid Phos*) 5X
    - **Arsenic trioxide** (*Ars Alb*) 6X
- **Brand Name(s):** **BM Hair Support Drops**; also referred to as **BMNP Hair Support Liquid** [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Oral liquid drops. Strengths are provided in homeopathic decimal scales (X) per 1 mL [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Total Operations Management LLC DBA: Bestmade Natural Products Micro [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
Based on traditional homeopathic practice, this product is used to temporarily relieve [US]:
- Hair thinning and hair loss.
- Scalp irritation, including itching, flaking, and dandruff.
- Dry or brittle hair.
- Premature greying (attributed to the Phosphorus component).

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
The solution should be mixed with approximately half a cup of water and consumed immediately. This process is repeated 3–4 times daily [US].

### Dosing by Age Group
- **Adults and Children (Ages 12 and older):** 10 drops per dose [US].
- **Children (Ages 3 to 11):** 5 drops per dose [US].
- **Children (Under 3 years):** Consult a physician prior to use [US].

## 4. Contraindications
