# Editorial Product Information: Jalapa (Ipomoea purga)

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial product information has been synthesized from the official product documentation provided by the **United States (FDA)**. 
- This document serves as a harmonised clinical overview for healthcare professionals and is not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing or dispensing within a specific jurisdiction.
- Note: Only a single jurisdictional source (United States) was available for this synthesis; therefore, jurisdictional comparisons are limited.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** *Ipomoea purga* root (commonly known as Jalap).
- **Brand Name:** Boiron Jalapa [United States].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Oral pellets, 200CK dilution (each pellet contains 0.443 mg of active ingredient) [United States].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral (Sublingual).
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Boiron, Inc. [United States].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Children's Diarrhea or Colic:** Indicated for the relief of diarrhea or colic in children. These claims are based on traditional homeopathic practice and have not been accepted as medical evidence by regulatory authorities [United States].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adults and Children):** Dissolve 5 pellets under the tongue 3 times daily.
- **Duration of Treatment:** Treatment should continue until symptoms are relieved or as directed by a healthcare professional.
- **Administration Instructions:** Turn the tube upside down and twist until 5 pellets are dispensed into the cap. Pour pellets directly from the cap under the tongue to avoid handling.
- **Missed Dose:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Seal Integrity:** Do not use if the pellet dispenser seal is broken [United States].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to the active ingredient or any of the excipients (lactose, sucrose) should be considered a contraindication [Editorial Note: implied by standard clinical practice].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None.
- **Persistence of Symptoms:** Patients should stop use and consult a physician if symptoms worsen or persist for more than 3 days [United States].
- **Regulatory Status:** This product has not been evaluated by the FDA for safety or efficacy, and the agency is not aware of scientific evidence to support homeopathy as effective [United States].
- **Excipients:** Contains lactose and sucrose. Clinicians should exercise caution in patients with rare hereditary problems of galactose intolerance, total lactase deficiency, or glucose-galactose malabsorption.
- **General Safety:** Keep out of reach of children.

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Frequency and Severity:** Not specified in available sources. 
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** None reported in the provided documentation.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Consult a health professional before use. No specific regulatory category (e.g., US Lettered Category) was assigned in the source document [United States].
- **Breastfeeding:** Consult a health professional before use [United States].
- **Fertility:** Data not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Indicated for use in children for colic and diarrhea. Dosing is the same as the adult dose (5 pellets sublingually) [United States].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Derived from the root of *Ipomoea purga*. Within the context of homeopathy, the mechanism is based on the "law of similars"; however, this has not been scientifically validated [United States].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Not specified in available sources.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- No pivotal clinical trial data is described in the source documentation. The source notes a lack of accepted medical evidence/evaluation for the claimed indications [United States].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage:** Not specifically detailed; however, general pharmaceutical standards for dry oral forms apply.
- **Shelf Life:** Marketing start date noted as 01/01/2024 [United States].
- **Handling:** Use the provided cap for dispensing to ensure hygienic administration.
