# Editorial Product Information: Lemon Balm Full Body Salve (Melissa officinalis / Tea Tree Oil / Eucalyptus Oil)

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official product labeling submitted to the **US (FDA)**. As of the time of this publication, no documents from other jurisdictions (e.g., Australian, European, Canadian) were provided for comparison.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, consolidating available clinical and regulatory data into a structured format.
- **Important Clinical Disclaimer:** This product is classified in the United States as an "unapproved drug." It has not been found by the FDA to be safe and effective, and the labeling has not been approved by the agency. This editorial is not a substitute for official prescribing information or local clinical guidelines.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** 
    - *Melissa officinalis* (Lemon Balm) leaf
    - *Melaleuca alternifolia* (Tea Tree) leaf oil
    - *Eucalyptus globulus* (Eucalyptus) oil
- **Chemical Class:** Herbal/Botanical Topical Combination
- **Brand Name:** Sea Jewels Lemon Balm Full Body Salve [US]
- **ATC Code:** Not assigned (generally categorized under D02AX - Other emollients and protectants, or D08AX - Other antiseptics and disinfectants)
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strengths:** 
    - Topical Salve/Ointment.
    - **US Concentrations (per 531.1 g of product):** 
        - Lemon Balm Leaf: 7 g 
        - Eucalyptus Oil: 2.3 g
        - Tea Tree Leaf Oil: 2.2 g
- **Route of Administration:** Topical
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** SEAJewels LLC [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
The following uses are listed on the product labeling [US]:
- **Skin Protection:** To comfort skin during periods of irritation or flare-ups.
- **Symptomatic Relief:** Helps relieve symptoms associated with:
    - Cold sores and herpes outbreaks.
    - Eczema and rashes.
    - Ringworm and fungal irritation.
    - Minor boils and abscesses.
    - Insect bites.
    - Shingles flare-ups.
    - Minor burns.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Application:** Apply a thin layer to the affected area.
- **Frequency:** 2–3 times daily, or as needed [US].

### Administration Instructions
1. Cleanse the affected area thoroughly before application.
2. For open wounds: apply the salve and cover with sterile gauze [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Deep Wounds:** Do not use on deep puncture wounds [US].
- **Severe Burns:** Do not use on serious burns [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **General Warning:** For external use only [US].
- **Ocular Exposure:** Avoid contact with eyes [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Stop use and consult a healthcare professional if the condition worsens or if a rash develops [US].
- **Ingestion:** Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, seek medical assistance or contact a Poison Control Center immediately [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Frequency Not Specified:** Specific clinical trial frequency data is not available. 
- **Dermatological:** Rash and worsening of the existing skin condition have been identified as potential adverse events requiring discontinuation of the product [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
