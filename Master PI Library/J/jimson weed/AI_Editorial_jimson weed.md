# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial: **US (FDA)**.
- This document serves as a harmonized editorial overview intended for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesizes available clinical data into a single reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official, legally approved PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory guidance for absolute compliance.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (a carrier molecule for long-chain fatty acid transport).
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection, USP [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Sterile solution for injection: 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency in Metabolic Errors:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that lead to secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
- **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg.
- **Administration:** Given as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion.
- **Severe Crisis:** A loading dose is often administered, followed by equivalent doses every 3 or 4 hours (never less than every 6 hours) for the subsequent 24 hours.
- **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses are typically in the range of 50 mg/kg, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilized.

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Recommended Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight).
- **Administration:** Administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
- **Dose Adjustments:** Guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy.

### Monitoring and Instructions
- **Target Plasma Levels:** Aim for plasma free carnitine concentrations between 35 and 60 µmol/L.
- **Compatibility:** Stable in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations of 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL for up to 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known at the time of publication [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Some reactions occurred within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately [US].
- **Seizure Risk:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **General Precautions:** The safety of oral levocarnitine has not been evaluated in renal insufficiency. In ESRD, chronic high doses of oral levocarnitine may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (TMA and TMAO) normally excreted in urine [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Common Reactions:** Transient nausea and vomiting, body odor, and gastritis.
- **ESRD Clinical Trial Data (Frequency ≥5% regardless of causality):**
    - **General:** Injection site reaction (33%), pain (30%), flu syndrome (25%), headache (22%), abdominal pain (9%).
    - **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (20%), hypotension (14%), chest pain (12%), tachycardia (6%).
    - **Gastrointestinal:** Diarrhea (16%), vomiting (15%), nausea (8%).
    - **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (23%), cough increase (9%), dyspnea (7%).
    - **Metabolic:** Hypercalcemia (9%), hyperkalemia (6%).

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when used concomitantly with levocarnitine. 
- **Management:** It is recommended to monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after any dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Classification:** Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (based on surface area) revealed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. No adequate, well-controlled studies exist in pregnant women; use only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. In dairy cows, carnitine concentrations in milk increase following exogenous administration. Risks to the infant from excess intake must be weighed against maternal benefits [US].
- **Fertility:** Mutagenicity tests (Salmonella, etc.) were negative. Long-term animal carcinogenicity studies have not been performed [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders is weight-based (50 mg/kg); see Section 3 for specific administration guidelines.
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** See Section 3 for ESRD-specific dosing. Safety of oral levocarnitine in renal impairment has not been established [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into the mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also facilitates the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids in patients with metabolic defects [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (feces).
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours (uncorrected for endogenous levels). Mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Efficacy:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients, 20 mg/kg three times weekly post-dialysis increased mean pre-dialysis trough concentrations from 21.3 µmol/L to 190 µmol/L by week 8.
- **Note:** While levocarnitine increases carnitine concentrations in ESRD, the effect on specific clinical outcomes in this population has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Precautions:** Avoid excessive heat and protect from freezing.
- **Handling:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as it contains no preservatives [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** 
    - **US:** Rx-only (Prescription only).
