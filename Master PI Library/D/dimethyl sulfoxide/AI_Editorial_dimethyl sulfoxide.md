**Editorial Note:** This unified Product Information (PI) document has been synthesized from official regulatory sources: the Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA, Australia) regarding systemic human applications and the Food and Drug Administration (FDA, United States) regarding topical veterinary applications. Healthcare professionals should note that these documents describe different clinical contexts: Document 1 [TGA] concerns dimethyl sulfoxide as a solvate in human oncology (trametinib), while Document 2 [FDA] concerns it as a high-concentration vehicle and active component in veterinary otic medicine.

# Product Information: Dimethyl Sulfoxide (DMSO)

## 1. Product Name and Active Ingredient
**Product Name:** Mekinist (Human Systemic); Synotic (Veterinary Otic)
**Active Ingredient:** Dimethyl sulfoxide (DMSO)
*   **Systemic Form:** Provided as trametinib dimethyl sulfoxide, a polycyclic, nitrogen-containing heterocycle [TGA].
*   **Topical Form:** Provided as a 60% solution in a propylene glycol vehicle [FDA].

## 2. Approved Indications
### Human Systemic Use [TGA]
In combination with dabrafenib, or as monotherapy in specific cases, for the treatment of:
*   **Melanoma:** Unresectable Stage III or metastatic (Stage IV) melanoma with BRAF V600 mutations; adjuvant treatment following complete resection in patients with lymph node involvement.
*   **Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC):** Advanced disease with BRAF V600 mutations.
*   **Anaplastic Thyroid Cancer (ATC):** Locally advanced or metastatic disease with BRAF V600 mutations and no satisfactory locoregional options.
*   **Glioma:** Pediatric patients (1 year+) with low-grade (LGG) or high-grade (HGG) glioma harboring BRAF V600E mutations.

### Veterinary Otic Use [FDA]
*   **Canine Otitis:** Indicated for the relief of pruritus and inflammation associated with acute and chronic otitis in dogs.

## 3. Contraindications
*   **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to dimethyl sulfoxide or any excipients [TGA, FDA].
*   **Tumor Genetics:** Should not be used in patients with BRAF wild-type tumors [TGA].
*   **Systemic Steroid Danger:** Contraindicated in veterinary cases where systemic corticosteroids would be dangerous (e.g., certain metabolic or infectious conditions) [FDA].

## 4. Warnings and Precautions
### Systemic Human Risks [TGA]
*   **Haemorrhage:** Major events, including fatal intracranial and gastrointestinal haemorrhage, have occurred.
*   **Cardiac Dysfunction:** Potential for LVEF reduction, cardiomyopathy, and bradycardia. Baseline and periodic LVEF monitoring is mandatory.
*   **Visual Impairment:** Risk of Retinal Vein Occlusion (RVO) and Retinal Pigment Epithelial Detachment (RPED). Documented RVO requires permanent discontinuation.
*   **Pulmonary Toxicity:** Interstitial Lung Disease (ILD) or pneumonitis can be fatal; withhold therapy for suspected cases.
*   **Severe Skin Reactions:** Risk of Stevens-Johnson syndrome and DRESS.

### Topical and General Risks [FDA]
*   **Hearing Loss:** Reported in a small number of dogs; treatment should be discontinued if vestibular dysfunction or hearing loss occurs.
*   **Ocular Safety:** Avoid contact with eyes [TGA, FDA].
*   **Occupational Exposure:** Humans administering topical forms should avoid bare hand contact [FDA].
*   **Physical Properties:** DMSO is a potent solvent; avoid contact with fabrics and plastics [FDA].

## 5. Dosage and Administration
### Human Oral Administration [TGA]
*   **Standard Dose:** 2 mg orally once daily.
*   **Administration:** Take without food, at least 1 hour before or 2 hours after a meal.
*   **Dose Modification:** Requires specific interruption or reduction protocols for pyrexia, skin toxicity, or LVEF reduction.

### Veterinary Otic Administration [FDA]
*   **Standard Dose:** 4 to 6 drops (0.2 mL) per ear twice daily.
*   **Duration:** Maximum of 14 days.
*   **Limit:** Total dosage should not exceed 17 mL per treatment course.

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Systemic/Human [TGA]
*   **Very Common:** Rash, diarrhea, fatigue, peripheral edema, nausea, dermatitis acneiform, hypertension, and pyrexia.
*   **Serious:** Neutropenia, haemorrhage, LVEF reduction, and visual disturbances.

### Topical/Veterinary [FDA]
*   **Local Reactions:** Transient stinging sensation when applied to denuded areas; temporary increase in local skin temperature.
*   **Systemic Absorption Effects:** Garlic-like or "oyster-like" breath odor/taste following absorption.
*   **Chronic Exposure:** High doses have been associated with changes in the refractive index of the lens (lenticular nuclear cataracts) in laboratory animals.

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **Absorption Potentiation:** DMSO is known to enhance the percutaneous absorption of various compounds, including steroids, vasoconstrictors, and antiseptics. It may potentiate the effects of drugs administered systemically [FDA].
*   **Metabolic Interactions:** Systemic forms are primarily metabolized via deacetylation; interactions with CYP enzymes (e.g., CYP3A4) are considered clinically negligible [TGA].
*   **Therapeutic Caution:** Use medications containing DMSO judiciously with preparations affecting the cardiovascular or central nervous systems [FDA].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy (Human):** Category D. Can cause fetal harm. Effective contraception is required during and for 4 months post-treatment [TGA].
*   **Pregnancy (Animal):** Studies in chick embryos and guinea pigs indicate DMSO is teratogenic and embryotoxic. Corticosteroids in DMSO may induce premature parturition or congenital anomalies (cleft palate) [FDA].
*   **Lactation:** No clinical data available; the potential for transfer into human milk and adverse reactions in nursing infants must be considered against the mother's clinical need [TGA].

## 9. Pharmacokinetics
*   **Absorption:** Systemic bioavailability is approximately 72%. Topical DMSO facilitates the rapid percutaneous absorption of associated active ingredients [TGA, FDA].
*   **Distribution:** High protein binding (97.4%) and a large volume of distribution (1,060 L) [TGA].
*   **Metabolism:** Primarily metabolized by deacetylation via hydrolytic enzymes (carboxylesterases) [TGA].
*   **Excretion:** Mean terminal half-life is 5.3 days. Primary elimination is via faeces (>80%) [TGA].

## 10. Mechanism of Action
*   **Systemic Oncology:** Acts as a reversible allosteric inhibitor of MEK1 and MEK2, inhibiting the RAS/RAF/MEK/ERK pathway to stop tumor cell growth [TGA].
*   **Topical Otic:** Acts as a solvent and vehicle that enhances the penetration of corticosteroids through the skin. It possesses inherent anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties [FDA].

## 11. Storage Conditions
*   **Temperature:** Store at 2°C to 8°C (refrigerate) for systemic tablets and powder [TGA]. Store at 20°C to 25°C for otic solutions [FDA].
*   **Stability:** Protect from light and moisture. DMSO is highly hygroscopic; keep containers tightly closed [TGA, FDA]. Discard reconstituted solutions after 35 days [TGA].
