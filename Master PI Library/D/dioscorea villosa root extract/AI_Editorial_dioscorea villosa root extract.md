## 4. Dosage and Administration
- Strength as provided in source: dioscorea villosa root extract 0.02 mg per 100 mg of product (0.02 mg/100 mg).
- General dosing (adult):
  - Apply an appropriate amount of product to clean, dry skin and gently massage until fully absorbed.
- Dosing by indication:
  - For menstruating women using for premenstrual symptoms: begin use one week before expected onset of menstruation and continue twice daily until the end of menstruation.
- Administration instructions:
  - After cleansing and drying the skin, apply an appropriate amount to the palms and gently massage onto the abdomen, chest, arms and inner thighs until completely absorbed.
  - Clean hands before use to ensure optimal application.
