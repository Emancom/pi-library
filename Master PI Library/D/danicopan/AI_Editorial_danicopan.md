# Product Information: VOYDEYA (Danicopan)

## 1. Product Name and Active Ingredient
**Product Name:** VOYDEYA  
**Active Ingredient:** Danicopan

VOYDEYA is available as film-coated tablets in 50 mg and 100 mg strengths.

## 2. Approved Indications
VOYDEYA is indicated as an add-on therapy to ravulizumab or eculizumab for the treatment of signs and symptoms of extravascular hemolysis (EVH) in adult patients with paroxysmal nocturnal hemoglobinuria (PNH). 

**Limitations of Use:** VOYDEYA must not be used as monotherapy; its efficacy has only been established as an adjunct to C5 inhibitor therapy.

## 3. Contraindications
Therapy must not be initiated in patients with:
*   **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to danicopan or any component of the formulation [TGA].
*   **Serious Infections:** Unresolved serious infections caused by encapsulated bacteria, specifically *Neisseria meningitidis*, *Streptococcus pneumoniae*, or *Haemophilus influenzae* type B [FDA].

## 4. Warnings and Precautions
### Serious Infections and Encapsulated Bacteria
VOYDEYA increases susceptibility to life-threatening infections caused by encapsulated bacteria (e.g., *N. meningitidis*, *S. pneumoniae*, *H. influenzae* type B). A boxed warning in the US highlights this risk [FDA].
*   **Vaccination:** Patients must be up-to-date with vaccinations against these bacteria at least two weeks prior to starting VOYDEYA. If urgent therapy is required, antibacterial prophylaxis must be provided immediately and vaccines administered as soon as possible.
*   **Monitoring:** Clinicians should maintain a high index of clinical suspicion for early signs of sepsis and infection.
*   **Access:** In the US, VOYDEYA is only available through a restricted program (REMS) [FDA].

### Hepatic Enzyme Increases
Elevations in alanine aminotransferase (ALT) have been observed during clinical trials. Liver enzyme tests should be performed before starting treatment and monitored periodically thereafter. Interruption or cessation of treatment should be considered if elevations are clinically significant or the patient becomes symptomatic.

### Hyperlipidemia
VOYDEYA has been associated with increases in total cholesterol and LDL-cholesterol. Lipid parameters should be monitored periodically, and lipid-lowering therapy initiated if clinically indicated [FDA].

### Monitoring Post-Discontinuation
Upon stopping VOYDEYA, patients must be closely monitored for at least two weeks for signs of hemolysis. Due to the risk of ALT elevations following cessation, a 6-day dose taper is recommended [TGA] (see *Dosage and Administration*).

## 5. Dosage and Administration
### Recommended Dosage
*   **Starting Dose:** 150 mg taken orally three times daily (approximately 8 hours apart).
*   **Dose Titration:** The dose may be increased to 200 mg three times daily based on clinical response (e.g., failure to increase hemoglobin by >2 g/dL after 4 weeks) [FDA].

### Administration Instructions
VOYDEYA can be taken with or without food.

### Discontinuation and Tapering [TGA]
To mitigate the risk of hepatic enzyme spikes, the following 6-day taper is recommended:
*   **From 150 mg TID:** 100 mg TID for 3 days, then 50 mg TID for 3 days.
*   **From 200 mg TID:** 100 mg TID for 3 days, then 100 mg BID for 3 days.

### Missed Doses
If a dose is missed, it should be taken as soon as remembered unless it is within 3 hours of the next scheduled dose [FDA]. Patients should not double-up on doses.

### Specific Populations
*   **Renal Impairment:** No adjustment is needed for mild to severe impairment.
*   **Hepatic Impairment:** No adjustment is needed for mild to moderate impairment (Child-Pugh A or B). Use is not recommended in patients with severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh C) [FDA/TGA].

## 6. Adverse Effects
The most common adverse reaction reported (≥10%) was headache. Other reactions (≥5%) reported more frequently than placebo include:
*   Vomiting and nausea
*   Pyrexia
*   Increased hepatic enzymes (ALT/AST)
*   Hypertension
*   Pain in extremities and arthralgia
*   Diarrhea and fatigue (observed in open-label phases) [TGA].

Serious but less frequent reactions included pancreatitis, cholecystitis, and clinically significant bilirubin increases.

## 7. Drug Interactions
### Effect of VOYDEYA on Other Drugs
Danicopan is an inhibitor of P-glycoprotein (P-gp) and Breast Cancer Resistance Protein (BCRP).
*   **BCRP Substrates:** Concomitant use increases substrate exposure. For rosuvastatin, the dose should not exceed 10 mg once daily [FDA]. Monitor other BCRP substrates (e.g., sulfasalazine) closely.
*   **P-gp Substrates:** Increased exposure may occur for drugs such as digoxin, dabigatran, and tacrolimus. Dose adjustments may be necessary.

### Effect of Other Drugs on VOYDEYA
No significant interactions were observed when co-administered with antacids or proton pump inhibitors (e.g., omeprazole). Danicopan is a substrate of P-gp but is not significantly affected by CYP-mediated metabolism.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
### Pregnancy
There are no clinical data on the use of VOYDEYA in pregnant women. Animal studies showed no malformations at high doses, though reduced fetal weight was noted at maternally toxic levels [TGA]. It should only be used if the potential benefit justifies the potential risk. (Pregnancy Category B3 [TGA]).

### Breastfeeding
It is unknown if danicopan is excreted in human milk, though it was present in the milk of lactating rabbits. Due to the potential for serious adverse reactions in the infant, breastfeeding is not recommended during treatment and for 3 days following the final dose [FDA].

## 9. Pharmacokinetics
*   **Absorption:** Median $T_{max}$ is 3.7 hours. A high-fat meal increases AUC by ~25% and $C_{max}$ by ~93%.
*   **Distribution:** Highly protein-bound (91.5% to 94.3%), primarily to albumin.
*   **Metabolism:** Extensively metabolized (96%) via oxidation, reduction, and hydrolysis; CYP-mediated metabolism is minimal.
*   **Elimination:** The mean half-life is approximately 7.9 hours. Excretion is primarily fecal (69%) and renal (25%).

## 10. Mechanism of Action
Danicopan is a reversible, small-molecule inhibitor of complement Factor D. By selectively inhibiting the alternative pathway (AP) of the complement system, it prevents the formation of the C3 convertase ($C3bBb$). In PNH, this mechanism specifically targets and controls C3 fragment-mediated extravascular hemolysis (EVH), while the background C5 inhibitor (ravulizumab or eculizumab) maintains control over terminal pathway-mediated intravascular hemolysis (IVH).

## 11. Storage Conditions
Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions are permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F and 86°F) [FDA]. Keep the tablets in their original container to protect from moisture. (TGA guidance suggests storage below 30°C).
