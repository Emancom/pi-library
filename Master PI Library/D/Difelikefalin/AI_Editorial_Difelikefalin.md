# Editorial Product Information: Difelikefalin

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the **Australian (TGA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, consolidating clinical information into a concise, structured format.
- This document is an editorial synthesis intended for informational use by healthcare professionals and does not substitute for the official PI or prescribing information required within a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Difelikefalin (as acetate)
- **Chemical Class:** Peripherally acting, selective kappa opioid receptor agonist; synthetic D-amino acid peptide.
- **Brand Name:** Korsuva [Australian]
- **ATC Code:** V03AX04
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Solution for injection: 50 micrograms/mL in a 1 mL single-use vial [Australian].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous bolus injection.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Seqirus Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Chronic Kidney Disease-Associated Pruritus (CKD-aP):** Treatment of moderate-to-severe pruritus in adult patients with chronic kidney disease who are undergoing haemodialysis [Australian-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Adult Dosing:** 0.5 micrograms/kg of dry body weight (target postdialysis weight).
- **Administration Schedule:** Administered three times per week as an intravenous bolus injection into the venous line of the dialysis circuit at the end of haemodialysis (during or after rinse-back).
- **Calculation:** The dose volume (mL) = 0.01 × dry body weight (kg), rounded to the nearest 0.1 mL.
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - If given after rinse-back, flush the line with at least 10 mL of 0.9% sodium chloride.
    - If given during rinse-back, no additional flush is required.
    - The dose must be administered within 60 minutes of syringe preparation.
    - Do not dilute or mix with other medicinal products.
- **Missed Dose:** If a dialysis treatment is missed, administer the usual dose at the next treatment session.
- **Extra Treatment:** If a fourth dialysis session is performed in a week, a fourth dose should be administered. Maximum of 4 doses per week [Australian].
- **Incomplete Treatment:** If dialysis lasts less than 1 hour, withhold the dose until the next session.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to difelikefalin or any excipients (acetic acid, sodium acetate trihydrate, sodium chloride) [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hyperkalaemia:** Frequently occurs in CKD patients on haemodialysis. Frequent monitoring of potassium levels is recommended [Australian].
- **Cardiac Failure and Atrial Fibrillation:** Numerical imbalances in these events were noted in trials, particularly in patients with a history of AF who missed or stopped treatment. No causal relationship is established, but caution is advised in NYHA Class IV heart failure as this population has not been studied.
- **Impaired Blood-Brain Barrier (BBB):** As a peripherally acting agent, BBB integrity is essential to limit CNS entry. Caution is advised in patients with disruptions to the BBB (e.g., brain malignancies, CNS metastases, multiple sclerosis, advanced Alzheimer’s disease) due to potential CNS effects.
- **Dizziness and Somnolence:** May occur, particularly in the first few weeks of treatment. Concomitant use of CNS depressants (e.g., sedating antihistamines, opioids) may increase this risk.
- **Use in the Elderly:** Incidence of somnolence is higher in patients ≥65 years (7.0%) compared to those <65 years (2.8%).

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions reported in clinical trials at the 0.5 mcg/kg dose (n=1306) are categorised by frequency:

| Frequency | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | None specified |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Somnolence, Diarrhoea, Dizziness, Nausea, Hyperkalaemia, Headache, Mental status changes (including confusion), Back pain |
| **Uncommon (0.1–1%)** | Not specifically listed as distinct from the common events in the provided source, though mental status changes and nausea were considered related in <1% of subjects in some analyses. |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** No serious adverse reactions were attributed to the drug in pooled analyses.
- **Post-Marketing:** A single case of Drug Reaction with Eosinophilia and Systemic Symptoms (DRESS) has been reported; causality is not established [Australian].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Difelikefalin is not a substrate, inhibitor, or inducer of major CYP450 enzymes (1A2, 2C8, 2C9, 2C19, 2D6, 3A). It does not inhibit major transporters (e.g., P-gp, BCRP, OAT1/3, OCT1/2).
- **Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Concurrent use of sedating antihistamines, opioid analgesics, or other CNS depressants (e.g., gabapentin, pregabalin, zolpidem, benzodiazepines) may increase the likelihood of dizziness and somnolence.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Category B1 [Australian]:** Drugs which have been taken by only a limited number of pregnant women and women of childbearing age, without an increase in the frequency of malformation or other direct or indirect harmful effects on the human fetus having been observed. 
    - Animal studies showed no teratogenicity, but increased skeletal variations were seen in rats at high doses. As a precaution, it is preferable to avoid use during pregnancy.
- **Breastfeeding:** Animal studies show excretion in milk. A risk to newborns/infants cannot be excluded. A decision must be made to discontinue breastfeeding or the drug, considering the benefit of each to the mother and child.
- **Fertility:** No effects on male or female fertility were observed in animal studies at doses significantly higher than the human therapeutic dose.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy in children aged 0–17 years have not been established [Australian].
- **Elderly Use:** No dose adjustment required. Monitor closely for somnolence and dizziness.
- **Renal Impairment:** Dosing is specifically for patients on haemodialysis. Difelikefalin is cleared by dialysis (70-80% reduction per session).
- **Hepatic Impairment:** 
    - **Mild to Moderate:** No dose adjustment required.
    - **Severe:** Not recommended as it has not been evaluated in this population [Australian].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Selective agonist of kappa opioid receptors (KOR). KORs are expressed on peripheral sensory neurons and immune cells; activation reduces itch signals and mediates anti-inflammatory effects. 
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Minimal CNS penetration due to hydrophilic properties and high polar surface area.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Plasma protein binding is approximately 30%.
    - **Metabolism:** Minimal metabolism; not a substrate for major CYP enzymes.
    - **Elimination:** In healthy subjects, primary elimination is renal (81%). In haemodialysis patients, elimination shifts toward faecal excretion (approx. 59%), with 19% recovered in dialysate. Half-life is prolonged roughly 10-fold in dialysis patients (23–31 hours) compared to healthy subjects (2–3 hours).

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pivotal Studies (KALM-1 and KALM-2):** Two Phase 3, double-blind, placebo-controlled trials (n=851) evaluated difelikefalin 0.5 mcg/kg 3x/week for 12 weeks.
- **Primary Outcomes:** A significantly higher percentage of patients achieved a ≥3-point reduction in the Worst Itching-Numerical Rating Scale (WI-NRS) compared to placebo (KALM-1: 51% vs 28%; KALM-2: 54% vs 42%).
- **Long-term Data:** Improvements in itch severity and quality of life were maintained for up to 52 weeks in open-label extension studies.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store below 30°C. 
- **Handling:** Do not freeze. 
- **Shelf Life:** Expiry date is located on the packaging; the product is for single use and any residue must be discarded [Australian].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule:** Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine) [Australian].
