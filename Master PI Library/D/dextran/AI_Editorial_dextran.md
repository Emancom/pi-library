# Editorial Product Information: Dextran 40

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial product information was synthesized from official Product Information (PI) documents provided by the **United States (FDA)**. 
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical data into a concise, professional reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official PI or local regulatory guidelines required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Dextran 40 (a linear glucose polymer/polysaccharide; average molecular weight 40,000 Daltons).
- **Chemical Class:** Artificial Colloid / Plasma Volume Expander.
- **Brand Names:** **LMD** [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - 10% LMD in 5% Dextrose Injection [US].
    - 10% LMD in 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Hospira, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Shock:** Adjunctive treatment of shock or impending shock resulting from hemorrhage, burns, surgery, or other trauma. It is not intended as a replacement for whole blood or blood components when available [US].
- **Extracorporeal Circulation:** Used as a priming fluid (sole prime or additive) in pump oxygenators [US].
- **Thromboembolism Prophylaxis:** Prophylaxis of venous thrombosis and pulmonary embolism in patients undergoing procedures with a high risk of thromboembolic complications (e.g., hip surgery) [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Adolescents)
- **Shock:** 
    - First 24 hours: Total dosage should not exceed 20 mL/kg. The initial 10 mL/kg may be infused rapidly to achieve clinical improvement.
    - Beyond 24 hours: Subsequent dosage should not exceed 10 mL/kg per day.
    - Maximum duration: Therapy should not exceed five days [US].
- **Extracorporeal Perfusion:** 
    - 10 to 20 mL/kg (1 to 2 g/kg) of the 10% solution added to the perfusion circuit. Total dosage should generally not exceed 2 g/kg [US].
- **VTE Prophylaxis:**
    - Day of surgery: 500 to 1,000 mL (approx. 10 mL/kg) initiated during surgery.
    - Post-operative: 500 mL daily for an additional 2 to 3 days. 
    - Extended risk: 500 mL every second or third day for up to two weeks [US].

### Administration Instructions
- **Pre-treatment:** **Dextran 1** should be administered prior to the infusion of clinical dextran solutions to reduce the risk of anaphylactoid reactions [US].
- **Monitoring:** Frequent monitoring of central venous pressure (CVP) is strongly recommended during the initial infusion to avoid vascular overload [US].
- **Filtration:** An administration set including a filter should be used when infusing concentrated LMD [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to dextran [US].
- Marked hemostatic defects of all types, including thrombocytopenia and hypofibrinogenemia (including those induced by drugs like heparin or warfarin) [US].
- Marked cardiac decompensation [US].
- Renal disease with severe oliguria or anuria [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Anaphylactoid Reactions:** Severe and fatal dextran-induced anaphylactoid reactions (DIAR) have been reported, often in patients with no prior exposure. Close observation is required during the first minutes of infusion. If an allergic reaction occurs, stop the infusion immediately and initiate resuscitative measures [US].
- **Circulatory Overload:** As a hypertonic colloid, LMD attracts extravascular water. Use with extreme caution in patients with heart failure or impaired renal clearance.
- **Renal Failure:** While LMD can have a "nephrotonic" effect, renal failure and osmotic nephrosis (tubular vacuolization) have been reported. Assessment of hydration via serum/urine osmolarity is recommended rather than specific gravity (which LMD artificially elevates) [US].
- **Bleeding Risk:** Doses exceeding recommended levels may increase the incidence of wound hematoma, melena, and hematuria. Use caution in patients with active hemorrhage as increased perfusion pressure may exacerbate blood loss [US].
- **Laboratory Interference:**
    - **Blood Sugar:** High acid concentrations used in assays may hydrolyze dextran, leading to falsely elevated glucose results.
    - **Laboratory Turbidity:** Dextran may cause turbidity interfering with bilirubin (alcohol-based) and total protein (biuret reagent) assays [US].
    - **Blood Typing:** May interfere with blood cross-matching (rouleaux formation). It is recommended to draw blood samples for typing/cross-matching *prior* to dextran administration [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Immune System:** Generalized urticaria, wheezing, tightness of chest, hypotension, nausea, vomiting, and severe anaphylactoid reactions (DIAR) [US].
- **Renal:** Osmotic nephrosis, renal failure, increased urine viscosity [US].
- **Hematologic:** Prolonged bleeding time, transient depression of platelet function, decreased plasma protein levels [US].
- **Infusion-related:** Febrile response, infection at the injection site, venous thrombosis, phlebitis, extravasation, and hypervolemia [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Additive Medications:** Should not be delivered via plasma volume expanders [US].
- **Corticosteroids/Corticotropin:** Exercise caution when administering sodium-containing solutions to patients receiving these agents [US].
- **Anticoagulants:** Increased risk of bleeding complications when used with heparin or warfarin (noted under contraindications/precautions) [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not specified]. Animal reproduction studies have not been conducted. It is unknown if dextran 40 causes fetal harm or affects reproduction capacity. Use in pregnant women only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** It is not known if dextran 40 is excreted in human milk. Exercise caution when administering to nursing women [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness in neonates are not established. Suggested dosing for infants is 5 mL/kg and for children 10 mL/kg [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed; however, the risk of circulatory overload and renal impairment (common in the elderly) necessitates cautious dosing.
- **Renal Impairment:** Dextran 40 is primarily excreted by the kidneys. Excessive doses in patients with advanced renal disease may precipitate acute renal failure [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Enhances blood flow and microcirculation through volume expansion (hemodilution), maintenance of red blood cell electronegativity, coating of platelets/RBCs, and decreasing blood viscosity [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Produces increases in central venous pressure, cardiac output, stroke volume, and urinary output. It reduces peripheral resistance and improves venous return. Unlike anticoagulants, it inhibits thrombus formation by reducing platelet adhesiveness and altering fibrin structure to make it more susceptible to plasmin digestion (lysis) [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** 
    - **Distribution:** Evenly distributed in the vascular system. Smaller molecules are excreted rapidly.
    - **Elimination:** In normovolemic subjects, ~50% is excreted in the urine within 3 hours, 60% within 6 hours, and 75% within 24 hours.
    - **Metabolism:** Unexcreted molecules diffuse into the extravascular space and are taken up by the reticuloendothelial system. Dextran is slowly degraded by the enzyme dextranase to glucose [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Thrombi Lysability:** Clinical trials have demonstrated that thrombi formed in the presence of dextran are more susceptible to lysis. Phlebography studies showed thrombi in dextran-treated patients have a higher tendency to undergo lysis compared to those not treated with dextran [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Precautions:** Protect from freezing. Do not use if the solution is not clear or if crystallization has occurred [US].
- **Single Use:** Contains no bacteriostat; discard any unused portion [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Container Safety:** Flexible plastic container contains DEHP; safety confirmed by USP biological standards for plastics [US].
