# Editorial Product Information: Docusate Sodium and Sennosides

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial information has been synthesised from official product labeling provided by the following jurisdiction: **US (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, consolidating regulatory information into a clinically relevant format.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and serves as a clinical reference. It is not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) or summary of product characteristics required for prescribing or dispensing in any specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** Docusate sodium (stool softener) and Sennosides (stimulant laxative).
- **Brand Names:** Equate Stool Softener + Stimulant Laxative [US].
- **ATC Code:** A06AB56 (Sennosides and docusate sodium).
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Oral tablet containing docusate sodium 50 mg and sennosides 8.6 mg [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Distributed by Walmart Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- Relief of occasional constipation (irregularity) [US].
- Generally expected to produce a bowel movement within 6 to 12 hours [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- Doses may be administered as a single daily dose, preferably in the evening, or in divided doses [US].
- **Adults and children 12 years and older:** 2 to 4 tablets daily [US].

### Pediatric Dosing
- **Children 6 to under 12 years:** 1 to 2 tablets daily [US].
- **Children 2 to under 6 years:** Up to 1 tablet daily [US].
- **Children under 2 years:** Consult a physician [US].

### Administration Instructions
- For oral use only [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Concomitant Medication:** Do not use in patients currently taking mineral oil unless directed by a physician [US].
- **Duration of Use:** Do not use laxative products for longer than 1 week unless directed by a physician [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### General Warnings
- Patients should consult a physician before use if they experience:
    - Abdominal pain, nausea, or vomiting [US].
    - A sudden change in bowel habits persisting for over 2 weeks [US].
- Treatment should be discontinued and a physician consulted if:
    - Rectal bleeding occurs [US].
    - A bowel movement fails to occur after use (may indicate a serious condition) [US].

### Monitoring Requirements
- Not specified in available sources.

### Specific Clinical Precautions
- **Sodium Content:** Each tablet contains 6 mg of sodium ("very low sodium") [US].
- **Calcium Content:** Each tablet contains 20 mg of calcium [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Rectal bleeding and failure to produce a bowel movement are noted as signs of potentially serious underlying conditions requiring medical intervention [US].
- **Frequency Data:** Not specified in available sources.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Mineral Oil:** Concomitant administration is contraindicated (unless directed by a physician) as docusate may increase the systemic absorption of mineral oil [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Healthcare professionals should be consulted before use in pregnant patients [US]. Specific pregnancy categories (e.g., US lettered categories) were not specified in the provided source.
- **Breastfeeding:** Healthcare professionals should be consulted before use in breastfeeding patients [US].
- **Fertility:** Not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Approved for use in children as young as 2 years of age with specific weight/age-based dosing. Use in children under 2 requires direct medical supervision [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources (standard adult dosing applies).
- **Renal Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** This is a combination therapy utilizing two mechanisms:
    - **Docusate sodium:** An anionic surfactant that acts as a stool softener by lowering the surface tension of stool, allowing water and fats to penetrate the fecal mass.
    - **Sennosides:** Stimulant laxatives (anthraquinones) that act on the intestinal mucosa to increase peristalsis and promote bowel evacuation.
- **Pharmacokinetics:** 
    - **Onset of action:** 6 to 12 hours [US].
    - **Absorption/Distribution/Metabolism/Elimination:** Not specified in available sources.

- **Tamper Evidence:** Do not use if the printed safety seal under the cap is broken or missing [US].
