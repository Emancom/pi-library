# Editorial Product Information: Dronabinol

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial synthesis is derived from the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following regulatory authority: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document is a harmonised editorial overview intended for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises clinical data and regulatory requirements into a single reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This editorial synthesis is not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific Product Information. Clinicians must consult the approved PI in their specific region before prescribing or dispensing this medication. As only the United States PI was provided for this synthesis, regional variations for other jurisdictions (e.g., Australia, Europe, Canada) are not included.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Dronabinol (synthetic delta-9-tetrahydrocannabinol [delta-9-THC]).
- **Chemical Class:** Cannabinoid.
- **Brand Names:** Not specified in provided source (generic identified from Major Pharmaceuticals) [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - Round, soft gelatin capsules [US]:
        - 2.5 mg: White (Identified M2)
        - 5 mg: Dark brown (Identified M5)
        - 10 mg: Orange (Identified MX)
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Major Pharmaceuticals; Distributed by Ascend Laboratories, LLC [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Anorexia associated with weight loss** in adult patients with Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (AIDS) [US-approved].
- **Nausea and vomiting associated with cancer chemotherapy** in adult patients who have failed to respond adequately to conventional antiemetic treatments [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Anorexia Associated with Weight Loss (AIDS)
- **Initial Dosage:** 2.5 mg orally twice daily, administered one hour before lunch and one hour before dinner [US].
- **Dosing for Intolerant/Elderly Patients:** Consider a starting dose of 2.5 mg once daily, administered one hour before dinner or at bedtime, to mitigate central nervous system (CNS) adverse effects [US].
- **Titration:** Dosage may be increased to 2.5 mg before lunch and 5 mg before dinner. Most patients respond to 2.5 mg twice daily. Further increases to 5 mg twice daily may be considered as tolerated [US].
- **Maximum Dosage:** 10 mg twice daily [US].

### Nausea and Vomiting (Chemotherapy)
- **Initial Dosage:** 5 mg/m² administered 1 to 3 hours prior to chemotherapy, then every 2 to 4 hours after chemotherapy, for a total of 4 to 6 doses per day [US].
- **Administration:** The first dose should be taken on an empty stomach at least 30 minutes before eating. Subsequent doses may be taken regardless of food [US].
- **Titration:** Increments of 2.5 mg/m² may be used to achieve clinical effect. 
- **Maximum Dosage:** 15 mg/m² per dose for 4 to 6 doses per day [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Dosing consistency relative to meal times should be maintained for each chemotherapy cycle once the titration is complete [US].
- Dosing later in the day may help reduce the frequency of CNS reactions [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- Patients with a history of hypersensitivity to dronabinol [US].
- Patients with a history of hypersensitivity to **sesame oil** (the vehicle for the drug) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Neuropsychiatric Adverse Reactions:** May exacerbate mania, depression, or schizophrenia. Cognitive impairment and altered mental states may occur. Avoid use in patients with a psychiatric history. Patients should be cautioned against operating heavy machinery or motor vehicles until they are certain the drug does not affect them adversely [US].
- **Hemodynamic Instability:** Use with caution in patients with cardiac disorders; hypotension, hypertension, syncope, and tachycardia have been reported [US].
- **Seizures:** Dronabinol may lower the seizure threshold. Weigh risks vs. benefits in patients with a history of seizures and discontinue if seizures occur [US].
- **Multiple Substance Abuse:** Patients with a history of substance abuse (including marijuana or alcohol) should be screened and monitored for signs of abuse or dependence [US].
- **Paradoxical Nausea, Vomiting, or Abdominal Pain:** Worsening symptoms may indicate Cannabinoid Hyperemesis Syndrome (CHS), requiring dose reduction or discontinuation [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common Adverse Effects (≥3% to 10%)
- **CNS:** Dizziness, euphoria, paranoid reaction, somnolence, thinking abnormal [US].
- **Gastrointestinal:** Abdominal pain, nausea, vomiting [US].

### Less Common Adverse Effects (1% to <3%)
- **Cardiovascular:** Palpitations, tachycardia, vasodilation/facial flush [US].
- **General:** Asthenia [US].
- **Other CNS:** Amnesia, anxiety/nervousness, ataxia, confusion, depersonalization, hallucination [US].

### Rare/Less Frequent (≤1%)
- Hypotension, conjunctival injection, diarrhea, fecal incontinence, myalgia, depression, nightmares, speech difficulties, tinnitus, chills, headache, and malaise [US].

### Serious Adverse Effects
- Seizures, loss of consciousness, disorientation, and severe hypersensitivity (e.g., lip swelling, throat tightness) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:**
    - **CYP2C9 and CYP3A4 Inhibitors** (e.g., amiodarone, fluconazole, ketoconazole, clarithromycin, grapefruit juice): May increase dronabinol exposure and risk of adverse effects [US].
    - **CYP2C9 and CYP3A4 Inducers:** May decrease dronabinol exposure and efficacy [US].
    - **Highly Protein-Bound Drugs** (e.g., warfarin, cyclosporine): Dronabinol may displace these drugs from protein binding sites; monitor for toxicity [US].
- **Pharmacodynamic Interactions:**
    - **Additive CNS Effects:** Concomitant use with alcohol, benzodiazepines, or other CNS depressants may increase sedation and cognitive impairment [US].
    - **Additive Cardiac Effects:** Concomitant use with amphetamines, tricyclic antidepressants, or anticholinergics may increase the risk of tachycardia or hypertension [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Dronabinol may cause fetal harm. Animal data show increased fetal mortality and resorptions at toxic maternal doses. Use should be avoided in pregnant women [US]. 
- **Breastfeeding:** 
    - **HIV-Infected Mothers:** Breastfeeding is recommended against due to the risk of HIV transmission to the infant [US].
    - **Chemotherapy Patients:** Dronabinol passes into human milk. Breastfeeding infants should be monitored for weight and potential adverse effects [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established in pediatric patients [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Increased sensitivity to neuropsychiatric and postural hypotensive effects. Consider lower starting doses and implement fall precautions, particularly in patients with dementia [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources, though extensive hepatic metabolism suggests caution [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Genetic Polymorphism:** Individuals with diminished **CYP2C9** function may experience 2- to 3-fold higher drug exposure [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Orally active cannabinoid with complex CNS effects, including central sympathomimetic activity. It acts via cannabinoid receptors in neural tissues [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** 90–95% absorbed, but significant first-pass metabolism results in 10–20% systemic bioavailability. Onset of action: 0.5–1 hour. Peak effect: 2–4 hours [US].
    - **Distribution:** Highly lipid-soluble with a volume of distribution of ~10 L/kg. 97% protein bound [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Extensive hepatic metabolism via CYP2C9 and CYP3A4 to active (11-hydroxy-delta-9-THC) and inactive metabolites [US].
    - **Elimination:** Biliary excretion is the primary route (~50% in feces, 10–15% in urine). Terminal half-life is 25–36 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **AIDS Appetite Stimulation:** A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study of 139 patients showed a statistically significant difference in appetite improvement at 4 and 6 weeks compared to placebo using a visual analog scale [US].
- **Antiemetic Efficacy:** Effectiveness was established in patients who failed conventional antiemetic treatments for cancer chemotherapy [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage:** Store in a cool environment between **8°C and 15°C (46°F to 59°F)**. Alternatively, store in a refrigerator [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing. Keep the container closed tightly [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as a **Schedule III (CIII)** controlled substance under the Controlled Substances Act [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified for other jurisdictions in available sources.
