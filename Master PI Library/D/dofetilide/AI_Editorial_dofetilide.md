# Editorial Product Information: Dofetilide

## Editorial Note
- Contributing Jurisdictions: United States (FDA)
- Purpose: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of **dofetilide** for healthcare professionals, synthesising regulatory information to support clinical decision-making.
- Disclaimer: This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Dofetilide (a Vaughan Williams Class III antiarrhythmic agent).
- **Chemical Class:** Methanesulfonamide derivative.
- **Brand Names:** Information based on generic **dofetilide** (Major Pharmaceuticals) [US].
- **ATC Code:** C01BD04.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - Oral Capsules: 125 mcg (0.125 mg), 250 mcg (0.25 mg), and 500 mcg (0.5 mg) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Major Pharmaceuticals; Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Maintenance of Normal Sinus Rhythm:** Indicated for the maintenance of normal sinus rhythm (delay in time to recurrence of atrial fibrillation/atrial flutter [AF/AFl]) in patients with AF/AFl of greater than one week duration who have been converted to normal sinus rhythm [US].
- **Conversion to Normal Sinus Rhythm:** Indicated for the conversion of atrial fibrillation and atrial flutter to normal sinus rhythm [US].
- **Note:** Dofetilide has not been shown to be effective in patients with paroxysmal atrial fibrillation [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
Dosing must be individualised based on calculated creatinine clearance (CrCl) and the QTc interval. The maximum recommended dose is 500 mcg twice daily [US].

### Initiation and Mandatory Monitoring
- **Hospitalization Requirement:** To minimize the risk of induced arrhythmia, therapy must be initiated (or re-initiated) in a facility that provides continuous ECG monitoring, cardiac resuscitation, and CrCl calculation for a minimum of **three days** [US].
- **Pre-initiation Assessment:** 
    - Calculate CrCl using the Cockcroft-Gault formula.
    - Measure baseline QTc (average of 5–10 beats). If QTc >440 msec (500 msec in patients with ventricular conduction abnormalities), dofetilide is contraindicated [US].

### Dosing Algorithm by Creatinine Clearance
| Calculated CrCl | Dofetilide Starting Dose |
| :--- | :--- |
| >60 mL/min | 500 mcg twice daily |
| 40 to 60 mL/min | 250 mcg twice daily |
| 20 to <40 mL/min | 125 mcg twice daily |
| <20 mL/min | **CONTRAINDICATED** |

### In-Hospital Titration and Adjustment
- **QTc Evaluation:** Measure QTc 2–3 hours after the first dose.
- **Dose Reduction:** If QTc increases by >15% from baseline OR exceeds 500 msec (550 msec for conduction abnormalities), reduce the dose as follows [US]:
    - 500 mcg BID $\rightarrow$ 250 mcg BID
    - 250 mcg BID $\rightarrow$ 125 mcg BID
    - 125 mcg BID $\rightarrow$ 125 mcg once daily
- **Discontinuation:** If at any time after the second dose the QTc exceeds 500 msec (550 msec for conduction abnormalities), dofetilide must be discontinued [US].

### Missed Dose Instructions
Patients should not double the next dose if a dose is missed. The next dose should be taken at the usual time [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- Congenital or acquired long QT syndromes (baseline QTc >440 msec or 500 msec with conduction abnormalities) [US].
- Severe renal impairment (CrCl <20 mL/min) [US].
- Known hypersensitivity to dofetilide [US].
- **Concomitant use of the following drugs [US]:**
    - **Cimetidine**
    - **Verapamil**
    - **Ketoconazole**
    - **Trimethoprim** (alone or with sulfamethoxazole)
    - **Hydrochlorothiazide** (alone or in combination)
    - **Prochlorperazine**
    - **Dolutegravir**
    - **Megestrol**

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warning [US]
**Dofetilide can cause serious ventricular arrhythmias, primarily Torsade de Pointes (TdP). To minimize this risk, patients must be initiated/re-initiated in a facility for at least 3 days for continuous ECG monitoring and CrCl calculation.**

### General Warnings
- **Ventricular Arrhythmia:** TdP risk is directly related to plasma concentration and QTc prolongation. Incidence of TdP is 0.8% in supraventricular arrhythmia patients but can reach 10.5% at doses >500 mcg BID [US].
- **Electrolyte Imbalance:** Hypokalemia and hypomagnesemia increase the risk of TdP. Potassium levels should be maintained >3.6–4.0 mEq/L [US].
- **Cardiac Conduction:** While dofetilide does not typically affect conduction velocity or the PR interval, patients with sick sinus syndrome or 2nd/3rd-degree heart block were excluded from trials unless a pacemaker was present [US].

### Monitoring Requirements
- Renal function and QTc should be re-evaluated every three months or as medically warranted [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions are categorised by frequency based on supraventricular arrhythmia studies [US]:

| Frequency | Adverse Event |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Headache (11%) |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Chest pain (10%), Dizziness (8%), Respiratory tract infection (7%), Dyspnea (6%), Nausea (5%), Insomnia (4%), Flu syndrome (4%), Back pain (3%), Rash (3%), Diarrhea (3%), Abdominal pain (3%), Ventricular tachycardia (3.3%) |
| **Serious** | **Torsade de Pointes** (0.8%), Ventricular fibrillation, Angioedema, Sudden death |

- **Gender Difference:** The risk of Torsade de Pointes was approximately 3 times higher in women than in men [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
### Pharmacokinetic Interactions
- **Renal Cationic Transport Inhibition:** Major clearance pathway for dofetilide. Concomitant use of inhibitors (e.g., cimetidine, trimethoprim) significantly increases dofetilide levels and is contraindicated [US].
- **CYP3A4 Metabolism:** Dofetilide is a minor substrate. Inhibitors (e.g., macrolides, azoles, protease inhibitors, grapefruit juice) should be used with extreme caution [US].

### Pharmacodynamic Interactions
- **QT-Prolonging Drugs:** Concomitant use with Class I/III antiarrhythmics, phenothiazines, tricyclic antidepressants, and certain fluoroquinolones is not recommended. Class I/III agents should be withheld for at least three half-lives before starting dofetilide [US].
- **Amiodarone:** Do not start dofetilide until amiodarone has been stopped for 3 months or serum levels are <0.3 mg/L [US].
- **Digoxin:** Associated with an increased risk of TdP (though PK interaction is absent) [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category C]. Dofetilide is teratogenic in rats and mice (cleft palate, adactyly, levocardia). There are no adequate studies in pregnant women; use only if potential benefit justifies the risk to the fetus [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Not specified if dofetilide is excreted in human milk. Due to the potential for serious adverse reactions in the infant, patients are advised not to breastfeed [US].
- **Fertility:** No effect on mating or fertility was observed in rats at doses up to 1.0 mg/kg/day, though testicular atrophy was noted in chronic studies across multiple species [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness in children (<18 years) have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** No overall differences in safety or efficacy were observed. However, as elderly patients are more likely to have decreased renal function, dose selection must be cautious and based on calculated CrCl [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Clearance decreases as CrCl decreases. Dose adjustment is mandatory (see Section 3). Contraindicated if CrCl <20 mL/min [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment required for mild to moderate (Child-Pugh Class A and B) impairment. Severe impairment has not been studied; use with particular caution [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Blockade of the rapid component of the delayed rectifier potassium current ($I_{Kr}$), which increases the monophasic action potential duration and effective refractory period in both the atria and ventricles [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Oral bioavailability >90%; $T_{max}$ is 2–3 hours (fasted). Unaffected by food or antacids [US].
    - **Distribution:** Plasma protein binding 60–70%; Volume of distribution is 3 L/kg [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily renal excretion; minor metabolism via CYP3A4 (N-dealkylation, N-oxidation) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 80% excreted in urine (80% as unchanged drug). Terminal half-life is ~10 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **AF/AFl Conversion/Maintenance:** Two randomized trials (996 patients) demonstrated that dofetilide 500 mcg BID achieved a ~30% acute conversion rate vs 1% for placebo. Maintenance of NSR at 12 months was ~46% for dofetilide 500 mcg BID vs ~22% for placebo [US].
- **DIAMOND Studies:** Two large trials (DIAMOND CHF and DIAMOND MI) in patients with LVEF $\leq$35% showed that dofetilide did not increase mortality in these high-risk populations when initiated carefully in a hospital setting [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C [US].
- **Conditions:** Protect from moisture and humidity. Dispense in tight, light-resistant containers [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Rx-only. Scheduled for institutional use only in unit-dose packaging [US].
