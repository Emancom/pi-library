# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the **United States (FDA)**.
- At the time of this editorial's publication, only the United States jurisdiction's source documentation was available for review.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical data into a concise, structured format.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific PI. Clinicians must consult the prevailing local regulatory documentation before prescribing.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine [(R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt].
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative (carrier molecule).
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection, USP (Baxter Healthcare Corporation).
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Sterile injection solution for intravenous use.
    - 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency in Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Indicated for both acute and chronic treatment of patients with metabolic defects resulting in secondary deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Pediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:**
    - **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg administered as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion [US].
    - **Loading Dose:** In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose may be given, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours.
    - **Frequency:** Administered every 3 or 4 hours (q3h or q4h); frequency should never be less than every 6 hours (q6h).
    - **Maximum Dose:** Doses up to 300 mg/kg have been administered [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight) [US].
    - **Administration:** Slow 2 to 3 minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
    - **Dose Adjustments:** Guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy if required.

### Administration Instructions
- Inspect visually for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration.
- Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as it contains no preservatives.

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known** [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### General Warnings
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients on dialysis. These may occur within minutes of administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately [US].
- **Neurologic Reactions (Seizures):** Seizures have been reported in both patients with and without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].

### Monitoring Requirements
- **Plasma Carnitine Levels:** It is recommended to obtain levels prior to parenteral therapy. 
- **Ongoing Monitoring:** Weekly and monthly monitoring should include blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine levels between 35 and 60 µmol/L) [US].

### Specific Clinical Precautions
- **Renal Impairment:** While the injection is used in ESRD, the safety/efficacy of *oral* levocarnitine in patients with severe renal compromise has not been established, as it may lead to accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and TMAO) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are categorised by frequency based on clinical trials in ESRD patients [US]:

- **Very Common (>10%):** 
    - **General:** Headache (up to 37%), Infection (up to 24%), Injection site reaction (up to 38%), Pain (up to 35%), Asthenia, Chest pain, Flu syndrome.
    - **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (18–21%), Hypotension (up to 19%).
    - **Digestive:** Diarrhea (9–35%), Vomiting (9–21%), Nausea (5–12%).
    - **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (15–27%), Cough increase, Dyspnea, Rhinitis.
- **Common (1–10%):** 
    - **General:** Abdominal pain, Fever, Allergic reaction, Back pain.
    - **Cardiovascular:** Arrhythmia, Atrial fibrillation, Palpitations, Tachycardia.
    - **Metabolic:** Hypercalcemia, Hyperkalemia, Hypervolemia, Peripheral edema, Weight changes.
    - **Neurologic:** Dizziness, Paresthesia, Insomnia, Depression, Anxiety.
    - **Other:** Body odor (transient), Gastritis, Anorexia, Constipation, Taste perversion, Pruritus, Rash, Eye disorders, Urinary tract infection.

### Serious Adverse Effects
- **Anaphylaxis and Severe Hypersensitivity.**
- **Seizures.**

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have occurred when used concurrently with levocarnitine. **Management:** Monitor INR levels at treatment initiation and following any dose adjustments [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not specified by letter, but descriptive]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine is a normal component of human milk. Studies in dairy cows indicate increased milk concentrations following exogenous administration. In nursing mothers, weigh the risk of excess carnitine intake in the infant against the benefits of supplementation for the mother [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing is weight-based (50 mg/kg for metabolic disorders; 10–20 mg/kg for ESRD). See Section 3.
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically detailed in provided sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Primarily indicated for ESRD patients on dialysis. Adjustments are based on pre-dialysis trough levels.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring carrier molecule required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent ATP production [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In patients with inborn errors of metabolism, levocarnitine promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters, which are rapidly excreted.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not protein-bound; mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces).
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours (uncorrected for endogenous levels). Terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Bioavailability Study:** In 15 healthy volunteers, oral tablets and solutions were found to be bioequivalent. Absolute bioavailability (corrected for endogenous levels) was approximately 15–16% [US].
- **ESRD Studies:** Clinical trials showed that 20 mg/kg doses three times weekly post-dialysis significantly increased plasma levocarnitine concentrations from ~20 µmol/L (pre-dialysis) to steady-state troughs of ~190–243 µmol/L over 8–24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Precautions:** Avoid excessive heat; protect from freezing [US].
- **Stability:** Compatible with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s (0.5 mg/mL to 8.0 mg/mL) for up to 24 hours at room temperature in PVC bags [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Rx-only.
