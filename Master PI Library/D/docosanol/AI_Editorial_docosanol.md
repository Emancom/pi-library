## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Docosanol 10% (100 mg per 1 g of cream).
- **Chemical Class:** Saturated fatty alcohol.
- **Brand Names:** Docosanol Cream, 10% [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Topical cream, 10% [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Distributed by Sportpharm LLC; Relabeled by Enovachem Pharmaceuticals [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Herpes Labialis (Cold Sores/Fever Blisters):** Indicated for the treatment of cold sores or fever blisters located on the face or lips [US].
- **Symptom Management:** Used to shorten healing time and reduce the duration of symptoms, including tingling, pain, burning, and/or itching [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adults and Children 12 years and older):** 
    - Apply to the affected area on the face or lips five (5) times daily until the lesion is healed [US].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Hands should be washed before and after application [US].
    - Treatment should ideally commence at the first sign of a cold sore or fever blister (e.g., tingling/prodrome) to ensure optimal results [US].
    - The cream should be rubbed in gently but completely [US].
    - Avoid application directly inside the mouth or in/near the eyes [US].
- **Missed Dose Instructions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Contraindicated in individuals with a known history of allergy or hypersensitivity to docosanol or any inactive ingredient in the formulation (e.g., benzyl alcohol, propylene glycol) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **General Warnings:**
    - **External Use Only:** For topical application to the face and lips only [US].
    - **Infection Control:** Patients should be advised not to share the product with others, as this may spread the viral infection [US].
- **Allergic Reactions:** The product may cause severe allergic reactions. Symptoms requiring immediate medical attention include hives, facial swelling, wheezing/respiratory difficulty, rash, or shock [US].
- **Duration of Use:** Patients should stop use and consult a physician if the cold sore worsens or if healing is not achieved within 10 days [US].
- **Monitoring:** Clinical assessment is required if symptoms persist beyond the standard treatment window.

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Immune System Disorders:**
    - **Rare/Frequency Not Specified:** Severe allergic reactions (hives, facial swelling, wheezing, anaphylactic shock, rash) [US].
- **Local Reactions:** While not explicitly listed as a frequency-based table in the source, the label implies potential for local irritation given the allergy alerts [US].
