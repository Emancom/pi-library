# Editorial Product Information: diphenhydramine hydrochloride

## 1. Product Identification
- Active ingredient (INN/generic name): **diphenhydramine hydrochloride**
- Chemical class: Antihistamine
- ATC code: Not specified in available sources
- Route(s) of administration: Oral

- Date of first registration/approval by jurisdiction:
  - [US] Marketing start date documented as 01/01/2012 (OTC monograph listing indicated in available source).
- Date of last revision/update of source PI(s): Not specified in available sources.
- Withdrawal, suspension, or restriction history: Not specified in available sources.

## 3. Approved Indications
- Temporary relief of symptoms due to hay fever or other upper respiratory allergies, including:
  - runny nose
  - sneezing
  - itching of the nose or throat
  - itchy, watery eyes
- Temporary relief of symptoms due to the common cold, including:
  - runny nose
  - sneezing

(Indications above reflect the uses stated in the available source documents.)

## 4. Dosage and Administration
### General dosing (adult)
- Adults and adolescents 12 years and over: 1 to 2 caplets every 4 to 6 hours as needed.
- Do not take more than 6 doses in 24 hours.

### Dosing by indication
- No indication-specific dose adjustments provided beyond the general dosing for symptomatic relief as stated above.

### Administration instructions
- Take every 4–6 hours as needed.
- Do not exceed the stated maximum dosing frequency (no more than 6 times in 24 hours).
- Do not use in children under 12 years of age (see Special Populations).

### Titration schedules
- Not specified in available sources.

### Missed dose instructions
- Not specified in available sources.

## 5. Contraindications
- Use in children under 12 years of age: do not use.
- Do not use diphenhydramine concurrently with another product containing diphenhydramine for the purpose of making a child sleepy (explicit contraindicated use mentioned in available sources).
- Any other contraindications (for example, known hypersensitivity to diphenhydramine) are not specified in the available sources.

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed warnings
- Not specified in available sources.

### General warnings and precautions
- **Marked drowsiness may occur.**
- Use caution when driving a motor vehicle or operating machinery while taking this product.
- Avoid alcoholic beverages while using diphenhydramine; alcohol may increase drowsiness.
- Alcohol, sedatives, and tranquilizers may increase the sedative effect of diphenhydramine.
- Excitability may occur, especially in children.
- Do not use to make a child sleepy with any other product containing diphenhydramine, including topical products.
- Keep out of the reach of children.
- In case of overdose, contact a physician or a poison control center immediately.

### Specific clinical precautions (ask a doctor before use)
- Patients with breathing problems such as emphysema or chronic bronchitis should consult a physician before use.
- Patients with difficulty in urinating due to enlargement of the prostate gland should consult a physician before use.
- Patients with glaucoma should consult a physician before use.
- Patients taking sedatives or tranquilizers should ask a doctor or pharmacist before use.
- If pregnant or breastfeeding, ask a health care professional before use.

## 8. Drug Interactions
### Pharmacodynamic interactions
- Alcohol: co‑use may increase drowsiness and sedation; avoid alcohol while using diphenhydramine.
- Sedatives and tranquilizers: concomitant use may increase sedative effects; consult a healthcare professional prior to coadministration.
- Any other pharmacodynamic interactions are not specified in available sources.

### Pharmacokinetic interactions
- Not specified in available sources.

### Clinically significant interactions and management notes
- Do not administer diphenhydramine with other products that contain diphenhydramine for the purpose of inducing sedation in a child.
- Exercise caution and consider avoidance or dose modification when coadministering with alcohol, sedatives, or tranquilizers due to additive CNS depressant effects.
- For other concomitant medications, consult a clinician or pharmacist; specific interaction management guidance is not provided in the available sources.
