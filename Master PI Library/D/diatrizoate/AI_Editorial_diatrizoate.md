# Editorial Product Information: Diatrizoate Meglumine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **US (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising available regulatory data into a single clinical reference.
- **Note:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI or local regulatory guidelines required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Diatrizoate meglumine (a radiopaque contrast agent).
- **Brand Name(s):** **Cystografin**, **Cystografin Dilute** [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Injection solution: 30% (300 mg/mL diatrizoate meglumine, equivalent to 141 mg/mL organically bound iodine) [US].
    - **Cystografin Dilute**: 18% (180 mg/mL diatrizoate meglumine) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Retrograde instillation (specifically for the lower urinary tract). **NOT intended for intravascular injection** [US].
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Bracco Diagnostics Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Retrograde cystourethrography:** Indicated for radiographic visualisation of the lower urinary tract [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **Patient Preparation:** Optimal results are achieved with appropriate bowel preparation, typically involving a laxative the night before and a low-residue diet the day preceding the procedure [US].
- **General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric):**
    - The standard dose for cystography or voiding cystourethrography ranges from **25 mL to 300 mL**.
    - The volume depends on the patient's age and bladder irritability/capacity.
    - Doses exceeding 300 mL may be utilized if bladder capacity permits.
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Administer via sterile catheterization using aseptic technique.
    - The bladder should be filled to capacity; bladder discomfort, spontaneous voiding, or reflux typically indicate a full bladder.
    - Avoid excessive pressure or rapid distention of the bladder to prevent trauma or intravasation [US].
- **Dilution Guidance:** **Cystografin** (30%) may be diluted with sterile water or sterile saline to reach desired concentrations (e.g., 18% to 24%). Diluted solutions must be used immediately [US].
- **Radiography:** A scout film is recommended prior to administration of the contrast agent [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to salts of diatrizoic acid [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Intravascular Injection:** This specific formulation is **not** for intravascular use [US].
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Severe reactions are more frequent in patients with a history of bronchial asthma, significant allergies, or previous contrast media reactions. A history of iodine sensitivity requires extreme caution during administration [US].
- **Emergency Preparedness:** Use requires the immediate availability of Resuscitative equipment, oxygen, and emergency drugs to manage anaphylactoid reactions (e.g., respiratory distress, convulsions, shock, or edema of the face/glottis) [US].
- **Urinary Tract Infection:** Exercise caution when performing retrograde procedures in the presence of active urinary tract infections [US].
- **Endocrine Effects:** Thyroid suppression or hypothyroidism has been reported uncommonly following iodinated contrast administration in both adults and infants [US].
- **Procedural Precautions:** Avoid excessive pressure during instillation to prevent urethral or bladder perforation and potential intravasation [US].
- **Laboratory Interference:** Contrast media may interfere with chemical urine testing. Collect urine specimens either before administration or at least two days following the procedure [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Procedural Complications:** Hematuria, urethral or bladder perforation, introduction of infection, and oliguria or anuria [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Anaphylactoid reactions ranging from mild to fatal shock.
- **Endocrine:** Transient thyroid suppression or hypothyroidism [US].
- **Systemic Reactions (if intravasation occurs):** If the drug enters the bloodstream via trauma or high pressure, systemic reactions associated with intravenous contrast media may occur [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Urine Chemistry:** May interfere with certain chemical determinations in urine specimens [US].
- **Other Interactions:** No specific pharmacokinetic or pharmacodynamic drug-drug interactions are listed in the available source [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not assigned (Traditional letter category not provided in the revised label).
    - Animal reproduction studies have not been conducted. It is unknown if **diatrizoate meglumine** affects reproductive capacity or causes fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Safety information and recommendations are not specified in available sources [US].
- **Fertility:** Data not specified in available sources [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing is adjusted based on age and bladder capacity. Reports of thyroid suppression have occurred in infants following iodinated contrast use [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in the available source [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Specific dose adjustments for renal or hepatic impairment are not specified, although oliguria/anuria are noted as possible adverse complications of the procedure [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** **Diatrizoate meglumine** is a radiopaque organic iodine compound. The organically bound iodine absorbs X-rays, allowing for the visualisation of the internal structures of the bladder and urethra during retrograde instillation [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified for the retrograde route in available sources.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** The diagnostic effect is immediate upon instillation into the urinary tract [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- Pivotal trial data were not provided in the available source document [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20-25°C (68-77°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from strong light. The solution is sterile and should be clear/colorless to pale yellow; discard if significant darkening or precipitates occur [US].
