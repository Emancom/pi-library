# Editorial Product Information: Dronedarone

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA). 
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of dronedarone, synthesising regulatory information for use by healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. At the time of writing, only the United States (FDA) source documentation was provided for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Dronedarone hydrochloride (a benzofuran derivative).
- **Brand Name(s):** **Multaq** [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 400 mg film-coated tablets [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** sanofi-aventis U.S. LLC; distributed by Cardinal Health [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Atrial Fibrillation (AF) / Atrial Flutter (AFL):** Indicated to reduce the risk of cardiovascular hospitalisation in patients currently in sinus rhythm but with a documented history of paroxysmal or persistent atrial fibrillation [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Adult Dosing:** 400 mg twice daily [US].
- **Administration Instructions:** Tablets must be taken with the morning and evening meals to increase bioavailability [US].
- **Pre-treatment Verification:** 
    - Ensure females of reproductive potential are not pregnant before initiation [US].
    - Discontinue Class I or III antiarrhythmics (e.g., amiodarone, sotalol, flecainide) or strong CYP3A inhibitors before starting treatment [US].
- **Missed Dose:** Patients should skip the missed dose and resume the next dose at the regularly scheduled time. Doses should not be doubled [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Heart Failure:** Symptomatic heart failure with recent decompensation requiring hospitalisation or NYHA Class IV symptoms [US].
- **Permanent Atrial Fibrillation:** Patients in whom normal sinus rhythm will not or cannot be restored [US].
- **Cardiac Conduction Abnormalities:** Second- or third-degree atrioventricular (AV) block or sick sinus syndrome (unless used with a functional pacemaker) [US].
- **Bradycardia:** Heart rate <50 bpm [US].
- **Concomitant Medications:**
    - Strong CYP3A inhibitors (e.g., ketoconazole, clarithromycin, ritonavir, cyclosporine) [US].
    - Drugs or herbal products that prolong the QT interval (e.g., phenothiazines, tricyclic antidepressants, certain macrolides) [US].
- **Prior Toxicity:** History of liver or lung toxicity related to previous amiodarone use [US].
- **ECG Parameters:** QTc interval >500 ms or PR interval >280 ms [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Severe hepatic impairment [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known allergy to dronedarone or any excipients [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning [US]:** 
    - **Increased Risk of Death, Stroke, and Heart Failure:** Dronedarone doubles the risk of death in patients with symptomatic heart failure and recent decompensation. 
    - In patients with permanent AF, the drug doubles the risk of death, stroke, and heart failure hospitalisation.
- **Cardiovascular Monitoring:** Monitor cardiac rhythm at least every 3 months. If AF is detected, either cardiovert the patient or discontinue the drug [US]. Ensure appropriate antithrombotic therapy is maintained [US].
- **Liver Injury:** Hepatocellular injury and acute liver failure (some requiring transplant) have been reported. Monitor hepatic enzymes periodically, especially during the first 6 months. Discontinue if injury is suspected [US].
- **Pulmonary Toxicity:** Interstitial lung disease, including pneumonitis and fibrosis, has been reported. Discontinue if pulmonary toxicity is confirmed [US].
- **Renal Function:** Dronedarone inhibits tubular secretion of creatinine, typically causing a small increase (~0.1 mg/dL) that plateaus within 7 days and is reversible. However, marked increases in creatinine and acute renal failure have occurred, often in the setting of heart failure or hypovolemia [US].
- **Electrolyte Imbalance:** Hypokalemia and hypomagnesemia should be corrected before and during treatment, especially when used with potassium-depleting diuretics [US].
- **Embryofetal Toxicity:** May cause fetal harm. Effective contraception is required during treatment and for 5 days after the final dose [US].

| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Diarrhea** | 9% | 6% |
| **Asthenic conditions** | 7% | 5% |
| **Nausea** | 5% | 3% |
| **Skin issues (Rash/Pruritus)** | 5% | 3% |
| **Abdominal pain** | 4% | 3% |
| **Bradycardia** | 3% | 1% |
| **Vomiting** | 2% | 1% |
| **Dyspepsia** | 2% | 1% |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** New-onset or worsening heart failure, acute liver failure, interstitial lung disease, and QTc prolongation [US].
- **Laboratory Changes:** Early increases in serum creatinine (≥10%) occurred in 51% of patients [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **CYP3A4 Interactions:** 
    - **Inhibitors:** Strong inhibitors are contraindicated (increases dronedarone exposure up to 17-fold). Avoid grapefruit juice [US]. 
    - **Inducers:** Avoid rifampin, St. John’s wort, or phenytoin (significantly decreases dronedarone exposure) [US].
- **P-glycoprotein (P-gp) Substrates:**
    - **Digoxin:** Dronedarone increases digoxin levels. Consider discontinuation or halving the digoxin dose; monitor levels closely [US].
    - **Dabigatran:** Reduce dose to 75 mg BID in moderate renal impairment (CrCL 30-50 mL/min). Avoid in severe renal impairment [US].
- **Statins:** Avoid simvastatin doses >10 mg daily. Monitor for statin-related toxicity [US].
- **Warfarin:** Monitor INR after initiation, as postmarketing cases of increased INR have occurred, although clinical trials showed no significant change in S-warfarin levels [US].
- **Beta-blockers/Calcium Channel Blockers:** May potentiate bradycardia and AV node conduction delay. Start at low doses and titrate based on ECG [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Not specified by letter category in source, but stated as potentially causing fetal harm based on animal malformations (visceral and skeletal) at doses equivalent to human exposure. A pregnancy test is required prior to initiation [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Discontinue breastfeeding during treatment and for 5 days after the last dose due to the risk of serious adverse reactions in the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** In animal studies, high doses caused irregular estrus cycles and decreased corpora lutea, but no effects on mating behavior or male fertility were noted [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy in patients under 18 years of age have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Exposure is approximately 23% higher in patients ≥65 years, but safety and efficacy profiles are generally similar to younger adults [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment required for mild, moderate, or severe impairment, as renal excretion is minimal. However, monitor renal function periodically [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment for mild to moderate impairment. Contraindicated in severe hepatic impairment [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Unknown, but exhibits properties of all four Vaughan-Williams classes (sodium, potassium, and calcium channel blockade, and non-competitive anti-adrenergic activity) [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Absolute bioavailability is low (4%), increasing to 15% when taken with food [US].
    - **Distribution:** >98% protein bound (primarily to albumin). Volume of distribution is ~1400 L [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Extensively metabolised by CYP3A4. The major active metabolite is N-debutyl dronedarone [US].
    - **Elimination:** Terminal half-life is 13–19 hours. Excretion is primarily fecal (84%) as metabolites; only 6% is excreted in urine [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ATHENA:** A large trial (N=4628) showing dronedarone reduced the risk of cardiovascular hospitalisation or death from any cause by 24.2% compared to placebo in patients with a history of AF/AFL [US].
- **EURIDIS and ADONIS:** Pooled data showed a 25% reduction in the risk of first AF/AFL recurrence over 12 months [US].
- **ANDROMEDA (Heart Failure):** Terminated early due to excess mortality in the dronedarone group, primarily due to worsening heart failure [US].
- **PALLAS (Permanent AF):** Terminated early due to significant increases in mortality, stroke, and heart failure hospitalisation [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F). Excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
- **Shelf Life:** Not specified in available sources.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Prescription Only (Rx-only) [US].
