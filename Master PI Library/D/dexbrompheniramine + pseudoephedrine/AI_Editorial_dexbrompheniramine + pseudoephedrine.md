# Editorial Product Information: dexbrompheniramine + pseudoephedrine

## 1. Product Identification
- Active ingredient (INN/generic name): **dexbrompheniramine + pseudoephedrine**
- Chemical class:
  - Dexbrompheniramine — antihistamine
  - Pseudoephedrine — nasal decongestant (sympathomimetic decongestant)
- ATC code: Not specified in available sources
- Route(s) of administration: Oral

## 2. Regulatory History
- Marketing start date: 12/01/2024 (as stated in the available source)
- Last revision/update of source PI: Revised: 12/2025 (as stated in the available source)
- Withdrawal, suspension, or restriction history: Not specified in available sources
- If other registration dates are required: Not specified in available sources

## 3. Approved Indications
- Temporary relief of nasal congestion due to the common cold, hay fever (allergic rhinitis), or other upper respiratory allergies.
- Decongestion of sinus openings and sinus passages; reduction of swelling of nasal passages to temporarily restore freer nasal breathing.
- Temporary alleviation of symptoms due to hay fever (allergic rhinitis):
  - Runny nose
  - Sneezing
  - Itching of the nose or throat
  - Itchy and watery eyes

## 4. Dosage and Administration
- Strength in provided product sample: dexbrompheniramine maleate 2 mg and pseudoephedrine hydrochloride 60 mg per 5 mL.
- General adult dosing:
  - Adults and children 12 years of age and over: two teaspoonfuls (10 mL) every 4 to 6 hours.
  - Do not exceed more than 4 doses in any 24‑hour period unless directed by a doctor.
- Paediatric dosing:
  - Children under 12 years of age: consult a doctor; dosing not provided in available sources.
- Administration instructions:
  - Do not exceed the recommended dosage.
  - Avoid alcoholic beverages while using this product.
  - Alcohol, sedatives and tranquilizers may increase drowsiness.
  - Use caution when driving a motor vehicle or operating machinery because drowsiness may occur.
  - Excitability may occur, especially in children.
- Titration schedules: Not specified in available sources
- Missed dose instructions: Not specified in available sources

## 5. Contraindications
- **Do not use** in patients taking a prescription monoamine oxidase inhibitor (MAOI) or within 2 weeks of stopping an MAOI. (If the patient is uncertain whether they are taking an MAOI, they should consult a doctor or pharmacist before use.)
- No other absolute contraindications are specified in the available sources.

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed warnings
- None specified in available sources.

### General warnings and precautions
- Ask a doctor before use if the patient is taking sedatives or tranquilizers.
- Ask a doctor before use if the patient has:
  - Heart disease
  - High blood pressure
  - Thyroid disease
  - Diabetes
  - Difficulty in urination due to enlargement of the prostate gland
- When using this product:
  - Do not exceed the recommended dosage.
  - Excitability may occur, especially in children.
  - Drowsiness may occur; avoid alcoholic beverages.
  - Alcohol, sedatives and tranquilizers may increase drowsiness.
  - Use caution when driving or operating machinery.
- Stop use and ask a doctor if:
  - Nervousness, dizziness, or sleeplessness occur.
  - Symptoms do not improve within 7 days or occur with a fever.
- Keep out of the reach of children.
- In case of overdose, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.
- If pregnant or breastfeeding, consult a health professional before use.

### Monitoring requirements
- Specific monitoring recommendations (e.g., blood pressure monitoring, urinary retention checks) are not specified in available sources.

### Specific clinical precautions
- Patients with cardiovascular disease, hypertension, thyroid disorders, diabetes, or prostatic hypertrophy should consult a physician prior to use.
- Use with caution in children due to risk of excitability and other CNS effects.

## 8. Drug Interactions
- Monoamine oxidase inhibitors (MAOIs): **Concomitant use is contraindicated**; do not use during MAOI therapy or for 2 weeks after stopping an MAOI.
- Alcohol: May increase drowsiness; avoid alcoholic beverages while taking the product.
- Sedatives and tranquilizers: May increase drowsiness when used concurrently; consult a physician before co‑administration.
- Other interactions: No additional pharmacokinetic or pharmacodynamic interactions are specified in the available sources.
- Management notes:
  - Verify current medications for MAOI use before dispensing.
  - Counsel patients regarding additive sedative effects with alcohol, sedatives, or tranquilizers.
