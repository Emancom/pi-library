## 3. Approved Indications
- Relief of upset stomach associated with nausea (general OTC indication for symptomatic relief).
- Jurisdictional notes: Indication as symptomatic relief of nausea/upset stomach is presented in the available source document [US].

## 4. Dosage and Administration
- Active ingredient strengths (as reported per 5 mL):
  - Dextrose (glucose) 1.87 g per 5 mL
  - Fructose (levulose) 1.87 g per 5 mL
  - Phosphoric acid 21.5 mg per 5 mL
- General dosing:
  - Adults and children 12 years of age and over: 15 mL or 30 mL
  - Children 2 to under 12 years of age: 5 mL or 10 mL
  - Dosing frequency: Repeat dose every 15 minutes or until distress subsides
  - Maximum dosing constraint: Do not take more than 5 doses in 1 hour without consulting a doctor
- Administration instructions:
  - Administer orally.
  - For maximum effectiveness, do not dilute or drink fluids of any kind immediately before or immediately after taking the product.
  - Measure doses only with the dosing cup provided and use that cup only with this product.
  - Do not use concomitantly with other products (label instruction: "Do not use with other products").
- Titration schedules: Not specified in available sources
- Missed dose instructions: Not specified in available sources

## 5. Contraindications
- Known hereditary fructose intolerance (HFI) — product contains fructose and should not be taken by persons with HFI.
- Known hypersensitivity or allergic reaction to any of the ingredients (active or inactive) in the product.
- Additional jurisdictional contraindications: Not specified in available sources.

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
- Boxed warnings: None specified in available sources.
- General warnings and precautions:
  - **Contains fructose** — contraindicated in hereditary fructose intolerance.
  - Ask a doctor before use if you have diabetes (precaution noted on labeling).
  - If pregnant or breastfeeding, ask a health professional before use.
  - Stop use and ask a doctor if symptoms persist, return, or get worse.
  - Keep out of reach of children.
  - Do not use if printed seal under cap is broken or missing (tamper-evidence precaution).
- Monitoring requirements: Not specified in available sources (no laboratory or clinical monitoring guidance provided).
- Specific clinical precautions:
  - Avoid diluting or ingesting other fluids immediately before or after dosing to maintain product effectiveness (administration precaution from label).
  - Use the provided dosing cup only with this product to ensure accurate dosing.
