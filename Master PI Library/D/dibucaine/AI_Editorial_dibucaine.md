# Editorial Product Information: Dibucaine

## Editorial Note
*   The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial: United States (FDA).
*   The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising available regulatory data into a single clinical reference.
*   This document is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official PI or summary of product characteristics required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredient:** Dibucaine 1% (10 mg/mL).
*   **Chemical Class:** Local anesthetic (Amide-type).
*   **Brand Name(s):** CVS Topical Analgesic Hemorrhoidal Ointment [US].
*   **ATC Code:** C05AD01 (Topical local anesthetics).
*   **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Topical ointment, 1% concentration [US].
*   **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical (External).
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** CVS Pharmacy, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Anorectal Pain and Pruritus:** Temporary relief of pain, itching, and burning associated with hemorrhoids or other anorectal disorders [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Children 12 years and over)
*   **Frequency:** Apply externally to the affected area up to 3 or 4 times daily [US].
*   **Preparation:** If possible, clean the affected area with mild soap and warm water. Rinse thoroughly. Dry by patting or blotting with a soft cloth or toilet tissue before application [US].

### Administration Instructions
*   **External Use Only:** This product is intended solely for external application. It must not be used in or near the eyes [US].
*   **Intrarectal Caution:** Do not insert this product into the rectum using fingers, mechanical devices, or applicators [US].

### Missed Dose
*   Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
*   **Pediatric Age:** Do not use in children under 2 years of age [US].
*   **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to dibucaine, other amide-type local anesthetics, or any inactive ingredients (e.g., lanolin) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
*   **Boxed Warnings:** None [US].
*   **Allergy Alert:** Certain individuals may develop allergic reactions to the ingredients in this product. Treatment should be discontinued if symptoms (e.g., redness, irritation, swelling, pain) develop or increase [US].
*   **Clinical Monitoring:** Patients should be advised to stop use and consult a physician if:
    *   Bleeding occurs.
    *   The condition worsens or does not improve within 7 days.
*   **Accidental Ingestion:** Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, medical assistance or contact with a poison control center is required immediately [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Frequency data is not provided in the source documents. The following potential adverse effects have been identified:
*   **Dermatologic:** Localized redness, irritation, and swelling [US].
*   **Immunologic:** Allergic hypersensitivity reactions [US].

*   **Mechanism of Action:** Acts as a local anesthetic [US]. (Note: As an amide-type anesthetic, dibucaine typically works by inhibiting sodium channels, though this detail was not specified in the source).
*   **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Pharmacodynamics:** Provides temporary relief of pain and itching [US]. Some formulations may offer up to 8-hour relief [US].
