# Editorial Product Information: dexchlorpheniramine + dextromethorphan

## 1. Product Identification
- Active ingredient (INN/generic name): **dexchlorpheniramine + dextromethorphan**
  - Dexchlorpheniramine maleate: 1 mg per 5 mL (as provided in available source)
  - Dextromethorphan hydrobromide: 10 mg per 5 mL (as provided in available source)
- Chemical class: Not specified in available sources
- ATC code: Not specified in available sources
- Route(s) of administration: Oral

- Marketing start date reported in source: 04/23/2026.
- Date of last revision/update reported in source PI: Revised: 4/2026.
- Withdrawal, suspension, or restriction history: Not specified in available sources.
- If additional registration or approval dates are required: Not specified in available sources.

## 3. Approved Indications
- Temporary relief of symptoms due to the common cold, hay fever (allergic rhinitis) or other upper respiratory allergies, including:
  - cough due to minor throat and bronchial irritation
  - runny nose
  - sneezing
  - itching of nose or throat
  - itchy, watery eyes

## 4. Dosage and Administration
- General dosing (oral):
  - Adults and children 12 years of age and over: 2 teaspoonfuls (10 mL) every 4 hours; do not exceed 12 teaspoonfuls (60 mL) in 24 hours, or use as directed by a doctor.
  - Children 6 to under 12 years of age: 1 teaspoonful (5 mL) every 4 hours; do not exceed 6 teaspoonfuls (30 mL) in 24 hours, or use as directed by a doctor.
  - Children under 6 years of age: consult a doctor.
- Dosing by indication: Not specified in available sources beyond the symptomatic relief indications above.
- Administration instructions:
  - Use the recommended teaspoonful dosing schedule above.
  - Avoid alcoholic drinks while using this product.
  - Alcohol, sedatives, and tranquilizers may increase drowsiness when taken concurrently.
  - Use caution when driving or operating machinery because this product may cause drowsiness.
- Titration schedules: Not specified in available sources.
- Missed dose instructions: Not specified in available sources.

## 5. Contraindications
- Do not use if the patient is currently taking a prescription monoamine oxidase inhibitor (MAOI) or within 2 weeks after stopping an MAOI.
  - The PI explicitly states this contraindication and advises consulting a doctor or pharmacist if the patient is unsure whether a concomitant prescription contains an MAOI.
- No additional explicit contraindications listed in the available sources.

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
- Boxed warnings: Not specified in available sources.
- General warnings and precautions:
  - Excitability may occur, especially in children.
  - This product may cause marked drowsiness.
  - Avoid alcoholic beverages while using this product.
  - Alcohol, sedatives, and tranquilizers may potentiate drowsiness.
  - Use caution when driving a motor vehicle or operating machinery.
- Conditions requiring medical advice before use (ask a doctor):
  - A cough that lasts or is chronic (for example, occurs with smoking, asthma, or emphysema).
  - A cough that occurs with excessive phlegm (mucus).
  - Breathing problems such as emphysema or chronic bronchitis.
  - Glaucoma.
  - Trouble urinating due to an enlarged prostate gland.
  - Heart disease.
  - High blood pressure.
  - Thyroid disease.
  - Diabetes.
- Drug interaction precaution:
  - Ask a doctor or pharmacist before use if the patient is taking sedatives or tranquilizers.
- Stopping use and seeking medical advice:
  - Stop use and ask a doctor if cough or nasal congestion persists for more than 1 week, tends to recur, or is accompanied by fever, rash, or persistent headache. A persistent cough may be a sign of a serious condition.
  - Stop use and ask a doctor if nervousness, dizziness, or sleeplessness occur, or if new symptoms occur.
- Overdose: In case of overdose, obtain medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.

- Monitoring requirements: Not specified in available sources.

- Specific clinical precautions: Not specified beyond the warnings and conditions above.

## 8. Drug Interactions
- Pharmacodynamic interactions:
  - Concomitant use with monoamine oxidase inhibitors (MAOIs) is contraindicated; do not use concurrently or within 2 weeks of stopping an MAOI.
  - Alcohol, sedatives, and tranquilizers may increase sedative effects (drowsiness) when taken with this product.
  - Advise caution if the patient is taking sedatives or tranquilizers; consult a doctor or pharmacist before use.
- Pharmacokinetic interactions: Not specified in available sources.
- Clinically significant interactions with management notes:
  - Do not co-administer with MAOIs; if patient is using or has recently (within prior 2 weeks) used an MAOI, avoid use.
  - Warn patients about additive central nervous system depression when alcohol, sedatives, or tranquilizers are used concomitantly; recommend avoidance of alcohol and consider adjusting or avoiding other CNS depressants.
- Region-specific interactions: Not specified in available sources.
