# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial Product Information (PI) was synthesized from the official product documentation provided by the following regulatory jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Note:** Although the initial context referred to "Dapagliflozin with sitagliptin," the provided source document is for **Levocarnitine Injection**. This editorial synthesis focuses exclusively on Levocarnitine.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonized, clinically accurate overview for healthcare professionals. This synthesis is an editorial resource and does not replace the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (chemical name: (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt).
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection, USP [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01 (Levocarnitine).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Sterile solution for injection: 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency in Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** For the acute and chronic management of patients with metabolic errors resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US-approved].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD) on Hemodialysis:** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Pediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    - The recommended dose is **50 mg/kg**, administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion [US].
    - In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose may be given, followed by equivalent doses every 3 to 4 hours (and never less frequently than every 6 hours) over the subsequent 24 hours [US].
    - Daily maintenance doses typically remain at 50 mg/kg, though doses up to **300 mg/kg** have been administered [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - The recommended starting dose is **10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg** (dry body weight) [US].
    - Administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration [US].
- Discard any unused portion as the formulation contains no preservatives [US].

### Titration and Monitoring
- Dosing in ESRD should be guided by pre-dialysis (trough) plasma levocarnitine levels. 
- Adjustments (e.g., reduction to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on clinical response and plasma concentrations (target levels: 35 to 60 µmol/L) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have occurred, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions may manifest within minutes of intravenous administration. If a severe reaction occurs, therapy should be discontinued immediately [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with and without pre-existing seizure disorders. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Renal Impairment (Oral Formulation Caution):** While not applicable to the injection, clinicians should note that chronic high-dose *oral* levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Serious Adverse Effects
- **Anaphylaxis** and other severe hypersensitivity reactions [US].
- **Seizures** [US].

### Common Adverse Effects (≥5% in ESRD clinical trials)
Based on double-blind, placebo-controlled trials in hemodialysis patients, the following were reported regardless of causality:
- **General:** Injection site reaction (33–38%), pain (21–35%), headache (3–37%), chest pain (6–15%), fever (5–12%), abdominal pain (5–21%), and flu syndrome (15–29%) [US].
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (18–21%), hypotension (3–19%), tachycardia (5–9%) [US].
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea (9–35%), vomiting (9–21%), nausea (5–12%) [US].
- **Nervous System:** Dizziness (10–18%), paresthesia (3–12%) [US].
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (15–27%), cough increase (10–18%), dyspnea (3–11%) [US].

### Other Reported Effects
- Transient nausea, vomiting, body odor, and gastritis have been observed [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin. It is recommended to monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after dose adjustments in patients on anticoagulant therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits using doses up to 3.8 times the human dose (based on surface area) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. No adequate, well-controlled studies exist in pregnant women. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine is a normal component of human milk. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk concentrations after exogenous administration. In nursing mothers, the risk of excess carnitine intake by the infant must be weighed against the benefit of supplementation to the mother [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility was found in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders and ESRD includes pediatric patients (see Section 3) [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Specific dose adjustments for the elderly are not detailed in available sources, though general caution regarding organ function is standard in clinical practice.
- **Renal Impairment:** Intravenous levocarnitine is specifically indicated for ESRD patients on dialysis. However, safety for the *oral* form in renal insufficiency has not been evaluated due to metabolite accumulation [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for energy metabolism. It facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into the mitochondria for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters, which are rapidly eliminated [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Following IV administration, approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. The mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of ESRD patients, IV levocarnitine (20 mg/kg post-dialysis) significantly increased plasma levocarnitine levels. Pre-dialysis (trough) concentrations rose from a baseline of ~21 µmol/L to ~190 µmol/L after 8 weeks. While efficacy in increasing carnitine concentrations is proven, the impact on specific clinical outcomes in this population remains undetermined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C [US].
- **Conditions:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable for up to 24 hours at room temperature when mixed in PVC bags with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8.0 mg/mL) [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only (Prescription only) [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources.
