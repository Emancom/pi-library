# Editorial Product Information: Doxorubicin Hydrochloride (Liposomal)

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information and regulatory communications from the following jurisdictions: **United States** (Food and Drug Administration) and the **United Kingdom** (Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency).
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. 
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult the current local PI for the product they are dispensing.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Doxorubicin hydrochloride (pegylated liposomal formulation).
- **Chemical Class:** Anthracycline antineoplastic antibiotic.
- **Brand Names:** 
    - **DOXIL** [United States]
    - **CAELYX** [United Kingdom]
- **ATC Code:** L01DB01
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Concentrate for solution for infusion; 2 mg/mL (available in 10 mL and 25 mL vials [United States]; 10 mL vials [United Kingdom]).
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [United States]; Baxter Healthcare Limited [United Kingdom].

## 2. Approved Indications
Doxorubicin (liposomal) is indicated for:
- **Ovarian Cancer:** 
    - Treatment of patients whose disease has progressed or recurred after platinum-based chemotherapy [United States, United Kingdom].
- **AIDS-Related Kaposi’s Sarcoma (KS):**
    - Treatment of patients after failure of prior systemic chemotherapy or intolerance to such therapy [United States]. 
    - Specifically for patients with low CD4 counts (< 200 CD4 lymphocytes/mm³) and extensive mucocutaneous or visceral disease [United Kingdom].
- **Multiple Myeloma:**
    - In combination with bortezomib for patients who have not previously received bortezomib and have received at least one prior therapy [United States].
    - In combination with bortezomib for patients with progressive disease who have received at least one prior therapy and who have already undergone or are unsuitable for bone marrow transplant [United Kingdom].
- **Metastatic Breast Cancer:** 
    - As monotherapy for patients where there is an increased cardiac risk [United Kingdom-approved; not United States-approved for this indication].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing:** Adult dosing is indication-specific. Clinicians must refer to jurisdiction-specific full prescribing information for exact mg/m² requirements.
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - **Warning:** Do not administer as an undiluted suspension or as an intravenous bolus.
    - **Diluent:** Must be diluted in 5% (50 mg/mL) Glucose (Dextrose) solution for infusion. 
    - **Concentration:** For doses < 90 mg, dilute in 250 mL; for doses ≥ 90 mg, dilute in 500 mL [United Kingdom, United States].
    - **Substitution:** Do not substitute for conventional doxorubicin hydrochloride on a mg-per-mg basis, as the formulations exhibit different pharmacokinetic properties.
    - **Filters:** Do not use in-line filters.
- **Missed Dose Instructions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- Not specified in the available summary sources; refer to the full jurisdiction-specific PI.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning [United States]:** 
    - **Cardiomyopathy:** Liposomal doxorubicin can cause myocardial damage, including acute left ventricular failure. Risk increases with cumulative anthracycline exposure (risk was 11% when cumulative dose was 450 mg/m² to 550 mg/m²). Monitor cardiac function prior to and during treatment.
    - **Infusion-Related Reactions:** Serious, life-threatening, and fatal reactions have occurred in approximately 11% of patients with solid tumors.
- **General Warnings [United Kingdom]:** Similar warnings regarding cardiomyopathy and infusion reactions are listed under "Special Warnings and Precautions for Use."
- **Handing Precautions:** 
    - This is a cytotoxic agent; follow applicable special handling and disposal procedures.
    - If contact with skin or mucosa occurs, wash immediately and thoroughly with soap and water.
- **Monitoring:** Periodic assessment of cardiac function (e.g., LVEF) and hematologic monitoring are required.

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Infusion-Related Reactions:** Common (approx. 11%). Symptoms may include flushing, shortness of breath, facial edema, headache, chills, back pain, tightness in the chest or throat, and hypotension.
- **Serious Adverse Effects:**
    - Cardiomyopathy and congestive heart failure.
    - Severe myelosuppression.
- **Other Effects:** Specific frequency data for common adverse effects (e.g., palmar-plantar erythrodysesthesia, stomatitis) were not provided in the summary comparison source.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic:** The liposomal formulation exhibits unique pharmacokinetic properties and should not be used interchangeably with other formulations of doxorubicin hydrochloride.
- **Specific Interactions:** The source documents indicate no differences in formulation between the US and UK products; however, specific drug-drug interaction lists were not provided in the summary source.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Pegylated liposomes are used to increase the circulation time of doxorubicin, potentially enhancing delivery to tumor tissue.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Pegylated liposomal doxorubicin exhibits a long half-life in the blood.
    - **Metabolism/Elimination:** Not specified in detail in available sources.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Exhibits unique properties compared to conventional doxorubicin hydrochloride.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Infusion Reactions:** Trial data in patients with solid tumors indicated an 11% incidence of acute infusion-related reactions [United States].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Unopened Vials:** Store in a refrigerator at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F). 
- **Freezing:** Do not freeze.
- **After Dilution:** 
    - Chemical and physical in-use stability has been demonstrated for 24 hours at 2°C to 8°C.
    - To maintain microbiological stability, the product should be used immediately. If not used immediately, in-use storage times and conditions are the responsibility of the user and should not exceed 24 hours at 2°C to 8°C [United Kingdom].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as a Human Prescription Drug (Rx-only).
- **United Kingdom:** Classified as a Prescription Only Medicine (POM).
- **Poison/Schedule:** Cytotoxic agent; handle in accordance with local cytotoxic guidelines.
