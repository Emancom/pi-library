# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical data and regulatory requirements. 
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis intended for informational use by healthcare professionals and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP).
- **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule/Amino acid derivative ((R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt).
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01 (Levocarnitine).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Sterile solution for injection: 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency:** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that result in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Administration:** Administer as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion [US].

### Indication-Specific Dosing
- **Metabolic Disorders:**
    - **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg [US].
    - **Loading Dose:** Often administered in patients with severe metabolic crisis, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours [US].
    - **Frequency:** Administer every 3 or 4 hours; never less than every 6 hours [US].
    - **Maximum Dose:** Doses up to 300 mg/kg have been administered [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight) [US].
    - **Timing:** Administer as a slow bolus into the venous return line after each dialysis session [US].
    - **Adjustment:** Dose adjustments (e.g., down to 5 mg/kg) may be made as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations [US].

### Monitoring
- Pre-therapy plasma carnitine concentrations should be obtained.
- **Target Range:** Plasma free carnitine concentrations should be maintained between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].
- Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma concentrations is recommended [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known:** There are no listed contraindications for levocarnitine in available source documents [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Some reactions occur within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately [US].
- **Seizures:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In patients with known seizure disorders, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Oral Formulation Limitation:** The safety and efficacy of **oral** levocarnitine have not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in these patients may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [US].
- **Visual Inspection:** Parenteral products must be inspected for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse events reported in clinical trials for ESRD patients (occurring at ≥5% regardless of causality) include:

| Body System | Adverse Event (Incidence ≥5%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Body as a Whole** | Abdominal pain, accidental injury, asthenia, chest pain, fever, flu syndrome, headache, infection, **injection site reaction (33%)**, pain [US] |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension (20%), hypotension, tachycardia, palpitations [US] |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea (16%), vomiting (15%), nausea, anorexia, constipation, dyspepsia [US] |
| **Nervous System** | Dizziness (13%), paresthesia, insomnia, depression [US] |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis (23%), cough increase, dyspnea, rhinitis [US] |
| **Metabolic** | Hypercalcemia, hypervolemia [US] |

**Serious/Other Effects:**
- **Neurologic:** Seizures [US].
- **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea and vomiting; body odor and gastritis occur less frequently [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have been observed when levocarnitine is used concomitantly with warfarin. **Management:** Monitor INR levels after initiating levocarnitine or following dose adjustments in patients on warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Category:** Not explicitly assigned a letter category in source, but reproductive studies in rats/rabbits (up to 3.8 times human dose) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm [US]. 
    - **Recommendation:** Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed, as animal studies are not always predictive of human response [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Risks to the child of excess carnitine intake must be weighed against the benefits of supplementation to the mother. Consider discontinuing nursing or the drug [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility was found in animal reproductive studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders is weight-based (50 mg/kg); see Section 3 [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Used specifically for ESRD on dialysis. Note the warning regarding toxic metabolite accumulation if using oral formulations in patients with severely compromised renal function [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Following IV bolus (20 mg/kg), approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours. The mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients (20 mg/kg three times weekly), trough concentrations increased from a baseline of 21.3 ± 7.7 µmol/L to 190 ± 55 µmol/L after 8 weeks. 
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** In ESRD patients receiving 10, 20, or 40 mg/kg, mean trough concentrations at 24 weeks were 148, 240, and 456 µmol/L, respectively [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Precautions:** Avoid excessive heat. Protect from freezing [US].
- **Handling:** Single-dose vials; discard any unused portion as the formulation contains no preservative [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable for up to 24 hours at room temperature in PVC bags when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer's (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources.
