# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' PI documents contributed to this editorial: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of Levocarnitine Injection for use by healthcare professionals. 
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis of the provided manufacturer data and is not a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. At the time of writing, only the United States (FDA) documentation was available for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (a carrier molecule for long-chain fatty acid transport).
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium.
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection, USP (Baxter Healthcare Corporation) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01 (Alimentary tract and metabolism).
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
- **General Dosing:** Recommended dose is 50 mg/kg.
- **Administration:** Delivered as a slow bolus injection (2–3 minutes) or by infusion. 
- **Loading Dose:** Often required in severe metabolic crises, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours.
- **Maintenance:** Administer every 3 or 4 hours (never less than every 6 hours) via infusion or IV injection.
- **Maximum Dose:** Doses up to 300 mg/kg have been administered [US].

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight.
- **Administration:** Administer as a slow 2–3 minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
- **Dose Adjustment:** Adjustments should be guided by pre-dialysis (trough) plasma levocarnitine concentrations (normal range: 40–50 µmol/L). Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].

### General Instructions
- **Monitoring:** Obtain baseline plasma carnitine levels. Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L) is recommended.
- **Inspection:** Visually inspect for particulate matter or discoloration before use.
- **Compatibility:** Stable for 24 hours in PVC bags at room temperature (25°C) when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known at the time of publication [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported. These often occur within minutes of administration, primarily in ESRD patients. Discontinue immediately if a reaction occurs [US].
- **Seizures:** Levocarnitine has been associated with seizures in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Metabolite Accumulation:** Chronic high-dose **oral** levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function or ESRD may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites, Trimethylamine (TMA) and Trimethylamine-N-oxide (TMAO), which are normally excreted in urine [US].
- **Monitoring:** INR should be monitored in patients receiving concomitant warfarin therapy (see Section 7) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Clinical Trial Experience (ESRD Patients)
Adverse events reported at a frequency of ≥5% (regardless of causality):
- **General:** Injection site reaction (33%), pain (30%), chest pain (12%), flu syndrome (25%), headache (22%), fever (7%), abdominal pain (9%).
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (20%), hypotension (14%), tachycardia (6%), arrhythmia (2%).
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea (16%), vomiting (15%), nausea (8%), anorexia (5%).
- **Nervous System:** Dizziness (13%), paresthesia (5%), insomnia (4%), depression (5%).
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (23%), cough increase (9%), dyspnea (7%), rhinitis (9%).

### Serious and Post-marketing Adverse Effects
- **Neurologic:** Seizures.
- **Allergic:** Anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, bronchospasm.
- **Gastrointestinal:** Gastritis, body odor (infrequent) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalised Ratio (INR) have been observed when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** It is recommended to monitor INR levels after initiating levocarnitine or following dose adjustments in patients on warfarin [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not formally assigned in provided text, but consistent with former Category B]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. No adequate, well-controlled studies exist in pregnant women. Use only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Specific studies in humans are unavailable. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine concentrations in milk after exogenous administration. Risks to the infant from excess carnitine should be weighed against the benefits of supplementation to the mother [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders and ESRD applies to pediatric patients as described in Section 3 [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Primarily indicated for use in ESRD. Use caution with high-dose oral supplementation in renal failure due to metabolite accumulation (see Section 5) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites are trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily feces).
    - **Elimination:** Mean terminal half-life is 17.4 hours. Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours (excluding endogenous carnitine) [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Increases plasma levocarnitine concentrations; in ESRD, alleviates metabolic abnormalities resulting from toxic organic acid accumulation [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients (20 mg/kg post-dialysis) showed a significant increase in trough carnitine levels from 21.3 µmol/L to 190 µmol/L at 8 weeks.
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** Studies using 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses over 12 and 24 weeks demonstrated dose-dependent increases in trough carnitine concentrations.
- **Clinical Outcome Note:** While efficacy in increasing carnitine concentrations is proven, the direct effect of supplementation on specific clinical outcomes (e.g., muscle weakness, malaise) in ESRD patients has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat.
- **Shelf Life:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial; contains no preservatives [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Rx only (Prescription only). 
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources for other jurisdictions.
