# Editorial Product Information: Dupilumab

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** This editorial synthesis is based on Product Information (PI) provided by the **Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration)**. 
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview intended for use by healthcare professionals.
- **Disclaimer:** This synthesis is an editorial summary and does not replace the official, full Product Information required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority's approved documentation for definitive guidance.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Dupilumab (rch)
- **Chemical Class:** Recombinant human IgG4 monoclonal antibody that inhibits interleukin-4 (IL-4) and interleukin-13 (IL-13) signalling.
- **Brand Name(s):** **Dupixent** [Australian]
- **ATC Code:** D11AH05
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):**
    - **Pre-filled Syringe with needle shield:** 300 mg/2 mL (150 mg/mL) and 200 mg/1.14 mL (175 mg/mL).
    - **Pre-filled Pen:** 300 mg/2 mL (150 mg/mL) and 200 mg/1.14 mL (175 mg/mL).
- **Route of Administration:** Subcutaneous (SC) injection.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** sanofi-aventis australia pty ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
**Dupilumab** is indicated for the treatment of diseases driven by type 2 inflammation:
- **Atopic Dermatitis:** 
    - Treatment of moderate-to-severe disease in adults and adolescents ($\ge$ 12 years) who are candidates for chronic systemic therapy.
    - Treatment of severe disease in children aged 6 months to 11 years who are candidates for chronic systemic therapy.
- **Asthma:** Add-on maintenance treatment for patients $\ge$ 6 years with moderate-to-severe asthma characterised by type 2 inflammation (elevated eosinophils or FeNO) that is inadequately controlled by existing maintenance therapies.
- **Chronic Rhinosinusitis with Nasal Polyposis (CRSwNP):** Add-on maintenance treatment in adults with inadequately controlled disease.
- **Prurigo Nodularis (PN):** Treatment of moderate-to-severe PN in adults who are candidates for systemic therapy.
- **Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD):** Add-on maintenance treatment in adults with uncontrolled COPD characterised by raised blood eosinophils on a stable combination of ICS/LABA/LAMA (or LABA/LAMA if ICS is inappropriate).

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults)
- **Atopic Dermatitis & Prurigo Nodularis:** Initial dose of 600 mg (two 300 mg injections), followed by 300 mg every other week (Q2W).
- **Asthma:** 
    - Standard: Initial 400 mg followed by 200 mg Q2W.
    - For patients with OCS-dependent asthma or comorbid moderate-to-severe atopic dermatitis/CRSwNP: Initial 600 mg followed by 300 mg Q2W.
- **CRSwNP:** 300 mg Q2W. Maintenance may be transitioned to 300 mg every four weeks (Q4W) after 24 weeks if the patient is stable and does not have comorbid asthma.
- **COPD:** 300 mg Q2W.

### Paediatric Dosing (Atopic Dermatitis)
Dosing is weight-based. Examples for children (6 months to 5 years):
- 5 kg to <15 kg: 200 mg Q4W.
- 15 kg to <30 kg: 300 mg Q4W.
*(Note: No loading dose is recommended for this age group)* [Australian].

### Administration Instructions
- For subcutaneous injection into the thigh or abdomen (avoiding 5 cm around the navel). The upper arm may be used if administered by a caregiver.
- **Pre-filled Pen:** Indicated for use in patients $\ge$ 2 years.
- **Pre-filled Syringe:** Indicated for use in patients $\ge$ 6 months.
- Rotation of injection sites is required with each dose.

### Missed Dose Instructions
- **Q2W Schedule:** Administer within 7 days of the missed dose, then resume the original schedule. If not given within 7 days, wait until the next scheduled dose.
- **Q4W Schedule:** Administer within 7 days of the missed dose, then resume original schedule. If not given within 7 days, administer the dose immediately and start a new schedule from that date.

## 4. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to **dupilumab** or any of its excipients (e.g., arginine hydrochloride, histidine, polysorbate 80, sodium acetate trihydrate, sucrose, glacial acetic acid).

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity:** Anaphylaxis, angioedema, and serum sickness-like reactions have been reported. Discontinue immediately if a systemic reaction occurs.
- **Helminth Infection:** Treat pre-existing infections before initiation. If a patient becomes infected during treatment and does not respond to anti-helminthics, discontinue **dupilumab** until the infection resolves.
- **Conjunctivitis and Keratitis:** These occur more frequently in atopic dermatitis patients. Advise patients to report new or worsening eye symptoms. Persistent cases require ophthalmological examination.
- **Eosinophilic Conditions:** Rarely, patients with asthma may present with systemic eosinophilia, eosinophilic pneumonia, or vasculitis consistent with EGPA, often following a reduction in oral corticosteroids.
- **Acute Symptoms:** Do not use to treat acute asthma or COPD exacerbations, acute bronchospasm, or status asthmaticus.
- **Corticosteroid Reduction:** Do not discontinue corticosteroids abruptly. Reductions must be gradual and supervised by a physician.
- **Arthralgia:** Patients should report new-onset joint symptoms. Evaluation by a rheumatologist may be necessary.

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Very Common (>10%):** Injection site reactions (erythema, oedema, pruritus, pain).
- **Common (1–10%):**
    - **Eye Disorders:** Conjunctivitis (allergic, bacterial), blepharitis, eye pruritus, dry eye.
    - **Infections:** Oral herpes, herpes simplex (mucocutaneous).
    - **Blood:** Eosinophilia.
    - **Respiratory:** Oropharyngeal pain (in asthma trials).
- **Uncommon (0.1–1%):** Keratitis [Australian].
- **Post-marketing/Rare:** Angioedema, ulcerative keratitis, facial rash.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Live Vaccines:** Safety and efficacy have not been established; avoid concurrent use.
- **Non-Live Vaccines:** Immune responses to Tdap and meningococcal vaccines were similar to placebo in clinical studies; concurrent administration is permissible.
- **CYP450 Substrates:** No clinically relevant effect on CYP1A2, 3A, 2C19, 2D6, or 2C9 activity has been observed.
- **Interactions with other immunomodulators:** Data are currently unavailable.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** **Category B1** [Australian]. Human IgG is known to cross the placental barrier. Use only if potential benefits outweigh potential risks to the foetus. Animal studies showed no embryofoetal toxicity.
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if **dupilumab** is excreted in human milk, though maternal IgG is excreted. Consider the benefits of breastfeeding against the clinical need for the mother.
- **Fertility:** Animal studies using surrogate antibodies showed no impairment of fertility in male or female mice.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** 
    - **Atopic Dermatitis:** Safety/efficacy not established < 6 months.
    - **Asthma:** Safety/efficacy not established < 6 years.
    - **PN, CRSwNP, COPD:** Safety/efficacy not established < 18 years.
- **Elderly:** No dose adjustment required. In COPD trials, efficacy/safety were similar to the overall population.
- **Renal Impairment:** No adjustment for mild or moderate impairment. No data available for severe renal impairment.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No data available. As a monoclonal antibody, significant hepatic elimination is not expected.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Inhibits IL-4/IL-13 signalling by binding to the IL-4R$\alpha$ subunit. This blocks the Type I receptor (IL-4R$\alpha$/$\gamma$c) and Type II receptor (IL-4R$\alpha$/IL-13R$\alpha$), reducing the release of pro-inflammatory cytokines, chemokines, and IgE.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** $t_{max}$ of 3–7 days; absolute bioavailability 61–64%.
    - **Distribution:** Volume of distribution is approximately 4.6 L, indicating primary distribution in the vascular system.
    - **Elimination:** Parallel linear (proteolytic) and non-linear (target-mediated) pathways. Elimination time to below detection limits is 9–13 weeks in adults.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Atopic Dermatitis:** Pivotal trials (SOLO 1, SOLO 2, CHRONOS) demonstrated significant improvement in IGA 0/1 and EASI-75 scores compared to placebo.
- **Asthma:** Trials (DRI12544, QUEST, VENTURE) showed significant reductions in severe exacerbation rates and improved $FEV_1$.
- **COPD:** BOREAS and NOTUS trials showed a 30–34% reduction in the annualised rate of moderate-to-severe exacerbations in patients with type 2 inflammation.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 2°C to 8°C (Refrigerate).
- **Conditions:** Keep in the original carton to protect from light. Do not freeze or shake.
- **Room Temperature Stability:** May be kept at $\le$ 25°C for a single period of up to 14 days. Must be used within 14 days or discarded.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule:** **Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine)** [Australian].
