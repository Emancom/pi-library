# Editorial Product Information: dextromethorphan + guaifenesin + phenylephrine

## 1. Product Identification
- Active ingredient (INN/generic name): **dextromethorphan + guaifenesin + phenylephrine**
- Chemical class:
  - Dextromethorphan — antitussive (cough suppressant)
  - Guaifenesin — expectorant (mucus loosening/secretory thinner)
  - Phenylephrine — nasal decongestant (alpha-adrenergic sympathomimetic)
- ATC code: Not specified in available sources
- Route(s) of administration: Oral
- Strength per stated dosing unit (as provided in sources): dextromethorphan HBr 20 mg / guaifenesin 400 mg / phenylephrine HCl 10 mg per 20 mL

- Date of first registration/approval by jurisdiction:
  - [US] Marketing start date: 07/14/2026

- Withdrawal/suspension/restriction history: Not specified in available sources

## 3. Approved Indications
- Temporarily relieves nasal congestion due to a cold.
- Helps loosen phlegm (mucus) and thin bronchial secretions to drain bronchial tubes and make coughs more productive.
- Temporarily relieves:
  - Cough due to minor throat and bronchial irritation as may occur with the common cold or inhaled irritants.
  - The intensity of coughing.
  - The impulse to cough to help you get to sleep.

(Indications as stated in the available source are presented without regional differentiation.)

## 4. Dosage and Administration
### General dosing (adult and adolescent)
- Adults and children 12 years of age and older: 20 mL every 4 hours.
- Do not take more than 6 doses in any 24-hour period (maximum total: 120 mL per 24 hours).
- **Children under 12 years of age: Do not use.**

## 5. Contraindications
- **Do not use in children under 12 years of age.**
- **Do not use if the patient is currently taking a prescription monoamine oxidase inhibitor (MAOI)** (certain drugs for depression, psychiatric or emotional conditions, or Parkinson's disease), or within 2 weeks after stopping an MAOI.
- If the patient is unsure whether a prescription contains an MAOI, they should consult a physician or pharmacist before taking this product.

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed warnings
- Not specified in available sources.

### General warnings and precautions
- Ask a doctor before use if the patient has:
  - Heart disease
  - High blood pressure
  - Thyroid disease
  - Diabetes
  - Trouble urinating due to an enlarged prostate gland
  - Persistent or chronic cough such as occurs with smoking, asthma, chronic bronchitis, or emphysema
  - Cough that occurs with too much phlegm (mucus)
- When using this product:
  - Do not use more than directed.
- Stop use and ask a doctor if:
  - Nervousness, dizziness, or sleeplessness occur.
  - Symptoms do not get better within 7 days or occur with fever.
  - Cough comes back, or occurs with rash or persistent headache — these could be signs of a serious condition.

### Monitoring requirements
- Specific laboratory or physiologic monitoring requirements are not specified in available sources.
- Monitor for nervous system/psychiatric adverse effects (nervousness, dizziness, sleeplessness) and for persistence/worsening of symptoms as described above.

### Specific clinical precautions
- Sodium content: each 20 mL contains 20 mg sodium — consider this for patients on sodium-restricted diets.
- In case of overdose, seek medical help or contact a Poison Control Center immediately.
