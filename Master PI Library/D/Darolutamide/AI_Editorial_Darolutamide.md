# Editorial Product Information: Darolutamide

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdiction: **Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration)**. 
- At the time of this writing, PI documents from other jurisdictions (e.g., US, European, UK, Canadian, Japanese) were not provided for comparison; therefore, clinical recommendations reflect the Australian regulatory position. 
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Always consult the current local PI before prescribing.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Darolutamide.
- **Chemical Class:** Non-steroidal androgen receptor antagonist.
- **Brand Name:** **NUBEQA** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** L02BB06.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Film-coated tablets, 300 mg. White to off-white, oval, marked with "300" and "BAYER".
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Bayer Australia Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
**NUBEQA** is indicated for the treatment of:
- Non-metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (nmCRPC).
- Metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer (mHSPC).
- Metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer (mHSPC) in combination with docetaxel.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults)
- **Standard Dose:** 600 mg (two 300 mg tablets) taken twice daily.
- **Total Daily Dose:** 1200 mg.
- **Duration:** Continue until disease progression or the occurrence of unacceptable toxicity.

### Administration Instructions
- Tablets must be swallowed whole.
- **Requirement:** Must be taken with food to ensure adequate absorption.
- **Concurrent Therapy:** Patients must also receive a gonadotropin-releasing hormone (GnRH) analog (LHRH agonist or antagonist) unless they have undergone bilateral orchiectomy.

### mHSPC Combination (with Docetaxel)
- The first cycle of docetaxel (75 mg/m²) should be initiated within 6 weeks of starting **NUBEQA**. 
- Refer to docetaxel PI for specific infusion instructions and premedication.

### Missed Dose
- The dose should be taken as soon as remembered if it is before the next scheduled dose.
- Do not double the dose to compensate for a missed one.

### Dose Modification
- **For ≥ Grade 3 toxicity or intolerable reactions:** Withhold dosing or reduce to 300 mg twice daily until symptoms improve.
- **Resumption:** Treatment may then be resumed at 600 mg twice daily.
- **Limit:** Dose reduction below 300 mg twice daily is not recommended.

## 4. Contraindications
- Hypersensitivity to darolutamide or any included excipients.
- Women who are or may become pregnant [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Cardiovascular
- **Ischemic Heart Disease:** Has occurred in patients, including fatal cases (0.4% in pooled trials). Monitor for signs/symptoms of ischemic events. 
- **Risk Management:** Optimise control of cardiovascular risk factors (hypertension, diabetes, dyslipidaemia). 
- **Note:** Safety has not been established in patients with recent (within 6 months) major cardiovascular events (e.g., stroke, MI, NYHA Class III/IV heart failure).

### Seizure
- Occurred in 0.2% to 0.6% of patients in clinical trials. 
- While patients with a history of seizure were included in studies, all observed events occurred in those without a prior history.

### Hepatotoxicity
- Idiosyncratic drug-induced liver injury (DILI) with Grade ≥ 3 increases in ALT/AST (and occasionally bilirubin ≥2x ULN) has been reported.
- **Monitoring:** Monitor LFTs (ALT, AST) as per routine clinical practice.
- **Action:** Permanently discontinue if LFT abnormalities suggest DILI.

### Specific Clinical Precautions
- **Excipients:** Contains lactose.

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Frequency Summary (Monotherapy in nmCRPC/mHSPC)
- **Very Common (>10%):** Fatigue (13.7%).
- **Common (1–10%):** Pain in extremity (6.6%), rash (3.4%), hypertension, fractures.
- **Laboratory Abnormalities:** Decreased neutrophil count (19.6%), increased AST (22.5%), increased bilirubin (16.4%).

### Frequency Summary (Combination with Docetaxel)
The addition of docetaxel alters the safety profile significantly:
- **Very Common (>10%):** Constipation (23%), decreased appetite (19%), rash (19%), hypertension (14%), alopecia (40.5%), fatigue (33.1%), anaemia (27.8%), arthralgia (27.3%), peripheral oedema (26.5%).
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Febrile neutropenia (6.1% in combination therapy).

## 7. Drug Interactions
### Effects of Other Drugs on Darolutamide
- **Strong CYP3A4 and P-gp Inducers (e.g., Rifampicin, St. John’s Wort):** Concomitant use should be avoided as they significantly decrease darolutamide exposure (AUC decreased by 72%).
- **Strong CYP3A4, P-gp, and BCRP Inhibitors (e.g., Itraconazole):** May increase darolutamide exposure (AUC increased by 1.7-fold). Monitor closely for adverse events.

### Effects of Darolutamide on Other Drugs
- **BCRP, OATP1B1, and OATP1B3 Substrates (e.g., Rosuvastatin):** Darolutamide is an inhibitor. Co-administration with rosuvastatin resulted in a 5-fold increase in its exposure. Monitor for increased side effects of these substrates (e.g., methotrexate, fluvastatin).
- **CYP3A4 Substrates:** Darolutamide is a weak inducer. No clinically relevant interaction is expected with midazolam, warfarin, or omeprazole.
- **P-gp Substrates:** No clinically relevant interaction expected (e.g., dabigatran).

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [Australian Category D]. Darolutamide is contraindicated in women who are pregnant or may become pregnant. Androgen receptor inhibition may cause fetal harm.
- **Breastfeeding:** Not indicated for use in women.
- **Contraception:**
    - **Male patients:** If having sex with a pregnant woman, a condom must be used. If having sex with a woman of childbearing potential, use a condom and a highly effective contraceptive method.
    - **Duration:** Continue contraception for 3 months after the last dose.
- **Fertility:** Animal studies showed atrophy and hypospermia in the male reproductive system.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy not established in patients <18 years of age.
- **Elderly Use:** No dose adjustment necessary. No clinically relevant differences in safety/efficacy observed across age groups (up to ≥85 years).
- **Renal Impairment:**
    - **Mild to Moderate:** No dose adjustment required.
    - **Severe (eGFR 15–29 mL/min/1.73m²):** Recommended dose is 300 mg twice daily.
    - **End-Stage Renal Disease (eGFR <15):** Not studied; no recommendation available.
- **Hepatic Impairment:**
    - **Mild:** No dose adjustment required.
    - **Moderate (Child-Pugh B):** Recommended dose is 300 mg twice daily.
    - **Severe (Child-Pugh C):** Not studied; pharmacokinetics are unknown.

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
Darolutamide is a non-steroidal androgen receptor (AR) antagonist. It competitively inhibits androgen binding, AR nuclear translocation, and AR-mediated transcription. Its structure allows for high-affinity binding to the receptor ligand-binding domain.

### Pharmacokinetics
- **Absorption:** Absolute bioavailability is ~30% when fasted. Food increases exposure by 2.0 to 2.5-fold. Steady state is reached in 2–5 days.
- **Distribution:** Apparent volume of distribution is 119 L. Plasma protein binding is 92%. Low likelihood of crossing the blood-brain barrier in humans to a clinically relevant extent.
- **Metabolism:** Primarily oxidative metabolism via CYP3A4 and direct glucuronidation via UGT1A9/UGT1A1.
- **Elimination:** Effective half-life is approximately 20 hours. Excretion is 63.4% via urine and 32.4% via faeces.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ARAMIS (nmCRPC):** Demonstrated significant improvement in metastasis-free survival (MFS) (40.4 vs 18.4 months; HR 0.413) and overall survival (HR 0.685).
- **ARANOTE (mHSPC):** Monotherapy significantly improved radiological progression-free survival (rPFS) (HR 0.541).
- **ARASENS (mHSPC + Docetaxel):** Combination therapy resulted in a 32.5% reduction in the risk of death compared to placebo + docetaxel (HR 0.675).

- **Temperature:** Store below 30°C.
- **Shelf Life:** 3 months after the first opening of the bottle [Australian].
- **Handling:** Store in the original container.
