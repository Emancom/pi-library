# Editorial Product Information: Dimeticone

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial has been synthesized from official product labeling provided by the following jurisdiction: **US (Food and Drug Administration)**. 
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of Dimeticone (5% w/w) for healthcare professionals.
- **Note:** This synthesis is an editorial reference and does not replace the official Product Information or "Drug Facts" required for prescribing or dispensing in any specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Dimeticone (also known as Dimethicone 350).
- **Chemical Class:** Silicone polymer/emollient.
- **Brand Name(s):** Avance Aloe Incontinence Care Barrier Cream [US].
- **ATC Code:** D02AA01 (Silicone preparations).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Topical cream containing Dimeticone 5% w/w [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Smart Choice Medical Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Skin Protection:** Used to help prevent, temporarily protect, and relieve chafed, chapped, or cracked skin and lips [US].
- **Environmental Protection:** Used to protect the skin from the drying effects of wind and cold weather [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** Apply the cream to the affected area as needed and rub in gently [US].
- **Administration Instructions:** For external use only.
- **Missed Dose:** Not applicable for PRN topical skin protectants.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Animal Bites:** Do not use on wild or domestic animal bites; immediate medical consultation is required for these injuries [US].
- **Infections:** Do not apply to areas with active infection [US].
- **Duration Limit:** Should not be used for more than 10 days; if symptoms persist or infection worsens, medical review is necessary [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None specified in available sources.
- **General Warnings:** 
    - For external use only [US].
    - Avoid contact with eyes [US].
    - Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, professional medical assistance or contact with a Poison Control Center is required immediately [US].
- **Specific Clinical Precautions:**
    - Therapy should be discontinued and a physician consulted if the skin condition worsens [US].
    - Medical advice should be sought if symptoms last more than 7 days, or if they clear up and recur within a few days [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Frequency Data:** Not specified in available sources.
- **General Profile:** Topical dimeticone is generally well-tolerated as a barrier agent. Potential for local hypersensitivity or irritation may exist given the complex inactive ingredient profile (e.g., fragrances, botanicals, and preservatives).

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources. Dimeticone is a pharmacologically inert polymer and systemic interactions are unlikely.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Breastfeeding:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Fertility:** Not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Use on adults is specified in the provided US labeling. Specific safety or dosing for infants/children is not detailed in the available source material [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Due to the lack of systemic absorption, dose adjustments are typically not required.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Dimeticone is a silicone oil that acts as an occlusive skin protectant. It forms a physical barrier on the stratum corneum, preventing transepidermal water loss (TEWL) and protecting the skin from external irritants (such as wind, cold, or moisture in incontinence care).
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Not systemically absorbed when applied topically.
    - **Distribution/Metabolism/Elimination:** Not applicable for this non-absorbed topical agent.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Acts locally through physical/mechanical properties.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- Not specified in available sources. (Dimeticone 5% is generally regulated under the OTC Monograph system for skin protectants).

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage Temperature:** Store at room temperature [US].
- **Handling:** Keep container tightly closed.
