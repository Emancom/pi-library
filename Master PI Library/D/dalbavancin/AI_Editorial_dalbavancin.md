# Editorial Product Information: Dalbavancin

## Editorial Note
- Contributing Jurisdictions: US (FDA)
- Purpose: This document provides a harmonised clinical overview of **dalbavancin** for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises the available information into a single reference point. 
- Disclaimer: This is an editorial synthesis intended for informational purposes. It does not replace the official Product Information required for prescribing within specific jurisdictions. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory guidelines and official labeling for the most current and region-specific information.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Dalbavancin (as dalbavancin hydrochloride)
- **Chemical Class:** Lipoglycopeptide antibacterial
- **Brand Name(s):** Dalbavancin [US]
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Lyophilized powder for injection: 500 mg per single-dose vial [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** BE Pharmaceuticals Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Acute Bacterial Skin and Skin Structure Infections (ABSSSI):** Treatment of adult and pediatric patients (from birth) with infections caused by susceptible strains of the following Gram-positive microorganisms [US]:
    - *Staphylococcus aureus* (including methicillin-susceptible and methicillin-resistant isolates).
    - *Streptococcus pyogenes*.
    - *Streptococcus agalactiae*.
    - *Streptococcus dysgalactiae*.
    - *Streptococcus anginosus* group (*S. anginosus, S. intermedius, S. constellatus*).
    - *Enterococcus faecalis* (vancomycin-susceptible isolates only).

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Adult Dosing (CLcr ≥30 mL/min or on regular hemodialysis)
- **Single Dose Regimen:** 1,500 mg administered as a single IV infusion [US].

### Pediatric Dosing (CLcr ≥30 mL/min/1.73m²)
- **Birth to <6 years:** 22.5 mg/kg (maximum 1,500 mg) as a single IV infusion [US].
- **6 to <18 years:** 18 mg/kg (maximum 1,500 mg) as a single IV infusion [US].

### Renal Impairment Adjustments
- **Adults (CLcr <30 mL/min, not on regular hemodialysis):** 1,125 mg administered as a single IV infusion [US].
- **Adults on regular hemodialysis:** No dosage adjustment required [US].
- **Pediatrics:** Insufficient information is available to recommend a dose for pediatric patients with CLcr <30 mL/min/1.73m² [US].

### Preparation and Administration Instructions
- **Reconstitution:** Reconstitute each 500 mg vial with 25 mL of Sterile Water for Injection or 5% Dextrose Injection (D5W). Do not shake; swirl gently to avoid foaming until completely dissolved (20 mg/mL concentration) [US].
- **Dilution:** Reconstituted solution must be further diluted **only with 5% Dextrose Injection (D5W)** to a final concentration of 1 mg/mL to 5 mg/mL [US].
- **Compatibility:** Do not co-infuse with other medications or electrolytes. **Saline-based infusion solutions must not be used** as they may cause precipitation [US]. 
- **Infusion Rate:** Administer via IV infusion over a total of 30 minutes [US].
- **Stability:** The total time from reconstitution to administration should not exceed 48 hours. Store reconstituted/diluted solutions refrigerated (2°C to 8°C) or at controlled room temperature (20°C to 25°C). Do not freeze [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to **dalbavancin** [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious anaphylactic and skin reactions have been reported. Use with caution in patients with known hypersensitivity to other glycopeptides (e.g., vancomycin) due to the risk of cross-sensitivity [US].
- **Infusion-Related Reactions:** Rapid IV infusion can cause "Red Man Syndrome"-like reactions, including flushing of the upper body, urticaria, pruritus, and rash. Stopping or slowing the infusion may resolve these symptoms [US].
- **Hepatic Effects:** Alanine Aminotransferase (ALT) elevations (>3x ULN) have been observed. Monitor liver function in patients with pre-existing hepatic impairment [US].
- **Clostridioides difficile-Associated Diarrhea (CDAD):** Reported with nearly all systemic antibacterials; severity can range from mild diarrhea to fatal colitis. Evaluate if diarrhea occurs during or after treatment [US].
- **Development of Drug-Resistant Bacteria:** Use only for proven or strongly suspected bacterial infections to maintain drug effectiveness [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Adult Patients
| Adverse Reaction | Frequency (%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Nausea** | 5.5% |
| **Headache** | 4.7% |
| **Diarrhea** | 4.4% |
| **Vomiting** | 2.8% |
| **Rash** | 2.7% |
| **Pruritus** | 2.1% |

### Pediatric Patients
- **Pyrexia** was the most common adverse reaction, occurring in >1% of pediatric patients [US].

### Serious and Other Adverse Effects
- **Serious Adverse Reactions:** Occurred in approximately 4.9% of adult patients. 
- **Infusion-Related:** Back pain [US post-marketing].
- **Hepatobiliary:** Hepatotoxicity, increased transaminases, increased alkaline phosphatase, and increased GGT [US].
- **Blood/Lymphatic:** Anemia, leukopenia, thrombocytopenia, and petechiae (<2%) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **CYP450 System:** **Dalbavancin** is not a substrate, inhibitor, or inducer of the CYP450 isoenzyme system. No clinically significant interactions were observed with acetaminophen, aztreonam, fentanyl, metronidazole, furosemide, proton pump inhibitors, or midazolam [US].
- **Drug-Laboratory Interactions:** **Dalbavancin** does not artificially prolong prothrombin time (PT) or activated partial thromboplastin time (aPTT) at therapeutic concentrations [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** No adequate and well-controlled studies in pregnant women. Animal data (rats) at 3.5 times the human dose showed delayed fetal maturation and increased fetal loss. Use during pregnancy only if the potential benefit justifies the potential risk to the fetus [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Dalbavancin is excreted in rat milk; it is unknown if it is excreted in human milk. Consider the developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding alongside the mother's clinical need and any potential adverse effects on the child [US].
- **Fertility:** In rats, impaired fertility and increased embryo resorptions were noted at doses 3.5 times the human dose (associated with parental toxicity) [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness established from birth to <18 years. Dosage is based on age and weight (see Section 3) [US].
- **Elderly Use:** No dosage adjustment is necessary based solely on age. However, since the drug is substantially excreted by the kidney, monitor for decreased renal function [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Dosage adjustment is required for adults with CLcr <30 mL/min who are not on hemodialysis. No adjustment for hemodialysis [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No adjustment for mild impairment (Child-Pugh A). Caution is advised for moderate to severe impairment (Child-Pugh B or C) as no data are available [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Bactericidal lipoglycopeptide. It interferes with cell wall synthesis by binding to the D-alanyl-D-alanine terminus of nascent peptidoglycan stem pentapeptides, preventing cross-linking [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Approximately 93% bound to human plasma proteins (primarily albumin). Distribution into skin blister fluid reaches mean concentrations >30 mg/L for up to 7 days [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Minimal metabolism; hydroxy-dalbavancin (minor metabolite) observed in urine but not in plasma [US].
    - **Excretion:** Approximately 33% excreted unchanged in urine; 20% excreted in feces over 70 days [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Activity correlates with the ratio of Area Under the Concentration-time curve to Minimal Inhibitory Concentration (AUC/MIC) for *S. aureus* [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Adult ABSSSI Trial (Trial 3):** A Phase 3, double-blind trial compared a single 1,500 mg dose to a multi-dose regimen. The single-dose regimen achieved an early clinical response (≥20% reduction in lesion area at 48-72 hours) in 81.4% of patients, compared to 84.2% in the comparator arm (Difference: -2.9%; 95% CI: -8.5, 2.8) [US].
- **Pediatric ABSSSI Trial (Trial 4):** A multicenter, open-label, randomized trial in patients from birth to <18 years. Clinical cure rates at 28 days were 94.7% for the single-dose **dalbavancin** arm compared to 100% in the active comparator arm (vancomycin or oxacillin/flucloxacillin) [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vial Storage:** Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
- **Reconstituted/Diluted Stability:** Total time from reconstitution to start of infusion must not exceed 48 hours [US].

- **United States:** Prescription-only (Rx only) [US].
