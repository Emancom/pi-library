# Editorial Product Information: Daptomycin

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical data and regulatory requirements into a single reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official PI. Clinicians must refer to the local approved product information when prescribing or administering daptomycin in their specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Daptomycin, a cyclic lipopeptide antibacterial agent.
- **Brand Names:** Cipla Daptomycin [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** J01XX09.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 350 mg and 500 mg lyophilised powder for injection in single-dose vials. Reconstitution to 50 mg/mL is required [Australian].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) injection or infusion.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Cipla Australia Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
**Daptomycin is active against Gram-positive bacteria only.** 

### Adult Patients (≥18 years)
- **Complicated Skin and Skin Structure Infections (cSSSI):** For patients requiring parenteral therapy who have failed other therapies, have an intolerance to alternatives (e.g., penicillin allergy), or where the infection is caused by susceptible organisms [Australian].
- **Staphylococcus aureus Bloodstream Infections (Bacteraemia):** Including right-sided native valve infective endocarditis (RIE) caused by methicillin-susceptible and methicillin-resistant isolates [Australian].

### Paediatric Patients (1 to 17 years)
- **Complicated Skin and Skin Structure Infections (cSSSI):** For patients requiring parenteral therapy with intolerance/failure of alternative agents [Australian].
- **Staphylococcus aureus Bloodstream Infections (Bacteraemia):** For infections not due to pneumonia, caused by susceptible isolates [Australian].

**Limitations of Use:**
- Not indicated for the treatment of pneumonia (inactivated by pulmonary surfactant).
- Efficacy in left-sided endocarditis or prosthetic heart valves has not been demonstrated [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Adult Dosing (Normal Renal Function)
- **cSSSI:** 4 mg/kg once every 24 hours for 7–14 days.
- **S. aureus Bacteraemia/RIE:** 6 mg/kg once every 24 hours for 2–6 weeks.

### Paediatric Dosing (1 to 17 years, Normal Renal Function)
Dosing is age-dependent and should be administered once every 24 hours [Australian]:
- **cSSSI:**
    - 12–17 years: 5 mg/kg (30-min infusion).
    - 7–11 years: 7 mg/kg (30-min infusion).
    - 2–6 years: 9 mg/kg (60-min infusion).
    - 1 to <2 years: 10 mg/kg (60-min infusion).
- **S. aureus Bacteraemia:**
    - 12–17 years: 7 mg/kg (30-min infusion).
    - 7–11 years: 9 mg/kg (30-min infusion).
    - 1–6 years: 12 mg/kg (60-min infusion).

### Administration
- **Adults:** May be given as a 2-minute IV injection or a 30-minute IV infusion.
- **Paediatrics:** Must **not** be given as a 2-minute injection; must be administered by infusion only (30 or 60 minutes depending on age/dose) [Australian].

## 4. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to daptomycin or the excipient (sodium hydroxide) [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Skeletal Muscle Effects:** Daptomycin may cause myopathy and rhabdomyolysis. Monitor creatine phosphokinase (CPK) levels at baseline and at least weekly. Consider more frequent monitoring in patients with renal impairment or those taking HMG-CoA reductase inhibitors [Australian].
- **Eosinophilic Pneumonia:** Typically develops 2–4 weeks after initiation. Discontinue immediately if suspected and treat with systemic steroids [Australian].
- **Peripheral Neuropathy:** Monitor for signs of neuropathy; paediatric patients <1 year are at specific risk due to muscle/nerve effects seen in neonatal animal models [Australian].
- **Severe Cutaneous Adverse Reactions:** DRESS (Drug Reaction with Eosinophilia and Systemic Symptoms) and Stevens-Johnson Syndrome/TEN have been reported [Australian].
- **Laboratory Interference:** Can cause false prolongation of Prothrombin Time (PT) and International Normalised Ratio (INR) with certain recombinant thromboplastin reagents [Australian].

## 6. Adverse Effects
| Frequency | Adverse Effect |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Fungal/Candida infections, UTI, anaemia, anxiety, insomnia, dizziness, headache, hypertension/hypotension, GI pain, nausea, vomiting, diarrhoea, rash, pruritus, limb pain, pyrexia, increased CPK, abnormal LFTs. |
| **Uncommon (0.1–1%)** | Fungemia, eosinophilia, thrombocytosis, hyperglycaemia, electrolyte imbalance, paraesthesia, taste disorder, vertigo, arrhythmia, flushing, dyspepsia, urticaria, arthralgia, muscle pain/weakness, renal impairment. |
| **Rare (<0.1%)** | Jaundice, prolonged PT. |
| **Post-marketing** | Thrombocytopenia, angioedema, rhabdomyolysis, Tubulointerstitial nephritis (TIN), C. difficile-associated diarrhoea, SJS/TEN. |

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **HMG-CoA Reductase Inhibitors (Statins):** Increased risk of myopathy. Consider temporary suspension of statins during daptomycin therapy [Australian].
- **Warfarin:** Limited data available; monitor anticoagulant activity for the first several days of co-administration [Australian].
- **Tobramycin:** Caution is warranted; though PK changes were not statistically significant at low doses, interaction at clinical doses is unknown [Australian].
- **CYP450 Metabolism:** Daptomycin does not significantly inhibit or induce CYP450 enzymes [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Australian Category B1. No evidence of foetal harm in animal studies. Use only if potential benefit outweighs risk [Australian].
- **Breastfeeding:** Excreted in breast milk at low concentrations (0.045 µg/mL). Women should be advised to avoid breastfeeding during treatment [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatrics:** Not indicated for patients <1 year of age due to risk of neurological/muscular toxicity [Australian].
- **Elderly:** No dose adjustment required for patients with CrCl ≥30 mL/min; however, lower clinical success and higher adverse event rates were noted in those ≥65 years [Australian].
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    - CrCl ≥30 mL/min: No adjustment required.
    - CrCl <30 mL/min (including Haemodialysis/CAPD): Extend dosing interval to **once every 48 hours**.
    - Dose on dialysis days should be administered post-dialysis [Australian].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No adjustment for Child-Pugh Class B. Not evaluated in Child-Pugh Class C (severe) [Australian].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Binds to bacterial membranes, causing rapid depolarisation. This leads to inhibition of protein, DNA, and RNA synthesis, resulting in bacterial death without significant cell lysis.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** 90–93% protein-bound (primarily albumin). Vss is approx 0.1 L/kg.
    - **Metabolism:** No metabolism by human liver microsomes in vitro; no metabolites found in plasma on Day 1.
    - **Elimination:** Primarily renal (78% in urine; 5.7% in faeces). Terminal half-life is approx 8 hours [Australian].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **cSSSI (Adults):** Pooled clinical success rates were 74.5% (ITT) vs. 74.7% for comparators (Vancomycin or semi-synthetic penicillins) [Australian].
- **S. aureus Bacteraemia/RIE (Adults):** Success rate of 44.2% (Daptomycin 6 mg/kg) vs. 41.7% (Comparator + Gentamicin) at test-of-cure [Australian].
- **Paediatric cSSSI:** Success rates in ITT population were 88.3% for daptomycin vs. 86.4% for standard of care [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vials:** Store at 2°C to 8°C (Refrigerated). Do not freeze.
- **Reconstituted/Diluted Solution:** To reduce microbiological hazard, use as soon as possible. If necessary, storage is permitted at 2°C to 8°C for a combined maximum (vial and bag) of 24 hours [Australian].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Australia:** Prescription Only Medicine (Schedule 4).
- **Sodium Content:** Essentially "sodium-free" (<1 mmol per dose) [Australian].
