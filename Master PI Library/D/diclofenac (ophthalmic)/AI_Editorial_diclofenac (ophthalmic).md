# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine Injection

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview of **levocarnitine** injection for healthcare professionals. 
- **Important:** This document is an editorial synthesis and serves as a clinical reference. It is not a substitute for the official, current Product Information approved by the regulatory authority in a specific jurisdiction where the drug is being prescribed or administered.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP). It is a carrier molecule essential for the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane.
- **Chemical Class:** (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter Healthcare Corporation) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01 (Not explicitly stated in the source but corresponds to INN).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Clear, colorless sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) single-dose vial [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** For the acute and chronic management of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that lead to secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance hemodialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Administration
- Administer exclusively via the intravenous route. 
- Parenteral products must be inspected for particulate matter and discoloration before use [US].

### Metabolic Disorders
- **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg given as a slow bolus injection (over 2 to 3 minutes) or via infusion [US].
- **Loading Dose:** In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose is often administered, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours [US].
- **Dosing Frequency:** Administer every 3 or 4 hours; intervals should never be less than every 6 hours [US].
- **Monitoring:** It is recommended to obtain plasma carnitine concentrations before therapy. Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine levels (target free carnitine: 35 to 60 µmol/L) is advised [US].

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight) [US].
- **Administration Timing:** Administer as a 2 to 3-minute slow bolus into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].
- **Adjustments:** Therapy initiation may be prompted by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levels <40 to 50 µmol/L. Downward dose adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be implemented as early as the third or fourth week of treatment based on trough concentrations [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known** [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have occurred, primarily in ESRD patients on dialysis. Some reactions manifest within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and initiate medical management [US].
- **Seizures:** Reports indicate seizures may occur in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in the frequency or severity of events has been observed [US].
- **Monitoring (Warfarin):** Reports suggest an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when **levocarnitine** is co-administered with warfarin. Monitor INR levels at initiation and following dose adjustments [US].
- **Oral Safety in Renal Impairment:** The safety and efficacy of oral **levocarnitine** have not been established in patients with renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in ESRD patients may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions observed in clinical trials for ESRD patients (frequency ≥5%, regardless of causality) include:

| Body System | Adverse Events (≥5%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Chest pain, fever, flu syndrome, headache, infection, injection site reaction, abdominal pain, asthenia, back pain, pain. |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension, hypotension, tachycardia, palpitation, arrhythmia. |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea, vomiting, nausea, anorexia, dyspepsia. |
| **Metabolic** | Hypercalcemia, hypervolemia, hyperkalemia, peripheral edema, weight changes. |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness, insomnia, depression, paresthesia. |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis, cough increase, dyspnea, rhinitis. |
| **Skin** | Pruritus, rash. |

- **Other Reactions:** Transient nausea and vomiting; body odor and gastritis occur less frequently [US].
- **Postmarketing Experience:** Serious hypersensitivity and seizures (see Section 5) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Concomitant use may increase INR levels. Clinical management requires regular INR monitoring [US].
- **Other Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not explicitly lettered, but indicates "should be used during pregnancy only if clearly needed."] Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (based on surface area) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. However, adequate and well-controlled human studies are absent [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine concentration in milk following administration. Risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant must be weighed against the benefits of maternal supplementation [US].
- **Fertility:** Mutagenicity tests (Salmonella, etc.) were negative. Long-term animal studies for carcinogenic potential have not been conducted [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders and ESRD is weight-based (see Section 3) [US].
- **Geriatric Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Specifically indicated for use in ESRD patients on hemodialysis. Safety of oral formulations is not established in this population (see Section 5) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** **Levocarnitine** facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids in patients with metabolic defects by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Following IV bolus (20 mg/kg), approximately 76% is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. The mean apparent terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients receiving 20 mg/kg post-dialysis showed that trough levels increased from a baseline of ~21 µmol/L to ~190 µmol/L at 8 weeks. Dose-ranging studies (10, 20, 40 mg/kg) demonstrated dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Precautions:** Avoid excessive heat; protect from freezing.
- **Vial Handling:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as it contains no preservative [US].
- **Stability:** Compatible and stable for up to 24 hours in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) stored at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Rx-only [US].
