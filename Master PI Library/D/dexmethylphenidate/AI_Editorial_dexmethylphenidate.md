# Editorial Product Information: Serdexmethylphenidate and Dexmethylphenidate

## Editorial Note
- The information contained in this editorial synthesis is derived from the official Product Information (PI) documents provided by the following regulatory authorities: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of serdexmethylphenidate/dexmethylphenidate for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises clinical, pharmacological, and regulatory data into a single reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific Product Information required for prescribing in any specific region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Serdexmethylphenidate chloride and dexmethylphenidate hydrochloride.
- **Chemical Class:** Central Nervous System (CNS) stimulant; phenidate derivative.
- **Brand Name(s):** **Azstarys** [US].
- **ATC Code:** N06BA15 (Serdexmethylphenidate and dexmethylphenidate).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - **Oral Capsules:** Fixed molar ratio of 70% serdexmethylphenidate and 30% dexmethylphenidate.
    - Available strengths [US]: 
        - 26.1 mg / 5.2 mg (equivalent to 28 mg / 6 mg of chloride/hydrochloride salts).
        - 39.2 mg / 7.8 mg (equivalent to 42 mg / 9 mg of chloride/hydrochloride salts).
        - 52.3 mg / 10.4 mg (equivalent to 56 mg / 12 mg of chloride/hydrochloride salts).
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Commave Sub, LLC; Distributed by Corium, LLC [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD):** Indicated for the treatment of ADHD in patients aged 6 years and older [US].
- **Limitations of Use:** Use is not recommended in pediatric patients younger than 6 years due to higher plasma exposure and an increased incidence of adverse reactions compared to older patients [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing:** Administer once daily in the morning, with or without food [US].
- **Pediatric Patients (6 to 12 years):**
    - **Starting Dose:** 39.2 mg/7.8 mg once daily.
    - **Titration:** Dosage may be adjusted after one week to 26.1 mg/5.2 mg or increased to 52.3 mg/10.4 mg daily based on response and tolerability.
    - **Maximum Dose:** 52.3 mg/10.4 mg daily [US].
- **Adults and Pediatric Patients (13 to 17 years):**
    - **Starting Dose:** 39.2 mg/7.8 mg once daily.
    - **Titration:** Increase to 52.3 mg/10.4 mg after one week [US].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Capsules may be swallowed whole or opened and the entire contents sprinkled onto two tablespoons of applesauce or added to 50 mL of water.
    - The mixture must be consumed within 10 minutes and not stored for future use.
- **Switching from Other Methylphenidate Products:** Do not substitute on a milligram-per-milligram basis due to differing pharmacokinetic profiles. Discontinue the previous methylphenidate treatment and titrate using the schedule above [US].
- **Discontinuation:** If symptoms are paradoxically aggravated or if no improvement is seen after one month of optimal dosing, the drug should be discontinued [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to serdexmethylphenidate, methylphenidate, or any excipients. Reactions including bronchospasm, rash, and pruritus have been reported [US].
- **Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors (MAOIs):** Concurrent use with MAOIs, or use within 14 days of discontinuing an MAOI, due to the risk of hypertensive crisis [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning: Abuse, Misuse, and Addiction [US]:** AZSTARYS has a high potential for abuse and misuse, which can lead to substance use disorder and addiction. Misuse may result in overdose or death. Risk increases with higher doses or unapproved administration routes (e.g., injection, snorting). Assess risk before prescribing and monitor throughout treatment.
- **Risks to Patients with Serious Cardiac Disease:** Avoid use in patients with known structural cardiac abnormalities, cardiomyopathy, serious arrhythmias, or coronary artery disease. Sudden death has been reported in such patients using CNS stimulants at recommended doses [US].
- **Blood Pressure and Heart Rate:** Can cause clinically significant increases in blood pressure and heart rate. Monitoring is required for all patients [US].
- **Psychiatric Adverse Reactions:** May exacerbate pre-existing psychosis or induce manic/mixed episodes in patients with bipolar disorder. New psychotic or manic symptoms (e.g., hallucinations, delusional thinking) can occur in patients without a prior history at recommended doses. Discontinue if such symptoms emerge [US].
- **Priapism:** Prolonged and painful erections, sometimes requiring surgery, have been reported with methylphenidate products. Seek immediate medical attention if this occurs [US].
- **Peripheral Vasculopathy (Raynaud’s Phenomenon):** Stimulants are associated with peripheral vasculopathy. Monitor for digital changes (e.g., numbness, color changes) [US].
- **Growth Suppression:** Monitor height and weight in pediatric patients. Treatment may need to be interrupted if growth is not as expected [US].
- **Ocular Effects:** 
    - **Acute Angle-Closure Glaucoma:** Reported with methylphenidate; evaluate at-risk patients (e.g., significant hyperopia) [US].
    - **Increased Intraocular Pressure (IOP):** Use with caution and monitor in patients with open-angle glaucoma or pre-existing increased IOP [US].
- **Motor and Verbal Tics:** May exacerbate or induce tics and Tourette’s syndrome. Evaluate family history and monitor patients [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Common Adverse Effects (>5% and twice placebo rate) [US]:** 
    - Appetite decreased, insomnia, nausea, vomiting, dyspepsia, abdominal pain, weight decreased, anxiety, dizziness, irritability, affect lability, tachycardia, and increased blood pressure.
- **Serious Adverse Effects:**
    - Sudden cardiac death, stroke, myocardial infarction.
    - Psychotic or manic symptoms.
    - Priapism.
    - Peripheral vasculopathy (Raynaud’s).
- **Postmarketing Experience (Methylphenidate products):** 
    - *Blood:* Pancytopenia, thrombocytopenia.
    - *Cardiac:* Angina pectoris, bradycardia, extrasystole.
    - *Immune:* Angioedema, anaphylaxis.
    - *Nervous System:* Convulsions, dyskinesia, serotonin syndrome (in combination with serotonergic drugs).
    - *Hepatobiliary:* Hepatocellular injury, acute hepatic failure.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **MAOIs:** Potential for fatal hypertensive crisis; do not use within 14 days of MAOI therapy [US].
- **Antihypertensive Drugs:** AZSTARYS may decrease the effectiveness of these medications; monitor blood pressure and adjust as needed [US].
- **Halogenated Anesthetics:** Risk of sudden blood pressure increase during surgery; avoid AZSTARYS on the day of surgery [US].
- **Risperidone:** Combined use with methylphenidate may increase the risk of extrapyramidal symptoms (EPS) during dosage changes of either medication [US].
- **CYP Enzymes:** Does not appear to be a substrate, inducer, or inhibitor of major CYP450 enzymes (1A2, 2C8, 2C9, 2C19, 2D6, 2E1, 3A) [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not assigned (Current US labeling uses Risk Summary).
    - **Risk Summary:** No available data on use in pregnant women to evaluate risk of major birth defects or miscarriage. Animal studies with dexmethylphenidate showed delayed fetal skeletal ossification at 3x the MRHD. Serdexmethylphenidate showed no developmental effects in rabbits at 49x the MRHD [US].
    - **Clinical Considerations:** Stimulants can cause vasoconstriction, potentially decreasing placental perfusion [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** 
    - **Safety:** Dexmethylphenidate is present in human milk (infant dose 0.16% to 0.7% of maternal weight-adjusted dose). Monitor breastfed infants for agitation, anorexia, and reduced weight gain [US].
- **Fertility:** Racemic methylphenidate did not impair fertility in mice at 10x the MRHD of dexmethylphenidate [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy established for ages 6 to 17. Not established/recommended for children under 6 years [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical trials did not include patients aged 65 and over [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Not studied; however, renal clearance is not a major elimination pathway for serdexmethylphenidate or methylphenidate, so impairment is expected to have little effect [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No experience in patients with hepatic impairment [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Serdexmethylphenidate is a prodrug of dexmethylphenidate. Dexmethylphenidate is a CNS stimulant that blocks the reuptake of norepinephrine and dopamine into the presynaptic neuron, increasing their concentration in the extraneuronal space [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Serdexmethylphenidate itself shows little to no binding to monoaminergic transporters [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** 
    - **Absorption:** Absolute bioavailability of serdexmethylphenidate is <3%. Tmax is approximately 2 hours. Food lengthens Tmax to 4–4.5 hours but does not significantly affect overall exposure.
    - **Distribution:** Plasma protein binding is ~56% (serdex) and ~47% (dex).
    - **Metabolism:** Serdexmethylphenidate is likely converted to dexmethylphenidate in the lower GI tract. Dexmethylphenidate is primarily metabolised via de-esterification to d-ritalinic acid (inactive).
    - **Elimination:** Half-life is ~5.7 hours for serdexmethylphenidate and ~11.7 hours for dexmethylphenidate. Excretion is primarily via urine (ritalinic acid) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Analog Classroom Study (Pediatrics 6-12 years):** A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study (n=150) showed that AZSTARYS-treated patients had a statistically significant improvement in ADHD symptoms compared to placebo, as measured by the SKAMP-Combined score averaged over a 13-hour laboratory classroom day [US].
- **Adolescent Efficacy:** Established via pharmacokinetic bridging to adult and younger pediatric data [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
- **Conditions:** Protect from moisture. Dispense in a tight container [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** 
    - **US:** Rx-only; Schedule II (CII) Controlled Substance (dexmethylphenidate component is CII; serdexmethylphenidate is Schedule IV, but the combination product is handled as CII) [US].
