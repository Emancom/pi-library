# Originality Audit Report: desogestrel

## Audit Summary
- **Originality Score:** 88/100
- **Verbatim Copied Passages Found:** 1
- **Overall Assessment:** Pass

## Verbatim Copied Passages
1. **Text:** "99% bound to serum proteins, primarily albumin and Sex Hormone Binding Globulin (SHBG)"
   - **Source:** Document 2 (FDA), Page 3 ("Etonogestrel... was found to be 99% protein bound, primarily to sex hormone-binding globulin (SHBG)") and Document 1 (TGA), Page 15 ("Etonogestrel is bound to serum albumin and to sex hormone binding globulin (SHBG)").
   - **Location:** Section 9, Pharmacokinetics (Desogestrel Distribution).
   - **Note:** While highly technical, the phrasing is an almost exact match to the FDA's "99% protein bound" combined with TGA's specific mention of albumin.

## Close Paraphrases
1. **Approved Indications:** "indicated for oral contraception for the prevention of pregnancy in women who choose this method of birth control."
   - **Source:** Document 2 (FDA), Page 4 ("indicated for the prevention of pregnancy in women who elect to use this product as a method of contraception").
   - **Note:** "Choose this method" is a direct synonym for "elect to use this product."

2. **Contraindications (Hepatic):** "Current or history of severe hepatic disease until liver function markers return to normal."
   - **Source:** Document 1 (TGA), Page 4 ("Presence or history of severe hepatic disease as long as liver function have not returned to normal").

3. **Pharmacokinetics (Ethinylestradiol Absorption):** "Bioavailability is approximately 60% due to first-pass metabolism."
   - **Source:** Document 1 (TGA), Page 15 ("Absolute bioavailability, as a result of presystemic conjugation and first pass metabolism, is approximately 60%").

4. **Pharmacokinetics (Desogestrel Excretion):** "Metabolites are excreted in urine and bile (ratio 6:4)."
   - **Source:** Document 1 (TGA), Page 15 ("excreted at a urinary to biliary ratio of about 6:4").

## Originality Strengths
- **Harmonization of Regimens:** The editorial successfully synthesizes two different product architectures (the standard 21/7 Marvelon regimen from the TGA and the "stepped" estrogen regimen from the FDA) into a single coherent section (Section 1).
- **Comparison of Clinical Advice:** In Section 5 (Post-Partum initiation), the writer explicitly compares the TGA (21–28 days) and FDA (4 weeks) recommendations, providing an original analytical layer rather than just copying one source.
- **Structural Overhaul:** The editorial reorganizes information into a more logical "Editorial PI" flow, particularly in the Adverse Effects and Drug Interactions sections, which are fragmented in the original sources.

## Differences from Source PIs
- **Synthesis of Formulations:** The editorial identifies "Regimen A" and "Regimen B," labels which do not exist in the source documents.
- **Metric Harmonization:** The editorial converts the FDA's 0.15 mg/0.02 mg units to 150 micrograms/20 micrograms in some instances to match international standards used in the TGA document.
- **Clarification of Sources:** The use of brackets (e.g., "[TGA]", "[FDA]") provides regulatory context not present in either original document.
- **Consolidation of Risks:** The VTE risk statistics (9–12 cases per 10,000) are extracted from a complex table in the TGA PI (p. 5) and summarized into a readable sentence.

## Recommendation
**PASS**

The editorial is a high-quality original synthesis. While technical sections like Pharmacokinetics and Contraindications inevitably mirror the source terminology (which is necessary for regulatory accuracy), the author has made significant efforts to reword the instructions for use, compare jurisdictional differences, and unify disparate data sets. No further revision is required.
