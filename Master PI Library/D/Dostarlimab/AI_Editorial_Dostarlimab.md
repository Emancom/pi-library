# Editorial Product Information: Dostarlimab

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdiction: **Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of dostarlimab for healthcare professionals. It synthesises clinical data and regulatory requirements into a single reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial summary and does not replace the official PI required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory documents for specific legal and prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Dostarlimab (a humanised monoclonal antibody of the IgG4 isotype).
- **Chemical Class:** Anti-programmed cell death protein-1 (PD-1) monoclonal antibody.
- **Brand Name:** **JEMPERLI** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** L01FF07.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Concentrate for solution for infusion; 500 mg/10 mL (50 mg/mL) in a single-use vial [Australian].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** GlaxoSmithKline Australia Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Endometrial Cancer (Combination Therapy):** Indicated in combination with carboplatin and paclitaxel for the treatment of adult patients with primary advanced or recurrent endometrial cancer (EC) [Australian-approved].
- **Endometrial Cancer (Monotherapy):** Indicated as monotherapy for adult patients with recurrent or advanced mismatch repair deficient (dMMR) endometrial cancer that has progressed on or following prior treatment with a platinum-containing regimen [Australian-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults)
The dosing schedule for **dostarlimab** follows an induction period (administered every 3 weeks) followed by a maintenance period (administered every 6 weeks).

### Dosing by Indication
- **Combination Therapy (with Carboplatin/Paclitaxel):**
    - **Induction:** 500 mg every 3 weeks for 6 doses.
    - **Maintenance:** 1,000 mg every 6 weeks for all subsequent cycles.
    - **Note:** Administer **dostarlimab** prior to chemotherapy when given on the same day.
- **Monotherapy:**
    - **Induction:** 500 mg every 3 weeks for 4 doses.
    - **Maintenance:** 1,000 mg every 6 weeks for all subsequent cycles.

### Administration Instructions
- Administer via IV infusion pump over 30 minutes.
- Do **not** administer as an IV push or bolus.
- A 0.2 or 0.22 micron in-line polyethersulfone (PES) filter must be used.
- **Dilution:** Must be diluted in sodium chloride 0.9% or glucose 5% to a final concentration between 2 mg/mL and 10 mg/mL. The total volume must not exceed 250 mL.

### Dose Modifications
Dose reductions are not recommended. Dosing should be delayed or discontinued based on the severity of immune-related adverse reactions (irARs).
- **Withhold for:** Grade 2 or 3 pneumonitis, Grade 2 or 3 nephritis, Grade 2 or 3 colitis, symptomatic endocrinopathies, and Grade 2 AST/ALT elevations.
- **Permanently Discontinue for:** Grade 4 reactions, recurrent Grade 3 reactions, Grade 3/4 pneumonitis, or life-threatening infusion-related reactions.

### Missed Dose Instructions
Not specified in available sources beyond the recommendation to continue according to the recommended dose and schedule.

## 4. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to **dostarlimab** or any of its excipients (sodium citrate dihydrate, citric acid monohydrate, arginine hydrochloride, sodium chloride, polysorbate 80) [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Immune-Related Adverse Reactions (irARs):** May involve any organ system and can be fatal. Most common include pneumonitis, colitis, hepatitis, endocrinopathies (hypothyroidism, hyperthyroidism, adrenal insufficiency, T1DM), and nephritis.
- **Monitoring Requirements:** Baseline and periodic liver function tests (LFTs), kidney function tests, and thyroid function tests (TFTs) are required. Monitor for clinical signs of irARs (e.g., cough, diarrhoea, rash).
- **Infusion-Related Reactions:** Monitor for signs of hypersensitivity during and after infusion.
- **Transplant Complications:** 
    - **Solid Organ:** Risk of rejection.
    - **Allogeneic HSCT:** Fatal and serious complications can occur if treated before or after HSCT.
- **Patient Card:** All patients must be provided with a Patient Card and educated on when to seek urgent medical attention [Australian].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Frequencies (Summary)
- **Very Common (>10%):** Anaemia, nausea, diarrhoea, vomiting, fatigue, asthenia, rash, pruritus, hypothyroidism, pyrexia, and increased transaminases.
- **Common (1–10%):** Hyperthyroidism, pneumonitis, colitis, pancreatitis, and infusion-related reactions.
- **Uncommon (0.1–1%):** Adrenal insufficiency, thyroiditis, hypophysitis, Type 1 diabetes mellitus, myocarditis, nephritis, uveitis, and encephalitis.

### Serious Adverse Effects
- **Sepsis:** Reported in 3.7% of patients in combination therapy (including urosepsis) [Australian].
- **irARs:** Severe pneumonitis and immune-mediated hepatitis are critical risks.
- **Fatalities:** Reported in 1.2% of combination therapy patients, including septic shock and myelosuppression.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** As a monoclonal antibody, **dostarlimab** is not a substrate for CYP450 enzymes or drug transporters. PK-based drug-drug interactions are not expected.
- **Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** No formal drug-drug interaction studies have been conducted.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [Australian Category D]. **Dostarlimab** can cause fetal harm based on its mechanism of action. It is not recommended during pregnancy. 
- **Contraception:** Women of childbearing potential should use highly effective contraception during treatment and for 4 months after the final dose.
- **Breastfeeding:** Presence in human milk is unknown. Because of the potential for serious adverse reactions in the infant, breastfeeding is not recommended during treatment and for 4 months after the last dose.
- **Fertility:** No specific human data; animal studies showed no effect on reproductive organs, though results may not be predictive of clinical risk [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy in children under 18 years have not been established; no data are available [Australian].
- **Elderly Use:** No dose adjustment is required for patients ≥65 years. Limited clinical data exist for patients ≥75 years.
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment is recommended for mild or moderate impairment. Data are limited for severe impairment or end-stage renal disease.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment is recommended for mild impairment. Data are limited for moderate or severe impairment.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** **Dostarlimab** binds to the PD-1 receptor and blocks its interaction with PD-L1 and PD-L2. This releases the PD-1 pathway-mediated inhibition of the immune response, including anti-tumour activity.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Full receptor occupancy is maintained throughout the dosing interval at the recommended therapeutic regimen.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Steady-state volume of distribution is approximately 5.81 L.
    - **Metabolism:** Catabolised into small peptides and amino acids via general protein degradation.
    - **Elimination:** Geometric mean clearance is 0.00681 L/h; terminal half-life is approximately 23.2 days.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **RUBY Study (Combination):** A Phase 3 trial in primary advanced or recurrent EC. **Dostarlimab** + carboplatin/paclitaxel significantly improved progression-free survival (PFS) in the dMMR/MSI-H population (HR 0.28) and the overall population (HR 0.64). Overall survival (OS) was also improved in the overall population (HR 0.69) [Australian].
- **GARNET Study (Monotherapy):** A Phase 1 multicohort study. In dMMR EC patients who progressed on platinum therapy, the objective response rate (ORR) was 45.4%, with 15.6% achieving a complete response. The median duration of response (DOR) was not reached at the time of analysis [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vials:** Store in a refrigerator at 2°C to 8°C. Do not freeze. Store in the original carton to protect from light.
- **Diluted Solution:** 
    - At room temperature (up to 25°C): Stable for up to 6 hours from dilution to the end of infusion.
    - Refrigerated (2°C to 8°C): Stable for up to 24 hours.
- **Chemical/Physical Stability:** Demonstrated for 24 hours (2-8°C) and 6 hours (up to 25°C) from vial puncture to the end of administration [Australian].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule:** Schedule 4 – Prescription Only Medicine [Australian].
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only.
