# Editorial Product Information: Delandistrogene moxeparvovec

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on Product Information (PI) provided by the following regulatory authority: **US (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, specifically clinical pharmacists and prescribers.
- **Note:** This document is an editorial synthesis for informational purposes and is not a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** delandistrogene moxeparvovec-rokl
- **Chemical Class:** Adeno-associated virus serotype rh74 (AAVrh74) vector-based gene therapy.
- **Brand Name:** ELEVIDYS [US]
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Suspension for intravenous (IV) infusion. Nominal concentration is $1.33 \times 10^{13}$ vector genomes (vg) per mL.
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous infusion.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Sarepta Therapeutics, Inc. [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy (DMD):** Indicated for the treatment of ambulatory patients 4 years of age and older with a confirmed mutation in the *DMD* gene [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Selection Criteria:** Treatment is only recommended for patients with anti-AAVrh74 total binding antibody titers <1:400 [US].
- **Dose:** A single-dose intravenous infusion.
    - **Patients weighing 10 to 70 kg:** $1.33 \times 10^{14}$ vg/kg of body weight.
    - **Patients weighing 70 kg or greater:** $9.31 \times 10^{15}$ vg (fixed dose).
- **Corticosteroid Regimen:** To reduce the risk of immune response, a corticosteroid regimen must be initiated 1 day prior to infusion and continued for a minimum of 60 days.
    - **Standard Dose:** 1 mg/kg/day (prednisone equivalent), maximum 60 mg/day.
    - **Adjustments:** If liver function abnormalities occur, the dose may be increased to 2.0–2.5 mg/kg/day (maximum 120 mg/day) [US].

### Administration Instructions
- **Preparation:** Thaw frozen vials. Vials are stable for 14 days in the refrigerator ($2^\circ\text{C}$ to $8^\circ\text{C}$) or 24 hours at room temperature. Do not shake or refreeze.
- **Infusion:** Administer via a syringe pump through a peripheral limb vein using an in-line 0.2-micron polyethersulfone (PES) filter. 
- **Rate:** Infuse over 1–2 hours. Do not exceed a rate of 10 mL/kg/hour.
- **Compatibility:** Do not mix with other products; flush the line with 0.9% sodium chloride before and after infusion [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Specific Mutations:** Contraindicated in patients with any deletion in exon 8 and/or exon 9 of the *DMD* gene [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warning [US]
- **Acute Serious Liver Injury and Acute Liver Failure:** These reactions, including fatal outcomes, have occurred. Pre-existing liver impairment may increase risk. Assess liver function prior to infusion and monitor weekly for 3 months post-infusion. Prompt specialist consultation is required if injury is suspected.

### General Warnings
- **Serious Infections:** Increased susceptibility due to concomitant corticosteroids. Postpone infusion in patients with active or recent (within 4 weeks) infections.
- **Myocarditis:** Acute, life-threatening myocarditis has been observed. Monitor troponin-I levels before infusion and weekly for the first month.
- **Immune-mediated Myositis:** Reported in patients with specific deletions (exons 1–17 and/or 59–71). Symptoms include severe muscle weakness, dysphagia, or dyspnea.
- **Infusion-related Reactions:** Includes hypersensitivity and anaphylaxis. Monitor during and for at least 3 hours after infusion [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions typically occur within the first two months post-infusion.

| Adverse Reaction | Frequency [US] |
| :--- | :--- |
| Vomiting | 65% |
| Nausea | 43% |
| Liver Injury (AST/ALT/GGT/Bilirubin increase) | 40% |
| Pyrexia | 28% |
| Thrombocytopenia | 8% |
| Troponin-I increased | 8% |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Acute liver failure (including fatal outcomes in non-ambulatory patients), life-threatening myocarditis, and immune-mediated myositis [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Vaccinations:** All vaccinations should be completed at least 4 weeks prior to starting the corticosteroid regimen. Proximity to gene therapy may interfere with immune response [US].
- **Hepatotoxic Agents:** Use caution with concomitant medications known to cause liver injury.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Not intended for use in pregnant women. No data available [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** No data available regarding presence in milk or effects on infants [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy established in patients $\ge 4$ years old. 
- **Elderly Use:** Not studied; DMD is a disease of childhood and young adulthood [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not recommended for patients with GGT > 2x ULN or total bilirubin > ULN (except Gilbert's). These patients were excluded from clinical trials and are at higher risk for liver failure [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** A non-replicating viral vector delivers a transgene encoding a shortened, functional version of the dystrophin protein (ELEVIDYS micro-dystrophin) to skeletal and cardiac muscle cells, localising to the sarcolemma.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Micro-dystrophin expression was confirmed via muscle biopsy (Western blot) at Week 12, showing significant increases from baseline across studies [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Vector DNA is detected in the liver, heart, and skeletal muscle.
    - **Elimination:** Vector genomes are cleared from the serum within ~1 week. Shedding occurs via urine, feces, and saliva, with complete elimination from excreta occurring between 50 and 160 days [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Study 1 (NCT 03769116):** A double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in 41 ambulatory boys (4–7 years). The primary endpoint (change in NSAA total score at Week 48) was not statistically significant (p=0.37), though a numerical advantage was seen in the 4–5 year subgroup.
- **Study 3 (NCT 05096221):** A double-blind trial in 125 ambulatory boys (4–7 years). The change in NSAA score at Week 52 was not statistically significant (p=0.24). However, secondary timed function tests (Time to Rise, 10-meter walk/run) showed changes of clinical relevance [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage:** Ship and store frozen at $\le -60^\circ\text{C}$ ($-76^\circ\text{F}$).
- **Handling:** Once thawed, do not refreeze. Store in the upright position. 
- **Biohazard:** Follow local guidelines for biological waste. Shedding precautions (hand hygiene, sealed disposal of waste) are recommended for one month post-infusion [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Product Tracking:** Individualised kits are weight-based and range from 10 to 70 vials (10 mL each) [US].
