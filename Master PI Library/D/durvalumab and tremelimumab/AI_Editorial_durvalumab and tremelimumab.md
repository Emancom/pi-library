# Editorial Product Information: **Levocarnitine**

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial synthesis is derived from official Product Information (PI) documents provided by the following regulatory authority: **US (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document is a harmonised editorial overview intended for use by healthcare professionals to provide a concise, clinically accurate summary of **levocarnitine** injection.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific Product Information required for prescribing.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** **Levocarnitine** (generic name); a carrier molecule involved in mitochondrial fatty acid transport. 
- **Chemical Name:** (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection, USP [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - **Intravenous Injection:** 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) single-dose vial [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Indicated for the acute and chronic management of patients with inborn metabolic errors resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg. This may be administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or via infusion. 
    - In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose is often administered, followed by an equivalent dose divided over the subsequent 24 hours.
    - Dosing frequency should be every 3 to 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours.
    - The maximum recorded dose administered is 300 mg/kg [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:** The recommended starting dose is 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight).
    - Administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
    - Dosing adjustments (e.g., reduction to 5 mg/kg) should be guided by pre-dialysis (trough) plasma concentrations, typically starting as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].

### Monitoring Requirements
- It is recommended to obtain plasma carnitine concentrations prior to initiating therapy.
- Weekly and monthly monitoring is advised, including blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma carnitine levels.
- The target range for plasma free carnitine is 35 to 60 µmol/L [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected visually for discoloration or particulate matter prior to use.
- **Compatibility:** Stable for up to 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations between 0.5 mg/mL and 8.0 mg/mL [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, bronchospasm, and laryngeal edema, have been reported. These often occur within minutes of administration, particularly in ESRD patients. Treatment must be discontinued immediately if severe reactions occur [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with a known history, there may be an increase in seizure frequency or severity [US].
- **General Precaution (Oral vs. IV):** The safety of high-dose oral **levocarnitine** in patients with severely compromised renal function has not been established. Chronic high oral doses may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) normally cleared by the kidneys [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions observed in clinical trials of ESRD patients (occurring at ≥5%, regardless of causality) include:

| Body System | Adverse Event (Incidence ≥5%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Pain, injection site reaction, headache, flu syndrome, chest pain, asthenia, accidental injury, fever, abdominal pain |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension, hypotension, tachycardia, palpitations |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea, vomiting, nausea, dyspepsia, anorexia |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis, cough, rhinitis, dyspnea |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness, paresthesia, insomnia, depression |
| **Musculoskeletal**| Leg cramps |
| **Skin** | Pruritus |
| **Metabolic** | Hypercalcemia, hyperkalemia, weight decrease/increase |

**Post-marketing Experience:**
- Transient nausea and vomiting.
- Less frequent reports of body odor and gastritis.
- Serious reactions: Anaphylaxis and seizures [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) has been reported when **levocarnitine** is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels closely following the initiation of **levocarnitine** or after any dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not assigned in source]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (by surface area) showed no evidence of fetal harm or impaired fertility. Because animal data are not always predictive, use during pregnancy only if clearly required [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Specific studies in nursing mothers are unavailable. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine concentrations in milk following exogenous administration. Clinicians should weigh the benefits to the mother against potential risks to the infant; consideration may be given to discontinuing breastfeeding or the drug [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility was found in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders is based on mg/kg body weight, applicable to paediatric patients [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Primarily indicated for use in ESRD patients on dialysis. Dose adjustments are based on pre-dialysis trough concentrations (target 40-50 µmol/L) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** **Levocarnitine** is a naturally occurring substance essential for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrates for oxidation and subsequent energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces).
    - **Elimination:** Following a single IV bolus (20 mg/kg), approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours. The mean apparent terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients (20 mg/kg post-dialysis), mean pre-dialysis carnitine levels rose from ~20 µmol/L to 190 ± 55 µmol/L at 8 weeks.
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** A study comparing 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses in ESRD patients showed dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine concentrations at 12 and 24 weeks.
- **Clinical Outcomes:** While **levocarnitine** successfully increases carnitine concentrations in ESRD patients, the definitive effect on specific signs, symptoms, or clinical outcomes in this population remains undetermined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Precautions:** Protect from freezing and avoid excessive heat.
- **Handling:** Vials do not contain preservatives; discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Rx-only [US].
