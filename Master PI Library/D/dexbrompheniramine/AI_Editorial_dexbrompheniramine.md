# Editorial Product Information: Dexbrompheniramine Maleate and Pseudoephedrine Hydrochloride

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA)
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of the combination product containing dexbrompheniramine maleate and pseudoephedrine hydrochloride, intended for use by healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This synthesis is an editorial summary based on provided regulatory documents and is not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) or prescribing information required in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** Dexbrompheniramine Maleate (alkylamine antihistamine) and Pseudoephedrine Hydrochloride (sympathomimetic amine/nasal decongestant).
- **Brand Names:** **CONEX** [US].
- **ATC Code:** R01BA52 (Pseudoephedrine, combinations) [Not explicitly stated in source; provided for clinical context].
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** Oral liquid/syrup containing 2 mg dexbrompheniramine maleate and 60 mg pseudoephedrine HCl per 5 mL [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Llorens Pharmaceutical International Division [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- Relief of nasal congestion associated with the common cold, hay fever, or other upper respiratory allergies [US].
- Decongestion of sinus openings and passages [US].
- Temporary reduction of swelling in nasal passages to restore free breathing [US].
- Alleviation of hay fever (allergic rhinitis) symptoms, including:
    - Rhinorrhea (runny nose) [US].
    - Sneezing [US].
    - Pruritus of the nose or throat [US].
    - Itchy and watery eyes [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Adults and Children (12 years and older):** 10 mL (two teaspoonfuls) administered orally every 4 to 6 hours [US].
- **Maximum Dose:** Do not exceed 4 doses (40 mL) in any 24-hour period [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Dosing should be measured with an appropriate medicinal measuring device.
- For children under 12 years of age, a physician must be consulted for specific dosing instructions [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors (MAOIs):** Concurrent use with a prescription MAOI (used for psychiatric conditions, emotional conditions, or Parkinson’s disease) or use within 14 days of discontinuing MAOI therapy is contraindicated [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### General Warnings
- **Cardiovascular/Endocrine:** Patients with heart disease, hypertension, thyroid disease, or diabetes should consult a physician before use [US].
- **Genitourinary:** Use with caution in patients with symptomatic prostatic hypertrophy (difficulty in urination due to enlargement of the prostate gland) [US].
- **CNS Effects:** 
    - Drowsiness may occur; patients should be advised to exercise caution when operating motor vehicles or heavy machinery [US].
    - Excitability may occur, particularly in paediatric populations [US].

### Monitoring and Stop Criteria
- Patients should discontinue use and consult a healthcare provider if:
    - Symptoms do not improve within 7 days or are accompanied by a fever [US].
    - Nervousness, dizziness, or sleeplessness occur [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
The following adverse effects are associated with the components of this formulation; however, specific frequency data (e.g., >10%) was not provided in the source documentation.

- **Central Nervous System:** Drowsiness, excitability (more common in children), nervousness, dizziness, sleeplessness [US].
- **Other Potential Effects:** Standard for these classes include dry mouth and gastrointestinal upset [Not specified in available sources].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **MAOIs:** Potential for hypertensive crisis; contraindicated during and within 14 days of MAOI therapy [US].
- **CNS Depressants:** Alcohol, sedatives, and tranquilizers may increase the sedative effects (drowsiness) of dexbrompheniramine [US].
- **Prescription Sedatives:** Patients should consult a doctor before combining this product with prescribed sedatives or tranquilizers [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Healthcare professionals should be consulted before use. No specific regulatory category (e.g., US Letter Category) was provided in the OTC source document [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Healthcare professionals should be consulted before use [US].
- **Fertility:** Data regarding fertility was not specified in the available source.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Use in children under 12 years of age requires professional medical advice. Excitability is a noted risk in children [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Specific geriatric dosing was not provided; however, elderly patients may be more sensitive to the sympathomimetic effects (pseudoephedrine) and anticholinergic effects (dexbrompheniramine) [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Dosing adjustments not specified in available sources.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Dosing adjustments not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** 
    - **Dexbrompheniramine Maleate:** An antihistamine that antagonizes H1-receptors, reducing allergic symptoms such as sneezing and watery eyes [US].
    - **Pseudoephedrine Hydrochloride:** A sympathomimetic amine that acts as a decongestant by constricting blood vessels in the nasal passages [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at controlled room temperature between 20°C and 25°C (68°F–77°F) [US].
- **Excursions:** Permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F–86°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container with a child-resistant cap as defined in the USP. Protect from light [US].
