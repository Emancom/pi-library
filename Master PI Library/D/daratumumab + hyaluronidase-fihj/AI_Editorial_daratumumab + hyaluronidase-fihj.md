# Editorial Product Information: daratumumab + hyaluronidase-fihj

## 1. Product Identification
- Active ingredient (INN/generic name): **daratumumab + hyaluronidase-fihj**
- Chemical class: monoclonal antibody (daratumumab, anti‑CD38) combined with an endoglycosidase (hyaluronidase)
- ATC code: Not specified in available sources
- Route(s) of administration: subcutaneous only

## 2. Regulatory History
- Initial U.S. approval: 2020.
- Last revision/update of the provided U.S. product information: February 2026.
- Withdrawal, suspension or restriction history: Not specified in available sources.
- Registration/approval dates for other jurisdictions: Not specified in available sources.

## 3. Approved Indications
Approved indications reported in the available product information (adult patients):
- Multiple myeloma:
  - In combination with bortezomib, lenalidomide and dexamethasone for induction and consolidation in newly diagnosed patients eligible for autologous stem cell transplant.
  - In combination with bortezomib, lenalidomide and dexamethasone in newly diagnosed patients who are ineligible for autologous stem cell transplant.
  - In combination with bortezomib, melphalan and prednisone in newly diagnosed patients who are ineligible for autologous stem cell transplant.
  - In combination with lenalidomide and dexamethasone
