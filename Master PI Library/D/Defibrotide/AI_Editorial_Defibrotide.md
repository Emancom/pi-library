# Editorial Product Information: Defibrotide

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** This editorial synthesis is based on the Product Information (PI) document provided from Australia (Therapeutic Goods Administration - TGA). 
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of **defibrotide** for healthcare professionals, consolidating regulatory and clinical data into a concise, structured format.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority's current approved PI for definitive guidance.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** **Defibrotide** (an oligonucleotide mixture produced from porcine intestinal mucosa).
- **Chemical Class:** Other antithrombotic agents.
- **Brand Name(s):** **Defitelio** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** B01AX01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Concentrated solution for infusion (sterile concentrate). One mL contains 80 mg of defibrotide (200 mg in 2.5 mL vials).
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Jazz Pharmaceuticals ANZ Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Hepatic Veno-Occlusive Disease (VOD):** Treatment of severe hepatic VOD, also known as sinusoidal obstruction syndrome (SOS), in haematopoietic stem-cell transplantation (HSCT) therapy.
- **Patient Population:** Indicated for adults, adolescents, children, and infants 1 month of age and older [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Paediatrics >1 month)
- **Recommended Dose:** 6.25 mg/kg body weight administered every 6 hours (total 25 mg/kg/day).
- **Duration:** Administer for a minimum of 21 days. Treatment should continue until symptoms and signs of severe VOD resolve.
- **Maximum Dose:** Doses above 25 mg/kg/day are not recommended due to limited efficacy and safety data.

### Administration Instructions
- **Preparation:** Must be diluted prior to use to a final concentration of 4 mg/mL to 20 mg/mL.
- **Diluents:** 5% glucose solution or 0.9% sodium chloride.
- **Infusion Rate:** Administer by IV infusion over two hours. 
- **Equipment:** Use of an infusion set with a 0.2 µm in-line filter is recommended.
- **Line Maintenance:** Flush the IV line with 5% glucose or 0.9% sodium chloride after infusion. Do not co-administer other drugs through the same line.
- **Bolus Administration:** Strictly avoided; bolus doses may cause flushing or sensations of "generalised heat."

### Missed Dose
- Not explicitly specified in the source; however, the strict 6-hourly regimen should be maintained.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to **defibrotide** or any excipients (sodium citrate dihydrate, hydrochloric acid, sodium hydroxide).
- **Concomitant Thrombolytic Therapy:** Use of thrombolytic agents (e.g., t-PA) is contraindicated due to an increased risk of haemorrhage.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Haemorrhage:** **Defibrotide** increases the risk of bleeding. Use is not recommended within 24 hours of medicines that increase haemorrhage risk (within 12 hours for unfractionated heparin). Discontinue if clinically significant acute bleeding occurs.
- **Anticoagulant Therapy:** Concomitant use of systemic anticoagulants (heparin, warfarin, direct thrombin inhibitors, or factor Xa inhibitors) requires intensive monitoring and consideration of **defibrotide** discontinuation.
- **Invasive Procedures:** Temporary discontinuation is recommended for patients undergoing surgery or invasive procedures with significant major bleeding risk.
- **Hemodynamic Instability:** Use is not recommended in patients unable to maintain mean arterial pressure with only single pressor support.
- **Sodium Content:** Each vial contains 20.4 mg of sodium.

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effect frequencies are based on pooled clinical trial data (N=176) for the 25 mg/kg/day dose.

| Frequency | Adverse Effects |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Hypotension (37%), Diarrhoea (24%), Multi-organ failure (22%), Veno-occlusive liver disease (18%), Vomiting (18%), Renal failure (17%), Nausea (16%), Epistaxis (14%), Respiratory failure (11%) |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Pyrexia, Hypertension, Hypoxia, Agitation, Haematuria, Pulmonary alveolar haemorrhage, Gastrointestinal haemorrhage, Sepsis, Abdominal pain, Graft versus host disease, Sinus tachycardia, Tachycardia, Bradycardia |
| **Uncommon (0.1–1%)** | Anaphylactic reaction, Cerebral haemorrhage, Subarachnoid haemorrhage, Spinal haematoma, Haemothorax, Menorrhagia, Puncture site haemorrhage |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Haemorrhagic events (including fatal cases in paediatric populations at high doses) and hypersensitivity reactions.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacodynamic Interactions:**
    - **Thrombolytics (e.g., t-PA):** Potentiate antithrombotic effects; co-administration is contraindicated.
    - **Antithrombotics/Fibrinolytics:** **Defibrotide** may enhance the activity of these agents. Use with LMWH, warfarin, or direct oral anticoagulants is not recommended.
    - **Antiplatelet Agents:** Use with NSAIDs or other platelet inhibitors requires close medical supervision.
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:**
    - **CYP450 System:** **Defibrotide** does not inhibit or induce CYP450 enzymes.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Australian Category D.** 
    - Animal studies showed high rates of haemorrhagic abortion. **Defibrotide** should not be used during pregnancy unless the clinical condition absolutely requires treatment.
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if **defibrotide** is excreted in human milk. While a risk to newborns is not expected based on the nature of the product, use during breastfeeding should be carefully considered.
- **Fertility:** No human data available. Animal studies showed no effects on reproductive organs.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Approved for infants ≥1 month. Dosing is identical to adults (6.25 mg/kg q6h). Safety/efficacy in infants <1 month is not established.
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical trials did not include sufficient numbers of subjects ≥65 years to determine if they respond differently; however, no differences in response have been identified in practice.
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment required, including for patients on intermittent haemodialysis.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No formal PK studies; however, it has been used in trials for patients with hepatic impairment without dose adjustment or identified safety issues.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** A multifactorial oligonucleotide mixture. It reduces excessive endothelial cell activation (endothelial dysfunction) and protects endothelial cells from damage (e.g., from fludarabine). It increases systemic tissue factor pathway inhibitor (TFPI) and t-PA while decreasing von Willebrand factor (vWF) and PAI-1, thereby modulating the thrombo-fibrinolytic balance.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Highly protein bound (93%). Volume of distribution is 8.1 to 9.1 L.
    - **Metabolism:** Does not involve the CYP450 system.
    - **Elimination:** Primarily via metabolism followed by urinary excretion (approx. 9.5% as unchanged drug). Total clearance is 3.4 to 6.1 L/h.
    - **Half-life:** Approximately 2 hours (102 minutes).

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pivotal Study (2005-01):** A Phase 3 historical-controlled study of 102 patients with severe VOD post-HSCT treated with 25 mg/kg/day. 
- **Outcomes:** 
    - **Day +100 Survival:** 38.2% in the **defibrotide** group vs. 25.0% in historical controls (p=0.0109).
    - **Complete Response:** 25.5% (defibrotide) vs. 12.5% (control) (p=0.016). Complete response was defined as total bilirubin <2 mg/dL and resolution of multiple organ failure.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Concentrate:** Store below 25°C. Do not freeze.
- **Shelf Life:** 3 years for unopened vials [Australian].
- **Diluted Solution:** From a microbiological perspective, use immediately. If necessary, store at 2–8°C for no longer than 24 hours.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule:** Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine) [Australian].
- **Monitoring:** Subject to additional monitoring for new safety information [Australian].
