# Editorial Product Information: Doxylamine Succinate and Pyridoxine Hydrochloride

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**. 
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. 
- Note: This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. At the time of writing, only the United States PI was available for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** Doxylamine succinate (ethanolamine derivative antihistamine) and Pyridoxine hydrochloride (Vitamin B$_6$ analog).
- **Brand Names:** Generic formulations (e.g., distributed by Bionpharma Inc.) [US].
- **ATC Code:** R06AA59 (Doxylamine, combinations).
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Delayed-release (DR) tablets containing 10 mg doxylamine succinate and 10 mg pyridoxine hydrochloride [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Bionpharma Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Nausea and Vomiting of Pregnancy (NVP):** Indicated for the management of nausea and vomiting of pregnancy in women who have failed to respond to conservative management (e.g., lifestyle and dietary modifications) [US].
- **Limitations of Use:** The combination has not been specifically studied in women presenting with hyperemesis gravidarum [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
The medication is administered as a daily prescription and is not intended for "as-needed" use. The woman’s need for continued treatment should be reassessed as the pregnancy progresses [US].

### Dosing and Titration Schedule [US]
The recommended starting dose is two tablets daily at bedtime. If symptoms are not adequately controlled, the dose may be escalated up to a maximum of four tablets daily as follows:
- **Day 1:** Two tablets orally at bedtime.
- **Day 2:** Two tablets at bedtime. If symptoms are controlled, maintain this dose.
- **Day 3:** If symptoms persist into the afternoon of Day 2, take two tablets at bedtime on Day 2, then take three tablets on Day 3 (one in the morning and two at bedtime).
- **Day 4:** If symptoms are controlled on three tablets, maintain this dose. If symptoms persist, take four tablets daily (one in the morning, one mid-afternoon, and two at bedtime).

### Administration Instructions
- Tablets must be swallowed whole. Do not crush, chew, or split [US].
- Take on an empty stomach with a glass of water, as food may delay the onset of action and reduce absorption [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to doxylamine succinate, other ethanolamine derivative antihistamines, pyridoxine hydrochloride, or any inactive ingredient in the formulation [US].
- **Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors (MAOIs):** Use is contraindicated in women taking MAOIs, as these agents prolong and intensify the anticholinergic effects of antihistamines [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Activities Requiring Mental Alertness:** The medication frequently causes somnolence. Patients should avoid driving or operating heavy machinery until cleared by a healthcare provider [US].
- **CNS Depressants:** Concurrent use with alcohol or other central nervous system (CNS) depressants (e.g., hypnotic sedatives, tranquilizers) is not recommended due to the risk of severe drowsiness and potential for falls or accidents [US].
- **Anticholinergic Effects:** Use with caution in patients with medical conditions that may be exacerbated by anticholinergic activity, including:
    - Increased intraocular pressure or narrow-angle glaucoma.
    - Stenosing peptic ulcer.
    - Pyloroduodenal obstruction.
    - Urinary bladder-neck obstruction [US].
- **Interference with Urine Drug Screens:** Use may result in false-positive urine screening tests for methadone, opiates, and phencyclidine phosphate (PCP). Confirmatory Gas Chromatography Mass Spectrometry (GC-MS) is required for positive results [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions are derived from clinical trial data and postmarketing reports.

### Common Adverse Effects
- **Somnolence:** Observed in 14.3% of patients vs. 11.7% in the placebo group [US].

### Postmarketing Experience (Frequency Not Established) [US]
- **Cardiac:** Palpitations, tachycardia, dyspnea.
- **Nervous System:** Dizziness, headache, migraines, paresthesia, psychomotor hyperactivity.
- **Psychiatric:** Anxiety, disorientation, insomnia, nightmares.
- **Gastrointestinal:** Abdominal pain, constipation, diarrhea, abdominal distension.
- **Ocular:** Blurred vision, visual disturbances.
- **Renal/Urinary:** Urinary retention, dysuria.
- **Skin:** Pruritus, rash (including maculo-papular), hyperhidrosis.
- **General:** Fatigue, irritability, malaise, chest discomfort.
- **Immune System:** Hypersensitivity.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **MAOIs:** Contraindicated; intensifies anticholinergic effects [US].
- **Alcohol and CNS Depressants:** Not recommended; increases risk of severe somnolence and CNS depression [US].
- **Food:** Delays absorption and reduces peak plasma concentrations (C$_{max}$) of both active ingredients [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Intended for use in pregnant women]. Extensive epidemiological data, including meta-analyses of cohort and case-control studies, have reported no increased risk of congenital malformations from first-trimester exposure [US]. 
- **Breastfeeding:** Use is not recommended. Doxylamine succinate is excreted in breast milk and may cause irritability, excitement, or sedation in nursing infants. Infants with apnea or respiratory conditions may be particularly vulnerable to these sedative effects. Pyridoxine is also excreted in breast milk [US].
- **Fertility:** Carcinogenicity studies in rats and mice for doxylamine suggest no likely human carcinogenic potential, but human fertility data is not specified in available sources [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness in children under 18 years of age have not been established. Fatalities have been reported in children following doxylamine overdose (toxic dose $>1.8$ mg/kg) [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources (indication is specific to pregnancy).
- **Renal Impairment:** No pharmacokinetic studies have been conducted in patients with renal impairment [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No pharmacokinetic studies have been conducted in patients with hepatic impairment [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** The exact mechanism for the treatment of NVP is unknown [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Doxylamine is an antihistamine with significant anticholinergic activity. Pyridoxine is a Vitamin B$_6$ analog [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** [US]
    - **Absorption:** Absorbed in the GI tract (mainly jejunum). C$_{max}$ is reached in approximately 7.5 hours for doxylamine and 5.5 hours for pyridoxine. 
    - **Distribution:** Pyridoxine is highly protein-bound (primarily to albumin).
    - **Metabolism:** Doxylamine is biotransformed in the liver via N-dealkylation. Pyridoxine is a prodrug primarily converted in the liver to active metabolites including pyridoxal 5’-phosphate (PLP).
    - **Elimination:** Doxylamine metabolites are excreted by the kidney. Terminal half-life: Doxylamine $\approx 12.5$ hours; Pyridoxine $\approx 0.5$ hours.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pivotal Study:** A double-blind, randomized, multi-center, placebo-controlled trial was conducted in 261 pregnant women (7–14 weeks gestation) with NVP [US].
- **Outcomes:** The primary endpoint was the change from baseline in the Pregnancy Unique-Quantification of Emesis (PUQE) score at Day 15. Treatment with doxylamine/pyridoxine resulted in a statistically significant improvement (mean decrease of 4.8 in PUQE score) compared to placebo (mean decrease of 3.9); p-value 0.006 [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F and 86°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Keep the bottle tightly closed and protect from moisture and light. Do not remove the desiccant from the bottle [US].
