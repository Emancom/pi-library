# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine Injection

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following regulatory jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, consolidating clinical and regulatory data into a single, concise reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI or local prescribing guidelines required for clinical practice in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule/Amino acid derivative.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter).
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Metabolic Disorders:** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD undergoing chronic hemodialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Pediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    - The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg, administered as a slow bolus injection (2–3 minutes) or by infusion. 
    - In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose is often followed by equivalent doses every 3 to 4 hours (and never less than every 6 hours) over the subsequent 24 hours. 
    - Subsequent daily doses typically remain at 50 mg/kg, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilised [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - The recommended starting dose is 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight.
    - Administration should occur as a slow (2–3 minute) bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
    - **Dose Adjustments:** Downward titration (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy, guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Inspect parenteral products for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration.
- **Compatibility:** Stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when diluted to concentrations between 0.5 mg/mL and 8.0 mg/mL in Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s [US].
- Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as the formulation contains no preservatives.

## 4. Contraindications
- None known at the time of publication [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Some reactions occurred within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and evaluate risks before re-administration [US].
- **Neurologic Effects (Seizures):** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In patients with a history of seizures, an increase in seizure frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **General Monitoring:** 
    - Obtain baseline plasma carnitine levels. 
    - Weekly and monthly monitoring should include blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L) [US].
- **Renal Insufficiency (Oral Formulation Note):** Safety/efficacy of oral levocarnitine has not been evaluated in renal impairment. Chronic high doses of the oral form in ESRD may lead to accumulation of toxic metabolites (TMA and TMAO) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse events reported in clinical trials for ESRD patients (frequency ≥5% regardless of causality) include:

| Body System | Adverse Event | Frequency (%) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Body as a Whole** | Injection site reaction, Pain, Flu syndrome, Headache, Infection, Abdominal pain | Common (>10%) |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension, Hypotension, Chest pain, Tachycardia | Common (>10%) |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea, Vomiting, Nausea | Common (>10%) |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness, Paresthesia | Common (>10%) |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis, Cough increase, Dyspnea, Rhinitis | Common (>10%) |

**Other Serious/Notable Effects:**
- **Neurologic:** Seizures (post-marketing).
- **Metabolic:** Hypercalcemia, hypervolemia [US].
- **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea, vomiting, and body odor have been observed. Gastritis is reported less frequently [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have been observed when co-administered with levocarnitine. **Management:** Monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not explicitly lettered, but noted as follows]: Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. There are no adequate/controlled studies in pregnant women. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine in milk after administration. Weigh the benefits of maternal supplementation against potential risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant; consider discontinuing nursing or the drug [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies revealed no evidence of impaired fertility [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders is based on mg/kg (see Section 3). Specific age-range limitations are not provided in the source [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Primarily indicated for ESRD patients on hemodialysis. Dosing is adjusted based on post-dialysis trough levels (target 40–50 µmol/L) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring carrier molecule required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (TMAO) and γ-butyrobetaine.
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of a single IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours (uncorrected for endogenous levels). Mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In ESRD patients, carnitine clears acylCoA compounds by forming acylcarnitine, which is rapidly excreted [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Study:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients receiving 20 mg/kg post-dialysis showed that trough concentrations reached between 54 µmol/L and 180 µmol/L (normal range: 40–50 µmol/L) after one week of therapy. 
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** Studies of 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses over 12 and 24 weeks demonstrated dose-dependent increases in trough carnitine concentrations.
- **Note:** While levocarnitine increases carnitine concentrations in ESRD, the effect on specific clinical outcomes or signs/symptoms of deficiency in this population has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Precautions:** Avoid excessive heat; protect from freezing [US].
- **Stability:** Discard any unused portion of the opened vial as it contains no preservatives.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Rx-only (Prescription Only). Poison schedule not applicable [US].
