# Unified Editorial Product Information: DaxibotulinumtoxinA

**Editorial Note:** This document represents a unified synthesis of Product Information (PI) and prescribing data for daxibotulinumtoxinA. It combines clinical data, safety warnings, and administration guidelines from the Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA) of Australia and the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) of the United States. Where regulatory guidance or safety labeling differs, the more comprehensive or conservative requirement has been included and labeled accordingly (e.g., [TGA] or [FDA]).

---

## 1. Product Name and Active Ingredient
**Product Name:** DAXXIFY®  
**Active Ingredient:** DaxibotulinumtoxinA (noted as daxibotulinumtoxinA-lanm in the United States [FDA]).

DAXXIFY is a sterile, vacuum-dried powder for injection. It contains a 150 kDa botulinum toxin type A, purified from *Clostridium botulinum*, formulated without human- or animal-derived serum albumin or accessory proteins. The active neurotoxin is stabilized by a proprietary 35-amino acid peptide (RTP004).

## 2. Approved Indications
DAXXIFY is indicated for:
*   **Glabellar Lines:** The temporary improvement in the appearance of moderate to severe glabellar lines (frown lines) associated with corrugator and/or procerus muscle activity in adult patients.
*   **Cervical Dystonia:** The treatment of cervical dystonia in adult patients to reduce the severity of abnormal head position and neck pain.

## 3. Contraindications
DAXXIFY is contraindicated in patients with:
*   Known hypersensitivity to daxibotulinumtoxinA or any of the excipients in the formulation (including the RTP004 peptide).
*   Active infection at the proposed injection site(s).

## 4. Warnings and Precautions
### Distant Spread of Toxin Effect
**Boxed Warning [FDA]:** Post-marketing reports indicate that the effects of all botulinum toxin products may spread from the area of injection to produce symptoms consistent with botulinum toxin effects. These may include asthenia, generalized muscle weakness, diplopia, blurred vision, ptosis, dysphagia, dysphonia, dysarthria, urinary incontinence, and breathing difficulties. These symptoms can be life-threatening and have resulted in death. The risk is greatest in children treated for spasticity, but symptoms can occur in adults, particularly those with underlying conditions that predispose them to these symptoms.

### Lack of Interchangeability
The potency units of DAXXIFY are specific to the preparation and assay method utilized. They are not interchangeable with other botulinum toxin products (e.g., onabotulinumtoxinA, abobotulinumtoxinA). Dosage units cannot be compared or converted between different brands.

### Serious Adverse Reactions with Unapproved Use
Serious reactions, including aspiration pneumonia and fatal outcomes, have been reported when botulinum toxins are used for unapproved indications (e.g., spasticity in children or adults) or at inappropriate injection sites.

### Neuromuscular Disorders
Patients with peripheral motor neuropathic diseases (e.g., amyotrophic lateral sclerosis) or neuromuscular junctional disorders (e.g., myasthenia gravis or Lambert-Eaton syndrome) should be monitored closely. These patients are at increased risk for severe dysphagia and respiratory compromise when receiving botulinum toxin.

### Ophthalmic and Facial Anatomy
*   **Dry Eye:** Reduced tear production and blinking may lead to corneal disorders. If symptoms persist, referral to an ophthalmologist is recommended.
*   **Anatomy:** Use caution in patients with marked facial asymmetry, deep scarring, or prior surgical alterations to facial anatomy.

## 5. Dosage and Administration
DAXXIFY must only be administered by qualified healthcare professionals with the appropriate expertise in the use of botulinum toxins.

### General Dosing Principles
*   Dosing intervals should be no more frequent than every three months.
*   **Glabellar Lines:** The recommended total dose is 40 Units, administered as five equal intramuscular injections of 8 Units each (two in each corrugator muscle and one in the procerus).
*   **Cervical Dystonia:** The recommended dose range is 125 Units to 250 Units, administered as a divided dose among the affected neck muscles. Dosing should be individualized based on the patient's head/neck position, muscle hypertrophy, and prior response to botulinum toxins.

### Reconstitution Instructions
Reconstitute only with sterile 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection.
*   **For 40 Units (Glabellar):** Reconstitute a 100 Unit vial with 1.2 mL of diluent to achieve 8 Units per 0.1 mL.
*   **For Cervical Dystonia:** Reconstitute a 100 Unit vial with 1.0 mL (10 Units/0.1 mL) or 2.0 mL (5 Units/0.1 mL) depending on the required concentration.

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions typically occur within the first week following injection and are generally transient.

*   **Glabellar Lines:** The most common reactions (incidence ≥1%) include headache (9%), nasopharyngitis (4%), injection site pain (4%), and eyelid ptosis (2%). Other reported effects include injection site erythema, edema, and bruising. 
*   **Cervical Dystonia:** Common reactions (incidence ≥5%) include headache, injection site pain, muscular weakness, musculoskeletal pain, and upper respiratory tract infection.
*   **Dysphagia:** In cervical dystonia trials, dysphagia occurred in up to 3.9% of patients [TGA].
*   **Laboratory Abnormalities:** Prolonged prothrombin time was noted in a small percentage of subjects (0.5%) in some trials [TGA].

## 7. Drug Interactions
No formal drug interaction studies have been performed. However, the effect of daxibotulinumtoxinA may be potentiated by:
*   Aminoglycosides or other agents interfering with neuromuscular transmission.
*   Anticholinergic drugs.
*   Muscle relaxants.
*   Other botulinum neurotoxin products administered concurrently or within close proximity.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
### Pregnancy
**Pregnancy Category B3 [TGA]:** There are no adequate and well-controlled studies in pregnant women. Animal studies in rats and rabbits have shown decreased fetal body weight and skeletal ossification at maternally toxic doses. Use during pregnancy is not recommended unless the potential benefit justifies the risk to the fetus.

### Breastfeeding
It is unknown whether daxibotulinumtoxinA is excreted in human milk. Caution should be exercised, and the use of DAXXIFY during lactation is generally not recommended.

## 9. Pharmacokinetics
Using current analytical technologies, daxibotulinumtoxinA is not detectable in the peripheral blood following intramuscular injection at recommended doses. The toxin is expected to undergo local uptake by nerve terminals followed by slow degradation.

## 10. Mechanism of Action
DaxibotulinumtoxinA is an acetylcholine release inhibitor and neuromuscular blocking agent. The toxin’s heavy chain binds to the presynaptic surface of cholinergic neurons. After internalization, the catalytic light chain is released into the cytosol, where it cleaves SNAP-25 (Synaptosomal-Associated Protein 25). This cleavage prevents the docking of synaptic vesicles with the nerve membrane, inhibiting the release of acetylcholine and resulting in temporary muscle paralysis. Muscular function gradually returns as the nerve terminals sprout and the original neuromuscular junction recovers.

## 11. Storage Conditions
### Unopened Vials
*   Store at room temperature (20°C to 25°C) or in a refrigerator (2°C to 8°C).
*   Keep in the original carton to protect from light.
*   Shelf life: 36 months [TGA].

### Reconstituted Solution
*   Reconstituted DAXXIFY should be used as soon as possible.
*   It may be stored under refrigeration (2°C to 8°C) for up to 72 hours.
*   Do not freeze the reconstituted solution. Discard any unused portion after 72 hours.
