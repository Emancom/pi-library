# Editorial Product Information: Dexchlorpheniramine Maleate and Dextromethorphan Hydrobromide

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA). 
- **Purpose:** This document is a harmonised editorial synthesis of provided Product Information (PI) for healthcare professionals. It aims to provide a single, coherent reference for the clinical use of the combination of dexchlorpheniramine maleate and dextromethorphan hydrobromide.
- **Note:** This editorial synthesis is based on available regulatory documentation and does not replace the official PI or "Drug Facts" required for prescribing or dispensing in a specific jurisdiction. At the time of writing, only United States (FDA) documentation was provided for this specific combination product.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** 
    - **Dexchlorpheniramine Maleate:** An alkylamine derivative antihistamine.
    - **Dextromethorphan Hydrobromide:** A non-opioid antitussive (d-isomer of levorphanol).
- **Brand Names:** **Poly-Hist DM** [US].
- **ATC Code:** R05DA20 (Dextromethorphan, combinations); R06AB02 (Dexchlorpheniramine).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - **Oral Liquid:** Each 5 mL (one teaspoonful) contains 1 mg dexchlorpheniramine maleate and 10 mg dextromethorphan hydrobromide [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Poly Pharmaceuticals, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Allergic Rhinitis and Upper Respiratory Allergy Relief:** For the temporary relief of runny nose, sneezing, and itching of the nose or throat, and itchy, watery eyes due to hay fever or other respiratory allergies [US].
- **Common Cold Symptom Relief:** Temporary relief of cough due to minor throat and bronchial irritation, and runny nose/sneezing associated with the common cold [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Adults and Children (12 years of age and over):** 10 mL (2 teaspoonfuls) administered every 4 hours. Do not exceed 60 mL (12 teaspoonfuls) in a 24-hour period [US].
- **Children (6 to under 12 years of age):** 5 mL (1 teaspoonful) administered every 4 hours. Do not exceed 30 mL (6 teaspoonfuls) in a 24-hour period [US].
- **Children (under 6 years of age):** Consult a physician prior to use [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Dosing should be measured with an appropriate calibrated device.
- May be taken with or without food.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors (MAOIs):** Use is contraindicated in patients currently taking a prescription MAOI (for depression, psychiatric/emotional conditions, or Parkinson’s disease), or for 2 weeks after discontinuing the MAOI [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **General Respiratory Precautions:** Patients should consult a physician before use if they have a chronic or persistent cough (e.g., from smoking, asthma, emphysema) or if the cough is accompanied by excessive phlegm [US].
- **Underlying Medical Conditions:** Caution is advised, and a physician should be consulted prior to use in patients with:
    - Glaucoma [US].
    - Urinary retention/prostate enlargement [US].
    - Cardiovascular disease or high blood pressure [US].
    - Thyroid disease [US].
    - Diabetes [US].
- **CNS Depression:** May cause marked drowsiness. Patients should avoid alcoholic beverages and use caution when driving a motor vehicle or operating heavy machinery [US].
- **Pediatric Safety:** Excitability may occur, particularly in children [US].
- **Monitoring/Discontinuation:** Use should be stopped and a physician consulted if:
    - Cough or nasal congestion persists for more than 1 week or tends to recur.
    - Symptoms are accompanied by fever, rash, or persistent headache.
    - Nervousness, dizziness, or sleeplessness occur.

## 6. Adverse Effects
*Note: Frequencies are not explicitly defined in the provided OTC labeling.*

- **Neurological/Psychiatric:** Marked drowsiness, dizziness, nervousness, sleeplessness, and excitability (especially in pediatric patients) [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Persistent cough may indicate a serious underlying condition [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors (MAOIs):** Concurrent use or use within 14 days of stopping an MAOI is contraindicated due to the risk of serotonin syndrome or severe hypertensive crisis [US].
- **CNS Depressants:** Alcohol, sedatives, and tranquilizers may increase the sedative/drowsiness effects of dexchlorpheniramine [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Not specified in available sources beyond the requirement to consult a healthcare professional before use [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Not specified in available sources beyond the requirement to consult a healthcare professional before use [US].
- **Fertility:** Not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** 
    - 6 to 12 years: See Section 3 for specific weight/age dosing.
    - Under 6 years: Physician consultation required [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in the source document; however, general precautions regarding cardiovascular disease and glaucoma are highly relevant to this population.
- **Renal Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** 
    - **Dexchlorpheniramine Maleate:** Competitively inhibits H1-receptor sites on effector cells [US: categorized as an Antihistamine].
    - **Dextromethorphan Hydrobromide:** Acts centrally on the cough center in the medulla to raise the cough threshold [US: categorized as a Cough Suppressant/Antitussive].
- **Pharmacokinetics/Pharmacodynamics:** Not specified in available source documents.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 15°–30°C (59°–86°F) [US].
- **Safety:** Tamper-evident seal under cap must be intact. Keep out of reach of children [US].
