# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
*   **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA). 
*   **Purpose:** This document is a harmonised editorial synthesis of official Product Information (PI) intended for use by healthcare professionals. It provides a structured overview of the clinical profile, dosing, and safety of levocarnitine.
*   **Note:** This editorial is a synthesis for informational purposes and is not a substitute for the official, legally approved Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
*   **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule/amino acid derivative.
*   **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
*   **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
*   **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    *   **Injection:** 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) single-dose vials [US].
*   **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US-approved].
*   **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD undergoing dialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
*   **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg. It should be administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion [US].
    *   **Severe Metabolic Crisis:** A loading dose is often given, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours.
    *   **Frequency:** Dosing should occur every 3 or 4 hours and never less than every 6 hours [US].
    *   **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses are recommended at 50 mg/kg or as clinically required. The maximum recorded dose is 300 mg/kg [US].

*   **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:** 
    *   **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
    *   **Administration:** Administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].
    *   **Dose Adjustment:** Adjustments should be guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].

### Administration Instructions
*   Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration [US].
*   **Monitoring:** It is recommended to obtain plasma carnitine concentrations prior to starting therapy. Weekly and monthly monitoring should include blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma free carnitine concentrations (target: 35 to 60 µmol/L) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
*   None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### General Warnings
*   **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients on dialysis. Reactions may occur within minutes of intravenous administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and initiate medical management [US].
*   **Neurologic Reactions (Seizures):** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In patients with known seizure disorders, an increase in seizure frequency or severity may occur [US].

### Specific Clinical Precautions
*   **Oral Formulation in Renal Impairment:** The safety and efficacy of oral levocarnitine have not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in patients with severely compromised renal function or ESRD may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites, trimethylamine (TMA) and trimethylamine-N-oxide (TMAO) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Clinical Trials Experience (ESRD Patients)
Adverse events reported in double-blind, placebo-controlled trials at a frequency ≥5% (regardless of causality) [US]:

| Body System | Adverse Event (Incidence ≥5%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Chest pain, injection site reaction, pain, flu syndrome, headache, infection, asthenia, abdominal pain, fever, accidental injury |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension, hypotension, tachycardia |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea, vomiting, nausea, dyspepsia, anorexia |
| **Metabolic/Nutritional** | Hypercalcemia, hyperkalemia, hypervolemia, peripheral edema, weight changes |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness, paresthesia, insomnia, depression |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis, cough increase, dyspnea, rhinitis |
| **Musculoskeletal** | Leg cramps |
| **Skin** | Pruritus |

### Serious/Other Notable Effects
*   **Common (>10%):** Transient nausea and vomiting [US].
*   **Less Frequent:** Body odor, gastritis [US].
*   **Post-marketing:** Seizures, anaphylaxis [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have been observed when used concurrently with levocarnitine. **Management:** Monitor INR levels after initiating levocarnitine or after dose adjustments in patients on warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy:** [US Category C]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (BSA basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. There are no adequate, well-controlled studies in pregnant women. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
*   **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk concentrations of carnitine after exogenous administration. Risks to the child must be weighed against the benefit of supplementation to the mother [US].
*   **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility was found in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders follows mg/kg adult recommendations. In premature infants and newborns, secondary deficiency is defined as plasma concentrations below age-related normal levels [US].
*   **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Renal Impairment:** See *Section 3 (ESRD Dosing)* and *Section 5 (Precautions regarding oral formulation)*. Levocarnitine is easily removed by dialysis [US].
*   **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production [US].

### Pharmacokinetics
*   **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin. Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours [US].
*   **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
*   **Elimination:** Primarily renal. Following a single IV bolus (20 mg/kg), approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. Mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].
*   **Bioavailability:** Absolute bioavailability of oral formulations is approximately 15–16% [US].

### Pharmacodynamics
In ESRD patients, levocarnitine clears accumulated toxic acyl-CoA compounds by forming acylcarnitine, which is rapidly excreted [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   **Pharmacokinetic Studies:** In ESRD patients receiving 20 mg/kg three times weekly, trough concentrations increased from a baseline of ~21 µmol/L to ~190 µmol/L after 8 weeks of therapy [US].
*   **Dose-Ranging Studies:** Trials using 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses showed dose-proportional increases in trough levocarnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F). Protect from freezing [US].
*   **Handling:** Discard any unused portion of an opened vial; the formulation contains no preservative.
*   **Compatibility:** Compatible and stable for 24 hours at room temperature in PVC bags when mixed with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) [US].
