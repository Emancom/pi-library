# Editorial Product Information: Dimethicone

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of dimethicone (specifically Mederma PM) for healthcare professionals, synthesising regulatory information into a single reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) or summary of product characteristics required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Dimethicone (20 mg per 1 g)
- **Chemical Class:** Silicone-based polymer; skin protectant.
- **Brand Name(s):** Mederma PM [US]
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Topical cream (2%) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** PMI Branded Pharmaceuticals, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Skin Protection:** Temporary protection and relief of chapped or cracked skin [US].
- **Scar Management:** Visibly reducing the appearance of scars [US-marketed use].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Topical Application:** Apply to the affected area as needed [US].

### Indication-Specific Instructions
- **Scar Management:** For optimal results, application should begin as soon as the wound has closed. Recommended duration of use is 8 weeks for new scars and 3 to 6 months for existing scars [US].
- **Nighttime Use:** Specifically formulated for application once nightly to align with the skin's nocturnal regenerative cycle [US].

### Administration Instructions
- For external use only. 

## 4. Contraindications
- **Deep or Punctured Wounds:** Use is contraindicated in deep tissue injuries [US].
- **Animal Bites:** Not for use on skin breaks caused by animals [US].
- **Serious Burns:** Contraindicated for use on severe thermal or chemical injuries [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **General Precautions:** For external use only. Avoid contact with the eyes [US].
- **Monitoring Requirements:** Patients should discontinue use and consult a healthcare professional if the skin condition worsens or if symptoms persist for more than 7 days [US].
- **Recurrent Symptoms:** Medical advice should be sought if symptoms clear up and then recur within a few days [US].
- **Accidental Ingestion:** Keep out of reach of children. In case of accidental ingestion, immediate medical intervention or contact with a Poison Control Center is required [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **General Profile:** Specific adverse effect frequencies (e.g., common, rare) are not detailed in the available US OTC source.
- **Clinical Cautions:** The requirement to stop use if the condition worsens suggests a potential for localised irritation or hypersensitivity in sensitive individuals [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources. Dimethicone is a topically applied inert polymer with minimal systemic absorption, reducing the likelihood of systemic drug interactions.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Use is permitted under the same "apply as needed" directions, though strict precautions regarding accidental ingestion must be maintained [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Dimethicone functions as a skin protectant by forming a physical barrier on the stratum corneum. This barrier helps prevent water loss and protects damaged skin from external irritants [US]. 
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Not specified; typically considered non-absorbed.
    - **Metabolism/Elimination:** Not specified.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** The formulation is designed to support the skin’s nighttime regenerative activity to improve scar softness and texture [US].
