## 3. Approved Indications
- Relieves occasional constipation (irregularity).
- Generally produces a bowel movement in 6 to 12 hours after administration.
- No additional or alternative indications are specified in the available sources.

## 4. Dosage and Administration
### General dosing (by tablet count and per-tablet strength)
- Each tablet: docusate sodium 50 mg + sennosides 8.6 mg.
- Adults and children 12 years and older: 2–4 tablets daily (equivalent to docusate sodium 100–200 mg and sennosides 17.2–34.4 mg daily, given tablet counts).
- Children 6 to under 12 years: 1–2 tablets daily.
- Children 2 to under 6 years: Up to 1 tablet daily.
- Children under 2 years: Ask a doctor before use.

### Administration instructions
- Take only by mouth.
- Doses may be taken as a single daily dose, preferably in the evening, or in divided doses.
- Follow specific product labelling directions for administration timing and dosing.

## 5. Contraindications
- Do not use laxative products for longer than 1 week unless instructed by a doctor.
- Do not use while taking mineral oil unless directed by a doctor.
- No additional contraindications are explicitly listed in the available sources.

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
- **Do not exceed 1 week of use without medical advice.**
- Ask a doctor before use if the patient has:
  - Stomach pain, nausea, or vomiting.
  - Noticed a sudden change in bowel habits that lasts over 2 weeks.
- Stop use and ask a doctor if:
  - There is rectal bleeding.
  - There is failure to have a bowel movement after use of a laxative.
  - These findings may indicate a serious condition requiring medical evaluation.
- Pregnancy and breastfeeding:
  - If pregnant or breastfeeding, ask a health professional before use.
- Keep out of reach of children.
- In case of overdose, obtain medical help or contact a Poison Control Center immediately.
- Monitoring requirements:
  - Discontinue and seek medical evaluation for rectal bleeding, persistent constipation despite treatment, or other concerning gastrointestinal symptoms.
- Specific clinical precautions:
  - Use with mineral oil only if specifically directed by a prescriber (see Contraindications/Interactions).
  - Use in children under 2 years requires physician consultation.
