# Editorial Product Information: Doxercalciferol

## Editorial Note
*   **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA).
*   **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of doxercalciferol, synthesised for use by healthcare professionals. It integrates clinical data, regulatory requirements, and safety information into a single reference.
*   **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult the current official PI from their respective regulatory authority before prescribing.

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredient:** Doxercalciferol (synthetic vitamin $D_2$ analog).
*   **Chemical Class:** Vitamin D analog ($1\alpha$-hydroxyvitamin $D_2$).
*   **Brand Names:** Noted in provided sources as doxercalciferol capsules (generic).
*   **ATC Code:** H05BX01.
*   **Dosage Forms and Strengths:**
    *   **Capsules:** 0.5 mcg, 1 mcg, and 2.5 mcg [US].
*   **Route of Administration:** Oral.
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** RemedyRepack Inc. (Repackager); Avet Pharmaceuticals Inc. (Manufacturer) [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Secondary Hyperparathyroidism:** Indicated for the treatment of secondary hyperparathyroidism in adult patients with Stage 3 or Stage 4 chronic kidney disease (CKD) and adult patients with CKD on dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
Before initiation, serum calcium levels must be confirmed to be within the normal range (not above the upper limit of normal) [US].

### Dosing by Indication
*   **CKD Stage 3 or 4:**
    *   **Initial Dose:** 1 mcg orally once daily [US].
    *   **Titration:** May increase the dose by 0.5 mcg at 2-week intervals to achieve target intact parathyroid hormone (iPTH) levels [US].
    *   **Maximum Dose:** 3.5 mcg daily [US].
*   **CKD on Dialysis:**
    *   **Initial Dose:** 10 mcg orally three times weekly during dialysis (no more frequently than every other day) [US].
    *   **Titration:** May increase the dose by 2.5 mcg at 8-week intervals to achieve target iPTH levels [US].
    *   **Maximum Dose:** 20 mcg three times weekly (60 mcg/week total) [US].

### Administration and Titration Instructions
*   **Targeting:** Maintenance doses should be adjusted based on iPTH levels while ensuring serum calcium remains within normal limits [US].
*   **Dose Suspension:** If iPTH levels become persistently and abnormally low, or if serum calcium is consistently above the normal range, the dose should be reduced or suspended [US].
*   **Resumption after Suspension:** Restart therapy after one week at a dose at least 0.5 mcg lower (Stage 3/4) or 2.5 mcg lower (Dialysis) than the previous dose [US].

### Monitoring
*   **CKD Stage 3/4:** Monitor serum calcium, phosphorus, and iPTH at least every two weeks for the first 3 months of therapy or following dose adjustments; then monthly for 3 months, and every 3 months thereafter [US].
*   **CKD on Dialysis:** Monitor serum calcium, phosphorus, and iPTH frequently (e.g., weekly) during initiation or dose adjustment [US].

## 4. Contraindications
*   Hypercalcemia [US].
*   Vitamin D toxicity [US].
*   Known hypersensitivity to doxercalciferol or any inactive ingredients (e.g., BHA, ethanol) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
*   **Hypercalcemia:** Treatment can cause hypercalcemia, which may lead to cardiac arrhythmias and seizures. Acute hypercalcemia can potentiate digitalis toxicity. Chronic hypercalcemia can cause generalized vascular and soft-tissue calcification [US].
*   **Digitalis Toxicity:** Hypercalcemia increases the risk of digitalis-induced arrhythmias. Monitor serum calcium and signs of digitalis toxicity closely when initiating or adjusting doxercalciferol in patients on digitalis [US].
*   **Serious Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Anaphylaxis, angioedema, hypotension, and cardiopulmonary arrest have been reported, particularly in hemodialysis patients. Discontinue immediately if a reaction occurs [US].
*   **Adynamic Bone Disease:** Oversuppression of iPTH to abnormally low levels may lead to adynamic bone disease and increased fracture risk. Monitor iPTH levels and adjust dosage accordingly [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **CYP450 Inhibitors (e.g., ketoconazole, erythromycin):** May hinder the formation of the active doxercalciferol moiety; dosage adjustment and close monitoring of iPTH and calcium may be required [US].
*   **Enzyme Inducers (e.g., phenobarbital, glutethimide):** May affect the activation of doxercalciferol, requiring dosage adjustment [US].
*   **Magnesium-containing Products (e.g., antacids):** Combined use may cause hypermagnesemia; avoid in patients on chronic renal dialysis [US].
*   **Cholestyramine & Mineral Oil:** May impair the intestinal absorption of doxercalciferol. Administer doxercalciferol at least 1 hour before or 4 to 6 hours after these substances [US].
*   **Thiazide Diuretics:** Known to induce hypercalcemia by reducing urinary calcium excretion; use with caution [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not specified under current labeling rules; however, data are insufficient to identify a drug-associated risk for major birth defects or miscarriage.] Animal studies in rats showed no adverse effects at 25 times the human dose, though rabbits showed risk at lower multiples. CKD in pregnancy itself increases risks for hypertension and preterm delivery [US].
*   **Breastfeeding:** No data available on the presence of doxercalciferol in human milk. Monitor infants for signs of hypercalcemia if the mother is treated while breastfeeding [US].
*   **Fertility:** No effect on male or female fertility was observed in rat studies at doses up to 3 times the maximum human dose [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy have not been established in paediatric patients with CKD Stage 3, 4, or on dialysis. A 12-week trial in patients aged 5 to 17 years failed to demonstrate effectiveness [US].
*   **Elderly Use:** Clinical studies did not include sufficient numbers of subjects aged 65 and over to determine if they respond differently. Dosing should be cautious, generally starting at the low end of the range [US].
*   **Hepatic Impairment:** Patients with hepatic impairment may not metabolize doxercalciferol (a pro-drug) into its active form efficiently. Frequent monitoring of iPTH, calcium, and phosphorus is required [US].
*   **Renal Impairment:** Doxercalciferol activation does not require the kidneys; however, the drug is primarily indicated for renal populations (CKD Stage 3/4 and Dialysis) [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
Doxercalciferol is a synthetic pro-drug that requires metabolic activation to form $1\alpha,25-(OH)_2D_2$, a biologically active vitamin D metabolite. This metabolite binds to the vitamin D receptor (VDR), leading to the selective inhibition of PTH synthesis and secretion [US].

### Pharmacokinetics
*   **Absorption:** Peak blood levels of the major metabolite are reached 8 hours after IV administration and 11 to 12 hours after oral capsule administration [US].
*   **Metabolism:** Activated in the liver by CYP27 to $1\alpha,25-(OH)_2D_2$ (major metabolite) and $1\alpha,24-(OH)_2D_2$ (minor metabolite). It does not require renal activation [US].
*   **Elimination:** The mean elimination half-life is approximately 32 to 37 hours, with a range up to 96 hours [US]. It is not removed by dialysis [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   **CKD Stage 3/4:** Two double-blind, 24-week studies showed that 74% of doxercalciferol-treated patients achieved a $\geq 30\%$ reduction in iPTH from baseline, compared to 7% in the placebo group [US].
*   **CKD on Dialysis:** Two 16-week open-label studies followed by 8-week double-blind periods demonstrated that over 94% of patients achieved significant iPTH reduction. Treatment maintained iPTH suppression, whereas the placebo group saw iPTH return to baseline levels [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   **Temperature:** Store at 25°C (77°F).
*   **Excursions:** Permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
*   **Packaging:** Keep in original container; repackaged versions may be supplied in pouches or boxes [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
*   **United States:** Rx-only. Poison schedule not applicable in the US system under the same terminology as other regions.
