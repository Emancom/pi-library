# Editorial Product Information: Donanemab

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **Australian (TGA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of donanemab for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises clinical data, dosing protocols, and safety information into a single reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific PI. Prescribers must consult the current approved PI in their specific region before initiating therapy.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Donanemab (recombinant monoclonal humanised IgG1 antibody).
- **Chemical Class:** Anti-amyloid monoclonal antibody.
- **Brand Name(s):** **KISUNLA** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Concentrated solution for intravenous (IV) infusion; 350 mg/20 mL (17.5 mg/mL) single-dose vial [Australian].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous infusion.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Eli Lilly Australia Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Early Symptomatic Alzheimer’s Disease:** Indicated for the treatment of patients with Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI) or mild Alzheimer’s dementia [Australian].
- **Genetic Restriction:** Approved only for patients who are **apolipoprotein E ε4 (ApoE ε4) heterozygotes or non-carriers**. Use is specifically **not indicated** in ApoE ε4 homozygous patients [Australian].
- **Diagnostic Requirement:** Evidence of beta-amyloid pathology consistent with Alzheimer's disease must be confirmed via validated testing (e.g., PET scan) prior to treatment [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
Donanemab follows a weight-independent titration schedule administered every four weeks:
- **Infusion 1:** 350 mg
- **Infusion 2:** 700 mg
- **Infusion 3:** 1050 mg
- **Infusion 4 and beyond:** 1400 mg (Maintenance dose)

### Administration Instructions
- **Preparation:** Must be diluted in 0.9% sodium chloride to a final concentration of 4 mg/mL to 10 mg/mL [Australian].
- **Infusion Rate:** Administered over approximately 30 minutes.
- **Post-Infusion:** Patients should be observed for a minimum of 30 minutes for infusion-related reactions.
- **Treatment Duration:** Treatment should continue until amyloid plaques are cleared (confirmed by validated method) or up to a maximum of 18 months if clearance monitoring is unavailable. Reassess benefit-risk if the patient progresses to moderate Alzheimer's disease [Australian].

### Monitoring Requirements
- **Mandatory MRI:** A baseline brain MRI (within 6 months) is required. Follow-up MRIs are mandatory prior to the 2nd, 3rd, 4th, and 7th doses to monitor for Amyloid-Related Imaging Abnormalities (ARIA) [Australian].

### Missed Dose
- Administer the missed dose as soon as possible, then resume the 4-week cycle based on the date of the last infusion [Australian].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to donanemab or any excipients.
- **Baseline MRI Findings:** 
    - Evidence of prior intracerebral haemorrhage (ICH) >1 cm.
    - More than 2 microhaemorrhages.
    - Superficial siderosis.
    - Vasogenic oedema (ARIA-E) suggestive of cerebral amyloid angiopathy (CAA).
- **Severe White Matter Disease.**
- **Physical Barrier:** Any condition preventing satisfactory MRI monitoring [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warnings [Australian]
- **ARIA Risk:** Donanemab can cause Amyloid-Related Imaging Abnormalities (ARIA-E and ARIA-H). These are usually asymptomatic but can be serious, life-threatening, or fatal. 
- **ApoE ε4 Status:** Testing for ApoE ε4 status is mandatory. Homozygotes have a significantly higher risk of severe ARIA and are excluded from the current indication.

### General Warnings
- **ARIA Management:** If ARIA is detected, dosing may need to be suspended or permanently discontinued based on radiographic severity and clinical symptoms (refer to specific MRI classification criteria).
- **Thrombolytics/Anticoagulants:** Exercise extreme caution when considering thrombolytic therapy (e.g., tPA) in a patient treated with donanemab due to the risk of ICH. Fatalities have occurred in this context [Australian].
- **Infusion-Related Reactions (IRR):** IRRs, including anaphylaxis, typically occur during or within 30 minutes of infusion. Discontinue immediately if serious reactions occur.

### Specific Precautions
- **Controlled Access:** In Australia, initiation must occur through a central registration system/controlled access programme.

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Frequency Data
| Adverse Reaction | Frequency [Australian] |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | ARIA-E (24.4%), ARIA-H (including microhaemorrhage/haemosiderin) (31.3%), Headache (13.1%), Fall (13.3%) |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Infusion-related reactions (8.5%), Superficial siderosis (7.7%), Dizziness (6.5%), Nausea (5.2%), Arthralgia (6.0%) |
| **Uncommon (0.1–1%)** | Anaphylaxis (0.3%), Serious ARIA-E (1.5%), Serious ARIA-H (0.4%) |

### Serious Adverse Effects
- **Intracerebral Haemorrhage:** Reported in 0.3% of treated patients vs 0.2% in placebo [Australian].
- **Symptomatic ARIA-E:** Characterised by headache, confusion, nausea, and seizure.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** No formal drug interaction studies have been performed. No PK interactions are expected for this monoclonal antibody [Australian].
- **Antithrombotics:** Patients on concomitant antiplatelets or anticoagulants did not show a statistically increased frequency of ARIA in trials, but limited exposure to non-aspirin agents prevents definitive conclusions. Use with caution [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** **Category B2** [Australian]. Donanemab is not recommended during pregnancy. IgG1 antibodies are known to cross the placenta, especially during the third trimester.
- **Breastfeeding:** Safety is unknown. Human IgG is excreted in milk; the risks to the infant are not established.
- **Fertility:** No animal or human data available [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Not established; no relevant use in this population [Australian].
- **Elderly Use:** No specific dose adjustment required; however, trials did not include enough younger adults to determine if those >65 respond differently.
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment necessary based on population PK analysis [Australian].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment necessary [Australian].
- **Body Weight:** Influences clearance and distribution but does not necessitate dose adjustment [Australian].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Donanemab targets the N-terminal truncated form of amyloid beta (N3pE Aβ), which is found only in brain amyloid plaques. It induces plaque removal via microglial-mediated phagocytosis.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Central volume of distribution is 3.36 L.
    - **Metabolism:** Degraded into small peptides and amino acids via catabolic pathways (like endogenous IgG).
    - **Elimination:** Half-life is approximately 12.1 days; clearance is 0.0255 L/h [Australian].

## 11. Clinical Trials
### TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 2 (Phase 3)
- **Design:** Double-blind, placebo-controlled study of 1,736 patients with early symptomatic AD and amyloid/tau pathology.
- **Outcomes:** Donanemab significantly slowed clinical decline. In the low-medium tau population, decline was slowed by 35% on the iADRS scale and 36% on the CDR-SB scale at 76 weeks compared to placebo [Australian].
- **Plaque Clearance:** 76.4% of the combined population achieved amyloid clearance by week 76.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Unopened Vials:** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C. Keep in the outer carton to protect from light. Do not freeze or shake.
- **Stability:** May be stored unrefrigerated for up to 3 days at 20°C to 25°C.
- **Diluted Solution:** Recommended for immediate use. Stable for up to 72 hours at 2°C to 8°C or 12 hours at room temperature [Australian].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule:** **Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine)** [Australian].
- **Monitoring Status:** Subject to additional monitoring (Black Triangle Scheme) [Australian].
