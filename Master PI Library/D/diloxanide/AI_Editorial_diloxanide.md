# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial synthesis is derived from the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical and regulatory data into a structured format.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information approved in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult the local approved PI for prescribing requirements in their region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN).
- **Chemical Class:** A naturally occurring carrier molecule involved in mammalian energy metabolism.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter Healthcare Corporation) [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources (A16AA01).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):**
    - **Intravenous Injection:** 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) single-dose vial [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency in Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Indicated for the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that lead to secondary carnitine deficiency [US-approved].
- **Carnitine Deficiency in End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    - Recommended dose is **50 mg/kg** administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion [US]. 
    - **Loading Dose:** Often administered in patients with severe metabolic crisis, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours.
    - **Frequency:** Should be administered every 3 or 4 hours (never less than every 6 hours) [US].
    - **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses are typically in the range of 50 mg/kg, though higher doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilised [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
    - **Administration:** Administered as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line after each dialysis session [US].
    - **Dose Adjustments:** Should be guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter or discoloration before use.
- **Compatibility:** Stable when mixed in Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations of 0.5 mg/mL to 8.0 mg/mL. Stable for up to 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known:** There are no known contraindications for levocarnitine reported in the provided source [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have occurred, often within minutes of administration. These are most frequently reported in ESRD patients [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with known seizure disorders, an increase in frequency or severity of seizures may occur [US].
- **Monitoring:** 
    - It is recommended to obtain plasma carnitine concentrations prior to starting parenteral therapy.
    - Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma carnitine (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L) is advised [US].
- **Renal Impairment (Oral vs. IV):** The safety of *oral* levocarnitine has not been evaluated in renal insufficiency; chronic high-dose oral administration in ESRD may lead to accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse events observed in clinical trials of ESRD patients on hemodialysis (incidence ≥5%) included:

| Body System | Adverse Event (Incidence ≥5%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Abdominal pain, accidental injury, asthenia, chest pain, fever, flu syndrome, headache, infection, injection site reaction, pain |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension, hypotension, palpitations, tachycardia |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea, nausea, vomiting, anorexia, dyspepsia |
| **Metabolic/Nutritional** | Hypercalcemia, hyperkalemia, hypervolemia, peripheral edema, weight change |
| **Nervous System** | Dizziness, insomnia, paresthesia, depression |
| **Respiratory** | Cough increase, dyspnea, pharyngitis, rhinitis |

- **Serious/Post-marketing Effects:** Seizures, anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm [US].
- **Common GI effects:** Transient nausea and vomiting. Body odor and gastritis are reported less frequently [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalised Ratio (INR) have been observed when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels in patients on warfarin after initiating levocarnitine or following dose adjustments [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Category [US]:** Not specifically assigned a letter, but reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm.
    - **Recommendation:** Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed, as animal studies are not always predictive of human response [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. While studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine in milk, the risks to the child must be weighed against the benefits to the mother [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** See **Section 3: Dosage and Administration** for metabolic disorder dosing.
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Specifically indicated for ESRD patients on dialysis. High-dose *oral* therapy is cautioned against due to metabolite accumulation (TMA/TMAO) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a carrier molecule required for the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, facilitating oxidation and energy production.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In patients with inborn errors of metabolism, levocarnitine promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine complexes, which are rapidly excreted.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not protein-bound.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces).
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours (uncorrected for endogenous levels). Mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Study:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients receiving 20 mg/kg IV three times weekly post-dialysis showed that trough levels reached a steady state (54–180 µmol/L) within one week [US].
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** Studies at 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg in ESRD patients showed dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [US].
- **Note:** Clinical outcomes (signs/symptoms) in the ESRD population related to supplementation have not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C [US].
- **Precautions:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat.
- **Shelf Life:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as it contains no preservative [US].
