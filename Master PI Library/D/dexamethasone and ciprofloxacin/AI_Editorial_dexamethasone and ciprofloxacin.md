# Editorial Product Information: Ciprofloxacin and Dexamethasone Otic Suspension

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA)
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of the combination product containing ciprofloxacin 0.3% and dexamethasone 0.1% otic suspension, designed for use by healthcare professionals.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. At the time of compilation, only the United States Product Information was available for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** Ciprofloxacin hydrochloride (fluoroquinolone antibacterial) and Dexamethasone (corticosteroid).
- **Brand Names:** Ciprofloxacin and Dexamethasone Otic Suspension [US].
- **ATC Code:** S02CA06
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Otic suspension containing ciprofloxacin hydrochloride 0.3% (equivalent to 3 mg ciprofloxacin base) and dexamethasone 0.1% (equivalent to 1 mg dexamethasone) per mL [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Otic use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Sportpharm LLC; Relabeled by Enovachem Pharmaceuticals [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Acute Otitis Media (AOM):** Indicated for pediatric patients (age 6 months and older) with tympanostomy tubes due to *Staphylococcus aureus*, *Streptococcus pneumoniae*, *Haemophilus influenzae*, *Moraxella catarrhalis*, and *Pseudomonas aeruginosa* [US].
- **Acute Otitis Externa (AOE):** Indicated for pediatric (age 6 months and older), adult, and elderly patients due to *Staphylococcus aureus* and *Pseudomonas aeruginosa* [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Standard Regimen:** Instill four drops into the affected ear(s) twice daily for a duration of seven days [US].

### Administration Instructions
- **Preparation:** Shake well immediately before use. Warm the bottle by holding it in the hand for one to two minutes to prevent dizziness associated with cold suspension instillation [US].
- **Positioning:** The patient should lie with the affected ear upward. Maintain this position for 60 seconds after instillation.
- **Indication-Specific Techniques [US]:**
    - **AOM with Tympanostomy Tubes:** The tragus should be pumped five times using an inward motion to facilitate the movement of the suspension into the middle ear.
    - **AOE:** The outer ear lobe should be pulled upward and backward to allow the drops to enter the ear canal.
- **Special Precautions:** For otic use only; not for ophthalmic use or injection. Avoid contaminating the dropper tip with the ear, fingers, or other surfaces [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known history of hypersensitivity to ciprofloxacin, other quinolones, or any excipients in the formulation [US].
- **Viral Infections:** Viral infections of the external ear canal, including herpes simplex infections [US].
- **Fungal Infections:** Fungal otic infections [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** While administered topically, serious and occasionally fatal systemic hypersensitivity (anaphylactic) reactions have been reported in patients receiving systemic quinolones. Therapy should be discontinued immediately at the first sign of a skin rash or hypersensitivity [US].
- **Microbial Overgrowth:** As with other antibacterials, prolonged use may result in the overgrowth of non-susceptible organisms, including fungi. If the infection does not improve after one week, cultures should be obtained [US].
- **Continued or Recurrent Otorrhea:** If otorrhea persists after a full course or recurs within six months, further evaluation (e.g., to exclude cholesteatoma, foreign body, or tumor) is recommended [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources for topical otic administration [US].
- **Note:** Due to minimal systemic absorption, systemic drug interactions are unlikely.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** No adequate data exist on the use of this product in pregnant women. Based on minimal systemic absorption, the risk of maternal or fetal toxicity is expected to be low. Animal studies with systemic ciprofloxacin at 200 times the otic dose showed no fetal malformations. Dexamethasone has shown teratogenicity in animals following systemic and ophthalmic administration [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Ciprofloxacin is excreted in human milk following systemic administration. It is unknown if it is present following otic use. Dexamethasone in breast milk may suppress growth or interfere with endogenous corticosteroid production. Use with caution, weighing benefits against potential infant exposure [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies with oral ciprofloxacin showed no impairment. The effects of topical otic dexamethasone on fertility have not been investigated [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy have been established in patients 6 months and older. No significant changes in hearing function were observed in pediatric audiometric testing [US].
- **Elderly Use:** No overall differences in safety or effectiveness have been observed between elderly and younger patients [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources; systemic absorption is minimal [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** 
    - **Ciprofloxacin:** A fluoroquinolone that inhibits DNA gyrase and topoisomerase IV, interfering with bacterial DNA synthesis.
    - **Dexamethasone:** A corticosteroid that suppresses the inflammatory response by inhibiting inflammatory cytokines, reducing edema, and decreasing capillary leakage [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Following bilateral 4-drop administration in pediatric patients, peak plasma concentrations were achieved within 15 minutes to 2 hours. Ciprofloxacin levels were approximately 0.1% of those achieved with a 250 mg oral dose. Dexamethasone levels were approximately 14% of those reported following an oral 0.5 mg tablet [US].
- **Microbiology:** Demonstrates bactericidal activity against *S. aureus*, *S. pneumoniae*, *H. influenzae*, *M. catarrhalis*, and *P. aeruginosa* [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **AOM with Tympanostomy Tubes:** A randomized, multicenter trial showed a clinical cure rate of 86% for ciprofloxacin/dexamethasone (BID x 7 days) compared to 79% for ofloxacin solution (BID x 10 days). Microbiological eradication was 91% vs 82%, respectively [US].
- **AOE:** Two randomized trials showed clinical cure rates of 87% and 94% for ciprofloxacin/dexamethasone, compared to 84% and 89% for a neomycin/polymyxin B/hydrocortisone preparation [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Avoid freezing. Protect from light. Discard unused portion after therapy is complete [US].
