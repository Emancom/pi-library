# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the following provided regulatory source:
    - **United States (FDA)**: Product Information for Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter Healthcare Corporation).
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. At the time of compilation, only the United States (FDA) documentation was provided for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); a carrier molecule involved in mitochondrial fatty acid transport.
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection [United States].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - **Injection:** 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [United States].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [United States].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency (Inborn Errors):** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [United States-approved].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD undergoing hemodialysis [United States-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Metabolic Disorders)
- **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg administered as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion.
- **Loading Dose:** Often utilised in severe metabolic crises, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours.
- **Frequency:** Administered every 3 to 4 hours (q3h or q4h), and never less frequently than every 6 hours (q6h).
- **Maximum Reported Dose:** 300 mg/kg [United States].

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Recommended Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight.
- **Administration:** Slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line after each dialysis session.
- **Titration:** Dose adjustments should be guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations (normal range: 40–50 µmol/L). Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [United States].

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration.
- Discard any unused portion as the formulation contains no preservative.

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known:** No specific contraindications are listed in available sources [United States].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions can occur within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, therapy must be discontinued immediately and appropriate medical treatment initiated [United States].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with known seizure disorders, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [United States].
- **Monitoring:** In metabolic disorders, weekly and monthly monitoring is recommended, including blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma free carnitine concentrations (target: 35–60 µmol/L).

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalised Ratio (INR) have occurred when used concurrently with levocarnitine. It is recommended to monitor INR levels after initiating treatment or following dose adjustments [United States].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not specified by letter, but reproductive studies in rats and rabbits (up to 3.8 times human dose) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [United States].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine concentrations in milk after administration. The benefits of maternal therapy should be weighed against potential risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant [United States].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Refer to Dosage and Administration; doses are weight-based (50 mg/kg) [United States].
- **Renal Impairment:** The safety and efficacy of **oral** levocarnitine have not been evaluated in renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in ESRD may lead to accumulation of toxic metabolites (TMA and TMAO), which are normally excreted in urine [United States]. Levocarnitine is easily removed by dialysis.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production (except in the brain). It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [United States].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not protein-bound. Distribution half-life is approximately 0.585 hours.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (TMAO) and γ-butyrobetaine.
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours. The apparent terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [United States].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** Clinical trials in ESRD patients undergoing hemodialysis (20 mg/kg three times weekly) showed significant increases in plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Trough levels increased from a baseline of ~21 µmol/L to ~190 µmol/L after 8 weeks of therapy. While carnitine concentrations increased, the specific effect on clinical outcomes (e.g., muscle weakness, cardiomyopathy) in this population has not been definitively determined [United States].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Protection:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat.
- **Stability:** Compatible and stable for up to 24 hours in PVC bags when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer's (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) at room temperature [United States].
