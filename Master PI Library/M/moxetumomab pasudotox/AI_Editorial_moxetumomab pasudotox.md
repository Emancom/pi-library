# Editorial Product Information: Moxetumomab Pasudotox

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following regulatory authority: **US (FDA)**. 
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical and regulatory data into a single, structured reference.
- **Editorial Note:** As only the United States (FDA) documentation was provided for this drug, this document reflects the approved standards and clinical data specific to that jurisdiction. This document is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific PI required for prescribing in a specific region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Moxetumomab pasudotox-tdfk, a CD22-directed cytotoxin. It consists of a recombinant murine immunoglobulin variable domain fused to a truncated Pseudomonas exotoxin (PE38).
- **Brand Name:** **LUMOXITI** [US]
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - 1 mg lyophilized cake or powder in a single-dose vial for reconstitution and further dilution [US].
    - Supplied with a separate vial of **IV Solution Stabilizer** (polysorbate 80 solution) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Innate Pharma, Inc. [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Hairy Cell Leukemia (HCL):** Indicated for the treatment of adult patients with relapsed or refractory HCL who have received at least two prior systemic therapies, including treatment with a purine nucleoside analog (PNA) [US].
- **Limitations of Use:** Not recommended for use in patients with severe renal impairment (CrCl ≤ 29 mL/min) [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** 0.04 mg/kg administered as a 30-minute IV infusion on Days 1, 3, and 5 of each 28-day cycle [US].
- **Treatment Duration:** Continue for a maximum of 6 cycles, or until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity occurs [US].
- **Concomitant Hydration:** 
    - Administer 1 L of isotonic solution (e.g., 5% Dextrose or 0.45%–0.9% NaCl) IV over 2–4 hours before and after each infusion. 
    - For patients weighing < 50 kg, reduce volume to 0.5 L.
    - Encourage oral hydration (up to 3 L/24h, or 2 L/24h if < 50 kg) on Days 1 through 8 of each cycle [US].
- **Premedication:** Administer 30–90 minutes prior to each infusion:
    - Antihistamine (e.g., diphenhydramine or hydroxyzine)
    - Acetaminophen (antipyretic)
    - H2-receptor antagonist (e.g., ranitidine, famotidine, or cimetidine) [US].
- **Thromboprophylaxis:** Consider low-dose aspirin on Days 1 through 8 of each cycle [US].
- **Preparation Notes:** 
    - Must be reconstituted with Sterile Water for Injection. 
    - **IV Solution Stabilizer** must be added to the 50 mL 0.9% NaCl infusion bag *prior* to adding the reconstituted drug to prevent aggregation [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None listed in available sources [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning: Capillary Leak Syndrome (CLS):** Life-threatening cases have occurred. Characterized by hypoalbuminemia, hypotension, fluid overload, and hemoconcentration. Monitor weight and blood pressure before every infusion. Withhold for Grade 2; discontinue for Grade 3 or 4 [US].
- **Boxed Warning: Hemolytic Uremic Syndrome (HUS):** Life-threatening cases reported. Characterized by the triad of microangiopathic hemolytic anemia, thrombocytopenia, and progressive renal failure. Discontinue **LUMOXITI** permanently if HUS is suspected [US].
- **Renal Toxicity:** Reported in 26% of patients. Monitor serum creatinine before each dose. Dosing should be delayed for Grade 2 or higher increases [US].
- **Infusion-Related Reactions (IRR):** Common (50% of patients). Premedicate as recommended. If a severe IRR occurs, interrupt infusion and manage clinically [US].
- **Electrolyte Abnormalities:** Occurred in 57% of patients (most commonly hypocalcemia). Monitor serum electrolytes before each dose and on Day 8 of each cycle [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources. As a therapeutic protein, moxetumomab pasudotox is expected to undergo proteolytic degradation rather than CYP450 metabolism [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not assigned under current labeling rules, but expected to cause fetal harm]. Based on the mechanism of action, it may cause embryo-fetal toxicity. Animal reproduction studies have not been conducted.
- **Contraception:** Females of reproductive potential should use effective contraception during treatment and for at least 30 days after the last dose [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** There are no data on the presence of the drug in human milk. Discontinuation of breastfeeding is advised during treatment [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use (≥ 65 years):** Higher incidence of adverse reactions leading to discontinuation (23% vs 7%) and renal toxicity (40% vs 20%) compared to younger patients [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    - Mild/Moderate (CrCl 30–89 mL/min): No clinically significant PK differences observed.
    - Severe (CrCl ≤ 29 mL/min): Not recommended [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No significant PK differences in mild impairment. Data are unavailable for moderate to severe hepatic impairment [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** A CD22-directed cytotoxin. The antibody fragment binds to CD22 on B-cells, is internalized, and releases the PE38 exotoxin, which catalyzes the ADP-ribosylation of elongation factor 2 (EF-2). This inhibits protein synthesis, leading to apoptotic cell death [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Mean $V_d$ is 6.5 L [US].
    - **Elimination:** Mean half-life is 1.4 hours. Systemic clearance decreases after the first dose (25 L/h) to subsequent doses (4 L/h) [US].
    - **Immunogenicity:** 59% of patients tested positive for anti-drug antibodies (ADA) at baseline; 88% were ADA-positive at some point during the study. High ADA titers are associated with decreased systemic drug concentrations [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Study 1053 (NCT01829711):** A multicenter trial of 80 patients with relapsed/refractory HCL (median 3 prior lines of therapy).
    - **Primary Outcome:** Durable Complete Response (CR) rate (maintenance of hematologic remission for > 180 days).
    - **Results:** Durable CR was achieved by 30% of patients. The Overall Response Rate (ORR) was 75% [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vials (Unopened):** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) in the original carton to protect from light [US].
- **Reconstituted Solution:** Do not store. Use immediately [US].
- **Diluted Infusion Bag:** 
    - Store at room temperature (20°C to 25°C) for up to 4 hours.
    - Store refrigerated (2°C to 8°C) for up to 24 hours. 
    - Protect from light. Do not freeze or shake [US].
