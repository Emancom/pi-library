# Editorial Product Information: Maribavir

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration)
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of maribavir, synthesising clinical and regulatory information for healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. At the time of preparation, only the Australian PI was available for synthesis.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Maribavir (a benzimidazole riboside antiviral).
- **Brand Name:** LIVTENCITY [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** J05AX29.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** 200 mg immediate-release film-coated tablets. The tablets are blue, oval-shaped, convex, and de-bossed with ‘SHP’ on one side and ‘620’ on the other.
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Takeda Pharmaceuticals Australia Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Post-Transplant Cytomegalovirus (CMV) Infection:** Treatment of adults with post-transplant CMV infection and disease that is resistant, refractory, or intolerant to one or more prior therapies (e.g., ganciclovir, valganciclovir, foscarnet, or cidofovir) [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **Standard Adult Dose:** 400 mg (two 200 mg tablets) administered twice daily, resulting in a total daily dose of 800 mg.
- **Dose Adjustment for Co-administered Anti-convulsants:**
    - **Carbamazepine:** Increase maribavir dose to 800 mg or 1200 mg twice daily.
    - **Phenytoin or Phenobarbital:** Increase maribavir dose to 1200 mg twice daily.
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - May be taken with or without food.
    - Tablets can be swallowed whole, crushed, or dispersed.
    - **Dispersed/Crushed (Oral):** Disperse in drinking water (30 mL for 400 mg; 60 mL for 800 mg; 90 mL for 1200 mg). Stir gently and administer immediately. Rinse the container with 15 mL of water and administer the rinse to ensure the full dose.
    - **Enteral Tube (NG/OG):** Use a French size 10 or larger tube. Disperse in a syringe with 30 mL of water, shake for 30–45 seconds, and administer. Flush with 15 mL of water.
- **Missed Dose:** Not specifically detailed beyond standard antiviral practice (refer to local clinical guidelines).
- **Treatment Duration:** Pivotal studies utilized an 8-week course; however, duration should be individualised based on clinical response.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to maribavir or any components of the formulation.
- **Antiviral Antagonism:** Co-administration with **ganciclovir** or **valganciclovir** is strictly contraindicated. Maribavir inhibits the human CMV UL97 serine/threonine kinase, which is essential for the activation (phosphorylation) of ganciclovir and valganciclovir.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Virologic Failure and Relapse:** Failure to respond or relapse (usually within 4–8 weeks post-treatment) can occur. Monitor CMV DNA levels regularly and test for resistance if the patient does not respond. Some UL97 mutations (e.g., F342Y, C480F) confer cross-resistance to ganciclovir.
- **CNS Infection:** Not studied in patients with CMV central nervous system infection (e.g., meningo-encephalitis). CNS penetration is expected to be low; maribavir is not recommended for treating CMV retinitis or other CNS involvement.
- **Drug Interaction Risk:** 
    - Concurrent use with strong CYP3A4 inducers (e.g., rifampicin, rifabutin, St John’s wort) may significantly decrease maribavir levels and should be avoided.
    - Maribavir can increase concentrations of immunosuppressants (tacrolimus, cyclosporin, sirolimus, everolimus).
- **Sodium Content:** Contains less than 1 mmol (23 mg) sodium per tablet; considered "sodium-free."

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions are classified by frequency based on clinical trial data (Study 303).

| System Organ Class | Very Common (≥10%) | Common (1–10%) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nervous System** | Taste disturbance (46%) | Headache |
| **Gastrointestinal** | Diarrhoea, nausea, vomiting | Abdominal pain upper |
| **General** | Fatigue | Decreased appetite |
| **Investigations** | | Increased immunosuppressant levels, weight decreased |

- **Taste Disturbance:** Includes ageusia, dysgeusia, and hypogeusia. While very common, it rarely led to discontinuation (0.9%) and typically resolved within 7 days of treatment cessation.
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Serious reactions (occurring at <1%) include diarrhoea, nausea, vomiting, fatigue, weight loss, and increased immunosuppressant levels. Maribavir showed a lower incidence of treatment-related neutropenia compared to valganciclovir/ganciclovir and lower renal toxicity compared to foscarnet.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:**
    - **CYP3A4 Induction:** Strong inducers (rifampicin, rifabutin) decrease maribavir exposure (avoid). Moderate inducers (carbamazepine, phenytoin) require maribavir dose increases (see Section 3).
    - **CYP3A4 Inhibition:** Inhibitors (e.g., ketoconazole) increase maribavir AUC but no dose adjustment is required.
    - **P-gp Inhibition:** Maribavir increases digoxin levels (AUC ↑21%). Monitor digoxin levels and reduce dose if necessary.
    - **BCRP Inhibition:** Maribavir may increase levels of BCRP substrates (e.g., rosuvastatin). Monitor for rosuvastatin-related toxicity (myopathy/rhabdomyolysis).
    - **Immunosuppressants:** Increases exposure of tacrolimus (AUC ↑51%), cyclosporin, sirolimus, and everolimus. **Frequent therapeutic drug monitoring (TDM) is essential** during and after maribavir therapy.
- **Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Strong antagonism with ganciclovir and valganciclovir (contraindicated). Synergy noted with sirolimus in vitro.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Australian Category D:** Maribavir is not recommended during pregnancy or in women of childbearing potential not using contraception. Animal studies showed decreased viable fetuses and increased resorptions at doses below human clinical exposure.
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if maribavir is excreted in human milk. Due to potential risks to the infant, breastfeeding should be discontinued during treatment.
- **Fertility:** Animal studies showed no effects on mating or fertility, though decreased sperm velocity was noted in rats at doses below human equivalents.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy in patients <18 years of age have not been established.
- **Elderly Use:** No dose adjustment required for patients >65 years.
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment required for mild, moderate, or severe impairment. Has not been studied in end-stage renal disease (ESRD) or dialysis.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment required for mild (Child-Pugh A) or moderate (Child-Pugh B) impairment. Not studied in severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh C).

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Maribavir is a benzimidazole riboside that inhibits CMV UL97 protein kinase. This prevents the phosphorylation of viral proteins, thereby inhibiting viral DNA replication, encapsidation, and nuclear egress.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Maribavir does not prolong the QTc interval at doses up to 1200 mg. It is highly selective for human CMV.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Rapidly absorbed (Tmax 1.0–3.0 h). AUC is unaffected by food, but Cmax decreases by 28%.
    - **Distribution:** 98% plasma protein bound. Volume of distribution (Vss) is ~24.9 L.
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily via CYP3A4 (major) and CYP1A2 (minor). The primary metabolite (VP 44469) is inactive.
    - **Elimination:** Primarily hepatic. 61% excreted in urine (<2% as unchanged drug) and 14% in feces. Half-life is ~4.3 hours in transplant patients.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Study 303 (SOLSTICE):** A Phase 3, open-label, superiority study comparing maribavir (400 mg BD) to Investigator Assigned Treatment (IAT) in 352 transplant recipients with refractory/resistant CMV.
    - **Primary Endpoint:** CMV viremia clearance at Week 8.
    - **Results:** Maribavir was superior to IAT (56% vs. 24%; p<0.001). 
    - **Subgroup Analysis:** Performance was consistent across Solid Organ Transplant (SOT) and Hematopoietic Stem Cell Transplant (HSCT) recipients.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage:** Store below 30°C. Do not freeze.
- **Suspension Stability:** Once prepared, the suspension can be stored at room temperature for up to 8 hours.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule:** Prescription Only Medicine (S4) [Australian].
- **Monitoring:** Subject to additional monitoring [Australian].
