# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

*   **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonized editorial overview of Levocarnitine injection for use by healthcare professionals.
*   **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis intended for informational purposes and is not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine ((R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt).
*   **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule/Amino acid derivative.
*   **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
*   **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
*   **Dosage Form and Strength:** Sterile solution for injection, 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) [US].
*   **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation (Manufactured by Baxter Pharmaceuticals India Private Ltd) [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
*   **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Pediatric)
*   **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    *   **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion [US].
    *   **Severe Crisis:** A loading dose is often administered, followed by equivalent doses every 3 or 4 hours (never less than every 6 hours) [US].
    *   **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses are typically 50 mg/kg, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been used [US].
*   **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    *   **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight) administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].
    *   **Dose Adjustment:** Guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy if levels are supratherapeutic [US].

### Administration Instructions
*   **Inspection:** Visually inspect for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration [US].
*   **Compatibility:** Stable for up to 24 hours at 25°C in PVC bags when diluted in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s (concentrations ranging from 0.5 mg/mL to 8.0 mg/mL) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
*   None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warnings
*   None specified in available sources.

### General Warnings and Precautions
*   **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions may occur within minutes of administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and initiate medical management [US].
*   **Seizures:** Reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In patients with a known seizure history, an increase in seizure frequency or severity has been observed [US].
*   **Oral Formulation Restriction in Renal Impairment:** The safety/efficacy of *oral* levocarnitine has not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency. High-dose oral administration in these patients may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [US].

### Monitoring Requirements
*   **Plasma Concentrations:** Monitor plasma free carnitine levels (normal range: 35 to 60 µmol/L) and overall clinical condition [US].
*   **Metabolic Parameters:** Monitoring should include blood chemistries and vital signs [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Clinical Trial Experience (ESRD Patients)
Adverse events reported with a frequency ≥5% (regardless of causality) include:
*   **General:** Injection site reaction (33–38%), Pain (30–35%), Headache (3–37%), Flu syndrome (15–29%), Chest pain (6–15%), Accidental injury (8–12%), Fever (5–12%) [US].
*   **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (18–21%), Hypotension (3–19%), Tachycardia (5–9%), Palpitations (3–8%) [US].
*   **Digestive:** Diarrhea (9–35%), Vomiting (9–21%), Nausea (5–12%), Abdominal pain (5–21%), Anorexia (3–6%) [US].
*   **Nervous System:** Dizziness (10–18%), Paresthesia (3–12%), Insomnia (3–6%), Depression (5–6%) [US].
*   **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (15–27%), Cough increase (10–18%), Dyspnea (3–11%), Rhinitis (6–11%) [US].

### Serious and Postmarketing Adverse Effects
*   **Neurologic:** Seizures.
*   **Hypersensitivity:** Anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, bronchospasm.
*   **Metabolic:** Hypercalcemia (up to 15%), Hyperkalemia (6%), Hypervolemia (3–12%).

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) when used concurrently with levocarnitine. Monitor INR levels closely after initiation or dose adjustment [US].
*   **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Levocarnitine does not bind to plasma proteins or albumin, reducing the likelihood of protein-binding displacement interactions [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy:** 
    *   **Jurisdiction Classification:** Not specified in available sources.
    *   **Animal Data:** Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at doses up to 3.8 times the human dose (based on surface area) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm [US].
    *   **Recommendation:** Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed, as animal studies are not always predictive of human response [US].
*   **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk carnitine concentrations. Weigh the benefits of maternal treatment against the potential risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant; consider discontinuing nursing or the drug [US].
*   **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders and ESRD includes weight-based calculations applicable to pediatric patients (see Section 3) [US].
*   **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in provided sources; generally requires cautious dose selection reflecting decreased organ function.
*   **Renal Impairment:** Specifically indicated for ESRD patients on dialysis. High-dose *oral* carnitine is contraindicated in severe renal impairment due to metabolite accumulation [US].
*   **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
*   **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring carrier molecule required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production [US]. 
*   **Pharmacokinetics:**
    *   **Distribution:** Not protein-bound; distribution half-life is 0.585 hours [US].
    *   **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and [3H]-γ-butyrobetaine (feces) [US].
    *   **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours. Terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   **Bioavailability Study:** A study of 15 healthy volunteers confirmed bioequivalence between oral tablets and solution, with absolute bioavailability of approximately 15% after correcting for endogenous carnitine [US].
*   **ESRD Studies:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients receiving 20 mg/kg IV thrice weekly showed pre-dialysis trough concentrations increased from 21.3 µmol/L to 190 µmol/L by week 8 [US].
*   **Dose-Ranging Study:** Evaluated 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses in ESRD patients; results showed dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
*   **Handling:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat. Discard any unused portion of a single-dose vial as the formulation contains no preservatives [US].
