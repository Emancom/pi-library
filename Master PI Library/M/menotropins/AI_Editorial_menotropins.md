# Editorial Product Information: Menotropins

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis has been developed based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**. 
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonized, clinically focused overview of **menotropins** (specifically the **Menopur** formulation) for healthcare professionals. 
- At the time of this publication, only the United States PI was available for review. Consequently, information regarding other major jurisdictions (e.g., Australian, European, Canadian, Japanese) is not specified in the current available sources.
- This document serves as an editorial summary and does not replace the official PI required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Menotropins (purified Follicle-Stimulating Hormone [FSH] and Luteinizing Hormone [LH] activity).
- **Chemical Class:** Gonadotropins (extracted from the urine of postmenopausal women).
- **Brand Name:** **Menopur** [US].
- **ATC Code:** G03GA02.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** 
    - Lyophilized powder for injection [US].
    - Each single-dose vial contains 75 International Units (IU) of FSH activity and 75 IU of LH activity [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Subcutaneous (SC) injection [US].
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Ferring Pharmaceuticals Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Assisted Reproductive Technology (ART):** Indicated for the development of multiple follicles and the induction of pregnancy in ovulatory women participating in an ART cycle [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing Information
- Prior to initiation, clinicians must perform a full gynecologic and endocrinologic evaluation, exclude pregnancy, and evaluate the male partner's fertility [US].
- **Initial Dose (First Cycle):** 225 IU daily, administered subcutaneously [US].
- **Dose Adjustment:** Dosage may be adjusted after 5 days of therapy. Subsequent adjustments should not occur more frequently than every 2 days and should not exceed 150 IU per adjustment [US].
- **Maximum Daily Dose:** 450 IU [US].
- **Maximum Duration:** Therapy should not exceed 20 days [US].

### Specific Co-administration
- **Menopur** may be administered in combination with **Bravelle** (urofollitropin). In clinical trials, a total starting dose of 225 IU was studied using ratios of 150 IU:75 IU or 75 IU:150 IU [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Reconstitute with 1 mL of sterile 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection, USP [US].
- Administer into the lower abdomen, alternating sides daily [US].
- Inspect visually for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration [US].

### Missed Dose
- Patients should be instructed not to double the dose. If a dose is missed, the patient should contact their healthcare provider immediately [US].

## 4. Contraindications
**Menopur** is contraindicated in women exhibiting any of the following [US]:
- Prior hypersensitivity to **menotropins** or any of the product's excipients.
- High levels of FSH indicating primary ovarian failure.
- Pregnancy (may cause fetal harm).
- Uncontrolled non-gonadal endocrinopathies (e.g., thyroid, adrenal, or pituitary disorders).
- Sex hormone-dependent tumors of the reproductive tract and accessory organs.
- Tumors of the pituitary gland or hypothalamus.
- Abnormal uterine bleeding of undetermined origin.
- Ovarian cysts or enlargement of undetermined origin (not due to polycystic ovary syndrome).

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning:** None present in the provided source.
- **Experienced Supervision:** Should only be used by physicians experienced in infertility treatment [US].
- **Ovarian Hyperstimulation Syndrome (OHSS):** A serious medical event resulting from increased vascular permeability. Early warning signs include pelvic pain, nausea, vomiting, and weight gain. Severe cases can lead to ascites, pleural effusion, and thromboembolic events. If OHSS is suspected, hCG should be withheld [US].
- **Pulmonary and Vascular Complications:** Serious pulmonary conditions (e.g., ARDS, asthma exacerbation) and thromboembolic events (e.g., stroke, myocardial infarction, pulmonary embolism) have been reported [US].
- **Ovarian Torsion:** Reported after gonadotropin treatment; may be related to OHSS or prior abdominal surgery [US].
- **Multi-fetal Gestation:** Increased risk of multiple births. In IVF trials, multiple pregnancy occurred in 35.3% of total pregnancies [US].
- **Congenital Malformations:** Incidence may be slightly higher after ART compared to spontaneous conception, likely due to parental characteristics or multiple gestations [US].
- **Monitoring:** Requires regular ultrasound evaluation of follicular growth and/or serum estradiol levels to minimize risks of OHSS and multiple gestations [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions observed at an incidence of ≥2% in women undergoing IVF/ICSI are summarized below [US]:

| Adverse Reaction | Frequency (%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Abdominal Pain** | 6.7% |
| **OHSS** | 6.2% |
| **Headache** | 6.2% |
| **Injection Site Pain/Reaction** | 3.9% |
| **Abdominal Cramps** | 3.0% |
| **Abdomen Enlarged** | 2.3% |
| **Injection Site Inflammation** | 2.3% |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Thromboembolic events, ovarian torsion, spontaneous abortion, and rarely, ovarian neoplasms (malignant or benign) [US].
- **Postmarketing Experience:** Additional reported reactions include nausea, vomiting, abdominal distension, fatigue, dizziness, acne, rash, and hot flushes [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Human Studies:** No formal drug-drug interaction studies have been conducted for **menotropins** in humans [US].
- **In vitro:** **Menopur** has been mixed with **Bravelle** without evidence of aggregation [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category X:** Contraindicated. There is no medical indication for **menotropins** in pregnant women. Fetal harm is possible if administered during pregnancy [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** It is not known if **menotropins** are excreted in human milk. A decision should be made whether to discontinue nursing or the drug, considering the importance of the drug to the mother [US].
- **Fertility:** Clinical use is specifically for the induction of ovulation and follicular development as part of fertility treatment [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness in pediatric patients have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not indicated for use in the geriatric population [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Safety, efficacy, and pharmacokinetics have not been established in patients with renal or hepatic insufficiency [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Administered for 7 to 20 days, **menotropins** provide exogenous FSH and LH activity to produce ovarian follicular growth and maturation in women without primary ovarian failure [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics (FSH):**
    - **Absorption:** Bioavailability is higher via the subcutaneous route compared to intramuscular administration [US].
    - **Distribution:** Not studied in human tissues or organs for this product [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Not studied in humans [US].
    - **Elimination:** The elimination half-life of FSH in the multiple-dose phase is approximately 11–13 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **IVF Study:** A randomized, open-label trial of 373 women undergoing IVF/ICSI demonstrated efficacy. Patients received a starting dose of 225 IU for five days, followed by individualized titration.
- **Outcomes:** The continuing pregnancy rate (visualized via ultrasound at ≥10 weeks) was 23%, and the clinical pregnancy rate was 26% [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Lyophilized Powder:** Store refrigerated or at room temperature (3°C to 25°C / 37°F to 77°F) until dispensed [US].
- **Light Protection:** Vials should be protected from light [US].
- **Reconstituted Solution:** Use immediately after mixing. Discard any unused material [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** 
    - **United States:** Prescription Only (Rx-only).
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in provided source [Note: US does not use the Australian SUSMP scheduling system].
