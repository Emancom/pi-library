# Editorial Product Information: Mepolizumab

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical data, regulatory requirements, and administration guidelines into a single reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official PI or summary of product characteristics (SmPC) required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory documents for legal prescribing compliance.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Mepolizumab (humanised monoclonal antibody, IgG1 kappa).
- **Chemical Class:** Interleukin-5 (IL-5) antagonist.
- **Brand Name(s):** **NUCALA** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Lyophilised powder for injection: 100 mg (144 mg per vial to deliver 100 mg upon reconstitution) [Australian].
    - Solution for injection: 100 mg/1 mL pre-filled pen (auto-injector) or pre-filled syringe [Australian].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Subcutaneous (SC) injection only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** GlaxoSmithKline Australia Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
Mepolizumab is indicated as an add-on treatment for the following conditions:
- **Severe Eosinophilic Asthma:** Patients aged 12 years and over [Australian].
- **Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD):** Maintenance treatment for adults with uncontrolled COPD and elevated blood eosinophils who are on stable triple inhaler therapy (or equivalent) [Australian].
- **Chronic Rhinosinusitis with Nasal Polyps (CRSwNP):** Adult patients (18 years and older) with an inadequate response to intranasal corticosteroids [Australian].
- **Relapsed or Refractory Eosinophilic Granulomatosis with Polyangiitis (EGPA):** Adult patients aged 18 years and over [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Adolescents)
- **Severe Eosinophilic Asthma:** 100 mg administered SC once every 4 weeks [Australian].
- **COPD:** 100 mg administered SC once every 4 weeks [Australian].
- **CRSwNP:** 100 mg administered SC once every 4 weeks [Australian].
- **Relapsed or Refractory EGPA:** 300 mg administered SC once every 4 weeks. For the 300 mg dose, it is recommended to administer as three separate 100 mg injections, with each site separated by at least 5 cm [Australian].

### Administration Instructions
- **Powder for Injection:** Must be reconstituted by a healthcare professional using 1.2 mL of sterile Water for Injection (WFI) to achieve a 100 mg/mL concentration. Direct the WFI stream vertically onto the center of the cake and swirl gently (do not shake) for 10 seconds, then allow to sit until dissolved [Australian].
- **Pre-filled Pen/Syringe:** May be self-administered by the patient or a caregiver if the healthcare professional deems it appropriate and training is provided [Australian].
- **Injection Sites:** Upper arm, thigh, or abdomen.

### Missed Dose
- Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to mepolizumab or any of the excipients (e.g., sucrose, sodium phosphate dibasic heptahydrate, polysorbate 80) [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Acute Use:** Mepolizumab should not be used to treat acute asthma or COPD exacerbations [Australian].
- **Corticosteroid Reduction:** Do not discontinue systemic or inhaled corticosteroids abruptly upon starting mepolizumab. Dose reductions, if required, should be gradual and supervised by a physician [Australian].
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Acute and delayed systemic reactions, including anaphylaxis, angioedema, rash, and bronchospasm, have occurred. These typically appear within hours but may have a delayed onset of several days. Treatment should be discontinued if a hypersensitivity reaction occurs [Australian].
- **Parasitic Infections:** Eosinophils are involved in the response to helminth infections. Pre-existing infections should be treated before starting therapy. If infection occurs during treatment and does not respond to anti-helminthics, temporary discontinuation of mepolizumab may be necessary [Australian].
- **Opportunistic Infections:** Cases of Herpes Zoster have been reported in clinical trials [Australian].
- **EGPA Cessation:** Symptoms may return upon discontinuation; other treatments may need to be increased accordingly [Australian].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Frequency Data
Adverse effects are categorised by frequency below based on clinical trial data for asthma, COPD, and CRSwNP (100 mg dose).

| Frequency | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Headache |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Injection site reactions (8%), back pain, fatigue, influenza, urinary tract infection, abdominal pain upper, pruritus, eczema, muscle spasms, oropharyngeal pain |
| **Rare (<0.1%)** | Anaphylaxis [Post-marketing] |

### Indication-Specific Variations
- **EGPA (300 mg dose):** Higher frequencies of certain events were noted: Headache (32%), arthralgia (22%), nausea (19%), sinusitis (21%), and vomiting (16%) [Australian].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Herpes zoster and serious hypersensitivity/systemic allergic reactions.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic:** No formal interaction studies have been conducted. No clinically significant interactions are expected based on the mechanism of action [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [Australian: Category B1]. Human data are missing. Animal studies (monkeys and mice) showed no evidence of embryo-foetal or postnatal development harm at doses providing much higher exposure than the clinical dose. Mepolizumab crosses the placenta. Use only if the benefit to the mother outweighs the potential risk to the foetus [Australian].
- **Breastfeeding:** There are no data on excretion in human milk. In monkeys, mepolizumab was excreted in milk at concentrations <0.5% of plasma levels. Use with caution [Australian].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies showed no impairment of fertility in male or female mice [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** 
    - **Asthma:** Approved for age 12 and over. Safety and efficacy not established below 12 years [Australian].
    - **COPD/CRSwNP/EGPA:** Use in patients under 18 is either not relevant or not established [Australian].
- **Elderly (≥65 years):** No dose adjustment required. Population PK analysis showed no effect of age [Australian].
- **Renal Impairment:** Dose adjustments are unlikely to be required for patients with CrCl 50-80 mL/min. Data are limited for CrCl <50 mL/min [Australian].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Dose adjustments are unlikely to be required as monoclonal antibodies are degraded by widely distributed proteolytic enzymes rather than restricted hepatic metabolism [Australian].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Mepolizumab is a humanised monoclonal antibody (IgG1, kappa) that binds to human IL-5 with high affinity and specificity. By inhibiting IL-5 bioactivity, it reduces the growth, differentiation, recruitment, and survival of eosinophils, thereby reducing eosinophilic inflammation [Australian].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Tmax is 4 to 8 days following SC administration. Absolute bioavailability is approximately 64% to 80% depending on the injection site [Australian].
    - **Distribution:** Mean volume of distribution is 55 to 85 mL/kg [Australian].
    - **Metabolism:** Degraded by proteolytic enzymes [Australian].
    - **Elimination:** Mean terminal half-life is approximately 16 to 22 days following SC administration. Systemic clearance is approximately 3.1 mL/day/kg [Australian].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Severe Eosinophilic Asthma:** In trials MEA115588 and MEA115575, mepolizumab 100 mg SC significantly reduced the rate of clinically significant exacerbations (53% reduction) and allowed for a significant reduction in daily oral corticosteroid doses compared to placebo [Australian].
- **COPD:** In the MATINEE trial, 100 mg SC reduced the annual rate of moderate/severe exacerbations by 21% (Rate Ratio 0.79) in patients with an eosinophilic phenotype [Australian].
- **CRSwNP:** In Study 205687, treatment significantly improved total endoscopic nasal polyp scores and nasal obstruction VAS scores compared to placebo over 52 weeks [Australian].
- **EGPA:** In trial MEA115921, 300 mg SC resulted in significantly more time in remission and a 50% reduction in the annualised relapse rate versus placebo [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vials (Unopened):** Store at 2°C to 8°C (Refrigerate) or below 25°C if specified on the pack. Protect from light [Australian].
- **Reconstituted Solution:** Stable for up to 6 hours when stored below 25°C. Do not freeze [Australian].
- **Pre-filled Pen/Syringe:** Store at 2°C to 8°C. Do not freeze. Can be kept at room temperature (up to 30°C) for up to 7 days if protected from light. Must be administered within 8 hours once the pack is opened [Australian].
