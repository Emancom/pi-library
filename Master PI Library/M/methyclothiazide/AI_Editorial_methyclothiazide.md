# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of **levocarnitine** injection for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical and regulatory data into a single reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official PI or local regulatory guidance required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); a carrier molecule involved in mitochondrial fatty acid transport.
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative [(R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt].
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection, USP [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01 (Not explicitly specified in the source document).
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Sterile solution for injection, 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency (Inborn Errors of Metabolism):** For the acute and chronic management of patients with metabolic defects that lead to secondary carnitine deficiency [US-approved].
- **Carnitine Deficiency in End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance dialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
- **General Dose:** 50 mg/kg administered as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) or via infusion [US].
- **Loading Dose:** In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose is often administered, followed by an equivalent dose over the next 24 hours [US].
- **Frequency:** Administer every 3 to 4 hours; intervals should never be less than every 6 hours [US].
- **Titration:** Subsequent daily doses are typically 50 mg/kg, though higher doses may be required. The maximum recorded dose administered is 300 mg/kg [US].

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight) [US].
- **Administration:** Administered as a slow 2- to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].
- **Adjustment:** Dose reductions (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy, guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma carnitine concentrations [US].

### Monitoring and Instructions
- **Target Levels:** Plasma free carnitine concentrations should ideally be maintained between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].
- **Inspection:** Visually inspect for particulate matter or discoloration before administration.
- **Compatibility:** Stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known:** There are currently no listed contraindications for levocarnitine injection [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. These may occur within minutes of administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue the drug immediately and initiate medical treatment [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure disorders. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Oral Formulation Caution:** The safety and efficacy of **oral** levocarnitine have not been established in patients with renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in ESRD patients may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites, trimethylamine (TMA) and trimethylamine-N-oxide (TMAO) [US].
- **Monitoring Requirements:** Weekly and monthly monitoring should include blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions are often confounded by the underlying pathology of ESRD or metabolic disorders.

### Common Adverse Effects (ESRD Clinical Trials ≥5%) [US]
- **Body as a Whole:** Injection site reaction (33%), pain (30%), headache (22%), abdominal pain (9%), fever (7%).
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (20%), hypotension (14%), tachycardia (6%), palpitations (5%).
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea (16%), vomiting (15%), nausea (8%), anorexia (5%).
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (23%), cough (9%), dyspnea (7%).
- **Other:** Asthenia (9%), infection (15%), dizziness (13%).

### Serious and Other Notable Reactions [US]
- **Neurologic:** Seizures (reported in post-marketing).
- **Hypersensitivity:** Anaphylaxis and bronchospasm.
- **Metabolic:** Hypercalcemia (9%), hyperkalemia (6%), hypervolemia (5%).
- **Miscellaneous:** Body odor, gastritis, and taste perversion.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate that levocarnitine may increase the International Normalized Ratio (INR) when used concomitantly with warfarin. **Clinical Management:** Monitor INR levels frequently in patients on warfarin following the initiation or dose adjustment of levocarnitine [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not specified by letter, but the PI states "Category B" logic in description]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine is a normal component of human milk, and levels in dairy cows increase after exogenous administration. Specific studies in nursing mothers have not been conducted. The risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant must be weighed against the benefit of supplementation to the mother [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility was found in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders and ESRD is weight-based (mg/kg) and applicable to paediatric populations as described in the Dosage section [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in the available source; general caution regarding organ function in the elderly is standard, though not explicitly detailed here.
- **Renal Impairment:** Specifically indicated for ESRD patients on dialysis. High-dose oral levocarnitine is cautioned against in severe renal impairment due to metabolite accumulation [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids into the mitochondria for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Levocarnitine alleviates metabolic abnormalities by clearing toxic acyl-CoA compounds and restoring plasma concentrations (deficiency defined as <20 µmol/L at one week post-term) [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin. Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an intravenous dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. Terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Efficacy:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients on hemodialysis receiving 20 mg/kg three times weekly showed that mean pre-dialysis carnitine levels rose from ~20 µmol/L to 190 ± 55 µmol/L after 8 weeks [US].
- **Dose-Ranging Data:** A study comparing 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses in ESRD patients (N~30 per group) demonstrated dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C [US].
- **Conditions:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat.
- **Handling:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as the formulation lacks preservatives [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as Rx-only [US].
