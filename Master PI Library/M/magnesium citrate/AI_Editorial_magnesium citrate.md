# Editorial Product Information: Magnesium Citrate

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) and labeling documentation from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical data into a concise, professional format.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis intended for informational purposes and is not a substitute for the official PI required for prescribing or dispensing in any specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Magnesium citrate (Saline laxative).
- **Brand Name(s):** Meijer Magnesium Citrate Oral Solution [US].
- **ATC Code:** A06AD12 (Magnesium citrate).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Oral solution containing 1.745 g of magnesium citrate per fluid ounce (29.6 mL) [US]. 
    - Each fl oz provides approximately 290 mg of elemental magnesium [US].
    - Each fl oz contains 1 mg of sodium [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Meijer Distribution, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Occasional Constipation:** For the short-term relief of occasional constipation and irregularity [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Instructions
- Shake the solution well before use.
- Each dose must be accompanied by a full glass (8 ounces/240 mL) of liquid to ensure efficacy and prevent dehydration [US].
- Dosage may be administered as a single daily dose or in divided doses [US].

### Dosing by Age Group [US]
- **Adults and children 12 years of age and over:** 6.5 to 10 fl oz (approx. 192 to 296 mL) in 24 hours. Maximum dose is 10 fl oz in 24 hours.
- **Children 6 to under 12 years of age:** 3 to 7 fl oz (approx. 89 to 207 mL) in 24 hours. Maximum dose is 7 fl oz in 24 hours.
- **Children 2 to under 6 years of age:** 2 to 3 fl oz (approx. 59 to 89 mL) in 24 hours. Maximum dose is 3 fl oz in 24 hours.
- **Children under 2 years of age:** Consult a physician before use.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Renal Disease:** Use is contraindicated/requires medical supervision in patients with kidney disease [US].
- **Acute Abdominal Symptoms:** Do not use in the presence of abdominal pain, nausea, or vomiting (potential signs of bowel obstruction or appendicitis) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Duration of Use:** Patients should consult a physician if they have used a laxative for longer than one week or if they notice a sudden change in bowel habits persisting for over two weeks [US].
- **Magnesium-Restricted Diet:** Patients on a magnesium-restricted diet must consult a physician prior to use [US].
- **Serious Condition Indicators:** Treatment should be discontinued and a physician consulted if rectal bleeding occurs or if the patient fails to have a bowel movement after use [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Gastrointestinal:** Abdominal cramping, diarrhea, and electrolyte imbalance (notably hypermagnesemia if overused).
- **Serious Adverse Effects:**
    - Rectal bleeding [US].
    - Failure to have a bowel movement after use (may indicate a more serious underlying condition) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **General Interaction Profile:** Laxatives may affect the absorption and efficacy of other concurrent oral medications. 
- **Management:** This product should be administered at least 2 hours before or 2 hours after other medications to minimize interference with absorption [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Pregnant patients are advised to consult a healthcare professional before use [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Breastfeeding patients are advised to consult a healthcare professional before use [US].
- **Fertility:** Data not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Approved for children as young as 2 years old with specific weight/age-based dosing. Children under 2 require direct medical supervision [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources, though caution is generally advised regarding electrolyte balance and renal function.
- **Renal Impairment:** Use with extreme caution and only under medical supervision due to the risk of magnesium accumulation and toxicity [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** As a saline laxative, magnesium citrate acts osmotically in the small intestine and colon. It draws water into the intestinal lumen, causing distension and promoting peristalsis and bowel evacuation.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Generally produces a bowel movement within 30 minutes to 6 hours after administration [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** 
    - **Absorption:** Magnesium is partially absorbed (approx. 15-30%) but primarily acts locally.
    - **Elimination:** Absorbed magnesium is excreted renally.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- Store at controlled room temperatures between 8°C and 30°C (46°F to 86°F) [US].
- Keep the container tightly closed and protect from excessive heat.
