# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
*   **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA)
*   **Purpose:** This document is a harmonised editorial overview of **levocarnitine** injection, synthesised for use by healthcare professionals. 
*   **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority's approved labelling for definitive guidance.

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
*   **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule/Amino acid derivative.
*   **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection, USP (Baxter Healthcare) [US].
*   **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    *   Intravenous solution: 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) single-dose vial [US].
*   **Routes of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US-approved].
*   **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD undergoing dialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
*   **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg [US].
*   **Administration:** Delivered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion [US].
*   **Loading Dose:** Often administered in patients with severe metabolic crisis, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours [US].
*   **Frequency:** Doses should be given every 3 to 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours [US].
*   **Maximum Dose:** The highest reported dose administered is 300 mg/kg [US].

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
*   **Recommended Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
*   **Administration:** Delivered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line after each dialysis session [US].
*   **Dose Adjustments:** Guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy if concentrations are below normal (40 µmol/L to 50 µmol/L) [US].

### Administration Instructions
*   Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration [US].
*   **Compatibility:** Stable for up to 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations ranging from 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL [US].

## 4. Contraindications
*   **None known** [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Hypersensitivity Reactions
*   Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients on dialysis. Some reactions occur within minutes of intravenous administration. Treatment should be discontinued immediately upon a severe reaction [US].

### Seizures
*   Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity receiving levocarnitine. In patients with known seizure disorders, an increase in seizure frequency or severity has been observed [US].

### Monitoring Requirements
*   **Metabolic Disorders:** Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine: 35 to 60 µmol/L) is recommended [US].
*   **ESRD:** Monitor trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations [US].

### Specific Clinical Precautions
*   **Oral Levocarnitine in Renal Impairment:** The safety/efficacy of oral levocarnitine in patients with renal insufficiency has not been evaluated. Chronic high doses of oral levocarnitine in ESRD patients may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites, trimethylamine (TMA) and trimethylamine-N-oxide (TMAO), which are normally excreted in urine [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Clinical Trial Experience (ESRD Patients)
In double-blind, placebo-controlled trials, the following adverse events were reported at a frequency of ≥5% (regardless of causality):

| Body System | Adverse Effect (Incidence ≥5%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Injection site reaction (33%), Pain (30%), Flu syndrome (25%), Headache (22%), Infection (15%), Abdominal pain (9%), Asthenia (9%), Fever (7%) |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension (20%), Hypotension (14%), Tachycardia (6%), Palpitations (5%) |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea (16%), Vomiting (15%), Nausea (8%), Anorexia (5%), Dyspepsia (5%) |
| **Metabolic/Nutritional** | Hypercalcemia (9%), Anemia (6%), Hyperkalemia (6%), Hypervolemia (5%), Peripheral edema (5%), Weight decrease (5%) |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis (23%), Cough increase (9%), Rhinitis (9%), Dyspnea (7%) |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness (13%), Paresthesia (5%), Insomnia (4%), Depression (5%) |
| **Other** | Pruritus (5%), Leg cramps (4%) |

### Serious and Less Common Effects
*   **Serious:** Anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, bronchospasm [US].
*   **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea and vomiting; gastritis [US].
*   **Miscellaneous:** Specific reports of body odor have been noted [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have been observed when co-administered with levocarnitine. It is recommended to monitor INR levels following initiation or dose adjustment of levocarnitine in patients on warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
### Pregnancy
*   **Risk Summary:** No adequate and well-controlled studies in pregnant women. Animal reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at doses up to 3.8 times the human dose (based on surface area) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].

### Breastfeeding
*   **Risk Summary:** Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Risks of excess carnitine intake to the infant must be weighed against the benefits to the mother. Discontinuation of nursing or the drug should be considered [US].

### Fertility
*   No evidence of impaired fertility was found in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use:** Refer to Dosage and Administration for metabolic disorder dosing; specific age-based restrictions are not detailed in available sources [US].
*   **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources [US].
*   **Renal Impairment:** Primarily indicated for ESRD patients on dialysis. See Dosage and Administration for specific guidance [US].
*   **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
*   Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring carrier molecule required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production [US].

### Pharmacokinetics
*   **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
*   **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and [³H]-γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
*   **Elimination:** Following IV administration, approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours. The mean apparent terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

### Pharmacodynamics
*   Levocarnitine promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters, which are quickly excreted. This clears toxic acyl-CoA compounds that otherwise disrupt intermediary metabolism and cause life-threatening acidosis [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   **ESRD Dosing Studies:** In a 9-week study of ESRD patients (n=12), 20 mg/kg doses three times weekly post-dialysis increased mean pre-dialysis trough concentrations from 21.3 µmol/L to 190 µmol/L after 8 weeks. 
*   **Dose-Ranging Data:** A study comparing 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses three times weekly showed dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine concentrations reaching 148, 240, and 456 µmol/L respectively after 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
*   Protect from freezing and excessive heat.
*   The formulation does not contain preservatives; discard any unused portion of opened vials [US].
