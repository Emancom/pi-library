# Editorial Product Information: Magnesium Salicylate

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) and labeling provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, incorporating regulatory requirements and clinical guidance.
- This document is an editorial synthesis intended for reference and does not substitute for the official PI or summary of product characteristics (SmPC) required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Magnesium salicylate tetrahydrate (Nonsteroidal Anti-inflammatory Drug; NSAID). 
- **Chemical Class:** Salicylates.
- **Brand Names:** Doan's Maximum Strength [United States].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources (Typically M01BA03).
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Oral caplets containing 580 mg magnesium salicylate tetrahydrate, equivalent to 467.2 mg of anhydrous magnesium salicylate [United States].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories Inc. [United States].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Minor Backache:** Temporary relief of minor backache pain [United States].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adults and Children 12 years and over):** 1160 mg (two 580 mg caplets) administered every 6 hours [United States].
- **Maximum Daily Dose:** Do not exceed 4640 mg (8 caplets) in a 24-hour period [United States].
- **Administration Instructions:** Each dose should be taken with a full glass of water [United States].
- **Missed Dose Instructions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known allergy to salicylates (including aspirin) or any other pain reliever/fever reducer [United States].
- **Paediatric Viral Recovery:** Children and teenagers who have or are recovering from chickenpox or flu-like symptoms, due to the risk of Reye's syndrome [United States].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Reye's Syndrome:** A rare but serious illness occurring in children/teenagers. If changes in behaviour with nausea and vomiting occur, magnesium salicylate should be discontinued and medical advice sought immediately [United States].
- **Stomach Bleeding Warning:** As an NSAID, this product may cause severe stomach bleeding. Risk factors include [United States]:
    - Age ≥ 60 years.
    - History of stomach ulcers or bleeding problems.
    - Concomitant use of anticoagulants or steroids.
    - Concomitant use of other NSAIDs (e.g., aspirin, ibuprofen, naproxen).
    - Consumption of 3 or more alcoholic drinks daily.
    - Use for longer than the directed duration.
- **Hypersensitivity Alert:** May cause severe allergic reactions, including hives, facial swelling, asthma (wheezing), or anaphylactic shock [United States].
- **Pre-existing Conditions:** Consult a healthcare provider before use in patients with asthma, high blood pressure, heart disease, liver cirrhosis, or kidney disease [United States].
- **Clinical Monitoring:** Discontinue use and consult a physician if [United States]:
    - Pain worsens or persists for >10 days.
    - Fever worsens or persists for >3 days.
    - New symptoms or redness/swelling occur.
    - Tinnitus (ringing in the ears) or hearing loss occurs.

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Hypersensitivity:** Anaphylactic shock, hives, facial swelling, and asthma (wheezing) [United States].
- **Gastrointestinal:** Severe stomach bleeding (signs include faintness, vomiting blood, bloody or black stools, or persistent stomach pain) [United States].
- **Otic:** Tinnitus (ringing in the ears) or loss of hearing [United States].
- **General:** Redness or swelling at the site of pain [United States].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Anticoagulants and Steroids:** Increased risk of gastrointestinal bleeding [United States].
- **Other NSAIDs:** Concurrent use with aspirin, ibuprofen, naproxen, or other prescription/non-prescription NSAIDs increases the risk of bleeding [United States].
- **Diuretics:** Potential for interaction; clinical consultation required before use [United States].
- **Chronic Disease Medications:** Professional consultation is advised for patients taking prescription drugs for:
    - Gout [United States].
    - Diabetes [United States].
    - Arthritis [United States].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - Consult a healthcare professional before use [United States].
    - **Specific Precaution:** It is especially important to avoid magnesium salicylate at 20 weeks or later in pregnancy unless specifically directed by a physician, as it may cause complications in the unborn child or during delivery [United States].
- **Breastfeeding:** Consult a healthcare professional before use [United States].
- **Fertility:** Not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** 
    - Contraindicated in children/teenagers recovering from viral infections (Reye's syndrome risk) [United States].
    - Children under 12 years: Do not use unless directed by a doctor [United States].
- **Elderly Use:** Increased risk of stomach bleeding in patients aged 60 years and older [United States].
- **Renal Impairment:** Consult a doctor before use; salicylates may impact renal function [United States].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Consult a doctor before use (specifically noted for liver cirrhosis) [United States].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** A nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID) and salicylate that acts as a pain reliever [United States].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified in available sources. Each caplet provides 45 mg of magnesium [United States].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Not specified in available sources.

## 11. Clinical Trials

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions are permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F–86°F) [United States].
- **Packaging:** Keep out of reach of children. Tamper-evident: Do not use if the imprinted safety seal under the cap is broken or missing [United States].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
