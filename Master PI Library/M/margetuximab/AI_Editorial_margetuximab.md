# Editorial Product Information: Margetuximab

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial product information is based on official documents from the following jurisdiction: United States (Food and Drug Administration).
- This document serves as a harmonised editorial synthesis intended for healthcare professionals. It provides a structured overview of the clinical and regulatory profile of margetuximab.
- **Note:** This editorial is not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) or Prescribing Information required for clinical practice in a specific jurisdiction. Always consult the current, locally approved PI before prescribing or administering this medication.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Margetuximab-cmkb (a chimeric Fc-engineered IgG1 kappa monoclonal antibody).
- **Brand Name:** **MARGENZA** [US].
- **ATC Code:** L01ED04 (Margetuximab).
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Injection (concentrate for solution for infusion). 250 mg/10 mL (25 mg/mL) in a single-dose vial [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** TerSera Therapeutics LLC [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Metastatic Breast Cancer:** Indicated, in combination with chemotherapy, for the treatment of adult patients with metastatic HER2-positive breast cancer who have received two or more prior anti-HER2 regimens, at least one of which was for metastatic disease [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** The recommended dose is 15 mg/kg administered as an intravenous infusion every 3 weeks (21-day cycle) until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity [US].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - **Initial Dose:** Administer over 120 minutes [US].
    - **Subsequent Doses:** Administer over a minimum of 30 minutes if the initial dose was well tolerated [US].
    - **Sequencing:** On days when both **MARGENZA** and chemotherapy are scheduled, **MARGENZA** may be administered immediately following the completion of chemotherapy [US].
    - **Requirements:** Administer through an intravenous line containing a sterile, non-pyrogenic, low-protein binding polyethersulfone (PES) 0.2 micron in-line or add-on filter [US]. Do not administer as an IV push or bolus [US].
- **Preparation:** Dilute in 100 mL or 250 mL of 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection, USP. Do not use 5% Dextrose Injection. Final concentration should be between 0.5 mg/mL and 7.2 mg/mL [US].
- **Missed Dose:** If a dose is missed, it should be administered as soon as possible. The administration schedule should be adjusted to maintain the 3-week interval between doses [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None specified in available sources [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning: Left Ventricular Dysfunction [US]:** **MARGENZA** can lead to reductions in left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF). Evaluate cardiac function prior to and during treatment. Discontinue treatment for a confirmed, clinically significant decrease in left ventricular function [US].
- **Boxed Warning: Embryo-Fetal Toxicity [US]:** Exposure during pregnancy can cause fetal harm. Advise patients of the potential risk to the fetus and the need for effective contraception [US].
- **Infusion-Related Reactions (IRRs):** IRRs are common (reported in 13% of patients in the SOPHIA trial). Symptoms may include fever, chills, tachycardia, dyspnea, and hypotension. Monitor patients during and after infusion. For mild-to-moderate reactions, slow or interrupt the infusion. Permanently discontinue for severe or life-threatening IRRs [US].
- **Cardiac Monitoring:** 
    - Assess LVEF within 4 weeks prior to initiation and every 3 months during treatment [US].
    - **Dose Withholding:** Withhold dose for at least 4 weeks for an absolute decrease in LVEF $\ge$ 16% from pretreatment values, or an LVEF below institutional limits of normal (or < 50%) AND an absolute decrease $\ge$ 10% [US].
    - **Discontinuation:** Permanently discontinue if LVEF decline persists > 8 weeks or if dosing is interrupted > 3 times for LVEF decline [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Very Common (>10%):** Fatigue/asthenia (57%), nausea (33%), diarrhea (25%), vomiting (21%), constipation (19%), headache (19%), alopecia (18%), pyrexia (19%), abdominal pain (17%), peripheral neuropathy (16%), arthralgia/myalgia (14%), cough (14%), decreased appetite (14%), dyspnea (13%), infusion-related reactions (13%), palmar-plantar erythrodysesthesia (13%), and extremity pain (11%) [US].
- **Common (1–10%):** Dizziness (10%), stomatitis (10%), decreased weight (6%), dysgeusia (6%), rash (6%), insomnia (6%), and hypertension (5%) [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** 
    - Occurred in 16% of patients. Most common (>1%) were febrile neutropenia (1.5%), neutropenia/neutrophil count decrease (1.5%), and infusion-related reactions (1.1%) [US].
    - Fatal adverse reactions (1.1%) included viral pneumonia and aspiration pneumonia [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Anthracyclines:** Patients receiving anthracyclines less than 4 months after stopping **MARGENZA** may be at increased risk of cardiac dysfunction. Avoid anthracycline-based therapy for up to 4 months after stopping margetuximab. If concomitant use is unavoidable, monitor cardiac function closely [US].
- **General:** No formal drug interaction studies have been performed. Margetuximab is not expected to be handled by cytochrome P450 enzymes [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **MARGENZA** can cause fetal harm when administered to a pregnant woman. Based on its mechanism of action and animal studies, it may cause oligohydramnios and delayed fetal kidney development [US].
    - **Pregnancy Category:** Not specified by letter [US]. 
    - **Verification:** Verify pregnancy status in females of reproductive potential prior to treatment [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** There is no information regarding the presence of margetuximab in human milk. Because human IgG is present in human milk, and the potential for serious adverse reactions in the breastfed child, consider the developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding alongside the mother's clinical need [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies showed no effect on male or female reproductive organs in cynomolgus monkeys [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use ($\ge$ 65 years):** 20% of trial patients were $\ge$ 65. No overall differences in efficacy were observed. However, those $\ge$ 65 had a higher incidence of Grade $\ge$ 3 adverse reactions (56% vs 47%) and cardiotoxicity (35% vs 18%) compared to younger patients [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** No clinically significant PK differences observed in mild to moderate renal impairment (CLcr 30–89 mL/min). The effect of severe renal impairment is unknown [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No clinically significant PK differences observed in mild hepatic impairment. The effect of moderate or severe hepatic impairment is unknown [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Margetuximab binds to the extracellular domain of the HER2 protein. It inhibits tumor cell proliferation, reduces HER2 shedding, and mediates antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity (ADCC). The Fc region is engineered to increase binding to the activating Fc receptor (CD16A) and decrease binding to the inhibitory Fc receptor (CD32B), enhancing ADCC and NK cell activation [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Steady-state volume of distribution is approximately 5.47 L [US].
    - **Elimination:** Terminal half-life is 19.2 days. Clearance is 0.22 L/day. Concentration decreases to ~3% of steady-state trough 4 months after discontinuation [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Expected to be metabolised into small peptides by catabolic pathways [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **SOPHIA (NCT02492711):** A randomized, open-label trial of 536 patients with HER2+ metastatic breast cancer. Patients received margetuximab (15 mg/kg) plus chemotherapy or trastuzumab plus chemotherapy. 
    - **PFS:** Margetuximab group showed a median Progression-Free Survival of 5.8 months vs 4.9 months in the trastuzumab group (HR 0.76; p=0.033) [US].
    - **OS:** Median Overall Survival was 21.6 months for margetuximab vs 21.9 months for trastuzumab (not statistically significant) [US].
    - **ORR:** Confirmed Objective Response Rate was 22% for margetuximab vs 16% for trastuzumab [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vials:** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) in the original carton to protect from light [US]. Do not freeze. Do not shake [US].
- **Diluted Solution:** If not used immediately, it can be stored at room temperature for up to 4 hours or refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C for up to 24 hours [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Prescription Only (Rx Only) [US].
