# Editorial Product Information: Malathion

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of malathion for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises clinical data, regulatory warnings, and administration guidelines into a single reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis intended for professional reference and is not a substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Malathion (an organophosphate).
- **Chemical Name:** (±) - [(dimethoxyphosphinothioyl) - thio] butanedioic acid diethyl ester.
- **Brand Names:** Ovide [US].
- **ATC Code:** P03AC03 (not explicitly stated in source, provided for clinical reference).
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Topical lotion, 0.5% (0.005 g malathion per mL) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Topical (scalp hair only).
- **Vehicle Components:** Isopropyl alcohol (78%), terpineol, dipentene, and pine needle oil [US].
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Sun Pharmaceutical Industries, Inc. (Labeler); Taro Pharmaceuticals U.S.A., Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Pediculosis Capitis:** Indicated for the treatment of patients infested with *Pediculus humanus capitis* (head lice) and their ova (nits) of the scalp hair [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adults and Children ≥6 years):**
    - Apply a sufficient amount of lotion to **dry** hair and scalp until thoroughly moistened.
    - Special attention should be paid to the back of the head and the neck during application.
    - **Note:** Wash hands immediately after applying the product [US].
- **Post-Application Instructions:**
    - Allow the hair to dry naturally. **Do not use electric heat sources** (e.g., hair dryers) and do not cover the hair [US].
    - The lotion must remain on the hair and scalp for 8 to 12 hours.
    - After the contact period, shampoo the hair.
    - Use a fine-toothed (nit) comb to remove dead lice and eggs.
- **Retreatment:** If live lice are still observed 7 to 9 days after the initial treatment, a second application should be administered [US].
- **Additional Measures:** Evaluate other family members for infestation and treat if necessary to prevent re-infestation [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Age-specific:** Contraindicated in neonates and infants. Their scalps are more permeable, which may lead to increased systemic absorption of the organophosphate [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known sensitivity to malathion or any inactive ingredients in the vehicle (e.g., isopropyl alcohol, pine needle oil) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Flammability Warning:** The lotion is highly flammable due to its 78% isopropyl alcohol content. The product and wet hair must not be exposed to open flames, cigarettes, or electric heat sources (dryers, curlers). Hair must dry naturally and remain uncovered [US].
- **Eye Irritation:** Avoid contact with eyes. In case of accidental exposure, flush immediately with water. If irritation or visual changes persist, seek medical evaluation [US].
- **Chemical Burns:** Use has been associated with stinging sensations and chemical burns, including second-degree burns [US].
- **Skin Irritation:** If skin irritation develops, discontinue use until resolved. If irritation recurs upon reapplication, consult a physician [US].
- **Supervision:** Must only be used on children under the direct supervision of an adult [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Common/Frequency Not Defined:**
    - Scalp and skin irritation [US].
    - Stinging sensations [US].
- **Serious/Rare:**
    - Chemical burns (including second-degree burns) [US].
    - Mild conjunctivitis (following accidental ocular contact) [US].
- **Note:** The potential for contact allergic sensitization with the 0.5% lotion is currently unknown [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Management Notes:** No specific drug-drug interactions are documented in the topical PI [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category B.** Animal reproduction studies (rats/rabbits) at high oral doses showed no evidence of teratogenicity. However, animal studies are not always predictive of human response; use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if malathion is excreted in human milk. Caution should be exercised when administered to nursing mothers [US].
- **Fertility:** Studies in rats at doses 180-fold higher than the anticipated human dose showed no evidence of impaired fertility [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** 
    - Safety and efficacy in children under 6 years of age have not been established in well-controlled trials [US].
    - Strictly contraindicated in neonates and infants due to increased skin permeability [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources (systemic absorption is expected to be low, <10% of dose) [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Malathion is an organophosphate that acts as a pediculicide by inhibiting cholinesterase activity *in vivo* [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** While primarily a pediculicide, it also possesses ovicidal activity [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** 
    - **Absorption:** Inadvertent transdermal absorption is possible; technical grade malathion in an acetone vehicle showed 8% absorption through human skin, though the 0.5% lotion specifically has not been fully characterised for percutaneous absorption [US].
    - **Metabolism/Elimination:** Not specified in available sources.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pivotal Data:** Two controlled clinical trials evaluated the efficacy of the 0.5% lotion.
- **Results:** 
    - Immediately after shampooing: 129/129 (100%) patients were free of live lice.
    - 7 days post-treatment: 114/126 (90.5%) patients remained free of live lice compared to 31/105 (29.5%) in the vehicle-only group [US].
- **Limitations:** Ovicidal efficacy at Day 7 and presence of lice at Day 14 were not evaluated in these specific studies [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Keep away from heat and open flame due to flammability [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only (Prescription Only) [US].
- **Product Code:** NDC 51672-5293-4 [US].
