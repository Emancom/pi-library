# Editorial Product Information: Miconazole Nitrate (Topical)

## Editorial Note

- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised clinical overview for healthcare professionals. It synthesises available data to facilitate safe and effective clinical decision-making.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information (PI) or "Drug Facts" required for prescribing or dispensing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Miconazole nitrate 2% (20 mg per gram).
- **Chemical Class:** Azole antifungal.
- **Brand Name(s):** Antifungal Ringworm Treatment Cream [US].
- **ATC Code:** D01AC02 (Miconazole).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Topical cream containing miconazole nitrate 0.02 g in 1 g of product [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical (External use only).
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Yunqi Cosmetics (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- Treatment of tinea pedis (athlete's foot) [US].
- Treatment of tinea cruris (jock itch) [US].
- Treatment of tinea corporis (ringworm) [US].
- Relief of associated symptoms including itching, burning, cracking, and scaling [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Children ≥2 years)
- **Application:** Apply a thin layer over the affected area twice daily (morning and night) or as directed by a healthcare professional [US].
- **Duration:** Use daily for a total of 4 weeks [US].
- **Administration Instructions:** 
    - Clean and dry the affected area thoroughly before application.
    - For athlete's foot: Pay close attention to spaces between the toes. Patients should wear well-fitting, ventilated shoes and change shoes and socks at least once daily [US].

### Missed Dose
- Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- Use in children under 2 years of age, unless directed by a physician [US].
- Application to the scalp or nails [US].
- Application to open wounds or broken skin [US].
- Known hypersensitivity to miconazole nitrate or any of the inactive ingredients (e.g., propylene glycol, cetearyl alcohol) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **General Warnings:** For external use only. Avoid contact with the eyes. If eye contact occurs, rinse thoroughly with water [US].
- **Monitoring Requirements:** Patients should be advised to stop use and consult a doctor if:
    - Local irritation occurs [US].
    - There is no clinical improvement within 4 weeks of treatment [US].
    - The condition worsens, or clears up and then recurs within a few days [US].
- **Accidental Ingestion:** Keep out of reach of children. In case of accidental ingestion, seek professional medical assistance or contact a Poison Control Center immediately [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Frequency not specified:** Local irritation (burning or stinging) may occur. If irritation is severe or persistent, treatment should be discontinued [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Miconazole is an antifungal agent. It typically acts by inhibiting the synthesis of ergosterol, a critical component of fungal cell membranes [US].
