# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the Product Information (PI) for Levocarnitine Injection provided by the following jurisdiction: **US (FDA)**. 
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for the official Product Information for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority's approved labeling before prescribing.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN).
- **Chemical Class:** A naturally occurring carrier molecule/amino acid derivative ((R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt).
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection, USP [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection, 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency in Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism which result in secondary carnitine deficiency [US-approved].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with end-stage renal disease who are undergoing dialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:**
    - Recommended dose: 50 mg/kg administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion.
    - Loading doses are often used in severe metabolic crises, followed by equivalent doses over the subsequent 24 hours.
    - Frequency: Administer every 3 or 4 hours; never less than every 6 hours.
    - Maintenance: Subsequent daily doses are typically in the range of 50 mg/kg, adjusted as therapy requires. The highest reported dose administered is 300 mg/kg.
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - Recommended starting dose: 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight.
    - Administration: Slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
    - Dose adjustment: Guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy if concentrations are adequate.

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration.
- **Compatibility:** Stable for up to 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when diluted in Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations ranging from 0.5 mg/mL to 8.0 mg/mL [US].

### Monitoring
- Obtain baseline plasma carnitine concentrations before initiating parenteral therapy.
- Weekly and monthly monitoring is recommended, including blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma concentrations (target free carnitine: 35 to 60 µmol/L) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known:** There are no listed contraindications in the available source [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Some reactions occur within minutes of administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and initiate medical management [US].
- **Seizures:** Levocarnitine (oral or intravenous) has been reported to cause seizures in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In patients with a history of seizures, an increase in seizure frequency or severity may occur [US].
- **TMA/TMAO Accumulation:** While not evaluated for the injection, chronic high doses of *oral* levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function or ESRD may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) normally excreted in the urine [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **General:** Transient nausea and vomiting are common. Body odor and gastritis have been reported less frequently.
- **Clinical Trial Data (ESRD patients, frequency ≥5%):**
    - **Body as a Whole:** Abdominal pain (5–21%), Accidental injury (8–12%), Asthenia (8–12%), Chest pain (6–15%), Fever (5–12%), Flu syndrome (15–29%), Headache (3–37%), Infection (10–24%), Injection site reaction (27–38%), Pain (21–35%).
    - **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (18–21%), Hypotension (3–19%), Tachycardia (5–9%).
    - **Digestive:** Diarrhea (9–35%), Vomiting (9–21%), Nausea (5–12%).
    - **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (15–27%), Cough increase (10–18%), Dyspnea (3–11%), Rhinitis (6–11%).
    - **Nervous:** Dizziness (10–18%), Paresthesia (3–12%).
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have occurred when used concomitantly with levocarnitine.
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels in patients on warfarin therapy following the initiation of levocarnitine or after any dose adjustments [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - [US]: Category not explicitly assigned by letter in current source, but reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) revealed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. No adequate or well-controlled studies exist in pregnant women; use only if clearly needed.
- **Breastfeeding:** 
    - Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows indicate increased milk concentrations of carnitine after administration. Risks to the infant from excess intake must be weighed against the benefit to the mother [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing is based on mg/kg (see Section 3). Safety and efficacy are established for the indicated uses.
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in available sources regarding unique dose adjustments, though general caution in dose selection is standard for patients with decreased organ function.
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    - Safety and efficacy of *oral* levocarnitine have not been evaluated in renal insufficiency. 
    - For the *injection*, dose adjustments in ESRD are guided by trough plasma levels (see Section 3) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic/fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites are trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces).
    - **Elimination:** Following a single IV bolus (20 mg/kg), approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours; mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Intravenous administration in ESRD patients results in increased plasma levocarnitine concentrations.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients receiving 20 mg/kg three times weekly after dialysis, mean trough levels increased from 21.3 µmol/L (baseline) to 190 µmol/L after 8 weeks.
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** Studies comparing 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg three times weekly demonstrated dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine concentrations at 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C.
- **Precautions:** Avoid excessive heat. Protect from freezing.
- **Handling:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial, as the formulation contains no preservative [US].
