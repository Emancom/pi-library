# Editorial Product Information: Mavorixafor

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**. 
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical data and regulatory guidance into a single, structured reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory documents for definitive prescribing authority.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Mavorixafor (as mavorixafor hydrobromide).
- **Chemical Class:** CXC chemokine receptor 4 (CXCR4) antagonist.
- **Brand Name:** XOLREMDI [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not yet assigned in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** 100 mg opaque hard gelatin capsules (white body/light blue cap) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** X4 Pharmaceuticals, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **WHIM Syndrome:** Indicated for use in adult and paediatric patients aged 12 years and older with warts, hypogammaglobulinemia, infections, and myelokathexis (WHIM) syndrome to increase the number of circulating mature neutrophils and lymphocytes [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric $\ge$ 12 years)
The recommended once-daily dosage is determined by body weight:
- **Weight > 50 kg:** 400 mg once daily [US].
- **Weight $\le$ 50 kg:** 300 mg once daily [US].

### Administration Instructions
- **Empty Stomach:** Must be administered on an empty stomach following an overnight fast and at least 30 minutes before food [US]. 
- **Integrity of Dosage Form:** Capsules must be swallowed whole; do not open, break, or chew [US].

### Dose Modifications (Drug Interactions)
- **Strong CYP3A4 Inhibitors:** Reduce mavorixafor dose to 200 mg once daily [US].
- **Moderate CYP3A4 or P-gp Inhibitors:** Monitor for adverse reactions; if necessary, reduce dose by 100 mg increments (not below 200 mg) [US].

### Missed Dose
- If a dose is missed, take the next dose as scheduled. Do not exceed one dose per day [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **CYP2D6 Substrates:** Concomitant use with drugs highly dependent on CYP2D6 for clearance is contraindicated due to the risk of increased substrate toxicity [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Embryo-Fetal Toxicity:** Based on its mechanism of action (CXCR4/SDF-1 signalling), mavorixafor is expected to cause fetal harm. Pregnancy must be ruled out before initiation. Women of reproductive potential must use effective contraception during treatment and for 3 weeks after the final dose [US].
- **QTc Interval Prolongation:** Mavorixafor causes concentration-dependent QTc prolongation. 
    - **Monitoring:** Assess QTc at baseline and periodically as clinically indicated.
    - **Risk Factors:** Correct modifiable risk factors (e.g., hypokalaemia) prior to start.
    - **Concomitant Medications:** Use with caution alongside other QTc-prolonging drugs or drugs that increase mavorixafor exposure [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Frequency of Adverse Reactions
The following data is based on Study 1 (a 52-week placebo-controlled trial) [US]:

| Adverse Reaction | Mavorixafor (N=14) | Placebo (N=17) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Thrombocytopenia** | 21% | 0% |
| **Pityriasis** | 14% | 0% |
| **Rash** | 14% | 0% |
| **Rhinitis** | 14% | 0% |
| **Epistaxis** | 14% | 6% |
| **Vomiting** | 14% | 6% |
| **Dizziness** | 14% | 6% |

### Serious Adverse Effects
- **Thrombocytopenia:** Occurred in 21% of treated patients; some cases occurred in the context of infection or febrile neutropenia [US].
- **QTc Prolongation:** Identified as a clinically significant risk [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
### Pharmacokinetic Interactions
- **Effect of Other Drugs on Mavorixafor:**
    - **Strong CYP3A4 Inhibitors:** Increase mavorixafor exposure (approx. 2-fold); dose reduction required [US].
    - **Strong CYP3A4 Inducers:** Expected to decrease mavorixafor effectiveness; avoid concomitant use [US].
    - **P-gp Inhibitors:** May increase mavorixafor exposure; monitor for toxicity [US].
- **Effect of Mavorixafor on Other Drugs:**
    - **CYP2D6 Substrates:** Mavorixafor is a CYP2D6 inhibitor. Use with highly dependent substrates is contraindicated [US].
    - **CYP3A4 Substrates:** May increase exposure of substrates (e.g., midazolam); monitor for adverse reactions [US].
    - **P-gp Substrates:** May increase exposure (e.g., 1.5-fold increase in Digoxin Cmax). Monitor serum digoxin levels [US].
    - **Metformin:** Mavorixafor may decrease metformin exposure (approx. 35%); monitor glycaemic control [US].

### Pharmacodynamic Interactions
- **QTc Prolonging Drugs:** Potential for additive effects on the QTc interval; monitor with ECG [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** No human data available. Animal models suggest essential roles for CXCR4 in embryo-fetal development; fetal harm is expected. [US: Not assigned a letter category; instructions emphasize avoidance and 3-week washout].
- **Breastfeeding:** No data on presence in human milk. Due to potential serious adverse reactions in the infant, breastfeeding is not recommended during treatment and for 3 weeks after the final dose [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy established in patients $\ge$ 12 years. Not established in patients < 12 years [US].
- **Geriatric Use:** Limited data (only 2 patients $\ge$ 65 years in clinical studies); no specific dose adjustments provided [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    - **Mild to Moderate (CLcr 30–89 mL/min):** No dosage adjustment required.
    - **Severe or ESRD (CLcr < 30 mL/min):** Not recommended [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:**
    - **Mild:** No dosage adjustment required.
    - **Moderate to Severe:** Not recommended [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** CXCR4 antagonist. It blocks the binding of the ligand SDF-1$\alpha$/CXCL12. In WHIM syndrome, gain-of-function CXCR4 mutations cause excessive leukocyte retention in bone marrow; mavorixafor mobilises these cells into peripheral circulation [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Absolute neutrophil (ANC) and lymphocyte (ALC) counts peak at 4 hours post-dose [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Median Tmax is 2.8 hours. Food significantly reduces absorption (High-fat meal: Cmax decreased 66%, AUC decreased 55%) [US].
    - **Distribution:** Volume of distribution is 768 L; >93% protein bound [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily CYP3A4; secondary CYP2D6 [US].
    - **Elimination:** Terminal half-life is approximately 82 hours. 74.2% recovered in feces, 13.2% in urine [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Study 1 (NCT03995108):** A 52-week, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of 31 patients (aged $\ge$ 12).
- **Primary Endpoint:** Time Above Threshold (TAT) for ANC ($\ge$ 500 cells/µL) and ALC ($\ge$ 1,000 cells/µL).
- **Results:** Mavorixafor-treated patients spent significantly more time above the ANC threshold (LS mean 15.0 hours) compared to placebo (2.8 hours; p<0.0001). There was also a 60% reduction in the annualized infection rate compared to placebo [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Keep bottle tightly closed and store in the original container to protect from moisture [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
