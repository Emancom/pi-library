# Editorial Product Information: Methylcellulose

## Editorial Note
- Jurisdictions contributing to this editorial: **United States (FDA)**.
- Purpose: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of methylcellulose for use by healthcare professionals, synthesising available regulatory data into a single clinical reference.
- Note: This is an editorial synthesis and does not replace the official Product Information required for prescribing or dispensing in any specific jurisdiction. At the time of this writing, only the United States (FDA) source was provided for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Methylcellulose (4000 mPa.s).
- **Chemical Class:** Bulk-forming laxative (hydrophilic semi-synthetic cellulose derivative).
- **Brand Names:** Citrucel (Reference product), Major Fiber Therapy [US].
- **ATC Code:** A06AC06.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Powder for oral solution. Each 19 g dose (approximately one rounded tablespoon) contains 2 g of methylcellulose [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Preferred Pharmaceuticals Inc., Major Pharmaceuticals [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- Relief of occasional constipation (irregularity) [US].
- Maintenance and restoration of bowel regularity [US].
- Management of bowel disorders when recommended by a healthcare provider [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Instructions
Methylcellulose must be administered with a full glass of liquid to prevent esophageal obstruction or choking.
- **Preparation:** Mix the prescribed dose with at least 8 ounces (approx. 240 mL) of cold water or other fluid. Stir briskly and consume immediately.
- **Additional Hydration:** Intake of an additional glass of water following the dose is recommended to enhance efficacy and safety [US].

### Adult Dosing (Ages 12 Years and Over)
- **Standard Dose:** 19 g (one rounded tablespoon) mixed in 8 ounces of fluid.
- **Frequency:** Up to three times daily at the first sign of constipation or irregularity [US].

### Paediatric Dosing (Ages 6 to 12 Years)
- **Standard Dose:** 9.5 g (half the adult dose) mixed in 8 ounces of fluid.
- **Frequency:** Once daily [US].

### Duration of Use
- Laxative products should not be used for a period exceeding one week unless directed by a physician [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Swallowing Difficulties:** Contraindicated in individuals with any history of difficulty swallowing [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known sensitivity to methylcellulose or any of the inactive ingredients (e.g., FD&C Yellow No. 6) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Critical Warning: Choking Risk
Taking methylcellulose without adequate fluid may cause the powder to swell and block the throat or esophagus. Healthcare professionals should advise patients to seek immediate medical attention if they experience chest pain, vomiting, or difficulty breathing/swallowing after ingestion [US].

### General Precautions
- **Pre-existing Symptoms:** Patients should consult a physician before use if they have abdominal pain, nausea, vomiting, or a sudden change in bowel habits persisting for more than two weeks [US].
- **Treatment Cessation:** Patients should discontinue use and consult a doctor if rectal bleeding occurs or if no bowel movement is produced after use, as these may indicate a more serious underlying condition [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Gastrointestinal:** Esophageal or throat obstruction (if taken with insufficient fluid); abdominal distension or flatulence (typical of bulk-forming fibers).
- **Serious Effects:** Rectal bleeding or failure of bowel movement may occur, requiring clinical evaluation [US].
- **Frequency Data:** Not specified in available source.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic:** Specific drug-drug interactions are not listed in the provided source. However, as a bulk-forming fiber, methylcellulose may potentially affect the absorption of concomitantly administered oral medications [US: Not specified].
- **Clinical Management:** In general, it is advisable to separate the dosing of bulk-forming laxatives from other medications by at least 2 hours.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Not specified in available source.
- **Breastfeeding:** Not specified in available source.
- **Fertility:** Not specified in available source.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Approved for children 6 years and older. For children under 6 years of age, a physician must be consulted before use [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available source; however, clinical caution regarding fluid intake and swallowing mechanics is recommended.
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available source.
- **Sodium/Potassium Content:** Each adult dose contains approximately 3 mg of sodium and 105 mg of potassium. This should be considered in patients on electrolyte-restricted diets [US].
- **Caloric Content:** Each adult dose contributes 60 calories from sucrose and sorbitol [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Methylcellulose is a bulk-forming fiber. It absorbs water in the intestinal lumen, increasing stool bulk and moisture, which mechanically stimulates peristalsis and promotes easier passage of stool.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Not absorbed systemically; it passes through the digestive tract unchanged.
    - **Metabolism:** Unlike some other fibers (e.g., psyllium), methylcellulose is non-fermentable by colonic bacteria, which may result in less intestinal gas production [US].
    - **Onset of Action:** Generally produces an effect within 12 to 72 hours [US].
