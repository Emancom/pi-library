# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of levocarnitine injection for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises available regulatory data into a single, clinically focused reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI or summary of product characteristics (SmPC) required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority's approved labeling for definitive prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP).
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter) [US]. 
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection, 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
- **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion [US].
- **Loading Dose:** In severe metabolic crisis, a loading dose is often followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours.
- **Frequency:** Administer every 3 or 4 hours; intervals should never be less than every 6 hours [US].
- **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses typically range around 50 mg/kg, adjusted based on clinical response. Doses up to 300 mg/kg have been administered [US].

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
- **Administration:** Slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].
- **Titration:** Dose adjustments may be made as early as the third or fourth week of therapy, guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations.

### General Administration Notes
- **Monitoring:** Pre-therapy plasma carnitine levels are recommended. Weekly and monthly monitoring should include blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine level: 35 to 60 µmol/L) [US].
- **Visual Inspection:** Parenteral products must be inspected for particulate matter and discoloration before use [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Some reactions occur within minutes of administration. Discontinue immediately if a severe reaction occurs [US].
- **Neurologic Reactions (Seizures):** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Oral Formulation Cautions:** The safety and efficacy of **oral** levocarnitine have not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency. Chronic high doses of the oral form in ESRD patients may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) [US].
- **Monitoring:** Regular monitoring of plasma carnitine levels and clinical status is essential.

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Serious Adverse Effects
- **Hypersensitivity:** Anaphylaxis and respiratory distress [US].
- **Neurologic:** New-onset or worsening seizures [US].

### Clinical Trial Experience (ESRD Patients)
In double-blind, placebo-controlled trials of patients on hemodialysis, the following events occurred at a frequency $\ge$ 5% regardless of causality:

| Body System | Adverse Events ($\ge$ 5%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Chest pain, headache, infection, injection site reaction, pain, fever, flu syndrome, asthenia, abdominal pain, accidental injury |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension, hypotension, tachycardia, palpitations |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea, vomiting, nausea, dyspepsia, anorexia |
| **Metabolic/Nutritional** | Hypercalcemia, hyperkalemia, hypervolemia, peripheral edema, weight changes |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness, insomnia, depression, paresthesia |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis, cough increase, dyspnea, rhinitis |
| **Musculoskeletal** | Back pain, leg cramps |
| **Skin** | Pruritus, rash |
| **Special Senses** | Amblyopia, taste perversion |
| **Urogenital** | Kidney failure, urinary tract infection |

- **Other Reactions:** Transient nausea/vomiting and body odor have been observed [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have occurred when used concomitantly with levocarnitine. Monitor INR levels after initiating treatment or following dose adjustments [US].
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Levocarnitine is not protein-bound [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Not assigned a letter category in provided source]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) revealed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine concentrations in milk. Risks of excess carnitine intake to the infant must be weighed against the benefits of maternal supplementation [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing recommendations for metabolic disorders apply; see Section 3 [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically detailed in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Specifically indicated for use in ESRD patients on hemodialysis. Use caution with oral formulations in this population due to metabolite accumulation [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria for oxidation and energy production.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In patients with organic acidemias, levocarnitine promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters, which are quickly eliminated.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
  - **Distribution:** Not protein-bound [US].
  - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and $\gamma$-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
  - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours (uncorrected for endogenous levels). Mean apparent terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Pharmacokinetic Study:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients on hemodialysis receiving 20 mg/kg three times weekly showed that trough concentrations (initially ~20 µmol/L) rose to between 54 µmol/L and 180 µmol/L after one week of therapy [US].
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** Studies using 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses in ESRD patients demonstrated a dose-proportional increase in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Protection:** Protect from freezing and avoid excessive heat [US].
- **Stability:** Compatible and stable for up to 24 hours in PVC bags at room temperature when mixed with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer's (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) [US].
- **Handling:** Single-dose vial; discard any unused portion [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources.
