# Editorial Product Information: Mineral Oil

## Editorial Note
- This editorial harmonises product information for **mineral oil** based on the following source:
    - **United States (FDA)**: Product Information/Drug Facts for Mineral Oil (CVS Pharmacy, Inc.)
- The purpose of this document is to provide a unified, clinically accurate overview for healthcare professionals. 
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and should not be used as a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing or dispensing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Mineral oil 99.9% [US]
- **Chemical Class:** Lubricant laxative (aliphatic hydrocarbons)
- **Brand Names:** CVS Mineral Oil [US]
- **ATC Code:** A06AA01
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Oral liquid (999 mg/mL) [US]
- **Route of Administration:** Oral
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** CVS Pharmacy, Inc. [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Occasional Constipation:** For the short-term relief of bowel irregularity; typically induces a bowel movement within 6 to 8 hours [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Children ≥12 years)
- **Dose:** 15 mL to 45 mL (1 to 3 tablespoons) daily [US].
- **Maximum Dose:** 45 mL in any 24-hour period [US].

### Paediatric Dosing (Children 6 to <12 years)
- **Dose:** 5 mL to 15 mL (1 to 3 teaspoons) daily [US].
- **Maximum Dose:** 15 mL in any 24-hour period [US].

### Administration Instructions
- May be administered as a single daily dose or in divided doses [US].
- **Timing:** Must be taken only at bedtime [US].
- **Dietary Restriction:** Do not administer with meals [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Dysphagia:** Do not use in patients with difficulty swallowing [US].
- **Age Restrictions:** Contraindicated in children under 6 years of age [US].
- **Pregnancy:** Do not use during pregnancy [US].
- **Patient Status:** Contraindicated in patients who are bedridden or aged [US].
- **Duration:** Do not use for a period exceeding one week [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None specified in available sources.
- **Undiagnosed Symptoms:** Patients should consult a physician before use if they experience abdominal pain, nausea, vomiting, or a sudden change in bowel habits lasting more than two weeks [US].
- **Treatment Cessation:** Patients should discontinue use and seek medical advice if rectal bleeding occurs or if they fail to have a bowel movement after use, as these may indicate a serious underlying condition [US].
- **Aspiration Risk:** The contraindications for bedridden, aged, and dysphagic patients are critical to mitigate the risk of lipid pneumonia caused by accidental aspiration of mineral oil.

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Frequency Not Specified:** Available sources do not provide frequency data (e.g., % incidence).
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** 
    - Rectal bleeding [US].
    - Potential for lipid pneumonia (inferred from administration warnings regarding aspiration risk).
- **Other:** Failure to have a bowel movement after use (may indicate serious condition) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Stool Softeners:** Do not use concurrently with stool softener laxatives (e.g., docusate) [US].
- **Absorption Interference:** Mineral oil may interfere with the absorption of other medications.
- **Management Note:** Administer mineral oil at least 2 hours before or after other drugs to minimize interaction risks [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **United States:** Contraindicated for use during pregnancy.
- **Breastfeeding:** 
    - **United States:** Patients are advised to consult a healthcare professional before use.
- **Fertility:** Not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** 
    - Children 6–12 years: See Section 3 for dosing. 
    - Children <6 years: Contraindicated [US].
- **Elderly Use:** 
    - Contraindicated in "aged" patients (likely due to aspiration risk) [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Acts as a lubricant laxative; it coats the faecal mass and the intestinal wall with a water-proof film, retaining moisture in the stool and facilitating easier passage through the colon [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** 
    - **Absorption:** Not specified, though generally considered to have minimal systemic absorption under normal conditions.
    - **Elimination:** Excreted in the faeces.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Generally produces a bowel movement within 6 to 8 hours [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage:** Keep the container tightly closed [US].
- **Protection:** Protect from sunlight [US].
- **Safety:** Sealed with printed foil under cap for consumer protection [US].
