# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA)
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of levocarnitine injection for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises the available clinical and regulatory data into a single, structured reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory documents for specific legal and prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter Healthcare Corporation).
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency (Inborn Errors of Metabolism):** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that result in secondary carnitine deficiency [US-approved].
- **Carnitine Deficiency in End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg.
    - **Administration:** Delivered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion.
    - **Initial/Crisis Management:** A loading dose is often administered in severe metabolic crises, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours.
    - **Frequency:** Administered every 3 or 4 hours; should not be administered less frequently than every 6 hours.
    - **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses typically range around 50 mg/kg, adjusted based on clinical response. Doses up to 300 mg/kg have been administered [US].

- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight.
    - **Administration:** Slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
    - **Adjustments:** Dose adjustments (e.g., down to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy, guided by pre-dialysis (trough) plasma levocarnitine concentrations [US].

### Monitoring
- It is recommended to obtain plasma carnitine concentrations prior to starting parenteral therapy.
- Weekly and monthly monitoring is advised, including blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine levels: 35 to 60 µmol/L) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known** [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in patients with ESRD on dialysis. Some reactions occur within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately [US].
- **Seizure Risk:** Seizures have been reported in patients receiving levocarnitine, regardless of whether they had pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **General Renal Precautions:** The safety and efficacy of oral levocarnitine have not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in patients with severely compromised renal function or ESRD may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are often difficult to distinguish from symptoms of the underlying pathology.

### Clinical Trial Experience (ESRD Patients)
In placebo-controlled trials, the following events occurred at a frequency ≥5% (regardless of causality):
- **Body as a Whole:** Abdominal pain (5–21%), headache (3–37%), infection (10–24%), injection site reaction (27–38%), pain (21–35%), fever (5–12%), asthenia (8–12%).
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (18–21%), hypotension (3–19%), tachycardia (5–9%), chest pain (6–15%).
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea (9–35%), nausea (5–12%), vomiting (9–21%).
- **Metabolic:** Hypercalcemia (6–15%), hypervolemia (3–12%).
- **Nervous:** Dizziness (10–18%), paresthesia (3–12%).
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (15–27%), cough increase (10–18%), dyspnea (3–11%).

### Postmarketing Experience
- **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea and vomiting; less frequent reports of body odor and gastritis.
- **Neurologic:** Seizures (see Warnings and Precautions).
- **Immune System:** Anaphylaxis, bronchospasm, and laryngeal edema.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalised Ratio (INR) have occurred when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
    - **Management:** Monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after any dose adjustments in patients on warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Category:** Not explicitly assigned in available source.
    - **Animal Data:** Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at doses up to 3.8 times the human dose revealed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm.
    - **Clinical Recommendation:** There are no adequate/well-controlled studies in pregnant women; use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine concentrations in milk after administration. The benefits of maternal therapy must be weighed against the potential risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders is calculated by weight (50 mg/kg) [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically detailed in available sources; however, monitoring of renal function and clinical status is generally advised.
- **Renal Impairment:** Dosage adjustments in ESRD are based on trough plasma carnitine levels (see Section 3). Oral levocarnitine is cautioned in severe renal impairment due to metabolite accumulation [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters, which are rapidly eliminated.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In ESRD, it restores carnitine levels depleted by reduced intake, reduced synthesis, and dialytic losses.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces).
    - **Elimination:** Following IV bolus (20 mg/kg), approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. Terminal elimination half-life is approximately 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients, 20 mg/kg IV three times weekly after dialysis significantly increased plasma levocarnitine. Trough concentrations rose from a baseline of 21.3 ± 7.7 µmol/L to 190 ± 55 µmol/L after 8 weeks.
- **Dose-Ranging Data:** Studies evaluating 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses showed dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine levels over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Precautions:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat.
- **Handling:** Single-dose vials; discard any unused portion as the formulation contains no preservative. Parenteral products should be visually inspected for particulate matter and discoloration before use [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable for up to 24 hours in PVC bags at room temperature when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer's (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Prescription only (Rx-only). Classification: Levocarnitine Injection, USP [US].
