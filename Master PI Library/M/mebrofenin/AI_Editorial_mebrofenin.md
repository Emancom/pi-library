# Editorial Product Information: Mebrofenin

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial product information has been synthesized from the official Product Information (PI) provided by the **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonized clinical overview for healthcare professionals, integrating regulatory data into a single, structured reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific PI. Clinicians must consult the local approved labeling for prescribing and legal requirements in their respective regions.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Mebrofenin (an iminodiacetic acid [HIDA] derivative).
- **Brand Name(s):** Choletec [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - **Lyophilized Powder for Injection:** Each multi-dose reaction vial contains 45 mg of mebrofenin [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous (after reconstitution with Sodium Pertechnetate Tc 99m).
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Bracco Diagnostics Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Hepatobiliary Imaging:** Indicated for use as a diagnostic radiopharmaceutical agent to visualize the hepatobiliary system [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults)
The suggested intravenous dose for an average (70 kg) patient varies based on serum bilirubin levels [US]:
- **Non-jaundiced patients:** 74–185 MBq (2–5 mCi).
- **Patients with serum bilirubin >1.5 mg/dL:** 111–370 MBq (3–10 mCi).

### Administration Instructions
- **Preparation:** Must be reconstituted with sterile, additive-free Sodium Pertechnetate Tc 99m (up to 3700 MBq/100 mCi). Reconstitution must maintain a nitrogen atmosphere; air should not be introduced.
- **Inspection:** Visually inspect for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration. Use only if the solution is clear.
- **Patient Status:** Patients should ideally fast for at least 4 hours prior to the exam to prevent false positives (non-visualization of the gallbladder due to post-prandial contraction).
- **Repeat Examination:** Allow at least 24 hours between repeat examinations.
- **Calibration:** Measure the patient dose using a radioactivity calibration system immediately before administration.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to mebrofenin or any component of the formulation [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Preparation Only:** Vial contents are intended only for the preparation of Technetium Tc 99m Mebrofenin and must **not** be administered directly to the patient [US].
- **Radiation Safety:** As with all radioactive materials, use caution to minimize radiation exposure to the patient and occupational workers.
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Allergic reactions are a theoretical possibility, particularly in patients receiving multiple doses.
- **Interpretation Caveats:** 
    - Non-visualization of the gallbladder may occur due to prolonged fasting, parenteral feeding, or immediate post-prandial states.
    - Conditions such as hepatocellular disease, chronic cholecystitis, or pancreatitis may result in delayed or absent visualization.
    - In septic patients, hepatobiliary clearance may be absent or delayed.
- **Quality of Radiopharmaceutical:** The labeling reaction requires stannous ion in a reduced state. Do not use sodium pertechnetate solutions containing oxidants [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effect frequencies are not strictly categorized by percentage in the source, but the following have been reported:
- **Rare:** Urticaria and rash [US].
- **Rare (Related Compounds):** Chills and nausea [US].
- **Serious/Class Effect:** Infrequent reports of death have been associated with the use of this class of hepatobiliary imaging agents [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacodynamic Interactions:**
    - **Narcotics (Morphine, Meperidine):** May delay intestinal transit of the imaging agent, potentially leading to non-visualization or false-positive results [US].
- **Chemical/Radiopharmaceutical Interactions:**
    - **Oxidants:** Presence of oxidants in the pertechnetate solution can adversely affect the stability and quality of the Technetium Tc 99m Mebrofenin complex [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:**
    - **US Category:** Not assigned in the current source.
    - Animal reproduction studies have not been conducted. It is unknown if mebrofenin causes fetal harm or affects reproductive capacity. Use in pregnant women only if the expected benefits clearly outweigh potential hazards [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Technetium Tc 99m is excreted in human milk. Formula feedings should be substituted for breastfeeding after administration [US].
- **Fertility:** Long-term animal studies to evaluate the effect on fertility have not been performed [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness in children under the age of 18 have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Elevated serum bilirubin levels increase the renal excretion of the agent (from 1% in 3 hours in normals to approximately 3% in 3 hours in jaundiced patients) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment (Jaundiced Patients):** In patients with jaundice, the blood clearance is slower, hepatobiliary transit may be delayed, and image quality may diminish due to increased background [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Mebrofenin is an iminodiacetic acid (HIDA) derivative that, when labeled with Tc 99m, serves as a tracer for hepatobiliary function. It has no known pharmacologic action at diagnostic doses [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Following IV injection, 17% of the dose remains in the blood at 10 minutes.
    - **Metabolism/Uptake:** Rapidly cleared from circulation; liver visualization occurs by 5 minutes, with maximum uptake at 11 minutes. 
    - **Elimination:** Primarily via the hepatobiliary system. In healthy subjects, intestinal activity is seen within 30–60 minutes. Urinary excretion is minimal (approx. 1% over 3 hours) in healthy subjects but increases in the presence of high serum bilirubin [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Principal radiation emission is Gamma-2 (140.5 keV). The physical half-life of Tc 99m is 6.02 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- Pivotal trial data summaries were not provided in the source document. Clinical utility is established through pharmacokinetic modeling and historical use as a diagnostic agent.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Kit Storage:** Store at 20–25°C (68–77°F) both before and after reconstitution [US].
- **Shelf Life Post-Reconstitution:** Use the prepared injection within 18 hours of formulation [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** 
    - **United States:** Rx-only. 
    - **Licensing:** Use is restricted to persons licensed by the U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission or an Agreement State [US].
