# Editorial Product Information: Mavacamten

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial: **Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial synthesis of mavacamten clinical data for use by healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial overview and does not replace the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Always consult the current local PI for legal prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Mavacamten (Selective, allosteric, and reversible cardiac myosin inhibitor).
- **Brand Name(s):** **CAMZYOS** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Hard capsules containing 2.5 mg, 5 mg, 10 mg, or 15 mg of mavacamten [Australian].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Bristol-Myers Squibb [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Symptomatic Obstructive Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy (oHCM):** Indicated for the treatment of adults with New York Heart Association (NYHA) class II-III obstructive HCM [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Starting Dose:** The recommended initial dose is 5 mg orally once daily [Australian].
- **Maintenance Range:** Individualised doses range from 2.5 mg to 15 mg once daily. The maximum daily dose is 15 mg [Australian].
- **Administration:** Capsules should be swallowed whole with water and may be taken with or without food [Australian].

### Monitoring and Titration
- **Pre-treatment Assessment:** Prior to initiation, left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF) must be assessed via echocardiography. Treatment must not be started if LVEF is <55% [Australian].
- **Initiation Phase:** Patients should be assessed for clinical response at Week 4 and Week 8.
    - **Week 4:** If the Valsalva Left Ventricular Outflow Tract (LVOT) gradient is <20 mmHg, the dose should be reduced to 2.5 mg. If $\ge$20 mmHg and LVEF remains $\ge$50%, 5 mg is maintained.
    - **Week 8:** Dose adjustments continue based on LVOT gradient and LVEF stability.
- **Maintenance Phase:** After the initial titration, LVEF and Valsalva LVOT gradient should be assessed every 3 months. Once a stable dose is achieved (at least 6 months), monitoring may be extended to every 6 months [Australian].

### Dose Interruption (LVEF <50%)
- If LVEF falls below 50% at any visit, treatment must be interrupted for 4 weeks. 
- **Restarting:** If LVEF returns to $\ge$50% after 4 weeks, treatment may be restarted at the next lower dose level (e.g., 10 mg to 5 mg).
- **Discontinuation:** Patients on the 2.5 mg dose who require interruption due to LVEF <50% on two consecutive occasions must discontinue the drug permanently [Australian].

### Missed Doses
- A missed dose should be taken as soon as possible on the same day. The next dose should be taken at the usual time the following day. Two doses must not be taken on the same day [Australian].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to mavacamten or any excipients [Australian].
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions [Australian]:**
    - Concomitant use with **strong CYP2C19 inhibitors**.
    - Concomitant use with **moderate to strong CYP2C19 inducers**.
    - Concomitant use with **moderate to strong CYP3A4 inducers**.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warning: Heart Failure Risk
**CAMZYOS** reduces LVEF and can cause heart failure due to systolic dysfunction. 
- Initiation is not recommended if LVEF <55%. 
- Treatment must be interrupted if LVEF drops below 50% [Australian].
- Regular echocardiogram monitoring is mandatory for safe titration and maintenance [Australian].

### General Warnings
- **Drug-Drug Interactions:** Concomitant use with CYP450 inhibitors or inducers can significantly alter mavacamten levels, increasing the risk of systolic dysfunction or loss of efficacy.
- **Intercurrent Illness:** Serious infections or arrhythmias (e.g., atrial fibrillation) may increase the risk of systolic dysfunction. Dose increases are not recommended during these events [Australian].
- **Embryo-Fetal Toxicity:** Based on animal data, mavacamten may cause fetal harm. Pregnancy must be ruled out before starting treatment [Australian].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse drug reactions (ADRs) identified in pivotal trials (EXPLORER-HCM) include:

| Frequency | Adverse Event |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common** ($\ge$10%) | Dizziness (up to 21%) |
| **Common** (1–10%) | Heart Failure, Systolic Dysfunction (LVEF <50%), Dyspnoea, Nasopharyngitis |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Systolic dysfunction and heart failure are the primary safety concerns. In clinical trials, approximately 6% of patients experienced a reversible reduction in LVEF to <50% [Australian].

## 7. Drug Interactions
### Pharmacokinetic Interactions
- **CYP2C19 and CYP3A4:** Mavacamten is primarily metabolised by CYP2C19 (74%) and CYP3A4 (18%).
    - **Inhibitors:** Weak-to-moderate CYP2C19 or moderate-to-strong CYP3A4 inhibitors increase mavacamten exposure. Dose reduction and increased monitoring are required when starting these agents [Australian].
    - **Inducers:** Strong inducers significantly decrease mavacamten levels and are contraindicated [Australian].
- **Effect on other drugs:** Mavacamten is an inducer of CYP2B6, 3A4, 2C8, 2C19, and 2C9 in vitro, though it did not significantly impact the efficacy of oral contraceptives in clinical studies [Australian].

### Pharmacodynamic Interactions
- **Negative Inotropes:** Use caution when combining mavacamten with beta-blockers, verapamil, or diltiazem. Limited data exists for the use of mavacamten with disopyramide; if initiated, close clinical and echocardiographic monitoring is required [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** **Category D [Australian]**. Mavacamten should not be used during pregnancy. 
- **Contraception:** Females of reproductive potential must use highly effective contraception during treatment and for at least **4 months** after the final dose [Australian].
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if mavacamten is excreted in human milk. A decision should be made to either discontinue breastfeeding or the drug, considering the benefit to the mother and infant [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy have not been established in patients under 18 years [Australian].
- **Elderly Use:** No dose adjustment is required for patients $\ge$65 years [Australian].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment for mild-to-moderate impairment (eGFR 30–90 mL/min/1.73m²). Data are unavailable for severe impairment (<30 mL/min/1.73m²) [Australian].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment for mild (Child-Pugh A) to moderate (Child-Pugh B) impairment. Use with caution in severe (Child-Pugh C) impairment as it has not been specifically studied [Australian].
- **CYP2C19 Poor Metabolisers:** These patients experience significantly higher mavacamten exposure (half-life of ~23 days vs 6-9 days in normal metabolisers) [Australian].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Mavacamten targets cardiac myosin to reduce the number of myosin heads entering power-generating states. This normalises contractility, reduces LVOT obstruction, and improves cardiac filling pressures in HCM patients [Australian].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** ~85% bioavailability; Tmax 1–2 hours.
    - **Distribution:** 97–98% protein bound.
    - **Elimination:** Primarily via CYP2C19-mediated metabolism. Approximately 7% excreted in faeces and 85% in urine (as metabolites) [Australian].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **EXPLORER-HCM:** A Phase 3 trial in 251 patients with oHCM. 37% of mavacamten patients reached the primary composite endpoint (improvement in exercise capacity and NYHA class) vs 17% on placebo. Significant reductions in LVOT gradient were observed [Australian].
- **VALOR-HCM:** Evaluated 112 patients eligible for septal reduction therapy (SRT). After 16 weeks, significantly fewer patients in the mavacamten group (18%) met the primary endpoint (proceeding with SRT or remaining SRT-eligible) compared to the placebo group (77%) [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store below 30°C [Australian].
- **Disposal:** Unused medicine should be returned to a pharmacy [Australian].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule:** **Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine) [Australian]**.
