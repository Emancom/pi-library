## 3. Approved Indications
- Indications stated in source labelling (topical symptomatic use; jurisdictional approval status not established in source):
  - Helps comfort skin during periods of irritation or flare-ups
  - Helps relieve symptoms associated with:
    - Cold sores and herpes outbreaks
    - Eczema and rashes
    - Ringworm and fungal irritation
    - Minor boils and abscesses
    - Insect bites
    - Shingles flare-ups
    - Minor burns
