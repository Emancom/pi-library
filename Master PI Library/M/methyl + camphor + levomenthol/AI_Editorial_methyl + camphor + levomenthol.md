# Editorial Product Information: methyl + camphor + levomenthol

## 1. Product Identification
- Active ingredient (INN/generic name): **methyl + camphor + levomenthol**

  - Camphor, (-)- (camphor) — 59 mg per 1 g
  - Levomenthol (l-menthol) — 8 mg per 1 g
- Chemical class: Not specified in available sources
- ATC code: Not specified in available sources
- Route(s) of administration: Topical (external use only)

## 3. Approved Indications
- For temporary relief of aches and pains of muscles and joints associated with:
  - Arthritis
  - Simple backache
  - Sprains
  - Strains
  - Bruises

(Region-specific indication statements were not provided; indications above are those listed in the available source.)

## 4. Dosage and Administration
### General dosing (adult and paediatric)
- Adults and children 2 years of age and older: apply to the affected area no more than 3 to 4 times daily.
- Children under 2 years of age: consult a doctor before use.

### Administration instructions
- Apply to the affected area as directed; do not exceed the recommended frequency (no more than 3–4 applications per day).
- For external use only.
- Avoid contact with eyes and mucous membranes.
- Do not apply to wounds or damaged skin.
- Do not bandage tightly over the application site or use with a heating pad.
- Product is flammable; keep away from fire, flame, or heat sources during and after application.
