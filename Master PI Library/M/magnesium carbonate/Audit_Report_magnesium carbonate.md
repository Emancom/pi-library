- **Section 13 (Regulatory Information):** This is original synthesis based on metadata within the documents (Schedule 3 vs. OTC).
- **Editorial Tone:** The document maintains a professional, third-party tone appropriate for a healthcare professional summary, rather than a consumer label.

## Differences from Source PIs
- **Harmonisation:** The editorial explicitly links the two jurisdictions (TGA/FDA), which is not present in either source.
- **Inclusion of ATC Codes:** The AI added ATC codes (A02AA01; A06AD), which are not present in the provided source PDF files.
- **Omission of Specifics:** The editorial correctly omits irrelevant details for an editorial overview, such as the full excipient lists, the 7-day lead-up instructions for iron medication, and the specific molecular weights of each component.
