# Editorial Product Information: Meclizine

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial has been synthesised from the official Product Information (PI) document provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document is a harmonised editorial overview designed for healthcare professionals. It aims to provide a clear, clinically accurate reference by consolidating data from the available regulatory source.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official, current Product Information required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Meclizine hydrochloride.
- **Chemical Class:** Histamine (H1) receptor antagonist (piperazine derivative).
- **Brand Name(s):** Not specified in available source (provided document refers to generic Meclizine Hydrochloride distributed by RemedyRepack Inc.).
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Oral tablets: 12.5 mg and 25 mg [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Jubilant Cadista Pharmaceuticals Inc. (Original Manufacturer); RemedyRepack Inc. (Repackager) [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Vertigo:** Indicated for the management of vertigo associated with diseases affecting the vestibular system in adult patients [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** The recommended therapeutic range is 25 mg to 100 mg daily [US].
- **Dosing Schedule:** Administered in divided doses, with the specific regimen determined by the individual patient's clinical response [US].
- **Administration Instructions:** Tablets must be swallowed whole; they should not be crushed or chewed [US].
- **Titration/Missed Dose:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Meclizine is contraindicated in individuals with a known hypersensitivity to meclizine hydrochloride or any of the inactive excipients (including lactose monohydrate and specific FD&C/D&C dyes) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **CNS Depression:** Meclizine may cause significant drowsiness. Patients should be cautioned against performing potentially hazardous tasks, such as driving or operating heavy machinery, until their individual response to the medication is established [US].
- **Anticholinergic Effects:** Due to potential anticholinergic activity, meclizine should be prescribed with caution in patients with a history of:
    - Asthma.
    - Glaucoma.
    - Prostatic hypertrophy (enlargement of the prostate gland) [US].
- **Monitoring:** In patients with known CYP2D6 polymorphisms, monitor for altered clinical effect or increased adverse reactions [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Frequency data is not precisely established as many reactions are reported voluntarily from a population of uncertain size. 
- **Commonly reported:** Drowsiness, dry mouth, headache, fatigue, and vomiting [US].
- **Serious/Systemic:** Anaphylactic reactions [US].
- **Special Senses:** Blurred vision (reported on rare occasions) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **CNS Depressants:** Concurrent use of meclizine with alcohol or other CNS depressants (e.g., sedatives, hypnotics) may result in additive or increased CNS depression [US].
- **CYP2D6 Inhibitors:** Meclizine is a substrate of the CYP2D6 isoenzyme. Co-administration with CYP2D6 inhibitors may increase systemic exposure to meclizine. Clinical monitoring for adverse effects is recommended [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not explicitly letter-coded in the source, but contains a risk summary. 
    - **Human Data:** Epidemiological studies have generally not identified a drug-associated risk of major birth defects during pregnancy [US].
    - **Animal Data:** Fetal malformations were observed in rats at doses of 25 mg/kg (approximately 2 times the maximum recommended human dose on a $mg/m^2$ basis) during organogenesis [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** There are no data regarding the presence of meclizine in human milk, its effects on milk production, or its impact on the breastfed infant. The developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding should be weighed against the maternal clinical need and potential risks [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies assessing the effects of meclizine on fertility have not been conducted [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness in paediatric patients have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Dosing should be cautious, typically starting at the lower end of the dose range (25 mg). This accounts for the higher prevalence of decreased hepatic, renal, or cardiac function and concomitant comorbidities or polypharmacy [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Meclizine has not been evaluated in renal impairment. Caution is advised due to the potential for drug or metabolite accumulation [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** The impact of hepatic impairment on pharmacokinetics has not been evaluated. As meclizine is extensively metabolised, hepatic impairment may increase systemic exposure; use with caution [US].
- **Genetic Polymorphism:** Variations in CYP2D6 (poor, intermediate, extensive, or ultrarapid metabolizers) can lead to significant inter-individual variability in drug exposure [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** The exact mechanism is unknown but is presumed to involve the antagonism of H1 receptors [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Relevant pharmacodynamic data are not currently available [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Median $T_{max}$ is 3 hours (range 1.5 to 6 hours) following oral administration [US].
    - **Distribution:** Human drug distribution characteristics are unknown [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily metabolised in the liver, with CYP2D6 identified as the dominant enzyme in vitro [US].
    - **Elimination:** The plasma elimination half-life is approximately 5 to 6 hours in humans [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions are permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F and 86°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as **Rx Only** (Prescription Only Medication) [US].
