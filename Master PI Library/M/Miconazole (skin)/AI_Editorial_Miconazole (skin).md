# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine Injection

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (US FDA)**. 
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of levocarnitine injection intended for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises clinical data, dosing protocols, and safety information into a single reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and serves as a clinical resource only. It is not a substitute for the official, current Product Information required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); a carrier molecule required for the transport of long-chain fatty acids.
- **Chemical Class:** (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection, USP (Baxter Healthcare Corporation) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL).
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that lead to secondary carnitine deficiency [US-approved].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance dialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg. It should be administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion.
- **Loading Doses:** In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose is often administered, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours.
- **Frequency:** Dosing should occur every 3 to 4 hours (q3h or q4h) and must not be less frequent than every 6 hours (q6h).
- **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses typically range around 50 mg/kg, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilised based on clinical requirement.

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight).
- **Administration:** Administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
- **Dose Adjustments:** Guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Adjustments (e.g., down to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of treatment.

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration.
- Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial.

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known:** There are no listed contraindications for levocarnitine injection in the available source [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions can occur within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, the drug must be discontinued and appropriate therapy initiated [US].
- **Seizures:** Levocarnitine has been associated with seizures in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Monitoring:** For patients with metabolic disorders, it is recommended to obtain plasma carnitine concentrations prior to therapy and perform weekly/monthly monitoring (blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma free carnitine levels between 35 and 60 µmol/L).
- **Renal Impairment (Oral vs IV):** While the IV form is used in ESRD, note that chronic high doses of *oral* levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse events are often confounded by the underlying pathology of ESRD or metabolic errors.

### Common Adverse Effects (≥5%)
In clinical trials of ESRD patients, the following were reported regardless of causality:
- **General:** Pain, injection site reaction, fever, chest pain, headache, infection, flu syndrome, and asthenia.
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension, hypotension, tachycardia.
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea, vomiting, nausea, abdominal pain, dyspepsia.
- **Metabolic:** Hypercalcemia, hypervolemia.
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis, cough increase, dyspnea, rhinitis.

### Serious/Specific Adverse Effects
- **Neurologic:** Seizures.
- **Endocrine:** Reports of body odor and gastritis.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in the International Normalized Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is used concomitantly with warfarin. It is recommended to monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after dose adjustments in patients on anticoagulant therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not specifically assigned in source, but reproductive studies in rats/rabbits at 3.8x human dose showed no evidence of harm]. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed.
- **Breastfeeding:** Specific studies in nursing mothers are unavailable. Levocarnitine is a normal component of human milk, and studies in dairy cows show increased milk concentrations after exogenous administration. Benefits to the mother must be weighed against the risk of excess carnitine intake in the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility was observed in animal reproductive studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy have been established for the treatment of inborn errors of metabolism. Dosing is weight-based (50 mg/kg) [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in the available source.
- **Renal Impairment:** Specifically indicated for use in ESRD patients on hemodialysis. The IV route is preferred in this population to avoid metabolite accumulation associated with oral dosing [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also facilitates the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters, which are renally cleared.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (feces).
    - **Excretion:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. The mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours; the mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Administration to ESRD patients results in a significant increase in plasma levocarnitine concentrations (trough levels often rising from ~20 µmol/L to ~190 µmol/L after 8 weeks of therapy) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** Efficacy was demonstrated in 9-week and 24-week studies in ESRD patients on hemodialysis. Doses of 20 mg/kg post-dialysis consistently maintained trough carnitine levels above the normal physiological range (40–50 µmol/L).
- **Dose-Ranging Data:** A study comparing 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses in ESRD patients showed a dose-proportional increase in trough levocarnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Conditions:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat.
- **Stability:** Compatible and stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) when mixed in Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer's in PVC bags (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL).

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule/Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
