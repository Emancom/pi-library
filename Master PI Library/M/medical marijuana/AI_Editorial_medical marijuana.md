# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- Contributing Jurisdictions: **US (FDA)**.
- Purpose: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of **levocarnitine** injection for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical and regulatory data.
- Disclaimer: This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. At the time of compilation, only the United States (FDA) source documentation was available for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative (carrier molecule).
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection, USP (Baxter Healthcare Corporation) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency in Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:** The recommended starting dose is 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight) administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].

### Titration and Monitoring
- **Metabolic Crisis:** A loading dose is often administered in severe crisis, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours. Dosing should occur every 3 to 4 hours (and no less frequently than every 6 hours) [US].
- **Maintenance (Metabolic):** Daily maintenance doses are typically 50 mg/kg or as clinically required. The maximum recorded dose is 300 mg/kg [US].
- **ESRD Adjustments:** Downward dose adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine levels [US].
- **Target Concentrations:** It is recommended to maintain plasma free carnitine concentrations between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected for particulate matter and discoloration before use.
- **Compatibility:** Stable for up to 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations between 0.5 mg/mL and 8 mg/mL [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known** [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions can occur within minutes of administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately [US].
- **Seizures:** Levocarnitine has been associated with seizures in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been reported [US].
- **Monitoring Requirements:** Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations is recommended [US].
- **Oral Levocarnitine in Renal Impairment:** The safety and efficacy of oral levocarnitine have not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in these patients may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (TMA and TMAO) [US].

- **Common (>10%):** Transient nausea and vomiting.
- **Uncommon/Rare:** Body odor, gastritis, seizures, and drug dependence [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin. Monitoring of INR is recommended upon initiation or dose adjustment of levocarnitine [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not explicitly assigned]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits (up to 3.8 times human dose) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Specific studies in nursing mothers are unavailable. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine concentrations in milk following administration. Risk-benefit analysis should consider the benefits of breastfeeding against the risk of excess carnitine intake in the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility was found in animal reproductive studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing is based on mg/kg body weight; see Section 3 [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Specifically indicated for ESRD patients on dialysis (see Section 3 for dosing and Section 5 for precautions regarding oral administration) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In ESRD, it alleviates metabolic abnormalities resulting from reduced renal synthesis and dialytic losses [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not protein-bound. Distribution half-life is 0.585 hours [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (TMAO) and γ-butyrobetaine [US].
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an intravenous dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. Terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours (uncorrected for endogenous levels) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Efficacy:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients on dialysis (20 mg/kg three times weekly post-dialysis) demonstrated that trough carnitine levels increased from ~20 µmol/L to between 54 and 180 µmol/L after one week, remaining stable thereafter. 
- **Symptom Improvement:** While levocarnitine increases carnitine concentrations, its effect on specific clinical outcomes (e.g., muscle weakness, malaise) in ESRD patients has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat.
- **Preservative:** None; discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial [US].
