## Recommendation
**REJECT / FAIL**

**Reasoning:** While the document successfully merges information from two sources, it relies far too heavily on verbatim strings. In several sections (Dosage, Mechanism of Action, Pharmacokinetics, and Warnings), the AI has simply copied and pasted sentences directly from the source PDFs. 

**Required Revisions:**
1.  **Rewrite Mechanism of Action:** Describe the binding to CCR4 and ADCC in original language.

4.  **Simplify PK:** Instead of copying the exact phrasing for distribution and clearance, summarize the clinical relevance of the 17-day half-life.
