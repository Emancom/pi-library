# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **US (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising regulatory data into a clinically relevant format.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific PI required for prescribing and dispensing in a particular region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** **Levocarnitine** (USP); chemical name: (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Not specifically named as a proprietary brand in the provided source (Generic Levocarnitine Injection by Baxter) [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available source.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile injection solution, 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency:** Indicated for both acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that lead to secondary carnitine deficiency (e.g., glutaric aciduria II, methyl malonic aciduria, propionic acidemia, and medium-chain fatty acylCoA dehydrogenase deficiency) [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance hemodialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg. This may be administered as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion. 
- **ESRD on Hemodialysis:** The recommended starting dose is 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight).

### Administration Instructions
- **Metabolic Disorders:** In acute respiratory or metabolic crisis, a loading dose is often followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours. Dosing should occur every 3 or 4 hours, and never less frequently than every 6 hours. The maximum recorded dose is 300 mg/kg [US].
- **ESRD:** Administer as a 2 to 3 minute bolus into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
- **Visual Inspection:** Parenteral products should be inspected for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration [US].
- **Compatibility:** **Levocarnitine** is stable for up to 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations between 0.5 mg/mL and 8 mg/mL [US].

### Titration and Monitoring
- Dosing in ESRD should be guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma **levocarnitine** levels. Adjustments (e.g., downward to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of treatment [US].
- Recommended plasma free carnitine concentrations should ideally be maintained between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in patients with ESRD on dialysis. These may occur within minutes of administration. In the event of a severe reaction, discontinue treatment immediately and assess the risks versus benefits before attempting re-administration [US].
- **Seizures:** **Levocarnitine** (oral or IV) has been associated with reports of seizures in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with a known history, an increase in seizure frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Oral Formulation Cautions:** The safety and efficacy of oral **levocarnitine** have not been established in patients with renal insufficiency. High-dose oral administration in these patients may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites, specifically trimethylamine (TMA) and trimethylamine-N-oxide (TMAO), which are normally excreted in the urine [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Commonly Observed:** Transient nausea and vomiting. Less frequent effects include body odor, nausea, and gastritis [US].
- **Dialysis Population Data (≥5% Frequency):**
    - **Body as a Whole:** Abdominal pain (5–21%), headache (3–37%), injection site reaction (27–38%), pain (21–35%), fever (5–12%), flu syndrome (15–29%) [US].
    - **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (18–21%), hypotension (3–19%), tachycardia (5–9%) [US].
    - **Digestive:** Diarrhea (9–35%), vomiting (9–21%), nausea (5–12%) [US].
    - **Neurological/Respiratory:** Dizziness (10–18%), paresthesia (3–12%), cough increase (10–18%), pharyngitis (15–27%) [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in the International Normalized Ratio (INR) when **levocarnitine** is co-administered with warfarin. It is recommended to monitor INR levels following the initiation of treatment or after dose adjustments [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Not formally categorised by the lettered system in the source, but reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (based on surface area) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed, as animal studies are not always predictive of human response [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Specific studies in nursing mothers have not been conducted. Levocarnitine is known to increase in the milk of dairy cows following administration. The benefits of maternal supplementation must be weighed against potential risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies (rats/rabbits) [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing recommendations are integrated into the general and metabolic disorder dosing sections [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in the available source.
- **Renal Impairment:** See "Warnings and Precautions" regarding the accumulation of metabolites with oral formulations and "Dosage and Administration" for ESRD IV dosing [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** **Levocarnitine** is a naturally occurring substance essential for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into the mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Following a single IV dose, approximately 76% is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. The mean apparent terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].
    - **Bioavailability:** The absolute bioavailability of oral formulations (tablets and solution) is approximately 15–16% [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients on hemodialysis, IV administration of 20 mg/kg three times weekly significantly increased plasma **levocarnitine** concentrations. Trough levels rose from a baseline of 21.3 ± 7.7 µmol/L to 190 ± 55 µmol/L after 8 weeks of therapy.
- **Dose-Ranging Data:** A study comparing 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses three times weekly showed dose-proportional increases in trough concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store between 20°C and 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat. The product contains no preservative; any unused portion of a single-dose vial must be discarded [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as Rx-only (Prescription Only) [US].
