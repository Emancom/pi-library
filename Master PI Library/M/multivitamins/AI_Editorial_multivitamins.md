# Editorial Product Information: Multivitamins with Sodium Fluoride (Flotrex™)

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official product documentation from the following jurisdiction:
    - **United States (FDA)**: Product labeling for Flotrex™ (multivitamins, sodium fluoride 0.25 mg tablet, chewable) by PureTek Corporation.
- **Purpose**: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical and regulatory information into a single reference.
- **Disclaimer**: This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. It is noted that the source document for the United States indicates that this specific formulation has not been found by the FDA to be safe and effective, and the labeling has not been approved by the FDA [US].

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients**: 
    - **Fluoride**: 0.25 mg (as Sodium Fluoride)
    - **Vitamin A**: 750 mcg RAE (as Retinyl Acetate)
    - **Vitamin C**: 60 mg (as Ascorbic Acid 24 mg and Sodium Ascorbate 36 mg)
    - **Vitamin D3**: 10 mcg / 400 IU (as Cholecalciferol)
    - **Vitamin E**: 6.75 mg (as DL-alpha Tocopheryl Acetate)
    - **Thiamin (B1)**: 1.05 mg (as Thiamine Mononitrate)
    - **Riboflavin (B2)**: 1.2 mg
    - **Niacin (B3)**: 13.5 mg (as Niacinamide)
    - **Vitamin B6**: 1.05 mg (as Pyridoxine HCl)
    - **Folate**: 510 mcg DFE / 300 mcg Folic Acid
    - **Vitamin B12**: 4.5 mcg (as Cyanocobalamin)
- **Brand Name**: **Flotrex™** [US]
- **ATC Code**: Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength**: Chewable tablet containing 0.25 mg Fluoride and ten essential vitamins [US].
- **Route of Administration**: Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor**: PureTek Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- Prevention and treatment of vitamin and fluoride deficiencies in patients at high risk for dental caries and inadequate dietary intake of essential vitamins [US].
- Support of normal growth and development, promotion of bone and dental health, and maintenance of overall metabolic and immune function [US].
- Clinical dietary management of the metabolic processes of caries prophylaxis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult/Pediatric)**: One tablet daily [US].
- **Administration Instructions**: 
    - Tablets should be chewed or dissolved in the mouth before swallowing [US].
    - **Caution**: Patients should not eat or drink dairy products within one hour of fluoride administration [US].
- **Age Restrictions**: Do not administer chewable tablets to children younger than 4 years of age due to the risk of choking [US].
- **Specific Recommendations**: The American Academy of Pediatrics recommends daily fluoride supplementation for children up to age 16 in areas where drinking water contains sub-optimal fluoride levels [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to any of the active ingredients or excipients (including aspartame, sucrose, and specific colourants like Red 27 Lake and FD&C Blue No. 1) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings**: None.
- **General Warnings**:
    - **Choking Hazard**: Not recommended for children under age 4 [US].
    - **Accidental Overdose**: Keep out of reach of children. Seek immediate professional assistance or contact a Poison Control Center in case of accidental overdose [US].
- **Specific Clinical Precautions**:
    - **Dental Fluorosis**: The suggested dose should not be exceeded; prolonged ingestion of excessive fluoride can lead to dental fluorosis [US].
    - **Pre-prescribing Assessment**: Clinicians should determine the fluoride content of the patient's primary drinking water and account for other fluoride sources (e.g., medications, swallowed toothpaste) before prescribing [US].
    - **Pernicious Anemia Masking**: Folate doses above 0.1 mg daily may obscure the diagnosis of pernicious anemia by inducing haematologic remission while allowing irreversible neurological damage to progress [US].
    - **Phenylketonurics**: Contains Phenylalanine (3.0 mg per tablet) [US].
- **Monitoring Requirements**: Children should receive semiannual dental examinations to monitor for dental caries and potential signs of fluorosis [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Frequency Not Defined**:
    - **Immune System**: Allergic sensitization has been reported following oral and parenteral administration of folate [US].
    - **General**: Allergic and idiosyncratic reactions are possible even at low levels [US].
    - **Note**: Adverse reactions are typically associated with vitamin/mineral levels substantially higher than those contained in this product [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Dairy Products**: Calcium-containing dairy products may interfere with fluoride absorption; avoid consumption within one hour of dosing [US].
- **Folate and Vitamin B12**: High doses of folate may mask B12 deficiency (pernicious anemia) [US].
- **General Note**: Clinicians should consider the patient's full medical history and consumption of other drugs, herbs, or supplements [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy**: Not specified in available sources.
- **Breastfeeding**: Not specified in available sources.
- **Fertility**: Not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use**:
    - Formulated for children 6–16 years of age in areas where drinking water fluoride is <0.3 ppm [US].
    - Contraindicated for children <4 years old due to choking risk [US].
- **Elderly Use**: Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment**: Not specified in available sources.
- **Hepatic Impairment**: Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action**:
    - Fluoride ions react with hydroxyapatite in developing teeth to form fluorapatite, a crystal more resistant to acid demineralization and caries [US].
    - Deposition occurs in three stages: incorporation into enamel crystals during formation, continued surface deposition after enamel is laid down, and post-eruption acquisition from water, food, and saliva [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics**:
    - **Absorption**: Tablets provide both topical and systemic sources of fluoride [US].
    - **Distribution/Metabolism/Elimination**: Not specified in available sources.
- **Pharmacodynamics**: Provides ten essential vitamins and fluoride to support metabolic processes and dental health [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- Not specified in available sources. The product labeling explicitly states the drug has not been found by the FDA to be safe and effective via the standard approval process [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature**: Store at controlled room temperature, 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [US].
- **Handling**: 
    - Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container with a child-resistant closure [US].
    - Do not use if the bottle seal is broken [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States**: Classified as a Human Prescription Drug (Rx-Only), though noted as "unapproved" [US].
- **Poison Schedule**: Not applicable/not specified in available sources.
