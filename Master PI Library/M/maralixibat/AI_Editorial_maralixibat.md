# Editorial Product Information: Maralixibat

## Editorial Note
- **Jurisdictions included in this editorial:** US (FDA).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of maralixibat product information for use by healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This synthesis is an editorial summary intended for informational purposes and is not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Maralixibat chloride (ileal bile acid transporter [IBAT] inhibitor).
- **Brand Name:** **LIVMARLI** [US].
- **ATC Code:** A04AB02 (Note: Not explicitly listed in source PI but assigned by WHO).
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths [US]:**
    - **Oral Solution:** 9.5 mg/mL (for ALGS) and 19 mg/mL (for PFIC).
    - **Oral Tablets:** 10 mg, 15 mg, 20 mg, and 30 mg.
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Mirum Pharmaceuticals, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Alagille Syndrome (ALGS):** Indicated for the treatment of cholestatic pruritus in pediatric patients aged 3 months and older [US].
- **Progressive Familial Intrahepatic Cholestasis (PFIC):** Indicated for the treatment of cholestatic pruritus in pediatric patients aged 12 months and older [US].
    - **Limitations of Use:** Not recommended for PFIC type 2 patients with specific *ABCB11* variants that result in a complete absence of functional bile salt export pump (BSEP) protein [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Administration
- Administer approximately 30 minutes before a meal [US].
- **Oral Solution Selection:** Use the 9.5 mg/mL strength for ALGS and the 19 mg/mL strength for PFIC. These strengths are not interchangeable for PFIC patients [US].
- **Tablets:** May be used for either indication in patients weighing $\ge$ 25 kg who are capable of swallowing tablets [US].

### Dosing for Alagille Syndrome (ALGS)
- **Starting Dose:** 190 mcg/kg once daily for one week [US].
- **Recommended Maintenance Dose:** 380 mcg/kg once daily [US].
- **Maximum Daily Dose:** 28.5 mg (3 mL) for the oral solution; 30 mg for the tablets [US].

### Dosing for Progressive Familial Intrahepatic Cholestasis (PFIC)
- **Titration Schedule:**
    - **Starting Dose:** 285 mcg/kg once daily in the morning [US].
    - **Week 2:** Increase to 285 mcg/kg twice daily [US].
    - **Week 3:** Increase to 428 mcg/kg twice daily [US].
    - **Maintenance:** 570 mcg/kg twice daily, as tolerated [US].
- **Maximum Daily Dose:** 38 mg (2 mL) for the oral solution; 40 mg for the tablets [US].

### Missed Dose
- **Once Daily Dosing:** Take within 12 hours of the missed time. If >12 hours, skip and resume the next day [US].
- **Twice Daily Dosing:** Take within 6 hours of the missed time. If >6 hours, skip and resume the next scheduled dose [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hepatic Decompensation:** Contraindicated in patients with prior or active hepatic decompensation events (e.g., variceal hemorrhage, ascites, or hepatic encephalopathy) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Hepatotoxicity
Maralixibat is associated with drug-induced liver injury. Baseline liver tests (ALT, AST, TB, DB, and INR) must be obtained. Monitor LFTs frequently for the first 6 to 8 months of therapy and as clinically indicated thereafter. Dose reduction or interruption should be considered if liver test abnormalities or signs of clinical hepatitis occur. **LIVMARLI** must be permanently discontinued if a hepatic decompensation event occurs or if liver abnormalities persist/recur upon rechallenge [US].

### Gastrointestinal Adverse Reactions
Diarrhea and abdominal pain are very common. Dosage reduction or interruption should be considered for persistent diarrhea, abdominal pain, or symptoms such as bloody stool, vomiting, or dehydration. If symptoms resolve, the drug may be restarted at the last tolerated dose and titrated upward as tolerated [US].

### Fat-Soluble Vitamin (FSV) Deficiency
Inhibition of the IBAT may interfere with the absorption of vitamins A, D, E, and K. Obtain baseline FSV levels and monitor during treatment. Supplementation is recommended if deficiency is identified. If FSV deficiency leads to clinical complications (e.g., bone fractures or bleeding), treatment should be interrupted until deficiency is corrected [US].

### Propylene Glycol Toxicity
The oral solution contains propylene glycol (364.5 mg/mL). Children under 5 years are at increased risk of toxicity. Clinicians must calculate the total daily intake of propylene glycol from all sources. Monitor for signs of toxicity, including hemolysis, hyperosmolarity, metabolic acidosis, and CNS toxicity [US].

### Postmarketing Experience
The following reactions have been identified: hematemesis, liver transplant, post-endoscopy/biopsy hemorrhage, intracranial hemorrhage, and increased GGT [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Bile Acid Sequestrants:** These may bind to maralixibat in the gut, reducing its efficacy. Administer maralixibat at least 4 hours before or 4 hours after taking bile acid binding resins (e.g., cholestyramine, colestipol) [US].
- **OATP2B1 Substrates:** Maralixibat is an *in vitro* OATP2B1 inhibitor. It may decrease the absorption of drugs relying on this transporter, such as certain statins. Monitoring of substrate efficacy is recommended [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Systemic absorption is minimal; however, maralixibat may decrease the absorption of fat-soluble vitamins, which are essential for fetal development. Monitoring and supplementation of FSVs are required during pregnancy [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** There are no data on the presence of the drug in human milk. Because systemic absorption is low, exposure to the infant via breast milk is expected to be minimal. FSV levels in the lactating patient should be monitored [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies in rats showed no effects on fertility at doses significantly higher than the human recommended dose [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Pediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy are established for ALGS in patients $\ge$ 3 months and for PFIC in patients $\ge$ 12 months. Not recommended for children under these respective age limits due to lack of data [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Safety and efficacy have not been established in patients aged 65 years and older [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Not studied in patients with renal impairment or end-stage renal disease [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Clinical trials included patients with baseline impairment. Efficacy and safety have not been established in patients with clinically significant portal hypertension or decompensated cirrhosis [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Maralixibat is a reversible inhibitor of the ileal bile acid transporter (IBAT), which reduces the reabsorption of bile acids from the terminal ileum, thereby increasing fecal excretion and reducing systemic bile acid levels [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Minimally absorbed; plasma concentrations are typically below the limit of quantification (<0.25 ng/mL) [US]. 
    - **Distribution:** 91% protein bound *in vitro* [US].
    - **Metabolism:** No metabolites detected in plasma [US].
    - **Elimination:** Primarily excreted in feces (73%), with <0.1% excreted in urine [US]. The mean half-life is 1.6 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ALGS (Trial 1):** An 18-week open-label study followed by a 4-week randomized withdrawal period. Patients receiving maralixibat maintained a significant reduction in pruritus (measured via ItchRO[Obs]) compared to those switched to placebo ($p < 0.05$) [US].
- **PFIC (Trial 2):** A 26-week randomized, placebo-controlled trial. Maralixibat demonstrated a statistically significant improvement in pruritus compared to placebo ($p < 0.0001$) in PFIC types 1, 2, 3, 4, and 6. No improvement was observed in BSEP3 (truncated protein) variants [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Unopened Bottles:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C [US].
- **Opened Oral Solution:** Store below 30°C (86°F). Discard any remaining solution 100 days after first opening the bottle [US].
