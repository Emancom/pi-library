# Editorial Product Information: Metronidazole (Vaginal)

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** US (FDA)
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of metronidazole vaginal gel (0.75%) for healthcare professionals, synthesised from official product information.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. At the time of this writing, only the United States (FDA) source documentation was provided for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Metronidazole (synthetic nitroimidazole antibacterial/antiprotozoal).
- **Brand Name(s):** Metronidazole Vaginal Gel [US].
- **ATC Code:** G01AF01 (Metronidazole).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Intravaginal gel, 0.75% (7.5 mg/g). Each 5 g applicator-full delivers approximately 37.5 mg of metronidazole [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravaginal use only. Not for ophthalmic, dermal, or oral use.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** RemedyRepack Inc. (Repacker); Saptalis Pharmaceuticals, LLC (Original Manufacturer) [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Bacterial Vaginosis:** Indicated for the treatment of bacterial vaginosis (formerly identified as *Haemophilus* vaginitis, *Gardnerella* vaginitis, nonspecific vaginitis, *Corynebacterium* vaginitis, or anaerobic vaginosis) [US].
- **Note on Diagnosis:** Diagnosis is clinically defined by a homogeneous vaginal discharge with pH >4.5, a "fishy" amine odor upon addition of 10% KOH, and the presence of clue cells on microscopy. Gram stain results typically show reduced *Lactobacillus* and predominance of *Gardnerella* morphotypes [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Adult Dosing:** One applicator-full (approx. 5 g of gel) administered intravaginally once or twice daily for five consecutive days [US].
- **Bedtime Administration:** For once-daily regimens, the dose should be administered at bedtime [US].
- **Administration Instructions:** 
    - Fill the provided applicator by screwing it onto the tube and squeezing until the plunger reaches the stop point.
    - Insert the applicator into the vagina and depress the plunger to deposit the gel.
    - The applicator should be washed in warm soapy water and rinsed thoroughly between uses if a twice-daily regimen is prescribed [US].
- **Missed Dose:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** History of hypersensitivity to metronidazole, parabens, other ingredients in the formulation, or other nitroimidazole derivatives [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None [US].
- **Neurologic Effects:** Convulsive seizures and peripheral neuropathy (characterised by extremity numbness or paresthesia) have occurred with systemic metronidazole. Therapy should be discontinued immediately if abnormal neurologic signs appear. Use with caution in patients with central nervous system diseases [US].
- **Psychotic Reactions:** Reported in patients using systemic metronidazole and disulfiram concurrently. Metronidazole should not be administered to patients who have taken disulfiram within the last two weeks [US].
- **Vaginal Candidiasis:** Symptomatic candidavaginitis may occur or become more prominent during or immediately after therapy (incidence 6–10%) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Patients with severe hepatic disease may experience accumulation of metronidazole and its metabolites; use with caution [US].
- **Ocular Irritation:** Ingredients may cause burning/irritation of the eye. In case of contact, rinse with cool tap water [US].
- **Patient Advice:** Patients should avoid vaginal intercourse during the treatment period [US].

**Serious Adverse Effects:** Convulsive seizures and peripheral neuropathy (rare but serious; reported primarily with systemic formulations) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin/Coumarin Anticoagulants:** Systemic metronidazole potentiates the anticoagulant effect, prolonging prothrombin time. Monitor PT/INR if co-administered [US].
- **Lithium:** Short-term systemic metronidazole has been associated with elevated serum lithium levels and potential toxicity [US].
- **Cimetidine:** May prolong the half-life and decrease plasma clearance of metronidazole [US].
- **Alcohol:** A disulfiram-like reaction (flushing, nausea, tachycardia) may occur. While systemic absorption is low with vaginal administration, an interaction cannot be excluded [US].
- **Drug/Laboratory Test Interactions:** Metronidazole may interfere with serum chemistry values (AST, ALT, LDH, triglycerides, glucose hexokinase) by affecting absorbance at 340 nm, potentially resulting in values of zero [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Narrative Risk Assessment]. Metronidazole is a carcinogen in rodents. While animal studies (oral dosing) showed no fetotoxicity at 6x human dose, some intrauterine deaths were seen with intraperitoneal dosing. There are no adequate, well-controlled studies in pregnant women; use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US]. 
- **Breastfeeding:** Metronidazole is secreted in human milk at concentrations similar to plasma following oral administration. Because of the potential for tumorigenicity in animal studies, a decision should be made to either discontinue nursing or the drug, considering the importance of the drug to the mother [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in mice at 6x human oral dose (mg/m²) [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness in children have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Use with caution in severe hepatic disease due to potential drug accumulation [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** The 5-nitro group of metronidazole is reduced by anaerobes. The reduced form interacts with bacterial DNA, though the exact bactericidal mechanism remains partially unknown. It is active against *Gardnerella vaginalis*, *Bacteroides* spp., *Mobiluncus* spp., and *Peptostreptococcus* spp [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Bioavailability is approximately 2–4% of a 500 mg oral dose. Mean peak serum concentration (Cmax) is 237 ng/mL (reached 6–12 hours post-dose) [US].
    - **Distribution:** Crosses the placental barrier and enters fetal circulation rapidly [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Metabolized primarily in the liver [US].
    - **Elimination:** Systemic metronidazole and metabolites are excreted; may cause darkened urine [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Efficacy Study:** In a randomized, single-blind trial of 505 non-pregnant women with bacterial vaginosis, clinical cure rates at 4 weeks post-treatment were 53% (once-daily regimen) and 57% (twice-daily regimen) [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at controlled room temperature 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
- **Precautions:** Protect from freezing. Avoid exposure to extreme heat or cold [US].

- **Legal Classification:** [US] Rx-only.
