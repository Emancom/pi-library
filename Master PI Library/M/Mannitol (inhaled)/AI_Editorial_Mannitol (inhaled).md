# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine Injection

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised clinical overview for healthcare professionals. It synthesises available regulatory data into a single, cohesive reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and serves as a clinical resource only. It is not a substitute for the official, current Product Information approved in the specific jurisdiction of practice, which should always be consulted prior to prescribing or administering medication.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP).
- **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule/Amino acid derivative [(R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium].
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter Healthcare Corporation) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency in Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that result in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
- **General Dosing:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg [US].
- **Administration:** Administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion [US].
- **Acute Crisis:** A loading dose is often given in patients with severe metabolic crisis, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours. Dosing should occur every 3 to 4 hours (q3h or q4h) and never less than every 6 hours (q6h) [US].
- **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses typically range around 50 mg/kg, though higher doses (up to 300 mg/kg) have been administered based on clinical requirements [US].

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
- **Administration:** Administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].
- **Dose Adjustment:** Adjustments should be guided by pre-dialysis (trough) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].

### Monitoring and General Instructions
- **Plasma Concentration Monitoring:** It is recommended to obtain plasma carnitine levels prior to therapy. Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L) is advised [US].
- **Visual Inspection:** Parenteral products must be inspected for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration [US].
- **Missed Dose:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Known Hypersensitivity:** While not explicitly listed as a "Contraindication" section entry in the source, the "Warnings" section dictates discontinuation in the event of severe hypersensitivity [US].
- **General:** None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None [US].
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients on dialysis. These may occur within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and initiate medical management [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with known seizure disorders, an increase in frequency or severity of seizures has been observed [US].
- **Oral Levocarnitine Limitation:** The safety and efficacy of **oral** levocarnitine have not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency. High oral doses in ESRD patients may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are often confounded by the underlying pathology of ESRD or metabolic disorders.

### Clinical Trial Experience (ESRD Patients)
In placebo-controlled trials, the following events occurred at a frequency of ≥5%:
- **Common (>10%):** Hypertension (18–21%), Diarrhea (9–35%), Headache (3–37%), Injection site reaction (27–38%), Pain (21–35%), Nausea (5–12%), Vomiting (9–21%), Fever (5–12%), Abdominal pain (5–21%) [US].
- **Common (1–10%):** Chest pain, Asthenia, Infection, Dizziness, Insomnia, Rhinitis, Dyspnea, Pharyngitis [US].

### Serious/Post-marketing Adverse Effects
- **Neurological:** Seizures [US].
- **Immunological:** Anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, bronchospasm [US].
- **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea and vomiting; body odor and gastritis (less frequent) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalised Ratio (INR) have occurred when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not specifically lettered]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine concentrations in milk after exogenous administration. Weigh the risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant against the benefits of supplementation to the mother. Consider discontinuing nursing or the drug [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility was found in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for paediatric patients is integrated into the general weight-based dosing (50 mg/kg) for metabolic disorders. Clinical trial data specific to paediatric ESRD was not detailed in the source.
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Primarily indicated for ESRD. Dosage is adjusted based on pre-dialysis carnitine levels [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring carrier molecule required for mammalian energy metabolism. it facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane for beta-oxidation and energy production [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Levocarnitine promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids in patients with metabolic defects by forming acylcarnitine esters, which are rapidly excreted [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and [³H]-γ-butyrobetaine (faeces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Following IV bolus (20 mg/kg), approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours. Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours; mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Study:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients receiving 20 mg/kg IV post-dialysis three times weekly showed that trough levocarnitine levels increased from ~20 µmol/L (pre-therapy) to 190 ± 55 µmol/L after 8 weeks. Normal levels are typically 40–50 µmol/L [US].
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** Studies comparing 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses showed dose-proportionate increases in trough carnitine levels over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Conditions:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat.
- **Handling:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial; the formulation contains no preservatives [US].
- **Stability (Admixture):** Compatible and stable for 24 hours in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer's solution when stored at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags [US].
