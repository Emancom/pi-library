## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredients:** Methyl Salicylate, Camphor (-), and Levomenthol (L-Menthol).
*   **Chemical Class:** Topical Analgesics/Counter-irritants.
*   **Brand Name(s):** Antiphlamine [US].
*   **ATC Code:** M02AC (Preparations with salicylic acid derivatives - suspected/not specified in available sources).
*   **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    *   **Topical Patch:** Each gram contains Methyl Salicylate 46 mg, Camphor (-) 59 mg, and Levomenthol 8 mg. 
    *   **Packaging:** 1 g of product per patch [US].
*   **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical.
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** 
    *   **Labeler/Registrant:** Masaem Company [US].
    *   **Distributor:** Bio Mission Group, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Musculoskeletal Pain:** For the temporary relief of minor aches and pains of muscles and joints associated with:
    *   Arthritis [US]
    *   Simple backache [US]
    *   Sprains [US]
    *   Strains [US]
    *   Bruises [US]

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Children 2 years of age and over)
*   **Application:** Apply the patch to the affected area.
*   **Frequency:** Do not apply more than 3 to 4 times daily [US].

### Administration Instructions
*   For external use only.
*   Do not bandage the treated area tightly [US].
*   Do not use with a heating pad [US].
*   Avoid contact with the eyes and mucous membranes [US].

### Missed Dose
*   Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
*   **Damaged Skin:** Do not apply to wounds or damaged skin [US].
*   **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to methyl salicylate, camphor, menthol, or any inactive ingredients (acetone, chloroxylenol) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### General Warnings
*   **Flammability:** The product is flammable. Keep away from fire, flame, sparks, and heated surfaces. Ensure the container is capped firmly [US].
*   **External Use Only:** Strictly for topical application; keep out of reach of children [US].

### Specific Clinical Precautions
*   **Symptom Persistence:** Discontinue use and consult a healthcare provider if the condition worsens, if symptoms persist for more than 7 days, or if symptoms clear up and recur within a few days [US].
*   **Application Site:** Use only as directed.
