# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**. 
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical data into a concise, structured format. 
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official PI or local prescribing guidelines in any specific jurisdiction. At the time of writing, only the United States (FDA) source was available for analysis.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP).
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for mitochondrial fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter Healthcare Corporation) [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
  - Sterile solution for injection: 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism which result in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with end-stage renal disease who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:**
  - **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg given as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion [US].
  - **Loading Dose:** Often required in severe metabolic crises, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours.
  - **Frequency:** Administer every 3 or 4 hours; intervals should never be less than every 6 hours.
  - **Dose Range:** Subsequent daily doses are typically 50 mg/kg, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been administered.
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
  - **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
  - **Administration:** Administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line after each dialysis session.
  - **Dose Adjustment:** Guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations (normal range: 40 to 50 µmol/L). Adjustments (e.g., down to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy.

### Administration Instructions
- Inspect visually for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration.
- Discard any unused portion as the formulation contains no preservatives.

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm have occurred, primarily in ESRD patients. Some reactions manifest within minutes of injection. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with a known history, an increase in seizure frequency or severity may occur [US].
- **Monitoring:** 
  - **Metabolic patients:** Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target 35–60 µmol/L) is recommended [US].
  - **Warfarin therapy:** Monitor International Normalized Ratio (INR) regularly in patients co-administered warfarin, as levocarnitine may increase the anticoagulant effect [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse events reported in clinical trials for ESRD patients (frequency $\ge$ 5% regardless of causality):

| System Organ Class | Very Common (>10%) | Common (1–10%) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **General/Injection Site** | Injection site reaction (33%), Pain (30%), Chest pain (12%), Flu syndrome (25%) | Fever, Asthenia, Abdominal pain, Accidental injury, Allergic reaction |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension (20%), Hypotension (14%) | Tachycardia, Palpitations, Arrhythmia, Vascular disorder |
| **Gastrointestinal** | Diarrhea (16%), Vomiting (15%) | Nausea, Anorexia, Constipation, Dyspepsia, Melena |
| **Nervous System** | Headache (22%), Dizziness (13%) | Insomnia, Paresthesia, Depression, Vertigo |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis (23%) | Cough increase, Dyspnea, Rhinitis, Sinusitis, Bronchitis |
| **Metabolic/Other** | Hypercalcemia (9%) | Hyperkalemia, Peripheral edema, Weight increase/decrease, Anemia |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Anaphylaxis, bronchospasm, and seizures [US].
- **Other Reported Effects:** Transient nausea and vomiting; less frequent reports of body odor and gastritis [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased INR have been observed. Recommendation: Monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after dose adjustments [US].
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
  - **US Category:** Not explicitly assigned a letter, but reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose revealed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine is a normal component of human milk. Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant should be weighed against the benefits of maternal supplementation [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies showed no impairment of fertility [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing recommendations for metabolic disorders are applicable to paediatric patients (see Section 3).
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** 
  - The safety and efficacy of **oral** levocarnitine have not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency. 
  - Chronic high-dose oral administration in patients with severely compromised renal function or ESRD may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites: trimethylamine (TMA) and trimethylamine-N-oxide (TMAO) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In ESRD patients, levocarnitine injection increases plasma carnitine concentrations and may alleviate metabolic abnormalities associated with inborn errors (e.g., glutaric aciduria II, methyl malonic aciduria).
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
  - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
  - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites are trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and $\gamma$-butyrobetaine (feces).
  - **Elimination:** Following IV bolus (20 mg/kg), the distribution half-life is 0.585 hours; the terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours. Approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Dose-Ranging Studies:** In a study of ESRD patients (N=12), 20 mg/kg three times weekly after dialysis increased mean pre-dialysis (trough) carnitine levels from 21.3 ± 7.7 µmol/L to 190 ± 55 µmol/L after 8 weeks. Higher doses (40 mg/kg) resulted in trough levels up to 456 ± 162 µmol/L after 24 weeks [US].
- **Clinical Outcomes:** While levocarnitine increases carnitine concentrations in ESRD patients, the effects on specific clinical outcomes (e.g., muscle weakness, cardiomyopathy) in this population have not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat.
- **Compatibility:** Stable for up to 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer's at concentrations ranging from 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as Rx-only [US].
- **Australian / Canadian / European:** Not specified in available sources.
