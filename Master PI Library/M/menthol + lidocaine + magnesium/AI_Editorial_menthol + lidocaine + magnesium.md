# Editorial Product Information: menthol + lidocaine + magnesium

## 1. Product Identification
- Active ingredient (INN/generic name): **menthol + lidocaine + magnesium**
  - Menthol (as menthol) 3.7 g per 100 mL (3.7%)
  - Lidocaine (as lidocaine hydrochloride) 1.0 g per 100 mL (1.0%)
  - Magnesium (as magnesium chloride / magnesium cation) 2.0 g per 100 mL (2.0%)
- Chemical class: Not specified in available sources
- ATC code: Not specified in available sources
- Route(s) of administration: Topical (cutaneous) application

## 3. Approved Indications
Note: The following indications are those stated in the product labeling. The regulatory approval status for these uses is noted in Section 2.
- Indications / uses described in labeling:
  - Soothing relief of pain, stiffness, tingling, or burning nerve pain associated with:
    - arthritis
    - muscle and joint pains
    - cramps and soreness
    - backache
    - sciatica
  - Purpose listed: Topical analgesic cream [US]

## 4. Dosage and Administration
### General dosing (adult)
- Adults and children 12 years of age and older: apply to the affected area not more than 3 to 4 times daily. [US]

### Paediatric dosing
- Children under 12 years of age: consult a doctor before use. [US]

### Administration instructions
- Apply topically to affected area only.
- Test a small amount of cream on intact skin first to assess skin reaction and comfort; wait 15 minutes prior to wider application if tolerated. [US]
- Avoid contact with eyes. Do not apply to damaged or broken skin. Do not bandage tightly over the application site. [US]

## 5. Contraindications
- Do not use on damaged or broken skin. [US]

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed warnings
- None reported in available sources.

### General warnings and precautions
- For external use only. [US]
- Avoid contact with the eyes. [US]
- Do not bandage tightly over the treated area. [US]
- Stop use and ask a doctor if a rash or irritation develops and persists. [US]
- Stop use and consult a health professional if the condition worsens, or if symptoms persist for more than 7 days, or clear up and occur again within a few days. [US]
- Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away. [US]
- If pregnant or breastfeeding, consult a health professional before use. [US]

### Monitoring requirements
- Not specified in available sources

### Specific clinical precautions
- Patch-test a small area and wait 15 minutes before wider application to assess for skin sensitivity. [US]
