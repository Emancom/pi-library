# Editorial Product Information: Marstacimab

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**. 
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis intended for informational purposes and is not a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority's current approved labeling for definitive prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Marstacimab-hncq (human monoclonal immunoglobulin G Type 1 [IgG1] antibody).
- **Chemical Class:** Tissue factor pathway inhibitor (TFPI) antagonist.
- **Brand Name:** **HYMPAVZI** [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths [US]:**
    - Single-dose prefilled syringe: 150 mg/mL and 75 mg/0.5 mL.
    - Single-dose prefilled pen: 150 mg/mL and 75 mg/0.5 mL.
- **Route of Administration:** Subcutaneous (SC) injection.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Pfizer Laboratories Div Pfizer Inc [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Routine Prophylaxis in Hemophilia:** Indicated to prevent or reduce the frequency of bleeding episodes in adults and pediatric patients aged 6 years and older with:
    - Hemophilia A (congenital factor VIII deficiency) with or without factor VIII inhibitors [US].
    - Hemophilia B (congenital factor IX deficiency) with or without factor IX inhibitors [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Adults and Pediatric Patients (12 years and older):**
    - **Loading Dose:** 300 mg (administered as two 150 mg SC injections) [US].
    - **Maintenance Dose:** 150 mg SC once weekly, starting one week after the loading dose [US].
    - **Dose Adjustment:** May be increased to 300 mg weekly if bleed control is inadequate in patients weighing $\ge$ 50 kg [US].
- **Pediatric Patients (6 to less than 12 years of age):**
    - **Loading Dose:** 150 mg (administered as one 150 mg or two 75 mg SC injections) [US].
    - **Maintenance Dose:** 75 mg SC once weekly, starting one week after the loading dose [US].
    - **Dose Adjustment:** May be increased to 150 mg weekly if bleed control is inadequate in patients weighing $\ge$ 25 kg [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Administer SC once weekly on the same day each week, at any time of day [US].
- Injection sites: Abdomen, front of thigh, or buttocks (pen only, by caregiver/HCP). The back of the upper arm may be used for syringes (by caregiver/HCP only) [US].
- Rotate injection sites with each dose.
- If warming is required, allow the device to reach room temperature (up to 30°C) for 15–30 minutes in the carton [US].

### Breakthrough Bleeds
- Factor VIII, Factor IX, or bypassing agents (e.g., rFVIIa, aPCC) can be used to treat breakthrough bleeds. Do **not** use additional doses of marstacimab to treat these bleeds [US].
- Recommended max doses for breakthrough treatment: rFVIIa 90 mcg/kg; aPCC 100 units/kg within 24 hours [US].

### Missed Dose Instructions
- Administer as soon as possible before the next scheduled dose. Resume the regular weekly schedule or establish a new schedule based on the date of the missed dose administration [US].
- **Loading Dose Requirement:** If > 13 days have passed since the last dose, a loading dose must be administered before resuming weekly maintenance [US].

### Perioperative Management
- Temporarily pause marstacimab at least 7 days before major surgery [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Thromboembolic Events:** As a TFPI antagonist, marstacimab increases coagulation potential. Venous and arterial thromboembolic events occurred in 0.8% of trial patients. Monitor for symptoms and interrupt treatment if thrombosis is suspected [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Reactions (e.g., urticaria, pruritus) may occur. Discontinue permanently in cases of severe allergic reactions [US].
- **Embryofetal Toxicity:** Based on its mechanism, fetal harm is possible. Verify pregnancy status in females of reproductive potential prior to initiation [US].
- **Laboratory Interference:** Marstacimab causes transient increases in Fibrin D-dimer and Prothrombin Fragment 1.2. These biomarkers may not be reliable for clinical decision-making regarding suspected thrombosis [US].
- **Immune Tolerance Induction (ITI):** Safety and efficacy have not been established in patients receiving ongoing ITI [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions reported in $\ge$ 2% of patients in clinical trials (BASIS and BASIS KIDS):

| Adverse Reaction | Pediatric (6 to <18y) | Adults ($\ge$18y) | All Groups (N=259) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Injection site reaction*** | 15% | 8% | 12% |
| **Headache** | 7% | 7% | 7% |
| **Pyrexia** | 10% | 2% | 6% |
| **Arthralgia** | 3% | 4% | 3% |
| **Diarrhea** | 4% | 2% | 3% |
| **Pruritus** | 1% | 4% | 2% |
| **Rash** | 2% | 2% | 2% |

*\*Grouped term including swelling, erythema, bruising, pain, and hematoma at the site.*

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Thromboembolic events and severe hypersensitivity [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Coagulation Parameters:** Marstacimab does not significantly impact standard activated partial thromboplastin time (aPTT) or prothrombin time (PT) [US].
- **Combination with Bypassing Agents (In Vitro):**
    - rFVIIa: No additive effect on thrombin generation [US].
    - aPCC: Increased thrombin generation (remained within/near normal non-hemophilic ranges) [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** No human data available. Monoclonal antibodies are actively transported across the placenta and may cause fetal harm [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Presence in human milk and effects on the breastfed infant are unknown. Consider the developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding alongside the mother’s clinical need for the drug [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies in rats showed no effect on male or female fertility at doses significantly exceeding clinical exposure [US].
- **Contraception:** Females of reproductive potential should use effective contraception during treatment and for 2 months after the final dose [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Approved for patients 6 years and older. Safety/efficacy in children < 6 years are not established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical trials did not include sufficient numbers of patients $\ge$ 65 years to determine if they respond differently than younger subjects [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment for mild impairment (eGFR 60–89 mL/min/1.73 m²). Data are unavailable for moderate to severe renal impairment [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment for mild impairment (total bilirubin > 1x to $\le$ 1.5x ULN). Data are unavailable for moderate to severe impairment [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Marstacimab-hncq is a human monoclonal IgG1 antibody that targets the Kunitz domain 2 (K2) of the Tissue Factor Pathway Inhibitor (TFPI). By neutralizing TFPI—the primary inhibitor of the extrinsic coagulation cascade—it enhances thrombin generation and improves hemostasis [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Increases total TFPI and biomarkers of thrombin generation (D-dimer, Prothrombin fragment 1+2). Effects persist over 7 days and are reversible after discontinuation [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Bioavailability is approximately 71%. Median $T_{max}$ is 23–59 hours [US].
    - **Distribution:** Apparent volume of distribution is approximately 6.0 L [US].
    - **Elimination:** Non-linear clearance due to target-mediated drug disposition (TMDD). Approximately 90% is eliminated within 1 month of the last dose [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **BASIS Trial:** An open-label, multi-center study in adults and adolescents ($\ge$ 12 years) with Hemophilia A or B (with or without inhibitors).
    - **On-demand cohort:** Marstacimab reduced the Annualized Bleeding Rate (ABR) for treated bleeds from 39.9 to 3.2 (92% reduction) [US].
    - **Prophylaxis cohort:** Marstacimab demonstrated non-inferiority compared to prior routine factor/bypassing agent prophylaxis (ABR 7.9 vs 5.1) [US].
- **BASIS KIDS Trial:** Evaluated patients aged 6 to < 18 years. Results showed comparable efficacy and safety profiles to the adult population [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Refrigeration:** Store at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) in the original carton to protect from light [US].
- **Room Temperature:** May be stored at room temperature (up to 30°C/86°F) for a single period of up to 7 days. Once stored at room temperature, it must not be returned to the refrigerator [US].
- **Handling:** Do not freeze. Do not shake [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison/Schedule Classification:** Rx-only [US].
