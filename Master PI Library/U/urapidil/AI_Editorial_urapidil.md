# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of levocarnitine injection for use by healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult the current approved local PI for full prescribing details.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine ((R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt).
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative/carrier molecule.
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection, USP [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Clear, colourless sterile solution for injection, 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg. It should be administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion [US].
- **ESRD on Hemodialysis:** The recommended starting dose is 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight). Administration should occur as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].

### Titration and Monitoring
- **Metabolic Crisis:** A loading dose is often given in severe cases, followed by an equivalent dose over the next 24 hours. Dosing frequency should be every 3 or 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours [US].
- **Maintenance (Metabolic):** Subsequent daily doses are typically 50 mg/kg, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been used [US].
- **ESRD Adjustments:** Dosage adjustments should be guided by pre-dialysis (trough) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Reductions (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].
- **Target Concentrations:** For metabolic disorders, plasma free carnitine levels should be monitored to maintain between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations of 0.5 mg/mL to 8.0 mg/mL [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions may occur within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately [US].
- **Seizures:** Levocarnitine has been associated with seizures in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Accumulation of Metabolites:** In patients with severely compromised renal function or ESRD, chronic high-dose oral administration (not specifically the IV route) may lead to accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites: trimethylamine (TMA) and trimethylamine-N-oxide (TMAO) [US].

### Monitoring Requirements
- Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations is recommended [US].
- International Normalised Ratio (INR) should be monitored in patients receiving concurrent warfarin therapy [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common Adverse Effects (≥5% in ESRD clinical trials)
Adverse events reported in ESRD patients on hemodialysis regardless of causality [US]:

| Body System | Adverse Event |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Body as a Whole** | Abdominal pain, headache, infection, injection site reaction, pain, fever, flu syndrome, asthenia |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension, hypotension, chest pain, tachycardia |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea, vomiting, nausea, anorexia |
| **Nervous System** | Dizziness, paresthesia, depression |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis, cough increase, dyspnea, rhinitis |
| **Metabolic/Nutritional**| Hypercalcemia, hypervolemia |

### Serious and Other Adverse Effects
- **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea and vomiting; less frequently, gastritis and body odor [US].
- **Neurologic:** Seizures (post-marketing) [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Anaphylaxis and laryngeal edema [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Increased INR has been reported when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin. Close monitoring of INR is required upon initiation or dose adjustment [US].
- **Other Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not assigned]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine is a normal component of human milk. Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. In dairy cows, milk carnitine concentrations increase after administration. A risk-benefit assessment should consider the risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant versus the benefits of maternal supplementation [US].
- **Fertility:** No mutagenic effects were found in standard bacterial and yeast tests. Long-term animal carcinogenicity studies have not been performed [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Use is approved for metabolic and ESRD indications; dosing is weight-based (see Section 3) [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Efficacy and safety of oral levocarnitine have not been evaluated in renal insufficiency. For IV use in ESRD, see dosage recommendations in Section 3 [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Levocarnitine clears acylCoA compounds, the accumulation of which can disrupt intermediary metabolism and lead to life-threatening acidosis [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Following IV bolus (20 mg/kg), approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours. The mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients (20 mg/kg three times weekly post-dialysis), mean plasma concentrations increased from a baseline trough of 21.3 ± 7.7 µmol/L to 190 ± 55 µmol/L after 8 weeks [US].
- **Dose-Ranging Data:** A study using 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses three times weekly showed a dose-proportional increase in trough carnitine concentrations at 12 and 24 weeks [US].
- **Note:** While levocarnitine increases carnitine concentrations in ESRD, its impact on specific clinical outcomes in this population has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F). Avoid excessive heat [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing. Vials are for single use; discard any unused portion as the formulation contains no preservatives [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Prescription Only Medicine (Rx only) [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources.
