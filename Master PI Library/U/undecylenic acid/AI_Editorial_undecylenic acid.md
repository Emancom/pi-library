- **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical (Ungual/Periungual) [US]
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Guangzhou Huixue Biotechnology Co., Ltd. [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Nail Fungus (Onychomycosis):** For the treatment of nail fungus and associated clinical signs, including discoloration, thickening, splitting, crumbling, and brittleness [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- Apply the solution to the affected area twice daily, once in the morning and once at night [US].

### Administration Instructions
- **Nail Preparation:** For initial use, soak the affected nail in warm water to soften it. Gently file the nail to reduce thickness and remove debris, taking care not to damage the nail bed [US].
- **Maintenance Filing:** For optimal results, file the nails every 2–3 days [US].
- **Application:** Hold the bottle at an angle and apply 2–3 drops of the solution evenly to the affected area [US].
- **Duration of Use:** Continue regular use to assist in preventing the regrowth of fungus. Improvement may be visible within 7 days [US].
