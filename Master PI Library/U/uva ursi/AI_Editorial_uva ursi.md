Based on traditional homeopathic practice, this product is intended for the temporary relief of:
- Fatigue and weakness (low energy).
- Frequent urination and urinary discomfort.

- Support for metabolic functions, including glucose irregularities and liver/pancreatic support [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Administration
The liquid should be mixed with half a cup of water and consumed immediately. The process should be repeated 3 to 4 times daily [US].

- **Children (Ages 3 to 11):** 5 drops per dose.
- **Children (Under 3 years):** Use only after consultation with a physician [US].
