# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the following provided Product Information (PI) document: **US (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, consolidating regulatory information regarding the clinical use of **levocarnitine** injection.
- **Note:** This synthesis is an editorial resource and does not replace official, jurisdiction-specific PI documents required for prescribing in clinical practice. At the time of writing, only the United States Product Information was provided for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN/USP)
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative (carrier molecule for fatty acid transport)
- **Chemical Name:** (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection [US]
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Injection for intravenous use: 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Indicated for the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that result in secondary carnitine deficiency [US-approved].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    - The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg administered as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion. 
    - In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose may be given, followed by an equivalent dose over the next 24 hours. 
    - Dosing should occur every 3 to 4 hours and never less than every 6 hours. 
    - Subsequent daily doses are typically in the range of 50 mg/kg, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilised.
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:** 
    - Recommended starting dose: 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight).
    - Administration: Slow 2 to 3 minute bolus injection into the venous return line after each dialysis session.
    - Dose adjustments: May be guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on clinical response.

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter and discoloration before administration.
- **Compatibility:** Stable for up to 24 hours in PVC bags at room temperature (25°C) when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations ranging from 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL.

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known** [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in patients with ESRD undergoing dialysis. These may occur within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, the drug must be discontinued immediately.
- **Seizures:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed.
- **General Monitoring:** For metabolic disorders, weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L) is recommended.
- **Oral Formulation Cautions:** While this document concerns the injection, it is noted that chronic high doses of *oral* levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide).

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are often confounded by the underlying pathology of ESRD or metabolic errors.

### Common Adverse Effects (≥5% Frequency in Dialysis Trials)
| Body System | Adverse Event |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Body as a Whole** | Abdominal pain (9%), Asthenia (9%), Chest pain (12%), Fever (7%), Flu syndrome (25%), Headache (22%), Infection (15%), Injection site reaction (33%), Pain (30%) |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension (20%), Hypotension (14%), Tachycardia (6%) |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea (16%), Nausea (8%), Vomiting (15%) |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness (13%), Paresthesia (5%), Depression (5%) |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis (23%), Cough increase (9%), Dyspnea (7%), Rhinitis (9%) |

### Serious and Other Notable Effects
- **Seizures:** Reported in patients with and without prior seizure history.
- **Hypersensitivity:** Anaphylaxis and laryngeal edema.
- **Gastrointestinal:** Gastritis and transient nausea/vomiting.
- **Body Odor:** Reported as a less frequent adverse reaction.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have been observed when levocarnitine is used concomitantly with warfarin. 
- **Management:** It is recommended that INR levels be monitored upon initiation of levocarnitine or following dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin therapy.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not specifically lettered, but descriptive]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (based on surface area) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. There are no adequate/well-controlled studies in pregnant women. Use only if clearly needed.
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows indicate increased carnitine concentrations in milk following administration. Risks to the child from excess intake must be weighed against the benefits of supplementation to the mother.
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility was found in animal reproductive studies.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for inborn errors of metabolism applies to both adults and children (see Section 3).
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in the source document; however, clinical monitoring is standard.
- **Renal Impairment:** Specifically indicated for use in ESRD patients on dialysis. Safety and efficacy of the *oral* form have not been evaluated in renal insufficiency (due to metabolite accumulation).
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring carrier molecule required for the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane. This process delivers substrates for oxidation and energy production. It also facilitates the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites are trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces).
    - **Elimination:** Following IV bolus (20 mg/kg), approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. The mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours, and the terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In ESRD patients, levocarnitine administration increases plasma concentrations. Pre-dialysis (trough) levels increase significantly over 8 to 24 weeks of therapy.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients, 20 mg/kg doses post-dialysis increased mean trough levocarnitine levels from 21.3 µmol/L (baseline) to 190 µmol/L at 8 weeks.
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** In ESRD patients receiving 10, 20, or 40 mg/kg, a dose-dependent increase in trough concentrations was observed over 12 and 24 weeks.
- **Note:** While levocarnitine increases carnitine concentrations in ESRD, the impact of this supplementation on specific clinical outcomes in this population has not been determined.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat.
- **Preservative:** The formulation contains no preservative; any unused portion of a single-dose vial must be discarded.
