# Editorial Product Information: Nandrolone Decanoate

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) documents from the following regulatory agencies:
    - **Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA)**, Australia (Product: Deca-Durabolin)
    - **Food and Drug Administration (FDA)**, United States (Product: Nandrolone Decanoate Injection USP)
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of nandrolone decanoate for healthcare professionals, consolidating regional clinical data and regulatory requirements.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not replace the official PI. Clinicians must refer to the specific PI approved in their jurisdiction for prescribing purposes.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Nandrolone decanoate (a synthetic derivative of testosterone).
- **Brand Names:** Deca-Durabolin [TGA].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:**
    - **Solution for Injection (50 mg/mL):** 1 mL glass ampoules. Contains arachis (peanut) oil and benzyl alcohol (100 mg/mL) [TGA].
    - **Solution for Injection (100 mg/mL and 200 mg/mL):** Multiple-dose (2 mL) and single-dose (1 mL) vials. Contains sesame oil and benzyl alcohol [FDA].
- **Route of Administration:** Deep intramuscular injection (preferably into the gluteal muscle).
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Aspen Pharmacare Pty Ltd [TGA]; Watson Laboratories, Inc. [FDA].

## 2. Approved Indications
Nandrolone decanoate is indicated for:
- **Management of the anaemia of renal insufficiency:** Specifically to increase haemoglobin and red cell mass [TGA, FDA].
- **Acute renal failure and chronic renal insufficiency** [TGA].
- **Palliative treatment of inoperable mammary carcinoma** [TGA].
- **Osteoporosis:** Specifically where oestrogen therapy is contraindicated [TGA].
- **Aplastic anaemia** [TGA].
- **Supportive adjunct for patients on long-term corticosteroid treatment** [TGA].
- **Supportive therapy in protein deficiency states:** Associated with chronic debilitating diseases, major surgery, or severe trauma [TGA].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults)
Dosage should be tailored to therapeutic response and the benefit-to-risk ratio. For optimal anabolic effect, adequate intake of vitamins, minerals, and protein in a calorie-rich diet is required [TGA, FDA]. Iron intake is specifically required for maximal response in renal anaemia [FDA].

### Indication-Specific Dosing
- **Anaemia of Renal Insufficiency/Failure:**
    - **Females:** 50–100 mg per week [FDA]; 100 mg per week [TGA].
    - **Males:** 100–200 mg per week [FDA]; 200 mg per week [TGA].
- **Renal conditions (Acute/Chronic):** 25–50 mg every 2–3 weeks. Higher initial doses (50 mg weekly) may be required [TGA].
- **Inoperable Mammary Carcinoma (Palliative):** 50 mg every 2–3 weeks [TGA].
- **Osteoporosis/Corticosteroid Support:** 50 mg every 2–3 weeks [TGA].
- **Aplastic Anaemia:** 50–150 mg per week [TGA].

### Administration Instructions
- Administer only by deep intramuscular injection [TGA, FDA].
- Therapy should ideally be intermittent [FDA].
- **Discontinuation:** If no haematologic improvement is seen within 3–6 months, treatment should be discontinued [TGA, FDA].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Pregnancy:** Due to the risk of foetal masculinisation [TGA, FDA].
- **Lactation/Nursing Mothers:** [TGA: Contraindicated; FDA: Discontinue nursing or drug].
- **Carcinoma:** Known or suspected prostatic carcinoma or breast carcinoma in males [TGA, FDA].
- **Breast Carcinoma in Females with Hypercalcaemia:** Androgens may stimulate osteolytic bone resorption [FDA].
- **Hypersensitivity:** To the active substance or any excipients (e.g., peanut/soya allergy [TGA], sesame oil [FDA]).
- **Renal Disease:** Nephrosis or the nephrotic phase of nephritis [TGA, FDA].
- **Organ Failure:** Cardiac failure or liver disease with impaired bilirubin excretion [TGA].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Serious Warnings
- **Peliosis Hepatis:** Reported with long-term therapy; liver/spleen tissue replaced by blood-filled cysts which may lead to liver failure or fatal intra-abdominal haemorrhage [FDA].
- **Liver Cell Tumors:** Most are benign and androgen-dependent, but fatal malignant tumors have occurred [FDA].
- **Lipid Changes:** Significant decreases in HDL and increases in LDL may occur, increasing the risk of atherosclerosis and coronary artery disease [FDA].
- **Hypercalcaemia:** May occur in women with disseminated breast carcinoma; requires treatment discontinuation [TGA, FDA].

### General Precautions
- **Virilisation:** Signs (hoarseness, acne, hirsutism, clitorimegaly) require assessment of the risk/benefit ratio. Discontinuation at first evidence of mild virilism is necessary to prevent irreversibility [TGA, FDA].
- **Oedema:** Anabolic steroids may cause water and electrolyte retention (sodium, chloride, potassium); use with extreme caution in patients with cardiac, renal, or hepatic disease [TGA, FDA].
- **Misuse/Abuse:** Anabolic steroids are subject to misuse in sports, carrying risks of testicular atrophy, inhibition of spermatogenesis, and hepatotoxicity [TGA, FDA].

### Monitoring Requirements
- **Hepatic:** Liver function tests should be obtained periodically, especially with high doses [TGA, FDA].
- **Haematologic:** Periodic checks of haemoglobin and haematocrit to exclude polycythemia [TGA, FDA].
- **Prostate:** Digital rectal examination (DRE) and PSA monitoring (quarterly for 12 months, then yearly) to exclude BPH or sub-clinical cancer [TGA].
- **Growth:** In children, bone age (wrist/hand X-rays) must be monitored every 6 months to avoid compromising final height [TGA, FDA].

Adverse effects are dose-dependent and related to the androgenic activity of the preparation.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Anticoagulants (Coumarin-type):** Anabolic steroids enhance anticoagulant action. Close monitoring of prothrombin time/INR is required; dose reduction of the anticoagulant may be necessary [TGA, FDA].
- **Insulin and Hypoglycaemics:** Anabolics may improve glucose tolerance and decrease requirements for these agents; monitor diabetic patients closely [TGA, FDA].
- **ACTH or Corticosteroids:** Concurrent use may enhance oedema formation; use with caution in patients with cardiac or hepatic disease [TGA, FDA].
- **Enzyme Inducers/Inhibitors:** May decrease or increase nandrolone levels respectively [TGA].
- **Recombinant Human Erythropoietin (rhEPO):** Combination may allow for a reduction in rhEPO dose [TGA].
- **Laboratory Tests:** May decrease PBI and thyroxine-binding globulin (decreased total T4, increased T3/T4 resin uptake), though free thyroid levels remain unchanged [TGA, FDA].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:**
    - **TGA Category D:** Contraindicated. High risk of foetal virilisation.
    - **FDA Category X:** Contraindicated.
- **Breastfeeding:** Contraindicated [TGA]. The amount excreted in milk is unknown, and the risk of virilisation in the neonate exists [TGA, FDA].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Use with extreme caution. May accelerate epiphyseal closure and sexual maturation without compensatory linear growth [TGA, FDA]. Average dose for children (2–13 years) with renal anaemia is 25–50 mg every 3–4 weeks [FDA]. Not recommended in infants/children under 3 years due to benzyl alcohol content [TGA].
- **Elderly Use:** Monitor for prostatic hypertrophy or carcinoma [TGA, FDA].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Use with caution due to the risk of complications from fluid retention [TGA, FDA].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Anabolic steroid with enhanced anabolic and reduced androgenic activity compared to testosterone. Positively influences calcium metabolism, increases bone mass in osteoporosis, and has a nitrogen-saving effect [TGA].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Slowly released from the injection site (half-life of 6 days) [TGA].
    - **Metabolism:** Rapidly hydrolysed to nandrolone (half-life <1 hour) and then metabolised by the liver [TGA].
    - **Excretion:** Metabolites (19-norandrosterone, 19-noretiocholanolone, and 19-norepiandrosterone) excreted in urine [TGA].

## 11. Clinical Trials
Specific pivotal trial data summaries were not provided in the source documents; however, clinical efficacy is noted for bone mass increases and objective regressions in mammary carcinoma [TGA]. Anephric patients are reported to be less responsive to treatment for renal anaemia [FDA].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **TGA (Deca-Durabolin):** Store below 25°C. Protect from light. Keep container in the original carton.
- **FDA:** Store at 20°–25°C (68°–77°F). Protect from light.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Australia [TGA]:** Schedule 4 – Prescription Only Medicine.
- **United States [FDA]:** Rx-only. Classified as a **Schedule III (C-III)** controlled substance under the Anabolic Steroids Control Act of 1990.
