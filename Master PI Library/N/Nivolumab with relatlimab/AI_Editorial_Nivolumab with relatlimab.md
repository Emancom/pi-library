# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical data and regulatory requirements into a single reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should refer to local regulatory guidelines for specific legal requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP).
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter Healthcare Corporation) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only [US].
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency (Metabolic):** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
- **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg [US].
- **Administration:** Administered as a slow bolus injection (over 2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion [US].
- **Loading Dose:** In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose is often followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours [US].
- **Frequency:** Administer every 3 or 4 hours; frequency should never be less than every 6 hours [US].
- **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses typically range around 50 mg/kg, though higher doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilised [US].

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Recommended Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
- **Administration Timing:** Administer as a slow 2 to 3 minute bolus injection into the venous return line after each dialysis session [US].
- **Dose Adjustments:** Should be guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Reductions (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].

### Administration Instructions
- **Visual Inspection:** Inspect for particulate matter and discoloration prior to use [US].
- **Compatibility:** Compatible and stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations between 0.5 mg/mL and 8.0 mg/mL [US].
- **Discarding:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Known Contraindications:** None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions may occur within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and evaluate risks before considering re-administration [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Levocarnitine has been associated with seizures in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with a known seizure disorder, an increase in seizure frequency or severity has been reported [US].
- **Monitoring:** Obtain baseline plasma carnitine levels. Weekly and monthly monitoring should include blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L) [US].
- **Metabolite Accumulation:** Chronic high-dose oral administration (not specific to IV) in patients with severely compromised renal function or ESRD may lead to the accumulation of trimethylamine (TMA) and trimethylamine-N-oxide (TMAO), which are potentially toxic [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common Adverse Effects
The following events were reported at a frequency of ≥5% in clinical trials of ESRD patients on hemodialysis, regardless of causality [US]:

| Body System | Adverse Event |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Body as a Whole** | Abdominal pain (9%), Headache (22%), Injection site reaction (33%), Pain (30%), Fever (7%), Flu syndrome (25%) |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension (20%), Hypotension (14%), Tachycardia (6%), Chest pain (12%) |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea (16%), Vomiting (15%), Nausea (8%) |
| **Metabolic/Nutritional** | Hypercalcemia (9%), Hypervolemia (5%) |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness (13%), Paresthesia (5%) |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis (23%), Cough increase (9%), Dyspnea (7%), Rhinitis (9%) |

### Other Reported Effects
- **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea and vomiting; less frequent: gastritis and body odor [US].
- **Postmarketing:** Seizures; serious hypersensitivity (anaphylaxis) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have been observed when used concomitantly with levocarnitine. It is recommended to monitor INR levels after initiating treatment or following dose adjustments [US].
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not assigned under current labeling, but reproductive studies in rats/rabbits at 3.8x human dose showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm]. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine is a normal component of human milk. While supplemental use in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied, studies in dairy cows show increased milk carnitine concentrations after exogenous administration. Weigh the benefits of maternal treatment against potential risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** See Section 3 (Dosage and Administration) for metabolic disorder dosing. Secondary deficiency in neonates is defined as plasma concentrations below age-related norms [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical documents did not specify age-specific dose adjustments; general monitoring is advised [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Primarily indicated for ESRD patients on dialysis. Safety and efficacy of *oral* levocarnitine have not been evaluated in renal insufficiency [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In ESRD, it alleviates metabolic abnormalities resulting from the accumulation of toxic acyl-CoA compounds [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Following a single IV bolus (20 mg/kg), approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours. Mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients, 20 mg/kg post-dialysis increased mean pre-dialysis (trough) carnitine levels from 21.3 µmol/L to 190 µmol/L after 8 weeks. Dose-ranging studies (10, 20, 40 mg/kg) showed dose-proportional increases in trough concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks. Clinical outcomes regarding signs and symptoms of deficiency in ESRD have not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F). Brief excursions to 15°C–30°C permitted [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat. The product contains no preservative; discard unused portions [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not applicable for US jurisdiction labeling.
