# Editorial Product Information: Nusinersen

## Editorial Note
- **Jurisdictions included in this editorial:** Australian (TGA). 
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised clinical overview of **nusinersen** for use by healthcare professionals, synthesising data from official product information sources.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and should not be used as a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Nusinersen heptadecasodium
- **Chemical Class:** Antisense oligonucleotide (ASO)
- **Brand Name:** **SPINRAZA**
- **ATC Code:** M09AX07
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Solution for injection. Each 5 mL single-use vial contains 12.6 mg nusinersen heptadecasodium (equivalent to 12 mg nusinersen free acid, or 2.4 mg/mL).
- **Route of Administration:** Intrathecal injection via lumbar puncture.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Biogen Australia Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **5q Spinal Muscular Atrophy (SMA):** Indicated for the treatment of patients with 5q SMA [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Recommended Dose:** 12 mg (5 mL) per administration.
- **Initiation (Loading):** Four loading doses administered on Days 0, 14, 28, and 63.
- **Maintenance:** 12 mg administered once every 4 months thereafter.

### Administration Instructions
- **Preparation:** Allow the vial to reach room temperature (25°C) without external heat. Inspect for particles; use only if clear and colourless. Do not dilute.
- **Procedure:** Must be administered by healthcare professionals experienced in lumbar punctures. Aseptic technique is mandatory. 
- **Removal of CSF:** It is recommended to remove a volume of cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) equivalent to the volume of **SPINRAZA** (5 mL) prior to injection.
- **Injection:** Administered as an intrathecal bolus over 1 to 3 minutes. Do not inject into areas of skin infection or inflammation.
- **Guidance:** Ultrasound or other imaging may be used to guide administration, particularly in younger patients. Sedation may be used if clinically indicated.

### Missed or Delayed Doses
| Status | Timing of Administration |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Loading Dose** | Administer as soon as possible, ensuring at least 14 days between doses. Continue subsequent doses at the prescribed intervals from the last dose. |
| **Maintenance (>4 to <8 months from last dose)** | Administer immediately; then next dose per original schedule (min. 14 days apart); then resume 4-month interval. |
| **Maintenance (≥8 to <16 months from last dose)** | Administer immediately; then a second dose 14 days later; then resume 4-month interval. |
| **Maintenance (≥16 to <40 months from last dose)** | Administer immediately; then a second dose 14 days later; then a third dose 14 days later; then resume 4-month interval. |
| **Maintenance (≥40 months from last dose)** | Re-initiate full loading regimen (Days 0, 14, 28, 63), then resume 4-month maintenance. |

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** History of hypersensitivity to nusinersen or any of the excipients (sodium chloride, potassium chloride, calcium chloride dihydrate, magnesium chloride hexahydrate, dibasic sodium phosphate, sodium phosphate monobasic dihydrate, sodium hydroxide, hydrochloric acid).

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### General Warnings
- **Thrombocytopenia and Coagulation Abnormalities:** Potential for increased risk of bleeding. Platelet and coagulation laboratory testing is recommended prior to administration if clinically indicated.
- **Renal Toxicity:** Risk of renal toxicity, including glomerulonephritis. Urine protein testing (preferably first morning urine) is recommended. Persistent elevations require further evaluation.
- **Hydrocephalus:** Post-marketing reports include cases of communicating hydrocephalus not related to meningitis or bleeding. Some cases required ventriculo-peritoneal (VP) shunts. Monitor for signs of increased intracranial pressure (e.g., decreased consciousness).

### Specific Clinical Precautions
- **Lumbar Puncture Risks:** Potential for procedure-related complications such as headache, back pain, and haematoma.
- **Post-Market Serious Events:** Aseptic meningitis and serious infections (meningitis, arachnoiditis) related to the administration procedure have occurred.

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse events (AEs) are primarily associated with the underlying SMA disease or the lumbar puncture procedure.

### Frequency Categories
- **Very Common (≥10%):**
  - Respiratory: Upper respiratory tract infection (30%), pneumonia (29%), nasopharyngitis, bronchiolitis, viral infection.
  - Gastrointestinal: Constipation (35%), vomiting (29%).
  - General: Pyrexia (43%), teething (18%), headache (29%), back pain (25%).
  - Skin: Rash (11%).
- **Common (1–10%):**
  - Ear infection, influenza, oral candidiasis, urinary tract infection, diarrhea, epistaxis, cough.
  - Respiratory: Hypoxia, rhinorrhoea, upper respiratory tract congestion.
  - Other: Salivary hypersecretion, weight gain poor, conjunctivitis.

### Serious and Post-Marketing Adverse Effects
- **Procedure-related:** Procedure-related haematoma, post-lumbar puncture syndrome, meningitis, arachnoiditis.
- **Systemic:** Aseptic meningitis, hypersensitivity, and communicating hydrocephalus.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **CYP450 System:** Nusinersen is not a substrate for, nor an inhibitor or inducer of, cytochrome P450 enzymes. 
- **Protein Binding:** Likelihood of interactions due to competition for plasma protein binding or transporters is low.
- **Metabolism:** Primarily metabolised via nucleases. 

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
  - **Australian Category B1:** No evidence of embryofoetal toxicity in animal studies (mice and rabbits). 
  - Human data are unavailable; use should be based on a risk-benefit assessment.
- **Breastfeeding:** Safety is not established. There are no data on the presence of nusinersen in human milk or its effects on the breastfed infant.
- **Fertility:** Animal studies showed no effect on male or female fertility. No human data available.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Studied in patients from newborn to 17 years.
- **Elderly Use:** No clinical data available for patients over 65 years.
- **Renal Impairment:** Not studied in this population.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not studied; however, as nusinersen is not metabolised by the liver, dose adjustment is unlikely to be required.

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
Nusinersen is an antisense oligonucleotide (ASO) that increases the inclusion of exon 7 in survival motor neuron 2 (*SMN2*) messenger RNA (mRNA) transcripts. It binds to an intronic splice silencing site (ISS-N1) in intron 7 of the *SMN2* pre-mRNA. By displacing splicing factors, it promotes the production of functional, full-length SMN protein.

### Pharmacokinetics
- **Distribution:** Broadly distributed within the central nervous system (CNS) following intrathecal administration. Levels are also detectable in skeletal muscle, liver, and kidney.
- **Metabolism:** Metabolised slowly via exonuclease-mediated hydrolysis.
- **Elimination:** Mean terminal elimination half-life in CSF is estimated at 135 to 177 days. Primary elimination is via urinary excretion of the drug and its metabolites.
- **Steady State:** In paediatric patients, steady state in CSF is reached by 7 to 8 years of treatment.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ENDEAR (Study CS3B):** A Phase 3 study in infantile-onset SMA (symptoms before 6 months). Treated patients showed significant improvement in motor milestones (HINE Section 2) and higher overall survival compared to sham-control (p=0.0041).
- **CHERISH (Study CS4):** A Phase 3 study in later-onset SMA (symptoms after 6 months). Treated patients achieved a statistically significant improvement in the Hammersmith Functional Motor Scale - Expanded (HFMSE) score compared to sham-control (p=0.0000001).
- **NURTURE (Study CS5):** An open-label study in presymptomatic infants. All 25 patients (100%) were alive and achieved sitting without support at the interim analysis, demonstrating the benefit of early treatment.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage:** Store between 2°C to 8°C (Refrigerate). Do not freeze.
- **Protection:** Keep the vial in the original carton to protect from light.
- **Stability:** Unopened vials may be removed from the refrigerator and returned up to 5 times. The total time out of refrigeration (up to 25°C) must not exceed 30 hours.
- **Drawn Syringe:** If not used within 6 hours of being drawn into a syringe, the solution must be discarded.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule:** Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine) [Australian].
