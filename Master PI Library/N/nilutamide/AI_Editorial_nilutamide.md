# Editorial Product Information: Nilutamide

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis was prepared using official Product Information from the following jurisdiction: **United States (Food and Drug Administration)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of nilutamide for use by healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis intended for professional reference and is not a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Nilutamide (a nonsteroidal, orally active antiandrogen).
- **Chemical Class:** Imidazolidinedione derivative.
- **Brand Names:** Nilutamide Tablets (generic equivalents available; historically marketed as Nilandron) [United States].
- **ATC Code:** L02BB02.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Oral tablets, 150 mg [United States].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** ANI Pharmaceuticals, Inc. [United States].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Metastatic Prostate Cancer:** Indicated for the treatment of Stage $D_2$ metastatic prostate cancer [United States].
- **Combination Therapy:** Must be used in combination with surgical castration (orchiectomy). For maximum efficacy, treatment must be initiated on the same day as, or the day following, surgical castration [United States].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **Initial Dose:** 300 mg (two 150 mg tablets) administered once daily for a duration of 30 days [United States].
- **Maintenance Dose:** 150 mg (one tablet) administered once daily thereafter [United States].
- **Administration:** May be taken with or without food [United States].
- **Missed Dose:** Not specified in available sources; however, patients are advised not to interrupt or stop dosing without physician consultation.

## 4. Contraindications
Nilutamide is contraindicated in the following populations:
- Patients with severe hepatic impairment [United States].
- Patients with severe respiratory insufficiency [United States].
- Patients with known hypersensitivity to nilutamide or any of the inactive ingredients (including calcium stearate, docusate sodium, lactose, povidone, corn starch, and talc) [United States].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Interstitial Pneumonitis:** Interstitial pneumonitis has been reported in 2% of patients overall. Incidence appears higher in specific populations; a small study in **Japanese** subjects showed a 17% incidence rate. Reports include cases of pulmonary fibrosis, hospitalisation, and death. Most cases occur within the first three months. If new or worsening shortness of breath occurs, nilutamide should be discontinued immediately until drug-related causes are ruled out [United States].
- **Hepatotoxicity:** Rare cases of severe liver injury, including death, have been reported, typically within the first 3 to 4 months of therapy. Treatment should be discontinued if jaundice occurs or if ALT levels exceed 2 times the upper limit of normal [United States].
- **Visual Disturbances:** Between 13% and 57% of patients report impaired adaptation to the dark. This effect may manifest when moving from a lighted to a dark area and may persist throughout treatment. Patients should be cautioned regarding night driving and may find relief by wearing tinted glasses [United States].
- **Alcohol Intolerance:** Approximately 5% of patients may experience a "disulfiram-like" reaction (facial flushing, malaise, hypotension) following alcohol ingestion [United States].
- **Antiandrogen Withdrawal Syndrome:** Patients whose disease progresses may experience clinical improvement upon discontinuation of the drug [United States].
- **Aplastic Anemia:** Isolated cases have been reported post-marketing, though a definitive causal relationship has not been established [United States].

## 6. Adverse Effects
The following adverse effects were reported in clinical trials (nilutamide vs. placebo, both in combination with surgical castration or leuprolide):

| System Organ Class | Adverse Effect | Frequency (Approx.) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Endocrine** | Hot flushes | 28% – 67% |
| **Special Senses** | Impaired adaptation to dark | 13% – 57% |
| | Abnormal vision | 7% – 13% |
| **Digestive** | Nausea | 10% – 24% |
| | Constipation | 7% – 20% |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness | 7% – 10% |
| | Insomnia | 16% |
| **Respiratory** | Dyspnea | 6% – 11% |
| **General** | Pain / Asthenia | 19% – 27% |
| **Metabolic** | Increased LFTs (AST/ALT) | 8% – 13% |

**Serious Adverse Effects:** Interstitial pneumonitis (2%), severe hepatitis (rare), and potential for aplastic anemia [United States].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Nilutamide has been shown *in vitro* to inhibit cytochrome P-450 isoenzymes. It may reduce the metabolism and increase the half-life of drugs with a narrow therapeutic margin, specifically:
    - **Vitamin K antagonists (e.g., Warfarin):** Requires careful monitoring of Prothrombin Time/INR; dosage reduction may be necessary.
    - **Phenytoin:** Risk of increased serum levels and toxicity.
    - **Theophylline:** Potential for delayed elimination.
- **Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Alcohol may cause a disulfiram-like intolerance reaction [United States].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [United States]: **Category C**. Animal reproduction studies have not been conducted. It is not known if nilutamide causes fetal harm. Use only if clearly needed.
- **Breastfeeding:** Safety information not established; nilutamide is not indicated for use in women [United States].
- **Fertility:** In animal studies (rats), nilutamide produced benign Leydig cell tumors due to elevated LH levels; however, this feedback mechanism is not observed in castrated men. No effects on reproductive function were seen in rats at therapeutic doses [United States].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness in paediatric patients have not been determined [United States].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical trials included patients aged 65 and over; no overall differences in safety or effectiveness were observed between these patients and younger patients [United States].
- **Renal Impairment:** Dosing adjustments by eGFR/stage are not specified in available sources.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Contraindicated in severe hepatic impairment. Baseline liver enzymes must be evaluated prior to treatment and monitored regularly (at least monthly for the first 4 months) [United States].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Nilutamide is a nonsteroidal antiandrogen that blocks testosterone at the androgen receptor level. It prevents the normal androgenic response in androgen-sensitive prostate cancer cells [United States].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Rapidly and completely absorbed following oral administration.
    - **Distribution:** Moderate binding to plasma proteins; low binding to erythrocytes.
    - **Metabolism:** Extensively metabolised in the liver; five metabolites have been identified. One metabolite possesses 25% to 50% of the activity of the parent drug.
    - **Elimination:** Primarily eliminated via urine (62%) as metabolites; less than 2% is excreted unchanged. The mean elimination half-life ranges from 38 to 59.1 hours [United States].

## 11. Clinical Trials
In a double-blind, multicenter study of 457 patients with metastatic prostate cancer, those treated with nilutamide plus orchiectomy showed statistically significant benefits compared to orchiectomy plus placebo:
- **Median Survival:** 27.3 months vs. 23.6 months.
- **Progression-Free Survival:** 21.1 months vs. 14.9 months.
- **Regression Rate (Complete/Partial):** 41% vs. 24%.
- **Bone Pain Improvement:** 54% vs. 37% [United States].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [United States].
- **Handling:** Protect from light. Keep out of reach of children.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** [United States]: Rx-only (Prescription only).
