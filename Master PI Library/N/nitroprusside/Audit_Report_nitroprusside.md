# Originality Audit Report: nitroprusside

## Audit Summary
- **Originality Score:** 72/100
- **Verbatim Copied Passages Found:** 5
- **Overall Assessment:** Concern

## Verbatim Copied Passages
The following passages are identical or near-identical to the source regulatory documents:

1.  **Indication phrasing (FDA):** 
    *   *Editorial:* "Immediate reduction of blood pressure in adult and pediatric patients [FDA]. Long-acting oral antihypertensive therapy should be initiated concurrently to minimize the duration of nitroprusside treatment."
    *   *Source (Doc 2, FDA, p. 6):* "...indicated for the immediate reduction of blood pressure of adult and pediatric patients in hypertensive crises. Concomitant longer-acting antihypertensive medication should be administered so that the duration of treatment with sodium nitroprusside can be minimized."

2.  **Cyanide Rate Limit (FDA):**
    *   *Editorial:* "If blood pressure is not adequately controlled after 10 minutes at this rate, the infusion must be terminated immediately."
    *   *Source (Doc 2, FDA, p. 7):* "If blood pressure has not been adequately controlled after 10 minutes of infusion at the maximum rate, administration of sodium nitroprusside should be terminated immediately."

3.  **Light Protection (TGA/FDA):**
    *   *Editorial:* "The infusion bag must be wrapped in the supplied opaque sleeve or aluminium foil. It is not necessary to cover the drip chamber or tubing."
    *   *Source (Doc 1, TGA, p. 2):* "The diluted solution should be protected from light, using the supplied opaque sleeve, aluminium foil... It is not necessary to cover the infusion drip chamber or the tubing."

4.  **Blood Pressure Monitoring (TGA/FDA):**
    *   *Editorial:* "If hypotension persists for more than a few minutes after stopping the infusion, an alternative cause must be investigated."
    *   *Source (Doc 1, TGA, p. 5):* "If hypotension persists more than a few minutes after discontinuation of the infusion... sodium nitroprusside is not the cause, and the true cause must be sought."

5.  **Metabolism (TGA/FDA):**
    *   *Editorial:* "The metabolite, thiocyanate, has an elimination half-life of 3 to 4 days, which may double or triple in patients with renal failure."
    *   *Source (Doc 1, TGA, p. 13):* "Clearance of SCN is primarily renal, with a half life of about 3 days. In renal failure, the half life can be doubled or tripled."

## Close Paraphrases
1.  **Mechanism of Action:** The editorial's Section 10 closely follows the structure of Section 5.1 in Document 1, particularly the distinction between arterial and venous dilation and the mention of "peripheral pooling."
2.  **Pregnancy (Ewes):** The description of the animal studies in Section 8 is a close paraphrase of Section 4.6 in the TGA document, maintaining the specific data points regarding fetal cyanide levels and maternal pH.
3.  **Contraindications:** The list in Section 3 uses the exact parenthetical examples (aortic coarctation, Leber's optic atrophy) found in both source documents.

## Originality Strengths
- **Section 6 (Adverse Effects):** This section is well-synthesized. Instead of a long list, the editorial categorizes effects by system (Gastrointestinal, Neurological, etc.) and distinguishes between immediate systemic effects and metabolic toxicities.
- **Section 11 (Storage):** Successfully merges different temperature and stability instructions from both agencies into a concise set of bullet points.
- **Section 1 (Note):** The editorial note is entirely original and provides clear context for the document's purpose.

## Differences from Source PIs
- **Harmonized Dilution Range:** The editorial adopts the more flexible FDA range (250–1000 mL) while Document 1 (TGA) specifically mentions 500–1000 mL.
- **Annotated Sourcing:** The editorial adds helpful bracketed tags like `[FDA]` or `[TGA]` to indicate where specific regional guidance originated.
- **Simplified Preparation:** The editorial streamlines the complex "Verification of integrity" text from the sources into a clear "Integrity Check" paragraph in Section 5.
- **Omission of Clinical Trial Data:** The editorial omits the lengthy descriptions of pediatric trials found in the FDA document, focusing instead on the actionable clinical guidance derived from them.

## Recommendation
**REVISION NEEDED.**

While the document is a high-quality synthesis, it fails a strict originality audit due to several verbatim strings in critical safety areas (Cyanide Toxicity limits and administration instructions). To pass, please:
1.  **Rewrite Section 4 (Cyanide Toxicity):** Specifically the "10 minutes" rule. Change "terminated immediately" to a synonymous original phrase like "the infusion must be ceased without delay."
2.  **Rewrite Section 5 (Light Protection):** Reword the instruction regarding the drip chamber and tubing to avoid the "It is not necessary to cover..." verbatim sentence.
3.  **Rephrase Section 2 (Indications):** Ensure the "Concomitant therapy" instruction is not a word-for-word copy of the FDA text.
