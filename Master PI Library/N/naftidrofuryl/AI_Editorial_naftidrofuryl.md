# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA)
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of levocarnitine product information for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises available regulatory data into a single clinically relevant resource.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN/USP).
- **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule for long-chain fatty acid transport; quaternary ammonium compound.
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - **Injection:** 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency in Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Indicated for the acute and chronic treatment of patients with metabolic defects resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US-approved].
- **Carnitine Deficiency in End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg.
    - **Administration:** Administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion.
    - **Loading Dose:** Often required in severe metabolic crises, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours.
    - **Frequency:** Dosing should occur every 3 or 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours.
    - **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses are typically 50 mg/kg or as clinically required. Doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilised.
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:** 
    - **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight.
    - **Administration:** Administered as a slow 2 to 3 minute bolus injection into the venous return line after each dialysis session.
    - **Adjustments:** Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be made as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough levocarnitine levels.

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration.
- **Compatibility:** Stable in Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations of 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL for up to 24 hours at room temperature in PVC bags [US].

### Monitoring
- Plasma carnitine concentrations should be obtained prior to therapy.
- Weekly and monthly monitoring is recommended, including blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine: 35 to 60 µmol/L) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have occurred, primarily in ESRD patients. Some reactions manifest within minutes of intravenous administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and consider the risks/benefits before any attempt at re-administration [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In those with established seizure disorders, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Metabolite Accumulation:** While this concerns oral administration, clinicians should note that chronic high doses of oral levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites, trimethylamine (TMA) and trimethylamine-N-oxide (TMAO) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Serious Adverse Effects
- **Hypersensitivity:** Anaphylaxis and laryngeal edema.
- **Neurologic:** Seizures.

### Common Adverse Effects (ESRD Trials)
Adverse events reported at a frequency ≥5% in double-blind, placebo-controlled trials for ESRD patients (regardless of causality):
- **Body as a Whole:** Abdominal pain, accidental injury, asthenia, chest pain, fever, flu syndrome, headache, infection, injection site reaction, pain.
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (20%), hypotension (14%), tachycardia (6%), palpitations, arrhythmia.
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea (16%), vomiting (15%), nausea (8%), anorexia, constipation, dyspepsia.
- **Metabolic/Nutritional:** Hypercalcemia (9%), hyperkalemia (6%), peripheral edema, weight changes.
- **Nervous:** Dizziness (13%), paresthesia (5%), insomnia, depression.
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (23%), cough increase (9%), dyspnea (7%), rhinitis.

### Other Observed Reactions
- Body odor and gastritis have been reported less frequently [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin. Monitoring of INR is recommended upon initiation or dose adjustment [US].
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Levocarnitine is not bound to plasma proteins or albumin; therefore, displacement interactions are unlikely [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Not specified by letter category]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at doses up to 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) revealed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine milk concentrations following administration. The benefits of maternal therapy must be weighed against potential risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies showed no impairment of fertility [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders applies to paediatric patients [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Specific dose adjustments for the elderly are not specified in available sources [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Used specifically in ESRD patients on dialysis (see Section 3). The safety of *oral* levocarnitine in renal impairment is not established due to metabolite accumulation [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids in patients with metabolic defects [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In ESRD, it increases plasma carnitine concentrations and may alleviate metabolic abnormalities related to toxic organic acid accumulation [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not protein-bound.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (feces).
    - **Elimination:** Mean apparent terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours. Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours (uncorrected for endogenous levels) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients, 20 mg/kg IV three times weekly post-dialysis increased mean pre-dialysis trough levels from ~20 µmol/L to 190 ± 55 µmol/L.
- **Dose-Ranging Studies:** Trials using 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg showed dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine levels over 12 and 24 weeks. While carnitine concentrations increased, clinical outcomes regarding specific signs and symptoms of deficiency in this population were not established in these trials [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Precautions:** Avoid excessive heat; protect from freezing.
- **Handling:** Discard any unused portion of an opened vial as the formulation is preservative-free [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Rx-only (Prescription only).
