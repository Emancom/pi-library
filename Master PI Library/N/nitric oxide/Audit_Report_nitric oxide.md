# Originality Audit Report: nitric oxide

## Audit Summary
- **Originality Score:** 68/100
- **Verbatim Copied Passages Found:** 4
- **Overall Assessment:** Concern

## Verbatim Copied Passages
The following passages are identical or near-identical to the source regulatory documents:

1.  **Indications (Neonatal):**
    -   **Editorial Text:** "...term and near-term (>34 weeks gestation) neonates presenting with hypoxic respiratory failure associated with clinical or echocardiographic evidence of pulmonary hypertension. The primary goals are to improve oxygenation and reduce the requirement for extracorporeal membrane oxygenation (ECMO)."
    -   **Source Match:** Document 1 (TGA), Section 4.1.
    -   **Note:** Almost identical word-for-word to the TGA indication, with only the addition of the word "gestation" and "requirement."

2.  **Contraindications:**
    -   **Editorial Text:** "Neonates who are known to be dependent on right-to-left shunting of blood."
    -   **Source Match:** Document 1 (TGA), Section 4.3; Document 2 (FDA), Section 4.
    -   **Note:** Standard regulatory phrasing, but used verbatim.

3.  **Mechanism of Action:**
    -   **Editorial Text:** "...binds to the heme moiety of cytosolic guanylate cyclase in vascular smooth muscle. This activates the enzyme, increasing levels of cyclic guanosine 3’,5’-monophosphate (cGMP), which induces smooth muscle relaxation."
    -   **Source Match:** Document 1 (TGA), Section 5.1.
    -   **Note:** Copied verbatim from the TGA "Mechanism of Action" section, except for changing "relaxes" to "induces... relaxation."

4.  **Pharmacokinetics (Metabolism):**
    -   **Editorial Text:** "...combines with oxyhemoglobin to form methemoglobin and nitrate. Within the lungs, it can also react with oxygen and water to form NO₂ and nitrite."
    -   **Source Match:** Document 1 (TGA), Section 5.2.
    -   **Note:** Verbatim match to the TGA description of the chemical reaction process.

## Close Paraphrases
1.  **Rebound Pulmonary Hypertension:** The description of "Sudden withdrawal may cause a rapid increase in pulmonary artery pressure (PAP) and a decrease in systemic oxygenation" is a very close paraphrase of TGA Section 4.4 and FDA Section 5.1.
2.  **Left Ventricular Dysfunction:** The list of symptoms (pulmonary edema, increased PCWP, worsening heart failure) follows the exact sequence found in FDA Section 5.4.
3.  **Methemoglobinemia Warning:** The phrasing "Nitric oxide combines with hemoglobin to form methemoglobin, which cannot transport oxygen" is nearly identical to FDA Section 5.2 ("...which does not transport oxygen").

## Originality Strengths
The editorial excels in **Section 5 (Dosage and Administration)** and the comparative threshold notes in **Section 4**. The writer successfully synthesized two different sets of data (e.g., TGA’s 96-hour limit vs. FDA’s 14-day limit, and the differing MetHb and NO₂ toxicity thresholds) into a single, cohesive narrative. This comparative analysis is genuinely original work and provides added value not found in either individual source document.

## Differences from Source PIs
-   **Harmonization:** The editorial explicitly contrasts TGA and FDA safety thresholds (e.g., 2.5% vs 5% MetHb), which is not present in the sources.
-   **Structural Merge:** The editorial combines the "Cardiac Surgery" indication (unique to the TGA document) with the "Neonatal" indication (common to both) in a unified list.
-   **Omissions:** The editorial omits extensive clinical trial tables and specific delivery system brand names (like DSIR Plus) mentioned in the FDA source to maintain a "global" editorial tone.
-   **Tone:** The editorial uses a more instructional, summary-based tone compared to the dense, multi-page clinical trial narratives in the TGA document.

## Recommendation
**REVISION NEEDED.**

While the synthesis of differing regulatory thresholds is excellent, the editorial relies too heavily on verbatim strings for the **Mechanism of Action** and **Approved Indications**. 

**Specific Changes Required:**
1.  **Rewrite Section 10 (Mechanism of Action):** Describe the activation of guanylate cyclase and the role of cGMP using original phrasing. Avoid the "heme moiety of cytosolic guanylate cyclase" sequence which is a signature of the TGA text.
2.  **Rewrite Section 2 (Approved Indications):** Paraphrase the neonatal indication more thoroughly. Instead of "clinical or echocardiographic evidence," consider "confirmed via clinical assessment or ultrasound."
3.  **Rewrite Section 9 (Pharmacokinetics):** Describe the metabolic pathway (conversion to nitrate/nitrite) using different sentence structures to avoid the TGA's specific technical phrasing.
