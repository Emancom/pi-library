# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial has been harmonised from official Product Information (PI) sources for **levocarnitine** injection.
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial synthesis for healthcare professionals. It serves as a clinically accurate overview but is not a substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. 
- *Note: While the prompt context mentioned Australian Nystatin, the provided source document is for Levocarnitine Injection (US); this editorial reflects the provided evidence base for Levocarnitine.*

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (L-carnitine); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection, USP [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Injection: 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) single-dose vial [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that result in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Administration:** Administer intravenously via slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion [US].

### Dosing by Indication
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    - Recommended dose: 50 mg/kg [US].
    - A loading dose may be given in severe metabolic crises, followed by an equivalent dose divided over the subsequent 24 hours.
    - Dosing frequency: q3h or q4h; never less than q6h [US].
    - Maximum recorded dose: 300 mg/kg [US].
- **ESRD on Hemodialysis:**
    - Recommended starting dose: 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight) [US].
    - Administration: Slow bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].

### Administration Instructions
- **Monitoring:** Obtain plasma carnitine concentrations before starting therapy. Recommended weekly and monthly monitoring includes blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine levels (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L) [US].
- **Dose Adjustment:** In ESRD, downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough (pre-dialysis) levels (normal range 40–50 µmol/L) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have occurred—often within minutes of administration. These are reported most frequently in ESRD patients on dialysis. If a reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity may occur [US].
- **Renal Impairment (Oral Form Precaution):** While this PI covers the injection, clinicians should note that chronic high-dose *oral* levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function or ESRD may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites: trimethylamine (TMA) and trimethylamine-N-oxide (TMAO) [US].
- **General Monitoring:** Parenteral products should be visually inspected for particulate matter and discoloration prior to use [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions are often confounded by the underlying pathology of ESRD or metabolic disorders.

### Very Common (>10%) and Common (1–10%) [US]
Based on clinical trials in ESRD patients, the following effects occurred at a frequency ≥5% (regardless of causality):
- **Body as a Whole:** Abdominal pain, accidental injury, asthenia, back pain, chest pain, fever, flu syndrome, headache, infection, injection site reaction, pain.
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension, hypotension, tachycardia, arrhythmia, palpitations.
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea, nausea, vomiting, dyspepsia, anorexia.
- **Metabolic/Nutritional:** Hypercalcemia, hypervolemia, hyperkalemia, peripheral edema, weight changes.
- **Nervous:** Dizziness, insomnia, paresthesia, depression.
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis, cough increase, dyspnea, rhinitis.
- **Skin:** Pruritus, rash.

### Serious Adverse Effects
- **Anaphylaxis/Hypersensitivity:** [Rare but serious]
- **Seizures:** [Post-marketing frequency not specified]

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have been observed when used concomitantly with levocarnitine. 
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not explicitly lettered, but indicates animal studies (rats/rabbits at 3.8x human dose) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Should be used during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows indicate increased carnitine concentrations in milk following exogenous administration. Clinicians should weigh the benefits to the mother against the potential risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** No impairment observed in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders and ESRD is weight-based (mg/kg) and applicable to paediatric populations as described in the Dosage and Administration section [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Primarily indicated for ESRD; however, caution is advised regarding metabolite accumulation if oral forms are used in this population [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production [US].

### Pharmacokinetics
- **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
- **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
- **Elimination:** Following IV bolus (20 mg/kg), approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours. The mean apparent terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

### Pharmacodynamics
Levocarnitine clears accumulated toxic acyl-CoA compounds by forming acylcarnitine, which is rapidly excreted [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Bioavailability Study:** In healthy volunteers, levocarnitine tablets and oral solutions were found to be bioequivalent, with an absolute bioavailability of approximately 15% after correction for endogenous levels [US].
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of ESRD patients (20 mg/kg post-dialysis), trough levels increased from a baseline of ~21 µmol/L to ~190 µmol/L after 8 weeks. A dose-ranging study (10, 20, 40 mg/kg) demonstrated dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine concentrations [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Avoid excessive heat and protect from freezing. Discard any unused portion of opened vials as the formulation contains no preservatives [US].
- **Stability (Admixture):** Compatible with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s (0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) for up to 24 hours at room temperature in PVC bags [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources.
