# Editorial Product Information: Nicardipine Hydrochloride

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial is derived from the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of nicardipine hydrochloride for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical data into a concise reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI or local clinical guidelines required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Nicardipine hydrochloride (a dihydropyridine calcium channel blocker).
- **Brand Name(s):** Nicardipine Hydrochloride in Sodium Chloride Injection (Caplin Steriles) [US].
- **ATC Code:** C08CA04.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):**
    - Premixed injection in 0.9% Sodium Chloride: 20 mg in 200 mL (0.1 mg/mL) [US].
    - Premixed injection in 0.9% Sodium Chloride: 40 mg in 200 mL (0.2 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Caplin Steriles Limited [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Short-term treatment of hypertension:** Indicated when oral therapy is not feasible or desirable [US]. 
- **Note:** For prolonged blood pressure control, patients should be transitioned to oral medication as soon as their clinical condition permits [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
Dosage must be individualised based on the severity of hypertension and the specific patient response.

### Initiation of Therapy (Drug-Free Patient)
- **Initial Rate:** 5 mg/hr (50 mL/hr of the 0.1 mg/mL concentration).
- **Titration for Gradual Reduction:** Increase the infusion rate by 2.5 mg/hr every 15 minutes.
- **Titration for Rapid Reduction:** Increase the infusion rate by 2.5 mg/hr every 5 minutes.
- **Maximum Rate:** 15 mg/hr.
- **Maintenance:** Adjust the rate as needed to maintain the target blood pressure.

### Substitution for Oral Nicardipine
When switching from oral nicardipine to IV infusion, the following equivalent rates are recommended [US]:
| Oral Nicardipine Dose | Equivalent IV Infusion Rate |
| :--- | :--- |
| 20 mg every 8 hours | 0.5 mg/hr |
| 30 mg every 8 hours | 1.2 mg/hr |
| 40 mg every 8 hours | 2.2 mg/hr |

### Administration Instructions
- **Preparation:** Ready-to-use bags. Do not dilute. Inspect for particulate matter or discoloration.
- **Infusion Site:** Administer via a central line or a large peripheral vein. 
- **Site Rotation:** Change the peripheral infusion site every 12 hours to minimise venous irritation [US].
- **Hypotension/Tachycardia Management:** Discontinue the infusion if these occur. Once the patient is stable, the infusion may be restarted at low doses (3 mg/hr to 5 mg/hr) and re-titrated [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Advanced Aortic Stenosis:** Nicardipine reduces afterload, which may worsen myocardial oxygen balance in these patients by reducing diastolic pressure [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Infusion Site Reactions:** To prevent venous thrombosis, phlebitis, and extravasation, avoid small veins (e.g., dorsum of hand/wrist).
- **Beta-Blocker Withdrawal:** Nicardipine is not a beta-blocker and does not prevent the risks associated with abrupt beta-blocker withdrawal; beta-blockers should be tapered gradually [US].
- **Angina Exacerbation:** Induction or worsening of angina has been observed in <1% of patients with coronary artery disease [US].
- **Congestive Heart Failure (CHF):** Use with caution. While it reduces afterload, negative inotropic effects may occur in patients with severe left ventricular dysfunction, especially when combined with beta-blockers [US].
- **Hepatic and Renal Impairment:** Use with caution and employ careful dose titration due to altered clearance and increased exposure (AUC) [US].
- **Acute Cerebral Infarction/Hemorrhage:** Avoid systemic hypotension in these patients [US].
- **Pheochromocytoma:** Limited clinical experience; use with caution [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions are typically related to vasodilation. In clinical trials, approximately 12% of patients discontinued therapy due to adverse events [US].

| Frequency | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Headache (13%) |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Hypotension (5%), Tachycardia (4%), Nausea/Vomiting (4%) |
| **Other (Incidence <2%)** | Fever, neck pain, angina pectoris, AV block, ST segment depression, inverted T wave, deep-vein thrombophlebitis, dyspepsia, thrombocytopenia, hypophosphatemia, peripheral edema, confusion, hypertonia, respiratory disorder, conjunctivitis, ear disorder, tinnitus, urinary frequency. |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Sinus node dysfunction and myocardial infarction have been reported in patients on chronic oral therapy, potentially related to disease progression [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Cimetidine:** Increases nicardipine plasma levels; monitor closely [US].
- **Cyclosporine and Tacrolimus:** Nicardipine inhibits CYP3A4, which may increase plasma levels of these immunosuppressants. Frequent monitoring of trough levels and dose adjustments are recommended [US].
- **Digoxin:** Oral nicardipine does not usually alter digoxin levels, but monitoring is still advised [US].
- **Antihypertensive Agents:** Potential for additive effects; monitor blood pressure closely [US].
- **In Vitro Data:** Nicardipine protein binding is not altered by furosemide, propranolol, warfarin, or naproxen [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Category C [US]:** There are no adequate/well-controlled studies in humans. Animal data show increased embryolethality at doses near human equivalent levels. Use only if the benefit justifies the potential risk to the foetus [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Nicardipine is minimally excreted into human milk (calculated daily infant dose <0.3 mcg). However, it is recommended that women who wish to breastfeed should not be given this drug [US].
- **Fertility:** No impairment of fertility was observed in rat studies at doses up to 8 times the maximum recommended human oral dose [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy in patients under 18 years of age have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Pharmacokinetics are similar to younger adults. However, use caution and start at the low end of the dosing range due to the higher frequency of decreased hepatic, renal, or cardiac function [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Lower systemic clearance and higher AUC are observed in moderate impairment. Careful titration is required [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Extensive hepatic metabolism means plasma concentrations are elevated in liver disease. Consider lower dosages and monitor closely [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Inhibits the transmembrane influx of calcium ions into cardiac and vascular smooth muscle (slow channel blocker). It is more selective for vascular smooth muscle than cardiac muscle [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Rapid dose-related increase in plasma levels; steady state reached in 24–48 hours. Highly protein bound (>95%).
    - **Metabolism:** Rapidly and extensively metabolised by the liver (CYP2C8, 2D6, and 3A4) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Tri-exponential decline (alpha-half-life 3m; beta-half-life 45m; gamma-half-life 14h). 49% excreted in urine and 43% in faeces; 0% as unchanged drug [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Produces a significant decrease in systemic vascular resistance. Heart rate may increase as a reflex response to vasodilation [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Hypertension Trials:** In two multicenter, double-blind trials, IV nicardipine (0.5 to 4 mg/hr) showed dose-dependent BP reduction. At 4 mg/hr, systolic BP decreased by 26 mmHg (17%) and diastolic by 21 mmHg (20%) [US].
- **Severe Hypertension:** In severe cases, higher rates (5–15 mg/hr) achieved therapeutic response in a mean of 77 minutes [US].
- **Postoperative Hypertension:** Therapeutic response was achieved in a mean of 12 minutes [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from light. Avoid elevated temperatures. Freezing does not adversely affect the product [US].
- **Usage:** Use immediately once removed from the overwrap [US].

- **Legal Classification:**
