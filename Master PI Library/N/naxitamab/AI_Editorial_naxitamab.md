# Editorial Product Information: Naxitamab

## Editorial Note
*   **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA). 
*   **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonized editorial overview of naxitamab, synthesized for use by healthcare professionals. 
*   **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. At the time of this writing, only the United States (FDA) source documentation was provided for synthesis.

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredient:** Naxitamab-gqgk
*   **Chemical Class:** Glycolipid disialoganglioside (GD2)-binding recombinant humanized monoclonal IgG1 antibody.
*   **Brand Name:** **DANYELZA** [US]
*   **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Dosage Form and Strength:** Injection: 40 mg/10 mL (4 mg/mL) in a single-dose vial [US]. The solution is clear to slightly opalescent and colorless to slightly yellow.
*   **Route of Administration:** Intravenous infusion.
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** BTG International Inc. [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Relapsed or Refractory High-Risk Neuroblastoma:** Indicated in combination with granulocyte-macrophage colony-stimulating factor (GM-CSF) for the treatment of pediatric patients (1 year of age and older) and adult patients with relapsed or refractory high-risk neuroblastoma in the bone or bone marrow who have achieved a partial response, minor response, or stable disease with prior therapy [US-approved].
*   *Editorial Note: This indication received accelerated approval based on overall response rate (ORR) and duration of response (DOR). Continued approval may be contingent upon verification of clinical benefit in confirmatory trials [US].*

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### 3.1 General Dosing (Adult and Pediatric ≥ 1 year)
*   **Recommended Dose:** 3 mg/kg/day (maximum 150 mg/day) administered on Days 1, 3, and 5 of each treatment cycle [US].
*   **Cycle Schedule:** Cycles are repeated every 4 weeks until a partial or complete response is achieved, followed by 5 additional cycles every 4 weeks. Subsequent cycles may be repeated every 8 weeks [US].
*   **Combination Therapy (GM-CSF):** Naxitamab must be administered in combination with GM-CSF.
    *   **Days -4 to 0:** GM-CSF 250 µg/m²/day (subcutaneous).
    *   **Days 1 to 5:** GM-CSF 500 µg/m²/day (subcutaneous), administered at least 1 hour prior to naxitamab on infusion days [US].

### 3.2 Premedication and Supportive Care
*   **Neuropathic Pain Prophylaxis:** Initiate a 12-day course of prophylactic medication (e.g., gabapentin) beginning 5 days prior to the first infusion. Administer oral opioids 45–60 minutes prior to infusion, with IV opioids available for breakthrough pain [US].
*   **Infusion-Related Reactions (IRR) and Nausea:**
    *   **Corticosteroids:** IV methylprednisolone (2 mg/kg; max 80 mg) 30 minutes to 2 hours before the first infusion. Required for subsequent infusions only if a severe IRR occurred previously.
    *   **Others:** Antihistamine, H2 antagonist, acetaminophen, and antiemetic 30 minutes prior to each infusion [US].

### 3.3 Administration Instructions
*   **Method:** Intravenous infusion only. Do NOT administer as a bolus or push [US].
*   **Rate:** 
    *   **First Infusion (Cycle 1, Day 1):** Over 60 minutes.
    *   **Subsequent Infusions:** Over 30 to 60 minutes as tolerated [US].
*   **Monitoring:** Observe patients for a minimum of 2 hours post-infusion [US].

### 3.4 Missed Doses
*   If a dose is missed, it should be administered the following week by Day 10. GM-CSF dosing must be adjusted accordingly (500 µg/m²/day for 5 days total surrounding the rescheduled naxitamab doses) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
*   **Hypersensitivity:** History of severe hypersensitivity reaction (including anaphylaxis) to naxitamab-gqgk [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### 5.1 Boxed Warnings [US]
*   **Serious Infusion-Related Reactions:** Can cause cardiac arrest, anaphylaxis, hypotension, bronchospasm, and stridor. Infusion reactions occurred in 94–100% of patients.
*   **Neurotoxicity:** Can cause severe neuropathic pain (occurring in 94-100% of patients), transverse myelitis, and Reversible Posterior Leukoencephalopathy Syndrome (RPLS).

### 5.2 General Warnings
*   **Myocarditis:** Has occurred in adolescent patients, usually within days of treatment. Monitor for chest pain, dyspnea, or palpitations [US].
*   **Hypertension:** Occurred in 44% of patients. Do not initiate in patients with uncontrolled hypertension. Monitor blood pressure during infusion and daily on Days 1–8 of each cycle [US].
*   **Orthostatic Hypotension:** Severe cases requiring hospitalization have occurred. Monitor postural blood pressure in symptomatic patients [US].
*   **Neurological Disorders of the Eye:** Includes unequal pupils, blurred vision, and photophobia [US].
*   **Prolonged Urinary Retention:** May occur, often in the context of opioid use for pain management. Discontinue if retention persists after stopping opioids [US].
*   **Embryo-Fetal Toxicity:** May cause fetal harm [US].

*   **Serious Adverse Effects:** Anaphylaxis, cardiac arrest, transverse myelitis, RPLS, and respiratory depression [US].
*   **Laboratory Abnormalities:** Very common (>10%) include decreased lymphocytes, neutrophils, hemoglobin, platelets, potassium, albumin, sodium, calcium, and glucose; increased ALT and AST [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** No formal drug interaction studies have been conducted. As a monoclonal antibody, naxitamab is not expected to interact with Cytochrome P450 enzymes [US].
*   **Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Use of systemic opioids for pain management may increase the risk of prolonged urinary retention [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not assigned under current labeling rules, but explicitly noted as potentially harmful to the fetus]. There are no human data. IgG1 antibodies are known to cross the placenta. Advise females of reproductive potential to use effective contraception during treatment and for **2 months** after the final dose [US].
*   **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if naxitamab is excreted in human milk. Due to the potential for serious adverse reactions in the infant, breastfeeding is not recommended during treatment and for **2 months** after the final dose [US].
*   **Fertility:** No dedicated animal or human studies have evaluated effects on fertility [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy established for patients ≥ 1 year of age. Not established for patients < 1 year [US].
*   **Elderly Use:** Neuroblastoma is primarily a pediatric/young adult disease; clinical trials did not include patients ≥ 65 years [US].
*   **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** No specific dosage adjustments are provided in the source documentation.
*   **Body Weight:** Systemic exposure in patients > 50 kg is not expected to differ significantly from those 30–50 kg when dosed at 3 mg/kg [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
*   **Mechanism of Action:** Binds to GD2, a disialoganglioside overexpressed on neuroblastoma cells. It induces complement-dependent cytotoxicity (CDC) and antibody-dependent cell-mediated cytotoxicity (ADCC) [US].
*   **Pharmacokinetics:** 
    *   **Maximum Plasma Concentration (Cmax):** 57.4 µg/mL (49% CV) following 3 mg/kg infusion [US].
    *   **Elimination:** Mean terminal half-life is 8.2 days [US].
    *   **Metabolism:** Expected to be degraded into small peptides via catabolic pathways [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   **Study 201 (NCT03363373):** An open-label, multicenter, single-arm trial (n=25). In the efficacy analysis (n=22), the ORR was **45%** (95% CI: 24%, 68%) with a 36% complete response rate. Median DOR was 6.2 months [US].
*   **Study 12-230 (NCT01757626):** A single-center, open-label, single-arm trial (n=72). In the efficacy analysis (n=38), the ORR was **34%** (95% CI: 20%, 51%) with a 26% complete response rate. 23% of responders maintained a response for ≥ 6 months [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   **Vial Storage:** Refrigerate at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F). Store in the original carton to protect from light [US].
*   **Diluted Solution:**
    *   **Room Temperature (15°C to 25°C):** Up to 8 hours.
    *   **Refrigerated (2°C to 8°C):** Up to 24 hours.
    *   *Note: Once removed from refrigeration, the infusion must be initiated within 8 hours [US].*
