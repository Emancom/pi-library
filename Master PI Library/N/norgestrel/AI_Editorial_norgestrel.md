# Editorial Product Information: Norgestrel

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) and Drug Facts labelling from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, consolidating clinical and regulatory data into a single reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI or Drug Facts required for prescribing or dispensing in a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** **Norgestrel** (0.075 mg).
- **Chemical Class:** Progestin; synthetic 19-nortestosterone derivative.
- **Brand Names:** Distributed under Walgreen Co. (Note: Label compares active ingredient to **Opill**) [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Oral tablet; 0.075 mg [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Walgreens Company [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Contraception:** Indicated for use by females of reproductive potential to prevent pregnancy [US].
- **Important Limitations:** 
    - This product is a daily oral contraceptive and is **NOT** indicated for use as emergency contraception (morning-after pill) [US].
    - It does not provide protection against HIV/AIDS or other sexually transmitted diseases (STDs) [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing:** One tablet (0.075 mg) taken orally at the same time every single day [US].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Tablets must be taken daily without any breaks between packs, regardless of the presence of menstrual bleeding or spotting [US].
    - Effectiveness relies on strict adherence to a 24-hour dosing interval.
- **Initiation:** May be started on any day of the month. If switching from another oral contraceptive, vaginal ring, or patch, the patient should start **norgestrel** the day after stopping the previous method [US].
- **Backup Contraception:** A barrier method (e.g., condoms) must be used for the first 48 hours of treatment, as the product requires 2 days to reach contraceptive efficacy [US].
- **Missed Dose Instructions:**
    - **Late (<3 hours):** Take the tablet immediately and resume the normal schedule the following day [US].
    - **Missed (>3 hours late or ≥1 tablets missed):** Take one tablet as soon as remembered (this may result in taking two tablets in one day). Use a barrier backup method for the next 48 hours [US].
- **Gastrointestinal Interference:** If vomiting or severe diarrhoea occurs within 4 hours of dosing, use a barrier backup method for the next 48 hours [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Breast Cancer:** Current or past history of breast cancer, as some tumours are hormone-sensitive [US].
- **Pregnancy:** Known or suspected pregnancy [US].
- **Concomitant Contraception:** Use in combination with other hormonal contraceptives (pills, rings, patches, implants, injections) or intrauterine devices (IUDs) [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known allergy to **norgestrel** or any inactive ingredients (e.g., FD&C Yellow No. 5/tartrazine) [US].
- **Gender:** Not for use in males [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Allergy Alert (Tartrazine):** This product contains FD&C Yellow No. 5 (tartrazine). Clinicians should note that patients with aspirin sensitivity often exhibit tartrazine hypersensitivity. Symptoms may include hives, asthma, or anaphylaxis [US].
- **Ectopic Pregnancy:** While less likely with correct use, persistent, severe lower abdominal pain (especially unilateral) should be evaluated immediately to rule out ectopic pregnancy [US].
- **Hepatic Effects:** Patients with liver disease or liver tumours should consult a physician before use. Treatment should be discontinued if jaundice (yellowing of the skin or eyes) develops [US].
- **Undiagnosed Vaginal Bleeding:** Patients with unexplained vaginal bleeding between periods must be evaluated before initiating or continuing therapy [US].
- **Migraine:** Patients experiencing new-onset migraines with aura or worsening of existing migraines should be monitored, as these patients may have an increased risk of stroke [US].
- **Hormone-Sensitive Cancers:** Caution is advised for patients with a history of any cancer sensitive to progestins [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Very Common (>10%):** 
    - Changes in menstrual patterns, including irregular periods, spotting, or breakthrough bleeding between periods.
    - Amenorrhoea (cessation of periods) may occur in some users [US].
- **Common (1–10%):**
    - Nervous system: Headaches, dizziness.
    - Gastrointestinal: Nausea, abdominal pain, cramps, bloating.
    - Other: Increased appetite [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:**
    - Ectopic pregnancy.
    - Potential for increased stroke risk in patients with migraine with aura.
    - Severe allergic reactions [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Enzyme Inducers:** Efficacy may be reduced by drugs that induce hepatic enzymes (CYP450), including:
    - **Anticonvulsants:** Barbiturates, carbamazepine, oxcarbazepine, phenytoin, topiramate, primidone [US].
    - **Antimycobacterials:** Rifampin, rifabutin [US].
    - **HIV/AIDS medications:** Efavirenz [US].
    - **Other:** Bosentan (for pulmonary hypertension) [US].
- **Herbal Supplements:** St. John’s Wort (*Hypericum perforatum*) may decrease the effectiveness of **norgestrel** [US].
- **Ulipristal Acetate:** Do not use **norgestrel** within 5 days of taking ulipristal acetate (emergency contraception), as this may reduce the efficacy of both agents [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Contraindicated. If pregnancy occurs or is suspected, the medication must be discontinued immediately. Treatment does not delay the return of fertility once stopped [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Considered safe and effective. Small amounts of progestin pass into breast milk, but available data indicate no adverse effects on milk production or infant health [US].
- **Fertility:** Not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Not specified in available sources [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified; typically not indicated post-menopause [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Not specified in available sources [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Patients with liver disease or liver tumours should seek medical advice before use [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** **Norgestrel** is a progestin-only pill (POP). It prevents pregnancy primarily by thickening the cervical mucus to inhibit sperm penetration. It may also suppress ovulation in some, but not all, menstrual cycles [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified in the provided Drug Facts labelling.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Provides contraceptive cover starting 48 hours after the first dose when taken consistently [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Efficacy Data:** In 8 clinical trials conducted in the United States, **norgestrel** demonstrated an efficacy rate of approximately 98% (2 pregnancies per 100 women) over one year of use [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store between 20°C and 25°C (68°F–77°F) [US].
- **Disposal:** Do not flush tablets. Use a drug take-back option for disposal [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as a Human OTC Drug (Full Prescription Strength available Over-the-Counter). NDC 0363-5510 [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** In case of overdose, contact a Poison Control Center (1-800-222-1222) [US].
