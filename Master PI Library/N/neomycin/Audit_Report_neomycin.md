# Originality Audit Report: neomycin

## Audit Summary
- **Originality Score:** 45/100
- **Verbatim Copied Passages Found:** 8
- **Overall Assessment:** **Fail**

The editorial demonstrates a high degree of reliance on the source text's sentence structure and specific phrasing. While the synthesis of human and veterinary data into one document is an original structural concept, the actual prose is frequently lifted verbatim or near-verbatim from the provided regulatory documents.

## Close Paraphrases

*   **Section 2 (Human Use):** The phrase "inflammatory and pruritic manifestations of dermatoses" is a direct carry-over from TGA Section 4.1. While technical, it should be simplified (e.g., "swelling and itching of skin conditions").
*   **Section 4 (General Safety):** "If irritation or sensitivity occurs during treatment, therapy should be discontinued" is very close to TGA Section 4.4: "If sensitivity or irritation develops, use of this medication should be discontinued."
*   **Section 5 (Veterinary Dosage):** "10 mg of neomycin sulfate per pound of body weight per day" is the clinical data point. While the data cannot change, the sentence structure matches the FDA document's heading instructions.

## Originality Strengths
*   **Synthesis Structure:** The editorial successfully identifies and segregates human-topical vs. veterinary-oral data, which requires an original organizational approach.
*   **Editorial Note:** The introductory paragraph is entirely original and provides helpful context for the reader.
*   **Section 11 (Storage):** The storage conditions for the powder are well-summarized from the technical FDA labels into a readable format.

## Differences from Source PIs
*   **Harmonization:** The editorial explicitly labels which information comes from which agency ([TGA] vs [FDA]), which is not present in the sources.
*   **Omission:** The editorial correctly omits the specific concentrations/doses for Triamcinolone, Gramicidin, and Nystatin to focus on Neomycin.
*   **Consolidation:** The withdrawal periods from the FDA document (found in a table/list) are consolidated into a concise bulleted summary.

## Recommendation
**REVISION REQUIRED**

The document fails the originality audit due to significant verbatim copying of technical descriptions and pharmacokinetic data. To pass, the following revisions are necessary:
1.  **Reword Clinical Descriptions:** Change phrases like "inflammatory and pruritic manifestations of various dermatoses" to more common editorial language.
2.  **Rewrite Pharmacokinetics:** Section 9 is almost a complete copy of the TGA text. This must be entirely rewritten (e.g., "The kidneys clear neomycin quickly from the bloodstream...").
3.  **Indication Summary:** The veterinary indications should be summarized rather than quoted. Instead of "colibacillosis (bacterial enteritis)," use "bacterial-related diarrhea and intestinal infections."
4.  **Pregnancy Section:** This section needs a total overhaul to describe the risks of aminoglycosides without using the specific phrasing of the TGA document.
