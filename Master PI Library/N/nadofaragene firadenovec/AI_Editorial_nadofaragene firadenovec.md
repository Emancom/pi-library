# Editorial Product Information: Nadofaragene firadenovec

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising regulatory data into a clinically focused format.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority's approved labelling for definitive prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Nadofaragene firadenovec-vncg.
- **Chemical Class:** Non-replicating, recombinant adenovirus serotype 5 (Ad5) vector-based gene therapy containing a transgene encoding human interferon alfa-2b (IFNα2b).
- **Brand Name:** **ADSTILADRIN** [US].
- **ATC Code:** L03AB05 (Interferon alfa-2b); gene therapy specific code not yet universally assigned across all hierarchies.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Sterile, preservative-free, clear to opalescent frozen suspension for intravesical instillation. Provided at a nominal concentration of $3 \times 10^{11}$ viral particles (vp)/mL in 20 mL single-use vials [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravesical instillation only. Not for intravenous, topical, or oral use.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Ferring Pharmaceuticals Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **High-Risk NMIBC:** Indicated for the treatment of adult patients with high-risk Bacillus Calmette-Guérin (BCG)-unresponsive non-muscle invasive bladder cancer (NMIBC) with carcinoma in situ (CIS) with or without papillary tumours [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** The recommended dose is 75 mL (at a concentration of $3 \times 10^{11}$ vp/mL) instilled into the bladder once every three (3) months [US].
- **Premedication:** Administration of an anticholinergic is recommended prior to each instillation to assist with retention [US].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Use a urinary catheter for instillation.
    - Completely empty the bladder before instillation.
    - **Dwell Time:** The suspension should be retained in the bladder for 1 hour.
    - **Patient Repositioning:** During the 1-hour dwell time, the patient should reposition every 15 minutes (left, right, back, and abdomen) to ensure maximum surface exposure to the urothelium. 
    - **Post-procedure:** After 1 hour, the patient voids. Voided urine for the first two days post-treatment must be disinfected (see Section 12).
- **Preparation:** Vials must be thawed and brought to room temperature (20°C to 25°C) before use. Thawing can occur in a water bath (approx. 25 mins), at room temperature (3–5 hours), or in a refrigerator (4–5 hours) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Contraindicated in patients with a history of hypersensitivity to interferon alfa or any component of the formulation [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Risk of Delayed Cystectomy:** Delaying radical cystectomy in patients with BCG-unresponsive CIS can lead to the development of metastatic bladder cancer, which may be lethal. If a complete response is not achieved after 3 months, or if CIS recurs, cystectomy should be considered [US].
- **Disseminated Adenovirus Infection:** There is a theoretical risk of disseminated infection due to low levels of replication-competent adenovirus. Exposure to the product should be avoided in immunocompromised or immunodeficient individuals [US].
- **Biosafety Handling:** As a gene therapy product, universal biosafety precautions must be followed during preparation and administration. Immunocompromised individuals should not handle the product [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Serious adverse reactions occurred in 11% of patients. The most common serious reactions (>1%) were coronary artery disease and hematuria. Permanent discontinuation due to adverse reactions occurred in 1.9% of patients [US].

| Frequency | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Instillation site discharge (33%), fatigue (24%), bladder spasm (21%), micturition urgency (20%), hematuria (18%), pyrexia (16%), chills (15%), dysuria (16%) |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Syncope (1.3%), coronary artery disease (1.3%), acute coronary syndrome (1.3%), atrial fibrillation (1.3%) |
| **Uncommon (<1%)** | Heart failure (0.6%), pericarditis (0.6%), sepsis (0.6%) |

**Laboratory Abnormalities (>15%):** Increased glucose (38%), increased triglycerides (30%), increased creatinine (17%), decreased phosphate (16%), and decreased hemoglobin (16%) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Dynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Note:** Clinical interaction studies have not been performed. As an intravesical gene therapy with minimal systemic absorption, the risk of systemic drug-drug interactions is considered low.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not assigned under current labelling rules, but advised of potential risk to fetus]. Animal reproductive studies have not been conducted. It is recommended to verify pregnancy status in females of reproductive potential before starting treatment.
- **Contraception:** Females should use effective contraception during treatment and for 6 months after the final dose. Males with female partners of reproductive potential should use effective contraception for 3 months after the final dose [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** There is no data regarding the presence of the drug in human milk. The developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding should be weighed against the clinical need for the drug and potential adverse effects on the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** No nonclinical or clinical studies have evaluated the effect on fertility [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness in patients under 18 years of age have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** In clinical trials, 32% of patients were ≥75 years. No overall differences in safety or efficacy were observed between older and younger patients, though data for patients <65 years is limited in this specific population [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources. Systematic absorption is low.
- **Immunocompromised Patients:** Use should be avoided due to the risk of disseminated adenovirus infection [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** A non-replicating adenoviral vector that delivers a copy of the human IFNα2b gene to the bladder urothelium. This results in cell transduction and transient local expression of IFNα2b protein, which exerts anti-tumour effects [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Quantifiable concentrations of IFNα2b protein are detectable in the urine for up to 10–12 days post-instillation, with levels generally increasing in a dose-dependent manner [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Primarily local; systemic exposure is minimal.
    - **Shedding:** Vector DNA is detectable in the urine. In clinical studies, vector DNA was detected in the blood of only one patient at 1 hour post-dose [US].
    - **Distribution:** Nonclinical monkey studies showed vector-specific DNA mainly in the bladder, with limited detection in the liver, kidney, and gonads, which cleared over time [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Study CS-003 (NCT02773849):** A multicenter, single-arm trial in 103 adults with BCG-unresponsive high-risk NMIBC with CIS.
- **Efficacy Results:**
    - **Complete Response (CR) Rate:** 51% (95% CI: 41%, 61%).
    - **Duration of Response:** The median duration of response was 9.7 months. 46% of responders maintained CR for at least 12 months [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage:** Ship and store frozen at $\leq -60^\circ\text{C}$ ($\leq -76^\circ\text{F}$) [US].
- **Thawed Stability:** 
    - Refrigerator (2°C to 8°C): Up to 7 days.
    - Room Temperature (20°C to 25°C): Up to 24 hours.
- **Syringe Stability:** Once drawn into syringes, the suspension may be stored for up to 6 hours at room temperature [US].
- **Special Handling:** Protect from light. Do not refreeze.
- **Disinfection/Spills:** Treat spills or unused portions with a virucidal agent (e.g., 0.5% sodium hypochlorite or 6% hydrogen peroxide) for 15 minutes. For 2 days post-instillation, patients should add half a cup of bleach to the toilet bowl before urinating and wait 15 minutes before flushing [US].
