# Editorial Product Information: Norgestrel and Ethinyl Estradiol

## Editorial Note
- Contributing Jurisdictions: United States (FDA)
- Purpose: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of norgestrel and ethinyl estradiol, synthesized for healthcare professionals. 
- Disclaimer: This is an editorial synthesis and should not be used as a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. At the time of this report, only US-based documentation was available for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** Norgestrel (progestin) and Ethinyl Estradiol (estrogen).
- **Chemical Class:** Combined Hormonal Contraceptive (COC).
- **Brand Names:** Turqoz [US].
- **ATC Code:** G03AA11 (Norgestrel and ethinylestradiol).
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - Oral Tablet: 0.3 mg norgestrel and 0.03 mg ethinyl estradiol [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Lupin Pharmaceuticals, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Contraception:** Indicated for use by females of reproductive potential to prevent pregnancy [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- One active tablet (white to off-white) is taken daily for 21 consecutive days, followed by one inert tablet (orange) daily for 7 consecutive days [US].
- Tablets must be taken at the same time every day, at intervals not exceeding 24 hours [US].

### Initiation Schedule (Sunday Start)
- Treatment should begin on the first Sunday after the onset of menstruation. If menstruation starts on a Sunday, the first tablet is taken that day.
- **Back-up Contraception:** A non-hormonal back-up method (e.g., condoms or spermicide) is required for the first 7 days of the first cycle [US].

### Switching from Other Contraceptives [US]
- **From 21-day pack:** Start the new pack 7 days after the last tablet of the previous brand.
- **From 28-day pack:** Start the new pack the day after the last tablet of the previous brand.
- **From Progestin-only pills:** Start the next day. Use back-up for the first 7 days.
- **From Injection/Implant/IUD:** Start on the day of removal or the day the next injection is due. Use back-up for the first 7 days if switching from an IUD not removed on Day 1 of the cycle.

### Missed Dose Instructions [US]
- **1 Active Tablet:** Take as soon as remembered. Take the next pill at the regular time (may result in 2 pills in one day). Use back-up for 7 days.
- **2 Active Tablets (Week 1 or 2):** Take 2 pills on the day remembered and 2 pills the following day. Resume daily dosing. Use back-up for 7 days.
- **2 Active Tablets (Week 3) or ≥3 Active Tablets:** Continue daily dosing until Sunday. On Sunday, discard the remaining pack and start a new pack. Use back-up for 7 days.

### Administration Notes
- In case of severe vomiting or diarrhea within 3 to 4 hours of ingestion, the dose should be treated as a missed pill [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Cigarette smoking** in women over 35 years of age [US].
- **Thromboembolic Disorders:** Current or history of deep-vein thrombosis (DVT), pulmonary embolism (PE), or known inherited/acquired coagulopathies [US].
- **Vascular Disease:** Cerebrovascular disease, coronary artery disease, uncontrolled hypertension, or diabetes mellitus with vascular involvement [US].
- **Cardiac Conditions:** Thrombogenic valvular or rhythm diseases (e.g., subacute bacterial endocarditis with valvular disease or atrial fibrillation) [US].
- **Headache:** Migraine with aura (at any age) or migraine headaches in women over 35 [US].
- **Liver Disease:** Benign or malignant liver tumors, or active liver disease [US].
- **Neoplasms:** Known or suspected breast cancer or other estrogen-sensitive carcinomas [US].
- **Other:** Undiagnosed abnormal uterine bleeding; pregnancy; hypersensitivity to any component [US].
- **Drug-specific:** Concomitant use of Hepatitis C drug combinations containing ombitasvir/paritaprevir/ritonavir, with or without dasabuvir [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warning [US]
- **Cigarette Smoking and Serious Cardiovascular Events:** Smoking increases the risk of serious cardiovascular side effects from COC use. This risk increases with age (particularly over 35) and with the number of cigarettes smoked. COCs are contraindicated in women over 35 who smoke.

### Vascular Risks
- **Thromboembolism:** Discontinue immediately if an arterial or venous thrombotic event occurs. COCs should be stopped at least 4 weeks before and through 2 weeks after major surgery or prolonged immobilization [US].
- **Ocular Lesions:** Discontinue if there is unexplained loss of vision, proptosis, or papilledema; evaluate immediately for retinal vein thrombosis [US].

### Liver and Biliary Disease
- **Hepatotoxicity:** Discontinue if jaundice develops. Hepatic adenomas and a very rare risk of hepatocellular carcinoma are associated with long-term COC use [US].
- **HCV Concomitant Use:** ALT elevations (>5x ULN) have been reported when used with certain HCV regimens; COCs can be restarted 2 weeks after completing HCV therapy [US].

### Other Precautions
- **Hypertension:** Monitor blood pressure; discontinue if significant elevations occur [US].
- **Metabolic Effects:** Monitor prediabetic and diabetic patients for glucose intolerance. Women with hypertriglyceridemia are at increased risk for pancreatitis [US].
- **Bleeding Irregularities:** Unscheduled bleeding/spotting is common in the first 3 months. If persistent, rule out pregnancy or malignancy [US].
- **Depression:** Carefully observe patients with a history of depression; discontinue if it recurs to a serious degree [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
### Effects of Other Drugs on COCs [US]
- **CYP3A4 Inducers:** Drugs such as phenytoin, barbiturates, carbamazepine, rifampicin, topiramate, and St. John's wort can decrease COC plasma concentrations, potentially leading to contraceptive failure. Use back-up for 28 days after discontinuing the inducer.
- **Bile Acid Sequestrants:** Colesevelam reduces ethinyl estradiol AUC. Administer COCs at least 4 hours apart from colesevelam.
- **CYP3A4 Inhibitors:** Itraconazole, fluconazole, and grapefruit juice may increase plasma hormone levels.
- **HIV/HCV Protease Inhibitors:** Significant increases or decreases in estrogen/progestin levels may occur.

### Effects of COCs on Other Drugs [US]
- **Metabolism Inhibition:** May increase levels of cyclosporine, prednisolone, theophylline, and tizanidine.
- **Glucuronidation Induction:** COCs significantly decrease lamotrigine concentrations, potentially requiring dose adjustments of the anticonvulsant.
- **Thyroid/Cortisol Therapy:** COCs increase binding globulins; replacement therapy doses may need to be increased.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Not specified by letter category]. Epidemiology studies show no consistent risk of birth defects with inadvertent use in early pregnancy. Discontinue if pregnancy is confirmed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** COCs can reduce milk production. Small amounts of steroids are excreted in breast milk. Use other forms of contraception until the child is weaned [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Established in women of reproductive age. Safety/efficacy is expected to be the same in post-pubertal adolescents under 16. Not indicated before menarche [US].
- **Geriatric Use:** Not studied in postmenopausal women; not indicated in this population [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Contraindicated in active liver disease or liver tumors [US]. Specific renal dosing not specified.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Primarily prevents pregnancy by suppressing ovulation. Secondary mechanisms may include changes in cervical mucus (to inhibit sperm penetration) and the endometrium (to reduce the likelihood of implantation) [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** The estrogen component increases thyroxine-binding globulin, sex hormone-binding globulin (SHBG), and cortisol-binding globulin [US]. Detailed ADME parameters not specified in available sources.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- In a pivotal study of 1,287 women (11,085 cycles/852.7 women-years), the pregnancy rate in subjects aged 15 to 40 was approximately 1 pregnancy per 100 women-years of use [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°–86°F). 
- Protect from light [US].
