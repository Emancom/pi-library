# Editorial Product Information: Neomycin, Polymyxin B, and Gramicidin

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial: United States (FDA). 
- **Purpose:** This document is a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical and regulatory data into a single reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Always consult the local approved labelling for the most current regulatory requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** Neomycin sulfate, Polymyxin B sulfate, and Gramicidin.
- **Chemical Class:** 
  - Neomycin: Aminoglycoside antibiotic.
  - Polymyxin B: Cyclic polypeptide antibiotic.
  - Gramicidin: Polypeptide antibiotic mixture.
- **Brand Names:** Neomycin and Polymyxin B Sulfates and Gramicidin Ophthalmic Solution (Generic/Multiple Manufacturers including Bausch & Lomb) [United States].
- **ATC Code:** S01AA30 (Sensory organs, Ophthalmologicals, Anti-infectives, Antibiotics, combinations of different antibiotics).
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** 
  - **Ophthalmic solution (sterile):** Each mL contains Neomycin sulfate (equivalent to 1.75 mg neomycin base), Polymyxin B sulfate (10,000 units), and Gramicidin (0.025 mg) [United States].
- **Route of Administration:** Topical ophthalmic.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Bausch & Lomb, A-S Medication Solutions [United States].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Superficial Ocular Infections:** Indicated for the topical treatment of superficial infections of the external eye and its adnexa caused by susceptible bacteria. This includes:
  - Conjunctivitis
  - Keratitis and Keratoconjunctivitis
  - Blepharitis and Blepharoconjunctivitis [United States].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Adult Dosing:** 
  - Instill one or two drops into the affected eye(s) every 4 hours for 7 to 10 days.
- **Severe Infections:** Dosage may be increased to as much as two drops every hour.
- **Administration Instructions:** 
  - Patients must avoid contacting the dispensing tip with the eye, eyelid, fingers, or any other surface to prevent contamination.
  - Use by more than one person may spread infection.
  - Keep the bottle tightly closed when not in use.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** This product is contraindicated in individuals with a known hypersensitivity to any of its components [United States].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Route Restriction:** **NOT FOR INJECTION INTO THE EYE.** The solution should never be introduced into the anterior chamber or injected subconjunctivally [United States].
- **Sensitization:** Topical neomycin sulfate may cause cutaneous sensitization (e.g., itching, reddening, edema). If sensitization occurs, including failure to heal, the product should be discontinued.
- **Cross-Hypersensitivity:** Allergic cross-reactions may occur, potentially precluding the future use of kanamycin, paromomycin, streptomycin, and possibly gentamicin [United States].
- **Superinfection:** Prolonged use may result in the overgrowth of non-susceptible organisms, including fungi.
- **Bacterial Resistance:** Resistance may develop; if inflammation or pain worsens, the patient should discontinue use and consult a physician.
- **Contamination Risk:** Use of contaminated multiple-dose containers has been associated with bacterial keratitis, particularly in patients with disrupted ocular surfaces.

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Frequency Data:** The exact incidence of adverse reactions is not specified in available sources.
- **Ocular and Skin Reactions:** 
  - Common/Frequent: Allergic reactions including itching, swelling, and conjunctival erythema.
  - General: Local irritation upon instillation.
- **Serious Reactions:** 
  - Rare: More serious hypersensitivity reactions, including anaphylaxis, have been reported [United States].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Management Notes:** Due to the topical nature and low systemic absorption, significant systemic drug interactions are less likely, though clinicians should be aware of potential cross-sensitization with other aminoglycosides.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
  - **Category:** Not explicitly assigned in the source text [United States].
  - **Risk Summary:** Animal reproductive studies have not been conducted with this combination. It is unknown if the product can cause fetal harm or affect reproductive capacity. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [United States].
- **Breastfeeding:** It is not known whether these drugs are excreted in human milk. Caution should be exercised when administered to a nursing woman [United States].
- **Fertility:** 
  - Long-term studies for carcinogenic/mutagenic potential have not been conducted for polymyxin B or gramicidin.
  - Polymyxin B has been reported to impair equine sperm motility; effects on human fertility are unknown.
  - In vitro neomycin treatment increased chromosome aberrations in human lymphocytes at high concentrations [United States].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness in paediatric patients have not been established [United States].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** No specific dosage adjustments provided for topical ophthalmic use.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** 
  - **Neomycin:** An aminoglycoside that inhibits protein synthesis by binding to ribosomal RNA, causing misreading of the bacterial genetic code.
  - **Polymyxin B:** Increases bacterial cell membrane permeability by interacting with phospholipid components.
  - **Gramicidin:** Increases bacterial cell membrane permeability to inorganic cations by forming channels through the lipid bilayer.
- **Microbiology:** Together, these agents provide a broad spectrum against:
  - *Staphylococcus aureus*
  - *Streptococci* (including *S. pneumoniae*)
  - *Escherichia coli*
  - *Haemophilus influenzae*
  - *Klebsiella-Enterobacter* species
  - *Neisseria* species
  - *Pseudomonas aeruginosa*
  - **Note:** This product does **not** provide adequate coverage against *Serratia marcescens*.
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified in available sources for topical ophthalmic administration.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store between 15°C and 25°C (59°F to 77°F).
- **Protection:** Protect from light [United States].
