# Editorial Product Information: Nirsevimab

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the Product Information (PI) document from the following jurisdiction: **Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration)**. 
- Information for other jurisdictions (e.g., US, European, Canadian, Japanese) was not provided for this synthesis and is marked as "Not specified in available sources" where applicable.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. It is an editorial synthesis and must not be used as a substitute for the official PI or prescribing information required for clinical practice in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Nirsevimab (recombinant human IgG1κ monoclonal antibody).
- **Chemical Class:** Long-acting monoclonal antibody targeting the prefusion conformation of the Respiratory Syncytial Virus (RSV) F protein.
- **Brand Name(s):** **BEYFORTUS** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Solution for injection in a pre-filled syringe [Australian].
    - 50 mg in 0.5 mL (100 mg/mL).
    - 100 mg in 1 mL (100 mg/mL).
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intramuscular (IM) injection.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** sanofi-aventis australia pty ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
**Nirsevimab** is indicated for the prevention of Respiratory Syncytial Virus (RSV) lower respiratory tract disease (LRTD) in [Australian-approved]:
- Neonates and infants born during or entering their first RSV season.
- Children up to 24 months of age who remain vulnerable to severe RSV disease through their second RSV season.

Official recommendations regarding the use of this product should be consulted [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Infants entering their first RSV season)
Dosing is based on body weight at the time of administration. A single dose is given via IM injection [Australian]:
- **Body weight < 5 kg:** 50 mg.
- **Body weight ≥ 5 kg:** 100 mg.

### High-Risk Children (Entering their second RSV season)
- **Dosage:** A single 200 mg dose, administered as two separate 100 mg IM injections at the same visit [Australian].

### Children Undergoing Cardiac Surgery (Cardiopulmonary Bypass)
An additional dose is recommended once the child is stable after surgery [Australian]:
- **First RSV season:** 
    - Surgery < 90 days after initial dose: Weight-based dosing (50 mg or 100 mg).
    - Surgery > 90 days after initial dose: 50 mg regardless of weight.
- **Second RSV season:**
    - Surgery < 90 days after initial dose: 200 mg regardless of weight.
    - Surgery > 90 days after initial dose: 100 mg regardless of weight.

### Administration Instructions
- Administer intramuscularly, preferably in the anterolateral aspect of the thigh.
- Avoid the gluteal muscle due to risk of sciatic nerve damage.
- Visually inspect for particulate matter; the solution should be clear to opalescent and colourless to yellow.
- If two injections are required (200 mg dose), use different injection sites.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Nirsevimab** is contraindicated in individuals with a history of severe hypersensitivity reactions, including anaphylaxis, to the active substance or any of the excipients (histidine, arginine hydrochloride, sucrose, polysorbate 80) [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None [Australian].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Serious reactions (urticaria, dyspnoea, cyanosis, hypotonia) have been reported. If anaphylaxis occurs, discontinue immediately and initiate appropriate therapy.
- **Bleeding Disorders:** Administer with caution to infants with thrombocytopenia, coagulation disorders, or those on anticoagulation therapy.
- **Protein-Losing Conditions:** Increased clearance may occur in patients with significant protein loss (e.g., nephrotic syndrome); protection levels may be reduced.
- **Infants < 1 kg:** Dosing is based on extrapolation; benefits and risks should be carefully weighed as exposure may be higher than in heavier infants.

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions observed in pooled clinical trials (MELODY and D5290C00003) were generally uncommon [Australian]:

| Frequency | System Organ Class | Event |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Uncommon** (≥0.1% to <1%) | Skin and subcutaneous tissue | Rash (typically within 14 days post-dose) |
| **Uncommon** (≥0.1% to <1%) | General / Administration Site | Injection site reaction (pain, swelling, induration) |
| **Uncommon** (≥0.1% to <1%) | General / Administration Site | Pyrexia (within 7 days post-dose) |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Anaphylaxis has been observed with IgG1 monoclonal antibodies as a class [Australian].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** No studies conducted. Monoclonal antibodies are not substrates of Cytochrome P450 enzymes or renal/hepatic transporters.
- **Vaccine Co-administration:** May be given concomitantly with childhood vaccines. No interference with the immune response to vaccines is expected. Use separate syringes and different injection sites.
- **Immunoglobulins:** **Nirsevimab** should not be mixed with any other medicinal product. **Palivizumab** should not be administered to infants who have already received **nirsevimab** in the same season [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** **[Australian Category B2]**. Nirsevimab is not indicated for use in females of reproductive potential. No human or animal data are available regarding risks in pregnancy.
- **Breastfeeding:** Not indicated for use in adults. No data on the presence of the drug in human milk or effects on the breastfed infant.
- **Fertility:** No data available.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy not established in children > 24 months of age [Australian].
- **Elderly Use:** No data available; not indicated for this population.
- **Renal Impairment:** Not expected to influence clearance as IgG antibodies are not cleared renally.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not primarily cleared via hepatic pathways. However, chronic liver disease associated with protein loss may increase clearance.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** **Nirsevimab** is a long-acting recombinant human IgG1κ monoclonal antibody. It binds to a highly conserved epitope (antigenic site Ø) on the prefusion conformation of the RSV F protein, neutralizing the virus and preventing viral entry and cell-to-cell fusion.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Absolute bioavailability is ~84%. Median $T_{max}$ is 6 days.
    - **Distribution:** Estimated volume of distribution (central/peripheral) is 216 mL and 261 mL respectively for a 5 kg infant.
    - **Metabolism:** Degraded by proteolytic enzymes; not metabolised by hepatic enzymes.
    - **Elimination:** Terminal half-life is approximately 71 days. Clearance is approximately 3.42 mL/day for a 5 kg infant.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Modified with a triple amino acid substitution (YTE) in the Fc region to extend the serum half-life.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **MELODY/D5290C00003:** Pivotal trials in term and preterm infants. Nirsevimab demonstrated ~70-75% efficacy against medically attended RSV LRTI through 150 days post-dose.
- **MEDLEY:** Evaluated safety and PK in high-risk infants (preterm, CLD, CHD). Safety was comparable to palivizumab.
- **HARMONIE:** A large real-world design trial showing ~83% efficacy in preventing RSV LRTI hospitalisation.
- **MUSIC:** Open-label trial in immunocompromised infants showing consistent PK and safety profiles.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C. Do not freeze.
- **Room Temperature:** May be kept at room temperature (below 25°C) for a maximum of 8 hours; discard if not used within this window.
- **Handling:** Keep in the outer carton to protect from light. Do not shake.
