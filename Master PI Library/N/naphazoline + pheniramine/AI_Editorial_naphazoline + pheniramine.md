# Editorial Product Information: naphazoline + pheniramine

## 1. Product Identification
- Active ingredient (INN/generic name): **naphazoline + pheniramine**
  - Naphazoline hydrochloride 0.02675% (0.2675 mg/mL)
  - Pheniramine maleate 0.315% (3.15 mg/mL)
- Chemical class:
  - Naphazoline: redness reliever (topical ocular decongestant/reducing ocular redness as listed in source)
  - Pheniramine: antihistamine (topical ocular antihistamine as listed in source)
- ATC code: Not specified in available sources
- Route(s) of administration: Ophthalmic (topical ocular)
- Inactive ingredients (listed in source): benzalkonium chloride; boric acid; edetate disodium; hypromellose; purified water; sodium borate; sodium chloride; hydrochloric acid may be used to adjust pH.

## 3. Approved Indications
- Temporary relief of itching and redness of the eye caused by pollen, ragweed, grass, animal hair and dander.
  - Indication language presented in the available source is specific to allergic ocular symptoms (ocular itch and redness secondary to common environmental allergens).

## 4. Dosage and Administration
- General dosing (adult and paediatric):
  - Adults and children 6 years of age and older: instill 1–2 drops in the affected eye(s) up to 4 times daily.
  - Children under 6 years: consult a doctor before use.
- Dosing by indication:
  - Allergic ocular itching/redness: follow the dosing above (no separate titration schedules provided).
- Administration instructions:
  - Remove contact lenses before administration.
  - Avoid touching the dropper tip to any surface to prevent contamination.
  - Replace cap after use.
  - Users may experience a brief tingling on instillation.
  - Pupils may become temporarily enlarged after dosing.
- Titration schedules: Not specified in available sources
- Missed dose instructions: Not specified in available sources

## 5. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to naphazoline, pheniramine, or any other ingredient in the product (do not use if sensitive to any ingredient).
- Do not use if the solution changes color or becomes cloudy.
- Other absolute contraindications: Not specified in available sources

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
- **Critical warnings**
  - For external ophthalmic use only.
  - Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, seek medical help or contact a Poison Control Center immediately; accidental oral ingestion in infants and children may lead to coma and marked reduction in body temperature.
- General precautions / when to consult a doctor before use:
  - Ask a doctor before use if the patient has: heart disease, high blood pressure, difficulty urinating due to an enlarged prostate gland, or narrow-angle glaucoma.
- Use-related warnings:
  - Overuse may cause increased eye redness (rebound hyperemia).
  - Pupillary dilation (mydriasis) may occur temporarily.
  - Stop use and consult a doctor if any of the following occur: eye pain, changes in vision, or redness/irritation of the eye that worsens or persists for more than 72 hours.
- Product handling precautions:
  - Do not touch the applicator tip to any surface to avoid contamination; replace cap after use.
- Monitoring requirements:
  - Monitor for worsening ocular pain, visual changes, or persistent/worsening redness; seek ophthalmic evaluation if these occur.
- Specific clinical precautions:
  - Contact lens wearers should remove lenses prior to instillation (contact lens compatibility and timing of re-insertion not further specified).
  - Pediatric poisoning risk: small-volume oral ingestion can cause serious systemic toxicity in infants and young children; supervise storage and administration carefully.
- Additional precautions: Not specified in available sources
