# Editorial Product Information: Nirmatrelvir and Ritonavir

## Editorial Note
*   **Contributing Jurisdictions:** This editorial synthesis is based currently on the Product Information (PI) provided by the **Australian (TGA)**. 
*   **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of the clinical pharmacology, administration, and safety profile of **nirmatrelvir** co-packaged with **ritonavir** for healthcare professionals.
*   **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and serves as a clinical reference. It is not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific Product Information required for prescribing and dispensing in any particular region.

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredients:** **Nirmatrelvir** (a peptidomimetic inhibitor of the SARS-CoV-2 main protease) and **ritonavir** (a CYP3A inhibitor and pharmacokinetic enhancer).
*   **Brand Name:** **PAXLOVID** [Australian].
*   **ATC Code:** J05AB17 (Nirmatrelvir), J05AE03 (Ritonavir).
*   **Dosage Form and Strength:** Co-packaged film-coated tablets:
    *   **Nirmatrelvir:** 150 mg (pink, oval, debossed with "PFE" and "3CL").
    *   **Ritonavir:** 100 mg (white to off-white, oval or capsule-shaped, debossed with "NK" or "H" and "R9").
*   **Route of Administration:** Oral.
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Pfizer Australia Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Treatment of COVID-19:** Indicated for the treatment of coronavirus disease 2019 (COVID-19) in adults (18 years and older) who do not require supplemental oxygen and are at increased risk of progression to hospitalisation or death [Australian-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### 3.1 General Dosing (Adults)
The standard regimen is **300 mg nirmatrelvir (two 150 mg tablets) and 100 mg ritonavir (one 100 mg tablet)** taken together orally every 12 hours for 5 consecutive days.
*   **Timing:** Treatment should be initiated as soon as possible after a COVID-19 diagnosis and within 5 days of symptom onset.
*   **Hospitalisation:** If a patient requires hospitalisation due to severe COVID-19 after starting treatment, they should complete the 5-day course at the discretion of the provider [Australian].

### 3.2 Administration Instructions
*   Tablets must be swallowed whole; do not chew, break, or crush.
*   May be taken with or without food.
*   **Nirmatrelvir** must be administered with **ritonavir**. Failure to co-administer results in sub-therapeutic nirmatrelvir plasma levels.

### 3.3 Missed Dose Instructions
*   **Within 8 hours:** Take the missed dose immediately and resume the normal schedule.
*   **More than 8 hours:** Skip the missed dose and take the next dose at the regularly scheduled time. Do not double the dose.

## 4. Contraindications
*   **Hypersensitivity:** History of clinically significant hypersensitivity to the active ingredients or excipients (e.g., lactose).
*   **Hepatic Impairment:** Contraindicated in severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh Class C) [Australian].
*   **CYP3A Interactions:** Contraindicated with drugs highly dependent on CYP3A for clearance, where elevated levels cause serious/life-threatening reactions. These include:
    *   **Alpha 1-adrenoreceptor antagonists:** Alfuzosin.
    *   **Antianginals:** Ranolazine.
    *   **Antiarrhythmics:** Amiodarone, flecainide.
    *   **Anticancer:** Neratinib, venetoclax.
    *   **Anti-gout:** Colchicine.
    *   **Antipsychotics:** Lurasidone, clozapine, cariprazine.
    *   **Cardiovascular:** Eplerenone, ivabradine.
    *   **Ergot derivatives:** Ergometrine.
    *   **Lipid-modifying (Statins):** Simvastatin.
    *   **PDE5 inhibitors (for PAH):** Sildenafil.
    *   **Sedative/hypnotics:** Diazepam, triazolam, oral midazolam, zolpidem.
*   **Potent CYP3A Inducers:** Contraindicated with drugs that significantly reduce plasma concentrations of **PAXLOVID**, potentially leading to resistance:
    *   **Anticonvulsants:** Carbamazepine, phenobarbital, phenytoin, primidone.
    *   **Antimycobacterials:** Rifampicin.
    *   **Herbal products:** St. John’s Wort (*Hypericum perforatum*).

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
*   **Drug Interactions:** Initiation in patients taking medications metabolised by CYP3A may increase plasma concentrations of those medications. Conversely, CYP3A inducers may reduce the efficacy of **PAXLOVID**.
*   **Hypersensitivity/Skin Reactions:** Anaphylaxis, Stevens-Johnson Syndrome (SJS), and Toxic Epidermal Necrolysis (TEN) have been reported. Discontinue immediately if suspected.
*   **Hepatotoxicity:** Transaminase elevations and jaundice have occurred. Use caution in patients with pre-existing liver disease or hepatitis.
*   **HIV-1 Resistance:** Because **ritonavir** is used, there is a risk of developing resistance to HIV protease inhibitors in patients with undiagnosed or uncontrolled HIV-1 infection.
*   **Excipients:** Contains **lactose**. Not recommended for patients with rare hereditary galactose intolerance or glucose-galactose malabsorption.

## 6. Adverse Effects
Safety data from the EPIC-HR, EPIC-SR, and EPIC-PEP trials indicate the following:

| Frequency | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Dysgeusia (approx. 6%), Diarrhoea (approx. 3%), Headache, Nausea, Vomiting |
| **Uncommon (0.1–1%)** | Hypersensitivity, Hypertension, Abdominal pain |
| **Rare (<0.1%)** | Anaphylaxis, Toxic epidermal necrolysis (TEN), Stevens-Johnson syndrome (SJS), Malaise |

*   **Post-marketing reports:** Hypersensitivity and serious skin reactions have been identified in the post-marketing setting [Australian].

## 7. Drug Interactions
**PAXLOVID** is a strong inhibitor of **CYP3A** and an inhibitor of **CYP2D6, P-gp, and OATP1B1**.
*   **Management:** Clinicians should consult a dedicated interaction database before prescribing. 
*   **Specific Note on Contraceptives:** Ethinylestradiol concentrations may be reduced. Patients should use an additional, non-hormonal method of contraception during treatment and for one menstrual cycle after stopping [Australian].
*   **Calcineurin/mTOR Inhibitors:** (e.g., ciclosporin, tacrolimus, sirolimus) require multidisciplinary consultation and therapeutic drug monitoring (TDM) due to high toxicity risk.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy:** **[Australian Category B3]**. There are limited data in humans. It is not recommended during pregnancy or in women of childbearing potential not using effective contraception.
*   **Breastfeeding:** Nirmatrelvir and ritonavir are excreted in human milk in small amounts. Breastfeeding should be discontinued during treatment and for 48 hours after the final dose [Australian].
*   **Fertility:** No human data available. Rat studies showed no effect on fertility at 5x human exposure.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
### 9.1 Renal Impairment
Dose adjustment is required based on eGFR (mL/min/1.73m²):
*   **Mild ($\ge$60 to <90):** No adjustment.
*   **Moderate ($\ge$30 to <60):** Reduce to 150 mg nirmatrelvir + 100 mg ritonavir every 12 hours.
*   **Severe (<30, including haemodialysis):** 
    *   **Day 1:** 300 mg/100 mg once.
    *   **Days 2–5:** 150 mg/100 mg once daily.
    *   *Note:* On dialysis days, administer the dose after the session [Australian].

### 9.2 Hepatic Impairment
*   **Mild (Child-Pugh A) or Moderate (Child-Pugh B):** No dose adjustment.
*   **Severe (Child-Pugh C):** Contraindicated.

### 9.3 Paediatric Use
Safety and efficacy have not been established in patients under 18 years of age [Australian].

### 9.4 Elderly Use
No specific dose adjustments are required; however, clinicians should be mindful of the higher frequency of renal impairment in this population.

## 10. Pharmacology
*   **Mechanism of Action:** **Nirmatrelvir** inhibits the SARS-CoV-2 main protease (Mpro/3CLpro), preventing the processing of polyprotein precursors and halting viral replication. **Ritonavir** inhibits CYP3A4-mediated metabolism of nirmatrelvir to maintain therapeutic systemic concentrations.
*   **Pharmacokinetics:**
    *   **Absorption:** Nirmatrelvir $T_{max} \approx 3$ hours. High-fat meals increase exposure by approx. 61%.
    *   **Distribution:** Protein binding: Nirmatrelvir $\approx 69\%$; Ritonavir $\approx 98-99\%$.
    *   **Metabolism:** Nirmatrelvir is primarily a CYP3A4 substrate.
    *   **Elimination:** Nirmatrelvir is mainly renally excreted (49.6%) and faecally excreted (35.3%). Half-life $\approx 6.1$ hours.

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   **EPIC-HR:** A phase 2/3 study in non-hospitalised symptomatic adults at high risk of progression. **PAXLOVID** reduced the risk of COVID-19-related hospitalisation or death by **89%** (within 3 days of symptom onset) and **86%** (within 5 days) compared to placebo.
*   **EPIC-SR:** Conducted in standard-risk or vaccinated patients with at least one risk factor. The primary endpoint (time to sustained symptom alleviation) was not met [Australian].
*   **EPIC-PEP:** Evaluated post-exposure prophylaxis in household contacts. The study did not meet its primary endpoint of reducing the risk of confirmed infection.

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   Store below 25°C [Australian].
*   Shelf life is specific to the regional batch; check packaging for the expiry date.
