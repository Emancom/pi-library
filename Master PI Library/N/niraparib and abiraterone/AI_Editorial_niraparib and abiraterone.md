# Editorial Product Information: Niraparib and Abiraterone Acetate

## Editorial Note
- The following clinical jurisdictions contributed to this harmonised editorial: United States (FDA).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial synthesis of the approved Product Information (PI) for **AKEEGA** to assist healthcare professionals with clinically accurate, cross-jurisdictional data.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial overview for educational and clinical reference and is not a substitute for the official PI required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** Niraparib (as tosylate monohydrate) and abiraterone acetate.
- **Chemical Class:** Niraparib is a poly (ADP-ribose) polymerase (PARP) inhibitor; Abiraterone acetate is a CYP17 inhibitor (androgen biosynthesis inhibitor).
- **Brand Name:** **AKEEGA** [US].
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths [US]:**
    - 50 mg niraparib / 500 mg abiraterone acetate film-coated tablets (yellowish orange to yellowish brown, oval, debossed with "N 50 A").
    - 100 mg niraparib / 500 mg abiraterone acetate film-coated tablets (orange, oval, debossed with "N 100 A").
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Janssen Biotech, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
**AKEEGA** is indicated for use in combination with prednisone for the treatment of adult patients with:
- **Metastatic Castration-Sensitive Prostate Cancer (mCSPC):** Specifically for deleterious or suspected deleterious *BRCA2*-mutated (*BRCA2m*) disease [US-approved].
- **Metastatic Castration-Resistant Prostate Cancer (mCRPC):** Specifically for deleterious or suspected deleterious *BRCA*-mutated (*BRCAm*) disease [US-approved].

*Editorial Note: Patient selection must be confirmed via a validated diagnostic test for BRCA mutations.*

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults)
- **Standard Dose:** 200 mg niraparib / 1,000 mg abiraterone acetate (taken as two 100 mg/500 mg tablets) administered orally once daily [US].

### Dosing by Indication
- **mCSPC (*BRCA2m*):** Administer with 5 mg prednisone daily [US].
- **mCRPC (*BRCAm*):** Administer with 10 mg prednisone daily [US].

### Administration Instructions
- **Food Requirements:** Must be taken on an empty stomach. Do not consume food for at least one hour before and at least two hours after the dose [US].
- **Concomitant Therapy:** Patients must also receive a concurrent gonadotropin-releasing hormone (GnRH) analog unless they have undergone bilateral orchiectomy [US].
- **Handling:** Tablets should be swallowed whole with water; do not break, crush, or chew [US].

### Missed Dose Instructions
- If a dose is missed, it should be taken as soon as possible on the same day. The normal schedule should resume the following day. Do not double the dose [US].

### Dose Modifications for Toxicity
- **General Principle:** If toxicity occurs, treatment may be interrupted or the dose reduced (e.g., to 100 mg niraparib / 1,000 mg abiraterone acetate or 100 mg / 500 mg) [US]. 
- **Haematological Toxicity:** Withhold for Grade 3/4 events. For Hemoglobin <8 g/dL or Platelets <100,000/mcL, specific interruption and reduction protocols apply [US].
- **Hepatotoxicity:** For ALT/AST >5x ULN or bilirubin >3x ULN, withhold and monitor. Permanent discontinuation is required for ALT/AST ≥20x ULN or concurrent severe elevation of ALT and bilirubin [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None specified in available [US] source.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Myelodysplastic Syndrome/Acute Myeloid Leukemia (MDS/AML):** Exposure to niraparib may lead to MDS/AML, which can be fatal. Discontinue if MDS/AML is confirmed [US].
- **Myelosuppression:** Significant anaemia, thrombocytopenia, and neutropenia are common. Monitor CBC weekly for the first month, then every two weeks for two months, and monthly thereafter for the first year [US].
- **Hypokalaemia, Fluid Retention, and Cardiovascular Reactions:** Inhibition of CYP17 may increase mineralocorticoids. Monitor for hypertension, hypokalaemia, and oedema at least weekly for the first two months, then monthly. Pre-existing hypertension must be controlled before starting therapy [US].
- **Hepatotoxicity:** Severe and fatal hepatic failure has been reported with abiraterone. Monitor LFTs (ALT, AST, Bilirubin) prior to initiation, every two weeks for three months, then monthly [US].
- **Adrenocortical Insufficiency:** May occur if prednisone is interrupted or during periods of stress. Monitor for symptoms; increased corticosteroid doses may be required [US].
- **Hypoglycaemia:** Abiraterone may cause severe low blood sugar in patients using pioglitazone or repaglinide. Monitor blood glucose in diabetic patients [US].
- **Increased Fractures and Mortality with Radium Ra 223:** Use of **AKEEGA** plus prednisone in combination with Radium Ra 223 dichloride is not recommended due to increased fracture and death risks [US].
- **Posterior Reversible Encephalopathy Syndrome (PRES):** Reported with niraparib. Discontinue if PRES is confirmed [US].
- **Embryo-Fetal Toxicity:** Can cause fetal harm. Advise males with female partners of reproductive potential to use effective contraception [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Frequency Data [US]
| Adverse Reaction | Frequency (Very Common >10%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Haematological** | Decreased haemoglobin, decreased lymphocytes, decreased platelets, decreased neutrophils |
| **Gastrointestinal** | Constipation (34–41%), Nausea (30–33%), Abdominal pain |
| **Metabolic/Lab** | Increased AST, increased ALT, increased bilirubin, increased alkaline phosphatase, increased creatinine, increased/decreased potassium, decreased appetite |
| **Vascular/Cardiac** | Hypertension (33–51%), Arrhythmia (10–23%) |
| **General** | Fatigue (39–43%), Musculoskeletal pain (44–45%), Edema, Pyrexia |
| **Respiratory** | Respiratory tract infection, Dyspnoea |

### Serious Adverse Effects
- **Fatalities:** Reported in 4.9% (mCSPC) and 9% (mCRPC) of patients, including sudden death, COVID-19 pneumonia, and cardiorespiratory arrest [US].
- **Grade 3–4 Reactions:** Most common include hypertension and anaemia [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Strong CYP3A4 Inducers (e.g., Rifampin):** Avoid coadministration. Inducers significantly decrease abiraterone exposure, potentially reducing efficacy [US].
- **CYP2D6 Substrates:** Abiraterone is a moderate CYP2D6 inhibitor. Avoid coadministration with CYP2D6 substrates that have a narrow therapeutic index (e.g., thioridazine). If no alternative, consider dose reduction of the substrate [US].
- **CYP2C8 Substrates:** Abiraterone inhibits CYP2C8. Monitor for increased toxicity of CYP2C8 substrates (e.g., pioglitazone) [US].
- **Transporters:** Niraparib inhibits BCRP, MATE1, and MATE2, and is a substrate of P-gp and BCRP [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Not established for use in females. Based on mechanism, can cause fetal harm and pregnancy loss. 
- **Contraception (Males):** Advise males with female partners of reproductive potential to use effective contraception during treatment and for **4 months** after the final dose [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Safety not established; it is unknown if components pass into human milk [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies suggest impairment of male fertility (reduced sperm counts and reproductive organ atrophy) which may be reversible [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness not established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** No overall differences in efficacy between patients ≥65 years and younger patients. However, patients ≥75 years experienced a higher incidence of fatal adverse reactions (13% vs 4.3% in younger patients) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:**
    - **Mild:** No dose adjustment required.
    - **Moderate or Severe:** Avoid use. Abiraterone exposure increases 3.6-fold in moderate and 7-fold in severe impairment [US].
- **Renal Impairment:**
    - **Mild to Moderate (CLcr 30–90 mL/min):** No dose adjustment required.
    - **Severe (CLcr 15–30 mL/min):** Monitor closely for increased adverse reactions; not formally studied [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:**
    - **Niraparib:** Inhibits PARP-1 and PARP-2 enzymes involved in DNA repair. It induces cytotoxicity in cells with or without *BRCA1/2* deficiencies.
    - **Abiraterone Acetate:** Converted to abiraterone, which selectively inhibits the CYP17 enzyme (17α-hydroxylase/C17,20-lyase). This inhibits androgen biosynthesis in the testes, adrenal glands, and prostate tumour tissue [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics [US]:**
    - **Absorption:** Niraparib Tmax ~3h (73% bioavailability). Abiraterone Tmax ~1.5h. *Crucial:* Abiraterone exposure increases up to 10-17 fold with food.
    - **Distribution:** Niraparib is 83% protein bound; Abiraterone is >99% protein bound.
    - **Elimination:** Niraparib half-life ~62 hours; Abiraterone half-life ~20 hours.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **AMPLITUDE Trial (mCSPC):** A Phase 3 study in patients with *HRR*-mutated mCSPC. In the *BRCA2m* subgroup, **AKEEGA** + prednisone significantly improved radiographic progression-free survival (rPFS) compared to placebo + abiraterone (Median rPFS: Not Estimable vs 26 months; Hazard Ratio 0.46) [US].
- **MAGNITUDE Trial (mCRPC):** A Phase 3 study in patients with *HRR*-mutated mCRPC. In the *BRCAm* subgroup, **AKEEGA** + prednisone significantly improved rPFS (16.6 months vs 10.9 months; Hazard Ratio 0.53) [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F and 86°F) [US].
- **Special Handling:** Females who are or may become pregnant should handle tablets with protection (e.g., gloves) due to the risk of fetal harm [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Prescription only (Rx-only).
