# Editorial Product Information: Nelarabine

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** Australian (TGA).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of nelarabine, synthesised from available official product information. It is intended for use by healthcare professionals as a clinical reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory documents for legal and prescribing compliance.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Nelarabine (a pro-drug of the deoxyguanosine analogue 9-β-D-arabinofuranosylguanine [ara-G]).
- **Chemical Class:** Antimetabolite; purine analogue.
- **Brand Name(s):** **Nelarabine-Reach** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** L01BB07.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Solution for infusion; each vial contains 250 mg of nelarabine in 50 mL (5 mg/mL) [Australian].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Reach Pharmaceuticals Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **T-cell Acute Lymphoblastic Leukaemia (T-ALL):** Treatment of patients with relapsed or refractory T-ALL who have not responded to or have relapsed following at least two prior chemotherapy regimens [Australian].
- **T-cell Lymphoblastic Lymphoma (T-LBL):** Treatment of patients with relapsed or refractory T-LBL who have not responded to or have relapsed following at least two prior chemotherapy regimens [Australian].

*Note: Due to the small patient populations, these indications are based on limited clinical data [Australian].*

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Adults and Adolescents (≥ 16 years):** 1,500 mg/m² administered intravenously over two hours on days 1, 3, and 5. The cycle is repeated every 21 days [Australian].
- **Children and Adolescents (≤ 21 years):** 650 mg/m² administered intravenously over one hour daily for 5 consecutive days. The cycle is repeated every 21 days [Australian].

### Administration Instructions
- **Dilution:** Nelarabine must **not** be diluted prior to administration [Australian].
- **Container Compatibility:** Transfer the dose into polyvinylchloride (PVC), ethyl vinyl acetate (EVA), or glass containers [Australian].
- **Supervision:** Must be administered under the supervision of a physician experienced in cytotoxic agents [Australian].

### Dose Modification
- **Neurological Events:** Nelarabine **must be discontinued** at the first sign of National Cancer Institute Common Terminology Criteria (NCI CTCAE) Grade 2 or greater neurological toxicity [Australian].
- **Other Toxicities:** Delaying subsequent doses is an option for other toxicities, including haematological events [Australian].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to nelarabine or any excipients (sodium chloride, hydrochloric acid, sodium hydroxide) [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Neurological Adverse Reactions:** Severe neurotoxicity is the dose-limiting toxicity. Effects include altered mental states (coma, confusion, somnolence), CNS effects (convulsions, ataxia, status epilepticus), and peripheral neuropathy (paralysis, motor weakness). Demyelination and Guillain-Barré-like syndromes have been reported. Recovery is not always complete upon treatment cessation [Australian].
- **Combination Therapy Risks:** Concomitant use with intrathecal chemotherapy or prior craniospinal irradiation increases the risk of neurotoxicity and is not recommended [Australian].
- **Haematological Toxicity:** Leukopenia, thrombocytopenia, anaemia, and neutropenia (including febrile neutropenia) are common. Regular monitoring of complete blood counts, including platelets, is required [Australian].
- **Tumour Lysis Syndrome (TLS):** Patients at risk of hyperuricaemia should receive intravenous hydration and consideration for allopurinol [Australian].
- **Immunisations:** Live vaccines are not recommended in immunocompromised hosts due to the potential for infection [Australian].
- **Sodium Content:** Each 50 mL vial contains 88.51 mg (3.85 mmol) of sodium, representing 4.4% of the WHO recommended daily intake for adults [Australian].

### Serious Adverse Effects
- **Progressive Multifocal Leukoencephalopathy (PML):** Single biopsy-confirmed case reported in adults [Australian].
- **Rhabdomyolysis:** Reported in post-marketing data; associated with increased blood creatine phosphokinase [Australian].
- **Fatalities:** Opportunistic infections and neurological events have been fatal in some cases [Australian].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Cytochrome P450:** In vitro data suggests nelarabine and ara-G do not significantly inhibit or induce major CYP450 isoenzymes (1A2, 2A6, 2B6, 2C8, 2C9, 2C19, 2D6, 3A4) [Australian].
- **Adenosine Deaminase (ADA) Inhibitors:** Concomitant use with inhibitors such as **pentostatin** is not recommended, as it may reduce the efficacy of nelarabine or alter the adverse event profile [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** **Category D** [Australian]. Animal data (rabbits) demonstrate fetal malformations, including absent gall bladders, cleft palate, and skeletal ossification delays. Nelarabine should only be used if the benefit outweighs the potential risk to the fetus [Australian].
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if nelarabine or its metabolites are excreted in human milk. Breastfeeding must be discontinued during treatment [Australian].
- **Contraception:** Sexually active men and women must use effective contraception. Men should use condoms during and for at least three months after treatment [Australian].
- **Fertility:** No human or animal data; however, based on cytotoxic action, impairment of fertility is possible [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Approved for children and adolescents. Limited pharmacology data is available for patients under 4 years of age [Australian].
- **Elderly Use:** Patients ≥ 65 years may have increased rates of neurological adverse events. No specific pharmacokinetic differences were noted based on age [Australian].
- **Renal Impairment:** Nelarabine and ara-G are partially renally excreted. There is insufficient data for dosing recommendations in patients with CrCl < 50 mL/min. Close monitoring for toxicity is required [Australian].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not studied in this population; treat with caution [Australian].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Nelarabine is demethylated by ADA to ara-G, then phosphorylated to the active 5'-triphosphate form, ara-GTP. Ara-GTP is incorporated into the DNA of leukaemic blasts, inhibiting synthesis and leading to cell death. T-cells are more sensitive than B-cells to these effects [Australian].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Metabolism:** Rapidly converted to ara-G by ADA.
    - **Distribution:** Extensively distributed; Vss of 115 L/m² in adults and 89.4 L/m² in children. Plasma protein binding is < 25%.
    - **Elimination:** Nelarabine and ara-G half-lives are approximately 30 minutes and 3 hours, respectively. Approximately 28% of the dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours [Australian].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Adult Study (CALGB/SWOG):** An open-label study of 39 adults (mean age 34). In 28 patients with ≥ 2 prior inductions, the complete response (CR) plus CR without haematological recovery (CR*) rate was 21%, with a median survival of 20.6 weeks [Australian].
- **Paediatric Study (COG):** An open-label study of 151 patients (≤ 21 years). In 39 patients with ≥ 2 prior inductions, the CR + CR* rate was 23%. In patients with only one prior induction, the response rate was significantly higher (42%) [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store below 25 °C [Australian].
- **Vial Type:** Type-I clear colorless glass vials [Australian].
- **Handling:** Cytotoxic agent; handle and dispose of according to local guidelines for hazardous medicinal products. Pregnant staff should be excluded from handling [Australian].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Australia:** Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine). Subject to additional monitoring by the TGA [Australian].
