# Product Information: SKYCLARYS (omaveloxolone)

## 1. Product Name and Active Ingredient
**Product Name:** SKYCLARYS  
**Active Ingredient:** Omaveloxolone (50 mg)  
**Pharmaceutical Form:** Hard capsules for oral administration. The capsules feature a light green body and blue cap, imprinted with "RTA 408" and "50".

## 2. Approved Indications
SKYCLARYS is indicated for the treatment of Friedreich’s ataxia in adults and adolescents aged 16 years and older.

## 3. Contraindications
*   **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to omaveloxolone or any of the inactive ingredients (including pregelatinised maize starch, croscarmellose sodium, magnesium stearate, silicified microcrystalline cellulose, and capsule shell components such as hypromellose and titanium dioxide) [TGA].

## 4. Warnings and Precautions
### Elevation of Aminotransferases
Treatment is associated with increases in hepatic transaminases (ALT and AST). In clinical trials, elevations ≥3x the upper limit of normal (ULN) were observed in approximately 29–31% of patients, typically within the first 12 weeks of therapy.
*   **Monitoring:** Obtain ALT, AST, and total bilirubin prior to initiation. Monitor monthly for the first 3 months of treatment and periodically thereafter [FDA/TGA].
*   **Intervention:** If transaminases rise to >5x ULN, or >3x ULN with evidence of liver dysfunction (e.g., elevated bilirubin), treatment should be discontinued immediately. If levels stabilize or resolve, re-initiation may be considered with increased monitoring frequency.

### Elevation of B-type Natriuretic Peptide (BNP)
SKYCLARYS can cause increases in BNP, a marker of cardiac function and fluid volume. While these increases were not always associated with clinical heart failure in trials, Friedreich’s ataxia patients often have underlying cardiomyopathy.
*   **Monitoring:** Assess BNP prior to initiation and monitor for signs of fluid overload (e.g., peripheral edema, shortness of breath, palpitations).
*   **Patient Education:** Advise patients to report sudden weight gain (defined as ≥1.4 kg in one day or ≥2.3 kg in one week [TGA]).
*   **Management:** If fluid overload develops, evaluate cardiac function and consider interrupting or discontinuing treatment.

### Lipid Abnormalities
Increases in low-density lipoprotein cholesterol (LDL-C) and decreases in high-density lipoprotein cholesterol (HDL-C) have been reported.
*   **Monitoring:** Assess lipid parameters prior to initiation and monitor periodically during treatment. Manage abnormalities according to standard clinical guidelines.

### Body Weight Decrease
Mild to moderate decreases in body weight have been observed. Patients should monitor weight regularly; unexplained or significant weight loss requires clinical evaluation [TGA].

## 5. Dosage and Administration
### Standard Dosage
The recommended dose is **150 mg (3 capsules) taken orally once daily**.
*   **Administration:** Must be taken on an empty stomach, at least 1 hour before or 2 hours after a meal.
*   **Method:** Capsules should be swallowed whole. For patients with dysphagia, capsules may be opened and the entire contents sprinkled onto 2 tablespoons of applesauce. The mixture must be consumed immediately; do not store for future use.

### Missed Doses
If a dose is missed, it should be taken the following day at the regular time. Do not double the dose to compensate for a missed one.

### Hepatic Impairment
*   **Mild (Child-Pugh A):** No dose adjustment required.
*   **Moderate (Child-Pugh B):** Reduce dose to 100 mg once daily. If adverse reactions emerge, further reduce to 50 mg once daily [TGA/FDA].
*   **Severe (Child-Pugh C):** Avoid use.

### Concomitant Therapy Adjustments
*   **Strong CYP3A4 Inhibitors:** Avoid use. If unavoidable, reduce SKYCLARYS dose to 50 mg once daily with close monitoring.
*   **Moderate CYP3A4 Inhibitors:** Avoid use. If unavoidable, reduce SKYCLARYS dose to 100 mg once daily.
*   **Strong or Moderate CYP3A4 Inducers:** Avoid concomitant use due to significant loss of efficacy.

## 6. Adverse Effects
The safety profile is based on clinical trials where the median exposure was approximately 0.92 patient years.
*   **Very Common (≥10%):** Elevated liver enzymes (ALT/AST), headache, nausea, abdominal pain, fatigue, diarrhea, musculoskeletal pain, oropharyngeal pain, influenza, vomiting, muscle spasms, back pain, decreased appetite, and rash.
*   **Post-marketing Experience:** Hypersensitivity reactions, including urticaria and rash, have been reported voluntarily.

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **CYP3A4 Inhibitors/Inducers:** Omaveloxolone is a primary substrate of CYP3A4. Co-administration significantly alters systemic exposure (see Section 5 for dosing adjustments).
*   **Effect on Other Drugs:** Omaveloxolone is a weak inducer of CYP3A4 and CYP2C8. It may reduce the exposure of drugs like midazolam (CYP3A4) and repaglinide (CYP2C8).
*   **Hormonal Contraceptives:** Concomitant use may reduce contraceptive efficacy. Patients using hormonal contraceptives should use an additional non-hormonal barrier method during treatment and for 28 days after discontinuation.
*   **Transporters:** Omaveloxolone is a weak inducer of BCRP; it may reduce the AUC of rosuvastatin by approximately 30% [TGA].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
### Pregnancy
**Category C [TGA].** There are no adequate data on the use of omaveloxolone in pregnant women. Animal studies have demonstrated embryofetal toxicity, including increased post-implantation loss and reduced fetal weight at exposures near or below the recommended human dose.
*   **Recommendation:** SKYCLARYS should not be used during pregnancy or in women of childbearing potential not using effective contraception.

### Breastfeeding
It is unknown if omaveloxolone is excreted in human milk. Because a risk to the newborn cannot be excluded, breastfeeding is not recommended during treatment [TGA].

## 9. Pharmacokinetics
*   **Absorption:** Peak plasma concentrations (Cmax) occur between 7 to 14 hours post-dose. Co-administration with a high-fat meal increases Cmax by approximately 350% to 450%.
*   **Distribution:** Highly protein-bound (97%). The apparent volume of distribution is 7361 L.
*   **Metabolism:** Extensively metabolized via CYP3A4, with minor contributions from CYP2C8 and CYP2J2.
*   **Excretion:** Primarily eliminated in the feces (92%). The mean terminal half-life is approximately 57–58 hours.

## 10. Mechanism of Action
The exact therapeutic mechanism in Friedreich’s ataxia is not fully established. Omaveloxolone is known to activate the **Nuclear factor (erythroid-derived 2)-like 2 (Nrf2) pathway**. This pathway is a primary regulator of the cellular response to oxidative stress. By binding to Keap1, omaveloxolone promotes the nuclear translocation of Nrf2, helping to restore mitochondrial function and maintain cellular redox balance.

## 11. Storage Conditions
*   **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C, with excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C [FDA]. TGA specifies storing below 30°C.
*   **Special Precaution:** Once the bottle is opened, the capsules should be used within 30 days [TGA]. Keep the container tightly closed to protect from moisture.
