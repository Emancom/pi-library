# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical data into a structured format for quick reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and serves as a clinical resource only. It is not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific Product Information required for prescribing.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP).
- **Chemical Class:** A naturally occurring carrier molecule; (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01 (Not explicitly stated in source, but standard for this molecule).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection, 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
- **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg given as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion [US].
- **Loading Dose:** Often required in severe metabolic crises, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours.
- **Frequency:** Administer every 3 or 4 hours (q3h or q4h); never less than every 6 hours (q6h) by infusion or IV injection.
- **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses are typically 50 mg/kg or as clinically required. The maximum recorded dose is 300 mg/kg.
- **Monitoring:** Obtain baseline plasma carnitine levels. Monitor weekly and monthly, including blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma concentrations (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L).

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
- **Administration:** Administer as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line after each dialysis session.
- **Titration:** Dose adjustments should be guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy if concentrations are below normal (40–50 µmol/L).

### Administration Instructions
- Visually inspect for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration.
- Discard any unused portion as the formulation contains no preservatives [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **None Known** [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions may occur within minutes of administration. Discontinue immediately if a severe reaction occurs [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with known seizure disorders, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Monitoring Requirements:** In patients with ESRD, monitor trough carnitine levels regularly. For all patients, monitor clinical response and vital signs.

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Serious Adverse Effects
- **Anaphylaxis and Severe Hypersensitivity:** [US]
- **Seizures:** [US]

### Common Adverse Effects (≥5% in ESRD clinical trials)
Based on placebo-controlled trials in chronic hemodialysis patients, the following were reported regardless of causality:
- **Body as a whole:** Abdominal pain, accidental injury, asthenia, chest pain, fever, flu syndrome, headache, infection, injection site reaction, pain.
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension, hypotension, tachycardia.
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea, vomiting, nausea, anorexia.
- **Metabolic:** Hypercalcemia, hypervolemia.
- **Nervous system:** Dizziness, paresthesia, insomnia, depression.
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis, cough increase, dyspnea, rhinitis.

### Other Reported Effects
- **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea and vomiting; less frequent reports of body odor and gastritis.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalised Ratio (INR) have occurred when levocarnitine is used concomitantly with warfarin. **Clinical Management:** Monitor INR levels after initiating levocarnitine or following dose adjustments in patients on warfarin therapy [US].
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Not assigned a specific letter category in the provided PI, but uses descriptive labeling]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) revealed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed.
- **Breastfeeding:** Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine concentrations in milk following exogenous administration. Risks of excess intake to the infant must be weighed against maternal benefits [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies revealed no evidence of impaired fertility [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Use according to weight-based dosing (50 mg/kg) as specified in Section 3 [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in source document; however, dosing in ESRD is based on body weight and clinical monitoring.
- **Renal Impairment:** Safely used in ESRD patients on dialysis (see Section 3 for specific IV dosing). Note: Safety and efficacy of *oral* levocarnitine have NOT been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency; chronic high-dose oral administration in these patients may lead to accumulation of toxic metabolites (TMA and TMAO) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
  - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
  - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces).
  - **Elimination:** Following IV bolus (20 mg/kg), approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in urine over 24 hours. The mean apparent terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In ESRD patients, IV administration increases plasma levocarnitine concentrations and corrects the ratio of acylcarnitine to carnitine.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients receiving 20 mg/kg IV three times weekly post-dialysis showed that trough concentrations reached 54–180 µmol/L (normal range: 40–50 µmol/L) within one week and remained stable.
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** ESRD patients (n≈30 per group) receiving 10, 20, or 40 mg/kg showed dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine concentrations at 12 and 24 weeks.
- **Clinical Outcome Note:** While IV levocarnitine increases carnitine concentrations in ESRD, the impact on specific signs, symptoms, or clinical outcomes in this population has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Conditions:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat.
- **Stability:** Compatible and stable for up to 24 hours in PVC bags when mixed with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations between 0.5 mg/mL and 8 mg/mL at room temperature [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as Rx-only. Supplied as single-dose vials [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources for other jurisdictions.
