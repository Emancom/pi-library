# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine Injection

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdiction: **US (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, consolidating clinical data and regulatory requirements.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis intended for informational purposes and is not a substitute for the official PI or local regulatory guidelines required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection, USP (Baxter Healthcare Corporation) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Metabolic Disorders:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US-approved].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD undergoing chronic hemodialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg.
    - **Administration:** Administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion.
    - **Acute Crisis:** A loading dose is often followed by equivalent doses every 3 or 4 hours (never less than every 6 hours) over the subsequent 24 hours.
    - **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses are typically 50 mg/kg or as clinically required; the maximum recorded dose is 300 mg/kg.
- **ESRD on Hemodialysis:**
    - **Initial Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight.
    - **Administration:** Administer as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line after each dialysis session.
    - **Adjustment:** Dosing may be adjusted downward (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine levels.

### Monitoring
- **Baseline:** Obtain plasma carnitine concentrations prior to starting therapy.
- **Routine Monitoring:** Weekly and monthly monitoring is recommended, including blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine range: 35 to 60 µmol/L) [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Inspect for particulate matter and discoloration prior to use.
- **Compatibility:** Compatible and stable for up to 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8.0 mg/mL) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Known Hypersensitivity:** While the US PI states "None known" in the dedicated section, it elsewhere warns against re-administration in patients who have experienced severe hypersensitivity to the product.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions can occur within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue immediately and do not re-administer without a rigorous risk-benefit assessment [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Monitoring Requirements:** Periodic monitoring of plasma carnitine levels is essential to guide dosing and ensure levels remain within the therapeutic range.

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions in ESRD patients are often confounded by the underlying pathology. Data from double-blind, placebo-controlled trials indicate the following (frequencies based on [US] data):

| Frequency | System Organ Class | Adverse Event |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | General/Injection Site | Injection site reaction (33%), Pain (30%), Headache (22%) |
| | Cardiovascular | Hypertension (20%), Hypotension (14%) |
| | Gastrointestinal | Diarrhea (16%), Vomiting (15%) |
| | Respiratory | Pharyngitis (23%) |
| | Nervous | Dizziness (13%) |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Gastrointestinal | Abdominal pain (9%), Nausea (8%), Dyspepsia (5%) |

- **Postmarketing Experience:** Seizures and severe hypersensitivity (anaphylaxis) have been reported [US].
- **Other:** Transient nausea, vomiting, and body odor have been observed.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have been observed when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels after initiating levocarnitine or following any dose adjustments in patients receiving anticoagulant therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Classification:** Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (based on surface area) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. No adequate, well-controlled studies in pregnant women exist. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk carnitine levels after administration. The benefits to the mother must be weighed against the potential risk of excess carnitine intake in the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders is weight-based (50 mg/kg), which is applicable to the paediatric population [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    - **Oral vs. IV:** Safety and efficacy of *oral* levocarnitine have not been evaluated in renal insufficiency. Chronic high doses of oral levocarnitine in ESRD may lead to accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and TMAO). 
    - **IV Use:** Levocarnitine is easily removed by hemodialysis [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for mammalian energy metabolism. it facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids in patients with metabolic defects [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In ESRD, it alleviates metabolic abnormalities by clearing acyl-CoA compounds through the formation of acylcarnitine, which is then excreted [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not protein-bound. Mean distribution half-life: 0.585 hours [US].
    - **Elimination:** Mean terminal elimination half-life: 17.4 hours. Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours [US].
    - **Bioavailability:** Absolute bioavailability of oral forms (tablets/solution) is approximately 15–16% [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients receiving 20 mg/kg post-dialysis showed mean trough levels increased from ~20 µmol/L to 190 ± 55 µmol/L at 8 weeks.
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** ESRD patients (n≈30 per group) received 10, 20, or 40 mg/kg. Trough levels at 24 weeks were 148, 240, and 456 µmol/L, respectively.
- **Note:** While IV levocarnitine increases carnitine concentrations in ESRD patients, the effect on clinical outcomes has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Precautions:** Avoid excessive heat; protect from freezing.
- **Handling:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as the formulation contains no preservatives [US].
