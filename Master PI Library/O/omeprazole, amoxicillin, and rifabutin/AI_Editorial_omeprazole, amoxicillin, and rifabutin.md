# Editorial Product Information: Omeprazole magnesium, Amoxicillin, and Rifabutin

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial is derived solely from the Product Information (PI) provided by the **United States (FDA)** for the drug **TALICIA**. 
- Corresponding documents from other jurisdictions, including Australia (TGA), Europe (EMA), United Kingdom (MHRA), Canada (Health Canada), and Japan (PMDA), were not provided for this synthesis.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonized editorial overview for healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official PI used for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** 
    - **Omeprazole magnesium** (Proton pump inhibitor)
    - **Amoxicillin** (Penicillin-class antibacterial)
    - **Rifabutin** (Rifamycin antibacterial)
- **Brand Name(s):** **TALICIA** [US]
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** 
    - **Delayed-release capsule:** Each capsule contains 10 mg of omeprazole (equivalent to 10.3 mg of omeprazole magnesium), 250 mg of amoxicillin, and 12.5 mg of rifabutin [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** RedHill Biopharma Ltd [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Helicobacter pylori Infection:** Indicated for the treatment of *H. pylori* infection in adult patients [US].
- **Antibacterial Stewardship:** To mitigate the development of drug-resistant bacteria, this combination should only be utilized for infections proven or strongly suspected to be caused by susceptible bacteria [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** Administer four (4) capsules three times daily (totaling 12 capsules per day) for 14 consecutive days [US].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Doses should be taken at least 4 hours apart (e.g., morning, mid-day, and evening) [US].
    - Capsules must be taken with food [US].
    - Capsules should be swallowed whole; do not crush or chew [US].
    - Patients should swallow each dose with a full glass of water (8 ounces) [US].
    - Alcohol must be avoided during therapy [US].
- **Missed Dose Instructions:**
    - If a dose is missed and the next scheduled dose is more than 4 hours away, the missed dose should be taken immediately.
    - If the next dose is due within 4 hours, the missed dose should be taken, and the subsequent dose delayed to ensure a 4-hour interval is maintained between doses [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to omeprazole (or other benzimidazoles), amoxicillin (or other beta-lactams), rifabutin (or other rifamycins), or any excipient [US].
- **Rilpivirine-containing products:** Contraindicated due to the omeprazole component [US].
- **Delavirdine:** Contraindicated due to the rifabutin component [US].
- **Voriconazole:** Contraindicated due to the rifabutin component [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None specified in available sources.
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious and occasionally fatal reactions (anaphylaxis) have been reported. Therapy should be discontinued immediately at the first sign of hypersensitivity [US].
- **Severe Cutaneous Adverse Reactions (SCAR):** Includes Stevens-Johnson syndrome (SJS), toxic epidermal necrolysis (TEN), and DRESS. Monitor and discontinue if suspected [US].
- **Drug-Induced Enterocolitis Syndrome (DIES):** Reported with amoxicillin use, primarily in pediatric patients, though this product is indicated for adults. Characterized by protracted vomiting 1–4 hours after ingestion [US].
- **Clostridioides difficile-Associated Diarrhea (CDAD):** May range from mild diarrhea to fatal colitis. Discontinue if confirmed [US].
- **Hormonal Contraceptives:** Efficacy may be reduced. Patients must use additional non-hormonal highly effective contraception during treatment [US].
- **Acute Tubulointerstitial Nephritis (TIN):** Observed with PPIs and penicillins; may occur at any point during therapy [US].
- **Lupus Erythematosus:** Both cutaneous (CLE) and systemic (SLE) forms have been reported with PPIs; most cases are CLE [US].
- **Uveitis:** Possible due to the rifabutin component; monitor closely if used with macrolides or fluconazole [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Common (≥1%):**
    - Diarrhea (10.1%–14.3%)
    - Headache (7.5%–15.6%)
    - Chromaturia (brown-orange discoloration of urine) (13.0%)
    - Nausea (3.9%–4.8%)
    - Abdominal pain (3.5%–3.9%)
    - Rash (2.6%–5.2%)
    - Dyspepsia (1.3%–2.2%)
    - Oropharyngeal pain (0.9%–3.9%)
    - Vomiting (1.3%–2.2%)
    - Vulvovaginal candidiasis (2.2%) [US]
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Anaphylaxis, SJS/TEN, CDAD, acute tubulointerstitial nephritis, and uveitis [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **CYP2C19/CYP3A4 Inducers (e.g., St. John’s Wort, Rifampin):** Avoid concomitant use as they significantly decrease omeprazole concentrations [US].
- **CYP2C19/CY43A4 Inhibitors:** 
    - **Voriconazole:** Contraindicated [US].
    - **Fluconazole/Itraconazole:** Avoid use; if unavoidable, monitor for rifabutin toxicity [US].
- **Antiretrovirals:**
    - **Rilpivirine and Delavirdine:** Contraindicated [US].
    - **Atazanavir/Nelfinavir:** Avoid use due to decreased antiretroviral exposure [US].
- **Clopidogrel:** Avoid concomitant use; omeprazole reduces the pharmacological activity of clopidogrel [US].
- **Warfarin:** Monitor INR and prothrombin time; increases in these markers can lead to abnormal bleeding [US].
- **Methotrexate:** Avoid in patients on high-dose methotrexate; PPIs may elevate serum levels of methotrexate [US].
- **pH-Dependent Drugs:** Omeprazole may interfere with the absorption of iron salts, erlotinib, dasatinib, and mycophenolate mofetil [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **[US]:** Generally not recommended. Animal data for omeprazole and rifabutin indicate potential for fetal harm, including embryo-lethality and skeletal anomalies. Epidemiologic data for amoxicillin have not established a consistent drug-associated risk for major birth defects [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** 
    - Amoxicillin is present in human milk and may cause diarrhea in the infant. 
    - Omeprazole may be present in human milk. 
    - No data available for rifabutin in human milk. 
    - The developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding should be weighed against the clinical need for the drug [US].
- **Fertility:** Rifabutin was found to impair fertility in male rats at 32 times the human dose [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness in patients under 18 years of age have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical trials did not include sufficient numbers of subjects ≥65 years to determine if they respond differently. Monitoring renal function may be useful due to higher likelihood of decreased renal clearance [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Avoid use in patients with severe renal impairment (GFR <30 mL/min) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Avoid use; omeprazole exposure increases significantly in patients with hepatic impairment [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** 
    - **Amoxicillin:** Inhibits bacterial cell wall biosynthesis.
    - **Rifabutin:** Inhibits DNA-dependent RNA polymerase in susceptible bacteria.
    - **Omeprazole:** A proton pump inhibitor that suppresses gastric acid secretion by inhibiting the H+/K+ ATPase enzyme system [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Omeprazole $C_{max}$ decreases by 92% and amoxicillin $C_{max}$ decreases by 30% when taken with a high-fat meal; however, the product is labeled to be taken with food [US].
    - **Distribution:** Protein binding is 95% for omeprazole, 85% for rifabutin, and 20% for amoxicillin [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Omeprazole is extensively metabolized by CYP2C19 (major) and CYP3A4 (minor). Rifabutin is a substrate and inducer of CYP3A4 [US].
    - **Elimination:** Half-lives are 1 hour (omeprazole), 1.4 hours (amoxicillin), and 34 hours (rifabutin) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pivotal Study 1:** A randomized, double-blind, active-controlled trial in treatment-naïve *H. pylori*-positive adults. TALICIA achieved an eradication rate of 83.8% compared to 57.7% in the control group (amoxicillin/omeprazole without rifabutin) (p < 0.0001) [US].
- **Pivotal Study 2:** A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial. TALICIA achieved an eradication rate of 76.6% compared to 2.4% for placebo [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Handling:** Store and dispense in the original container with a child-resistant closure. Keep the bottle tightly closed [US].
