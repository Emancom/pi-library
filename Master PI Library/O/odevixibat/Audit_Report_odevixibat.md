- **Jurisdictional Comparison:** The editorial explicitly side-by-sides age indications (e.g., PFIC 3 months for FDA vs 6 months for TGA) and ALGS indications.
- **Tone and Concision:** The editorial removes extensive clinical trial data and detailed pharmacokinetic tables (V/F, CL/F), condensing them into high-level summaries.
- **Formatting:** The use of a frequency table in Section 6 (Adverse Effects) is a synthesis of the various "Trial 1" and "Trial 3" data tables found in the sources.
- **Omission of Non-Clinical Details:** It omits the full list of excipients, IUPAC names, and detailed molecular weights, focusing instead on clinical utility.

## Recommendation
The editorial **PASSES** the originality review. 

The document is a genuine synthesis that compares and contrasts two distinct regulatory documents. While some technical medical descriptions (specifically animal study results) are used verbatim to ensure clinical accuracy, the vast majority of the text is original writing. No revision is required, though the writer should remain cautious when listing clinical findings from animal studies to ensure they are integrated into original sentence structures.
