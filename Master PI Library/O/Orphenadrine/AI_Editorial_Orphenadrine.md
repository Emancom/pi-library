# Editorial Product Information: Orphenadrine

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdiction: **Australian (TGA)**. 
- This document provides a harmonised overview for healthcare professionals based on available regulatory documentation. 
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific Product Information required for prescribing or dispensing in a particular region. At the time of compilation, only the Australian source was available; information from other jurisdictions (e.g., US, European, Canadian) is marked as "Not specified in available sources."

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Orphenadrine citrate (a skeletal muscle relaxant).
- **Chemical Class:** Ethanolamine ether derivative.
- **Brand Names:** **Norflex** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** M03BC01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Slow release (modified release) tablets: 100 mg [Australian].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** iNova Pharmaceuticals (Australia) Pty Limited [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Skeletal Muscle Spasm:** Relief of painful muscle spasms associated with conditions such as fibrositis, whiplash injuries, torticollis, prolapsed intervertebral disc, strains, and sprains [Australian].
- **Secondary Indications:** Treatment of tension headaches and persistent hiccups [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** 100 mg administered twice daily [Australian].
- **Maximum Dosage:** In severe cases, the dosage may be increased to a maximum of 300 mg within a 24-hour period [Australian].
- **Administration Instructions:** Tablets are a slow-release formulation and should be swallowed whole; do not crush or chew.
- **Titration and Missed Doses:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
Orphenadrine is contraindicated in patients with conditions that may be exacerbated by anticholinergic activity, including:
- **Glaucoma** [Australian].
- **Prostatic Hypertrophy** or obstruction of the bladder neck [Australian].
- **Myasthenia Gravis** [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None reported in available sources.
- **Cardiac Precautions:** Use with caution in patients with tachycardia, cardiac decompensation, coronary insufficiency, or cardiac arrhythmias [Australian].
- **Long-term Use:** The safety of continuous long-term therapy is not established. If prescribed for prolonged periods, periodic monitoring of blood, urine, and liver function is recommended [Australian].
- **Psychomotor Impairment:** May impair the ability to operate machinery or drive a motor vehicle; patients should be cautioned regarding hazardous activities [Australian].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are generally associated with anticholinergic activity and are rarely reported at recommended dosages [Australian].

| Frequency | Adverse Effect |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Common (>1%)** | Nausea, dry mouth, blurred vision [Australian] |
| **Uncommon (0.1–1%)** | Hallucinations [Australian] |
| **Rare (<0.1%)** | Rash, drowsiness [Australian] |

**Clinical Note:** Symptoms typically resolve rapidly upon dose reduction or cessation of medication [Australian].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacodynamic Interactions:**
    - **Anticholinergics/Phenothiazines:** Interactions reported with phenothiazines and other agents possessing anti-muscarinic properties [Australian].
    - **CNS Depressants:** Concomitant use with alcohol or other central nervous system (CNS) depressants should be avoided due to additive sedative effects [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Australian Category B2:** Safe use regarding foetal development has not been established. Use in women of childbearing potential (especially during early pregnancy) only when the potential benefits outweigh the possible hazards to the foetus [Australian].
- **Breastfeeding:** Safety data not available in provided sources [Australian].
- **Fertility:** Safety data not available in provided sources [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** No data available [Australian].
- **Elderly Use:** No specific data available; however, caution is generally advised with anticholinergics in the elderly [Australian].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources, though periodic monitoring is recommended during long-term use [Australian].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Acts as a central skeletal muscle relaxant [Australian]. 
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Exhibits anticholinergic (anti-muscarinic) activity [Australian].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption/Distribution/Metabolism/Elimination:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Physicochemical Properties:** White or almost white crystalline powder; sparingly soluble in water and slightly soluble in alcohol [Australian].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Summary:** Pivotal trial data was not detailed in the provided regulatory documentation. The product's value in tension headache and persistent hiccup is noted based on established clinical use [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store below 30°C [Australian].
- **Packaging:** Supplied in PVC/PVDC Al foil blister packs of 10, 30, and 100 tablets [Australian].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Australia:** Prescription Only Medicine – **Schedule 4 (S4)**.
