# Editorial Product Information: Obinutuzumab

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following regulatory authority: **Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of **obinutuzumab** for healthcare professionals, intended to provide a concise, clinically accurate reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not replace the official PI. Healthcare professionals must refer to the approved PI in their specific jurisdiction before prescribing or administering this medication.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Obinutuzumab, a recombinant, humanised, and glycoengineered Type II anti-CD20 monoclonal antibody (IgG1 isotype) [Australian].
- **Brand Name:** **Gazyva** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** L01FA03.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** 1000 mg/40 mL (25 mg/mL) concentrate solution for infusion in a single-use vial [Australian].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Roche Products Pty Limited [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Chronic Lymphocytic Leukaemia (CLL):** In combination with chlorambucil for previously untreated patients [Australian].
- **Follicular Lymphoma (FL):** 
  - In combination with chemotherapy, followed by **Gazyva** maintenance, for previously untreated advanced FL [Australian].
  - In combination with bendamustine, followed by **Gazyva** maintenance, for patients with FL who were refractory to or progressed during/within 6 months of a rituximab-containing regimen [Australian].
- **Glofitamab Pre-treatment:** To reduce the risk of Cytokine Release Syndrome (CRS) induced by glofitamab [Australian].
- **Lupus Nephritis (LN):** Treatment of adult patients with active Class III or IV (with or without Class V) lupus nephritis receiving standard therapy [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Administration
- Administer only as an IV infusion through a dedicated line. **Do not administer as a bolus or IV push** [Australian].
- Dilute in 0.9% sodium chloride only. Do not use dextrose-based solutions [Australian].
- Full resuscitation facilities must be available during administration [Australian].

### Dosing Schedules
- **CLL:** 1000 mg total dose. Cycle 1: Day 1 (100 mg), Day 2 (900 mg), Day 8 (1000 mg), Day 15 (1000 mg). Cycles 2–6: 1000 mg on Day 1 [Australian].
- **FL (Untreated):** 1000 mg with chemotherapy (bendamustine, CHOP, or CVP). Maintenance: 1000 mg every 2 months for 2 years or until progression [Australian].
- **FL (Relapsed/Refractory):** 1000 mg with bendamustine for six 28-day cycles. Maintenance: 1000 mg every 2 months for 2 years or until progression [Australian].
- **Glofitamab Pre-treatment:** A single 1000 mg dose administered 7 days before the first glofitamab dose [Australian].
- **Lupus Nephritis:** 1000 mg on Day 1 and Week 2 (Dose 1 and 2), then 1000 mg at Week 24 and Week 26. Subsequent doses: 1000 mg every 6 months [Australian].

### Premedication and Prophylaxis
- **Tumour Lysis Syndrome (TLS):** Prophylaxis (hydration and uricostatics) is required for patients with high tumour burden, high lymphocyte count (>25 x 10⁹/L), or renal impairment (CrCl <70 mL/min) [Australian].
- **Infusion-Related Reactions (IRRs):**
  - **CLL/LN/Glofitamab Pre-treatment:** IV corticosteroids (e.g., dexamethasone or methylprednisolone) are mandatory for the first infusion [Australian].
  - **All Indications:** Administer oral analgesics/antipyretics and antihistamines before each infusion [Australian].
  - **Hypotension Management:** Consider withholding antihypertensive medications for 12 hours prior to, during, and for 1 hour after the infusion [Australian].

## 4. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to obinutuzumab, murine proteins, or any excipients [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warning
- **Progressive Multifocal Leukoencephalopathy (PML):** Fatal cases have occurred. Monitor for new or worsening neurological symptoms. If PML is suspected, suspend treatment; if confirmed, discontinue permanently [Australian].

### General Warnings
- **Infusion-Related Reactions (IRRs):** Extremely common, especially during the first 1000 mg. Severe and life-threatening reactions have occurred. IRRs may be clinically indistinguishable from anaphylaxis [Australian].
- **Hepatitis B Reactivation:** Screen all patients for HBV (HBsAg and anti-HBc) before starting. Monitor for 12 months post-treatment. Discontinue if reactivation occurs [Australian].
- **Neutropenia and Infections:** Severe and late-onset neutropenia (occurring >28 days post-treatment) can occur. Fatal infections have been reported. Monitor CBC regularly [Australian].
- **Thrombocytopenia:** Acute thrombocytopenia (within 24 hours of infusion) is a risk, particularly in Cycle 1. Monitor for haemorrhage [Australian].
- **Disseminated Intravascular Coagulation (DIC):** Reported mainly in NHL and CLL, often associated with IRRs or TLS [Australian].
- **Immunisation:** Do not administer live virus vaccines during treatment and until B-cell recovery [Australian].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Very Common (>10%):** Infusion-related reactions (up to 69% in CLL), neutropenia, thrombocytopenia, diarrhoea, pyrexia, cough, upper respiratory tract infections, and urinary tract infections [Australian].
- **Common (1–10%):** Anaemia, leukopenia, arthralgia, back pain, nasopharyngitis, pneumonia, and COVID-19 (noted specifically in LN studies) [Australian].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** PML, HBV reactivation, fatal IRRs, TLS, febrile neutropenia, and gastrointestinal perforation (mainly in NHL) [Australian].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- No formal drug-drug interaction studies have been conducted.
- Sub-studies indicate no pharmacokinetic interactions between obinutuzumab and bendamustine, fludarabine, cyclophosphamide, or chlorambucil [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Australian Category C. Avoid use unless benefits outweigh risks. B-cell depletion in infants exposed in utero has been documented. Effective contraception is required during and for 18 months after treatment [Australian].
- **Breastfeeding:** Discontinue breastfeeding during treatment and for 18 months following the final dose due to the potential for IgG excretion in milk [Australian].
- **Fertility:** No specific animal studies; however, no adverse effects on reproductive organs were noted in 6-month monkey studies [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy not established in patients under 18 years [Australian].
- **Elderly Use:** 
  - **CLL/FL:** No dose adjustment required, though patients ≥65–75 years may experience more serious adverse events [Australian].
  - **LN:** Safety and efficacy not established in patients ≥65 years [Australian].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment for mild (CrCl 50–89 mL/min) or moderate (CrCl 30–49 mL/min). Not formally studied in severe renal impairment [Australian].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Safety and efficacy not established [Australian].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** A Type II anti-CD20 antibody that targets the extracellular loop of the CD20 antigen on B-cells. It induces direct cell death and mediates ADCC (antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity) and ADCP (antibody-dependent cellular phagocytosis) via glycoengineering of the Fc region, which increases affinity for FcγRIII receptors [Australian].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** 
  - **Distribution:** Restricted largely to plasma and interstitial fluid (Vd approx. 2.7 L) [Australian].
  - **Elimination:** Follows a biphasic clearance: a non-linear (target-mediated) pathway dominant during initial treatment and a linear pathway (IgG catabolism) that predominates after B-cell depletion [Australian].
  - **Half-life:** Approximately 26.4 days in CLL and 36.8 days in iNHL [Australian].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **CLL (CLL11):** Demonstrated significant improvement in PFS for obinutuzumab + chlorambucil (26.7 months) vs. rituximab + chlorambucil (15.2 months) [Australian].
- **FL (GALLIUM):** Untreated FL patients showed reduced risk of progression or death (HR 0.66) compared to rituximab-based therapy [Australian].
- **FL (GADOLIN):** In rituximab-refractory FL, obinutuzumab + bendamustine significantly improved PFS vs. bendamustine alone (NR vs. 13.8 months) [Australian].
- **LN (REGENCY):** Showed a higher complete renal response (CRR) at Week 76 (46.4%) compared to placebo + standard therapy (33.1%) [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vial:** Store at 2°C to 8°C. Keep in the outer carton to protect from light. Do not freeze or shake [Australian].
- **Diluted Solution:** Physical/chemical stability demonstrated for 24 hours at 2–8°C, followed by 24 hours at ≤30°C. However, for microbiological safety, use immediately or hold at 2–8°C for no more than 24 hours [Australian].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Australia:** Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine) [Australian].
