# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) document from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical data and regulatory requirements into a single, concise reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI or the local legally required labelling for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (a carrier molecule for long-chain fatty acid transport).
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection, USP [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** Sterile solution for injection: 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) single-dose vial [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency in Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** For the acute and chronic management of patients with metabolic defects resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US-approved].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance dialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg, administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion.
    - **Acute Crisis:** Often initiated with a loading dose, followed by an equivalent dose divided over the subsequent 24 hours.
    - **Frequency:** Doses should be administered every 3 or 4 hours, and never less frequently than every 6 hours.
    - **Dose Range:** Subsequent daily doses are typically 50 mg/kg, though higher doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilised.
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight) administered as a slow 2 to 3 minute bolus into the venous return line after each dialysis session.
    - **Dose Adjustment:** Adjustments should be guided by pre-dialysis (trough) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy if levels are supratherapeutic.

### Administration and Monitoring
- **Monitoring:** Plasma carnitine concentrations should be obtained prior to therapy. Weekly and monthly monitoring is recommended, including blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine levels (target free carnitine range: 35 to 60 µmol/L) [US].
- **Inspection:** Inspect for particulate matter and discoloration before administration.
- **Stability:** Compatible with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations from 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL for up to 24 hours in PVC bags at room temperature [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm have occurred, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions may occur within minutes of administration. Discontinue immediately if a reaction occurs [US].
- **Seizure Risk:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with pre-existing conditions, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Renal Insufficiency (Oral Formulation Context):** While this PI concerns the injectable form, clinicians should note that chronic high-dose oral levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) [US].
- **Monitoring Requirements:** Periodic monitoring of plasma carnitine levels and clinical status is essential to guide dosing.

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are often confounded by the underlying pathology (ESRD or metabolic disease).

### Clinical Trial Experience (ESRD Patients)
Adverse events reported at a frequency ≥5% in double-blind, placebo-controlled trials include:

| Body System | Adverse Event (Incidence ≥5%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Chest pain, headache, injection site reaction, pain, fever, flu syndrome, asthenia, abdominal pain |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension, hypotension, tachycardia, palpitations |
| **Gastrointestinal** | Diarrhea, vomiting, nausea, anorexia, dyspepsia |
| **Metabolic** | Hypercalcemia, hypervolemia, hyperkalemia, peripheral edema, weight changes |
| **Neurologic** | Dizziness, insomnia, paresthesia, depression |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis, cough increase, dyspnea, rhinitis |
| **Other** | Anemia, pruritus, back pain, eye disorders, urinary tract infection |

### Serious/Other Notable Effects
- **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea and vomiting; body odor and gastritis occur less frequently.
- **Neurologic:** Seizures [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is used concomitantly with warfarin. **Clinical Management:** Monitor INR levels at therapy initiation and following dose adjustments [US].
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not assigned via letter system]. Animal studies at 3.8 times the human dose (based on surface area) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine concentrations in milk following administration. Decisions should weigh the benefits to the mother against potential risks to the infant from excess carnitine intake [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders is weight-based (50 mg/kg), which applies to paediatric populations. Safety and efficacy for ESRD carnitine deficiency in paediatrics are not explicitly detailed beyond general weight-based dosing [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Used specifically in ESRD [US]. Note caution regarding metabolite accumulation (TMA/TMAO) if transitioned to oral therapy [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is essential for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Terminal elimination half-life is approximately 17.4 hours. Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Administration to ESRD patients on hemodialysis results in increased plasma levocarnitine concentrations.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** Efficacy has been demonstrated in increasing carnitine concentrations in ESRD patients. However, the document notes that the specific effects of carnitine supplementation on clinical outcomes (e.g., muscle weakness, cardiomyopathy) in this population have not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Precautions:** Avoid excessive heat; protect from freezing.
- **Handling:** Discard any unused portion of a single-dose vial as the formulation is preservative-free [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Prescription only (Rx only). NDC 36000-368-10 (10 x 5 mL vials).
