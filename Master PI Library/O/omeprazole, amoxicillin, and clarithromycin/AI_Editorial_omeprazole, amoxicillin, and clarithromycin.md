# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
*   This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following regulatory authority:
    *   **US (FDA)**: Levocarnitine Injection, USP (Baxter Healthcare Corporation)
*   The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating data from the provided source.
*   **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and must not be used as a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific Product Information when prescribing or administering this medication in clinical practice.

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
*   **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
*   **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection, USP [US].
*   **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
*   **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) single-dose vials [US].
*   **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that result in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
*   **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
*   **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg.
    *   **Acute/Crisis:** A loading dose may be given followed by an equivalent dose divided over the subsequent 24 hours.
    *   **Frequency:** Dosing should be administered every 3 or 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours.
    *   **Titration:** Subsequent daily doses typically range around 50 mg/kg, though doses as high as 300 mg/kg have been utilised [US].
*   **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    *   **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight.
    *   **Frequency:** Administered after each dialysis session.
    *   **Administration Site:** Inject into the venous return line over 2 to 3 minutes.
    *   **Dose Adjustment:** Adjustments (e.g., reduction to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine levels [US].

### Administration Instructions
*   **Bolus/Infusion:** Administer as a slow bolus injection (2–3 minutes) or by infusion [US].
*   **Visual Inspection:** Solutions should be inspected for particulate matter or discoloration before use.
*   **Stability:** Stable in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations between 0.5 mg/mL and 8 mg/mL for up to 24 hours at 25°C when stored in PVC bags [US].

## 4. Contraindications
*   None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
*   **Boxed Warnings:** None [US].
*   **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions may occur within minutes of administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately [US].
*   **Seizures:** Levocarnitine (oral or IV) has been associated with reports of seizures in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In patients with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity may occur [US].
*   **Monitoring Requirements:** 
    *   **Metabolic Disorders:** Obtain baseline plasma carnitine levels. Conduct weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L) [US].
    *   **ESRD:** Monitor trough (pre-dialysis) concentrations. Therapy is often prompted by levels below 40–50 µmol/L [US].
*   **Oral Formulation Caution:** The safety/efficacy of oral levocarnitine in patients with renal impairment or ESRD has not been established. Chronic high-dose oral administration in these patients may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions in ESRD trials were often confounded by underlying pathology.
*   **Common (>1% to 10%):** Nausea, vomiting, body odor, and gastritis.
*   **Trial-Observed Events (≥5% in ESRD patients):** 
    *   **General:** Injection site reaction (33–38%), pain (30–35%), headache (3–37%), flu syndrome (25–29%), chest pain (6–15%), fever (5–12%), abdominal pain (5–21%) [US].
    *   **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (18–21%), hypotension (3–19%), tachycardia (5–9%) [US].
    *   **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (15–27%), cough increase (10–18%), dyspnea (3–11%) [US].
    *   **Digestive:** Diarrhea (9–35%), vomiting (9–21%) [US].
    *   **Metabolic:** Hypercalcemia (6–15%), hypervolemia (3–12%) [US].
*   **Serious Adverse Effects:** Anaphylaxis, seizures [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalised Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
    *   **Management:** Monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after any dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin therapy [US].
*   **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy:** 
    *   **US Category:** Not explicitly lettered, but indicates "use during pregnancy only if clearly needed."
    *   **Data:** Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm [US].
*   **Breastfeeding:** Not specifically studied in humans. Studies in dairy cows showed increased milk concentrations of levocarnitine following exogenous administration. Clinicians should weigh the benefits of maternal therapy against potential risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant [US].
*   **Fertility:** Animal studies showed no impairment of fertility [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use:** Dosing guidelines for metabolic disorders apply to paediatric patients. See Section 3 [US].
*   **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Renal Impairment:** Specific dose adjustments for ESRD are provided (see Section 3). Oral levocarnitine is not recommended in severe renal impairment due to metabolite accumulation [US].
*   **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
*   **Mechanism of Action:** Naturally occurring carrier molecule required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into the mitochondria for oxidation and subsequent energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
*   **Pharmacokinetics:**
    *   **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin. Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours [US].
    *   **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    *   **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. Mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients, 20 mg/kg IV three times weekly after dialysis increased mean pre-dialysis plasma levocarnitine from ~20 µmol/L to 190 µmol/L. 
*   **Dose-Ranging:** Studies comparing 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses showed a dose-dependent increase in trough concentrations at 12 and 24 weeks.
*   **Note:** While IV levocarnitine increases carnitine concentrations in ESRD, the impact on specific clinical outcomes in this population has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
*   **Handling:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat.
*   **Stability:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as the formulation contains no preservatives [US].
