# Editorial Product Information: Omidubicel

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising regulatory information into a clinically relevant reference.
- **Note:** This synthesis is an editorial summary and does not replace the official PI required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Omidubicel-onlv (nicotinamide-modified allogeneic hematopoietic progenitor cell therapy).
- **Brand Name:** OMISIRGE [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Cryopreserved suspension for intravenous infusion. A single dose consists of two separate components derived from the same cord blood unit:
    - **Cultured Fraction (CF):** Minimum of 8.0 × 10⁸ total viable cells, including a minimum of 9.2 × 10⁷ CD34+ cells.
    - **Non-cultured Fraction (NF):** Minimum of 4.0 × 10⁸ total viable cells, including a minimum of 2.4 × 10⁷ CD3+ cells.
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous infusion only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Gamida Cell Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Hematologic Malignancies:** For use in adults and paediatric patients 12 years and older planned for umbilical cord blood transplantation following myeloablative conditioning. Indicated to reduce the time to neutrophil recovery and decrease infection incidence [US-approved].
- **Severe Aplastic Anemia (SAA):** For use in adults and paediatric patients 6 years and older following reduced-intensity conditioning [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing:** A single-dose treatment consisting of a one-time intravenous infusion of the CF and NF components.
- **Administration Sequence:** 
    - The **Cultured Fraction (CF)** must be administered **FIRST**.
    - Infusion of the CF must be completed within 2 hours of dilution.
    - Infusion of the **Non-cultured Fraction (NF)** should begin within 1 hour of completing the CF infusion and must be completed within 1 hour of its own dilution.
- **Premedication:** 
    - **Hematologic Malignancies:** Administer diphenhydramine (50 mg IV or 0.5 mg/kg), hydrocortisone (50 mg IV or 0.5 mg/kg), and acetaminophen (650 mg PO or 10 mg/kg) 30–60 minutes prior to infusion. Avoid prophylactic methylprednisolone.
    - **SAA:** For patients on anti-thymocyte globulin (ATG), provide oral prednisone (1 mg/kg/day) or IV methylprednisolone starting 1 day prior to ATG. Administer diphenhydramine and acetaminophen 30 minutes prior to omidubicel infusion.
- **Administration Instructions:** 
    - Use gravity infusion; do **not** use a leukodepleting filter.
    - Do not irradiate the product.
    - Central venous access is recommended.
    - Prime tubing with normal saline; flush tubing after each fraction to ensure total cell delivery.

## 4. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to:
    - Dimethyl sulfoxide (DMSO).
    - Dextran 40.
    - Gentamicin.
    - Human serum albumin (HSA).
    - Bovine-derived materials.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning [US]:**
    - **Graft-versus-Host Disease (GvHD):** May be fatal. Use immunosuppressive prophylaxis and monitor for signs (rash, GI symptoms, elevated bilirubin).
    - **Infusion Reactions:** May be fatal. Monitor during and after infusion; discontinue for severe reactions.
    - **Autoimmune Cytopenias:** Observed specifically in SAA treatment. Monitor blood counts post-transplant.
    - **Graft Failure:** Failure to reach an ANC >500/mcL by Day 42 may be fatal.
    - **Engraftment Syndrome:** Characterised by fever, rash, and pulmonary infiltrates; treat promptly with corticosteroids.
- **Malignancies of Donor Origin:** Potential for post-transplant lymphoproliferative disorder (PTLD) or donor-cell derived myelodysplastic syndrome (MDS). Monitor patients life-long.
- **Transmission of Infectious Agents:** Risk exists as the product is derived from human cord blood and involves bovine reagents during manufacturing. Screening is performed, but transmission of known or unknown pathogens cannot be entirely excluded.
- **Transmission of Rare Genetic Diseases:** Screening for common haemoglobinopathies is performed, but the ability to exclude all genetic diseases is limited by donor age.

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Very Common (>10%):** 
    - **Hematologic Malignancies:** Infections (viral 75%, bacterial 65%), GvHD (acute 62%, chronic 35%), pain (79%), fever (81%), mucosal inflammation (75%), fatigue (60%), and GI toxicity (77%).
    - **SAA:** Infections (HHV-6 94%, BK virus 76%, bacterial 59%), hyperglycemia (65%), skin rash (47%), febrile neutropenia (41%), and acute kidney injury (24%).
- **Serious Adverse Effects:**
    - Fatalities (17% in malignancy trials) occurred due to infection, acute GvHD, veno-occlusive disease (VOD), and pulmonary haemorrhage. 
    - Grade 3-5 reactions: Pain (33%), mucosal inflammation (31%), and hypertension (25%) were frequent in malignancy populations.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Management:** Interaction with conditioning regimens (myeloablative or reduced-intensity) and GvHD prophylaxis (e.g., tacrolimus, cyclosporine, mycophenolate mofetil) is fundamental to the treatment protocol.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Category:** Not assigned by letter [US]. 
    - **Recommendation:** No data available in pregnant women. Use only if potential benefit justifies the risk to the foetus. 
    - **Requirement:** Verify pregnancy status in females of reproductive potential prior to starting the conditioning regimen.
- **Breastfeeding:** No information regarding presence in human milk or effects on the breastfed infant.
- **Fertility:** No data available on the effects of omidubicel on fertility.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** 
    - Hematologic malignancies: Safety and efficacy established for ages 12 years and older [US].
    - SAA: Safety and efficacy established for ages 6 years and older [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical studies did not include sufficient numbers of patients aged 65 and over to determine if they respond differently than younger patients.
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources; however, acute kidney injury and transaminase elevations are noted as potential adverse reactions or symptoms of GvHD.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Omidubicel is an allogeneic stem cell donor source. It uses nicotinamide (NAM) technology to culture cord blood-derived hematopoietic progenitor cells (HPCs). NAM inhibits accelerated differentiation and cellular stress typically seen in ex-vivo cultures, thereby preserving the "stemness," homing capacity, and engraftment potential of the cells.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Results in rapid immune reconstitution (NK cells and T-cells as early as 1 week, B-cells by 28 days post-transplant). Neutrophil recovery time is inversely correlated with the CD34+ cell dose in the CF.
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Conventional absorption, distribution, metabolism, and excretion studies are not applicable to cellular therapies.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Hematologic Malignancies (Study P0501):** A randomised trial compared omidubicel (n=62) to standard umbilical cord blood (UCB, n=63). Omidubicel significantly reduced the median time to neutrophil recovery (12 days vs. 22 days) and reduced the incidence of Grade 2/3 bacterial or Grade 3 fungal infections (39% vs. 60%).
- **Severe Aplastic Anemia (Study 17-H-0091):** In a single-arm study (n=14 for efficacy), 86% of patients achieved early and sustained neutrophil recovery at 100 days, with a median recovery time of 11 days.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Cell Fractions (CF and NF):** Must be stored in the vapor phase of liquid nitrogen at **≤ -150°C**.
- **Infusion Solution (IS):** Store in a refrigerator at **2–8°C**.
- **Shelf Life:** Verify the expiry date on the product labels and the Release for Infusion (RFI) Certificate upon receipt.
- **Handling:** Do not x-ray. Use universal precautions and aseptic technique.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Prescription only (Rx Only).
