# Editorial Product Information: Opium Tincture (Morphine Tincture)

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA)
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of Opium Tincture Product Information for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises available regulatory data into a single clinical reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. The source material for this document includes information for a product that has not been formally evaluated by the FDA for safety and efficacy [US].

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Morphine (as anhydrous morphine). 100 mL of tincture contains 1 g of anhydrous morphine, which is equivalent to 10 g of Powdered Opium, USP.
- **Chemical Class:** Opioid alkaloid.
- **Brand Names:** Opium Tincture, USP (Deodorized) [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Oral hydroalcoholic solution containing 10 mg/mL of anhydrous morphine [US]. 
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Inactive Ingredients:** Alcohol (19% by volume) and water [US].
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Edenbridge Pharmaceuticals, LLC [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Diarrhea:** Indicated for the symptomatic treatment of diarrhea [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Adult Dose:** 0.6 mL administered orally four times daily [US].
- **Administration Instructions:** The preparation is "deodorized" (denarcotized with petroleum distillate) to remove unpleasant odors and nauseating substances, resulting in a clear, reddish-brown solution [US].
- **Titration:** Use the smallest effective dose for the shortest duration necessary to minimize the risk of tolerance and physical dependence [US].
- **Missed Dose:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Pediatric Use:** This preparation is not recommended for use in children [US].
- **Poisoning:** Contraindicated in the treatment of diarrhea caused by poisoning until the toxic material has been eliminated from the gastrointestinal tract [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None specified in available sources; however, the document contains significant warnings regarding addiction.
- **Addiction and Dependence:** Usage can result in addiction. Practitioners should exercise caution to minimize the development of physical dependence and tolerance [US].
- **Central Nervous System (CNS) Effects:** Use with caution in patients with increased intracranial pressure or cerebral arteriosclerosis [US].
- **Respiratory and Hepatic Conditions:** Caution is required in patients with bronchial asthma, emphysema, hepatic cirrhosis, or liver insufficiency [US].
- **Other Clinical Precautions:** Use with caution in patients with gastrointestinal hemorrhage, myxedema, or those who are debilitated [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Gastrointestinal:** Constipation, nausea, and vomiting [US].
- **Dermatological:** Pruritus and urticaria have been observed [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Respiratory depression, CNS depression, and potential for circulatory collapse in cases of overdosage [US].
- **Frequency Data:** Specific frequency categories (e.g., >10%) are not defined in the source document [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacodynamic Interactions:**
    - **CNS Depressants:** Enhanced effects and increased risk of respiratory depression when combined with other central nervous system depressants. Dosage reduction is indicated in patients receiving concurrent CNS depressants [US].
- **General Note:** Clinicians should consider the individual cautions applicable to every ingredient when opium preparations are used in combination products [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Category C [US]:** Animal reproduction studies have not been conducted. It is unknown if the drug causes fetal harm or affects reproduction capacity. Should be administered to pregnant women only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Caution should be exercised when the tincture is administered to a nursing woman [US].
- **Fertility:** Data not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established. Use is generally not recommended [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Use with caution; dosage reduction may be required in very old or poor-risk patients [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Not specifically addressed in available sources, though general caution for "poor-risk" patients applies [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Use with caution in patients with hepatic cirrhosis or liver insufficiency [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Opiates enhance the tone of the longitudinal muscle segments and inhibit the propulsive contractions of both circular and longitudinal muscles in the gastrointestinal tract. The antidiarrheal effect is achieved at doses lower than those required for significant analgesia [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Morphine is well absorbed from the gastrointestinal tract following oral administration [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Undergoes rapid first-pass metabolism in the liver. Primary metabolism is via conjugation with glucuronic acid at the 3-hydroxyl group (morphine-3-glucuronide) and secondary conjugation at the 6-hydroxyl group (morphine-3,6-diglucuronide) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Excreted primarily in the urine as glucuronide conjugates and small amounts of unchanged drug. Approximately 75% of a dose is eliminated via the urine within 48 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Summary:** The source document notes that this specific drug product has not been found by the FDA to be safe and effective (Unapproved Drug) [US]. No pivotal trial data were provided in the source documentation.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at controlled room temperature, 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined by the USP [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as a **Schedule II** controlled substance (CII). Available by prescription only (Rx only) [US].
- **Regulatory Status:** This product is marketed as an "unapproved drug" and has not undergone the standard FDA new drug approval process [US].
