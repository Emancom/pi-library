# Editorial Product Information: Olanzapine and Samidorphan

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA)
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of the combination product olanzapine and samidorphan for healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This synthesis is an editorial reference and does not replace the official Product Information required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory authorities for specific legal and prescribing requirements. At the time of this writing, only the United States (FDA) documentation was available for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** Olanzapine (atypical antipsychotic) and Samidorphan L-malate (opioid antagonist).
- **Brand Name:** **LYBALVI** [US]
- **ATC Code:** N05AH03 (olanzapine); samidorphan does not yet have a widely established standalone ATC code in this combination.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - **Oral Tablets (Bilayer, Film-coated):** 
        - 5 mg olanzapine / 10 mg samidorphan (Yellow)
        - 10 mg olanzapine / 10 mg samidorphan (Orange)
        - 15 mg olanzapine / 10 mg samidorphan (Blue)
        - 20 mg olanzapine / 10 mg samidorphan (Pink)
- **Route of Administration:** Oral
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Alkermes, Inc. [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Schizophrenia:** Treatment of schizophrenia in adults [US].
- **Bipolar I Disorder:** 
    - Acute treatment of manic or mixed episodes as monotherapy or as an adjunct to lithium or valproate in adults [US].
    - Maintenance monotherapy in adults [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Administration:** Administer once daily, with or without food. Tablets must be swallowed whole; do not divide, crush, or combine different strengths to achieve a dose.

### Indication-Specific Dosing (Adults)
- **Schizophrenia:** 
    - **Starting dose:** 5 mg/10 mg or 10 mg/10 mg once daily.
    - **Target dose:** 10 mg/10 mg, 15 mg/10 mg, or 20 mg/10 mg daily.
    - **Titration:** Adjust in 5 mg increments (based on the olanzapine component) at weekly intervals based on clinical response.
- **Bipolar I Disorder (Monotherapy):** 
    - **Starting dose:** 10 mg/10 mg or 15 mg/10 mg once daily.
    - **Target dose:** 5 mg/10 mg to 20 mg/10 mg daily.
    - **Titration:** Adjustments of 5 mg may occur at intervals no shorter than 24 hours.
- **Bipolar I Disorder (Adjunct to Lithium or Valproate):** 
    - **Starting dose:** 10 mg/10 mg once daily.
    - **Target dose:** 10 mg/10 mg to 20 mg/10 mg daily.

### Special Dosing Populations
- **Predisposition to Hypotension/Slow Metabolism:** Start at 5 mg/10 mg daily and escalate cautiously [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Opioid Use:** Patients currently using any opioid agonists [US].
- **Opioid Withdrawal:** Patients undergoing acute opioid withdrawal [US].
- **Combination Therapy:** When used as an adjunct for Bipolar I, refer to the contraindications for lithium or valproate.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning (Increased Mortality in Elderly Patients with Dementia-Related Psychosis):** Antipsychotic drugs are associated with an increased risk of death in elderly patients with dementia-related psychosis. This drug is not approved for this population [US].
- **Opioid Withdrawal Precipitation:** Due to the samidorphan component, this product can precipitate severe, potentially hospitalised withdrawal in opioid-dependent patients. Ensure an opioid-free interval of at least 7 days for short-acting opioids and 14 days for long-acting opioids before initiation [US].
- **Risk of Fatal Opioid Overdose:** 
    - Patients may attempt to overcome the samidorphan blockade with high doses of opioids, leading to fatal respiratory arrest.
    - After discontinuing treatment, patients may have reduced opioid tolerance, increasing overdose risk if prior doses are resumed.
- **Neuroleptic Malignant Syndrome (NMS):** Potentially fatal; requires immediate discontinuation and intensive monitoring.
- **Drug Reaction with Eosinophilia and Systemic Symptoms (DRESS):** Discontinue immediately if suspected.
- **Metabolic Changes:** Significant risk of hyperglycemia/diabetes mellitus (sometimes fatal), dyslipidemia, and weight gain. Monitor fasting glucose, lipids, and weight at baseline and periodically.
- **Tardive Dyskinesia:** Risk increases with duration of treatment and cumulative dose.
- **Orthostatic Hypotension and Syncope:** Monitor vital signs, particularly in patients with known cardiovascular or cerebrovascular disease.
- **Leukopenia, Neutropenia, and Agranulocytosis:** Monitor CBC in patients with a history of low WBC/ANC; discontinue if a significant decline occurs.
- **Anticholinergic (Antimuscarinic) Effects:** Use with caution in patients with urinary retention, prostatic hypertrophy, or paralytic ileus.
- **Laboratory Interference:** May cause false-positive results for opioids on urine immunoassay screens; use confirmatory chromatographic methods [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common Adverse Effects (≥5% and twice placebo)
- **Schizophrenia:** Weight gain, somnolence, dry mouth, headache.
- **Bipolar I (Monotherapy/Olanzapine data):** Somnolence, dry mouth, dizziness, asthenia, constipation, dyspepsia, increased appetite, tremor.
- **Bipolar I (Adjunctive to Li/VPA/Olanzapine data):** Dry mouth, weight gain, increased appetite, dizziness, back pain, constipation, speech disorder, amnesia, paresthesia.

### Frequency and Severity
- **Very Common (>10%):** Weight increased (up to 25% in long-term schizophrenia trials), somnolence, dry mouth. [US]
- **Metabolic:** In a 4-week trial, 26% of patients gained ≥7% of their baseline body weight compared to 5% in the placebo group [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Strong CYP3A4 Inducers (e.g., Rifampin):** Not recommended; significantly decreases exposure of both components.
- **Strong CYP1A2 Inhibitors (e.g., Fluvoxamine):** Consider reducing the olanzapine component dose.
- **CYP1A2 Inducers (e.g., Carbamazepine, Smoking):** Consider increasing the olanzapine component dose; smoking increases olanzapine clearance by 40%.
- **CNS Acting Drugs/Alcohol:** May potentiate orthostatic hypotension and sedation.
- **Antihypertensive Agents:** May enhance hypotensive effects.
- **Levodopa and Dopamine Agonists:** Not recommended; may antagonise the therapeutic effects.
- **Anticholinergic Drugs:** Increased risk of severe gastrointestinal adverse reactions.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Classification:** Not specified by letter category in available sources, but follows the US labelling rule [US].
    - **Clinical Considerations:** Neonates exposed to antipsychotics during the third trimester are at risk for extrapyramidal and/or withdrawal symptoms. 
- **Breastfeeding:** Both olanzapine and samidorphan are present in human milk. Monitor infants for excess sedation, irritability, and poor feeding. The benefits of breastfeeding should be weighed against the clinical need for the mother and potential risks to the infant.
- **Fertility:** Hyperprolactinemia may cause a reversible reduction in female fertility.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness not established in patients <18 years [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Increased risk of cerebrovascular accidents and death in patients with dementia. Consider lower starting doses.
- **Renal Impairment:** No adjustment for mild to severe impairment. **Not recommended** for patients with end-stage renal disease (eGFR <15 mL/min/1.73m²) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment required for mild to moderate impairment. Not studied in severe hepatic impairment.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** 
    - **Olanzapine:** Efficacy mediated via D2 and 5-HT2 antagonism.
    - **Samidorphan:** Opioid antagonist at mu, kappa, and delta receptors; intended to mitigate olanzapine-induced weight gain.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Absolute bioavailability of samidorphan is 69%. Steady state reached in 7 days.
    - **Distribution:** Olanzapine is 93% protein-bound; Samidorphan is 23-33% protein-bound.
    - **Metabolism:** Olanzapine (primarily CYP1A2, UGT1A4); Samidorphan (primarily CYP3A4).
    - **Elimination:** Olanzapine t½ (35–52 hours); Samidorphan t½ (7–11 hours).

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Schizophrenia (Study 1):** A 4-week trial showed statistically significant improvement in PANSS total scores compared to placebo (-23.9 vs -17.5).
- **Weight Mitigation (Study 2):** A 24-week trial in schizophrenia patients showed significantly less weight gain with the combination product (mean 4.2% increase) compared to olanzapine monotherapy (mean 6.6% increase). The proportion of patients gaining ≥10% body weight was 17.8% (combination) vs 29.8% (monotherapy) [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C).
- **Handling:** Keep the bottle tightly closed and protect it from moisture.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as Rx-only. Samidorphan is not currently a scheduled controlled substance, but the product requires a specific Medication Guide for patients regarding opioid risks.
