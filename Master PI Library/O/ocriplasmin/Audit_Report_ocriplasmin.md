## Verbatim Copied Passages
The following passages are identical or near-identical to the TGA source (Document 1), representing a failure to synthesize the information into original prose.

1.  **Product Description (Section 1):**
    *   **Editorial:** "Ocriplasmin (ryp) is a recombinant, truncated version of human plasmin, produced via recombinant DNA technology in a *Pichia pastoris* expression system. It has a molecular weight of 27.2 kDa..."
    *   **Source (TGA, p. 1):** "Ocriplasmin (ryp) is a recombinant truncated form of human plasmin with a molecular weight of 27.2 kDa produced by recombinant DNA technology in a Pichia pastoris expression system."
    *   **Location:** Section 1, paragraph 2.

2.  **Pharmacokinetics Data (Section 9):**
    *   **Editorial:** "...activity was measured at only 9% of the theoretical starting concentration 2 to 4 hours post-injection and was undetectable by day 7 [TGA]."
    *   **Source (TGA, p. 14):** "...mean ocriplasmin activity was 9% of theoretical start concentration 2-4 hours after injection and below the lower level of quantification at 7 days."
    *   **Location:** Section 9, paragraph 1.

3.  **Metabolism Pathway (Section 9):**
    *   **Editorial:** "...specifically α2-antiplasmin and α2-macroglobulin."
    *   **Source (TGA, p. 14):** "...via its interactions with protease inhibitor α2-antiplasmin or α2-macroglobulin."
    *   **Location:** Section 9, paragraph 2.

4.  **Mechanism of Action Targets (Section 10):**
    *   **Editorial:** "...specifically laminin, fibronectin, and collagen."
    *   **Source (TGA, p. 11):** "...(e.g. laminin, fibronectin and collagen)..."
    *   **Location:** Section 10.

5.  **Indications (Section 2):**
    *   **Editorial:** "...associated with a macular hole with a diameter of 400 microns or less."
    *   **Source (TGA, p. 1):** "...associated with macular hole of diameter less than or equal to 400 microns."
    *   **Location:** Section 2, Bullet 1.

## Close Paraphrases
1.  **Contraindications:** The list of inactive ingredients in Section 3 is a direct lift from the TGA p. 1 composition list, merely reformatted into a parenthetical.
2.  **Decreased Visual Acuity (Section 4):** The sentence "In clinical trials, approximately 5.6% of patients experienced a decrease of 3 lines or more in best-corrected visual acuity (BCVA) compared to 3.2% in the control group" follows the exact structure of the TGA source on p. 4, changing only "vehicle" to "control group."
3.  **Storage (Section 11):** The instructions for storage after thawing ("kept below 25°C for up to 8 hours but must not be refrozen") are taken directly from TGA p. 16 with minimal word substitution.

## Originality Strengths
The editorial note at the beginning is original and correctly identifies that the second file provided by the user was Levocarnitine, not ocriplasmin. The structure of the document is well-organized, and the author successfully merged specific TGA categories (like Pregnancy Category B2) into a unified format.

## Differences from Source PIs
*   **Harmonization Attempt:** The editorial attempts to distinguish between TGA and FDA terminology (e.g., VMT vs. VMA), although it could not have verified the FDA terminology from the provided files (since Document 2 was Levocarnitine).
*   **Abridgment:** The editorial significantly condenses the "Preparation for Administration" and "Adverse Effects" sections, moving away from the exhaustive tables in the source to a summary of "Very Common" and "Common" reactions.
*   **Exclusion:** The editorial omits specific clinical trial names (e.g., TG-MV-006) and detailed animal study data, focusing instead on the clinical takeaways.

## Recommendation
The editorial **NEEDS REVISION**. 

While the organization is professional, the document relies too heavily on verbatim strings for technical data (pharmacokinetics, mechanism of action, and product description). To pass an originality audit, the following changes are required:
1.  **Rewrite the Active Ingredient description:** Avoid the specific technical string used by the TGA.
2.  **Paraphrase the Pharmacokinetics:** Describe the rapid decline in activity and the 7-day clearance without using the exact "9% of theoretical start concentration" phrasing.
3.  **Recast the Mechanism of Action:** Describe the enzymatic targeting of the vitreoretinal interface proteins in a more narrative style rather than just listing the three proteins found in the source's parentheses.
4.  **Verification:** Ensure that any information attributed to the "FDA" in the text is verified against a legitimate FDA ocriplasmin PI, as the source provided for this audit was an unrelated medication.
