## Originality Strengths
- **Harmonisation Context:** The editorial successfully identifies and highlights specific differences between jurisdictions that do not exist in either single source. For example, it correctly distinguishes the FDA’s "less than 2 years of age" criterion from the TGA’s weight-based "2.6 kg to 21 kg" criterion.
- **Comparative Analysis:** The "Administration Instructions" section synthesizes the conflicting stability data (8 hours for FDA vs. 6 hours for TGA) into a single, easy-to-read comparison.
- **Structural Overhaul:** The editorial abandons the linear structure of the source documents in favor of a thematic healthcare professional (HCP) overview, grouping monitoring requirements and jurisdictional differences effectively.
- **Data Condensation:** The Clinical Trials section (Section 11) successfully condenses several pages of dense trial data from both PIs into three high-level bullet points without copying the narrative text of the results.

## Differences from Source PIs
- **Jurisdictional Tagging:** The editorial uses bracketed tags (e.g., [FDA], [TGA]) to denote which regulatory body issued specific guidance, a feature entirely absent from the source documents.
- **Omission of Redundancy:** The editorial strips away legalistic boilerplate and repeated headings found in the "Highlights" and "Full Prescribing Information" sections of the FDA document.
- **Consolidated Monitoring:** It merges monitoring advice found in "Dosage and Administration" and "Warnings and Precautions" into a single "Monitoring Requirements" subsection (Section 5), making it more useful for clinical practice.
- **Merged Tables:** The Adverse Effects table (Section 6) uses the N=99 dataset from the TGA document but presents it in a cleaner, editorialised format compared to the source tables.

The editorial **PASSES** the originality review.
