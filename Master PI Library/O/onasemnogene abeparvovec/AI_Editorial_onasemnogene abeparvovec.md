# Editorial Product Information: Onasemnogene Abeparvovec

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Regulatory Agencies:** Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA - Australia); Food and Drug Administration (FDA - United States).
- **Purpose:** This document is a harmonised editorial synthesis of the official Product Information (PI) from multiple jurisdictions. It is designed to provide healthcare professionals with a coherent, clinically accurate overview of the drug's profile.
- **Disclaimer:** This editorial synthesis is for informational purposes for healthcare professionals and is not a substitute for the official PI or summary of product characteristics (SmPC) required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

---

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Onasemnogene abeparvovec (referred to as **onasemnogene abeparvovec-xioi** in the USA [FDA]).
- **Chemical Class:** Gene therapy; non-replicating recombinant adeno-associated virus serotype 9 (AAV9) vector containing the cDNA of the human survival motor neuron (*SMN*) gene.
- **Brand Name:** **ZOLGENSMA**
- **ATC Code:** M09AX09
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Suspension for intravenous (IV) infusion. Each mL contains a nominal concentration of 2.0 × 10¹³ vector genomes (vg).
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous infusion only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Novartis Pharmaceuticals.

## 2. Approved Indications
**Zolgensma** is indicated for the treatment of patients with spinal muscular atrophy (SMA) with bi-allelic mutations in the survival motor neuron 1 (*SMN1*) gene, subject to the following jurisdictional criteria:
- **Age/Weight Criteria:** 
    - Indicated for paediatric patients less than 2 years of age [FDA].
    - Indicated for paediatric patients with safety and efficacy established in patients weighing 2.6 kg to 21 kg [TGA].
- **Genotypic Criteria:**
    - Specifically indicated for patients with 3 or fewer copies of the *SMN2* gene [TGA].
- **Limitations of Use:**
    - Safety and efficacy of repeat administration have not been evaluated.
    - Use in patients with advanced SMA (e.g., complete paralysis, permanent ventilator dependence) has not been established.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Recommended Dose:** A single-dose intravenous infusion of 1.1 × 10¹⁴ vector genomes per kilogram (vg/kg) of body weight.
- **Dosing Schedule:** Zolgensma is for **single treatment only**. Re-dosing is not recommended due to the immune response generated against the AAV9 capsid.

### Concomitant Corticosteroid Regimen
To mitigate potential hepatotoxicity, all patients must receive systemic corticosteroids:
1. **Pre-infusion:** Administer oral prednisolone (or equivalent) at 1 mg/kg/day beginning one day prior to Zolgensma infusion.
2. **Post-infusion:** Continue 1 mg/kg/day prednisolone for 30 days.
3. **Tapering:** 
    - If liver function is unremarkable (normal clinical exam, total bilirubin/prothrombin time, and ALT/AST < 2 × ULN), taper the dose over the next 28 days.
    - If liver abnormalities persist, continue the 1 mg/kg/day dose until values fall below 2 × ULN, then taper over at least 28 days.

### Administration Instructions
- **Preparation:** Thaw before use (approx. 12 hours in refrigerator or 4 hours at room temperature). Do not shake. Use within 8 hours [FDA] or 6 hours [TGA] of drawing into a syringe.
- **Infusion:** Administer via a venous catheter as a slow infusion over 60 minutes. **Do not administer as an IV push or bolus.**
- **Stability:** Do not refreeze.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Known Hypersensitivity:** Hypersensitivity to the active substance or any excipients [TGA].
- **FDA Position:** None listed [FDA].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warnings
- **Hepatotoxicity:** **Acute serious liver injury, acute liver failure, and elevated aminotransferases can occur.** Fatal outcomes have been reported. Patients with pre-existing hepatic impairment are at higher risk.

### General Warnings
- **Systemic Immune Response:** Infusion may trigger a serious immune response. Patients should be clinically stable (absence of infection) prior to infusion. Postpone treatment in the presence of active infection.
- **Thrombocytopenia:** Transient decreases in platelet counts, including cases of clinical significance, typically occur within the first two weeks post-infusion.
- **Thrombotic Microangiopathy (TMA):** Cases of TMA (characterised by thrombocytopenia, haemolytic anaemia, and acute kidney injury) have occurred, usually within two weeks of infusion. Concurrent infections or vaccinations may be contributing factors.
- **Elevated Troponin-I:** Increases in cardiac troponin-I have been observed. Consider cardiac evaluation if clinically indicated.
- **Risk of Tumorigenicity:** There is a theoretical risk of tumorigenicity due to the integration of AAV vector DNA into the host genome.

### Monitoring Requirements
- **Baseline:** AAV9 antibody testing (titre must be ≤ 1:50); Liver function tests (AST, ALT, total bilirubin, prothrombin time, INR, albumin); CBC (including platelets); Creatinine.
- **Post-infusion:** 
    - **Liver Function:** Weekly for the first month, then weekly during the corticosteroid taper. If stable, monitor every two weeks for an additional month.
    - **Platelets:** Weekly for the first month, then every other week for the second and third months until return to baseline.

## 6. Adverse Effects
The following frequencies are based on clinical trial data (N=99) for the recommended dose.

| Adverse Reaction | Frequency | Category |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Hepatic enzyme increased | 24.2% | Very Common |
| Vomiting | 8.1% | Common |
| Hypertransaminasaemia | 8.1% | Common |
| Thrombocytopenia | 6.1% | Common |
| Pyrexia | 5.1% | Common |
| Troponin increased | 5.1% | Common |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Acute liver failure, acute kidney injury, and thrombotic microangiopathy [Post-marketing].
- **Post-marketing Reactions:** Infusion-related reactions (e.g., rash, hypotension, dyspnoea).

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Vaccinations:** Where feasible, adjust the vaccination schedule to accommodate corticosteroid administration. Live vaccines (e.g., MMR, varicella) are contraindicated in patients on substantially immunosuppressive steroid doses (≥ 20 mg or 2 mg/kg/day of prednisone for ≥ 2 weeks).
- **Other SMA Therapies:** The safety and efficacy of using Zolgensma in conjunction with other SMA therapies (e.g., nusinersen, risdiplam) have not been established.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Australian Category B2:** No data in pregnant women. Animal studies in mice showed no evidence of fetal harm.
    - **FDA Risk Summary:** No available data on use in pregnant women.
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if Zolgensma is excreted in human milk. Consideration should be given to the mother's clinical need and potential effects on the infant.
- **Fertility:** No human data available. Animal studies in mice showed no adverse effects on male or female fertility.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Use in premature neonates before reaching full-term gestational age is not recommended, as corticosteroids may adversely affect neurological development.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Zolgensma has not been studied in this population. Use requires careful risk-benefit consideration. Patients with baseline LFTs > 2 × ULN were excluded from trials (excepting neonatal jaundice).
- **Renal Impairment:** Safety and efficacy have not been established.
- **Elderly Use:** Not applicable.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Zolgensma delivers a functional copy of the human *SMN* gene into the nucleus of transduced cells. This resides as episomal DNA, providing stable expression of the SMN protein to support motor neuron survival.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Vector DNA is found highest in the liver, but also in the spleen, heart, pancreas, and various other tissues.
    - **Elimination/Shedding:** Vector DNA is shed via saliva, urine, and stool. Clearance is primarily via faeces; the majority is cleared within 30 days.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** There are no clinically relevant pharmacodynamic data for this product.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **STR1VE (Study 303):** A Phase 3 study in Type 1 SMA patients (N=22) showed 90.9% event-free survival at 14 months, significantly higher than the 25% survival rate in natural history cohorts. 64% of patients achieved independent sitting for ≥ 30 seconds.
- **Study 101:** A Phase 1 study where all 12 patients treated with the therapeutic dose were event-free at 24 months. Long-term follow-up (up to 5.7 years) showed maintained or improved motor milestones.
- **Study COAV101A12306:** Evaluated 24 patients weighing 8.5 kg to 21 kg. Most patients showed clinically meaningful improvements in motor scale scores (HFMSE) at 52 weeks [TGA].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Frozen Storage:** Store and transport at ≤ -60°C.
- **Refrigerated Storage:** Once received, store at 2°C to 8°C in the original carton.
- **Shelf Life:** Stable for 14 days from receipt when stored at 2°C to 8°C.
- **Special Handling:** Contains a genetically modified organism (GMO). Follow local biohazard waste guidelines. Use double bagging for materials in contact with the product. Caregivers should practice strict hand hygiene when handling patient waste (e.g., nappies) for 1 month post-infusion.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Australia:** Prescription Only Medicine (Schedule 4). Subject to additional monitoring [TGA].
- **United States:** Rx Only [FDA].
