## 3. Approved Indications
- General indication: Helps prevent sunburn (sunscreen).  
- Regulatory/indication distinctions: Not specified in available sources beyond the general sunscreen indication.

## 4. Dosage and Administration
### General dosing (adult)
- Apply liberally and evenly to exposed skin 15 minutes before sun exposure.
- Reapply at least every 2 hours.
- Use a water-resistant sunscreen if swimming or sweating.

### Dosing by indication
- Not specified in available sources beyond general sunburn prevention directions.

### Administration instructions
- Apply liberally and evenly 15 minutes before exposure to sunlight.
- Reapply at least every 2 hours and after swimming or sweating when a water-resistant product is indicated.
- Keep product out of the eyes; rinse with water if product enters the eyes.

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
- **For external use only.**
- **Do not use on damaged or broken skin.**
- Eye exposure: Keep out of eyes. If product gets into the eyes, rinse with water to remove.
- Discontinue use and seek medical advice if a rash or other skin reaction occurs.
- Keep out of the reach of children.
- If swallowed, obtain medical help or contact a Poison Control Center immediately.
- Skin cancer / skin aging alert: Spending time in the sun increases the risk of skin cancer and premature skin aging. This product has been shown only to help prevent sunburn, not skin cancer or early skin aging.
- Pediatric precaution: For children under 6 months of age, consult a physician before use.
- Product handling precaution: Protect the product from excessive heat and direct sunlight.
- Monitoring requirements: Not specified in available sources.
- Specific clinical precautions: Not specified in available sources beyond the above warnings.
