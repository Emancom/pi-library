# Editorial Product Information: Oxandrolone

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating regulatory data into a clinically relevant format.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult the current approved PI in their specific region before prescribing.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Oxandrolone (a synthetic derivative of testosterone; 17β-hydroxy-17α-methyl-2-oxa-5α-androstan-3-one).
- **Chemical Class:** Anabolic androgenic steroid (17-alpha-alkylated androgen).
- **Brand Names:** Oxandrin [US].
- **ATC Code:** A14AA08 (Note: Not explicitly in source, but standard for the active ingredient).
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - Oral tablets: 2.5 mg and 10 mg [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Savient Pharmaceuticals, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Weight Gain Facilitation:** Adjunctive therapy to promote weight gain following significant weight loss associated with extensive surgery, chronic infections, or severe trauma [US].
- **Weight Maintenance:** Treatment of patients who fail to gain or maintain normal weight without a definitive pathophysiologic cause [US].
- **Protein Catabolism Offset:** To counteract protein catabolism resulting from the prolonged administration of corticosteroids [US].
- **Osteoporosis Pain Relief:** For the relief of bone pain frequently accompanying osteoporosis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Adult Dosing:** The response to anabolic steroids varies by individual. The standard daily dose is 2.5 mg to 20 mg, administered in 2 to 4 divided doses [US].
- **Duration of Therapy:** A course of 2 to 4 weeks is typically adequate. Treatment may be repeated intermittently as clinically indicated [US].
- **Administration Instructions:** Therapy with **oxandrolone** is intended as an adjunct and should not replace conventional primary treatments [US].
- **Missed Dose Instructions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- Known or suspected carcinoma of the prostate or the male breast [US].
- Carcinoma of the breast in females with concurrent hypercalcemia (androgens may exacerbate osteolytic bone resorption) [US].
- Pregnancy (due to the risk of foetal masculinization) [US].
- Nephrosis or the nephrotic phase of nephritis [US].
- Hypercalcemia [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning [US]:**
    - **Peliosis Hepatis:** Replacement of liver (and sometimes splenic) tissue with blood-filled cysts. This may present with minimal dysfunction or progress to life-threatening liver failure/hemorrhage. Discontinuation usually results in lesion regression.
    - **Hepatic Neoplasms:** Reports of benign (androgen-dependent) and fatal malignant liver cell tumors. These are highly vascular and may remain silent until intra-abdominal hemorrhage occurs.
    - **Lipid Abnormalities:** Therapy is associated with decreased High-Density Lipoprotein (HDL) and sometimes increased Low-Density Lipoprotein (LDL), significantly elevating the risk of atherosclerosis and coronary artery disease.
- **Hepatotoxicity:** Cholestatic hepatitis and jaundice may occur even at low doses. **Oxandrolone** must be discontinued if liver function tests (LFTs) become abnormal or if jaundice appears [US].
- **Hypercalcemia:** In patients with breast cancer, therapy may stimulate osteolysis; discontinue if hypercalcemia develops [US].
- **Edema:** Serious complications can arise in patients with pre-existing cardiac, renal, or hepatic disease. Concomitant use with adrenal steroids or ACTH may exacerbate fluid retention [US].
- **Pediatric Precautions:** Androgens may accelerate bone maturation without a compensatory gain in linear growth, potentially compromising final adult height. Bone age (left wrist/hand) must be monitored every 6 months [US].
- **Athletic Performance:** Patients should be advised that anabolic steroids have not been shown to enhance athletic ability [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Frequency data is not categorised by percentage in the source material but is grouped by system and population [US].

| System/Population | Adverse Reaction(s) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Hepatic** | Cholestatic jaundice, hepatic necrosis (rare), death (rare), hepatocellular neoplasms, peliosis hepatis, elevated LFTs (AST, ALT, bilirubin, alkaline phosphatase) |
| **Males (Prepubertal)** | Phallic enlargement, increased frequency/persistence of erections |
| **Males (Postpubertal)** | Inhibition of testicular function, testicular atrophy, oligospermia, impotence, chronic priapism, epididymitis, bladder irritability |
| **Females** | Clitoral enlargement, menstrual irregularities, deepening of the voice, hirsutism, male pattern baldness |
| **CNS** | Habituation, excitation, insomnia, depression, libido changes |
| **Hematologic** | Bleeding (especially with anticoagulants), polycythemia |
| **Metabolic/Endocrine** | Decreased glucose tolerance, increased creatinine excretion, increased CPK, decreased thyroxine-binding globulin |
| **Other** | Acne, gynecomastia, edema, retention of serum electrolytes (Na, Cl, K, PO4, Ca) |

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Significant interaction. **Oxandrolone** may markedly increase the INR or prothrombin time (PT). In clinical studies, a 5.5-fold decrease in warfarin dose (80-85% reduction) was required to maintain target INR. Close monitoring is essential when starting or stopping therapy [US].
- **Oral Hypoglycemic Agents:** **Oxandrolone** may inhibit the metabolism of these agents, potentially increasing their effect [US].
- **Adrenal Steroids / ACTH:** May increase the severity of edema in predisposed patients [US].
- **Laboratory Test Interference:** 
    - Decreased levels of thyroxine-binding globulin leading to decreased total T4 and increased resin uptake of T3 and T4. Free thyroid hormone levels remain unchanged [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category X]. Contraindicated due to embryotoxicity, fetotoxicity, and masculinization of the female foetus as seen in animal studies at 9 times the human dose [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** It is not known if **oxandrolone** is excreted in human milk. Due to the potential for serious adverse reactions in infants, a decision must be made to either discontinue nursing or discontinue the drug [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies show dose-related reductions in spermatogenesis and decreased organ weights (testes, prostate, seminal vesicles, ovaries, uterus) [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Must be used with extreme caution by specialists. Accelerates epiphyseal maturation more rapidly than linear growth; monitoring of bone age every 6 months is required to prevent compromised adult height [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Recommended dose is 5 mg twice daily. Clinical trials showed mean weight gain similar to younger cohorts, but elderly patients (especially women) exhibited greater sensitivity to fluid retention and transaminase elevations [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Contraindicated in the nephrotic phase of nephritis. Use with caution in other renal diseases due to the risk of edema [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Contraindicated in severe impairment or peliosis hepatis. Use with extreme caution due to potential hepatotoxicity [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Synthetic testosterone derivative. Androgenic anabolic steroids suppress the gonadotropic functions of the pituitary and exert a direct effect on the testes and protein synthesis [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Specifically inhibits pituitary luteinizing hormone (LH) and, at high doses, follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) via feedback inhibition [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Not specified in available sources.
    - **Distribution:** Not specified in available sources.
    - **Metabolism:** 17-alpha-alkylation reduces first-pass metabolism.
    - **Elimination:** Mean elimination half-life is 10.4 hours in younger volunteers and 13.3 hours in elderly subjects [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Geriatric Trials:** Evaluated in 339 patients across four clinical trials with a maximum duration of 4 months. Results showed 5 mg bid and 10 mg bid were equally effective for weight gain. Elderly patients showed an increased half-life and greater sensitivity to hepatic and fluid-related adverse events [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage:** Not specified in available sources; however, products are supplied in bottles of 60 or 100 tablets [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** 
    - **United States:** Schedule III (non-narcotic) controlled substance under the Anabolic Steroids Control Act; Prescription only (Rx-only) [US].
