## 3. Approved Indications
- Helps prevent sunburn when applied as directed.  
- When used as directed together with other sun protection measures, decreases the risk of skin cancer and early skin aging caused by the sun.  
- Use in children under 6 months: consult a physician prior to use.  
- Other indications: Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Dosage and Administration
### General dosing (adult)
- Apply generously and evenly 15 minutes before sun exposure. (Specific volume per application not specified in available sources.)
- Reapply:
  - After 40 minutes of swimming or sweating.
  - Immediately after towel drying.
  - At least every 2 hours.

### Dosing by indication
- Not specified in available sources beyond general sun-protection instructions above.

### Administration instructions
- Apply to intact skin only. Do not use on damaged or broken skin.
- Avoid contact with eyes. If product gets into eyes, rinse thoroughly with water to remove.
- Use in combination with other sun-protection measures (e.g., limiting time in sun, protective clothing, hats, sunglasses).

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
- **For external use only.**
- **Do not apply to damaged or broken skin.**
- Keep product out of the eyes. If contact with eyes occurs, rinse with water to remove.
- **Stop use and ask a doctor if skin rash occurs.**
- Keep out of reach of children. If product is swallowed, obtain medical help or contact a poison control center immediately.
- Children under 6 months of age: consult a doctor before use.
- Use of sunscreen does not eliminate all risk; combine with other sun protection measures (limit sun exposure especially during 10 a.m.–2 p.m., wear long-sleeved shirts, pants, hats, and sunglasses).
- Protect product from excessive heat and direct sunlight (storage handling precaution reported).
- Monitoring requirements: Monitor for signs of skin reaction (rash, irritation). Specific laboratory or other monitoring was not specified.
