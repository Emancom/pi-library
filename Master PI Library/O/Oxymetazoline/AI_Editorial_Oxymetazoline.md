# Editorial Product Information: Oxymetazoline Hydrochloride

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdictions: **US (FDA)**. 
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview of oxymetazoline hydrochloride for healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI or summary of product characteristics required for prescribing or dispensing in any specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Oxymetazoline hydrochloride (0.05%)
- **Chemical Class:** Sympathomimetic amine; alpha-adrenergic agonist
- **Brand Names:** Generic (distributed by Taro Pharmaceuticals U.S.A., Inc.); comparable to **Afrin** [US].
- **ATC Code:** R01AA05
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Nasal spray solution, 0.05% (0.05 g per 100 mL) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Nasal
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Sun Pharmaceutical Industries, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- Temporary relief of nasal congestion associated with:
    - The common cold [US].
    - Hay fever or other upper respiratory allergies [US].
    - Sinusitis [US].
- Reduction of swollen nasal membranes [US].
- Restoration of freer breathing through the nose [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Adults and Children (6 to <12 years)
- **Dosage:** 2 to 3 sprays in each nostril [US].
- **Frequency:** No more often than every 10 to 12 hours [US].
- **Maximum Dose:** 2 doses in any 24-hour period [US].
- **Administration:** Squeeze the bottle quickly and firmly while keeping the head upright (do not tilt the head backward). Wipe the nozzle clean after each use [US].

### Missed Dose
- Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Duration of Use:** Use for more than 3 days is contraindicated as it may lead to recurrence or worsening of nasal congestion [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to oxymetazoline or any inactive ingredients (e.g., benzalkonium chloride) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### General Warnings
- **Rebound Congestion:** Frequent or prolonged use may cause nasal congestion to recur or worsen (rhinitis medicamentosa) [US].
- **Dosage Compliance:** Do not exceed the recommended dosage [US].
- **Infection Control:** Use by more than one person may spread infection [US].

### Specific Clinical Precautions
Healthcare professionals should advise patients to consult a physician before use if they have:
- Heart disease [US].
- Hypertension (high blood pressure) [US].
- Thyroid disease [US].
- Diabetes [US].
- Difficulty in urination due to an enlarged prostate gland [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are generally local and related to the sympathomimetic nature of the drug.
- **Common (1–10%):**
    - Temporary nasal discomfort (burning, stinging) [US].
    - Sneezing [US].
    - Increased nasal discharge [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** 
    - Rebound congestion with chronic use [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** While not explicitly detailed in the provided OTC labeling, clinicians should exercise caution when combining with other sympathomimetic agents or MAOIs due to potential hypertensive effects [US/Clinical Inference]. 

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Healthcare professional consultation is required before use [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Healthcare professional consultation is required before use [US].
- **Fertility:** Not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** 
    - Children 6 to under 12 years: Use with supervision [US].
    - Children under 6 years: Consult a doctor prior to use [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Oxymetazoline is a direct-acting sympathomimetic amine that stimulates alpha-adrenergic receptors in the arterioles of the nasal mucosa, resulting in vasoconstriction and reduction of nasal congestion [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** 
    - **Onset of Action:** Described as "Fast Acting" [US].
    - **Duration:** Provides relief for up to 12 hours [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Reduces swelling of nasal passages and restores airflow [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- Data not provided in the available OTC labeling sources.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage Temperature:** Store between 15°C and 30°C (59°F–86°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from excessive heat. Do not use if the imprinted safety band on the bottle is broken or missing [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as a Human OTC Drug (OTC Monograph Drug M012) [US].
