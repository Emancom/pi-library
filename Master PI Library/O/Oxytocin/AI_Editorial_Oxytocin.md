# Editorial Product Information: Oxytocin

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** Australian (TGA). 
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of Oxytocin product information for healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. At the time of this publication, only the Australian PI was available for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Oxytocin (synthetic nonapeptide).
- **Chemical Class:** Pituitary hormone (posterior lobe).
- **Brand Names:** Oxytocin APX [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** H01BB02.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** 10 IU in 1 mL solution for injection in clear glass ampoules [Australian].
- **Routes of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion, slow IV bolus, or intramuscular (IM) injection.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Apotex Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- Induction of labour for medical reasons.
- Stimulation of labour in cases of inadequate uterine effort.
- Management of the third stage of labour.
- Prevention and treatment of post-partum haemorrhage (PPH).
- Management of uterine activity following Caesarean section (post-delivery of the foetus).

## 3. Dosage and Administration

### Induction or Augmentation of Labour
Oxytocin must be administered via **intravenous infusion** only, preferably using a variable-speed infusion pump. It should **not** be given via IV bolus, IM, or subcutaneous routes for this indication [Australian].
- **Initial Rate:** 1–4 milliunits/min.
- **Titration:** Increase at intervals of no less than 20 minutes until a contraction pattern similar to normal labour is reached.
- **Maintenance:** Near-term pregnancy often responds to <10 milliunits/min. The dose can often be reduced once adequate uterine activity is established.
- **Maximum Rate:** 20 milliunits/min. 
- **Failure to Respond:** If regular contractions are not established after 5 IU, terminate the attempt. It may be repeated the following day.

### Third Stage of Labour and Puerperium
- **Standard Dose:** 5–10 IU via IM injection OR 5 IU via slow IV bolus.
- **Note:** If the patient is already receiving an infusion for induction/augmentation, the infusion should continue through the third stage.

### Caesarean Section
- **Dose:** 5 IU via slow IV bolus or IV infusion following the delivery of the foetus.

### Administration Instructions
- **Compatibility:** 0.9% saline and 5% dextrose. 
- **Incompatibility:** Solutions containing bisulphites or metabisulphites as preservatives.
- **Preparation (Drip Infusion):** 10 IU in 1 litre of infusion fluid. (1 mU/min = 0.1 mL/min; 20 mU/min = 2 mL/min).
- **Precaution:** Avoid using 5% dextrose in water alone; use dextrose with an electrolyte solution to mitigate water intoxication risks [Australian].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Foetal Distress:** Presence of foetal compromise prior to delivery.
- **Obstetric Contraindications:** Any condition where spontaneous labour or vaginal delivery is inadvisable (e.g., cephalopelvic disproportion, abnormal/cord presentation, prolapse, vasa praevia, placenta praevia, placental abruption).
- **Uterine Factors:** Excessive uterine distension or impaired resistance (e.g., multiple pregnancy, polyhydramnios), parity >4, or presence of uterine scars from major surgery (including previous C-sections).
- **Maternal Factors:** Severe toxaemia, hypertonic contractions, or predispositions to amniotic fluid embolism.
- **Drug-Drug:** Within 6 hours of administering vaginal prostaglandins [Australian].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known allergy to oxytocin or excipients (Glacial acetic acid, Chlorobutanol hemihydrate).

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None specified in available sources.
- **Supervision:** Must only be administered in a hospital setting under continuous observation by trained personnel. A qualified physician must be immediately available.
- **Uterine Over-stimulation:** Excessive dosing can lead to hypertonicity, tetanic contractions, or uterine rupture. Monitor foetal heart rate and uterine motility (tocometry) constantly.
- **Water Intoxication:** Oxytocin has weak anti-diuretic activity. High doses with electrolyte-free fluids can cause maternal/neonatal hyponatraemia, seizures, and pulmonary oedema. 
    - *Management:* Restrict fluid intake, measure electrolytes (8–12 hourly), and avoid low-sodium infusion fluids.
- **Cardiovascular Effects:** Rapid IV bolus injections (even at 2 IU) may cause acute, short-lasting hypotension with flushing and reflex tachycardia. This may result in myocardial ischaemia or QTc prolongation, especially in patients with pre-existing cardiovascular disease.
- **Disseminated Intravascular Coagulation (DIC):** Rare risk (<0.0006) associated with pharmacological induction. Increased risk in women ≥35 years, gestational age >40 weeks, or those with pregnancy complications.
- **Latex Allergy:** Potential for anaphylaxis due to structural homology between oxytocin and latex [Australian].

| **Skin** | Rare | Rash |

### Post-Marketing Experience (Frequency Not Known)
- **Cardiac/Vascular:** Myocardial ischaemia, QTc prolongation, hypotension, flushing.
- **Obstetric:** Uterine hypertonicity, tetanic contractions, rupture of the uterus.
- **Metabolic:** Water intoxication, hyponatraemia.
- **Other:** Angioedema, DIC, acute pulmonary oedema.

### Foetal/Neonatal Adverse Effects
- Foetal distress, asphyxia, death (secondary to uterine over-stimulation).
- Neonatal hyponatraemia.
- Neonatal jaundice, convulsions, and ECG changes have been reported.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Prostaglandins:** May potentiate the uterotonic effect. Require very careful monitoring if used concomitantly.
- **Inhalation Anaesthetics:** (e.g., halothane, sevoflurane, desflurane) Produce uterine relaxation and may reduce the oxytocic effect while enhancing the hypotensive effect. May also cause rhythm disturbances.
- **QT-Prolonging Drugs:** Caution is required as oxytocin may be arrhythmogenic.
- **Vasoconstrictors/Sympathomimetics:** Oxytocin may enhance pressor effects, particularly when used with caudal block anaesthesia.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Australian Category A:** Drugs which have been taken by a large number of pregnant women and women of childbearing age without any proven increase in the frequency of malformations or other direct or indirect harmful effects on the fetus having been observed.
- **Breastfeeding:** Small quantities found in breast milk. Not expected to cause harm as oxytocin is rapidly inactivated in the infant's alimentary tract.
- **Fertility:** No data available [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** No studies performed.
- **Elderly Use:** No studies in patients ≥65 years. Note: Contraindicated in elderly multiparae [Australian].
- **Renal Impairment:** Caution is required in severe impairment due to potential water retention and drug accumulation.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No studies performed.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Synthetic oxytocin stimulates the smooth muscle of the uterus, increasing the number of specific receptors as pregnancy reaches term. It also contracts myo-epithelial cells in the mammary alveoli to facilitate milk let-down.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Elicits rhythmic contractions indistinguishable from spontaneous labour at low doses; high doses/boluses can cause tetanic contractions.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Onset:** <1 minute (IV bolus); 2–4 minutes (IM).
    - **Duration:** 30–60 minutes (IM); potentially less (IV).
    - **Steady State (IV Infusion):** Reached within 20–40 minutes.
    - **Distribution:** Throughout extracellular fluid; minimal foetal transfer. Low protein binding.
    - **Metabolism:** Degraded by oxytocinase (produced during pregnancy in the placenta and plasma). No degradation by plasma in men or non-pregnant women.
    - **Elimination:** Mainly liver and kidneys. Half-life is 3–20 minutes. <1% excreted unchanged in urine.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 2°C to 8°C (Refrigerate. Do not freeze).
- **Conditions:** Protect from light.
