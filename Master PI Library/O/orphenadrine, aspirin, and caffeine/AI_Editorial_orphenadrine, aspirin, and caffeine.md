# Editorial Product Information: Orphenadrine Citrate, Aspirin, and Caffeine

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial synthesis is derived from the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (Food and Drug Administration)**.
- **Purpose:** This document is a harmonised editorial overview designed for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises complex regulatory data into a single, clinically focused reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not replace the official PI required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory documents for final prescribing decisions.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** Orphenadrine citrate (centrally acting skeletal muscle relaxant/anticholinergic), Aspirin (non-opiate analgesic/NSAID), and Caffeine (methylxanthine/CNS stimulant).
- **Brand Names:** Norgesic™ [US].
- **ATC Code:** M03BC51 (Orphenadrine, combinations excluding psycholeptics) [Editorial Note: Not explicitly in source, but standard classification].
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:**
    - **Tablet [US]:** Orphenadrine citrate 25 mg, Aspirin 385 mg, Caffeine 30 mg. 
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Galt Pharmaceuticals, LLC [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Musculoskeletal Pain:** Symptomatic relief of mild to moderate pain associated with acute musculoskeletal disorders [US].
- **Adjunctive Therapy:** Indicated as an adjunct to rest, physical therapy, and other measures for the relief of discomfort associated with acute, painful musculoskeletal conditions [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Adult Dosage:** 1 to 2 tablets administered 3 to 4 times daily [US].
- **Administration Instructions:** For oral use.
- **Missed Dose:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Titration:** Not specified in available sources; however, side effects may be managed by dosage reduction [US].

## 4. Contraindications
Due to the pharmacologic profile of the individual components, use is contraindicated in the following [US]:
- **Anticholinergic Sensitivity:** Patients with glaucoma, pyloric or duodenal obstruction, achalasia, prostatic hypertrophy, or bladder neck obstructions.
- **Neuromuscular Disorders:** Myasthenia gravis.
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known sensitivity to orphenadrine, aspirin, or caffeine, or a history of previous hypersensitivity to the product.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Reye’s Syndrome:** Aspirin-containing products are not recommended for use in children or adolescents with symptoms of chickenpox, influenza, or other viral flu symptoms due to the risk of Reye's Syndrome [US].
- **Fetal Toxicity (NSAID Class Warning):** Use of NSAIDs, including aspirin, at approximately 20 weeks gestation or later may cause fetal renal dysfunction leading to oligohydramnios. Use at approximately 30 weeks gestation or later increases the risk of premature closure of the fetal ductus arteriosus [US].
- **Psychomotor Impairment:** Orphenadrine may impair the ability to perform potentially hazardous tasks (e.g., driving or operating machinery); patients should be cautioned accordingly [US].
- **Gastrointestinal and Coagulation Risks:** Use with extreme caution in patients with a history of peptic ulcers or coagulation abnormalities [US].
- **Serious Skin Reactions:** Drug Reaction with Eosinophilia and Systemic Symptoms (DRESS) has been reported with NSAIDs. Discontinue at the first sign of rash or fever [US].
- **Monitoring:** Periodic monitoring of blood, urine, and liver function values is recommended during prolonged therapy [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are primarily associated with the anticholinergic properties of orphenadrine or the GI/systemic effects of aspirin and caffeine.

| System Organ Class | Adverse Effects (Frequency Not Quantified) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Cardiovascular** | Tachycardia, palpitations, syncope. |
| **Gastrointestinal** | Nausea, vomiting, constipation, dry mouth; Rare: G.I. hemorrhage (aspirin-related). |
| **Nervous System** | Dizziness, headache, drowsiness, light-headedness, confusion (primarily in elderly), tremors, mild central excitation, hallucinations. |
| **Ocular** | Blurred vision, pupil dilation (mydriasis), increased intraocular tension. |
| **Renal/Urinary** | Urinary hesitancy or retention. |
| **Hematologic** | Aplastic anemia (one case reported; no causal relationship established). |
| **Dermatologic/Allergic** | Urticaria and other dermatoses, rash, DRESS. |

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Propoxyphene:** Concomitant use with orphenadrine has been associated with reports of confusion, anxiety, and tremors. Dose reduction or discontinuation of one or both agents is recommended [US].
- **NSAID-class interactions:** Aspirin may interact with other agents affecting coagulation or gastric mucosa [US].
- **Other interactions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **General [US]:** Data regarding risks in the first and second trimesters are inconclusive.
    - **20-30 Weeks Gestation [US]:** Limit dose and duration to the lowest/shortest possible. Monitor for oligohydramnios via ultrasound if treatment exceeds 48 hours.
    - **30 Weeks and Later [US]:** Avoid use due to the risk of premature closure of the fetal ductus arteriosus.
- **Breastfeeding:** Safety information not specified in available sources.
- **Fertility:** Based on animal data, aspirin/prostaglandin inhibitors may interfere with embryo-fetal development and implantation [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Not recommended for children under 12 years of age. Safety and efficacy have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Older patients may be more susceptible to orphenadrine-induced confusion [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** 
    - **Orphenadrine:** A centrally acting (brain stem) compound. It selectively blocks facilitatory functions of the reticular formation. It does not produce myoneural block or directly relax tense muscles in humans.
    - **Aspirin:** Non-opiate analgesic with anti-inflammatory and antipyretic activity.
    - **Caffeine:** Central nervous system stimulant.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Orphenadrine possesses anticholinergic actions. It prevents nicotine-induced convulsions in animals but not strychnine-induced convulsions [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified in available sources.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Summary:** Source documents note that extensive experience with combinations of aspirin and caffeine has established their safety. The addition of orphenadrine does not alter the toxicity of the other components. Specific pivotal trial data were not provided in the source PI [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store below 30°C (86°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as Rx-Only (Prescription drug) [US].
