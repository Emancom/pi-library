# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial: **US (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising regulatory data into a clinical reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN/generic); a carrier molecule for long-chain fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection: 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Acute and chronic management of patients with inborn metabolic errors resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    - Recommended dose: 50 mg/kg [US].
    - Administration: Slow intravenous bolus (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion [US].
    - Acute Crisis: A loading dose may be given, followed by an equivalent dose over the next 24 hours. Dosing frequency should be every 3 to 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours [US].
    - Maximum Dose: The highest administered dose reported is 300 mg/kg [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - Recommended starting dose: 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
    - Administration: Slow 2 to 3 minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].
    - Dose Adjustment: Adjustments (e.g., down to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy, guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine levels [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter and discoloration before use [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when diluted in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations between 0.5 mg/mL and 8.0 mg/mL [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known at the time of publication [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have occurred—often within minutes of administration. Most reports involve patients with ESRD on dialysis. Discontinue immediately if a severe reaction occurs [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure disorders. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity may occur [US].
- **Monitoring:** 
    - For metabolic disorders, weekly and monthly monitoring is recommended, including blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L) [US].
    - For ESRD, monitor pre-dialysis (trough) levels; normal range is 40–50 µmol/L [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are often confounded by underlying pathology (e.g., ESRD symptoms).
- **Very Common (>10%):** 
    - General: Headache (up to 37%), Pain (up to 35%), Injection site reaction (up to 38%), Flu syndrome (up to 29%) [US].
    - Cardiovascular: Hypertension (up to 21%), Hypotension (up to 19%) [US].
    - Digestive: Diarrhea (up to 35%), Vomiting (up to 21%), Nausea (up to 12%) [US].
    - Respiratory: Pharyngitis (up to 27%), Cough increase (up to 18%) [US].
- **Common (1–10%):** Abdominal pain, Fever, Chest pain, Dizziness, Dyspnea, and Pruritus [US].
- **Other Reported Effects:** Body odor, gastritis, and seizures (Neurologic) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when used concomitantly with levocarnitine. Monitoring of INR is recommended upon initiation or dose adjustment [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - [US]: Category not explicitly assigned in text; however, animal studies (rats/rabbits) at 3.8 times the human dose showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly required [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** 
    - Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows suggest increased milk concentrations following administration. Risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant should be weighed against the benefits of maternal supplementation [US].
- **Fertility:** No mutagenic effects seen in standard tests; long-term carcinogenicity studies have not been performed [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Refer to weight-based dosing in Section 3.
- **Renal Impairment:**
    - Intravenous levocarnitine is indicated for use in ESRD patients on dialysis.
    - Safety and efficacy of *oral* levocarnitine have not been evaluated in renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in patients with severely compromised renal function may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites are trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Following IV bolus (20 mg/kg), approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours. Terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In clinical trials of ESRD patients undergoing hemodialysis, IV administration of 20 mg/kg three times weekly significantly increased plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Trough levels typically reached steady state within one week, increasing from a baseline of ~20 µmol/L to between 54 and 180 µmol/L [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing. Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as the formulation contains no preservatives [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources.
