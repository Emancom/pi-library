# Editorial Product Information: Oxytetracycline (Veterinary Soluble Powder)

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA)
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonized editorial overview of oxytetracycline hydrochloride soluble powder. It is intended for healthcare professionals (including veterinarians and pharmacists) as a clinical reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This synthesis is an editorial summary based on the provided labeling for **Oxytet Soluble**. It does not replace the official Product Information (PI) or regional regulatory labeling required for prescribing and administration in any specific jurisdiction. Note that the provided source material pertains exclusively to veterinary medicine; this agent is strictly **not for human use**.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Oxytetracycline hydrochloride
- **Chemical Class:** Tetracycline antibiotic
- **Brand Names:** **Oxytet Soluble** [US]
- **ATC Code:** QJ01AA06 (Veterinary)
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Soluble powder for oral administration via drinking water. Strength is 0.357 g of oxytetracycline hydrochloride per 1 g of powder [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral (via drinking water only).
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Huvepharma, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
The following indications are approved for veterinary use in the United States:

### Chickens
- **Infectious Synovitis:** Control of infections caused by *Mycoplasma synoviae* susceptible to oxytetracycline [US].
- **Chronic Respiratory Disease (CRD) and Air Sac Infections:** Control of infections caused by *Mycoplasma gallisepticum* and *Escherichia coli* [US].
- **Fowl Cholera:** Control of infections caused by *Pasteurella multocida* [US].

### Turkeys
- **Hexamitiasis:** Control of infections caused by *Hexamita meleagridis* [US].
- **Infectious Synovitis:** Control of infections caused by *Mycoplasma synoviae* [US].
- **Bluecomb (Transmissible/Coronaviral Enteritis):** Control of complicating bacterial organisms in growing turkeys [US].

### Swine
- **Bacterial Enteritis:** Treatment of infections caused by *Escherichia coli* and *Salmonella choleraesuis* [US].
- **Bacterial Pneumonia:** Treatment of infections caused by *Pasteurella multocida* [US].
- **Leptospirosis:** For breeding swine to reduce the incidence of abortions and leptospira shedding caused by *Leptospira pomona* [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Administration Instructions
- Solutions must be mixed fresh daily [US].
- Medicated water must be the sole source of oxytetracycline during treatment [US].
- Do not mix the powder directly with milk or milk replacers. When used in animals receiving milk, administer the medicated water either 1 hour before or 2 hours after milk feeding [US].
- Dosage concentrations may require adjustment based on animal age, ambient temperature, and humidity, as these factors significantly influence water consumption [US].

### Specific Dosing by Species [US]
| Species | Indication | Dosage | Duration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Chickens** | Infectious Synovitis | 200–400 mg/gallon | 7–14 days |
| **Chickens** | CRD / Fowl Cholera | 400–800 mg/gallon | 7–14 days |
| **Turkeys** | Hexamitiasis | 200–400 mg/gallon | 7–14 days |
| **Turkeys** | Infectious Synovitis | 400 mg/gallon | 7–14 days |
| **Turkeys** | Bluecomb | 25 mg/lb body weight | 7–14 days |
| **Swine** | Enteritis / Pneumonia | 10 mg/lb body weight | Up to 5 days |
| **Breeding Swine**| Leptospirosis | 10 mg/lb body weight | Up to 5 days |

## 4. Contraindications
- **Human Use:** Strictly contraindicated for use in humans [US].
- **Egg-Laying Birds:** Do not administer to birds producing eggs intended for human consumption [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Although not explicitly listed in the brief provided labeling, hypersensitivity to tetracyclines is a class-wide contraindication.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hazardous Remedy:** Handle with appropriate caution [US].
- **Duration Limits:** Treatment must not exceed 5 consecutive days in swine or 14 consecutive days in poultry [US].
- **Water Consumption Variables:** Total drug intake is dependent on water consumption. If animals do not consume sufficient medicated water, therapeutic levels will not be achieved [US].
- **Environmental Factors:** High temperatures and humidity increase water intake, necessitating monitoring of drug concentration to prevent overdosage or underdosage [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Frequency Data:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Not specified in available sources. Adverse reactions to tetracyclines in veterinary species may include gastrointestinal disturbances or hypersensitivity; however, the provided US labeling does not enumerate specific side effects.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Cations (Milk/Milk Replacers):** Oxytetracycline may chelate with calcium found in milk and milk replacers, significantly reducing antibiotic absorption. Administration must be timed to avoid direct contact with these substances [US].
- **Liquid Medicated Feeds:** This product is explicitly not for use in liquid medicated feeds [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy/Reproduction:** Approved for use in breeding swine for the reduction of abortions related to Leptospirosis [US].
- **Lactation:** Use in milk-producing animals requires specific timing (see Section 3) to prevent absorption interference [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric (Young Animals):** Water consumption rates vary by age (e.g., 200 chickens typically consume 1 gallon of water per day for each week of age) [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Oxytetracycline is a bacteriostatic antibiotic that inhibits bacterial protein synthesis by binding to the 30S ribosomal subunit, thereby blocking the attachment of aminoacyl-tRNA to the mRNA-ribosome complex.
- **Pharmacokinetics/Pharmacodynamics:** Not detailed in the provided clinical labeling beyond the interaction with milk cations which inhibits absorption [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- Data regarding pivotal clinical trials were not provided in the source documentation. Approval is maintained under NADA # 130-435 [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Standard Temperature:** Store between 20°C and 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Excursions:** Permitted between 15°C and 40°C (59°F to 104°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Keep out of reach of children [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Restricted to use by or on the order of a licensed veterinarian (Rx-only). Classification: Antibiotic / Hazardous Remedy. Restricted drug in California (use only as directed) [US].
