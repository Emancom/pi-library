# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial has been synthesized from official Product Information (PI) documents provided for the following jurisdiction: **US (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating regulatory data into a single clinically relevant reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This synthesis is an editorial summary for professional reference and is not a substitute for the official PI or prescribing information required for practice in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP).
- **Chemical Class:** A naturally occurring carrier molecule (amino acid derivative) required for mammalian energy metabolism; specifically (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium.
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter Healthcare Corporation).
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Sterile injection solution, 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) single-dose vial [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency:** Indicated for the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that result in secondary carnitine deficiency (e.g., glutaric aciduria II, methyl malonic aciduria, propionic acidemia, and medium-chain fatty acyl-CoA dehydrogenase deficiency) [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    - **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion [US].
    - **Loading Dose:** Often required in severe metabolic crises, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours [US].
    - **Frequency:** Administer every 3 or 4 hours; frequency should never be less than every 6 hours [US].
    - **Maximum Dose:** The highest reported administered dose is 300 mg/kg [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - **Recommended Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
    - **Administration:** Slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].
    - **Titration:** Adjustments may be made as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations [US].

### Administration Instructions
- **Monitoring:** Obtain baseline plasma carnitine concentrations. Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine levels (target: 35 to 60 µmol/L) is recommended [US].
- **Inspection:** Visually inspect parenteral products for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations of 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL for up to 24 hours at 25°C in PVC bags [US].

### Missed Dose
- Not specified in available sources for the intravenous formulation.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Known Hypersensitivity:** No specific contraindications listed in provided sources; however, caution is advised regarding serious hypersensitivity reactions (see Section 5) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions may occur within minutes of IV administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue treatment and initiate medical management [US].
- **Neurologic Reactions (Seizures):** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In patients with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Oral Formulation Cautions:** Chronic high-dose oral administration in patients with severely compromised renal function may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) [US].
- **Monitoring:** Monitor International Normalized Ratio (INR) in patients receiving concomitant warfarin therapy [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Clinical Trials Experience (ESRD Patients)
Adverse events reported at a frequency of ≥5% (regardless of causality) in double-blind, placebo-controlled trials include:
- **Body as a Whole:** Injection site reaction (33%), pain (30%), flu syndrome (25%), headache (22%), infection (15%), abdominal pain (9%), fever (7%).
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (20%), hypotension (14%), tachycardia (6%), palpitations (5%).
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea (16%), vomiting (15%), nausea (8%), anorexia (5%), dyspepsia (5%).
- **Nervous System:** Dizziness (13%), insomnia (4%), paresthesia (5%).
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (23%), cough increase (9%), rhinitis (9%), dyspnea (7%).

### Other Reported Effects
- **General:** Transient nausea and vomiting, body odor, and gastritis have been observed [US].
- **Postmarketing:** Seizures, anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased INR have occurred when levocarnitine is used concurrently with warfarin. Frequent monitoring of INR is recommended upon initiation or dose adjustment of levocarnitine [US].
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Levocarnitine is not bound to plasma proteins, reducing the likelihood of protein-binding displacement interactions [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not assigned by letter, but reproductive studies in rats/rabbits at 3.8 times human dose showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm]. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine concentrations in milk following exogenous administration. Weigh maternal benefits against potential risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** Mutagenicity tests (Salmonella, etc.) were negative. Long-term animal carcinogenicity studies have not been performed [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders and ESRD is weight-based (mg/kg) and applicable to paediatric patients [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Dosing for ESRD patients is specifically defined (10–20 mg/kg after dialysis). Oral levocarnitine has not been evaluated for safety/efficacy in patients with renal insufficiency [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for β-oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not protein bound. Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Excretion:** Following IV bolus (20 mg/kg), ~76% of the dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours. Mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].
- **Bioavailability:** Absolute bioavailability of oral formulations is approximately 15% [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Pharmacokinetic Studies:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients receiving 20 mg/kg three times weekly post-dialysis showed that trough levocarnitine concentrations increased from a baseline of ~21 µmol/L to ~190 µmol/L by week 8 [US].
- **Dose-Ranging Studies:** Trials comparing 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses in ESRD patients demonstrated dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F). Avoid excessive heat [US].
- **Protection:** Protect from freezing [US].
- **Handling:** Discard any unused portion of a single-dose vial; the formulation contains no preservatives [US].
