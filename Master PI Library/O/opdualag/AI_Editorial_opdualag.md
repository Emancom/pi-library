# Editorial Product Information: Opdualag (nivolumab and relatlimab)

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Regulatory Agencies:** Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA, Australia); Food and Drug Administration (FDA, United States).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial synthesis of official Product Information (PI) from the above jurisdictions. It is intended for use by healthcare professionals as a clinical reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI or prescribing information required in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory approved documents for legal prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** 
    - **Nivolumab:** A programmed death receptor-1 (PD-1) blocking antibody (Human IgG4).
    - **Relatlimab:** A lymphocyte activation gene-3 (LAG-3) blocking antibody (Human IgG4). [FDA: relatlimab-rmbw; TGA: relatlimab (rch)].
- **Brand Name:** **Opdualag**
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** 
    - Sterile concentrate for solution for infusion (preservative-free).
    - Single-dose vial containing 240 mg nivolumab and 80 mg relatlimab in 20 mL (12 mg/mL and 4 mg/mL, respectively).
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** 
    - [TGA]: Bristol-Myers Squibb Australia Pty Ltd.
    - [FDA]: E.R. Squibb & Sons, L.L.C. (New Jersey, USA).

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Unresectable or Metastatic Melanoma:** Indicated for the treatment of adult and paediatric patients 12 years of age or older with unresectable or metastatic melanoma.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### 3.1 General Dosing
- **Adults and Paediatrics (12 years or older weighing at least 40 kg):** 480 mg nivolumab and 160 mg relatlimab (one vial) administered intravenously every 4 weeks.
- **Duration:** Continue treatment until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity.

### 3.2 Administration Instructions
- **Infusion Time:** Administer as a 30-minute IV infusion.
- **Filter Requirements:** Must use a sterile, non-pyrogenic, low protein-binding in-line filter (pore size 0.2 $\mu$m to 1.2 $\mu$m).
- **Preparation:** May be administered undiluted or diluted with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Glucose 5% [FDA: Dextrose 5%]. Final concentration range: 3 mg/mL to 12 mg/mL (nivolumab) and 1 mg/mL to 4 mg/mL (relatlimab).
- **Contraindicated Administration:** Do not administer as an IV push or bolus injection. Do not co-administer other medications through the same line.

### 3.3 Dosage Modifications
- **Dose Reductions:** Not recommended.
- **Withholding and Discontinuation:** Generally, withhold for severe (Grade 3) immune-mediated adverse reactions and permanently discontinue for life-threatening (Grade 4) reactions or recurrent Grade 3 reactions.
- **Specific Modifications (Conservative Harmonisation):**
    - **Pneumonitis:** Withhold for Grade 2; permanently discontinue for Grade 3–4.
    - **Colitis:** Withhold for Grade 2–3; permanently discontinue for Grade 4.
    - **Hepatitis:** Withhold if AST/ALT >3 to 5x ULN [FDA: >3 to 8x ULN] or Total Bilirubin >1.5 to 3x ULN. Permanently discontinue if AST/ALT >5x [FDA: >8x] ULN or Total Bilirubin >3x ULN.
    - **Myocarditis:** Permanently discontinue for Grade 2, 3, or 4.

## 4. Contraindications
- [TGA]: Hypersensitivity to the active substances or any excipient listed in the formulation.
- [FDA]: None listed.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Immune-Mediated Adverse Reactions (IMARs):** Can occur in any organ or tissue and may be fatal. They can manifest during treatment or after discontinuation.
    - **Pneumonitis:** Monitor for cough, chest pain, and shortness of breath.
    - **Colitis:** Monitor for diarrhoea and abdominal pain. Consider CMV reactivation if corticosteroid-refractory.
    - **Hepatitis:** Monitor liver enzymes and bilirubin at baseline and periodically.
    - **Endocrinopathies:** Monitor for changes in thyroid function, hyperglycaemia, and adrenal insufficiency.
    - **Nephritis/Renal Dysfunction:** Monitor serum creatinine.
    - **Dermatologic Reactions:** Can include SJS, TEN, or DRESS.
    - **Myocarditis:** High index of suspicion required for cardiac symptoms.
- **Myocarditis-Myositis-Myasthenia Gravis Overlap Syndrome:** Cases have been reported (some fatal); early recognition is essential.
- **Infusion-Related Reactions:** Monitor for chills, rash, flushing, and hypotension.
- **Allogeneic HSCT Complications:** Fatal and serious complications can occur in patients receiving HSCT before or after treatment with PD-1/PD-L1 blockers.
- **Embryo-Fetal Toxicity:** May cause fetal harm. Verify pregnancy status before initiation.

## 6. Adverse Effects
### 6.1 Frequency Data (Based on RELATIVITY-047)
| Frequency | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>20%)** | Musculoskeletal pain (45%), Fatigue (39%), Rash (28%), Pruritus (25%), Diarrhoea (24%) |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Nausea (17%), Headache (18%), Hypothyroidism (17%), Decreased appetite (15%), Cough (15%) |
| **Serious ($\ge$1%)** | Adrenal insufficiency, Anaemia, Colitis, Pneumonia, Myocardial infarction, Back pain, Diarrhoea, Myocarditis, Pneumonitis |

### 6.2 Laboratory Abnormalities ($\ge$20% worsening from baseline)
- Decreased haemoglobin (37%)
- Decreased lymphocytes (32%)
- Increased AST (30%)
- Increased ALT (26%)
- Decreased sodium (24%)

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** No formal studies conducted. As monoclonal antibodies, nivolumab and relatlimab are not metabolised by CYP450 or other drug-metabolising enzymes.
- **Systemic Immunosuppression:** Avoid baseline use of systemic corticosteroids or other immunosuppressants prior to starting **Opdualag** due to potential interference with pharmacodynamic activity. They may, however, be used to manage IMARs after treatment has started.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - [TGA]: Category D. 
    - [FDA]: May cause fetal harm. 
    - **Recommendation:** Advise females of reproductive potential to use effective contraception during treatment and for at least 5 months following the final dose.
- **Breastfeeding:** Due to the potential for serious adverse reactions in the breastfed child, advise patients not to breastfeed during treatment and for at least 5 months after the last dose.
- **Fertility:** Animal studies have not been performed to evaluate effects on fertility.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy established in patients $\ge$12 years weighing $\ge$40 kg. Not established for patients <12 years or those $\ge$12 years weighing <40 kg.
- **Elderly Use:** No overall differences in safety or effectiveness observed in patients $\ge$65 years compared to younger patients; no dose adjustment required.
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment required for mild or moderate impairment (eGFR 30–89 mL/min/1.73 m²). Data for severe impairment is unavailable.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment required for mild impairment. [TGA]: No adjustment for moderate impairment. [FDA]: Data for moderate and severe impairment are unavailable.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** 
    - **Relatlimab:** Binds LAG-3 on T cells, blocking interaction with MHC Class II and reducing LAG-3 pathway-mediated inhibition.
    - **Nivolumab:** Binds PD-1 on T cells, blocking interaction with PD-L1 and PD-L2, reversing PD-1 pathway-mediated inhibition.
    - **Synergy:** Combined inhibition results in increased T-cell activation and anti-tumour activity compared to either agent alone.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Steady State:** Reached by 16 weeks. 
    - **Volume of Distribution (Vss):** 6.65 L (relatlimab) and 6.6 L (nivolumab).
    - **Elimination:** Effective half-life of 26.2 days (relatlimab) and 26.5 days (nivolumab).

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pivotal Trial:** RELATIVITY-047 (NCT03470922/CA224047).
- **Design:** Double-blind, randomised trial (1:1) comparing **Opdualag** to nivolumab monotherapy in previously untreated metastatic or unresectable melanoma.
- **Key Results:**
    - **Primary Endpoint (PFS):** Median 10.1 months for **Opdualag** vs 4.6 months for nivolumab (Hazard Ratio 0.75, p=0.0055).
    - **Overall Response Rate (ORR):** 43% for **Opdualag** vs 33% for nivolumab.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vial Storage:** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C. Do not freeze. Store in original carton to protect from light.
- **Temperature Excursion:** Unopened vials can be stored at room temperature (up to 25°C) in room light for up to 72 hours [TGA].
- **Stability After Preparation:**
    - Room temperature/room light: Use within 8 hours of preparation.
    - Refrigerated (2°C to 8°C): Use within 24 hours of preparation.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Australia [TGA]:** Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine).
- **United States [FDA]:** Rx only.
- **Patient Safety [TGA]:** Requires the use of a Patient Card to be carried by the patient at all times.
