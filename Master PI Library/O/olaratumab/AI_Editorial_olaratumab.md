# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The following information is derived from the official Product Information (PI) provided by the **United States (FDA)** for levocarnitine injection (Baxter Healthcare Corporation). 
- This document provides a harmonised editorial synthesis intended for healthcare professionals. 
- **Note:** This is an editorial overview and does not replace the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. At the time of compilation, only the United States PI was available for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN/USP), a carrier molecule for long-chain fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency (Inborn Errors of Metabolism):** Indicated for the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **Carnitine Deficiency in End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD undergoing maintenance hemodialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
- **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg administered as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion [US].
- **Loading Dose:** Often used in severe metabolic crises, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours [US].
- **Frequency:** Administered every 3 to 4 hours; should not be administered less frequently than every 6 hours [US].
- **Dose Range:** Subsequent daily doses typically 50 mg/kg, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been used [US].

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight) [US].
- **Administration:** Administer as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line after each dialysis session [US].
- **Dose Adjustment:** Guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy if levels are supranormal [US].

### General Instructions
- **Monitoring:** In metabolic disorders, weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma carnitine (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L) is recommended [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients on dialysis. Reactions may occur within minutes of administration. Discontinue immediately if severe reactions occur [US].
- **Monitoring Requirements:** Trough plasma levocarnitine levels should be monitored to guide dosing, particularly in ESRD patients [US].
- **Re-administration:** If re-challenging after a hypersensitivity reaction, monitor the patient closely for recurrent signs [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) when co-administered with levocarnitine. **Recommendation:** Monitor INR levels at treatment initiation and after dose adjustments in patients on warfarin [US].
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category B (equivalent)]. Reproduction studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. No adequate/controlled human studies exist; use only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Specific studies on levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers are unavailable. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk carnitine after exogenous administration. Weigh the risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant against the benefits of maternal supplementation [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Use is established for the same indications as adults (metabolic disorders and ESRD). See Section 3 for weight-based dosing [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Specific clinical studies in the elderly were not detailed in the source document [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Safety/efficacy of oral levocarnitine in renal insufficiency or ESRD has not been evaluated; chronic high doses may lead to accumulation of toxic metabolites (TMA and TMAO). For intravenous use, see Section 3 for ESRD-specific dosing [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into cellular mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Following IV administration, approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours; terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pharmacokinetics in ESRD:** In a 9-week study of ESRD patients (20 mg/kg 3x weekly post-dialysis), mean trough concentrations increased from 21.3 µmol/L at baseline to 190 µmol/L at 8 weeks [US].
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** ESRD patients receiving 10, 20, or 40 mg/kg showed dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine concentrations at 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing. Discard any unused portion of a single-dose vial as it contains no preservative [US].
