# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial has been synthesized from the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document is a harmonised editorial overview intended for use by healthcare professionals. It aims to provide a clear, concise, and clinically accurate reference by consolidating data from available regulatory sources.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific Product Information required for prescribing. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory guidelines and full PI for specific legal and prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP), a carrier molecule belonging to the chemical class of quaternary ammonium compounds.
- **Chemical Name:** (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - **Injection:** 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) single-dose vial [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous (IV).
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Indicated for the acute and chronic treatment of secondary carnitine deficiency resulting from inborn metabolic errors [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD undergoing hemodialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
- **General Dosing:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg.
- **Administration:** Administer as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion.
- **Acute Management:** In cases of severe metabolic crisis, a loading dose may be given, followed by an equivalent dose divided over the subsequent 24 hours. Dosing frequency should be every 3 or 4 hours and never less than every 6 hours.
- **Maximum Dose:** The highest reported dose administered is 300 mg/kg.
- **Monitoring:** It is recommended to obtain baseline plasma carnitine levels. Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma carnitine (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L) is advised [US].

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight.
- **Administration:** Administer as a slow 2 to 3 minute bolus into the venous return line after each dialysis session.
- **Titration:** Adjustments should be guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma carnitine levels. Downward titration (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of treatment if levels are adequate [US].

### General Instructions
- **Visual Inspection:** Parenteral products should be inspected for particulate matter and discoloration before administration.
- **Compatibility:** Stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when diluted in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations between 0.5 mg/mL and 8.0 mg/mL [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Known Hypersensitivity:** While the source states "None known," clinical practice dictates contraindication in patients with a history of severe hypersensitivity to levocarnitine or its components [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions can occur within minutes of IV administration. Discontinue immediately if a reaction occurs [US].
- **Seizures:** Levocarnitine has been associated with seizures in patients with or without a history of seizure activity. In those with pre-existing conditions, an increase in frequency or severity of seizures has been reported [US].
- **Thromboembolic Risk:** Not specifically detailed as a primary warning for this formulation, though monitoring of renal and cardiovascular status is inherent in the ESRD population [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Very Common (>10%):**
    - **General:** Injection site reaction (33–38%), pain (30%), headache (22%), fever (7–12%).
    - **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (18–21%), hypotension (14–19%).
    - **Digestive:** Diarrhea (16–35%), vomiting (15–21%), nausea (8–12%).
    - **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (15–27%), cough increase (9–18%), dyspnea (3–11%).
- **Common (1–10%):**
    - **General:** Abdominal pain, asthenia, chest pain, flu syndrome, infection.
    - **Cardiovascular:** Arrhythmia, palpitations, tachycardia, atrial fibrillation.
    - **Metabolic:** Hypercalcemia, hypervolemia, peripheral edema, weight changes.
    - **Neurological/Psychiatric:** Dizziness, insomnia, paresthesia, depression, anxiety.
- **Uncommon/Rare (<1%):**
    - Body odor (reported with oral and IV use) [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Anaphylaxis, seizures, and laryngeal edema [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** It is recommended to monitor INR levels frequently after initiating levocarnitine or after dose adjustments in patients receiving anticoagulant therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category B (implied)]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at doses up to 3.8 times the human dose (based on surface area) revealed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Specific studies in nursing mothers have not been conducted. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine concentrations in milk after administration. Risks to the infant from excess carnitine should be weighed against the benefits of maternal treatment. Consider discontinuing nursing or the drug [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders is weight-based (50 mg/kg), which applies to the paediatric population [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically detailed; clinical judgment should consider age-related declines in organ function [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Primarily indicated for ESRD. However, chronic administration of high oral doses in patients with severely compromised renal function may lead to accumulation of metabolites (TMA and TMAO), which are normally excreted in urine [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent ATP production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In ESRD patients, it alleviates the metabolic consequences of carnitine loss during dialysis and reduced meat/dairy intake [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (feces).
    - **Elimination:** Following IV bolus (20 mg/kg), approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. Terminal elimination half-life is approximately 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients, 20 mg/kg doses three times weekly after dialysis increased mean pre-dialysis plasma carnitine from 21.3 µmol/L to 190 µmol/L. 
- **Dose-Ranging:** Studies comparing 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg showed dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine levels. While carnitine levels were effectively increased, the impact on specific clinical outcomes in the ESRD population was not definitively determined in these trials [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat.
- **Shelf Life:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as the formulation is preservative-free [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources (refer to local jurisdiction for specific scheduling, e.g., Australian S4) [US].
