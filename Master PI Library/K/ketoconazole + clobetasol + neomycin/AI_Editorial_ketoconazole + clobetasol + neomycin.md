# Editorial Product Information: ketoconazole + clobetasol + neomycin

## 1. Product Identification
- Active ingredient (INN/generic name): **ketoconazole + clobetasol + neomycin**
  - Strengths stated in available source:
    - Ketoconazole 10 mg per 1 g
    - Clobetasol propionate 0.25 mg per 1 g
    - Neomycin sulfate 5000 IU per 1 g
- Chemical class: Not specified in available sources
- ATC code: Not specified in available sources
- Route(s) of administration: Topical (external use)

## 2. Regulatory History
- Marketing start date listed in source PI: 06/01/2024
- Marketing category listed in source PI: unapproved drug (as stated in source PI)
- Date of last revision of source PI: Revised: 2/2025
- Regulatory status statement in source material: [US] the source PI notes that the product "has not been found by US authorities to be safe and effective and the labeling has not been approved by US authorities."  
- Withdrawal, suspension, or restriction history: Not specified in available sources

## 3. Approved Indications
- Indications stated in the source PI:
  - Treatment of superficial fungal infections of the skin including:
    - tinea corporis
    - tinea cruris
    - tinea pedis
    - tinea manus
    - tinea versicolor
  - Management of various dermatitis, eczema and pruritus
- Jurisdictional differences in indications: Not specified in available sources

## 4. Dosage and Administration
### General dosing (adult)
- Apply externally to affected area twice daily.

### Dosing by indication (as suggested in source PI)
- Tinea corporis and tinea cruris: suggested duration 2 weeks
- Tinea pedis and tinea manus: suggested duration 4 weeks

### Administration instructions
- For topical external use only.
- Avoid contact with eyes.
- Application to face or to large surface areas, or prolonged continuous use, should be under a physician's supervision.

### Titration schedules
- Not specified in available sources

### Missed dose instructions
- Not specified in available sources

## 5. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to ketoconazole, clobetasol, neomycin, or any excipient listed in the product (as stated in source PI).
- Not for ophthalmic use (do not apply in or near the eye).
- Pregnancy/infancy: the source PI states to "take special precautions" when used in pregnancy and infancy but does not list pregnancy or infancy as absolute contraindications. (See Section 9.)
- Any other contraindications: Not specified in available sources

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed warnings
- None specified in available sources

### General warnings and precautions
- Use only externally.
- Avoid contact with eyes.
- Application to the face, application to large areas, or prolonged use should be performed under medical supervision.
- Special precautions when used in pregnancy and infancy (source PI advises caution; see Section 9).
- Keep out of reach of children.

### Monitoring requirements
- Not specified in available sources

### Specific clinical precautions
- Monitor for local skin irritation or burning sensation; if severe hypersensitivity reactions occur, discontinue and treat appropriately.
- Due to presence of a topical corticosteroid (clobetasol propionate), exercise caution with prolonged use, use on thin skin areas (including face), or use under occlusion; specific steroid-related systemic effects (e.g., HPA axis suppression) are not detailed in the available sources.
- Due to neomycin component, be aware of potential for contact sensitisation or allergic contact dermatitis; specific incidence rates are not provided in the available sources.

## 11. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of action
- Ketoconazole: described in source PI as a broad‑spectrum antifungal agent that alters fungal cell membrane permeability.
- Clobetasol propionate: described as a corticosteroid with potent anti‑inflammatory and anti‑allergic activity.
- Neomycin sulfate: described as an antibacterial agent effective against Gram‑positive cocci and Gram‑negative bacilli.
