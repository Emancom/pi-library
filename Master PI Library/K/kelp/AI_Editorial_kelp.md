# Editorial Product Information: Zinc Oxide (Kinfield Daily Dew Sea Kelp Moisturizer Sunscreen)

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient**: Zinc Oxide (20% w/w).
- **Chemical Class**: Inorganic mineral / Physical UV filter.
- **Brand Name(s)**: Kinfield Daily Dew Sea Kelp Moisturizer Sunscreen Broad Spectrum SPF 35 [US].
- **ATC Code**: D02AB (Zinc products) [Note: Not explicitly listed in source; derived from class].
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s)**: Topical lotion containing 20g of Zinc Oxide per 100g (20%) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration**: Topical.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor**: Kinfield, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Sunburn Prevention**: Indicated to help prevent sunburn [US].
- **Risk Reduction**: If used as directed with other sun protection measures, it decreases the risk of skin cancer and early skin aging caused by the sun [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Application**: Apply generously to the skin 15 minutes before sun exposure [US].
- **Reapplication Intervals**: 
    - At least every 2 hours [US].
    - After 40 minutes of swimming or sweating [US].
    - Immediately after towel drying [US].

### Administration Instructions
- For external use only.
- Should be used as part of a comprehensive Sun Protection Measure strategy, including limiting time in the sun (especially 10 a.m. – 2 p.m.) and wearing protective clothing (long-sleeved shirts, pants, hats, and sunglasses) [US].

### Missed Dose
- Not applicable for this topical prophylactic product.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Damaged Skin**: Do not use on damaged or broken skin [US].
- **Hypersensitivity**: While not explicitly listed as a contraindication section, the label advises stopping use if a rash occurs, implying sensitivity [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Ocular Exposure**: Keep out of eyes during application. If contact occurs, rinse thoroughly with water to remove [US].
- **Dermatologic Effects**: Discontinue use and consult a physician if a rash develops and persists [US].
- **Ingestion Hazard**: Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, professional medical assistance or contact with a Poison Control Center is required immediately [US].

- **Mechanism of Action**: Zinc Oxide is a physical (mineral) sunscreen that sits on top of the skin to reflect and scatter UV radiation (UVA and UVB) [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics**: Not specified in available sources; however, inorganic mineral filters like zinc oxide typically exhibit minimal systemic absorption when applied to intact skin.
