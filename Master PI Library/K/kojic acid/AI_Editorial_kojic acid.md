# Editorial Product Information: Kojic Acid Skin Lightening Soap (Glutathione/Kojic Acid Complex)

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial synthesis is derived from official product documentation provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising available regulatory data into a clinical reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing or dispensing in any specific jurisdiction. At the time of writing, only United States regulatory data was available for this specific formulation.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** 
    - Tocopherol (0.1 g / 100 g) [US]
    - Glutathione (0.1 g / 100 g) [US]
    - Alpha-Arbutin (0.1 g / 100 g) [US]
    - Ascorbic Acid (0.1 g / 100 g) [US]
- **Chemical Class:** Topical antioxidants and skin-lightening agents.
- **Brand Name:** Kojic Acid Skin Lightening Soap [US]
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Topical soap bar containing 100 g of product. [US]
- **Route of Administration:** Topical.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** HANCHOYEANBIOTEC Co., Ltd. [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
- Indicated for topical skin cleansing and maintenance of skin moisture and smoothness. [US]
- Approved for skin whitening and brightening of the complexion. [US]
- Indicated to reduce the formation of dark spots for a more translucent skin appearance. [US]

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- Recommended for daily cleaning and care. [US]

### Administration Instructions
- Users should wash their hands prior to use to ensure optimal product results. [US]
- **Application:** Rub the soap bar between wet palms until a rich foam is produced. Apply the foam to the face and body, massaging gently into the skin. 
- **Removal:** Rinse thoroughly with clean water. [US]

### Missed Dose Instructions
- Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to any of the active or inactive ingredients (e.g., kojic acid, glutathione, alpha-arbutin, niacinamide, or tocopherol). [US]

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **General Precautions:** For external use only. Users should be advised not to swallow the product. [US]
- **Skin Irritation:** Use should be discontinued immediately if signs of a rash or skin irritation occur. [US]
- **Hygiene:** Hand hygiene prior to application is recommended to maintain product efficacy. [US]
- **Accidental Ingestion:** Keep out of reach of children. [US]

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Frequency not specified:**
    - Skin irritation. [US]
    - Cutaneous rash. [US]

- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** 
    - **Glutathione, Alpha-Arbutin, and Ascorbic Acid:** Function as skin-lightening agents, likely through the inhibition of tyrosinase or antioxidant pathways that affect melanogenesis. [US]
    - **Kojic Acid:** (Listed as an inactive ingredient in this formulation) Traditionally used as a tyrosinase inhibitor to prevent melanin production. [US]
    - **Tocopherol:** Acts as an antioxidant to protect skin cells. [US]
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** The formula is designed to be mild and non-irritating for daily cleaning while providing skin-lightening effects. [US]
