# Editorial Product Information: Ketotifen

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the following official Product Information (PI) source:
    - **Australian (TGA)**: Product Information — Zaditen (Ketotifen) Eye Drops Solution.
- **Purpose**: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical and regulatory data into a single reference.
- **Disclaimer**: This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific PI required for prescribing or dispensing in a particular region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient**: Ketotifen (present as ketotifen fumarate).
- **Chemical Class**: Benzocyclohepta-thiophene derivative; antihistamine and mast cell stabiliser.
- **Brand Names**: **Zaditen** [Australian].
- **ATC Code**: Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths**: 
    - **Ophthalmic Solution**: 250 µg/mL ketotifen (as 345 µg/mL ketotifen fumarate) [Australian].
- **Routes of Administration**: Ocular use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor**: 
    - Care Pharmaceuticals Pty Limited [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Seasonal Allergic Conjunctivitis**: Symptomatic short-term treatment in adults and children aged 3 years and older [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adults, Elderly, and Children $\ge$ 3 years)**:
    - One drop into the conjunctival sac of the affected eye(s) twice daily [Australian].
- **Administration Instructions**:
    - **Contamination Prevention**: Do not touch the dropper tip to the eye or any other surface.
    - **Multidose Bottles**: Contain benzalkonium chloride. Discard 4 weeks after opening.
    - **Single-Dose Units**: Use immediately after opening and discard any residue. Once a blister is opened, unused units must be discarded after 4 weeks (or 3 months if maintained in the outer carton) [Australian].
    - **Sequential Eye Medications**: If using more than one topical ophthalmic drug, allow an interval of at least 5 minutes between applications [Australian].

## 4. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to **ketotifen** or any of the excipients in the formulation [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Contact Lens Wear**: 
    - The multidose formulation contains benzalkonium chloride, which may deposit in and discolour soft contact lenses. 
    - Lenses should be removed prior to instillation and not reinserted for at least 15 minutes [Australian].
- **Effects on Ability to Drive/Use Machines**: 
    - Patients experiencing blurred vision or somnolence must refrain from driving or operating machinery [Australian].
- **Ocular Use Only**: Not for injection or oral ingestion [Australian].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions are ranked by frequency according to the following convention: Very common ($\ge$10%); Common ($\ge$1% to <10%); Uncommon ($\ge$0.1% to <1%).

| System Organ Class | Frequency | Adverse Event |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Eye Disorders** | Common | Punctate keratitis, corneal erosion, eye irritation, eye pain |
| | Uncommon | Blurred vision (during instillation), dry eye, eyelid disorder, conjunctivitis, photophobia, conjunctival haemorrhage |
| **Immune System** | Uncommon | Hypersensitivity, allergic reactions |
| **Nervous System** | Uncommon | Headache, somnolence |
| **Gastrointestinal** | Uncommon | Dry mouth |
| **Skin/Subcutaneous** | Uncommon | Rash, eczema, urticaria |

- **Post-Marketing Experience (Frequency Not Known)**:
    - **Ocular**: Contact dermatitis, eye swelling, eyelid pruritus, and oedema [Australian].
    - **Systemic**: Facial swelling/oedema, exacerbation of pre-existing allergic conditions (e.g., asthma, eczema), and dizziness [Australian].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Topical Ocular Interactions**: If used concomitantly with other eye medications, a 5-minute interval between drugs is required to prevent washout [Australian].
- **Systemic Interactions**: Not specified in available sources for the ophthalmic formulation.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy**: 
    - **Australian Category B1**: Oral animal studies (rats/rabbits) showed no embryofoetal malformations at high doses. Increased postnatal mortality was seen at maternally toxic oral doses.
    - **Clinical Recommendation**: No adequate data in pregnant women. Caution should be exercised; benefits to the mother should outweigh potential risks to the fetus [Australian].
- **Breastfeeding**: 
    - Ketotifen is identified in rat milk after oral administration. It is unknown if topical ocular use results in detectable levels in human milk. Use with caution in breastfeeding women [Australian].
- **Fertility**: 
    - Animal data: A trend for decreased fertility was noted in male rats at oral doses $\ge$2 mg/kg/day. No effect was seen in female rats up to 50 mg/kg/day. Human data are unavailable [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use**: 
    - Safety and efficacy established for children aged 3 years and older. 
    - Safety and effectiveness in children under 3 years of age have not been established [Australian].
- **Elderly Use**: 
    - No specific data available; however, dosing is the same as for younger adults [Australian].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment**: 
    - Not specified in available sources for the ophthalmic route.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action**: **Ketotifen** is a mast cell stabiliser and a non-specific anti-inflammatory agent. It inhibits the chemotaxis, activation, and degranulation of eosinophils. 
- **Pharmacodynamics**: Phosphodiesterase inhibition leading to increased cAMP levels may contribute to its cell-stabilising effects [Australian].
- **Pharmacokinetics**:
    - **Absorption**: In a 14-day study of healthy volunteers using the eye drops, plasma levels were predominantly below the limit of quantitation (20 pg/mL).
    - **Metabolism/Elimination**: Following oral administration, elimination is biphasic (initial $t_{1/2}$ 3–5 hours; terminal $t_{1/2}$ 21 hours). Approximately 60–70% is excreted in urine as metabolites, primarily the inactive ketotifen-N-glucuronide [Australian].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Efficacy**: Evaluated in 6 conjunctival allergen challenge (CAC) studies ($n=634$) and 2 four-week environmental studies ($n=1023$).
- **Outcomes**: In CAC studies, **ketotifen** was significantly more effective than placebo in preventing ocular itching. The onset of action was rapid (within minutes), and the duration of action for a single dose lasted 8 to 12 hours [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature**: Store below 25°C.
- **Shelf Life**: Refer to the expiry date on packaging.
- **Special Handling**: Multidose bottles should be discarded 4 weeks after first opening [Australian].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule**: 
    - **Schedule 2 (Pharmacy Medicine)** [Australian].
