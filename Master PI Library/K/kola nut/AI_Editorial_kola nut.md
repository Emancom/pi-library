# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial: US (FDA).
- Purpose: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising regulatory data into a single clinical reference.
- Disclaimer: This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP).
- **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule/amino acid derivative; (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection (Generic/Baxter) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Sterile injection: 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** For the acute and chronic management of patients with inborn errors of metabolism leading to secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    - Recommended dose is 50 mg/kg.
    - Administration: Slow IV bolus (2 to 3 minutes) or via infusion.
    - Acute Crisis: A loading dose is often followed by equivalent doses administered every 3 or 4 hours (minimum every 6 hours) over the first 24 hours.
    - Maintenance: Subsequent daily doses are generally 50 mg/kg or as clinically required. Doses up to 300 mg/kg have been administered.
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - Recommended starting dose: 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight).
    - Administration: Slow IV bolus (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
    - Adjustment: Trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levels should guide adjustments. Downward titration (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may occur as early as the third or fourth week of therapy.

### Monitoring
- **Metabolic:** Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L).
- **ESRD:** Monitor trough plasma levels (normal range 40–50 µmol/L) [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Inspect visually for particulate matter or discoloration.
- Compatible and stable for 24 hours at room temperature in Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer's (concentration range 0.5 mg/mL to 8.0 mg/mL) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions can occur within minutes of administration. Discontinue immediately if severe reactions occur [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with a known history, an increase in seizure frequency or severity may occur [US].
- **Renal Impairment (Oral Form Precaution):** While this document focuses on the IV form, note that chronic high doses of *oral* levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalised Ratio (INR) when used concurrently with levocarnitine. 
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels after initiating levocarnitine treatment or following dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not assigned]. Animal reproductive studies (rats/rabbits) at 3.8 times the human dose (BSA-based) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Studies in dairy cows show increased milk carnitine concentrations after exogenous administration. It is not specifically studied in humans. Weigh the benefits of maternal supplementation against potential risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant. Consider discontinuing nursing or the drug based on clinical priority [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy are established for the management of inborn errors of metabolism. Dosing is weight-based (50 mg/kg) [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in available sources [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** IV levocarnitine is specifically indicated for carnitine deficiency in ESRD patients on dialysis. Dose adjustments are required based on pre-dialysis trough concentrations [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane for β-oxidation and subsequent energy production.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In patients with organic acidurias, levocarnitine promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters, which are rapidly cleared [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin. Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (feces).
    - **Elimination:** Mean apparent terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours. Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients (20 mg/kg three times weekly), mean pre-dialysis trough concentrations increased from 21.3 ± 7.7 µmol/L at baseline to 190 ± 55 µmol/L after 8 weeks. While carnitine levels increased, the effect on specific clinical outcomes in this population has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat.
- **Shelf Life:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as the formulation is preservative-free [US].
