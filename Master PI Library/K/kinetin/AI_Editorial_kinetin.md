# Editorial Product Information: Octocrylene, Octisalate, Homosalate, and Avobenzone (Sea Clearly Hydrating Facial SPF 35)

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) and labeling data from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, consolidating regulatory data into a clinically relevant format.
- This document is an editorial synthesis and is not intended to replace the official PI or "Drug Facts" label required for prescribing or dispensing in any specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** Octocrylene, Octisalate, Homosalate, and Avobenzone.
- **Chemical Class:** Organic UV filters (Sunscreen).
- **Brand Name(s):** Sea Clearly Hydrating Facial SPF 35 Kinetin (Sonrei) [US].
- **ATC Code:** D02BA (Sunscreen for topical use).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Topical Cream/Gel containing:
    - Octocrylene 10% [US]
    - Octisalate 5% [US]
    - Homosalate 5% [US]
    - Avobenzone 2.5% [US]
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Allure Labs [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Prevention of Sunburn:** Indicated to help prevent sunburn [US].
- **Risk Reduction of Photodamage:** When used as directed with other sun protection measures, this product decreases the risk of skin cancer and early skin aging caused by solar radiation [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Administration:** Apply generously and evenly to the skin 15 minutes before sun exposure [US].
- **Reapplication Schedule:**
    - After 40 minutes of swimming or sweating [US].
    - Immediately following towel drying [US].
    - At minimum, every 2 hours [US].
- **Sun Protection Measures:** To maximize efficacy, use should be combined with limiting time in the sun (especially between 10 a.m. and 2 p.m.) and wearing protective clothing (long-sleeved shirts, pants, hats, and sunglasses) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Damaged Skin:** Do not use on broken or damaged skin [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Hypersensitivity to any active or inactive ingredient [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **General Warnings:** For external use only [US].
- **Ocular Exposure:** Keep out of eyes. If contact occurs, rinse thoroughly with water to remove [US].
- **Dermatologic Precautions:** If a skin rash occurs, discontinue use and consult a physician [US].
- **Accidental Ingestion:** Keep out of reach of children. In case of ingestion, seek medical assistance or contact a Poison Control Center immediately [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Dermatologic:** Skin rash and irritation have been noted as potential adverse reactions requiring discontinuation of the product [US].
- **Frequency Data:** Specific frequency data for adverse effects are not provided in the available source document.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Management Notes:** No clinically significant drug-drug interactions were reported in the provided labeling [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Not specified in available sources. No specific US FDA pregnancy category is assigned in the provided OTC documentation.
- **Breastfeeding:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Fertility:** Not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** 
    - For children under 6 months of age, a physician must be consulted before use [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources; however, systemic absorption of topically applied sunscreens is generally low.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** This product utilizes a combination of chemical filters (Avobenzone, Octocrylene, Octisalate, and Homosalate) that absorb UVA and UVB radiation, converting it into heat and preventing it from damaging the underlying skin cells.
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Provides a Sun Protection Factor (SPF) of 35 and is Broad Spectrum, protecting against both UVA and UVB rays [US].
- **Key Inactive Ingredients:** Contains **Kinetin**, Squalane, Tocopherol (Vitamin E), and various botanical oils (Camellia, Apricot, Safflower) for hydration and antioxidant support [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Summary:** Specific pivotal trial data were not provided in the source monograph; however, the product is listed under the US FDA OTC Monograph Drug (M020) for Sunscreen Products for Human Use [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage:** Protect the product from excessive heat and direct sunlight [US].
- **Packaging:** Provided in a 50g bottle with a pump [US].
