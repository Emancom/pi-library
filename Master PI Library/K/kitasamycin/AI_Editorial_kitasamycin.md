# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis was developed using official Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical data and regulatory guidance into a single, concise reference. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official, legally approved Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority's approved PI for full prescribing details.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); a naturally occurring carrier molecule required for mammalian energy metabolism.
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter).
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Sterile injection solution: 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism leading to secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    - Recommended dose is 50 mg/kg administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion [US].
    - **Loading Dose:** In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose may be given, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours.
    - **Frequency:** Administer every 3 or 4 hours (q3h or q4h); frequency should never be less than every 6 hours (q6h).
    - **Maximum Dose:** Doses up to 300 mg/kg have been administered [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
    - **Administration:** Administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
    - **Adjustment:** Dose reductions (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations.

### Monitoring
- **Plasma Concentrations:** It is recommended to obtain plasma carnitine levels prior to starting therapy. Weekly and monthly monitoring is advised. 
- **Target Range:** Free carnitine concentrations should ideally be maintained between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients on dialysis. Reactions can occur within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and do not re-administer without a strict risk-benefit assessment and close monitoring [US].
- **Seizures:** Post-marketing reports indicate that seizures may occur in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In patients with a known seizure history, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **General Precautions:** The safety and efficacy of oral levocarnitine have not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in these patients may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are often confounded by the underlying pathology of metabolic disorders or ESRD.

| System Organ Class | Adverse Effect | Frequency (in Dialysis Trials) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Injection site reaction, pain, headache, fever, flu syndrome | Common (≥5%) |
| **Gastrointestinal** | Nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain, diarrhea, gastritis | Common (≥5%) |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension, hypotension, tachycardia, arrhythmia | Common (≥5%) |
| **Metabolic** | Hypercalcemia, hypervolemia, weight changes | Common (≥5%) |
| **Neurological** | Dizziness, paresthesia, seizures (rate unknown) | Common (≥5%) |
| **Other** | Body odor | Uncommon/Rare |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Anaphylaxis and other severe hypersensitivity reactions [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have been observed when levocarnitine is used concomitantly with warfarin. 
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after any dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Category:** Not specified by letter, but reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm [US].
    - **Recommendation:** Should be used during pregnancy only if clearly needed.
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. In dairy cows, carnitine concentrations in milk increase following administration.
    - **Recommendation:** Weigh the risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant against the benefits to the mother. Discontinuation of nursing or the drug may be considered [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders is weight-based (50 mg/kg), applicable to paediatric populations [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically detailed; however, dosing should be cautious, reflecting the greater frequency of decreased hepatic, renal, or cardiac function.
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    - **ESRD:** Specifically indicated for carnitine deficiency in dialysis. 
    - **General Renal Insufficiency:** Safety of high-dose *oral* levocarnitine is not established due to metabolite accumulation (TMA/TMAO) [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids in patients with metabolic defects by forming acylcarnitine esters.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces).
    - **Elimination:** Following IV bolus (20 mg/kg), the mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours and the terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours. Approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** Clinical data demonstrate that IV administration of 20 mg/kg levocarnitine three times weekly after dialysis significantly increases plasma carnitine levels. Trough concentrations at 8 weeks reached a mean of 190 ± 55 µmol/L compared to a baseline of 21.3 ± 7.7 µmol/L. 
- **Note:** While levocarnitine increases carnitine concentrations in ESRD patients, the effect on specific clinical outcomes in this population has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C.
- **Conditions:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat.
- **Handling:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as the formulation contains no preservatives [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable for up to 24 hours in PVC bags at room temperature when mixed with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Rx-only (Prescription only).
