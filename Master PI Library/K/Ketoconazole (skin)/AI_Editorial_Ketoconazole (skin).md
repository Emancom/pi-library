# Editorial Product Information: Ketoconazole, Clobetasol Propionate, and Neomycin Sulfate (VIXA SKINEAL)

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial is based on product labeling for **VIXA SKINEAL** as presented in documentation submitted to the **US (FDA)**.
- **IMPORTANT REGULATORY NOTE:** This specific formulation and labeling have not been found by the **US (FDA)** to be safe and effective; the product is currently classified as an "unapproved drug." 
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised clinical overview for healthcare professionals based on provided manufacturer data. It is not a substitute for official regulatory-approved Product Information required for prescribing in specific jurisdictions.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** 
    - Ketoconazole (Antifungal)
    - Clobetasol Propionate (Corticosteroid)
    - Neomycin Sulfate (Aminoglycoside antibiotic)
- **Brand Name:** VIXA SKINEAL [US]
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources (Likely D01AC60 or D07CC01 derivative)
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** 
    - Compound cream [US]
    - Each gram contains: Ketoconazole 10 mg, Clobetasol Propionate 0.25 mg, and Neomycin Sulfate 5000 IU [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Topical
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Yunnan Yuyao Bio-pharmaceutical Co., Ltd. (P.R. China) [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
Based on provided documentation, this compound cream is indicated for the following [US-marketed/unapproved]:
- **Fungal Skin Infections:** Treatment of tinea manus, tinea pedis, tinea corporis, tinea cruris, and tinea versicolor.
- **Inflammatory Skin Conditions:** Treatment of various types of dermatitis, eczema, and associated pruritus (itching).
- **Bacterial Involvement:** Indicated for use where secondary bacterial infection by Gram-positive cocci or Gram-negative bacilli is present or suspected.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** Apply a thin layer to the affected area twice daily [US].
- **Dosing by Indication:**
    - **Tinea corporis and tinea cruris:** Suggested treatment duration of 2 weeks [US].
    - **Tinea pedis and tinea manus:** Suggested treatment duration of 4 weeks [US].
- **Administration Instructions:** For external topical use only. Avoid contact with eyes and mucous membranes.
- **Missed Dose Instructions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to ketoconazole, clobetasol propionate, neomycin sulfate, or any excipient in the formulation [US].
- **Ophthalmic Use:** VIXA SKINEAL is strictly contraindicated for ophthalmic application [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None specified in provided source.
- **General Warnings:** 
    - **FDA Safety Disclaimer:** This product has not been evaluated by the FDA for safety or efficacy [US].
    - **External Use Only:** Strictly for topical application. Avoid contact with eyes [US].
- **Specific Clinical Precautions:**
    - **Supervised Use:** Application to the face or to large surface areas for extended periods must be performed under strict medical supervision [US].
    - **Infancy:** Use with caution in the infant population [US].
- **Monitoring Requirements:** Not specified in available sources; however, clinicians should monitor for signs of systemic corticosteroid absorption if used over large areas or under occlusion.

## 6. Adverse Effects
The source indicates that topical application is generally well-tolerated, with few side effects reported.
- **Uncommon (0.1–1%):** 
    - Local irritation [US]
    - Burning sensation at the site of application [US]
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Not explicitly listed in the source, though the presence of neomycin carries a theoretical risk of contact dermatitis/sensitization, and clobetasol carries a risk of skin atrophy with prolonged use.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:**
    - **Ketoconazole:** A broad-spectrum antifungal that alters fungal cell membrane permeability by inhibiting ergosterol synthesis [US].
    - **Clobetasol Propionate:** A highly potent corticosteroid providing anti-inflammatory and anti-allergic effects [US].
    - **Neomycin Sulfate:** An antibiotic effective against various Gram-positive and Gram-negative bacteria [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - Absorption, distribution, metabolism, and elimination data are not specified for this topical combination in the provided source.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** The combination provides simultaneous antifungal, antibacterial, anti-inflammatory, and antipruritic activity [US].
