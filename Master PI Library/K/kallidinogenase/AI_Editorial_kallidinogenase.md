# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical data and regulatory guidance.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific Product Information required for prescribing.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN/USP).
- **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule/amino acid derivative ((R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium).
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection (Generic).
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Clear, colorless sterile solution for injection, 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only [US].
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Indicated for the acute and chronic management of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that lead to secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    - The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg administered as a slow (2 to 3 minute) bolus injection or by infusion [US].
    - In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose may be given, followed by equivalent doses every 3 to 4 hours (and no less than every 6 hours) over the subsequent 24 hours [US].
    - Subsequent daily maintenance doses typically range around 50 mg/kg, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilised [US].
- **ESRD on Hemodialysis:**
    - Recommended starting dose: 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight) [US].
    - Administration: Slow 2 to 3 minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products should be visually inspected for particulate matter and discoloration prior to use [US].
- For ESRD patients, therapy initiation may be prompted by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine levels below the normal range of 40 to 50 µmol/L [US].

### Titration and Monitoring
- In ESRD patients, dose adjustments should be guided by trough plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be implemented as early as the third or fourth week of treatment [US].
- For metabolic patients, weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L) is recommended [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known at the time of publication [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### General Warnings
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm have occurred, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions can occur within minutes of administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue the drug and initiate medical treatment. Re-administration should only be considered after a risk-benefit assessment [US].

### Specific Clinical Precautions
- **Seizures:** Cases of seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In those with established seizure activity, an increase in the frequency or severity of seizures has been observed [US].
- **Oral Formulation Cautions:** Oral levocarnitine should not be used in patients with severely compromised renal function or ESRD on dialysis due to the potential accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common Adverse Effects (≥5% in ESRD clinical trials)
Adverse events observed in chronic hemodialysis patients, regardless of causality [US]:
- **General:** Injection site reaction (27–38%), pain (21–35%), flu syndrome (15–29%), headache (3–37%), chest pain (6–15%), fever (5–12%), abdominal pain (5–21%).
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (18–21%), hypotension (3–19%), tachycardia (5–9%).
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea (9–35%), vomiting (9–21%), nausea (5–12%).
- **Metabolic/Nutritional:** Hypercalcemia (6–15%), hypervolemia (3–12%).
- **Nervous:** Dizziness (10–18%), paresthesia (3–12%).
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (15–27%), cough increase (10–18%).

### Serious and Postmarketing Adverse Effects
- **Hypersensitivity:** Anaphylaxis [US].
- **Neurological:** Seizures [US].
- **Gastrointestinal:** Gastritis, body odor, and transient nausea/vomiting have been noted [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
### Pharmacodynamic Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin. It is recommended to monitor INR levels upon initiation or dose adjustment of levocarnitine in patients receiving warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US]: Classified as a drug to be used during pregnancy only if clearly needed. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. There are no adequate/well-controlled studies in pregnant women.
- **Breastfeeding:** [US]: Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine milk concentrations following administration. Risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant must be weighed against maternal benefit; consideration may be given to discontinuing nursing or the drug.
- **Fertility:** No impairment of fertility was noted in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing is based on mg/kg for metabolic disorders; safety and efficacy have been established in the paediatric population for indicated uses [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Oral levocarnitine is cautioned in severe renal impairment. Parenteral levocarnitine is indicated for use in ESRD patients on hemodialysis [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane for beta-oxidation and energy production [US].

### Pharmacokinetics
- **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
- **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours (uncorrected for endogenous levels) [US].
- **Half-life:** Mean distribution half-life of 0.585 hours; mean terminal elimination half-life of 17.4 hours [US].
- **Clearance:** Mean total body clearance of 4.00 L/h [US].

### Pharmacodynamics
In metabolic disorders, levocarnitine promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters, which are rapidly cleared [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients (20 mg/kg post-dialysis) showed pre-dialysis trough concentrations increased from 21.3 µmol/L (baseline) to 190 µmol/L after 8 weeks. 
- **Dose-Ranging Trials:** Studies using 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg 3 times weekly showed dose-dependent increases in trough carnitine levels. While these doses successfully increase carnitine concentrations, the impact on specific clinical outcomes in the ESRD population has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Conditions:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat [US].
- **Stability:** Compatible with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s (0.5 mg/mL to 8.0 mg/mL) for up to 24 hours in PVC bags at room temperature [US].
- **Shelf Life:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as it contains no preservative [US].

- **Legal Classification:** [US]: Rx-only (Prescription only).
- **Poison Schedule:** Not applicable in the US jurisdiction.
