# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine Injection

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical data and regulatory requirements into a single reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not intended as a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific Product Information required for prescribing or dispensing in any specific region. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN). It is a carrier molecule for long-chain fatty acid transport and is chemically designated as (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Brand Names:** Generic formulations (Sponsor: Baxter Healthcare Corporation).
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - Sterile injection: 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** For the acute and chronic management of patients presenting with an inborn error of metabolism that leads to secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Route:** Administered intravenously.
- **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg. It may be administered as a slow bolus injection (2–3 minutes) or by infusion.
- **ESRD on Hemodialysis:** The recommended starting dose is 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight). It should be administered as a slow bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].

### Dosing by Indication
- **Acute Metabolic Crisis:** Treatment often begins with a loading dose, followed by an equivalent dose administered over the subsequent 24 hours. Dosing frequency should be every 3 to 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours [US].
- **Maintenance (Metabolic):** Subsequent daily doses typically range around 50 mg/kg, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilised [US].

### Administration Instructions
- **Visual Inspection:** Parenteral products should be inspected for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable for up to 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when diluted in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations between 0.5 mg/mL and 8.0 mg/mL [US].

### Titration and Monitoring
- **Monitoring:** Plasma carnitine levels should be obtained prior to therapy. Weekly and monthly monitoring is recommended, including blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma free carnitine concentrations (target range: 35–60 µmol/L) [US].
- **ESRD Adjustments:** Downward dose adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levels [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known at the time of publication [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in patients with ESRD on dialysis. Reactions can occur within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, the drug must be discontinued immediately [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Both oral and intravenous levocarnitine have been associated with seizures in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with a known seizure disorder, an increase in frequency or severity has been reported [US].
- **Renal Impairment (Oral Formulations):** While this PI focuses on the injection, it is noted that safety for oral levocarnitine has not been evaluated in renal insufficiency. High-dose oral therapy in ESRD may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effect frequency is difficult to estimate due to the confounding nature of underlying pathologies (e.g., ESRD). The following have been reported in clinical trials and post-marketing:

- **Common (>5% in ESRD trials):**
    - **General:** Abdominal pain, asthenia, fever, flu syndrome, headache, infection, injection site reaction, pain.
    - **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension, hypotension, tachycardia.
    - **Digestive:** Diarrhea, nausea, vomiting.
    - **Metabolic:** Hypercalcemia, hypervolemia.
    - **Nervous:** Dizziness, insomnia, paresthesia.
    - **Respiratory:** Cough, pharyngitis, rhinitis.
- **Uncommon/Rare/Post-marketing:**
    - **Neurologic:** Seizures [US].
    - **Gastrointestinal:** Body odor, gastritis [US].
    - **Senses:** Taste perversion [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalised Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** It is recommended to monitor INR levels after initiating levocarnitine or after dose adjustments in patients receiving anticoagulant therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Not formally categorised by letter in the current source, but reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Specific studies in humans are unavailable. Studies in dairy cows indicate levocarnitine concentrations in milk increase following administration. Clinicians should weigh the benefits of maternal treatment against the potential risk of excess carnitine intake in the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility was observed in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders in children is calculated by weight (50 mg/kg), similar to adults [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in available sources [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Evaluated specifically for ESRD patients on dialysis (see Section 3). The injection is preferred over oral forms in this population to avoid metabolite accumulation [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into cellular mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In ESRD, it alleviates metabolic abnormalities resulting from the accumulation of toxic organic acids and improves carnitine deficiency caused by dialysis losses and reduced renal synthesis.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not protein-bound. 
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites are trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces).
    - **Elimination:** Following IV bolus (20 mg/kg), the mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours and the terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours. Approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Trough Levels:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients receiving 20 mg/kg IV three times per week showed that trough levels increased from a baseline of 21.3 ± 7.7 µmol/L to 190 ± 55 µmol/L after 8 weeks.
- **Dose-Ranging Data:** Studies using 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg showed dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks.
- **Note:** While levocarnitine increases carnitine concentrations in ESRD, the specific impact on clinical outcomes (e.g., muscle weakness, malaise) has not been definitively determined in this population [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Cautions:** Protect from freezing and avoid excessive heat.
- **Shelf Life:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as the formulation contains no preservatives [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources for other jurisdictions.
