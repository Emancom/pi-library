# Editorial Product Information: Candesartan

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration). 
- **Purpose:** This document is a harmonised editorial overview of **candesartan cilexetil**, synthesised for use by healthcare professionals. It integrates clinical data, regulatory requirements, and prescribing considerations.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and serves as a clinical reference. It is not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory documents for definitive legal and prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Candesartan cilexetil
- **Chemical Class:** Angiotensin II receptor antagonist (selective AT1 subtype)
- **Brand Name(s):** **ADESAN** [Australian]
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - **Oral Tablet:** 4 mg, 8 mg, 16 mg, and 32 mg [Australian].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Viatris (Alphapharm Pty Ltd) [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Hypertension:** Treatment of essential hypertension [Australian].
- **Heart Failure:** Treatment of patients with heart failure and impaired left ventricular systolic function (LVEF ≤ 40%). This is indicated as add-on therapy to ACE inhibitors or as an alternative when ACE inhibitors are not tolerated [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Hypertension
- **Adult Dosing:** The recommended maintenance dose is 8 mg or 16 mg once daily. The maximal antihypertensive effect is typically achieved within 4 weeks.
- **Titration:** If further blood pressure reduction is required, the dose may be increased to 32 mg once daily.
- **Combination Therapy:** Addition of a thiazide-type diuretic is recommended if monotherapy is insufficient.

### Heart Failure
- **Initial Dosing:** The recommended starting dose is 4 mg once daily.
- **Titration:** The dose should be doubled at intervals of at least 2 weeks, targeting a maximum dose of 32 mg once daily or the highest tolerated dose.

### General Administration
- Candesartan should be taken once daily.
- Tablets may be administered with or without food.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to candesartan cilexetil or any tablet excipients.
- **Pregnancy and Lactation:** Contraindicated during pregnancy and breastfeeding [Australian].
- **Hepatic:** Severe hepatic impairment and/or cholestasis [Australian].
- **Dual RAAS Blockade:** Concomitant use with aliskiren-containing products in patients with diabetes mellitus (Type I or II) or moderate-to-severe renal impairment (GFR < 60 mL/min/1.73 m²) [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Renal Function Dependence:** In patients whose vascular tone and renal function depend predominantly on the RAAS (e.g., severe CHF, renal artery stenosis), treatment may be associated with acute hypotension, azotaemia, oliguria, or acute renal failure.
- **Excessive Hypotension:** Risks are higher in patients with ischaemic cardiopathy or cerebrovascular disease; excessive BP drops may lead to myocardial infarction or stroke.
- **Triple Blockade Warning:** The combination of an ACE inhibitor, an ARB, and a mineralocorticoid receptor antagonist in heart failure is not recommended due to increased risks of hypotension and renal dysfunction.
- **Renal Artery Stenosis:** May increase blood urea and serum creatinine in patients with bilateral renal artery stenosis or stenosis to a solitary kidney.
- **Hyperkalaemia:** Risk is increased with concomitant use of potassium-sparing diuretics, potassium supplements, or salt substitutes. Periodic monitoring of serum potassium is essential, particularly in heart failure.
- **Aortic/Mitral Stenosis:** Use with caution in patients with haemodynamically relevant valvular stenosis or obstructive hypertrophic cardiomyopathy.
- **The "Triple Whammy":** Concomitant use of an ACE inhibitor or ARB, an NSAID/COX-2 inhibitor, and a thiazide diuretic significantly increases the risk of renal impairment, especially in the elderly.

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Hypertension (Clinical Trial Data)
Adverse events were generally mild, transient, and comparable to placebo.
- **Common (1–10%):** Headache (10.9%), Back pain (4.0%), Upper respiratory tract infection (URTI) (6.1%), Dizziness (2.7%).
- **Uncommon to Rare:** Nausea, abdominal pain, diarrhoea, vomiting, fatigue, and influenza-like symptoms.

### Heart Failure (Clinical Trial Data)
- **Common (≥ 1/100, < 1/10):** Hypotension, hyperkalaemia, and renal impairment.

### Post-Marketing (Very Rare < 0.01%)
- **Blood:** Leukopenia, neutropenia, agranulocytosis.
- **Metabolic:** Hyponatraemia.
- **Hepato-biliary:** Hepatitis, abnormal liver function, increased liver enzymes.
- **Skin:** Angioedema, rash, urticaria, pruritus.
- **Musculoskeletal:** Myalgia, rhabdomyolysis (rarely reported with ARBs).

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Aliskiren:** Contraindicated in patients with diabetes or significant renal impairment due to dual RAAS blockade risks.
- **Lithium:** Reversible increases in serum lithium concentrations and toxicity have been reported. Careful monitoring is required.
- **NSAIDs (including COX-2 inhibitors):** May attenuate the antihypertensive effect of candesartan and increase the risk of worsening renal function and hyperkalaemia.
- **Other Antihypertensives:** May enhance the blood pressure-lowering effect.
- **Pharmacokinetics:** No clinically significant interactions identified with hydrochlorothiazide, warfarin, digoxin, oral contraceptives, glibenclamide, nifedipine, or enalapril. Candesartan is minimally metabolised by CYP2C9.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** **Category D** [Australian]. Exposure to ARBs during the second and third trimesters is known to induce human fetotoxicity (decreased renal function, oligohydramnios, skull ossification retardation) and neonatal toxicity (renal failure, hypotension, hyperkalaemia). Treatment must be stopped immediately if pregnancy is detected.
- **Breastfeeding:** Use is not recommended. It is unknown if candesartan is excreted in human milk, though it is present in the milk of lactating rats.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy have not been established in children [Australian].
- **Elderly:** No initial dose adjustment is required for hypertension, though an initial dose of **8 mg** is recommended [Australian].
- **Renal Impairment:**
    - Mild to moderate (CrCl 30–80 mL/min): No initial adjustment needed.
    - Severe (CrCl < 30 mL/min) or haemodialysis: Consider a lower initial dose of **4 mg**.
- **Hepatic Impairment:**
    - Mild to moderate chronic liver disease: Consider a lower starting dose of **4 mg**.
    - Severe impairment/cholestasis: Contraindicated.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Candesartan cilexetil is a prodrug converted to candesartan via ester hydrolysis. It is a selective AT1 receptor antagonist that blocks the vasoconstrictor and aldosterone-secreting effects of angiotensin II. It does not inhibit ACE or potentiate bradykinin.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Bioavailability:** Approximately 14% (tablet).
    - **Distribution:** Highly protein-bound (> 99%).
    - **Metabolism:** Minor hepatic metabolism via CYP2C9.
    - **Excretion:** Primarily unchanged via urine (30%) and bile/faeces (70%). Terminal half-life is approximately 9 hours.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Hypertension:** Trials demonstrated dose-dependent blood pressure reduction with trough/peak ratios confirming once-daily dosing. The **CALM study** showed significant reductions in urinary albumin/creatinine ratios in patients with Type II diabetes and hypertension.
- **Heart Failure:** The **CHARM program** (Alternative, Added, and Preserved) evaluated over 7,500 patients. 
    - **CHARM-Alternative:** Showed a hazard ratio of 0.77 (p < 0.001) for CV mortality or CHF hospitalisation in ACE-intolerant patients.
    - **CHARM-Added:** Showed a hazard ratio of 0.85 (p = 0.011) when added to ACE inhibitor therapy.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store below 25°C.
- **Conditions:** Keep in the original container to protect from light [Australian].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule:** **Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine)** [Australian].
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only.
