# Editorial Product Information: Coal Tar (Topical)

## Editorial Note
- Contributing Jurisdictions: United States (FDA)
- Purpose: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of topical coal tar (1.25%) for healthcare professionals, synthesising available regulatory information.
- Disclaimer: This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing or dispensing in a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Coal Tar 1.25% (derived from Coal Tar Topical Solution, USP).
- **Chemical Class:** Keratoplastic agent / Antipsoriatic.
- **Brand Name(s):** **Psoriasin** Daytime Relief Vanishing Gel [US].
- **ATC Code:** D05AA (Tar products).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Topical gel containing 12.5 mg of coal tar per 1 g of product [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Kobayashi Healthcare International, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Psoriasis:** For the relief of itching, scaling, flaking, redness, and irritation associated with psoriasis.
- **Seborrheic Dermatitis:** For the relief of symptoms and prevention of recurrence associated with seborrheic dermatitis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Application:** Apply a small amount of gel to the affected areas one to four times daily, or as directed by a physician.
- **Preparation:** The affected area should be washed at least daily with mild soap and warm water, then dried thoroughly before application.
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - The gel should be gently massaged into thoroughly dry skin.
    - **Hair Colour Warning:** Coal tar may affect very light hair colour. It is recommended to test the product on a few strands of hair before applying to larger areas where light-coloured hair is present.
    - Read all package warnings and directions prior to use.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to coal tar or any excipients listed in the formulation (e.g., aloe vera, alcohol, isopropyl alcohol, polyethylene glycol).
- **Regional specifics:** Not specified in available sources as formal contraindications, though specific usage restrictions apply (see Section 5).

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **General Warnings:** For external use only. Avoid contact with the eyes; if contact occurs, rinse thoroughly with water.
- **Photosensitivity:** Use caution when exposing skin to sunlight after application. Coal tar may increase the tendency to sunburn for up to 24 hours post-application [US].
- **Medical Consultation Required:** Patients should consult a physician before use if:
    - The condition covers a large area of the body.
    - The condition is located around the rectum, genital area, or groin.
    - The product is to be used for prolonged periods.
    - The patient is currently using other forms of psoriasis therapy, such as prescription drugs or ultraviolet (UV) radiation.
- **Cessation of Use:** Patients should stop use and consult a doctor if the condition worsens or does not improve after regular use.
- **Carcinogenicity Warning:** This product contains chemicals known to the State of California to cause cancer (California Proposition 65) [US].
- **Safety:** The active ingredient has been recognised as safe and effective by an FDA Expert Advisory Panel when used as directed [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
*Note: Specific frequency data (e.g., >10%) is not provided in the source documentation.*

- **Dermatologic:** Redness, irritation, and potential staining of skin or light hair.
- **Photosensitivity:** Increased susceptibility to ultraviolet radiation (sunburn) for 24 hours.
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** While not listed by frequency, patients are advised to report serious side effects to the manufacturer's provided consumer line [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Phototoxic Agents:** Potential for additive effects when used with other photosensitising medications.
- **Combined Psoriasis Therapies:** Patients should consult a physician before combining coal tar with other prescription drugs or UV radiation therapy.
- **Topical Steroids:** Coal tar may be used to complement topical steroid therapies. Some protocols suggest "pulse therapy" (e.g., topical steroids for three consecutive days followed by four days of coal tar) to reduce steroid-related side effects such as skin thinning [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Breastfeeding:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Fertility:** Not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Intended for use by normally healthy adults. Persons under 18 years of age should use this product only as directed by a doctor [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Sensitive Skin:** Individuals with highly sensitive or allergic skin should use this product only under the direction of a doctor [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Coal tar slows down excessive skin cell production (keratoplastic action), which is considered the underlying cause of psoriasis symptoms.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Reduces itching, scaling, and redness associated with hyperkeratotic skin conditions.
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified in available sources.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Summary:** Source documents refer to the recognition of safety and efficacy by an FDA Expert Advisory Panel but do not provide specific pivotal trial data or primary outcomes.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Keep container tightly closed when not in use. Store away from excessive heat and cold.
- **Safety:** Keep out of reach of children. In case of ingestion, seek medical assistance or contact a poison control centre immediately.
