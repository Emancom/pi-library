## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Cimetidine
- **Chemical Class:** Histamine H₂-receptor antagonist
- **Brand Names:** Acid Controller, Tagamet HB 200 (reference brand) [US]
- **ATC Code:** A02BA01
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Film-coated tablet: 200 mg [US]
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** CVS Woonsocket Prescription Center, Inc. [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Heartburn Relief:** Symptomatic relief of heartburn associated with acid indigestion and sour stomach [US-approved].
- **Heartburn Prevention:** Prevention of heartburn associated with acid indigestion and sour stomach brought on by consuming specific foods or beverages [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adults and Children 12 years and over):**
    - **Symptomatic Relief:** 200 mg (1 tablet) swallowed with a glass of water [US].
    - **Prophylaxis:** 200 mg (1 tablet) swallowed with a glass of water immediately before, or up to 30 minutes prior to, consuming food or beverages that cause heartburn [US].
- **Maximum Dose:** Do not exceed 400 mg (2 tablets) in a 24-hour period [US].
- **Duration of Use:** Treatment should not exceed 14 days unless directed by a physician [US].
- **Paediatric Dosing:** For children under 12 years of age, consult a physician prior to use [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known allergy to cimetidine or other acid reducers (H₂-receptor antagonists) [US].
- **Co-administration:** Concurrent use with other acid reducers [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None specified in available sources.
- **Urgent Medical Assessment (Red Flags):** Patients should not use cimetidine and must seek medical advice if they experience:
    - Trouble or pain when swallowing food [US].
    - Vomiting with blood [US].
    - Bloody or black stools [US].
- **General Cautions:** Patients should consult a physician before use if they have:
    - Kidney disease [US].
    - Liver disease [US].
    - Unexplained weight loss [US].
    - Stomach pain [US].
    - Nausea or vomiting [US].
    - Heartburn lasting over 3 months (may indicate a more serious condition) [US].
- **Cardiovascular/Respiratory Symptoms:** Seek medical advice if heartburn is accompanied by lightheadedness, sweating, dizziness, shortness of breath, or pain radiating to the arms, neck, or shoulders [US].
- **Monitoring Requirements:** Not specified in available sources.

## 6. Adverse Effects

- **Serious Adverse Effects:**
    - Potential for severe allergic reactions (Allergy Alert) [US].
    - Symptoms requiring discontinuation: Persistent or worsening heartburn, or continued stomach pain [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Healthcare professional consultation is required before use during pregnancy [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Healthcare professional consultation is required before use while breastfeeding [US].
- **Fertility:** Not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy in children under 12 years have not been established for OTC use; professional medical advice is required [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Use with caution; patients must consult a physician before use [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Use with caution; patients must consult a physician before use [US].
