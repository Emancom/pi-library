# Editorial Product Information: Capivasertib

## Editorial Note
- Contributing Jurisdictions: Australian (TGA).
- This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of **capivasertib** for healthcare professionals, synthesized from the official Product Information documents provided.
- This synthesis is an editorial resource and is not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Capivasertib (serine/threonine kinase AKT inhibitor).
- **Brand Name(s):** **TRUQAP** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - 160 mg film-coated tablets (round, biconvex, beige, debossed with ‘CAV’ above ‘160’).
    - 200 mg film-coated tablets (capsule-shaped, biconvex, beige, debossed with ‘CAV 200’).
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** AstraZeneca Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Hormone Receptor (HR)-Positive, HER2-Negative Breast Cancer:** In combination with fulvestrant for the treatment of adult patients with locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer following recurrence or progression on or after an endocrine-based regimen [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** 400 mg (two 200 mg tablets) orally twice daily, approximately 12 hours apart.
- **Dosing Schedule:** Intermittent schedule of 4 days on treatment followed by 3 days off treatment each week.
- **Combination Therapy:** 
    - Administered with fulvestrant 500 mg (Days 1, 15, 29, then monthly).
    - Pre/peri-menopausal women should also receive a luteinising hormone-releasing hormone (LHRH) agonist.
    - For men, LHRH agonist administration should be considered according to clinical practice.
- **Administration Instructions:** Tablets must be swallowed whole with water (do not chew, crush, or divide). May be taken with or without food.
- **Missed Dose:** Can be taken within 4 hours of the scheduled time. If more than 4 hours have passed, skip the dose. There must be at least 8 hours between doses. 
- **Vomiting:** If vomiting occurs, an additional dose should not be taken; the next dose should be taken at the usual time.
- **Dose Reductions for Toxicity:**
    - **Starting Dose:** 400 mg twice daily.
    - **First Reduction:** 320 mg twice daily (two 160 mg tablets).
    - **Second Reduction:** 200 mg twice daily (one 200 mg tablet).
    - If further reduction is needed, treatment should be discontinued.

## 4. Contraindications
- Prior severe hypersensitivity to capivasertib or any of its excipients.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hyperglycaemia:** Severe cases, including diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA) with fatal outcomes, have been reported. DKA can occur in less than 10 days of treatment.
    - *Monitoring:* Test fasting glucose (FG) and HbA1c prior to initiation. Monitor FG at weeks 1, 2, 4, 6, and 8, then monthly.
    - *Management:* Patients with a history of diabetes may require intensified anti-diabetic treatment. Withhold or discontinue **TRUQAP** based on the severity of hyperglycaemia.
- **Diarrhoea:** Very common and may lead to dehydration and acute kidney injury. 
    - *Management:* Advise patients to start anti-diarrhoeal treatment at the first sign of symptoms and increase fluid intake.
- **Rash and Skin Reactions:** Severe reactions including Erythema Multiforme, Dermatitis Exfoliative Generalised, and DRESS (Drug Reaction with Eosinophilia and Systemic Symptoms) have been reported. 
    - *Management:* Early dermatology consultation is recommended. Dose interruption or discontinuation may be required.
- **Cardiac Precautions:** Patients with a history of significant cardiac disease or QTcF > 470 msec were excluded from pivotal trials; caution is advised in these populations.

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions are based on the CAPItello-291 trial (N=355).

| Frequency | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Diarrhoea (72.4%), Rash (40.3%), Nausea (34.6%), Fatigue (32.1%), Vomiting (20.6%), Hyperglycaemia (17.2%), Decreased appetite (16.6%), Stomatitis (17.2%), Urinary tract infection (13.5%), Anaemia (10.4%). |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Pyrexia (9.6%), Dysgeusia (5.9%), Dyspepsia (5.1%), Hypokalaemia (4.5%), Weight decreased (3.4%), Blood creatinine increased (4.5%). |
| **Uncommon (0.1–1%)** | Diabetic ketoacidosis (0.3%), DRESS, Hypersensitivity, Erythema multiforme. |

- **Serious Adverse Reactions:** Reported in 6.8% of patients, most notably rash (2.3%), diarrhoea (1.7%), and vomiting (1.1%).
- **Discontinuation:** 9.9% of patients discontinued due to adverse reactions (primarily rash and diarrhoea).

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:**
    - **Strong CYP3A4 Inhibitors:** (e.g., itraconazole) Increase capivasertib exposure. Avoid concomitant use; if unavoidable, reduce **TRUQAP** dose to 320 mg twice daily.
    - **Moderate CYP3A4 Inhibitors:** (e.g., erythromycin, verapamil) Increase exposure. Reduce **TRUQAP** dose to 320 mg twice daily. 
    - **Strong CYP3A4 Inducers:** (e.g., rifampicin, phenytoin) Significantly decrease capivasertib exposure. Concomitant use is not recommended.
    - **Moderate CYP3A4 Inducers:** Use with caution.
- **Effect of Capivasertib on Other Drugs:**
    - **CYP3A Substrates:** Capivasertib is a weak CYP3A inhibitor. Monitor drugs with a narrow therapeutic window (e.g., cyclosporine, tacrolimus).
    - **Transporters:** Capivasertib inhibits OCT2, MATE1, and MATE2K. May increase concentrations of substrates like dofetilide or procainamide. It also inhibits OATP1B1 and OATP1B3.
- **Acid-Reducing Agents:** No clinically relevant interaction with proton pump inhibitors (e.g., rabeprazole).

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Category D [Australian]:** Based on animal studies, capivasertib can cause embryo-fetal toxicity and post-implantation loss. Not recommended during pregnancy.
    - **Contraception:** Women of childbearing potential must use effective contraception during treatment and for at least 4 weeks after the final dose. Males with female partners of childbearing potential should use effective contraception for 16 weeks after the final dose.
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if capivasertib is excreted in human milk. Breastfeeding should be discontinued during treatment.
- **Fertility:** Animal studies showed tubular degeneration in testes and decreased spermatocytes, which were largely reversible. No clinical data available.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy not established in patients aged 0–18 years.
- **Elderly Use:** No dose adjustment required for patients ≥ 65 years. Limited data in patients ≥ 75 years.
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    - *Mild to Moderate:* No dose adjustment required. 
    - *Severe/End-stage:* Not recommended (not studied).
- **Hepatic Impairment:**
    - *Mild:* No dose adjustment required.
    - *Moderate:* Administer only if benefits outweigh risks; monitor closely.
    - *Severe:* Not recommended (not studied).

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Potent, selective inhibitor of all three isoforms (1, 2, and 3) of the serine/threonine kinase AKT. It inhibits the phosphorylation of AKT substrates (GSK3β and PRAS40), thereby blocking the PI3K/AKT/mTOR signalling pathway involved in cell survival and proliferation.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Rapid; peak plasma concentration ($C_{max}$) reached in 1–2 hours. Absolute bioavailability is 29%.
    - **Distribution:** Mean volume of distribution ($V_{ss}$) is 205 L. Plasma protein binding is 78%.
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily metabolised by CYP3A4 and UGT2B7.
    - **Elimination:** Effective half-life is 8.3 hours. Cleared via metabolism and renal excretion (21% of total clearance).

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **CAPItello-291 Trial:** A Phase 3, randomised, double-blind study (N=708) comparing capivasertib + fulvestrant vs. placebo + fulvestrant in HR+/HER2- locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer.
- **Efficacy Results:** 
    - **Overall Population:** Median Progression-Free Survival (PFS) was 7.2 months for the capivasertib arm vs. 3.6 months for placebo (HR 0.60; p < 0.001).
    - **AKT Pathway-Altered Subgroup (PIK3CA/AKT1/PTEN altered):** Median PFS was 7.3 months vs. 3.1 months (HR 0.50; p < 0.001).

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store below 30°C.
- **Packaging:** Supplied in aluminium/aluminium blisters.
