# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the following jurisdictional Product Information:
    - **US (FDA)**: Levocarnitine Injection, USP (Baxter Healthcare Corporation)
- **Purpose**: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer**: This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. At the time of compilation, only the US-specific documentation was available for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient**: Levocarnitine [(R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt].
- **Chemical Class**: Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule.
- **Brand Name(s)**: Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **ATC Code**: Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s)**: 
    - Sterile solution for injection: 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration**: Intravenous.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor**: 
    - Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency (Inborn Errors of Metabolism)**: For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that result in secondary carnitine deficiency [US-approved].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD)**: For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Pediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders**:
    - **Recommended Dose**: 50 mg/kg administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion [US].
    - **Loading Dose**: Often required in severe metabolic crises, followed by an equivalent dose over the next 24 hours [US].
    - **Frequency**: Administered every 3 or 4 hours; never less than every 6 hours [US].
    - **Maintenance**: Subsequent daily doses are typically in the range of 50 mg/kg, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been used [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis**:
    - **Starting Dose**: 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
    - **Administration**: Administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line after each dialysis session [US].
    - **Dose Adjustments**: Should be guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Inspect visually for particulate matter or discoloration prior to use [US].
- Discard any unused portion as the formulation contains no preservative [US].

### Monitoring
- **Metabolic Disorders**: Obtain baseline plasma carnitine levels. Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L) is recommended [US].
- **ESRD**: Monitor trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levels (normal range 40–50 µmol/L) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Serious Warnings
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions**: Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients on dialysis. Some reactions occurred within minutes of administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately [US].

### General Warnings and Precautions
- **Seizure Activity**: Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with a known seizure disorder, an increase in seizure frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Renal Impairment (Oral Levocarnitine Reference)**: While not directly studied for the injection, the US PI notes that chronic high doses of *oral* levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (TMA and TMAO) [US].

### Monitoring Requirements
- **Warfarin Therapy**: Reports of increased International Normalised Ratio (INR) have been observed when levocarnitine is used concomitantly with warfarin. Monitor INR after initiation or dose adjustment [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are often confounded by the underlying pathology (ESRD or metabolic errors).

### Frequent Adverse Effects (≥5% in ESRD Clinical Trials)
| Body System | Adverse Event |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Body as a Whole** | Abdominal pain, asthenia, chest pain, fever, flu syndrome, headache, infection, injection site reaction, pain [US] |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension, hypotension, tachycardia [US] |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea, nausea, vomiting, anorexia, dyspepsia [US] |
| **Metabolic/Nutritional** | Hypercalcemia, hypervolemia, peripheral edema [US] |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness, insomnia, paresthesia, depression [US] |
| **Respiratory** | Cough increase, dyspnea, pharyngitis, rhinitis [US] |

### Other Adverse Effects
- **Common/Uncommon**: Body odor, gastritis, palpitations, anemia, weight changes, and taste perversion [US].
- **Serious**: Anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, bronchospasm, and seizures [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin**: Potential for increased INR and bleeding risk. Monitoring of INR is recommended [US].
- **Other Interactions**: Not specified in available sources.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy**: 
    - **Category**: Not assigned a lettered category in the provided source, but reproductive studies in rats and rabbits (up to 3.8 times the human dose) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm [US]. 
    - **Recommendation**: Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding**: 
    - **Information**: Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk carnitine concentrations after administration [US].
    - **Recommendation**: Weigh the risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant against the benefits of maternal supplementation. Consideration may be given to discontinuing nursing or the drug [US].
- **Fertility**: No evidence of impaired fertility in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use**: Dosing is weight-based (50 mg/kg) as described in Section 3 [US].
- **Elderly Use**: Not specifically addressed in the source document.
- **Renal Impairment**: Injection is indicated for ESRD patients on dialysis. Note the potential for metabolite accumulation if oral forms are used in this population [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment**: Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for mammalian energy metabolism. it facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production [US].

### Pharmacokinetics
- **Distribution**: Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
- **Metabolism**: Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (TMAO) and γ-butyrobetaine [US].
- **Elimination**: Following IV administration, approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. The mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours; the mean apparent terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies**: In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients, IV levocarnitine (20 mg/kg post-dialysis) increased mean trough concentrations from 21.3 µmol/L to 190 µmol/L at 8 weeks. While carnitine concentrations were increased, the effect on clinical outcomes in the ESRD population has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature**: Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C [US].
- **Precautions**: Avoid excessive heat. Protect from freezing [US].
- **Stability**: Compatible and stable for up to 24 hours in PVC bags when mixed with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) at room temperature [US].

- **Legal Classification**: Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule**: Not specified for other jurisdictions.
