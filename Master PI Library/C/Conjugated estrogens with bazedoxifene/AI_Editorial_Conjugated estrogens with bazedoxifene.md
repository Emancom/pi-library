## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN/USP); chemical name: (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule for long-chain fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Names:** Not specified in available sources (Generic name: Levocarnitine Injection).
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency (Inborn Errors of Metabolism):** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
- **Standard Dose:** 50 mg/kg administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion [US].
- **Loading Dose:** In cases of severe metabolic crisis, a loading dose may be given, followed by equivalent doses every 3 to 4 hours (and never less than every 6 hours) via infusion or IV injection [US].
- **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses are generally 50 mg/kg, though higher doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilized based on clinical requirements [US].

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight) administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus into the venous return line after each dialysis session [US].
- **Dose Adjustment:** Adjustments should be guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma concentrations. Downward titration (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Inspect for particulate matter or discoloration prior to use.
- **Compatibility:** Compatible and stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer's (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known** [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions can occur within minutes of administration. Discontinue immediately if a severe reaction occurs [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with known seizure disorders, an increase in frequency or severity of seizures has been observed [US].
- **General Safety (Oral vs. IV):** The safety of oral levocarnitine has not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in ESRD patients may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (TMA and TMAO) [US].
- **Monitoring:** For metabolic disorders, obtain baseline plasma concentrations and monitor weekly/monthly. Free carnitine levels should ideally be maintained between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Clinical Trial Experience (ESRD Patients)
Adverse events reported at a frequency ≥5% (regardless of causality) in double-blind, placebo-controlled trials include [US]:
- **Body as a Whole:** Abdominal pain (5–21%), headache (3–37%), infection (10–24%), injection site reaction (27–38%), pain (21–35%), fever (5–12%).
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (18–21%), hypotension (3–19%), chest pain (6–15%), tachycardia (5–9%).
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea (9–35%), vomiting (9–21%), nausea (5–12%).
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (15–27%), cough increase (10–18%), dyspnea (3–11%).
- **Metabolic:** Hypercalcemia (6–15%), hypervolemia (3–12%).
- **Nervous:** Dizziness (10–18%), paresthesia (3–12%).

### Postmarketing and General Experience
- **Common:** Transient nausea and vomiting.
- **Uncommon:** Body odor and gastritis.
- **Neurologic:** Seizures (noted in both seizure-prone and non-seizure patients) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have been observed when used concomitantly with levocarnitine. Monitoring of INR is recommended upon initiation or dose adjustment [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not lettered]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine concentrations in milk after exogenous administration. Weigh the benefits to the mother against potential risks of excess intake to the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Refer to standard weight-based dosing (50 mg/kg) [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Specifically indicated for ESRD on dialysis. Dose adjustments based on pre-dialysis trough levels are required (see Section 3) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids into cellular mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin. Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Excretion:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours. Mean apparent terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In patients with inborn errors of metabolism, levocarnitine promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine complexes that are rapidly eliminated [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients (20 mg/kg dose), pre-dialysis trough concentrations increased from a baseline of ~20 µmol/L to 190 ± 55 µmol/L after 8 weeks of therapy. Trough levels remained stable or increased over the study duration [US].
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** Studies using 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg three times weekly showed a dose-proportional increase in trough levocarnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat.
- **Preservative Status:** Preservative-free; discard any unused portion of a single-dose vial [US].
