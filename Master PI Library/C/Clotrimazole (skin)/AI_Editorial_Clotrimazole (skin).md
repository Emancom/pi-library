# Editorial Product Information: Clotrimazole (Topical)

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **US (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical data into a concise, professional reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for the official PI or summary of product characteristics (SmPC) required for prescribing or dispensing in any specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Clotrimazole (1% w/w).
- **Chemical Class:** Imidazole antifungal.
- **Brand Names:** **Tagrid Intertrigo Cream** [US].
- **ATC Code:** D01AC01 (Clotrimazole, dermatological).
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Topical cream containing Clotrimazole 1g per 100g (1%) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Topical.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Tagrid LLC [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Skin Irritation/Intertrigo:** Alleviation of symptoms associated with skin irritation caused by friction, including redness, itching, and discomfort. Specifically indicated for use in skin folds, such as the groin, underarms, and other intertriginous areas [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adults and Children ≥ 2 years):** Apply a thin layer to the affected area twice daily, specifically in the morning and at night [US].
- **Administration Instructions:** 
    - The affected area should be washed and dried thoroughly before application.
    - For external use only; not for ophthalmic or internal use [US].
- **Monitoring:** If the condition persists or there is no improvement within 4 weeks of treatment, the patient should consult a physician [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Age Restriction:** Not to be used on children under 2 years of age unless specifically directed by a physician [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Although not explicitly listed as a contraindication in the provided source, the instruction to stop use if irritation occurs suggests caution in patients with known sensitivity to imidazoles or other ingredients in the formulation [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **General Warnings:** For external use only.
- **Ocular Exposure:** Avoid contact with the eyes. If contact occurs, rinse thoroughly with water [US].
- **Treatment Duration:** Treatment should be discontinued and a physician consulted if irritation occurs or if symptoms persist beyond 4 weeks [US].
- **Safety:** Keep out of reach of children. In case of accidental ingestion, seek medical help or contact a Poison Control Center immediately [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Dermatological:** Local irritation may occur. 
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Not specified in available sources, though systemic absorption is generally minimal for topical clotrimazole.
- **Frequency:** Frequency data for adverse effects is not provided in the source documentation. Patients are advised to stop use if irritation develops [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources for this topical formulation.
- **Clinically Significant Interactions:** None listed in the provided documentation.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Breastfeeding:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Fertility:** Not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** 
    - Approved for use in children aged 2 years and older [US].
    - Use in children must be supervised by an adult [US].
    - Use in children under 2 years of age requires direct medical advice [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Clotrimazole is classified as an antifungal [US]. (Note: The mechanism typically involves the inhibition of ergosterol synthesis, leading to structural and functional impairment of the fungal cytoplasmic membrane).
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Not specified in available sources.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Summary:** Pivotal trial data was not provided in the source documentation. The product is marketed under the OTC Monograph Drug system (M005) [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store between 15°C and 30°C (59°F–86°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Ensure the cap is closed tightly after each use [US].
