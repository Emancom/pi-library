# Editorial Product Information: Coagulation Factor VIIa (Recombinant)-jncw

## Editorial Note
*   **Contributing Jurisdictions:** This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information provided by the United States (Food and Drug Administration).
*   **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of **SEVENFACT** for healthcare professionals, integrating regulatory data into a clinical reference format.
*   **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis for informational purposes and does not replace the official, jurisdiction-specific Product Information required for prescribing.

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredient:** Coagulation factor VIIa (recombinant)-jncw.
*   **Chemical Class:** Recombinant analog of human Factor VIIa; a vitamin K-dependent coagulation factor.
*   **Brand Name:** **SEVENFACT** [US].
*   **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Dosage Form and Strengths:** Lyophilized powder for solution for intravenous injection. Available in single-dose vials containing 1 mg, 2 mg, or 5 mg of coagulation factor VIIa (recombinant)-jncw [US].
*   **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV).
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Laboratoire Français du Fractionnement et des Biotechnologies (LFB S.A.) [US]. Distributed by HEMA Biologics [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Hemophilia A or B with Inhibitors:** Treatment and control of bleeding episodes in adults and adolescents aged 12 years and older [US].
*   **Limitations of Use:** Not indicated for the treatment of patients with congenital Factor VII deficiency [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Adolescent ≥12 Years)
Treatment should be initiated as soon as a bleeding event occurs. The dose and duration depend on bleeding location, severity, and patient responsiveness to prior bypassing agents [US].

### Dosing by Indication
*   **Mild to Moderate Bleeding Episodes (e.g., joint, superficial muscle, soft tissue, mucous membranes):**
    *   75 mcg/kg repeated every 3 hours until hemostasis is achieved; **OR**
    *   Initial dose of 225 mcg/kg. If hemostasis is not achieved within 9 hours, additional 75 mcg/kg doses may be administered every 3 hours as needed [US].
*   **Severe Bleeding Episodes (e.g., life- or limb-threatening, intracranial, gastrointestinal):**
    *   225 mcg/kg initially, followed if necessary 6 hours later with 75 mcg/kg every 2 hours until hemostasis is achieved [US].

### Administration Instructions
*   **Reconstitution:** Must be reconstituted with the provided pre-filled syringe of Sterile Water for Injection to a final concentration of 1 mg/mL. Bring to room temperature before mixing. Do not shake; swirl gently to dissolve [US].
*   **Injection:** Administer as an IV bolus over 2 minutes or less. Infusion must be completed within 4 hours of reconstitution. Do not mix with other infusion solutions or store in a syringe [US].
*   **Failure of Response:** Consider alternative treatments if bleeding is not controlled within 24 hours of the first dose [US].

## 4. Contraindications
*   **Rabbit Protein Allergy:** Known hypersensitivity to rabbits or rabbit proteins [US].
*   **Hypersensitivity:** Severe hypersensitivity reaction to **SEVENFACT** or any of its components [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
*   **Boxed Warning: Thrombosis [US]:**
    *   Serious arterial and venous thrombotic events may occur following administration.
    *   Risk is increased in patients with other thrombotic risk factors (e.g., atherosclerotic disease, coronary artery disease, crush injury, or septicemia).
    *   Monitor for signs/symptoms of activation of the coagulation system or thrombosis.
*   **Hypersensitivity and Infusion-Related Reactions:** Anaphylaxis may occur. Patients with IgE-based hypersensitivity to casein may be at higher risk. Discontinue immediately if symptoms (e.g., hives, wheezing, hypotension) occur [US].
*   **Neutralizing Antibodies:** Suspect antibody development if hemostasis is not achieved. While not studied in this specific population, neutralizing antibodies to other FVIIa products have been seen in congenital Factor VII-deficient patients [US].
*   **Monitoring:** Laboratory parameters (PT/INR, aPTT, FVII:C) do not necessarily correlate with clinical hemostatic effectiveness [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
The safety database reflects exposure in 42 patients across two clinical studies. Most common reactions (incidence ≥1%) include:

| Adverse Reaction | Frequency/Notes |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Headache** | Common (≥1%) [US] |
| **Dizziness** | Common (≥1%) [US] |
| **Infusion-site discomfort** | Common (≥1%) [US] |
| **Infusion-site hematoma** | Common (≥1%) [US] |
| **Infusion-related reaction** | Common (≥1%); resolved without intervention [US] |
| **Fever** | Common (≥1%) [US] |

**Serious Adverse Effects:** Arterial and venous thrombosis (refer to Section 5).

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **Activated Prothrombin Complex Concentrates (aPCC/PCC):** Simultaneous use with FVIIa-containing products carries an elevated risk of serious thrombotic events. Avoid concurrent administration [US].
*   **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy:** 
    *   **US Category:** Not specified (current FDA labeling uses Risk Summary). There are no adequate/controlled studies in pregnant women. Animal embryo-fetal studies have not been conducted [US].
*   **Breastfeeding:** Presence in human milk is unknown. Consider the developmental/health benefits of breastfeeding against the clinical need for the drug and potential risks to the infant [US].
*   **Fertility:** Animal studies in male rats (up to 13-fold human dose) showed no effect on mating index, fertility, or conception rate [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use:** 
    *   Approved for ≥12 years of age [US].
    *   Safety and effectiveness NOT established in children <12 years. In a trial of 25 pediatric patients (6 months to <12 years), effectiveness was not demonstrated [US].
*   **Elderly Use:** Safety/effectiveness in patients >65 years not evaluated in clinical trials. Consideration of age-related comorbidities and thrombotic risk is advised for patients older than 50 years [US].
*   **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
*   **Mechanism of Action:** Recombinant Factor VIIa, in complex with tissue factor (TF), activates Factor X to Xa, bypassing the need for Factor VIII or Factor IX to initiate the coagulation cascade and form a hemostatic plug [US].
*   **Pharmacokinetics:** 
    *   **Elimination:** Exhibits biexponential decay.
    *   **Half-life:** Approximately 2 hours [US].
    *   **Clearance:** 4.5 – 5.1 L/h [US].
    *   **Volume of Distribution (Vss):** 7.0 – 8.2 L [US].
*   **Pharmacodynamics:** Shortens aPTT and PT and increases thrombin generation in a dose-dependent manner [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   **Study 1:** A global, multicenter, randomized, open-label, cross-over study of 27 patients with hemophilia A or B with inhibitors. 
    *   **Outcome:** Hemostatic efficacy at 12 hours was 82% for the 75 mcg/kg regimen and 91% for the 225 mcg/kg regimen [US].
    *   **Median time to hemostasis:** 6 hours (75 mcg/kg) vs. 3 hours (225 mcg/kg) [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   **Vials:** Store at room temperature, but may be refrigerated at 2°C to 30°C (36°F to 86°F). Protect from light; do not freeze [US].
*   **Reconstituted Solution:** Stable for up to 4 hours at room temperature (up to 30°C). Do not freeze or store in syringes [US].
