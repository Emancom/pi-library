# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial synthesis is derived from the official Product Information provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- Purpose: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical and regulatory data into a single reference.
- Note: This is an editorial synthesis intended for professional reference and is not a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP).
- **Chemical Class:** A naturally occurring carrier molecule required for mammalian energy metabolism; chemically (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium.
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter).
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Sterile injection solution, 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency in Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Indicated for both acute and chronic management of patients with metabolic defects that result in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **Carnitine Deficiency in End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
- **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg administered as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) or via infusion. 
- **Loading Dose:** In cases of severe metabolic crisis, a loading dose may be given, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours.
- **Frequency:** Dosing should occur every 3 to 4 hours, and never less frequently than every 6 hours.
- **Maximum Dose:** The highest reported dose administered is 300 mg/kg.
- **Monitoring:** It is recommended to obtain baseline plasma carnitine levels and monitor weekly/monthly. Targeted plasma free carnitine levels should ideally be between 35 and 60 µmol/L.

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight).
- **Administration:** Slow 2 to 3 minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
- **Titration:** Therapy initiation may be prompted by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levels below 40–50 µmol/L. Downward dose adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be made as early as the third or fourth week based on trough concentrations.

### General Administration
- Inspect visually for particulates or discoloration before use.
- **Compatibility:** Stable for up to 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) when diluted in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations between 0.5 mg/mL and 8 mg/mL in PVC bags.

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known:** There are currently no listed contraindications for levocarnitine injection in the available source [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients on dialysis. Reactions can occur within minutes of administration. Treatment must be discontinued immediately if severe reactions occur [US].
- **Neurologic Reactions (Seizures):** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Renal Impairment/Metabolite Accumulation:** Safety of high-dose oral levocarnitine has not been evaluated in renal insufficiency. Chronic administration may lead to the accumulation of trimethylamine (TMA) and trimethylamine-N-oxide (TMAO), which are potentially toxic and normally excreted renally [US].
- **Monitoring:** In ESRD patients, the clinical benefit of supplemental carnitine on signs/symptoms of deficiency beyond increasing plasma concentrations has not been fully determined [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse events observed in clinical trials for ESRD patients (occurring at ≥5% frequency) include:

| Body System | Adverse Effect |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Chest pain, fever, flu syndrome, headache, infection, injection site reaction, pain, abdominal pain, asthenia |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension (Common, ~20%), Hypotension, Tachycardia, Arrhythmia |
| **Gastrointestinal** | Diarrhea (Common, ~16%), Vomiting, Nausea, Dyspepsia |
| **Metabolic** | Hypercalcemia, Hypervolemia, Hyperkalemia, Weight changes |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness (Common, ~13%), Insomnia, Paresthesia |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis (Common, ~23%), Cough, Dyspnea, Rhinitis |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Anaphylaxis and other severe hypersensitivity reactions [US].
- **Other Effects:** Body odor and gastritis have been reported but are difficult to isolate from underlying pathology [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) has been reported when used concomitantly with levocarnitine.
- **Management:** It is recommended to monitor INR levels frequently after starting levocarnitine or following dose adjustments in patients on warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not specifically lettered; formerly Category B]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed as animal studies are not always predictive of human response [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine is a normal component of human milk. Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Increased carnitine in milk has been noted in dairy cows following exogenous administration. Risks to the infant from excess intake should be weighed against the benefits of maternal supplementation [US].
- **Fertility:** No impairment of fertility was observed in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders is based on mg/kg body weight (see Section 3).
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Specifically indicated for ESRD on dialysis; however, caution is advised regarding the accumulation of metabolites with oral dosing (see Section 5).
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters, which are quickly eliminated.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces).
    - **Elimination:** Following IV bolus (20 mg/kg), approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours. The mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours, and the terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Data:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients, 20 mg/kg doses three times weekly after dialysis increased mean pre-dialysis plasma carnitine levels from 20 µmol/L to 190 ± 55 µmol/L after 8 weeks.
- **Dose-Ranging Studies:** Trials using 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses demonstrated dose-dependent increases in trough carnitine concentrations at 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat.
- **Shelf Life:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial, as it contains no preservative [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** 
    - **United States:** Rx-only (Prescription only).
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources [US].
