# Editorial Product Information: Coagulation Factor X (Human)

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial has been synthesised from the official Product Information (PI) source provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised clinical overview for healthcare professionals. It is an editorial synthesis intended for reference and does not replace the official, legally approved Product Information required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. 
- Clinical practitioners should consult the full, current PI from their local regulatory authority before prescribing.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Coagulation Factor X (Human).
- **Chemical Class:** Plasma-derived coagulation factor.
- **Brand Name(s):** **COAGADEX** [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Lyophilized powder for reconstitution in single-dose vials. Available in nominal strengths of 250 IU or 500 IU of Factor X activity [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous injection.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Manufactured by Bio Products Laboratory Ltd. (United Kingdom); Marketed by Kedrion Biopharma, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
**COAGADEX** is indicated for use in adult and paediatric patients with hereditary Factor X deficiency for the following [US]:
- Routine prophylaxis to decrease the frequency of bleeding events.
- On-demand treatment and control of active bleeding episodes.
- Perioperative management of bleeding in patients with mild, moderate, or severe deficiency.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing Principles
Dosage must be individualised based on the patient's clinical condition, the severity of the Factor X deficiency, the site/extent of bleeding, and the patient's age. The maximum daily dose should not exceed 60 IU/kg [US].

### Prophylaxis of Bleeding
- **Adults and Adolescents (≥12 years):** 25 IU/kg administered twice weekly [US].
- **Children (<12 years):** 40 IU/kg administered twice weekly [US].
- **Management:** Adjust dosage based on clinical response and trough levels (targeting ≥5 IU/dL). Peak levels should not exceed 120 IU/dL [US].

### On-demand Treatment (Control of Bleeding)
- **Adults and Adolescents (≥12 years):** 25 IU/kg [US].
- **Children (<12 years):** 30 IU/kg [US].
- **Management:** Infuse at the first sign of bleeding. The dose may be repeated every 24 hours until the bleeding is controlled [US].

### Perioperative Management
Calculations are based on the required rise in Factor X levels and the patient's weight.
- **Target Levels:**
    - **Pre-surgery:** Raise plasma Factor X levels to 70–90 IU/dL.
    - **Post-surgery:** Maintain levels at ≥50 IU/dL until the risk of bleeding from surgery has passed [US].
- **Dose Calculation Formulae [US]:**
    - **Patients ≥12 years:** Dose (IU) = Body weight (kg) × Desired Factor X rise (IU/dL) × 0.5.
    - **Patients <12 years:** Dose (IU) = Body weight (kg) × Desired Factor X rise (IU/dL) × 0.6.

### Administration Instructions
- Reconstitute using the provided Sterile Water for Injection and the Mix2Vial transfer device.
- The final concentration post-reconstitution is approximately 100 IU/mL.
- Administer by intravenous infusion at a rate of 10 mL/min (not exceeding 20 mL/min) [US].
- Reconstituted product must be used within one hour and should not be refrigerated [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **COAGADEX** is contraindicated in individuals who have experienced life-threatening hypersensitivity reactions to the product or its components [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None [US].
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Allergic-type reactions, including anaphylaxis, may occur. Early signs include angioedema, infusion site inflammation, urticaria, tachycardia, and wheezing. If symptoms occur, discontinue use immediately and initiate appropriate emergency care [US].
- **Neutralizing Antibodies (Inhibitors):** The formation of Factor X inhibitors is possible. Monitor patients clinically and via laboratory assays (e.g., Nijmegen-Bethesda) if expected plasma levels are not reached or if bleeding is not controlled [US].
- **Transmissible Infectious Agents:** As a plasma-derived product, there is a theoretical risk of transmitting infectious agents (e.g., viruses, vCJD). This risk is mitigated by donor screening and manufacturing processes (solvent/detergent treatment, 15-nm filtration, and dry-heat treatment) [US].
- **Monitoring:** Monitor plasma Factor X activity using validated assays (e.g., one-stage clotting assay) to ensure adequate levels are maintained [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common Adverse Reactions (Frequency ≥5%)
The following reactions were observed in clinical trials [US]:
- Infusion site erythema
- Infusion site pain
- Fatigue
- Back pain

### Immunogenicity
In clinical studies of both adults and children, no subjects developed neutralizing antibodies (inhibitors) to Factor X [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Formal drug interaction studies have not been conducted [US].
- **Plasma Products:** Use caution when co-administering other plasma products that may contain Factor X (e.g., Fresh Frozen Plasma, Prothrombin Complex Concentrates) [US].
- **Anticoagulants:** The therapeutic effect of **COAGADEX** is likely to be counteracted by direct or indirect Factor Xa inhibitors [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not assigned under current labeling rules]. Data in pregnant women are limited. It is unknown if the drug causes fetal harm or affects reproductive capacity. Use only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** There is no information regarding the presence of the drug in human milk or its effects on the breastfed infant. The developmental benefits of breastfeeding should be weighed against the clinical need for the drug [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies showed no pathologies in reproductive organs at doses up to 6 times the maximum clinical dose; however, formal fertility studies have not been conducted [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Approved for all age groups. Incremental recovery is lower in children <12 years (approx. 1.7 IU/dL per IU/kg) compared to adults (approx. 2.0 IU/dL per IU/kg), necessitating higher doses per kg in younger children [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical trials did not include sufficient numbers of subjects aged 65 and over to determine if they respond differently. Dose selection should be cautious, typically starting at the lower end of the range [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** The impact of renal or hepatic function on pharmacokinetics has not been formally evaluated [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** **COAGADEX** temporarily replaces the missing Factor X required for the coagulation cascade. Factor X is converted to Factor Xa, which forms the prothrombinase complex to convert prothrombin into thrombin, ultimately leading to the formation of a fibrin clot [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Administration increases plasma Factor X levels and can temporarily correct defects in aPTT and PT [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Half-life:** Approximately 30.3 hours in subjects ≥12 years [US].
    - **Incremental Recovery:** 2.04 IU/dL per IU/kg in adults/adolescents; 1.45–1.83 IU/dL per IU/kg in children <12 years [US].
    - **Volume of Distribution ($V_{ss}$):** 56.3 mL/kg [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Prophylaxis Study:** A study of 9 children (<12 years) on routine prophylaxis (mean dose ~39 IU/kg) for at least 6 months showed "excellent" efficacy in preventing bleeds, with a mean of 2.1 bleeds per year (mostly traumatic or epistaxis) [US].
- **On-demand Study:** 16 subjects (aged 12–58) were treated for 187 evaluable bleeding episodes. Efficacy was rated as "excellent" or "good" in 98% of episodes. 83% of bleeds were controlled with a single infusion [US].
- **Surgical Study:** 5 subjects (aged 14–59) underwent 7 surgical procedures. Hemostatic control was rated as "excellent" for all procedures [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- Store in the original package to protect from light [US].
- **Temperature:** Store in a refrigerator or at room temperature (2°C to 30°C / 36°F to 86°F) [US].
- **Restrictions:** Do not freeze. Do not use after the expiration date. Reconstituted solution should be used within one hour [US].
