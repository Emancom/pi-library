# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine Injection

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical and regulatory data into a concise reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Always consult the current local PI for full prescribing details and legal requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP), a carrier molecule for long-chain fatty acids.
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium inner salt.
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Clear, colourless sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) single-dose vial [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency in Metabolic Disorders:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US-approved].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD undergoing maintenance haemodialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Administration
- Administer exclusively via the intravenous route.
- Parenteral products must be inspected for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration.

### Metabolic Disorders
- **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg administered as a slow bolus (2–3 minutes) or by infusion.
- **Severe Crisis:** A loading dose is often followed by equivalent doses every 3 to 4 hours (not less than every 6 hours) over the subsequent 24 hours.
- **Maintenance:** Daily doses are typically in the range of 50 mg/kg; however, the dose should be adjusted based on clinical response.
- **Maximum Dose:** The highest reported dose administered is 300 mg/kg [US].

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight.
- **Timing:** Administer as a slow bolus (2–3 minutes) into the venous return line after each dialysis session.
- **Dose Adjustment:** Initiation is prompted by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma concentrations <40–50 µmol/L. Adjustments should be guided by these concentrations; downward titration (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may occur as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known** [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal oedema, and bronchospasm, have been reported. These often occur within minutes of administration, particularly in ESRD patients. Discontinue immediately if a severe reaction occurs [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Levocarnitine (oral or IV) has been associated with seizures in patients with or without pre-existing seizure disorders. In those with established epilepsy, an increase in seizure frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Clinical Monitoring:** 
    - Obtain baseline plasma carnitine levels.
    - Weekly and monthly monitoring is recommended, including blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L).
- **ESRD Specifics:** Efficacy in improving clinical outcomes (signs/symptoms) in ESRD has not been fully determined despite demonstrated increases in carnitine concentrations.

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common Adverse Effects (ESRD Patients)
Based on placebo-controlled trials in chronic haemodialysis patients (frequency ≥5%):
- **General:** Abdominal pain (21%), headache (37% at 20 mg/kg dose), fever (12%), injection site reaction (38%), chest pain (15%).
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (18–21%), hypotension (19% at 20 mg/kg dose), tachycardia (5–9%).
- **Gastrointestinal:** Diarrhoea (35% at 40 mg/kg dose), nausea (12%), vomiting (21%).
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (27%), cough increase (18%), dyspnea (11%).

### Other Reported Effects
- **Metabolic:** Hypercalcemia, hypervolemia.
- **Neurological:** Dizziness, paresthesia.
- **Special Senses:** Taste perversion (9% at 40 mg/kg dose).
- **Body Odour:** Transient nausea, vomiting, and body odour have been observed with chronic use.

### Serious Adverse Effects
- **Hypersensitivity:** Anaphylaxis and bronchospasm.
- **Neurological:** Seizures (post-marketing report) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalised Ratio (INR) have occurred when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin. Monitor INR levels at therapy initiation and following any dose adjustments of **levocarnitine** [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not specified by letter, but PLLR-style data provided]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (based on surface area) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed, as animal studies are not always predictive of human response.
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine is a normal component of human milk. Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine milk concentrations following exogenous administration. Weigh the benefits to the mother against potential risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** No mutagenic effects observed in standard tests (*Salmonella*, *Saccharomyces*). Long-term carcinogenicity studies have not been performed.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing is weight-based (50 mg/kg for metabolic disorders; 10–20 mg/kg for ESRD). See Section 3 for specifics.
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in available source; generally, dose selection should be cautious, reflecting the greater frequency of decreased hepatic, renal, or cardiac function.
- **Renal Impairment:** The safety and efficacy of **oral** levocarnitine have not been evaluated in renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral use in ESRD may lead to accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and TMAO). **Intravenous** levocarnitine is specifically indicated for use in ESRD patients on dialysis.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites are trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and [³H]-γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in faeces).
    - **Elimination:** Following IV bolus (20 mg/kg), approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours. Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours; terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours. Total body clearance is approximately 4.00 L/h [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients, 20 mg/kg three times weekly after dialysis increased mean pre-dialysis trough levels from 21.3 ± 7.7 µmol/L to 190 ± 55 µmol/L after 8 weeks.
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** Studies comparing 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses showed dose-proportional increases in trough levocarnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Protection:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat.
- **Handling:** Single-dose vials; discard any unused portion as the formulation contains no preservatives.
- **Compatibility:** Stable for 24 hours in PVC bags at room temperature when diluted in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8.0 mg/mL) [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as Rx-only. NDC 36000-368-10 (10-vial carton) [US].
