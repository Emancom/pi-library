# Editorial Product Information: capsicum oleoresin

## 1. Product Identification
- Active ingredient (INN/generic name): **capsicum oleoresin** (contains capsaicin 0.025% as stated in available sources)
- Chemical class: Not specified in available sources
- ATC code: Not specified in available sources
- Route(s) of administration: Topical (cutaneous)

## 3. Approved Indications
- For the temporary relief of minor aches and pains of muscles and joints associated with:
  - simple backache
  - arthritis
  - strains
  - bruises
  - sprains
- Provides local warming analgesic relief.
- Regional differences in indications: Not specified in available sources

## 4. Dosage and Administration
- General dosing (adult):
  - Apply to the affected area not more than 3 to 4 times daily.
- Paediatric dosing:
  - Adults and children 2 years of age and older: apply to affected area not more than 3 to 4 times daily.
  - Children under 2 years of age: do not use; consult a doctor.
- Administration instructions:
  - For external use only.
  - Avoid contact with eyes and other mucous membranes.
  - Wash hands with soap and water after each application to avoid spreading to eyes or other sensitive mucous membranes.
  - Do not apply within 1 hour before or after bathing, showering, hot tub, sauna or vigorous exercise.
  - Do not use with a heating pad or any source of heat.
  - Do not bandage tightly after application.
- Titration schedules: Not specified in available sources
- Missed dose instructions: Not specified in available sources

## 5. Contraindications
- Do not use on wounds or damaged skin.
- Do not use with a heating pad or any source of heat.
- Do not use if allergic to capsicum or any ingredient of the product.
- Do not bandage tightly after application.
- Children under 2 years of age: do not use (consult a doctor).
- For external use only.
- Any additional contraindications: Not specified in available sources

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed warnings
- None specified in available sources.

### General warnings and precautions
- For external use only.
- Avoid contact with eyes and other mucous membranes; accidental contact may cause burning or irritation.
- Do not apply within 1 hour before or after bath, shower, hot tub, sauna or vigorous exercise.
- Do not use concomitantly with local external heat sources (e.g., heating pads).
- Do not bandage tightly over the treated area.
- If swallowed, seek medical help or contact a Poison Control Center immediately.
- Keep out of reach of children.
- If pregnant or breastfeeding, consult a doctor before use.

### Monitoring requirements
- Specific monitoring parameters and schedules: Not specified in available sources

### Specific clinical precautions
- Discontinue use and advise medical review if:
  - Condition worsens.
  - Symptoms persist for more than 7 days.
  - Symptoms clear up and recur within a few days.
  - Redness or irritation develops after application.
