## 3. Approved Indications

- Temporary relief of discomfort due to exposure to wind or sun.  
- May be used as a protectant against further irritation of the eye.

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
- **For external use only.**
- To avoid contamination, do not touch tip of container to any surface. Do not reuse single-use containers; discard after use.
- Do not use if single‑use container is not intact.
- If the solution changes color, do not use.
- Stop use and consult a physician if any of the following occur:
  - Eye pain
  - Changes in vision
  - Continued redness or irritation of the eye
  - Condition worsens or persists for more than 72 hours
- Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, seek medical help or contact a Poison Control Center immediately.
- Boxed warnings: None specified in available sources.

### Monitoring requirements
