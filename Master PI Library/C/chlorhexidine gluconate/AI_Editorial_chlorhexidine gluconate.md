# Editorial Product Information: Chlorhexidine Gluconate

**Editorial Note:** This unified Product Information (PI) document has been compiled by an editorial team based on official regulatory documentation from the Australian Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA) and the United States Food and Drug Administration (FDA). It combines clinical data for chlorhexidine gluconate used both as a wound irrigation solution (often in combination with cetrimide) and as a preservative concentrate for emergency eyewash stations.

---

## 1. Product Name and Active Ingredient
**Product Name:** Chlorhexidine Gluconate (Various Formulations)
**Active Ingredient:** Chlorhexidine gluconate

Formulations may include:
*   **Irrigation Solution:** Chlorhexidine gluconate (0.015% to 0.05% w/v), typically combined with cetrimide (0.15% to 0.5% w/v) [TGA].
*   **Additive Concentrate:** Chlorhexidine gluconate (1% w/v) for use as a preservative [FDA].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Wound and Skin Care:** For the cleansing and irrigation of the skin and contaminated wounds, particularly when a surfactant effect is required to remove debris [TGA].
*   **Ocular Safety Equipment:** For use as a preservative in potable, self-contained emergency eyewash stations [FDA].

## 3. Contraindications
*   **Hypersensitivity:** Known history of allergic reactions to chlorhexidine gluconate or cetrimide [TGA].
*   **Specific Anatomical Sites:** Do not use for the irrigation of the brain, meninges, or the perforated eardrum [TGA].
*   **Concentration:** Do not use additive concentrates in full strength [FDA].
*   **Ocular Irrigation:** The irrigation solution formulation should not be used for direct irrigation of the eyes [TGA].

## 4. Warnings and Precautions
*   **Route of Administration:** For external use only. These products are not for injection and are not isotonic. Chlorhexidine solutions are hemolytic [TGA].
*   **Neonatal Safety:** Use with extreme caution in newborns, particularly premature infants. Application of chlorhexidine may result in significant skin irritation or chemical burns in this population [TGA].
*   **Wound Healing:** Cetrimide, when present in the formulation, may delay the wound healing process. Use should be limited to infection control and initial cleansing rather than long-term wound management [TGA].
*   **Ocular Safety (Concentrates):** If undiluted concentrate comes into contact with the eyes, they must be flushed immediately with clean water. Users should discontinue use and seek medical advice if they experience eye pain, changes in vision, or persistent redness [FDA].
*   **Contamination:** When used in eyewash stations, do not mix with other chemicals and do not reuse the solution once the station is drained [FDA].

## 5. Dosage and Administration
### For Skin and Wound Irrigation [TGA]
1.  Pre-rinse the target area with water.
2.  Apply the minimum volume of solution required to cover the area.
3.  Wash the area gently.
4.  Rinse the area thoroughly.
5.  Discard any remaining solution after a single use.

### For Eyewash Station Maintenance [FDA]
1.  Wear appropriate protective eyewear and gloves during handling.
2.  Clean and rinse the potable eyewash station with water.
3.  Add the entire contents of the additive concentrate bottle to the station.
4.  Fill the station to the manufacturer's required level with potable water.
5.  Stations should be cleaned and refilled every 120 days.

## 6. Adverse Effects
*   **Dermatological:** Local skin irritation and hypersensitivity reactions have been reported. Use should be discontinued if these occur [TGA].
*   **Systemic:** Chlorhexidine may cause severe allergic reactions, including anaphylaxis [TGA].
*   **Ocular:** If used in eyewash stations, potential adverse effects include eye pain, redness, irritation, and changes in vision [FDA].
*   **Wound Management:** Potential for prolonged healing times in wounds treated with cetrimide-containing solutions [TGA].

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **General:** Data regarding specific drug interactions for the irrigation solution are currently unavailable [TGA].
*   **Chemical Incompatibility:** Do not change the specified dilution ratio or mix additive concentrates with other chemicals in eyewash systems [FDA].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Fertility, Pregnancy, and Lactation:** There is currently no available data regarding the effects of these formulations on human fertility, pregnancy, or lactation. Benefit-risk assessment should be conducted by a healthcare professional prior to use [TGA].

## 9. Pharmacokinetics
*   **Absorption:** No specific pharmacokinetic data is available for these topical/irrigation formulations [TGA].

## 10. Mechanism of Action
*   **Chlorhexidine:** A potent antiseptic and disinfectant. It is highly effective against a broad spectrum of vegetative Gram-positive bacteria and, to a lesser extent, Gram-negative organisms. It also shows activity against certain fungi and viruses. It is most active at neutral or slightly acidic pH levels. Its efficacy is reduced in the presence of blood or organic matter [TGA].
*   **Cetrimide:** A quaternary ammonium compound with cationic surfactant properties. It acts as a detergent and emulsifier with bactericidal activity against Gram-positive and Gram-negative bacteria (higher concentrations required for the latter). It is ineffective against bacterial spores, viruses, and acid-fast bacteria [TGA].
*   **Preservative Action:** Used in eyewash stations to prevent the growth of microorganisms in potable water over extended periods [FDA].

## 11. Storage Conditions
*   Store below 25°C (77°F).
*   Protect from light.
*   Single-use only for irrigation; discard unused portions [TGA].
*   Ensure tamper-evident seals are intact before use [FDA].
