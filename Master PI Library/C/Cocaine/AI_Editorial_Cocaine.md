# Editorial Product Information: Cocaine Hydrochloride

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial has been synthesized from the following jurisdiction-specific Product Information (PI) source:
    - **US (FDA):** Numbrino (cocaine hydrochloride) nasal solution [Note: Provided document is a US FDA Prescribing Information document].
- **Purpose:** This document is a harmonized editorial overview intended for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesizes clinical data and regulatory requirements into a single reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI or summary of product characteristics required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians must consult local regulations and official labeling before administration.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Cocaine hydrochloride (an ester-type local anesthetic).
- **Brand Names:** **Numbrino** [US].
- **ATC Code:** R02AD03 (Local anesthetics, cocaine).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Nasal solution: 4% (40 mg/mL) [US].
    - Available in 4 mL single-use and 10 mL multi-use bottles [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intranasal (topical) only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Omnivium Pharmaceuticals LLC [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Local Anesthesia:** Induction of local anesthesia of the mucous membranes for diagnostic procedures and surgeries performed on or through the nasal cavities in adults [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** The recommended dose ranges from 40 mg to 160 mg, depending on the procedure and surface area.
- **Maximum Dose:** The total dose for any single procedure must not exceed 3 mg/kg of cocaine hydrochloride [US].
- **Preparation and Application via Pledgets:**
    - Use cotton or rayon pledgets (recommended size 0.5 inch x 3 inch).
    - Draw up 4 mL of the 4% solution into a calibrated syringe.
    - Stack four pledgets and apply 2 mL to one side, then 2 mL to the reverse side to ensure even distribution.
    - Apply a maximum of two soaked pledgets to each nasal cavity (total four pledgets) for up to 20 minutes [US].
- **Administration Notes:**
    - Do not apply to damaged nasal mucosa.
    - Remove pledgets immediately if signs of an adverse event occur.
    - Discard unused solution and pledgets according to local controlled substance protocols [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known history of hypersensitivity to cocaine hydrochloride, other ester-based local anesthetics, or any inactive components (purified water, citric acid, sodium benzoate, D&C Yellow No. 10, FD&C Green No. 3) [US].
- **Sympathomimetic Sensitivity:** Contraindicated in patients with known idiosyncrasy to sympathomimetic amines [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning (Abuse and Dependence):** CNS stimulants, including cocaine hydrochloride, have a high potential for abuse and dependence [US].
- **Seizures:** Cocaine may lower the convulsive threshold. Monitoring is required, particularly in patients with a history of seizures or EEG abnormalities [US].
- **Cardiovascular Effects:** Significant increases in blood pressure and heart rate occur following administration.
    - **Avoid use** in patients with a history of myocardial infarction, coronary artery disease, congestive heart failure, irregular heart rhythms, abnormal ECG, or uncontrolled hypertension [US].
- **Ophthalmic Use:** **Not for ophthalmic use.** Topical cocaine can cause sloughing, pitting, and ulceration of the corneal epithelium [US].
- **Toxicology Screening:** Cocaine and its metabolites may be detected in plasma for up to one week and in urine for longer than one week post-administration [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions are primarily related to systemic absorption and sympathetic stimulation.
- **Very Common (>10%):** Hypertension (78% at 4% strength), Cardiac disorders (12% at 4% strength) [US].
- **Common (1–10%):** Tachycardia (5%), Sinus tachycardia (2%), QRS prolongation (2%), QT interval prolongation (3%), Bradycardia (3%), Headache, Anxiety [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Myocardial ischemia/infarction, ventricular arrhythmias, seizures, and death have been reported with cocaine use [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacodynamic Interactions:**
    - **Epinephrine/Phenylephrine:** Concomitant use with other vasoconstrictors increases the risk of myocardial ischemia and infarction. Avoid additional vasoconstrictors if possible; if necessary, prolonged ECG monitoring is required [US].
    - **CNS Stimulants:** May result in excessive stimulation, nervousness, or convulsions [US].
    - **MAO Inhibitors:** Cocaine may potentiate the effects and toxicity of MAOIs [US].
    - **TCAs and Sympathomimetics:** Increased risk of cardiovascular adverse reactions [US].
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:**
    - **Disulfiram:** Treatment increases plasma cocaine exposure (AUC and Cmax) several-fold. Avoid use in patients taking disulfiram [US].
    - **Cholinesterase Inhibitors:** Cocaine is metabolized by plasma cholinesterase; inhibitors (e.g., neostigmine, cyclophosphamide, organophosphates) may increase cocaine toxicity [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not explicitly assigned under current PLLR, but data suggests potential fetal harm. Animal studies show hydronephrosis (0.5x HRD), cerebral hemorrhage (2x HRD), and reduced fetal survival (3.7x HRD) [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Cocaine is present in human milk. Due to the risk of serious adverse reactions (vomiting, tremors, seizures, tachycardia), breastfeeding is not recommended during treatment. Patients should pump and discard breast milk for **48 hours** after administration [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal data suggests suppression of ovulation and abnormal sperm morphology at doses 1–2 times the human reference dose [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness in patients under 18 years have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Geriatric patients may be more susceptible to hypertension and cardiovascular events. Dose selection should be cautious and reflect their physical status [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Not studied in this population. Initiation should follow a conservative approach with close monitoring for headache, epistaxis, or heart rate increases [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Avoid use. Cocaine is predominantly metabolized by the liver, and clearance in hepatically impaired patients has not been evaluated [US].
- **Pseudocholinesterase Deficiency:** Patients with genetic abnormalities or conditions that reduce plasma cholinesterase (e.g., severe liver/kidney disease, pregnancy, certain tumors) may have reduced cocaine clearance and increased risk of toxicity [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Local anesthetic of the ester type. It blocks voltage-gated sodium channels, preventing the transient rise in sodium conductance necessary for action potential generation. It also inhibits the reuptake of catecholamines (norepinephrine, dopamine, serotonin) [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Concentration-dependent QTc prolongation occurs, though 4% doses are typically below the regulatory threshold of concern. Significant increases in heart rate (mean 12 bpm increase) are observed [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Estimated mean systemic absorption from a 160 mg dose via pledgets is 23.44% [US].
    - **Distribution:** 84–92% protein bound; crosses the blood-brain barrier and placenta [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily via two hydrolytic pathways: to benzoylecgonine (by hepatic carboxylesterase-1) and ecgonine methyl ester (by plasma butyrylcholinesterase) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Excreted almost exclusively in urine (<5% as unchanged drug). Apparent half-life is approximately 1.54 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Nasal Anesthesia Efficacy:** A multicenter, randomized, double-blind trial (N=639) compared 4% cocaine HCl, 10% cocaine HCl, and placebo.
- **Primary Endpoint:** Anesthetic success (NPRS score of 0 at 20 minutes and sustained throughout the procedure).
- **Outcome:** Success rate was 71% for the 4% concentration compared to 20% for placebo [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F and 86°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Keep in a secure area to protect from diversion [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **US:** Schedule II (CII) controlled substance; Rx-only [US].
- **Australian:** Not specified in provided source [Note: Typically Schedule 8].
