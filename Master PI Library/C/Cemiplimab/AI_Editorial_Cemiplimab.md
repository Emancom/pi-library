# Editorial Product Information: Cemiplimab

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following regulatory authority: **Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of cemiplimab for healthcare professionals, consolidating clinical, pharmacological, and regulatory data.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI or local clinical guidelines. Prescribers should consult the current approved PI in their specific jurisdiction before prescribing.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Cemiplimab
- **Chemical Class:** Fully human immunoglobulin G4 (IgG4) monoclonal antibody; Programmed cell death receptor-1 (PD-1) inhibitor.
- **Brand Name:** **Libtayo** [Australian]
- **ATC Code:** L01FF06
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** 350 mg concentrate for solution for infusion (50 mg/mL) in a 7 mL single-use vial.
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Medison Pharma Australia Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
Cemiplimab is indicated for the following adult populations [Australian]:

- **Cutaneous Squamous Cell Carcinoma (CSCC):** Monotherapy for metastatic (mCSCC) or locally advanced (laCSCC) disease in patients who are not candidates for curative surgery or curative radiation.
- **Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC):** 
    - **First-line Monotherapy:** Adult patients with locally advanced NSCLC (not candidates for resection or chemoradiation) or metastatic NSCLC, expressing PD-L1 (TPS $\ge$ 50%), with no EGFR, ALK, or ROS1 aberrations.
    - **First-line Combination Therapy:** In combination with platinum-based chemotherapy for locally advanced or metastatic NSCLC with no EGFR, ALK, or ROS1 aberrations.
- **Basal Cell Carcinoma (BCC):** Monotherapy for locally advanced or metastatic BCC in patients previously treated with a hedgehog pathway inhibitor (HHI) or for whom an HHI is inappropriate.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** 350 mg administered every 3 weeks (Q3W).
- **Administration:** Intravenous infusion over 30 minutes. Use an intravenous line containing a sterile, non-pyrogenic, low-protein binding, in-line or add-on filter (0.2 to 5 micron pore size).
- **Preparation:** Dilute in 0.9% sodium chloride or 5% glucose to a final concentration of 1 mg/mL to 20 mg/mL. Mix by gentle inversion; do not shake.
- **Dose Modifications:** No dose reductions are recommended. Management of adverse reactions requires withholding or permanent discontinuation based on severity (see Section 5).
- **Duration of Treatment:** Continue until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity.

## 4. Contraindications
- Hypersensitivity to the active substance or any excipients (histidine, sucrose, proline, polysorbate 80).

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Immune-Mediated Adverse Reactions (IMARs):** Cemiplimab can cause severe or fatal IMARs in any organ system. These may occur during or after treatment.
    - **Pneumonitis:** Monitor for signs (dyspnoea, cough). Withhold for Grade 2; permanently discontinue for Grade 3, 4, or recurrent Grade 2.
    - **Colitis:** Monitor for diarrhoea or abdominal pain. Withhold for Grade 2 or 3; permanently discontinue for Grade 4 or recurrent Grade 3.
    - **Hepatitis:** Monitor liver function tests (LFTs). Withhold for Grade 2 with significant ALT/AST/Bilirubin elevations; permanently discontinue for Grade $\ge$ 3.
    - **Endocrinopathies:** Includes thyroid disorders (hypo/hyperthyroidism, thyroiditis), hypophysitis, adrenal insufficiency, and Type 1 Diabetes Mellitus (including DKA). Monitor thyroid function and blood glucose.
    - **Skin Reactions:** Includes rash and severe cutaneous adverse reactions (SCARs) such as SJS and TEN. Permanently discontinue for Grade 4 or confirmed SJS/TEN.
    - **Nephritis:** Monitor renal function. Permanently discontinue for Grade 3 or 4 creatinine increase.
- **Infusion-Related Reactions:** Monitor for signs of hypersensitivity. Interrupt or slow infusion for Grade 1-2; permanently discontinue for Grade 3-4.
- **Traceability:** Ensure the name and batch number of the biological product are recorded.
- **Organ Transplant Rejection:** Reported in the post-marketing setting for PD-1 inhibitors.

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Frequency Category (Monotherapy) [Australian]
| Frequency | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Fatigue (29.9%), Musculoskeletal pain (28.3%), Rash (21.4%), Diarrhoea (16.3%), Anaemia (15.0%), Nausea (14.7%), Decreased appetite (13.0%), Pruritus (12.7%), Constipation (12.3%), Abdominal pain (11.5%), Cough (10.8%) |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Vomiting, Dyspnoea, Pneumonitis, Urinary tract infection, Hypertension, Pyrexia, Peripheral neuropathy, Increased LFTs, Increased creatinine |
| **Uncommon (0.1–1%)** | Stomatitis, Colitis, Gastritis, Myocarditis, Pericarditis, Hypophysitis, Adrenal insufficiency, Sjogren’s syndrome |
| **Rare (<0.1%)** | Type 1 Diabetes Mellitus, Meningitis, Encephalitis, Myasthenia gravis, Guillain-Barre syndrome |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Occurred in 32.4% of monotherapy patients and 25.3% of combination therapy patients.
- **Specific Risks:** Fatalities have been reported due to immune-mediated pneumonitis, hepatitis, nephritis, and myocarditis.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** No formal drug-drug interaction studies have been conducted. As a monoclonal antibody, cemiplimab is not expected to be a substrate for CYP450 enzymes.
- **Corticosteroids/Immunosuppressants:** Avoid systemic corticosteroids (except physiological doses $\le$ 10 mg/day prednisone equivalent) before starting cemiplimab due to potential interference with efficacy. They may be used after treatment initiation to manage IMARs.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** **Category D** [Australian]. Based on its mechanism of action and animal studies, cemiplimab may cause foetal harm. PD-1 blockade is associated with increased foetal loss.
- **Contraception:** Women of childbearing potential must use effective contraception during treatment and for at least 4 months after the final dose.
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if cemiplimab is secreted in human milk. IgG4 is known to be present in milk. Breastfeeding is not recommended during treatment and for 4 months post-treatment.
- **Fertility:** No clinical data available. Animal studies showed no effects on reproductive organs.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy in children <18 years have not been established [Australian]. Study 1690 did not demonstrate OS/PFS improvement in paediatric CNS tumours.
- **Elderly Use:** No dose adjustment required. No overall differences in safety or effectiveness observed between elderly ($\ge$ 65 years) and younger patients.
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment recommended for mild, moderate, or severe (CLcr 15-29 mL/min) impairment. Data are limited for severe impairment.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment for mild or moderate impairment. Not studied in patients with severe hepatic impairment.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Cemiplimab is an IgG4 mAb that binds to the PD-1 receptor, blocking its interaction with PD-L1 and PD-L2. This releases PD-1 pathway-mediated inhibition of T-cell responses, including anti-tumour activity.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Primarily vascular ($V_{ss}$ approx. 5.9 L).
    - **Metabolism:** Degraded into small peptides and amino acids.
    - **Elimination:** Steady-state clearance is 0.22 L/day. Half-life at steady state is 22 days.
    - **Steady State:** Achieved after approximately 4 months.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **CSCC (Study 1540):** Phase 2 trial. Confirmed ORR (by ICR) was 47.2% in combined mCSCC/laCSCC cohorts. Median duration of response (DOR) was 41.3 months.
- **NSCLC (Study 1624 - Monotherapy):** Statistically significant improvement in OS (Median 22.1 vs 14.3 months) and PFS compared to chemotherapy in patients with PD-L1 $\ge$ 50%.
- **NSCLC (Study 16113 - Combination):** Combination with platinum-chemo showed superior OS (Median 21.9 vs 13.0 months) compared to chemotherapy alone.
- **BCC (Study 1620):** ORR was 32.1% in laBCC and 24.1% in mBCC for patients previously treated with HHIs.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Unopened Vial:** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C. Store in original carton to protect from light. Do not freeze or shake.
- **Shelf Life:** 48 months [Australian].
- **After Dilution:** Administer immediately. If not used immediately, store for up to 8 hours at room temperature (up to 25°C) or up to 10 days refrigerated (2°C to 8°C).

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule:** Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine) [Australian].
- **Additional Monitoring:** This product is subject to additional monitoring in Australia (indicated by the inverted black triangle).
