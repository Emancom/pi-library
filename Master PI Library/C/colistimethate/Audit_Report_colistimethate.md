## Originality Strengths

*   **Synthesis of Regulatory Variation:** The document successfully identifies and labels region-specific content (e.g., highlighting the TGA-specific Myasthenia Gravis contraindication vs. the FDA-specific CDAD warning).
*   **Structure:** The use of standard medical headers and a clear layout for healthcare professionals is an original organizational effort.
*   **Editorial Note:** The introductory paragraph is entirely original and clearly states the methodology of the document.

## Differences from Source PIs

*   **Harmonization of Units:** The editorial helpfully includes both mg (FDA standard) and IU (TGA standard) in the product description.
*   **Consolidation of Safety Data:** The editorial merges the TGA's detailed warning on Myasthenia Gravis with the FDA's detailed warning on Pseudo-Bartter syndrome, which are not combined in either original document.
*   **Omission of Commercial Names:** The editorial removes brand names (Coly-Mycin M / Colistin Link) in favor of the generic active ingredient name.

## Recommendation

**NEEDS REVISION**

The editorial fails the originality audit because it relies too heavily on "cut-and-paste" text for the clinical descriptions, warnings, and administration instructions. While medical accuracy is paramount, regulatory compliance also requires that editorial summaries be original works of synthesis to avoid copyright infringement of manufacturer-held PI text.

**Required Changes:**
1.  **Rewrite Indications:** Rephrase the therapeutic indications using original prose (e.g., "This medication is utilized for managing persistent or sudden infections...").
2.  **Rewrite Warnings:** The "Neurological Disturbances" and "Electrolyte Abnormalities" sections contain long strings of verbatim text. These must be rewritten (e.g., "Patients should be monitored for sensory issues like numbness or tingling...").
3.  **Instructional Prose:** The reconstitution and administration sections should be reworded to explain the process rather than copying the manufacturer's manual step-by-step.
4.  **Table Formatting:** While the data in the table cannot change, the headers and presentation should be distinct from the source documents.
