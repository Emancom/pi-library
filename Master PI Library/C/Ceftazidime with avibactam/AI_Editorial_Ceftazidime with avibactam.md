# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
*   **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA)
*   **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of **levocarnitine** injection, synthesized for use by healthcare professionals. 
*   **Prescribing Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and should not be used as a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. The information provided is based on the United States [US] regulatory submission for **levocarnitine** injection.

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (a naturally occurring carrier molecule required for mammalian energy metabolism).
*   **Chemical Class:** (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
*   **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection, USP (Baxter Healthcare Corporation).
*   **ATC Code:** A16AA01 (Not explicitly stated in source, but standard for levocarnitine).
*   **Dosage Form and Strength:** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) single-dose vial [US].
*   **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only [US].
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency (Metabolic):** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that result in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
*   **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with end-stage renal disease who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
*   **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg, administered as a slow 2- to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion [US]. 
    *   **Loading Dose:** In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose is often administered, followed by an equivalent dose over the next 24 hours [US].
    *   **Frequency:** Doses should be given every 3 or 4 hours (q3h or q4h), and never less frequently than every 6 hours (q6h) [US].
    *   **Maximum Dose:** The highest reported dose is 300 mg/kg [US].
*   **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:** The recommended starting dose is 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
    *   **Administration:** Administered as a slow 2- to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line after each dialysis session [US].

### Monitoring and Titration
*   **Metabolic Disorders:** Dosing should be guided by weekly and monthly monitoring of plasma free carnitine concentrations (target range: 35 to 60 µmol/L), blood chemistries, and vital signs [US].
*   **ESRD:** Therapy may be initiated if trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine levels are below the normal range of 40 to 50 µmol/L. Downward dose adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be made as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough levels [US].

### Administration Instructions
*   Inspect parenteral products for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration [US].
*   **Compatibility:** Stable when mixed in Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations ranging from 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL. Mixed solutions in PVC bags are stable for up to 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
*   There are no known contraindications specified in the available sources [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
*   **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, particularly in ESRD patients on dialysis. Some reactions occur within minutes of administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and consider the risks/benefits before re-administration [US].
*   **Seizures:** Levocarnitine has been associated with seizures in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been reported [US].
*   **Renal Impairment (Oral Formulation Caution):** The safety of *oral* levocarnitine in patients with renal insufficiency has not been established. Chronic high-dose oral administration in these patients may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites, trimethylamine (TMA) and trimethylamine-N-oxide (TMAO) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Serious Adverse Effects
*   **Anaphylaxis and Hypersensitivity:** [US].
*   **Seizures:** Reported in patients with and without history [US].

### Common Adverse Effects (ESRD Clinical Trials)
The following events were reported at a frequency of ≥5% in placebo-controlled trials of ESRD patients (regardless of causality) [US]:
*   **Body as a Whole:** Injection site reaction (33%), pain (30%), flu syndrome (25%), headache (22%), infection (15%), abdominal pain (9%).
*   **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (20%), hypotension (14%), tachycardia (6%), palpitations (5%).
*   **Digestive:** Diarrhea (16%), vomiting (15%), nausea (8%), anorexia (5%), dyspepsia (5%).
*   **Nervous:** Dizziness (13%), insomnia (4%), paresthesia (5%), depression (5%).
*   **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (23%), cough increase (9%), rhinitis (9%), dyspnea (7%).
*   **Other:** Anemia (6%), hypercalcemia (9%), hyperkalemia (6%), pruritus (5%).

### Post-Marketing Experience
*   Transient nausea and vomiting, gastritis, and body odor have been observed [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have been observed when used concomitantly with levocarnitine. It is recommended to monitor INR levels following initiation or dose adjustment of levocarnitine in patients on warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy:** Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. No adequate, well-controlled studies in pregnant women exist. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
*   **Breastfeeding:** Not specifically studied in humans. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine concentrations in milk after exogenous administration. Weigh the benefits to the mother against potential risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant; discontinuation of nursing or treatment may be considered [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders follows the weight-based 50 mg/kg protocol. Safety and efficacy in children for ESRD are not separately detailed beyond the general dosing instructions [US].
*   **Elderly Use:** Specific dose adjustments for the elderly are not provided in the source material [US].
*   **Renal Impairment:** See **Warnings and Precautions**. Levocarnitine is easily cleared by dialysis [US].
*   **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
Levocarnitine is a carrier molecule essential for the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane. This process delivers substrates for oxidation and energy production. In patients with inborn errors of metabolism, levocarnitine promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters, which are rapidly eliminated [US].

### Pharmacokinetics
*   **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
*   **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily feces) [US].
*   **Elimination:** Following a single IV bolus (20 mg/kg), approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. The mean apparent terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].
*   **Bioavailability:** Oral bioavailability is low (approximately 15–16%) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   **ESRD Dosing Studies:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients receiving 20 mg/kg IV three times weekly post-dialysis showed mean pre-dialysis (trough) levels increased from ~20 µmol/L to ~190 µmol/L after 8 weeks.
*   **Dose-Ranging Data:** A study comparing 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses in ESRD patients found that trough levels increased in a dose-dependent manner over 12 and 24 weeks.
*   **Clinical Outcome Note:** While IV levocarnitine increases carnitine concentrations in ESRD patients, the effects on specific clinical outcomes and signs/symptoms of deficiency in this population have not been determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
*   **Handling:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat.
*   **Vial Use:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as it contains no preservatives [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
*   **Legal Classification:** Rx only [US].
*   **Pharmacopeial Status:** Levocarnitine Injection, USP [US].
