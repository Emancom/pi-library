# Editorial Product Information: Coagulation Factor IX (Recombinant) [RIXUBIS]

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided from the following jurisdiction: **US (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical and regulatory data into a single reference point.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for the official PI for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory documents for specific legal and prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Coagulation factor IX (recombinant).
- **Chemical Class:** Recombinant DNA-derived coagulation factor.
- **Brand Name(s):** **RIXUBIS** [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Lyophilized powder for solution in single-dose vials containing nominally 250, 500, 1000, 2000, or 3000 international units (IU). Each kit includes 5 mL of Sterile Water for Injection and a BAXJECT II transfer device [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) injection.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Takeda Pharmaceuticals America, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
**RIXUBIS** is indicated in adults and children with hemophilia B (congenital factor IX deficiency) for [US]:
- Control and prevention of bleeding episodes (on-demand treatment).
- Perioperative management of bleeding.
- Routine prophylaxis to reduce the frequency of bleeding episodes.

**Important Limitation:** **RIXUBIS** is not indicated for the induction of immune tolerance in patients with hemophilia B [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing Principles
- Treatment should be initiated under the supervision of a physician experienced in hemophilia management.
- Dosage depends on the severity of the deficiency, location/extent of bleeding, clinical condition, age, and individual pharmacokinetic parameters (recovery and half-life).
- Dosing may differ from plasma-derived products; individual recovery monitoring is essential.
- Monitor factor IX (FIX) activity levels using a one-stage clotting assay to ensure targets are met.

### Calculating Initial Dose
The initial dose is determined by the patient's age and observed incremental recovery [US]:
- **Patients <12 years:** 1 IU/kg increases circulating FIX by 0.7 IU/dL.
    - *Calculation:* Dose (IU) = Body weight (kg) × Desired FIX increase (% or IU/dL) × 1.4 dL/kg.
- **Patients ≥12 years:** 1 IU/kg increases circulating FIX by 0.9 IU/dL.
    - *Calculation:* Dose (IU) = Body weight (kg) × Desired FIX increase (% or IU/dL) × 1.1 dL/kg.

### On-demand Treatment and Perioperative Management [US]
| Type of Bleed/Surgery | Target FIX Level (% or IU/dL) | Dosing Interval (Hours) | Duration of Therapy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Minor Bleed** | 20–30 | 12–24 | At least 1 day until healing |
| **Moderate Bleed** | 25–50 | 12–24 | 2–7 days until healing |
| **Major Bleed** | 50–100 | 12–24 | 7–10 days until healing |
| **Minor Surgery** | 30–60 | 24 | At least 1 day until healing |
| **Major Surgery** | 80–100 | 8–24 | 7–10 days until healing |

### Routine Prophylaxis [US]
- **Patients <12 years:** 60 to 80 IU/kg twice weekly.
- **Patients ≥12 years:** 40 to 60 IU/kg twice weekly.

### Administration Instructions
- **Reconstitution:** Use the provided BAXJECT II device and Sterile Water for Injection. Do not refrigerate after reconstitution. Administer within 3 hours.
- **Infusion:** Intravenous bolus only. Rate should not exceed 10 mL/min.
- Inspect for particulate matter; the solution should be clear and colorless.

## 4. Contraindications
**RIXUBIS** is contraindicated in patients with [US]:
- Known hypersensitivity to the drug, its excipients, or hamster protein.
- Disseminated Intravascular Coagulation (DIC).
- Clinical signs of fibrinolysis.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Anaphylaxis and allergic reactions (angioedema, chest tightness, hypotension, wheezing) may occur. Risk is highest during early exposure in previously untreated patients (PUPs). Discontinue immediately if symptoms arise.
- **Neutralizing Antibodies (Inhibitors):** Patients should be monitored for FIX inhibitors using Bethesda Units (BUs). If target levels are not achieved or bleeding is not controlled, perform an inhibitor assay.
- **Nephrotic Syndrome:** Reported following attempted immune tolerance induction with FIX products in patients with inhibitors.
- **Thromboembolic Complications:** Risk of pulmonary embolism, venous/arterial thrombosis, and DIC. Monitor patients with liver disease, signs of fibrinolysis, or those in perioperative settings.
- **Monitoring:** Monitor FIX activity levels to confirm adequate coverage.

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Clinical Trial Experience [US]
Common adverse reactions (>1% of subjects):
- **Nervous System:** Dysgeusia (1.01%).
- **Musculoskeletal:** Pain in extremity (1.01%).
- **Investigations:** Positive furin antibody test (2.02%); note that no clinical adverse findings were associated with these antibodies.

### Postmarketing Experience [US]
The following have been reported voluntarily (frequency not established):
- **Immune System:** Hypersensitivity (dyspnea, pruritus).
- **Skin:** Urticaria, rash.
- **Class Effects:** Inadequate FIX recovery, inhibitor development, anaphylaxis, angioedema, hypotension, and thrombosis.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not specified in modern labeling; stated as "no data"]. Animal reproduction studies have not been conducted. It is unknown if the product causes fetal harm or affects reproduction.
- **Breastfeeding:** Presence in human milk is unknown. Consider the developmental benefits of breastfeeding alongside the mother’s clinical need and potential infant risks.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Evaluated in 23 previously treated patients (PTPs) <12 years. Clearance (per kg body weight) was 30% higher in children 6–12 years and 59% higher in children <6 years compared to adults. Dose adjustment is required due to lower recovery (22% lower than adults) [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical studies did not include subjects ≥65 years. Dosing should be individualized [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Specific dose adjustments for renal or hepatic impairment are not specified, though patients with liver disease require monitoring for thromboembolic risks [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Temporarily replaces missing coagulation factor IX required for hemostasis in hemophilia B patients.
- **Pharmacokinetics (Adults/Adolescents ≥12 years):**
    - **Mean Half-life:** ~25–27 hours.
    - **Mean Residence Time:** ~30 hours.
    - **Clearance:** ~6.0–6.4 mL/(kg·hr).
    - **Volume of Distribution (Vss):** ~178–202 mL/kg.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Increases plasma FIX levels and corrects the coagulation defect by decreasing activated partial thromboplastin time (aPTT).

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **On-demand Treatment (≥12 years):** Hemostatic efficacy rated "excellent" or "good" in 96% of 249 bleeding episodes. Most bleeds (84.7%) were controlled with 1–2 infusions [US].
- **On-demand Treatment (<12 years):** Hemostatic efficacy rated "excellent" or "good" in 96.2% of 26 bleeds [US].
- **Routine Prophylaxis (≥12 years):** Mean annualized bleeding rate (ABR) was 4.3 in the prophylaxis arm compared to 33.9 in the on-demand arm. 42.9% of subjects had zero bleeds [US].
- **Routine Prophylaxis (<12 years):** Mean ABR was 2.7, with 39.1% of subjects experiencing zero bleeds [US].
- **Perioperative Management:** Efficacy rated "excellent" in 11/14 major/minor surgeries at discharge [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at refrigerated temperature [2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F)] or room temperature [not to exceed 30°C (86°F)] for up to 36 months [US].
- **Handling:** Do not freeze. Use within 3 hours of reconstitution. Discard any unused portion.
- **Latex:** Not made with natural rubber latex [US].
