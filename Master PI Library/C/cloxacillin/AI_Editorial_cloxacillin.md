# Editorial Product Information: Cloxacillin Benzathine (Intramammary)

## Editorial Note
*   **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA)
*   **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonized editorial overview of cloxacillin benzathine (intramammary formulation) for use by healthcare professionals and veterinary practitioners. 
*   **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not intended to replace the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing in specific jurisdictions. This particular product is indicated for veterinary use in bovine subjects.

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredient:** Cloxacillin benzathine (a semisynthetic penicillin derived from 6-aminopenicillanic acid).
*   **Brand Name(s):** Orbenin-DC [US].
*   **ATC Code:** QJ51CF02 (Veterinary).
*   **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Intramammary infusion suspension. Each 7.5 g disposable syringe contains cloxacillin benzathine equivalent to 500 mg of cloxacillin in a stable, non-irritating base [US].
*   **Route(s) of Administration:** Intramammary infusion.
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Intervet Inc., a subsidiary of Merck & Co., Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Treatment and Prophylaxis of Bovine Mastitis:** Indicated for use in non-lactating (dry) cows to address infections caused by *Staphylococcus aureus* and *Streptococcus agalactiae* [US].
*   **Penicillinase-Resistant Activity:** Specific for use against penicillin G-resistant staphylococci that may cause mastitis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
*   **General Dosing (Adult Bovine):** Infuse the contents of one syringe (500 mg cloxacillin equivalent) into each quarter of the udder [US].
*   **Administration Instructions:**
    *   Administration must occur at the final milking of the lactation period [US].
    *   The cow should be milked out normally prior to infusion [US].
    *   Teats must be cleaned and disinfected using the provided alcohol swabs [US].
    *   The syringe should be warmed to room temperature before infusion [US].
    *   Following infusion, do not "milk out." The cow should be milked normally only after calving [US].
*   **Clinical Strategy:** In heavily infected herds, clinical data supports treating all cows as they are dried off. In herds with lower infection rates, selective treatment of infected quarters may be considered [US].

## 4. Contraindications
*   **Short Dry Period:** Due to the relatively low solubility and prolonged activity of cloxacillin benzathine, this product must not be used in cows with a dry period of less than four weeks (28 days) [US]. 
*   **Rationale:** Use in cows with a short dry period may result in antibiotic residues in the milk following the removal of colostrum [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
*   **Boxed Warnings:** None specified in available sources.
*   **General Warnings:**
    *   **Human Use:** Veterinary use only; not for human use [US].
    *   **Withdrawal Period (Food Safety):** Animals must not be slaughtered for food, and milk must not be used for food, within 4 weeks (28 days) of treatment [US].
*   **Clinical Precautions:**
    *   **Allergic Potential:** As a derivative of 6-aminopenicillanic acid, there is a potential for allergic reactions. While rare, these should be managed with standard emergency agents (e.g., antihistamines, pressor amines) [US].
*   **Monitoring Requirements:** Perform appropriate laboratory tests, including aseptic collection of pretreatment milk samples for in vitro culturing and susceptibility testing [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
*   **Allergic Reactions:** Rare potential for hypersensitivity reactions typical of the penicillin class [US].
*   **Local Irritation:** The suspension is formulated to be non-irritating to mammary tissue [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy:** Indicated specifically for use during the "dry period" (the non-lactating stage of the bovine pregnancy cycle) [US].
*   **Breastfeeding (Lactation):** Not for use in actively lactating cows. Must be administered at least 28 days prior to calving to avoid residue in milk intended for consumption [US].
*   **Fertility:** Not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use:** Not applicable for this bovine-specific product.
*   **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
*   **Mechanism of Action:** Cloxacillin benzathine is bactericidal. It inhibits the biosynthesis of the bacterial cell wall mucopeptide during the stage of active multiplication [US].
*   **Pharmacokinetics:** 
    *   **Solubility:** The benzathine salt has low solubility [US].
    *   **Duration:** The low solubility results in an extended period of antimicrobial activity within the mammary gland [US].
*   **Microbiology:** Active against Gram-positive organisms, including *Staphylococcus aureus* (including penicillinase-producing strains) and *Streptococcus agalactiae* [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   **Herd Management Studies:** Source documents reference clinical studies proving the efficacy of "dry cow therapy" in heavily infected herds to reduce the buildup of subclinical mastitis, which otherwise impacts milk yield and increases the risk of cross-infection and clinical flare-ups in subsequent lactations [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   **Temperature:** Do not store above 24°C (75°F) [US].
*   **Handling:** Supplied in pails of 144 single-dose syringes or cartons of 12 syringes. Alcohol wipes are included for disinfection [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
*   **Legal Classification:** 
    *   **United States:** Prescription Animal Drug (Caution: Federal law restricts this drug to use by or on the order of a licensed veterinarian) [US].
