# Editorial Product Information: Crizanlizumab

## Editorial Note
- The information contained in this editorial was synthesized from the following jurisdiction-specific Product Information (PI):
    - **United States (FDA)**: Adakveo (crizanlizumab-tmca) injection.
- **Purpose**: This document provides a harmonized editorial overview for healthcare professionals, consolidating regulatory guidance on the clinical use of crizanlizumab. 
- **Disclaimer**: This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official, current Product Information required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient**: Crizanlizumab (specifically crizanlizumab-tmca in the US).
- **Chemical Class**: Selectin blocker; humanized IgG2 kappa monoclonal antibody.
- **Brand Name(s)**: Adakveo [US].
- **ATC Code**: Not specified in available sources (Suggested: B06EB03).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s)**: Injection: 100 mg/10 mL (10 mg/mL) solution supplied in a single-dose vial [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration**: Intravenous (IV) infusion only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor**: Novartis Pharmaceuticals Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Sickle Cell Disease (SCD)**: Indicated to reduce the frequency of vaso-occlusive crises (VOCs) in adult and pediatric patients aged 16 years and older [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult and Pediatric ≥ 16 years)**: The recommended dose is 5 mg/kg administered via intravenous infusion.
- **Schedule**: Doses are administered at Week 0, Week 2, and every 4 weeks thereafter [US].
- **Administration Instructions**:
    - Must be prepared and administered by a healthcare professional using aseptic technique.
    - Dilute in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or 5% Dextrose to a total volume of 100 mL.
    - Infuse over a period of 30 minutes.
    - Use an infusion line containing a sterile, nonpyrogenic, low-protein binding 0.2-micron inline filter.
    - Do not co-administer with other drugs through the same intravenous line.
    - Flush the line with at least 25 mL of compatible diluent following the infusion [US].
- **Missed Dose Instructions**:
    - Administer the missed dose as soon as possible.
    - If administered within 2 weeks of the missed date, resume the original schedule.
    - If administered more than 2 weeks after the missed date, continue dosing every 4 weeks from that point forward [US].
- **Monitoring**: Physicians should reevaluate treatment at least annually to assess response and consider discontinuation if no clinical benefit is perceived [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None listed in the available source document [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Infusion-Related Reactions (IRRs)**:
    - May occur during or within 24 hours of infusion. Symptoms include pain, nausea, vomiting, fatigue, dizziness, pruritus, fever, and chills.
    - **Management**: Monitor for signs of IRRs. For severe reactions, discontinue the infusion and manage medically (permanent discontinuation may be necessary). For mild or moderate reactions, temporarily interrupt or slow the infusion rate and initiate symptomatic treatment (e.g., acetaminophen, NSAIDs, opioids).
    - **Premedication**: Consider premedication or rate reduction for subsequent infusions if IRRs have occurred.
    - **Corticosteroids**: Exercise caution when using corticosteroids in SCD patients unless clinically indicated (e.g., anaphylaxis), as they may increase the risk of acute chest syndrome or fat embolism [US].
- **Laboratory Test Interference**: 
    - Crizanlizumab interferes with automated platelet counts by causing platelet clumping, particularly in EDTA-containing tubes. This may lead to falsely decreased platelet counts.
    - **Mitigation**: Run tests as soon as possible after collection or use citrate tubes. If necessary, estimate platelet counts via peripheral blood smear [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Very Common (≥10%)**: 
    - Headache (25%)
    - Arthralgia (18%)
    - Nausea (18%)
    - Back pain (15%)
    - Fatigue/Asthenia/Malaise (13%)
    - Abdominal pain/tenderness (12%)
    - Pyrexia (11%)
    - Diarrhea (11%)
    - Vomiting (10%)
    - Oropharyngeal pain (10%) [US].
- **Common (1–10%)**: Musculoskeletal chest pain, myalgia, dizziness, pruritus, and infusion-site reactions (pain, swelling, extravasation) [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects**: Pyrexia and pain were reported as serious adverse reactions in clinical trials [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Laboratory Interactions**: Interaction with EDTA in automated platelet counting leads to platelet clumping [US].
- **Hydroxyurea**: No clinically meaningful effect on crizanlizumab pharmacokinetics was observed when co-administered with hydroxyurea [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy**: 
    - **US Category**: Not specified (Lettered categories are no longer used in current US labeling; however, the PI states "May cause fetal harm").
    - Animal studies in monkeys showed increased fetal loss at doses 2.8 times the human clinical exposure. Use during pregnancy only if the potential benefit justifies the potential risk to the fetus [US].
- **Breastfeeding**: Data are unavailable regarding the presence of crizanlizumab in human milk or its effects on the breastfed infant. The developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding should be weighed against the mother's clinical need and potential risks [US].
- **Fertility**: No adverse effects on male or female reproductive organs were observed in a 26-week repeat-dose toxicity study in monkeys [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use**: Safety and efficacy are established in patients aged 16 years and older. Safety and efficacy in children under 16 years of age have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use**: Clinical trials did not include sufficient numbers of patients aged 65 and over to determine if they respond differently than younger patients [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment**: The effects of renal or hepatic impairment on the pharmacokinetics of crizanlizumab are unknown [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action**: Crizanlizumab is a P-selectin blocker. By binding to P-selectin on the surface of activated endothelium and platelets, it blocks interactions with ligands like PSGL-1. This prevents the adhesion of platelets, leukocytes, and red blood cells to the vessel wall, thereby inhibiting the multicellular adhesion that contributes to vaso-occlusion [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics**: Causes dose-dependent P-selectin inhibition (ex vivo) [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics**: 
    - **Distribution**: Mean volume of distribution is 4.26 L.
    - **Metabolism**: Expected to be degraded into small peptides via catabolic pathways.
    - **Elimination**: Terminal elimination half-life is approximately 10.6 days in healthy volunteers and 11.4 days in patients with SCD [US].
- **Immunogenicity**: Treatment-induced anti-drug antibodies were rarely detected (0% in one trial, 1.6% in another) and did not appear to impact pharmacokinetics or pharmacodynamics [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **SUSTAIN Trial**: A 52-week, double-blind, placebo-controlled study (N=198). Crizanlizumab 5 mg/kg significantly reduced the median annual rate of VOCs compared to placebo (1.63 vs. 2.98, p=0.010). The median time to the first VOC was 4.1 months for crizanlizumab vs. 1.4 months for placebo [US].
- **STAND Trial**: A randomized, placebo-controlled study in 252 patients. This trial failed to confirm the statistical superiority of crizanlizumab over placebo in reducing VOCs leading to a healthcare visit during the first year of treatment [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Unopened Vials**: Store and transport refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) in the original carton to protect from light. Do not shake or freeze [US].
- **Diluted Solution**: 
    - Store at room temperature (up to 25°C) for no more than 4.5 hours from the start of preparation to the completion of infusion.
    - Store under refrigeration at 2°C to 8°C for no more than 24 hours (including warming time). Protect from light [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification**: 
    - **United States**: Rx-only (Prescription only) [US].
