# Editorial Product Information: Candida Albicans Skin Test Antigen (**CANDIN**)

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information provided by the **United States (FDA)**. 
- At the time of this publication, only the US-specific regulatory documentation was available for review. 
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. 
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** *Candida albicans* skin test antigen (derived from the culture filtrate and cells of two strains of *Candida albicans*).
- **Brand Name:** **CANDIN** [US].
- **ATC Code:** V04CL (Other allergenic extracts, diagnostic agents).
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Solution for intradermal injection; 1 mL multi-dose vial containing ten 0.1 mL doses. Each 0.1 mL dose is intended to elicit an induration response $\ge$ 5 mm in immunologically competent persons with cellular hypersensitivity [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intradermal (intracutaneous) injection only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Nielsen BioSciences, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Recall Antigen for Cellular Hypersensitivity:** Indicated for use as a recall antigen to assess cell-mediated (delayed-type) hypersensitivity (DTH) by intracutaneous testing [US].
- **Assessment of Cellular Immunity:** May be utilised to evaluate the cellular immune response in patients where reduced cellular hypersensitivity is suspected [US].
- **Limitation of Use:** This product is NOT indicated for the diagnosis or treatment of Type 1 (immediate) allergy to *Candida albicans* [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **Standard Dose:** 0.1 mL administered intradermally [US].
- **Administration Technique:** 
    - Inspect the solution for particulates or discoloration prior to use.
    - Cleanse the skin (volar surface of the forearm or outer aspect of the upper arm) with 70% alcohol.
    - Inject as superficially as possible to create a distinct, sharply defined bleb. 
    - **Caution:** Subcutaneous injection may result in an unreliable reaction [US].
- **Reading the Results:**
    - The skin test should be read at 48 hours by visual inspection and palpation.
    - **Measurement:** Measure two diameters: the longest diameter and the midpoint orthogonal diameter.
    - **Interpretation:** The DTH response is the mean of these two diameters. A positive reaction is defined as a mean induration of $\ge$ 5 mm [US].
- **Concurrent Testing:** Because not all individuals with normal immunity are hypersensitive to *Candida*, the concurrent use of other licensed DTH skin test antigens is recommended [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Extreme Hypersensitivity:** **CANDIN** must not be used in patients who have experienced a previous unacceptable adverse reaction (e.g., extreme hypersensitivity or allergy) to this antigen or similar products [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning (Systemic Reactions):**
    - Systemic reactions, including life-threatening anaphylaxis, can occur with skin test antigens. 
    - Patients must be observed for at least 20 minutes following administration. 
    - Emergency facilities, equipment, and trained personnel (including epinephrine and oxygen) must be immediately available [US].
- **Administration Site Reactions:** The expected response is a local area of inflammation reaching maximum diameter at 24–48 hours. Larger, accelerated reactions may occur, requiring cold compresses and anti-inflammatory medication [US].
- **Intravenous Injection:** **CANDIN** must never be given intravenously. Care must be taken to avoid accidental injection into a blood vessel [US].
- **Bleeding Tendencies:** In patients with bleeding tendencies, bruising and non-specific induration may occur due to the mechanical trauma of the skin test [US].
- **Immediate Hypersensitivity:** Some patients may exhibit exquisite immediate hypersensitivity to the antigen [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Local (Common):** 
    - Swelling, pruritus, and vesiculation at the test site.
    - Immediate local reactions (edematous hive with erythema) appearing within 15–20 minutes.
    - In HIV-positive patients, pruritus and swelling typically clear within 48 hours, though vesiculation with weeping edema may take one week to resolve [US].
- **Serious/Rare:**
    - Necrosis and ulceration (theoretically possible in highly sensitive individuals).
    - Severe local reactions: rash, bullae, dermal exfoliation, and cellulitis.
    - Systemic reactions: Sneezing, coughing, dyspnea, abdominal cramps, vomiting, tachycardia, and hypotension [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Corticosteroids/Immunosuppressants:** Pharmacologic doses of corticosteroids may suppress the DTH skin test response after two weeks of therapy. Sensitivity usually returns to baseline several weeks after discontinuation [US].
- **Beta-blocking Drugs:** Patients receiving beta-blockers may be refractive to standard doses of epinephrine if needed to treat a systemic allergic reaction [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Animal reproduction studies have not been conducted. It is unknown if **CANDIN** causes fetal harm or affects reproductive capacity. Administer to pregnant women only if clearly needed [US].
- **Labor and Delivery:** Information regarding the effects on childbirth is unavailable [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** It is not known if the antigen is excreted in human milk. Exercise caution when administering to nursing mothers [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness in the paediatric population have not been established [US].
- **Geriatric Use:** Not adequately studied in geriatric patients. DTH responses may be diminished in this population due to the natural age-related decline in cell-mediated immunity [US].
- **Immunocompromised Patients:** HIV-infected patients and those with certain cancers often show significantly reduced or absent responses to **CANDIN** (see Clinical Trials) [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Assessment of DTH via intracutaneous testing. A positive test indicates prior antigenic exposure, T-cell competency, and an intact inflammatory response [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** The DTH response involves an infiltration of CD4+ and CD8+ T lymphocytes and macrophages at the site of antigen deposition. The reaction typically peaks between 24 and 48 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Healthy Adults:** In clinical studies, 60% to 78% of healthy adults exhibited a positive response ($\ge$ 5 mm induration) at 48 hours [US].
- **HIV Infection:** Response rates are significantly lower in patients with AIDS (28%) compared to healthy controls (78%). In HIV-infected patients, **CANDIN** may increase the accuracy of interpreting negative tuberculin reactions [US].
- **Cancer:** In patients with lung cancer, 28% reacted positively. In patients with metastatic cancer, 0% of those studied exhibited a positive response [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store between 2°C and 8°C (35°F–46°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Do not freeze. Discard the product if particles or discoloration are observed [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as Rx-only. No U.S. Standard of Potency [US].
