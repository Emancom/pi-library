## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Crotamiton (synthetic).
- **Chemical Class:** N-ethyl-N-(o-methylphenyl)-2-butenamide.
- **Brand Name:** Eurax [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources (standard code is P03AX03).
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Topical cream, 10% (w/w) [US]. 
- **Route of Administration:** Topical (External use only).
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Journey Medical Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Scabies:** Eradication of *Sarcoptes scabiei* infestations [US].
- **Pruritus:** Symptomatic management of pruritic skin conditions [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Scabies Management
- **Adult Dosing:** 
    - **Initial Application:** After bathing and drying the skin, thoroughly massage the cream into the skin from the chin down to the toes. Pay specific attention to skin folds, creases, interdigital spaces, and under the fingernails (which should be trimmed short).
    - **Second Application:** A second application is recommended 24 hours after the first.
    - **Completion:** A cleansing bath should be taken 48 hours after the final application.
- **Environmental Hygiene:** Patients should change clothing and bed linens the morning after application. Contaminated items should be dry-cleaned or laundered in a hot wash cycle [US].

### Pruritus Management
- **Adult Dosing:** Massage the cream gently into the affected areas until fully absorbed. Repeat the application as necessary [US].

### Administration Notes
- **Quantity:** A 60g tube is generally sufficient for two complete applications in an adult [US].
- **Precautions:** Do not apply to the eyes, mouth, or intravaginal areas.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known allergy or sensitivity to crotamiton or any excipients in the formulation [US].
- **Primary Irritation:** Patients who exhibit a primary irritation response to topical medications [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Severe Irritation:** Treatment must be discontinued if severe irritation or sensitisation occurs [US].
- **Skin Integrity:** Do not apply to acutely inflamed, raw, or weeping skin surfaces until the inflammation has subsided [US].
- **Accidental Ingestion:** Avoid contact with the mouth. Ingestion may lead to mucosal burning, nausea, and vomiting [US].
- **Ophthalmic Exposure:** Avoid contact with the eyes; if accidental exposure occurs, irritation may result [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions are generally reported as occurring in "a few patients" (Rare <0.1% frequency estimated based on description).

- **Dermatological:** Primary irritation reactions including dermatitis, pruritus, and rash [US].
- **Immunological:** Allergic sensitivity reactions [US].
- **Acute Toxicity (Oral Ingestion):** Burning sensation in the mouth; irritation of the buccal, esophageal, and gastric mucosa; nausea, vomiting, and abdominal pain [US].
- **Serious Events:** A single death has been reported in literature, though the causal link to crotamiton was not confirmed [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic:** No known drug interactions have been identified in the provided source [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not assigned a letter category in the source; however, animal reproduction studies have not been conducted. It is unknown if crotamiton causes fetal harm or affects reproductive capacity. Use during pregnancy only if clearly required [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Safety information regarding excretion into human milk and subsequent effects on the infant is not specified in available sources [US].
- **Fertility:** Long-term animal studies to evaluate the impact on fertility have not been conducted [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy in the paediatric population have not been established [US].
- **Geriatric Use:** Clinical trials did not include a sufficient number of subjects aged 65 and older to determine if they respond differently than younger patients. While specific differences have not been identified, clinicians should be aware of the potential for greater sensitivity in some older individuals [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology

- **Pharmacokinetics:** 
    - **Absorption:** The degree of systemic absorption following topical application has not been determined [US].
    - **Distribution/Metabolism/Elimination:** Not specified in available sources.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at controlled room temperature, 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Excursions:** Brief excursions are permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Keep the container tightly closed and out of reach of children.
