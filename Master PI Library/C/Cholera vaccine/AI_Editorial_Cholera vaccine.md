# Editorial Product Information: Cholera Vaccine, Live, Oral (**Vaxchora**)

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) document for **Vaxchora** (Cholera Vaccine, Live, Oral) from the following jurisdiction: 
    - **United States** (Food and Drug Administration)
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical data and regulatory guidance into a single, concise reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for the official, current Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority's approved labelling for definitive guidance.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Live, attenuated *Vibrio cholerae* strain CVD 103-HgR.
- **Brand Name:** **Vaxchora** [US].
- **Chemical Class:** Live attenuated bacterial vaccine.
- **ATC Code:** J07AE01 (Cholera, biological, live attenuated).
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Suspension for oral administration supplied as a kit containing two foil packets:
    - **Active Component:** Lyophilised *V. cholerae* CVD 103-HgR (4 x 10⁸ to 2 x 10⁹ colony forming units [CFU]).
    - **Buffer Component:** Sodium bicarbonate, sodium carbonate, ascorbic acid, and anhydrous lactose.
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Bavarian Nordic A/S [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Active Immunisation:** Indicated for the prevention of disease caused by *Vibrio cholerae* serogroup O1 in individuals aged 2 through 64 years traveling to cholera-affected areas [US].
- **Limitations of Use:**
    - Effectiveness has not been established in persons living in cholera-affected areas [US].
    - Effectiveness has not been established in persons with pre-existing immunity from prior exposure or previous cholera vaccination [US].
    - No protection is conferred against *V. cholerae* serogroup O139 or other non-O1 serogroups [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### 3.1 General Dosing (Adults and Paediatrics)
- **Primary Vaccination:** A single oral dose administered at least 10 days before potential exposure to cholera [US].
- **Ages 6 to 64 years:** 100 mL dose.
- **Ages 2 to 5 years:** 50 mL dose.
- **Revaccination:** The safety and effectiveness of booster doses have not been established [US].

### 3.2 Administration Instructions
- **Food and Drink Restrictions:** Recipients must avoid eating or drinking for 60 minutes before and after oral ingestion of the vaccine [US].
- **Reconstitution Requirements:**
    1. Use 100 mL of cold or room temperature (5°C–22°C) bottled water (purified, spring, or sparkling). **Do not** use tap water, non-purified water, or other beverages [US].
    2. Dissolve the buffer component (Packet 1) first.
    3. **For children 2–5 years:** Discard half (50 mL) of the buffer solution before adding the active component.
    4. Add the active component (Packet 2) and stir for 30 seconds.
    5. Optional: Up to 4 g of sucrose (table sugar) or 1 g of stevia may be added to improve taste. Do not use other sweeteners [US].
- **Timing:** Consume within 4 hours of reconstitution (or 30 minutes if sugar/stevia is added) [US].

### 3.3 Disposal Instructions
- The vaccine contains live bacteria; dispose of packets, cups, and stirrers as medical waste. Inactivate spills with 70% isopropyl alcohol or 10% bleach solution [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Severe Allergic Reactions:** History of anaphylaxis or severe allergic reaction to any component of **Vaxchora** or to any previous cholera vaccine [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Altered Immunocompetence:** Safety and efficacy have not been established in immunocompromised individuals. The immune response may be diminished in this population [US].
- **Shedding and Transmission:** The vaccine strain may be shed in stools for at least 7 days post-vaccination. There is a potential for transmission to non-vaccinated close contacts. Use caution when vaccinating individuals who have close contact with immunocompromised persons [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions were solicited for 7 days post-vaccination. Frequencies vary by age group.

### 6.1 Adults (18–64 years)
The most common reactions (>3%) included:
- **Very Common (>10%):** Tiredness (31%), headache (29%), abdominal pain (19%), nausea/vomiting (18%), lack of appetite (17%).
- **Common (1–10%):** Diarrhea (4%).

### 6.2 Paediatrics (2–17 years)
The most common reactions (incidence ≥10%) included:
- **Ages 12 to <18 years:** Headache (45%), tiredness (41%), abdominal pain (38%), lack of appetite (29%), nausea (22%).
- **Ages 6 to <12 years:** Tiredness (35%), abdominal pain (27%), headache (26%), lack of appetite (15%), nausea (14%).
- **Ages 2 to <6 years:** Tiredness (31%), lack of appetite (19%), abdominal pain (17%).

### 6.3 Serious Adverse Events
- Reported in 0.6% of adult recipients and 0.2% of paediatric recipients; however, none were considered related to the vaccine in pivotal trials [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Antibiotics:** Avoid concomitant administration with systemic antibiotics, as they may be active against the vaccine strain. **Vaxchora** should not be administered to patients who have received oral or parenteral antibiotics within 14 days prior to vaccination [US].
- **Antimalarial Prophylaxis (Chloroquine):** Immune responses may be diminished. Administer **Vaxchora** at least 10 days before starting chloroquine prophylaxis [US].
- **Concomitant Vaccines:** No data are available regarding co-administration with other vaccines [US].
- **Immunosuppressive Treatments:** Therapies such as irradiation, antimetabolites, alkylating agents, cytotoxic drugs, and supra-physiologic corticosteroid doses may reduce the immune response to the vaccine [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Risk Summary:** **Vaxchora** is not absorbed systemically; maternal use is not expected to result in fetal exposure [US].
    - **Clinical Considerations:** Maternal cholera infection is associated with high risks of fetal death. The vaccine strain may be shed in stool for 7 days; potential transmission to the infant may occur during vaginal delivery [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Systemic absorption by the mother does not occur; breastfeeding is not expected to result in infant exposure to the vaccine [US].
- **Fertility:** Not evaluated for potential impairment of fertility [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Approved for children aged 2 years and older. Safety and effectiveness have not been established in children under 2 years [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established in adults aged 65 years and older [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources; systemic absorption is not expected [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Contains live attenuated *V. cholerae* that replicate in the gastrointestinal tract. While the exact protective mechanism is undetermined, rises in serum vibriocidal antibodies 10 days post-vaccination are associated with protection [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** **Vaxchora** is not systemically absorbed. Shedding of the vaccine strain in stool was highest on day 7 (7.5% of recipients) [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** The vaccine strain (CVD 103-HgR) is derived from the classical Inaba strain 569B. The *ctxA* gene (encoding the catalytic domain of cholera toxin) is deleted to prevent toxin synthesis, while the immunogenic B subunit is retained [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Adult Efficacy (Study 2):** A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled challenge study in adults (18–45 years). 
    - **10-day Challenge:** Vaccine efficacy was 90.3% [95% CI: 62.7, 100.0] against moderate-to-severe diarrhea.
    - **3-month Challenge:** Vaccine efficacy was 79.5% [95% CI: 49.9, 100.0] [US].
- **Immunobridging (Studies 4 & 5):** Effectiveness in adults 46–64 years and children 2–17 years was demonstrated by comparing vibriocidal antibody seroconversion rates to the 18–45 year cohort. Seroconversion was ≥90% across all approved age groups at 10 days post-vaccination [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Packets:** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) [US].
- **Protection:** Protect from light and moisture [US].
- **Temporary Excursions:** Packets may be stored at 9°C to 25°C (48°F to 77°F) for no more than 24 hours prior to reconstitution [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only (Prescription only) [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources [Australian].
