## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Chlorzoxazone (USP)
- **Chemical Class:** Benzoxazolone derivative; centrally acting skeletal muscle relaxant.
- **Brand Name(s):** Chlorzoxazone (various generics) [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available source (Standard: M03BB03).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - **Oral Tablet:** 500 mg [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Bryant Ranch Prepack (Repackager); Original ANDA holder: Senores Pharmaceuticals Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Musculoskeletal Conditions:** Indicated as an adjunct to rest, physical therapy, and other measures for the relief of discomfort associated with acute, painful musculoskeletal conditions [US].
- **Note:** The clinical effect is a reduction in skeletal muscle spasm, resulting in relief of pain and increased mobility [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Adult Dosing
- **Standard Dosage:** 500 mg (one tablet) administered three or four times daily [US].
- **Titration:** If an adequate response is not achieved with the standard dose, the dosage may be increased to 750 mg (one and one-half tablets) three or four times daily [US].
- **Maintenance:** As clinical improvement occurs, the dosage should usually be reduced [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Tablets are administered orally.
- The drug may be taken with or without food (not specified in source).

### Missed Dose Instructions
- Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Contraindicated in patients with a known intolerance or hypersensitivity to **chlorzoxazone** [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Hepatotoxicity (Major Warning)
- **Serious Hepatocellular Toxicity:** Rare, idiosyncratic, and sometimes fatal hepatocellular toxicity has been reported. The mechanism is unpredictable and predisposing factors are unknown [US].
- **Monitoring:** Patients should be monitored for signs of liver dysfunction (e.g., AST, ALT, alkaline phosphatase, and bilirubin). The drug must be discontinued immediately if abnormal liver enzymes are detected [US].
- **Patient Advice:** Instruct patients to report early symptoms of hepatotoxicity, including fever, rash, anorexia, nausea, vomiting, fatigue, right upper quadrant pain, dark urine, or jaundice [US].

### General Precautions
- **CNS Depression:** Concomitant use with alcohol or other central nervous system (CNS) depressants may result in additive sedative effects [US].
- **Sensitivity Reactions:** Use with caution in patients with known allergies or a history of allergic reactions to drugs. Discontinue use if sensitivity reactions (e.g., urticaria, redness, or itching) occur [US].
- **Mechanism Note:** Chlorzoxazone does not directly relax tense skeletal muscles in humans; its effects may be related to its sedative properties [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Available data indicates the drug is generally well tolerated, though the following have been reported:

| Frequency | Adverse Effect |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Occasional** | Drowsiness, dizziness, lightheadedness, malaise, or over-stimulation. |
| **Rare** | Gastrointestinal bleeding, allergic-type skin rashes, petechiae, or ecchymoses. |
| **Extremely Rare** | Angioneurotic edema, anaphylactic reactions. |
| **Other** | Discoloration of the urine (orange/purple-red) due to a phenolic metabolite; this is of no known clinical significance. |

- **Renal Safety:** There is no evidence of drug-induced renal damage [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **CNS Depressants:** Alcohol and other CNS depressants may enhance the sedative effects of **chlorzoxazone** (pharmacodynamic interaction) [US].
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Safety during pregnancy has not been established. Use in women of childbearing potential only if the potential benefits outweigh the possible risks to the fetus [US]. (Specific US Pregnancy Category not listed in source).
- **Breastfeeding:** Safety information and recommendations for use during lactation are not specified in the available source.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness in children have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in source; however, clinical caution regarding CNS depression is generally advised for this population.
- **Renal Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** See **Section 5 (Warnings)**. Use is generally contraindicated or requires immediate cessation if liver dysfunction is suspected [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
- **Chlorzoxazone** is a centrally acting agent that acts primarily at the level of the spinal cord and subcortical areas of the brain. It inhibits multisynaptic reflex arcs involved in producing and maintaining skeletal muscle spasms of varied etiology [US].

### Pharmacokinetics
- **Absorption:** Detected in the blood within 30 minutes of oral administration. Peak blood levels are reached in approximately 1 to 2 hours [US].
- **Metabolism:** Rapidly metabolised, primarily in the liver.
- **Elimination:** Excreted in the urine, mainly as a glucuronide conjugate. Less than 1% of the dose is excreted unchanged in the urine over 24 hours [US].
