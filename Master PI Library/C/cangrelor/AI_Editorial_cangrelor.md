# Editorial Product Information: Cangrelor

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following regulatory authority: **US (FDA)**. 
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and serves as a clinical reference. It is not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific Product Information required for prescribing in a particular region. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory guidelines before administration.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Cangrelor (tetrasodium salt).
- **Chemical Class:** Direct-acting P2Y12 platelet receptor inhibitor; adenosine triphosphate (ATP) analogue.
- **Brand Name(s):** **Kengreal** [US].
- **ATC Code:** B01AC25.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Lyophilized powder for injection, 50 mg in a single-use 10 mL vial [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) bolus and infusion.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Chiesi USA, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Adjunct to Percutaneous Coronary Intervention (PCI):** Indicated to reduce the risk of periprocedural myocardial infarction (MI), repeat coronary revascularization, and stent thrombosis (ST) [US].
- **Patient Criteria:** Approved for use in patients who have not been pre-treated with a P2Y12 inhibitor and are not receiving a glycoprotein IIb/IIIa inhibitor [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### 3.1 General Dosing (Adult)
- **Bolus:** 30 mcg/kg intravenous bolus administered rapidly (<1 minute) prior to the start of PCI [US].
- **Infusion:** 4 mcg/kg/min intravenous infusion initiated immediately after the bolus [US].
- **Duration:** The infusion should continue for at least 2 hours or the duration of the PCI, whichever is longer [US].

### 3.2 Transitioning to Oral P2Y12 Therapy
To maintain platelet inhibition after stopping the **cangrelor** infusion, patients should be transitioned to oral P2Y12 inhibitors as follows [US]:
- **Ticagrelor:** 180 mg loading dose administered at any time during the **cangrelor** infusion or immediately after discontinuation.
- **Prasugrel:** 60 mg loading dose administered immediately after discontinuation of **cangrelor**. Do not administer prior to discontinuation.
- **Clopidogrel:** 600 mg loading dose administered immediately after discontinuation of **cangrelor**. Do not administer prior to discontinuation.

### 3.3 Preparation and Administration
- **Reconstitution:** Reconstitute the 50 mg vial with 5 mL of Sterile Water for Injection. Swirl gently; avoid vigorous mixing. The resulting solution should be clear and colourless to pale yellow [US].
- **Dilution:** Further dilute the reconstituted solution in a 250 mL bag of Normal Saline (0.9% NaCl) or 5% Dextrose Injection (D5W) to achieve a final concentration of 200 mcg/mL [US].
- **Administration:** Must be administered via a dedicated IV line.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Significant Active Bleeding:** Contraindicated in patients with active pathological bleeding [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity (e.g., anaphylaxis) to **cangrelor** or any component of the formulation [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Bleeding Risk:** Like other P2Y12 inhibitors, **cangrelor** increases the risk of bleeding. In the CHAMPION PHOENIX trial, bleeding events (GUSTO and TIMI criteria) were more frequent with **cangrelor** than with clopidogrel [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Serious cases, including anaphylactic shock, bronchospasm, angioedema, and stridor, have been reported [US].
- **Monitoring:** Monitor for signs of bleeding and hypersensitivity during administration.
- **Duration of Effect:** Platelet function typically returns to normal within 1 hour of stopping the infusion [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### 6.1 Frequency Data
| Adverse Reaction | Frequency | Jurisdiction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bleeding (GUSTO Mild)** | Very Common (>10%) | US |
| **Worsening Renal Function** | Common (1–10%)* | US |
| **Dyspnea** | Common (1–10%) | US |
| **Hypersensitivity** | Uncommon (0.1–1%) | US |
*\*Note: Reported in 3.2% of patients with severe renal impairment (CrCl <30 mL/min) compared to 1.4% with clopidogrel [US].*

### 6.2 Serious Adverse Effects
- **Major Bleeding:** TIMI major bleeding occurred in 0.2% of patients; GUSTO severe/life-threatening bleeding occurred in 0.2% [US].
- **Anaphylaxis:** Serious hypersensitivity reactions including anaphylactic shock [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Thienopyridines (Clopidogrel/Prasugrel):** The antiplatelet effect of clopidogrel or prasugrel loading doses is blocked if administered during the **cangrelor** infusion. These agents must be administered only after the infusion is terminated [US].
- **Ticagrelor:** No significant interaction; ticagrelor may be administered during or after the **cangrelor** infusion [US].
- **Other Agents:** No PK/PD interactions were observed with heparin, aspirin, or nitroglycerin [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: No category assigned under PLLR]. There are no available data on use in pregnant women. Animal studies (rats/rabbits) at 2x the maximum recommended human dose (MRHD) did not show fetal malformations, though fetal growth retardation and increased pregnancy loss were noted at higher doses [US].
- **Labor and Delivery:** Discontinue 1 hour prior to labor, delivery, or neuraxial blockade due to bleeding risk [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** No data on presence in human milk. Due to the short half-life (3–6 minutes), exposure to the breastfed infant is expected to be very low [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness not established [US].
- **Geriatric Use:** No overall differences in safety or efficacy were observed between patients ≥75 years and younger patients [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dosage adjustment is required for mild, moderate, or severe impairment [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not studied in this population; however, since metabolism is independent of hepatic function, no dose adjustment is required [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** A direct-acting, selective, and reversible P2Y12 receptor antagonist. It prevents ADP-induced platelet activation and aggregation [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Platelet inhibition (approx. 95%) is achieved within 2 minutes of the bolus and infusion. Function returns to baseline within 60 minutes of discontinuation [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Volume of distribution is 3.9 L; 97–98% protein bound [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Deactivated rapidly in the circulation via dephosphorylation to a nucleoside metabolite with negligible activity [US].
    - **Elimination:** Half-life is 3–6 minutes. Approximately 58% is recovered in urine and 35% in feces [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **CHAMPION PHOENIX:** A randomized, double-blind trial (n=11,145) comparing **cangrelor** to clopidogrel (300 or 600 mg) in P2Y12-naive PCI patients. **Cangrelor** significantly reduced the primary composite endpoint (all-cause mortality, MI, ischemia-driven revascularization, or stent thrombosis) at 48 hours (4.7% vs 5.9%; OR 0.78; p=0.005) [US].
- **CHAMPION PCI and PLATFORM:** Two earlier trials were terminated for futility based on their specific primary endpoints and clopidogrel dosing schedules [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vials:** Store at USP Controlled Room Temperature: 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F). Excursions between 15°C and 30°C (59°F to 86°F) are permitted [US].
- **Diluted Solution:** Stable for up to 12 hours in D5W and 24 hours in Normal Saline at room temperature [US].
