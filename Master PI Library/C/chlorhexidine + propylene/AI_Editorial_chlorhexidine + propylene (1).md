## 4. Dosage and Administration
General dosing (adult)
- Add the entire contents of one concentrate container to the eyewash station as directed below (see Administration instructions).
- Strength in supplied concentrate: Chlorhexidine gluconate 1% w/v in a propylene glycol/water vehicle (concentrate intended for dilution as described).

Administration instructions
- Intended use: additive concentrate for preparation of eyewash solution in potable self-contained emergency eyewash stations.
- Preparation and filling:
  - Wear protective eyewear and gloves during handling.
  - Clean the potable eyewash station and rinse with potable water prior to use.
  - Partially fill the station with potable water.
  - Remove the tamper-evident seal and cap of the concentrate container.
  - Add the entire contents of the concentrate to the eyewash station container.
  - Fill the station to the manufacturer's required level with potable water.
  - Date and initial an inspection tag after filling.
- Maintenance:
  - Station should be cleaned and refilled every 120 days when using this product.
  - Prepare solution in advance of emergencies as needed (add concentrate to potable water to have solution available).
- Do not change dilution or use with other chemicals.
- Do not reuse the concentrate container.
- If concentrate contacts the eye in undiluted form, flush with clean water immediately.

Titration schedules: Not applicable / Not specified in available sources.
Missed dose instructions: Not applicable.

## 5. Contraindications
- Use in undiluted (full strength) form in the eye is contraindicated — **do not use in full strength**.
- For external use only.
- No additional specific contraindications (e.g., documented hypersensitivity to chlorhexidine or propylene glycol) are listed in the available sources; hypersensitivity information is not specified in available sources.

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
- Boxed warnings: None specified in available sources.
- General warnings and precautions:
  - **For external use only.**
  - **Do not use in full strength.** If the concentrate contacts the eye undiluted, flush thoroughly with clean water.
  - Do not change dilution or mix with other chemicals.
  - Do not reuse the container.
  - Stop use and seek medical attention if any of the following occur: changes in vision, eye pain, condition worsens or persists, continued redness or irritation of the eye.
  - Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, seek medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.
  - Observe tamper-evident seal prior to use; do not use if seal is missing or broken.
- Monitoring requirements: Not specified in available sources.
- Specific clinical precautions:
  - Use protective gloves and eyewear when preparing/filling eyewash stations.
  - Adhere to station manufacturer's filling and inspection procedures.
