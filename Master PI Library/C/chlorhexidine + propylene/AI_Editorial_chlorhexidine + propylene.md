# Editorial Product Information: chlorhexidine + propylene

## 1. Product Identification
- Active ingredient (INN/generic name): **chlorhexidine + propylene**
  - Contains chlorhexidine gluconate and propylene glycol as active components (labelled composition includes chlorhexidine gluconate 1% w/v).
- Chemical class: Not specified in available sources.
- ATC code: Not specified in available sources.
- Route(s) of administration: Ophthalmic (intended as a concentrate/additive for potable self-contained emergency eyewash stations).

## 2. Regulatory History
- Date of first marketing/registration (where stated): 03/05/2021 [US].
- Date of last revision/update of source PI: Revised 7/2026 [US].
- Marketing category (where stated): Over‑the‑counter monograph listing (marketing information in source).
- Withdrawal, suspension, or restriction history: Not specified in available sources.
- Other jurisdictions’ registration dates: Not specified in available sources.

## 3. Approved Indications
- Preservative for use in potable self‑contained emergency eyewash stations (intended to be added to potable water in such stations for preservation) [US].

## 4. Dosage and Administration
- General dosing (adult):
  - Add the entire contents of the concentrate bottle to the eyewash station container after partially filling the station with potable water; then fill the station to the manufacturer's required level.
  - Specific concentrate volume per station and final dilution ratio: Not specified in available sources beyond the instruction to add entire bottle contents to the station as directed on the label.
- Dosing by indication:
  - Intended use is as a preservative in eyewash station solutions only; no patient/individual dosing is provided.
- Administration instructions:
  - Wear protective eyewear and gloves when handling the concentrate.
  - Clean the potable eyewash station and rinse with potable water prior to adding concentrate.
  - Partially fill the station with potable water, remove tamper‑evident seal and cap of the bottle, add the entire contents of the bottle to the station, then fill to the manufacturer’s required level.
  - Date and initial the inspection tag after filling.
  - Station should be cleaned and refilled every 120 days when using this product.
  - When preparing in advance of an emergency, add the concentrate to potable water to have a solution available.
  - Do not change the recommended dilution or mix with other chemicals.
  - Do not reuse the concentrate bottle.
- Titration schedules: Not applicable / Not specified in available sources.
- Missed dose instructions: Not applicable / Not specified in available sources.

## 5. Contraindications
- Do not use the product at full strength in contact with the eye (i.e., undiluted concentrate is not to be instilled into the eye).
- Other absolute contraindications (e.g., known hypersensitivity to chlorhexidine or propylene glycol): Not specified in available sources.

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
- Boxed warnings: None specified in available sources.
- General warnings and precautions:
  - For external use only.
  - In case of contact with the eye in undiluted form (i.e., full‑strength concentrate), flush the eye with clean water immediately.
  - Do not use the product at full strength in eyewash stations; the product is to be used as a concentrate added to potable water per the label directions.
  - Do not change the dilution or combine with other chemicals.
  - Do not reuse empty concentrate bottles.
  - Keep out of reach of children.
  - If swallowed, seek medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.
- Monitoring requirements:
  - Advise inspection/logging of eyewash station fill date and inspection tag; station cleaning and refilling recommended every 120 days.
  - Discontinue use and seek medical evaluation if ocular symptoms develop or persist (see Adverse Effects).
- Specific clinical precautions:
  - If ocular irritation, continued redness, eye pain, or changes in vision occur after exposure to the eyewash solution, instruct the patient to stop use and seek a healthcare professional evaluation.
