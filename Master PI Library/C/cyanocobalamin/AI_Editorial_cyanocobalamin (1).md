## 3. Approved Indications
- Intended use as stated in available sources:
  - Formulated to supplement total dietary intake of vitamin B12 and to
    support energy metabolism. The product is described and marketed as a
    dietary supplement.
- Clinical disease treatment indications (e.g., for deficiency states,
  pernicious anaemia, etc.): Not specified in available sources
- Regulatory differences in indications by region: Not specified in available

## 4. Dosage and Administration

### General dosing (adult)
- Typical labeling instruction in available sources: one oral film strip once
  daily for adults, or as directed by a healthcare practitioner.
- Strength per labeled serving: 500 µg cyanocobalamin (500 mcg).

### Dosing by indication
- Specific therapeutic dosing by indication (e.g., deficiency treatment,
  intramuscular regimens): Not specified in available sources.

### Administration instructions
- Each film strip is placed on the tongue, where it dissolves on contact with
  saliva; the dissolved contents are then swallowed through the normal
  digestive process. No water is required for administration.

### Missed dose instructions

## 6. Warnings and Precautions

### Boxed warnings
- None specified in available sources

### General warnings and precautions
- The recommended dose should not be exceeded.
- Individuals who are pregnant or breastfeeding, persons under 18 years of
  age, and those with a pre-existing medical condition are advised to seek
  guidance from a healthcare practitioner prior to use.
- Keep out of reach of children.
- A region-specific warning is included in the product labeling: the product
  may expose users to chemicals, including nickel, which are recognised under
  California law as causes of cancer. [US — Proposition 65]

### Monitoring requirements
- Not specified in available sources

### Specific clinical precautions
- Healthcare practitioner consultation is recommended before use in
  pregnancy, lactation, minors (under 18 years), and persons with known
  medical conditions (as per labeling cautions).
- For any other clinical precautions (e.g., potential masking of deficiency,
  haematologic monitoring, allergy to excipients), information is not
  specified in available sources.
