# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial synthesis is derived from the official Product Information provided by the following jurisdiction: **US (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document serves as a harmonized editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesizing clinical data and regulatory guidance into a concise reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) approved for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority's approved labeling for definitive prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP).
- **Chemical Class:** A naturally occurring carrier molecule; (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt. 
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter Healthcare Corporation) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Sterile solution for injection: 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Acute and chronic treatment of secondary carnitine deficiency resulting from metabolic errors [US-approved].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD undergoing maintenance hemodialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg.
- **ESRD on Hemodialysis:** Starting dose is 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].

### Administration Instructions
- **Metabolic Disorders:** Administer as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion. In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose is followed by equivalent doses every 3, 4, or 6 hours (never less than 6 hours) over the subsequent 24 hours. Daily maintenance doses are typically 50 mg/kg, though up to 300 mg/kg has been used [US].
- **ESRD:** Administer as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line after each dialysis session [US].

### Titration and Monitoring
- **Monitoring:** Obtain baseline plasma carnitine levels. Monitor plasma concentrations, blood chemistry, and vital signs weekly or monthly.
- **Target Levels:** Aim for free carnitine concentrations between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].
- **Dose Adjustment (ESRD):** Reductions (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be made as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levels [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known in available sources [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have occurred—often within minutes of administration. Most cases involve ESRD patients. Discontinue immediately if a severe reaction occurs [US].
- **Seizures:** Reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity may occur [US].
- **Oral Formulation Cautions:** Safety and efficacy of *oral* levocarnitine have not been evaluated in renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral use in ESRD may lead to accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) normally cleared by the kidneys [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have been observed. Monitor INR levels closely upon initiation of levocarnitine or following dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin anticoagulation [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not explicitly assigned, but notes Category B style data]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine is naturally present in human milk. Supplemental use in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. In dairy cows, exogenous administration increases milk carnitine concentrations. Weigh the risks of excess infant intake against maternal benefits [US].
- **Fertility:** Mutagenicity tests (Salmonella, S. cerevisiae, S. pombe) were negative. Long-term carcinogenicity studies have not been performed [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders is calculated by mg/kg, which applies to pediatric populations [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in provided sources [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** See **Section 3** for ESRD dosing. High-dose oral therapy should be avoided in patients with severely compromised renal function due to metabolite accumulation [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not protein or albumin bound. Distribution half-life is 0.585 hours [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. Apparent terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Clears acyl-CoA compounds by forming acylcarnitine, which is rapidly excreted, thereby preventing autointoxication and subsequent acidosis [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients, 20 mg/kg IV three times weekly post-dialysis increased mean pre-dialysis trough carnitine concentrations from ~20 µmol/L to 190 ± 55 µmol/L by week 8. While carnitine levels increased, clinical outcomes regarding signs and symptoms of deficiency in this population remain undetermined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Protection:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat.
- **Handling:** Single-dose vials; discard any unused portion as the formulation contains no preservatives [US].
- **Stability:** Compatible with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s (0.5 mg/mL to 8.0 mg/mL) for up to 24 hours at room temperature in PVC bags [US].

- **Legal Classification:** Rx only [US].
