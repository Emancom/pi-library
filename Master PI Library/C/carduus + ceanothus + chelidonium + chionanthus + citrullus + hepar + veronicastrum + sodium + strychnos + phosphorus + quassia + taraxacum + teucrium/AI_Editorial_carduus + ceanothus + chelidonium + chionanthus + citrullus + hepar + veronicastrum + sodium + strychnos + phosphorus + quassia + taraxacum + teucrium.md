## 3. Approved Indications
- Indications as stated on product labeling (claims based on traditional homeopathic practice):
  - Temporary relief of sour or bitter taste after eating
  - Relief of fullness and soreness over the liver
  - Coated tongue
  - Flatulence
  - Relief of alternating diarrhea and constipation
- Ingredient-level traditional purposes listed on labeling:
  - Carduus marianus — low energy
  - Ceanothus americanus — fatigue
  - Chelidonium majus — poor digestion
  - Chionanthus virginica — constipation
  - Citrullus/Colocynthis — flatulence
  - Hepar bovine — liver support
  - Veronicastrum/Leptandra/Leptandra virginica — fever (Leptandra listed separately on label)
  - Natrum sulphuricum (sodium sulfate) — low energy
  - Nux vomica — nausea
  - Phosphorus — fatigue
  - Quassia — flatulence
  - Taraxacum officinale — low energy
  - Teucrium marum — intestinal spasms
- Regulatory caveat on labeling: claims presented are based on traditional homeopathic practice and have not been evaluated for safety or efficacy by regulatory authority; the labeling explicitly states lack of accepted medical evidence for the claims.
