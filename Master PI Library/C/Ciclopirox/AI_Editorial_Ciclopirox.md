# Editorial Product Information: Ciclopirox

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial has been synthesised from the following source: US (FDA) Product Information for **Ciclopirox** Topical Solution, 8% (Nail Lacquer) by Acella Pharmaceuticals, LLC.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. 
- **Important:** This is an editorial synthesis and must not be used as a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) relevant to the clinician's specific jurisdiction. Prescribers should always consult the locally approved PI for the most current and relevant safety and dosing information.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Ciclopirox 
- **Chemical Class:** Synthetic antifungal agent (pyridone derivative)
- **Brand Names:** Ciclopirox Topical Solution, USP 8% (Nail Lacquer) [US]
- **ATC Code:** D01AE14 (not specified in available source, but standard classification)
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Topical solution containing 80 mg of ciclopirox per gram (8%) [US]
- **Route of Administration:** Topical (nails and immediately adjacent skin)
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Acella Pharmaceuticals, LLC [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Onychomycosis:** Indicated as a component of a comprehensive management program for the topical treatment of mild to moderate onychomycosis of the fingernails and toenails, without lunula involvement, caused by *Trichophyton rubrum* in immunocompetent patients [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Application:** Apply once daily, preferably at bedtime or eight hours before washing, to all affected nails and the immediately adjacent skin [US].
- **Method:** Use the provided applicator brush to apply the lacquer evenly over the entire nail plate. Where possible, apply to the nail bed, hyponychium, and the under surface of the nail plate if separated from the nail bed [US].
- **Cycle:** The lacquer should not be removed daily. New layers are applied over previous coats. All layers should be removed with alcohol every seven days. This cycle is repeated for the duration of therapy [US].

### Administration Instructions
- **Nail Care by Professional:** A comprehensive management program includes the removal of unattached, infected nails as frequently as monthly by a healthcare professional [US].
- **Nail Care by Patient:** Patients should trim nails and file away loose nail material every seven days, following the removal of the lacquer with alcohol [US].
- **Safety Note:** Allow the solution to dry for approximately 30 seconds before putting on socks or stockings [US].

### Duration of Treatment
- Clinical trials evaluated daily application for up to 48 weeks [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Contraindicated in individuals who have shown hypersensitivity to ciclopirox or any components of the formulation [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None [US].
- **Route Restrictions:** For external use only. Not for ophthalmic, oral, or intravaginal use [US].
- **Sensitivity Reactions:** If a reaction suggesting sensitivity or chemical irritation occurs, treatment should be discontinued and appropriate therapy instituted [US].
- **Diabetic Patients:** Use with caution in patients with insulin-dependent diabetes or diabetic neuropathy due to the risks associated with nail trimming and professional nail removal. Such patients should inform their healthcare provider before initiating treatment [US].
- **Flammability:** The solution is flammable; avoid use near heat or open flames [US].
- **Cosmetic Products:** Patients should be advised not to use nail polish or other cosmetic nail products on treated nails [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are generally localised to the application site.
- **Common (1–10%):** 
    - Periungual erythema and erythema of the proximal nail fold (5%) [US].
    - Nail disorders, including shape change, irritation, ingrown toenail, and discoloration (2%) [US].
- **Uncommon (0.1–1%):**
    - Application site reactions and/or burning of the skin (1%) [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** 
    - In an open-label extension study, one patient experienced an elevated creatine phosphokinase level, and another experienced a great toenail "breaking away" [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Systemic Antifungals:** No studies have been conducted to determine the impact of ciclopirox on the efficacy of systemic antifungal agents. Concomitant use of **ciclopirox** topical solution and systemic antifungals for onychomycosis is not recommended [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Systemic absorption is minimal (<5%); therefore, significant pharmacokinetic drug-drug interactions are not expected, though not formally studied for the topical solution [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** **Category B** [US]. Teratology studies in animals (oral and topical) showed no significant fetal malformations. There are no adequate or well-controlled studies in pregnant women; use only if the potential benefit justifies the potential risk to the fetus [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if ciclopirox is excreted in human milk. Caution should be exercised when administered to a nursing woman [US].
- **Fertility:** Oral reproduction studies in rats at doses up to 3.85 mg/kg/day revealed no specific effects on fertility [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy in children under 12 years of age have not been established [US].
- **Geriatric Use:** Clinical studies did not include sufficient numbers of subjects aged 65 and over to determine if they respond differently than younger subjects. No overall differences in safety or effectiveness have been identified [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Ciclopirox acts by chelating polyvalent cations ($Fe^{+3}$ or $Al^{+3}$), which inhibits metal-dependent enzymes responsible for the degradation of peroxides within the fungal cell [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** 
    - **Absorption:** Systemic absorption from the 8% lacquer is low, estimated at <5% of the applied dose. Serum levels in clinical studies ranged from 12–80 ng/mL [US].
    - **Metabolism:** The main metabolic pathway is glucuronidation [US].
    - **Elimination:** Following oral administration, 96% is excreted renally within 12 hours, primarily as glucuronides [US].
- **Microbiology:** Demonstrates *in vitro* activity against *Trichophyton rubrum* (MIC values 1–20 mcg/mL) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Study Design:** Two double-blind, vehicle-controlled trials (Study 312 and 313) were conducted in the US over 48 weeks [US].
- **Outcomes:** 
    - "Complete Cure" (clear nail and negative mycology) was achieved in 5.5% to 8.5% of the active group vs 0% to 0.9% in the vehicle group [US].
    - "Almost Clear" (≤10% involvement and negative mycology) was achieved in 6.5% to 12% of the active group [US].
- **Conclusion:** A completely clear nail may not be achieved in the majority of patients; successful outcomes require 48 weeks of daily application and professional nail debridement [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F and 86°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from light by storing the bottle in the carton after every use. Keep the bottle tightly closed to prevent the solution from drying out or the cap from sticking [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx Only (Prescription only) [US].
- **Packaging:** Supplied in 6.6 mL glass bottles fitted with brushes [US].
