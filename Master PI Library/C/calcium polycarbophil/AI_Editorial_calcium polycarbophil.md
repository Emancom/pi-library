# Editorial Product Information: Calcium Polycarbophil

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial has been synthesized from official Product Information (PI) and labeling sources provided for the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonized editorial overview of calcium polycarbophil for use by healthcare professionals. It aims to consolidate clinical data into a concise, scannable reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official PI or "Drug Facts" labeling required for prescribing or dispensing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Calcium polycarbophil (a synthetic polyacrylic resin).
- **Chemical Class:** Bulk-forming laxative.
- **Brand Names:** **FiberCon** [US].
- **ATC Code:** A06AC08 (Note: Not explicitly stated in source but assigned by WHO for this active).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Oral caplet containing 625 mg of calcium polycarbophil (equivalent to 500 mg of polycarbophil) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Foundation Consumer Brands, LLC [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Occasional Constipation:** For the relief of occasional constipation and irregularity [US].
- **Bowel Management:** Promotes bowel movements, generally within 12 to 72 hours [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Children 12 years and over)
- **Standard Dose:** 2 caplets per dose [US].
- **Maximum Frequency:** Do not exceed 8 caplets within a 24-hour period unless directed by a physician [US].

### Administration Instructions
- **Fluid Requirement:** Each dose must be taken with a full glass (at least 8 ounces or 240 mL) of water or other fluid [US].
- **Onset of Action:** Full clinical benefit is typically achieved within one to three days of continued use [US].

### Titration and Duration
- Dosage requirements may vary based on individual factors such as diet, exercise levels, and the severity of constipation [US].
- **Duration of Use:** Patients should not use this product for more than 7 days without consulting a physician [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Difficulty Swallowing:** Contraindicated in individuals who have difficulty swallowing, due to the risk of esophageal obstruction [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Choking Hazard:** Failure to take this product with adequate fluid may cause the medication to swell and block the throat or esophagus. Immediate medical attention is required if chest pain, vomiting, or difficulty breathing/swallowing occurs after ingestion [US].
- **Pre-existing Medical Conditions:** Patients should consult a physician before use if they have:
    - Abdominal pain, nausea, or vomiting [US].
    - A sudden change in bowel habits persisting for over 2 weeks [US].
- **Clinical Monitoring:** Therapy should be discontinued, and a physician consulted if:
    - Rectal bleeding occurs [US].
    - Bowel movements do not occur after use (may indicate a more serious condition) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Gastrointestinal:** Unlike some other fiber supplements, this product is promoted as not causing gas or bloating [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** 
    - **Esophageal Obstruction/Choking:** Occurs if taken with insufficient fluid [US].
- **Frequency Data:** Not specified in available sources, though generally well-tolerated when taken with appropriate hydration.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Absorption Interference:** Like other laxatives, calcium polycarbophil may affect the absorption and efficacy of other medications [US].
- **Management:** To minimize interference, this product should be administered at least 2 hours before or 2 hours after taking any other medication [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** A warning for "Reproductive Harm" is noted in specific regional labeling (California Proposition 65) [US]. Formal US FDA pregnancy categories were not provided in the source documentation.
- **Breastfeeding:** Safety information not specified in available sources.
- **Fertility:** Data not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** 
    - **12 years and older:** Refer to adult dosing (2 caplets/dose) [US].
    - **Under 12 years:** A physician must be consulted before use [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Specific dose adjustments not specified; however, clinical caution regarding fluid intake and swallowing ability is advised [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Not specified in available sources. Note that each caplet contains 140 mg of calcium [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Functions as a bulk-forming laxative. It absorbs water in the gastrointestinal tract to create a soft, bulky stool, thereby stimulating peristalsis [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Polycarbophil is not absorbed systemically. 
    - **Metabolism/Elimination:** Not specified; acts locally within the intestinal lumen.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Generally produces a bowel movement within 12 to 72 hours [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20-25°C (68-77°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Protect contents from moisture [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as a **Human OTC Drug** (Over-the-Counter).
- **Poison/Schedule Classification:** Not specified in source; available for retail purchase without a prescription [US].
- **Electrolyte Content:** Each caplet contains 140 mg of calcium and 10 mg of magnesium [US].
