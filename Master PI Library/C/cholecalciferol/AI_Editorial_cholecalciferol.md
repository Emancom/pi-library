## 4. Dosage and Administration
### General dosing (adult)
- Typical recommended adult dose as provided: one oral film strip daily (contains cholecalciferol 50 µg / 2000 IU) or as directed by a healthcare practitioner.

### Dosing by indication
- Not specified in available sources.

### Administration instructions
- Place film strip on the tongue; it dissolves on contact with saliva and the contents are swallowed. Water is not required for administration.
- Do not exceed the recommended dose.
