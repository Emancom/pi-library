# Editorial Product Information: CARVYKTI (ciltacabtagene autoleucel)

**Editorial Note:** This unified Product Information (PI) document has been compiled by an editorial team of pharmaceutical experts. It integrates the clinical guidelines, safety mandates, and pharmacological data provided by the Australian Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA) and the United States Food and Drug Administration (FDA). Where regulatory requirements or clinical data sets differ between these agencies, the most comprehensive or conservative guidance has been included and attributed accordingly.

---

## 1. Product Name and Active Ingredient
**Product Name:** CARVYKTI  
**Active Ingredient:** Ciltacabtagene autoleucel

CARVYKTI is a B-cell maturation antigen (BCMA)-directed, genetically modified autologous T-cell immunotherapy. It consists of a patient’s own T-cells that have been transduced ex vivo with a replication-incompetent lentiviral vector to express a chimeric antigen receptor (CAR) comprising two anti-BCMA single-domain antibodies.

## 2. Approved Indications
CARVYKTI is indicated for the treatment of adult patients with relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma who:
*   Have received at least one prior line of therapy, including a proteasome inhibitor (PI) and an immunomodulatory agent (IMiD), and are refractory to lenalidomide [FDA, TGA].
*   Alternatively, for patients who have received at least three prior lines of therapy, including a PI, an IMiD, and an anti-CD38 monoclonal antibody [TGA].

## 3. Contraindications
*   **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to the active substance or any excipients, including dimethyl sulfoxide (DMSO) [TGA].
*   **Lymphodepleting Chemotherapy:** Clinicians must also observe the contraindications associated with the specific lymphodepleting regimen (cyclophosphamide and fludarabine) used prior to CARVYKTI infusion [TGA].
*   *Note:* The FDA label lists no specific contraindications [FDA].

## 4. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warnings
*   **Cytokine Release Syndrome (CRS):** Severe, life-threatening, or fatal CRS has occurred. Do not administer to patients with active infections or inflammatory disorders.
*   **Immune Effector Cell-Associated Neurotoxicity Syndrome (ICANS):** ICANS may be fatal and can occur independently of or concurrently with CRS. 
*   **Parkinsonism and Guillain-Barré Syndrome (GBS):** Fatalities have been reported due to movement and neurocognitive toxicities (parkinsonism) and GBS following CARVYKTI treatment.
*   **Hemophagocytic Lymphohistiocytosis (HLH)/Macrophage Activation Syndrome (MAS):** CARVYKTI may trigger life-threatening HLH/MAS.
*   **Prolonged and Recurrent Cytopenias:** Patients may experience Grade 3 or higher cytopenias for several weeks or months.
*   **Secondary Hematological Malignancies:** There is a risk of developing secondary malignancies, including myeloid neoplasms and T-cell malignancies.

### Increased Early Mortality
In the CARTITUDE-4 study, a numerically higher rate of early deaths was observed in the CARVYKTI arm compared to the control arm within the first 10 months following randomization [FDA].

### Viral Reactivation and Infections
Hepatitis B virus (HBV) reactivation and Progressive Multifocal Leukoencephalopathy (PML) resulting from John Cunningham (JC) virus reactivation have been reported. Prophylactic antimicrobials should be administered per local guidelines [TGA].

### Hypogammaglobulinemia
Low immunoglobulin levels are common and may require replacement therapy (IVIG) to mitigate infection risk.

## 5. Dosage and Administration
**For autologous use only.** CARVYKTI is intended for a single intravenous infusion.

### Dosing
The recommended dose range is 0.5–1.0 × 10⁶ CAR-positive viable T-cells per kg of body weight. The maximum total dose is 1.0 × 10⁸ CAR-positive viable T-cells per infusion.

### Regimen and Timing
1.  **Lymphodepletion:** Administer cyclophosphamide (300 mg/m²) and fludarabine (30 mg/m²) daily for 3 days.
2.  **Infusion Window:** Infuse CARVYKTI 2 to 4 days after the completion of chemotherapy [FDA] or 5 to 7 days after the start of the lymphodepleting regimen [TGA].
3.  **Pre-medication:** Administer antipyretics (acetaminophen) and H1-antihistamines (diphenhydramine or equivalent) 30 to 60 minutes before infusion. Avoid systemic corticosteroids unless treating a severe reaction.

### Administration Requirements
*   A minimum of two doses of tocilizumab must be available on-site prior to infusion.
*   Do **NOT** use a leukodepleting filter.
*   Infusion should be completed within 2.5 hours of thawing.

## 6. Adverse Effects
The safety profile of CARVYKTI is characterized by significant immunological and hematological toxicities. 

*   **Very Common (≥20%):** Pyrexia, CRS, hypogammaglobulinemia, hypotension, musculoskeletal pain, fatigue, upper respiratory tract infection, diarrhea, nausea, headache, cough, and chills.
*   **Hematologic Abnormalities (Grade 3/4):** Neutropenia (88-95%), lymphopenia (33-99%), thrombocytopenia (44-63%), and anemia (34-72%) [FDA, TGA].
*   **Serious Reactions:** Pneumonia, sepsis, and various neurologic toxicities (ICANS, motor dysfunction, encephalopathy).

## 7. Drug Interactions
No formal drug interaction studies have been conducted. 
*   **HIV Testing:** Due to the use of a lentiviral vector in manufacturing, some commercial HIV nucleic acid tests (NATs) may return false-positive results in patients treated with CARVYKTI.
*   **Corticosteroids:** Use of high-dose systemic corticosteroids prior to infusion may interfere with the activity of the CAR T-cells and should be avoided unless medically necessary.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
### Pregnancy
There are no data on the use of CARVYKTI in pregnant women. It is unknown if the product can cause fetal harm or affect reproductive capacity. CARVYKTI is not recommended for pregnant women or women of childbearing potential not using contraception [TGA]. Pregnancy status must be verified before starting treatment.

### Contraception
Females of childbearing potential and males with partners of childbearing potential should use highly effective contraception for at least one year following the CARVYKTI infusion [TGA, FDA].

### Breastfeeding
The presence of CARVYKTI in human milk is unstudied. The benefits of breastfeeding should be weighed against the potential for adverse effects on the infant.

## 9. Pharmacokinetics
Following a single infusion, CARVYKTI typically exhibits an initial expansion phase. 
*   **Peak Expansion:** Median time to maximum levels (Tmax) in peripheral blood is approximately 7 to 14 days post-infusion.
*   **Persistence:** The CAR transgene remains detectable in most patients, with levels returning to baseline at a median of approximately 100 days [TGA, FDA].
*   **Special Populations:** Age, gender, body weight, and race do not significantly impact the pharmacokinetics. No significant differences were observed in patients with mild renal or mild hepatic impairment.

## 10. Mechanism of Action
CARVYKTI is an autologous T-cell therapy reprogrammed to target BCMA, a protein highly expressed on the surface of malignant multiple myeloma B-lineage cells. The CAR protein contains two anti-BCMA targeting domains linked to a 4-1BB costimulatory domain and a CD3-zeta signaling domain. Upon binding to BCMA-expressing cells, the CAR T-cells undergo activation and expansion, leading to the directed lysis and elimination of the target myeloma cells.

## 11. Storage Conditions
*   **Cryopreservation:** CARVYKTI must be stored and transported in the vapor phase of liquid nitrogen (at or below -120°C).
*   **Post-Thaw:** Once thawed, the product must be administered immediately. It cannot be refrozen or refrigerated.
*   **Integrity:** The infusion bag must remain in its metal cassette until the moment of preparation for thawing.
