# Editorial Product Information: Cosyntropin (Veterinary)

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information provided by the following jurisdiction: **US (FDA - NADA #141-576)**. 
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals and veterinary practitioners.
- This is an editorial synthesis and serves as a clinical reference; it is not a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Cosyntropin (synthetic polypeptide consisting of the first 24 amino acids of naturally occurring adrenocorticotropic hormone [ACTH]).
- **Chemical Class:** ACTH analogue/diagnostic agent.
- **Brand Names:** **Cosacthen** [US].
- **ATC Code:** H01AA02 (Cosyntropin/Tetracosactide).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Clear, aqueous solution for injection, 0.25 mg/mL [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) or Intramuscular (IM) [US].
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Dechra Veterinary Products, LLC [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Evaluation of Adrenal Function:** Specifically approved for use in dogs to assess adrenocortical response [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Dogs):** A single dose of 0.25 mg (1 mL) is administered to dogs weighing between 4.5 kg and 50 kg (10–110 lbs) [US].
- **ACTH Stimulation Test Protocol:**
    1. Collect a baseline blood sample to measure initial cortisol concentration immediately prior to administration.
    2. Administer **cosyntropin** via IM or IV injection.
    3. Collect a second blood sample exactly 1 hour post-injection to measure the stimulated cortisol response [US].
- **Administration Instructions:** For single use only. The product does not contain preservatives; any unused portion must be discarded [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known history of hypersensitivity reactions to cosyntropin or synthetic ACTH analogues [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **User Safety Warnings (Human Health):**
    - **Not for human use.** Keep out of reach of children [US].
    - Individuals with known hypersensitivity to cosyntropin or ACTH should avoid direct contact with the product.
    - **Accidental Self-Injection:** Pregnant or breastfeeding women should exercise extreme caution to avoid accidental self-injection. In the event of exposure, seek immediate medical attention and present the product label to a physician [US].
    - **Skin Contact:** If contact occurs, wash the area thoroughly with soap and water.
- **Animal Safety Precautions:** 
    - Safe use has not been established in breeding, pregnant, or lactating dogs [US].
- **Monitoring Requirements:** Evaluation should be performed in the context of clinical signs of hypoadrenocorticism or hyperadrenocorticism.

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects observed during clinical field studies and post-approval monitoring in dogs:

| Frequency | Adverse Effect |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Vomiting (within 8 hours of administration), injection site hematoma (following IV use) [US]. |
| **Post-marketing/Foreign Market Experience** | Lethargy, anxiety, muscle tremors/weakness, abdominal pain, anorexia, diarrhea, injection site pain/bruising, lameness, and hypersensitivity reactions [US]. |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Hypersensitivity reactions, including facial edema, tachycardia, erythema, and salivation, have been reported in safety studies [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources. 
- **Note:** Clinicians should consider that exogenous glucocorticoids or drugs affecting the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal (HPA) axis may interfere with cortisol results during diagnostic testing.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Veterinary Use:** The safety of **cosyntropin** has not been evaluated in pregnant or lactating dogs [US].
- **Human Safety:** Pregnant and breastfeeding women are advised to take specific care to avoid accidental self-injection due to potential hormonal effects [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric (Veterinary):** Safety was evaluated in healthy beagle dogs as young as 5–6 months old during target animal safety studies [US].
- **Elderly (Veterinary):** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** No specific dosage adjustments provided in the source documentation.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Cosyntropin binds to specific receptors (MC2R) in the plasma membrane of adrenal cortical cells within the zona fasciculata. This activates adenylate cyclase, increasing cyclic AMP, which stimulates the conversion of cholesterol to pregnenolone. This process results in the synthesis and secretion of glucocorticoids (cortisol) [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics/Pharmacodynamics:** Noted to provoke a cortisol response that is measured 60 minutes post-administration for diagnostic purposes [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Laboratory Effectiveness Study:** In 16 healthy beagles, a dose of 0.005 mg/kg resulted in a positive cortisol response (defined as >6.5 μg/dL) in 100% of the treatment group at 30, 60, and 90 minutes [US].
- **Field Study:** A multi-site study of 119 dogs with suspected adrenal dysfunction used a 0.25 mg/dog dose. 
    - **Hypoadrenocorticism:** 100% sensitivity and specificity were observed in suspected cases.
    - **Hyperadrenocorticism:** Diagnostic accuracy was approximately 70%, with sensitivity between 83.9% and 87.1% [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store refrigerated between 2°C and 8°C (36°F–46°F) [US].
- **Light Protection:** Keep the vial in the original outer carton to protect from light [US].
- **Stability:** Single-use vial; discard any unused portion immediately [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **US:** Classified as a Prescription Animal Drug (Rx). Federal law restricts this drug to use by or on the order of a licensed veterinarian [US].
