# Editorial Product Information: Carboxymethylcellulose sodium, Glycerin, and Polysorbate 80

## Editorial Note
*   This editorial synthesis has been compiled using official Product Information (PI) and labeling from the following jurisdiction:
    *   **United States (US):** Food and Drug Administration (FDA)
*   **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of **Refresh Optive Mega-3** for use by healthcare professionals. 
*   **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing or dispensing in any specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredients:** Carboxymethylcellulose sodium, Glycerin, and Polysorbate 80.
*   **Chemical Class:** Ophthalmic lubricants (artificial tears).
*   **Brand Name(s):** **Refresh Optive Mega-3** (Preservative-Free) [US].
*   **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    *   Ophthalmic solution/drops (preservative-free) [US].
    *   Carboxymethylcellulose sodium 0.5% (5 mg/mL) [US].
    *   Glycerin 1% (10 mg/mL) [US].
    *   Polysorbate 80 0.5% (5 mg/mL) [US].
*   **Route(s) of Administration:** Ophthalmic.
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Allergan, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   Temporary relief of burning, irritation, and discomfort associated with xerophthalmia (dry eye) [US].
*   Relief of eye discomfort caused by exposure to environmental factors such as wind or sun [US].
*   Ocular protectant against further irritation [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
*   **General Dosing (Adult):** Instill 1 or 2 drops into the affected eye(s) as needed [US].
*   **Administration Instructions:** 
    *   For ophthalmic use only [US].
    *   To ensure sterility, the tip of the unit-dose container must not touch any surface, including the eye itself [US].
    *   Use only if the single-use container is intact [US].
    *   Containers must be discarded immediately after use and must not be reused [US].
*   **Titration Schedules:** Not applicable.
*   **Missed Dose Instructions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
*   Not specified in available sources; however, use should be avoided in patients with known hypersensitivity to any ingredient in the formulation (see Section 1).

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
*   **Boxed Warnings:** None [US].
*   **General Warnings and Precautions:**
    *   **External Use Only:** Strictly for ophthalmic administration [US].
    *   **Contamination Avoidance:** Do not touch the unit-dose tip to the eye or any other surface to avoid microbial contamination [US].
    *   **Product Integrity:** If the solution changes color or becomes cloudy, it should not be used [US].
*   **Monitoring Requirements:** Patients should be advised to stop use and consult a healthcare provider if they experience:
    *   Eye pain [US].
    *   Changes in vision [US].
    *   Persistent redness or irritation of the eye [US].
    *   Condition that worsens or persists for more than 72 hours [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
*   **Frequencies:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Serious Adverse Effects:** While not formally listed as adverse effects, the labeling identifies eye pain, vision changes, and persistent redness as clinical signals to discontinue therapy and seek medical advice [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Management:** Clinicians should generally advise spacing the administration of different ophthalmic products by at least 5 minutes.

*   **Pregnancy:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Breastfeeding:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Fertility:** Not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use:** Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, medical help or Poison Control Center contact is required immediately [US].
*   **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Renal Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
*   **Mechanism of Action:** The product functions as an eye lubricant (artificial tear) to supplement natural tear production and provide a protective barrier on the ocular surface [US].
*   **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified in available sources; however, carboxymethylcellulose and polysorbate 80 are generally not absorbed systemically in significant quantities following topical ophthalmic administration.
*   **Pharmacodynamics:** The formulation includes **HydroCell** technology and is enhanced with flaxseed oil [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   **Storage Temperature:** Store at 15°C to 25°C (59°F to 77°F) [US].
*   **Special Handling:** Vials should be kept in the original pouch until the time of use [US].
*   **Shelf Life:** Use before the expiration date marked on the container [US].
