# Editorial Product Information: Choriogonadotropin alfa

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following regulatory authority: Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of **choriogonadotropin alfa** for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical, pharmacological, and regulatory data.
- **Prescribing Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Choriogonadotropin alfa (rch).
- **Chemical Class:** Recombinant human chorionic gonadotrophin (r-hCG); a water-soluble glycoprotein consisting of α and β subunits.
- **Brand Names:** **Ovidrel** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** G03GA08 (Not explicitly stated in source, but clinically standard).
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - Solution for injection in a single-dose pre-filled pen: 250 micrograms in 0.5 mL [Australian].
- **Route of Administration:** Subcutaneous (SC) injection.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Merck Healthcare Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Assisted Reproductive Technologies (ART):** Triggering final follicular maturation and luteinisation in women undergoing superovulation prior to procedures such as in vitro fertilisation (IVF) [Australian].
- **Ovulation Induction (OI):** Triggering ovulation and luteinisation in anovulatory or oligo-ovulatory women following stimulation of follicular growth [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
Treatment must be performed under the supervision of a physician experienced in fertility management.
- **Superovulation (ART):** A single 250 microgram dose is administered 24 to 48 hours after the final administration of follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) or human menopausal gonadotrophin (hMG) preparations (i.e., when optimal follicular growth is achieved) [Australian].
- **Anovulatory/Oligo-ovulatory Women:** A single 250 microgram dose is administered 24 to 48 hours after optimal stimulation of follicular growth is achieved. Patients are advised to have intercourse on the day of and the day after the injection [Australian].

### Administration Instructions
- Intended for subcutaneous administration, typically in the abdomen.
- The 250 microgram dose is considered bioequivalent to approximately 6500 IU of urinary-derived hCG (u-hCG).
- Patients or partners may self-inject after receiving adequate instruction in sterile technique and administration [Australian].

## 4. Contraindications
**Choriogonadotropin alfa** is contraindicated in women with:
- Hypersensitivity to hCG or any excipient.
- Primary ovarian failure.
- Uncontrolled thyroid, pituitary, or adrenal dysfunction.
- Active tumours of the hypothalamus or pituitary gland.
- Ovarian enlargement or cysts not related to polycystic ovarian disease (PCOS).
- Gynaecological haemorrhage of unknown etiology.
- Cancers of the ovary, uterus, or mammary glands.
- Extrauterine (ectopic) pregnancy within the previous 3 months.
- Active thromboembolic disorders.
- Uterine fibroids or other conditions incompatible with pregnancy.
- Postmenopausal status [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Specialist Supervision:** Therapy should only be initiated by physicians experienced in infertility management.
- **Ovarian Hyperstimulation Syndrome (OHSS):** A significant risk of gonadotrophin therapy. OHSS is a medical event distinct from uncomplicated ovarian enlargement, characterised by increased vascular permeability and fluid accumulation in the peritoneal, pleural, and pericardial cavities. 
    - **Risk Factors:** Young age, lean body mass, PCOS, high/rapidly rising oestradiol, and large follicle numbers.
    - **Monitoring:** Clinical response must be monitored via ultrasound and serum oestradiol. If oestradiol is >5500 pg/mL or ≥40 follicles are present, hCG should be withheld [Australian].
- **Multiple Births:** Increased risk compared to natural conception. In ART, risk relates to the number of embryos transferred; in OI, it relates to the number of mature follicles.
- **Thromboembolism:** As with other hCG products, a potential for arterial thromboembolism exists, particularly in women with established risk factors or severe OHSS.
- **Interference with Testing:** Administration may interfere with immunological determination of serum or urinary hCG for up to 10 days, potentially leading to a false-positive pregnancy test [Australian].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are often difficult to distinguish from the symptoms of ovarian stimulation or the underlying pregnancy.
### Frequency Data [Australian]
| Frequency | Adverse Effect |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Injection site reactions (pain, erythema, swelling), headache, nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain, mild/moderate OHSS. |
| **Uncommon (0.1–1%)** | Diarrhoea, abdominal discomfort, severe OHSS. |
| **Very Rare (<0.1%)** | Thromboembolism, mild to severe hypersensitivity reactions (including anaphylaxis and rash). |

### Serious Adverse Effects
- Severe OHSS (incidence <0.5% in clinical trials) [Australian].
- Thromboembolic events (e.g., pulmonary embolism, ischaemic stroke).
- Ovarian torsion (rarely as a complication of OHSS).

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Dynamic Interactions:** No clinically significant drug interactions have been reported during hCG therapy [Australian].
- **Thyroid Stimulation:** Minor thyroid stimulation is possible during therapy; however, the clinical relevance is currently unknown [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Australian Category B3:** Not indicated for use during pregnancy. No clinical data on exposed pregnancies are available. Animal reproduction studies have not been performed [Australian].
- **Breastfeeding:** Safety data are unavailable regarding the excretion of **choriogonadotropin alfa** in human milk. It should not be administered to breastfeeding women [Australian].
- **Fertility:** Indicated for use in infertility treatment (refer to Section 2).

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** No data available; not indicated for use in the paediatric population [Australian].
- **Elderly Use:** No data available; not indicated for use in the elderly [Australian].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** **Choriogonadotropin alfa** is an analogue of luteinising hormone (LH). It binds to the LH/hCG receptor on ovarian granulosa and theca cells to trigger the resumption of oocyte meiosis, follicular rupture (ovulation), and the formation of the corpus luteum [Australian].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Absolute bioavailability is approximately 40% following SC injection. Peak serum concentrations (Cmax) are reached within 12–24 hours [Australian].
    - **Distribution:** Distributed to the extracellular fluid space; central compartment volume is ~3 L.
    - **Metabolism/Elimination:** Total body clearance is ~0.29 L/h. Terminal half-life is approximately 38 hours following SC administration [Australian].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Assisted Reproductive Technologies:** In three pivotal trials (Studies 9073, 7927, 7648), 250 mcg of **choriogonadotropin alfa** was found clinically equivalent to 5000 IU or 10,000 IU of urinary-derived hCG regarding the number of oocytes retrieved and pregnancy rates [Australian].
- **Ovulation Induction:** In Study 8209, 250 mcg of **choriogonadotropin alfa** was equivalent to 5000 IU of u-hCG, with an ovulation rate of ~92% in the recombinant group [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage:** Store at 2°C to 8°C (Refrigerate. Do not freeze) in the original container to protect from light [Australian].
- **Excursion:** Within its shelf life, the pre-filled pen may be stored at or below 25°C for a single period of up to 28 days. It must be discarded if not used within this 28-day period [Australian].
