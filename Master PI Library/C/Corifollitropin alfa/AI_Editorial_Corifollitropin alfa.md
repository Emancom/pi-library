# Editorial Product Information: Corifollitropin alfa

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial has been synthesised from the following jurisdiction's official Product Information: Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of corifollitropin alfa for use by healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should refer to the local approved PI before prescribing.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Corifollitropin alfa (rch)
- **Chemical Class:** Gonadotrophin; recombinant Follicle Stimulating Hormone (FSH) analogue.
- **Brand Name(s):** **ELONVA** [Australian]
- **ATC Code:** G03GA09
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
  - Solution for injection in a pre-filled syringe: 100 micrograms/0.5 mL and 150 micrograms/0.5 mL [Australian].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Subcutaneous (SC) injection.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Organon Pharma Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Controlled Ovarian Stimulation (COS):** For the development of multiple follicles and pregnancy in women undergoing in-vitro fertilisation (IVF) techniques [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
Treatment must be initiated under the supervision of a physician experienced in fertility treatment. The dose is determined by the patient's age and body weight.

| Patient Age | Body Weight < 50 kg | Body Weight 50 – 60 kg | Body Weight > 60 kg |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **≤ 36 years** | 100 mcg | 100 mcg | 150 mcg |
| **> 36 years** | Not studied | 150 mcg | 150 mcg |

*Note: Women > 42 years or women < 35 years weighing > 90 kg have not been studied [Australian].*

### Administration Instructions
- **Stimulation Day 1:** A single SC injection, preferably in the abdominal wall, during the early follicular phase of the menstrual cycle.
- **Stimulation Day 5 or 6:** Start a Gonadotrophin Releasing Hormone (GnRH) antagonist to prevent premature LH surges.
- **Stimulation Day 8:** If needed, daily injections of recombinant FSH (recFSH) may begin (standard dose 150 IU) until the criterion for final oocyte maturation (3 follicles ≥ 17 mm) is met.
- **Final Maturation:** Administer 5,000 to 10,000 IU of human Chorionic Gonadotrophin (hCG) once the follicle criteria are met.

### Missed Dose and Titration
- **ELONVA** is intended for a single injection per cycle. Additional injections of **ELONVA** must not be given within the same cycle. 
- No additional FSH-containing products should be administered prior to stimulation day 8.

## 4. Contraindications
- Tumours of the ovary, breast, uterus, pituitary, or hypothalamus.
- Abnormal vaginal bleeding (non-menstrual) of unknown cause.
- Primary ovarian failure.
- Ovarian cysts or enlarged ovaries.
- Factors associated with Ovarian Hyperstimulation Syndrome (OHSS) risk: 
  - History of OHSS.
  - Previous COS cycle with > 30 follicles ≥ 11 mm.
  - Basal antral follicle count > 20.
  - Polycystic ovarian syndrome (PCOS).
- Fibroid tumours or malformations of reproductive organs incompatible with pregnancy.
- Pregnancy or lactation.
- Hypersensitivity to the active substance or excipients [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Ovarian Hyperstimulation Syndrome (OHSS):** A clinical event distinct from uncomplicated ovarian enlargement. Severe cases are life-threatening and may include ascites, pleural effusion, and thromboembolism. Adherence to weight-based dosing and monitoring (ultrasound/estradiol) is critical.
- **Thromboembolic Events:** Gonadotrophin treatment may increase the risk of venous or arterial thrombosis, especially in women with existing risk factors (obesity, family history).
- **Ovarian Torsion:** Reported following gonadotrophin treatment; often associated with OHSS or previous surgery.
- **Multi-foetal Gestation:** Increased risk of multiple births, primarily related to the number of embryos transferred.
- **Ectopic Pregnancy:** Increased incidence in women undergoing assisted reproductive technology (ART).
- **Infertility Evaluation:** Before treatment, evaluate for hypothyroidism, adrenocortical insufficiency, and hyperprolactinemia.

| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Psychiatric** | | Mood swings |
| **Nervous System** | Headache | Dizziness |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** OHSS, ovarian torsion, and thromboembolic events.
- **Post-marketing:** Hypersensitivity reactions (including rash) have been reported.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic:** No specific interaction studies performed. Corifollitropin alfa is not a substrate for CYP450 enzymes; metabolic interactions are unlikely.
- **Laboratory Interference:** May cause a false-positive hCG pregnancy test if administered during the stimulation phase due to cross-reactivity with the carboxy-terminal peptide of the beta subunit [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Contraindicated.
  - **Australian Category B3:** Clinical data are insufficient to exclude teratogenic effects following inadvertent exposure. Animal studies showed embryotoxicity and (in rabbits) teratogenicity, likely linked to the superovulatory state.
- **Breastfeeding:** Contraindicated [Australian].
- **Fertility:** Intended for use in fertility treatment. In animal studies, it stimulated multiple follicle development without impairing fertility.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Not relevant for the approved indication.
- **Elderly Use:** No data available; not relevant for the approved indication.
- **Renal Impairment:** Use is **not recommended** as the rate of elimination may be reduced [Australian].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specifically studied, but unlikely to affect elimination as metabolism contributes only minimally to clearance.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** A sustained follicle stimulant with the same pharmacodynamic profile as recFSH but with a markedly prolonged duration of activity. This is achieved by the addition of the carboxy-terminal peptide (CTP) of the beta-subunit of hCG to the beta-chain of FSH.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
  - **Absorption:** Absolute bioavailability is approximately 58%. Mean $T_{max}$ is 44 hours.
  - **Distribution:** $V_d$ is ~9.2 L; distributed mainly to ovaries and kidneys.
  - **Metabolism:** Minor hepatic contribution.
  - **Elimination:** Primarily renal. Mean elimination half-life ($t_{1/2}$) is 70 hours (range 59–82 hours).
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Does not display intrinsic LH or hCG activity.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ENSURE Study:** 100 mcg dose in women ≤ 60 kg (ages 18-36). Demonstrated non-inferiority to recFSH in oocytes retrieved.
- **ENGAGE Study:** 150 mcg dose in women > 60 kg (ages 18-36). Demonstrated comparable ongoing pregnancy rates and oocyte retrieval to recFSH.
- **PURSUE Study:** 150 mcg dose in women ≥ 50 kg (ages 35-42). Confirmed vital pregnancy rates were non-inferior to daily recFSH.
- **Safety Profile:** Across all trials, the safety profile of a single injection was comparable to daily recFSH injections [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 2°C to 8°C (Refrigerate. Do not freeze).
- **Room Temperature:** May be stored below 25°C for a single period of up to 1 month.
- **Handling:** Keep the syringe in the outer carton to protect from light. Discard any residue [Australian].
