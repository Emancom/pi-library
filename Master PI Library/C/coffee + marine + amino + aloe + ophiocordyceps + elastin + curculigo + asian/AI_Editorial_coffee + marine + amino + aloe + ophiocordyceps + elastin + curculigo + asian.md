## 4. Dosage and Administration
### General dosing (adult)
- Manufacturer directions: Use one packet per serving. Pour one packet into a mug, add 4–6 oz (approximately 120–180 mL) hot water, stir, and consume.
- Pack weight per serving: 19 g per packet.

### Composition per 19 g packet
- Coffee bean: 17 g
- Marine collagen, soluble: 1,000 mg
- Amino acids, source unspecified: 100 mg
- Aloe vera leaf: 100 mg
- Ophiocordyceps sinensis: 100 mg
- Curculigo orchioides (whole): 100 mg
- Asian ginseng (Panax ginseng) extract powder: 100 mg
- Elastin (bovine): 10 mg
