# Editorial Product Information: Pilocarpine Hydrochloride

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided from the following jurisdiction: **Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of pilocarpine hydrochloride (ophthalmic) for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises clinical data to support safe and effective prescribing and dispensing.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not intended to replace the official PI or summary of product characteristics (SmPC) required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. No data from other major jurisdictions (e.g., US, Europe, Canada, Japan) was provided for this synthesis; where information is absent from the provided Australian source, it is noted as "Not specified in available sources."

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Pilocarpine hydrochloride (a cholinergic agonist/miotic).
- **Brand Names:** Isopto Carpine [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** S01EB01 (Pilocarpine).
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - Ophthalmic solution (clear eye drops): 1% (10 mg/mL), 2% (20 mg/mL), and 4% (40 mg/mL) [Australian].
- **Route of Administration:** Topical ophthalmic.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Novartis Pharmaceuticals Australia Pty Limited [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Glaucoma:** For the treatment of elevated intraocular pressure [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Adult Dosing:** 1 to 2 drops instilled into the affected eye(s) three to four times daily [Australian].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - To limit systemic absorption and potentially reduce systemic adverse effects, nasolacrimal occlusion or gentle eyelid closure after administration is recommended [Australian].
    - If using multiple strengths, the concentration and frequency of administration are determined by the severity of the intraocular pressure and the patient's response.
- **Contact Lens Care:** This product contains benzalkonium chloride, which may discolour soft contact lenses. Patients should remove lenses before application and wait at least 15 minutes before reinsertion [Australian].
- **Missed Dose:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Undesirable Miosis:** Situations where pupillary constriction is clinically disadvantageous, such as acute iritis or anterior uveitis [Australian].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to pilocarpine hydrochloride or any excipient in the formulation [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Retinal Detachment:** Miotics have been associated with retinal detachment in susceptible individuals, particularly young patients with myopia or those with a history of retinal detachment [Australian].
- **Ocular Inflammation:** Use should be avoided in the presence of acute inflammatory diseases of the anterior chamber [Australian].
- **Paradoxical Intraocular Pressure (IOP) Response:** Patients with significantly compromised trabecular outflow may experience a paradoxical rise in IOP [Australian].
- **Systemic Toxicity Risk:** 
    - Caution is required in the presence of corneal or conjunctival damage, as excessive penetration may lead to systemic toxicity [Australian].
    - Use with caution in patients with: acute cardiac failure, bronchial asthma, peptic ulcer, hyperthyroidism, gastrointestinal spasms, Parkinson’s disease, urinary tract obstruction, recent myocardial infarction, or significant blood pressure fluctuations (hypertension/hypotension) [Australian].
- **Visual Impairment:** Miosis may cause blurred vision and impaired dark adaptation. Patients should exercise caution during night driving or hazardous activities in low light [Australian].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions are classified by frequency: Very common (≥10%), Common (≥1% to <10%), Uncommon (≥0.1% to <1%), and Not Known.

| System Organ Class | Frequency | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nervous System** | Very Common | Headache |
| | Common | Dizziness |
| **Eye Disorders** | Very Common | Blurred vision |
| | Common | Visual impairment, reduced visual acuity, eye pain, photopsia (flashing lights), vitreous floaters, myodesopsia (eye floaters), eye irritation, ocular hyperaemia |
| | Uncommon | Retinal tear, vitreous haemorrhage, eyelid oedema, miosis, vitreous detachment, glare, foreign body sensation |
| | Not Known | Increased intraocular pressure, corneal oedema, ciliary spasms (temporary reduction in visual acuity) |
| **Gastrointestinal** | Common | Nausea |
| | Not Known | Vomiting |

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Interaction Studies:** No formal interaction studies have been performed [Australian].
- **Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Although not specifically listed in the source, as a miotic, its effects may be antagonised by anticholinergic agents.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Australian Category B3:** Animal studies have demonstrated harmful effects of systemic pilocarpine regarding reproductive toxicity in rats. There are no adequate/controlled studies in pregnant women. Use should be avoided during pregnancy as a precautionary measure [Australian].
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if pilocarpine is excreted in human milk, though excretion should be expected. A risk to the suckling infant cannot be excluded. A clinical decision is required whether to discontinue breastfeeding or the medication, weighing the maternal benefit against infant risk [Australian].
- **Fertility:** Studies evaluating the effect of topical ocular pilocarpine on fertility have not been performed [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** No data available [Australian].
- **Elderly Use:** No data available [Australian].
- **Renal Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Pilocarpine is a cholinergic agonist that lowers intraocular pressure by increasing the outflow of aqueous humour via its miotic action [Australian].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Specifically lowers intraocular pressure [Australian].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified in available sources.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pivotal Trials:** No clinical trial data provided in the available source document [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store below 25°C [Australian].
- **Handling:** Do not freeze. Protect from light.
- **Shelf Life:** Discard the container 4 weeks after initial opening [Australian].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** 
    - **Australian:** Prescription Only Medicine (Schedule 4).
- **Poison Schedule:** Schedule 4 [Australian].
