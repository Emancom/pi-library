# Editorial Product Information: Pantoprazole

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) documents from the following jurisdiction: **Australia (Therapeutic Goods Administration)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, consolidating clinical and regulatory data into a single, accessible reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI or local regulatory guidelines required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Pantoprazole (as sodium sesquihydrate).
- **Chemical Class:** Proton Pump Inhibitor (PPI); substituted benzimidazole.
- **Brand Names:** APO-Pantoprazole [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** A02BC02.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
  - Enteric-coated tablets: 20 mg and 40 mg [Australian].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Arrotex Pharmaceuticals Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
Pantoprazole is indicated for the symptomatic improvement and healing of acid-related gastrointestinal diseases [Australian]:
- **Duodenal ulcer.**
- **Gastric ulcer.**
- **Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease (GORD):** Treatment of symptomatic GORD (heartburn and associated symptoms) and reflux oesophagitis.
- **Maintenance of healing:** Long-term management of healed reflux oesophagitis in patients with prior moderate to severe disease.
- **Gastrointestinal lesions refractory to H2-receptor antagonists.**
- **Zollinger-Ellison Syndrome.**
- **NSAID-associated prophylaxis:** Prevention of gastroduodenal lesions and dyspeptic symptoms in high-risk patients requiring continuous non-selective NSAID treatment.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Instructions
Tablets must be swallowed whole with water and should not be crushed or chewed [Australian].

### Adult Dosing by Indication
- **Duodenal Ulcer:** 40 mg once daily. Healing is typically achieved within 2–4 weeks.
- **Gastric Ulcer:** 40 mg once daily. Healing usually occurs within 4–8 weeks.
- **Reflux Oesophagitis:** 20 mg or 40 mg once daily. Dose may be increased to 80 mg/day if required.
- **Symptomatic GORD:** 20 mg once daily. If symptoms persist after 4 weeks, further investigation is warranted.
- **Maintenance of Healed Oesophagitis:** 20 mg or 40 mg once daily, adjusted based on patient response.
- **Zollinger-Ellison Syndrome:** Dosage must be individually titrated to maintain acid output below 10 mmol/L.
- **NSAID Prophylaxis:** 20 mg once daily in patients at increased risk (e.g., age >65, history of ulcers).

### Missed Dose
Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to pantoprazole or any excipients in the formulation [Australian].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Cirrhosis or severe liver disease [Australian].
- **Drug-Drug Interactions:** Co-administration with atazanavir [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Malignancy:** Treatment may alleviate symptoms of gastric malignancy and delay diagnosis. Exclude malignancy if "alarm symptoms" occur (e.g., unintended weight loss, recurrent vomiting, dysphagia, haematemesis, or melaena) [Australian].
- **Vitamin B12 Malabsorption:** Long-term therapy may lead to malabsorption of cyanocobalamin due to hypo- or achlorhydria. Caution is advised in patients with low body stores or risk factors for B12 deficiency [Australian].
- **Monitoring:** Patients on long-term treatment (exceeding one year) should undergo regular surveillance.
- **NSAID Use:** Prophylactic use should be limited to patients requiring continuous NSAID therapy with high risk for GI complications [Australian].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Pantoprazole is generally well-tolerated. Frequencies are categorised as follows based on [Australian] data:

| Frequency | Adverse Effects |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Headache, gastrointestinal upset (diarrhoea, constipation, abdominal pain, flatulence). |
| **Uncommon (0.1–1%)** | Dizziness, nausea, vomiting, pruritus, skin rash, blurred vision. |
| **Rare (<0.1%)** | Fever, anaphylactic shock, peripheral oedema, hypertension, dry mouth, myalgia, arthralgia, hypertriglyceridaemia, depression, hallucination, disorientation, confusion, anaemia. |
| **Very Rare (<0.01%)** | Circulatory collapse, interstitial nephritis, thrombocytopenia, leucopenia, Stevens-Johnson syndrome, toxic epidermal necrolysis (TEN), hepatic failure, jaundice. |

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Atazanavir:** PPIs significantly reduce the bioavailability of atazanavir; co-administration is contraindicated [Australian].
- **pH-Dependent Absorption:** Reduced gastric acidity may alter the absorption of drugs like ketoconazole [Australian].
- **Coumarin Anticoagulants (e.g., Warfarin):** While clinical studies showed no interaction, post-marketing reports indicate changes in INR. Monitoring of prothrombin time/INR is recommended during concurrent use [Australian].
- **CYP450 System:** Pantoprazole is metabolised via CYP2C19 and CYP3A4. No clinically significant interactions were observed with carbamazepine, diazepam, digoxin, ethanol, phenytoin, or oral contraceptives [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [Australian] **Category B3**. Animal studies showed foetotoxicity at high doses (skeletal ossification delays, reduced pup growth). There is no information on safety in human pregnancy; use only if the benefit clearly outweighs the potential risk to the foetus.
- **Breastfeeding:** Animal data showed decreased pup growth. Safety in humans has not been established. Use during lactation only if benefits outweigh risks [Australian].
- **Fertility:** No effect on fertility was observed in animal models at doses up to 60-fold the clinical exposure [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** No data currently available; use is not recommended in children [Australian].
- **Elderly:** No dose adjustment is required for patients over 65 years of age [Australian].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment is required, including for patients undergoing dialysis [Australian].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** 
  - **Severe:** Contraindicated [Australian].
  - **Mild:** Reduce the initial dose; the minimum effective dose has not been determined [Australian].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Specific inhibitor of the gastric H+/K+-ATPase enzyme (the "proton pump"). It accumulates in the acidic environment of the parietal cells where it is converted to its active sulfenamide form, which binds to the pump and inhibits acid secretion [Australian].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Acid suppression is potent and long-lasting, affecting both basal and stimulated secretion. It increases serum gastrin levels proportionally to acid reduction; this effect is reversible.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
  - **Absorption:** Rapidly absorbed; absolute bioavailability is ~77%. Peak plasma concentrations (Cmax) are reached in approximately 2.5 hours [Australian].
  - **Distribution:** 98% protein bound; volume of distribution is ~0.15 L/kg.
  - **Metabolism:** Extensively metabolised in the liver via the CYP450 system (mainly demethylation by CYP2C19). 
  - **Elimination:** Approximately 80% is excreted renally as metabolites; the remainder is excreted in faeces. The terminal half-life is ~1 hour.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Symptomatic GORD:** Placebo-controlled trials demonstrated that 20 mg daily significantly relieved heartburn and reflux symptoms compared to placebo within 1–2 weeks [Australian].
- **Reflux Oesophagitis:** Multicentre studies showed 20 mg was superior to ranitidine (300 mg) for healing Stage 1 oesophagitis at 4 and 8 weeks [Australian].
- **NSAID Prophylaxis:** In a 6-month study of 515 patients, pantoprazole 20 mg was significantly more effective than misoprostol (200 mcg twice daily) in preventing "therapeutic failure" (endoscopic lesions or severe symptoms) [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage Temperature:** Store below 25°C [Australian].
- **Shelf Life:** Refer to the expiry date on the packaging.
- **Handling:** Protect from light and moisture.
