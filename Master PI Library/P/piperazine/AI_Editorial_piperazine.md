## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Piperazine Citrate 275 mg per 5 mL (Equivalent to 110 mg piperazine base).
- **Chemical Class:** Anthelmintic (Piperazine derivative).
- **Brand Names:** WormEze Liquid Canine & Feline Anthelmintic, WormEze Liquid Feline Anthelmintic [US].
- **ATC Code:** QP52AH01 (Piperazine).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Oral liquid/gel; 275 mg piperazine citrate per 5 mL teaspoon [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Durvet Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- Removal of large roundworms (ascarids), specifically *Toxocara canis*, *Toxocara cati*, and *Toxascaris leonina* in canine and feline populations [US].
- **Note:** This product is effective against intestinal stages of roundworms but does not remove migrating larval stages [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Veterinary)
- **Standard Dose:** 1 teaspoonful (5 mL) per 5 pounds (approx. 2.27 kg) of body weight [US].
- **Administration Instructions:** 
    - May be applied directly into the animal's mouth or mixed into food or drinking water [US].
    - Suitable for dogs and cats aged 6 weeks or older [US].
- **Schedule:** 
    - Initial treatment followed by a repeat dose 10 days after the first treatment [US].
    - Animals should not be wormed more than twice yearly unless otherwise advised by a veterinarian [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Age/Weight Restrictions:** Do not use in puppies or kittens under 6 weeks of age. Do not use in puppies weighing less than 5 lbs or kittens weighing less than 3 lbs [US].
- **Clinical Status:** Never administer to a sick or severely debilitated pet [US].
- **Target Parasites:** Not indicated for the treatment of heartworms or tapeworms [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Renal Impairment:** Animals with known kidney pathology should only be treated under the direct supervision of a veterinarian [US].
- **Monitoring:** Animals should be checked periodically by a veterinarian for the presence of other intestinal parasites [US].
- **General Safety:** Side effects are typically associated with overdosage; strictly adhere to the recommended weight-based dosing [US].
- **Human Safety:** Keep out of reach of children. For oral animal use only [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
While piperazine generally possesses a wide margin of safety, the following adverse effects may occur, particularly in the event of overdosage:
- **Gastrointestinal:** Nausea, vomiting [US].
- **Neurological:** Muscular tremors [US].
- **Management:** If side effects are noticed, a veterinarian should be consulted immediately [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Not recommended for use in pregnant females [US].
- **Lactation:** Not recommended for use in nursing females [US].
- **Fertility:** Data not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric (Veterinary):** Safety and efficacy have not been established for animals under 6 weeks of age or those falling below the minimum weight requirements (5 lbs for puppies; 3 lbs for kittens) [US].
- **Elderly (Veterinary):** Use with caution; consult a veterinarian before treating severely debilitated or aged animals [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Use with extreme caution. Professional veterinary oversight is mandatory for animals with known kidney pathology [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Piperazine acts as a neuromuscular blocking agent in susceptible parasites. It causes flaccid paralysis of the worm's musculature, leading to its expulsion from the host's intestinal tract via peristalsis.
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not detailed in the provided veterinary source.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Specifically targeted toward intestinal stages of ascarids [US].
