# Editorial Product Information: Permethrin

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information and labeling documentation from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**. 
- **Note on Source Material:** Although the user-provided context mentions an Australian source, the primary documentation provided for review is the US "Drug Facts" labeling and FDA-structured NDC data for **Kakshal Permethrin**. Consequently, this synthesis reflects US regulatory standards and labeling requirements.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. It is an editorial synthesis and should not be used as a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) or "Drug Facts" required for prescribing or dispensing in any specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Permethrin 1% (w/v).
- **Chemical Class:** Pyrethroid.
- **Brand Name(s):** Kakshal [US].
- **ATC Code:** P03AC04.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Topical Lotion, 1% (1 g per 100 mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Manufactured by Yash Pharma Laboratories Pvt Ltd for Kakshal [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Head Lice Treatment:** Indicated for the treatment of infestations with *Pediculus humanus capitis* (head lice) [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric > 2 months)
- **Application:** Apply a sufficient quantity of lotion to saturated hair and scalp after washing with a non-conditioning shampoo and towel-drying.
- **Target Areas:** Ensure complete coverage of the hair and scalp, paying particular attention to the area behind the ears and the back of the neck.
- **Contact Time:** The lotion must be left on the hair and scalp for exactly 10 minutes [US].
- **Removal:** Rinse thoroughly with warm water.
- **Mechanical Removal:** Use a fine-tooth or nit comb to remove remaining dead lice and nits (eggs) following the rinse.

### Re-treatment Schedule
- Patients should be inspected 7 days after the initial treatment.
- A second application is mandatory if live lice are observed seven days or more after the first treatment [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to permethrin, any of the inactive ingredients, or other pyrethroids/pyrethrins [US]. 
- **Specific Sensitivities:** Clinical caution is advised for individuals with known allergies to ragweed or chrysanthemums, as permethrin products may trigger allergic reactions in these populations [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **External Use Only:** This product is for topical use; it must not be ingested or applied to mucous membranes [US].
- **Ocular Exposure:** Avoid contact with the eyes. If the product enters the eyes, they should be flushed immediately and thoroughly with water [US].
- **Infection/Irritation:** Discontinue use and consult a physician if skin irritation persists or if signs of a secondary skin infection occur [US].
- **Systemic Symptoms:** Patients should seek medical advice if breathing difficulties occur following application [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are generally localized to the site of application.

| Frequency | Adverse Event |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Scalp itching (pruritus), redness (erythema), or mild edema [US]. |
| **Post-Marketing/Rare** | Burning, stinging, or tingling sensations at the application site. |
| **Serious** | Difficulty breathing or severe allergic reactions (rare) [US]. |

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources. Topical absorption of permethrin 1% is minimal, limiting the potential for systemic drug-drug interactions.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Patients who are pregnant are advised to consult a healthcare professional before use [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Patients who are breastfeeding should consult a healthcare professional before use [US].
- **Fertility:** Data not provided in the available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy have not been established in infants under 2 months of age. Use in this age group requires direct medical supervision [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources; however, no specific age-related dosage adjustments are typically required for topical pediculicides.
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Due to negligible systemic absorption of topical permethrin, dosage adjustments are not specified [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Permethrin is a neurotoxin that acts as a sodium channel blocker. It disrupts the sodium channel current in the nerve cell membranes of parasites, leading to delayed repolarization, paralysis, and death of the lice [General Clinical Knowledge; not specified in source].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Minimal systemic absorption following topical application.
    - **Metabolism:** Rapidly esterified in the liver if absorbed systemically.
    - **Elimination:** Metabolites are primarily excreted in the urine.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pivotal Data:** Specific pivotal trial summaries were not provided in the source documentation. Approval is based on the established safety and efficacy of permethrin 1% under the US OTC Monograph system [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at controlled room temperature between 20°C and 25°C (68°F–77°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Avoid exposure to excessive heat [US].
