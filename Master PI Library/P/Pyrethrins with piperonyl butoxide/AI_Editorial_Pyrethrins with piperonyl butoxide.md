# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial synthesis is derived from the official Product Information (PI) provided by the **United States (FDA)** for Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter Healthcare Corporation).
- Note: Although the introductory context mentioned "Pyrethrins with piperonyl butoxide," the provided source document pertains exclusively to **Levocarnitine Injection**. Consequently, this editorial focuses on Levocarnitine to ensure clinical accuracy and traceability to the provided source.
- This document is intended as a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals and is not a substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); a carrier molecule for long-chain fatty acid transport.
- **Chemical Name:** (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US-approved].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Administration
- Administer via slow intravenous bolus (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion.
- Parenteral products should be inspected for particulate matter and discoloration prior to use [US].

### Metabolic Disorders
- **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg.
- **Acute/Severe Crisis:** A loading dose is often administered, followed by an equivalent dose divided over the subsequent 24 hours.
- **Frequency:** Administer every 3 or 4 hours; intervals should never be less than every 6 hours.
- **Dose Range:** Subsequent daily doses are typically 50 mg/kg, though higher doses up to 300 mg/kg may be required based on clinical response.

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Recommended Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight).
- **Administration:** Slow 2 to 3 minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
- **Titration:** Dose adjustments should be guided by pre-dialysis (trough) plasma levocarnitine concentrations (target range: 40 µmol/L to 50 µmol/L). Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known based on available sources [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Hypersensitivity Reactions
- **Serious Reactions:** Anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm have been reported. These may occur within minutes of intravenous administration, particularly in patients with ESRD on dialysis.
- **Management:** Discontinue treatment immediately and initiate appropriate medical care. Re-administration should only occur after a careful risk-benefit analysis and under close monitoring [US].

### Neurologic Precautions (Seizures)
- Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].

### General Precautions
- **Renal Impairment (Oral Levocarnitine):** While this PI focuses on the injection, it notes that high doses of *oral* levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common Adverse Effects (≥5% in ESRD Trials)
Reported regardless of causality in clinical trials of patients on chronic hemodialysis:
- **Body as a Whole:** Abdominal pain (21%), Asthenia (9% to 12%), Chest pain (6% to 15%), Fever (5% to 12%), Flu syndrome (15% to 29%), Headache (3% to 37%), Infection (10% to 24%), Injection site reaction (27% to 38%), Pain (21% to 35%).
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (18% to 21%), Hypotension (3% to 19%), Tachycardia (5% to 9%).
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea (9% to 35%), Nausea (5% to 12%), Vomiting (9% to 21%).
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (15% to 27%), Cough increase (10% to 18%), Dyspnea (3% to 11%).
- **Neurologic:** Dizziness (10% to 18%), Paresthesia (3% to 12%).

### Other Reported Effects
- **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea/vomiting, gastritis, and body odor [US].
- **Serious:** Anaphylaxis, seizures [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have occurred when used concomitantly with levocarnitine.
- **Monitoring:** INR levels should be monitored closely after initiating levocarnitine or following dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
### Pregnancy
- **Risk Summary:** Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at doses up to 3.8 times the human dose (based on surface area) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm.
- **Clinical Recommendation:** There are no adequate/well-controlled studies in pregnant women. Levocarnitine should be used during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].

### Breastfeeding
- **Risk Summary:** Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine concentrations in milk following exogenous administration.
- **Clinical Recommendation:** The risk of excess carnitine intake in the infant must be weighed against the benefit of supplementation to the mother. Discontinuation of nursing or the drug may be considered [US].

### Fertility
- No evidence of impaired fertility was observed in animal studies (rats/rabbits) [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Recommended for the treatment of inborn errors of metabolism (secondary carnitine deficiency) as per the "Metabolic Disorders" dosing section [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Specifically indicated for use in ESRD patients on hemodialysis. Monitor for toxic metabolite accumulation if oral forms are used [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
- Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production [US].
- It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine complexes, which are rapidly eliminated.

### Pharmacokinetics
- **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin. The distribution half-life is approximately 0.585 hours.
- **Excretion:** Approximately 76% of an intravenous dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours.
- **Elimination Half-life:** The mean apparent terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients, 20 mg/kg doses three times weekly post-dialysis significantly increased plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Trough levels remained stable or increased over 8 weeks.
- **Dose-Ranging:** Studies comparing 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses over 12 and 24 weeks demonstrated dose-dependent increases in trough levocarnitine concentrations [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Protection:** Avoid excessive heat and protect from freezing.
- **Stability:** Compatible with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s (0.5 mg/mL to 8.0 mg/mL concentrations) for up to 24 hours at room temperature in PVC bags [US].
- **Handling:** Single-dose vials; discard any unused portion as the formulation contains no preservative [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only (Prescription only) [US].
- **Poison/Schedule:** Not specified for other jurisdictions in the provided source.
