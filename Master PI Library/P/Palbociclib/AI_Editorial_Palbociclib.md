# Editorial Product Information: Palbociclib

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** Australian (TGA). 
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial synthesis of the official Product Information for **palbociclib**. It is designed as a high-level reference for healthcare professionals.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial summary and does not replace the official, legally approved Product Information in any specific jurisdiction. Prescribers should consult the local PI before initiating therapy.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Palbociclib; a small-molecule inhibitor of cyclin-dependent kinases (CDK) 4 and 6.
- **Brand Name:** **Ibrance** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** L01EF01 (Not specified in available sources; sourced from standard clinical reference).
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:**
    - **Hard Capsules:** 75 mg, 100 mg, and 125 mg [Australian].
    - **Film-coated Tablets:** 75 mg, 100 mg, and 125 mg [Australian].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Pfizer Australia Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Advanced or Metastatic Breast Cancer:** Indicated for the treatment of hormone receptor (HR)-positive, human epidermal growth factor receptor 2 (HER2)-negative advanced or metastatic breast cancer in combination with:
    - An aromatase inhibitor (as initial endocrine-based therapy).
    - Fulvestrant (in patients who have received prior therapy) [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **Standard Adult Dose:** 125 mg taken once daily for 21 consecutive days, followed by 7 days off treatment to complete a 28-day cycle [Australian].
- **Combination Therapy Dosing:**
    - **Aromatase Inhibitors:** Administer according to the specific agent's approved schedule.
    - **Fulvestrant:** 500 mg intramuscularly on Days 1, 15, 29, and then monthly thereafter [Australian].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - **Capsules:** Must be taken **with food** to ensure consistent exposure.
    - **Tablets:** May be taken **with or without food**.
    - Dose should be taken at approximately the same time each day. Swallow whole; do not chew, crush, or open [Australian].
- **Missed or Vomited Doses:** If a patient vomits or misses a dose, an additional dose must not be taken. The next dose should be taken at the usual time [Australian].
- **Dose Modifications:**
    - **Dose Levels:** 125 mg (Starting) -> 100 mg (First Reduction) -> 75 mg (Second Reduction). If further reduction below 75 mg is required, discontinue treatment [Australian].
    - **Haematological Toxicity:** Withhold dose for Grade 3 or 4 events. For Grade 3 neutropenia on Day 1, withhold until recovery to ≤ Grade 2 before starting the next cycle. For Grade 3 neutropenia with fever or Grade 4 events, resume at the next lower dose [Australian].
    - **Non-Haematological Toxicity:** For Grade ≥3 persistent toxicity, withhold until symptoms resolve to ≤ Grade 1 (or ≤ Grade 2 if not a safety risk) and resume at the next lower dose [Australian].

## 4. Contraindications
- Hypersensitivity to **palbociclib** or any excipients [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Myelosuppression:** Neutropenia is the most common adverse reaction. Monitor full blood count (FBC) prior to starting therapy, at the beginning of each cycle, on Day 15 of the first two cycles, and as clinically indicated. Severe neutropenia is associated with increased exposure, particularly in Asian populations [Australian].
- **Interstitial Lung Disease (ILD)/Pneumonitis:** Can be severe, life-threatening, or fatal. Monitor for pulmonary symptoms (cough, dyspnoea, hypoxia). Interrupt treatment immediately for new or worsening symptoms. Permanently discontinue for severe ILD/pneumonitis [Australian].
- **Infections:** Due to myelosuppression, patients are predisposed to infections. Monitor for signs/symptoms and treat promptly.
- **Venous Thromboembolism (VTE):** Monitor for signs of deep vein thrombosis and pulmonary embolism; treat as medically appropriate [Australian].
- **Lactose Intolerance:** **Ibrance** capsules contain lactose; use with caution in patients with hereditary galactose intolerance or glucose-galactose malabsorption [Australian].

## 6. Adverse Effects
*Frequencies based on pooled clinical trial data (N=872).*

| Frequency | Adverse Event |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Neutropenia, leukopenia, anaemia, thrombocytopenia, infections, fatigue, nausea, stomatitis, diarrhoea, alopecia, vomiting, decreased appetite, rash, asthenia, pyrexia, increased ALT/AST. |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Febrile neutropenia, epistaxis, dysgeusia, dry skin, blurred vision, increased lacrimation, dry eye. |
| **Uncommon (0.1–1%)** | Not specifically listed in primary frequency table. |
| **Post-marketing** | ILD/pneumonitis, VTE, Palmar-plantar erythrodysesthesia syndrome (PPES), erythema multiforme. |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Infections (4.6%), febrile neutropenia (1.3%), and ILD [Australian].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **CYP3A Inhibitors:** Strong inhibitors (e.g., itraconazole, clarithromycin, grapefruit juice) increase **palbociclib** exposure and should be avoided. If coadministration is unavoidable, consider a dose reduction [Australian].
- **CYP3A Inducers:** Strong inducers (e.g., rifampin, phenytoin, St. John’s Wort) significantly decrease exposure and should be avoided [Australian].
- **CYP3A Substrates:** **Palbociclib** is a weak time-dependent inhibitor of CYP3A. Doses of sensitive CYP3A substrates with a narrow therapeutic index (e.g., fentanyl, cyclosporine, sirolimus) may need reduction [Australian].
- **Acid-Reducing Agents:**
    - **Capsules:** Solubility is pH-dependent. Avoid PPIs under fasted conditions. PPIs have less impact when taken with food.
    - **Tablets:** No clinically relevant effect from PPIs [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** **Category D** [Australian]. Based on animal data and mechanism of action, **palbociclib** can cause fetal harm. Effective contraception is required during therapy and for at least 1 month (females) or 14 weeks (male partners of females of childbearing potential) after the final dose [Australian].
- **Breastfeeding:** Safety not established. It is unknown if the drug is excreted in human milk. Breastfeeding is not recommended [Australian].
- **Fertility:** Based on animal findings, male fertility may be compromised. Men should consider sperm preservation prior to treatment [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy in patients <18 years have not been established [Australian].
- **Elderly Use:** No dose adjustment required in patients ≥65 years [Australian].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment required for mild, moderate, or severe impairment (CrCl ≥15 mL/min). Data are insufficient for patients requiring haemodialysis [Australian].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No adjustment for mild/moderate (Child-Pugh A and B). For severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh C), the recommended dose is **75 mg once daily** on the 21/7 schedule [Australian].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Highly selective, reversible inhibitor of CDK4 and 6. It prevents the phosphorylation of retinoblastoma (Rb) protein, blocking cell cycle progression from G1 to S phase [Australian].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** $T_{max}$ 6–12 hours (capsules) or 4–12 hours (tablets). Mean bioavailability is 46%.
    - **Distribution:** ~85% protein bound.
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily metabolised by CYP3A and sulfotransferase enzyme SULT2A1.
    - **Elimination:** Mean plasma half-life is 28.8 hours. Excreted primarily in faeces (74.1%) and urine (17.5%) [Australian].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **PALOMA-1 & PALOMA-2:** Phase 2 and 3 trials respectively, evaluating **palbociclib** + letrozole vs letrozole alone in first-line treatment. PALOMA-2 showed a median PFS of 24.8 months vs 14.5 months (HR 0.576).
- **PALOMA-3:** Phase 3 trial evaluating **palbociclib** + fulvestrant vs placebo + fulvestrant in patients who progressed on prior endocrine therapy. Median PFS was 9.2 months vs 3.8 months (HR 0.422) [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store below 30°C.
- **Handling:** Tablets should be stored in the original blister pack to protect from moisture [Australian].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule:** **S4 (Prescription Only Medicine)** [Australian].
