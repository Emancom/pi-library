# Editorial Product Information: Pimavanserin

## Editorial Note
- Contributing Jurisdictions: **United States (FDA)**.
- Purpose: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of **pimavanserin** for healthcare professionals, synthesising regulatory information into a clinically relevant format.
- Note: This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing in specific jurisdictions. As only the United States PI was available at the time of writing, jurisdictional comparisons are limited to that region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Pimavanserin (as tartrate).
- **Chemical Class:** Atypical antipsychotic; selective serotonin inverse agonist (SSIA).
- **Brand Name(s):** **NUPLAZID** [US].
- **ATC Code:** N05AX17 (Not explicitly stated in source, but standard classification).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):**
  - **Capsules:** 34 mg (equivalent to 40 mg pimavanserin tartrate) [US].
  - **Tablets:** 10 mg (equivalent to 11.8 mg pimavanserin tartrate) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Acadia Pharmaceuticals Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Parkinson’s Disease Psychosis:** Treatment of hallucinations and delusions associated with Parkinson’s disease psychosis [US].
- **Note:** **Pimavanserin** is not approved for the treatment of patients with dementia-related psychosis unless the symptoms are specifically related to Parkinson’s disease [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** The recommended dosage is 34 mg taken orally once daily [US].
- **Titration:** No titration is required when initiating therapy [US].
- **Administration Instructions:**
  - May be taken with or without food [US].
  - **Capsules:** May be swallowed whole or opened and the contents sprinkled over one tablespoon (15 mL) of soft food (e.g., applesauce, yogurt, pudding). The mixture must be consumed immediately without chewing; do not store for later use [US].
- **Dosage Modifications (Drug Interactions):**
  - **Strong CYP3A4 Inhibitors:** Reduce dose to 10 mg once daily [US].
  - **Strong/Moderate CYP3A4 Inducers:** Avoid concomitant use [US].
- **Missed Dose:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known history of hypersensitivity to **pimavanserin** or any component of the formulation. Reported reactions include rash, urticaria, and angioedema (e.g., tongue/throat swelling, dyspnea) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning: Increased Mortality in Elderly Patients with Dementia-Related Psychosis [US]:**
  - Elderly patients with dementia-related psychosis treated with antipsychotic drugs are at an increased risk of death.
  - **Pimavanserin** is not approved for dementia-related psychosis unrelated to Parkinson's disease.
- **QT Interval Prolongation:** **Pimavanserin** prolongs the QT interval. Avoid use in patients with:
  - Known QT prolongation.
  - History of cardiac arrhythmias.
  - Congenital long QT syndrome.
  - Risk factors such as symptomatic bradycardia, hypokalemia, or hypomagnesemia [US].
- **Monitoring:** Monitor for signs of cardiac arrhythmia if **pimavanserin** is used in patients with significant cardiac risk factors.

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Very Common (>10%):** Not specified in available sources as meeting this threshold.
- **Common (1–10%):** 
  - Nausea (7%)
  - Peripheral edema (7%)
  - Confusional state (6%)
  - Hallucination (5%)
  - Constipation (4%)
  - Gait disturbance (2%) [US]
- **Serious Adverse Effects:**
  - **Increased mortality** in elderly patients with dementia-related psychosis [US].
  - **QT interval prolongation** [US].
  - **Hypersensitivity reactions:** Including angioedema [US].
- **Postmarketing Experience:** Somnolence, falls, agitation, aggression, and fecal incontinence have been reported [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacodynamic Interactions:**
  - **QT-Prolonging Drugs:** Avoid co-administration with Class 1A antiarrhythmics (e.g., quinidine, procainamide), Class 3 antiarrhythmics (e.g., amiodarone, sotalol), certain antipsychotics (e.g., ziprasidone, thioridazine), and certain antibiotics (e.g., moxifloxacin) [US].
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:**
  - **Strong CYP3A4 Inhibitors (e.g., ketoconazole):** Increase **pimavanserin** exposure (AUC) by approximately 3-fold. Reduce **pimavanserin** dose to 10 mg [US].
  - **Strong or Moderate CYP3A4 Inducers (e.g., rifampin, efavirenz):** Significantly decrease **pimavanserin** exposure. Concomitant use should be avoided [US].
- **No Clinically Significant Interaction:** No dose adjustment is required for carbidopa/levodopa [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:**
  - **US Category:** No letter category assigned (Current US labeling format).
  - **Risk Summary:** There are no data on use in pregnant women to inform drug-associated risks. Animal studies (rats/rabbits) showed no teratogenicity at 10–12 times the Maximum Recommended Human Dose (MRHD), but maternal toxicity and decreased pup survival were noted at higher doses (14 times MRHD) [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** There is no information on the presence of the drug in human milk or its effects on the breastfed infant. The clinical need for the mother should be weighed against potential risks to the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal data showed changes in uterine parameters and decreased sperm density/motility at maternally toxic doses (15–22 times MRHD), but no effect on overall mating performance or fertility was observed [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established. Clinical trials in pediatric patients with irritability associated with autism spectrum disorder failed to demonstrate efficacy [US].
- **Elderly Use:** No dose adjustment is required. However, see the Boxed Warning regarding increased mortality risk in dementia-related psychosis [US].
- **Renal Impairment:**
  - **Mild to Moderate:** No dosage adjustment needed.
  - **Severe/ESRD (CrCL <30 mL/min):** Use with caution. Increased exposure occurs, but dialysis does not significantly affect drug concentrations [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dosage adjustment is recommended. **Pimavanserin** should be used with caution in patients with hepatic impairment [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** The exact mechanism is unknown, but efficacy is thought to be mediated through a combination of inverse agonist and antagonist activity at serotonin 5-HT2A receptors and, to a lesser extent, 5-HT2C receptors [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Exhibits high affinity for 5-HT2A and lower affinity for 5-HT2C. It shows no appreciable affinity for dopaminergic (D2), muscarinic, histaminergic, or adrenergic receptors [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
  - **Absorption:** Median Tmax of 6 hours. Bioavailability is not significantly affected by high-fat meals.
  - **Distribution:** Highly protein-bound (~95%).
  - **Metabolism:** Predominantly metabolised by CYP3A4 and CYP3A5. The major active metabolite is **N-desmethylated pimavanserin (AC-279)**.
  - **Elimination:** Mean plasma half-life is ~57 hours for **pimavanserin** and ~200 hours for its active metabolite. Excretion is primarily minimal in urine (<1% as parent/active metabolite) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pivotal Study:** A 6-week, randomised, placebo-controlled study (n=199) demonstrated that **pimavanserin** 34 mg was statistically significantly superior to placebo in reducing the frequency and severity of hallucinations and delusions.
- **Efficacy Measure:** Measured by the Parkinson’s Disease-Adapted Scale for the Assessment of Positive Symptoms (SAPS-PD).
- **Motor Function:** **Pimavanserin** did not show a negative effect on motor function compared to placebo, as measured by the Unified Parkinson’s Disease Rating Scale (UPDRS) Parts II and III [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F and 86°F) [US].
- **Handling:**
  - **Capsules:** Protect from light to prevent potential color fading [US].
  - **Shelf Life:** Not specified in available sources.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison/Schedule Classification:**
  - **United States:** Prescription Only (Rx-only).
- **Legal Status:** Approved for Parkinson's disease psychosis in the United States (2016).
