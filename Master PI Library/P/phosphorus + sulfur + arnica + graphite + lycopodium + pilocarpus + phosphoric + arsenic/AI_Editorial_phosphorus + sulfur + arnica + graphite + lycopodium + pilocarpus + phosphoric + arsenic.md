## 3. Approved Indications
- Indicated uses (as stated):
  - Helps temporarily relieve:
    - Hair thinning and hair fall
    - Scalp irritation including itching, flaking, and dandruff
    - Dry or brittle hair
- Specific ingredient-associated claims (as described in product information):
  - Acid Phos (Acid Phos 5X): hair loss, thinning strands
  - Arnica Mont (7X): scalp stimulation, hair strengthening
  - Ars Alb (6X): hair weakening, brittle strands
  - Graphites (12X): hair texture, scalp support
  - Jaborandi / Pilocarpus (4X): scalp nourishment, hair growth
  - Lycopodium (6X): hair follicles, growth support
  - Phosphorus (7X): hair pigmentation, premature greying
  - Sulphur (6X): scalp health, dandruff control

## 4. Dosage and Administration
- General dosing (adult and paediatric):
  - Ages 12 years and older: 10 drops, mixed with half a cup of water and swallowed immediately. Repeat 3–4 times daily.
  - Ages 3 to 11 years: 5 drops, mixed with half a cup of water and swallowed immediately. Repeat 3–4 times daily.
  - Ages under 3 years: consult your doctor before use.
- Administration instructions:
