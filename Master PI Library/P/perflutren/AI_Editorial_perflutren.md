# Editorial Product Information: Perflutren

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesises information from official Product Information (PI) documents provided by the Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA, Australia) and the Food and Drug Administration (FDA, United States).
- **Contributing Agencies:** TGA (Australia), FDA (USA).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. It is a clinical synthesis intended for reference and does not replace the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Always consult the locally approved PI for the specific product brand available in your region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Perflutren (Synonyms: octafluoropropane, perfluoropropane).
- **Chemical Class:** Ultrasound contrast agent; fluorocarbon gas encapsulated in microspheres.
- **Brand Names:** 
    - **DEFINITY** (Lipid microspheres) [TGA].
    - **OPTISON** (Protein-type A / Human albumin microspheres) [FDA].
- **ATC Code:** V08DA04 (Perflutren).
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:**
    - **DEFINITY [TGA]:** 1.1 mg/mL perflutren in a lipid-encapsulated microsphere suspension (post-activation). Supplied in 2 mL vials (6.52 mg/mL perflutren in headspace).
    - **OPTISON [FDA]:** 0.22 mg/mL perflutren in human albumin microspheres (protein-type A). Supplied in 3 mL vials containing 5–8 × 10⁸ microspheres/mL.
- **Routes of Administration:** Intravenous (IV).
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** 
    - **DEFINITY:** Global Medical Solutions Australia Pty Limited [TGA].
    - **OPTISON:** GE Healthcare Inc. [FDA].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Echocardiography (Adults):** Indicated for use in patients with suboptimal echocardiograms to provide opacification of cardiac chambers and improve left ventricular endocardial border delineation [TGA, FDA].
- **Echocardiography (Paediatric):** Indicated for left ventricular opacification and endocardial border delineation [FDA-approved; not TGA-approved].
- **Abdominal Ultrasound (Liver and Kidney):** To improve characterisation of focal lesions of the liver and kidney [TGA-approved; not specified in FDA source].

## 3. Dosage and Administration

### General Administration
- **Visual Inspection:** Suspensions should be opaque and milky-white. Do not use if the liquid is clear (post-activation/resuspension) or if foreign particulate matter is present.
- **Preparation:**
    - **DEFINITY [TGA]:** Must be activated using the **VIALMIX** apparatus (shaken for 45 seconds). After activation, if not used within 5 minutes, resuspend by 10 seconds of hand agitation.
    - **OPTISON [FDA]:** Resuspend by inverting and gently rotating the vial. Do not use if the upper white layer is absent.
- **Site of Administration:** Inject into a peripheral vein. Do not aspirate blood into the syringe to avoid clot formation [FDA].

### Adult Dosing
- **Echocardiography (DEFINITY [TGA]):**
    - **Bolus:** 10 µL/kg by slow IV bolus, followed by 10 mL 0.9% saline flush. A second dose may be given after 5 minutes if needed. Maximum total dose 1.6 mL.
    - **Infusion:** 1.3 mL added to 50 mL 0.9% saline. Initiate at 4.0 mL/min; titrate to a maximum of 10 mL/min.
- **Echocardiography (OPTISON [FDA]):**
    - **Bolus:** 0.5 mL IV at a rate not exceeding 1 mL/s. If enhancement is inadequate, additional 0.5 mL doses may be repeated (max 5 mL in 10 minutes; total study max 8.7 mL).
- **Abdominal Ultrasound (DEFINITY [TGA]):**
    - **Non-linear technique:** Multiple 0.1–0.4 mL injections (max 1.6 mL total).
    - **Fundamental technique:** 10 µL/kg slow bolus.

### Paediatric Dosing (OPTISON [FDA] only)
- Administer 1:1 dilution with 0.9% saline at a rate $\le 0.05$ mL/s.
    - $\le 28$ kg: 0.2 mL.
    - 29–40 kg: 0.3 mL.
    - $\ge 41$ kg: 0.4 mL.

## 4. Contraindications
- Known or suspected hypersensitivity to perflutren or any component of the formulation [TGA, FDA].
- Known or suspected hypersensitivity to human albumin [FDA/OPTISON].
- **Cardiac Shunts:** Known or suspected right-to-left, bi-directional, or transient right-to-left cardiac shunts [TGA-contraindicated; FDA-warning].
- **Intra-arterial injection:** Strictly prohibited [TGA-contraindicated; FDA-warning].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions

### Boxed Warning [FDA]
- **Serious Cardiopulmonary Reactions:** Fatalities and serious reactions (e.g., cardiac arrest, arrhythmias, hypotension) have occurred uncommonly, typically within 30 minutes of administration. The risk is highest in patients with unstable cardiopulmonary conditions. Resuscitation equipment must be readily available.

### General Precautions
- **Serious Cardiopulmonary Reactions:** Monitor all patients for at least 30 minutes after administration. Use with extreme caution in patients with acute myocardial infarction, unstable angina, or severe heart failure [TGA, FDA].
- **Anaphylaxis:** Acute anaphylactoid reactions (shock, bronchospasm, angioedema) have been reported in patients with no prior exposure to perflutren [TGA, FDA].
- **Systemic Embolization:** In patients with cardiac shunts, microspheres can bypass pulmonary filtering and enter arterial circulation, potentially leading to embolic phenomena in small arterioles [FDA, TGA].
- **High Ultrasound Mechanical Index:** Mechanical indices $>0.8$ may cause microsphere cavitation/rupture and trigger ventricular arrhythmias [TGA, FDA].
- **QTc Prolongation:** Prolongation of $>30$ msec was observed in 29% of subjects in Definity trials. Use caution in patients with proarrhythmic conditions [TGA].
- **Sickle Cell Disease:** Acute pain episodes and vaso-occlusive crises have been reported post-marketing [TGA].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions are generally mild to moderate and resolve within 15 minutes.

| Frequency | Adverse Effect |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Headache, flushing, dizziness, nausea, chest pain, dyspnoea, back pain [TGA, FDA], fatigue [TGA], dysgeusia [FDA]. |
| **Uncommon (0.1–1%)** | Throat irritation, pruritus, increased sweating, abdominal pain, hypertension, hypotension, injection site pain/discomfort [TGA, FDA]. |
| **Rare (<0.1%)** | Paresthesia, syncope, peripheral coldness, cough, dry throat, rash, urticaria, arthralgia, pyrexia, rigors, abnormal ECG [TGA]. |

- **Serious Effects:** Fatal cardiac/respiratory arrest, symptomatic arrhythmias (VT, VF, AF), hypoxia, and loss of consciousness [TGA, FDA].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- Specific drug-drug interaction studies have not been performed [TGA, FDA].
- **Management Note:** Clinicians should consider potential risks when administering alongside drugs known to prolong the QTc interval [TGA].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **TGA Category B:** Animal studies showed no direct fetotoxic or teratogenic effects. Use only if clearly needed.
    - **FDA:** No data in pregnant women. Animal studies at 10x human doses showed no significant fetal findings [FDA/OPTISON].
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if perflutren is excreted in human milk. Exercise caution when administering to nursing women [TGA, FDA].
- **Fertility:** No effect on male or female fertility was found in rat studies [TGA].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** 
    - Approved for patients aged 9–17 years for echocardiography [FDA].
    - Safety and efficacy not established for patients <18 years; use is not recommended [TGA].
- **Elderly Use:** Pharmacokinetics have not been specifically studied in the elderly [TGA]. FDA trials for Optison included 35% of subjects $\ge 65$ years; no overall differences in safety were observed, though greater sensitivity cannot be ruled out.
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** No specific dosing adjustments are provided in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Microspheres provide an echogenic contrast effect because their acoustic impedance is significantly lower than blood. This causes ultrasound waves to scatter and reflect, opacifying the cardiac chambers or enhancing organ vasculature.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Metabolism:** Perflutren is a stable gas and is not metabolised. Lipid/protein components are handled by normal metabolic pathways.
    - **Elimination:** Perflutren is cleared via the lungs.
    - **Half-life:** Mean pulmonary elimination half-life is approximately 1.3 minutes [TGA, FDA]. Recovery is $\sim 96\%$ within 10 minutes [FDA].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Enhancement duration is dose-dependent; typically 3.4 minutes after bolus or 7.1 minutes after infusion [TGA].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Echocardiography:** Trials demonstrated significant improvement in endocardial border length visualization and ventricular opacification compared to baseline [TGA, FDA]. Definity converted 76% of non-diagnostic examinations to diagnostic [TGA].
- **Abdominal Imaging [TGA]:** Showed 93% sensitivity and 92% specificity in differentiating benign from malignant focal lesions of the liver.
- **Paediatric [FDA]:** A study of 37 patients (9–17 years) showed a significant increase in the number of visualized left ventricular wall segments following Optison administration.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C [TGA, FDA].
- **Handling:** Do not freeze. 
- **Definity [TGA]:** Activated suspension may be stored at room temperature for up to 12 hours.
- **Optison [FDA]:** Vials may be stored at room temperature (up to 25°C) for up to 24 hours upon receipt. Discard diluted product if not used within 10 minutes.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Australia [TGA]:** Unscheduled (diagnostic agent).
- **United States [FDA]:** Rx-only.
