# Editorial Product Information: Polyethylene Glycol

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is derived from official Product Information (PI) and Drug Facts documents provided by the **Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA)** of Australia and the **Food and Drug Administration (FDA)** of the United States.
- **Contributing Documents:**
    - Document 1: Australian Product Information – Mircera (methoxy polyethylene glycol-epoetin beta), TGA.
    - Document 2: Drug Facts – Polyethylene Glycol 3350 (Powder for Solution), FDA.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. It distinguishes between two distinct therapeutic applications of polyethylene glycol (PEG) found in the provided sources: a PEGylated erythropoiesis-stimulating agent (ESA) and an oral osmotic laxative.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific PI. Clinicians must consult local regulations and the specific product’s full PI before prescribing.

---

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** 
    - Methoxy polyethylene glycol-epoetin beta [TGA]
    - Polyethylene Glycol 3350 [FDA]
- **Chemical Class:** 
    - Erythropoietin receptor activator (PEGylated recombinant human erythropoietin) [TGA]
    - Osmotic laxative [FDA]
- **Brand Names:** 
    - **Mircera** [TGA]
    - Multiple generic/OTC labels (e.g., Strides Pharma Inc.) [FDA]
- **ATC Code:** B03XA03 (Methoxy polyethylene glycol-epoetin beta) [TGA]
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:**
    - **Injectable Solution (Pre-filled Syringes):** 30, 50, 75, 100, 120, 150, 200, 250, and 360 $\mu$g per 0.3 mL or 0.6 mL [TGA].
    - **Oral Powder for Solution:** 17 g per dose (available in bottles with measuring caps or individual packets) [FDA].
- **Routes of Administration:** 
    - Subcutaneous (SC) or Intravenous (IV) [TGA]
    - Oral [FDA]

---

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Anaemia associated with Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD):** Indicated for treatment in adult patients, including those on dialysis and not on dialysis [TGA].
- **Occasional Constipation:** Indicated for the relief of irregularity; generally produces a bowel movement in 1 to 3 days [FDA].

---

## 3. Dosage and Administration

### Methoxy polyethylene glycol-epoetin beta (Mircera) [TGA]
- **General Principle:** Use the lowest dose necessary to gradually increase haemoglobin (Hb) to a target of 100–120 g/L.
- **Patients Not Currently on an ESA:**
    - *Not on dialysis:* 1.2 $\mu$g/kg once monthly (SC) or 0.6 $\mu$g/kg every two weeks (IV/SC).
    - *On dialysis:* 0.6 $\mu$g/kg every two weeks (IV/SC).
- **Conversion from other ESAs (Epoetin or Darbepoetin):** Administer once monthly or every two weeks based on the previous weekly dose. (e.g., if previous epoetin was <8000 Units/week, start Mircera at 120 $\mu$g/month).
- **Titration:** Adjust dose by ~25% no more than once monthly. Reduce dose if Hb rise exceeds 10 g/L in two weeks or approaches 120 g/L.

### Polyethylene Glycol 3350 [FDA]
- **Adults and Children $\ge$17 years:** 17 g of powder per day.
- **Preparation:** Dissolve in 4 to 8 ounces (120–240 mL) of beverage (cold, hot, or room temperature).
- **Duration:** Use for no more than 7 days unless directed by a physician.

---

## 4. Contraindications
- **Known hypersensitivity** to polyethylene glycol or any excipients.
- **Uncontrolled hypertension** [TGA].
- **Kidney disease** (for oral laxative use, unless under medical supervision) [FDA].

---

## 5. Warnings and Precautions

### Cardiovascular and Thrombotic Risks [TGA]
- **Increased Mortality/Serious CV Events:** ESAs increase the risk of death, myocardial infarction, stroke, and congestive heart failure when targeted to Hb >120 g/L.
- **Thrombosis:** Increased risk of haemodialysis graft occlusion and deep vein thrombosis.

### Pure Red Cell Aplasia (PRCA) [TGA]
- Cases caused by neutralising anti-erythropoietin antibodies have been reported. If PRCA is suspected or confirmed, therapy must be discontinued immediately and the patient should not be switched to another ESA.

### Severe Cutaneous Adverse Reactions (SCARs) [TGA]
- Stevens-Johnson syndrome (SJS) and toxic epidermal necrolysis (TEN) have been reported. Discontinue permanently if these reactions occur.

### Hypertension [TGA]
- Blood pressure must be adequately controlled before and during treatment.

### Oral Laxative Specific Precautions [FDA]
- **Allergy Alert:** Do not use if allergic to PEG.
- **Clinical Cautions:** Consult a doctor before use if the patient has nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain, sudden change in bowel habits lasting >2 weeks, or irritable bowel syndrome (IBS).
- **Starch-based thickener interaction:** Do not combine PEG 3350 with starch-based thickeners used for dysphagia.

---

## 6. Adverse Effects

### Methoxy polyethylene glycol-epoetin beta [TGA]
- **Very Common (>10%):** Hypertension (19%), Diarrhoea (17%), Nasopharyngitis (15%), Headache (13%), Procedural hypotension (13%), Muscle spasms (11%), Upper respiratory tract infection (11%), Cough (10%), Back pain (10%), Fluid overload (10%).
- **Common (1–10%):** Vomiting, constipation, nausea, urinary tract infection, dizziness, arteriovenous fistula thrombosis.
- **Uncommon/Rare:** Vascular access thrombosis, hypertensive encephalopathy, anaphylactic reactions, rash (maculo-papular).

### Polyethylene Glycol 3350 (Oral) [FDA]
- **Stool Changes:** Loose, watery, or more frequent stools.
- **Serious Symptoms (Seek medical advice):** Rectal bleeding, worsening bloating/cramping, or persistent diarrhoea.

---

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Mircera:** No formal interaction studies performed. Clinical trials showed no indication of interaction with concomitant medications [TGA].
- **PEG 3350:** Patients should consult a healthcare professional if they are taking any prescription drugs before using this osmotic laxative [FDA].

---

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:**
    - **Mircera:** Australian **Category B3**. Animal studies showed reduced foetal weight and increased post-implantation loss at clinical exposure levels. Use only if potential benefit justifies risk to the foetus [TGA].
    - **PEG 3350:** Consult a health professional before use [FDA].
- **Breastfeeding:**
    - **Mircera:** Unknown if excreted in human milk. Excreted in rat milk with adverse effects noted in offspring (increased mortality, developmental delays). Caution advised [TGA].
    - **PEG 3350:** Consult a health professional before use [FDA].

---

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatrics:** 
    - **Mircera:** Not recommended for use in patients <18 years due to lack of safety/efficacy data [TGA].
    - **PEG 3350:** For children $\le$16 years, consult a doctor before use [FDA].
- **Elderly:** No adjustment of the starting dose is required for patients $\ge$65 years [TGA].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment required for any degree of hepatic impairment [TGA].
- **Renal Impairment:** See Section 3 for Mircera CKD dosing. For oral PEG 3350, patients with kidney disease must consult a doctor before use [FDA].

---

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:**
    - **Mircera:** A continuous erythropoietin receptor activator. Compared to epoetin, it has a slower association with the receptor and faster dissociation, leading to lower affinity but a significantly longer half-life (approx. 134–142 hours) [TGA].
    - **PEG 3350:** An osmotic agent that increases the frequency of bowel movements and softens the stool by retaining water in the intestinal lumen [FDA].
- **Pharmacokinetics (Mircera):** Absolute bioavailability is 62% (dialysis) to 54% (non-dialysis) after SC injection. Volume of distribution is ~5 L. It is not detected in urine, suggesting minimal renal excretion [TGA].

---

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Mircera:** Evaluated in Phase III trials (CORDATUS, ARCTOS, AMICUS) involving patients not previously treated with ESAs. Mircera was clinically non-inferior to darbepoetin alfa. In maintenance trials (MAXIMA, PROTOS, STRIATA, RUBRA), Mircera effectively maintained Hb levels in patients switched from epoetin or darbepoetin [TGA].

---

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Mircera:** Store in a refrigerator at 2°C to 8°C. Do not freeze or shake. Protect from light. The end user may store it at room temperature (up to 30°C) for a single period of 1 month; if not used within this month, it must be discarded [TGA].
- **PEG 3350:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F–77°F) [FDA].
