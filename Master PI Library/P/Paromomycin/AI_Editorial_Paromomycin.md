# Editorial Product Information: Paromomycin

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Note on Jurisdictional Sources:** While the introductory prompt references an Australian source, the source material provided for review is the **United States (FDA)** Prescribing Information for **Humatin**. Consequently, this editorial primarily reflects United States regulatory labeling.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonized editorial overview intended for use by healthcare professionals. It serves as a clinical synthesis and is not a substitute for the official PI required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Paromomycin sulfate (an aminoglycoside antibiotic).
- **Brand Name(s):** **Humatin** [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Oral capsules containing paromomycin sulfate equivalent to 250 mg of paromomycin [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Waylis Therapeutics LLC [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Intestinal Amebiasis:** Treatment of acute and chronic intestinal amebiasis. **Note:** Paromomycin is not effective for extraintestinal amebiasis [US].
- **Hepatic Coma:** Management of hepatic coma as adjunctive therapy [US].
- **Antibacterial Stewardship:** Use should be reserved for infections proven or strongly suspected to be caused by susceptible bacteria to prevent the development of drug-resistant organisms [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Intestinal Amebiasis (Adult and Pediatric Patients)
- **Usual Dosage:** 25 to 35 mg/kg body weight daily.
- **Frequency:** Administered in three divided doses.
- **Administration:** Must be taken with meals.
- **Duration:** Five to ten days [US].

### Management of Hepatic Coma (Adults)
- **Usual Dosage:** 4 g daily.
- **Frequency:** Administered in divided doses at regular intervals.
- **Duration:** Five to six days [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** History of previous hypersensitivity reactions to paromomycin sulfate [US].
- **Intestinal Obstruction:** Use is contraindicated in the presence of intestinal obstruction [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None specified in available sources [US].
- **Superinfection:** As with other antibiotics, prolonged use may result in the overgrowth of non-susceptible organisms, including fungi. Constant clinical observation is essential [US].
- **Renal Toxicity Risk:** Use with caution in patients with ulcerative bowel lesions. Although poorly absorbed, significant intestinal damage may allow for inadvertent systemic absorption, potentially leading to renal toxicity [US].
- **Diagnostic Uncertainty:** Prescribing in the absence of a proven or strongly suspected bacterial infection is unlikely to provide benefit and increases the risk of antimicrobial resistance [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are primarily gastrointestinal in nature, particularly at higher dosages.

| Frequency | Adverse Event |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Common (>3g daily)** | Nausea, abdominal cramps, diarrhea [US] |
| **Not Specified** | Overgrowth of non-susceptible organisms/fungi [US] |

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources [US].
- **Clinically Significant Interactions:** Not specified in available sources [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Not specified in available sources. No specific pregnancy category or safety data provided in the US PI [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Not specified in available sources [US].
- **Fertility:** Not specified in available sources [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Approved for intestinal amebiasis; dosing is weight-based (25 to 35 mg/kg/day) [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Dosing adjustments not specified; however, caution is advised in patients with intestinal ulceration due to the risk of systemic absorption and subsequent renal toxicity [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources (beyond the use of the drug for the management of hepatic coma) [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Paromomycin is a broad-spectrum aminoglycoside antibiotic produced by *Streptomyces riomosus var. paromomycinus*. Its antibacterial action closely parallels that of neomycin [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Poorly absorbed following oral administration.
    - **Elimination:** Almost 100% of the oral dose is recoverable in the stool [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from moisture. Preserve in tight containers as defined in the USP [US].
