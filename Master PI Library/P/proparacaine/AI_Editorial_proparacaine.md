# Editorial Product Information: Proparacaine Hydrochloride

- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of proparacaine hydrochloride for use by healthcare professionals. It serves as a clinically accurate reference for the drug's properties, indications, and safety profile across the evaluated jurisdiction.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis intended for informational purposes and is not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific Product Information required for prescribing.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Proparacaine hydrochloride (a benzoic acid ester local anesthetic).
- **Brand Name(s):** **Alcaine** [US].
- **ATC Code:** S01BA05 (not explicitly stated in source, provided for clinical context).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Ophthalmic solution, 0.5% (5 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical Ophthalmic.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Alcon Laboratories, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Corneal anesthesia of short duration:** Indicated for use in procedures where rapid, topical anesthesia is required, including:
    - Tonometry [US].
    - Gonioscopy [US].
    - Removal of corneal foreign bodies and sutures [US].
    - Short corneal and conjunctival procedures [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Minor procedures (e.g., tonometry, suture removal, foreign body removal):** Instil 1 to 2 drops in single instillations into each eye immediately before the procedure [US].
- **Short corneal and conjunctival procedures:** Instil 1 drop into each eye every 5 to 10 minutes for a total of 5 to 7 doses [US].

### Administration Instructions
- **Topical Ophthalmic Use Only:** Not for injection [US].
- **Supervision:** Must be administered under the direct supervision of a healthcare provider. It is not intended for patient self-administration [US].
- **Contamination Prevention:** Do not touch the dropper tip to any surface [US].
- **Solution Inspection:** The solution should be clear to straw-coloured. If the solution becomes darker, it must be discarded [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to proparacaine or any other ingredient in the formulation [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### General Warnings
- **Prolonged Use:** Long-term or repeated use is strongly discouraged. Chronic use may result in permanent corneal opacification and subsequent visual loss [US].
- **Administration Route:** Strictly for topical ophthalmic use; must not be injected [US].

### Specific Clinical Precautions
- **Self-Administration:** Patients should not be given the medication for self-use [US].
- **Corneal Protection:** Because the blink reflex is temporarily suspended during anesthesia, the eye should be protected from foreign bodies and irritation until sensation returns.

## 6. Adverse Effects
The following adverse effects have been reported; however, specific frequency data (e.g., % incidence) is not specified in available sources.

- **Ocular Effects:**
    - Occasional temporary stinging, burning, and conjunctival redness [US].
    - Corneal edema [US].
    - **Rare/Severe:** Acute, intense, and diffuse epithelial keratitis; "ground glass" appearance of the cornea; sloughing of large areas of necrotic epithelium; corneal filaments; iritis; and descemetitis [US].
- **Dermatologic Effects:** Allergic contact dermatitis, which may present with drying and fissuring of the fingertips [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not assigned by letter, but noted as follows]: Animal reproduction studies have not been conducted. It is unknown if proparacaine can cause fetal harm or affect reproductive capacity. Use in pregnant women only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if proparacaine is excreted in human milk. Exercise caution when administering to a nursing woman [US].
- **Fertility:** Long-term animal studies to evaluate effects on fertility have not been performed [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness are supported by evidence from well-controlled studies in adults and children over the age of 12. Safety information is also available for neonates and younger paediatric patients [US].
- **Elderly Use:** No overall clinical differences in safety or effectiveness have been observed between geriatric and younger adult patients [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** No specific dosage adjustments or precautions are provided in the available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Proparacaine is a rapidly-acting topical local anesthetic. It stabilises the neuronal membrane by inhibiting the ionic fluxes required for the initiation and conduction of impulses.
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Induced anesthesia typically occurs rapidly and lasts for approximately 10 to 20 minutes [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- Pivotal trial data was not detailed in the available source document. Use is supported by long-standing clinical practice and studies in adults and children over 12 [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage Temperature:** Store under refrigeration between 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) [US].
- **Light Protection:** The bottle must be stored in its original unit carton to protect it from light [US].
- **Shelf Life/Opening:** Following initial opening, the product may be used until the expiration date printed on the bottle, provided it is stored correctly and the solution has not darkened [US].
