# Editorial Product Information: Balfaxar (Prothrombin Complex Concentrate [Human-lans])

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising regulatory information regarding **Balfaxar**.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis designed for clinical reference and is not a substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Prothrombin complex concentrate (human-lans). It is a human plasma-derived, purified, virus-inactivated, and nanofiltered non-activated 4-factor prothrombin complex concentrate (PCC).
- **Brand Name:** **Balfaxar** [US].
- **ATC Code:** B02BD01 (Interpreted based on class; not explicitly stated in source).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Lyophilized powder for reconstitution (intravenous use).
    - Nominal 500 Factor IX units in a 20 mL reconstitution volume.
    - Nominal 1000 Factor IX units in a 40 mL reconstitution volume.
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous infusion only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Octapharma Pharmazeutika Produktionsges.m.b.H. (Austria); Distributed by Octapharma USA Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Urgent Reversal of Vitamin K Antagonist (VKA) Therapy:** Indicated for the urgent reversal of acquired coagulation factor deficiency induced by VKA (e.g., warfarin) in adult patients requiring urgent surgery or an invasive procedure [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing Principles:** Dosing must be individualised based on the patient’s baseline International Normalized Ratio (INR) and actual body weight.
- **Dosing Table (Reversal of VKA) [US]:**
| Pre-treatment INR | Dose of Balfaxar (Factor IX units/kg) | Maximum Dose (Factor IX units) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 – <4 | 25 units/kg | Not to exceed 2500 units |
| 4 – 6 | 35 units/kg | Not to exceed 3500 units |
| >6 | 50 units/kg | Not to exceed 5000 units |
- **Body Weight Limit:** Dosing is based on body weight up to, but not exceeding, 100 kg. For patients weighing >100 kg, the maximum dose should not be exceeded [US].
- **Vitamin K Co-administration:** Administer Vitamin K concurrently to maintain factor levels once the effect of **Balfaxar** diminishes.
- **Repeat Dosing:** Safety and effectiveness of repeat dosing have not been established and is not recommended [US].
- **Reconstitution and Administration:**
    - Reconstitute using the provided transfer device (Nextaro®).
    - Administration should begin promptly or within 8 hours of reconstitution if stored at room temperature (20°C to 25°C).
    - **Infusion Rate:** Administer at 0.12 mL/kg/min (~3 units/kg/min), up to a maximum rate of 8.4 mL/min (~210 units/min).
    - Do not mix with other medicinal products; use a separate infusion line.

## 4. Contraindications
- Known anaphylactic or severe systemic reactions to **Balfaxar** or any of its components (including heparin, antithrombin III, human albumin, and sodium citrate) [US].
- Known allergy to heparin or history of heparin-induced thrombocytopenia (HIT) [US].
- IgA-deficient patients with known antibodies against IgA [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning: Arterial and Venous Thromboembolic Complications:**
    - Both fatal and non-fatal arterial and venous thromboembolic complications have been reported.
    - Risk is elevated in patients with underlying disease states predisposed to thromboembolism. 
    - **Balfaxar** may not be suitable for patients who have experienced a thromboembolic event within the prior 3 months [US].
    - Monitor patients closely for signs and symptoms of thromboembolism.
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Discontinue infusion immediately if allergic or anaphylactic-type reactions occur.
- **Transmissible Infectious Agents:** As a product of human plasma, there is a theoretical risk of transmitting infectious agents, including viruses and the variant Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease (vCJD) agent.
- **Monitoring:** Monitor INR and clinical response during and after treatment. In clinical trials, INR typically decreased to ≤1.5 within 30 minutes of infusion [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Common Adverse Reactions (≥3%):**
    - Procedural pain (47.6%)
    - Postoperative wound complications (14.3%)
    - Asthenia (12.4%)
    - Anemia (5.7%)
    - Dysuria (4.8%)
    - Procedural vomiting (3.8%)
    - Catheter site related reaction (3.8%)
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Cerebral infarction, pulmonary embolism, unstable angina, myocardial ischemia, and subdural hemorrhage have been reported in the clinical trial setting [US].
- **Postmarketing Experience:** Outside the US, reports include anaphylactic shock, cerebrovascular accident, cardiac arrest, circulatory collapse, respiratory failure, and skin reactions (urticaria, rash).

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Incompatibilities:** Do not mix with other medicinal products. The infusion line may be flushed with normal saline before and after administration [US].
- **Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Use with other pro-coagulants or anticoagulants should be managed with caution given the risk of thromboembolism.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Pregnancy Category Not Assigned]. There are no data on use in pregnant women to inform drug-associated risks. Animal reproduction studies have not been conducted. It is unknown if **Balfaxar** can cause fetal harm.
- **Breastfeeding:** There is no information regarding the excretion of **Balfaxar** in human milk. The developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding should be weighed against the mother’s clinical need and potential adverse effects on the infant.
- **Fertility:** Long-term animal studies to evaluate the effect on fertility have not been performed.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy have not been evaluated in paediatric patients [US].
- **Elderly Use:** In clinical trials, 59% of subjects were ≥65 years and 20% were ≥75 years. No overall differences in safety or effectiveness were observed between these subjects and younger subjects [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specifically addressed in available sources, though monitoring for thromboembolic complications is advised in patients with liver disease [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Provides a rapid increase in plasma levels of Vitamin K-dependent coagulation factors (II, VII, IX, and X) and antithrombotic proteins C and S.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In clinical trials, the median INR dropped from 3.0 to 1.30 within 30 minutes post-infusion [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Given intravenously, bioavailability is proportional to the dose. It is distributed, metabolised, and excreted in the same manner as endogenous proteins.
    - **Incremental Recovery:** FII (1.73), FVII (0.68), FIX (1.17), FX (1.47), Protein C (1.25), Protein S (1.47) [%/IU/kg].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pivotal Trial:** A prospective, randomised, double-blind, multicenter non-inferiority trial compared **Balfaxar** (n=105) to Kcentra (n=103) in patients requiring urgent VKA reversal for surgery.
- **Primary Outcome:** Hemostatic efficacy was achieved in 94.3% of the **Balfaxar** group vs 94.2% in the Kcentra group, confirming non-inferiority.
- **INR Reduction:** 78.1% of **Balfaxar** patients achieved an INR ≤1.5 within 30 minutes of infusion [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 2°C to 25°C (36°F to 77°F).
- **Shelf Life:** Up to 36 months from the date of manufacture.
- **Special Precautions:** Do not freeze. Store in the original package to protect from light.
- **Post-reconstitution:** Use immediately or within 8 hours if stored at 20°C to 25°C. Discard partially used vials.
