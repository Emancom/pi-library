# Editorial Product Information: Lotilaner, Moxidectin, Praziquantel, and Pyrantel (Credelio Quattro)

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the **United States (FDA)** for **Credelio Quattro** and **Credelio Quattro-CA1**. 
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised clinical overview for healthcare professionals, specifically veterinarians and veterinary pharmacists. 
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing or dispensing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult the current local regulatory documentation before use.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** 
    - **Lotilaner** (Isoxazoline class)
    - **Moxidectin** (Macrocyclic lactone class)
    - **Praziquantel** (Isoquinolone class)
    - **Pyrantel** (as pamoate salt; Tetrahydropyrimidine class)
- **Brand Names:** Credelio Quattro, Credelio Quattro-CA1 [US]
- **ATCvet Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Flavored chewable tablets available in five sizes:
    - 56.25 mg lotilaner / 0.056 mg moxidectin / 14.25 mg praziquantel / 14.25 mg pyrantel
    - 112.5 mg lotilaner / 0.113 mg moxidectin / 28.5 mg praziquantel / 28.5 mg pyrantel
    - 225 mg lotilaner / 0.225 mg moxidectin / 57 mg praziquantel / 57 mg pyrantel
    - 450 mg lotilaner / 0.45 mg moxidectin / 114 mg praziquantel / 114 mg pyrantel
    - 900 mg lotilaner / 0.9 mg moxidectin / 228 mg praziquantel / 228 mg pyrantel
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Elanco US Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Heartworm Prevention:** Prevention of heartworm disease caused by *Dirofilaria immitis*.
- **Ectoparasite Treatment and Control:**
    - Treatment and prevention of flea infestations (*Ctenocephalides felis*).
    - Treatment and control of tick infestations, including: *Amblyomma americanum* (lone star tick), *Dermacentor variabilis* (American dog tick), *Ixodes scapularis* (black-legged tick), *Rhipicephalus sanguineus* (brown dog tick), and *Haemaphysalis longicornis* (longhorned tick).
    - Prevention of *Borrelia burgdorferi* (Lyme disease) infections by killing the vector tick (*Ixodes scapularis*).
- **Intestinal Helminth Treatment and Control:**
    - **Roundworms:** Immature adult and adult *Toxocara canis* and adult *Toxascaris leonina*.
    - **Hookworms:** Fourth-stage larvae (L4), immature adult, and adult *Ancylostoma caninum* and adult *Uncinaria stenocephala*.
    - **Tapeworms:** *Dipylidium caninum, Taenia pisiformis,* and *Echinococcus granulosus*.
- **Myiasis Treatment:** Treatment of infestations caused by New World screwworm (*Cochliomyia hominivorax*) larvae [US-conditionally approved as Credelio Quattro-CA1].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Minimum Dosage:** 20 mg/kg lotilaner, 0.02 mg/kg moxidectin, 5 mg/kg praziquantel, and 5 mg/kg pyrantel.
- **Frequency:** Administered orally once a month.

### Dosing Schedule [US]
| Body Weight (lbs) | Tablets | Lotilaner (mg) | Moxidectin (mg) | Praziquantel (mg) | Pyrantel (mg) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3.3 – 6 | 1 | 56.25 | 0.056 | 14.25 | 14.25 |
| 6.1 – 12 | 1 | 112.5 | 0.113 | 28.5 | 28.5 |
| 12.1 – 25 | 1 | 225 | 0.225 | 57 | 57 |
| 25.1 – 50 | 1 | 450 | 0.45 | 114 | 114 |
| 50.1 – 100 | 1 | 900 | 0.9 | 228 | 228 |
| > 100 | *Appropriate combination of tablets* | | | | |

### Administration Instructions
- **Food Requirement:** Must be administered with food or within 30 minutes of feeding to ensure optimal bioavailability of lotilaner.
- **Compliance:** Ensure the entire dose is consumed. If vomiting occurs within one hour of administration, redosing is required.
- **Heartworm Prophylaxis:** Start within one month of first seasonal exposure to mosquitoes and continue until one month after the last exposure.

### Missed Dose
- If a dose is missed, administer immediately and resume the monthly schedule.

## 4. Contraindications
- There are no known contraindications listed in available sources [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Human Safety:** Not for human use. Keep out of reach of children. Wash hands after handling. In case of accidental ingestion, seek medical attention immediately.
- **Neurological Precautions:** Lotilaner is an isoxazoline. This class is associated with potential neurological adverse reactions, including tremors, ataxia, and seizures. Use with caution in dogs with a history of seizures or neurological disorders.
- **Heartworm Testing:** Dogs should be tested for existing heartworm infections prior to administration. This product is not effective against adult *D. immitis*.
- **Screwworm Myiasis:** Effective treatment requires mechanical removal of larvae and appropriate wound care in addition to medication [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions observed in field studies (N=191) are categorized by frequency:

| Frequency | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Diarrhea (11%), Vomiting (9.4%), Lethargy (6.3%), Anorexia (5.8%), Dermatitis (5.2%), Weight loss (3.1%), Pruritus (1.6%), Alopecia (1.0%) |
| **Uncommon (0.1–1%)** | Seizure (0.5%), Ataxia (0.5%), Nystagmus (0.5%), Anisocoria (0.5%) |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Seizures and cluster seizures have been reported with the isoxazoline class. In field studies, some dogs without a prior history experienced seizures while receiving components of this drug [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Concurrent Medications:** Field studies indicated no adverse reactions when used concurrently with vaccines, antimicrobials, anthelmintics, antiemetics, steroids, NSAIDs, anesthetics, and analgesics [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy/Lactation:** The safety of this product has not been evaluated in breeding, pregnant, or lactating dogs. 
- **Fertility:** Data not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Indicated for puppies 8 weeks of age and older, weighing at least 3.3 lbs (1.5 kg).
- **Avermectin Sensitive Collies:** Safe use was demonstrated in healthy avermectin-sensitive Collies at up to 5X the maximum dose, though dose-dependent salivation and vomiting were observed [US].
- **Heartworm Positive Dogs:** Safe use was demonstrated in dogs with patent adult heartworm infections at up to 3X the maximum dose; GI signs (vomiting/diarrhea) were the primary observed effects [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
- **Lotilaner:** Inhibits GABA-gated chloride channels in insects and acarines, leading to uncontrolled neuromuscular activity and death.
- **Moxidectin:** Interferes with chloride channel-mediated neurotransmission in parasites, causing paralysis and death.
- **Praziquantel:** Induces muscular paralysis and tegument disruption in tapeworms via calcium ion influx.
- **Pyrantel:** Acts as a depolarizing neuromuscular-blocking agent in susceptible nematodes, leading to paralysis and expulsion.

### Pharmacokinetics [US]
- **Absorption:** Oral bioavailability (fed state): Lotilaner (93%), Praziquantel (41%), Pyrantel (31%). Moxidectin bioavailability is not fully described but reaches peak concentrations.
- **Tmax:** Lotilaner (10h), Moxidectin (4.5h), Praziquantel (0.75h), Pyrantel (2.5h).
- **Elimination Half-life:** Lotilaner (~33.5 days), Moxidectin (~26.4 days), Praziquantel (3.64h), Pyrantel (4.87h).

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Heartworm Prevention:** 100% effective in laboratory studies and field trials (Day 330) [US].
- **Intestinal Parasites:** ≥ 97% effective against target roundworms and hookworms. 100% effective against *E. granulosus* [US].
- **Ectoparasites:** Flea kill begins within 4 hours [European study] and reaches 100% within 24 hours [US]. Tick efficacy ≥ 97% for 30–32 days.
- **New World Screwworm:** Demonstrated 100% overall effectiveness (expulsion and larvicidal activity) within 24 hours in a Brazilian pilot study [US-CA1 data].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 15-25°C (59-77°F).
- **Excursions:** Permitted between 5 and 40°C (41 and 104°F).
- **Packaging:** Available in 1, 6, or 12 tablet packs.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Prescription Animal Drug (Caution: Federal law restricts this drug to use by or on the order of a licensed veterinarian).
- **Conditional Approval:** Credelio Quattro-CA1 is conditionally approved by the FDA pending full demonstration of effectiveness for the New World Screwworm indication (Application 141-619).
