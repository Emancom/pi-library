# Editorial Product Information: Pancrelipase

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesises information from the following regulatory sources:
    - Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA), Australia: Product Information for **Panzytrat 25000**.
    - Food and Drug Administration (FDA), United States: Product Information for **Creon** (Bulk Ingredient/Export documentation).
- Purpose: To provide a harmonised clinical overview of pancrelipase for healthcare professionals. 
- Disclaimer: This document is an editorial synthesis and does not replace the official, jurisdiction-specific Product Information required for prescribing.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Pancrelipase (Lipase, Amylase, and Protease).
- **Chemical Class:** Pancreatic enzymes (porcine origin).
- **Brand Names:** 
    - **Panzytrat 25000** [TGA]
    - **Creon** [FDA]
- **ATC Code:** A09AA02
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - **Panzytrat 25000 (Australia):** Capsules containing enteric-coated microtablets. Each capsule provides minimum activities of: Lipase 25,000 B.P. units; Amylase 22,500 B.P. units; Protease 1,250 Ph. Eur. units [TGA].
    - **Creon (United States - Bulk):** Delayed-release capsules containing pellets. Strengths (Lipase USP units): 3,000; 6,000; 12,000; 24,000; 36,000 [FDA].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** 
    - Technipro-PulmoMed Pty Ltd [TGA].
    - Abbott Laboratories GmbH [FDA].

## 2. Approved Indications
- Pancreatic enzyme replacement therapy for conditions associated with pancreatic exocrine insufficiency (PEI), including:
    - Cystic fibrosis.
    - Chronic pancreatitis.
    - Post-pancreatectomy.
    - Post-gastrointestinal bypass surgery (e.g., Billroth II gastroenterostomy).
    - Ductal obstruction.
- **Age Restriction:** Indicated for patients aged 18 months or older [TGA].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- Dosage should be individualised based on the severity of the disease, control of steatorrhoea, and nutritional status.
- **Initial Adult/Paediatric Dose (≥18 months):** One capsule with every meal and snack [TGA].
- **Standard Adult Dose:** Typically 6 capsules daily with food [TGA].
- **Standard Paediatric Dose (>18 months):** Typically 4 capsules daily with food [TGA].

### Dosing for Cystic Fibrosis [TGA]
- **Children (>18 months):** 1,500 – 6,000 B.P. units of lipase/kg body weight per meal.
- **Maximum Recommended Dose:** 20,000 B.P. units of lipase/kg body weight per day.
- **Total Pancreatic Insufficiency:** Generally requires up to 400,000 units of lipase daily.

### Administration Instructions
- Capsules should be taken with meals and snacks.
- **Swallowing:** Capsules should ideally be swallowed whole. If necessary, capsules may be opened and the enteric-coated microtablets/pellets taken with liquid or soft food (pH < 5.5) without chewing.
- **Integrity of Coating:** Neither the capsule nor the internal microtablets/pellets should be crushed or chewed, as this destroys the enteric coating and can cause oral mucosal irritation or early enzyme inactivation.
- **Hydration:** Ensure adequate hydration (approx. 100 mL fluid per dose) [TGA].

## 4. Contraindications
- Early stages of acute pancreatitis [TGA].
- Acute attacks of chronic pancreatitis [TGA].
- Known hypersensitivity to porcine protein or any excipients (e.g., lactose in Panzytrat) [TGA].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Fibrosing Colonopathy:** Reported in cystic fibrosis patients using high-dose enzyme supplements. Patients with a history of GI complications or those on very high dosages should be monitored (e.g., ultrasound) [TGA].
- **Viral Transmission Risk:** As pancrelipase is derived from porcine pancreatic tissue, the presence of porcine viruses (e.g., porcine parvovirus) cannot be totally excluded. No cases of infectious illness transmission have been reported [TGA].
- **Mucosal Irritation:** Disruption of the enteric coating (chewing/crushing) may lead to irritation of the oral mucosa.
- **Excipients:** Panzytrat contains lactose; caution is advised for patients with hereditary galactose intolerance or glucose-galactose malabsorption [TGA].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are generally reported infrequently.
- **Gastrointestinal:** Diarrhoea, constipation, abdominal discomfort, nausea [TGA].
- **Dermatological:** Skin reactions/rashes [TGA].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Bowel stricture formation (fibrosing colonopathy) has been reported, primarily in children with cystic fibrosis taking high-dose lipase supplements [TGA].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Antacids:** Calcium carbonate or magnesium hydroxide-based antacids may increase the pH in the duodenum and break down the enteric coating prematurely. If required, allow at least one hour between taking antacids and pancrelipase [TGA].
- **H2-Antagonists and Proton Pump Inhibitors (PPIs):** These agents increase gastric pH, which may increase the activity of the administered lipase. While not an approved indication for PPIs, clinicians may use this synergy based on evidence [TGA].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Safe use has not been established. Panzytrat should not be used in the first trimester unless the benefits clearly outweigh the potential hazards [TGA].
    - **Jurisdictional Category:** Not explicitly assigned in the provided bulk-label FDA document; TGA notes "Safe use... not been established."
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if components are excreted in human milk. Use only if potential benefits outweigh risks [TGA].
- **Fertility:** No data available [TGA].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Indicated for children ≥ 18 months [TGA]. (Note: FDA bulk labels for Creon include low-strength 3,000 unit capsules, though clinical use details were not provided in the bulk label PDF).
- **Elderly Use:** No specific data available in provided sources [TGA].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Replaces deficient pancreatic enzymes (lipase, amylase, protease) to assist in the digestion of fats, carbohydrates, and proteins.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** The microtablets are designed to resist gastric acid (pH 1) and distribute homogeneously with chyme. The enteric coating dissolves rapidly in the alkaline environment of the duodenum (pH > 5.5), releasing enzymes at the site of action [TGA].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** As enzymes, these are not absorbed but act locally in the gastrointestinal tract and are subsequently digested.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Panzytrat (TGA):** Store below 25°C in a dry place. Keep container tightly closed.
- **Creon (FDA - Bulk):** Store at room temperature up to 25°C (77°F) protected from moisture. Excursions permitted between 25°C to 40°C (77°F to 104°F) for up to 30 days [FDA].
