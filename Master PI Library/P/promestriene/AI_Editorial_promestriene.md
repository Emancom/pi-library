# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
*   **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA)
*   **Purpose:** This document is a harmonised clinical editorial synthesis of the official Product Information (PI) for levocarnitine injection. It is intended as a concise reference for healthcare professionals.
*   **Disclaimer:** This synthesis is an editorial summary and does not replace the official PI required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. At the time of compilation, only the US (FDA) source documentation was provided for review.

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN/USP).
*   **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule for mitochondrial fatty acid transport; amino acid derivative.
*   **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter Healthcare Corporation).
*   **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    *   **United States:** Sterile solution for injection: 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) single-dose vials [US].
*   **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
*   **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance hemodialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
*   **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    *   **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg administered as a slow 2- to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion [US].
    *   **Severe Metabolic Crisis:** A loading dose is often followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours. Dosing frequency should be every 3 to 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours [US].
    *   **Maintenance:** Daily doses typically range around 50 mg/kg, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilised [US].
*   **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    *   **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
    *   **Administration:** Slow 2- to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].
    *   **Dose Adjustments:** Reductions (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine levels [US].

### Administration and Monitoring
*   **Monitoring:** Obtain baseline plasma carnitine levels. Monitor weekly and monthly thereafter, including blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma concentrations (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L) [US].
*   **Compatibility:** Stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when diluted in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations ranging from 0.5 mg/mL to 8.0 mg/mL [US].
*   **Visual Inspection:** Inspect for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration [US].

## 4. Contraindications
*   None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
*   **Boxed Warnings:** None [US].
*   **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Some reactions occurred within minutes of administration. If severe reactions occur, discontinue immediately and initiate appropriate medical treatment [US].
*   **Seizure Activity:** Levocarnitine has been associated with reports of seizures in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with a known history, an increase in seizure frequency or severity has been observed [US].
*   **Renal Impairment (Oral Form Caution):** While the injection is indicated for ESRD, the safety and efficacy of **oral** levocarnitine have not been evaluated in renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in these patients may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
*   **Common (>10%):** Transient nausea and vomiting.
*   **Less Frequent:** Body odor, gastritis [US].
*   **Clinical Trial Data (ESRD patients, ≥5% incidence):**
    *   **Body as a whole:** Abdominal pain (9%), headache (22%), injection site reaction (33%), pain (30%), chest pain (12%), flu syndrome (25%), infection (15%).
    *   **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (20%), hypotension (14%), tachycardia (6%).
    *   **Digestive:** Diarrhea (16%), vomiting (15%), nausea (8%).
    *   **Nervous System:** Dizziness (13%), paresthesia (5%), depression (5%).
    *   **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (23%), cough increase (9%), dyspnea (7%), rhinitis (9%).
*   **Serious Adverse Effects:** Anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, bronchospasm, and seizures [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalised Ratio (INR) have occurred with concomitant use. 
*   **Management:** Monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy:** 
    *   **US Classification:** Not specified in lettered format in source. 
    *   **Safety Data:** Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at doses up to 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) revealed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
*   **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. It is known to increase in the milk of dairy cows following administration. Risks of excess intake to the child must be weighed against maternal benefits; discontinuation of nursing or the drug may be considered [US].
*   **Fertility:** Mutagenicity tests (Salmonella, yeast) were negative. Long-term animal carcinogenicity studies have not been performed [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use:** Dosing is weight-based (50 mg/kg). See *Section 3: Dosage and Administration* [US].
*   **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Renal Impairment:** Dosing is specifically adjusted post-dialysis for ESRD patients. Caution is advised regarding the accumulation of metabolites with oral formulations [US].
*   **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
*   **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring carrier molecule required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and ATP production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
*   **Pharmacokinetics:**
    *   **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin. Following a 20 mg/kg IV bolus, distribution half-life is approximately 0.6 hours [US].
    *   **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    *   **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. The mean apparent terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   **ESRD Efficacy:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients (20 mg/kg 3x/week post-dialysis), mean trough plasma concentrations increased from a baseline of ~21 µmol/L to ~190 µmol/L at 8 weeks.
*   **Dose-Response:** A dose-ranging study (10, 20, 40 mg/kg) demonstrated dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [US].
*   **Clinical Outcomes:** While efficacy in increasing carnitine concentrations is proven, the effects on signs, symptoms, or clinical outcomes in ESRD patients have not been determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
*   **Precautions:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat.
*   **Handling:** Single-dose vials; discard any unused portion as the formulation contains no preservatives [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
*   **Legal Classification:** 
    *   **United States:** Rx-only (Prescription only medication) [US].
*   **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources.
