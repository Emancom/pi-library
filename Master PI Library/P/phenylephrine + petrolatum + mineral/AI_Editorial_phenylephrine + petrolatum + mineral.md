# Editorial Product Information: phenylephrine + petrolatum + mineral

## 1. Product Identification
- Active ingredient (INN/generic name): **phenylephrine + petrolatum + mineral**
- Chemical class:
  - Phenylephrine: vasoconstrictor
  - Petrolatum, mineral oil: protectant/emollient
- ATC code: Not specified in available sources.
- Route(s) of administration: Topical (external anorectal / intrarectal) — product labeling states for external use and/or intrarectal use only.

## 2. Regulatory History
- Date of first marketing start (as stated in source): 11/01/2021 [US].
- Date of last revision/update of source PI: Revised: 7/2026 [US].
- Withdrawal, suspension, or restriction history: Not specified in available sources.
- If registration dates by other jurisdictions are required: Not specified in available sources.

## 3. Approved Indications
- Temporary relief of local itching and discomfort associated with hemorrhoids.
- Temporary shrinkage of hemorrhoidal tissue (vasoconstrictor effect) and relief of burning.
- Temporary coating to relieve anorectal discomfort and to protect irritated anorectal areas and make bowel movements less painful.
- Indication notes:
  - These indications are presented as stated in the available product information [US]. No other jurisdictional indication differences are specified in available sources.

## 4. Dosage and Administration
- General dosing (adult):
  - Apply externally to the affected anorectal area up to 4 times daily (especially at night, in the morning, or after each bowel movement).
- Dosing by indication:
  - No separate indication-specific dosing schedules provided beyond the general adult dosing above.
- Administration instructions:
  - When practical, cleanse the affected area by patting or blotting with an appropriate cleansing wipe.
  - Gently dry by patting or blotting with a tissue or soft cloth before applying the product.
  - When first opening the container, puncture the foil seal with the top end of the cap (as described in the product labeling).
  - Do not insert this product into the rectum by using fingers or any mechanical device or applicator.
- Titration schedules: Not specified in available sources.
- Missed dose instructions: Not specified in available sources.
- Paediatric dosing:
  - Children under 12 years of age: ask a doctor before use.

## 5. Contraindications
- Specific formal contraindications are not listed in the available source PI.

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
- **For external use only and/or intrarectal use only.**
- Do not exceed the recommended daily dosage unless directed by a doctor.
- Do not put this product into the rectum by using fingers or any mechanical device or applicator.
- Stop use and seek medical advice if:
  - Bleeding occurs.
  - The condition worsens or does not improve within 7 days.
- Seek medical advice before use if the patient has:
  - Heart disease.
  - High blood pressure.
  - Thyroid disease.
  - Diabetes.
  - Difficulty in urination due to enlargement of the prostate gland.
- Ask a doctor or pharmacist before use if the patient is currently taking prescription medication for:
  - High blood pressure.
  - Depression.
- Pregnancy and lactation:
  - If pregnant or breastfeeding, ask a health professional before use.
- Safety for children:
  - Children under 12 years of age: consult a doctor before use.
- Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.
- Monitoring requirements:
  - Monitor for worsening symptoms, bleeding, or lack of improvement within 7 days; discontinue and seek medical care if observed.

## 11. Pharmacology
- Mechanism of action:
  - Phenylephrine: described as a vasoconstrictor that temporarily shrinks hemorrhoidal tissue.
  - Petrolatum and mineral oil: function as protectants/emollients that provide coating and help protect irritated anorectal surfaces and reduce discomfort during bowel movements.
- Pharmacokinetics (absorption, distribution, metabolism, elimination): Not specified in available sources.
- Pharmacodynamics:
  - Phenylephrine’s vasoconstrictive action leads to temporary reduction of swelling of anorectal tissues; petrolatum/mineral oil provide topical protection and lubrication to reduce irritation and pain. No further pharmacodynamic parameters are provided.
