# Editorial Product Information: Proxymetacaine

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information from Australia (Therapeutic Goods Administration - TGA).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonized editorial overview of proxymetacaine for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesizes clinical data, dosing, and safety information into a single reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory documents for definitive regional guidance.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Proxymetacaine hydrochloride (an ester-type local anaesthetic).
- **Brand Names:** **ALCAINE** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** S01BA05.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Sterile ophthalmic solution (eye drops) containing 5 mg/mL (0.5%) proxymetacaine hydrochloride [Australian].
- **Route of Administration:** Topical ocular use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Alcon Laboratories (Australia) Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
Proxymetacaine is indicated for ophthalmic procedures requiring rapid, short-acting topical anaesthesia, including:
- Cataract surgery and suture removal from the cornea.
- Tonometry and gonioscopy.
- Removal of corneal foreign bodies.
- Conjunctival scraping for diagnostic purposes.
- Other brief corneal and conjunctival procedures.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Tonometry and short-duration procedures:** Instil 1–2 drops immediately prior to the evaluation [Australian].
- **Minor surgical procedures (e.g., foreign body or suture removal):** Instil 1–2 drops every 5 to 10 minutes for a total of 1 to 3 doses [Australian].
- **Prolonged anaesthesia (e.g., cataract extraction):** Instil 1–2 drops every 5 to 10 minutes for 3 to 5 doses [Australian].

### Administration Instructions
- **Ocular Use Only:** This product is not for injection.
- **Avoid Contamination:** Do not touch the dropper tip to the eyelids or any other surfaces.
- **Multi-product use:** If using multiple topical ophthalmic agents, administer them at least 5 minutes apart. Eye ointments should be applied last [Australian].
- **Patient Monitoring:** Because the blink reflex is temporarily eliminated, the eye should be protected (e.g., with a patch) until anaesthesia wears off. Patients must be cautioned against rubbing the eye while anaesthetised to prevent accidental injury [Australian].
- **Handling:** Discard the container 4 weeks after opening [Australian].

## 4. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to proxymetacaine, other ester-type anaesthetics, or any excipient (including benzalkonium chloride) [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Corneal Toxicity:** Prolonged use or abuse of topical ocular anaesthetics is associated with corneal epithelial toxicity. This may manifest as epithelial defects, corneal damage, infection, perforation, or opacification, which can lead to permanent vision loss [Australian].
- **Systemic Toxicity:** Although rare with topical application, systemic toxicity (CNS stimulation followed by depression) may occur.
- **Underlying Conditions:** Use with caution in patients with:
    - Known allergies or respiratory problems.
    - Epilepsy or cardiac disease.
    - Hyperthyroidism.
    - Myasthenia gravis (patients are particularly sensitive to anaesthetic effects).
- **Pseudocholinesterase Deficiency:** Patients with low plasma acetylcholinesterase or those treated with cholinesterase inhibitors are at an increased risk of systemic side effects with ester-type anaesthetics [Australian].
- **Allergic Contact Dermatitis:** Proxymetacaine may cause contact dermatitis; avoid skin contact.
- **Contact Lenses:** The preservative benzalkonium chloride may discolour soft contact lenses. Lenses should not be worn until the anaesthetic effect has completely worn off [Australian].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Frequencies for many adverse effects cannot be estimated from available post-marketing data.

| System Organ Class | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Immune System** | Hypersensitivity |
| **Nervous System** | Syncope, dizziness |
| **Eye Disorders** | **Common/Expected:** Transient stinging, burning, or redness (may occur several hours after instillation). <br> **Rare:** Severe hyperallergic corneal reactions (intense epithelial keratitis, sloughing of necrotic epithelium, iritis). <br> **Other:** Corneal erosion, opacity, or oedema; mydriasis; photophobia; blurred vision; eye pain/swelling; lacrimation increased. |
| **Skin** | Allergic contact dermatitis (including drying/fissuring of fingertips) |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Overuse or uncontrolled use may lead to permanent ocular lesions due to epithelial toxicity [Australian].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Anticholinesterases:** The metabolism of ester-type local anaesthetics may be inhibited by anticholinesterases, potentially increasing the risk of systemic toxicity [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Australian Category B2:** Animal reproduction studies have not been conducted. It is unknown if proxymetacaine causes foetal harm. Use is not recommended during pregnancy unless essential.
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if proxymetacaine is excreted in human milk. A risk to the suckling child cannot be excluded. Use only when considered essential by a physician [Australian].
- **Fertility:** No data available regarding the effects on human fertility [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy in children have not been established through controlled clinical trials, though literature supports its use in this population [Australian].
- **Elderly:** No specific information is available for patients over 65 years of age [Australian].
- **Renal Impairment:** Safety and efficacy have not been established [Australian].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Safety and efficacy have not been established [Australian].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Proxymetacaine is a potent ester-type local anaesthetic. It stabilizes the nerve cell membrane by inhibiting the transient increase in sodium ion permeability, thereby blocking the generation and conduction of action potentials [Australian].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** 
    - **Onset:** Approximately 30 seconds after a single drop.
    - **Duration:** 15 minutes or longer.
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Following topical administration, it is absorbed systemically and typically decomposes rapidly in the plasma. High doses may lead to undesirable systemic effects [Australian].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- No specific clinical trial data were provided in the source documentation [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 2°C to 8°C (Refrigerate). Do not freeze [Australian].
- **Conditions:** Protect from light.
- **Shelf Life:** 30 months [Australian].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Australia:** Prescription Only Medicine (Schedule 4) [Australian].
