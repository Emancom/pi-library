## 3. Approved Indications
- Temporarily protects an inflamed, irritated anorectal surface to help make bowel movements less painful from irritation and abrasion during bowel movement.
- Provides temporary relief of pain, soreness, or burning associated with anorectal conditions.
- Helps relieve local anorectal itching and discomfort associated with hemorrhoids.
- Indication scope: For external and/or intrarectal symptomatic relief of anorectal irritation and hemorrhoidal discomfort.

## 4. Dosage and Administration
- General dosing (adult and adolescent):
  - Adults and children 12 years of age and over: apply to the affected area up to 4 times daily, especially at night, in the morning, or after each bowel movement.
- Administration instructions:
  - When practical, cleanse the area with mild soap and warm water and rinse thoroughly.
  - Gently dry by patting or blotting with toilet tissue or a soft cloth before applying.
  - For intrarectal use: after wetting, apply around the anal area and then insert into the rectum.
- Paediatric dosing / special notes:
  - Children under 12 years of age: ask a doctor.

## 11. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of action
- The source PI describes component-associated actions as follows:
  - Activated charcoal (carbon): adsorbent and detoxifying properties.
  - Sodium sulfate (mirabilite): described as anti-inflammatory and soothing.
  - Peppermint oil: cooling and analgesic effect.
  - Borneol: analgesic and anti-inflammatory effect.
  - Glycerin: humectant and moisturizing effect.
- The combined product is intended to provide local protection of inflamed anorectal mucosa and symptomatic relief (analgesia, cooling, moisture restoration, and adsorbent/detoxifying effects) for anorectal irritation and hemorrhoidal discomfort.
