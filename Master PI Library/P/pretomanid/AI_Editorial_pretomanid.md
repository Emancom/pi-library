# Editorial Product Information: Pretomanid

## Editorial Note
- Contributing Jurisdictions: US (FDA). 
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of pretomanid for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises clinical, regulatory, and pharmacological data to facilitate evidence-based decision-making.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should refer to local regulatory guidance for legal compliance.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Pretomanid (a nitroimidazooxazine antimycobacterial).
- **Brand Names:** Pretomanid [US].
- **ATC Code:** J04AK08.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Oral tablets, 200 mg. Tablets are white to off-white, oval, and debossed with 'M' on one side and 'P200' on the reverse [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Viatris Specialty LLC (under license from TB Alliance) [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
Pretomanid is indicated for use in a **limited and specific population** of adults with pulmonary tuberculosis (TB). It must be used only as part of a combination regimen with **bedaquiline** and **linezolid**.

**Approved Indications [US]:**
*   Treatment of adults with pulmonary TB resistant to isoniazid, rifamycins, a fluoroquinolone, and a second-line injectable antibacterial drug.
*   Treatment of adults with pulmonary TB resistant to isoniazid and rifampin who are treatment-intolerant or non-responsive to standard therapy.

**Limitations of Use:**
Pretomanid is **not** indicated for:
*   Drug-sensitive (DS) TB.
*   Latent infection due to *M. tuberculosis*.
*   Extra-pulmonary TB.
*   MDR-TB (resistant to isoniazid and rifampin) that is responsive to standard therapy and where the patient is treatment-tolerant.
*   TB with known resistance to any component of the combination regimen (bedaquiline or linezolid).

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults)
*   **Pretomanid:** 200 mg orally once daily for 26 weeks [US].
*   **Combination Requirement:** Must be administered with bedaquiline and linezolid via Directly Observed Therapy (DOT).

### Administration Instructions
*   **Food:** Must be taken with food to ensure adequate absorption.
*   **Swallowing Difficulties:** Tablets may be crushed and suspended in 5 mL of water for immediate administration. Alternatively, tablets may be soaked in 5 mL of water for 4–5 minutes before crushing. The mixture must not be stored for later use [US].
*   **Missed Doses:** If the regimen is interrupted for safety reasons, missed doses may be made up at the end of treatment. However, missed doses of linezolid due to adverse reactions should **not** be made up.

### Companion Drug Dosing (Standard 26-week regimen) [US]
*   **Bedaquiline:** 400 mg once daily for 2 weeks, then 200 mg three times per week (with at least 48 hours between doses).
*   **Linezolid:** 600 mg once daily is the preferred starting dose. An alternative dose of 1,200 mg once daily may be used but is associated with higher toxicity.

## 4. Contraindications
*   **Regimen-Specific Contraindications:** Pretomanid is contraindicated in any patient for whom bedaquiline or linezolid is contraindicated [US]. 
*   Consult the individual PI for bedaquiline and linezolid for specific contraindication details (e.g., hypersensitivity, specific drug-drug interactions).

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### General Warnings
*   **Hepatotoxicity:** Hepatic adverse reactions occur with the combination regimen. Monitor ALT, AST, bilirubin, and alkaline phosphatase at baseline, at 2 weeks, and then monthly. Interrupt the entire regimen if ALT/AST >8x ULN, or >5x ULN persisting beyond 2 weeks, or if accompanied by total bilirubin >2x ULN [US].
*   **Myelosuppression:** Primarily associated with linezolid. Monitor CBC at baseline, 2 weeks, and monthly. Reduce or interrupt linezolid if significant myelosuppression develops.
*   **Peripheral and Optic Neuropathy:** Known risks of long-term linezolid use. Monitor visual function. If neuropathy occurs, reduce linezolid dose or interrupt therapy.
*   **QT Prolongation:** Primarily associated with bedaquiline. Obtain baseline ECG and repeat at weeks 2, 12, and 24. Discontinue the regimen if the QTcF interval exceeds 500 ms or if significant ventricular arrhythmia develops [US].
*   **Lactic Acidosis:** A known risk of linezolid. Evaluate patients who develop recurrent nausea or vomiting immediately.
*   **Reproductive Toxicity:** Based on animal data, pretomanid may cause testicular atrophy and impaired fertility in males. The effects on human male fertility have not been fully evaluated [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
### Pharmacokinetic Interactions
*   **CYP3A4 Inducers:** Strong or moderate inducers (e.g., rifampin, efavirenz) significantly reduce pretomanid plasma concentrations. **Avoid co-administration** [US].
*   **OAT3 Substrates:** Pretomanid inhibits OAT3 in vitro. Co-administration may increase concentrations of OAT3 substrates (e.g., methotrexate, ciprofloxacin). Monitor for substrate toxicity and consider dose reduction [US].
*   **Transporters (BCRP, OATP1B3, P-gp):** In vitro data suggests potential inhibition. Monitor for adverse effects of co-administered substrates of these transporters.

### Pharmacodynamic Interactions
*   **QT Prolonging Drugs:** Use with caution due to additive risks with bedaquiline.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy:** No human data available. Animal studies in rats showed increased post-implantation loss at doses 4 times the human exposure. No adverse embryo-fetal effects were seen in rabbits. US category: Not specified (following current labeling rules) [US].
*   **Breastfeeding:** Not recommended. Pretomanid is excreted in rat milk. The risk to the nursing infant from the drug or the underlying maternal TB must be considered [US].
*   **Fertility:** Reduced fertility and testicular toxicity were observed in male rats and mice. These effects were only partially reversible after a 10-week recovery period [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established [US].
*   **Elderly Use:** Clinical trials did not include sufficient numbers of subjects aged 65+ to determine if they respond differently than younger subjects.
*   **Renal Impairment:** The effect of renal impairment on pretomanid pharmacokinetics is unknown [US].
*   **Hepatic Impairment:** The effect of hepatic impairment on pretomanid pharmacokinetics is unknown [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
Pretomanid is a prodrug activated by a mycobacterial deazaflavin-dependent nitroreductase (Ddn). It acts by:
1.  **Inhibiting mycolic acid biosynthesis:** Killing actively replicating *M. tuberculosis* by blocking cell wall synthesis.
2.  **Respiratory poisoning:** Under anaerobic conditions, it causes the release of nitric oxide, acting as a respiratory poison to non-replicating bacteria [US].

### Pharmacokinetics
*   **Absorption:** $T_{max}$ is 4–5 hours. Taking with a high-fat, high-calorie meal increases $C_{max}$ by 76% and AUC by 88%.
*   **Distribution:** Plasma protein binding is approximately 86.4%.
*   **Metabolism:** Approximately 20% is metabolised by CYP3A4. Multiple reductive and oxidative pathways are involved.
*   **Elimination:** Mean terminal half-life is approximately 16–17 hours. Excretion: 53% in urine (mainly as metabolites) and 38% in faeces [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   **Trial 1 (Nix-TB):** A single-arm, open-label trial in South Africa (n=109). Patients with XDR-TB or treatment-intolerant/non-responsive MDR-TB received the BPaL regimen. Success (culture negative 6 months post-treatment) was achieved in 89% of patients [US].
*   **Trial 2 (ZeNix):** A randomized trial assessing different linezolid doses. Success rates were 93% for the 1,200 mg linezolid arm and 91% for the 600 mg linezolid arm (both for 26 weeks). The 600 mg dose demonstrated a more favorable safety profile regarding neuropathy and myelosuppression [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   Store below 30°C (86°F).
*   Dispense only in the original container and keep tightly closed [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
