# Editorial Product Information: Pacritinib

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA)
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of pacritinib product information, synthesised for use by healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This editorial synthesis is intended as a clinical reference and does not substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Pacritinib (as pacritinib citrate).
- **Chemical Class:** Kinase inhibitor.
- **Brand Name:** **VONJO** [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Oral capsules, 100 mg [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Sobi, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Myelofibrosis (MF):** Treatment of adults with intermediate or high-risk primary or secondary (post-polycythemia vera or post-essential thrombocythemia) myelofibrosis who have a platelet count below 50 × 10⁹/L [US-approved].
    - *Note:* In the US, this indication received accelerated approval based on spleen volume reduction; continued approval may depend on clinical benefit verification in confirmatory trials.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing:** 200 mg orally twice daily [US].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - May be taken with or without food [US].
    - Capsules must be swallowed whole; do not open, break, or chew [US].
    - Patients transitioning from other kinase inhibitors must taper or discontinue those agents according to their specific product information before starting pacritinib [US].
- **Missed Dose:** Take the next dose at the scheduled time. Do not take extra capsules to compensate for a missed dose [US].
- **Surgical Interventions:** Discontinue pacritinib 7 days prior to elective surgery or invasive procedures due to hemorrhage risk. Restart only after achieving adequate hemostasis [US].
- **Dose Modifications for Toxicity:**
    - **Standard Dose Levels:** 200 mg BID (initial) $\rightarrow$ 100 mg BID (first reduction) $\rightarrow$ 100 mg QD (second reduction). Discontinue if 100 mg QD is not tolerated [US].
    - **Diarrhea (Grade 3/4):** Hold until resolved to Grade 1 or baseline. Restart at the same dose. If it recurs, hold until resolved and restart at 50% of the last dose [US].
    - **Thrombocytopenia (clinically significant >7 days):** Hold dose; restart at 50% of the last dose once resolved. If it recurs, restart at 50% of the last dose [US].
    - **Hemorrhage:** Hold for moderate bleeding until resolved. For severe or life-threatening bleeding, discontinue pacritinib [US].
    - **QTc Prolongation (>500 msec or >60 msec increase):** Hold dose. If resolved to $\le$480 msec within one week, restart at the same dose; if resolution takes >1 week, restart at a reduced dose [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Concomitant Medications:** Use of strong CYP3A4 inhibitors or inducers is contraindicated as they significantly alter pacritinib exposure [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hemorrhage:** Serious (11%) and fatal (2%) bleeding events have occurred. Avoid use in patients with active bleeding. Monitor platelet counts periodically [US].
- **Diarrhea:** Occurs in approximately 48% of patients. Manage promptly with antidiarrheals (e.g., loperamide), hydration, or dose modification. Severe cases may lead to acute kidney injury [US].
- **Thrombocytopenia:** Pacritinib can worsen pre-existing thrombocytopenia. Monitor complete blood counts (CBC) regularly [US].
- **Prolonged QT Interval:** Avoid use if baseline QTc >480 msec. Correct hypokalemia prior to and during treatment. Monitor ECGs as clinically indicated [US].
- **Major Adverse Cardiac Events (MACE):** Increased risk of cardiovascular death, MI, and stroke (observed with other JAK-inhibitors). Risk is higher in smokers and those with cardiovascular risk factors [US].
- **Thrombosis:** Deep venous thrombosis, pulmonary embolism, and arterial thrombosis have been reported with the JAK-inhibitor class [US].
- **Secondary Malignancies:** Risk of lymphoma and other malignancies (excluding non-melanoma skin cancer) is increased, particularly in current or past smokers [US].
- **Infection:** Delay initiation until active serious infections resolve. Monitor for signs of bacterial, mycobacterial, fungal, or viral infections [US].
- **Discontinuation Syndrome:** Abrupt cessation may cause symptom flare (e.g., fever, respiratory distress, multi-organ failure). Tapering should be considered if discontinuing for reasons other than severe toxicity [US].

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Anemia (8%), thrombocytopenia (6%), pneumonia (6%), and cardiac failure (4%) [US].
- **Postmarketing Experience:** Acute or subacute kidney injury secondary to diarrhea [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **CYP3A4 Inhibitors/Inducers:** Strong inhibitors/inducers are contraindicated. Moderate CYP3A4 inhibitors (e.g., fluconazole) increase exposure; monitor for adverse reactions [US].
- **CYP Substrates:**
    - **CYP1A2:** Pacritinib is an inhibitor; increases concentrations of CYP1A2 substrates [US].
    - **CYP2C19 & CYP3A4:** Pacritinib is an inducer; may decrease efficacy of these substrates [US].
- **Transporters:** 
    - **P-gp & BCRP:** Pacritinib is an inhibitor. Increases digoxin (P-gp) and rosuvastatin (BCRP) concentrations. Rosuvastatin dose should not exceed 20 mg QD when co-administered [US].
- **Hormonal Contraceptives:** Pacritinib may reduce the effectiveness of oral/systemic hormonal contraceptives (except levonorgestrel IUDs). Use effective non-hormonal or IUD backup for 30 days after the last dose [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not assigned (new labeling format). No human data available. Animal studies at sub-therapeutic exposures showed maternal toxicity, post-implantation loss, and fetal malformations (cleft palate) [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Advise against breastfeeding during treatment and for 2 weeks after the final dose due to potential serious adverse reactions in the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** Based on animal data, pacritinib may impair male fertility [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness not established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical trials included patients up to 85 years; however, data are insufficient to determine if patients $\ge$65 respond differently than younger subjects [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Avoid use in patients with eGFR <30 mL/min. Exposure increases by ~30% in severe renal impairment or hemodialysis [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:**
    - **Mild/Moderate (Child-Pugh A/B):** No dose adjustment required [US].
    - **Severe (Child-Pugh C):** Initial dose 100 mg BID. May increase to 200 mg BID after 12 weeks if tolerated and required for efficacy [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Oral kinase inhibitor targeting JAK2, mutant JAK2V617F, FLT3, IRAK1, and ACVR1/ALK2. It does not inhibit JAK1 at clinically relevant concentrations [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Inhibits STAT5 phosphorylation in a dose-dependent manner [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** $C_{max}$ reached in 4–5 hours. No significant food effect [US].
    - **Distribution:** ~98.8% plasma protein bound [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Predominantly via CYP3A4 [US].
    - **Elimination:** Mean effective half-life is 27.7 hours. 87% excreted in feces, 6% in urine [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **PERSIST-2 Trial:** A randomized study in MF patients with platelets $\le$100 x 10⁹/L comparing pacritinib (200 mg BID or 400 mg QD) against Best Available Therapy (BAT).
- **Outcome:** In patients with baseline platelets <50 x 10⁹/L, 29% achieved $\ge$35% spleen volume reduction with pacritinib 200 mg BID compared to 3% in the BAT group [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at room temperature, below 30°C (86°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Keep bottle tightly closed and protect from light. Dispense in original package or light-resistant container [US].
