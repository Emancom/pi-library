# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- Contributing Jurisdictions: United States (FDA)
- Purpose: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of **levocarnitine** injection, synthesized for use by healthcare professionals.
- Note: This is an editorial synthesis based on available regulatory documentation and is not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. At the time of publication, only the United States (FDA) documentation was available for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); a carrier molecule involved in mitochondrial fatty acid transport.
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection, USP [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Sterile injection: 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency in Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Indicated for both acute and chronic treatment of patients with metabolic defects that lead to secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance hemodialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg. It may be administered as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) or via infusion. In cases of severe metabolic crisis, a loading dose is typically followed by equivalent doses every 3 or 4 hours (not less than every 6 hours) over the subsequent 24 hours. Daily maintenance doses are typically 50 mg/kg, though higher doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilized [US].
- **ESRD on Hemodialysis:** The recommended starting dose is 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight). Administration should be via slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].

### Monitoring and Titration
- For metabolic disorders, weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target range: 35 to 60 µmol/L) is recommended [US].
- In ESRD patients, dose adjustments may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine levels (target range: 40 to 50 µmol/L) [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration [US].
- Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known:** There are currently no listed contraindications for levocarnitine injection in the available source [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### General Warnings
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported. These often occur within minutes of intravenous administration, particularly in ESRD patients on dialysis. If a reaction occurs, the drug must be discontinued immediately and appropriate medical intervention initiated [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients receiving levocarnitine, regardless of pre-existing seizure history. In patients with a known history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].

### Specific Clinical Precautions
- **Oral Formulation in Renal Impairment:** The safety and efficacy of **oral** levocarnitine have not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in these patients may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites, trimethylamine (TMA) and trimethylamine-N-oxide (TMAO) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are often confounded by the underlying pathology (e.g., ESRD or metabolic crisis).

### Common to Very Common Adverse Effects (≥5%)
In clinical trials of ESRD patients on hemodialysis, the following events occurred at a frequency of ≥5%:
- **Body as a Whole:** Abdominal pain, accidental injury, asthenia, chest pain, fever, headache, infection, injection site reaction, and generalized pain [US].
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension, hypotension, tachycardia [US].
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea, nausea, vomiting [US].
- **Metabolic/Nutritional:** Hypercalcemia, hypervolemia, peripheral edema [US].
- **Nervous System:** Dizziness, depression, paresthesia [US].
- **Respiratory:** Cough increase, dyspnea, pharyngitis, rhinitis [US].

### Other Reported Effects
- **Gastrointestinal:** Gastritis, transient nausea/vomiting, and a "fishy" body odor have been reported [US].
- **Serious Effects:** Anaphylaxis and seizures [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin. It is recommended that INR levels be monitored at the initiation of therapy or following dose adjustments [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Not assigned a specific letter category in current PI]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at doses up to 3.8 times the human dose revealed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine is a normal component of human milk. Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. The benefits of maternal supplementation must be weighed against potential risks to the infant; consideration may be given to discontinuing nursing or the drug [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility was observed in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for pediatric patients with inborn errors of metabolism is consistent with adult weight-based dosing (50 mg/kg) [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Use with caution; see "Warnings and Precautions" regarding the accumulation of metabolites if using oral formulations in patients with severe renal impairment [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In ESRD patients, levocarnitine injection increases plasma carnitine concentrations and may alleviate metabolic abnormalities associated with carnitine deficiency [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours. Approximately 76% of an intravenous dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Bioavailability Studies:** Oral tablets and solution were found to be bioequivalent with an absolute bioavailability of approximately 15% after correction for endogenous levels [US].
- **ESRD Studies:** Clinical trials in ESRD patients receiving 20 mg/kg three times per week showed significant increases in pre-dialysis (trough) carnitine levels over 8 to 24 weeks. However, the impact of this supplementation on specific clinical outcomes in the ESRD population has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Precautions:** Avoid excessive heat and protect from freezing. The product contains no preservative; discard unused portions [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable for up to 24 hours in PVC bags at room temperature when diluted in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Rx-only [US].
