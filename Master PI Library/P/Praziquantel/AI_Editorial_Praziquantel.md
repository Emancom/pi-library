# Editorial Product Information: Lotilaner, Moxidectin, Praziquantel, and Pyrantel (Credelio Quattro)

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) and regulatory documentation from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**. 
- Clinical data within this document also references specific field studies conducted in the **Australian** jurisdiction as cited in the primary source.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals (veterinary clinicians and pharmacists). 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** 
    - **Lotilaner:** Isoxazoline class parasiticide.
    - **Moxidectin:** Semisynthetic macrocyclic lactone (endectocide).
    - **Praziquantel:** Isoquinolone anthelmintic.
    - **Pyrantel (as pamoate salt):** Tetrahydropyrimidine anthelmintic.
- **Brand Names:** Credelio Quattro, Credelio Quattro-CA1 [US].
- **Dosage Form:** Flavored chewable tablets.
- **Strengths (Lotilaner/Moxidectin/Praziquantel/Pyrantel):**
    - 56.25 mg / 0.056 mg / 14.25 mg / 14.25 mg
    - 112.5 mg / 0.113 mg / 28.5 mg / 28.5 mg
    - 225 mg / 0.225 mg / 57 mg / 57 mg
    - 450 mg / 0.45 mg / 114 mg / 114 mg
    - 900 mg / 0.9 mg / 228 mg / 228 mg
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Elanco US Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
Credelio Quattro is indicated for use in dogs and puppies 8 weeks of age and older, weighing 3.3 pounds or greater, for:
- **Heartworm Prevention:** Prevention of heartworm disease caused by *Dirofilaria immitis*.
- **Ectoparasite Treatment and Control:** 
    - Killing adult fleas and prevention of flea infestations (*Ctenocephalides felis*).
    - Treatment and control of tick infestations: *Amblyomma americanum* (lone star tick), *Dermacentor variabilis* (American dog tick), *Ixodes scapularis* (black-legged tick), *Rhipicephalus sanguineus* (brown dog tick), and *Haemaphysalis longicornis* (longhorned tick).
- **Lyme Disease Prevention:** Prevention of *Borrelia burgdorferi* infections via the killing of *Ixodes scapularis* vector ticks.
- **Intestinal Helminth Treatment and Control:**
    - **Roundworms:** Immature adult and adult *Toxocara canis* and adult *Toxascaris leonina*.
    - **Hookworms:** Fourth stage larvae, immature adult, and adult *Ancylostoma caninum* and adult *Uncinaria stenocephala*.
    - **Tapeworms:** *Dipylidium caninum*, *Taenia pisiformis*, and *Echinococcus granulosus*.
- **New World Screwworm [US-conditional approval]:** Treatment of infestations caused by *Cochliomyia hominivorax* larvae (myiasis).

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Minimum Dosage:** 20 mg/kg lotilaner, 0.02 mg/kg moxidectin, 5 mg/kg praziquantel, and 5 mg/kg pyrantel.
- **Frequency:** Administered orally once a month.
- **Administration:** **Must be administered with food** or within 30 minutes of feeding to ensure optimal bioavailability of lotilaner.
- **Vomiting:** If vomiting occurs within one hour of administration, redosing is required.

### Specific Instructions
- **Heartworm:** Administer year-round or starting within one month of mosquito exposure and continuing until one month after the last exposure.
- **Missed Dose:** If a dose is missed, administer immediately and resume the monthly schedule.

## 4. Contraindications
- There are no known contraindications for the use of this product [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### General Warnings
- **Human Safety:** Not for human use. Keep out of reach of children. Wash hands after handling.
- **Animal Safety:** Keep in a secure location to prevent accidental ingestion or overdose by dogs, cats, or other animals.

### Clinical Precautions
- **Neurologic Effects:** Lotilaner belongs to the isoxazoline class, which has been associated with adverse neurologic reactions including tremors, ataxia, and seizures. Use with caution in dogs with a history of seizures or neurologic disorders.
- **Heartworm Testing:** Dogs should be tested for existing heartworm infections prior to administration. This product is not effective against adult *D. immitis*. Infected dogs should be treated with an adulticide at the discretion of a veterinarian.
- **Screwworm Treatment:** Effective treatment of myiasis must include mechanical removal of larvae, lesion cleaning, and appropriate wound care/pain management.

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions observed in a 330-day field study (n=191) include:

| Clinical Sign | Frequency (Percentage) |
| :--- | :--- |
| Diarrhea (with or without blood) | 11% |
| Vomiting | 9.4% |
| Lethargy | 6.3% |
| Anorexia | 5.8% |
| Dermatitis | 5.2% |
| Weight Loss | 3.1% |
| Pruritus | 1.6% |
| Alopecia, Seizure, Ataxia, Nystagmus, Anisocoria | ≤ 1.0% |

**Serious Adverse Effects:**
- **Neurologic Events:** In a field study [Australian], a dog with a history of seizures experienced seizure activity six days post-lotilaner administration. In US studies, seizures (including cluster seizures) were reported in dogs with no prior history of neurologic disease.
- **Other:** Hypersalivation associated with vomiting was observed in high-dose safety studies (5X dose).

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Concurrent Medications:** In a well-controlled US field study, no adverse reactions were associated with the concurrent use of Credelio Quattro and vaccines, antimicrobials, anthelmintics, antiemetics, steroids, NSAIDs, anesthetics, or analgesics.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Reproductive Safety:** The safe use of this combination product in breeding, pregnant, or lactating dogs has not been evaluated.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatrics:** Approved for puppies 8 weeks of age and older weighing at least 3.3 lbs. 
- **Geriatrics:** One geriatric dog in studies experienced vomiting, ataxia, and nystagmus but recovered within 24 hours.
- **Avermectin-Sensitive (Collie) Safety:** Studies at up to 5X the maximum dose in healthy avermectin-sensitive Collies showed transient mild depression, salivation, and vomiting in a dose-dependent manner.
- **Heartworm-Positive Dogs:** Safe administration was observed in dogs with patent adult heartworm infections; however, GI upset (diarrhea/vomiting) was more frequent at 3X doses.

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
- **Lotilaner:** Inhibits GABA-gated chloride channels, leading to uncontrolled neuromuscular activity and death in insects and acarines.
- **Moxidectin:** Interferes with chloride channel-mediated neurotransmission in parasites, resulting in paralysis and death.
- **Praziquantel:** Causes muscular paralysis in tapeworms via calcium ion influx and tegument disruption.
- **Pyrantel:** Acts as a depolarising neuromuscular-blocking agent, causing paralysis and expulsion of nematodes.

### Pharmacokinetics
- **Bioavailability:** Lotilaner (93%), Praziquantel (41%), and Pyrantel (31%) in the fed state. Bioavailability for lotilaner is significantly reduced in the fasted state.
- **Absorption (Tmax):** Praziquantel (0.75h), Pyrantel (2.5h), Moxidectin (4.5h), Lotilaner (10h).
- **Elimination Half-life:** Praziquantel (3.64h), Pyrantel (4.87h), Moxidectin (634h), Lotilaner (806h).

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Heartworm:** 100% effective in laboratory studies and 0% infection rate in a 11-month US field study.
- **Helminths:** ≥ 97% effective against *Toxocara canis*, *Ancylostoma caninum*, and *Uncinaria stenocephala*. 100% effective against *Echinococcus granulosus* and other tapeworms.
- **Ectoparasites:** 100% effective against adult fleas within 24 hours for 36 days. ≥ 97.1% effective against various tick species for 30–32 days.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 15–25°C (59–77°F).
- **Excursions:** Permitted between 5–40°C (41–104°F).

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as a Prescription Animal Drug (Rx-only). 
- **Conditional Approval [US]:** Credelio Quattro-CA1 is conditionally approved by the FDA for the treatment of New World Screwworm pending full demonstration of effectiveness.
