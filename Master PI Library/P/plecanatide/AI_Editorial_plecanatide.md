# Editorial Product Information: Plecanatide

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) provided by the **US (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating regulatory data into a single clinical reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This editorial synthesis is not a substitute for the official Product Information approved in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should refer to their local regulatory authority’s approved PI for prescribing purposes.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active ingredient:** Plecanatide (a guanylate cyclase-C [GC-C] agonist).
- **Brand name(s):** **Trulance** [US].
- **ATC code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage form(s) and strength(s):** 3 mg oral tablets. Tablets are white to off-white, plain, round, and debossed with “SP” on one side and “3” on the other [US].
- **Route(s) of administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/sponsor:** Salix Pharmaceuticals Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Chronic Idiopathic Constipation (CIC):** Treatment of CIC in adult patients [US].
- **Irritable Bowel Syndrome with Constipation (IBS-C):** Treatment of IBS-C in adult patients [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- The recommended dosage for both CIC and IBS-C is **3 mg taken orally once daily** [US].

### Administration Instructions
- **General:** Tablets may be taken with or without food and should be swallowed whole [US].
- **Missed Dose:** If a dose is missed, skip the missed dose and resume at the next scheduled time. Do not double the dose [US].
- **Swallowing Difficulties:** For patients unable to swallow tablets whole, **Trulance** may be crushed and administered as follows:
    - **Applesauce:** Mix one crushed tablet with one teaspoon of room-temperature applesauce and consume immediately.
    - **Water:** Dissolve one tablet in approximately 30 mL of room-temperature water. Swirl gently for 10 seconds; the tablet will disintegrate. Swallow the mixture immediately.
- **Nasogastric (NG) or Gastric Feeding Tube:** Dissolve one tablet in 30 mL of water (swirl 15 seconds). Flush the tube with 30 mL of water, administer the mixture, and flush again with 10 mL of water [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Pediatric Patients:** Contraindicated in children less than 6 years of age due to the risk of fatal dehydration [US].
- **Mechanical Obstruction:** Patients with known or suspected mechanical gastrointestinal obstruction [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warning [US]
- **Risk of Serious Dehydration in Pediatric Patients:** **Trulance** is contraindicated in children < 6 years of age. In nonclinical studies, young juvenile mice experienced death from dehydration after a single dose. Use should be avoided in patients aged 6 to less than 18 years. The safety and efficacy in patients < 18 years have not been established.

### General Warnings and Precautions
- **Diarrhea:** This is the most common adverse reaction. Severe diarrhea has been reported (0.6% to 1%). If severe diarrhea occurs, treatment should be suspended, and the patient rehydrated [US].
- **Accidental Ingestion:** Patients should be advised to store the medication securely, as accidental ingestion in children (especially those < 6 years) may lead to severe dehydration [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common Adverse Effects
Diarrhea was the primary adverse event observed in clinical trials, typically occurring within the first 4 weeks of therapy [US].

| Adverse Reaction | CIC (Trulance 3mg) | CIC (Placebo) | IBS-C (Trulance 3mg) | IBS-C (Placebo) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Diarrhea** | 5% | 1% | 4.3% | 1% |

### Less Common Adverse Effects (<2%)
- **Gastrointestinal:** Abdominal distension, flatulence, abdominal tenderness, nausea.
- **Infections:** Sinusitis, upper respiratory tract infection, nasopharyngitis, urinary tract infection.
- **Neurological:** Dizziness.
- **Laboratory:** Increased liver biochemical tests (ALT > 5–15x upper limit of normal reported in rare cases) [US].

### Postmarketing Experience
- **Hypersensitivity:** Skin itching, hives, and rash.
- **Gastrointestinal:** Vomiting [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Profile:** Plecanatide and its active metabolite are negligibly absorbed systemically.
- **CYP Enzymes:** Does not inhibit CYP2C9 or CYP3A4; does not induce CYP3A4 in vitro [US].
- **Transporters:** Neither plecanatide nor its metabolite are substrates or inhibitors of P-gp or BCRP in vitro [US].
- **Management:** No clinically significant drug-drug interactions have been identified in available sources [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Plecanatide is minimally absorbed, and maternal use is not expected to result in significant fetal exposure. Animal studies (mice/rabbits) at doses much higher than the human equivalent showed no embryo-fetal developmental harm [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Following 3 mg daily dosing for 2 weeks, plecanatide and its active metabolite were not measurable in breast milk. Maternal use is not expected to result in clinically relevant exposure to breastfed infants [US].
- **Fertility:** No effects on fertility or reproductive function were observed in animal models at oral doses up to 600 mg/kg/day [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Contraindicated in patients < 6 years. Avoid use in patients 6 to < 18 years. Safety and efficacy not established in minors [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical trials included approximately 8-10% of patients ≥ 65 years. No overall differences in response were identified compared to younger adults, though data for patients ≥ 75 years (approximately 2%) are limited [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Not specified in available sources. (Note: Systemic absorption is negligible).
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Plecanatide is a structural analog of human uroguanylin. It binds and activates guanylate cyclase-C (GC-C) receptors on the luminal surface of the intestinal epithelium. This increases cGMP, stimulating the secretion of chloride and bicarbonate into the lumen (via CFTR channel activation). This results in increased intestinal fluid and accelerated transit [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Food may lead to looser stools up to 24 hours post-dose. Plecanatide has been shown to reduce visceral pain in animal models [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Negligible systemic bioavailability.
    - **Metabolism:** Metabolized in the GI tract by losing the terminal leucine moiety to form an active metabolite. Both are proteolytically degraded into smaller peptides and amino acids within the intestinal lumen.
    - **Excretion:** No human excretion studies have been conducted [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **CIC (Studies 1 & 2):** Two 12-week trials (n=1,775). The primary endpoint was the proportion of "durable responders" (≥ 3 CSBMs/week and increase of ≥ 1 CSBM from baseline for ≥ 9 of 12 weeks). Responder rates were significantly higher for **Trulance** 3 mg (21%) vs placebo (10–13%) [US].
- **IBS-C (Studies 3 & 4):** Two 12-week trials (n=1,453). Responders met criteria for both abdominal pain reduction (≥ 30%) and CSBM increase (≥ 1/week) for at least 6 of 12 weeks. **Trulance** 3 mg showed significantly higher responder rates (21–30%) vs placebo (14–18%) [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
- **Special Conditions:** Keep in a dry place and protect from moisture. Store in the original bottle. Do not remove the desiccant or polyester coil until the bottle is empty. Do not subdivide or repackage [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Rx-only. Poison schedule/classification not applicable in US terminology beyond prescription status [US].
