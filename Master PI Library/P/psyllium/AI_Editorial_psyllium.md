# Editorial Product Information: Psyllium Husk

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) and "Drug Facts" labeling from the following jurisdiction: 
    - **United States** (Food and Drug Administration)
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. 
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official PI or "Drug Facts" label required for prescribing or dispensing in a specific jurisdiction. At the time of compilation, only the United States source was available for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Psyllium husk (approx. 3.4 g per 12 g of powder) [US]
- **Chemical Class:** Bulk-forming laxative; Soluble fiber
- **Brand Name(s):** Metamucil [US]
- **ATC Code:** A06AC01
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Oral powder in individual 12 g packets (containing 3.4 g psyllium husk) [US]
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** The Procter & Gamble Manufacturing Company [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Laxative Use:** For the relief of occasional constipation (irregularity) [US].
- **Dietary Support/Supplementation:** 
    - Support of digestive health [US].
    - Support of heart health by lowering cholesterol [US].
    - Maintenance of healthy blood sugar levels [US].
    - Appetite control [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Children 12 years and older)
- **Constipation/Regularity:** One packet mixed with 8 oz of liquid at the first sign of irregularity, up to 3 times daily [US].
- **New Users:** Recommended to start with one dose per day, gradually increasing to three doses per day as the body adjusts to increased fiber intake [US].

### Paediatric Dosing
- **Children 6–11 years:** 1/2 packet mixed with 8 oz of liquid, up to 3 times daily [US].
- **Children under 6 years:** Consult a physician [US].

### Administration Instructions
- **Fluid Requirement:** Each dose must be mixed with at least 8 ounces (a full glass) of water or other fluid. 
- **Preparation:** Stir briskly and drink promptly. If the mixture thickens, add more liquid and stir. 
- **Warning:** Taking this product without enough liquid may cause choking (see Section 5) [US].

### Timing with Other Medications
- Administer at least 2 hours before or after other medications to prevent potential interference with drug absorption [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Difficulty Swallowing:** Do not use if the patient has difficulty swallowing [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to psyllium or any of the inactive ingredients (e.g., FD&C Yellow No. 6, sucrose) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Choking Warning:** Taking psyllium without adequate fluid may cause it to swell and block the throat or esophagus. Seek immediate medical attention if chest pain, vomiting, or difficulty swallowing/breathing occurs after ingestion [US].
- **Allergy Alert:** Psyllium may cause allergic reactions in individuals sensitive to inhaled or ingested psyllium [US].
- **Pre-existing GI Conditions:** Patients should consult a physician before use if they have:
    - Abdominal pain, nausea, or vomiting.
    - A sudden change in bowel habits persisting for more than 2 weeks.
- **Monitoring/Consultation:** Patients should stop use and consult a doctor if constipation lasts longer than 7 days or if rectal bleeding occurs [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Gastrointestinal:** Minor bloating or changes in bowel habits may occur as the body adjusts to increased fiber intake [US].
- **Serious Effects:**
    - **Hypersensitivity:** Allergic reactions (frequency not specified) [US].
    - **Esophageal/Intestinal Obstruction:** Risk of choking or blockage if taken with insufficient fluid [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **General Interaction Mechanism:** Bulk-forming laxatives may affect how other medications work by altering their absorption. 
- **Management:** Psyllium should be taken 2 or more hours before or after other drugs [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Consult a health professional before use [US]. US FDA Pregnancy Category is not specified in the provided OTC label.
- **Breastfeeding:** Consult a health professional before use [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Approved for use in children aged 6 years and older. Use in children under 6 requires medical consultation [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in available source; follow adult dosing [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Psyllium husk is a bulk-forming fiber. It absorbs liquid in the intestines, swelling to create a soft, bulky stool that stimulates bowel movement [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Generally produces a bowel movement within 12 to 72 hours [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Psyllium is not absorbed into the systemic circulation; it acts locally within the gastrointestinal tract.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Heart Health Claim:** The source notes that soluble fiber from psyllium husk, as part of a diet low in saturated fat and cholesterol, may reduce the risk of heart disease [US]. 
- Specific pivotal trial data was not detailed in the provided Drug Facts label.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store below 86° F (30° C) [US].
- **Protection:** Individual packets are sealed for protection [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as a Human OTC (Over-the-Counter) Drug under the OTC Monograph M007 [US].
