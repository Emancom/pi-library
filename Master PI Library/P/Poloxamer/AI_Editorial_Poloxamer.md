# Editorial Product Information: Poloxamer 188 (Vaginal Gel)

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information and Drug Facts labeling provided by the following jurisdiction: **US (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating available clinical and regulatory data.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) or prescribing information required for clinical practice in any specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Poloxamer 188 (2.7% w/w).
- **Chemical Class:** Non-ionic triblock copolymer (surfactant).
- **Brand Name(s):** Mayer / Kang Du Qing Cybercy [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Vaginal gel containing 2.7 g of Poloxamer 188 per 100 g (delivered as 3 g of gel per single-use tube) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Vaginal.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Shenzhen Senton Science & Technology Co. Ltd [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Vaginal Cleansing:** Indicated for the cleansing of the vagina and the elimination of germs (antimicrobial purpose) [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** One single-use tube (3 g) administered once daily [US].
- **Administration Instructions:** 
    - The product should be administered before bedtime [US].
    - **Procedure:** The patient should lie down and slowly insert the applicator into the vagina. If resistance or discomfort occurs, insertion should be stopped to prevent injury. The gel is released for one-time use only.
    - **Post-Administration:** The patient should remain lying down for 15 minutes following application [US].
- **Missed Dose:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Pregnancy:** Use is contraindicated during pregnancy [US].
- **Lactation:** Use is contraindicated while breastfeeding [US].
- **Menstruation:** Do not use during the menstrual period or within four days after the period ends [US].
- **Paediatric Population:** Contraindicated in children under 12 years of age [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **General Warnings:** 
    - For external (vaginal) use only. Not for ophthalmic use [US].
    - Not intended for use as a contraceptive; do not use to prevent pregnancy [US].
- **Hypersensitivity/Irritation:** Discontinue use immediately if burning, itching, or vaginal irritation occurs. If these symptoms develop, the area should be washed or douched with water [US].
- **Specific Clinical Precautions:** Use with caution in individuals with known skin allergies [US].
- **Ingestion:** In case of accidental ingestion, immediate medical attention or contact with a poison control center is required [US].
- **Packaging:** Do not use if the package seal is broken [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Frequency Not Defined:**
    - Localised irritation, including burning and itching [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** None specified in available sources beyond the potential for significant irritation requiring discontinuation.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Clinical Management:** No specific drug-drug interactions are listed; however, healthcare professionals should consider the potential for physical or chemical interactions if other vaginal products are used concurrently.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** The manufacturer explicitly states "Do not use during pregnancy" [US]. No specific regulatory pregnancy category (e.g., A, B, C) is assigned in the provided source.
- **Breastfeeding:** The manufacturer explicitly states "Do not use during breast feeding" [US].
- **Fertility:** Data not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy have not been established in children under 12 years of age; use is contraindicated in this population [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Poloxamer 188 is a surfactant with antimicrobial properties used here for mechanical and chemical cleansing of the vaginal mucosa [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** The product is intended for local action within the vaginal vault to "kill germs" and cleanse [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- Key pivotal trial data was not provided in the source documentation. The product is listed under the OTC Monograph system [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at controlled room temperature: 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Conditions:** Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight [US].
- **Handling:** Each tube is for single use only and should be discarded after application [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as a "Human OTC Drug" under the OTC Monograph Drug (M003) category. NDC: 68991-002-10 [US].
