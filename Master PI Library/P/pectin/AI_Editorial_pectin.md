## 3. Approved Indications
- For temporary relief of minor discomfort of the throat (oral demulcent).  
  - Indication language as provided in source documents is consistently limited to symptomatic relief of minor throat discomfort.

## 4. Dosage and Administration
- Active strength per unit (as presented in available sources): Pectin 11.5 mg per lozenge/drop unit.
- General dosing (adult):
  - Allow one lozenge/drop to dissolve slowly in the mouth. May be repeated as needed or as directed by a doctor.
- Dosing by product/age (as presented in source labels):
  - Adults and children 3 years of age and older: allow one lozenge/pop to dissolve slowly in the mouth; may be repeated as needed. [Note: several product labels in the provided sources specify this age threshold.]
  - Adults and children 10 years of age and older (certain drop formulations): allow one drop to dissolve slowly in the mouth; may be repeated as needed. Children under 10 years: ask a doctor for those products.
  - Children under 3 years of age: ask a doctor for lozenge/pop products that specify this lower-age limit.
- Administration instructions:
  - Allow the unit to dissolve slowly in the mouth; do not chew or swallow whole.
  - Use only as directed on the label or by a healthcare professional.

- Missed dose instructions: Not specified in available sources

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed warnings
- None specified in available sources.

### General warnings and precautions
- Sore throat warning (conservative unified recommendation): If sore throat is severe, persists for more than 2 days, or is accompanied or followed by fever, headache, rash, swelling, nausea, or vomiting, consult a doctor promptly. These features may indicate a more serious condition.  
  - Regional/product variation: some product labels in the available sources state "persists for more than 7 days" as the threshold for medical review—[US: some labels state >7 days].
- Stop use and ask a doctor if:
  - Sore throat does not improve in 7 days.
  - Irritation, pain, or redness persists or worsens.
- Oral/mouth guidance:
  - If sore mouth symptoms do not improve in 7 days, see your dentist or doctor promptly.
- Pediatric precautions:
  - For products specifying age restrictions, do not use in children below the labeled minimum without consulting a physician (see Section 4).
- Other safety information:
  - **Keep out of reach of children.**

### Monitoring requirements
- Specific monitoring requirements: Not specified in available sources

### Specific clinical precautions
- If systemic signs or symptoms develop (fever, rash, persistent headache, vomiting), obtain medical assessment because these may indicate more serious pathology rather than simple local throat irritation.
