# Editorial Product Information: Naphazoline Hydrochloride and Pheniramine Maleate

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA). 
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of the combination ophthalmic solution containing naphazoline hydrochloride and pheniramine maleate, intended for use by healthcare professionals. 
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis of available regulatory information and does not replace the official Product Information required for prescribing or dispensing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** Naphazoline hydrochloride (Alpha-1 adrenergic agonist) and Pheniramine maleate (H1-receptor antagonist).
- **Brand Name(s):** **Opcon-A** [US].
- **ATC Code:** S01GA51 (Naphazoline, combinations).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Ophthalmic solution containing naphazoline hydrochloride 0.02675% (0.2675 mg/mL) and pheniramine maleate 0.315% (3.15 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Ophthalmic.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Bausch & Lomb Incorporated [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Allergic Conjunctivitis Symptoms:** For the temporary relief of ocular itching and redness caused by exposure to pollen, ragweed, grass, animal hair, and dander [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Children $\ge$ 6 years)
- **Instillation:** 1 or 2 drops into the affected eye(s) [US].
- **Frequency:** Up to 4 times daily [US].

### Administration Instructions
- **Contamination Prevention:** Do not touch the tip of the container to any surface [US].
- **Contact Lenses:** Lenses should be removed prior to the use of this product [US].
- **Container Care:** Replace the cap after every use [US].
- **Solution Integrity:** Discard if the solution changes color or becomes cloudy [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known sensitivity to any ingredient in the formulation [US].
- **Solution Degradation:** Contraindicated if the solution is cloudy or discoloured [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **General Warnings:** For external ophthalmic use only [US].
- **Rebound Congestion:** Overuse of the product may result in increased ocular redness [US].
- **Ocular Effects:** Users may experience a brief tingling sensation upon instillation. Temporary mydriasis (enlarged pupils) may occur [US].
- **Conditions Requiring Medical Consultation:**
    - Heart disease [US].
    - Hypertension (High blood pressure) [US].
    - Narrow-angle glaucoma [US].
    - Urinary retention due to prostatic hypertrophy (enlarged prostate gland) [US].
- **Monitoring/Discontinuation:** Patients should stop use and consult a physician if they experience eye pain, changes in vision, or if redness/irritation worsens or persists beyond 72 hours [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Common:** Brief tingling or stinging sensation upon instillation [US].
- **Ocular:** Temporary mydriasis (pupil dilation), rebound ocular redness (associated with overuse) [US].
- **Systemic (Serious/Acute):** Accidental oral ingestion, particularly in infants and children, may lead to coma and a significant reduction in body temperature [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available source documents. 
- **Editorial Note:** Healthcare professionals should be aware of potential additive effects with other sympathomimetics or antihistamines, though systemic absorption is expected to be low when used as directed.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Breastfeeding:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Fertility:** Not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** 
    - Approved for children 6 years of age and older [US].
    - For children under 6 years of age, medical consultation is required prior to use [US].
    - **Warning:** Accidental oral ingestion in infants and children is a medical emergency and may cause coma and hypothermia [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources; refer to "Warnings and Precautions" regarding cardiovascular disease and urinary retention.
- **Renal Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** 
    - **Naphazoline hydrochloride:** An imidazoline derivative sympathomimetic amine with alpha-adrenergic activity. It acts as a vasoconstrictor to reduce ocular vascular congestion [US].
    - **Pheniramine maleate:** An antihistamine that acts as an H1-receptor antagonist to relieve allergic symptoms such as itching [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Provides temporary relief of redness (vasoconstriction) and itching (antihistaminic action) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Summary:** Source document notes the product is "clinically proven" for eye allergy relief, but specific pivotal trial data is not detailed in the provided labeling [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20-25°C (68-77°F) [US].
- **Light Protection:** Protect the solution from light [US].
- **Safety:** Product is supplied in child-resistant packaging [US].
