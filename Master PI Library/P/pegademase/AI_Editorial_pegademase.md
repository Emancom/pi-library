# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA)
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of levocarnitine injection for healthcare professionals, synthesising information from provided regulatory sources.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and should not be used as a substitute for the official Product Information approved in a specific jurisdiction for prescribing purposes.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP). It is a carrier molecule for long-chain fatty acid transport across the inner mitochondrial membrane.
- **Chemical Class:** (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - **Injection:** 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) single-dose vial [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    - **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion [US].
    - **Loading Dose:** Often required in severe metabolic crisis, followed by an equivalent dose over the next 24 hours.
    - **Frequency:** Administer every 3 to 4 hours; never less than every 6 hours.
    - **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses range from 50 mg/kg or as clinically required.
    - **Maximum Dose:** Up to 300 mg/kg has been administered [US].

- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
    - **Administration:** Slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
    - **Dose Adjustment:** Guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine levels (target: 40–50 µmol/L). Reductions (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be implemented as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Inspect visually for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration.
- Discard any unused portion of a single-dose vial.

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have occurred, often within minutes of administration. Primarily reported in ESRD patients on dialysis. Discontinue immediately if a severe reaction occurs [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with a known history, an increase in seizure frequency or severity may occur [US].
- **Oral Formulation Cautions:** The safety/efficacy of oral levocarnitine in renal failure has not been established. Chronic high-dose oral administration in ESRD or severe renal impairment may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) [US].
- **Monitoring:** For metabolic disorders, obtain a baseline carnitine concentration. Weekly and monthly monitoring should include blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma carnitine levels (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common and Very Common Adverse Effects (≥5%)
Data based on placebo-controlled trials in ESRD patients on chronic hemodialysis [US]:
- **Body as a whole:** Abdominal pain (9%), Asthenia (9%), Chest pain (12%), Flu syndrome (25%), Headache (22%), Infection (15%), Injection site reaction (33%), Pain (30%), Fever (7%), Accidental injury (10%).
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (20%), Hypotension (14%), Tachycardia (6%), Palpitations (5%).
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea (16%), Vomiting (15%), Nausea (8%), Anorexia (5%).
- **Metabolic/Nutritional:** Hypercalcemia (9%), Hyperkalemia (6%), Hypervolemia (5%), Peripheral edema (5%), Weight decrease (5%).
- **Nervous:** Dizziness (13%), Paresthesia (5%), Depression (5%).
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (23%), Cough increase (9%), Rhinitis (9%), Dyspnea (7%).
- **Skin:** Pruritus (5%).

### Serious Adverse Effects
- **Anaphylaxis and respiratory distress** (bronchospasm, laryngeal edema) [US].
- **Seizures** [US].

### Other Noted Effects
- **Gastrointestinal:** Gastritis and transient nausea/vomiting.
- **Body Odor:** Reported as an infrequent reaction [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have been observed. Monitor INR levels after initiating levocarnitine or following dose adjustments in patients on warfarin therapy [US].
- **Other Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Jurisdictional Category:** Not assigned a lettered category in provided source [US].
    - **Data:** Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Animal data (dairy cows) indicate increased milk concentrations following exogenous administration. Weigh the risks of excess carnitine intake in the child against maternal benefits; consider discontinuing nursing or the drug [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders and ESRD is based on mg/kg body weight, applicable to paediatric patients [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    - **ESRD:** Specifically indicated for carnitine deficiency in dialysis patients.
    - **Other:** Safety of the oral formulation is not evaluated in renal insufficiency (risk of metabolite accumulation) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria for β-oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces).
    - **Elimination:** Following IV administration, ~76% of the dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. Mean terminal elimination half-life is approximately 17.4 hours [US].
    - **Bioavailability:** Absolute bioavailability for oral tablets and solution is ~15–16% [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Pharmacokinetics:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients receiving 20 mg/kg after dialysis showed that trough levels (pre-dialysis) increased from a baseline of ~21 µmol/L to ~190 µmol/L at 8 weeks. After one week of therapy, all patients maintained trough concentrations above the normal range (40–50 µmol/L) [US].
- **Dose-Ranging:** Studies in ESRD patients (10, 20, 40 mg/kg) confirmed dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine levels over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C [US].
- **Conditions:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat.
- **Compatibility:** Compatible and stable for 24 hours at room temperature in PVC bags when mixed with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8.0 mg/mL) [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources for other jurisdictions.
