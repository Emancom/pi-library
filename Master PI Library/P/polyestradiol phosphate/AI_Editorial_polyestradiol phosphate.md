# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of levocarnitine injection for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises clinical and regulatory data into a single reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. At the time of publication, only the United States (FDA) source was provided for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); a carrier molecule involved in long-chain fatty acid transport.
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative [(R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium].
- **Brand Names:** Not specified in available sources (Generic: Levocarnitine Injection, USP).
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - **United States:** Sterile solution for injection, 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
- **General Dosing:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg [US].
- **Administration:** Administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion [US].
- **Severe Metabolic Crisis:** A loading dose is often given, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours. Dosing frequency should be every 3 to 4 hours (q3h or q4h), and never less than every 6 hours (q6h) [US].
- **Maximum Dose:** The highest administered dose reported is 300 mg/kg [US].

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
- **Administration:** Administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line after each dialysis session [US].
- **Dose Adjustment:** Adjustments should be guided by pre-dialysis (trough) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].

### General Instructions
- **Monitoring:** Obtain baseline plasma carnitine levels before therapy. Weekly and monthly monitoring is recommended, including blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine level: 35 to 60 µmol/L) [US].
- **Visual Inspection:** Parenteral products should be inspected for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Known Hypersensitivity:** Not specified as a formal contraindication in available sources, but serious reactions are warned against.
- **General:** None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warnings
- None [US].

### General Warnings
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. These may occur within minutes of administration. Discontinue immediately if a severe reaction occurs [US].

### Specific Clinical Precautions
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with a known history, an increase in seizure frequency or severity may occur [US].
- **Renal Insufficiency (Oral Formulation):** While this document concerns the injection, it is noted that chronic high doses of *oral* levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) [US].
- **Monitoring:** INR monitoring is required for patients also receiving warfarin [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common Adverse Effects (≥5% in ESRD clinical trials)
Adverse events reported in double-blind, placebo-controlled trials in ESRD patients (regardless of causality) include [US]:
- **Body as a Whole:** Abdominal pain (5–21%), headache (3–37%), infection (10–24%), injection site reaction (27–38%), pain (21–35%), fever (5–12%), asthenia (8–12%).
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (18–21%), hypotension (3–19%), tachycardia (5–9%), chest pain (6–15%).
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea (9–35%), nausea (5–12%), vomiting (9–21%).
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (15–27%), cough increase (10–18%), dyspnea (3–11%), rhinitis (6–11%).
- **Metabolic:** Hypercalcemia (6–15%), hypervolemia (3–12%).
- **Nervous:** Dizziness (10–18%), paresthesia (3–12%).

### Serious and Other Notable Effects
- **Neurologic:** Seizures (post-marketing) [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Anaphylaxis, bronchospasm, laryngeal edema [US].
- **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea and vomiting; body odor and gastritis (less frequent) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have been observed when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin. **Management:** Monitor INR levels after initiating levocarnitine or after dose adjustments in patients on warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not assigned a lettered category in provided text, but states it should be used "only if clearly needed."
    - **Animal Data:** Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (based on surface area) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk carnitine after administration. **Management:** Weigh the risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant against the benefits of supplementation to the mother; consider discontinuing nursing or the drug [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders applies to paediatric patients (see Section 3).
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Safety and efficacy of oral formulations are not established in renal insufficiency due to metabolite accumulation; however, the IV formulation is specifically indicated for carnitine deficiency in ESRD patients on dialysis [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production. It also facilitates the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours. The mean apparent terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients (20 mg/kg three times weekly), mean pre-dialysis trough levels increased from 21.3 µmol/L at baseline to 190 µmol/L after 8 weeks [US].
- **Dose-Ranging:** A study comparing 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses showed dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F). Protect from freezing and excessive heat [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable for up to 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) when mixed in PVC bags with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer's at concentrations of 0.5 mg/mL to 8.0 mg/mL [US].
- **Handling:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison/Schedule Classification:** Not specified for other jurisdictions in available sources.
