# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- Contributing Jurisdictions: United States (FDA)
- Purpose: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of levocarnitine injection for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises available regulatory information into a clinically relevant format.
- Note: This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. At the time of writing, only the United States (FDA) source documentation was provided for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection, USP (Baxter Healthcare Corporation) [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection: 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US-approved].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD undergoing dialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    - Recommended dose: 50 mg/kg [US].
    - Administration: Administer as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion [US].
    - Frequency: Often initiated with a loading dose, followed by equivalent doses every 3 or 4 hours (never less than every 6 hours) over the first 24 hours [US].
    - Maintenance: Subsequent daily doses are typically 50 mg/kg, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been used in therapy [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - Recommended starting dose: 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
    - Administration: Slow 2 to 3 minute bolus injection into the venous return line after each dialysis session [US].
    - Dose Adjustment: Based on trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Adjustments (e.g., down to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Inspect visually for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration [US].
- Discard any unused portion as the formulation contains no preservatives [US].

### Compatibility and Stability
- Compatible and stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s in concentrations ranging from 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions may occur within minutes of IV administration. Discontinue immediately if severe reactions occur [US].
- **Seizures:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In patients with known seizure disorders, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Monitoring:** 
    - Obtain baseline plasma carnitine levels [US].
    - Monitor weekly/monthly, including blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations [US].
    - Target plasma free carnitine concentration: 35 to 60 µmol/L [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **General:** Transient nausea and vomiting are the most common; less frequent effects include body odor and gastritis [US].
- **Common Effects (≥5%) in Hemodialysis Patients:** Due to the complexity of ESRD, causality is difficult to define, but the following were reported at rates ≥5% [US]:
    - **Body as a whole:** Abdominal pain, asthenia, chest pain, fever, flu syndrome, headache, infection, injection site reaction, pain.
    - **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension, hypotension, tachycardia.
    - **Digestive:** Diarrhea, vomiting, nausea.
    - **Metabolic:** Hypercalcemia, hypervolemia.
    - **Respiratory:** Cough increase, dyspnea, pharyngitis, rhinitis.
    - **Nervous:** Dizziness.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have occurred when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after any dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Category:** Not assigned a letter category in the provided source, but animal data is available [US].
    - **Data:** Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. No adequate, well-controlled studies in pregnant women exist. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. It is known that levocarnitine concentrations in dairy cow milk increase following exogenous administration. Weigh the benefits to the mother against potential risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant; consider discontinuing nursing or the drug [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility was found in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders is applicable to paediatric patients; specifically mentioned for newborns and premature infants regarding secondary deficiency definitions [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    - Specific IV dosing provided for ESRD patients on dialysis (see Section 3) [US].
    - **Precaution:** Safety and efficacy of *oral* levocarnitine have not been evaluated in renal insufficiency; chronic high-dose oral use in these patients may lead to accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production in all tissues except the brain [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Levocarnitine promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids in patients with metabolic defects by forming acylcarnitine complexes, which are rapidly excreted [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin. Mean distribution half-life (IV bolus 20 mg/kg): 0.585 hours [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours (uncorrected for endogenous carnitine). Mean terminal elimination half-life: 17.4 hours [US]. Total body clearance: 4.00 L/h [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients, 20 mg/kg IV three times weekly after dialysis increased mean pre-dialysis trough concentrations from ~20 µmol/L to 190 ± 55 µmol/L by week 8 [US].
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** In patients receiving 10, 20, or 40 mg/kg, a dose-dependent increase in trough concentrations was observed over 12 and 24 weeks. While carnitine levels increased, effects on clinical outcomes in the ESRD population were not determined in these trials [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Precautions:** Avoid excessive heat; protect from freezing [US].
- **Shelf Life:** Not specified in available sources, but a revision date of 2026-02-27 is noted on the label [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** 
    - **United States:** Rx only (Prescription only) [US].
