## Close Paraphrases
1.  **Missed Dose Instructions:** The editorial flips the logic of the source ("If more than 12 hours... skip" vs editorial's "If less than 12 hours... take immediately"), but the wording remains very close to the instructions in TGA Section 4.2.
2.  **Warning - Second Primary Malignancies:** The editorial states "Reported in 9% of patients, most commonly non-melanoma skin cancers. Advise sun protection and routine skin monitoring." This closely follows the FDA Warning 5.5 (Document 2, Page 6).
3.  **Drug Interactions (CYP3A4):** The description of the 71% reduction in AUC with rifampin is a direct lift of the data point, though the surrounding sentence structure is modified for the editorial summary.

## Originality Strengths
- **Section 7 (Drug Interactions):** The editorial does an excellent job of distinguishing between FDA mandates (50 mg reduction) and TGA observations (noting the 48% AUC increase without a mandate). This demonstrates active cross-referencing rather than passive copying.
- **Section 8 (Pregnancy and Breastfeeding):** The writer successfully synthesizes the vastly different requirements for contraception (5 weeks for TGA vs. 1 week for FDA), presenting them clearly in a comparative format.
- **Section 11 (Clinical Trials):** The editorial merges the "primary analysis" data used by the FDA (11.2 months PFS) with the "updated data" provided in the TGA document (14.0 months PFS). This is a high-level synthesis of disparate data points from different approval timelines.

## Differences from Source PIs
- **Jurisdictional Comparison:** The editorial explicitly notes where the TGA and FDA differ (e.g., MCL indication being FDA-specific; the "None" listing for contraindications in the US vs. hypersensitivity in Australia).
- **Structure:** The editorial uses a condensed "Editorial Product Information" format, which is significantly more concise than the 17-page TGA or 33-page FDA documents.
- **Data Integration:** Adverse effect frequencies (Section 6) are presented as "Integrated Data" ranges (e.g., 26–36%), which is a calculation performed by the editor by comparing the different trial cohorts (MCL vs. CLL/SLL) across both documents.
- **Omission of Regulatory Chaff:** The editorial strips away administrative sections (e.g., "Nature and contents of container" details like PVC/PCTFE) to focus on clinical utility.

## Recommendation
**PASS.**

The editorial is a high-quality, original synthesis. While small fragments of technical data (tablet markings and the dose modification footnote) are verbatim, these are necessary for clinical accuracy and patient safety. The document shows clear evidence of independent "harmonisation" by identifying and explaining the differences in regulatory requirements between Australia and the United States, particularly regarding trial data updates and contraception windows. No revision is required.
