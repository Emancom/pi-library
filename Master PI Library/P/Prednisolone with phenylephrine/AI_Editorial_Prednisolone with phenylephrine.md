# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **US (FDA)**. 
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical data and regulatory requirements. 
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific Product Information required for prescribing in any specific region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that result in secondary carnitine deficiency [US-approved].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with end-stage renal disease who are undergoing dialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg.
    - **Administration:** Administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion.
    - **Loading Dose:** In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose may be given, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours.
    - **Frequency:** Dosing should occur every 3 or 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours.
    - **Maximum Dose:** The highest reported dose administered is 300 mg/kg [US].

### Dosing by Indication (ESRD)
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight.
- **Administration:** Administered as a slow 2 to 3 minute bolus injection into the venous return line after each dialysis session.
- **Titration:** Dose adjustments (e.g., reduction to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy, guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration.
- **Compatibility:** Stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations between 0.5 mg/mL and 8.0 mg/mL [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known at the time of publication [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### General Warnings
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported. These can occur within minutes of intravenous administration, particularly in ESRD patients. If a reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and consider the risks/benefits before re-administration [US].

### Specific Clinical Precautions
- **Seizures:** Levocarnitine has been associated with reports of seizures in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Renal Impairment (Oral vs. IV):** While the IV formulation is indicated for ESRD, the safety and efficacy of **oral** levocarnitine have not been evaluated in renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in these patients may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [US].

### Monitoring Requirements
- **Plasma Carnitine Levels:** It is recommended to obtain levels prior to therapy. Weekly and monthly monitoring should follow.
- **Target Concentration:** Plasma free carnitine levels should ideally be maintained between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].
- **General Monitoring:** Vital signs and blood chemistries should be monitored regularly during treatment.

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Clinical Trials Experience (ESRD Patients)
Adverse events reported in $\geq$5% of patients in double-blind, placebo-controlled trials (regardless of causality) include:
- **General:** Abdominal pain (9%), chest pain (12%), fever (7%), headache (22%), injection site reaction (33%), pain (30%).
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (20%), hypotension (14%), tachycardia (6%).
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea (16%), nausea (8%), vomiting (15%).
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (23%), cough increase (9%), dyspnea (7%).
- **Neurological:** Dizziness (13%), paresthesia (5%).

### Post-Marketing Experience
- **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea and vomiting; gastritis.
- **Neurological:** Seizures [US].
- **Other:** Body odor [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have occurred when levocarnitine is used concomitantly with warfarin. 
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels at the start of levocarnitine therapy or after dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Category:** Not explicitly assigned a letter category in the primary source, but animal data exists.
    - **Animal Data:** Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm.
    - **Human Data:** No adequate or well-controlled studies in pregnant women. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows indicate increased carnitine concentrations in milk after exogenous administration. Weigh the benefits to the mother against potential risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** Mutagenicity tests (Salmonella, etc.) were negative. Long-term animal carcinogenicity studies have not been performed [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing recommendations for metabolic disorders apply to paediatric patients (see Section 3) [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Specifically indicated for carnitine deficiency in ESRD patients undergoing dialysis. Dose adjustments are required based on plasma trough levels [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** It promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids in patients with metabolic defects by forming acylcarnitine esters, which are rapidly excreted.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not protein-bound.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and $\gamma$-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces).
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. The mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Efficacy:** Efficacy in increasing carnitine concentrations in ESRD patients has been demonstrated in 9-week and 24-week studies. Trough levels in patients receiving 20 mg/kg three times weekly increased from a baseline of ~21 µmol/L to ~190 µmol/L at 8 weeks. 
- **Clinical Outcomes:** The effects of supplemental carnitine on the specific signs and symptoms of carnitine deficiency or on overall clinical outcomes in the ESRD population have not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C–30°C.
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat.
- **Shelf Life:** Discard any unused portion of a single-dose vial as it contains no preservatives [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **US:** Rx-only. Poison Schedule classification is not applicable; regulated as a Human Prescription Drug under the ANDA process (ANDA 217896) [US].
