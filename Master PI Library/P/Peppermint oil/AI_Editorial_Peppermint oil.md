# Editorial Product Information: Peppermint Oil, Carbon, Sodium Sulfate, Borneol, and Glycerin (Anabalm)

- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) and labeling data provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**. 
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating active ingredient data and regulatory requirements.
- **Note:** This document is an editorial synthesis intended for professional reference and is not a substitute for the official Product Information/labeling required for prescribing or dispensing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** Peppermint Oil, Activated Charcoal (Carbon), Sodium Sulfate Anhydrous (Mirabilite), Borneol, and Glycerin.
- **Brand Name(s):** **Anabalm** [United States].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Suppository/Topical form (7g per unit). 
    - Strengths (per 100g): Peppermint oil 0.2g (0.2%), Glycerin 8g (8%), Activated Charcoal 1g (1%), Sodium Sulfate Anhydrous 2g (2%), Borneol 3g (3%) [United States].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical (External and/or Intrarectal) [United States].
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Wu Xi Zhong Zhi Wei Na Technology Co., Ltd. [United States].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Anorectal Protection:** Temporary protection of inflamed or irritated anorectal surfaces to facilitate less painful bowel movements by reducing irritation and abrasion [United States].
- **Symptomatic Relief:** Temporary relief of pain, soreness, burning, local itching, and general discomfort associated with hemorrhoids [United States].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adults and Children 12 years and over):** Apply to the affected area up to 4 times daily [United States].
- **Administration Timing:** Ideally applied at night, in the morning, or after each bowel movement [United States].
- **Administration Instructions:** 
    - Cleanse the affected area with mild soap and warm water; rinse thoroughly. 
    - Gently dry the area by patting or blotting before application.
    - **Intrarectal use:** After wetting the suppository, apply around the anal area and insert into the rectum [United States].
- **Missed Dose Instructions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Known Hypersensitivity:** To any of the active ingredients or excipients (Polyethylene Glycol, Polysorbate 60) [United States].
- **Pre-existing Conditions (Medical Consultation Required):** Use is contraindicated without prior medical consultation in patients with:
    - Heart disease [United States].
    - High blood pressure [United States].
    - Thyroid disease [United States].
    - Diabetes [United States].
    - Difficulty in urination due to enlargement of the prostate gland [United States].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None specified in provided sources.
- **General Warnings:**
    - For external and/or intrarectal use only [United States].
    - Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, seek immediate medical assistance or contact a Poison Control Center [United States].
- **Specific Clinical Precautions (Stop Use and Ask a Doctor If):**
    - The introduction of the applicator into the rectum causes additional pain [United States].
    - Symptoms do not improve within seven (7) days [United States].
    - Rectal bleeding occurs [United States].
    - An allergic reaction develops [United States].
- **Monitoring Requirements:** Not specified in available sources.

## 6. Adverse Effects

- **Identified Adverse Reactions:**

    - Rectal bleeding [United States].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:**
    - **Activated Charcoal (Carbon):** Adsorbent and detoxifying properties [United States].
    - **Sodium Sulfate (Mirabilite):** Anti-inflammatory and soothing effects [United States].
    - **Peppermint Oil:** Provides cooling and analgesic sensations [United States].
    - **Borneol:** Analgesic and anti-inflammatory activity [United States].
    - **Glycerin:** Functions as a humectant and moisturizing agent [United States].
