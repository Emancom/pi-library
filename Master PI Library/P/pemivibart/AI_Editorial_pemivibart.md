# Editorial Product Information: Pemivibart

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) and Fact Sheets from the following jurisdictions: **United States** (Food and Drug Administration - FDA).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of **pemivibart** for healthcare professionals. It synthesises available regulatory data to facilitate clinical decision-making.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians must consult local regulatory documents for legal prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Pemivibart, a recombinant human monoclonal IgG1$\lambda$ (mAb) [US].
- **Chemical Class:** SARS-CoV-2 spike protein-directed attachment inhibitor [US].
- **Brand Name:** **PEMGARDA** [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - **Injection:** 500 mg/4 mL (125 mg/mL) as a sterile, preservative-free, clear to slightly opalescent, colorless to yellow solution in a single-dose vial [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion after dilution [US].
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Invivyd, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Pre-exposure Prophylaxis of COVID-19:** Authorized for use in adults and adolescents (12 years of age and older weighing at least 40 kg) who are not currently infected with SARS-CoV-2 and have no known recent exposure. Eligible patients must have moderate-to-severe immune compromise due to medical conditions or immunosuppressive treatments and are unlikely to mount an adequate immune response to COVID-19 vaccination [US-Emergency Use Authorization (EUA)].

**Limitations of Authorized Use [US]:**
- Not authorized for the treatment of COVID-19.
- Not authorized for post-exposure prophylaxis.
- Authorized only when the national frequency of variants with substantially reduced susceptibility to **pemivibart** is $\le$ 90%.
- Not a substitute for vaccination in individuals for whom COVID-19 vaccination is recommended.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adults and Adolescents $\ge$ 12 years and $\ge$ 40 kg):** 
    - **Initial Dose:** 4500 mg administered as a single IV infusion [US].
    - **Repeat Dose:** 4500 mg administered every 3 months if ongoing protection is required [US].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Must be infused over a minimum of 60 minutes [US].
    - Dilute in 50 mL of 0.9% Sodium Chloride; total volume for administration is approximately 50 mL (consisting of 36 mL **pemivibart** and 14 mL saline) [US].
    - Use an IV extension set with an inline 0.2-micron filter [US].
    - Following infusion, flush the line with 0.9% Sodium Chloride [US].
    - **Monitoring:** Clinically monitor the patient during the infusion and for at least 2 hours after completion for signs of hypersensitivity or anaphylaxis [US].
- **Missed Dose:** Repeat dosing should be timed from the date of the most recent dose [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Severe Hypersensitivity:** **Pemivibart** is contraindicated in patients with a history of severe hypersensitivity reactions, including anaphylaxis, to any component of the product [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning: Anaphylaxis [US]:** 
    - Anaphylaxis has been observed in 0.6% of clinical trial participants, occurring during both the first and second infusions. 
    - Reactions can be life-threatening. 
    - Administer only in settings with immediate access to emergency medications and the ability to activate the emergency medical system (EMS).
    - Permanently discontinue **pemivibart** if anaphylaxis or severe systemic reactions occur.
- **Hypersensitivity and Infusion-Related Reactions (IRRs):** IRRs may occur during or up to 24 hours post-infusion. Symptoms include fever, dyspnea, chills, fatigue, arrhythmia, chest pain, and hypotension. If a mild reaction occurs, consider slowing the infusion rate; for significant reactions, discontinue immediately [US].
- **Cross-Hypersensitivity with COVID-19 Vaccines:** Contains polysorbate 80, which is structurally similar to polyethylene glycol (PEG). Consultation with an allergist-immunologist is recommended for patients with a history of severe allergic reactions to COVID-19 vaccines [US].
- **Viral Resistance:** There is a risk of prophylaxis failure due to emergent SARS-CoV-2 variants with reduced susceptibility. Patients should be informed of this risk and advised to seek medical attention if COVID-19 symptoms develop [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effect data is derived from the CANOPY trial [US].

| Frequency | Adverse Event |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | None reported at this threshold (excluding pooled viral infections) |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Systemic infusion-related/hypersensitivity reactions (6% cumulative), Infusion site infiltration/extravasation (5% in Cohort A; 1% in Cohort B), Viral infection (7%), Upper respiratory tract infection (7-8%), Influenza-like illness (4-5%) |
| **Uncommon (0.1–1%)** | Anaphylaxis (0.6%) |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Anaphylaxis (life-threatening) and severe infusion-related reactions [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Drug-drug interaction studies have not been performed. **Pemivibart** is not renally excreted and is not metabolized by cytochrome P450 enzymes; therefore, interactions with medications that are CYP substrates, inducers, or inhibitors are unlikely [US].
- **Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** No specific studies reported.
- **Vaccine Interaction:** **Pemivibart** should be administered at least 2 weeks after COVID-19 vaccination [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not assigned under current labeling rules [US].
    - **Risk Summary:** Insufficient data exist to evaluate drug-associated risks of major birth defects or miscarriage. **Pemivibart** should only be used during pregnancy if the potential benefit justifies the potential risk to the mother and fetus [US]. Human IgG1 is known to cross the placental barrier [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** No data available regarding the presence of **pemivibart** in human milk, effects on the breastfed infant, or effects on milk production. The developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding should be considered alongside the clinical need for the drug [US].
- **Fertility:** Nonclinical fertility studies have not been conducted [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Authorized for adolescents $\ge$ 12 years weighing $\ge$ 40 kg. Safety and efficacy in children < 12 years or weighing < 40 kg have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** No clinically meaningful differences in pharmacokinetics (PK) were observed in participants $\ge$ 65 years (25% of trial population) compared to younger adults [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** As a monoclonal antibody > 69 kDa, **pemivibart** is not expected to undergo renal elimination. Renal impairment or dialysis is not expected to impact PK [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** The effect of hepatic impairment on the PK of **pemivibart** is unknown, though it is expected to be degraded via catabolic pathways like endogenous IgG [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** **Pemivibart** is a recombinant human monoclonal antibody that targets the SARS-CoV-2 spike protein receptor-binding domain (RBD). By binding to the RBD, it blocks the virus from attaching to the human ACE2 receptor on host cells [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics [US]:**
    - **Distribution:** Steady-state volume of distribution is 5.62 L.
    - **Metabolism:** Degraded into small peptides and amino acids via catabolic pathways.
    - **Elimination:** Terminal half-life is approximately 49 days. Clearance is 0.0862 L/day.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** A positive relationship exists between serum neutralizing antibody titers and pre-exposure prophylactic efficacy. Repeat dosing every 3 months is expected to maintain titers at steady state approximately 33% higher than those following the first dose [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **CANOPY Trial (VYD222-PREV-001):** An ongoing Phase 3 trial evaluating **pemivibart** in two cohorts [US]:
    - **Cohort A:** Single-arm, open-label trial in adults with moderate-to-severe immune compromise ($n=306$).
    - **Cohort B:** Randomized, placebo-controlled trial in adults without immune compromise ($n=317$).
- **Efficacy Outcome:** Efficacy was determined using an immunobridging approach, comparing neutralizing titers achieved with **pemivibart** against the JN.1 variant to historical data from prior SARS-CoV-2 mAbs that demonstrated clinical efficacy. Exploratory data in Cohort B showed a relative risk reduction in symptomatic COVID-19 of 84% through Month 6 [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Unopened Vials:** Must be refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) [US].
- **Protection:** Store in the original carton to protect from light. Do not freeze or shake [US].
- **Diluted Solution:** If not used immediately, the diluted solution may be stored at room temperature (up to 26°C/79°F) for up to 4 hours [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as Rx-only. Available only under Emergency Use Authorization (EUA) [US].
- **Other Jurisdictions:** Not specified in available sources.
