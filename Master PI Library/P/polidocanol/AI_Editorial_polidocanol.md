# Editorial Product Information: Polidocanol (Injectable Foam)

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial synthesis is derived from the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, intended to support clinical decision-making by consolidating regulatory data into a concise, professional format.
- **Note:** This synthesis is an editorial reference and does not replace the official PI required for prescribing within any specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Polidocanol (a non-ionic surfactant sclerosing agent).
- **Chemical Class:** Polyoxyl lauryl ether.
- **Brand Name:** **Varithena** [United States].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:**
  - Injectable foam: Each mL of generated foam contains 1.3 mg of polidocanol.
  - Supplied as a 10 mg/mL polidocanol solution in 7.75 mL or 18 mL canisters, which is activated to produce the foam [United States].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only (via cannula or direct injection).
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Boston Scientific Corporation (Distributed by); Biocompatibles UK Ltd (Manufactured by) [United States].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Venous Incompetence and Varicosities:** Treatment of incompetent great saphenous veins (GSV), accessory saphenous veins, and visible varicosities of the GSV system above and below the knee [United States].
- **Clinical Benefit:** Indicated to improve the symptoms of superficial venous incompetence and the physical appearance of visible varicosities [United States].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** 
  - Use up to 5 mL per single injection.
  - Total dose must not exceed 15 mL per treatment session [United States].
- **Administration Instructions:**
  - Must be administered under ultrasound guidance when treating the GSV.
  - Administer foam slowly (approximately 1 mL/second in the GSV; 0.5 mL/second in accessory veins or varicosities).
  - Foam must be used within 75 seconds of extraction from the canister to maintain its properties.
  - Physicians must be experienced in venous procedures and trained in the use of this specific product [United States].
- **Treatment Frequency:** Separate treatment sessions by a minimum of 5 days [United States].
- **Post-Procedure Care:** 
  - Apply compression bandaging and stockings.
  - Patients should walk for at least 10 minutes immediately following the procedure while being monitored.
  - Maintain compression for 2 weeks [United States].
- **Retained Coagulum:** May be removed via aspiration (microthrombectomy) to improve patient comfort and reduce the risk of skin staining [United States].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known allergy to polidocanol [United States].
- **Thromboembolic Disease:** Patients with acute thromboembolic disease [United States].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Anaphylaxis:** Severe and potentially fatal allergic reactions have been reported with liquid polidocanol. Patients must be observed for at least 10 minutes post-injection, and facilities for emergency resuscitation must be available [United States].
- **Tissue Ischemia and Necrosis:** Intra-arterial injection or extravasation can cause severe necrosis, ischemia, or gangrene. Use extreme caution in patients with underlying arterial disease (e.g., Buerger’s disease). If intra-arterial injection occurs, immediate consultation with a vascular surgeon is required [United States].
- **Venous Thrombosis:** Treatment can induce venous thrombosis. Monitor patients closely. Increased risk is associated with reduced mobility, history of DVT/PE, recent major surgery, or pregnancy [United States].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Common Adverse Effects (≥3%):**
  - Pain in extremity (16.8%)
  - Infusion site thrombosis/retained coagulum (16.1%)
  - Injection site hematoma or pain (15.4%)
  - Limb discomfort (12.1%)
  - Tenderness (10.7%)
  - Superficial thrombophlebitis (5.4%)
  - Deep vein thrombosis (4.7%) [United States].
- **Neurological Events:** Although not reported in the specific **Varithena** trials, cerebrovascular accidents and migraines have been reported with physician-compounded foam sclerosants [United States].
- **Skin Discoloration:** Reported in approximately 1.1% of patients [United States].
- **Laboratory Interference:** D-dimer levels are commonly elevated post-treatment and are not useful for diagnosing DVT following this procedure [United States].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** No specific drug interaction studies have been performed.
- **Known Interactions:** There are no known drug interactions with this product [United States].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
  - **Risk Summary:** Limited data from case reports have not identified drug-associated risks for major birth defects or miscarriage. However, there is minimal clinical benefit to treating lower extremity varicosities during pregnancy.
  - **Animal Data:** No adverse developmental effects were observed in rats and rabbits at doses 12 to 13.5 times the maximum human dose [United States].
- **Breastfeeding:** There are no data on the presence of polidocanol in human milk. To minimize infant exposure, a lactating woman may consider interrupting breastfeeding and discarding milk for 8 hours following administration [United States].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies in rats showed no adverse effects on fertility at doses approximately 13.5 times the maximum human dose [United States].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness in paediatric patients have not been established [United States].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical studies included 9.1% of subjects aged 65 years and older. No overall differences in safety or efficacy were observed between older and younger patients [United States].
- **Renal Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Polidocanol is a non-ionic surfactant that damages the endothelium of blood vessels. When administered as a foam, it displaces blood and allows the polidocanol to contact the vessel wall, causing disruption of the osmotic barrier and destruction of the venous endothelium. This leads to thrombus formation and subsequent vessel occlusion, which is eventually replaced by fibrous connective tissue [United States].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Polidocanol causes dose-dependent damage to the endothelium [United States].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** 
  - **Distribution:** Mean volume of distribution (Vd) ranges from 35 to 82 L.
  - **Elimination:** Mean systemic clearance ranges from 0.2 to 0.4 L/min. The terminal elimination half-life is approximately 102 to 153 minutes [United States].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pivotal Studies (VANISH-1 and VANISH-2):** 
  - Two randomised, blinded, multicenter trials evaluated polidocanol foam (0.5%, 1.0%, 2.0%) against placebo.
  - **Primary Outcome:** Significant improvement in patient-reported symptoms (VVSymQ score) at Week 8.
  - **Secondary Outcomes:** Significant improvement in the appearance of visible varicosities as rated by both patients and independent photography review panels [United States].
  - **Physiological Response:** Duplex ultrasound confirmed elimination of reflux or complete occlusion in 80.4% (VANISH-1) and 86.2% (VANISH-2) of patients treated with the 1% concentration [United States].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage Temperature:** Store at or below 30°C (86°F). 
- **Handling:** Do not refrigerate or freeze. Do not shake the canisters.
- **Shelf Life:** Once activated, the canister must be used within 30 days.
- **Special Handling:** Canisters contain gas under pressure (oxygen and carbon dioxide) and may explode if heated. Store away from heat and light [United States].
