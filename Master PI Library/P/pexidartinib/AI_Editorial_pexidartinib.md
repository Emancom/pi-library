# Editorial Product Information: Pexidartinib

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating regulatory data into a clinical framework.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory documents for legal prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Pexidartinib hydrochloride (kinase inhibitor).
- **Brand Name(s):** **Turalio** [US].
- **ATC Code:** L01XE53 (Not explicitly listed in source, provided for reference).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - **Capsules:** 125 mg [US].
    - Description: Size 1 capsule with a white opaque body and powder blue opaque cap, imprinted with "DSC521" in black ink [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Daiichi Sankyo, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Tenosynovial Giant Cell Tumor (TGCT):** Indicated for the treatment of adult patients with symptomatic TGCT associated with severe morbidity or functional limitations and not amenable to improvement with surgery [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** 250 mg taken orally twice daily [US].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Must be taken with a **low-fat meal** (approximately 11 to 14 grams of total fat) [US].
    - Capsules must be swallowed whole; do not open, break, or chew [US].
    - **Note on Fat Content:** Taking the drug with a high-fat meal (55 to 65 grams of fat) significantly increases pexidartinib concentrations and the risk of adverse reactions, including hepatotoxicity [US].
- **Titration and Dose Reductions for Toxicity:**
    - **First reduction:** 375 mg total daily dose (125 mg in the morning, 250 mg in the evening) [US].
    - **Second reduction:** 250 mg total daily dose (125 mg twice daily) [US].
    - **Discontinuation:** Permanently discontinue if the patient cannot tolerate 125 mg twice daily [US].
- **Missed Dose:** If a patient vomits or misses a dose, the next dose should be taken at the scheduled time; do not double the dose [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None listed in available sources [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning: Hepatotoxicity [US]:**
    - Pexidartinib can cause serious and potentially fatal liver injury, including vanishing bile duct syndrome (VBDS).
    - Liver tests must be monitored prior to initiation and at specified intervals.
    - Available only through a restricted program: **Turalio** Risk Evaluation and Mitigation Strategy (REMS) [US].
- **Monitoring Requirements (Liver):**
    - **Baseline:** AST, ALT, Total Bilirubin, Direct Bilirubin, ALP, and GGT.
    - **First 8 weeks:** Weekly.
    - **Next month:** Every 2 weeks.
    - **Thereafter:** Every 3 months [US].
- **Embryo-Fetal Toxicity:** May cause fetal harm. Verify pregnancy status in females of reproductive potential prior to initiation [US].
- **Risk of High-Fat Meals:** Increased drug exposure and risk of hepatotoxicity if taken with high-fat content [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Very Common (>10%):**
    - **Laboratory:** Increased lactate dehydrogenase (LDH), increased AST, increased ALT, increased alkaline phosphatase (ALP), increased cholesterol, decreased neutrophils, decreased lymphocytes, decreased hemoglobin, decreased phosphate.
    - **Clinical:** Hair color changes (67%), fatigue (64%), eye edema (30%), rash (28%), dysgeusia (26%), vomiting (20%), peripheral edema (20%), pruritus (18%), decreased appetite (16%), hypertension (15%), constipation (12%).
- **Common (1–10%):**
    - Neuropathy (10%).
- **Serious Adverse Effects:**
    - Hepatotoxicity (including VBDS and liver failure).
    - Serious liver-related outcomes occurred in 5.3% of patients in a REMS registry [US].
- **Postmarketing Experience:** Increased blood creatine phosphokinase [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:**
    - **Strong/Moderate CYP3A Inhibitors:** Increase pexidartinib levels. Avoid concomitant use; if unavoidable, reduce pexidartinib dose [US].
    - **Strong CYP3A Inducers:** Decrease pexidartinib levels (e.g., Rifampin, St John's wort). Avoid concomitant use [US].
    - **UGT Inhibitors:** Increase pexidartinib levels. Reduce dose if use is unavoidable [US].
    - **Acid-Reducing Agents:** PPIs decrease pexidartinib levels; avoid concomitant use. Use H2-receptor antagonists (stagger: pexidartinib 2 hours before or 10 hours after H2RA) or antacids (stagger: 2 hours before or after) [US].
- **Pharmacodynamic Interactions:**
    - **Hepatotoxic Products:** Avoid coadministration with other drugs known to cause hepatotoxicity [US].
- **Effect of Pexidartinib on Other Drugs:**
    - **CYP3A Substrates:** Pexidartinib is a moderate CYP3A inducer. Avoid use with CYP3A substrates where minimal concentration changes lead to serious therapeutic failure [US].
    - **Hormonal Contraceptives:** Pexidartinib may render hormonal contraceptives ineffective [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Category:** Not specified by letter [US-approved labeling follows PLLR format]. 
    - **Risk:** Based on animal data, pexidartinib may cause fetal harm (malformations and post-implantation loss) [US].
- **Contraception:**
    - **Females:** Use effective non-hormonal contraception during treatment and for 1 month after the final dose [US].
    - **Males:** Use effective contraception during treatment and for 1 week after the final dose [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Avoid breastfeeding during treatment and for at least 1 week after the final dose [US].
- **Fertility:** Based on animal studies, pexidartinib may impair both male and female fertility [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical studies did not include sufficient numbers of subjects aged 65+ to determine if they respond differently than younger subjects [US].
- **Renal Impairment:**
    - **Mild to Severe (CLcr 15–89 mL/min):** Reduce dose to 125 mg in the morning and 250 mg in the evening [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:**
    - **Mild:** No dose adjustment required [US].
    - **Moderate (Total bilirubin >1.5 to 3x ULN):** Reduce dose to 125 mg twice daily [US].
    - **Severe (Total bilirubin >3x ULN):** Not studied; use not recommended [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** A small molecule tyrosine kinase inhibitor targeting:
    - Colony stimulating factor 1 receptor (CSF1R).
    - KIT proto-oncogene receptor tyrosine kinase (KIT).
    - FMS-like tyrosine kinase 3 (FLT3) with internal tandem duplication (ITD) mutation [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** $T_{max}$ of 2.5 hours. Low-fat meal increases $C_{max}$ and $AUC$ by ~60% compared to fasting [US].
    - **Distribution:** >99% plasma protein bound [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily via CYP3A4 oxidation and UGT1A4 glucuronidation [US].
    - **Elimination:** Half-life ($t_{1/2}$) of ~26.6 hours. Excreted 65% in feces and 27% in urine [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ENLIVEN Trial (NCT02371369):** A multicenter, double-blind, randomized (1:1), placebo-controlled trial in patients with symptomatic TGCT not amenable to surgery [US].
    - **Primary Outcome:** Overall Response Rate (ORR) at Week 25.
    - **Results:** ORR was 38% in the pexidartinib arm (n=61) vs. 0% in the placebo arm (n=59). 
    - **Range of Motion:** Significant improvement in the range of motion of the affected joint compared to placebo [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Keep containers closed; do not remove the desiccant from the bottle [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** 
    - **United States:** Prescription only (Rx-only); restricted distribution via REMS program [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources (US system uses Rx/OTC/Controlled Substance classifications).
