# Editorial Product Information: FOLIVEX (Multivitamin Combination)

## Editorial Note
*   **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA)
*   **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of **Folivex** capsules, synthesised for healthcare professionals. 
*   **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and serves as a clinical reference. It is not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing or dispensing in a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredients:** A combination of vitamins including Thiamine, Riboflavin, Niacinamide, Pantothenic Acid, Pyridoxine HCl, Methylcobalamin, Ascorbic Acid, Cholecalciferol, Vitamin E, and Folic Acid.
*   **Chemical Class:** Multivitamin/Mineral combination.
*   **Brand Name(s):** **Folivex** (Premium Multivitamin) [US].
*   **ATC Code:** A11BA (Multivitamins, plain - *Approximate*).
*   **Dosage Form and Strength [US]:**
    *   **Capsule (Oral):**
        *   Vitamin B1 (Thiamine Mononitrate): 1.5 mg
        *   Vitamin B2 (Riboflavin): 1.7 mg
        *   Vitamin B3 (Niacinamide): 15 mg
        *   Vitamin B5 (Pantothenic Acid): 10 mg
        *   Vitamin B6 (Pyridoxine HCl): 2.5 mg
        *   Vitamin B12 (Methylcobalamin): 6 mcg
        *   Vitamin C (Ascorbic Acid): 75 mg
        *   Vitamin D3 (Cholecalciferol): 20 mcg (800 IU)
        *   Vitamin E (dl-alpha Tocopherol Acetate): 10 mg
        *   Folate (as Folic Acid): 1,000 mcg (1,667 mcg DFE)
*   **Route of Administration:** Oral.
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Blue Heron Pharmaceuticals, LLC [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Nutritional Supplementation:** To provide significant amounts of essential vitamins to supplement the diet and assist in preventing the development of nutritional deficiencies [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
*   **General Adult Dosing:** The usual dose is one (1) capsule taken orally once or twice daily [US].
*   **Administration Instructions:** Capsules should be administered under the supervision of a licensed medical practitioner [US].
*   **Titration/Missed Dose:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
*   **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to any of the active ingredients or excipients (e.g., Microcrystalline Cellulose, Silicon Dioxide, Magnesium Stearate) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
*   **Boxed Warnings:** None [US].
*   **Professional Supervision:** This product is intended for use under the direction and supervision of a licensed medical practitioner [US].
*   **General Precautions:** Use with caution in patients with underlying medical conditions or those currently taking other medications [US].
