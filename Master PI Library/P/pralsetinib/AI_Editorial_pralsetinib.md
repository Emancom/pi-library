# Editorial Product Information: Pralsetinib

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the Product Information (PI) provided from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of pralsetinib for use by healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official PI for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority’s approved labeling for definitive prescribing information.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Pralsetinib (a receptor tyrosine kinase inhibitor).
- **Brand Name:** **GAVRETO** [US].
- **ATC Code:** L01EX19 (Not specified in provided source, but standard for this class).
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Oral capsules, 100 mg [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Rigel Pharmaceuticals, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC):** Treatment of adult patients with metastatic rearranged during transfection (*RET*) fusion-positive NSCLC, as detected by a regulatory-approved test [US].
- **Thyroid Cancer:** Treatment of adult and pediatric patients aged 12 years and older with advanced or metastatic *RET* fusion-positive thyroid cancer who require systemic therapy and are radioactive iodine-refractory (if radioactive iodine is appropriate) [US]. 
    - *Note: The thyroid cancer indication is granted under accelerated approval based on overall response rate and duration of response. Continued approval may be contingent upon verification of clinical benefit in confirmatory trials [US].*

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing:** 400 mg taken orally once daily [US].
- **Administration Instructions:** 
    - Administer on an empty stomach. No food should be consumed for at least 2 hours before and at least 1 hour after the dose [US].
    - Capsules should be swallowed whole.
- **Dose Modifications for Toxicity:**
    - **First reduction:** 300 mg once daily.
    - **Second reduction:** 200 mg once daily.
    - **Third reduction:** 100 mg once daily.
    - **Discontinuation:** Permanently discontinue if the patient is unable to tolerate 100 mg once daily [US].
- **Missed/Vomited Doses:** 
    - If a dose is missed, it can be taken as soon as possible on the same day. Resume regular schedule the following day. 
    - If vomiting occurs after administration, do not take an additional dose; continue with the next scheduled dose [US].
- **Specific Adjustments (Drug Interactions):**
    - **Strong/Moderate CYP3A or P-gp Inhibitors:** Reduce pralsetinib dose [US].
    - **Strong/Moderate CYP3A Inducers:** Increase pralsetinib dose starting on Day 7 of coadministration [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None identified in available source [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning: Serious Infections [US]:** Pralsetinib may increase the risk of serious, including fatal and opportunistic infections (e.g., pneumonia, fungal infections, *Pneumocystis jirovecii*). Monitor for signs and symptoms; withhold or discontinue based on severity.
- **Interstitial Lung Disease (ILD)/Pneumonitis:** Severe and fatal cases have occurred. Withhold for Grade 1 or 2; permanently discontinue for Grade 3, 4, or recurrent ILD/pneumonitis [US].
- **Hypertension:** Do not initiate in patients with uncontrolled hypertension. Monitor blood pressure after 1 week, then monthly. Adjust antihypertensive therapy or pralsetinib dose as needed [US].
- **Hepatotoxicity:** Increased AST/ALT observed. Monitor liver function tests (LFTs) prior to initiation, every 2 weeks for the first 3 months, and monthly thereafter [US].
- **Hemorrhagic Events:** Severe and fatal events reported. Permanently discontinue for severe or life-threatening hemorrhage [US].
- **Tumor Lysis Syndrome (TLS):** Reported particularly in patients with medullary thyroid cancer. Monitor at-risk patients and treat appropriately [US].
- **Risk of Impaired Wound Healing:** Withhold for at least 5 days prior to elective surgery and for 2 weeks following major surgery until adequate healing occurs [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Very Common ($\ge$ 25%):** Musculoskeletal pain, constipation, hypertension, diarrhea, fatigue, edema, pyrexia, and cough [US].
- **Clinically Significant Serious Effects:** Pneumonia (13%), anemia, sepsis, and pulmonary embolism [US].
- **Laboratory Abnormalities (Grade 3-4, $\ge$ 2%):** 
    - **Hematology:** Decreased lymphocytes, neutrophils, hemoglobin, leukocytes, and platelets.
    - **Chemistry:** Decreased phosphate, sodium, calcium, and potassium; increased AST, ALT, alkaline phosphatase, potassium, and bilirubin [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **CYP3A and P-gp Inhibitors:** Coadministration increases pralsetinib exposure. Avoid coadministration. If unavoidable, reduce pralsetinib dose (e.g., from 400 mg to 200 mg for combined P-gp and strong CYP3A inhibitors) [US].
- **CYP3A Inducers:** Coadministration decreases pralsetinib exposure and efficacy. Avoid coadministration. If unavoidable, increase pralsetinib dose starting on Day 7 (e.g., from 400 mg to 800 mg for strong inducers) [US].
- **Acid-Reducing Agents:** No clinically significant effect on pralsetinib pharmacokinetics [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not specified by letter, but label warns of fetal harm]. Based on animal studies, pralsetinib can cause malformations and embryolethality. Verify pregnancy status prior to initiation. 
- **Contraception:** 
    - **Females:** Use effective non-hormonal contraception during treatment and for 2 weeks after the final dose. Pralsetinib may render hormonal contraceptives ineffective [US].
    - **Males:** Use effective contraception with female partners of reproductive potential during treatment and for 1 week after the final dose [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Advise against breastfeeding during treatment and for 1 week after the final dose [US].
- **Fertility:** Based on animal data, pralsetinib may impair male and female fertility [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy established in patients $\ge$ 12 years for *RET* fusion-positive thyroid cancer. Safety/efficacy not established in NSCLC or in children < 12 years [US]. Monitor growth plates in adolescent patients.
- **Geriatric Use:** No overall differences in safety or efficacy observed between patients $\ge$ 65 years and younger patients [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment required for mild to moderate impairment (CLcr 30-89 mL/min). Not studied in severe renal impairment (CLcr < 15 mL/min) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment required for mild, moderate, or severe hepatic impairment [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** A kinase inhibitor targeting wild-type *RET* and oncogenic *RET* fusions and mutations. It inhibits *RET* at concentrations significantly lower than those required for VEGFR2, FGFR2, and JAK2 [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Median $T_{max}$ is 2-4 hours. A high-fat meal increases $C_{max}$ by 2-fold and AUC by 2.2-fold [US].
    - **Distribution:** Plasma protein binding is 97%.
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily metabolised by CYP3A4, and to a lesser extent by CYP2D6 and CYP1A2 [US].
    - **Elimination:** Mean plasma elimination half-life is 16-20 hours. Excretion is primarily via faeces (73%) and minimally via urine (6%) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ARROW Trial:** A multicentre, non-randomised, open-label trial. 
    - **NSCLC Cohort:** Demonstrated an Overall Response Rate (ORR) of 63% in previously treated patients and 78% in treatment-naïve patients [US].
    - **Thyroid Cancer Cohort:** Demonstrated an ORR of 89% in *RET* fusion-positive thyroid cancer [US].
- **AcceleRET-Lung:** Comparison of pralsetinib vs. chemotherapy/immunotherapy in NSCLC, showing higher infection rates in the pralsetinib arm (72% vs. 52%) [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F and 86°F). 
- **Handling:** Protect from moisture [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Prescription only (Rx-only) [US].
