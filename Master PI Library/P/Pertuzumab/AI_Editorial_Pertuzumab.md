# Editorial Product Information: Pertuzumab

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration)
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of **pertuzumab** Product Information (PI) for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises clinical data to support safe and effective prescribing and administration.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official, legally binding Product Information required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult the local PI for definitive regulatory requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Pertuzumab
- **Chemical Class:** Recombinant humanised monoclonal antibody; HER2/neu receptor antagonist.
- **Brand Name:** **Perjeta** [Australian]
- **ATC Code:** L01XC13
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Concentrate for intravenous (IV) infusion; 420 mg per 14 mL vial (30 mg/mL).
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous infusion only. Do not administer as an IV push or bolus.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Roche Products Pty Limited [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
**Pertuzumab** is indicated for use in combination with trastuzumab and chemotherapy in the following settings:

- **Early Breast Cancer (EBC):**
    - **Neoadjuvant treatment:** Patients with HER2-positive inflammatory, locally advanced, or early-stage breast cancer (tumour >2 cm or node-positive) as part of a complete treatment regimen.
    - **Adjuvant treatment:** Patients with HER2-positive early breast cancer at high risk of recurrence [Australian].
- **Metastatic Breast Cancer (MBC):**
    - First-line treatment of patients with HER2-positive metastatic disease who have not received prior anti-HER2 therapy or chemotherapy for their metastatic condition (administered with trastuzumab and docetaxel) [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Requirements
- **HER2 Testing:** Patient selection must be based on confirmed HER2-positive tumour status (IHC score of 3+ and/or ISH ratio ≥ 2.0) performed in a specialised laboratory.
- **Combination Therapy:** **Pertuzumab** must be used in combination with trastuzumab.

### Adult Dosing (EBC and MBC)
- **Loading Dose:** 840 mg administered as a 60-minute IV infusion.
- **Maintenance Dose:** 420 mg administered every 3 weeks, as a 30–60 minute IV infusion.
- **Observation Period:** 30–60 minutes post-infusion is recommended before starting subsequent infusions of trastuzumab or chemotherapy.

### Sequence of Administration
- If a taxane is part of the regimen, **pertuzumab** and trastuzumab should precede the taxane.
- In anthracycline-based regimens, **pertuzumab** and trastuzumab should be administered after the completion of the entire anthracycline course.

### Dosing Modifications
- **Dose Reductions:** Not recommended for **pertuzumab**.
- **Infusion Reactions:** Slow the rate or interrupt the infusion if a reaction occurs. Discontinue permanently for serious hypersensitivity/anaphylaxis.
- **Missed Doses:**
    - **If < 6 weeks since last dose:** Administer the 420 mg maintenance dose as soon as possible.
    - **If ≥ 6 weeks since last dose:** Re-administer the 840 mg loading dose, followed by 420 mg maintenance every 3 weeks.

### Administration Instructions
- **Dilution:** Dilute in 250 mL of 0.9% or 0.45% sodium chloride. **Do not use Glucose (5%) solution** as the drug is unstable in this medium.
- **Compatibility:** No incompatibilities observed with PVC, polyethylene, or non-PVC polyolefin bags.

## 4. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to **pertuzumab**, Chinese hamster ovary (CHO) cell proteins, or any excipients [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Left Ventricular Dysfunction (LVD):** Decreases in LVEF have been reported. Assessment of LVEF is mandatory prior to initiation and every 12 weeks during treatment (or once during neoadjuvant treatment).
    - **Metastatic Setting:** Withhold for ≥3 weeks if LVEF drops <40% or is 40–45% with a ≥10% point drop from baseline.
    - **Early Breast Cancer Setting:** Withhold for ≥3 weeks if LVEF drops <50% with a ≥10% point drop from baseline.
- **Infusion-Related/Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Monitor closely for 60 minutes after the first dose and 30 minutes after subsequent doses. Facilities for resuscitation must be available.
- **Febrile Neutropenia:** Risk is increased when combined with docetaxel, particularly in Asian patients [Australian].
- **Diarrhoea:** May be severe; most frequent during concurrent taxane therapy. Elderly patients (>65 years) are at higher risk.
- **Tumour Lysis Syndrome (TLS):** Reported in post-marketing settings; monitor patients with high-grade or bulky tumours.

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions are reported for the combination of **pertuzumab** with trastuzumab and chemotherapy.

| Frequency | Adverse Event |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Diarrhoea (67.9%), Alopecia, Nausea, Fatigue, Neutropenia, Vomiting, Rash, Anaemia, Stomatitis, Myalgia, Mucosal inflammation. |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Left ventricular dysfunction (1.4%), Paronychia, Infusion-related reaction, Hypersensitivity, Chills. |
| **Uncommon (0.1–1%)** | Congestive heart failure, Anaphylactic reaction, Interstitial lung disease, Pleural effusion. |
| **Rare (<0.1%)** | Cytokine release syndrome. |

**Serious Adverse Effects:** Symptomatic heart failure, severe neutropenia, and anaphylaxis are the most significant risks.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic (PK) Interactions:** No clinically relevant PK interactions were observed between **pertuzumab** and trastuzumab, docetaxel, paclitaxel, gemcitabine, capecitabine, carboplatin, or erlotinib.
- **Management:** No dose adjustments are required for concomitant medications based on PK data.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** **Category D [Australian]**. **Pertuzumab** can cause embryo-fetal harm or death (e.g., oligohydramnios sequence). Pregnancy must be ruled out before starting.
- **Contraception:** Women of childbearing potential (and their partners) must use effective contraception during treatment and for **7 months** after the final dose [Australian].
- **Breastfeeding:** Safety not established. Because human IgG is secreted in milk, a decision must be made to discontinue nursing or the drug, considering the mother's clinical need and the long half-life of **pertuzumab**.
- **Fertility:** No specific animal studies; however, no adverse effects on reproductive organs were seen in 6-month toxicity studies in monkeys.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy in children <18 years have not been established.
- **Elderly Use:** No overall differences in efficacy/safety in patients ≥65 years, except for a higher incidence of diarrhoea. Data for patients >75 years are limited.
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment for mild or moderate impairment. No recommendation possible for severe impairment (eGFR <30 mL/min) due to limited data.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not studied in this population.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** **Pertuzumab** targets the extracellular dimerization domain (Subdomain II) of the HER2 protein. It blocks ligand-dependent heterodimerization of HER2 with other HER family members (EGFR, HER3, HER4), inhibiting MAP kinase and PI3K signalling pathways. It also mediates antibody-dependent cell-mediated cytotoxicity (ADCC).
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Central volume (Vc) ~3.11 L; Peripheral volume (Vp) ~2.46 L.
    - **Elimination:** Primarily via catabolism. Median clearance ~0.235 L/day.
    - **Half-life:** Approximately 18 days.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **CLEOPATRA (MBC):** Addition of **pertuzumab** to trastuzumab/docetaxel significantly increased median Progression-Free Survival (PFS) from 12.4 to 18.5 months (HR 0.62) and improved Overall Survival (OS) (HR 0.68).
- **NEOSPHERE (EBC Neoadjuvant):** Showed a statistically significant increase in pathological complete response (pCR) in the breast (45.8% vs 29.0%) when **pertuzumab** was added to trastuzumab and docetaxel.
- **APHINITY (EBC Adjuvant):** Adjuvant treatment for 1 year showed a 19% reduction in the risk of invasive breast cancer recurrence or death (HR 0.81). Benefit was most pronounced in node-positive and hormone receptor-negative subgroups.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vials:** Store at 2°C to 8°C. Refrigerate; do not freeze. Keep vial in the outer carton to protect from light.
- **Diluted Solution:** If not used immediately, the diluted solution is chemically and physically stable for 24 hours at 2°C to 8°C (refrigeration) or up to 30°C for non-refrigerated use [Australian].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule:** **Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine) [Australian]**.
