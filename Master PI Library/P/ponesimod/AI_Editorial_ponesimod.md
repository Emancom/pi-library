# Editorial Product Information: Ponesimod

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial synthesis is derived from the following provided regulatory source: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of ponesimod for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises clinical, regulatory, and pharmacological data into a single reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult the current local PI when making treatment decisions.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Ponesimod (a sphingosine 1-phosphate [S1P] receptor modulator)
- **Brand Name:** **PONVORY** [US]
- **ATC Code:** N03AE01 (not explicitly stated in source, but standard for this class)
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Film-coated tablets: 2 mg, 3 mg, 4 mg, 5 mg, 6 mg, 7 mg, 8 mg, 9 mg, 10 mg, and 20 mg [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Vanda Pharmaceuticals Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Relapsing Forms of Multiple Sclerosis (MS):** Indicated for the treatment of adults with relapsing forms of MS, including:
    - Clinically isolated syndrome
    - Relapsing-remitting disease
    - Active secondary progressive disease [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Maintenance Dosage:** 20 mg orally once daily, starting on Day 15.

### Titration Schedule
Treatment must be initiated with a 14-day titration to reduce the risk of bradyarrhythmia and atrioventricular (AV) conduction delays [US].
- **Days 1–2:** 2 mg daily
- **Days 3–4:** 3 mg daily
- **Days 5–6:** 4 mg daily
- **Day 7:** 5 mg daily
- **Day 8:** 6 mg daily
- **Day 9:** 7 mg daily
- **Day 10:** 8 mg daily
- **Day 11:** 9 mg daily
- **Days 12–14:** 10 mg daily

### Administration Instructions
- Tablets should be swallowed whole and can be taken with or without food [US].

### Missed Dose Instructions
- **During Titration:**
    - If <4 consecutive doses are missed: Resume with the first missed titration dose and follow the schedule.
    - If ≥4 consecutive doses are missed: Reinitiate with Day 1 of the titration regimen.
- **During Maintenance:**
    - If <4 consecutive doses are missed: Resume with the maintenance dose.
    - If ≥4 consecutive doses are missed: Reinitiate with Day 1 of the titration regimen [US].

### First-Dose Monitoring
Monitoring for 4 hours post-dose is recommended for patients with specific pre-existing cardiac conditions (e.g., sinus bradycardia [HR <55 bpm], first- or second-degree [Mobitz type I] AV block, or history of MI/HF occurring >6 months ago) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Recent Cardiovascular Events:** Myocardial infarction, unstable angina, stroke, transient ischemic attack (TIA), decompensated heart failure requiring hospitalisation, or Class III/IV heart failure within the last 6 months [US].
- **Cardiac Conduction Abnormalities:** Presence of Mobitz type II second-degree AV block, third-degree AV block, sick sinus syndrome, or sino-atrial block (unless the patient has a functioning pacemaker) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Infections:** May increase susceptibility to infections. Obtain a CBC (including lymphocyte count) before initiation. Delay treatment in patients with active infections. Vigilance for Progressive Multifocal Leukoencephalopathy (PML) is required [US].
- **Bradyarrhythmia and AV Conduction Delays:** Transient heart rate reduction occurs upon initiation. Titration is mandatory. ECG is required before the first dose [US].
- **Respiratory Effects:** Dose-dependent reductions in FEV₁ and DLCO may occur. Spirometry should be performed if clinically indicated [US].
- **Liver Injury:** Elevations in transaminases may occur. Obtain transaminase and bilirubin levels before initiation. Discontinue if significant liver injury is confirmed [US].
- **Increased Blood Pressure:** Monitor blood pressure during treatment and manage appropriately [US].
- **Cutaneous Malignancies:** Increased risk of basal cell carcinoma, squamous cell carcinoma, and melanoma. Periodic skin examinations are recommended [US].
- **Macular Edema:** Risk is increased, particularly in patients with diabetes or uveitis. Obtain a baseline fundus examination.
- **Posterior Reversible Encephalopathy Syndrome (PRES):** Rare cases reported with S1P modulators; monitor for sudden severe headache, confusion, or visual loss [US].
- **Fetal Risk:** May cause fetal harm. Effective contraception is required during treatment and for 1 week after the last dose [US].
- **Discontinuation Rebound:** Severe increase in disability and disease rebound have been reported after stopping S1P modulators [US].

**Serious Adverse Effects:** Progressive Multifocal Leukoencephalopathy (PML), Cryptococcal meningitis, serious opportunistic infections, and hypertensive crisis [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Antineoplastic/Immune-modulating Therapies:** Caution due to additive immunosuppression. Initiation after alemtuzumab is not recommended [US].
- **Heart Rate Lowering Drugs:** Caution with beta-blockers, digoxin, or calcium channel blockers (e.g., diltiazem, verapamil) due to additive effects on heart rate [US].
- **QT Prolonging Drugs:** Advice from a cardiologist is recommended before use [US].
- **Vaccinations:** Avoid live attenuated vaccines during and for 1–2 weeks after treatment. Vaccines may be less effective during ponesimod therapy [US].
- **Carbamazepine:** May decrease ponesimod exposure by ~26%; however, this is not considered clinically significant [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US]: Based on animal data, ponesimod may cause fetal harm. Women of childbearing potential must use effective contraception during treatment and for 1 week after discontinuation.
- **Breastfeeding:** There are no data on the presence of ponesimod in human milk. Animal studies showed excretion in milk. Consider the developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding alongside the mother's clinical need [US].
- **Fertility:** No effects on fertility were observed in rat studies at exposures significantly higher than human doses [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness in patients <18 years have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical trials did not include sufficient numbers of patients ≥65 years. Use with caution [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment is required [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** 
    - Mild (Child-Pugh A): No dose adjustment required.
    - Moderate/Severe (Child-Pugh B and C): **Not recommended**, as exposure is significantly increased [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Ponesimod is an S1P receptor 1 modulator that binds with high affinity to S1P1 receptors. It blocks the egress of lymphocytes from lymph nodes, reducing peripheral lymphocyte counts and potentially limiting their migration into the central nervous system [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Tmax is 2–4 hours; absolute bioavailability is 84%. Steady state is reached in 3 days.
    - **Distribution:** Highly protein bound (>99%). Vd is 160 L.
    - **Metabolism:** Extensively metabolized via multiple pathways (CYP3A4, 2J2, 4F3A, 4F12, and UGT1A1/2B7).
    - **Elimination:** Elimination half-life is approximately 33 hours. Excreted primarily in feces (57–80%) and urine (10–18%) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Study 1 (NCT02425644):** A randomized, double-blind, active-controlled superiority study (N=1133) comparing ponesimod 20 mg to teriflunomide 14 mg over 108 weeks.
    - **Primary Outcome:** Ponesimod significantly reduced the Annualized Relapse Rate (ARR) by 30.5% compared to teriflunomide (0.202 vs 0.290, p=0.0003).
    - **Secondary Outcomes:** Significant reductions were observed in the mean number of new or enlarging T2 lesions (56% reduction) and Gd-enhancing T1 lesions (59% reduction) per year [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F to 86°F).
- **Handling:** Store in the original package. Do not discard the desiccant sachet. Protect from moisture [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Rx-only [US].
