## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN).
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency (Inborn Errors of Metabolism):** For the acute and chronic management of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
- **General Dosing:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg.
- **Administration:** Administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion.
- **Severe Metabolic Crisis:** A loading dose may be given, followed by an equivalent dose divided over the next 24 hours. Dosing frequency should be every 3 or 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours.
- **Monitoring:** Daily doses are typically in the range of 50 mg/kg, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilized. Plasma carnitine levels should be monitored to maintain levels between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight).
- **Administration:** Administered as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
- **Titration:** Adjustments (e.g., down to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy, guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma carnitine concentrations (target: 40–50 µmol/L) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known:** There are no listed contraindications for levocarnitine injection in the provided source [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have occurred—often within minutes of administration. These are reported primarily in ESRD patients. Treatment must be discontinued immediately if a severe reaction occurs [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Levocarnitine has been associated with seizures in patients with or without pre-existing seizure disorders. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been reported [US].
- **Accumulation of Metabolites:** The safety of **oral** levocarnitine has not been established in patients with severe renal impairment. Chronic high doses of oral levocarnitine in ESRD patients may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse events reported in double-blind, placebo-controlled trials for ESRD patients (occurring at ≥5% frequency) include:

| Body System | Adverse Event (Incidence ≥5%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Chest pain (12%), Flu syndrome (25%), Headache (22%), Infection (15%), Injection site reaction (33%), Pain (30%) |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension (20%), Hypotension (14%), Tachycardia (6%) |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea (16%), Nausea (8%), Vomiting (15%), Abdominal pain (9%) |
| **Metabolic** | Hypercalcemia (9%), Hyperkalemia (6%), Hypervolemia (5%) |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness (13%), Paresthesia (5%), Depression (5%) |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis (23%), Cough increase (9%), Dyspnea (7%), Rhinitis (9%) |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Anaphylaxis and other severe hypersensitivity reactions [US].
- **Other Effects:** Transient nausea and vomiting; less frequent reports of body odor and gastritis [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is used concomitantly with warfarin. **Clinical Management:** Monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after any dose adjustment in patients receiving warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not specifically lettered in source]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Specific studies in humans are unavailable. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk carnitine concentrations after exogenous administration. Weigh the benefits of maternal supplementation against potential risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant; discontinuation of nursing or the drug may be considered [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders is weight-based (50 mg/kg). See Section 3 [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Indicated for use in ESRD patients on hemodialysis. Use caution with **oral** formulations in this population due to metabolite accumulation (see Section 5) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into cellular mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Levocarnitine clears accumulated acyl-CoA compounds (toxic in certain inborn errors) by forming acylcarnitine, which is rapidly excreted [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not protein or albumin bound [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Terminal half-life is approximately 17.4 hours. Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours (excluding endogenous carnitine) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In 9-week clinical trials, ESRD patients receiving 20 mg/kg three times weekly post-dialysis showed significant increases in plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Trough levels rose from a baseline of ~21 µmol/L to ~190 µmol/L after 8 weeks of therapy [US].
- **Dose-Ranging:** Studies comparing 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses confirmed dose-dependent increases in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing. Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as the formulation contains no preservatives [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable for up to 24 hours in PVC bags at room temperature when mixed with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8.0 mg/mL) [US].
