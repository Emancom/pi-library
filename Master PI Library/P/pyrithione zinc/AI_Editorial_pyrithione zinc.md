## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Pyrithione zinc (1%)
- **Chemical Class:** Cytostatic/Antifungal agent
- **Brand Name(s):** The Original Mane 'n Tail Daily Control 2 in 1 Anti Dandruff [US]
- **ATC Code:** D11AC11
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 1% topical shampoo/conditioner [US]
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical (Scalp)
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Straight Arrow Products Inc [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Dandruff:** Indicated for the prevention of the recurrence of scalp flaking and itching associated with dandruff [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Frequency:** For best results, the product should be used at least twice a week, or as directed by a physician [US].
- **Maximum Control:** For maximum dandruff control, the product may be used every time the user shampoos [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Apply to wet hair and massage onto the scalp [US].
- Rinse thoroughly [US].
- Application may be repeated if desired [US].

## 4. Contraindications

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### General Warnings and Precautions
- **External Use Only:** This product is for topical application to the scalp only [US].
- **Ocular Avoidance:** Care should be taken to avoid contact with the eyes. If contact occurs, the eyes should be rinsed thoroughly with water [US].
- **Clinical Worsening:** Use should be discontinued, and a healthcare professional consulted, if the condition worsens or fails to improve after regular use as directed [US].

### Specific Clinical Precautions
