# Editorial Product Information: Penciclovir

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA)
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of penciclovir 1% topical cream for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises the available regulatory information into a single, clinically focused reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority's current approved labeling for definitive prescribing guidance.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Penciclovir (a synthetic acyclic guanine derivative).
- **Chemical Class:** Deoxynucleoside analog HSV DNA polymerase inhibitor.
- **Brand Names:** Penciclovir Cream, 1% [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** Topical cream containing 10 mg of penciclovir per gram (1% w/w) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Topical.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Torrent Pharmaceuticals Limited [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Recurrent Herpes Labialis (Cold Sores):** Indicated for the treatment of recurrent herpes labialis in adults and paediatric patients 12 years of age and older [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric $\ge$ 12 years):** Apply the cream every 2 hours during waking hours for a period of 4 days [US].
- **Administration Instructions:** 
    - Treatment should be initiated as early as possible following the first sign or symptom (e.g., prodrome, tingling, redness, itching, or when lesions appear) [US].
    - Patients should wash their hands with soap and water after application [US].
- **Missed Dose Instructions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to penciclovir or any component of the cream base (including cetostearyl alcohol, mineral oil, polyoxyl 20 cetostearyl ether, propylene glycol, and white petrolatum) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **General Precautions:** 
    - For topical use on the lips and face only [US].
    - Application to human mucous membranes is not recommended as specific data are unavailable [US].
    - Avoid application in or near the eyes, as the product may cause irritation [US].
- **Clinical Monitoring:** 
    - Lesions that fail to improve or that worsen during therapy should be evaluated for secondary bacterial infection [US].
    - The safety and efficacy of the product have not been established in immunocompromised patients [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Clinical trial data involving 1,516 patients treated with penciclovir 1% cream showed that local adverse reactions were reported by 3% of patients (compared to 4% in the placebo group) [US].

### Post-Marketing Experience
The following reactions have been identified during worldwide post-approval use. Because these are reported voluntarily, frequency cannot be reliably estimated:
- **General:** Headache, oral/pharyngeal edema, parosmia [US].
- **Skin and Appendages:** Aggravated condition, decreased therapeutic response, local edema, pain, paresthesia, pruritus, skin discoloration, and urticaria [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** No specific drug interaction studies have been conducted [US].
- **Management Notes:** Due to the minimal systemic absorption of penciclovir following topical application, systemic drug interactions are considered unlikely [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not assigned; uses Risk Summary format]. Penciclovir is not absorbed systemically following topical administration. Maternal use is not expected to result in fetal exposure to the drug [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Systemic absorption by the mother is negligible; therefore, breastfeeding is not expected to result in exposure of the child to the drug [US].
- **Fertility:** In humans, there is no evidence of clinically significant effects on sperm count, motility, or morphology following oral administration of the prodrug famciclovir for up to two spermatogenic cycles [US]. Animal studies using high-dose intravenous penciclovir (at exposures $>1000$x the human theoretical AUC) showed testicular toxicity, including atrophy and reduced sperm counts [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy are established for patients $\ge$ 12 years of age. An open-label trial (n=102) in patients aged 12–17 years showed an adverse event profile similar to adults [US]. Safety and effectiveness in patients <12 years have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** In clinical trials (n=74 subjects $\ge$ 65 years), the adverse event profile was comparable to that seen in younger patients [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Not specified in available sources; however, systemic exposure is negligible [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Penciclovir is an antiviral agent active against HSV-1 and HSV-2. Viral thymidine kinase phosphorylates the drug to a monophosphate form, which is then converted by cellular kinases to the active penciclovir triphosphate. This active form competitively inhibits HSV DNA polymerase, thereby selectively inhibiting viral DNA synthesis and replication [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Negligible. Measurable concentrations were not detected in plasma or urine of healthy volunteers following single or repeat applications of the 1% cream at 67 times the estimated usual clinical dose [US].
    - **Intracellular Half-life:** 10 hours in HSV-1 and 20 hours in HSV-2 infected cells in culture [US].
- **Microbiology:** Resistance may occur due to mutations in the viral thymidine kinase (TK) or DNA polymerase (POL) genes. Cross-resistance has been observed with acyclovir and foscarnet [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Recurrent Herpes Labialis:** Two double-blind, placebo-controlled trials (n=3,057) evaluated healthy adults. Treatment initiated within 1 hour of symptom onset and continued for 4 days (every 2 hours while awake) resulted in a mean duration of lesions approximately 0.5 days shorter in the penciclovir group (4.5 days) compared to the placebo group (5.0 days). The mean duration of lesion pain was also reduced by approximately 0.5 days [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F). Excursions are permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F and 86°F) [US].
- **Shelf Life:** Not specified in available sources.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Prescription Only (Rx-only) [US].
