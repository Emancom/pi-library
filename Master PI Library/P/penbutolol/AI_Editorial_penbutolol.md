# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- Contributing Jurisdictions: United States (FDA)
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of Levocarnitine Injection for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises available regulatory data into a clinically focused reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) or Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection, USP (Baxter Healthcare Corporation) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection: 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
- **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg [US].
- **Administration:** Administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion [US].
- **Loading Dose:** Often required in severe metabolic crises, followed by equivalent doses divided every 3 to 4 hours (q3h or q4h). Doses should never be spaced more than 6 hours (q6h) apart [US].
- **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses typically range around 50 mg/kg, though higher doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilised based on clinical requirement [US].

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Recommended Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
- **Administration:** Administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line after each dialysis session [US].
- **Dose Adjustments:** Guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].

### Monitoring and Administration Notes
- **Target Plasma Levels:** For metabolic disorders, plasma free carnitine concentrations should be maintained between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].
- **Visual Inspection:** Parenteral products must be inspected for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration [US].
- **Missed Doses:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions may occur within minutes of IV administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and consider the risks/benefits before re-administration [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In those with a known history, an increase in seizure frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Metabolite Accumulation:** While specific to high-dose *oral* therapy, it is noted that severely compromised renal function or ESRD may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) [US].
- **Monitoring:** In ESRD, monitor trough carnitine levels regularly. In metabolic disorders, monitor blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Clinical Trial Experience (ESRD Patients)
Adverse events reported at a frequency ≥5% regardless of causality in double-blind, placebo-controlled trials:
- **Body as a Whole:** Abdominal pain (5–21%), headache (3–37%), injection site reaction (27–38%), pain (21–35%), fever (5–12%), chest pain (6–15%) [US].
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (18–21%), hypotension (3–19%), tachycardia (5–9%) [US].
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea (9–35%), vomiting (9–21%), nausea (5–12%) [US].
- **Neurological:** Dizziness (10–18%), paresthesia (3–12%) [US].
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (15–27%), cough increase (10–18%), dyspnea (3–11%) [US].

### Post-marketing and Other Experience
- **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea and vomiting; less frequent: gastritis and body odor [US].
- **Neurological:** Seizures (see Section 5) [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Anaphylaxis and laryngeal edema [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalised Ratio (INR) have been observed when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin. It is recommended to monitor INR levels after initiating levocarnitine or following dose adjustments [US].
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category C]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. No adequate/well-controlled studies in pregnant women exist; use only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine concentrations in milk following exogenous administration. Risks to the infant from excess intake must be weighed against benefits to the mother [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing recommendations for metabolic disorders apply to paediatric patients (see Section 3) [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Safety and efficacy of high-dose *oral* levocarnitine have not been evaluated in renal insufficiency; however, IV levocarnitine is specifically indicated for carnitine deficiency in ESRD patients on dialysis (see Section 3) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into cellular mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production. It also facilitates the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In ESRD, levocarnitine clears accumulated acyl-CoA compounds (which can cause life-threatening acidosis) by forming acylcarnitine, which is subsequently excreted [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Following a 20 mg/kg IV bolus, approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours. Mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Efficacy:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients (20 mg/kg 3x weekly after dialysis) showed mean pre-dialysis trough concentrations increased from ~20 µmol/L to 190 ± 55 µmol/L after 8 weeks. While the increase in carnitine concentration is proven, supplemental carnitine’s effect on specific clinical outcomes in this population remains to be determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
- **Precautions:** Avoid excessive heat. Protect from freezing [US].
- **Stability:** Compatible with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s (0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) for up to 24 hours in PVC bags at room temperature [US].
- **Handling:** Single-dose vials; discard any unused portion as the formulation contains no preservative [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources.
