# Editorial Product Information: Polatuzumab vedotin

## Editorial Note
- **Jurisdictions contributing to this editorial:** Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of polatuzumab vedotin for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises clinical, pharmacological, and regulatory data into a single reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. At the time of this publication, only the Australian PI was provided for synthesis.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Polatuzumab vedotin, a CD79b-targeted antibody-drug conjugate (ADC).
- **Chemical Class:** Antineoplastic monoclonal antibody conjugated with a microtubule inhibitor (monomethyl auristatin E; MMAE).
- **Brand Name:** **POLIVY** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Lyophilised powder for concentrate for solution for infusion. Supplied in single-dose vials delivering 30 mg or 140 mg. Following reconstitution, the concentrate contains 20 mg/mL.
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Roche Products Pty Limited [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Previously Untreated DLBCL:** In combination with rituximab, cyclophosphamide, doxorubicin, and prednisone (R-CHP) for adult patients with previously untreated diffuse large B-cell lymphoma [Australian].
- **Relapsed/Refractory DLBCL:** In combination with bendamustine and rituximab (BR) for adult patients with previously treated DLBCL who are not candidates for haematopoietic stem cell transplant [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Standard Dose:** 1.8 mg/kg administered via IV infusion every 21 days.
- **Duration:** 6 cycles.
- **Premedication:** An antihistamine and anti-pyretic must be administered prior to every dose.

### Regimen Specifics
- **Untreated DLBCL:** Administer with R-CHP on Day 1 of cycles 1–6. Prednisone is given on Days 1–5. Cycles 7 and 8 consist of rituximab monotherapy [Australian].
- **Previously Treated DLBCL:** Administer with BR on Day 1 of each cycle. Bendamustine (90 mg/m²/day) is administered on Days 1 and 2 of each cycle [Australian].

### Administration Instructions
- **Initial Infusion:** Administer over 90 minutes. Monitor for infusion-related reactions (IRRs) during and for at least 90 minutes post-infusion.
- **Subsequent Infusions:** If the first dose was well tolerated, subsequent doses may be given over 30 minutes, with a 30-minute post-infusion monitoring period.
- **Precautions:** Do not administer as an IV push or bolus. Use a dedicated infusion line with a sterile, non-pyrogenic, low-protein binding in-line or add-on filter (0.2 or 0.22 μm pore size).

### Missed Dose
- If a dose is missed, administer as soon as possible. Adjust the schedule to maintain the 21-day interval between doses.

## 4. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to polatuzumab vedotin or any of its excipients [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Myelosuppression:** Serious neutropenia, febrile neutropenia, thrombocytopenia, and anaemia may occur as early as Cycle 1. Monitor CBC before each dose. Consider prophylactic G-CSF.
- **Peripheral Neuropathy (PN):** Predominantly sensory; however, motor and sensorimotor neuropathy are reported. Risk increases with cumulative doses. Monitor for hypoesthesia, paresthesia, or weakness.
- **Infections:** Serious, life-threatening, or fatal bacterial, fungal, or viral infections (including opportunistic infections like PJP or CMV) have occurred.
- **Progressive Multifocal Leukoencephalopathy (PML):** Monitor for new or worsening neurological/cognitive symptoms. Discontinue permanently if confirmed.
- **Tumour Lysis Syndrome (TLS):** High risk in patients with high tumour burden. Prophylaxis and monitoring are required.
- **Infusion-Related Reactions (IRRs):** Can occur up to 24 hours post-infusion. Interrupt or discontinue based on severity.
- **Hepatic Toxicity:** Hepatocellular injury and elevations in transaminases/bilirubin have occurred.
- **Extravasation:** Monitor infusion site for tissue damage. If suspected, stop infusion immediately and restart in an alternate limb.

### Serious Adverse Effects
- **Febrile Neutropenia:** Occurred in 14.9% (untreated) and 11.3% (treated) of patients.
- **Pneumonia:** Reported in 8.7% (untreated) and 14.6% (treated) of patients.
- **Discontinuation:** The most common ADR leading to discontinuation in untreated DLBCL was pneumonia (1.1%) and in treated DLBCL was thrombocytopenia (6.0%).

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **CYP3A Interactions:** PBPK models suggest strong CYP3A inhibitors (e.g., ketoconazole) may increase unconjugated MMAE AUC by 48%. Strong CYP3A inducers (e.g., rifampicin) may decrease it by 49%.
- **Transporters:** MMAE is a P-gp substrate but not an inhibitor at clinical concentrations.
- **Rituximab:** Co-administration may increase acMMAE plasma AUC by 24% and decrease unconjugated MMAE by 37%, though no dose adjustment is required.
- **Bendamustine:** Does not affect the pharmacokinetics of polatuzumab vedotin.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy Category:** **Category D** [Australian]. Polatuzumab vedotin can cause foetal harm (embryofoetal lethality and malformations) based on its mechanism of action and animal studies.
- **Contraception:**
    - **Females:** Use effective contraception during and for at least 9 months after the final dose.
    - **Males:** Use effective contraception during and for at least 6 months after the final dose.
- **Breastfeeding:** Discontinue breastfeeding during treatment and for at least 3 months after the final dose.
- **Fertility:** Animal studies indicate potential for impaired male fertility. Advise men to seek information on sperm cryopreservation before starting treatment.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric:** Safety and efficacy in children <18 years have not been established.
- **Elderly:** No dose adjustment required for patients ≥65 years. No clinically significant PK differences compared to younger patients.
- **Renal Impairment:** No adjustment for CrCL ≥30 mL/min. Data are insufficient for CrCL <30 mL/min or dialysis.
- **Hepatic Impairment:**
    - **Mild:** No starting dose adjustment needed (though MMAE exposure may increase by 40%).
    - **Moderate/Severe:** Avoid administration (total bilirubin >1.5 x ULN).

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** The antibody component binds to CD79b on B-cells. The ADC is internalised, and the linker is cleaved by lysosomal proteases to release MMAE. MMAE binds to microtubules, inhibiting cell division and inducing apoptosis.
- **Pharmacokinetics:** 
    - **Distribution:** Central volume of distribution is ~3.15 L (approximates plasma volume).
    - **Metabolism:** Undergoes catabolism into small peptides, amino acids, and MMAE. Unconjugated MMAE is a CYP3A4/5 substrate.
    - **Elimination:** Terminal half-life is ~12 days. Clearance is ~0.9 L/day. Excretion is primarily faecal (95%).

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **POLARIX (Study GO39942):** Randomised, double-blind trial in 879 untreated DLBCL patients. **Polivy** + R-CHP showed significantly better progression-free survival (HR 0.73; 95% CI: 0.57, 0.95) compared to R-CHOP. 2-year PFS estimate was 76.7% vs 70.2%.
- **Study GO29365:** Open-label trial in 80 relapsed/refractory patients. **Polivy** + BR showed a higher Complete Response (CR) rate (40%) compared to BR alone (18%).

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vials:** Store at 2°C to 8°C. Keep in the outer carton to protect from light. Do not freeze or shake.
- **Reconstituted Solution:** Stable for up to 72 hours at 2°C to 8°C or 20 hours at room temperature (9°C to 25°C).
- **Diluted Solution:** Storage times vary by diluent; generally up to 72 hours (refrigerated) or 4–8 hours (room temperature) depending on the IV bag material and concentration.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule:** **Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine)** [Australian].
