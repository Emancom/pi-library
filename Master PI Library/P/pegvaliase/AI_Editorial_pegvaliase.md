# Editorial Product Information: Pegvaliase

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesises Product Information (PI) documents from the Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA, Australia) and the Food and Drug Administration (FDA, United States).
- **Purpose:** To provide a harmonised editorial overview of pegvaliase for healthcare professionals, integrating regional clinical guidelines and safety requirements.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not replace the official PI. Clinicians must refer to the specific PI approved in their jurisdiction for prescribing purposes.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Pegvaliase (pegvaliase-pqpz [FDA]).
- **Chemical Class:** PEGylated recombinant phenylalanine ammonia lyase (rAvPAL) enzyme.
- **Brand Name:** **Palynziq**.
- **ATC Code:** A16AB19.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Single-dose pre-filled syringes (PFS) in 2.5 mg/0.5 mL (white plunger), 10 mg/0.5 mL (green plunger), and 20 mg/1 mL (blue plunger).
- **Route of Administration:** Subcutaneous injection.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** BioMarin Pharmaceutical Inc.

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Phenylketonuria (PKU):** Indicated to reduce blood phenylalanine (Phe) concentrations in patients with PKU who have inadequate blood Phe control (typically >600 micromol/L) despite previous management with available treatment options.
- **Age Thresholds:**
    - Pediatric and adult patients 12 years of age and older [FDA].
    - Patients aged 16 years and older [TGA].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Pediatrics ≥12 years [FDA])
Treatment must be managed by a physician experienced in PKU management. Baseline blood Phe levels must be obtained prior to initiation. 

- **Induction:** 2.5 mg once weekly for 4 weeks.
- **Titration:** Escalated gradually based on tolerability to a target dose of 20 mg once daily. A typical titration schedule involves:
    - 2.5 mg twice weekly (1 week)
    - 10 mg once weekly (1 week)
    - 10 mg twice weekly (1 week)
    - 10 mg four times a week (1 week)
    - 10 mg daily (1 week)
- **Maintenance:** Individualised to achieve blood Phe between 120–600 micromol/L.
    - Start at 20 mg once daily for at least 24 weeks.
    - May increase to 40 mg once daily if Phe control is not achieved after 24 weeks.
    - Maximum recommended dose: 60 mg once daily [Note: Discontinue if no adequate response is achieved after 16 weeks at the 60 mg maximum dose].

### Administration Instructions
- Administer by subcutaneous injection into the front middle of thighs or the abdomen (avoiding 5 cm around the navel).
- Rotate injection sites; if multiple syringes are needed for one dose, sites should be at least 5 cm apart.
- **Premedication:** Consider/Required [TGA] premedication with an H1-receptor antagonist, H2-receptor antagonist, and antipyretic prior to each dose during induction and titration. 

### Missed Dose
- If a dose is missed, the patient should take the next dose as scheduled; two doses should not be taken to make up for a missed dose.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Known Hypersensitivity:** Recurrence of mild-to-moderate acute systemic hypersensitivity or any history of severe systemic hypersensitivity to pegvaliase, any excipients, or other PEGylated products [TGA].
- **None Listed:** The FDA PI does not specify any absolute contraindications but manages risks via a Boxed Warning and REMS.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warning: Anaphylaxis [FDA]
- **Anaphylaxis Risk:** Reported in 10% [FDA] to 6% [TGA] of patients. It can occur at any time during treatment.
- **Supervision:** The initial dose and first dose following an escalation must be administered under healthcare professional supervision. Observe patients for at least 60 minutes.
- **REMS Program [FDA]:** Available only through a restricted program (Palynziq REMS) requiring prescriber, pharmacy, and patient certification.
- **Epinephrine:** All patients must be prescribed and trained to use an adrenaline (epinephrine) auto-injector and must carry it at all times.

### General Warnings
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions (Type III):** Non-IgE mediated immune-complex reactions may occur, manifesting as serum sickness, angioedema, or arthralgia.
- **Injection Site Infections:** Serious infections (abscess, cellulitis, necrosis) have been reported. Do not inject into affected areas until resolved.
- **Hypophenylalaninaemia:** Low blood Phe (<30 micromol/L) may occur, especially during maintenance. Monitor blood Phe levels every 4 weeks [FDA] or at least monthly [TGA]. Adjust diet or reduce dose as necessary.
- **Effect on other PEGylated products:** Pegvaliase may increase hypersensitivity to other PEGylated injectables (e.g., medroxyprogesterone acetate).

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions are most frequent during the induction and titration phases.
- **Very Common (>10%):** Injection site reactions (93%), arthralgia (86%), hypersensitivity reactions (75%), headache, nausea, abdominal pain, vomiting, diarrhea, cough, fatigue, and dizziness.
- **Common (1–10%):** Alopecia, urticaria, rash, lymphadenopathy, angioedema (7%), acute systemic hypersensitivity (4.6% during titration), and serum sickness (2%).
- **Serious:** Anaphylaxis, severe angioedema, and serious injection site infections.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Formal Studies:** None performed.
- **PEGylated Medications:** Potential for immune-mediated interactions with other PEGylated products. Cases of hypersensitivity were reported with concomitant medroxyprogesterone acetate.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **TGA Category D:** Pegvaliase is not recommended unless alternative strategies fail.
    - **FDA Recommendation:** Maintain blood Phe between 120–360 micromol/L. Uncontrolled high Phe is associated with miscarriage and major birth defects (Maternal PKU Syndrome).
    - **Animal Data:** Toxicity noted in rabbits (lethality/malformations) and rats (skeletal variations) at doses above the human equivalent.
- **Breastfeeding:** Excretion in human milk is unknown. Safety not established; benefits must outweigh potential risks to the infant.
- **Fertility:** Animal studies showed impaired female fertility at high doses (associated with maternal toxicity), but no effect on male fertility.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** 
    - Approved for ages 12–17 [FDA]. 
    - No data available for patients <16 years [TGA].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical studies did not include patients ≥65 years; safety and efficacy are not established in this population.
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specifically studied. Pegvaliase is expected to undergo catabolic degradation; the PEG moiety is eliminated renally.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Pegvaliase substitutes for the deficient phenylalanine hydroxylase (PAH) enzyme. It converts Phe into ammonia and trans-cinnamic acid, which are subsequently metabolised by the liver and excreted.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Slow; Tmax is approximately 3.5 to 4 days [TGA] or 8 hours [FDA at steady state].
    - **Distribution:** Mean Vd at steady state is approximately 22–26 L.
    - **Elimination:** Mean half-life is approximately 47–60 hours. Clearance is primarily immune-mediated.
- **Immunogenicity:** Nearly all patients develop anti-pegvaliase Antibodies (TAb). Higher antibody titres are associated with lower systemic drug exposure and may require higher doses for efficacy.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pivotal Studies:** Studies 301 and 302 (PRISM trials) involved 261 adult patients.
- **Efficacy:** In a randomised withdrawal period, patients remaining on pegvaliase (20 mg or 40 mg daily) maintained significantly lower blood Phe levels compared to those switched to placebo, who returned to baseline Phe levels within 8 weeks (p < 0.0001).
- **Long-term Outcomes:** Mean blood Phe was reduced from 1233 micromol/L at baseline to 311 micromol/L at 24 months.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Refrigeration:** Store at 2°C to 8°C. Do not freeze or shake.
- **Room Temperature:** May be stored in the original carton at room temperature (up to 25°C) for a single period of up to 30 days. Do not return to the refrigerator once warmed.
- **Shelf Life:** Discard after the 30-day room temperature period or the expiration date.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Australia:** Poison Schedule S4 (Prescription Only Medicine) [TGA].
- **United States:** Rx-only; restricted via REMS program [FDA].
