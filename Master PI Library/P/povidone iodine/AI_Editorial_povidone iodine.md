## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Povidone-iodine 7.5% (equivalent to 1% available iodine).
- **Chemical Class:** Iodophore (Antiseptic).
- **Brand Names:** PVP Scrub Solution USP; PVP Solution USP [United States].
- **ATC Code:** D08AG02 (Povidone-iodine).
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - Sudsing Skin Cleanser/Scrub: 7.5% Povidone-iodine [United States].
    - Topical Solution (First Aid): 7.5% Povidone-iodine [United States].
- **Routes of Administration:** Topical (External use only).
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** McKesson Medical-Surgical Inc. [United States].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Preoperative Surgical Scrub:** For maximum degerming of hands and washing whenever a germicidal soap is required [United States].
- **Preoperative Patient Preparation:** For use on patients prior to surgery to reduce microbial flora [United States].
- **First Aid Antiseptic:** To help prevent infection in minor cuts, scrapes, and burns [United States].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Preoperative Washing (Operating Personnel) [United States]
1. Wet hands with water.
2. Apply approximately 5 mL (1 teaspoonful) of the scrub solution to the palms and spread over both hands.
3. Without adding additional water, rub the scrub thoroughly over all areas of the hands and forearms for approximately five minutes. A brush may be used if desired.
4. Clean thoroughly under the fingernails.
5. Add a small amount of water to develop copious suds and rinse under running water.
6. Repeat the entire procedure with another 5 mL of scrub solution.

### Preoperative Use on Patients [United States]
1. After the skin area is shaved, wet the site with water.
2. Apply the scrub solution (1 mL is sufficient to cover 20–30 square inches).
3. Develop lather and scrub the site thoroughly for approximately five minutes.
4. Rinse using sterile gauze saturated with water.
5. The area may subsequently be "painted" with the solution and allowed to dry.

### First Aid Antiseptic [United States]
1. Clean the affected area.
2. Apply a small amount of the product to the area 1 to 3 times daily.
3. The treated area may be covered with a sterile bandage. If bandaged, ensure the solution has dried first.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Iodine Hypersensitivity:** Do not use on individuals who are allergic or sensitive to iodine [United States].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **External Use Only:** Strictly for topical application. Avoid contact with the eyes [United States].
- **Thermal Precautions:** Do not heat the solution prior to application [United States].
- **Application Limits:** Do not apply over large areas of the body. In the event of deep or puncture wounds, animal bites, or serious burns, a physician should be consulted prior to use [United States].
- **Duration of Use:** Stop use and consult a healthcare professional if the condition persists, gets worse, or if the use is required for longer than one week [United States].
- **Ingestion Risk:** If swallowed, immediate medical assistance or contact with a Poison Control Center is required [United States].
- **Tamper Evidence:** Do not use if the product seal is broken or missing [United States].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Povidone-iodine is a broad-spectrum antiseptic. It slowly releases inorganic iodine, which exerts bactericidal and virucidal effects through the oxidation of microbial proteins and nucleic acids [United States].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Functions as a sudsing skin cleanser with germicidal efficacy [United States].
