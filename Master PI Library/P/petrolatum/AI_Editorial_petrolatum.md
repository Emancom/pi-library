## 3. Approved Indications
- Temporarily protects minor cuts, scrapes, and burns [US].
- Helps relieve chapped or cracked skin and lips from the drying effects of wind and cold weather [US].
- Helps treat and prevent diaper rash; helps seal out wetness [US].
- General purpose: skin protectant (stated as purpose) [US].

## 4. Dosage and Administration
### General dosing (adult)
- Strength as stated: petrolatum 99.8 g per 100 g product [US].
- General dosing instruction: apply as needed [US].

### Dosing by indication
- Minor cuts, scrapes, burns: apply to affected area as needed (no specific amount or frequency provided) [US].
- Chapped or cracked skin and lips: apply as needed (no specific amount or frequency provided) [US].
- Diaper rash (treatment and prevention): apply as needed to affected or exposed areas to help seal out wetness (no age-specific quantity or frequency provided) [US].

### Administration instructions
- For external topical use only [US].
- Avoid contact with eyes; do not get into eyes [US].

### Titration schedules

## 5. Contraindications
- Do not use on deep or puncture wounds [US].
- Do not use on animal bites [US].
- Do not use on serious burns [US].
- For external use only; ingestion is not an intended route of administration (see Warnings) [US].

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed warnings
- None stated in available sources.

### General warnings and precautions
- For external use only [US].
- Do not get into eyes; avoid eye contact [US].
- Do not use on deep or puncture wounds, animal bites, or serious burns [US].
- Stop use and consult a doctor if the condition lasts more than 7 days [US].
- Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away (phone number provided in source) [US].

### Monitoring requirements
- Seek medical consultation if signs persist beyond 7 days or worsen; no other monitoring requirements specified.

### Specific clinical precautions
- Avoid inadvertent ingestion and ocular exposure [US].
- Use in the presence of wounds described under Contraindications is not recommended [US].
