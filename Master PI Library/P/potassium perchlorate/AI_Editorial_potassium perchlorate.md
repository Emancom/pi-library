# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **US (FDA)**.
- **Note:** Information regarding other jurisdictions (e.g., Australian, European, Canadian, Japanese) was not provided in the source material and is therefore marked as "Not specified in available sources" where applicable.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. It is not a substitute for the official PI required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); a carrier molecule for long-chain fatty acid transport.
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter Healthcare Corporation) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Sterile injection: 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency in Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** For the acute and chronic management of patients with metabolic defects resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:**
    - **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg administered as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) or via infusion [US].
    - **Loading Dose:** Often utilised in severe metabolic crises, followed by an equivalent dose over the next 24 hours [US].
    - **Frequency:** Doses should be given every 3 to 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours [US].
    - **Maximum Dose:** The highest recorded dose administered is 300 mg/kg [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight) [US].
    - **Administration:** Slow 2 to 3 minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].
    - **Titration:** Adjustments (e.g., down to 5 mg/kg) may be made as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine levels [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected for particulate matter and discoloration prior to use [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations between 0.5 mg/mL and 8.0 mg/mL [US].

### Monitoring
- Pre-therapy plasma carnitine levels should be obtained [US].
- Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L) is recommended [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known** [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have occurred—often within minutes of administration. Most reports involve ESRD patients on dialysis. If a reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately [US].
- **Seizures:** Reports exist of seizures occurring in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Renal Insufficiency (Oral Formulation Precaution):** While this PI concerns the injection, clinicians should note that chronic high doses of *oral* levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are often confounded by the underlying pathology (ESRD or metabolic crisis).

### Common Adverse Effects (ESRD Clinical Trials)
| Body System | Adverse Event (Frequency ≥5%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Body as a Whole** | Abdominal pain (9%), Asthenia (9%), Fever (7%), Headache (22%), Injection site reaction (33%), Pain (30%) |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension (20%), Hypotension (14%), Tachycardia (6%) |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea (16%), Nausea (8%), Vomiting (15%) |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness (13%), Paresthesia (5%), Insomnia (4%) |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis (23%), Cough increase (9%), Dyspnea (7%), Rhinitis (9%) |

### Other Noted Effects
- **General:** Transient nausea, vomiting, and "fishy" body odor have been observed [US].
- **Metabolic:** Hypercalcemia (9%), Hypervolemia (5%) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when used concomitantly with levocarnitine. **Management:** Monitor INR levels frequently after initiating levocarnitine or following dose adjustments in patients on warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not specified by letter, but reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm [US].
    - **Recommendation:** Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** 
    - Levocarnitine is a normal component of human milk. Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied [US].
    - **Management:** Weigh the risks of potential excess carnitine intake in the infant against the benefits of supplementation to the mother [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impairment in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** See Section 3 for dosing. Safety and efficacy are established for metabolic disorders and ESRD [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in provided sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Primarily indicated for use in ESRD patients on dialysis. Monitoring of metabolites (TMA/TMAO) is relevant for oral dosing, but less so for the intravenous route [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. In patients with metabolic disorders, it facilitates the excretion of toxic acyl-CoA compounds by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not protein bound [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. Terminal elimination half-life is approximately 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** Double-blind, placebo-controlled trials in ESRD patients undergoing chronic hemodialysis demonstrated that 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses significantly increased plasma carnitine levels. Trough levels reached steady state by 12 weeks and remained stable through 24 weeks of therapy [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat. Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as it contains no preservatives [US].
