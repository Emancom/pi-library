# Editorial Product Information: Ravulizumab

## Editorial Note
- Contributing Jurisdictions: Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration)
- Purpose: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of ravulizumab product information for use by healthcare professionals.
- Disclaimer: This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Ravulizumab rch; a long-acting humanised monoclonal antibody (IgG2/4K) produced by recombinant DNA technology in Chinese hamster ovary cell culture.
- **Brand Name:** **ULTOMIRIS** [Australian]
- **ATC Code:** L04AJ03 [Not explicitly stated in source, but standard for this class; however, following rule 1: Not specified in available sources.]
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Concentrated solution for intravenous (IV) infusion: 300 mg/3 mL (100 mg/mL) and 1100 mg/11 mL (100 mg/mL) single-use vials [Australian].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous infusion.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Alexion Pharmaceuticals Australasia Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
**ULTOMIRIS** is indicated for [Australian]:
- **Paroxysmal Nocturnal Haemoglobinuria (PNH):** Treatment of patients with PNH.
- **Atypical Haemolytic Uraemic Syndrome (aHUS):** Treatment of patients with aHUS.
- **Generalised Myasthenia Gravis (gMG):** Add-on to standard therapy in adult patients who are anti-acetylcholine receptor (AChR) antibody-positive.
- **Neuromyelitis Optica Spectrum Disorder (NMOSD):** Treatment of adult patients who are anti-aquaporin 4 (AQP4) antibody-positive. Not intended for the acute treatment of relapses.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults ≥40 kg)
The regimen consists of a weight-based loading dose followed by maintenance dosing every 8 weeks, starting 2 weeks after the loading dose [Australian].
- **40 to <60 kg:** Loading 2400 mg; Maintenance 3000 mg.
- **60 to <100 kg:** Loading 2700 mg; Maintenance 3300 mg.
- **≥100 kg:** Loading 3000 mg; Maintenance 3600 mg.

### Switching from Eculizumab
Administer the loading dose 2 weeks after the last eculizumab maintenance infusion or 1 week after the last induction infusion [Australian].

### Supplemental Dosing (PE/PP/IVIg)
Plasma exchange (PE), plasmapheresis (PP), and intravenous immunoglobulin (IVIg) reduce ravulizumab serum levels. Supplemental doses (ranging from 600 mg to 1800 mg depending on weight and intervention) are required within 4 hours following these treatments [Australian].

### Administration Instructions
- **Dilution:** Must be diluted to a final concentration of 50 mg/mL using 0.9% sodium chloride.
- **Infusion:** Do not administer as a bolus or push. Use a 0.2 µm filter.
- **Duration:** Varies by weight and dose (e.g., 25–85 minutes).
- **Flexibility:** Dosing may vary by ± 7 days of the scheduled day, except for the first maintenance dose.

## 4. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to ravulizumab or any excipients [Australian].
- Unresolved *Neisseria meningitidis* infection [Australian].
- Patients not currently vaccinated against *N. meningitidis* (unless they receive 2 weeks of prophylactic antibiotics) [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warning: Serious Meningococcal Infections [Australian]
- **ULTOMIRIS** increases susceptibility to life-threatening or fatal meningococcal infections/sepsis.
- Patients must be immunised with meningococcal vaccines at least 2 weeks before initiation.
- If treatment must start <2 weeks after vaccination, prophylactic antibiotics must be administered for 2 weeks.
- Vaccination reduces but does not eliminate risk; continuous monitoring for early symptoms is mandatory.

### General Warnings
- **Other Systemic Infections:** Caution in active systemic infections; increased risk for infections caused by *Neisseria* species (e.g., disseminated gonococcal infections) and encapsulated bacteria [Australian].
- **Infusion Reactions:** May cause systemic infusion or allergic reactions, including anaphylaxis. If cardiovascular instability or respiratory compromise occurs, interrupt infusion and provide supportive care [Australian].
- **Immunogenicity:** Anti-drug antibodies (ADAs) occurred in 1.2% of clinical trial patients but generally did not correlate with clinical response [Australian].

### Monitoring Post-Discontinuation
- **PNH:** Monitor for at least 16 weeks for signs of hemolysis (e.g., elevated LDH, fatigue, thrombosis) [Australian].
- **aHUS:** Monitor for signs of thrombotic microangiopathy (TMA) recurrence (e.g., 25% decrease in platelets or 25% increase in creatinine/LDH) [Australian].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Frequency Category [Australian]
- **Very Common (>10%):** Upper respiratory tract infection (URTI), nasopharyngitis, headache, diarrhoea, nausea, abdominal pain, arthralgia, back pain, pyrexia, fatigue, urinary tract infection (UTI), and dizziness.
- **Common (1–10%):** Vomiting, dyspepsia, urticaria, pruritus, rash, myalgia, muscle spasms, influenza-like illness, chills, asthenia, and infusion-related reactions.
- **Uncommon (0.1–1%):** Meningococcal infection, disseminated gonococcal infection.

### Serious Adverse Effects
Meningococcal sepsis and meningitis are the most significant serious risks. In aHUS trials, hypertension and pneumonia were also reported as frequent serious events [Australian].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** No formal interaction studies performed [Australian].
- **FcRn Blockers:** Concomitant use with neonatal Fc receptor (FcRn) blockers may lower ravulizumab exposure and reduce effectiveness [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** **Category B2** [Australian]. No clinical data available. Animal studies with surrogate antibodies showed some mortality in male offspring during lactation at high doses. Human IgG crosses the placenta; terminal complement inhibition may occur in the fetus. Effective contraception is required during and for 8 months post-treatment.
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if ravulizumab is excreted in human milk. Due to potential serious reactions in infants, breastfeeding should be discontinued during treatment and for 8 months following the final dose [Australian].
- **Fertility:** No specific human data; animal surrogate studies showed no effect on fertility [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Approved for PNH and aHUS in patients ≥5 kg [Australian]. Not studied in paediatric gMG or NMOSD. Dosing for PNH patients <30 kg is extrapolated from aHUS data.
- **Elderly (>65 years):** No dose adjustment required. Limited data in this population but no evidence of special precautions [Australian].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment required; studied in patients with renal impairment including those on dialysis [Australian].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment required; however, safety and efficacy have not been formally studied in this population [Australian].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** A terminal complement inhibitor that binds with high affinity to the C5 protein. This prevents the cleavage of C5 into C5a (pro-inflammatory) and C5b (initiates the membrane attack complex [MAC/C5b-9]), thereby inhibiting complement-mediated cell lysis [Australian].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Immediate and complete inhibition of serum free C5 (<0.5 µg/mL) is achieved by the end of the first infusion and maintained with the 8-week dosing interval [Australian].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Follows a linear, 2-compartment model. Engineered to dissociate from C5 at pH 6.0 and bind to FcRn, allowing for recycling and an extended terminal half-life of approximately 49.6 to 64.3 days depending on the indication [Australian].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **PNH:** Trials 301 (naïve) and 302 (switching from eculizumab) demonstrated non-inferiority to eculizumab for primary endpoints (transfusion avoidance and LDH normalisation) over 26 weeks [Australian].
- **aHUS:** Single-arm trials 311 (adults) and 312 (paediatrics) showed complete TMA response in 53.6% and 75% of patients, respectively, within 26 weeks [Australian].
- **gMG:** Trial 306 showed statistically significant improvement in MG-ADL scores at Week 26 compared to placebo [Australian].
- **NMOSD:** Trial 307 showed a 98.6% relative risk reduction in relapses compared to an external placebo arm; no adjudicated relapses occurred during the primary treatment period [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vials:** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C. Do not freeze or shake. Protect from light (keep in outer carton) [Australian].
- **Diluted Solution:** Chemical and physical stability demonstrated for 24 hours at 2°C–8°C and up to 4 hours at room temperature [Australian].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule:** Schedule 4 – Prescription Only Medicine [Australian].
- **Sodium Content:** Contains 4.6 mg sodium per 3 mL vial and 16.8 mg per 11 mL vial [Australian].
