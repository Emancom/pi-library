# Editorial Product Information: Red Back Spider Antivenom

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following regulatory authority: **Australian (TGA)**. 
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised, clinically focused overview for healthcare professionals. 
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing and administration within a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local guidelines and the most current official PI from their respective regulatory body.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Red-back Spider Antivenom (equine).
- **Chemical Class:** Purified equine globulins (antivenom).
- **Brand Names:** Red Back Spider Antivenom [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** J06AA (Antivenoms - Note: Not explicitly stated in the source, but the standard class for equine antitoxins/antivenoms).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Solution for injection in vials containing 500 units of antivenom. 
    - Standardised such that one vial (500 units) neutralises 5 mg of venom from the female red back spider (*Latrodectus hasselti*).
    - Contains phenol (2.2 mg/mL), sodium chloride (8 mg/mL), and equine plasma protein (≤ 100 mg/mL).
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intramuscular (IM) injection; Intravenous (IV) infusion (in life-threatening cases).
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Seqirus Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Systemic Envenoming:** For the treatment of patients exhibiting manifestations of systemic envenoming following a bite by a red back spider (*Latrodectus hasselti*) [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Initial Dose:** 500 units (the contents of one vial).
- **Paediatric Dosing:** The dose is the same for both adults and children [Australian].
- **Repeat Dosing:** If symptoms are not completely reversed within two hours, a second dose may be administered if deemed safe. While symptoms may persist for long periods, it is rare to require more than three vials. If no improvement is seen after three vials, alternative diagnoses (e.g., the bite was not from a red back spider) should be considered.

### Administration Instructions
- **Intramuscular (IM):** The standard route for evidence of severe local or systemic envenoming.
- **Intravenous (IV):** Reserved for life-threatening envenoming. The antivenom must be diluted 1:10 in Hartmann’s Solution or 0.9% w/v Sodium Chloride.
    - *Note:* The IV route is associated with a higher risk of precipitating anaphylactoid reactions. Expert advice should be sought to avoid fluid overload during dilution.
- **Setting:** Administration should ideally occur in an intensive care unit and must always take place in a setting with immediate access to resuscitation facilities.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Absolute Contraindications:** There are no absolute contraindications to the use of Red Back Spider Antivenom [Australian].
- **Clinical Threshold:** The product should not be used unless there is clear evidence of systemic envenoming with potential for serious toxicity.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity and Anaphylaxis:** As a horse-serum-derived product, severe allergic reactions, including fatal anaphylactic shock, may occur. Risk is increased in patients with known allergies to horses or horse dander.
- **Atopy and Cross-reactivity:** Patients with a history of animal allergies (e.g., cats, dogs, sheep) or those who received Tetanus Antitoxin prior to 1974 may be at increased risk of cross-reactivity with equine proteins.
- **Pre-administration Preparation:** Adrenaline must be prepared and ready for use before administration. Routine skin testing is considered unreliable and is not recommended [Australian].
- **Infectious Disease Transmission:** Because this is derived from animal plasma, the risk of transmitting infectious agents cannot be entirely excluded, though no cases of viral transmission have been recorded for this product.
- **Delayed Serum Sickness:** Common following the use of foreign proteins. Patients must be warned of symptoms (fever, rash, joint pain) which may appear days or weeks later.

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions are classified by frequency based on post-approval data [Australian]:

| Frequency | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Allergic reactions, including anaphylactic shock and delayed serum sickness. |
| **Uncommon (0.1–1%)** | Urticaria, rash, local injection site reactions. |
| **Rare (<0.1%)** | Lymphadenopathy, pyrexia, chest pain, albuminuria, arthralgia. |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Anaphylaxis and delayed complications such as nephritis, neuropathy, or vasculitis (associated with serum sickness) [Australian].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **General:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Laboratory Tests:** No data available regarding interference with laboratory investigations.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Information is limited and inconclusive. Clinicians must weigh the risks of untreated envenoming against the potential risks of antivenom administration to the foetus [Australian].
- **Breastfeeding:** No data available. A risk-benefit analysis is recommended prior to use.
- **Fertility:** No data available.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** No specific age-related dose adjustments; children receive the same dose as adults [Australian].
- **Elderly Use:** No specific data available; however, systemic envenoming management should be tailored to the patient's clinical state.
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Passive immunotherapy. The product contains purified specific antibodies (globulins) that bind to and neutralise the toxic components of *Latrodectus hasselti* venom.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Antivenom has been reported to be effective in alleviating symptoms (particularly severe pain) up to 10 days after a confirmed bite.
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified in available sources.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pivotal Data:** Not specified in available sources. Use is supported by long-standing clinical practice and post-marketing surveillance.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 2°C to 8°C (Refrigerate. Do not freeze).
- **Light:** Protect from light.
- **Shelf Life:** The expiry date is indicated on the packaging.
- **Stability:** Contains no antimicrobial preservative; use in one patient on one occasion and discard any residue.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Australia:** Prescription Only Medicine (Schedule 4).
