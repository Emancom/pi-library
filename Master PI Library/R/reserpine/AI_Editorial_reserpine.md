## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None specified in available sources [US].
- **Safety Hazards:** 
    - **Acute Toxicity:** Harmful if swallowed [US].
    - **Neurological Effects:** May cause drowsiness or dizziness [US].
    - **Carcinogenicity:** Suspected of causing cancer [US].
    - **Reproductive Toxicity:** May damage fertility or the unborn child [US].
- **Handling Precautions:**
    - Obtain special instructions before use and do not handle until all safety precautions are read and understood [US].
    - Avoid breathing dust, fumes, gas, mist, vapours, or spray [US].
    - Wash skin thoroughly after handling. Use only outdoors or in a well-ventilated area [US].
    - Wear protective gloves, eye protection, and face protection during handling [US].
