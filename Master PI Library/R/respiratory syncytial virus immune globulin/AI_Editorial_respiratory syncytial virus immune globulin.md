# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine Injection

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **US (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview of **levocarnitine** injection for healthcare professionals, consolidating clinical and regulatory data.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis intended for information purposes. It does not replace the official PI or local prescribing guidelines required for clinical practice in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative (carrier molecule).
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection, 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Metabolic Disorders:** For the acute and chronic management of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance hemodialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Metabolic Disorders)
- **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg [US].
- **Administration:** Delivered as a slow bolus injection (2–3 minutes) or by infusion [US].
- **Loading Dose:** Often required in patients presenting with severe metabolic crisis, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours [US].
- **Frequency:** Dosing should occur every 3 or 4 hours (q3h or q4h) and never less than every 6 hours (q6h) [US].
- **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses typically range around 50 mg/kg, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been administered [US].

### Dosing for ESRD on Hemodialysis
- **Recommended Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
- **Administration:** Delivered as a slow (2–3 minute) bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].
- **Adjustments:** Downward dose adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine levels [US].

### Monitoring
- **Baseline:** Obtain plasma carnitine concentrations before initiating parenteral therapy [US].
- **Ongoing:** Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma carnitine levels is recommended. Target free carnitine concentrations should generally be between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warnings
- None specified in available sources.

### General Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, bronchospasm, and laryngeal edema, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients on hemodialysis. These may occur within minutes of administration. If a reaction occurs, treatment must be discontinued and appropriate medical management initiated [US].
- **Neurologic Reactions (Seizures):** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Renal Impairment (Oral Safety Note):** While this PI concerns the IV formulation, it is noted that chronic high doses of *oral* levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function or ESRD may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) [US].

### Monitoring Requirements
- **Warfarin Therapy:** Reports indicate that levocarnitine may increase the International Normalized Ratio (INR) in patients taking warfarin. Monitor INR levels closely upon initiation or dose adjustment [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Serious Adverse Effects
- **Anaphylaxis/Hypersensitivity:** Reported during IV administration [US].
- **Seizures:** Reported in both pre-disposed and non-pre-disposed patients [US].

### Frequency Data (ESRD Trials)
The following adverse events occurred at a frequency of ≥5% in placebo-controlled trials of hemodialysis patients, regardless of causality [US]:

| Body System | Adverse Event (Incidence ≥5%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Abdominal pain, accidental injury, asthenia, chest pain, fever, flu syndrome, headache, infection, injection site reaction, pain. |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension, hypotension, tachycardia, vascular disorder, palpitations. |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea, nausea, vomiting, anorexia, constipation, dyspepsia. |
| **Metabolic** | Hypercalcemia, hyperkalemia, hypervolemia, peripheral edema, weight changes. |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness, insomnia, depression, paresthesia. |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis, cough, dyspnea, rhinitis. |
| **Other** | Body odor (less frequent), gastritis, pruritus, rash, eye disorders. |

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Pharmacodynamic interaction resulting in increased INR. Close monitoring of coagulation parameters is required [US].
- **Other Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not assigned by letter, but noted as use only if clearly needed]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at doses up to 3.8 times the human surface area-based dose revealed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. No adequate, well-controlled studies exist for human pregnancy [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Although dairy cow studies show increased carnitine in milk, the risks to the infant from excess intake must be weighed against the benefits to the mother. Discontinuation of nursing or the drug may be considered [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders includes paediatric applications (see Section 3).
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in the source document.
- **Renal Impairment:** Dosing is specifically defined for ESRD patients on hemodialysis. Safety of oral formulations in severe renal impairment is a concern due to metabolite accumulation [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and energy (ATP) production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic/fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].

### Pharmacokinetics
- **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
- **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
- **Elimination:** Following IV administration, approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours. Terminal elimination half-life is approximately 17.4 hours [US].
- **Bioavailability:** Absolute bioavailability of oral formulations is low (approx. 15–16%) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** Clinical trials in patients undergoing hemodialysis demonstrated that IV administration of 20 mg/kg levocarnitine three times weekly resulted in significantly increased pre-dialysis plasma carnitine levels. Trough concentrations stabilized or increased over 8 to 24 weeks of therapy [US].
- **Outcome Limitations:** While levocarnitine increases plasma concentrations, its effect on clinical outcomes (e.g., muscle weakness, cardiomyopathy) in the ESRD population has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Protection:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat [US].
- **Stability:** Compatible with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s for up to 24 hours in PVC bags at room temperature [US].
- **Handling:** Single-dose vials; discard any unused portion as the product contains no preservatives [US].
