# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. 
- Information for other jurisdictions (e.g., Australian, European, UK, Canadian, Japanese) was not provided in the source material; where information is absent from the available source, it is marked as "Not specified in available sources."
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis intended for educational and reference purposes and is not a substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN).
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection, USP (Baxter Healthcare Corporation) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01 (Levocarnitine).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only [US].
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Indicated for the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that result in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance haemodialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
- **General Dosing:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg [US].
- **Administration:** Delivered as a slow bolus injection (over 2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion [US].
- **Loading Dose:** In cases of severe metabolic crisis, a loading dose may be administered, followed by equivalent doses over the subsequent 24 hours [US].
- **Frequency:** Dosing should occur every 3 to 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours [US].
- **Monitoring:** It is recommended to obtain plasma carnitine levels prior to therapy. Weekly and monthly monitoring should include blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine range: 35 to 60 µmol/L) [US].

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
- **Administration:** Administered as a slow 2- to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].
- **Dose Adjustment:** Adjustments should be guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine levels. Downward titration (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of treatment [US].

### General Instructions
- **Inspection:** Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable and compatible in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations of 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known** [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None [US].
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions including anaphylaxis, laryngeal oedema, and bronchospasm have been reported. These may occur within minutes of administration, particularly in ESRD patients. Discontinue immediately if a reaction occurs [US].
- **Seizures:** Levocarnitine may increase the frequency or severity of seizures in patients with pre-existing seizure disorders. Seizures have also been reported in patients without a history of seizure activity [US].
- **Renal Impairment (Oral Formulation Caution):** While the injection is used in ESRD, note that chronic high-dose *oral* levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [US].
- **Monitoring:** Monitor INR in patients concurrently receiving warfarin [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **General Effects:** Transient nausea and vomiting have been observed. Less frequent effects include body odour and gastritis [US].
- **ESRD Clinical Trial Data (≥5% frequency):**
    - **General:** Chest pain (12%), Flu syndrome (25%), Headache (22%), Injection site reaction (33%), Pain (30%), Abdominal pain (9%) [US].
    - **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (20%), Hypotension (14%), Tachycardia (6%) [US].
    - **Digestive:** Diarrhoea (16%), Vomiting (15%), Nausea (8%) [US].
    - **Metabolic:** Hypercalcaemia (9%), Hypervolaemia (5%) [US].
    - **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (23%), Cough increase (9%), Dyspnea (7%) [US].
    - **Nervous System:** Dizziness (13%), Paresthesia (5%) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalised Ratio (INR) have occurred when used with levocarnitine. Frequent monitoring of INR is recommended following the initiation of treatment or dose adjustments [US].
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not specifically assigned a letter in this PI version, but noted that reproductive studies in rats and rabbits (up to 3.8 times the human dose) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm [US].
    - **Recommendation:** Should be used during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine is a normal component of human milk. Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. In dairy cows, levocarnitine concentrations in milk increase following exogenous administration. Benefits to the mother should be weighed against risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for inborn errors of metabolism is weight-based (50 mg/kg), applicable to paediatric patients [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Used specifically in ESRD patients on dialysis via the IV route. High-dose oral administration is cautioned against in severe renal impairment [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also facilitates the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Corrects carnitine deficiency (defined as <20 µmol/L at one week post-term) and restores the acylcarnitine/levocarnitine ratio [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not protein-bound. Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites are trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in faeces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. Terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of ESRD patients (n=12), 20 mg/kg doses three times weekly post-dialysis increased mean pre-dialysis (trough) plasma carnitine levels from 21.3 µmol/L at baseline to 190 µmol/L at 8 weeks [US].
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** Studies comparing 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses showed dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F). Brief excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat. Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as it contains no preservatives [US].
- **Admixtures:** Once diluted in PVC bags (0.9% NaCl or Lactated Ringer's), the solution is stable for up to 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Rx-only (Prescription only) [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources.
