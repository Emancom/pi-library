# Editorial Product Information: Ramucirumab

## Editorial Note
- This editorial is synthesised from the official Product Information (PI) documents provided by the Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA, Australia) and the Food and Drug Administration (FDA, United States).
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals.
- This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Always consult local regulatory documents for final clinical decision-making.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Ramucirumab (a recombinant human IgG1 monoclonal antibody).
- **Chemical Class:** Vascular Endothelial Growth Factor Receptor 2 (VEGFR2) antagonist.
- **Brand Name:** **CYRAMZA**.
- **ATC Code:** L01FG02.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Concentrate for intravenous infusion; 10 mg/mL solution provided in 100 mg/10 mL and 500 mg/50 mL single-use vials.
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion only. Do not administer as a bolus or IV push.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Eli Lilly and Company.

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Gastric or Gastro-oesophageal Junction (GEJ) Adenocarcinoma:** For use as monotherapy or in combination with paclitaxel for adults with advanced or metastatic disease that has progressed on or after prior platinum- or fluoropyrimidine-containing chemotherapy.
- **Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC):** 
    - In combination with erlotinib for first-line treatment of metastatic NSCLC with EGFR exon 19 deletions or exon 21 (L858R) mutations [FDA-approved; not explicitly detailed in provided TGA source].
    - In combination with docetaxel for metastatic NSCLC with disease progression on or after platinum-based chemotherapy [FDA-approved; not explicitly detailed in provided TGA source].
- **Metastatic Colorectal Cancer (mCRC):** In combination with FOLFIRI for patients whose disease has progressed on or after prior therapy with bevacizumab, oxaliplatin, and a fluoropyrimidine [FDA-approved; not explicitly detailed in provided TGA source].
- **Hepatocellular Carcinoma (HCC):** As monotherapy for patients with an alpha-fetoprotein (AFP) of ≥400 ng/mL who have been previously treated with sorafenib [FDA-approved; not explicitly detailed in provided TGA source].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Premedication:** Required for all patients prior to each infusion. Administer an IV histamine-1 (H1) receptor antagonist (e.g., diphenhydramine). For patients who experience a Grade 1 or 2 infusion-related reaction (IRR), subsequent premedication must include the H1 antagonist, dexamethasone (or equivalent), and paracetamol/acetaminophen.

### Indication-Specific Regimens
- **Gastric Cancer:** 8 mg/kg every 2 weeks.
- **NSCLC (+ Erlotinib):** 10 mg/kg every 2 weeks [FDA].
- **NSCLC (+ Docetaxel):** 10 mg/kg on Day 1 of a 21-day cycle [FDA].
- **mCRC (+ FOLFIRI):** 8 mg/kg every 2 weeks.
- **HCC (Monotherapy):** 8 mg/kg every 2 weeks.

### Administration Instructions
- Infuse over approximately 60 minutes. If the first infusion is well-tolerated, subsequent infusions may be administered over 30 minutes [FDA].
- **Diluent:** Use only 0.9% Sodium Chloride. **Do not use dextrose.**
- Use a separate infusion line with a protein-sparing 0.22-micron filter.

### Dose Adjustments
- **Infusion-Related Reactions (IRR):** Reduce infusion rate by 50% for Grade 1–2; permanently discontinue for Grade 3–4.
- **Proteinuria:** Withhold if urine protein ≥2 g/24h. Resume at a reduced dose (e.g., reduce 8 mg/kg to 6 mg/kg; 10 mg/kg to 8 mg/kg) once levels return to <2 g/24h. Permanently discontinue if protein >3 g/24h or in the setting of nephrotic syndrome.
- **Hypertension:** Withhold for severe hypertension until controlled. Permanently discontinue if hypertension cannot be controlled with medical management.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Known Hypersensitivity:** To ramucirumab or any excipient [TGA].
- **FDA Position:** No contraindications listed [FDA].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hemorrhage:** Increased risk of severe and sometimes fatal hemorrhagic events, including gastrointestinal (GI) hemorrhage. Permanently discontinue in patients with Grade 3 or 4 bleeding.
- **Gastrointestinal Perforation:** Potentially fatal events reported. Permanently discontinue if perforation occurs.
- **Impaired Wound Healing:** Withhold for at least 28 days (4 weeks) prior to elective surgery. Do not resume for at least 2 weeks after major surgery and only until the wound is fully healed.
- **Arterial Thromboembolic Events (ATEs):** Serious events (MI, stroke, cerebral ischaemia) reported. Permanently discontinue for any severe ATE.
- **Posterior Reversible Encephalopathy Syndrome (PRES):** Rare (<0.1%) but potentially fatal neurological disorder. Confirm with MRI and permanently discontinue if it develops.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Risk of clinical deterioration (ascites, encephalopathy, hepatorenal syndrome) in patients with Child-Pugh B or C cirrhosis. Use only if benefits outweigh risks.
- **Thyroid Dysfunction:** Monitor thyroid function during treatment [FDA].
- **Aneurysms and Artery Dissections:** Use of VEGF pathway inhibitors may promote these events; consider risk factors (e.g., hypertension) before initiation.

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effect profiles vary by combination regimen:
- **Very Common (>10%):** 
    - *Monotherapy (Gastric):* Hypertension (16%), Diarrhea (14%), Headache (9%), Hyponatremia (6%) [TGA/FDA].
    - *Combination with Paclitaxel:* Fatigue/asthenia (57%), Neutropenia (54%), Diarrhea (32%), Epistaxis (31%), Peripheral edema (25%), Hypertension (25%), Stomatitis (20%), Proteinuria (17%), GI hemorrhage (10%).
- **Serious Adverse Effects:**
    - Febrile neutropenia, sepsis, GI perforation, ATE, and severe hemorrhage.
    - Sepsis was reported in 3.1% of patients in the RAINBOW trial [TGA].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- No clinically significant pharmacokinetic interactions were observed between ramucirumab and paclitaxel, docetaxel, erlotinib, or irinotecan (including its metabolite SN-38).

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **TGA Category D:** Likely to cause fetal harm and inhibit angiogenesis critical for fetal development. 
    - **FDA Recommendation:** Advise females of reproductive potential of the risk to the fetus and to use effective contraception during and for 3 months after the final dose.
- **Breastfeeding:** Discontinue breastfeeding during treatment. Recommendations for the washout period vary: avoid for **2 months** [FDA] to **3 months** [TGA] after the last dose.
- **Fertility:** Based on animal data, ramucirumab may impair female fertility.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy not established in patients <18 years. Effects on epiphyseal growth plates have been noted in animal studies and one paediatric case [FDA].
- **Elderly Use:** No overall differences in efficacy/safety in gastric cancer. However, in NSCLC (RELAY trial), patients ≥65 years had higher incidences of diarrhea, hypertension, and increased liver enzymes compared to younger patients [FDA].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment required for mild to moderate impairment. No safety data available for severe impairment (eGFR <30 mL/min) [TGA].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment for mild to moderate hepatic impairment. Not studied in severe hepatic impairment.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** A human receptor-targeted monoclonal antibody that specifically binds to VEGF Receptor 2. It blocks the binding of VEGF-A, VEGF-C, and VEGF-D, thereby inhibiting ligand-stimulated activation of the receptor and suppressing endothelial cell proliferation and migration.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Steady-state volume of distribution (Vss) is approximately 5.4 L.
    - **Elimination:** Cleared primarily via catabolism. Mean elimination half-life is approximately 14 days. 
    - **Steady State:** Achieved by approximately 12 weeks.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **REGARD:** Phase 3 monotherapy trial in gastric/GEJ cancer. Showed improved Median OS (5.2 vs 3.8 months) and PFS (2.1 vs 1.3 months) compared to placebo.
- **RAINBOW:** Phase 3 trial in gastric cancer (+ paclitaxel). Showed improved Median OS (9.6 vs 7.4 months) and PFS (4.4 vs 2.9 months) compared to paclitaxel alone.
- **RELAY:** Phase 3 trial in NSCLC (+ erlotinib). Improved PFS (19.4 vs 12.4 months) [FDA].
- **REVEL:** Phase 3 trial in NSCLC (+ docetaxel). Improved OS (10.5 vs 9.1 months) and PFS (4.5 vs 3.0 months) [FDA].
- **RAISE:** Phase 3 trial in mCRC (+ FOLFIRI). Improved OS (13.3 vs 11.7 months) and PFS (5.7 vs 4.5 months) [FDA].
- **REACH-2:** Phase 3 trial in HCC (monotherapy, AFP ≥400). Improved OS (8.5 vs 7.3 months) [FDA].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vials:** Store in a refrigerator at 2°C to 8°C. Keep in the original carton to protect from light. **Do not freeze or shake.**
- **Diluted Solution:** Chemical and physical in-use stability demonstrated for 24 hours at 2°C to 8°C or 4 hours at room temperature (<25°C).

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Australia:** Poison Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine).
- **United States:** Rx-only.
