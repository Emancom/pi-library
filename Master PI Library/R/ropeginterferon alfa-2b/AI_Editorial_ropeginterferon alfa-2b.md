# Editorial Product Information: Ropeginterferon alfa-2b

## Editorial Note
- Contributing Jurisdictions: United States (FDA).
- Purpose: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of ropeginterferon alfa-2b product information for use by healthcare professionals. 
- Disclaimer: This is an editorial synthesis based on available regulatory documents and is not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. At the time of writing, only the United States (FDA) PI was provided for synthesis.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Ropeginterferon alfa-2b-njft; a type I interferon; specifically, an N-terminal monopegylated covalent conjugate of proline interferon alfa-2b [US].
- **Chemical Class:** Interferon alfa.
- **Brand Name:** **BESREMi** [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Injection: 500 mcg/mL solution in a single-dose prefilled syringe [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Subcutaneous [US].
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** PharmaEssentia Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Polycythemia Vera:** Indicated for the treatment of adults with polycythemia vera [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Patients not on hydroxyurea:** Recommended starting dose is 100 mcg via subcutaneous injection every 2 weeks [US].
- **Patients transitioning from hydroxyurea:** Recommended starting dose is 50 mcg via subcutaneous injection every 2 weeks in combination with hydroxyurea [US].

### Titration Schedule
- **Titration:** Increase the dose by 50 mcg every 2 weeks (up to a maximum of 500 mcg) until hematological parameters are stable (Hematocrit <45%, Platelets <400 x 10⁹/L, Leukocytes <10 x 10⁹/L) [US].
- **Transitioning:** For those on hydroxyurea, gradually taper the hydroxyurea dose by 20–40% every 2 weeks during weeks 3–12; discontinue hydroxyurea by week 13 [US].
- **Maintenance:** Maintain the 2-week dosing interval for at least 1 year after achieving hematological stability. Following 1 year of stability on a stable dose, the dosing interval may be expanded to every 4 weeks [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Administered subcutaneously into the abdomen (at least 2 inches from the navel) or the top of the thighs. Injection sites should be rotated [US].
- Allow the syringe to reach room temperature (15°C to 25°C) for 15–30 minutes before use [US].

### Dose Modifications
- **Toxicities:** If drug-related toxicities occur, reduce the dose to the next lower level or interrupt therapy. Resume at previous levels only after recovery to grade 1 toxicity [US]. 
- **Hepatotoxicity:** Interrupt treatment if liver enzymes increase above baseline with concomitant bilirubin elevation. For isolated liver enzyme elevations (>5x ULN but ≤20x ULN), reduce dose by 50 mcg [US].
- **Cytopenia:** Interrupt treatment for life-threatening or urgent-intervention-needed levels of anemia, thrombocytopenia (Platelets <25,000/mm³), or leukopenia (WBC <1,000/mm³) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- History of severe psychiatric disorders, particularly severe depression, suicidal ideation, or suicide attempt [US].
- Hypersensitivity to interferons or any component of the formulation [US].
- Moderate (Child-Pugh B) or severe (Child-Pugh C) hepatic impairment [US].
- History or presence of active serious or untreated autoimmune disease [US].
- Immunosuppressed transplant recipients [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warning [US]
- **Risk of Serious Disorders:** Interferon alfa products, including ropeginterferon alfa-2b, may cause or aggravate fatal or life-threatening neuropsychiatric, autoimmune, ischemic, and infectious disorders. Patients must be monitored closely; therapy should be withdrawn if signs or symptoms of these disorders are persistently severe or worsening.

### General Warnings and Precautions
- **Depression and Suicide:** Life-threatening neuropsychiatric reactions can occur. Monitor all patients for symptoms of psychiatric disorders (e.g., irritability, restlessness, depression). Discontinuation is recommended if symptoms worsen [US].
- **Endocrine Toxicity:** May cause or aggravate thyroid disorders (hypo/hyperthyroidism) and hyperglycemia/Type 1 diabetes. Discontinue if endocrine disorders cannot be medically managed [US].
- **Cardiovascular Toxicity:** Includes cardiomyopathy, MI, atrial fibrillation, and coronary ischemia. Avoid use in patients with severe, acute, or unstable cardiovascular disease [US].
- **Decreased Peripheral Blood Counts:** Thrombocytopenia, anemia, and leukopenia may occur. Monitor CBC at baseline, every 2 weeks during titration, and every 3–6 months during maintenance [US].
- **Ophthalmologic Toxicity:** Severe eye disorders (retinal hemorrhage, detachment, or artery/vein occlusion) have been reported. Perform eye exams at baseline and periodically [US].
- **Hepatotoxicity:** Monitor liver enzymes and hepatic function. Discontinue in patients who develop evidence of hepatic decompensation [US].
- **Pancreatitis and Colitis:** Discontinue if pancreatitis is confirmed or if signs of ulcerative/ischemic colitis develop [US].
- **Pulmonary Toxicity:** Monitor for dyspnea, infiltrates, or pneumonia. Discontinue if pulmonary function is impaired [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **CYP450 Substrates:** Interferons may suppress CYP450 enzymes. Monitor patients taking CYP substrates with a narrow therapeutic index for potential dose adjustments [US].
- **Myelosuppressive Agents:** Concomitant use may produce additive myelosuppression; avoid combined use where possible [US].
- **CNS Depressants:** Avoid use with narcotics, hypnotics, or sedatives due to additive neuropsychiatric/central nervous system toxicity [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not specified - post-2015 labeling]. Available human data are insufficient to identify drug-associated risks of birth defects or miscarriage. Animal studies in monkeys showed ropeginterferon alfa-2b is an abortifacient. Advise females of reproductive potential to use effective contraception during treatment and for 8 weeks after the final dose [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** There are no data on presence in human milk. Breastfeeding is not recommended during treatment and for 8 weeks after the final dose [US].
- **Fertility:** Based on its mechanism of action, it may cause disruption of the menstrual cycle in females. No animal fertility studies have been conducted [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established in patients below 18 years of age [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical studies did not include sufficient numbers of subjects ≥65 years to determine if they respond differently. In general, dose selection should be cautious, starting at the low end of the range [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment is required for eGFR ≥30 mL/min. **Avoid use** in patients with eGFR <30 mL/min [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** **Contraindicated** in moderate (Child-Pugh B) or severe (Child-Pugh C) hepatic impairment [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Binds to the transmembrane interferon alfa receptor (IFNAR), initiating a signaling cascade via Janus kinase 1 (JAK1), tyrosine kinase 2 (TYK2), and STAT proteins. This modulates gene expression programs to exert therapeutic effects in the bone marrow, though the full mechanism in polycythemia vera is not entirely elucidated [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Efficacy is concentration-dependent and linked to the stabilization of hematological parameters. Maximum probability of complete hematological response (CHR) is typically reached after 2 years of continuous treatment [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Steady-state Cmax occurs between 2 to 5 days [US].
    - **Distribution:** Apparent volume of distribution is approximately 4.8 L [US].
    - **Elimination:** Undergoes receptor-independent degradation/excretion and receptor-mediated degradation. The terminal half-life is approximately 7 days [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **PEGINVERA Study:** A 7.5-year prospective, multicenter, single-arm trial in 51 adults with polycythemia vera.
- **Outcomes:** Complete Hematological Response (CHR)—defined as Hematocrit <45% without phlebotomy, Platelets ≤400 x 10⁹/L, Leukocytes ≤10 x 10⁹/L, and normal spleen size—was achieved in 61% of patients. The median time to response was 7.8 months [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- Store in a refrigerator between 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) [US].
- Keep the prefilled syringe in the original carton to protect it from light [US].
- **Do not freeze** [US].
