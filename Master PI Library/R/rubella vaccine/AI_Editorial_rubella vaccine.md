# Editorial Product Information: Measles, Mumps, and Rubella Vaccine, Live (PRIORIX)

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of the Product Information for **PRIORIX**, synthesised for use by healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and should not be used as a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. At the time of writing, only the United States PI was provided for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** Live attenuated virus strains:
    - Measles: Schwarz strain.
    - Mumps: RIT 4385 strain (derived from Jeryl Lynn strain).
    - Rubella: Wistar RA 27/3 strain.
- **Brand Name:** **PRIORIX** [US].
- **ATC Code:** J07BD52.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Lyophilized antigen component for reconstitution with a sterile water diluent. Each 0.5 mL reconstituted dose contains:
    - $\ge$ 3.4 $log_{10}$ CCID$_{50}$ (Cell Culture Infective Dose 50%) of measles virus.
    - $\ge$ 4.2 $log_{10}$ CCID$_{50}$ of mumps virus.
    - $\ge$ 3.3 $log_{10}$ CCID$_{50}$ of rubella virus.
- **Route of Administration:** Subcutaneous injection.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** GlaxoSmithKline Biologicals SA [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Active Immunisation:** Indicated for the prevention of measles, mumps, and rubella in individuals aged 12 months and older [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Pediatric and Adult):**
    - A single dose is approximately 0.5 mL [US].
- **Standard Schedule [US]:**
    - **First Dose:** 12 through 15 months of age.
    - **Second Dose:** 4 through 6 years of age.
- **Catch-up/Flexible Dosing:** If the second dose is not administered at the standard age, a minimum interval of 4 weeks must be maintained between the first and second doses [US].
- **Interchangeability:** **PRIORIX** may be used as a second dose in individuals who previously received a different measles, mumps, and rubella (MMR) vaccine [US].
- **Preparation and Administration:**
    - Reconstitute the lyophilized powder only with the provided sterile water diluent.
    - The reconstituted vaccine should appear as a clear, peach- to fuchsia pink-colored suspension.
    - Inspect visually for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration.
    - Administer subcutaneously immediately after reconstitution or store refrigerated (2°C to 8°C) and use within 8 hours.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Severe Allergic Reactions:** History of anaphylaxis or severe allergic reaction to any component of the vaccine (e.g., neomycin) or following a prior dose of any MMR-containing vaccine [US].
- **Immunosuppression:** Individuals with severe immunodeficiency due to disease (e.g., leukemia, lymphoma, HIV/AIDS) or medical therapy (e.g., high-dose corticosteroids, chemotherapy) [US].
- **Pregnancy:** Vaccination is contraindicated during pregnancy [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Febrile Seizures:** There is an increased risk of febrile seizures following MMR vaccination [US].
- **Thrombocytopenia:** Cases of thrombocytopenia and thrombocytopenic purpura have been reported post-vaccination [US].
- **Syncope:** Fainting can occur with injectable vaccines. Ensure procedures are in place to prevent injury from falls [US].
- **Latex Sensitivity:** The tip caps of the prefilled diluent syringes contain natural rubber latex, which may trigger allergic reactions in sensitive individuals [US].
- **Vaccine Virus Transmission:** While live attenuated rubella virus has been detected in the nose and throat of vaccinees, confirmed transmission to susceptible contacts has not been documented [US].
- **Efficacy Limitation:** Vaccination may not protect 100% of susceptible individuals [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions are categorised by frequency based on clinical trial data.

### Solicited Adverse Reactions (Clinical Trials)
| Age Group | Local Reactions (within 4 days) | Systemic Reactions (within 15-43 days) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **12–15 Months** | Pain (26%), Redness (25%) | Irritability (63%), Loss of appetite (45%), Drowsiness (45%), Fever $\ge$ 38.0°C (35%) |
| **4–6 Years** | Pain (41%), Redness (22%), Swelling (11%) | Drowsiness (27%), Fever $\ge$ 38.0°C (24%), Loss of appetite (21%) |
| **$\ge$ 7 Years** | Pain (12%), Redness (12%) | Fever $\ge$ 38.0°C (3%) |

### Postmarketing Experience [US]
The following events have been reported voluntarily; frequency cannot be established:
- **Blood/Lymphatic:** Thrombocytopenia, thrombocytopenic purpura.
- **Immune System:** Anaphylactic reactions.
- **Infections:** Meningitis, measles-like illness, mumps-like illness (including orchitis, epididymitis, and parotitis).
- **Nervous System:** Encephalitis, cerebellitis (and transient ataxia), Guillain-Barré syndrome, transverse myelitis, peripheral neuritis, afebrile seizures, syncope.
- **Vascular:** Vasculitis (including Kawasaki syndrome and Henoch-Schönlein purpura).
- **Skin:** Erythema multiforme, chronic cutaneous granulomas (associated with rubella vaccine virus).

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Immune Globulins and Blood Products:** Concurrent administration may interfere with the immune response. Refer to CDC/local guidelines for appropriate intervals between blood product administration and MMR vaccination [US].
- **Tuberculin Skin Testing (TST):** MMR vaccines may temporarily suppress tuberculin reactivity. TST should be performed either before, simultaneously with, or at least 4 weeks after **PRIORIX** to avoid false-negative results [US].
- **Other Live Vaccines:** If not administered simultaneously, **PRIORIX** should be given at least 1 month apart from other live viral vaccines to avoid immune interference [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Contraindicated]. Use of live attenuated vaccines during pregnancy carries a theoretical risk to the fetus. Pregnancy must be avoided for 1 month following vaccination. If a pregnant woman is inadvertently vaccinated, she should be counselled on the theoretical risks, though Congenital Rubella Syndrome (CRS) following inadvertent vaccination has not been definitively linked in most cases [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if measles or mumps vaccine components are excreted in human milk. Rubella vaccine virus may be secreted in breast milk and transmitted to infants, potentially causing mild clinical illness, but not severe disease [US].
- **Fertility:** **PRIORIX** has not been evaluated for its impact on fertility [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy have not been established in infants younger than 12 months [US].
- **Geriatric Use:** Clinical trials did not include a sufficient number of participants aged 65 and older to determine if they respond differently than younger subjects [US].
- **Immunocompromised:** Vaccination is contraindicated in severely immunocompromised individuals [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** **PRIORIX** induces a humoral immune response. IgG antibodies against measles, mumps, and rubella viruses are produced, which correlate with protection [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** As a vaccine, standard pharmacokinetic studies (absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion) are not applicable [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Immunogenicity:** In a pivotal US study of children 12–15 months of age, **PRIORIX** was shown to be non-inferior to the comparator MMR vaccine (M-M-R II). Seroresponse rates (SRR) were 98.3% for measles, 98.0% for mumps, and 97.3% for rubella [US].
- **Concomitant Administration:** Clinical studies confirmed that **PRIORIX** can be administered simultaneously with other common pediatric vaccines (e.g., Hepatitis A, Varicella, DTaP-IPV, and Pneumococcal conjugate vaccines) without interfering with the immune response to any of the components [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Before Reconstitution:**
    - **Vials:** Store refrigerated between 2°C and 8°C (36°F to 46°F). Protect from light [US].
    - **Diluent:** Store refrigerated or at controlled room temperature up to 25°C (77°F) [US].
    - **Do not freeze** the lyophilized antigen or the diluent [US].
- **After Reconstitution:**
    - Administer immediately. If not used, store refrigerated (2°C to 8°C) and discard if not used within 8 hours. Do not freeze [US].
