# Editorial Product Information: retinol + ergocalciferol + dl-alpha-tocopherol + phytomenadione

## 1. Product Identification
- Active ingredient (INN/generic name): **retinol + ergocalciferol + dl-alpha-tocopherol + phytomenadione**
- Chemical class:
  - Retinol (Vitamin A) ester (fat‑soluble vitamin)
  - Ergocalciferol (Vitamin D2; fat‑soluble vitamin)
  - dl‑alpha‑tocopherol (Vitamin E; fat‑soluble vitamin)
  - Phytomenadione (Vitamin K1; fat‑soluble vitamin)
- ATC code: Not specified in available sources
- Route(s) of administration: Intravenous administration as an additive to parenteral nutrition admixtures (must be diluted/added prior to infusion)

## 2. Regulatory History
- Date of first registration/approval: 21 August 1992 [Australian]
- Date of last revision/update of the source PI: 01 June 2022 [Australian]
- Withdrawal, suspension or restriction history: Not specified in available sources for this product

## 3. Approved Indications
- Supplementation of fat‑soluble vitamins (A, D2, E, K1) as part of complete parenteral nutrition regimens to meet daily requirements.
- Paediatric indication: supplementation for paediatric patients up to 11 years of age [Australian]

## 4. Dosage and Administration
### General dosing (adults)
- Must be diluted/added to an appropriate lipid emulsion/parenteral nutrition admixture prior to infusion. Add aseptically within one hour of commencing the infusion and use within 24 hours after addition.

### Paediatric dosing
- For paediatric patients (up to 11 years of age) the recommended dose when used as an additive is 1 mL per kg bodyweight per day (do not exceed 10 mL per day). This preparation is added to a lipid emulsion prior to infusion. Add aseptically within one hour of commencement and use within 24 hours after addition. [Australian]

### Administration instructions
- Add to an appropriate lipid emulsion or total parenteral nutrition (TPN) admixture under aseptic conditions.
- Ensure homogeneous admixture (e.g. invert the lipid container/bag several times immediately before infusion).
- Use the admixture within 24 hours of preparation. From a microbiological standpoint, the product should be used immediately where possible; if not used immediately, in‑use storage times and conditions are the responsibility of the user and would normally not exceed 24 hours at 2–8°C, with infusion completed within 24 hours of preparation. (Storage specifics paraphrased from source recommendations.)
- Do not add other medicinal products/solutions to the admixture unless documented compatibility is available.

### Titration schedules

### Missed dose instructions

## 5. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to any of the active substances (retinol palmitate, ergocalciferol, dl‑alpha‑tocopherol, phytomenadione) or to any excipient in the product.
- Pre‑existing hypervitaminosis (A or D).
- Known hypersensitivity to egg or soya (product contains soya oil and egg lecithin) or known peanut allergy/cross‑reactivity.
- Contraindications related to the lipid emulsion vehicle (e.g., soybean‑oil based lipid emulsions) used for admixture: acute shock and severe disturbances in lipid metabolism such as pathologic hyperlipidaemia — avoid use in these contexts when the product is added to such lipid emulsions. [Australian]

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed warnings
- Not specified in available sources.

### General warnings and precautions
- The recommended doses are insufficient to correct severe deficiency states and may be inadequate for patients with markedly increased vitamin requirements.
- Risk of overdosage: daily vitamin requirements must be calculated to avoid toxicity, particularly for vitamins A and D; caution is required in paediatric patients.
- In patients receiving prolonged parenteral nutrition, regular monitoring of vitamin status (particularly vitamins A and D) should be considered.
- Routine supplementation with fat‑soluble and water‑soluble vitamins is recommended in patients on total parenteral nutrition to prevent deficiency states; individual daily requirements should be calculated to avoid excess.
- Contains soya oil and egg lecithin; may rarely cause allergic reactions. Cross‑allergic reactions between soya and peanut have been observed; exercise caution in patients with peanut allergy.
- When this product is added to a lipid emulsion for infusion, rapid infusion of the lipid emulsion has been associated with fat embolism; appropriate infusion rates and monitoring of the infusion vehicle are required. [Australian]
- Other drugs and solutions should not be added to the admixture containing this product unless compatibility with that specific admixture is documented.

### Monitoring requirements
- Consider periodic monitoring of plasma vitamin levels, especially vitamins A and D, in patients receiving prolonged parenteral nutrition.
- Monitor for signs of hypervitaminosis A and D (see Adverse Effects/Overdose).
- Monitor for allergic reactions during and after infusion in patients with known egg/soya/peanut allergy or prior sensitivities.

### Specific clinical precautions
- Pregnancy and lactation: recommended doses may be insufficient in pregnancy and lactation due to increased maternal requirements; clinical needs should be evaluated. The product has been administered to pregnant women with no adverse reactions reported in the source PI, but specific pregnancy safety categorisation is not specified. Use only if clearly indicated and monitor maternal and fetal status. [Australian]
- Fertility: Not specified in available sources.
- Ability to drive/use machines: The effect on driving and use of machines has not been assessed.

## 7. Adverse Effects
- The source PI states that no adverse effects have been reported with the product in adult or infant presentations. However, potential adverse events related to the product components or to the admixture process are described in the source PI and must be considered in clinical practice.

### Summary table of reported/potential adverse effects

| System organ class | Adverse effect / event | Frequency (as reported) |
|---|---:|---|
| Immune system disorders | Hypersensitivity reactions (allergic reactions) related to soya oil or egg lecithin; cross‑reactivity with peanut reported | Rare (described as "may rarely cause") |
| Metabolism and nutrition disorders | Hypervitaminosis A — fatigue, irritability, anorexia, weight loss, vomiting, other gastrointestinal disturbances, polyuria, cracking and bleeding lips | Not specified |
| Metabolism and nutrition disorders | Hypervitaminosis D — hypercalciuria, intermittent hypercalcaemia, osteomalacia, bone pain; fractures reported in patients receiving prolonged parenteral nutrition | Not specified |
| Vascular disorders | Fat embolism reported as a complication of rapid infusion of the lipid emulsion vehicle when used for parenteral nutrition admixtures | Reported (frequency not specified; associated with rapid infusion) |
| General (product-specific) | No adverse effects reported with product in source PI | Not applicable — explicit statement in source |

- Serious adverse effects highlighted: fat embolism (associated with rapid infusion of the lipid emulsion vehicle); hypervitaminosis A and D with prolonged/excessive dosing leading to systemic effects including metabolic bone disease and fractures (hypervitaminosis D) and systemic symptoms (hypervitaminosis A).
- Reporting: Healthcare professionals should report suspected adverse reactions to appropriate local pharmacovigilance mechanisms. (Specific reporting contacts are not provided here; refer to local systems.)

## 8. Drug Interactions
- Vitamin K (phytomenadione) may interact with coumarin‑type anticoagulants (warfarin and related agents) — administration may reduce anticoagulant effect; monitor anticoagulation parameters (e.g., INR) and adjust anticoagulant dosing as clinically indicated.
- When added to lipid emulsions/parenteral nutrition admixtures, interactions and incompatibilities may occur with other drugs/solutions; do not add other medicinal products to the admixture unless compatibility has been demonstrated for that combination.
- Interaction with the lipid emulsion vehicle: specific contraindications and precautions apply when admixed with certain lipid emulsions (see Contraindications and Warnings).
- Other pharmacokinetic or pharmacodynamic interactions: Not specified in available sources beyond the interactions described above.

Critical prescribing and handling notes (summary)
- This product supplies fat‑soluble vitamins A, D2, E and K1 for parenteral nutrition supplementation; it must be added to an appropriate lipid emulsion or TPN admixture under aseptic conditions and used within 24 hours of preparation.
- Calculate total daily vitamin intake to avoid hypervitaminosis, especially A and D; monitor vitamin status in prolonged therapy.
- Exercise caution in patients with egg/soya/peanut allergy and in those with conditions contraindicating use of the lipid emulsion vehicle (e.g., acute shock, severe disturbances in lipid metabolism).
- Vitamin K may antagonise coumarin anticoagulants; monitor anticoagulation and adjust therapy.
- For compatibility with specific parenteral nutrition components and for admixture preparation technique, rely on validated local compatibility data and institutional compounding guidance.
