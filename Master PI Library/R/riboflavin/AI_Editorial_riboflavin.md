# Editorial Product Information: Riboflavin-containing Vitamin Complexes

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on Product Information (PI) and labelling documentation from the Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA; Australia) and the Food and Drug Administration (FDA; United States).
- **Contributing Documents:**
    1. TGA PI: Biological Therapies B-Dose 2 mL Injection (thiamine, riboflavine, nicotinamide, dexpanthenol, pyridoxine, cyanocobalamin).
    2. FDA/Blue Heron Pharmaceuticals labelling: Altriva Chewable Tablets (.beta.-carotene, ascorbic acid, cholecalciferol, .alpha.-tocopherol, thiamine, riboflavin, niacinamide, pyridoxine, folic acid, cyanocobalamin, ferrous fumarate).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised clinical overview for healthcare professionals. It is an editorial synthesis and does not replace the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient (INN):** Riboflavin (Vitamin B2) in combination with various B-complex vitamins, antioxidants, and minerals.
- **Brand Names:** 
    - **Biological Therapies B-Dose** [TGA]
    - **Altriva** [FDA]
- **ATC Code:** A11BA (Multivitamins, plain) / A11EA (Vitamin B-complex, plain)
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:**
    - **Parenteral Injection [TGA]:** 2 mL vial containing Riboflavine sodium phosphate (5 mg), Thiamine HCl (20 mg), Nicotinamide (50 mg), Dexpanthenol (5 mg), Pyridoxine HCl (10 mg), and Cyanocobalamin (1 mg).
    - **Oral Chewable Tablet [FDA]:** Riboflavin (3 mg), Thiamine mononitrate (2 mg), Niacinamide (20 mg), Pyridoxine HCl (10 mg), Cyanocobalamin (12 mcg), Vitamin A/Beta-carotene (300 mcg RAE), Vitamin C (120 mg), Vitamin D3 (10 mcg), Vitamin E (11 mg), Folate (1,667 mcg DFE), and Iron/Ferrous fumarate (29 mg).
- **Route of Administration:** Intramuscular (IM) or slow Intravenous (IV) infusion [TGA]; Oral [FDA].
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Biological Therapies (Australia); Blue Heron Pharmaceuticals, LLC (USA).

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Vitamin Deficiency Correction:** Treatment of B vitamin deficiencies that cannot be corrected by oral intake [TGA].
- **Dietary Supplementation:** To supplement the diet and prevent nutritional deficiencies [FDA].
- **Specific Clinical Conditions [TGA]:**
    - Rapid saturation of B group vitamins.
    - Recovery from Beriberi and Wernicke’s syndrome (Note: B-Dose contains insufficient thiamine for acute treatment).
    - Pellagra (Vitamin B3 deficiency).
    - Peripheral neuritis related to B group deficiencies.
    - Pernicious anaemia (Vitamin B12 deficiency).
    - Angular stomatitis and glossitis.
    - General energy depletion.
- **Malabsorption/Risk Groups [TGA]:** For use in alcoholism, gastrectomy, gastrointestinal pathologies, or in debilitated and elderly patients where oral intake is insufficient.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Adult Dosing
- **Parenteral [TGA]:** 
    - **Standard:** 1 vial (2 mL) via slow deep intragluteal (IM) injection weekly for 2 to 6 weeks.
    - **Severe Cases:** 1 vial daily until acute symptoms subside.
- **Oral [FDA]:** 1 chewable tablet by mouth once or twice daily.

### Administration Instructions
- **Parenteral [TGA]:** 
    - Solution should be warmed to body temperature before injection for comfort. 
    - IM is preferred due to anaphylaxis risks. 
    - If IV is used, it must be administered by slow infusion in diluted solutions (e.g., TPN).
    - Discard any residue (single-use only).
- **Oral [FDA]:** Tablets are orange-flavored and chewable.

## 4. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to any ingredient in the formulation.
- Pre-existing B hyper-vitaminosis [TGA].
- **Specific to Dexpanthenol [TGA]:** Contraindicated in patients with haemophilia or ileus caused by mechanical obstruction.
- **Specific to Cyanocobalamin [TGA]:** Do not use for megaloblastic anaemia caused by folate deficiency.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warnings
- **Accidental Overdose (Iron) [FDA]:** Accidental overdose of iron-containing products is a leading cause of fatal poisoning in children under 6. Keep out of reach of children. In case of accidental overdose, contact a physician or poison control centre immediately.

### General Warnings
- **Anaphylaxis [TGA]:** Rare cases of anaphylactic shock have occurred following parenteral thiamine. Allergic reactions have also been reported for dexpanthenol and cyanocobalamin.
- **Folic Acid Caution [FDA]:** Folic acid doses >0.1 mg daily may obscure the diagnosis of pernicious anaemia by producing haematologic remission while neurological damage remains progressive.
- **Parenteral Administration [TGA]:** Use with extreme caution if administering IV; monitor for signs of anaphylaxis.

### Monitoring and Clinical Precautions
- **Allergy Testing [TGA]:** Patients with a known allergic disposition should receive a supervised intradermal test dose before parenteral administration.
- **Iron and Medical Conditions [FDA]:** Use with caution in patients with underlying medical conditions, those taking other medications, or those under 18 years of age.

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **General/Common:** Bright yellow discolouration of urine due to riboflavin (may interfere with laboratory tests) [TGA].
- **Rare/Serious:**
    - **Anaphylaxis:** Associated primarily with parenteral B1 and B12 [TGA].
    - **Peripheral Neuropathy:** Associated with long-term, high-dose pyridoxine (>1000 mg/day) [TGA].
    - **Sensitization:** Allergic sensitization reported with oral folic acid [FDA].
- **Other Reported Effects (Nicotinamide at 3g/day) [TGA]:** Heartburn, nausea, headaches, hives, fatigue, sore throat, and blurred focus.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Levodopa:** Pyridoxine (B6) reduces the efficacy of levodopa. This interaction does not occur if a dopa decarboxylase inhibitor is co-administered [TGA].
- **Folate/B12:** Indiscriminate use of B12 (>10 mcg/day) may produce a haematological response in folate-deficient patients, masking the underlying diagnosis [TGA].
- **Iron:** Potential interactions with other medications [FDA].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:**
    - **TGA:** Exempt from Australian Pregnancy Classification.
    - **FDA:** Use with caution and under medical supervision.
- **Breastfeeding:** Use with caution; medical supervision is recommended [FDA]. Not specified in [TGA].
- **Fertility:** Data not available [TGA].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Reserved for professional recommendation [FDA]. No specific data provided in [TGA]. Note boxed warning regarding iron toxicity in children under 6 [FDA].
- **Elderly Use:** Indicated for elderly patients with inadequate diets or malabsorption [TGA]. No specific dosage adjustments provided.
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:**
    - **Riboflavin:** An essential electron carrier for aerobic metabolism; coenzyme for FMN and FAD. Necessary for B6 and B3 function [TGA].
    - **Thiamine:** Essential for carbohydrate metabolism (pyruvate to Acetyl CoA) [TGA].
    - **Nicotinamide:** Component of NAD/NADP, essential for oxidative/reductive reactions [TGA].
    - **Pyridoxine:** Involved in amino acid, carbohydrate, and fat metabolism, and haem production [TGA].
    - **Cyanocobalamin:** Converted to active mecobalamin and adenosylcobalamin for energy production and neurological function [TGA].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not studied as a combination, but individual vitamins behave as per their standard pharmacokinetic profiles [TGA]. B12 is rapidly converted to active coenzyme forms after injection [TGA].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- No specific clinical trial data described in the provided source documents.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Parenteral [TGA]:** Store below 25°C. Protect from light.
- **Oral [FDA]:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F). Protect from moisture and excessive heat. Contact with moisture may cause surface discolouration of tablets.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Australia [TGA]:** Schedule 4 (S4) – Prescription Only Medicine.
- **United States [FDA]:** Dispensed by Prescription (as a high-potency vitamin/mineral supplement).
