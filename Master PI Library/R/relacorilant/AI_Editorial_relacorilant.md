# Editorial Product Information: Relacorilant

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**. 
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, consolidating regulatory data into a single clinically relevant reference. 
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official, current Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Relacorilant (INN/USAN)
- **Chemical Class:** Glucocorticoid receptor antagonist [US]
- **Brand Name:** **LIFYORLI** [US]
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - Soft gelatin capsules: 25 mg (opaque dark brown, oval, printed "CR25") [US]
    - Soft gelatin capsules: 100 mg (opaque yellow, oblong, printed "CR100") [US]
- **Route of Administration:** Oral
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Corcept Therapeutics Incorporated [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Platinum-Resistant Ovarian Cancer:** Indicated in combination with nab-paclitaxel for the treatment of adults with platinum-resistant epithelial ovarian, fallopian tube, or primary peritoneal cancer. Eligible patients must have received one to three prior systemic treatment regimens, including at least one regimen containing bevacizumab [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** 
    - The recommended dose is 150 mg (one 100 mg and two 25 mg capsules) taken orally [US].
- **Dosing Schedule:** 
    - Administer once daily on the day before, the day of, and the day after each nab-paclitaxel infusion [US].
    - Relacorilant is administered in conjunction with nab-paclitaxel (80 mg/m² intravenously on Days 1, 8, and 15 of a 28-day cycle) [US].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Take with food [US].
    - Swallow capsules whole; do not crush, chew, dissolve, or split [US].
    - If vomiting occurs after a dose, do not take an additional dose [US].
- **Dose Modifications (Adverse Reactions):**
    - **First Reduction:** 125 mg once on the day before, day of, and day after nab-paclitaxel [US].
    - **Second Reduction:** Permanently discontinue if the 125 mg dose is not tolerated [US].
    - Relacorilant should be interrupted or discontinued whenever nab-paclitaxel is interrupted or discontinued [US].
- **Missed Dose Instructions:** 
    - If missed by <12 hours: Take the missed dose immediately [US].
    - If missed by ≥12 hours: Skip the missed dose and resume at the next scheduled time. Do not double the dose [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Lifesaving Glucocorticoid Therapy:** Relacorilant is contraindicated in patients requiring systemic glucocorticoids for lifesaving indications (e.g., immunosuppression following organ transplantation) due to its potent glucocorticoid receptor antagonism [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Neutropenia and Severe Infection:** The combination of relacorilant and nab-paclitaxel can cause severe neutropenia (74% incidence) and febrile neutropenia (3.7%). One fatal case of septic shock was reported. Monitor CBC prior to each weekly treatment. Withhold, reduce dose, or discontinue based on severity [US].
- **Adrenal Insufficiency:** As a reversible glucocorticoid receptor antagonist, relacorilant may cause adrenal insufficiency. This risk increases during stress (infection, surgery). 
    - **Note:** Serum cortisol levels are not reliable for assessment during therapy. 
    - Treatment: Withhold relacorilant and administer high-dose rescue glucocorticoids if suspected. Due to the 27.5-hour half-life, antagonism may persist for up to 6 days after the last dose [US].
- **Exacerbation of Glucocorticoid-Treated Conditions:** Relacorilant reduces the efficacy of systemic glucocorticoids used for conditions such as autoimmune disorders. Monitor for worsening of the underlying condition [US].
- **Embryo-Fetal Toxicity:** Based on animal data (embryo-fetal mortality in rabbits), relacorilant can cause fetal harm [US].

- **Fatal Reactions:** Reported in 2.1% of patients, including septic shock, cardiac arrest, ischemic stroke, and intestinal perforation [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Strong CYP3A Inducers:** Avoid coadministration. May significantly decrease relacorilant and paclitaxel concentrations, reducing efficacy [US].
- **CYP2C8 Inducers and Moderate CYP3A Inducers:** Monitor for reduced effectiveness of the combination [US].
- **CYP2C8 Inhibitors:** Monitor for increased adverse reactions due to potential increases in paclitaxel exposure [US].
- **CYP3A Substrates:** Avoid concomitant use. Relacorilant is a **strong CYP3A inhibitor** and can significantly increase exposure to CYP3A substrates (e.g., midazolam AUC increased 8.9-fold) [US].
- **CYP2C8 Substrates:** Avoid use with substrates where minimal concentration changes lead to reduced effectiveness. Relacorilant is a weak CYP2C8 inducer [US].
- **Systemic Glucocorticoids:** Mutual antagonism; avoid coadministration unless required for the treatment of relacorilant-induced adrenal insufficiency [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** No letter category assigned (as per current FDA labeling rules). 
    - There are no human data on use in pregnancy. Animal studies show embryo-fetal mortality and structural abnormalities at sub-therapeutic exposures (0.6x human AUC) [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** There are no data on the presence of relacorilant in milk. Due to potential serious adverse reactions in the infant, breastfeeding is not recommended during treatment and for **one week** after the last dose [US].
- **Fertility/Contraception:** 
    - **Pregnancy Testing:** Verify status in females of reproductive potential before initiation [US].
    - **Contraception:** Females and males with female partners of reproductive potential must use effective contraception during treatment and for **one week** after the final dose [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established in patients <18 years [US].
- **Elderly Use:** In the ROSELLA trial, 38% of patients were ≥65 years. While no overall differences in efficacy were observed, patients ≥65 experienced a higher incidence of Grade 3-4 adverse events and required more dose modifications [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    - **Mild to Moderate (CLcr 30–90 mL/min):** No dosage adjustment required [US].
    - **Severe/ESRD:** Not studied; effect unknown [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:**
    - **Mild:** No dosage adjustment required [US].
    - **Moderate to Severe:** Avoid use. Exposure to both relacorilant and paclitaxel is expected to increase [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Relacorilant is a competitive, reversible antagonist of the glucocorticoid receptor (GR). It does not exhibit agonist or antagonist activity at the mineralocorticoid receptor. In cancer models, it enhances the pro-apoptotic and anti-tumor activity of paclitaxel [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** At 3.8x the recommended dose, relacorilant does not cause clinically significant QTc prolongation [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Median Tmax is 2.5 hours. Food increases AUC by 2- to 2.4-fold [US].
    - **Distribution:** Protein binding is >99%. Apparent Vd is 2490 L [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily metabolized by cytosolic reductases and CYP3A. An active metabolite (CORT125295) exists with 5-fold lower activity than the parent drug [US].
    - **Elimination:** Terminal half-life is approximately 27.5 hours. Excretion is primarily faecal (73%) with 17% in urine [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ROSELLA Trial:** A multicenter, open-label, randomized trial (n=381) in patients with platinum-resistant ovarian cancer who previously received bevacizumab.
- **Outcomes:** Relacorilant + nab-paclitaxel demonstrated a statistically significant improvement in:
    - **Progression-Free Survival (PFS):** Median 6.5 months vs. 5.5 months (HR 0.70) [US].
    - **Overall Survival (OS):** Median 16.0 months vs. 11.9 months (HR 0.65) [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F and 86°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Store and dispense in the original carton to protect from light [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Rx-only. Initial approval 2026 [US].
