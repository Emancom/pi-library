# Editorial Product Information: Relugolix

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Regulatory Agencies:** Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA - Australia), Food and Drug Administration (FDA - United States).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of **relugolix** Product Information (PI) for use by healthcare professionals, synthesising data from multiple jurisdictions.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific PI. Prescribers should consult the current approved PI in their specific region for definitive prescribing information.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** **Relugolix**; or **relugolix** in combination with **estradiol** (as hemihydrate) and **norethisterone acetate**.
- **Chemical Class:** Gonadotropin-releasing hormone (GnRH) receptor antagonist.
- **Brand Names:** 
  - **Ryeqo** (relugolix/estradiol/norethisterone acetate) [TGA]
  - **Orgovyx** (relugolix monotherapy) [FDA]
- **ATC Code:** H01CC54
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:**
  - **Combination film-coated tablets:** 40 mg relugolix / 1 mg estradiol / 0.5 mg norethisterone acetate [TGA].
  - **Monotherapy film-coated tablets:** 120 mg relugolix [FDA].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Gedeon Richter Australia Pty Ltd [TGA]; Sumitomo Pharma America, Inc. [FDA].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Advanced Prostate Cancer:** Treatment of adult patients with advanced prostate cancer [FDA].
- **Uterine Fibroids:** Treatment of moderate to severe symptoms of uterine fibroids in adult women of reproductive age [TGA].
- **Endometriosis:** Symptomatic treatment of endometriosis in women with a history of previous medical or surgical treatment for their endometriosis [TGA].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Administration
- Tablets should be swallowed whole; do not crush or chew [FDA]. 
- May be taken with or without food [TGA, FDA]. 
- Doses should be taken at approximately the same time each day [TGA, FDA].

### Advanced Prostate Cancer [FDA]
- **Loading Dose:** 360 mg (three 120 mg tablets) on the first day.
- **Maintenance Dose:** 120 mg once daily.
- **Note:** Treatment is usually continued despite the development of castration-resistant disease.

### Uterine Fibroids and Endometriosis [TGA]
- **Standard Dose:** One tablet (40/1/0.5 mg) once daily.
- **Initiation:** Pregnancy must be ruled out. The first tablet should be taken within 5 days of the onset of menstrual bleeding. If initiated on another day, irregular or heavy bleeding may occur initially.
- **Duration of Use:** Recommended to be limited to 24 months. Extension of therapy is conditional on Bone Mineral Density (BMD) stability and reassessment of risk/benefit.
- **Contraceptive Properties:** Provides adequate contraception after one month of use. Any prior hormonal contraception must be stopped before initiation. Non-hormonal contraception is required for the first month of treatment.

### Missed Doses
- **Prostate Cancer (120 mg):** Take as soon as remembered. If missed by >12 hours, skip the dose and resume at the next scheduled time [FDA].
- **Fibroids/Endometriosis (40 mg combination):** Take as soon as possible, then continue the next day at the usual time. If missed for 2 or more consecutive days, use non-hormonal contraception for the next 7 days [TGA].

### Interruption of Therapy
- If **relugolix** 120 mg therapy is interrupted for >7 days, restart with a 360 mg loading dose [FDA].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known severe hypersensitivity to **relugolix** or any excipients [TGA, FDA].
- **Jurisdiction-Specific (Combination Therapy [TGA]):**
  - Venous thromboembolic disorder (past or present).
  - Arterial thromboembolic cardiovascular disease (past or present).
  - Known thrombophilic disorders (e.g., Protein C/S deficiency, Factor V Leiden).
  - Known osteoporosis.
  - Migraine with aura or headaches with focal neurological symptoms.
  - Known or suspected sex steroid-influenced malignancies (e.g., breast, genital organs).
  - Presence or history of liver tumours or severe hepatic disease (until liver function normalises).
  - Pregnancy (known or suspected) and breastfeeding.
  - Genital bleeding of unknown aetiology.
  - Concomitant use of hormonal contraceptives.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Cases of angioedema (including pharyngeal oedema) have been reported. Discontinue immediately for severe reactions [TGA, FDA].
- **QT/QTc Interval Prolongation:** Androgen deprivation therapy (ADT) may prolong the QT interval. Consider risk/benefit in patients with congenital long QT, electrolyte abnormalities, or congestive heart failure. Periodic ECG monitoring is advised [FDA].
- **Embryo-Fetal Toxicity:** Can cause fetal harm. Advise males with female partners of reproductive potential to use effective contraception during and for 2 weeks after treatment [FDA]. Women must use non-hormonal contraception for at least 1 month after treatment initiation [TGA].
- **Bone Mineral Density (BMD) Loss:** **Relugolix** combination therapy may cause bone loss. A DXA scan is recommended at baseline, at 52 weeks, and annually thereafter [TGA]. 
- **Thromboembolic Disorders:** Risk of ATE/VTE is increased with estrogen/progestogen products. Discontinue immediately if these occur [TGA].
- **Depression and Suicidal Ideation:** Associated with GnRH receptor antagonists. Evaluate women with a history of depression prior to initiation and monitor for mood changes [TGA].
- **Laboratory Testing Monitoring:** 
  - Periodic PSA monitoring is required to assess efficacy in prostate cancer; measure testosterone if PSA increases [FDA].
  - Liver tests: Asymptomatic ALT elevations (>3x ULN) occurred in <1% of patients [TGA]. Discontinue if jaundice develops.
- **Uterine Fibroid Prolapse/Expulsion:** Submucosal fibroids may prolapse through the cervix or be expelled, potentially worsening uterine bleeding [TGA].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Frequency by Indication

| System Organ Class | Advanced Prostate Cancer (≥10%) [FDA] | Uterine Fibroids / Endometriosis (Common ≥1% to <10%) [TGA] |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vascular** | Hot flush (54%) | Hot flush (8.3% - 11.7%) |
| **Gastrointestinal** | Diarrhea (12%), Constipation (12%) | Dyspepsia |
| **Nervous System** | Fatigue (26%) | Headache (Very common ≥10% in endometriosis) |
| **Musculoskeletal** | Musculoskeletal pain (30%) | Back pain, Arthralgia |
| **Reproductive** | Decreased libido | Uterine bleeding, Libido decreased, Breast cyst, Vulvovaginal dryness |
| **Skin/Subcutaneous** | - | Hyperhidrosis, Night sweats, Alopecia |
| **Laboratory Changes** | ↑ Glucose (44%), ↑ Triglycerides (35%), ↓ Hemoglobin (28%), ↑ ALT (27%), ↑ AST (18%) | - |

**Serious Adverse Effects:**
- **Prostate Cancer:** Myocardial infarction (0.8%), acute kidney injury (0.6%), arrhythmia (0.6%), and haemorrhage (0.6%) [FDA].
- **Gynaecological:** Uterine myoma expulsion, angioedema [TGA].

## 7. Drug Interactions
### Pharmacokinetic Interactions
- **P-glycoprotein (P-gp) Inhibitors:** **Relugolix** is a P-gp substrate. 
  - **Management:** Avoid co-administration. If unavoidable, take **relugolix** first and separate dosing by at least 6 hours [TGA, FDA]. 
  - **Prostate Cancer:** If unavoidable with oral P-gp inhibitors, monitor closely [FDA].
  - **Fibroids/Endometriosis:** Concomitant use with oral P-gp inhibitors is not recommended. Erythromycin increased **relugolix** AUC by 4.1-fold [TGA].
- **Combined P-gp and Strong CYP3A Inducers:** (e.g., rifampicin, carbamazepine, St. John's wort). 
  - **Management:** Avoid co-administration. 
  - **Prostate Cancer:** If unavoidable, increase **relugolix** dose to 240 mg daily [FDA].
  - **Fibroids/Endometriosis:** Co-administration is not recommended; relugolix AUC was reduced by 55% with rifampicin [TGA].
- **BCRP Inhibition:** **Relugolix** is an inhibitor of BCRP *in vitro*. Co-administration with rosuvastatin (BCRP substrate) decreased rosuvastatin exposure, but no dose adjustment is required [TGA].

### Pharmacodynamic Interactions
- **Hormonal Products:** Concomitant use of hormonal contraceptives is contraindicated with **Ryeqo** [TGA].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
  - **Australia:** Category D. Contraindicated [TGA]. 
  - **USA:** Based on animal data, can cause fetal harm and loss of pregnancy [FDA].
- **Breastfeeding:** 
  - **Australia:** Contraindicated during and for 2 weeks after stopping treatment [TGA].
  - **USA:** Not recommended; **relugolix** was detected in human milk (Relative Infant Dose 0.1% of maternal dose) [FDA].
- **Fertility:** **Relugolix** suppresses ovulation (reversible upon discontinuation). It may impair fertility in males [FDA].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy in patients <18 years have not been established [TGA, FDA].
- **Elderly Use:** No dose adjustment required. In prostate cancer trials, no overall differences in safety or efficacy were observed between patients ≥65 years and younger patients [FDA].
- **Renal Impairment:**
  - **Mild, Moderate, or Severe:** No dose adjustment required [TGA, FDA].
  - **End-stage renal disease/Haemodialysis:** Not evaluated [TGA, FDA].
- **Hepatic Impairment:**
  - **Mild or Moderate:** No dose adjustment required [TGA, FDA].
  - **Severe:** Contraindicated [TGA]; not evaluated [FDA].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** **Relugolix** competitively binds to pituitary GnRH receptors, reducing the release of LH and FSH. This leads to a dose-dependent decrease in testosterone in men [FDA] and estrogen/progesterone in women [TGA].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
  - **Absorption:** Rapid; absolute bioavailability ~12% (11.6%). Median $T_{max}$ ~2.25 hours. High-fat/high-calorie meals decrease **relugolix** AUC by ~38-55% [TGA, FDA].
  - **Distribution:** Plasma protein binding 68–71%. Blood-to-plasma ratio 0.78.
  - **Metabolism:** Primarily via CYP3A (45%) and CYP2C8 (37%).
  - **Elimination:** Effective half-life ~25 hours; terminal half-life ~61 hours. Excretion is primarily faecal (81%, with 4.2% as unchanged drug) [FDA].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Advanced Prostate Cancer (HERO Study):** A randomised trial (N=934) showed a medical castration rate (<50 ng/dL) of 96.7% for **relugolix** versus 88.8% for leuprolide through 48 weeks. Castration was achieved as early as Day 4 in 56% of patients [FDA].
- **Uterine Fibroids (Studies L1 and L2):** 73.4% and 71.2% of women treated with **Ryeqo** achieved a significant reduction in menstrual blood loss compared to placebo (18.9% and 14.7%). Reductions were seen by Week 4 [TGA].
- **Endometriosis (Studies S1 and S2):** Significant responder rates for dysmenorrhoea (74.5–75.2% vs 26.9–30.4% for placebo) and non-menstrual pelvic pain were demonstrated over 24 weeks [TGA].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- Store below 30°C (86°F) [TGA, FDA].
- Dispense in original container and keep bottle tightly closed [FDA].
- For **relugolix** 120 mg, the bottle contains a desiccant; do not remove [FDA].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Australia:** Prescription Only Medicine (Schedule 4) [TGA].
- **United States:** Prescription Only (Rx-only) [FDA].
