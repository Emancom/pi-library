# Editorial Product Information: Retifanlimab

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesises information from the following jurisdictions: United States (FDA). [Note: Only the US Product Information was provided for this synthesis.]
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of **retifanlimab** for healthcare professionals, integrating regulatory data into a single clinical reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not replace the official Product Information required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult the current local PI before prescribing.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Retifanlimab-dlwr
- **Chemical Class:** Programmed death receptor-1 (PD-1) blocking antibody; humanized IgG4 kappa monoclonal antibody.
- **Brand Name:** **ZYNYZ** [US]
- **ATC Code:** L01FF09
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Injection: 500 mg/20 mL (25 mg/mL) solution in a single-dose vial.
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous infusion.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Incyte Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
**Retifanlimab** is indicated for the following adult populations:

- **Squamous Cell Carcinoma of the Anal Canal (SCAC):**
    - As first-line treatment in combination with carboplatin and paclitaxel for patients with inoperable locally recurrent or metastatic SCAC [US-approved].
    - As a monotherapy for patients with locally recurrent or metastatic SCAC who have experienced disease progression on, or are intolerant to, platinum-based chemotherapy [US-approved].
- **Merkel Cell Carcinoma (MCC):**
    - For the treatment of metastatic or recurrent locally advanced MCC [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults)
Retifanlimab is administered via intravenous infusion over 30 minutes.

- **SCAC (Combination or Monotherapy):** 500 mg every 4 weeks OR 375 mg every 3 weeks [US].
- **MCC:** 500 mg every 4 weeks [US].

### Duration of Treatment
- **SCAC (Combination Therapy):** Continue until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or for a maximum of 12 months [US].
- **SCAC (Monotherapy) and MCC:** Continue until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or for a maximum of 24 months [US].

### Administration and Preparation
- **Dilution:** Must be diluted prior to use. Use 0.9% Sodium Chloride or 5% Dextrose Injection to a final concentration between 1.4 mg/mL and 10 mg/mL.
- **Incompatibility:** Do not co-administer other drugs through the same infusion line. Do not administer as an IV push or bolus.
- **Filter Requirements:** Use a dedicated line with a sterile, non-pyrogenic, low-protein binding in-line or add-on filter (0.2 to 5 micron, or 15 micron mesh).

### Dose Modifications
No dose reductions are recommended. Management of adverse reactions requires withholding or permanent discontinuation based on the severity (Grade) of the reaction. For most Grade 3 immune-mediated reactions, withhold the dose; for Grade 4, permanently discontinue.

## 4. Contraindications
- None specified in available [US] sources.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Immune-Mediated Adverse Reactions
Retifanlimab can cause severe or fatal immune-mediated reactions in any organ system. These may occur during or after treatment discontinuation.
- **Pneumonitis:** Monitor for new or worsening respiratory symptoms.
- **Colitis:** Monitor for diarrhea and abdominal pain. CMV infection/reactivation has occurred in corticosteroid-refractory cases.
- **Hepatitis:** Monitor for changes in liver function.
- **Endocrinopathies:** Includes adrenal insufficiency, hypophysitis, thyroid disorders (hypothyroidism/hyperthyroidism), and Type 1 Diabetes Mellitus.
- **Nephritis:** Monitor for changes in renal function.
- **Dermatologic Reactions:** Can range from mild rash to Stevens-Johnson Syndrome (SJS) and Toxic Epidermal Necrolysis (TEN).

### Infusion-Related Reactions
Severe reactions have been reported. Monitor patients for chills, wheezing, or flushing. Interrupt or slow the rate for Grade 1–2; permanently discontinue for Grade 3–4.

### Allogeneic Hematopoietic Stem Cell Transplantation (HSCT) Complications
Fatal and serious complications (e.g., hyperacute GVHD) can occur in patients receiving allogeneic HSCT before or after PD-1/PD-L1 blocking therapy.

### Embryo-Fetal Toxicity
Based on its mechanism of action, retifanlimab can cause fetal harm. Verify pregnancy status in females of reproductive potential prior to initiation.

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effect profiles vary by regimen and indication:

### Very Common (>10%)
- **SCAC (Combination with Chemo):** Fatigue, peripheral neuropathy, nausea, alopecia, diarrhea, musculoskeletal pain, constipation, hemorrhage, rash, vomiting, decreased appetite, pruritus, abdominal pain, and hypothyroidism [US].
- **SCAC (Monotherapy):** Fatigue, musculoskeletal pain, diarrhea, non-urinary tract infections, perineal pain, hemorrhage, urinary tract infection, rash, nausea, decreased appetite, and headache [US].
- **MCC:** Musculoskeletal pain, fatigue, pruritus, diarrhea, rash, pyrexia, nausea, and constipation [US].

### Serious Adverse Effects
- **SCAC (Combination):** Sepsis (3.2%), pulmonary embolism (3.2%), and diarrhea (2.6%) [US].
- **MCC:** Fatigue, arrhythmia, and pneumonitis (≥2%) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources. Formal drug interaction studies have not been conducted.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** There are no available data on use in pregnant women. Animal models suggest that PD-1/PD-L1 blockade disrupts fetal tolerance and increases the risk of fetal loss. [US: Pregnancy Category not assigned; warning of fetal harm]. 
- **Contraception:** Females of reproductive potential should use effective contraception during treatment and for **4 months** after the final dose [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if retifanlimab is excreted in human milk. Due to the potential for serious adverse reactions in the breastfed child, advise against breastfeeding during treatment and for **4 months** after the final dose [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established in patients under 18 years of age [US].
- **Geriatric Use:** In clinical trials, no overall differences in safety or efficacy were observed between patients ≥65 years and younger patients. However, high proportions of trial participants were elderly (up to 76% in MCC trials) [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** No clinically meaningful effect on pharmacokinetics was observed in patients with mild or moderate renal impairment (eGFR ≥ 26 mL/min/1.73 m²). Data are unavailable for severe impairment [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No clinically meaningful effect on pharmacokinetics was observed in patients with mild hepatic impairment. Retifanlimab has not been studied in patients with moderate or severe hepatic impairment [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Retifanlimab-dlwr is a humanized monoclonal antibody that binds to the PD-1 receptor. It blocks the interaction between PD-1 and its ligands (PD-L1 and PD-L2), thereby releasing PD-1 pathway-mediated inhibition of the immune response, including anti-tumor immune responses.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Steady-state volume of distribution is approximately 6.0 L.
    - **Elimination:** Terminal half-life is 19 days at steady state. 
    - **Clearance:** Steady-state clearance is approximately 0.23 L/day. Clearance decreases by approximately 23% from the first dose to steady state.
- **Immunogenicity:** The incidence of treatment-emergent anti-drug antibodies (ADAs) was 0% in SCAC trials and 2.8% in MCC trials [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **POD1UM-303 (SCAC Combination):** A randomized, double-blind trial (N=308) comparing retifanlimab + chemo vs. placebo + chemo. Retifanlimab significantly improved progression-free survival (9.3 vs 7.4 months; HR 0.63) [US].
- **POD1UM-202 (SCAC Monotherapy):** An open-label, single-arm trial (N=94) in platinum-refractory patients. The objective response rate (ORR) was 14%, with a median duration of response (DOR) of 9.5 months [US].
- **POD1UM-201 (MCC):** An open-label, single-arm trial (N=101) in systemic therapy-naïve patients. The ORR was 55%, with 69% of responders maintaining response for at least 12 months [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vials:** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) in the original carton to protect from light. Do not freeze or shake.
- **Diluted Solution:** 
    - Store at room temperature (up to 25°C) for no more than 8 hours total (including infusion time).
    - Store under refrigeration (2°C to 8°C) for no more than 24 hours. If refrigerated, allow the solution to reach room temperature before administration; the total time out of the refrigerator must not exceed 4 hours [US].
