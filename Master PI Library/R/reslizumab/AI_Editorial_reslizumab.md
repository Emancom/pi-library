# Editorial Product Information: Reslizumab

## Editorial Note
- The following clinical editorial was developed using Product Information (PI) documentation from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonized editorial overview of reslizumab for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesizes regulatory information into a single, clinically focused reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not replace the official Product Information required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority’s approved labeling for definitive guidance.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Reslizumab (a humanized interleukin-5 antagonist monoclonal antibody, IgG4 kappa).
- **Brand Names:** **CINQAIR** [US].
- **ATC Code:** R03DX08 (Not specified in available source, but standard for class).
- **Dosage Form and Strength:**
  - **Injection:** 100 mg/10 mL (10 mg/mL) solution in a single-use vial [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Teva Respiratory, LLC [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Severe Asthma:** Indicated for the add-on maintenance treatment of patients with severe asthma, aged 18 years and older, who exhibit an eosinophilic phenotype [US].
- **Limitations of Use:**
  - Not indicated for the treatment of other eosinophilic conditions [US].
  - Not indicated for the relief of acute bronchospasm or status asthmaticus [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** The recommended dosage is 3 mg/kg administered once every 4 weeks [US].
- **Administration Instructions:**
  - Administer only as an intravenous infusion over 20 to 50 minutes. Do **not** administer as a bolus or push [US].
  - Must be administered by a healthcare professional prepared to manage anaphylaxis.
  - **Preparation:**
    1. Withdraw the appropriate volume from the vial based on patient weight.
    2. Dilute in an infusion bag containing 50 mL of 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection.
    3. Gently invert the bag to mix; do **not** shake.
    4. Use an infusion set with an in-line, low protein-binding filter (pore size 0.2 micron) [US].
  - **Compatibility:** Compatible with polyvinylchloride (PVC) or polyolefin bags [US].
- **Storage After Dilution:** If not used immediately, store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) or at room temperature up to 25ºC (77ºF) for up to 16 hours [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to **reslizumab** or any of its excipients [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning: Anaphylaxis [US]:**
  - Anaphylaxis was observed in 0.3% of patients in placebo-controlled clinical trials.
  - Reactions can be life-threatening and have occurred as early as the second dose.
  - Patients must be observed for an appropriate period after administration.
  - If anaphylaxis occurs, discontinue **reslizumab** immediately and permanently [US].
- **Malignancy:** Malignant neoplasms were reported in 0.6% of patients receiving **reslizumab** 3 mg/kg compared to 0.3% in the placebo group. Malignancies were diverse and typically diagnosed within six months of exposure [US].
- **Acute Asthma Symptoms:** Do not use to treat acute exacerbations, acute bronchospasm, or status asthmaticus [US].
- **Corticosteroid Reduction:** Do not discontinue systemic or inhaled corticosteroids abruptly upon starting **reslizumab**. Reductions should be gradual and supervised by a physician [US].
- **Parasitic (Helminth) Infection:** Eosinophils are involved in the immune response to some helminth infections. Treat pre-existing infections before starting therapy. If a patient becomes infected during treatment and does not respond to anti-helminthics, discontinue **reslizumab** until the infection resolves [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Common (1–10%):**
  - Oropharyngeal pain (2.6% vs 2.2% placebo) [US].
- **Other Clinical Effects:**
  - **Creatine Phosphokinase (CPK) Elevations:** Transient elevations were more frequent with **reslizumab** (20%) than placebo (18%). Asymptomatic elevations >10 x ULN occurred in 0.8% of patients [US].
  - **Myalgia:** Reported in 1% of patients [US].
  - **Musculoskeletal Reactions:** 2.2% reported various musculoskeletal pains (neck, chest, spasms) on the day of infusion [US].
- **Immunogenicity:** 5.4% of patients developed treatment-emergent anti-reslizumab antibodies (low titer, often transient) with no detectable impact on efficacy or safety [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Formal Studies:** No formal clinical drug interaction studies have been conducted [US].
- **Pharmacokinetic Observations:** Concomitant use of corticosteroids or leukotriene antagonists does not appear to affect **reslizumab** pharmacokinetics [US].
- **In vitro data:** **Reslizumab** is unlikely to affect CYP1A2, 2B6, or 3A4 enzyme activity [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:**
  - **US Category:** Not explicitly letter-coded in modern labeling, but noted that data are insufficient to inform drug-associated risk.
  - **Clinical Considerations:** Monoclonal antibodies are transported across the placenta, particularly in the second and third trimesters. Poorly controlled asthma in pregnancy increases risks of preeclampsia, prematurity, and low birth weight [US].
  - **Animal Data:** No evidence of embryo-fetal harm in mice (6x MRHD) or rabbits (17x MRHD) [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if **reslizumab** is present in human milk. Human IgG is known to be excreted in human milk. The benefits of breastfeeding should be weighed against the clinical need for the drug [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies in mice showed no effect on male or female fertility [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Not indicated for patients under 18 years of age. In clinical trials of adolescents (12–17 years), the asthma exacerbation rate was higher in the **reslizumab** group than the placebo group [US].
- **Elderly Use:** No overall differences in safety or efficacy were observed in patients aged 65 years and older. No dose adjustment is required [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Population PK analysis suggests no significant difference in clearance between patients with normal renal function and those with mild to moderate impairment [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No formal studies conducted. Population PK analysis suggests no significant difference between normal liver function and mild impairment [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** **Reslizumab** is an IL-5 antagonist. IL-5 is the primary cytokine for eosinophil growth, recruitment, and activation. By binding to IL-5, **reslizumab** blocks its interaction with the IL-5 receptor alpha chain on the eosinophil surface, reducing eosinophil production and survival [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Treatment leads to a significant reduction in blood eosinophil counts (approximately 92% reduction from baseline at 3 mg/kg) [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
  - **Distribution:** Volume of distribution is approximately 5 L.
  - **Metabolism:** Degraded by enzymatic proteolysis into small peptides and amino acids.
  - **Elimination:** Clearance is approximately 7 mL/hour; half-life is approximately 24 days [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pivotal Studies (Studies I–IV):** Four randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trials (16–52 weeks) in 981 patients [US].
- **Efficacy Results:** 
  - **Exacerbations:** In 52-week studies, patients receiving **reslizumab** 3 mg/kg showed a 50% to 59% reduction in the rate of asthma exacerbations compared to placebo [US].
  - **Lung Function:** Significant improvements in FEV1 (ranging from 93 mL to 160 mL increase over placebo) were observed as early as 4 weeks and maintained through 52 weeks [US].
  - **Eosinophil Threshold:** Efficacy was not observed in patients with baseline blood eosinophil counts <400/mcL [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vials:** Refrigerate at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F).
- **Protection:** Keep vials in the original carton to protect from light.
- **Handling:** Do not freeze. Do not shake [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Prescription only (Rx-only). Contains a Boxed Warning regarding anaphylaxis [US].
