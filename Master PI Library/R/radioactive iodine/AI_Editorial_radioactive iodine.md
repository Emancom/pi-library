# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical data into a structured format.
- **Note:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. At the time of writing, only the United States PI was provided for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN/USP); chemical name: (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Brand Name:** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** For the acute and chronic management of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg. This may be administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion [US].
- **Loading Doses:** In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose is often administered, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours. Dosing frequency should be every 3 to 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours [US].
- **ESRD on Hemodialysis:** The recommended starting dose is 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight). This should be administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration.
- The solution is compatible and stable for up to 24 hours in PVC bags at room temperature (25°C) when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations ranging from 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL [US].

### Titration and Monitoring
- **Metabolic Disorders:** Subsequent daily doses should be adjusted based on clinical response, with the highest recorded dose being 300 mg/kg [US].
- **Monitoring:** It is recommended to obtain plasma carnitine concentrations prior to therapy. Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma carnitine (target free carnitine level: 35 to 60 µmol/L) is advised [US].
- **ESRD Dose Adjustment:** Adjustments should be guided by pre-dialysis (trough) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy if levels are adequate [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known:** There are no listed contraindications for levocarnitine injection in the provided source material [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### General Warnings
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. These may occur within minutes of administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue the drug and initiate medical treatment [US].

### General Precautions
- **Seizures:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a known history, an increase in seizure frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Oral Formulation Note:** The safety and efficacy of **oral** levocarnitine have not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency. Chronic high doses of oral levocarnitine in ESRD patients may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [US].

### Monitoring Requirements
- **Warfarin Interaction:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have occurred when levocarnitine is used with warfarin. Monitor INR levels after initiating treatment or following dose adjustments [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse events observed in clinical trials (ESRD patients on dialysis) with a frequency ≥5% (regardless of causality) include:

| Body System | Adverse Event |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Body as a Whole** | Abdominal pain, accidental injury, asthenia, chest pain, fever, flu syndrome, headache, infection, injection site reaction, pain |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension, hypotension, tachycardia, vascular disorder, palpitations |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea, vomiting, nausea, anorexia, constipation, dyspepsia |
| **Metabolic/Nutritional** | Hypercalcemia, hyperkalemia, hypervolemia, peripheral edema, weight changes |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness, insomnia, paresthesia, depression |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis, cough increase, dyspnea, rhinitis |

- **Other Reactions:** Transient nausea and vomiting, body odor, and gastritis have been observed. Seizures and hypersensitivity reactions (anaphylaxis) are noted in postmarketing reports [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Concomitant use may result in an increased INR. Management: Frequent monitoring of INR is required for patients on warfarin therapy [US].
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Levocarnitine is not protein-bound, suggesting a low potential for protein-binding displacement interactions [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Not assigned a letter category in this PI; however, reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm.] Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk carnitine after administration. Discontinuation of nursing or the drug should consider the risk of excess carnitine intake in the child versus the benefit to the mother [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility was observed in animal reproductive studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Use in paediatric patients is mentioned in the context of secondary carnitine deficiency and inborn errors of metabolism, but specific age-range dosage adjustments are not detailed separately from weight-based dosing (see Section 3) [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Primarily indicated for ESRD patients on dialysis (see Section 3 for dosing). Oral levocarnitine is cautioned against in severe renal impairment due to metabolite accumulation [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring carrier molecule required for the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane. This facilitates the delivery of substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours (uncorrected for endogenous levels). Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours; mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Bioequivalence:** In healthy volunteers, levocarnitine tablets were found to be bioequivalent to the oral solution [US].
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of ESRD patients, 20 mg/kg IV doses three times weekly significantly increased plasma carnitine levels from a pre-dialysis mean of 20 µmol/L to a trough mean of 190 µmol/L after 8 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Precautions:** Avoid excessive heat. Protect from freezing [US].
- **Shelf Life:** Discard any unused portion of a single-dose vial as the formulation contains no preservatives [US].
