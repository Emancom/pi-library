# Editorial Product Information: Resmetirom

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the Product Information (PI) document from the following jurisdiction: United States (Food and Drug Administration). [US]
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, consolidating regulatory information into a single reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory guidance for specific legal requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Resmetirom (INN/generic).
- **Chemical Class:** Thyroid hormone receptor-beta (THR-β) agonist.
- **Brand Name(s):** **REZDIFFRA** [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
  - Film-coated tablets: 60 mg, 80 mg, and 100 mg [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Madrigal Pharmaceuticals, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Nonalcoholic Steatohepatitis (NASH):** Indicated as an adjunct to diet and exercise for the treatment of adults with noncirrhotic NASH who have moderate to advanced liver fibrosis (consistent with stages F2 to F3 fibrosis). [US]
- **Regulatory Note:** This indication was granted under accelerated approval based on improvements in NASH and fibrosis. Continued approval may depend on clinical benefit verification in confirmatory trials. [US]
- **Limitations of Use:** Resmetirom should be avoided in patients with decompensated cirrhosis. [US]

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
The recommended daily dosage is determined by actual body weight:
- **Patients <100 kg:** 80 mg orally once daily. [US]
- **Patients ≥100 kg:** 100 mg orally once daily. [US]

### Administration Instructions
- Tablets must be swallowed whole; do not split, crush, or chew. [US]
- May be administered with or without food. [US]

### Dose Adjustments (Drug Interactions)
- **Moderate CYP2C8 Inhibitors (e.g., clopidogrel):**
  - <100 kg body weight: Reduce dose to 60 mg once daily. [US]
  - ≥100 kg body weight: Reduce dose to 80 mg once daily. [US]
- **Strong CYP2C8 Inhibitors (e.g., gemfibrozil):** Concomitant use is not recommended. [US]

### Missed Dose Instructions
If a dose is missed, the patient should skip that dose and resume the normal schedule the following day. Two doses should not be taken at the same time. [US]

## 4. Contraindications
- There are no contraindications listed in the available source. [US]

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hepatotoxicity:** Liver test elevations have been observed. Cases resembling drug-induced autoimmune-like hepatitis (DI-ALH) have occurred, resolving upon discontinuation. Clinicians should monitor liver tests at baseline and periodically during treatment. If hepatotoxicity is suspected, discontinue use. [US]
- **Gallbladder-Related Adverse Reactions:** Increased incidence of cholelithiasis, cholecystitis, and obstructive pancreatitis has been reported. Diagnostic follow-up is required if symptoms occur; treatment should be interrupted for acute gallbladder events. [US]
- **Statin Interactions:** Resmetirom increases exposure to several HMG-CoA reductase inhibitors, potentially increasing the risk of myopathy or rhabdomyolysis. Statin dose limits are required (see Section 7). [US]

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common Adverse Reactions
Reported at a frequency of ≥5% and higher than placebo:
- **Very Common (>10%):** Diarrhea, Nausea. [US]
- **Common (1–10%):** Pruritus, Vomiting, Constipation, Abdominal pain, Dizziness. [US]

### Serious Adverse Effects
- Hepatotoxicity (DI-ALH). [US]
- Acute cholecystitis. [US]

### Laboratory Abnormalities
- **Thyroid Function:** A mean decrease in free T4 (FT4) levels (approx. 13–17%) was observed without corresponding clinical findings or changes in TSH. [US]

## 7. Drug Interactions
### Pharmacokinetic Interactions
- **CYP2C8 Inhibitors:** Resmetirom is a CYP2C8 substrate. Strong inhibitors (e.g., gemfibrozil) significantly increase exposure and are not recommended. Moderate inhibitors (e.g., clopidogrel) require dose reduction of resmetirom. [US]
- **CYP2C8 Substrates:** Resmetirom is a weak CYP2C8 inhibitor. Monitor substrates (e.g., pioglitazone) for adverse reactions. [US]
- **OATP1B1/1B3 and BCRP Substrates:** Resmetirom increases plasma concentrations of drugs that are substrates of these transporters. [US]

### Management of Statin Interactions
To mitigate the risk of statin-related toxicity, the following daily dose limits apply when co-administered with resmetirom:
- **Rosuvastatin:** Max 20 mg/day. [US]
- **Simvastatin:** Max 20 mg/day. [US]
- **Atorvastatin:** Max 40 mg/day. [US]
- **Pravastatin:** Max 40 mg/day. [US]

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** There are no available human data. In animal studies, embryo-fetal toxicity (increased stillbirths and postnatal mortality) occurred in rats at 37 times the maximum recommended human dose, associated with maternal thyroid hormone suppression. [US]
- **Breastfeeding:** Information on the presence of resmetirom in human milk or its effects on the breastfed infant is unavailable. Consider the maternal clinical need and potential infant risks. [US]
- **Fertility:** No effects on fertility or reproductive function were observed in animal models. [US]

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness in patients under 18 years of age have not been established. [US]
- **Elderly Use:** No overall differences in efficacy were noted between patients ≥65 years and younger adults, though a numerically higher incidence of adverse reactions was reported in the elderly. [US]
- **Renal Impairment:** No dosage adjustment is required for patients with mild, moderate, or severe renal impairment. [US]
- **Hepatic Impairment:**
  - **Mild (Child-Pugh A):** No dosage adjustment required. [US]
  - **Moderate to Severe (Child-Pugh B or C):** Avoid use; exposure is significantly increased (up to 19-fold AUC increase in Child-Pugh C), which may increase the risk of adverse reactions. [US]

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Resmetirom is a partial agonist of the thyroid hormone receptor-beta (THR-β). By selectively targeting the β-isoform (the primary THR in the liver), it reduces intrahepatic triglycerides and improves NASH-related pathology while minimizing systemic effects mediated by THR-α (found in heart and bone). [US]
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
  - **Absorption:** Median Tmax is approx. 4 hours. Steady state is reached in 3 to 6 days. [US]
  - **Distribution:** >99% protein-bound; Vd is approx. 68 L. [US]
  - **Metabolism:** Primarily metabolized via CYP2C8. The major metabolite (MGL-3623) is 28-fold less potent than the parent drug. [US]
  - **Elimination:** Half-life is approx. 4.5 hours. Excretion is 67% via feces (mostly metabolites) and 24% via urine. [US]

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Trial 1 (MAESTRO-NASH):** A 54-month, randomized, double-blind study (NCT03900429) in 888 patients with F2-F3 NASH. 
- **Primary Outcomes (12 months):** 
  - **NASH Resolution:** Significant improvement in resmetirom groups (26–36%) compared to placebo (9–13%). 
  - **Fibrosis Improvement:** At least one stage improvement without worsening of NASH was significant for both 80 mg and 100 mg doses vs. placebo. [US]

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F). [US]
- **Excursions:** Permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F to 86°F). [US]
- **Packaging:** Dispensed in high-density polyethylene bottles with child-resistant closures. [US]

- **Legal Classification:** Prescription Only (Rx-only). [US]
