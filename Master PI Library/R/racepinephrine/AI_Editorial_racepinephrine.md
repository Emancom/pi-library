# Editorial Product Information: Racepinephrine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. 
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific Product Information required for prescribing or dispensing in a specific region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Racepinephrine hydrochloride (a sympathomimetic amine/bronchodilator).
- **Brand Names:** NebuClear MD [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources (typically classified under R03AA03).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Inhalation solution, USP 2.25% [US].
    - Each 0.5 mL sterile unit-of-use vial contains 11.25 mg racepinephrine (equivalent to 13.5 mg racepinephrine HCl) [US].
    - This concentration is equivalent to 1% epinephrine [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Respiratory (Inhalation) via nebulizer.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Blue Echo Care LLC [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Intermittent Asthma:** For the temporary relief of mild symptoms associated with intermittent asthma, including wheezing, chest tightness, and shortness of breath [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Children $\ge$ 4 years)
- **Standard Dose:** 1 to 3 inhalations administered via nebulizer [US].
- **Frequency:** Not more often than every 3 hours [US].
- **Maximum Daily Dose:** Do not exceed 12 inhalations in a 24-hour period [US].

### Administration Instructions
- For use specifically with a **jet or ultrasonic nebulizer** [US].
- The contents of one single-use vial (0.5 mL) should be added to the nebulizer reservoir [US].
- Pediatric use (ages 4–12) must be supervised by an adult [US].

### Missed Dose Instructions
- Not specified in available sources; however, as this is for the "temporary relief" of "intermittent" symptoms, it is used on an as-needed (PRN) basis within the frequency limits stated above [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **MAOI Therapy:** Do not use if currently taking a prescription monoamine oxidase inhibitor (MAOI) or for 2 weeks after discontinuing the MAOI [US].
- **Undiagnosed Asthma:** Do not use unless a formal diagnosis of asthma has been made by a physician [US].
- **Product Degradation:** Do not use if the solution is brown, cloudy, pinkish, or darker than slightly yellow, or if a precipitate is present [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warnings
- None specified in available sources.

### General Warnings and Precautions
- **Asthma Alert:** Patients should seek immediate medical attention if symptoms do not improve within 20 minutes, if the condition worsens, if more than 12 inhalations are required in 24 hours, or if they experience more than 2 asthma attacks per week [US].
- **Cardiovascular Risk:** This product may increase blood pressure and heart rate. Increased risk of heart attack or stroke is noted, particularly in patients with a history of hypertension or heart disease, or if the product is used in excess of the recommended dose [US].
- **Stimulant Avoidance:** Patients should avoid caffeine-containing foods/beverages and dietary supplements with stimulant effects while using this product [US].

### Specific Clinical Precautions
- Consultation with a physician is required prior to use in patients with:
    - History of hospitalization for asthma [US].
    - Heart disease or high blood pressure [US].
    - Diabetes or thyroid disease [US].
    - Seizures [US].
    - Narrow-angle glaucoma [US].
    - Psychiatric or emotional conditions [US].
    - Enlarged prostate causing urinary difficulty [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Frequency of adverse effects is not quantified in the provided source. However, the following effects are identified as reasons to discontinue use:
- **Cardiovascular:** Rapid heartbeat, palpitations, increased blood pressure [US].
- **Neurological/Psychiatric:** Tremors, nervousness, seizure, difficulty sleeping [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
### Pharmacodynamic Interactions
- **MAOIs:** Potential for severe hypertensive crisis; contraindicated [US].
- **Sympathomimetics:** Use caution when taking other drugs containing phenylephrine, pseudoephedrine, or ephedrine (commonly found in allergy or cough/cold medications) due to additive cardiovascular effects [US].
- **Caffeine:** Additive stimulant effects [US].

### Clinically Significant Interactions
- **Weight Control/Obesity Drugs:** Professional consultation required prior to concurrent use [US].
- **Psychiatric Medications:** Professional consultation required prior to use with drugs for depression or emotional conditions [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy/Breastfeeding:** There are no specific pregnancy categories listed in the source, but the label mandates that individuals who are pregnant or breastfeeding must consult a healthcare professional before use [US].
- **Fertility:** Not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** 
    - Children $\ge$ 4 years: Same dosing as adults; must be supervised [US].
    - Children < 4 years: Consultation with a physician is required before use [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically delineated, though the general warnings regarding hypertension and heart disease are particularly relevant to this population [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Racepinephrine is a sympathomimetic bronchodilator. It acts on adrenergic receptors to relax bronchial smooth muscle, thereby relieving bronchospasm associated with asthma [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Rapid onset for the relief of shortness of breath and wheezing [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- Not specified in available sources.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store between 2°C and 25°C (36°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from light and excessive heat. Protect from freezing [US].
- **Integrity:** Vials are supplied in individual pouches; do not use if the pouch is damaged [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as a **Human OTC Drug** (Over-the-Counter) under the OTC Monograph Drug M012 [US].
