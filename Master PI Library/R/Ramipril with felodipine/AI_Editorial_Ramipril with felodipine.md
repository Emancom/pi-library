# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (US - FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised clinical overview for healthcare professionals. 
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. At the time of compilation, only the US Product Information was available for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN/USP), a carrier molecule for long-chain fatty acid transport.
- **Chemical Name:** (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection, USP (Baxter Healthcare Corporation) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Sterile injection solution: 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency in Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Indicated for the acute and chronic management of patients with metabolic errors resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US-approved].
- **Carnitine Deficiency in End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    - The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg.
    - Administration should be via slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion.
    - In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose is often followed by equivalent doses every 3 or 4 hours (and never less frequently than every 6 hours) over the first 24 hours.
    - Maintenance daily doses are typically 50 mg/kg, though higher doses up to 300 mg/kg have been administered [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - The recommended starting dose is 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight.
    - Administer as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line after each dialysis session [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration.
- **Compatibility:** Stable and compatible when mixed in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations between 0.5 mg/mL and 8 mg/mL. Mixed solutions are stable for up to 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags [US].

### Titration and Monitoring
- In ESRD, initiation may be prompted by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine levels <40 µmol/L to 50 µmol/L.
- Dose adjustments should be guided by trough plasma levels. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be considered as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].
- For metabolic disorders, weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine levels (target: 35–60 µmol/L) is recommended [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known** [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported. These reactions may occur within minutes of intravenous administration, particularly in ESRD patients on dialysis. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In patients with a known history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed following levocarnitine administration [US].
- **General Safety (Renal Impairment):** The safety of *oral* levocarnitine has not been established in patients with renal insufficiency. High-dose oral administration in these patients may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]), which are normally excreted in urine [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects observed in clinical trials of ESRD patients on hemodialysis (incidence ≥5% regardless of causality) include:

| Body System | Adverse Event (Incidence at 10–40 mg/kg) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Chest pain (12%), Headache (22%), Infection (15%), Injection site reaction (33%), Pain (30%), Fever (7%), Abdominal pain (9%) |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension (20%), Hypotension (14%), Tachycardia (6%) |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea (16%), Vomiting (15%), Nausea (8%) |
| **Metabolic** | Hypercalcemia (9%), Hyperkalemia (6%), Peripheral edema (5%) |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis (23%), Cough increase (9%), Dyspnea (7%), Rhinitis (9%) |
| **Neurological** | Dizziness (13%), Paresthesia (5%), Insomnia (4%) |

- **Other Reactions:** Transient nausea and vomiting. Less frequent effects include body odor and gastritis [US].
- **Serious Post-marketing Effects:** Anaphylaxis and seizures [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have occurred when levocarnitine is used concomitantly with warfarin. **Clinical Management:** Monitor INR levels at the start of treatment or following dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not specified by letter, but reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at doses up to 3.8 times the human dose revealed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** 
    - Levocarnitine is a normal component of human milk. Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine concentrations in milk after exogenous administration. Weigh the benefits of maternal supplementation against potential risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing and administration instructions for metabolic disorders are applicable to paediatric patients; however, specific age-range trial data is not detailed in the source [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Used specifically for ESRD on dialysis. Note the risk of metabolite accumulation with high-dose *oral* therapy [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production [US].

### Pharmacokinetics
- **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
- **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
- **Elimination:** Following IV bolus (20 mg/kg), the mean apparent terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours. Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours [US].
- **Bioavailability:** Absolute bioavailability of oral formulations is approximately 15–16% [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Dosing Study:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients receiving 20 mg/kg IV three times weekly after dialysis showed that trough levocarnitine concentrations increased from a baseline of ~21 µmol/L to ~190 µmol/L after 8 weeks.
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** Studies using 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses confirmed dose-dependent increases in trough carnitine concentrations at 12 and 24 weeks. While carnitine levels increased, the clinical outcomes related to supplemental carnitine in this population were not determined in these trials [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Conditions:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat.
- **Handling:** Discard any unused portion of an opened vial; the formulation contains no preservatives [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified for other jurisdictions.
