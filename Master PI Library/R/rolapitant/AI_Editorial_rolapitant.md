# Editorial Product Information: Rolapitant

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA)
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of rolapitant, synthesised for healthcare professionals. It serves as a clinically accurate reference regarding the drug's regulatory status, pharmacology, and therapeutic application.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory documents for specific legal and prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Rolapitant hydrochloride (Substance P/neurokinin 1 (NK1) receptor antagonist).
- **Brand Names:** **Varubi** [US].
- **ATC Code:** A04AD14.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - **Oral Tablet:** 90 mg rolapitant (equivalent to 100 mg rolapitant hydrochloride) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** TerSera Therapeutics LLC [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Prevention of Chemotherapy-Induced Nausea and Vomiting (CINV):** Indicated for use in adults, in combination with other antiemetic agents, to prevent delayed nausea and vomiting associated with initial and repeat courses of emetogenic cancer chemotherapy. This includes, but is not limited to, highly emetogenic chemotherapy (HEC) [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **Standard Adult Dose:** 180 mg (administered as two 90 mg tablets) [US].
- **Timing:** Administer as a single dose within 2 hours prior to the initiation of chemotherapy on Day 1 [US].
- **Frequency:** May be given before each chemotherapy cycle, provided the interval between doses is at least 2 weeks [US].
- **Combination Therapy:** 
    - Must be administered in conjunction with a 5-HT₃ receptor antagonist and dexamethasone.
    - **Dexamethasone Dosing:** Unlike some other NK1 antagonists, rolapitant does not require a dose adjustment for dexamethasone. A 20 mg oral dose is recommended on Day 1, followed by 8 mg twice daily on Days 2–4 for HEC [US].
- **Administration Instructions:** Tablets may be taken with or without food [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **CYP2D6 Substrates with Narrow Therapeutic Index:** Concomitant use with thioridazine or pimozide is contraindicated due to the risk of significantly increased plasma concentrations, which may lead to QT prolongation and Torsades de Pointes [US].
- **Pediatric Population:** Contraindicated in patients under 2 years of age due to evidence from juvenile animal studies showing irreversible impairment of sexual development and fertility [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **CYP2D6 Interactions:** Rolapitant is a moderate inhibitor of CYP2D6. Because it has a long half-life, the inhibitory effect is persistent; plasma concentrations of CYP2D6 substrates may remain elevated for at least 28 days post-dose [US].
- **Monitoring Requirements:** In patients taking other CYP2D6 substrates (not contraindicated), clinicians should monitor for increased adverse reactions related to the substrate [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Use should be avoided in patients with severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh Class C). If use is unavoidable, close monitoring for adverse effects is required [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions reported in clinical trials occurred in approximately 7% of patients receiving the rolapitant regimen compared to 6% in the control group.

| Frequency | Highly Emetogenic Chemotherapy (HEC) | Moderately Emetogenic (MEC) / AC Regimens |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Neutropenia (9%), Hiccups (5%), Abdominal pain (3%) | Decreased appetite (9%), Neutropenia (7%), Dizziness (6%), Dyspepsia (4%), UTI (4%), Stomatitis (4%), Anemia (3%) |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Significant neutropenia and potential for cardiac arrhythmias if co-administered with contraindicated CYP2D6 substrates [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
### Pharmacokinetic Interactions
- **CYP2D6 Inhibition:** Rolapitant increases the AUC of CYP2D6 substrates (e.g., dextromethorphan) by approximately 3-fold. This effect persists for at least 28 days [US].
- **BCRP Inhibition:** Rolapitant is an inhibitor of Breast Cancer Resistance Protein (BCRP). It can increase concentrations of substrates such as methotrexate, topotecan, or irinotecan. Use the lowest effective dose of rosuvastatin when co-administered [US].
- **P-gp Inhibition:** Rolapitant inhibits P-glycoprotein. Monitor concentrations of substrates with a narrow therapeutic index, such as digoxin [US].
- **CYP3A4 Induction:** Strong inducers (e.g., rifampin) significantly reduce rolapitant concentrations (AUC decreased by ~85%), potentially reducing efficacy. Avoid chronic use of strong CYP3A4 inducers [US].
- **CYP3A4 Inhibition:** Strong inhibitors (e.g., ketoconazole) do not cause clinically significant changes in rolapitant exposure [US].

### Pharmacodynamic Interactions
- **Warfarin:** While S-warfarin exposure is not significantly altered, clinicians should monitor the International Normalised Ratio (INR) and prothrombin time, adjusting warfarin as needed [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not assigned under current labeling rules, but data in pregnant women are insufficient to inform drug-associated risks. Animal studies showed no developmental effects at doses 1.2 to 2.9 times the recommended human dose [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** There are no data on the presence of the drug in human milk. Because the drug was present in the milk of lactating rats, the developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding should be weighed against the clinical need for the drug and potential risks to the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** Based on animal data, rolapitant may impair fertility in females of reproductive potential. This effect was shown to be reversible in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy not established. Use is contraindicated in those <2 years of age [US].
- **Elderly Use:** No overall differences in safety or efficacy observed in patients ≥65 years; however, greater sensitivity in some older individuals cannot be ruled out [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment for mild-to-moderate impairment. Data are insufficient for severe renal impairment or end-stage renal disease [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No adjustment for mild (Class A) or moderate (Class B) impairment. Avoid use in severe (Class C) impairment [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Rolapitant is a highly selective and competitive antagonist at human substance P/neurokinin 1 (NK1) receptors. It crosses the blood-brain barrier to inhibit NK1 receptors in the striatum [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Peak plasma concentration (Cmax) reached in ~4 hours. High-fat meals do not significantly alter absorption [US].
    - **Distribution:** Highly protein bound (99.8%); large volume of distribution (460 L) indicates extensive tissue distribution [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily metabolised by CYP3A4 to an active metabolite (M19) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Terminal half-life is approximately 7 days (169–183 hours). Primarily eliminated via the hepato-biliary route [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **HEC Studies (Study 1 & 2):** In multicenter, double-blind trials, rolapitant combined with granisetron and dexamethasone showed a significantly higher "Complete Response" rate (no emesis and no rescue medication) in the delayed phase (25–120 hours) compared to control (72.7% vs 58.4% in Study 1; 70.1% vs 61.9% in Study 2) [US].
- **MEC/AC Study (Study 3):** Rolapitant demonstrated superiority in the delayed phase for patients receiving moderately emetogenic chemotherapy or anthracycline/cyclophosphamide combinations (71.3% vs 61.6%) [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F). Brief excursions are permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F and 86°F) [US].
- **Packaging:** Dispense in the original child-resistant wallet to protect the blister set [US].
