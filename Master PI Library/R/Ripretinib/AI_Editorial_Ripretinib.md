# Editorial Product Information: Ripretinib

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial synthesis is derived from the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following regulatory authority: **Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview intended for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises clinical, pharmacological, and regulatory data into a single reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory documents for definitive prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Ripretinib (a switch-control tyrosine kinase inhibitor).
- **Chemical Class:** Phenylurea derivative.
- **Brand Name:** **QINLOCK** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** L01EX19.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** 50 mg oral tablets (white to off-white, oval-shaped, debossed with 'DC1').
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Specialised Therapeutics PM Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Gastrointestinal Stromal Tumours (GIST):** Indicated for the treatment of adult patients with advanced GIST who have received prior treatment with three or more kinase inhibitors, including imatinib [Australian-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing:** 150 mg (three 50 mg tablets) administered orally once daily.
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Tablets should be swallowed whole.
    - May be taken with or without food.
    - Administration should occur at the same time each day.
    - Treatment continues until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity.
- **Missed Dose:** 
    - If <8 hours have passed since the scheduled time, the dose should be taken immediately.
    - If >8 hours have passed, the dose should be skipped, and the patient should resume the normal schedule the next day.
    - If vomiting occurs after administration, an additional dose should **not** be taken; resume at the next scheduled time.
- **Dose Modifications for Adverse Reactions:**
    - First dose reduction: 100 mg once daily.
    - If 100 mg daily is not tolerated, treatment must be permanently discontinued.
- **Specific Interaction Adjustments:**
    - **Moderate CYP3A Inducers:** If co-administration is unavoidable, increase ripretinib frequency to 150 mg twice daily. Monitor for clinical response and toxicity. Revert to once-daily dosing 14 days after the inducer is discontinued.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to ripretinib or any excipients (including lactose) [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Palmar-plantar Erythrodysaesthesia Syndrome (PPES):** Very common. Based on severity, treatment may require withholding, dose reduction, or permanent discontinuation.
- **New Primary Cutaneous Malignancies:** Cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma (cuSCC), keratoacanthoma, and melanoma have been reported. Perform dermatologic evaluations at initiation and routinely during treatment. Suspicious lesions should be excised and evaluated; ripretinib may typically be continued at the same dose.
- **Hypertension:** Do not initiate in patients with uncontrolled hypertension. Monitor blood pressure closely. Withhold or reduce dose if hypertension is severe or persistent.
- **Cardiac Failure:** Cardiac dysfunction (including decreased ejection fraction and heart failure) has occurred. Assess LVEF via echocardiogram or MUGA scan prior to initiation and as clinically indicated. Permanently discontinue for Grade 3 or 4 left ventricular systolic dysfunction.
- **Risk of Impaired Wound Healing:** Due to potential VEGF signaling pathway inhibition, withhold ripretinib for at least 1 week prior to elective surgery and for 2 weeks following major surgery until adequate healing is confirmed.
- **Phototoxicity:** Patients should avoid or minimise UV exposure during treatment and for at least one week after the final dose. Use of protective clothing and high-SPF sunscreen is recommended.

### Laboratory Abnormalities (Grade 3-4)
- Increased lipase (7%), increased hypertension (7%), decreased phosphate (4.9%), increased INR (3.8%), and increased triglycerides (2.4%).

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Effect of Other Drugs on Ripretinib:**
    - **Strong CYP3A Inhibitors:** Increase ripretinib exposure (AUC increased by 99%). Use with caution and monitor for toxicity.
    - **Strong CYP3A Inducers:** Decrease ripretinib exposure (AUC decreased by 61%). Avoid concomitant use.
    - **Moderate CYP3A Inducers:** Decrease exposure; requires dose increase to 150 mg BID [Australian].
    - **Acid-reducing agents:** Pantoprazole (PPI) did not significantly affect ripretinib exposure.
- **Effect of Ripretinib on Other Drugs:**
    - **CYP2C8 Substrates:** Ripretinib is a weak inhibitor; may slightly increase exposures (e.g., repaglinide).
    - **Transporters:** Ripretinib and its active metabolite (DP-5439) inhibit P-gp, BCRP, and MATE1. May increase exposure to substrates of these transporters.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Australian Category D:** Ripretinib can cause embryo-fetal harm based on animal data (cardiovascular and skeletal malformations). 
    - **Contraception:** Females of reproductive potential must use effective contraception during treatment and for 1 week after the final dose. Males with female partners of reproductive potential must also use effective contraception for the same duration.
- **Breastfeeding:** Breastfeeding is not recommended during treatment and for at least 1 week after the final dose due to potential adverse reactions in the infant.
- **Fertility:** Animal studies indicate potential impairment of male fertility (testicular atrophy and degeneration).

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established in patients <18 years of age.
- **Elderly Use:** No overall differences in safety or efficacy were observed between patients ≥65 years and younger patients, though data for those ≥75 years is limited.
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment for mild to moderate impairment (CrCl 30–89 mL/min). Not studied in severe renal impairment (CrCl 15–29 mL/min).
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment recommended for mild (Child-Pugh A), moderate (Child-Pugh B), or severe (Child-Pugh C) impairment. Close monitoring is advised for patients with severe hepatic impairment.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Ripretinib is a switch-control tyrosine kinase inhibitor. It dual-inhibits KIT and PDGFRA by binding to both the switch pocket and the activation loop, locking the kinase in an inactive state. This prevents downstream signaling in both wild-type and mutant kinases.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Peak plasma concentration (Cmax) at ~4 hours. Steady state reached by Day 15. High-fat meals increase ripretinib AUC by 30%.
    - **Distribution:** >99% protein bound. Volume of distribution is ~307 L.
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily metabolised by CYP3A4/5, with minor contributions from CYP2C8 and CYP2D6. Active metabolite: DP-5439.
    - **Excretion:** Mean elimination half-life is ~14.8 hours. Primary route is faeces (34% as unchanged drug; >87% in animal models). Minimal urinary excretion (0.02%).

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **INVICTUS Trial:** A Phase 3, randomised (2:1), double-blind, placebo-controlled study in patients with advanced GIST previously treated with imatinib, sunitinib, and regorafenib.
- **Primary Outcome:** Median progression-free survival (PFS) was **6.3 months** for the ripretinib arm vs. **1.0 month** for the placebo arm (HR 0.15; p<0.0001).
- **Secondary Outcomes:** Overall response rate (ORR) was 9% for ripretinib vs. 0% for placebo. Median overall survival (OS) was 15.1 months for ripretinib vs. 6.6 months for placebo.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage:** Store below 25°C. 
- **Handling:** Keep tablets in the original container to protect from light and moisture.
- **Shelf Life:** Refer to the expiry date on the packaging [Australian].
