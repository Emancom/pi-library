# Editorial Product Information: Rimantadine

## Editorial Note
- Jurisdictions contributing to this editorial: **US (FDA)**.
- Purpose: This document provides a harmonised clinical overview of **rimantadine hydrochloride** for healthcare professionals, synthesising regulatory data into a single reference.
- Disclaimer: This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority's approved PI for definitive legal prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Rimantadine hydrochloride (a synthetic antiviral drug of the adamantane class).
- **Brand Names:** Rimantadine Hydrochloride Tablets, USP [US].
- **ATC Code:** J05AC02.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 100 mg film-coated tablets (orange, oval, debossed with "G" and "1911") [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Amneal Pharmaceuticals of New York LLC [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Prophylaxis of Influenza A:** Indicated for adults and children (1 year to 16 years of age) [US].
- **Treatment of Influenza A:** Indicated for adults (17 years and older) who develop influenza-like illness during known or suspected outbreaks. [US-approved; treatment NOT indicated for pediatric patients 16 years or younger in the US].
  - *Clinical Note:* Treatment should be initiated within 48 hours of symptom onset to reduce the duration of fever and systemic symptoms.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults 17+)
- **Standard Dose:** 100 mg twice daily.
- **Severe Hepatic/Renal Impairment (CrCl 5–29 mL/min):** Reduce dose to 100 mg once daily [US].
- **Elderly Nursing Home Patients:** Reduce dose to 100 mg once daily [US].

### Dosing by Indication
- **Prophylaxis (Children 1–9 years):** 5 mg/kg once daily, not to exceed 150 mg per dose [US].
- **Prophylaxis (Children 10–16 years):** 100 mg twice daily [US].
- **Treatment (Adults 17+):** 100 mg twice daily for approximately seven days.

### Administration Instructions
- Tablets are administered orally.
- For patients with difficulty swallowing or requiring lower doses, an oral suspension (10 mg/mL) can be compounded using a specific vehicle (e.g., Ora-Sweet®) [US].

### Missed Dose Instructions
- Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to the adamantane class of drugs, including **rimantadine** and amantadine [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Seizures:** An increased incidence of seizures has been reported in patients with a history of epilepsy when using adamantanes. If seizures develop, **rimantadine** should be discontinued [US].
- **Viral Resistance:** Influenza A strains resistant to **rimantadine** can emerge rapidly during treatment. Resistant viruses are transmissible and may cause typical illness. Prescribers should monitor local CDC or public health data regarding current resistance patterns [US].
- **Bacterial Superinfections:** **Rimantadine** does not prevent serious bacterial complications that may arise during or after an influenza infection [US].
- **Hepatic/Renal Impairment:** Due to the risk of drug and metabolite accumulation, use caution and monitor for adverse effects in patients with severe hepatic dysfunction or renal failure [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Acetaminophen:** Co-administration reduced the Cmax and AUC of **rimantadine** by approximately 11% [US].
- **Aspirin:** Co-administration reduced the Cmax and AUC of **rimantadine** by approximately 10% [US].
- **Cimetidine:** No statistically significant effect on **rimantadine** pharmacokinetics [US].
- **Live Attenuated Influenza Vaccine (LAIV):** Antiviral drugs may inhibit the replication of the live vaccine virus. LAIV should not be administered within 48 hours after stopping **rimantadine**, and **rimantadine** should not be administered for two weeks after LAIV administration unless medically indicated [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not assigned in modern labeling format]. **Rimantadine** is embryotoxic in rats at high doses (11x MRHD). No well-controlled studies exist in pregnant women. Use only if the potential benefit outweighs the risk to the fetus [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** **Rimantadine** should **not** be administered to nursing mothers. It is concentrated in rat milk (2x serum levels), and adverse effects were noted in nursing rat offspring [US].
- **Fertility:** No detectable impairment of fertility in rats at 3x the MRHD [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Recommended for prophylaxis in children aged 1–16 years. Safety and efficacy for treatment in children or prophylaxis in infants under 1 year are not established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Geriatric patients, particularly those in nursing homes, may have 2- to 4-fold higher steady-state concentrations. Dose reduction to 100 mg daily is recommended for elderly nursing home residents [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment needed for mild or moderate impairment. In severe impairment (CrCl < 30 mL/min), AUC increases by ~81%; dose reduction to 100 mg daily is required [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** In severe hepatic dysfunction, AUC increased 3-fold and half-life doubled. Dose reduction to 100 mg daily is recommended [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Inhibits the early viral replicative cycle, likely the uncoating of the virus. The virion M2 protein plays a critical role in susceptibility [US].
- **Microbiology:** Active against Influenza A subtypes H1N1, H2N2, and H3N2. It has little to no activity against Influenza B [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
  - **Absorption:** Well absorbed orally; Tmax is ~6 hours.
  - **Distribution:** Plasma protein binding is ~40% (primarily albumin).
  - **Metabolism:** Extensively hepatic; less than 25% excreted unchanged in urine.
  - **Elimination:** Elimination half-life is ~25.4 hours in healthy young adults and ~32 hours in the elderly [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- Prophylactic efficacy has been demonstrated in children, healthy adults, and elderly populations. In adults, **rimantadine** 200 mg/day for 11 days to 6 weeks was effective in preventing Influenza A symptoms [US].
- Treatment studies in adults showed a reduction in fever duration and systemic symptoms when therapy started within 48 hours of illness [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Tablets:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Compounded Suspension:** Stable for 14 days at room temperature when stored in amber glass or PET plastic bottles [US].
