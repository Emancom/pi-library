# Editorial Product Information: Ruxolitinib

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration)**. 
- Information regarding other regulatory jurisdictions (e.g., US, European, Canadian, Japanese) was not specified in the available sources provided for this synthesis.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. It is a clinical synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Ruxolitinib (as phosphate salt).
- **Chemical Class:** Janus Associated Kinase (JAK) inhibitor.
- **Brand Name:** **Jakavi** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 5 mg, 10 mg, 15 mg, and 20 mg oral tablets [Australian].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Novartis Pharmaceuticals Australia Pty Limited [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Myelofibrosis (MF):** Treatment of disease-related splenomegaly or symptoms in adult patients with primary myelofibrosis, post-polycythemia vera myelofibrosis, or post-essential thrombocythemia myelofibrosis [Australian].
- **Polycythemia Vera (PV):** Treatment of adult patients who are resistant to or intolerant of hydroxyurea [Australian].
- **Acute Graft-versus-Host Disease (aGvHD):** Treatment of patients aged 12 years and older with an inadequate response to corticosteroids [Australian].
- **Chronic Graft-versus-Host Disease (cGvHD):** Treatment of patients aged 12 years and older with an inadequate response to corticosteroids [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing Principles
A complete blood count (CBC) must be performed prior to initiation. Monitoring should continue every 2 to 4 weeks until doses are stabilised, then as clinically indicated [Australian].

### Myelofibrosis (MF)
The starting dose is determined by the baseline platelet count:
- **>200 x 10⁹/L:** 20 mg twice daily.
- **100 to 200 x 10⁹/L:** 15 mg twice daily.
- **50 to <100 x 10⁹/L:** 5 mg twice daily.
- **Maximum dose:** 25 mg twice daily.
Treatment should be discontinued after 6 months if no spleen size reduction or symptom improvement is observed [Australian].

### Polycythemia Vera (PV)
- **Starting dose:** 10 mg twice daily.
- **Maximum dose:** 25 mg twice daily [Australian].

### Graft-versus-Host Disease (GvHD)
- **Acute GvHD:** 5 mg to 10 mg twice daily.
- **Chronic GvHD:** 10 mg twice daily.
Tapering may be considered in responders after discontinuing corticosteroids, typically reducing the dose by 50% every two months [Australian].

### Administration Instructions
- May be taken with or without food.
- **Missed Dose:** The patient should not take an additional dose to make up for the missed one but should resume the usual prescribed schedule [Australian].

## 4. Contraindications
- Hypersensitivity to ruxolitinib or any of the product excipients [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Additional Monitoring:** This product is subject to additional monitoring in Australia to facilitate the identification of new safety information [Australian].
- **Hematologic Effects:** Can cause significant thrombocytopenia, anaemia, and neutropenia. Dose reductions or interruptions are frequently required based on CBC monitoring.
- **Infections:** Serious bacterial, mycobacterial, fungal, and viral infections have occurred. **Ruxolitinib** should not be started until active serious infections resolve. Patients should be screened for Tuberculosis and Hepatitis B [Australian].
- **Herpes Zoster:** Patients should be educated on early symptoms to allow prompt treatment.
- **Progressive Multifocal Leukoencephalopathy (PML):** Reported with treatment; suspend dosing if PML is suspected.
- **Non-Melanoma Skin Cancer (NMSC):** Reported in patients; periodic skin examinations are recommended.
- **Lipid Abnormalities:** Increases in cholesterol and triglycerides may occur; monitor and treat according to clinical guidelines.
- **Withdrawal Effects:** Abrupt discontinuation may lead to a return of MF symptoms or, rarely, more severe adverse events. Gradual tapering is recommended unless an emergency stop is required [Australian].
- **Cardiovascular/Thrombotic Risks:** Other JAK inhibitors have shown increased risks of major adverse cardiovascular events (MACE) and thrombosis. Caution is advised, particularly in smokers or those with cardiovascular risk factors [Australian].

| Frequency | Adverse Reaction |

## 7. Drug Interactions
### Pharmacokinetic Interactions
- **Strong CYP3A4 Inhibitors:** (e.g., ketoconazole, clarithromycin) Increase ruxolitinib exposure. Reduce total daily dose by approximately 50% (except in GvHD) [Australian].
- **Dual Moderate CYP2C9 and CYP3A4 Inhibitors:** (e.g., fluconazole ≤200 mg) Increase ruxolitinib exposure. A 50% dose reduction should be considered. Avoid fluconazole doses >200 mg [Australian].
- **CYP3A4 Inducers:** (e.g., rifampicin, phenytoin) Decrease ruxolitinib exposure. Monitor closely and titrate dose based on response [Australian].
- **P-gp and BCRP:** **Ruxolitinib** may inhibit these transporters in the intestine, potentially increasing exposure to substrates like digoxin or dabigatran [Australian].

### Pharmacodynamic Interactions
- **Cytoreductive Therapies:** Concomitant use with drugs like hydroxyurea has not been studied; safety and efficacy are unknown [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** **Category C** [Australian]. Not recommended for use during pregnancy. Animal studies show embryolethal and fetotoxic effects at maternotoxic doses.
- **Breastfeeding:** Breastfeeding must be discontinued during treatment. In animal studies, ruxolitinib was excreted in milk at concentrations 13-fold higher than maternal plasma [Australian].
- **Fertility:** No human data. Animal studies showed increased post-implantation loss in females at high doses [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy established for GvHD in patients ≥12 years. Not established for MF or PV. Not established for any indication in children <12 years [Australian].
- **Elderly Use:** No specific dose adjustments are recommended.
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    - Moderate/Severe (CrCl <60 mL/min): Reduce MF starting dose by 50%. For PV, start at 5 mg twice daily. 
    - ESRD on dialysis: MF dose 15-20 mg (once after dialysis); PV dose 10 mg (once after dialysis) [Australian].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** 
    - MF: Reduce starting dose by 50%.
    - PV: Start at 5 mg twice daily. 
    - GvHD: No adjustment needed if liver GvHD is present, but monitor counts more frequently [Australian].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Inhibits JAK1 and JAK2, which mediate signaling for cytokines and growth factors essential for hematopoiesis and immune function. It modulates the JAK-STAT pathway, which is dysregulated in myeloproliferative neoplasms and GvHD [Australian].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Rapidly absorbed; Tmax ~1 hour. Absolute bioavailability ≥95%.
    - **Distribution:** 97% protein bound (mostly albumin).
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily metabolised by CYP3A4 (~60%) and CYP2C9.
    - **Excretion:** 74% in urine and 22% in faeces. Mean elimination half-life is approximately 3 hours [Australian].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Myelofibrosis:** Supported by **COMFORT-I** (placebo-controlled) and **COMFORT-II** (best available therapy-controlled). Both met the primary endpoint of ≥35% reduction in spleen volume at week 24 or 48.
- **Polycythemia Vera:** Supported by **RESPONSE** and **RESPONSE-2** studies, showing superior hematocrit control and spleen volume reduction compared to best available therapy in hydroxyurea-resistant/intolerant patients.
- **GvHD:** Supported by **REACH1** (acute), **REACH2** (acute), and **REACH3** (chronic), demonstrating significantly higher overall response rates compared to best available therapy [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- Store below 30°C.
- Protect from moisture [Australian].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule:** **Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine)** [Australian].
