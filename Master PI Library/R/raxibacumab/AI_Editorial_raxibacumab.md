# Editorial Product Information: Raxibacumab

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdiction:
    - **United States**: US Food and Drug Administration (FDA)
- **Purpose**: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical and regulatory data into a single reference.
- **Disclaimer**: This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority’s current approved documentation for definitive prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient**: Raxibacumab (a human IgG1λ monoclonal antibody).
- **Brand Name**: Raxibacumab [US].
- **ATC Code**: Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength**: Injection, 1,700 mg/34 mL (50 mg/mL) solution in a single-use vial [US].
- **Route of Administration**: Intravenous (IV) infusion.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor**: Emergent Manufacturing Operations Baltimore LLC [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inhalational Anthrax Treatment**: Indicated for adult and paediatric patients for the treatment of inhalational anthrax due to *Bacillus anthracis* in combination with appropriate antibacterial agents [US].
- **Inhalational Anthrax Prophylaxis**: Indicated for prophylaxis of inhalational anthrax when alternative therapies are not available or are not appropriate [US].
- **Limitations of Use**: 
    - Efficacy is based solely on studies in animal models [US].
    - Raxibacumab does not cross the blood-brain barrier; it is not indicated for the prevention or treatment of anthrax meningitis [US].
    - It must be used in combination with appropriate antibacterial drugs [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Standard Dose**: 40 mg/kg administered as a single intravenous infusion [US].
- **Premedication**: Administer 25 to 50 mg diphenhydramine (oral or IV) within 1 hour prior to infusion to reduce the risk of infusion reactions [US].

### Paediatric Dosing (Younger than 18 Years)
Dosing is weight-based to match adult exposure (derived from simulations) [US]:
- **Body weight >40 kg**: 40 mg/kg.
- **Body weight >10 kg to 40 kg**: 60 mg/kg.
- **Body weight 10 kg or less**: 80 mg/kg.

### Preparation and Administration
- **Dilution**: Dilute in 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection (preferred) or 0.45% Sodium Chloride Injection.
- **Final Concentration**: For fluid-restricted patients, the maximal concentration is 32 mg/mL [US].
- **Infusion Duration**: Total infusion time is 2 hours and 15 minutes [US].
- **Infusion Rates**: 
    - **Adults**: 15 mL/hour for the first 20 minutes, then 125 mL/hour for the remainder [US].
    - **Paediatrics**: Rate varies by weight tier (refer to specific weight-based tables in the local PI) [US].
- **Stability**: Prepared solution is stable for 8 hours at room temperature [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None identified in the source documentation [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning: Hypersensitivity and Anaphylaxis [US]**:
    - Hypersensitivity reactions, including anaphylaxis, have been reported during and after infusion.
    - Administration must occur in monitored settings with immediate access to emergency equipment (including epinephrine), medications, and personnel trained in managing shock.
- **Infusion Monitoring**: Monitor patients closely during and for a period after the infusion. Discontinue immediately if severe reactions occur [US].
- **Premedication Limitations**: Diphenhydramine may mask or delay the onset of hypersensitivity symptoms but does not prevent anaphylaxis [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions were studied primarily in healthy adult volunteers [US].
### Common Adverse Reactions (Frequency ≥1.5%) [US]
- **General**: Injection site reactions (erythema, pain), headache, somnolence.
- **Dermatologic**: Rash (erythematous/papular), pruritus.
- **Musculoskeletal**: Pain in extremity.

### Less Common Significant Reactions (<1.5%) [US]
- **Cardiovascular**: Palpitations, hypertension, flushing.
- **Neurological/Psychiatric**: Syncope, vertigo, insomnia.
- **Haematological**: Anemia, leukopenia, lymphadenopathy.
- **Laboratory**: Increased blood amylase, increased creatine phosphokinase, prolonged prothrombin time.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Ciprofloxacin**: Co-administration of raxibacumab 40 mg/kg IV with ciprofloxacin (IV or oral) does not alter the pharmacokinetics of either drug [US].
- **Anthrax Vaccine Adsorbed (AVA)**: Co-administration does not affect the immunogenicity of the AVA vaccine regimen [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy**: 
    - **US Category**: Not assigned (post-PLLR labelling). 
    - **Risk Summary**: No data in pregnant women. Animal studies in rabbits at 3x human exposure showed no teratogenicity, though maternal toxicity (reduced weight gain) was observed [US].
- **Breastfeeding**: Data on the presence of raxibacumab in human milk is unavailable. Maternal IgG is known to be present in human milk [US].
- **Fertility**: No studies have been conducted to evaluate the effect on fertility [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use**: Safety and effectiveness have not been studied in children; dosing was extrapolated based on weight and predicted exposure [US].
- **Elderly Use**: Clinical trials included limited subjects ≥65 years (6.4%). No dose adjustment is required based on age [US].
- **Renal Impairment**: Raxibacumab clearance is not mediated by the kidneys; no dose adjustment is expected to be necessary [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment**: Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action**: Raxibacumab is a monoclonal antibody that binds with high affinity (Kd 2.78 nM) to the free Protective Antigen (PA) of *B. anthracis*. This prevents PA from binding to cellular receptors, thereby inhibiting the entry of lethal factor and edema factor into cells [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics**: 
    - **Distribution**: Steady-state volume of distribution is slightly greater than plasma volume.
    - **Elimination**: Clearance is much smaller than the glomerular filtration rate, indicating no renal clearance [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics**: Neutralises toxin activity; PA levels fall to undetectable in animals responding to treatment [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Efficacy Basis**: Approved under the "Animal Rule" because human efficacy trials are unethical.
- **Key Findings**: In rabbit and monkey models, raxibacumab demonstrated a statistically significant improvement in survival compared to placebo when administered at the onset of symptoms or as post-exposure prophylaxis [US].
- **Combination Therapy**: In a rabbit model, the combination of raxibacumab and levofloxacin resulted in 82% survival compared to 65% with levofloxacin alone (p=0.0874) [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature**: Refrigerate at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) [US].
- **Handling**: Do not freeze. Protect from light by storing in the original carton until time of use [US].
- **Vial**: Single-use only; contains no preservatives. Discard unused portions [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States**: Rx-only; Biologics License Application (BLA) approved [US].
