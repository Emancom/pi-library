# Editorial Product Information: Remdesivir

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** This editorial synthesis is based solely on the Product Information (PI) provided by the **Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration)**. While the prompt anticipated multiple regional documents, only the Australian PI was available for this synthesis.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals (HCPs), synthesising clinical, regulatory, and pharmacological data into a concise reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and serves as a clinical resource only. It is not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific Product Information required for prescribing and dispensing in any specific region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Remdesivir (an adenosine analogue nucleotide prodrug).
- **Brand Name:** **VEKLURY** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** J05AB16.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Powder for injection (lyophilised), 100 mg per single-dose vial. After reconstitution, the solution contains 5 mg/mL of remdesivir [Australian].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Gilead Sciences Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
**VEKLURY** is indicated for the treatment of COVID-19 in the following populations:
- Adults and paediatric patients (at least 4 weeks of age and weighing at least 3 kg) who have pneumonia due to SARS-CoV-2 and require supplemental oxygen [Australian].
- Adults and paediatric patients (weighing at least 40 kg) who do not require supplemental oxygen but are at high risk of progressing to severe COVID-19 [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Adults and Paediatrics (≥40 kg):**
    - **Day 1:** Single loading dose of 200 mg.
    - **Day 2 onwards:** 100 mg once daily.
- **Paediatrics (4 weeks of age and 3 kg to <40 kg):**
    - **Day 1:** Single loading dose of 5 mg/kg.
    - **Day 2 onwards:** 2.5 mg/kg once daily.

### Treatment Duration
- **Pneumonia requiring supplemental oxygen:** Total duration of at least 5 days and no more than 10 days.
- **High-risk patients not requiring supplemental oxygen:** Daily for 3 days, initiated as soon as possible (and within 7 days of symptom onset).

### Administration Instructions
- **Preparation:** Reconstitute with 19 mL of Sterile Water for Injection. Dilute immediately into 0.9% sodium chloride to a final concentration of 1.25 mg/mL (for paediatrics <40 kg) or via standard infusion bag (for adults/paediatrics ≥40 kg).
- **Infusion Rate:** Administer over 30 to 120 minutes.
- **Post-infusion:** Flush the line with at least 30 mL of 0.9% sodium chloride.
- **Missed Dose:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- Hypersensitivity to remdesivir or any of the excipients (including sulfobutyl betadex sodium) [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity and Infusion Reactions:** Anaphylaxis and severe infusion-related reactions have been reported. Monitor patients during and after infusion. If a clinically significant reaction occurs, discontinue immediately and initiate appropriate therapy.
- **Transaminase Elevations:** Increased ALT and AST have been observed in healthy volunteers and COVID-19 patients. 
    - **Monitoring:** Perform hepatic laboratory testing before starting and during treatment as clinically appropriate.
    - **Discontinuation:** Consider stopping if ALT >10x ULN; must stop if ALT elevation is accompanied by signs of liver inflammation.
- **Renal Impairment Risk:** The excipient sulfobutyl betadex sodium is renally cleared and may accumulate in patients with reduced renal function, potentially affecting renal health.
- **Reduced Antiviral Activity:** Co-administration with chloroquine or hydroxychloroquine is not recommended due to potential antagonism of the intracellular metabolic activation of remdesivir.

## 6. Adverse Effects
| Frequency | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Increased transaminases, increased prothrombin time |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Nausea, headache, rash |
| **Rare (<0.1%)** | Hypersensitivity, infusion-related reactions |
| **Post-marketing** | Anaphylactic reaction, sinus bradycardia, administration site extravasation |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Transaminase elevations (Grade 3 or higher) and anaphylaxis are the primary serious concerns.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Chloroquine/Hydroxychloroquine:** Avoid concomitant use; in vitro data show antagonistic effects on remdesivir activation [Australian].
- **CYP Enzyme Interactions:** Remdesivir is a substrate for CYP3A4, esterases, and OATP1B1/P-gp transporters.
    - **Inducers:** Strong CYP3A4 inducers (e.g., rifampicin) may decrease remdesivir concentrations; co-administration is not recommended.
    - **Dexamethasone:** Unlikely to have a clinically significant effect on remdesivir despite being a moderate CYP3A inducer.
- **Effect on Other Drugs:** Remdesivir may transiently inhibit CYP3A4 and UGT1A1. Caution is advised when co-administering with CYP1A2 substrates with narrow therapeutic indices (e.g., theophylline).

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** **Category B2** [Australian]. 
    - Data are limited, especially for the first trimester. Use during the first trimester is not recommended.
    - Use in the second or third trimester may be considered if the potential benefit outweighs the risk to the mother and foetus.
- **Breastfeeding:** Remdesivir and its metabolite GS-441524 are excreted in human milk. Breastfeeding should only be undertaken after a careful individual benefit-risk assessment.
- **Fertility:** No human data available. Animal studies (rats) showed no effect on male fertility but noted some effects on female corpora lutea and implantation at doses 1.3x human exposure.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Approved for patients ≥4 weeks and ≥3 kg. Safety and efficacy not established for those <4 weeks or <3 kg [Australian].
- **Elderly Use:** No dose adjustment required for patients >65 years.
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment is required, including for patients on dialysis. However, safety data are limited in severe renal impairment (eGFR <30 mL/min). Monitor closely for adverse events.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment required for mild, moderate, or severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh Class A, B, or C).

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Remdesivir is an adenosine analogue nucleotide prodrug. It is metabolised intracellularly into the active triphosphate form (GS-443902), which competes with ATP for incorporation into viral RNA by the SARS-CoV-2 RNA-dependent RNA polymerase (RdRp). This leads to delayed chain termination and inhibition of viral replication.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** 88% protein bound.
    - **Metabolism:** Extensively metabolised via esterases (80% CES1; 10% Cathepsin A) to intermediate GS-704277 and eventually the nucleoside metabolite GS-441524.
    - **Elimination:** Primarily renal (74%), with 49% recovered as GS-441524.
    - **Half-life:** Remdesivir (~1 hour); GS-441524 (~27 hours).

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **NIAID ACTT-1:** A double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in hospitalised adults. Remdesivir showed a median time to recovery of 10 days vs 15 days for placebo (p < 0.001). Mortality at Day 29 was 11% (remdesivir) vs 15% (placebo).
- **GS-US-540-9012 (PINETREE):** A trial in non-hospitalised high-risk patients. A 3-day course reduced COVID-19-related hospitalisation or death by 87% compared to placebo.
- **GS-US-540-5823 (CARAVAN):** An open-label study in paediatric patients (4 weeks to <18 years) which demonstrated a safety and PK profile consistent with adult data.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vials:** Store the lyophilised powder below 30 °C [Australian].
- **Reconstituted Solution:** Must be used immediately to prepare the diluted infusion.
- **Diluted Infusion:** Stable for 24 hours at room temperature (20 °C to 25 °C) or 48 hours in the refrigerator (2 °C to 8 °C).
- **Handling:** Single-use only; discard any unused portion.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule:** **Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine)** [Australian].
- **Monitoring:** Subject to additional monitoring in Australia to allow quick identification of new safety information.
