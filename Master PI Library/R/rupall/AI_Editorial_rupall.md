# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, consolidating regulatory and clinical data into a single reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI or local regulatory guidance required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (Generic name); chemical class: carrier molecule/amino acid derivative.
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection [United States].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - Sterile solution for injection: 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL) [United States].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [United States].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Indicated for the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism leading to secondary carnitine deficiency [United States].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance dialysis [United States].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
- **General Dosing:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg.
- **Administration:** Administer as a slow bolus injection (over 2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion.
- **Loading Dose:** In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose is often administered, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours.
- **Frequency:** Dosing should occur every 3 to 4 hours (q3h or q4h), and never less frequently than every 6 hours (q6h).
- **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses are typically 50 mg/kg or as clinically required. The maximum recorded dose administered is 300 mg/kg [United States].

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight.
- **Administration:** Administer as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
- **Titration:** Initiation of therapy may be guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Adjustments (e.g., down to 5 mg/kg) can be made as early as the third or fourth week of treatment [United States].

### General Instructions
- **Monitoring:** For metabolic disorders, it is recommended to obtain plasma carnitine levels before therapy and monitor weekly or monthly. The target plasma free carnitine concentration is 35 to 60 µmol/L.
- **Visual Inspection:** Parenteral products should be inspected for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration [United States].

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known:** There are currently no listed contraindications for levocarnitine injection in the provided source [United States].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in patients with ESRD on dialysis. Reactions may occur within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and evaluate the risks versus benefits before re-administration [United States].
- **General Precautions:** The safety and efficacy of **oral** levocarnitine have not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in these patients may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites, trimethylamine (TMA) and trimethylamine-N-oxide (TMAO), which are normally excreted in urine [United States].
- **Monitoring:** Monitor INR in patients receiving concomitant warfarin therapy [United States].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Common Effects:** Transient nausea and vomiting have been observed. Other less frequent reactions include body odor and gastritis.
- **Clinical Trial Data (ESRD patients):** Adverse events occurring at a frequency ≥5% (regardless of causality) include:
    - **General:** Abdominal pain (5–21%), fever (5–12%), injection site reaction (27–38%), chest pain (6–15%), and flu syndrome (15–29%).
    - **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (18–21%), hypotension (3–19%), and tachycardia (5–9%).
    - **Digestive:** Diarrhea (9–35%) and vomiting (9–21%).
    - **Nervous System:** Headache (3–37%) and dizziness (10–18%).
    - **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (15–27%) and cough increase (10–18%).
- **Neurologic Reactions:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been noted [United States].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is used concomitantly with warfarin. Recommendation: Monitor INR levels after initiating levocarnitine or after dose adjustments in patients on warfarin therapy [United States].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Classification:** Not specified by letter category in source, but reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) revealed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm.
    - **Recommendation:** Levocarnitine should be used during pregnancy only if clearly needed [United States].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. While studies in dairy cows show increased milk concentrations after administration, the risks to a human child of excess carnitine must be weighed against the benefits of supplementation to the mother [United States].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility was found in animal studies [United States].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** See Dosage and Administration; dosing for metabolic disorders is weight-based (mg/kg) [United States].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically detailed in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** See ESRD dosing. Caution is advised regarding the accumulation of metabolites (TMA/TMAO) with oral formulations in patients with severe renal impairment [United States].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production [United States].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces).
    - **Elimination:** Following IV administration, approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. The mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [United States].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In ESRD patients, IV administration increases plasma levocarnitine concentrations and may alleviate metabolic abnormalities associated with toxic organic acid accumulation [United States].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients, 20 mg/kg IV three times per week after dialysis increased mean pre-dialysis (trough) carnitine levels from ~20 µmol/L to 190 ± 55 µmol/L after 8 weeks.
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** A study comparing 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg in ESRD patients showed dose-proportionate increases in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [United States].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Handling:** Avoid excessive heat and protect from freezing.
- **Stability:** Compatible and stable for up to 24 hours in PVC bags at room temperature when mixed with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer's (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) [United States].
- **Vial Integrity:** Discard any unused portion of an opened vial as the formulation contains no preservative [United States].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as Rx-only (Prescription only).
