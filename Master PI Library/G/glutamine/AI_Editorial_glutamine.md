# Editorial Product Information: L-glutamine

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) provided by the **United States (FDA)**. 
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised, clinically focused overview for healthcare professionals. 
- This document is an editorial synthesis and should not be used as a substitute for the official PI required for prescribing and dispensing within a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** L-glutamine (Amino acid)
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid; (S)-2,5-diamino-5-oxopentanoic acid
- **Brand Name(s):** L-glutamine [US]
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Oral powder: 5 grams of L-glutamine per paper-foil-plastic laminate packet [US]
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Manufactured for Camber Pharmaceuticals, Inc. by Annora Pharma Pvt. Ltd. [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Sickle Cell Disease Complications:** Indicated to reduce acute complications associated with sickle cell disease in adult and paediatric patients aged 5 years and older [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric $\ge$ 5 years)
Dosing is tiered based on total body weight and administered orally, twice daily [US].

| Weight in Kilograms | Weight in Pounds | Per Dose (grams) | Per Day (grams) | Packets per Day |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Less than 30 kg | Less than 66 lbs | 5 g | 10 g | 2 |
| 30 to 65 kg | 66 to 143 lbs | 10 g | 20 g | 4 |
| Greater than 65 kg | Greater than 143 lbs | 15 g | 30 g | 6 |

### Administration Instructions
- **Preparation:** Mix the powder immediately before ingestion. Each dose should be mixed with 8 oz (240 mL) of a cold or room temperature beverage (e.g., water, milk, apple juice) or 4 oz to 6 oz of food (e.g., applesauce, yogurt) [US].
- **Handling:** Complete dissolution of the powder is not required prior to administration. Do **not** use hot liquids or foods, as temperature may affect stability [US].
- **Missed Dose:** Patients should take the missed dose as soon as remembered, provided it is not time for the next dose. Do not double the dose to compensate for a missed one [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None identified in the provided source [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **General Precautions:** While no specific boxed warnings are noted, clinical caution is advised in geriatric populations and those with organ impairment due to a lack of specific study data [US].
- **Monitoring:** Clinical monitoring should follow standard protocols for sickle cell disease management.

## 6. Adverse Effects
The following adverse reactions were identified in clinical trials with an incidence greater than 10% [US]:

| Adverse Reaction | L-glutamine (N=187) | Placebo (N=111) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Constipation | 21% | 18% |
| Nausea | 19% | 14% |
| Headache | 18% | 15% |
| Abdominal Pain* | 17% | 16% |
| Cough | 16% | 14% |
| Pain in Extremity | 13% | 7% |
| Back Pain | 12% | 5% |
| Chest Pain | 12% | 8% |

*\*Includes upper abdominal pain.*

**Serious Adverse Effects:**
- Treatment discontinuation due to adverse reactions occurred in 2.7% of patients. Discontinuation causes included single cases of hypersplenism, dyspepsia, burning sensation, and hot flashes [US].
- While deaths occurred during clinical trials (1.6% in the treatment group), none were considered related to the study drug [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** No formal drug interaction studies have been conducted [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US]: There are no available data regarding use in pregnant women to inform drug-associated risks. Animal reproduction studies have not been conducted. The background risk of major birth defects and miscarriage in the indicated population is unknown.
- **Breastfeeding:** [US]: There are no data on the presence of exogenous L-glutamine in human milk or its effects on the breastfed infant or milk production. The developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding should be weighed against the mother's clinical need for the medication.
- **Fertility:** Animal potential for impairment of fertility has not been conducted [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy are established for patients aged 5 years and older. Use in patients under 5 years of age has not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical trials did not include sufficient numbers of subjects aged 65 and over to determine if they respond differently than younger subjects. Dosing should be cautious, typically starting at the lower end of the range, reflecting the higher frequency of renal, hepatic, or cardiac dysfunction [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** The pharmacokinetics of L-glutamine have not been studied in subjects with renal or hepatic impairment [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** The exact mechanism in sickle cell disease (SCD) is not fully understood. It is proposed that L-glutamine increases the availability of reduced glutathione and improves the NAD redox potential in sickle red blood cells (RBCs). This may reduce oxidative stress, which contributes to the hemolysis and vaso-occlusive events characteristic of SCD [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Follows a saturable absorption process. Peak concentrations occur approximately 30 to 60 minutes after a single oral dose.
    - **Distribution:** Apparent volume of distribution is approximately 750 mL/kg.
    - **Metabolism:** Endogenous L-glutamine is metabolized into glutamate and used in the synthesis of proteins and nucleotides.
    - **Elimination:** The terminal elimination half-life is approximately one hour. Metabolism is the primary route of clearance; urinary excretion is minimal (<0.3%) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Efficacy Summary:** A Phase 3, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial (n=230) evaluated patients (ages 5–58) with sickle cell anaemia or sickle $\beta^0$-thalassaemia who had $\ge$ 2 painful crises in the prior year.
- **Outcomes:** L-glutamine treated patients showed a significant reduction in the median number of sickle cell crises (3 vs 4 in placebo) and hospitalisations (2 vs 3 in placebo). The median time to the first crisis was longer in the L-glutamine group (84 days) compared to placebo (54 days). Clinical benefits were observed regardless of concomitant hydroxyurea use [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Protection:** Keep the product away from direct sunlight [US].
- **Handling:** Keep out of reach of children.
