# Editorial Product Information: Gadodiamide

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA)
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonized editorial overview of gadodiamide, synthesized from official Product Information (PI) for use by healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not replace the official PI required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority’s current approved labeling for definitive prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Gadodiamide (a nonionic gadolinium-based contrast agent).
- **Brand Name(s):** **OMNISCAN** [US].
- **ATC Code:** V08CA03.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Sterile aqueous solution for intravenous injection; 287 mg/mL (equivalent to 0.5 mol/L).
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous injection only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** GE Healthcare Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Central Nervous System (CNS) Imaging:** Indicated for use in MRI to visualize lesions with abnormal vascularity (or those suspected of causing blood-brain barrier abnormalities) in the brain, spine, and associated tissues [US].
- **Body Imaging:** Indicated for use in MRI to facilitate the visualization of lesions with abnormal vascularity within the thoracic (noncardiac), abdominal, pelvic cavities, and the retroperitoneal space [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Pediatrics $\ge$ 2 years)
- **CNS Imaging:** 0.1 mmol/kg (0.2 mL/kg) administered as a bolus intravenous injection [US].
- **Body Imaging:**
    - **General (Thoracic, Abdominal, Pelvic):** 0.1 mmol/kg (0.2 mL/kg) [US].
    - **Kidney:** 0.05 mmol/kg (0.1 mL/kg) [US].

### Administration Instructions
- **Visual Inspection:** Solutions should be clear and colorless to slightly yellow. Discard if particulate matter or discoloration is present [US].
- **Preparation:** Draw into a syringe and use immediately. Discard any unused portion [US].
- **Post-Injection Flush:** Follow the injection with a 5 mL flush of 0.9% sodium chloride to ensure complete delivery of the contrast [US].
- **Imaging Timeline:** Complete the MRI procedure within 1 hour of administration [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Renal Status:** Patients with chronic, severe kidney disease (GFR < 30 mL/min/1.73m²) or acute kidney injury (AKI) [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Prior history of hypersensitivity to **OMNISCAN** [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warnings [US]
- **Nephrogenic Systemic Fibrosis (NSF):** Gadolinium-based contrast agents (GBCAs) increase the risk of NSF in patients with impaired elimination. NSF is a debilitating and potentially fatal condition affecting the skin, muscle, and internal organs. Avoid use in patients with severe renal impairment (GFR < 30 mL/min/1.73m²) or AKI unless diagnostic information is essential and unavailable via other modalities.
- **Intrathecal Administration:** **OMNISCAN** is not approved for intrathecal use. Intrathecal administration can cause death, coma, seizures, and encephalopathy.

### General Warnings
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Anaphylactoid and anaphylactic reactions, including fatalities, have occurred. Resuscitation equipment and trained personnel must be available prior to administration [US].
- **Gadolinium Retention:** Gadolinium may be retained for months or years in the brain, bone, skin, and other organs. Linear GBCAs (like gadodiamide) result in higher retention levels than macrocyclic agents. Clinical consequences of retention in the brain are not established [US].
- **Acute Renal Failure:** Risk is increased in patients with pre-existing renal insufficiency. Use the lowest effective dose and monitor renal function [US].
- **Laboratory Test Interference:** 
    - **Serum Calcium:** Gadodiamide interferes with colorimetric (complexometric) methods, causing falsely low calcium readings. This effect lasts 12–24 hours in patients with normal renal function but may persist longer in those with renal impairment [US].
    - **Serum Iron:** Transitory, asymptomatic changes in serum iron have been observed [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
The most frequent adverse reactions observed in clinical trials ($\le$ 3%) include nausea, headache, and dizziness [US].

| Frequency | Adverse Effects |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Common ($\le$ 3%)** | Nausea, headache, dizziness |
| **Uncommon ($\le$ 1%)** | Injection site reaction, vasodilation, fever, hot flushes, rigors, fatigue, malaise, syncope, abdominal pain, diarrhea, vomiting, dry mouth, tinnitus, arthralgia, myalgia, rhinitis, dyspnea, pruritus, rash, urticaria, taste perversion, abnormal vision. |
| **Serious/Rare** | Cardiac failure, arrhythmia, myocardial infarction (typically in patients with ischemic heart disease), convulsions (including grand mal), and acute reversible renal failure. |

**Postmarketing Experience:** Acute pancreatitis (within 48 hours of administration), Nephrogenic Systemic Fibrosis (NSF), pulmonary edema, and gadolinium-associated plaques [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Specific Studies:** No formal drug interaction studies have been conducted [US].
- **Laboratory Interference:** Clinically significant interference with colorimetric calcium assays (see Section 5) [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Use only if imaging is essential and cannot be delayed. Gadolinium crosses the placenta and results in fetal exposure and retention. Animal data (rabbits) showed embryo-fetal toxicity at doses 0.6 times the human dose, though some effects may be related to maternal toxicity [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Approximately 0.01% to 0.04% of the maternal dose is excreted in breast milk. Given limited gastrointestinal absorption by the infant, the risk is likely low, but the developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding should be weighed against the clinical need for the drug [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy established for children $\ge$ 2 years at a single dose of 0.05 to 0.1 mmol/kg. Not studied in patients < 2 years; neonates and infants have significantly lower GFR, potentially affecting clearance [US].
- **Elderly Use:** No overall differences in safety/efficacy compared to younger patients, but cautious dose selection is advised due to the higher prevalence of decreased renal function in this population [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Contraindicated in severe impairment (GFR < 30 mL/min/1.73m²) due to NSF risk. **OMNISCAN** is dialyzable [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Specific dose adjustments have not been studied [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** A paramagnetic agent that generates a local magnetic field, shortening both T1 (spin-lattice) and T2 (spin-spin) relaxation times. In clinical doses, the effect is primarily on T1, increasing signal intensity (brightness) [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Conforms to an open, two-compartment model. Volume of distribution (200 $\pm$ 61 mL/kg) is equivalent to extracellular water [US].
    - **Metabolism:** None known [US].
    - **Elimination:** Primarily renal (95.4 $\pm$ 5.5% excreted in urine within 24 hours). Mean elimination half-life is approximately 77.8 minutes [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **CNS Study:** In a study of 439 adults, contrast enhancement was evident in 96% of patients receiving 0.1 mmol/kg. Increased numbers of brain/spine lesions were noted in 42% of patients compared to non-contrast MRI [US].
- **Body Study:** In a trial of 276 patients, diagnostic value was "improved" or "remarkably improved" in 90% of thorax, liver, and pelvis cases. For kidney imaging, 0.05 mmol/kg was established as the lowest effective dose [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from strong daylight and direct sunlight. **Do not freeze**; freezing may cause vial cracks and compromise sterility [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Prescription Only (Rx-only) [US].
