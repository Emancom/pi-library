# Originality Audit Report: glofitamab

## Audit Summary
- **Originality Score:** 82/100
- **Verbatim Copied Passages Found:** 3
- **Overall Assessment:** Pass

## Verbatim Copied Passages
1. **Approved Indications Statement**
   - **Copied Text:** "...treatment of adult patients with relapsed or refractory (R/R) diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL), not otherwise specified (NOS), or large B-cell lymphoma (LBCL) arising from follicular lymphoma, after two or more lines of systemic therapy."
   - **Source Match:** Document 2 (FDA), Section 1 (Indications and Usage), Page 1.
   - **Location in Editorial:** Section 2, first paragraph.

2. **Contraindications**
   - **Copied Text:** "...contraindicated in patients with a known hypersensitivity to glofitamab or any of its excipients."
   - **Source Match:** Document 1 (TGA), Section 4.3 (Contraindications), Page 9.
   - **Location in Editorial:** Section 3.

3. **Mechanism of Action (Lysis phrase)**
   - **Copied Text:** "...subsequent release of cytolytic proteins that cause the lysis of CD20-expressing B-cells."
   - **Source Match:** Document 1 (TGA), Section 5.1 (Mechanism of Action), Page 23.
   - **Location in Editorial:** Section 10.

## Close Paraphrases
1. **ICANS Manifestations:** The list "confusional state, delirium, somnolence, and cognitive impairment" in Section 4 is a direct rearrangement of the TGA/FDA lists (TGA p.10: "somnolence, cognitive disorder, confusional state, delirium, and disorientation").
2. **Step-up Dosing Table:** The schedule and obinutuzumab pre-treatment details (1,000 mg, 7 days prior) are functionally identical to the source tables. However, these are fixed clinical parameters that cannot be significantly altered without compromising accuracy.
3. **Storage Dilution Times:** The storage limits for diluted solution (64 hours refrigerated / 4 hours room temperature) are verbatim clinical facts from TGA Section 6.3 (p. 31) and FDA Section 2.7 (p. 13).

## Originality Strengths
- **Comparative Synthesis:** The editorial successfully compares and contrasts the TGA and FDA guidelines. The "Editorial Note" and the "Specific Agency Provisions" subsections are entirely original in their structure and intent.
- **Conservative Harmonization:** In Section 8 (Pregnancy and Breastfeeding), the author identifies a discrepancy between the agencies (2 months vs 1 month) and makes an editorial decision to recommend the "most conservative" option, which demonstrates professional synthesis rather than rote copying.
- **Section Headers and Structure:** The author has reorganized the information into a "Unified PI" format that does not mirror the layout of either source document.

## Differences from Source PIs
- **Unified Comparison:** The editorial combines Australian and US labels into a single document, a feature not present in either source.
- **Clarification Brackets:** The use of source tags like `[TGA]` or `[FDA]` provides metadata to the reader that is absent in the original regulatory documents.
- **Selective Omission:** The editorial omits large sections of technical clinical trial data (Section 4.8 of the TGA/Section 14 of the FDA) and focuses instead on the practical clinical instructions and safety warnings.
- **Harmonized Adverse Events:** Section 6 merges data from various tables in the sources into a concise list of "most common" effects.

## Recommendation
**PASS**

The editorial is a high-quality synthesis. While the "Approved Indications" and "Contraindications" sections are near-verbatim, this is standard and often required in regulatory writing to ensure legal and clinical accuracy. The document provides significant added value by identifying discrepancies between the FDA and TGA (specifically regarding combination therapy, pregnancy duration, and PML reporting) and harmonizing them into a single guide. No further revision is required for originality.
