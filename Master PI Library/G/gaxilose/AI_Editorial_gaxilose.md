# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) document from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview of **levocarnitine** for use by healthcare professionals. 
- This document is an editorial synthesis and should not be used as a substitute for the official PI required for prescribing and dispensing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine ((R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt).
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection, USP [United States].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
  - Sterile injection: 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) single-dose vial [United States].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [United States].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that result in secondary carnitine deficiency [United States].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [United States].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
- **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg [United States].
- **Administration:** Delivered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion. 
- **Acute Crisis:** A loading dose is often administered, followed by an equivalent dose divided over the subsequent 24 hours. Dosing frequency should be every 3 or 4 hours (q3h or q4h), and never less than every 6 hours (q6h).
- **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses are typically in the range of 50 mg/kg, though higher doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilised based on clinical requirement [United States].

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Recommended Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [United States].
- **Administration:** Administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line after each dialysis session.
- **Dose Adjustment:** Adjustments should be guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [United States].

### Monitoring
- It is recommended to obtain baseline plasma carnitine concentrations before initiating therapy.
- Ongoing monitoring should include weekly and monthly blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (targeted range: 35 to 60 µmol/L) [United States].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [United States].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions may occur within minutes of intravenous administration. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately [United States].
- **Seizures:** Both new-onset seizures and increased seizure frequency/severity in patients with pre-existing seizure disorders have been reported with both oral and intravenous levocarnitine [United States].
- **Monitoring:** In ESRD patients, dose adjustments should be strictly guided by trough carnitine levels [United States].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Very Common (>10%)
- **Body as a Whole:** Abdominal pain, headache, infection, injection site reaction, pain, flu syndrome [United States].
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension, hypotension [United States].
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea, vomiting [United States].
- **Nervous:** Dizziness [United States].
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis, cough increase, dyspnea, rhinitis [United States].

### Common (1–10%)
- **Body as a Whole:** Accidental injury, allergic reaction, asthenia, back pain, chest pain, fever [United States].
- **Cardiovascular:** Tachycardia, palpitations, vascular disorder [United States].
- **Digestive:** Nausea, anorexia, dyspepsia, constipation, melena [United States].
- **Metabolic/Nutritional:** Hypercalcemia, hyperkalemia, hypervolemia, peripheral edema, weight changes [United States].
- **Nervous:** Paresthesia, insomnia, anxiety, depression [United States].
- **Skin:** Pruritus, rash [United States].
- **Special Senses:** Eye disorder, taste perversion [United States].

### Other Noted Effects
- **Gastrointestinal:** Gastritis and body odor have been reported [United States].
- **Postmarketing:** Seizures [United States].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have been observed when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin. It is recommended that INR levels be monitored upon initiation or dose adjustment of levocarnitine in patients on anticoagulant therapy [United States].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not assigned]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) revealed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [United States].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine is a normal component of human milk. Studies in dairy cows indicate increased milk concentrations following exogenous administration. In nursing mothers, the benefits of supplementation must be weighed against the potential risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant [United States].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal models [United States].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing recommendations for metabolic disorders apply to paediatric patients. In ESRD, safety and efficacy have been established through clinical trials including paediatric subjects, though specific age-stratified data may be limited [United States].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically detailed in provided sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** The safety and efficacy of **oral** levocarnitine have not been evaluated in renal insufficiency. Chronic high doses of oral levocarnitine in ESRD patients may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [United States].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids in patients with specific metabolic defects [United States].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
  - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
  - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily feces).
  - **Elimination:** Following a single IV bolus (20 mg/kg), approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours. The distribution half-life is 0.585 hours and the terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [United States].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** Clinical data in ESRD patients undergoing hemodialysis (N=12) showed that 20 mg/kg doses three times weekly after dialysis successfully increased mean pre-dialysis (trough) plasma levocarnitine concentrations from ~20 µmol/L to ~190 µmol/L after 8 weeks. Efficacy in increasing carnitine concentrations is well-demonstrated, though clinical outcomes regarding signs and symptoms of deficiency in this population are not definitively determined [United States].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Precautions:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat.
- **Handling:** Single-dose vials; discard any unused portion. Stable for 24 hours at room temperature when diluted in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s in PVC bags [United States].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Prescription only (Rx only). NDC 36000-368-10 (10 x 5 mL vials).
