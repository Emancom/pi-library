# Editorial Product Information: Gemfibrozil

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information documents contributed to this editorial: **Australian (TGA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonized editorial overview of Gemfibrozil for use by healthcare professionals, synthesizing clinical data and regulatory requirements.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Gemfibrozil (a non-halogenated phenoxypentanoic acid).
- **Brand Name(s):** AUSGEM [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** C10AB04.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 600 mg film-coated, biconvex, oval tablets [Australian].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Arrotex Pharmaceuticals Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
Gemfibrozil is indicated as an adjunct to diet and other non-pharmacological measures (exercise, weight loss) when such measures have proved inadequate. Approved indications include:
- **Severe Hypertriglyceridaemia (Types IV and V):** Specifically for patients at risk of pancreatitis who have not responded to dietary efforts [Australian].
- **Dyslipidaemia associated with diabetes** [Australian].
- **Reduction of Coronary Heart Disease (CHD) Risk:** For patients with Type IIa and IIb hypercholesterolaemia [Australian].

*Note: In Type IIa patients with elevations of LDL-cholesterol only, the potential benefits are unlikely to outweigh the risks of potential toxicity (e.g., malignancy, gallbladder disease) [Australian].*

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Adult Dose:** 600 mg administered twice daily (total daily dose 1200 mg) [Australian].
- **Administration Timing:** Tablets should be taken 30 minutes before the morning and evening meals. Bioavailability is higher when taken before food; however, it may be taken with food if gastrointestinal intolerance occurs [Australian].
- **Administration Instructions:** Tablets must be swallowed whole and should not be halved [Australian].
- **Duration of Therapy:** Lipid response should be assessed periodically. Treatment should be withdrawn if the response is deemed inadequate after 3 months of therapy [Australian].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hepatic Dysfunction:** Including primary biliary cirrhosis [Australian].
- **Renal Dysfunction:** Severe renal impairment [Australian].
- **Biliary Disease:** Pre-existing gallbladder or biliary tract disease, including gallstones [Australian].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known allergy to gemfibrozil or any excipients [Australian].
- **Pregnancy and Lactation:** Refer to Section 8 [Australian].
- **Type I Hyperlipoproteinaemia** [Australian].
- **Specific Drug Combinations:** 
    - Concurrent use of **cerivastatin**, **simvastatin**, or high-dose **rosuvastatin** (40 mg) [Australian].
    - Concurrent use of **repaglinide** or **selexipag** due to serious pharmacokinetic interactions [Australian].
- **Photosensitivity:** History of photoallergic or phototoxic reactions during treatment with fibrates [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Clofibrate Analogy:** Due to chemical and pharmacological similarities, the serious adverse findings associated with clofibrate (e.g., increased non-cardiovascular mortality, malignancy, pancreatitis) may potentially apply to gemfibrozil [Australian].
- **Muscle Disorders (Myopathy/Rhabdomyolysis):** Risk is increased, especially when used with HMG-CoA reductase inhibitors (statins). Treatment must be discontinued if CPK levels significantly rise (>5x ULN) or if myositis is suspected [Australian].
- **Cholelithiasis:** Gemfibrozil may increase cholesterol excretion into the bile. If gallstones are found upon investigation, therapy should be discontinued [Australian].
- **Haematological Changes:** Mild decreases in haemoglobin, haematocrit, and white cell counts may occur upon initiation. Periodic blood counts are recommended during the first 12 months of therapy [Australian].
- **Monitoring:** Periodic determination of serum lipids, lipoproteins (including LDL/HDL ratio), and liver function tests (LFTs) is required [Australian].

**Serious Adverse Effects:** Rhabdomyolysis (leading to acute renal failure), severe anaemia, and cholelithiasis [Australian].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **HMG-CoA Reductase Inhibitors (Statins):** 
    - **Contraindicated:** Simvastatin, Rosuvastatin (40 mg) [Australian].
    - **Caution:** Combined use with other statins (e.g., atorvastatin, pravastatin, lovastatin) should generally be avoided; if necessary, use the lowest starting doses and monitor for muscle toxicity [Australian].
- **Anticoagulants (e.g., Warfarin):** Gemfibrozil may potentiate the effect of oral anticoagulants. Prothrombin time (INR) must be monitored frequently, and anticoagulant dosage reduced accordingly [Australian].
- **CYP2C8 Substrates:** Gemfibrozil is a potent inhibitor of CYP2C8. Concurrent use with **repaglinide**, **dasabuvir**, or **selexipag** is contraindicated [Australian]. Use with **enzalutamide** should be avoided [Australian].
- **Bile Acid-Binding Resins:** May reduce gemfibrozil bioavailability. Administer medications at least 2 hours apart [Australian].
- **Colchicine:** Increased risk of myopathy and rhabdomyolysis, particularly in the elderly or those with renal impairment [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Australian Category B3. Studies in rats/rabbits showed minor fetotoxicity at high doses. There are no adequate studies in pregnant women. Use should be reserved for cases where benefits clearly outweigh risks [Australian].
- **Breastfeeding:** Safety has not been established. It is unknown if gemfibrozil is excreted in human milk. Use is not recommended [Australian].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies showed a dose-related suppression of fertility in male rats, likely due to weight gain suppression [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy in children have not been established; use is not recommended [Australian].
- **Elderly Use:** No specific data provided; however, caution is advised due to increased risk of colchicine-induced myopathy [Australian].
- **Renal Impairment:** Contraindicated in severe renal dysfunction [Australian].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Contraindicated in hepatic dysfunction. Periodic LFTs are recommended [Australian].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Gemfibrozil is a lipid-regulating agent. It inhibits peripheral lipolysis and decreases hepatic extraction of free fatty acids, thereby reducing VLDL production. It also inhibits the synthesis of apolipoprotein B and increases HDL-cholesterol (subfractions HDL2 and HDL3) and apolipoproteins AI and AII [Australian].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** 
    - **Absorption:** Rapidly absorbed; peak plasma levels in 1 to 2 hours [Australian].
    - **Distribution:** Highly protein-bound. Half-life is approximately 1.5 hours [Australian].
    - **Metabolism:** Undergoes oxidation of a ring methyl group to form hydroxymethyl and carboxyl metabolites [Australian].
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 70% excreted in urine (mostly as glucuronide conjugate); 6% in faeces [Australian].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Helsinki Heart Study:** A 5-year, large-scale, double-blind, placebo-controlled primary prevention trial in 4,081 male patients. Gemfibrozil therapy resulted in a **34% reduction in serious coronary events** (sudden cardiac deaths and MI) and a **37% reduction in non-fatal MI**. The greatest benefit was observed in Type IIb patients with elevated LDL and triglycerides [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage:** Store below 30°C [Australian].
- **Protection:** Protect from light [Australian].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Australia:** Schedule 4 (S4) – Prescription Only Medicine.
