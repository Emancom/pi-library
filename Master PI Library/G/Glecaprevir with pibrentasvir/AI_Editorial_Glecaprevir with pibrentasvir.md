# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
*   **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA). 
*   **Purpose:** This document is a harmonised editorial overview of **levocarnitine** injection, synthesized for healthcare professionals. 
*   **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority's approved labelling for definitive prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine ((R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium).
*   **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule/amino acid derivative.
*   **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter Healthcare Corporation) [US].
*   **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
*   **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection, 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
*   **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous.
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
*   **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
*   **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg, administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion [US].
    *   **Loading Dose:** Often administered in severe metabolic crises, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours [US].
    *   **Frequency:** Dosing should occur every 3 to 4 hours, and not less than every 6 hours [US].
    *   **Maximum Dose:** Doses up to 300 mg/kg have been administered [US].
*   **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    *   **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
    *   **Administration:** Administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].
    *   **Dose Adjustment:** Adjustments (e.g., downward to 5 mg/kg) should be guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations, typically starting as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].

### Administration Instructions
*   Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter and discoloration before use [US].
*   Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial [US].

### Missed Dose Instructions
*   Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
*   **None known** [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
*   **Boxed Warnings:** None [US].
*   **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients on dialysis. Some reactions occur within minutes of administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and initiate medical management [US].
*   **Seizures:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity may occur [US].
*   **Monitoring:** 
    *   **Metabolic Disorders:** Weekly and monthly monitoring is recommended, including blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma free carnitine concentrations (target range: 35 to 60 µmol/L) [US].
    *   **Warfarin Therapy:** Reports indicate an increase in INR when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin. Monitor INR levels after initiation or dose adjustment [US].
*   **Renal Impairment (Oral Levocarnitine):** The safety of *oral* levocarnitine has not been evaluated in renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in ESRD patients may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide), which are normally renally excreted [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Very Common (>10%) [US Hemodialysis Trials]
*   **General:** Injection site reaction (33%), Pain (30%), Headache (22%), Fever (12%), Chest pain (12%).
*   **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (20%).
*   **Digestive:** Diarrhea (16%), Vomiting (15%).
*   **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (23%), Cough increase (18%), Dyspnea (11%).

### Common (1–10%) [US Hemodialysis Trials]
*   **General:** Abdominal pain, Accidental injury, Asthenia, Flu syndrome, Infection.
*   **Cardiovascular:** Hypotension, Tachycardia, Palpitations.
*   **Digestive:** Nausea, Anorexia, Constipation, Dyspepsia.
*   **Metabolic:** Hypercalcemia, Hyperkalemia, Hypervolemia, Peripheral edema, Weight decrease/increase.
*   **Musculoskeletal:** Leg cramps.
*   **Nervous:** Dizziness, Depression, Insomnia, Paresthesia.
*   **Skin:** Pruritus, Rash.
*   **Special Senses:** Amblyopia, Eye disorder, Taste perversion.

### Serious Adverse Effects
*   Anaphylaxis and severe hypersensitivity [US].
*   Seizures [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **Warfarin:** Levocarnitine may increase the International Normalized Ratio (INR). Frequent INR monitoring is required [US].
*   **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Levocarnitine is not protein-bound, suggesting a low potential for protein-binding displacement interactions [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy:** 
    *   **Jurisdiction Category:** Not specified [US].
    *   **Clinical Data:** Animal reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
*   **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine concentrations in milk following administration. Risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant must be weighed against maternal benefits [US].
*   **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders is calculated by weight (50 mg/kg) [US]. (See Section 3).
*   **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Renal Impairment:** Used specifically in ESRD patients on dialysis (See Section 3). Caution is advised regarding the accumulation of toxic metabolites if high-dose *oral* levocarnitine is used in this population [US].
*   **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into cellular mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters, which are rapidly excreted [US].

### Pharmacokinetics
*   **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
*   **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
*   **Elimination:** Following IV bolus (20 mg/kg), approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. The mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   **ESRD Studies:** In 9-week clinical trials of ESRD patients (20 mg/kg three times weekly post-dialysis), trough plasma levocarnitine concentrations increased from a baseline of ~21 µmol/L to ~190 µmol/L after 8 weeks. While levocarnitine concentrations were successfully increased, the impact on specific clinical outcomes in the ESRD population has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C [US].
*   **Handling:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat.
*   **Stability (Admixture):** Compatible with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations of 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL. Stable for up to 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags [US].
