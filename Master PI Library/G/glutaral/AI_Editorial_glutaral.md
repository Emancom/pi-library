# Editorial Product Information: Adacel (Tetanus Toxoid, Reduced Diphtheria Toxoid and Acellular Pertussis Vaccine Adsorbed)

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial: US (FDA).
- **Purpose:** This is a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising regulatory information into a single reference.
- **Note:** This document serves as an editorial synthesis for clinical reference and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** Tetanus Toxoid, Reduced Diphtheria Toxoid, and Acellular Pertussis antigens (Pertussis Toxoid [PT], Filamentous Haemagglutinin [FHA], Pertactin [PRN], and Fimbriae Types 2 and 3 [FIM]).
- **Chemical Class:** Vaccine, combined bacterial toxoids and fractional antigens.
- **Brand Name(s):** **Adacel** [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Suspension for injection: 0.5 mL single-dose vials and prefilled syringes [US].
    - Each 0.5 mL dose contains: 5 Lf tetanus toxoid, 2 Lf diphtheria toxoid, 2.5 mcg detoxified PT, 5 mcg FHA, 3 mcg PRN, and 5 mcg FIM [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intramuscular (IM) injection.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Sanofi Vaccines US Inc. (Distributed in the US); Manufactured by Sanofi Vaccines Canada Ltd. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Active Booster Immunization:** Indicated for protection against tetanus, diphtheria, and pertussis in individuals aged 10 through 64 years [US].
- **Neonatal Pertussis Prevention:** Immunization during the third trimester of pregnancy to prevent pertussis in infants under 2 months of age [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult/Adolescent):** A single 0.5 mL injection administered intramuscularly [US].
- **Routine Booster Schedule:**
    - **First Dose:** Administer 5 years or more after the completion of the primary DTaP series or 5 years or more after the last Td vaccine [US].
    - **Second Dose:** A second dose of **Adacel** may be administered 8 years or more following the initial Tdap dose [US].
- **Pregnancy:** Administer during the third trimester of pregnancy [US].
- **Wound Management:** For tetanus prophylaxis in tetanus-prone wounds, a booster may be given if at least 5 years have elapsed since the last tetanus-toxoid-containing vaccine [US].
- **Administration Instructions:** 
    - Shake well until a uniform, white, cloudy suspension is formed.
    - Inspect visually for particulate matter or discoloration; discard if present.
    - Do not mix with other vaccines or products in the same syringe or vial [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known severe allergic reaction (e.g., anaphylaxis) to any component of the vaccine or following a previous dose of any vaccine containing diphtheria toxoid, tetanus toxoid, or pertussis antigens [US].
- **Encephalopathy:** Occurrence of encephalopathy (e.g., coma, decreased consciousness, prolonged seizures) within 7 days of a previous pertussis-containing vaccine, not attributed to another identifiable cause [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None specified in available sources.
- **Guillain-Barré Syndrome (GBS) and Brachial Neuritis:** If GBS occurred within 6 weeks of a prior tetanus-toxoid vaccine, the decision to give **Adacel** should be based on careful consideration of benefits and risks. A causal relationship between tetanus toxoid and brachial neuritis has been acknowledged by the Institute of Medicine [US].
- **Neurologic Disorders:** Progressive or unstable neurologic conditions (e.g., uncontrolled epilepsy, progressive encephalopathy) are grounds to defer vaccination until the condition is stabilized [US].
- **Arthus-Type Hypersensitivity:** Individuals who experienced an Arthus-type reaction following a prior tetanus-toxoid vaccine should not receive **Adacel** unless at least 10 years have passed since the last dose [US].
- **Syncope:** Fainting can occur following injectable vaccines. Procedures should be in place to prevent falling injuries and manage syncopal reactions [US].
- **Altered Immunocompetence:** Immunocompromised persons or those on immunosuppressive therapy may have a reduced immune response [US].

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Neuropathic events (e.g., facial paralysis, nerve compression) have been reported rarely within 28 days of administration. 
- **Postmarketing Experience:** Spontaneous reports include anaphylactic reactions, angioedema, hypotension, myelitis, paresthesia, facial palsy, convulsion, myocarditis, and large injection site reactions (>50 mm) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Concomitant Vaccine Administration:**
    - **Influenza Vaccine:** When co-administered with trivalent inactivated influenza vaccine (TIV), a lower antibody response to the pertactin (PRN) antigen was observed compared to separate administration [US].
    - **Hepatitis B Vaccine:** No interference was observed in immune responses when co-administered with Hepatitis B vaccine [US].
    - **Administration Note:** Always use separate syringes and different injection sites for concomitant injections [US].
- **Immunosuppressive Treatments:** Therapies such as radiation, antimetabolites, alkylating agents, or high-dose corticosteroids may reduce the vaccine's effectiveness [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not specified as a letter grade, but available human data from pregnancy registries and surveillance studies suggest that major birth defects and miscarriage rates in women receiving **Adacel** are consistent with background rates in the general population [US].
    - **Animal Data:** Developmental toxicity studies in rabbits revealed no evidence of fetal harm [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if vaccine components are excreted in human milk. The developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding should be weighed against the clinical need for the vaccine [US].
- **Fertility:** **Adacel** has not been evaluated for impairment of male fertility. Animal studies in female rabbits showed no effects on parturition or lactation [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Approved for adolescents aged 10 years and older. Safety and effectiveness in children under 10 years of age have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** **Adacel** is not approved for use in individuals aged 65 years and older. Clinical studies showed lower antibody concentrations to certain pertussis antigens in this population compared to infants receiving primary series [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** 
    - **Tetanus & Diphtheria:** Induces production of neutralizing antibodies against the respective exotoxins. A serum antitoxin level of 0.01 IU/mL is the minimum protective level for both; 0.1 IU/mL is generally regarded as protective [US].
    - **Pertussis:** The specific role of individual antigens in conferring immunity is not fully defined, but the vaccine induces antibody responses to PT, FHA, PRN, and FIM [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** As a vaccine, standard absorption, distribution, metabolism, and elimination studies are not applicable.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Potency is evaluated by antibody responses in animal models and human clinical trials [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Study Td506 (First Vaccination):** A randomized, observer-blind, active-controlled trial (N=4,480) demonstrated that **Adacel** was non-inferior to Td vaccine regarding tetanus and diphtheria seroprotection. Pertussis antibody levels were non-inferior to those observed in infants protected by the DTaP series [US].
- **Study NCT01439165 (Second Vaccination):** Evaluated adults (18-64 years) receiving a second dose 8-12 years after their first. Tetanus and diphtheria responses were robust, though the pre-specified non-inferiority margin for tetanus was not strictly met compared to Td vaccine [US].
- **Pregnancy Effectiveness Study:** An observational study (NCT05040802) estimated vaccine effectiveness at 88.0% in preventing pertussis in infants under 2 months of age when the mother was vaccinated in the third trimester [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 2°C to 8°C (35°F to 46°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Do not freeze. Discard if the product has been exposed to freezing temperatures [US].
- **Shelf Life:** Do not use after the expiration date shown on the label [US].
