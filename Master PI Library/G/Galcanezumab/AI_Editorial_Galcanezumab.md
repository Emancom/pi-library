# Editorial Product Information: Galcanezumab

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdiction: **Australian (TGA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical data into a concise, structured format.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official, legally binding Product Information required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Galcanezumab (INN); a humanised monoclonal antibody (IgG4) produced by recombinant DNA technology in Chinese Hamster Ovary (CHO) cells.
- **Chemical Class:** Calcitonin gene-related peptide (CGRP) antagonist.
- **Brand Name:** Emgality [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** N02CD02.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Solution for injection in a single-dose pre-filled pen (autoinjector): 120 mg/1 mL.
    - Solution for injection in a single-dose pre-filled syringe: 120 mg/1 mL.
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Subcutaneous (SC) injection.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Eli Lilly Australia Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Migraine Prophylaxis:** Indicated for the preventive treatment (prophylaxis) of migraine in adult patients [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adults):** 
    - **Loading Dose:** 240 mg administered as a single initial dose (two consecutive SC injections of 120 mg).
    - **Maintenance Dose:** 120 mg administered once monthly.
- **Administration Instructions:** 
    - Intended for subcutaneous use only. 
    - Injection sites include the abdomen, thigh, back of the upper arm, or buttocks.
    - Patients may self-inject after receiving proper training. 
    - Prior to use, the solution should be inspected for particulate matter or cloudiness; the liquid should be clear and colourless to slightly yellow.
- **Missed Dose Instructions:** If a dose is missed, it should be administered as soon as possible. Thereafter, resume the regular monthly dosing schedule from the date of the last dose.
- **Clinical Evaluation:** Treatment response should be assessed by the prescriber within 8–12 weeks of initiation. Ongoing need for treatment should be evaluated regularly on an individual patient basis [Australian].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known serious hypersensitivity to galcanezumab or any of the excipients (histidine, histidine hydrochloride monohydrate, polysorbate 80, sodium chloride) [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypertension:** New-onset hypertension or the exacerbation of pre-existing hypertension has been reported with CGRP antagonists, including galcanezumab. These events most frequently occur within 7 days of the first dose. Blood pressure should be monitored, and discontinuation should be considered if hypertension is not adequately controlled or if no alternative etiology is found [Australian].
- **Serious Hypersensitivity:** Reports include anaphylaxis, angioedema, and urticaria. These reactions may occur days after administration and can be prolonged. If a serious reaction occurs, discontinue the drug immediately and start appropriate therapy [Australian].
- **Cardiovascular Risk:** Patients with recent acute cardiovascular events (e.g., MI, stroke, unstable angina) were excluded from clinical trials; safety in these populations is not established [Australian].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions observed in clinical trials were generally mild to moderate. Less than 2.5% of patients discontinued therapy due to adverse events.

| Frequency | Adverse Event |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (≥10%)** | Injection site pain, injection site reactions (general) |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Vertigo, constipation, pruritus, rash (post-marketing) |
| **Uncommon (0.1–1%)** | Urticaria |
| **Rare (0.01–0.1%)** | Anaphylaxis, angioedema |
| **Frequency Unknown** | Hypertension, fatigue |

- **Injection Site Reactions:** These are the most frequent events. While pain is common, other reactions include erythema, pruritus, bruising, and swelling. Most resolve within 1 to 5 days.
- **Immunogenicity:** Anti-drug antibodies (ADAs) developed in up to 12.5% of patients over 12 months. Most ADAs were of low titre and did not appear to affect pharmacokinetics, efficacy, or safety [Australian].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Galcanezumab is not metabolised by cytochrome P450 enzymes. As a monoclonal antibody, it is cleared via catabolic pathways into small peptides and amino acids; therefore, metabolic drug-drug interactions are not expected.
- **CGRP Combination Therapy:** There are no data regarding the safety or efficacy of using galcanezumab in combination with other agents targeting the CGRP pathway (e.g., gepants or other CGRP mAbs) [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Australian Category B1.**
    - Data in pregnant women are limited. Animal studies (rats and rabbits) showed no evidence of embryofetal harm at doses significantly higher than clinical exposures. Galcanezumab is known to cross the placental barrier in animals. Use during pregnancy only if the potential benefit justifies the potential risk [Australian].
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if galcanezumab is excreted in human milk. While human IgG is excreted in breast milk, the risk to the infant is unknown. Clinical need for the mother and potential effects on the infant should be weighed [Australian].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies showed no impact on male or female fertility parameters [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy have not been established in patients under 18 years of age; no data are available [Australian].
- **Elderly (≥65 years):** No dose adjustment is required. Data are limited in patients over 65, and it is unknown if they respond differently than younger subjects [Australian].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment is required. Renal elimination of IgG monoclonal antibodies is low, and creatinine clearance was not found to influence clearance in population PK analyses [Australian].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment is required. Monoclonal antibodies are not primarily eliminated via hepatic pathways [Australian].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Galcanezumab is a humanised IgG4 monoclonal antibody that binds with high affinity (KD = 31 pM) and specificity to the calcitonin gene-related peptide (CGRP) ligand. It prevents CGRP from binding to its receptor, thereby inhibiting the biological activity associated with migraine pathophysiology without blocking the receptor itself.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Tmax is approximately 5 days post-dose.
    - **Distribution:** Apparent volume of distribution (V/F) is 7.3 L.
    - **Metabolism:** Degraded into small peptides via general catabolic pathways.
    - **Elimination:** Apparent clearance (CL/F) is approximately 0.008 L/h; elimination half-life is roughly 27 days.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Single doses can attenuate capsaicin-induced dermal blood flow (a biomarker of CGRP activity) by Day 3, with effects lasting over 100 days following repeated dosing [Australian].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Episodic Migraine (EVOLVE-1 & EVOLVE-2):** Six-month, double-blind, placebo-controlled studies. Galcanezumab 120 mg/month (following a 240 mg loading dose) significantly reduced mean monthly migraine headache days (MHDs) by approximately 4.3 to 4.7 days compared to 2.3 to 2.8 days for placebo.
- **Chronic Migraine (REGAIN):** A three-month double-blind study in patients with ≥15 headache days per month. Galcanezumab significantly reduced MHDs by approximately 4.8 days compared to 2.7 days for placebo.
- **Long-term Efficacy:** Open-label studies showed sustained efficacy for up to one year, with over 72% of patients achieving a ≥50% reduction in MHDs at month 12 [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C.
- **Room Temperature Allowance:** May be stored unrefrigerated at temperatures up to 30°C for a single period of up to 7 days. If these conditions are exceeded, the product must be discarded and not returned to the refrigerator.
- **Handling:** Do not freeze. Do not shake. Protect from light by keeping the product in the original carton until the time of use [Australian].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule:** Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine) [Australian].
- **Classification:** Rx-only.
