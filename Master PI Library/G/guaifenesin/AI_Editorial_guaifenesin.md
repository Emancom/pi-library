# Editorial Product Information: Dextromethorphan Hydrobromide, Guaifenesin, and Phenylephrine Hydrochloride

## Editorial Note
- **Jurisdictions contributing to this editorial:** United States (FDA)
- **Purpose:** This document is a harmonised editorial synthesis of Product Information (PI) for a combination liquid formulation containing a cough suppressant, expectorant, and nasal decongestant. It is intended for use by healthcare professionals as a concise clinical reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This editorial synthesis is not a substitute for the official PI or summary of product characteristics (SmPC) required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** 
    - **Dextromethorphan Hydrobromide:** Centrally acting antitussive (morphinan derivative).
    - **Guaifenesin:** Expectorant (propanediol derivative).
    - **Phenylephrine Hydrochloride:** Sympathomimetic amine (alpha-1 adrenoceptor agonist).
- **Brand Name(s):** Maximum Strength Mucus Relief [US], Quality Choice Maximum Strength Mucus Relief [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Oral liquid containing 20 mg Dextromethorphan HBr, 400 mg Guaifenesin, and 10 mg Phenylephrine HCl per 20 mL [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Hyvee Inc (Labeler), Guardian Drug Company (Manufacturer) [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Expectorant:** Facilitates the clearance of mucus by loosening phlegm and thinning bronchial secretions to make coughs more productive.
- **Cough Suppressant:** Temporary relief of cough associated with minor throat and bronchial irritation resulting from the common cold or inhaled irritants.
- **Nasal Decongestant:** Temporary relief of nasal congestion due to the common cold.
- **Symptomatic Relief:** Reduces the intensity of coughing and the impulse to cough (aiding sleep) [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Adult and Paediatric (12 years and older) Dosing
- **Standard Dose:** 20 mL every 4 hours.
- **Maximum Daily Dose:** Do not exceed 6 doses (120 mL) in any 24-hour period [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Measurement should only be performed using the provided dosing cup to ensure accuracy.
- Do not use the provided dosing cup for other medications [US].

### Missed Dose
- Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Paediatric Use:** Contraindicated in children under 12 years of age [US].
- **MAOI Therapy:** Do not use in patients currently taking a prescription monoamine oxidase inhibitor (MAOI) or within 2 weeks of discontinuing an MAOI. This includes medications for depression, psychiatric/emotional conditions, or Parkinson’s disease [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### General Precautions
Healthcare professionals should advise patients to consult a physician prior to use if they have:
- Cardiovascular disease (Heart disease, Hypertension).
- Thyroid disease.
- Diabetes mellitus.
- Prostatic hypertrophy (difficulty urinating due to an enlarged prostate).
- Persistent or chronic cough (e.g., associated with smoking, asthma, chronic bronchitis, or emphysema).
- Cough accompanied by excessive phlegm (mucus).

### Monitoring and Discontinuation
Discontinue use and consult a healthcare provider if:
- Nervousness, dizziness, or sleeplessness occur.
- Symptoms persist for more than 7 days.
- Symptoms are accompanied by fever, rash, or persistent headache (may indicate a serious underlying condition).

## 6. Adverse Effects
Frequency data is not provided in the source documentation.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors (MAOIs):** Concurrent use or use within 14 days of cessation may lead to serious interactions, including hypertensive crisis or serotonin syndrome (related to the dextromethorphan and phenylephrine components) [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
- **Dextromethorphan HBr:** Acts on the cough centre in the medulla to elevate the cough threshold.
- **Guaifenesin:** Increases the volume and reduces the viscosity of secretions in the trachea and bronchi, aiding in the removal of mucus.
- **Phenylephrine HCl:** Potent postsynaptic alpha-receptor agonist with minimal effect on beta-receptors, causing vasoconstriction of the nasal mucosa to reduce congestion.
