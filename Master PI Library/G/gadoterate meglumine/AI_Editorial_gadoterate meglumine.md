# Editorial Product Information: Gadoterate Meglumine

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA). 
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of gadoterate meglumine for healthcare professionals, synthesising data from the provided official Product Information. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory documents for definitive prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Gadoterate meglumine (a paramagnetic macrocyclic ionic contrast agent).
- **Brand Name(s):** **Clariscan** [US].
- **ATC Code:** V08CA02 (Gadoteric acid).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile, clear, colorless to yellow aqueous solution for intravenous injection.
    - 0.5 mmol/mL (equivalent to 376.9 mg/mL of gadoterate meglumine) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous injection (manual or power injector).
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** GE HealthCare [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **CNS Imaging [US-approved]:** Indicated for use with magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) in the brain (intracranial), spine, and associated tissues in adult and paediatric patients (including term neonates) to detect and visualize areas with disruption of the blood-brain barrier (BBB) and/or abnormal vascularity.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric):** The recommended dose is **0.1 mmol/kg (0.2 mL/kg)** body weight.
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Administered as an intravenous bolus injection.
    - **Flow Rate:** Approximately 2 mL/second for adults; 1 to 2 mL/second for paediatric patients (including term neonates) [US].
    - To ensure complete delivery, the injection may be followed by a normal saline flush.
    - Contrast MRI can begin immediately following the injection.
- **Drug Handling:**
    - Visually inspect for particulate matter; do not use if present or if the container is damaged.
    - Do not mix with other drugs or parenteral nutrition.
    - Discard any unused portions.
- **Missed Dose Instructions:** Not applicable (single-dose diagnostic administration).

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** History of clinically important hypersensitivity reactions to **Clariscan** [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings [US]:**
    - **Risk Associated with Intrathecal Use:** Intrathecal administration of gadolinium-based contrast agents (GBCAs) can cause serious adverse reactions including death, coma, encephalopathy, and seizures. Gadoterate meglumine is NOT approved for intrathecal use.
    - **Nephrogenic Systemic Fibrosis (NSF):** GBCAs increase the risk of NSF in patients with impaired elimination. Avoid use in patients with chronic, severe kidney disease (GFR < 30 mL/min/1.73 m²) or acute kidney injury (AKI) unless the diagnostic information is essential and unavailable via non-contrasted MRI.
- **General Warnings:**
    - **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Anaphylactic/anaphylactoid reactions (cardiovascular, respiratory, or cutaneous) may occur, including fatalities. Patients with a history of asthma or prior contrast reactions are at increased risk.
    - **Gadolinium Retention:** Gadolinium is retained for months or years in various organs (bone, brain, skin, liver, spleen). Highest retention is in bone. Macrocyclic agents (like gadoterate meglumine) generally result in lower retention than linear agents.
    - **Acute Kidney Injury:** In patients with chronically reduced renal function, GBCAs may increase the risk of AKI requiring dialysis. Use the lowest effective dose.
    - **Extravasation:** Ensure venous patency; extravasation may cause local tissue irritation.

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Frequency Data (Clinical Trials):**
    - **Common (1–10%):** Not specified in the primary clinical trial summary (overall reaction rate was 4%).
    - **Uncommon (0.1–1%):** Nausea (0.6%), Headache (0.4%), Injection site pain (0.4%), Injection site coldness (0.2%), Rash (0.2%).
- **Paediatric Population:** Adverse reactions are similar to adults; headache (1.1%) was the most frequent in clinical trials.
- **Serious Adverse Effects (Postmarketing):**
    - **Immune System:** Anaphylactic reactions, cardiac arrest, respiratory arrest, angioedema.
    - **Nervous System:** Coma, convulsions, syncope.
    - **Gastrointestinal:** Acute pancreatitis (onset within 48 hours of administration).
    - **Cardiac:** Bradycardia, tachycardia, arrhythmia.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Laboratory Interferences:** Gadoterate meglumine does not interfere with serum and plasma calcium measurements determined by colorimetric assays [US].
- **Clinically Significant Interactions:** Specific drug interaction studies have not been conducted.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Category:** Not assigned a lettered category in the provided source, but noted to cross the human placenta.
    - **Recommendation:** Use only if imaging is essential and cannot be delayed. While animal studies at high doses (10–16x human dose) showed no developmental toxicity, human data are limited and inconclusive [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** 
    - **Safety:** There are no data on gadoterate meglumine in human milk. Other GBCAs show very low excretion (0.01% to 0.04% of maternal dose) into breast milk.
    - **Recommendation:** The developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding should be considered along with the mother’s clinical need for the drug.
- **Fertility:** No impairment of male or female fertility was observed in rat studies at doses up to 16 times the recommended human dose [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Approved for use from birth (including term neonates ≥ 37 weeks gestational age). Safety and efficacy are established based on clinical data and extrapolation from adults. No dosage adjustment by age is required [US].
- **Elderly Use:** No overall differences in safety or efficacy were observed; however, caution is advised due to the higher frequency of renal impairment in this population.
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    - No dose adjustment is recommended for the procedure itself, but patients are at high risk for NSF (see Section 5). 
    - Gadoterate meglumine is dialyzable; after three hemodialysis sessions, serum concentrations decrease by approximately 99.7% [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources (primarily renally excreted).

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** A paramagnetic molecule that enhances the relaxation rates of water protons. This increases signal intensity (brightness) of tissues in MRI. 
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Shortens T1 and T2 relaxation times. Disruption of the BBB allows distribution into lesions such as neoplasms or abscesses.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Volume of distribution is approximately 179–211 mL/kg (equivalent to extracellular water). No protein binding.
    - **Metabolism:** Not metabolised.
    - **Elimination:** Excreted primarily unchanged in the urine. Mean elimination half-life is approximately 1.4 hours (females) to 2.0 hours (males) in healthy adults [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Study A (CNS Imaging):** A multi-center trial of 364 adults and 38 paediatric patients (≥ 2 years). Gadoterate meglumine demonstrated statistically significant improvement in lesion visualization (contrast enhancement, border delineation, and internal morphology) compared to pre-contrast images. Improvement was seen in 56% to 94% of patients across different readers.
- **Study C (Pediatric < 2 years):** A non-randomized study of 28 patients supported the extrapolation of efficacy findings from older populations.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C–30°C (59°F–86°F).
- **Special Handling:** Do not freeze pre-filled syringes; discard if frozen. If solidification occurs in vials due to cold, allow to stand at room temperature for at least 90 minutes. Do not use if solids persist [US].

- **Legal Classification:**
