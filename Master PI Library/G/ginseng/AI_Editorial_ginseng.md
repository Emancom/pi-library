- **Brand Name**: BM Vitagene Forte [US]
- **ATC Code**: Not available
- **Dosage Form and Strength**: Round, white tablets (90mm size) for oral administration. Strengths are defined by homeopathic potencies (X/decimal scale) per 1g of product [US].

- **Manufacturer/Sponsor**: BM Private Limited (Pakistan); Marketed in the US by Bestmade Natural Products [US].
