## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); a carrier molecule involved in mitochondrial fatty acid transport.
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative [(R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium].
- **Brand Names:** Not specified in available sources [US: Generic/Baxter Healthcare Corporation].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - Intravenous Injection: 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Metabolic Disorders:** For the acute and chronic management of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that lead to secondary carnitine deficiency [US-approved].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance dialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Metabolic Disorders:**
    - **Initial/Loading Dose:** 50 mg/kg as a slow bolus (2–3 minutes) or infusion.
    - **Maintenance:** 50 mg/kg/day, often divided and administered every 3 or 4 hours (and never less than every 6 hours) via bolus or infusion. 
    - **Titration:** Subsequent daily doses may be adjusted based on clinical response and plasma carnitine levels. Doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilized in clinical practice.
- **ESRD on Hemodialysis:**
    - **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight.
    - **Administration:** Administer as a slow bolus (2–3 minutes) into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
    - **Dose Adjustment:** Adjustments may be made as early as the third or fourth week of therapy, guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma **levocarnitine** concentrations.

### Administration Instructions
- Inspect visually for particulate matter or discoloration prior to use.
- **Compatibility:** Stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when diluted in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations ranging from 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have occurred, primarily in ESRD patients. Some reactions manifest within minutes of IV administration. Discontinue treatment immediately if a severe reaction occurs and evaluate the risks/benefits before attempting re-administration [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Reports indicate that seizures may occur in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with known seizure disorders, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Monitoring:** 
    - Obtain baseline plasma carnitine levels before starting therapy. 
    - Monitor plasma carnitine concentrations weekly or monthly, alongside blood chemistries and vital signs.
    - Target plasma free carnitine concentrations are generally between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].
- **Renal Impairment (Oral vs. IV):** The safety of oral **levocarnitine** has not been established in renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in ESRD may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) that are normally excreted renally [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Clinical Trial Experience (ESRD Patients)
Adverse events reported at a frequency ≥5% in double-blind, placebo-controlled trials (regardless of causality) include:

| Body System | Adverse Event (Incidence ≥5%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Abdominal pain, accidental injury, asthenia, chest pain, fever, flu syndrome, headache, infection, injection site reaction, pain |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension, hypotension, palpitations, tachycardia |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea, nausea, vomiting, anorexia, constipation, dyspepsia |
| **Metabolic** | Hypercalcemia, hyperkalemia, hypervolemia, peripheral edema, weight changes |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness, depression, insomnia, paresthesia |
| **Respiratory** | Cough increase, dyspnea, pharyngitis, rhinitis |
| **Skin** | Pruritus, rash |

### Specific Adverse Effects
- **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea and vomiting are common. Gastritis occurs less frequently.
- **Body Odor:** Body odor has been reported during therapy.
- **Neurologic:** Seizures (see Warnings and Precautions).

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when **levocarnitine** is co-administered with warfarin.
    - **Management:** Monitor INR levels closely upon initiation of **levocarnitine** or following dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not specified by letter category in provided source.
    - **Data:** Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** 
    - **levocarnitine** is a normal component of human milk. Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied.
    - **Data:** In dairy cows, milk carnitine concentrations increase following exogenous administration.
    - **Recommendation:** Weigh the risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant against the benefits of maternal supplementation [US].
- **Fertility:** No impairment observed in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing is weight-based (50 mg/kg) for metabolic disorders; clinical trials in ESRD patients have included pediatric subjects, but specific age-related dose adjustments are not explicitly detailed beyond weight-based protocols [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** **levocarnitine** is cleared by hemodialysis. The IV formulation is specifically indicated for carnitine deficiency in ESRD. Avoid high-dose oral formulations in this population due to metabolite accumulation [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
**levocarnitine** is a naturally occurring substance required for energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].

### Pharmacokinetics
- **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
- **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces).
- **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine over 24 hours (uncorrected for endogenous levels).
- **Half-Life:** Distribution half-life is 0.585 hours; terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

### Pharmacodynamics
In ESRD patients, IV administration of **levocarnitine** (20 mg/kg) increases plasma carnitine concentrations. Trough levels typically stabilize or increase over 8 to 24 weeks of therapy [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients (20 mg/kg three times weekly), mean pre-dialysis carnitine levels rose from ~20 µmol/L at baseline to 190 ± 55 µmol/L after 8 weeks.
- **Dose-Ranging Data:** Studies comparing 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses three times weekly showed dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine concentrations at 12 and 24 weeks [US].
- **Clinical Outcomes Note:** While **levocarnitine** effectively increases carnitine concentrations in ESRD, the impact on specific signs, symptoms, or clinical outcomes in this population remains undetermined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Precautions:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat.
- **Handling:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial; the formulation contains no preservatives [US].
