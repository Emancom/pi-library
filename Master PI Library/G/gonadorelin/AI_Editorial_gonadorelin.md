# Editorial Product Information: Gonadorelin

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on Product Information (PI) provided by the following regulatory authority: **United States** (Food and Drug Administration - FDA).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. It is intended for informational purposes and synthesises clinical data from a veterinary-specific product information document.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official PI for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. The source material refers to a product approved exclusively for veterinary use.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Gonadorelin hydrochloride (synthetic gonadotropin-releasing hormone [GnRH]) [US].
- **Brand Name(s):** **Factrel** [US].
- **ATC Code:** H01CA01 (Gonadorelin) [Note: Not explicitly stated in source].
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection containing 50 mcg gonadorelin (as hydrochloride) per mL [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intramuscular (IM) injection [US].
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Zoetis Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Treatment of Ovarian Follicular Cysts:** Indicated for use in lactating dairy cows, beef cows, and replacement dairy and beef heifers to reduce the number of days to first estrus [US].
- **Synchronization of Estrous Cycles:** Indicated for use in conjunction with dinoprost tromethamine (e.g., **Lutalyse**) to allow for fixed-time artificial insemination (FTAI) in lactating dairy cows [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Ovarian Follicular Cysts
- **Dose:** 2 mL (100 mcg gonadorelin) administered as a single intramuscular injection [US].

### Fixed-Time Artificial Insemination (FTAI)
The following treatment regimen is approved for synchronizing estrous cycles in lactating dairy cows [US]:
- **Day 0:** Administer first dose of **Factrel** (2–4 mL; 100–200 mcg gonadorelin) IM.
- **Day 6 to 8:** Administer 25 mg dinoprost (as dinoprost tromethamine) IM.
- **30 to 72 Hours after Dinoprost:** Administer a second dose of **Factrel** (2–4 mL; 100–200 mcg gonadorelin) IM.
- **FTAI:** Perform 0 to 24 hours after the second dose of **Factrel**, or inseminate upon detection of estrus.

## 4. Contraindications
- Not specified in the available source document for the target animal population [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Human Safety:** For use in animals only. Not for human use. Keep out of reach of children [US].
- **Residue Warnings:** No withdrawal period or milk discard time is required when the product is used according to label directions [US].
- **General Caution:** Federal law (US) restricts this drug to use by or on the order of a licensed veterinarian [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
In clinical field studies involving 1142 cows, 148 adverse health events were documented in 118 animals [US]. 
- **Observed Events:** Mastitis, lameness, and pneumonia.
- **Causality:** These events are common conditions in dairy cattle and were not considered treatment-related [US].
- **Injection Site:** No evidence of irritation at the injection site was found in any animal [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Dinoprost Tromethamine:** Used concurrently in a specific sequence to facilitate synchronization of estrous cycles [US].
- No other pharmacokinetic or pharmacodynamic interactions were specified in the available source [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Single intramuscular doses of 500 mcg (5 times the standard cyst dose) did not interfere with pregnancy in target animals [US]. 
- **Lactation:** No milk discard time is required [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric/Heifers:** Approved for use in replacement dairy and beef heifers for the treatment of ovarian cysts [US].
- **Elderly/Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Gonadorelin is chemically and physiologically identical to endogenous gonadotropin-releasing hormone (GnRH). It is produced by the hypothalamus and stimulates the anterior pituitary to release luteinizing hormone (LH) and follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In animals with follicular cysts (enlarged non-ovulatory follicles), the administration of **Factrel** induces the release of endogenous LH, which causes ovulation or regression of the cysts. During the estrous cycle, the induced LH release triggers the ovulation of dominant ovarian follicles [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified in available sources.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Synchronization Study:** A field study of 1142 cows across six commercial dairies compared control (0 mL) to gonadorelin doses of 2, 3, or 4 mL in an FTAI regimen.
- **Results:** Pregnancy rates were significantly higher in all gonadorelin-treated groups compared to those receiving dinoprost tromethamine alone. Pregnancy rates were 17.1% (control), 27.3% (2 mL), 29.1% (3 mL), and 32.2% (4 mL) [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) [US].
- **Excursions:** Permitted up to 25°C (77°F) [US].
- **Stability:** Use contents within 1 month of first vial puncture [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Prescription animal drug (Rx-only). Approved under NADA # 139-237 [US].
