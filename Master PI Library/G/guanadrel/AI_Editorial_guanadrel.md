# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of levocarnitine injection for use by healthcare professionals.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and serves as a clinical reference. It is not a substitute for the official Product Information mandated for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt. 
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative (carrier molecule).
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection, USP [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - **Intravenous Injection:** 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) single-dose vial [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with end-stage renal disease who are undergoing maintenance dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
- **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion [US].
- **Loading Dose:** Often administered in severe metabolic crises, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours [US].
- **Frequency:** Should be administered every 3 or 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours [US].
- **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses are recommended in the range of 50 mg/kg or as clinically required. The maximum recorded dose administered is 300 mg/kg [US].

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
- **Administration:** Administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line after each dialysis session [US].
- **Titration:** Dose adjustments should be guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].

### Monitoring
- **Baseline:** Obtain plasma carnitine concentrations before initiating parenteral therapy [US].
- **Frequency:** Weekly and monthly monitoring is recommended [US].
- **Targets:** Plasma free carnitine concentrations should ideally be maintained between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions may occur within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately [US].
- **Seizure Risk:** Seizures have been reported in patients receiving levocarnitine, regardless of pre-existing seizure history. In patients with a known seizure disorder, an increase in seizure frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Monitoring Requirements:** Periodic monitoring of blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations is required [US].
- **Thromboembolic/Anticoagulant Effects:** Reports of increased International Normalised Ratio (INR) have occurred when used with warfarin. Monitor INR levels after initiation or dose adjustment [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common Adverse Effects (ESRD Clinical Trials)
Based on placebo-controlled trials in ESRD patients, the following events occurred at a frequency of ≥5% [US]:

| Body System | Adverse Event |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Injection site reaction (33%), Pain (30%), Flu syndrome (25%), Headache (22%), Abdominal pain (9%) |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension (20%), Hypotension (14%), Tachycardia (6%), Chest pain (12%) |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea (16%), Vomiting (15%), Nausea (8%) |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis (23%), Cough increase (9%), Dyspnea (7%) |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness (13%), Paresthesia (5%), Depression (5%) |
| **Metabolic** | Hypercalcemia (9%), Anemia (6%) |

### Other Effects
- **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea and vomiting; gastritis [US].
- **Body Odour:** Transient body odour has been observed [US].
- **Serious:** Anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, bronchospasm, and seizures [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Concomitant use may result in an increased INR. Healthcare providers should monitor INR levels closely following the initiation of levocarnitine or any change in dosage [US].
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not explicitly assigned in the provided source; however, reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at doses up to 3.8 times the human dose revealed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine concentrations in milk. The risks to the infant of excess carnitine must be weighed against the benefits of supplementation to the mother [US].
- **Fertility:** No impairment of fertility was observed in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Used for the treatment of inborn errors of metabolism (metabolic disorders). Safety and efficacy in ESRD paediatric patients have not been specifically detailed in the provided clinical trial data beyond general metabolic use [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in the source document [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    - The safety and efficacy of **oral** levocarnitine have not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency. 
    - Chronic high doses of oral levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function or ESRD may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites, trimethylamine (TMA) and trimethylamine-N-oxide (TMAO), which are normally excreted in urine [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring carrier molecule required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In patients with inborn errors of metabolism, it promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters, which are rapidly excreted [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and [³H]-γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Following a single IV bolus (20 mg/kg), approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours. The mean apparent terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of ESRD patients (n=12), 20 mg/kg administered three times weekly after dialysis increased mean pre-dialysis plasma levocarnitine from 20 µmol/L to 190 µmol/L after 8 weeks [US].
- **Bioavailability:** Absolute bioavailability of oral formulations is approximately 15% to 16% [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Protection:** Protect from freezing.
- **Handling:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial; the formulation contains no preservative [US].
- **Stability in Solution:** Compatible and stable for 24 hours in PVC bags at room temperature when mixed with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Rx-only [US].
