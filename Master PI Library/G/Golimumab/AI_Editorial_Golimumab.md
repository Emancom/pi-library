# Editorial Product Information: Golimumab

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration)
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of the Product Information (PI) for **golimumab**, synthesised for use by healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and clinical reference tool. It is not a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult the current local PI for definitive regulatory requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Golimumab (rmc)
- **Chemical Class:** Human IgG1κ monoclonal antibody (produced by a recombinant DNA-derived murine hybridoma cell line)
- **Brand Name(s):** **Simponi**, **Simponi IV** [Australian]
- **ATC Code:** L04AB06
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):**
    - **Subcutaneous Injection (Simponi):**
        - 50 mg in 0.5 mL (pre-filled syringe or SmartJect injector pen)
        - 100 mg in 1.0 mL (pre-filled syringe or SmartJect injector pen)
    - **Intravenous Infusion (Simponi IV):**
        - 50 mg in 4 mL (12.5 mg/mL) single-use vial
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Subcutaneous (SC) injection; Intravenous (IV) infusion.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Janssen-Cilag Pty Ltd [Australian]

## 2. Approved Indications
### Subcutaneous Administration (**Simponi**)
- **Rheumatoid Arthritis (RA):** In combination with methotrexate (MTX) for moderate to severely active RA in adults where DMARD response has been inadequate. Shown to inhibit progression of joint damage via X-ray.
- **Psoriatic Arthritis (PsA):** Alone or with MTX for active and progressive PsA in adults with inadequate DMARD response. Shown to inhibit peripheral joint damage progression and improve physical function.
- **Ankylosing Spondylitis (AS):** Treatment of active AS in adults.
- **Non-radiographic Axial Spondyloarthritis (nr-Axial SpA):** Treatment of active nr-Axial SpA with objective signs of inflammation (elevated CRP or MRI evidence) in adults with inadequate response/intolerance to NSAIDs.
- **Ulcerative Colitis (UC):** Treatment of moderately to severely active UC in adults with inadequate response to conventional therapy.

### Intravenous Administration (**Simponi IV**)
- **Rheumatoid Arthritis (RA):** In combination with MTX for moderate to severely active RA in adults.
- **Psoriatic Arthritis (PsA):** Alone or with MTX for active and progressive PsA in adults.
- **Ankylosing Spondylitis (AS):** Treatment of active AS in adults.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Subcutaneous (RA, PsA, AS, nr-Axial SpA):** 50 mg injected once monthly on the same date each month.
- **Subcutaneous (UC):** Induction dose of 200 mg at Week 0, followed by 100 mg at Week 2. Maintenance dose is 100 mg every 4 weeks.
- **Intravenous (RA, PsA, AS):** 2 mg/kg administered as a 30-minute infusion at Weeks 0 and 4, then every 8 weeks thereafter.

### Administration Instructions
- **Subcutaneous:** Patients may self-inject after training. Rotate injection sites; do not inject into tender, bruised, or hard skin.
- **Intravenous:** Must be diluted in 0.9% w/v sodium chloride (or 0.45% w/v sodium chloride) to a total volume of 100 mL. Use a 0.22-micrometer or smaller in-line, sterile, low protein-binding filter. Do not co-administer with other drugs in the same IV line.

### Missed Dose
- **Subcutaneous:** If a dose is missed, it should be administered as soon as remembered. The next dose should then be scheduled from that date (monthly) or as originally planned depending on clinical judgement. [Australian]

## 4. Contraindications
- **Active tuberculosis (TB)** or other severe infections (e.g., sepsis, opportunistic infections).
- **Moderate to severe heart failure** (NYHA Class III/IV).
- **Concurrent administration** with anakinra or abatacept.
- **Hypersensitivity** to golimumab or any excipients.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Infections:** Serious and sometimes fatal infections (bacterial, mycobacterial, invasive fungal, viral) have been reported. Do not initiate in patients with active infections. Monitor closely for 5 months post-treatment due to the long half-life.
- **Tuberculosis:** Patients must be evaluated for latent and active TB prior to and during treatment. Initiate treatment for latent TB before starting **golimumab**.
- **Hepatitis B (HBV) Reactivation:** Reactivation can occur in chronic carriers. Screen all patients for HBV before initiation. Discontinue if reactivation occurs.
- **Malignancies:** Lymphoma and other malignancies have been reported. A rare, often fatal, hepatosplenic T-cell lymphoma (HSTCL) has been reported in patients (mostly young males with IBD) using TNF-blockers with azathioprine or 6-mercaptopurine. 
- **Heart Failure:** Use with caution in patients with mild heart failure (NYHA I/II). Discontinue if new or worsening symptoms occur.
- **Demyelinating Disorders:** Use with caution in patients with central or peripheral nervous system demyelinating disorders (e.g., Multiple Sclerosis, Guillain-Barré syndrome).
- **Haematological Reactions:** Pancytopaenia, leucopaenia, and neutropaenia have been reported. Advise patients to seek medical help for symptoms of blood dyscrasias (fever, bruising, bleeding).
- **Vaccinations:** Live vaccines should not be given concurrently. Infants exposed *in utero* should not receive live vaccines for 6 months after the mother’s last dose.
- **Latex Sensitivity:** The needle cover of the SC pre-filled syringe and pen contains dry natural rubber (latex) and may cause reactions in sensitive individuals. [Australian]

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Very Common (>10%):** Upper respiratory tract infection (nasopharyngitis, pharyngitis).
- **Common (1–10%):** 
    - Bacterial infections (cellulitis, pneumonia), viral infections (influenza, herpes).
    - Lower respiratory tract infections, bronchitis, sinusitis.
    - Anaemia, leucopaenia.
    - Dizziness, headache, paraesthesia.
    - Hypertension.
    - Elevated liver enzymes (ALT/AST).
    - Rash, alopecia, dermatitis, pruritus.
    - Pyrexia, asthenia, injection site reactions (erythema, pain, bruising).
- **Uncommon (0.1–1%):** Sepsis, septic shock, opportunistic infections, thrombocytopaenia, thyroid disorders, skin cancers (non-melanoma), visual disorders, arrhythmia, thrombosis.
- **Rare (<0.1%):** Tuberculosis, HBV reactivation, lymphoma, leukaemia, systemic vasculitis, lupus-like syndrome, interstitial lung disease.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Anakinra / Abatacept:** Concurrent use is contraindicated/not recommended due to increased risk of serious infections without added clinical benefit.
- **Rituximab:** Increased risk of infection observed in RA patients treated with rituximab followed by a TNF-blocker.
- **Methotrexate:** Concomitant use increases steady-state trough concentrations of **golimumab** in RA, PsA, and AS, but dose adjustment is not required.
- **Live Vaccines:** Potential for clinical infection/dissemination; avoid concurrent use.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Australian Category C:** TNF-blockers cross the placenta and may affect immune response in the infant. Golimumab should only be used during pregnancy if clearly needed.
    - Women of childbearing potential should use adequate contraception during and for at least 6 months after the last treatment.
- **Breastfeeding:** Detection of **golimumab** in monkey breast milk is confirmed (low concentrations). Use in humans is not recommended; breastfeeding should be discontinued for 6 months post-treatment.
- **Fertility:** Potential effects have not been investigated in animal studies.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Not recommended for use in children under 18 years due to lack of efficacy and safety data. [Australian]
- **Elderly Use:** No overall differences in safety/efficacy in patients ≥65 years, but use with caution due to the higher baseline risk of infection in this population.
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Specific studies have not been conducted. No dose recommendations can be made.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** A human monoclonal antibody that forms high-affinity, stable complexes with both soluble and transmembrane forms of human TNF-alpha. This prevents TNF-alpha from binding to its receptors, thereby inhibiting the inflammatory process and structural damage associated with chronic inflammatory diseases.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Absolute bioavailability of 50 mg SC dose is approximately 51%.
    - **Distribution:** Distributed primarily in the circulatory system (Volume of distribution 58–126 mL/kg).
    - **Excretion:** Mean systemic clearance is 4.9–6.7 mL/day/kg.
    - **Half-life:** Approximately 12 ± 3 days.
    - **Steady State:** Reached by Week 12 when administered every 4 weeks.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Rheumatoid Arthritis:** Studied in GO-FORWARD (DMARD-inadequate), GO-AFTER (Anti-TNF experienced), GO-BEFORE (MTX-naïve), and GO-FURTHER (IV administration). Significant improvements in ACR 20/50/70 responses and inhibition of structural damage (vdH-S score) were observed.
- **Psoriatic Arthritis:** GO-REVEAL (SC) and GO-VIBRANT (IV) demonstrated significant improvement in ACR 20 and PASI 75 responses compared to placebo.
- **Ankylosing Spondylitis:** GO-RAISE (SC) and GO-ALIVE (IV) showed significant ASAS 20/40 responses.
- **Ulcerative Colitis:** PURSUIT-Induction and Maintenance trials showed significant clinical response, remission, and mucosal healing through Week 54.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Refrigeration:** Store at 2°C to 8°C. Do not freeze or shake. Protect from light.
- **Room Temperature Allowance:** **Simponi** (SC or IV) may be stored at room temperature (up to 25°C) for a single period of up to 30 days in the original carton. Once removed from refrigeration, it must not be returned. Discard if not used within 30 days. [Australian]
- **Diluted IV Solution:** Should be infused within 6 hours of preparation (stored at room temperature).

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule:** Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine) [Australian].
