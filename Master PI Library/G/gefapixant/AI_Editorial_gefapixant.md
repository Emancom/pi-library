# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis was prepared using official Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of levocarnitine for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises complex regulatory data into a clinical reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not intended to replace the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority’s approved labeling for definitive prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP).
- **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule/amino acid derivative.
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter Healthcare) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - Sterile injection: 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Indicated for the acute and chronic management of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that result in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Pediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:**
    - **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion.
    - **Acute Crisis:** A loading dose is often given, followed by an equivalent dose divided over the subsequent 24 hours.
    - **Frequency:** Administer every 3 or 4 hours; intervals should never be less than every 6 hours.
    - **Maximum Dose:** Doses up to 300 mg/kg have been administered [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight).
    - **Administration:** Slow 2 to 3 minute bolus injection into the venous return line after each dialysis session.
    - **Adjustments:** Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be made as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough plasma concentrations.

### Administration Instructions
- Inspect visually for particulate matter or discoloration prior to use.
- Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as it contains no preservatives.

### Monitoring
- **Metabolic Patients:** Obtain baseline plasma carnitine levels. Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine: 35 to 60 µmol/L) is recommended.
- **ESRD Patients:** Monitor trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Normal levels range from 40 to 50 µmol/L.

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients on dialysis. Reactions can occur within minutes of administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue the drug and initiate medical treatment [US].
- **Seizures:** Levocarnitine has been associated with seizures in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In patients with a known seizure history, an increase in seizure frequency or severity has been reported [US].
- **Accumulation of Metabolites (Oral Formulation):** While this PI refers to the IV form, it is noted that chronic high doses of *oral* levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions in ESRD patients are often confounded by the underlying pathology of end-stage renal disease.

### Frequency ≥5% (Regardless of Causality) [US]:
| Body System | Adverse Event |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Injection site reaction (33–38%), pain (30–35%), headache (3–37%), chest pain (6–15%), flu syndrome (15–29%), fever (5–12%), abdominal pain (5–21%) |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension (18–21%), hypotension (3–19%), tachycardia (5–9%) |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea (9–35%), vomiting (9–21%), nausea (5–12%) |
| **Metabolic** | Hypercalcemia (6–15%), hypervolemia (3–12%) |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis (15–27%), cough increase (10–18%), dyspnea (3–11%) |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness (10–18%), paresthesia (3–12%) |

### Serious/Postmarketing Adverse Effects
- **Neurologic:** Seizures.
- **Immune:** Anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, bronchospasm.
- **Gastrointestinal:** Body odor, gastritis (less frequent).

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in the International Normalized Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is used concomitantly with warfarin. 
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels after initiating levocarnitine or following dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not specified, but text indicates]: Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at doses up to 3.8 times the human dose showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Animal data (dairy cows) suggest increased carnitine concentrations in milk following administration. Risk-benefit analysis should consider the benefits of breastfeeding against the risk of excess carnitine intake in the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing is weight-based (see Section 3). Specific safety and efficacy profiles are consistent with adult populations for the indicated metabolic and ESRD conditions.
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically detailed in available sources; weight-based dosing applies.
- **Renal Impairment:** Primarily indicated for use in ESRD patients on dialysis. No dose adjustment is specified for other stages of renal impairment, though the IV route avoids the TMA/TMAO accumulation seen with oral dosing in renal failure [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent ATP production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not protein-bound. Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and [³H]-γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces).
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours. The apparent terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours. It is easily removed by hemodialysis [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of ESRD patients (n=12) receiving 20 mg/kg three times weekly after dialysis, trough levocarnitine levels increased from a baseline of ~21 µmol/L to ~190 µmol/L at 8 weeks.
- **Dose-Ranging Studies:** Trials using 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses showed a dose-proportional increase in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks. While carnitine levels increased, the effect on specific clinical outcomes in this population has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Precautions:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat.
- **Stability:** Compatible and stable for 24 hours in PVC bags at room temperature when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer's (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8.0 mg/mL) [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only (Prescription only) [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources.
