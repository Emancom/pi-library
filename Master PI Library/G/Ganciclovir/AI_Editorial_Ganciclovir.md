# Editorial Product Information: Ganciclovir

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration)
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of ganciclovir product information, synthesised for use by healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. At the time of compilation, only the Australian source document was provided; information from other jurisdictions (US, Europe, Canada, Japan) is listed as "Not specified in available sources" where comparative data is absent.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Ganciclovir (as ganciclovir sodium)
- **Chemical Class:** Synthetic nucleoside analogue of 2-deoxyguanosine
- **Brand Names:** **Cymevene** [Australian]
- **ATC Code:** J05AB06
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** 
    - Sterile lyophilised powder for intravenous (IV) infusion: 500 mg per vial (equivalent to 543 mg ganciclovir sodium) [Australian]
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous infusion only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Pharmaco (Australia) Ltd [Australian]

## 2. Approved Indications
- **CMV Disease Treatment:** Palliative treatment of confirmed sight-threatening cytomegalovirus (CMV) disease in individuals with AIDS or other severe immunocompromise [Australian].
- **CMV Pneumonitis:** Treatment of confirmed CMV pneumonitis in bone marrow transplant recipients [Australian].
- **CMV Prophylaxis:** Prevention of CMV infection and disease in patients at risk following bone marrow or solid organ transplantation [Australian].
- **Note on Limitations:** Not indicated for congenital or neonatal CMV disease, nor for CMV treatment in non-immunocompromised patients [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults with Normal Renal Function)
- **Induction Treatment:** 5 mg/kg via IV infusion (over 1 hour) every 12 hours for 14 to 21 days [Australian].
- **Maintenance Treatment:** For patients at risk of relapse: 5 mg/kg daily 7 days per week, or 6 mg/kg daily 5 days per week [Australian].
- **Transplant Prophylaxis:** 
    - **Liver:** 5 mg/kg every 12 hours for 7–14 days, then maintenance [Australian].
    - **Heart:** 5 mg/kg every 12 hours for 14 days, then 6 mg/kg daily 5 days per week [Australian].
    - **Bone Marrow:** 5 mg/kg every 12 hours for 7 days, then maintenance [Australian].

### Administration Instructions
- **Reconstitution:** Dissolve 500 mg in 10 mL sterile water for injection (50 mg/mL). Do **not** use bacteriostatic water containing parabens [Australian].
- **Dilution:** Further dilute in compatible fluids (NS, D5W, Ringer's, or Hartmann's) to a concentration $\le$ 10 mg/mL [Australian].
- **Infusion Rate:** Must be delivered over exactly 1 hour. Do **not** administer via rapid or bolus injection due to toxicity risks [Australian].
- **Handling:** Solutions are highly alkaline (pH ~11). Avoid contact with skin and mucous membranes. Use of gloves and safety glasses is recommended during preparation [Australian].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to ganciclovir, valganciclovir, or any excipients [Australian].
- **Pregnancy and Lactation:** Contraindicated in pregnant and breastfeeding women [Australian].
- **Severe Cytopenia:** Must not be started if:
    - Absolute Neutrophil Count (ANC) < 0.5 x $10^9$/L (500 cells/µL)
    - Platelet count < 25 x $10^9$/L (25,000/µL)
    - Haemoglobin < 80 g/L (8 g/dL) [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None specified in provided source; however, significant warnings regarding myelosuppression and reproductive toxicity are prominent [Australian].
- **Haematologic Toxicity:** Severe leucopenia, neutropenia, anaemia, and thrombocytopenia occur frequently. Monitoring of CBC with platelets is mandatory. Neutropenia typically occurs within the first two weeks of induction [Australian].
- **Reproductive Hazard:** Considered a potential teratogen, carcinogen, and mutagen. It may cause temporary or permanent inhibition of spermatogenesis in males and suppression of fertility in females [Australian].
- **Cross-Hypersensitivity:** Potential cross-reactivity with acyclovir and penciclovir due to structural similarity [Australian].
- **Renal Impairment:** Increased half-life and plasma levels occur; dose adjustment and frequent monitoring (every 2 weeks) are required [Australian].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common Adverse Effects
| Body System | HIV-infected patients ($\ge$ 2%) | Bone Marrow Transplant ($\ge$ 5%) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Haemic/Lymphatic** | Neutropenia (25.7%), Anaemia (19.7%) | Pancytopenia (31%), Leucopenia (20%) |
| **Gastrointestinal** | Diarrhoea (26.5%), Abdominal pain (9%) | Diarrhoea (24%), Nausea (20%) |
| **Systemic** | Pyrexia (35.9%), Sepsis (6.1%) | Headache (15%), Pyrexia (11%) |
| **Metabolic** | Increased creatinine (3.2%) | Increased creatinine (16%) |
| **Cardiovascular** | Not specified | Tachycardia (16%), Hypertension (20% in heart transplant) |

### Serious/Rare Adverse Effects
- **Fatalities:** Sepsis, pancreatitis, and multiple organ failure have been reported [Australian].
- **Post-marketing:** Stevens-Johnson syndrome, anaphylaxis, rhabdomyolysis, hepatic failure, and Torsades de Pointes (rare) [Australian].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Imipenem-cilastatin:** Reports of generalised seizures; avoid concomitant use unless benefits outweigh risks [Australian].
- **Zidovudine:** Increased risk of haematologic toxicity (neutropenia/anaemia); patients may not tolerate full doses of both [Australian].
- **Didanosine:** Ganciclovir significantly increases didanosine AUC (38-67%). Monitor closely for didanosine toxicity (e.g., pancreatitis) [Australian].
- **Probenecid:** Increases ganciclovir AUC by ~40% and reduces renal clearance; monitor for ganciclovir toxicity [Australian].
- **Myelosuppressive Agents:** Enhanced toxicity with dapsone, pentamidine, flucytosine, vincristine, adriamycin, and amphotericin B [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** **Category D** [Australian]. High likelihood of foetal damage. Effective contraception is required for women during and 30 days post-treatment.
- **Breastfeeding:** Contraindicated. It is unknown if ganciclovir is excreted in human milk, but animal data confirms excretion in rat milk. Discontinue nursing or the drug [Australian].
- **Fertility:** Males should use barrier contraception during and for at least 90 days after treatment [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety/efficacy not established. Use with extreme caution due to long-term carcinogenicity risks. Not indicated for neonatal/congenital CMV [Australian].
- **Elderly Use:** No specific studies. Dose selection should be cautious, reflecting the higher frequency of decreased renal function [Australian].
- **Renal Impairment:** Dose must be modified based on CrCl [Australian]:

| CrCl (mL/min) | Induction Dose (mg/kg) | Interval (hrs) | Maintenance Dose (mg/kg) | Interval (hrs) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| $\ge$ 70 | 5.0 | 12 | 5.0 | 24 |
| 50 – 69 | 2.5 | 12 | 2.5 | 24 |
| 25 – 49 | 2.5 | 24 | 1.25 | 24 |
| 10 – 24 | 1.25 | 24 | 0.625 | 24 |
| < 10 | 1.25 | 3x/week post-HD | 0.625 | 3x/week post-HD |

- **Hepatic Impairment:** Safety and efficacy not studied; however, ganciclovir is primarily renally excreted [Australian].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Ganciclovir is phosphorylated to ganciclovir-monophosphate by a viral kinase (UL97 in CMV). Cellular kinases further convert it to ganciclovir-triphosphate, which inhibits viral DNA synthesis by competitive inhibition of viral DNA polymerase and direct incorporation into viral DNA, causing chain termination [Australian].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** 
    - **Distribution:** Vd correlates with body weight (0.54–0.87 L/kg). Penetrates CSF. Protein binding is low (1–2%) [Australian].
    - **Metabolism:** Not significantly metabolised [Australian].
    - **Elimination:** Primarily renal (>90% unchanged drug) via glomerular filtration and active tubular secretion. Half-life is ~2.9 hours in normal renal function [Australian].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **CMV Retinitis (ICM 1653):** A randomised study compared IV vs. oral ganciclovir maintenance. Mean time to progression was 62 days for IV vs. 57 days for oral [Australian].
- **Transplant Prophylaxis (ICM 1496/1689):** In heart transplant patients, ganciclovir reduced CMV illness from 42.5% to 15.8% (p=0.0004). In bone marrow transplant, CMV disease at Day 100 was reduced from 42.9% to 2.7% (p=0.00005) [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Unopened Vials:** Store below 30 °C [Australian].
- **Reconstituted Solution:** Should be used within 24 hours. May be stored at 2–8 °C for up to 24 hours (do not freeze) [Australian].
- **Special Stability:** Compounding centres with validated aseptic techniques may apply a shelf-life of 15 days when refrigerated at 2–8 °C, provided the diluent is 0.9% NaCl or D5W [Australian].
