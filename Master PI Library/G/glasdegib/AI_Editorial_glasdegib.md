# Editorial Product Information: Glasdegib

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA)
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of glasdegib for healthcare professionals, synthesising regulatory information into a single reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. At the time of this publication, only the United States (FDA) source documentation was available for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Glasdegib (as maleate). 
- **Chemical Class:** Hedgehog pathway inhibitor.
- **Brand Name(s):** **DAURISMO** [US].
- **ATC Code:** L01XJ03.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - **Film-coated tablets:** 25 mg (round, yellow, debossed with "Pfizer" and "GLS 25") [US].
    - **Film-coated tablets:** 100 mg (round, pale orange, debossed with "Pfizer" and "GLS 100") [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Pfizer Laboratories Div Pfizer Inc [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Acute Myeloid Leukemia (AML):** Indicated in combination with low-dose cytarabine (LDAC) for the treatment of newly-diagnosed AML in adult patients who are ≥75 years old or who have comorbidities that preclude the use of intensive induction chemotherapy [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** 100 mg taken orally once daily [US].
- **Dosing Schedule:** Administer on days 1 to 28 of a 28-day cycle in combination with cytarabine (20 mg subcutaneously twice daily on days 1 to 10). Treatment should continue for at least 6 cycles to allow for clinical response, unless unacceptable toxicity or loss of disease control occurs [US].
- **Administration Instructions:** 
    - May be taken with or without food.
    - Tablets must not be split, crushed, or chewed.
    - Administer at approximately the same time each day [US].
- **Missed Dose:** If a dose is missed or not taken at the usual time, it should be administered as soon as possible, provided it is at least 12 hours before the next scheduled dose. Resume the normal schedule the following day. Do not administer two doses within 12 hours [US].
- **Vomiting:** If a dose is vomited, do not take a replacement dose; wait until the next scheduled dose [US].
- **Dose Modifications for Interactions:** 
    - **Moderate CYP3A4 Inducers:** If concomitant use cannot be avoided, increase glasdegib to 200 mg daily (if the current dose is 100 mg) or 100 mg daily (if the current dose is 50 mg). Resume the original dose 7 days after the inducer is discontinued [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None specified in available sources [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning: Embryo-Fetal Toxicity [US]:** Glasdegib can cause embryo-fetal death or severe birth defects. It is embryotoxic, fetotoxic, and teratogenic in animals. Pregnancy must be excluded before initiation. Females of reproductive potential must use effective contraception during treatment and for 30 days after the final dose. Males must use condoms with pregnant partners or female partners of reproductive potential during treatment and for 30 days after the final dose.
- **Blood and Semen Donation:** Patients must not donate blood or blood products [US] or semen [US] during treatment and for at least 30 days after the last dose to prevent potential exposure to a fetus.
- **QTc Interval Prolongation:** Prolongation of the QTc interval and ventricular arrhythmias (including ventricular fibrillation and tachycardia) can occur. 
    - **Monitoring:** Obtain ECGs and electrolytes prior to initiation, approximately one week after start, and then monthly for the first two months. Monitor more frequently in patients with congestive heart failure, electrolyte abnormalities, or those taking other QTc-prolonging medications [US].
- **Musculoskeletal Adverse Reactions:** Myalgia and muscle spasms (including Grade 3 events) are common.
    - **Monitoring:** Obtain baseline creatine phosphokinase (CPK) and serum creatinine. Monitor CPK levels as clinically indicated (e.g., if muscle symptoms are reported) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Very Common (>10%):** Anemia (43%), fatigue (36%), hemorrhage (36%), febrile neutropenia (31%), musculoskeletal pain (30%), edema (30%), nausea (29%), thrombocytopenia (30%), dyspnea (23%), decreased appetite (21%), dysgeusia (21%), mucositis (21%), constipation (20%), rash (20%), and renal insufficiency (19%) [US].
- **Common (1–10%):** Atrial arrhythmia (13%), hyponatremia (11%), dizziness (18%), and headache (12%) [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** 
    - Reported in 79% of patients in clinical trials. 
    - Most common serious reactions (≥5%): Febrile neutropenia (29%), pneumonia (23%), hemorrhage (12%), anemia (7%), and sepsis (7%) [US].
    - Adverse reactions leading to death in ≥2% of patients included pneumonia, febrile neutropenia, sepsis, sudden death, and myocardial infarction [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Strong CYP3A4 Inhibitors:** Co-administration increases glasdegib plasma concentrations (2.4-fold increase in AUC). Consider alternative therapies or monitor closely for increased toxicity and QTc prolongation [US].
- **Strong CYP3A4 Inducers:** Avoid concomitant use; these significantly decrease glasdegib concentrations and may reduce efficacy [US].
- **Moderate CYP3A4 Inducers:** Avoid concomitant use. If unavoidable, increase glasdegib dose (see Section 3) [US].
- **QTc Prolonging Drugs:** Avoid co-administration. If unavoidable, increase ECG monitoring frequency [US].
- **Gastric Acid Reducing Agents:** Co-administration with rabeprazole decreased glasdegib Cmax by 20% but did not alter AUC; no dose adjustment is required [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not assigned (narrative warning). 
    - Glasdegib is not recommended during pregnancy due to high risk of teratogenicity and fetal death [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** There are no data on glasdegib in human milk. Due to the potential for serious adverse reactions in the breastfed child, advise patients not to breastfeed during treatment and for at least 30 days after the last dose [US].
- **Fertility:** Based on animal studies, glasdegib may impair male fertility. Effects on male reproductive organs were not fully reversible in rats. Men should seek advice on fertility preservation prior to treatment [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established [US]. Animal studies showed adverse changes in growing bone (epiphyseal plate closure) and teeth [US].
- **Elderly Use:** In clinical trials, 98% of subjects were ≥65 years and 60% were ≥75 years. No overall differences in safety or effectiveness were observed between these patients and younger patients, though data for those <65 is limited [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    - **Mild to Severe (eGFR 15 to 89 mL/min):** No dose adjustment recommended.
    - **Severe (eGFR 15 to 29 mL/min):** Monitor more closely for adverse reactions, including QTc prolongation, due to increased exposure (2.1-fold increase in AUC) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** 
    - **Mild to Severe (Child-Pugh A, B, or C):** No dose adjustment recommended based on pharmacokinetics [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Glasdegib inhibits the Hedgehog pathway by binding to and inhibiting Smoothened (SMO), a transmembrane protein involved in signal transduction. Inhibition leads to decreased activation of GLI transcription factors and reduced expression of Hedgehog target genes [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Absolute bioavailability is 77%. Median Tmax is 1.3 to 1.8 hours. A high-fat, high-calorie meal reduces Cmax by 31% and AUC by 16% [US].
    - **Distribution:** 91% protein bound in vitro. Vz/F is 188 L [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily metabolised via CYP3A4, with minor contributions from CYP2C8 and UGT1A9 [US].
    - **Elimination:** Mean half-life is 17.4 hours. Clearance is 6.45 L/h. Excretion is 49% in urine (17% unchanged) and 42% in feces (20% unchanged) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **BRIGHT AML 1003 (NCT01546038):** A multicenter, open-label trial of 115 patients with newly diagnosed AML who were ≥75 years old or had comorbidities. Patients were randomised 2:1 to receive glasdegib + LDAC (n=77) or LDAC alone (n=38).
    - **Primary Outcome:** Overall Survival (OS).
    - **Results:** Median OS was 8.3 months (95% CI: 4.4, 12.2) for the glasdegib + LDAC arm versus 4.3 months (95% CI: 1.9, 5.7) for the LDAC alone arm (HR 0.46; 95% CI: 0.30, 0.71; p=0.0002) [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Excursions:** Permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F and 86°F) [US].
