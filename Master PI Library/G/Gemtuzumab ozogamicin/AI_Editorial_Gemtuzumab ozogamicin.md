# Editorial Product Information: Gemtuzumab ozogamicin

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial has been synthesised from the following official Product Information (PI) source: Australian (TGA).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of **gemtuzumab ozogamicin** for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical data, regulatory warnings, and therapeutic guidelines.
- **Note:** This synthesis is an expert editorial interpretation and does not replace the official PI required for prescribing within any specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Gemtuzumab ozogamicin.
- **Chemical Class:** CD33-directed antibody-drug conjugate (ADC). It consists of a humanised IgG4 kappa antibody (hP67.6) covalently linked to the cytotoxic agent N-acetyl gamma calicheamicin.
- **Brand Name:** **Mylotarg** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** L01FX02.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Powder for injection; 5 mg single-dose vial. Upon reconstitution, the solution contains 1 mg/mL.
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous infusion.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Pfizer Australia Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Acute Myeloid Leukaemia (AML):** Indicated for use in combination with standard anthracycline and cytarabine (AraC) for the treatment of patients aged 15 years and older with previously untreated, *de novo* CD33-positive AML. This indication specifically excludes acute promyelocytic leukaemia (APL) [Australian-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Adolescents ≥15 years)
**Gemtuzumab ozogamicin** is administered in a fractionated dosing schedule.

- **Induction Cycle:** 3 mg/m² (maximum of one 5 mg vial) administered on Days 1, 4, and 7.
- **Note on Second Induction:** If a second induction is required, **gemtuzumab ozogamicin** should **not** be administered. Only standard chemotherapy (anthracycline/cytarabine) should be used.
- **Consolidation Cycle:** For patients in complete remission (CR), the recommended dose is 3 mg/m² (maximum of one 5 mg vial) on Day 1.

### Administration Instructions
- **Premedication:** Administer a corticosteroid, antihistamine, and acetaminophen (paracetamol) 1 hour prior to infusion to mitigate infusion-related symptoms.
- **Hyperleukocytosis Management:** In patients with a leukocyte count >30,000/mm³, cytoreduction (leukapheresis or hydroxyurea) is recommended 48 hours prior to **gemtuzumab ozogamicin** to reduce the risk of Tumor Lysis Syndrome (TLS).
- **Infusion Method:** Administer via intravenous infusion over 2 hours. Do **not** administer as a bolus or IV push.
- **Filtration:** Use an in-line, low protein-binding 0.2 micron polyethersulphone (PES) filter.
- **Light Protection:** The drug is light-sensitive. The reconstituted and diluted solution must be protected from light (including UV) during preparation and administration.

### Reconstitution and Dilution
- Reconstitute the 5 mg vial with 5 mL of water for injections to reach 1 mg/mL.
- Dilute to a final concentration between 0.075 mg/mL and 0.234 mg/mL using 0.9% sodium chloride.

## 4. Contraindications
- Hypersensitivity to the active substance (gemtuzumab ozogamicin) or any of the excipients (Dextran 40, dibasic sodium phosphate, monobasic sodium phosphate monohydrate, sodium chloride, and sucrose).

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warnings
- Not specified in the provided Australian PI; however, the document includes prominent "Special Warnings" regarding hepatotoxicity.

### Hepatotoxicity (VOD/SOS)
- **Veno-occlusive disease (VOD)/Sinusoidal Obstruction Syndrome (SOS):** Life-threatening and fatal cases have been reported. 
- **Risk Factors:** Prior or subsequent haematopoietic stem cell transplant (HSCT), and moderate-to-severe hepatic impairment.
- **Monitoring:** Monitor for signs of VOD (weight gain, painful hepatomegaly, ascites, elevations in bilirubin/ALT/AST). If VOD occurs, discontinue **gemtuzumab ozogamicin** permanently.

### Infusion-Related Reactions
- Severe reactions, including anaphylaxis, have been reported. Monitor vital signs closely during infusion. If severe reactions (dyspnoea, bronchospasm, hypotension) occur, interrupt the infusion immediately. 

### Myelosuppression
- Severe neutropenia, thrombocytopenia, and anaemia are expected. Monitor complete blood counts (CBC) prior to each dose. Complications may include fatal infections or haemorrhagic events.

### Tumour Lysis Syndrome (TLS)
- Risk is highest in patients with high peripheral blast counts. Ensure adequate hydration and consider antihyperuricaemic agents (e.g., allopurinol or rasburicase).

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effect profiles differ between combination therapy and monotherapy settings.

| System Organ Class | Frequency (Combination Therapy) | Common Reactions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Infections** | Very Common (>10%) | Sepsis, fungal/bacterial infections |
| **Vascular** | Very Common (>10%) | Haemorrhage (CNS, GI, epistaxis) |
| **Hepatobiliary** | Common (1–10%) | Veno-occlusive liver disease |
| **Investigations** | Very Common (>10%) | Decreased Hb, platelets, and neutrophils; increased AST/ALT, bilirubin |
| **General** | Very Common (>10%) | Pyrexia, fatigue, chills, oedema |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** VOD/SOS, Grade 3/4 infections, severe haemorrhage, and TLS.
- **Paediatric nuance:** In studies, children experienced higher rates of prolonged neutrophil recovery compared to adults.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** No formal clinical drug-laboratory interaction studies have been conducted.
- **Metabolism:** N-acetyl gamma calicheamicin is primarily metabolised via nonenzymatic reduction; co-administration with CYP or UGT inhibitors/inducers is unlikely to alter exposure.
- **Transporters:** In vitro, the cytotoxic moiety is a substrate of P-gp.
- **Chemotherapy:** **Gemtuzumab ozogamicin** is not predicted to cause meaningful changes in the pharmacokinetics of daunorubicin or cytarabine.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** **Category D** [Australian]. There is evidence of embryofetal lethality and malformations in animal studies. **Gemtuzumab ozogamicin** should not be used during pregnancy unless the benefit outweighs the risk to the fetus.
- **Contraception:** Females of childbearing potential should use effective contraception during and for at least 7 months after the final dose. Males with female partners of childbearing potential should use contraception for at least 4 months after the final dose.
- **Breastfeeding:** Breastfeeding is not recommended during treatment and for at least 1 month after the final dose.
- **Fertility:** Based on animal findings, both male and female fertility may be compromised. Fertility preservation advice should be sought prior to treatment.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy in children <15 years have not been established [Australian].
- **Elderly Use (≥65 years):** No dose adjustment is required. Efficacy and safety were similar to younger subjects in clinical trials.
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment is required for mild to moderate impairment. Not studied in severe renal impairment.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No adjustment to the starting dose is required if total bilirubin ≤ 2 × ULN and AST/ALT ≤ 2.5 × ULN. Postpone dosing if levels exceed these thresholds until recovery. Not studied in severe hepatic impairment.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** The antibody binds to CD33, causing the ADC to be internalised. The cytotoxic calicheamicin is released via hydrolytic cleavage, leading to DNA double-strand breaks, cell cycle arrest, and apoptosis.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Volume of distribution is approximately 25 L.
    - **Metabolism:** Hydrolytic release of calicheamicin, followed by nonenzymatic reduction of the disulphide moiety.
    - **Elimination:** Terminal half-life of the hP67.6 antibody is approximately 160 hours.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Trial ALFA-0701:** A Phase 3, randomised, open-label study in patients (50–70 years) with *de novo* AML.
- **Outcomes:** The addition of **gemtuzumab ozogamicin** to daunorubicin and cytarabine significantly improved Event-Free Survival (EFS) (Median 17.3 months vs 9.5 months; HR 0.562; p=0.0002). There was no significant difference in Overall Survival (OS) or Complete Remission (CR) rates in this specific study population.
- **Cytogenetics:** Benefit was most pronounced in favourable and intermediate-risk cytogenetics; no clear benefit was seen in adverse-risk cytogenetics.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Unopened Vials:** Store at 2°C to 8°C (Refrigerate. Do not freeze). Protect from light by keeping in the original carton.
- **Reconstituted Solution:** Use immediately or store in the vial for up to 16 hours (2°C–8°C) or 3 hours (room temperature).
- **Diluted Solution:** May be stored up to 18 hours (2°C–8°C) and 6 hours (room temperature). Total time from reconstitution to completion of administration should not exceed 24 hours.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule:** **S4 (Prescription Only Medicine)** [Australian].
