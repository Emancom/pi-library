# Editorial Product Information: Levocetirizine Dihydrochloride

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial has been synthesised from official Product Information (PI) and Drug Facts documentation provided by the following regulatory jurisdiction: **United States (Food and Drug Administration)**.
- **Purpose:** This document is a harmonised editorial overview intended for use by healthcare professionals. It aims to provide a clear, clinically accurate reference by consolidating data from the provided source.
- **Note:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI or "Drug Facts" label required for prescribing or dispensing in a specific jurisdiction. Always consult the local approved labelling for legal and regulatory compliance.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocetirizine dihydrochloride (an H1-receptor antagonist).
- **Brand Name(s):** Xyzal, Children’s Xyzal Allergy 24HR [US].
- **ATC Code:** R06AE09 (Levocetirizine).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Oral solution (liquid): 2.5 mg levocetirizine dihydrochloride per 5 mL [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Chattem, Inc. (A Sanofi Company) [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Allergic Rhinitis:** Temporary relief of symptoms associated with hay fever or other respiratory allergies, including:
    - Rhinorrhea (runny nose)
    - Sneezing
    - Epiphora (itchy, watery eyes)
    - Pruritus of the nose or throat [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Children)
Levocetirizine should be administered once daily in the evening.

- **Adults and Children (12–64 years):** 5 mL or 10 mL once daily, depending on the severity of symptoms. Maximum dose is 10 mL per 24 hours [US].
- **Paediatric (6–11 years):** 5 mL once daily. Maximum dose is 5 mL per 24 hours [US].
- **Paediatric (2–5 years):** 2.5 mL once daily. Maximum dose is 2.5 mL per 24 hours [US].
- **Paediatric (Under 2 years):** Use is not recommended [US].
- **Geriatric (65 years and older):** Patients should consult a physician for appropriate dosing [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Use only the enclosed dosing cup to ensure accurate measurement [US].

### Renal Impairment
- Levocetirizine is contraindicated in consumers with kidney disease in the over-the-counter setting [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Renal Disease:** Contraindicated in patients with kidney disease [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Contraindicated in patients with a known history of allergic reaction to levocetirizine, any of its inactive ingredients, or any antihistamine containing cetirizine [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **CNS Depression:** Drowsiness may occur. Patients should be cautioned when driving a motor vehicle or operating machinery [US].
- **Alcohol and Sedatives:** Concurrent use of alcohol, sedatives, or tranquilizers may increase the risk of drowsiness [US].
- **Urinary Retention:** Use with caution in patients with a history of trouble urinating or emptying the bladder [US]. 
- **Post-Discontinuation Pruritus:** In rare cases, patients may experience itching after stopping levocetirizine [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Common:** Somnolence (drowsiness) is a known effect of this class and the medication [US].
- **Rare:** Pruritus (itching) upon discontinuation of the medication [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Hypersensitivity/allergic reactions (seek immediate medical attention if they occur) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacodynamic Interactions:**
    - **Alcohol:** May increase the sedative effects of levocetirizine; avoid concurrent consumption [US].
    - **CNS Depressants:** Sedatives and tranquilizers may increase the level of drowsiness [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Healthcare professionals should be consulted before use. [US: Pregnancy Category not specified on the Drug Facts label; advice is to ask a health professional before use].
- **Breastfeeding:** Use is not recommended during breastfeeding [US].
- **Fertility:** Not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Approved for children 2 years and older. Dosing is age-dependent (see Section 3). Use in children under 2 years of age is not recommended [US].
- **Geriatric Use:** Patients aged 65 and older are advised to consult a physician before use, likely due to the potential for decreased renal function [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Do not use if kidney disease is present [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocetirizine is an antihistamine that acts as a potent and selective antagonist of peripheral H1-receptors [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Not specified in available sources.
    - **Distribution:** Not specified in available sources.
    - **Metabolism:** Not specified in available sources.
    - **Elimination:** Primarily renal (inferred from the contraindication in kidney disease) [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Provides relief for 24 hours [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store between 20° and 25°C (68° and 77°F) [US].
- **Safety Packaging:** Do not use if the carton was opened or if the printed foil inner seal on the bottle is torn or missing [US].
- **Sodium Content:** Contains 3 mg of sodium per 5 mL [US].
