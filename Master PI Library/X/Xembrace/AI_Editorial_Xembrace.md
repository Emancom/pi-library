# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview intended for healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official PI used for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult the current local PI for regulatory compliance.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP).
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection: 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency (Inborn Errors of Metabolism):** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that result in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Administration:** Administered intravenously. For ESRD patients, the dose is delivered as a slow 2- to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].

### Dosing by Indication
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    - **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg given as a slow 2- to 3-minute bolus or by infusion [US].
    - **Loading Dose:** Often administered in patients with severe metabolic crisis, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours [US].
    - **Frequency:** Doses should be administered every 3 or 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours [US].
    - **Maximum Dose:** The highest administered dose reported is 300 mg/kg [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - **Recommended Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight) [US].
    - **Dose Adjustments:** Should be guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].

### Monitoring
- **Baseline:** Obtain plasma carnitine concentrations before initiating parenteral therapy [US].
- **Ongoing:** Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine level: 35–60 µmol/L) is recommended [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Known Hypersensitivity:** Not specified in available sources, though the document notes "None known" regarding specific contraindications [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None [US].
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions may occur within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and evaluate risks before re-administration [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with known seizure disorders, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **TMA/TMAO Accumulation:** While this concern primarily relates to chronic high-dose *oral* administration in patients with renal impairment, clinicians should be aware that toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) are normally excreted in the urine [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Clinical Trial Experience (ESRD Patients)
Adverse events reported in double-blind, placebo-controlled trials at a frequency ≥5% (regardless of causality) include:

| Body System | Adverse Event (Incidence ≥5%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Abdominal pain, accidental injury, asthenia, back pain, chest pain, fever, flu syndrome, headache, infection, injection site reaction, pain |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension, hypotension, tachycardia, palpitations |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea, vomiting, nausea, anorexia, constipation, dyspepsia |
| **Metabolic** | Hypercalcemia, hypervolemia, peripheral edema, weight decrease |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness, insomnia, depression, paresthesia |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis, rhinitis, cough increase, dyspnea |
| **Skin** | Pruritus, rash |
| **Special Senses** | Amblyopia |
| **Urogenital** | Kidney failure |

### Other Notable Effects
- **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea and vomiting; gastritis.
- **Other:** Body odor [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have occurred when levocarnitine is used concomitantly with warfarin. **Clinical Management:** Monitor INR levels after initiating levocarnitine or following dose adjustments in patients on warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not explicitly assigned a lettered category in current labeling, but reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm [US].
    - **Recommendation:** Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk carnitine concentrations after exogenous administration. **Clinical Management:** Risks to the child of excess carnitine intake should be weighed against the benefits of supplementation to the mother [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders is weight-based (50 mg/kg). Safety and efficacy in children have been established for the indicated conditions [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** The safety and efficacy of oral levocarnitine have not been evaluated in renal insufficiency. For intravenous use in ESRD, see Section 3 [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also facilitates the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an intravenous dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. The mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours; the mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Trough Levels:** A 9-week study of ESRD patients (20 mg/kg three times weekly after dialysis) showed that trough concentrations stabilized between 54 and 180 µmol/L (normal range: 40–50 µmol/L) after one week [US].
- **Dose-Ranging Data:** Studies at 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg indicated that carnitine concentrations increase in a dose-dependent manner over 12 and 24 weeks, though clinical outcomes related to supplemental carnitine in ESRD have not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing. Discard any unused portion of a single-dose vial as it contains no preservatives [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable for up to 24 hours in PVC bags at room temperature when mixed with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer's (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as Rx-only [US].
