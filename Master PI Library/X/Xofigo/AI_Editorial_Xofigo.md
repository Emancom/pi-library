# Editorial Product Information: Xofigo (Radium Ra 223 Dichloride)

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is derived from the official Product Information (PI) documents provided by the following regulatory jurisdictions: **Australian (TGA)** and **US (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising regional variations in clinical guidance, warnings, and regulatory classifications.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific Product Information required for prescribing in a specific region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Radium (223Ra) dichloride.
- **Chemical Class:** Therapeutic alpha particle-emitting radiopharmaceutical.
- **Brand Name:** **Xofigo**.
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Clear, colourless, sterile isotonic solution for injection. 
    - Strength: 1,100 kBq/mL (30 microcurie/mL) at the reference date. 
    - Total radioactivity per vial: 6.6 MBq (178 microcurie) in a 6 mL single-dose vial.
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Bayer Australia Ltd [Australian]; Bayer HealthCare Pharmaceuticals Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Castration-Resistant Prostate Cancer (CRPC):** Treatment of patients with castration-resistant prostate cancer, symptomatic bone metastases, and no known visceral metastatic disease.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** The recommended dose is 55 kBq (1.49 microcurie) per kg body weight.
- **Frequency:** Administered at 4-week intervals for a total of 6 injections.
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Administer via slow intravenous injection (generally up to 1 minute).
    - The intravenous line or cannula must be flushed with isotonic saline (0.9% NaCl) before and after the injection.
    - **Xofigo** is a ready-to-use solution; it must not be diluted or mixed with other medicinal products.
    - Each vial is for single use in one patient only.
- **Dose Calculation:** 
    - The volume to be administered (mL) is calculated as: **(Body weight [kg] × 55 kBq/kg) / (Decay Factor × 1100 kBq/mL)**.
    - A specific Decay Correction (DK) Factor table is provided in the PI to account for the physical half-life (11.4 days) of Radium-223.
- **Note on Safety:** Safety and efficacy beyond 6 injections have not been studied.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Combination Therapy:** **Xofigo** is contraindicated in combination with abiraterone acetate plus prednisone/prednisolone [Australian; US: Not recommended outside of clinical trials].
- **Pregnancy:** Contraindicated in women who are or may become pregnant [Australian (Category X); US: Not indicated for use in females].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Bone Marrow Suppression:** Notably thrombocytopenia, neutropenia, leucopenia, and pancytopenia. 
    - **Monitoring:** Haematological evaluation is required at baseline and prior to every dose. 
    - **Treatment Thresholds:**
        - **First Dose:** Absolute Neutrophil Count (ANC) must be ≥ 1.5 x 10⁹/L and Platelets ≥ 100 x 10⁹/L.
        - **Subsequent Doses:** ANC must be ≥ 1.0 x 10⁹/L and Platelets ≥ 50 x 10⁹/L.
    - If counts do not recover within 6 to 8 weeks after the last administration despite standard care, continue treatment only after careful benefit/risk evaluation.
- **Increased Fractures and Mortality:** Increased incidence of fractures and deaths was observed in a clinical trial (ERA-223) when **Xofigo** was added to abiraterone acetate and prednisone/prednisolone. 
- **Bone Fractures:** **Xofigo** increases the risk of bone fractures, particularly in patients with a history of osteoporosis or fewer than 6 bone metastases. Bone status and fracture risk should be assessed for at least 24 months. Consider preventive measures (e.g., bisphosphonates or denosumab).
- **Spinal Cord Compression:** In patients with imminent or established spinal cord compression, standard-of-care treatment should be completed before starting or resuming **Xofigo** [Australian].
- **Fluid Status:** Dehydration may occur secondary to gastrointestinal effects; monitor oral intake and fluid status closely [US].
- **Radiation Protection:** Administration involves potential risks to others from radiation or contamination from body fluids (spills of urine, faeces, vomit). Use ALARA (As Low As Reasonably Achievable) principles, including adequate shielding and minimising time spent in radiation areas.

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effect frequencies based on the ALSYMPCA phase III study (n=600).

| Frequency | Adverse Effect |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Diarrhoea (25%), Nausea (36%), Vomiting (19%), Thrombocytopenia (11.5%), Peripheral Edema [US] (13%), Anemia [US] (93% all grades) |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Neutropenia (5%), Leucopenia (4.2%), Pancytopenia (2%), Injection site reactions (1.2%), Renal failure/impairment [US] (3%) |
| **Uncommon (0.1–1%)** | Lymphopenia (0.8%) |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Bone marrow failure and ongoing pancytopenia (2% in **Xofigo** arm vs 0% placebo) [US].
- **Secondary Malignancies:** Radium-223 contributes to long-term cumulative radiation exposure, which is associated with an increased risk of cancer and hereditary defects. Osteosarcomas have been observed in animal studies.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** No formal clinical drug interaction studies have been performed.
- **Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** 
    - **Chemotherapy:** Concomitant use may have additive effects on bone marrow suppression. Safety and efficacy with concurrent chemotherapy have not been established.
    - **Supportive Agents:** Subgroup analyses indicate that concurrent use of bisphosphonates or calcium channel blockers does not affect safety or efficacy [US].
- **Management:** If abiraterone acetate plus prednisone/prednisolone was used, **Xofigo** should not be initiated for at least 5 days after the last dose of abiraterone. Subsequent systemic cancer treatment should not start for at least 30 days after the last **Xofigo** injection [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Australian Category X:** Radioactive therapeutic agents are not to be used in pregnant women.
    - **US:** Can cause fetal harm based on mechanism of action. 
- **Breastfeeding:** Not indicated in women; no data available on the presence of Radium-223 in human milk.
- **Fertility:** Potential risk of radiation-induced adverse effects on testes and impaired fertility. Advise men to use condoms and their female partners of reproductive potential to use highly effective contraception during and for 6 months after treatment.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy have not been studied in children and adolescents below 18 years of age.
- **Elderly Use:** No overall differences in safety or effectiveness observed in patients aged ≥ 65 years. No dose adjustment necessary.
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    - **Mild to Moderate:** No dose adjustment required.
    - **Severe (CrCl < 30 mL/min):** No dose recommendation can be made due to limited data.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** 
    - **Mild:** No dose adjustment required. 
    - **Moderate to Severe:** No dose recommendation can be made due to lack of clinical data.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Radium-223 is a calcium mimic that selectively targets bone, specifically areas of high bone turnover (metastases), by forming complexes with hydroxyapatite. It emits high-energy alpha particles with a short range (<100 micrometers), inducing double-strand DNA breaks in adjacent tumour cells and the bone microenvironment (osteoblasts/osteoclasts) while limiting damage to surrounding healthy tissue.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Rapidly cleared from blood; 61% of the dose is present in bone and 49% in the intestine 4 hours post-injection.
    - **Metabolism:** Radium-223 is a radioisotope that decays; it is not metabolised by the liver.
    - **Elimination:** Primarily faecal (median 76% excreted from the body within 7 days). Urinary excretion is approximately 5%. There is no evidence of hepatobiliary excretion.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ALSYMPCA Trial:** A Phase III, double-blind, randomised trial in 921 patients with symptomatic CRPC bone metastases. 
    - **Primary Outcome:** Overall survival (OS) was significantly longer with **Xofigo** plus best standard of care (BSoC) compared to placebo plus BSoC.
    - **Results:** Median OS was 14.9 months for **Xofigo** vs 11.3 months for placebo (HR 0.695; 95% CI 0.581-0.832).
    - **Secondary Outcomes:** Significant delay in time to first symptomatic skeletal event (SSE) (15.6 months vs 9.8 months) and significant improvements in quality of life (HRQOL) scores.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store below 40°C (104°F).
- **Conditions:** Store in the original container or within equivalent radiation shielding. 
- **Shelf Life:** Not explicitly stated, but radioactivity concentration is defined at a specific "reference date" provided on the label.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule:**
    - **Australian:** Unscheduled (Poisons Standard); however, receipt, storage, and disposal are subject to radiation protection licenses (ARPANSA).
    - **US:** Rx-only (Prescription Drug).
- **Legal Classification:** Approved for use only by persons authorised/licensed to handle radiopharmaceuticals in designated clinical settings.
