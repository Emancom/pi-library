# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial harmonisation is based on the Product Information (PI) provided from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of levocarnitine injection for use by healthcare professionals.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for the official PI or summary of product characteristics required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. At the time of compilation, only the United States (FDA) PI was available for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine [(R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt].
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter).
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Indicated for the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that result in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg. It should be administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or via infusion [US].
    - **Acute Crisis:** A loading dose is often given, followed by an equivalent dose divided over the subsequent 24 hours.
    - **Frequency:** Dosing should occur every 3 or 4 hours, and never less frequently than every 6 hours.
    - **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses are typically 50 mg/kg, though higher doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilised [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:** 
    - **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
    - **Administration:** Administer as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
    - **Adjustment:** Dosing should be guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Reductions (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be implemented as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on clinical response [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Inspect parenteral products for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration.
- **Compatibility:** Stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer's at concentrations ranging from 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known at the time of publication [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in patients with ESRD on dialysis. Reactions may occur within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and initiate appropriate medical management [US].
- **Seizure Risk:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Monitoring:** Pre-therapy plasma carnitine levels should be obtained. Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine: 35 to 60 µmol/L) is recommended [US].
- **Oral Formulation Cautions:** While this document concerns the injection, clinicians should note that chronic high-dose oral levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions are often confounded by the underlying pathology of ESRD or metabolic errors.

### Frequency $\ge$ 5% (regardless of causality) [US]
| Body System | Adverse Event |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Abdominal pain, accidental injury, chest pain, fever, flu syndrome, headache, infection, injection site reaction, pain |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension, hypotension, tachycardia |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea, nausea, vomiting |
| **Metabolic** | Hypercalcemia, hypervolemia |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness |
| **Respiratory** | Cough increase, dyspnea, pharyngitis |

### Other Notable Reactions
- **Uncommon/Rare:** Body odor, gastritis, seizures, and hypersensitivity reactions (anaphylaxis) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalised Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after any dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin anticoagulation [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Category:** Not formally assigned a letter category in the provided source, but reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm [US].
    - **Recommendation:** Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows suggest increased carnitine concentrations in milk following administration. Risks to the infant from excess intake must be weighed against maternal benefits. Discontinuation of nursing or treatment may be considered [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility was found in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** See Dosage and Administration (weight-based dosing) [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Safety and efficacy of *oral* levocarnitine have not been evaluated in renal insufficiency; however, the *injection* is specifically indicated for use in ESRD patients on hemodialysis. Monitor for metabolite accumulation if transitioning to oral therapy [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In patients with metabolic errors or ESRD, levocarnitine helps clear accumulated toxic acyl-CoA compounds by forming acylcarnitine, which is rapidly excreted [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and $\gamma$-butyrobetaine (feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Following a single IV bolus (20 mg/kg), approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. The mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours, and the terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Dialysis Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients (20 mg/kg three times weekly after dialysis), trough concentrations increased from a baseline of 21.3 µmol/L to 190 µmol/L after 8 weeks. While the ability to increase carnitine concentrations is proven, the direct effect on clinical outcomes in this specific population has not been definitively determined in the trial data provided [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Handling:** Avoid excessive heat; protect from freezing.
- **Stability:** Single-dose vial; discard any unused portion as the formulation contains no preservatives [US].

- **Legal Classification:** 
    - **United States:** Rx-only (Prescription only) [US].
    - **Other Jurisdictions:** Not specified in available sources.
