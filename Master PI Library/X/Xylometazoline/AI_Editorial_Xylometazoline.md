## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient**: Xylometazoline Hydrochloride.
- **Chemical Class**: Sympathomimetic; Imidazoline derivative.
- **Brand Names**: **Metarivin Soln. 0.1%** [US].
- **ATC Code**: R01AA07 (Xylometazoline).
- **Dosage Form and Strength**: Nasal liquid (spray) containing 1 mg/mL (0.1%) of Xylometazoline Hydrochloride [US].
- **Route of Administration**: Nasal use.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor**: Lydia Co., Ltd. (South Korea); Distributed by Bio Mission Group, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- Relief of cold symptoms [US].
- Relief of symptoms associated with cold rhinitis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Adults and children 7 years and older**: Administer as a spray into the nose three times daily [US].

### Administration Instructions
- For nasal use only [US].

### Missed Dose and Titration
- Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- Not specified in available sources. (Note: While standard clinical practice excludes use in patients with narrow-angle glaucoma or those taking MAOIs, these were not explicitly listed in the provided source document).

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **General Warnings**: Patients should stop use and consult a physician if they experience nervousness, dizziness, or sleepiness [US].
- **Duration of Use**: Treatment should be discontinued and medical advice sought if:
    - Nasal congestion, pain, or cough worsens or lasts more than 7 days [US].
    - Fever worsens or lasts more than 3 days [US].
    - New symptoms occur [US].
- **Safety**: Keep out of reach of children. In case of overdose, immediate medical assistance or contact with a Poison Control Center is required [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
