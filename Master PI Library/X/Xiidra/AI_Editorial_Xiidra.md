# Editorial Product Information: Xiidra (lifitegrast)

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA). 
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of lifitegrast ophthalmic solution, synthesised for healthcare professionals to facilitate clinical decision-making. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority’s approved labeling for definitive guidance.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Lifitegrast (a lymphocyte function-associated antigen-1 [LFA-1] antagonist).
- **Brand Name(s):** **Xiidra** [United States].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Ophthalmic solution 5% (50 mg/mL) [United States].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical ophthalmic use.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Novartis Pharmaceuticals Corporation [United States].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Dry Eye Disease (DED):** Indicated for the treatment of both the signs and symptoms of dry eye disease [United States].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** One drop instilled into each eye twice daily (approximately 12 hours apart) [United States].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Use a single-use container for both eyes.
    - Discard the container immediately after a single administration.
    - **Contact Lenses:** Patients should remove contact lenses before instilling the solution. Lenses may be reinserted 15 minutes after administration [United States].
- **Missed Dose:** If a drop is missed, the patient should proceed with the next dose as scheduled. Do not double the dose to compensate.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to lifitegrast or any of the excipients in the formulation [United States].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **General Warnings:** 
    - **Hypersensitivity:** Post-marketing reports include serious reactions such as anaphylaxis and bronchospasm. If signs of a serious allergic reaction occur, discontinue use and seek immediate medical attention [United States].
- **Contamination Prevention:** To avoid eye injury or contamination of the solution, patients should be advised not to touch the tip of the single-use container to the eye or any other surface [United States].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions observed in clinical trials were generally mild to moderate in severity.

| Frequency | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Instillation-site irritation, dysgeusia (unusual taste sensation), decreased visual acuity [United States]. |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Instillation-site irritation, dysgeusia, and reduced visual acuity (lower end of the 5-25% range) [United States]. |
| **Uncommon (1–5%)** | Blurred vision, conjunctival hyperemia, eye irritation, headache, increased lacrimation, eye discharge, eye discomfort, eye pruritus, and sinusitis [United States]. |

- **Serious/Post-marketing Adverse Effects:** 
    - Hypersensitivity reactions (Rare/Frequency not established): Anaphylaxis, bronchospasm, respiratory distress, pharyngeal edema, swollen tongue, urticaria, allergic conjunctivitis, dyspnea, angioedema, and allergic dermatitis [United States].
    - Eye swelling and rash [United States].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Dynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources. Systematic absorption is minimal, reducing the likelihood of systemic drug-drug interactions.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Classification:** Data in pregnant women are unavailable. Animal studies (IV administration) showed an increased incidence of omphalocele in rabbits at 400 times the human plasma exposure. However, because systemic exposure via the ophthalmic route is very low, the clinical relevance of these findings is unclear [United States].
- **Breastfeeding:** There are no data regarding the presence of lifitegrast in human milk or its effects on the breastfed infant. The low systemic exposure following ocular administration suggests minimal risk, but the developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding should be weighed against the mother's clinical need [United States].
- **Fertility:** IV doses up to 30 mg/kg/day (5400-fold human exposure) showed no effect on fertility or reproductive performance in rats [United States].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy in patients under 17 years of age have not been established [United States].
- **Elderly Use:** No overall differences in safety or effectiveness have been observed between elderly and younger adult patients [United States].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Specific dose adjustments are not specified in available sources, likely due to minimal systemic absorption.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Lifitegrast is an LFA-1 antagonist. It binds to LFA-1 (an integrin on leukocytes) and blocks its interaction with intercellular adhesion molecule-1 (ICAM-1). ICAM-1 is often overexpressed in corneal and conjunctival tissues in DED. This blockage inhibits T-cell activation, migration, and the secretion of inflammatory cytokines [United States].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Low systemic exposure. In clinical trials, 19% of patients had trough plasma concentrations above the limit of quantitation (0.5 ng/mL), ranging up to 3.74 ng/mL [United States].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** The exact mechanism of action in DED is not fully elucidated [United States].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Study Design:** Safety and efficacy were evaluated in four 12-week, randomized, multi-center, double-masked, vehicle-controlled studies (n=1181) [United States].
- **Primary Outcomes:** 
    - **Symptoms:** A larger reduction in Eye Dryness Score (EDS) favoring lifitegrast was observed in all four studies at Day 42 and Day 84 [United States].
    - **Signs:** A larger reduction in Inferior Fluorescein Corneal Staining Score (ICSS) favoring lifitegrast was observed in three of the four studies at Day 84 [United States].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [United States].
- **Special Handling:** Store single-use containers in the original foil pouch to protect from light and excessive light exposure [United States].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** 
    - **United States:** Rx-only (Prescription only).
- **Poison/Schedule Classification:** Not specified for other jurisdictions in available sources.
