# Editorial Product Information: Zinc Gluconate (Veterinary Oral Solution)

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Zinc gluconate (1.5%) [US].
- **Brand Name(s):** Clunia EasyDent Rinse and Water [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources (typically A01AD11 for oral zinc in humans, but specific veterinary codes may vary).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Liquid oral solution containing 15 mg of zinc gluconate per 1 mL [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Dental/Oral [US].
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Vetnova Animal Health LLC [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*Note: These indications are based on manufacturer labeling and have not been formally approved by the US FDA.*
- Daily maintenance of oral hygiene and health in dogs, cats, exotics, and horses [US].
- Management of halitosis (bad breath) by neutralising sulfur compounds [US].
- Reduction and control of dental plaque and tartar [US].
- Support for gum and oral wound healing [US].
- Use as a peri-procedural adjunct (before, during, and after dental procedures) [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult Animals)
The product is intended for daily use in multi-pet households and animals that may be difficult to handle for traditional brushing [US].

### Administration Instructions
Two methods of administration are supported:
- **Direct Application:** Lift the animal's upper lip and press the bottle once to apply the solution. Care should be taken to avoid contact between the applicator tip and the oral mucosa or other surfaces to prevent contamination and potential injury [US].
- **Water Administration:** Add 1 teaspoon (5 mL) per 1 cup (250 mL) of the animal's drinking water. The medicated water must be changed daily [US].

### Maintenance
- After direct application, the spout tip should be wiped clean and the container recapped tightly [US].
- Suitable for both short-term and prolonged daily use [US].

## 4. Contraindications

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
- **Zinc Gluconate:** Helps maintain the physiological structure of the gums [US].
- **Taurine:** Provides a chelating action on sulfur compounds (e.g., hydrogen sulfide) that are responsible for halitosis [US].

### Pharmacokinetics
- Not specified in available sources.

### Pharmacodynamics
- The solution is formulated with a neutral pH to ensure compatibility with the oral environment.
- It is alcohol-free and xylitol-free [US].
- Does not stain tooth enamel or household fabrics [US].
