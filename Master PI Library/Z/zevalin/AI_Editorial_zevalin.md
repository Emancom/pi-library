# Editorial Product Information: Zevalin (ibritumomab tiuxetan)

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of **Zevalin** for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises complex clinical and regulatory data into a single reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory approved product information before prescribing.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Ibritumomab tiuxetan (a CD20-directed radiotherapeutic antibody). 
- **Chemical Class:** Immunoconjugate (murine IgG1 kappa monoclonal antibody covalently linked to the linker-chelator tiuxetan).
- **Brand Name:** **Zevalin** [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Injection: 3.2 mg of ibritumomab tiuxetan in 2 mL of 0.9% Sodium Chloride (1.6 mg/mL) provided in a single-dose vial as part of a preparation kit [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous infusion.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Acrotech Biopharma Inc [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Relapsed or Refractory B-cell NHL:** Treatment of adult patients with relapsed or refractory, low-grade or follicular B-cell non-Hodgkin's lymphoma (NHL) [US].
- **Consolidation Therapy:** Treatment of previously untreated follicular NHL in adult patients who achieve a partial or complete response to first-line chemotherapy [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing Schedule [US]
The **Zevalin** therapeutic regimen is administered in two steps:
1.  **Day 1:** Intravenous infusion of **rituximab** at 250 mg/m².
2.  **Day 7, 8, or 9:** 
    *   Intravenous infusion of **rituximab** at 250 mg/m².
    *   Followed by an intravenous infusion of **Y-90 Zevalin** within 4 hours.

### Y-90 Zevalin Dosing by Indication and Platelet Count [US]
- **Relapsed or Refractory Patients:**
    *   Platelets ≥150,000/mm³: 0.4 mCi/kg (14.8 MBq/kg).
    *   Platelets 100,000 to 149,000/mm³: 0.3 mCi/kg (11.1 MBq/kg).
- **Consolidation Therapy (Following first-line chemotherapy):**
    *   Initiate only after recovery of platelets to ≥150,000/mm³.
    *   Dose: 0.4 mCi/kg (14.8 MBq/kg).
- **Maximum Dose:** Regardless of body weight, the maximum allowable dose of Y-90 Zevalin is **32 mCi (1184 MBq)**.

### Administration Instructions [US]
- **Premedication:** Administer acetaminophen 650 mg and diphenhydramine 50 mg orally prior to rituximab.
- **Rituximab Rate:** 
    *   *Day 1:* Start at 50 mg/hr; increase by 50 mg/hr every 30 minutes (max 400 mg/hr).
    *   *Day 7-9:* Start at 100 mg/hr; increase by 100 mg/hr every 30 minutes (max 400 mg/hr).
- **Y-90 Zevalin Infusion:** Administer over 10 minutes via a free-flowing IV line using a 0.22-micron low-protein-binding in-line filter. Flush the line with ≥10 mL of normal saline.
- **Requirement:** Administer only in facilities with immediate access to resuscitative measures.

## 4. Contraindications
- **United States:** None listed in the available source.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warnings [US]
- **Serious Infusion Reactions:** Often associated with rituximab; deaths have occurred within 24 hours. Fatalities were linked to hypoxia, pulmonary infiltrates, ARDS, myocardial infarction, ventricular fibrillation, or cardiogenic shock. Discontinue immediately for severe reactions.
- **Prolonged and Severe Cytopenias:** Occur in most patients. Do not administer to patients with ≥25% lymphoma marrow involvement or impaired bone marrow reserve.
- **Severe Cutaneous and Mucocutaneous Reactions:** Some fatal reactions (e.g., SJS, TEN) reported. Discontinue treatment if severe skin reactions occur.

### General Warnings and Precautions [US]
- **Secondary Malignancies:** Development of Myelodysplastic Syndrome (MDS) and Acute Myelogenous Leukemia (AML) has been observed.
- **Extravasation:** Monitor closely. If extravasation occurs, terminate the infusion immediately and restart in another limb.
- **Immunization:** Safety of live viral vaccines has not been studied. Do not administer live vaccines to patients who recently received **Zevalin**.
- **Radionuclide Precautions:** Maintain standard institutional radiation safety practices to minimize exposure to patients and staff.
- **Embryo-Fetal Toxicity:** Radioactivity may cause fetal harm. Advise patients of potential risks and require effective contraception.

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Hematologic Toxicity [US]
Cytopenias are the most common and serious adverse reactions, typically characterized by:
- **Thrombocytopenia:** 95% (Grade 3-4: 63%).
- **Neutropenia:** 77% (Grade 3-4: 60%).
- **Anemia:** 61% (Grade 3-4: 17%).
- **Timing:** Median nadir for ANC and platelets occurs at approximately 61-62 days and 49-53 days, respectively. Recovery may take up to 12 weeks or longer.

### Non-Hematologic Reactions (>10% frequency) [US]
- Fatigue (33%)
- Nasopharyngitis (19%)
- Nausea (18%)
- Abdominal pain (17%)
- Asthenia (15%)
- Cough (11%)
- Diarrhea (11%)
- Pyrexia (10%)

### Serious Adverse Effects [US]
- Secondary malignancies (MDS/AML)
- Severe infections (including sepsis and neutropenic sepsis)
- Infusion-site erythema and ulceration (secondary to extravasation)
- Radiation injury to tissues near lymphomatous involvement

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Platelet-interfering medications:** Patients taking drugs that interfere with platelet function or coagulation (e.g., anticoagulants, NSAIDs) should be monitored more frequently for thrombocytopenia [US].
- **General:** No formal drug interaction studies have been conducted [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy [US]:** There are no available data on use in pregnant women; however, based on radioactivity, it may cause fetal harm. Conduct pregnancy testing prior to treatment. 
- **Contraception [US]:** 
    *   *Females:* Use effective contraception during treatment and for **12 months** after the last dose.
    *   *Males:* Use effective contraception during treatment and for **12 months** after the final dose.
- **Breastfeeding [US]:** Human IgG is excreted in milk. Advise women to avoid breastfeeding during treatment and for **6 months** after the last dose.
- **Fertility [US]:** There is a potential risk of toxic effects on male and female gonads.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established in paediatric patients [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical studies included a significant proportion of patients ≥65 years. No overall differences in safety/efficacy were observed between elderly and younger subjects, though greater sensitivity in some older individuals cannot be ruled out [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources beyond general requirements for acceptable baseline function in clinical trials.

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action [US]
Ibritumomab tiuxetan binds specifically to the CD20 antigen expressed on pre-B and mature B lymphocytes. The tiuxetan chelator holds **Yttrium-90 (Y-90)**, which emits beta radiation. This radiation induces cellular damage via free radical formation in the target and neighboring cells.

### Pharmacodynamics [US]
Treatment results in a sustained depletion of circulating B-cells. Median B-cell counts were zero at 4 weeks, with recovery beginning around 12 weeks and returning to normal range by 9 months.

### Pharmacokinetics [US]
- **Effective Half-life:** The mean effective half-life for Y-90 activity in the blood is 30 hours.
- **Elimination:** Over 7 days, a median of 7.2% of the injected activity is excreted in the urine.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Relapsed/Refractory follicular NHL (Study 106-04):** A randomized trial (n=130) showed a significantly higher Overall Response Rate (ORR) for the **Zevalin** regimen (83%) compared to rituximab alone (55%) [US].
- **Rituximab-Refractory follicular NHL (Study 106-06):** A single-arm study (n=54) demonstrated an ORR of 74% with a 15% complete response rate [US].
- **First-line Consolidation (FIT Study):** A randomized study (n=414) in patients who achieved response to first-line chemotherapy showed **Zevalin** significantly prolonged median progression-free survival (38 months vs. 18 months for observation; HR 0.46) [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store the kit at 2-8°C (36-46°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Do not freeze [US]. 
- **Post-labeling Storage:** Store radiolabeled Y-90 Zevalin at 2-8°C and use within 8 hours of preparation [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Rx-only. Poison schedule/classification not specified in source beyond prescription status.
