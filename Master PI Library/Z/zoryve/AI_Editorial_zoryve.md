# Editorial Product Information: ZORYVE (roflumilast)

## Editorial Note
- Jurisdictions contributing to this editorial: **United States (FDA)**.
- Purpose: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of **Zoryve** (roflumilast) cream for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises available regulatory data into a single clinically relevant resource.
- Disclaimer: This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. Always consult the current local PI for legal and regulatory compliance.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Roflumilast
- **Chemical Class:** Phosphodiesterase 4 (PDE4) inhibitor
- **Brand Name(s):** **Zoryve** [US]
- **ATC Code:** D11AH (Proposed/Related)
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Cream, 0.3%: 3 mg of roflumilast per gram [US]
    - Cream, 0.15%: 1.5 mg of roflumilast per gram [US]
    - Cream, 0.05%: 0.5 mg of roflumilast per gram [US]
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Arcutis Biotherapeutics, Inc. [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Plaque Psoriasis:** Topical treatment of plaque psoriasis, including intertriginous areas, in adult and paediatric patients aged 2 years and older (using 0.3% strength) [US].
- **Atopic Dermatitis:**
    - Topical treatment of mild-to-moderate atopic dermatitis in adult and paediatric patients aged 6 years and older (using 0.15% strength) [US].
    - Topical treatment of mild-to-moderate atopic dermatitis in paediatric patients aged 2 to 5 years (using 0.05% strength) [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Plaque Psoriasis (Adults and Paediatrics $\geq$ 2 years):** Apply **Zoryve** cream 0.3% once daily to affected areas [US].
- **Atopic Dermatitis (Adults and Paediatrics $\geq$ 6 years):** Apply **Zoryve** cream 0.15% once daily to affected areas [US].
- **Atopic Dermatitis (Paediatrics 2–5 years):** Apply **Zoryve** cream 0.05% once daily to affected areas [US].

### Administration Instructions
- For topical use only. Not for ophthalmic, oral, or intravaginal use [US].
- Apply once daily and rub in completely until the cream is no longer visible [US].
- Wash hands thoroughly after application, unless the hands are being treated [US].

### Missed Dose
- Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Contraindicated in patients with moderate to severe liver impairment (Child-Pugh B or C) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None [US].
- **General Warnings:** No specific systemic safety warnings provided in the topical labelling; however, use is restricted by hepatic status due to increased systemic exposure [US].
- **Monitoring Requirements:** Not specified in available sources.

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions vary slightly by indication. Frequency data is based on clinical trials of 4 to 8 weeks duration.

### Plaque Psoriasis (0.3% Strength)
| Adverse Reaction | Frequency ($\geq$1%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| Diarrhea | 3.1% |
| Headache | 2.4% |
| Insomnia | 1.4% |
| Nausea | 1.2% |
| Application site pain | 1.0% |
| Upper respiratory tract infection | 1.0% |
| Urinary tract infection | 1.0% |

### Atopic Dermatitis (0.15% and 0.05% Strengths)
- **Common (1–10%):** Headache, nausea, application site pain, diarrhea, vomiting, upper respiratory tract infection, rhinitis, and conjunctivitis [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Not specified in available sources; however, application site urticaria led to discontinuation in 0.3% of subjects in psoriasis trials [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
No formal drug-drug interaction studies were conducted with the topical cream [US].

### Pharmacokinetic Interactions
- **CYP3A4 and CYP1A2 Inhibitors:** Co-administration with systemic CYP3A4 inhibitors (e.g., erythromycin, ketoconazole) or dual inhibitors of CYP3A4 and CYP1A2 (e.g., fluvoxamine, enoxacin, cimetidine) may increase roflumilast systemic exposure. Clinicians should weigh the potential for increased adverse reactions against therapeutic benefit [US].
- **Oral Contraceptives:** Co-administration with oral contraceptives containing gestodene and ethinyl estradiol may increase systemic exposure of roflumilast [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not assigned (Current US labelling uses Risk Summary) [US].
    - **Risk Summary:** Insufficient human data. In animal studies, oral administration during organogenesis at doses significantly higher than the Maximum Recommended Human Dose (MRHD) produced post-implantation loss (rats), stillbirth, and decreased pup viability (mice).
    - **Labor/Delivery:** Avoid use during labor and delivery; animal studies showed disruption of the labor process [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** No human data on presence in milk or effects on milk production. Roflumilast is excreted in the milk of lactating rats. Use on the smallest area of skin for the shortest duration. Advise patients not to apply directly to the nipple or areola to prevent direct infant exposure [US].
- **Fertility:** Oral roflumilast at 1.8 mg/kg/day decreased fertility rates in male rats. No effect on female rat fertility observed [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:**
    - **Plaque Psoriasis:** Approved for $\geq$ 2 years [US].
    - **Atopic Dermatitis:** Approved for $\geq$ 2 years [US].
    - **Note:** Safety and effectiveness not established in patients younger than 2 years [US].
- **Elderly Use ($\geq$ 65 years):** No overall differences in safety or effectiveness were observed between elderly and younger subjects; however, greater sensitivity in some older individuals cannot be ruled out [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** No clinically significant differences in roflumilast pharmacokinetics were observed in subjects with severe renal impairment following oral administration [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** 
    - **Mild (Child-Pugh A):** No dosage adjustment required [US].
    - **Moderate to Severe (Child-Pugh B or C):** Contraindicated [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Roflumilast and its active metabolite (roflumilast N-oxide) are inhibitors of PDE4, which leads to the accumulation of intracellular cyclic AMP (cAMP). The precise mechanism of therapeutic action in psoriasis and atopic dermatitis is not fully defined [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Plasma concentration profile is relatively flat following topical application.
    - **Distribution:** Plasma protein binding is approximately 99%.
    - **Metabolism:** Extensively metabolized via CYP450 (1A2 and 3A4) and Phase II (conjugation) reactions. Roflumilast N-oxide is the major active metabolite.
    - **Elimination:** Half-lives following topical administration are approximately 4.0 days for roflumilast and 4.6 days for the N-oxide metabolite [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Plaque Psoriasis:** Two multicenter, randomized, double-blind, vehicle-controlled trials (DERMIS-1 and DERMIS-2) in 881 subjects. IGA Treatment Success (Clear or Almost Clear + 2-grade improvement) at Week 8 was 41.5% and 36.7% for **Zoryve** 0.3% vs. 5.8% and 7.1% for vehicle [US].
- **Atopic Dermatitis (Adult/Ped $\geq$ 6yr):** Two trials (INTEGUMENT-1 and INTEGUMENT-2) in 1337 subjects. vIGA-AD Treatment Success at Week 4 was 32.0% and 28.9% for **Zoryve** 0.15% vs. 15.2% and 12.0% for vehicle [US].
- **Atopic Dermatitis (Ped 2–5yr):** One trial (INTEGUMENT-PED) in 652 subjects. vIGA-AD success at Week 4 was 25.4% for **Zoryve** 0.05% vs. 10.7% for vehicle [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F and 86°F) [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Rx-only [US].
