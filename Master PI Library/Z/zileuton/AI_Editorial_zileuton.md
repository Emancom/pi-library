# Editorial Product Information: Zileuton

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial: **US (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document is a harmonised editorial overview designed for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises clinical data to provide a concise reference for the use of zileuton.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority's approved labelling for definitive prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Zileuton (a specific inhibitor of 5-lipoxygenase). 
- **Chemical Class:** Hydroxyurea derivative; (±)-1-(1-Benzo[b]thien-2-ylethyl)-1-hydroxyurea.
- **Brand Name(s):** **Zyflo** [US].
- **ATC Code:** R03AK (Not explicitly specified in source, but standard for this class).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - **Immediate-release tablet:** 600 mg [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Chiesi USA, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Asthma:** Indicated for the prophylaxis and chronic treatment of asthma in adults and children 12 years of age and older [US].
- **Important Limitation:** Zileuton is not indicated for the reversal of bronchospasm in acute asthma attacks, including status asthmaticus. Therapy may be continued during acute exacerbations.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **Adult and Adolescent (≥12 years) Dosing:** 600 mg four times daily (total daily dose of 2400 mg) [US].
- **Administration:** 
    - Tablets may be taken with or without food.
    - Tablets may be swallowed whole or split in half for ease of administration.
    - For optimal control, the medication must be taken regularly, including during asymptomatic periods.
- **Monitoring:** Hepatic transaminases must be evaluated prior to initiation and periodically during treatment (see Section 5).

## 4. Contraindications
- **Active Liver Disease:** Contraindicated in patients with active liver disease or persistent transaminase elevations ≥3 times the upper limit of normal (ULN) [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to zileuton or any inactive ingredients in the formulation [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hepatotoxicity:** Elevations in liver function tests (principally ALT) may occur. ALT is the most sensitive indicator of injury.
    - **Monitoring Schedule [US]:** Assess ALT at baseline, then once monthly for the first 3 months, every 2–3 months for the remainder of the first year, and periodically thereafter.
    - **Management:** Discontinue if transaminase elevations >5x ULN occur or if clinical signs of liver dysfunction develop (e.g., jaundice, right upper quadrant pain, fatigue, pruritus).
- **Neuropsychiatric Events:** Reports include sleep disorders (e.g., insomnia) and behavioural changes. Prescribers should monitor for these symptoms and evaluate the risk/benefit of continuing therapy if they occur [US].
- **Acute Asthma:** Not for use as a rescue medication. Patients should be advised to have appropriate short-acting bronchodilators available.
- **Alcohol Use:** Use with caution in patients who consume substantial quantities of alcohol or have a history of liver disease.

## 6. Adverse Effects
Clinical trial data involving over 5,000 patients indicate the following profile:

| Frequency | Adverse Effect |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Common (≥3% and >placebo)** | Headache (24.6%), Dyspepsia (8.2%), Pain (7.8%), Nausea (5.5%), Abdominal pain (4.6%), Asthenia (3.8%), Myalgia (3.2%) |
| **Less Common (>1%)** | Arthralgia, chest pain, conjunctivitis, constipation, dizziness, fever, flatulence, insomnia, lymphadenopathy, malaise, pruritus, somnolence, urinary tract infection, and vomiting. |
| **Serious/Notable** | ALT elevation ≥3x ULN (3.2% overall; 4.6% in long-term use), symptomatic hepatitis with jaundice, and transient leucopenia (1.0%). |

- **Post-Marketing Experience:** Rash, urticaria, sleep disorders, and behaviour changes have been reported [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
Zileuton is a known inhibitor of Cytochrome P450 isoenzymes 1A2, 2C9, and 3A4.

- **Theophylline:** Co-administration results in an approximate doubling of serum theophylline concentrations. **Management:** Reduce theophylline dose by approximately 50% and monitor serum levels closely [US].
- **Warfarin:** Increases prothrombin time (PT) significantly. **Management:** Monitor PT/INR closely and adjust anticoagulant dose as required [US].
- **Propranolol:** Doubles the AUC of propranolol, increasing beta-blocker activity (e.g., bradycardia). **Management:** Monitor patients and reduce propranolol dose as necessary [US].
- **Terfenadine:** Increases terfenadine AUC and $C_{max}$ by approximately 35%. Co-administration is **not recommended** due to potential for interaction, although QTc prolongation was not observed in small studies [US].
- **Other CYP3A4 Substrates:** Use caution and appropriate monitoring with dihydropyridine calcium channel blockers, cyclosporine, cisapride, and astemizole [US].
- **No Significant Interaction:** No clinical interaction found with prednisone, ethinyl estradiol, digoxin, phenytoin, sulfasalazine, or naproxen [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category C:** Developmental studies in rats showed reduced body weight and increased skeletal variations at high doses. Cleft palate was observed in 2.5% of rabbit fetuses at doses equivalent to human exposure. 
    - **Recommendation:** Use only if potential benefit justifies the potential risk to the fetus [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Zileuton and its metabolites are excreted in rat milk; human excretion is unknown. Due to the potential for tumorigenicity observed in animal studies, a decision should be made to discontinue nursing or the drug [US].
- **Fertility:** In rats, reductions in fetal implants and stillbirths were noted at high doses. No effect on general fertility was noted at doses up to 8 times the human exposure [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness in children <12 years of age have not been established. Use is **not recommended** in this age group due to the risk of hepatotoxicity [US].
- **Geriatric Use:** Pharmacokinetics are similar to younger adults. However, females >65 years appear to be at an increased risk for ALT elevations [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Pharmacokinetics are not significantly altered in mild, moderate, or severe renal insufficiency, or in patients requiring haemodialysis. No dosage adjustment is necessary [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Contraindicated in active liver disease (see Section 4).

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** A specific, orally active inhibitor of 5-lipoxygenase. It prevents the formation of leukotrienes ($LTB_4, LTC_4, LTD_4, LTE_4$) from arachidonic acid. These leukotrienes are mediators of inflammation, edema, mucus secretion, and bronchoconstriction in asthma.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Rapidly absorbed; $T_{max}$ of 1.7 hours. Food increases $C_{max}$ by 27% but does not affect overall AUC [US].
    - **Distribution:** Volume of distribution is ~1.2 L/kg; 93% protein bound (primarily to albumin).
    - **Metabolism:** Predominantly metabolised to diastereomeric O-glucuronide conjugates (major) and an N-dehydroxylated metabolite. CYP1A2, 2C9, and 3A4 are involved in oxidative metabolism.
    - **Elimination:** Primarily renal (94.5% in urine); terminal half-life is 2.5 hours.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Efficacy:** Two double-blind, placebo-controlled trials in mild-to-moderate asthmatics (baseline $FEV_1$ ~2.3 L).
- **Outcomes:** The 600 mg four-times-daily regimen showed statistically significant improvements in trough $FEV_1$ (mean increase of 0.10 L over placebo), AM/PM PEFR, and reduced $\beta$-agonist use.
- **Exacerbations:** In these trials, 7% of the zileuton group required systemic corticosteroids for exacerbations compared to 18.7% of the placebo group [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at controlled room temperature, 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from light and keep the container tightly closed.
