# Editorial Product Information: Zelnorm (Tegaserod)

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information documents contributed to this editorial: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document is a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising regulatory data into a single clinical reference. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority's approved labeling for definitive guidance.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Tegaserod maleate (a serotonin-4 [5-HT₄] receptor agonist).
- **Brand Name(s):** **Zelnorm** [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 6 mg oral tablets (whitish to slightly yellowish, round, flat, beveled edge, engraved with "ZEL" and "6") [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Alfasigma USA, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Irritable Bowel Syndrome with Constipation (IBS-C):** Indicated for the treatment of adult women less than 65 years of age [US].
- **Limitations of Use:** Safety and effectiveness in men with IBS-C have not been established [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Adult Dosing:** 6 mg taken twice daily [US].
- **Administration Instructions:** Tablets should be taken at least 30 minutes before meals [US].
- **Treatment Duration:** Discontinue therapy if the patient has not achieved adequate symptom control after 4 to 6 weeks of treatment [US].
- **Missed Dose:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
**Zelnorm** is contraindicated in patients with:
- **Cardiovascular History:** A history of myocardial infarction (MI), stroke, transient ischemic attack (TIA), or angina [US].
- **Intestinal Ischemia:** A history of ischemic colitis or other forms of intestinal ischemia [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Severe renal impairment (eGFR < 15 mL/min/1.73 m²) or end-stage renal disease [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Moderate or severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh B or C) [US].
- **Gastrointestinal/Biliary Disorders:** History of bowel obstruction, symptomatic gallbladder disease, suspected sphincter of Oddi dysfunction, or abdominal adhesions [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to tegaserod [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Cardiovascular Ischemic Events (including MACE):** Stroke, MI, and cardiovascular death have been reported. The risks must be balanced against the clinical benefits. Assessment of cardiovascular risk factors is required prior to initiation. Discontinue immediately if the patient experiences an MI, stroke, TIA, or angina [US].
- **Ischemic Colitis:** Postmarketing reports include ischemic colitis, mesenteric ischemia, and gangrenous bowel. Patients should be monitored for rectal bleeding, bloody diarrhea, or new/worsening abdominal pain. Discontinue immediately if these symptoms occur and do not reinitiate [US].
- **Volume Depletion associated with Diarrhea:** Diarrhea is a common side effect and may be severe, leading to hypotension, syncope, and the need for hospitalization for rehydration. Avoid use in patients currently experiencing diarrhea [US].
- **Suicidal Ideation and Behavior:** Clinical trials showed a higher frequency of suicidal ideation/behavior compared to placebo. Monitor patients for worsening depression or emergent suicidal thoughts, especially in the first few months of therapy [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions reported in >2% of patients treated with **Zelnorm** (and at a higher rate than placebo) are summarized below:

| Adverse Reaction | Zelnorm 6 mg BID (N=1,184) | Placebo (N=1,159) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| Headache | 14% | 10% |
| Abdominal Pain | 11% | 10% |
| Nausea | 8% | 7% |
| Diarrhea | 8% | 3% |
| Flatulence | 6% | 5% |
| Dyspepsia | 4% | 3% |
| Dizziness | 4% | 3% |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Major Adverse Cardiovascular Events (MACE), ischemic colitis, suicidal ideation, and severe diarrhea leading to volume depletion [US].
- **Less Common (<2%):** Anemia, vertigo, rectal hemorrhage, asthenia, increased creatine phosphokinase, increased appetite, arthropathy, tendonitis, and migraine [US].
- **Postmarketing Reports:** Sphincter of Oddi spasm, bile duct stones, cholecystitis with elevated transaminases, hepatitis, and alopecia [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Quinidine:** Co-administration increases tegaserod AUC by 50–71% and Cmax by 44–63% due to P-gp inhibition. The clinical relevance is unclear [US].
- **P-gp Inhibitors (e.g., ritonavir, clarithromycin):** May modestly increase tegaserod bioavailability [US].
- **Digoxin:** Zelnorm may reduce the AUC and Cmax of digoxin by approximately 15%; however, this is not considered clinically relevant [US].
- **Oral Contraceptives/Warfarin:** No clinically significant impact on the pharmacokinetics of these agents has been observed [US].
- **CYP Enzymes:** Tegaserod does not inhibit most major CYP isoforms (2B6, 2C8, 2C9, 2C19, 2E1, 3A4) and does not induce 3A4 or 2B6 [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Available case reports have not identified a drug-associated risk of major birth defects or miscarriage. Animal studies at high doses (71x human dose) showed decreased pup survival and developmental delays [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Breastfeeding is not recommended. While human data are absent, tegaserod is excreted in rat milk at high concentrations (33:1 milk-to-plasma ratio), and there is a potential for serious adverse reactions (including tumorigenicity) in the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** No effects on fertility or reproductive performance were observed in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Zelnorm is not indicated for patients aged 65 years and older [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    - No adjustment for mild-to-moderate impairment (eGFR ≥ 30 mL/min/1.73 m²). 
    - Contraindicated in severe renal impairment (eGFR < 15 mL/min/1.73 m²) or ESRD [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** 
    - No adjustment for mild impairment (Child-Pugh A).
    - Contraindicated in moderate or severe impairment (Child-Pugh B or C) [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** A serotonin-4 (5-HT₄) receptor agonist. It stimulates the peristaltic reflex and intestinal secretion, inhibits visceral sensitivity, and enhances basal motor activity in the GI tract [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Absolute bioavailability is ~10% (fasted). Tmax is ~1 hour.
    - **Food Effect:** AUC is reduced by 40–65% and Cmax by 20–40% if taken with food.
    - **Distribution:** Protein binding is ~98%.
    - **Metabolism:** Hydrolysis in the stomach and direct glucuronidation. The main metabolite (M29) is inactive.
    - **Elimination:** Approximately two-thirds is excreted unchanged in feces; one-third is excreted in urine as metabolites. Terminal half-life is 4.6 to 8.1 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
Efficacy was established in three multicenter, double-blind, placebo-controlled trials involving 2,470 women with IBS-C. Responders were defined by a categorical scale of "relief" regarding overall well-being and bowel habits. 
- **Month 1 Results:** Zelnorm (31–35%) significantly outperformed placebo (17–22%) across the trials.
- **Month 3 Results:** Zelnorm (39–44%) remained superior to placebo (28–39%), though the treatment difference narrowed in some studies [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C–30°C (59°F–86°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from moisture [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Prescription-only (Rx only) [US].
