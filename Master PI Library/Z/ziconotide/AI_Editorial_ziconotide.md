# Editorial Product Information: Ziconotide

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial synthesis: United States (FDA). 
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of ziconotide for healthcare professionals, synthesising regulatory information into a coherent clinical reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority's approved documentation for definitive prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Ziconotide acetate; a synthetic equivalent of a naturally occurring conopeptide (from the *Conus magus* marine snail).
- **Chemical Class:** N-type calcium channel antagonist; polybasic 25-amino acid peptide.
- **Brand Name(s):** **Prialt** [US].
- **ATC Code:** N02BG08.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Intrathecal solution [US]:
        - 25 mcg/mL (20 mL single-use vial).
        - 100 mcg/mL (1 mL or 5 mL single-use vials).
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intrathecal (IT) infusion only. **PRIALT is not for intravenous administration** [US].
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** TerSera Therapeutics LLC [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Management of Severe Chronic Pain:** Indicated for adult patients where intrathecal therapy is clinically warranted and who are either intolerant of or refractory to other treatment modalities, including systemic analgesics, adjunctive therapies, or intrathecal morphine [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Administration:** Must be administered by or under the direction of a physician experienced in intrathecal administration and the use of microinfusion devices [US].
- **Device Compatibility:** Intended for use with the Medtronic SynchroMed II or SynchroMed III Infusion Systems [US].
- **Preparation:** 
    - The 25 mcg/mL formulation is used undiluted for pump priming and initial dosing [US].
    - The 100 mcg/mL formulation may be used undiluted once a stable dose is reached, or diluted with preservative-free 0.9% Sodium Chloride [US].
    - **Note:** Saline containing preservatives must never be used due to neurotoxicity risks [US].

### Titration Schedule
- **Initial Dose:** No more than 2.4 mcg/day (0.1 mcg/hr) [US].
- **Titration:** Increase by up to 2.4 mcg/day (0.1 mcg/hr) at intervals of no more than 2 to 3 times per week [US].
- **Maximum Dose:** 19.2 mcg/day (0.8 mcg/hr) by Day 21 [US].
- **Dose Adjustments:** Frequency of titration and dose increments should be guided by analgesic response and the occurrence of adverse events [US].

### Pump Management
- **Initial Fill:** The pump reservoir should be refilled within 14 days of the initial fill to account for drug adsorption to device surfaces [US].
- **Refills:** Subsequent refills should occur at least every 40 days (if diluted) or every 84 days (if undiluted) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to ziconotide or any excipients [US].
- **Hazardous IT Conditions:** Presence of infection at the injection site, uncontrolled bleeding diathesis, or spinal canal obstruction impairing CSF circulation [US].
- **Neuropsychiatric History:** Patients with a pre-existing history of psychosis [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warning [US]
- **Neuropsychiatric Adverse Reactions:** Severe psychiatric symptoms and neurological impairment may occur. Ziconotide is contraindicated in patients with a history of psychosis. All patients must be monitored for cognitive impairment, hallucinations, mood changes, or altered consciousness. Therapy should be discontinued if serious signs or symptoms emerge.

### General Warnings
- **Cognitive Impairment:** Occurs gradually over several weeks. Common symptoms include confusion (33%), memory impairment (22%), and speech disorders [US]. Reversal typically occurs within 2 weeks of discontinuation.
- **Meningitis:** Risk is significantly higher with external microinfusion devices (93% in certain trials) compared to internal pumps [US]. Strict aseptic technique is mandatory.
- **Reduced Level of Consciousness:** Patients may become unresponsive or stuporous. There is no known pharmacological antagonist [US].
- **Creatine Kinase (CK) Elevation:** Approximately 40% of patients show elevated CK levels. Monitor serum CK periodically (e.g., every two weeks for the first month, then monthly) [US]. Discontinue if levels remain high or rise alongside neuromuscular symptoms.
- **Opiate Withdrawal:** Ziconotide cannot prevent opiate withdrawal symptoms. If transitioning from intrathecal opiates, taper the opiate dose gradually over several weeks [US].
- **Driving/Machinery:** Cognitive impairment and sedation may impair the ability to perform hazardous tasks [US].

| Nausea | 40% |
| Confusional state | 15% - 33% |
| Somnolence | 17% |

| Ataxia | 14% |
| Headache | 13% |
| Blurred vision | 12% |

### Serious and Medically Important Reactions
- **Psychiatric:** Hallucinations (12%), paranoid reactions (3%), suicidal ideation, and suicide attempts (<2%) [US].
- **Neurological:** Aphasia, seizures (clonic/grand mal), stupor, and loss of consciousness [US].
- **Systemic:** Rhabdomyolysis and acute renal failure associated with extreme CK elevations [US].
- **Postmarketing Experience:** Angioedema, bullous dermatitis, and skin ulcers [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Formal Studies:** No formal pharmacokinetic drug-drug interaction studies have been conducted [US].
- **Metabolism-based Interactions:** Interactions with cytochrome P450 enzymes are unlikely, as ziconotide is a peptide degraded by ubiquitous peptidases [US].
- **Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** 
    - **CNS Depressants:** Combined use with antidepressants, anxiolytics, or neuroleptics increases the risk of confusion and dizziness [US].
    - **Intrathecal Opiates:** The combination is not recommended [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Risk Summary [US]:** Available human data are insufficient to identify drug-associated risks. 
    - **Animal Data:** No malformations observed in rats or rabbits at doses significantly higher than human exposure. However, embryo-fetal mortality occurred in rats at 700-fold the human dose [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** No data on presence in human milk. Monitor infants for sedation, respiratory depression, and feeding difficulties [US].
- **Fertility:** Significant reductions in corpora lutea and implantation sites were seen in female rats at high intravenous doses [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness in patients under 18 years have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Higher incidence of confusion (42% in those $\ge$ 65 years vs 29% in younger patients) [US]. Start dosing at the lower end of the range.
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not formally studied; however, systemic exposure is low following intrathecal administration [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
Ziconotide binds selectively to N-type voltage-sensitive calcium channels on primary nociceptive afferent nerves (A-$\delta$ and C) in the superficial layers of the dorsal horn. This blockade inhibits the release of excitatory neurotransmitters, providing antinociception [US].

### Pharmacokinetics
- **Distribution:** Approximately 50% plasma protein bound. CSF volume of distribution is approximately 140 mL [US].
- **Metabolism:** Cleaved by endopeptidases and exopeptidases.
- **Elimination:** 
    - **CSF Half-life:** 4.6 hours [US].
    - **Serum Half-life:** 1.3 hours [US].
    - **Excretion:** Less than 1% recovered unchanged in urine [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
The efficacy of ziconotide was established in three double-blind, placebo-controlled multicenter trials (N=457). A "slow titration" schedule (up to 19.2 mcg/day over 21 days) was found to be more tolerable than "fast titration" [US]. 
- **Primary Outcome:** In the slow titration study, ziconotide showed a 12% mean improvement in Visual Analog Scale of Pain Intensity (VASPI) compared to 5% for placebo at Week 3 [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Refrigerate at $2^\circ\text{C}$ to $8^\circ\text{C}$ ($36^\circ\text{F}$ to $46^\circ\text{F}$) [US].
- **Handling:** Do not freeze. Protect vials from light.
- **Diluted Solution:** Once diluted aseptically, may be stored refrigerated for up to 24 hours [US].
