# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) provided from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical and regulatory data into a single reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult the current local approved PI for full prescribing details.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP).
- **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule/amino acid derivative ((R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt).
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Sterile solution for injection, 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency (Inborn Errors of Metabolism):** For the acute and chronic management of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that result in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg [US].
    - **Administration:** Administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion.
    - **Titration:** In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose is often followed by equivalent doses every 3 or 4 hours (never less than every 6 hours) over the first 24 hours.
    - **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses typically remain at 50 mg/kg, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been used.
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
    - **Administration:** Administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
    - **Titration:** Adjustments should be guided by pre-dialysis (trough) plasma levocarnitine levels. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of treatment.

### Monitoring
- **Metabolic Patients:** Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target range 35 to 60 µmol/L) is recommended [US].
- **ESRD Patients:** Therapy may be prompted by trough plasma levels below the normal range of 40 to 50 µmol/L [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Inspect visually for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration.
- Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as it contains no preservatives [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **None Known:** There are no known contraindications for levocarnitine injection listed in the available source [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions may occur within minutes of injection. If a reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and monitor for recurrence if re-administration is attempted [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with a known history, an increase in seizure frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Oral vs. Intravenous Use:** The safety and efficacy of **oral** levocarnitine have not been established in patients with renal insufficiency. High doses of oral levocarnitine in ESRD patients may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse events in ESRD clinical trials (occurring at ≥5%) regardless of causality included:

| Body System | Adverse Event (Incidence ≥5%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Injection site reaction (33%), Pain (30%), Flu syndrome (25%), Headache (22%), Abdominal pain (9%), Fever (7%) |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension (20%), Hypotension (14%), Tachycardia (6%), Palpitations (5%) |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea (16%), Vomiting (15%), Nausea (8%), Anorexia (5%) |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis (23%), Cough increase (9%), Rhinitis (9%), Dyspnea (7%) |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness (13%), Paresthesia (5%), Depression (5%) |
| **Other** | Hypercalcemia (9%), Anemia (6%), Pruritus (5%) |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm (rare/post-marketing) [US].
- **Nuisance Effects:** Transient nausea, vomiting, and body odor have been observed [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have been observed when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after dose adjustments in patients on warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not specified in current format]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows indicate increased carnitine concentrations in milk following administration. Risk to the infant of excess carnitine intake must be weighed against the benefit of maternal supplementation [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders is based on mg/kg, which applies to paediatric patients. Specific age-related secondary deficiency definitions are noted for premature infants and newborns (levels below age-related normal) [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in the available source [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Intravenous levocarnitine is indicated for ESRD patients on dialysis. However, **oral** levocarnitine is not recommended in severe renal impairment due to metabolite accumulation [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring carrier molecule required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites are trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Mean apparent terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours. Approximately 76% of an intravenous dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours (uncorrected for endogenous levels) [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In ESRD patients, intravenous administration results in significantly increased plasma levocarnitine concentrations (Cmax ~1190 µmol/L following 20 mg/kg) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of ESRD patients (n=12), 20 mg/kg doses three times weekly after dialysis increased mean pre-dialysis trough concentrations from 21.3 µmol/L to 190 µmol/L after 8 weeks. While the efficacy in increasing carnitine levels is proven, the direct effect on clinical outcomes in the ESRD population has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Precautions:** Avoid excessive heat. Protect from freezing.
- **Stability:** Compatible with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s (0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) for up to 24 hours at room temperature in PVC bags [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified for other jurisdictions as only US source was provided.
