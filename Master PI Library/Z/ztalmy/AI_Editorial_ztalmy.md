# Editorial Product Information: ZTALMY (ganaxolone)

## Editorial Note
*   **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA)
*   **Purpose:** This document is a harmonised editorial synthesis of the available Product Information for **ZTALMY**. It is intended as a clinical overview for healthcare professionals and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. 
*   **Note on Sources:** Information provided in this editorial is derived solely from the United States (FDA) PI. Information regarding other jurisdictions (e.g., Australian, European, Canadian) is "Not specified in available sources" unless otherwise noted.

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredient:** Ganaxolone (neuroactive steroid GABA$_A$ receptor positive modulator)
*   **Brand Name(s):** **ZTALMY** [US]
*   **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Dosage Form and Strength:** Oral suspension, 50 mg/mL (white to off-white cherry-flavored). [US]
*   **Route of Administration:** Oral
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Immedica Pharma US Inc. [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Seizures in CDKL5 Deficiency Disorder (CDD):** Indicated for the treatment of seizures associated with cyclin-dependent kinase-like 5 (CDKL5) deficiency disorder in patients 2 years of age and older. [US-approved]

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
*   **Administration:** Must be administered three times daily (TID) with food. [US]
*   **Preparation:** Shake the bottle thoroughly for at least 1 minute. Wait 1 minute before measuring the dose with the provided oral syringe.
*   **In-use Shelf Life:** Discard any unused suspension 30 days after first opening the bottle. [US]

### Weight-Based Titration Schedule
Dosage should be increased no more frequently than every 7 days based on tolerability.

## 4. Contraindications
*   None. [US]

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
*   **Somnolence and Sedation:** Highly prevalent (44% vs 24% for placebo). Patients should be monitored for sedative effects. Advise against driving or operating machinery until the drug's effect on the individual is known. Concomitant use with alcohol or other CNS depressants may potentiate these effects. [US]
*   **Suicidal Behavior and Ideation:** As with all antiepileptic drugs (AEDs), **ZTALMY** increases the risk of suicidal thoughts or behavior. Monitor patients for the emergence or worsening of depression or unusual changes in mood. [US]
*   **Withdrawal of AEDs:** Abrupt discontinuation should be avoided to minimize the risk of increased seizure frequency and status epilepticus. If a serious adverse event necessitates rapid withdrawal, it should be done under close medical supervision. [US]

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions reported at an incidence of $\ge$ 5% and at least twice the placebo rate:
*   **Very Common (>10%):** Somnolence (38%), Pyrexia (18%), Upper respiratory tract infection (10%). [US]
*   **Common (1–10%):** Salivary hypersecretion (6%), Sedation (6%), Seasonal allergy (6%), Influenza (4%), Bronchitis (4%), Gait disturbance (4%), Nasal congestion (4%). [US]

**Serious Adverse Effects:**
*   Treatment discontinuation occurred in 2% of patients due to somnolence and seizure. [US]

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **UGT Inhibitors (e.g., valproic acid):** May increase ganaxolone exposure. Consider dosage reduction of **ZTALMY** if initiating a UGT inhibitor. [US]
*   **CYP3A4 Inducers:** Strong (e.g., rifampin) or moderate inducers significantly decrease ganaxolone exposure (e.g., AUC decreased by 68% with rifampin). Avoid concomitant use; if unavoidable, consider a dosage increase of **ZTALMY** without exceeding maximum recommended limits. [US]
*   **CNS Depressants and Alcohol:** Potentiate somnolence and sedation. [US]
*   **CYP3A4 Substrates:** No clinically relevant interaction expected with CYP3A4 substrates (e.g., midazolam). [US]

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy:** [US: No available human data]. Animal studies (mice and rats) demonstrated fetal malformations, neurobehavioral impairment, and growth impairment at exposures lower than the human maximum recommended dose (MRHD). In rats, neuronal death was observed during a period of brain development corresponding to the human third trimester through early childhood. [US]
*   **Pregnancy Registry:** North American Antiepileptic Drug (NAAED) Pregnancy Registry. [US]
*   **Breastfeeding:** Ganaxolone is excreted in human milk. The relative infant dose is estimated to be $<1\%$ of the maternal dose. The effects on milk production or the breastfed infant are unknown. [US]

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy established for patients $\ge$ 2 years of age. Safety in patients $<2$ years has not been established. [US]
*   **Elderly Use:** CDD is primarily a paediatric and young adult condition; no clinical data available for patients $\ge$ 65 years. [US]
*   **Hepatic Impairment:**
    *   **Mild/Moderate (Child-Pugh A/B):** No dosage adjustment required. [US]
    *   **Severe (Child-Pugh C):** Requires a reduced titration and maintenance schedule due to significantly increased (up to 269%) ganaxolone exposure. Maintenance max is 7 mg/kg TID ($\le$ 28 kg) or 200 mg TID (> 28 kg). [US]
*   **Renal Impairment:** No clinically significant changes in exposure expected in patients with creatinine clearance $<90$ mL/min. [US]

## 10. Pharmacology
*   **Mechanism of Action:** Positive allosteric modulator of the GABA$_A$ receptor in the central nervous system.
*   **Pharmacokinetics:**
    *   **Absorption:** $T_{max}$ of 2 to 3 hours. Administration with a high-fat meal increases $C_{max}$ 3-fold and AUC 2-fold compared to fasted conditions. [US]
    *   **Distribution:** 99% plasma protein bound. [US]
    *   **Metabolism:** Extensively metabolized via CYP3A4/5, CYP2B6, CYP2C19, CYP2D6, and multiple UGT enzymes (1A3, 1A6, 1A9, 2B7, 2B15). [US]
    *   **Elimination:** Terminal half-life is approximately 34 hours. Primarily excreted in feces (55%) and urine (18%). [US]

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   **Study 1 (NCT03572933):** A double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in 101 patients (ages 2–19) with CDD. 
*   **Primary Outcome:** Median percent change in 28-day frequency of major motor seizures.
*   **Results:** Patients treated with **ZTALMY** showed a 31% reduction in seizure frequency compared to a 7% reduction in the placebo group (p=0.0036). [US]

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   **Temperature:** Store upright at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F to 86°F). [US]
*   **Handling:** Keep the cap tightly closed. 
*   **Shelf Life:** Discard unused portion 30 days after first opening. [US]
