# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial has been synthesized from the official Product Information (PI) provided by the **United States (FDA)**. 
- At the time of compilation, official PI documents from other jurisdictions (e.g., Australian (TGA), European (EMA), UK (MHRA), Canadian (Health Canada), Japanese (PMDA)) were not provided for review. Consequently, certain regional nuances, such as specific pregnancy categories or poison schedules for those regions, are noted as "Not specified in available sources."
- This document is a harmonized editorial overview intended for use by healthcare professionals and is not a substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP).
- **Chemical Class:** A naturally occurring carrier molecule; (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Brand Names:** Generic (manufactured for Baxter Healthcare Corporation) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Injection (solution): 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Indicated for the acute and chronic management of patients with inborn metabolic errors resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
- **General Dosing:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg [US].
- **Administration:** Administer as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion [US].
- **Severe Crisis:** A loading dose is often administered, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours. Dosing frequency should be every 3 to 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours [US].
- **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses typically range around 50 mg/kg, though higher doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilized [US].

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
- **Administration:** Administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].
- **Dose Adjustment:** Adjustments should be guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine levels. Downward titration (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].

### General Instructions
- **Monitoring:** For metabolic disorders, weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L) is recommended [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable for 24 hours at room temperature in PVC bags when mixed with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known:** There are no listed contraindications for levocarnitine injection in the available source [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in dialysis patients. Reactions may occur within minutes of administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue immediately and do not re-administer without a careful risk-benefit analysis [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with a known seizure disorder, an increase in the frequency or severity of seizures has been observed [US].
- **Metabolite Accumulation:** Chronic administration of high-dose **oral** levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function or ESRD may lead to the accumulation of trimethylamine (TMA) and trimethylamine-N-oxide (TMAO) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Common (>10%):** Transient nausea and vomiting [US].
- **Trial-Specific Data (ESRD Patients):** In placebo-controlled trials, the following events occurred at a frequency ≥5% (regardless of causality):
    - **General:** Hypertension (18–21%), Diarrhea (9–35%), Headache (3–37%), Fever (5–12%), and Injection site reaction (27–38%) [US].
    - **Other:** Pharyngitis, chest pain, and asthenia [US].
- **Uncommon/Rare (<1%):** Body odor and gastritis [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Anaphylaxis and seizures [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have been observed when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after any dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin anticoagulation [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not assigned a letter category in the current PI, but animal studies (rats/rabbits) at 3.8 times the human dose showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
    - **Other Regions:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk concentrations after administration. The benefits to the mother must be weighed against the potential risk of excess carnitine intake in the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility was found in animal reproductive studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for paediatric patients with metabolic disorders is included in the general weight-based dosing (50 mg/kg) [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically detailed in the provided source; clinical trials primarily focused on adult ESRD populations.
- **Renal Impairment:** Oral levocarnitine safety/efficacy has not been evaluated in renal insufficiency; intravenous use is specifically indicated for carnitine deficiency in ESRD patients undergoing dialysis [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for mammalian energy metabolism. it facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids into cellular mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In patients with organic acidemias, levocarnitine promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters, which are rapidly excreted [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Following IV administration, approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. The mean terminal elimination half-life is approximately 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients, 20 mg/kg IV three times weekly after dialysis increased mean pre-dialysis (trough) plasma carnitine levels from 20 µmol/L to 190 µmol/L at 8 weeks.
- **Dose-Ranging Data:** Studies using 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses showed dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks. While carnitine levels were increased, the specific effect on clinical outcomes in the ESRD population has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Conditions:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat [US].
- **Handling:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial, as the formulation contains no preservatives [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Rx-only [US].
- **Other Jurisdictions:** Poison/schedule classifications (e.g., Australian S4) are not specified in available sources.
