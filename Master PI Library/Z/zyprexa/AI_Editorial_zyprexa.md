# Editorial Product Information: Olanzapine (Parenteral Formulations)

## Editorial Note
- The provided jurisdictional Product Information (PI) documents were sourced from the following regulatory authorities: **Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration)** and the **United States (Food and Drug Administration)**.
- **Purpose:** This document is a harmonised editorial synthesis intended for use by healthcare professionals. It integrates clinical data and regulatory requirements for different injectable formulations of olanzapine (short-acting and long-acting).
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial summary and does not replace the official PI or labelling required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Olanzapine (thienobenzodiazepine class).
- **Brand Names:** 
    - **ZYPREXA IM** (Short-acting injection) [Australian].
    - **ZYPREXA RELPREVV** (Long-acting/extended-release injection) [US].
- **ATC Code:** N05AH03.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:**
    - **Short-acting:** 10 mg lyophilised powder for reconstitution [Australian].
    - **Long-acting (Extended-Release):** 210 mg, 300 mg, and 405 mg powder for suspension in a single-dose convenience kit [US].
- **Routes of Administration:** Intramuscular (IM) only. **Do not administer intravenously or subcutaneously.**
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** 
    - Pharmaco (Australia) Ltd [Australian].
    - H2-Pharma LLC; licensed by Cheplapharm [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Schizophrenia:** Management and treatment of schizophrenia in adults [Australian, US].
- **Acute Agitation:** Rapid control of agitation and disturbed behaviour in patients with schizophrenia, related psychoses, or acute mania associated with Bipolar 1 Disorder, specifically when oral therapy is inappropriate [Australian-approved; not specified for Relprevv in US sources].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Short-Acting Intramuscular (Zyprexa IM) [Australian]
- **General Dosing:** Recommended dose is 10 mg as a single IM injection. A range of 5–10 mg may be used based on clinical status.
- **Repeat Dosing:** A second injection (up to 10 mg) may be given 2 hours after the first. A third dose (up to 10 mg) may be given 4 hours after the second. 
- **Maximum Dose:** 30 mg per 24-hour period.
- **Administration:** Reconstitute with 2.1 mL sterile water for injection (yields 5 mg/mL). Use within 1 hour.

### Long-Acting Intramuscular (Zyprexa Relprevv) [US]
- **General Dosing:** Dosing is based on the established oral olanzapine dose.
- **Maintenance Schedule:**
    - Oral 10 mg/day $\rightarrow$ 210 mg/2 weeks or 405 mg/4 weeks.
    - Oral 15 mg/day $\rightarrow$ 300 mg/2 weeks.
    - Oral 20 mg/day $\rightarrow$ 300 mg/2 weeks.
- **Administration:** Deep gluteal injection only. Must be administered by a healthcare professional. 
- **Observation:** Patients must be observed for at least 3 hours post-injection in a facility with emergency access due to the risk of Post-Injection Delirium/Sedation Syndrome (PDSS) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to olanzapine or any excipients.
- **Simultaneous Benzodiazepines:** Simultaneous administration of **Zyprexa IM** and parenteral benzodiazepines is not recommended due to risks of excessive sedation and cardiorespiratory depression [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warnings [US]
- **Post-Injection Delirium/Sedation Syndrome (PDSS):** Risk of severe sedation (including coma) and delirium following each injection of the long-acting formulation. Must be administered in a certified setting.
- **Increased Mortality in Elderly Patients with Dementia-Related Psychosis:** Antipsychotic drugs increase the risk of death in elderly patients with dementia-related psychosis. Olanzapine is not approved for this population.

### General Warnings
- **Metabolic Changes:** Atypical antipsychotics are associated with hyperglycaemia (sometimes extreme, leading to ketoacidosis), dyslipidaemia, and significant weight gain. Regular monitoring of blood glucose, lipids, and weight is required.
- **Neuroleptic Malignant Syndrome (NMS):** Potentially fatal complex; requires immediate discontinuation.
- **Tardive Dyskinesia:** Risk increases with duration of treatment; consider discontinuation if signs appear.
- **Drug Reaction with Eosinophilia and Systemic Symptoms (DRESS):** Discontinue if suspected.
- **Orthostatic Hypotension/Bradycardia:** Risk of syncope and heart rate changes, especially during initial dose titration or following IM administration. Patients should remain recumbent if drowsy or dizzy [Australian].
- **Cerebrovascular Adverse Events (CVAE):** Increased risk of stroke and TIA in elderly patients with dementia.

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Very Common (>10%):** Weight gain, somnolence, increased prolactin, triglycerides (fasting), total cholesterol (fasting), glucose (fasting), orthostatic hypotension [Australian, US].
- **Common (1–10%):** Dizziness, akathisia, increased appetite, constipation, dry mouth, ALT/AST elevations, fatigue, and injection site pain [Australian, US].
- **Formulation-Specific:**
    - **Short-acting IM:** Bradycardia, tachycardia [Australian].
    - **Long-acting Relprevv:** PDSS (delirium, confusion, disorientation, slurred speech) [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Sudden cardiac death, thromboembolism, rhabdomyolysis, and neutropenia/agranulocytosis.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **CYP1A2 Inducers:** Smoking and carbamazepine increase olanzapine clearance by 33–44% [Australian, US].
- **CYP1A2 Inhibitors:** Fluvoxamine and ciprofloxacin can significantly increase olanzapine concentrations.
- **CNS Activating Substances:** Caution with alcohol and other centrally acting drugs.
- **Antihypertensives:** Olanzapine may potentiate hypotensive effects.
- **Levodopa/Dopamine Agonists:** Olanzapine may antagonise these agents.
- **Benzodiazepines:** Enhanced somnolence with lorazepam; risk of cardiorespiratory depression with parenteral benzodiazepines [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Category C** [Australian]. 
    - **Risk Summary:** Neonates exposed in the third trimester are at risk for extrapyramidal and/or withdrawal symptoms [US]. Use only if potential benefit outweighs risk.
- **Breastfeeding:** Olanzapine is excreted in human milk. It is recommended that women should not breastfeed while receiving olanzapine [Australian]. If breastfeeding, monitor the infant for sedation and extrapyramidal symptoms [US].
- **Fertility:** May cause a reversible reduction in female fertility due to hyperprolactinaemia [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy not established in patients under 18 years. Adolescents show higher rates of weight gain and lipid/prolactin elevations compared to adults [Australian, US].
- **Elderly:** Increased risk of death and stroke in dementia-related psychosis. Consider a lower starting dose (5 mg/injection [Australian] or 150 mg/4 weeks [US]).
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Clearance is not significantly altered by renal or hepatic impairment alone, but a lower starting dose (5 mg/day or 150 mg/4 weeks) should be considered as clinical experience is limited [Australian, US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Atypical antipsychotic with high affinity for serotonin 5HT2A/2C, 5HT6, dopamine D1-4, histamine H1, and alpha-1 adrenergic receptors. Its efficacy is mediated through D2 and 5HT2 antagonism.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Rapid with short-acting IM (peak in 15–45 mins). Extended-release Relprevv dissolves slowly (peak in first week; half-life ~30 days) [US].
    - **Distribution:** ~93% protein bound.
    - **Metabolism:** Hepatic via CYP1A2 and CYP2D6 (minor), and glucuronidation.
    - **Elimination:** ~57% in urine, ~30% in faeces.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Schizophrenia:** Pivotal 6-week and 8-week trials established superiority over placebo and comparability/superiority to haloperidol in reducing PANSS and BPRS scores.
- **Agitation:** Three short-term (24-hour) trials showed olanzapine 2.5–10 mg IM was significantly more effective than placebo and comparable to haloperidol/lorazepam in reducing agitation (PANSS-EC) [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Short-acting IM:** Store below 25°C. Protect from light. Use within 1 hour of reconstitution [Australian].
- **Long-acting Relprevv:** Store at room temperature not exceeding 30°C. Reconstituted suspension stable for 24 hours at room temperature [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule:** Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine) [Australian].
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Restricted Access:** **Zyprexa Relprevv** is only available through a restricted distribution REMS program in the United States.
