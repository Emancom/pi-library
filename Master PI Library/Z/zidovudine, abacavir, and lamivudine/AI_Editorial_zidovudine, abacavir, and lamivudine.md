# Editorial Product Information: Abacavir, Lamivudine, and Zidovudine

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial:
    - **United States (FDA)**: Abacavir, Lamivudine and Zidovudine Tablets (Lupin Pharmaceuticals, Inc.)
- **Purpose**: This is a harmonised editorial synthesis of the provided product information, designed as a concise reference for healthcare professionals.
- **Prescribing Note**: This document is an editorial summary and does not replace the official, jurisdiction-specific PI required for prescribing in any specific region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients**: Abacavir (as sulfate), Lamivudine, and Zidovudine.
- **Chemical Class**: Nucleoside Reverse Transcriptase Inhibitors (NRTIs).
- **Brand Names**: Not specified as a single brand in the primary source; provided as a generic triple combination [US].
- **ATC Code**: J05AR04.
- **Dosage Form and Strength**: Film-coated tablets containing **abacavir** 300 mg, **lamivudine** 150 mg, and **zidovudine** 300 mg [US].
- **Route of Administration**: Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor**: Lupin Pharmaceuticals, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **HIV-1 Infection**: Indicated as part of a combination antiretroviral regimen for the treatment of Human Immunodeficiency Virus type 1 (HIV-1) infection [US].
- **Limitations of Use**: Data are limited regarding use as monotherapy in patients with high baseline viral loads (>100,000 copies/mL) [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adults and Paediatrics $\ge$ 40 kg)**: One tablet twice daily [US].
- **Administration**: May be taken with or without food [US].
- **Fixed-Dose Limitations**: Because this is a fixed-dose combination, it should not be used in patients requiring dose adjustments, such as those weighing <40 kg or those with renal or hepatic impairment [US].
- **Missed Doses**: Patients should take a missed dose as soon as remembered, but must not double the next dose [US].
- **Pre-treatment Screening**: All patients must be screened for the **HLA-B\*5701** allele before initiating or reinitiating therapy [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Genetic Predisposition**: Presence of the **HLA-B\*5701** allele [US].
- **Hypersensitivity**: Prior hypersensitivity to **abacavir**, **lamivudine**, or **zidovudine** [US].
- **Hepatic Function**: Moderate or severe hepatic impairment [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning: Hypersensitivity Reactions [US]**: 
    - Serious and sometimes fatal reactions have occurred with **abacavir**. 
    - Discontinue immediately if hypersensitivity is suspected. 
    - **NEVER** restart this product or any other **abacavir**-containing product following a suspected reaction, as life-threatening hypotension or death can occur within hours.
- **Boxed Warning: Hematologic Toxicity [US]**: **Zidovudine** is associated with neutropenia and severe anemia, particularly in advanced HIV disease.
- **Boxed Warning: Myopathy [US]**: Symptomatic myopathy and myositis are associated with prolonged **zidovudine** use.
- **Boxed Warning: Lactic Acidosis and Severe Hepatomegaly with Steatosis [US]**: Fatal cases reported with nucleoside analogues. Discontinue therapy if clinical or laboratory signs of lactic acidosis or hepatotoxicity occur.
- **Boxed Warning: Exacerbation of Hepatitis B (HBV) [US]**: Severe acute exacerbations of HBV have been reported in co-infected patients who discontinued **lamivudine**. Monitor hepatic function closely for several months post-treatment.
- **Immune Reconstitution Syndrome**: Inflammatory responses to indolent or residual opportunistic infections (e.g., MAC, CMV, PCP, TB) may occur. Autoimmune disorders (e.g., Graves’ disease) are also reported [US].
- **Lipoatrophy**: Prolonged **zidovudine** use is associated with loss of subcutaneous fat (face, limbs, buttocks) [US].
- **Myocardial Infarction**: Observational studies have shown an association between **abacavir** and MI; however, meta-analyses of randomised trials are inconclusive [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common Adverse Reactions
| Adverse Reaction | Frequency (Grades 2-4) [US] |
| :--- | :--- |
| Nausea | 19% |
| Headache | 13% |
| Malaise and Fatigue | 12% |
| Nausea and Vomiting | 10% |
| Hypersensitivity Reaction | 8% |
| Diarrhea | 7% |
| Depressive Disorders | 6% |

### Serious Adverse Effects
- **Hematologic**: Grade 3/4 neutropenia (<750/mm³) reported in 5% of subjects [US].
- **Biochemical**: Elevated CPK (>4 x ULN) in 7%; elevated ALT (>5 x ULN) in 6% [US].
- **Postmarketing Reports**: Pancreatitis, stomatitis, aplastic anemia, pure red cell aplasia, rhabdomyolysis, Stevens-Johnson syndrome (SJS), and toxic epidermal necrolysis (TEN) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Methadone**: **Abacavir** increases methadone clearance (22% increase). A small number of patients may require methadone dose escalation [US].
- **Riociguat**: **Abacavir** may increase riociguat exposure via CYP1A1 inhibition. Consider riociguat dose reduction [US].
- **Sorbitol**: Co-administration of **lamivudine** and sorbitol (excipient) results in a dose-dependent decrease in **lamivudine** exposure. Avoid chronic co-administration where possible [US].
- **Zidovudine Antagonists**: Avoid concomitant use with stavudine, doxorubicin, or ribavirin [US].
- **Hematologic Toxins**: Agents like ganciclovir and interferon alfa may increase **zidovudine** toxicity [US].
- **Valproic Acid**: May increase **zidovudine** AUC by approximately 80% [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy**:
    - **US Classification**: No letter category provided in the current source, but data from the Antiretroviral Pregnancy Registry (APR) show no difference in the risk of birth defects compared to the background population [US].
    - **Clinical Consideration**: **Zidovudine** may cause hyperlactatemia (mitochondrial dysfunction) in the neonate [US].
- **Breastfeeding**: HIV-1-infected mothers are instructed **not** to breastfeed to avoid the risk of postnatal HIV transmission and potential adverse reactions in the infant [US].
- **Fertility**: No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies at clinically relevant exposures [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use**: Not recommended for children weighing <40 kg due to the fixed-dose nature of the tablet [US].
- **Geriatric Use**: Use caution; clinical trials did not include sufficient numbers of subjects $\ge$ 65 years to determine different responses [US].
- **Renal Impairment**: **Contraindicated** in patients with CrCl <50 mL/min because individual components cannot be dose-adjusted [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment**:
    - **Mild (Child-Pugh Class A)**: Avoid this combination; use individual components to allow for **abacavir** dose reduction [US].
    - **Moderate to Severe (Child-Pugh Class B or C)**: **Contraindicated** [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action**: All three components are NRTIs. They are intracellularly converted to active triphosphates which inhibit HIV-1 reverse transcriptase by competing with natural substrates and causing viral DNA chain termination [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics**:
    - **Absorption**: Rapidly absorbed; oral bioavailability is 86% for **abacavir** and **lamivudine**, and 64% for **zidovudine** [US].
    - **Distribution**: All three drugs cross the blood-brain barrier and the placenta [US].
    - **Metabolism**: **Abacavir** (alcohol dehydrogenase/glucuronidation); **Zidovudine** (glucuronidation); **Lamivudine** (minimal metabolism) [US].
    - **Elimination Half-life**: **Abacavir** (1.45h), **Lamivudine** (13-19h), **Zidovudine** (0.5-3h) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Study CNA3005**: A double-blind, multicenter trial (n=562) compared the triple NRTI combination to indinavir plus lamivudine/zidovudine. 
- **Outcome**: At 48 weeks, 49% of the triple NRTI group achieved HIV-1 RNA <400 copies/mL compared to 50% in the indinavir group. 
- **Sub-group Analysis**: Patients with baseline viral loads >100,000 copies/mL showed a lower response rate (31% achieving <50 copies/mL) compared to the protease inhibitor-containing arm (45%) [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature**: Store at 25°C (77°F).
- **Excursions**: Permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
