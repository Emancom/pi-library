# Editorial Product Information: Zolbetuximab

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** Australian (TGA), US (FDA).
- **Purpose:** This document is a harmonised editorial synthesis of official Product Information from the listed jurisdictions, intended as a coherent reference for healthcare professionals.
- **Disclaimer:** This editorial synthesis is not a substitute for the official Product Information approved in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority's approved labelling before prescribing.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Zolbetuximab (zolbetuximab-clzb [US]).
- **Chemical Class:** Claudin 18.2-directed cytolytic antibody; chimeric (mouse/human IgG1) monoclonal antibody.
- **Brand Name:** **VYLOY**.
- **ATC Code:** L01FX31.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Lyophilised powder for reconstitution in single-dose vials, available as 100 mg and 300 mg strengths.
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous infusion only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Astellas Pharma.

## 2. Approved Indications
**VYLOY** is indicated in combination with fluoropyrimidine- and platinum-containing chemotherapy for the first-line treatment of adult patients with locally advanced unresectable or metastatic human epidermal growth factor receptor 2 (HER2)-negative gastric or gastro-oesophageal junction (GOJ [Australian]; GEJ [US]) adenocarcinoma.

**Patient Selection Requirement:** Tumours must be Claudin (CLDN) 18.2 positive, defined as ≥75% of tumour cells demonstrating moderate to strong membranous CLDN18 staining, as determined by a validated [Australian] or FDA-approved [US] test.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults)
The dosage is based on body surface area (BSA).
- **Loading Dose:** 800 mg/m² intravenously on Cycle 1, Day 1.
- **Maintenance Doses (Two approved schedules):**
    - 600 mg/m² intravenously every 3 weeks, starting 3 weeks after the loading dose; **OR**
    - 400 mg/m² intravenously every 2 weeks, starting 2 weeks after the loading dose.
- **Duration:** Continue treatment until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity.

### Administration Instructions
- **Route:** Intravenous infusion only. **Do not** administer as an IV push or bolus.
- **Sequence:** If administered on the same day as chemotherapy, **VYLOY** must be administered first.
- **Infusion Rates:** To mitigate adverse reactions, start each infusion at a slower rate for the first 30–60 minutes, then increase as tolerated.
    - **800 mg/m² dose:** Start at 75–100 mg/m²/hr; increase to 150–300 mg/m²/hr [Australian] or 200–265 mg/m²/hr [US].
    - **600 mg/m² dose:** Start at 75 mg/m²/hr; increase to 150–300 mg/m²/hr [Australian] or 150–265 mg/m²/hr [US].
    - **400 mg/m² dose:** Start at 50 mg/m²/hr; increase to 100–200 mg/m²/hr.

### Premedication and Symptom Management
- **Antiemetics:** Prior to each infusion, premedicate with a combination of antiemetics (e.g., NK-1 receptor blockers and/or 5-HT3 receptor blockers).
- **Hypersensitivity:** [US] recommends premedication with antihistamines for subsequent infusions if a hypersensitivity reaction has occurred.
- **Prior Symptoms:** If a patient is experiencing nausea/vomiting before administration, symptoms should resolve to Grade ≤1 before the infusion.

### Dose Modifications
No dose reductions are recommended. Adverse reactions are managed by infusion rate reduction, interruption, or permanent discontinuation.
- **Grade 3–4 Hypersensitivity/IRR or Anaphylaxis:** Permanently discontinue.
- **Grade 2 Hypersensitivity/IRR:** Interrupt infusion. Upon resolution to Grade ≤1, resume at a reduced rate.
- **Grade 4 Vomiting:** Permanently discontinue.
- **Grade 2–3 Nausea/Vomiting:** Interrupt infusion. Upon resolution to Grade ≤1, resume at a reduced rate.

## 4. Contraindications
- **US:** None listed.
- **Australian:** Contraindicated in patients with known hypersensitivity to zolbetuximab or any of the excipients (Arginine, Phosphoric Acid, Sucrose, Polysorbate 80).

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, have occurred. Monitor patients during the infusion and for at least 2 hours post-completion.
- **Infusion-Related Reactions (IRRs):** Common and potentially severe. Signs include nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain, salivary hypersecretion, pyrexia, chills, and hypertension.
- **Severe Nausea and Vomiting:** **VYLOY** is highly emetogenic. Severe vomiting may lead to GI haemorrhage [Australian]. Proactive management with antiemetics and fluid replacement is essential.
- **Posterior Reversible Encephalopathy Syndrome (PRES):** [Australian] A rare neurological disorder (seizures, headache, confusion, visual disturbances) has been noted. Confirm via MRI and discontinue treatment if suspected.
- **Monitoring:** Closely monitor for gastrointestinal toxicities, especially during the first treatment cycle when incidence is highest.

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Very Common (>10%)
- **Gastrointestinal:** Nausea (up to 82%), vomiting (up to 67%), abdominal pain, constipation, diarrhoea, salivary hypersecretion.
- **General:** Fatigue, asthenia, pyrexia, peripheral oedema.
- **Metabolic/Nutritional:** Decreased appetite, decreased weight, hypoalbuminaemia.
- **Neurological:** Peripheral sensory neuropathy.
- **Laboratory Abnormalities:** Neutropenia, anaemia, leucopenia, lymphopenia, increased AST/ALT, increased creatinine, decreased electrolytes (sodium, potassium, magnesium, phosphate), increased/decreased glucose.

### Serious Adverse Effects
- **Common (2–10%):** Vomiting (6.8%), nausea (4.9%), intestinal obstruction, pneumonia, sepsis, pulmonary embolism.
- **Fatalities:** Occurred in 5–8% of patients in clinical trials, including causes such as sepsis, pneumonia, and upper GI haemorrhage.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Cytochrome P450/Transporters:** Zolbetuximab is not a cytokine modulator; no known effects on CYP450 enzymes or drug transporters.
- **Chemotherapy Compatibility:** No clinically meaningful changes in exposure were observed when co-administered with mFOLFOX6. This finding is expected to apply to CAPOX as well. No dose adjustments are required for these combinations.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Australian Category B1.**
    - **US:** No data in pregnant women. Animal studies show zolbetuximab crosses the placental barrier but did not show malformations at 1.9x to 35x the human dose. Use only if the benefit outweighs the potential risk.
- **Breastfeeding:** Due to the potential for serious adverse effects in the child, breastfeeding is not recommended during treatment and for **8 months** after the final dose [US].
- **Fertility:** No human data available.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy have not been established.
- **Geriatric Use:** No dose adjustment required for patients ≥65 years. Data for patients ≥75 years are limited.
- **Renal Impairment:** No adjustment required for mild (CrCL 60–89 mL/min) or moderate (CrCL 30–59 mL/min) impairment. Data for severe impairment (CrCL <30 mL/min) are very limited [Australian] or unknown [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No adjustment required for mild hepatic impairment. Data for moderate-to-severe impairment are very limited [Australian] or unknown [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Zolbetuximab binds selectively to CLDN18.2, a tight junction molecule. It induces cell death via antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity (ADCC) and complement-dependent cytotoxicity (CDC).
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Steady-state volume of distribution is approximately 14–16.4 L.
    - **Metabolism:** Expected to be catabolised into small peptides and amino acids.
    - **Elimination:** Estimated clearance is 0.013–0.015 L/h; elimination half-life is approximately 41–43.6 days.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** No clinically meaningful effect on QTc prolongation at recommended doses.

## 11. Clinical Trials
Efficacy was established in two Phase 3 trials (**SPOTLIGHT** and **GLOW**) involving 1,072 patients with CLDN18.2-positive, HER2-negative advanced gastric/GOJ adenocarcinoma.
- **SPOTLIGHT:** **VYLOY** + mFOLFOX6 vs. Placebo + mFOLFOX6. Median Progression-Free Survival (PFS) was 10.6 vs 8.7 months; Median Overall Survival (OS) was 18.2 vs 15.6 months.
- **GLOW:** **VYLOY** + CAPOX vs. Placebo + CAPOX. Median PFS was 8.2 vs 6.8 months; Median OS was 14.4 vs 12.2 months.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Unopened Vials:** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C. Do not freeze. Keep in the original carton to protect from light.
- **Reconstituted Solution:** [US: Use within 5 hours at room temperature; Australian: Use within 6 hours].
- **Diluted Infusion Bag:**
    - **Refrigerated (2°C to 8°C):** [US: up to 16 hours; Australian: up to 24 hours], including infusion time.
    - **Room Temperature (15°C to 30°C):** [US: up to 6 hours; Australian: up to 12 hours], including infusion time.
- **Note:** The product contains no preservatives. If storage times are exceeded, the solution must be discarded.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Australian:** Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine). Subject to additional monitoring (Black Triangle Scheme).
- **US:** Rx Only.
