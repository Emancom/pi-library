# Editorial Product Information: Zynlonta (loncastuximab tesirine)

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis harmonizes Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdictions:
    - **Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration)**
    - **US (Food and Drug Administration)**
- **Purpose:** This document is a consolidated clinical reference intended for healthcare professionals. It provides a unified overview of the drug's regulatory and clinical profile across multiple major regions.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official, legally binding Product Information or Prescribing Information required for practice within a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Loncastuximab tesirine (referred to as **loncastuximab tesirine-lpyl** in the [US]).
- **Chemical Class:** CD19-directed antibody-drug conjugate (ADC) consisting of a humanized IgG1 kappa monoclonal antibody conjugated to a pyrrolobenzodiazepine (PBD) dimer cytotoxic alkylating agent (SG3199).
- **Brand Name:** ZYNLONTA
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Lyophilized powder for concentrate for solution for infusion. Each single-dose vial contains 10 mg of loncastuximab tesirine. After reconstitution (2.2 mL), the final concentration is 5 mg/mL.
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** 
    - Swedish Orphan Biovitrum Pty Ltd [Australian].
    - ADC Therapeutics America, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Relapsed or Refractory Diffuse Large B-cell Lymphoma (DLBCL):** For the treatment of adult patients with relapsed or refractory DLBCL after two or more lines of systemic therapy.
    - This includes DLBCL not otherwise specified (NOS), DLBCL arising from low-grade lymphoma, and high-grade B-cell lymphoma.
    - **Regulatory Status:** This indication is approved under provisional approval [Australian] or accelerated approval [US] based on overall response rate. Continued approval may be contingent upon verification of clinical benefit in confirmatory trials.
    - **Limitation of Use:** Not indicated for patients with primary central nervous system lymphoma [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
The recommended dosing schedule is administered intravenously every 3 weeks (21-day cycles):
- **Cycles 1 and 2:** 0.15 mg/kg.
- **Subsequent Cycles:** 0.075 mg/kg.

### Adjusted Body Weight (ABW)
For patients with a body mass index (BMI) $\ge$ 35 kg/m², the dose must be calculated based on an adjusted body weight using the following formula:
- $ABW (kg) = 35 kg/m^2 \times (height in meters)^2$

### Administration Instructions
- **Infusion Time:** Administer over 30 minutes.
- **Premedication:** Unless contraindicated, administer dexamethasone 4 mg orally or intravenously twice daily for 3 days, beginning the day before ZYNLONTA administration. If not started the day before, dexamethasone should begin at least 2 hours prior to ZYNLONTA.
- **Preparation:** Reconstitute with 2.2 mL Sterile Water for Injection. Dilute the calculated volume into a 50 mL infusion bag of 5% glucose (Dextrose) [Australian/US]. Do not shake.
- **Filters:** Use a dedicated infusion line equipped with a sterile, non-pyrogenic, low-protein binding in-line or add-on filter (0.2 or 0.22 micron pore size).

### Dose Modifications for Toxicity
- **Neutropenia (ANC < 1 x 10⁹/L):** Withhold until ANC returns to $\ge$ 1 x 10⁹/L.
- **Thrombocytopenia (Platelets < 50,000/mcL):** Withhold until platelets return to $\ge$ 50,000/mcL.
- **Oedema/Effusion (Grade 2 or higher):** Withhold until toxicity resolves to Grade 1 or less. Discontinue if Grade 2 pericardial effusion recurs [US].
- **Hepatotoxicity (Grade 3 or higher LFTs):** Withhold until LFTs return to Grade 1 or less. Discontinue if DILI is confirmed [US].
- **Dose Reduction:** If dosing is delayed by more than 3 weeks due to toxicity, reduce subsequent doses by 50%. If toxicity reoccurs after two dose reductions, consider permanent discontinuation.

### Missed Doses
If a dose is missed, administer as soon as possible and adjust the schedule to maintain a 21-day interval between doses.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Australian:** Known hypersensitivity to loncastuximab tesirine or any excipients.
- **US:** None listed.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Effusion and Oedema:** Serious events including pleural effusion, pericardial effusion, ascites, and peripheral oedema have occurred. **Capillary Leak Syndrome (CLS)** should be considered in patients with worsening oedema, weight gain, hypotension, and hypoalbuminemia. Monitor for new or worsening symptoms and use diagnostic imaging if needed.
- **Myelosuppression:** Severe neutropenia, thrombocytopenia, and anaemia can occur. Monitor complete blood counts (CBC) prior to each dose.
- **Infections:** Fatal and serious infections (sepsis, pneumonia) have been reported. Monitor for signs of infection and treat promptly.
- **Hepatotoxicity:** Grade 3 or higher elevations in ALT, AST, and bilirubin have occurred. Monitor LFTs at baseline and throughout treatment.
- **Cutaneous Reactions:** Serious reactions (rash, erythema) and photosensitivity have been reported. Advise patients to minimize exposure to direct sunlight and use sun-protective clothing/sunscreen.
- **Embryo-Fetal Toxicity:** May cause fetal harm due to the genotoxic PBD component. Advise of potential risks and use of effective contraception.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Human Studies:** No formal drug interaction studies have been conducted in humans.
- **In Vitro Data:** 
    - SG3199 does not inhibit major CYP450 enzymes (CYP1A2, 2A6, 2B6, 2C8, 2C9, 2C19, 2D6, 2E1, or 3A4/5) at clinically relevant concentrations.
    - SG3199 is a substrate of P-gp but not BCRP, OATP1B1, OATP1B3, or OCT1.
    - SG3199 does not inhibit major transporters (P-gp, BCRP, OATP1B1, OATP1B3, OAT1, OAT3, OCT1, OCT2, MATE1, MATE2-K, or BSEP).

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:**
    - **Australian Category D:** Loncastuximab tesirine may cause embryofetal toxicity. It is not recommended during pregnancy unless benefit outweighs risk.
    - **US:** Based on its mechanism, the drug can cause fetal harm. There are no available data in pregnant women.
    - **Pregnancy Testing:** Recommended for females of reproductive potential prior to starting treatment.
- **Breastfeeding:** There is no data on the presence of the drug in human milk. Advise patients not to breastfeed during treatment and for **3 months** after the final dose.
- **Contraception:**
    - **Females:** Use effective contraception during treatment and for **10 months** after the last dose.
    - **Males:** Use effective contraception during treatment and for **7 months** after the last dose.
- **Fertility:** Animal studies showed testicular toxicity and reduced spermatogenesis, which were not reversible. Advise male patients to consider sperm preservation prior to treatment.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy have not been established in patients under 18 years of age.
- **Elderly Use ($\ge$ 65 years):** No dose adjustment required. No overall differences in safety or effectiveness were observed between elderly and younger patients.
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    - **Mild to Moderate (CrCl 30-89 mL/min):** No dose adjustment required. 
    - **Severe (CrCl 15-29 mL/min):** Not studied; no recommendation provided.
- **Hepatic Impairment:**
    - **Mild:** No dose adjustment required; monitor for adverse reactions.
    - **Moderate/Severe:** ZYNLONTA has not been studied in these populations [Australian]. Avoid use in patients with severe hepatic impairment [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Loncastuximab tesirine binds to CD19 on the B-cell surface. The ADC is internalized, and the PBD dimer (SG3199) is released via proteolytic cleavage. SG3199 binds to the DNA minor groove, forming cytotoxic interstrand crosslinks that induce cell death.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Volume of distribution is approximately 7.11 L [US] or 4.26 L (Cycle 1) to 6.42 L (Cycle 2) [Australian].
    - **Metabolism:** The antibody portion is catabolized into peptides; SG3199 is metabolized by CYP3A4/5 *in vitro*.
    - **Elimination:** Half-life is approximately 20.8 days at steady state [US]. Total clearance decreases over time.
    - **Steady State:** Reached at approximately 15 weeks (105 days).

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **LOTIS-2 Study:** A phase 2, open-label, single-arm trial in 145 patients with relapsed/refractory DLBCL.
    - **Primary Outcome (ORR):** 48.3% (95% CI: 39.9, 56.7).
    - **Complete Response (CR):** 24.1% – 24.8%.
    - **Median Duration of Response:** 10.3 months [US] to 13.4 months [Australian].
    - **Time to Response:** Median 1.3 months.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage:** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C. Do not freeze. Keep the vial in the outer carton to protect from light.
- **Reconstituted Solution:** Should be used immediately. If not, it can be stored for up to 4 hours (refrigerated at 2°C–8°C or at room temperature 20°C–25°C).
- **Diluted Solution:** Should be used immediately. If not, store refrigerated (2°C–8°C) for up to 24 hours or at room temperature (20°C–25°C) for up to 8 hours.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Australian:** Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine).
- **US:** Rx-only. Classified as a Hazardous Agent; requires special handling and disposal procedures.
