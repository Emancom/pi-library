# Editorial Product Information: Zilucoplan

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) document from the following jurisdiction: **Australian (TGA)**. 
- At the time of this publication, PI documents from other major jurisdictions (e.g., US, European, Canadian, Japanese) were not provided for comparison. Consequently, this document reflects the harmonised position of the available source.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. It is an editorial synthesis and must not be used as a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific PI when prescribing or administering this medication in a specific region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Zilucoplan tetrasodium.
- **Chemical Class:** Synthetic macrocyclic peptide (15 amino acids); complement inhibitor.
- **Brand Name(s):** **ZILBRYSQ** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):**
    - **Solution for injection (Pre-filled syringe):** 16.6 mg (0.416 mL), 23.0 mg (0.574 mL), and 32.4 mg (0.810 mL) [Australian].
- **Route of Administration:** Subcutaneous (SC) injection.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** UCB Pharma [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Generalized Myasthenia Gravis (gMG):** Indicated as an adjunct to standard therapy for adult patients who are anti-acetylcholine receptor (AChR) antibody positive [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** The recommended dose is approximately 0.3 mg/kg, administered once daily via subcutaneous injection [Australian].
- **Weight-Based Dosing:**
    - **43 kg to <56 kg:** 16.6 mg daily.
    - **56 kg to <77 kg:** 23.0 mg daily.
    - **77 kg to <150 kg:** 32.4 mg daily.
    - *Note:* Limited data exists for patients <43 kg or >150 kg; these cohorts should receive the lowest (16.6 mg) or highest (32.4 mg) dose, respectively [Australian].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Administer at approximately the same time each day.
    - Suitable sites include the abdomen, thighs, and the back of the upper arms. 
    - Rotate injection sites; avoid areas of tender, bruised, scarred, or erythematous skin [Australian].
- **Missed Dose:** If a dose is missed, administer as soon as possible on the same day. Resume the regular schedule the following day. Do not exceed one dose per day [Australian].

## 4. Contraindications
- Hypersensitivity to **zilucoplan** or any included excipients.
- Patients who have not been vaccinated against *Neisseria meningitidis* (unless receiving appropriate prophylactic antibiotics until 2 weeks post-vaccination).
- Patients with active, unresolved *Neisseria meningitidis* infection [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning: Serious Meningococcal Infections [Australian]:** 
    - Life-threatening and fatal meningococcal infections have occurred with complement inhibitors. 
    - Patients must be vaccinated at least 2 weeks prior to therapy. 
    - If urgent therapy is required before the 2-week post-vaccination window, prophylactic antibiotics must be used. 
    - Vaccination reduces but does not eliminate risk; clinicians must monitor for early signs of infection and provide patients with a "patient alert card."
- **Other Neisseria Infections:** Potential increased susceptibility to gonococcal infections; advise patients on prevention/treatment [Australian].
- **General Infection Risk:** Inhibition of the terminal complement pathway increases susceptibility to encapsulated bacteria.
- **Pancreatic Enzyme Elevations:** Markedly increased lipase and/or amylase (CTCAE grade 3/4) have been observed. While a direct link to pancreatitis was not confirmed, abdominal pain should be investigated via enzyme measurement [Australian].
- **Monitoring:** Clinical monitoring for signs of infection and pancreatic pathology is required throughout treatment.

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Very Common (>10%):** Injection site reactions (e.g., bruising, pain, haematoma), upper respiratory tract infections (e.g., nasopharyngitis, sinusitis) [Australian].
- **Common (1–10%):** Diarrhea, increased lipase, increased amylase, urinary tract infection, contusion, and morphoea (localised scleroderma) [Australian].
- **Uncommon (0.1–1%):** Increased blood eosinophils (usually peaking at 2 months and transient) [Australian].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Serious meningococcal infections and potential pancreatitis. Morphoea has been reported with long-term use (onset >1 year) [Australian].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** No clinical interaction studies have been conducted. In vitro data indicates **zilucoplan** is not a substrate, inhibitor, or inducer of major CYP450 enzymes (1A2, 2B6, 2C8, 2C9, 2C19, 2D6, 3A4) or major transporters (P-gp, BCRP, OATP1B1, OATP1B3). Clinically relevant interactions are not expected [Australian].
- **MRP3 Inhibition:** Some in vitro inhibition of MRP3 was observed, but the clinical relevance is unknown [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Australian Category D:** There are no data in pregnant women. Animal studies (monkeys) showed increased embryofetal death at all doses in the absence of maternal toxicity. **Zilucoplan** is not recommended during pregnancy or for women of childbearing potential not using contraception [Australian].
- **Breastfeeding:** No data available regarding the presence in human milk or effects on the infant. A risk-benefit assessment must be performed to decide whether to discontinue nursing or the drug [Australian].
- **Fertility:** Human data are unavailable. Monkey studies showed minimal testicular germ cell depletion of uncertain clinical relevance [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy in children under 18 years have not been established [Australian].
- **Elderly Use:** No dose adjustment is required based on age [Australian].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment is required for patients with renal impairment. Data are unavailable for patients requiring dialysis [Australian].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment is required for mild to moderate impairment. Data are unavailable for severe hepatic impairment [Australian].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** **Zilucoplan** is a complement component C5 inhibitor. It binds specifically to C5, preventing its cleavage by C5 convertase into C5a and C5b. Additionally, it sterically hinders the binding of C5b to C6, thereby blocking the assembly of the membrane attack complex (MAC) [Australian].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Rapid complement inhibition is observed within 1–3 hours of administration. Daily dosing maintains sustained inhibition (>95%) [Australian].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Tmax is 3 to 6 hours post-dose.
    - **Distribution:** Highly protein bound (>99%); volume of distribution ~3.51 L.
    - **Metabolism:** Degraded into small peptides and amino acids via catabolic pathways. RA103488 is a minor active metabolite formed via CYP4F2.
    - **Elimination:** Terminal half-life is approximately 172 hours (7–8 days) [Australian].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Study MG0010 (RAISE):** A 12-week, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial (n=174) in AChR+ gMG patients. 
- **Primary Outcome:** **Zilucoplan** demonstrated a statistically significant improvement in the Myasthenia Gravis-Activities of Daily Living (MG-ADL) total score compared to placebo (LS mean change -4.39 vs -2.30; p <0.001) [Australian].
- **Secondary Outcomes:** Significant improvements were also observed in the Quantitative Myasthenia Gravis (QMG) score, MG Composite score, and MG-QoL15r [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage:** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C. Do not freeze [Australian].
- **Room Temperature Excursion:** Pre-filled syringes may be stored in the original carton at room temperature (up to 30°C) for a single period of up to 3 months. Once stored at room temperature, they must not be returned to the refrigerator [Australian].
- **Handling:** Protect from light; syringes are for single use only.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule:** **Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine)** [Australian].
