## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Zinc Oxide (15.52%; 155.2 mg/mL)
- **Chemical Class:** Inorganic physical sunscreen filter
- **Brand Name(s):** BaseLife Pure Mineral Shield Sunscreen [US]
- **ATC Code:** D02AB01 (Zinc oxide)
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Cream/Topical emulsion containing 15.52% Zinc Oxide [US]
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Wintech America, Inc. [US]; Manufactured by Cit Co., Ltd. [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Prevention of Sunburn:** Indicated to help prevent sunburn [US].
- **Risk Reduction of Skin Cancer and Photoaging:** When used as directed in conjunction with other sun protection measures, this product decreases the risk of skin cancer and early skin aging caused by the sun [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Application:** Apply liberally to the skin 15 minutes before sun exposure [US].
- **Reapplication:** Must be reapplied at least every two hours [US].
- **Activity-Specific Use:** Use a water-resistant sunscreen if swimming or sweating [US].

### Sun Protection Measures
To maximize efficacy and reduce skin risks, users should:
- Limit time in the sun, particularly during peak hours (10:00 am to 2:00 pm) [US].
- Wear protective clothing, including long-sleeved shirts, pants, hats, and sunglasses [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Damaged Skin:** Do not use on damaged or broken skin [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to zinc oxide or any of the inactive ingredients listed in the formulation [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **General Warnings:** For external use only [US].
- **Ocular Exposure:** Keep out of eyes. If contact occurs, rinse thoroughly with water to remove [US].
- **Dermatological Reactions:** Stop use and consult a physician if a rash occurs [US].
- **Accidental Ingestion:** Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, professional medical assistance or a Poison Control Center should be contacted immediately [US].
