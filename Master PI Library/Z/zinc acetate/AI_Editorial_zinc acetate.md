# Editorial Product Information: Pramoxine Hydrochloride and Zinc Acetate

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) and labeling data provided from the following jurisdiction: **United States (Food and Drug Administration)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. 
- **Note:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific Product Information required for prescribing or dispensing in any specific region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** Pramoxine Hydrochloride (1% w/v) and Zinc Acetate (0.1% w/v).
- **Chemical Class:** Pramoxine is a morpholine-derivative local anesthetic; Zinc acetate is a metallic salt used as a skin protectant/astringent.
- **Brand Name(s):** Clear Anti-Itch Lotion (H-E-B) [US].
- **ATC Code:** D04AB07 (Pramoxine); D02AB (Zinc products).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Topical lotion containing Pramoxine HCl 10 mg/mL and Zinc Acetate 1 mg/mL [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Manufactured for H-E-B by Nice-Pak Products, LLC [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Minor Skin Irritations:** For the temporary relief of pain and pruritus associated with minor skin irritations and rashes [US].
- **Rhus Dermatitis:** Relief of symptoms caused by contact with poison ivy, poison oak, or poison sumac [US].
- **Insect Bites:** Relief of pain and itching associated with insect bites [US].
- **Astringent Use:** To dry the oozing and weeping of skin reactions caused by poison ivy, oak, or sumac [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adults and Children $\ge$ 2 years):** Apply to the affected area no more than 3 to 4 times daily [US].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Shake the container well before use.
    - Cleanse the affected area of the skin prior to application.
- **Missed Dose Instructions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to pramoxine, zinc salts, or any of the inactive ingredients (see Section 1 for full list) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **General Warnings:** For external use only [US].
- **Ocular Exposure:** Avoid contact with eyes [US].
- **Duration of Use:** Patients should be advised to discontinue use and consult a healthcare provider if the condition worsens or if symptoms persist for more than 7 days [US].
- **Recurrence:** If symptoms clear up and then recur within a few days, the patient should seek medical advice [US].
- **Ingestion:** Accidental ingestion requires immediate medical attention or contact with a Poison Control Center [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** 
    - **Pramoxine HCl:** Acts as a topical anesthetic by decreasing the permeability of the neuronal membrane to sodium ions, thereby inhibiting the depolarization and conduction of nerve impulses to provide temporary relief of pain and itching.
    - **Zinc Acetate:** Functions as a skin protectant and astringent; it facilitates the drying of weeping or oozing skin lesions through protein precipitation and vasoconstriction at the application site.
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified in available sources; however, systemic absorption of pramoxine is generally low following topical application to intact skin.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Provides rapid, temporary relief of localised sensory symptoms (pain, itch).
