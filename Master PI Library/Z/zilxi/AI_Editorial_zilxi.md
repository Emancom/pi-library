# Editorial Product Information: ZILXI (Minocycline) Topical Foam

## Editorial Note
*   This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following regulatory authority: **United States (FDA)**.
*   The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. 
*   **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis intended for information purposes and is not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific Product Information required for prescribing.

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredient:** Minocycline (as minocycline hydrochloride).
*   **Chemical Class:** Tetracycline-class antibacterial.
*   **Brand Name:** **ZILXI** [US].
*   **ATC Code:** D10AF52 (Note: Not explicitly stated in source, but based on standard classification).
*   **Dosage Form and Strength:** Topical foam, 1.5%. Each gram of foam contains 15 mg of minocycline (equivalent to 16 mg of minocycline hydrochloride) [US].
*   **Route of Administration:** Topical (skin use only).
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Journey Medical Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Inflammatory Lesions of Rosacea:** Indicated for the treatment of inflammatory lesions of rosacea in adult patients [US].
*   **Limitations of Use:** The efficacy and safety of this specific formulation have not been evaluated for the treatment of infections. To mitigate the development of drug-resistant bacteria, use should be limited to the approved indication [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
*   **General Dosing (Adult):** Apply a thin layer to all areas of the face once daily [US].
*   **Administration Instructions:** 
    *   Shake the container thoroughly before use.
    *   Dispense a small amount (e.g., cherry-sized) onto fingertips and rub gently into the skin.
    *   Apply at approximately the same time each day, at least one hour before bedtime.
    *   Patients should refrain from bathing, showering, or swimming for at least one hour following application.
    *   Wash hands immediately after application [US].
*   **Special Instructions:** The foam is for topical use only; it is not for oral, ophthalmic, or intravaginal use. The product may stain fabric [US].

## 4. Contraindications
*   **Hypersensitivity:** Contraindicated in individuals with a known hypersensitivity to any tetracycline-class drug or any component of the formulation [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
*   **Flammability:** The propellant is highly flammable. Patients must avoid fire, open flames, and smoking during and immediately after application [US].
*   **Systemic Tetracycline Risks:** Although systemic absorption is low, the following risks associated with oral tetracyclines should be considered:
    *   **Teratogenic Effects:** May cause fetal harm; use during the second and third trimesters can lead to permanent tooth discoloration and inhibition of bone growth [US].
    *   **Hepatotoxicity:** Serious liver injury and fulminant hepatic failure have been reported with oral use. Discontinue if liver injury is suspected [US].
    *   **Intracranial Hypertension:** Associated with tetracycline use, particularly in women of childbearing age who are overweight. Discontinue if visual disturbances or persistent headaches occur [US].
    *   **Autoimmune Syndromes:** Drug-induced lupus-like syndrome and vasculitis have been reported. Discontinue immediately if symptoms arise [US].
    *   **Clostridioides difficile-Associated Diarrhea (CDAD):** Reported with nearly all antibacterial agents; range from mild diarrhea to fatal colitis [US].
*   **Photosensitivity:** Exaggerated sunburn reactions can occur. Patients should minimise exposure to natural and artificial sunlight and use sun protection [US].
*   **Tissue Hyperpigmentation:** Tetracyclines may cause pigmentation in skin, scars, teeth, gums, and internal organs [US].
*   **Microbial Overgrowth:** Prolonged use may result in the overgrowth of non-susceptible organisms, including fungi [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
*   **Common Adverse Effects (≥1%):** 
    *   Diarrhea (1% vs 0% in vehicle) [US].
*   **Local Tolerability (Phase 3 Trials):**
    *   Erythema: 36.2% (Mild), 18.3% (Moderate).
    *   Telangiectasia: 61.0% (Mild), 18.8% (Moderate).
    *   Flushing/Blushing: 39.0% (Mild), 9.6% (Moderate).
    *   Hyperpigmentation: 22.5% (Mild), 2.8% (Moderate).
    *   Other local reactions include burning/stinging, dryness, itching, and skin peeling [US].
*   **Serious Adverse Effects:** Anaphylaxis, Stevens-Johnson syndrome, erythema multiforme, and DRESS syndrome have been associated with oral minocycline [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **Anticoagulants:** Tetracyclines can depress plasma prothrombin activity. Patients on anticoagulant therapy may require a downward adjustment of their dose [US].
*   **Penicillin:** Bacteriostatic drugs may interfere with the bactericidal action of penicillin; co-administration should be avoided [US].
*   **Drug/Laboratory Test Interactions:** False elevations of urinary catecholamine levels may occur due to interference with fluorescence testing [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy:** Data regarding topical ZILXI in pregnant women are insufficient to determine drug-associated risks. However, oral minocycline is known to cross the placenta and can cause skeletal malformations and permanent tooth discoloration [US].
*   **Breastfeeding:** Oral minocycline is present in human milk. Due to the potential for serious adverse reactions in the infant, breastfeeding is not recommended during ZILXI therapy [US].
*   **Fertility:** In animal studies, oral minocycline adversely affected spermatogenesis in rats but did not affect female reproductive performance [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established in paediatric patients. Use in children under 8 years is generally avoided due to the risk of tooth discoloration [US].
*   **Geriatric Use:** No overall differences in safety or effectiveness were observed in subjects aged 65 and older compared to younger subjects, though greater sensitivity in some individuals cannot be ruled out [US].
*   **Renal Impairment:** If significant renal impairment exists, even topical use warrants caution to avoid excessive systemic accumulation, as oral doses typically require adjustment in these patients [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
*   **Mechanism of Action:** The exact mechanism of action for the treatment of inflammatory rosacea lesions is unknown [US].
*   **Pharmacokinetics:**
    *   **Absorption:** Following once-daily topical application of 2g for 14 days, the mean $C_{max}$ was 1.3 ± 0.9 ng/mL. Steady-state is reached by Day 1.
    *   **Accumulation:** No evident systemic accumulation of minocycline was observed following repeated topical application [US].
*   **Pharmacodynamics:** The pharmacodynamics of ZILXI for rosacea are unknown [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   **Study Design:** Two 12-week, multicenter, randomised, double-blind, vehicle-controlled trials (N=1,522) in adults with moderate-to-severe rosacea (15-75 inflammatory lesions).
*   **Primary Outcomes:**
    *   **IGA Success:** Defined as an IGA score of 0 ("clear") or 1 ("almost clear") and ≥2-grade improvement. Trial 1: 52.1% (ZILXI) vs 43.0% (Vehicle). Trial 2: 49.1% (ZILXI) vs 39.0% (Vehicle).
    *   **Lesion Count:** Significant reduction in mean absolute lesion count was achieved compared to vehicle in both studies [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   **Temperature:** 
    *   **Pre-dispensing:** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F). 
    *   **Post-dispensing:** Store at room temperature below 25°C (77°F) for 90 days. Do not refrigerate after dispensing.
*   **Handling:** Contents are under pressure and flammable. Do not puncture, incinerate, or expose to heat above 49°C (120°F) [US].
