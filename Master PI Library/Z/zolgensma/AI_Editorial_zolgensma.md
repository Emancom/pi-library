# Editorial Product Information: Zolgensma

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents were utilized for this editorial synthesis: Australian (TGA) and US (FDA).
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonized editorial overview of **Zolgensma** for healthcare professionals, consolidating global clinical data and regulatory requirements.
- This document is an editorial synthesis intended for informational purposes and is not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific PI required for prescribing.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Onasemnogene abeparvovec [Australian]; Onasemnogene abeparvovec-xioi [US].
- **Chemical Class:** Recombinant adeno-associated virus serotype 9 (AAV9) vector-based gene therapy.
- **Brand Name:** **Zolgensma**
- **ATC Code:** M09AX09
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Injection for intravenous infusion (suspension). Nominal concentration is $2.0 \times 10^{13}$ vector genomes (vg) per mL. Supplied in single-use vials containing extractable volumes of no less than 5.5 mL or 8.3 mL.
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous infusion only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Novartis Pharmaceuticals Australia Pty Limited [Australian]; Novartis Innovative Technologies Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
**Zolgensma** is indicated for the treatment of paediatric patients with 5q spinal muscular atrophy (SMA) with bi-allelic mutations in the *survival motor neuron 1* (SMN1) gene.

**Jurisdictional Nuances:**
- **Age/Weight limits:** Indicated for patients less than 2 years of age [US]. Indicated for patients weighing 2.6 kg to 21.0 kg [Australian].
- **Genetic criteria:** Specifically indicated for patients with 3 or fewer copies of the SMN2 gene [Australian].
- **Limitations of Use:** Safety and efficacy have not been established in patients over 21 kg [Australian] or for repeat administration [US, Australian]. Use in advanced SMA (e.g., complete paralysis, permanent ventilator dependence) has not been evaluated [US, Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing:** A single-dose intravenous infusion of $1.1 \times 10^{14}$ vector genomes per kilogram (vg/kg) of body weight.
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Administered as a slow intravenous infusion over approximately 60 minutes via a venous catheter.
    - **Do not** administer as an intravenous push or bolus.
    - Must be prepared aseptically.
    - Infusion must be completed within 8 hours of drawing into the syringe [US; Australian: 6 hours].
- **Pre-treatment/Concomitant Corticosteroids:**
    - Starting one day prior to infusion, administer systemic corticosteroids equivalent to oral prednisolone at 1 mg/kg/day for a total of 30 days.
    - **Tapering:** At the end of 30 days, if clinical findings and liver function tests (LFTs) are unremarkable (ALT/AST < 2x ULN), taper the dose over the next 28 days.
    - [Australian] specific taper: 2 weeks at 0.5 mg/kg/day, followed by 2 weeks at 0.25 mg/kg/day.
    - If LFT abnormalities persist, continue 1 mg/kg/day until values are < 2x ULN, then taper over 28 days or longer.
- **Infection Management:** Postpone infusion in patients with active infections until the infection has resolved and the patient is clinically stable.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to the active substance or any of the excipients (trometamol, magnesium chloride hexahydrate, sodium chloride, poloxamer 188) [Australian]. 
- [US]: None listed.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning: Hepatotoxicity [US, Australian]**
    - **Acute serious liver injury, acute liver failure, and elevated aminotransferases** can occur. Fatalities have been reported.
    - Patients with pre-existing hepatic impairment may be at higher risk.
    - Assessment of liver function (clinical exam and LFTs) is mandatory before infusion and for at least 3 months post-infusion.
- **Systemic Immune Response:** Risk of serious immune response; patients must be clinically stable (absence of infection, proper hydration/nutrition) prior to dosing.
- **Thrombocytopenia:** Transient decreases in platelet counts, often within the first two weeks, have been observed.
- **Thrombotic Microangiopathy (TMA):** Post-marketing reports of TMA (characterised by thrombocytopenia, microangiopathic haemolytic anaemia, and acute kidney injury) usually within 2 weeks of infusion. Concurrent immune activation (infection, vaccination) may be a factor.
- **Elevated Troponin-I:** Increases in cardiac troponin-I (up to 0.2 micrograms/L) were seen in trials. Clinical relevance is unknown; consider cardiac evaluation as needed.
- **Tumorigenicity:** Theoretical risk due to AAV vector DNA integration. [US]: Post-marketing cases of tumors have been reported, though a causal relationship is not established.
- **Vaccinations:** Adjust schedules to accommodate steroid use. Live vaccines (e.g., MMR, varicella) are contraindicated for patients on immunosuppressive steroid doses ($\geq 2$ weeks of 2 mg/kg/day or 20 mg/day).

### Serious Adverse Effects
- Acute liver failure/injury (including fatal cases).
- Thrombotic Microangiopathy (TMA).
- Serious systemic immune responses.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Vaccines:** High-dose corticosteroids used for **Zolgensma** pre- and post-treatment may reduce the immune response to vaccines.
- **Other SMA Therapies:** Not studied in conjunction with other SMA treatments (e.g., nusinersen).
- **Antivirals:** No interactions expected with antiviral medicinal products.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - [Australian]: **Category B2.** No data in pregnant women. Animal studies in mice showed no fetal risk at clinical doses.
    - [US]: No available human data. Risk summary relies on animal data showing no evidence of embryo-fetal toxicity.
- **Breastfeeding:** No information on presence in human milk or effects on the infant.
- **Fertility:** No human data. Animal studies in mice showed no adverse effects on male or female fertility.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** 
    - [US]: Indicated for children < 2 years.
    - [Australian]: Indicated for children weighing 2.6 kg to 21 kg.
    - Use in premature neonates is not recommended before reaching full-term gestational age due to potential corticosteroid effects on neurodevelopment.
- **Elderly Use:** Not applicable.
- **Renal Impairment:** Safety and efficacy not established.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Patients with ALT, AST, or total bilirubin > 2x ULN (excluding neonatal jaundice) were not studied. Use requires careful risk/benefit consideration.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** AAV9 vector delivers a functional human SMN1 cDNA copy under the control of a CMV enhancer/chicken-$\beta$-actin-hybrid promoter. The transgene resides as episomal DNA in the nucleus, providing stable SMN protein expression.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Shedding:** Vector DNA is shed in saliva, urine, and stool. Clearance is primary via faeces and usually occurs within 30 days.
    - **Biodistribution:** Highest vector DNA levels found in the liver, followed by spleen, heart, and other tissues.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Transduction and SMN protein expression have been confirmed in human case studies.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **AVXS-101-CL-303 (STR1VE):** Phase 3, single-arm study in Type 1 SMA (N=22). Resulted in 90.9% event-free survival at 14 months compared to 25% in natural history cohorts. 13 of 22 achieved independent sitting for $\geq 30$ seconds at 18 months.
- **AVXS-101-CL-101:** Phase 1 study. All 12 patients in the therapeutic dose cohort were event-free at 24 months. Long-term follow-up (up to 5.7 years) showed maintained or improved milestones.
- **AVXS-101-CL-304 (SPRINT):** Ongoing study in pre-symptomatic newborns. Patients with 2 or 3 copies of SMN2 demonstrated achievement of age-appropriate motor milestones.
- **COAV101A12306:** Post-authorisation study in older/heavier patients (8.5 kg to 21 kg). Mean HFMSE score increase of 3.7 at 52 weeks [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vials:** Store and transport frozen ($\leq -60^\circ\text{C}$).
- **Thawing:** Thaw in refrigerator ($2^\circ\text{C}$ to $8^\circ\text{C}$) for $\approx 12-16$ hours or at room temperature ($20^\circ\text{C}$ to $25^\circ\text{C}$) for $\approx 4-6$ hours.
- **Post-thaw:** Stable for 14 days when stored at $2^\circ\text{C}$ to $8^\circ\text{C}$.
- **Handling:** **Do not shake.** Do not refreeze. Once drawn into a syringe, use within 6-8 hours.
- **Disposal:** Contains a genetically modified organism. Dispose of all materials in contact with the product as biohazardous waste.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Australia:** Prescription Only Medicine (Schedule 4). Subject to additional monitoring (Black Triangle scheme).
- **US:** Rx-only.
