# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial: United States (FDA).
- Purpose: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of levocarnitine injection for use by healthcare professionals.
- This is an editorial synthesis and should not be used as a substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); chemical class: carrier molecule/amino acid derivative.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection, USP [United States].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01 (not explicitly stated in source, but standard for levocarnitine).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Sterile injection solution: 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) single-dose vial [United States].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [United States].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Indicated for the acute and chronic treatment of patients where these errors result in secondary carnitine deficiency [United States-approved].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients undergoing dialysis [United States-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
- **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion.
- **Loading Dose:** In cases of severe metabolic crisis, a loading dose followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours may be utilized.
- **Dosing Frequency:** Administer every 3 or 4 hours; intervals should never be less than every 6 hours.
- **Maximum Dose:** Doses up to 300 mg/kg have been administered in clinical practice.

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Initial Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight).
- **Administration:** Slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
- **Dose Adjustment:** Adjustments should be guided by pre-dialysis (trough) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of treatment.

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected visually for discoloration or particulate matter before administration.
- **Compatibility:** Stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer's at concentrations ranging from 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL [United States].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known at the time of publication [United States].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions can occur within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, therapy must be discontinued immediately.
- **Seizure Risk:** Levocarnitine may increase the frequency or severity of seizures in patients with pre-existing seizure disorders. Seizures have also been reported in patients without a prior history of epilepsy.
- **Monitoring:** For patients with metabolic disorders, it is recommended to obtain plasma carnitine levels prior to therapy. Weekly and monthly monitoring should include blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma free carnitine concentrations (target range: 35 to 60 µmol/L).
- **Renal Impairment (Oral Formulations):** While this PI focuses on the injection, clinicians should note that chronic high doses of oral levocarnitine are not recommended in patients with severely compromised renal function due to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites (TMA and TMAO) [United States].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects vary by population. In ESRD patients, distinguishing drug effects from the underlying pathology can be difficult.

### Very Common (>10%)
- **General:** Injection site reaction (33–38%), pain (30–35%), headache (22%), fever (12%).
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (18–21%), hypotension (14–19% [United States: placebo rate was 19%]).
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea (16–35%), vomiting (15–21%), nausea (8–12%).
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (23%), cough (18%).

### Common (1%–10%)
- **General:** Abdominal pain, asthenia, chest pain, flu syndrome, infection.
- **Metabolic:** Hypercalcemia, hyperkalemia, hypervolemia, peripheral edema, weight changes.
- **Nervous:** Dizziness, paresthesia, depression, insomnia.
- **Special Senses:** Taste perversion (up to 9%), eye disorders.

### Postmarketing/Rare Experience
- **Neurologic:** Seizures.
- **Digestive:** Gastritis, transient nausea/vomiting, body odor.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after any dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin therapy [United States].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not explicitly assigned a letter, but reproductive studies in rats and rabbits (up to 3.8 times the human dose) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [United States].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Although studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine concentrations in milk after administration, the risks to the infant must be weighed against the benefits to the mother. Discontinuation of nursing or the drug should be considered [United States].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility was found in animal reproductive studies [United States].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Specific dosing for metabolic disorders (50 mg/kg) is applicable to paediatric patients; however, clinical monitoring is essential.
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in the available source; general caution regarding hepatic, renal, or cardiac function in the elderly is standard clinical practice.
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    - Levocarnitine is cleared by dialysis. 
    - In ESRD patients, dosing is specifically adjusted based on post-dialysis timing and trough levels (see Section 3).

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria for oxidation and subsequent energy production.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In patients with metabolic disorders, levocarnitine promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters, which are rapidly eliminated.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (TMAO) and γ-butyrobetaine.
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours (uncorrected for endogenous levels).
    - **Half-life:** Distribution half-life is 0.585 hours; terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [United States].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients, levocarnitine 20 mg/kg three times weekly after dialysis increased mean pre-dialysis trough concentrations from 21.3 µmol/L to 190 µmol/L by week 8.
- **Dose-Ranging Data:** Studies using 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg demonstrated a dose-dependent increase in carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks. However, the impact of this supplemental carnitine on specific clinical outcomes in the ESRD population has not been definitively determined [United States].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C.
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing. Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as the formulation contains no preservatives.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as Rx-only. Supplied as 1 g/5 mL single-dose vials in cartons of 10.
