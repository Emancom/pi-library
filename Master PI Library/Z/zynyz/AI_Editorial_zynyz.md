# Editorial Product Information: Zynyz (retifanlimab-dlwr)

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, incorporating regulatory data into a single clinically focused reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information (PI) or Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory approved labeling before prescribing.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Retifanlimab-dlwr (a humanized IgG4 kappa monoclonal antibody).
- **Chemical Class:** Programmed death receptor-1 (PD-1)–blocking antibody.
- **Brand Name:** **ZYNYZ** [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Injection (500 mg/20 mL; 25 mg/mL solution) in a single-dose vial for intravenous use [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous infusion.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Incyte Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
**ZYNYZ** is a PD-1–blocking antibody indicated for the following: [US-approved]

- **Squamous Cell Carcinoma of the Anal Canal (SCAC):**
    - In combination with carboplatin and paclitaxel for the first-line treatment of adult patients with inoperable locally recurrent or metastatic SCAC.
    - As a single agent for the treatment of adult patients with locally recurrent or metastatic SCAC whose disease has progressed on or who are intolerant to platinum-based chemotherapy.
- **Merkel Cell Carcinoma (MCC):**
    - For the treatment of adult patients with metastatic or recurrent locally advanced MCC.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults)
- **SCAC (Combination or Monotherapy):** 500 mg every 4 weeks OR 375 mg every 3 weeks [US].
- **MCC:** 500 mg every 4 weeks [US].
- **Duration of Treatment:**
    - **SCAC (First-line combination):** Until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or up to 12 months.
    - **SCAC (Single agent):** Until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or up to 24 months.
    - **MCC:** Until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or up to 24 months.

### Administration Instructions
- Administer as an intravenous infusion over 30 minutes.
- Do not administer as an intravenous push or bolus.
- **Preparation:** Dilute in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or 5% Dextrose Injection to a final concentration between 1.4 mg/mL and 10 mg/mL. Mix by gentle inversion; do not shake.
- **Filtration:** Use an intravenous line containing a sterile, non-pyrogenic, low-protein binding in-line or add-on filter (0.2 micron to 5 micron, or a 15 micron mesh).

### Dosage Modifications
No dose reductions are recommended. Treatment should be withheld or permanently discontinued based on the severity of immune-mediated adverse reactions (IMARs) or infusion-related reactions. In general, withhold for Grade 2 or 3 reactions and permanently discontinue for Grade 4 or recurrent Grade 3 reactions [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Immune-Mediated Adverse Reactions (IMARs):** May be severe or fatal and can occur in any organ or tissue. Common sites include the lungs (pneumonitis), colon (colitis), liver (hepatitis), endocrine system (endocrinopathies), and kidneys (nephritis). IMARs can occur during or after treatment discontinuation.
- **Monitoring:** Evaluate liver enzymes, creatinine, and thyroid function at baseline and periodically during treatment.
- **Management of IMARs:** For severe reactions, initiate systemic corticosteroids (1 to 2 mg/kg/day prednisone or equivalent). Taper steroids over at least 1 month once improvement to Grade 1 or less is achieved.
- **Infusion-Related Reactions:** Monitor for signs of hypersensitivity. Interrupt or slow the rate for Grade 1–2; permanently discontinue for Grade 3–4.
- **Complications of Allogeneic HSCT:** Fatal and serious complications (e.g., hyperacute GVHD) can occur in patients receiving HSCT before or after PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitor therapy.
- **Embryo-Fetal Toxicity:** Can cause fetal harm. Advise of potential risk and use of effective contraception.

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Frequency in SCAC (Combination with Carboplatin/Paclitaxel)
- **Very Common (≥20%):** Fatigue, peripheral neuropathy, nausea, alopecia, diarrhea, musculoskeletal pain, constipation, hemorrhage, rash, vomiting, decreased appetite, pruritus, abdominal pain.
- **Serious Adverse Reactions (47%):** Sepsis (3.2%), pulmonary embolism (3.2%), and diarrhea (2.6%) were most frequent.

### Frequency in SCAC (Single Agent)
- **Very Common (≥10%):** Fatigue, musculoskeletal pain, diarrhea, non-urinary tract infections, perineal pain, hemorrhage, urinary tract infection, rash, nausea, decreased appetite, constipation, abdominal pain, dyspnea, pyrexia, vomiting, cough, pruritus, hypothyroidism, headache, decreased weight.

### Frequency in MCC (Single Agent)
- **Very Common (≥10%):** Musculoskeletal pain, fatigue, pruritus, diarrhea, rash, pyrexia, nausea, constipation.

### Serious Immune-Mediated Reactions
Incidence rates noted in pooled safety data: Pneumonitis (3.1%), Colitis (2.7%–10%), Hepatitis (3.5%), Adrenal Insufficiency (0.9%–5.8%), Hypophysitis (0.7%), Nephritis (2.0%), and Skin Reactions (10%) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- No formal drug interaction studies have been reported in available sources [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: No specific category letter; risk summary provided]. Based on mechanism of action and animal models, **ZYNYZ** can cause fetal harm. Human IgG4 is known to cross the placenta.
- **Contraception:** Females of reproductive potential should use effective contraception during treatment and for **4 months** after the final dose [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** There are no data on the presence of the drug in human milk. Due to the potential for serious adverse reactions in the breastfed child, advise women not to breastfeed during treatment and for **4 months** after the final dose [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established [US].
- **Geriatric Use:** No overall differences in safety or effectiveness were observed between patients ≥65 years and younger patients. In MCC trials, 76% of patients were ≥65 years [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** No clinically meaningful effect on pharmacokinetics was observed in patients with an estimated GFR ≥ 26 mL/min/1.73 m² [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No clinically meaningful effect in patients with mild hepatic impairment. The drug has not been studied in patients with moderate or severe hepatic impairment [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Retifanlimab-dlwr binds to the PD-1 receptor on T cells, blocking its interaction with ligands PD-L1 and PD-L2. This removes the inhibition of the immune response and potentiates T-cell activity against tumor cells.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Steady-state volume of distribution is 6.0 L.
    - **Elimination:** Elimination half-life is approximately 19 days.
    - **Clearance:** Steady-state clearance is 0.23 L/day. Steady-state concentrations are reached by 3 months [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **POD1UM-303 (SCAC Combination):** A randomized, double-blind trial in chemotherapy-naïve SCAC. Zynyz + carboplatin/paclitaxel significantly improved Progression-Free Survival (PFS) (9.3 months vs. 7.4 months; HR 0.63, p=0.0006).
- **POD1UM-202 (SCAC Monotherapy):** An open-label, single-arm study in platinum-refractory SCAC. Objective Response Rate (ORR) was 14%, with 65% of responses lasting ≥6 months.
- **POD1UM-201 (MCC Monotherapy):** An open-label, single-arm study in treatment-naïve MCC. ORR was 55% (95% CI: 44, 64), with a median duration of response (DOR) ranging from 1.1 to 55.3+ months.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vials:** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) in the original carton to protect from light. Do not freeze or shake.
- **Diluted Solution:** 
    - Room temperature: Up to 8 hours (from preparation to end of infusion).
    - Refrigerated (2°C to 8°C): Up to 24 hours. If refrigerated, allow to reach room temperature before use; once removed from the refrigerator, must be administered within 4 hours.
