# Editorial Product Information: Sertraline (Zoloft)

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** Australian (TGA) and US (FDA).
- **Purpose:** This document is a harmonised editorial synthesis of the Product Information (PI) from the above jurisdictions, designed for use by healthcare professionals as a single, coherent clinical reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This editorial is not a substitute for the official PI of a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians must consult the relevant national regulatory authority's current approved labeling for prescribing and legal purposes.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Sertraline hydrochloride (SSRI).
- **Brand Names:** Zoloft.
- **ATC Code:** N06AB06.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:**
    - **Film-coated Tablets:** 25 mg [US], 50 mg, and 100 mg [Australian, US]. 
    - **Note:** Australian 50 mg tablets feature a functional score line.
    - **Oral Solution:** 20 mg/mL (clear, colorless, menthol scent; contains 12% alcohol) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Viatris (Australia); Viatris Specialty LLC (United States).

## 2. Approved Indications
Sertraline is indicated for the treatment of:
- **Major Depressive Disorder (MDD):** Adults. 
- **Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder (OCD):** Adults and pediatric patients aged 6–17 years.
- **Panic Disorder:** Adults.
- **Social Anxiety Disorder (SAD):** Adults.
- **Premenstrual Dysphoric Disorder (PMDD):** Adult women.
- **Posttraumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD):** [US-approved; not specified in Australian source].
- **Relapse Prevention:** MDD and SAD [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Adult Dosing
| Indication | Starting Dosage | Therapeutic Range |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **MDD** | 50 mg once daily | 50 mg – 200 mg |
| **OCD** | 50 mg once daily | 50 mg – 200 mg |
| **Panic Disorder, SAD, PTSD [US]** | 25 mg once daily* | 50 mg – 200 mg |
| **PMDD (Continuous Dosing)** | 50 mg once daily | 50 mg – 150 mg |
| **PMDD (Intermittent Luteal Phase)**| 50 mg daily** | 50 mg – 100 mg |

*\*Panic Disorder/SAD/PTSD: Increase to 50 mg after one week.*
*\**Intermittent PMDD: Start 14 days prior to expected menses through the first full day of menses.*

### Pediatric Dosing (OCD only)
- **Ages 6–12 years:** Start at 25 mg once daily; increase to 50 mg after one week.
- **Ages 13–17 years:** Start at 50 mg once daily.
- **Maximum:** 200 mg daily.

### Administration Instructions
- **General:** Administer once daily (morning or evening) with or without food.
- **Titration:** If the response is inadequate, increase dosage by 25–50 mg increments at intervals of no less than one week.
- **Oral Solution [US]:** Must be diluted immediately before use. Use the supplied calibrated dropper and mix with 4 ounces (1/2 cup) of water, ginger ale, lemon/lime soda, lemonade, or orange juice only. 
- **Discontinuation:** Taper dosage gradually to minimize withdrawal symptoms.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to sertraline or excipients.
- **Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors (MAOIs):** Concomitant use or use within 14 days of stopping an MAOI is contraindicated due to the risk of serotonin syndrome. At least 14 days must elapse after stopping sertraline before starting an MAOI.
- **Pimozide:** Concomitant use is contraindicated due to the risk of QTc prolongation and ventricular arrhythmias.
- **Disulfiram:** Contraindicated with Zoloft **oral solution** only, due to the alcohol content in the solution [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning (Suicidality):** [US] Antidepressants increase the risk of suicidal thoughts and behaviors in pediatric and young adult patients. Closely monitor all patients for clinical worsening and emergence of suicidality, particularly during the first few months of therapy and at dose changes.
- **Serotonin Syndrome:** Potentially life-threatening; risk increases with other serotonergic drugs (e.g., triptans, fentanyl, lithium, St John's Wort).
- **Abnormal Bleeding:** Increased risk of bleeding (e.g., GI bleeding, ecchymoses, postpartum hemorrhage) especially when combined with NSAIDs, aspirin, or anticoagulants.
- **Activation of Mania/Hypomania:** Screen for bipolar disorder before initiating treatment.
- **Seizures:** Use with caution in patients with seizure disorders; avoid in unstable epilepsy.
- **QTc Prolongation:** Use with caution in patients with risk factors for QTc prolongation or Torsade de Pointes.
- **Hyponatremia:** Often secondary to SIADH; elderly patients and those on diuretics are at higher risk.
- **Angle-Closure Glaucoma:** Mydriasis may trigger an angle-closure attack in patients with anatomically narrow angles.
- **Sexual Dysfunction:** May cause symptoms such as decreased libido and ejaculatory delay/failure. 

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions are typically dose-dependent and may resolve with continued therapy.
- **Very Common (>10%):** Nausea (26%), insomnia (20%), diarrhea/loose stools (20%), dry mouth (14%), fatigue (12%), dizziness (12%), somnolence (11%), tremor (9%).
- **Common (1–10%):** Agitation, anxiety, decreased libido, dyspepsia, constipation, vomiting, palpitations, visual impairment, hyperhidrosis, decreased appetite, ejaculation failure (8% in males).
- **Pediatric-Specific:** Fever, hyperkinesia, urinary incontinence, aggression, and epistaxis occurred at higher rates than in adults.
- **Serious Post-marketing Reports:** Stevens-Johnson Syndrome (SJS), toxic epidermal necrolysis (TEN), acute renal failure, pancreatitis, and serious liver injury.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacodynamic:** 
    - **MAOIs/Serotonergic drugs:** High risk of serotonin syndrome.
    - **Antiplatelets/Anticoagulants:** Increased bleeding risk.
- **Pharmacokinetic:**
    - **CYP2D6 Substrates:** Sertraline is a moderate inhibitor; may increase levels of drugs like desipramine, flecainide, and propafenone.
    - **Protein-Bound Drugs:** Sertraline is highly protein-bound; monitor for shifts in plasma concentrations of other bound drugs (e.g., warfarin).
    - **Phenytoin:** Monitor levels upon initiation or titration of sertraline.
    - **Cimetidine:** Increases sertraline AUC by ~50%.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Category C** [Australian].
    - **Clinical Considerations:** Use in the third trimester may lead to neonatal complications requiring prolonged hospitalization (respiratory distress, feeding difficulties) or Persistent Pulmonary Hypertension of the Newborn (PPHN). 
    - **US Note:** Oral solution contains 12% alcohol; not recommended during pregnancy.
- **Breastfeeding:** Sertraline is excreted in human milk in low amounts (~2% of maternal serum levels). The benefits of breastfeeding should be weighed against potential infant risk.
- **Fertility:** Animal data show a decrease in fertility in rats at doses exceeding human equivalents; clinical significance is unknown.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Established for OCD (6–17 years) only. Not recommended/established for MDD in children. Monitor growth and weight during long-term treatment.
- **Elderly Use:** No overall difference in safety compared to younger adults, but higher risk of hyponatremia. Dose selection should be conservative.
- **Hepatic Impairment:**
    - **Mild (Child-Pugh 5–6):** Reduce starting and maximum dosage by half [US]. Use with caution [Australian].
    - **Moderate to Severe (Child-Pugh 7–15):** Not recommended [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dosage adjustment required based on the degree of renal impairment.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Potent and selective inhibitor of CNS neuronal reuptake of serotonin (5-HT). It has minimal effects on norepinephrine and dopamine reuptake.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Peak plasma levels (Cmax) reached 4.5–8.4 hours post-dose. Steady state reached in one week. Food increases Cmax by ~25%.
    - **Distribution:** Highly protein-bound (98%). 
    - **Metabolism:** Extensive first-pass metabolism via N-demethylation to N-desmethylsertraline (substantially less active).
    - **Elimination:** Terminal half-life ~26 hours. Primarily excreted as metabolites in urine and feces.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **MDD:** Efficacy shown in 6–8 week trials and a 44-week maintenance trial showing significantly lower relapse rates (8%) vs placebo (39%) [US].
- **OCD:** Pediatric (12-week) and adult (up to 12-week) trials showed significant reduction in Y-BOCS scores. Long-term efficacy maintained up to 52 weeks.
- **Panic/SAD/PTSD:** Multiple placebo-controlled trials demonstrated significant improvement in symptom scales (e.g., LSAS for SAD, CAPS for PTSD).

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Australian:** Store below 30°C.
- **US:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C. Keep bottle tightly closed.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule:** 
    - **Schedule 4 (Prescription Only Medicine)** [Australian].
    - **Rx-only** [US].
- **Legal Classification:** Not a controlled substance.
