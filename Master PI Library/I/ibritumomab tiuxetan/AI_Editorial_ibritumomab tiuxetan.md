# Editorial Product Information: Ibritumomab tiuxetan

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial has been synthesized from the official Product Information provided by the following regulatory authority: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of **ibritumomab tiuxetan** for use by healthcare professionals.
- **Disclaimer:** This synthesis is an editorial resource and does not replace the official Product Information required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult the current local PI before administration.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Ibritumomab tiuxetan.
- **Chemical Class:** CD20-directed radiotherapeutic antibody (monoclonal antibody-radionuclide immunoconjugate).
- **Brand Name:** Zevalin [US].
- **ATC Code:** V10XX02.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Injection; 3.2 mg per 2 mL in a single-dose vial [US]. 
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous infusion.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Acrotech Biopharma Inc [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Relapsed or Refractory Non-Hodgkin’s Lymphoma (NHL):** Treatment of adult patients with relapsed or refractory, low-grade or follicular B-cell NHL [US].
- **Previously Untreated Follicular NHL:** Treatment of adult patients with previously untreated follicular NHL who achieve a partial or complete response to first-line chemotherapy [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
**Ibritumomab tiuxetan** is administered as part of the Zevalin therapeutic regimen, which includes **rituximab**.

### General Dosing Schedule [US]
The regimen is administered over 7 to 9 days:
- **Day 1:** Premedicate with acetaminophen (650 mg) and diphenhydramine (50 mg) orally. Administer **rituximab** at 250 mg/m² IV.
- **Day 7, 8, or 9:** 
    1. Premedicate with acetaminophen and diphenhydramine.
    2. Administer **rituximab** 250 mg/m² IV.
    3. Within 4 hours of the **rituximab** infusion, administer **Y-90 Zevalin** as a 10-minute IV infusion.

### Y-90 Zevalin Specific Dosing [US]
Dosage is based on actual body weight and baseline platelet count:
- **Platelets ≥ 150,000/mm³:** 0.4 mCi/kg (14.8 MBq/kg).
- **Platelets 100,000 to 149,000/mm³ (Relapsed/Refractory patients only):** 0.3 mCi/kg (11.1 MBq/kg).
- **Maximum Dose:** Do not exceed **32 mCi (1184 MBq)** regardless of body weight.
- **Threshold for Non-Treatment:** Do not treat patients with less than 100,000 platelets/mm³.

### Administration Instructions
- Only administer in facilities with immediate access to resuscitative measures.
- Use a 0.22-micron low-protein-binding in-line filter for the Y-90 infusion.
- Flush the line with at least 10 mL of normal saline following the infusion.
- Monitor closely for extravasation; if it occurs, terminate the infusion and restart in another limb.

## 4. Contraindications
- No contraindications are listed in the available source document [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warnings [US]
- **Serious Infusion Reactions:** Deaths have occurred within 24 hours of rituximab infusion. Fatalities were associated with hypoxia, pulmonary infiltrates, ARDS, MI, or cardiogenic shock.
- **Prolonged and Severe Cytopenias:** Most patients experience severe and prolonged cytopenias (thrombocytopenia and neutropenia).
- **Severe Cutaneous and Mucocutaneous Reactions:** Potentially fatal reactions, including Stevens-Johnson syndrome (SJS) and toxic epidermal necrolysis (TEN), have been reported.
- **Dosing Limitation:** The total dose of Y-90 Zevalin must not exceed 32 mCi (1184 MBq).

### General Warnings and Precautions
- **Bone Marrow Reserve:** Do not administer to patients with ≥ 25% lymphoma marrow involvement or impaired bone marrow reserve [US].
- **Secondary Malignancies:** There is a risk of developing Myelodysplastic Syndrome (MDS) and Acute Myelogenous Leukemia (AML). Median time to diagnosis was 1.9 years post-treatment [US].
- **Immunization:** Safety of live viral vaccines has not been studied; avoid live vaccines in patients who recently received this regimen [US].
- **Radionuclide Safety:** Standard radiation safety practices must be followed to minimize exposure to patients and medical personnel.

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Frequency Data [US]
| Frequency | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (> 10%)** | Thrombocytopenia (95%), neutropenia (77%), anemia (61%), fatigue (33%), nasopharyngitis (19%), nausea (18%), abdominal pain (17%), asthenia (15%), cough (11%), diarrhea (11%) |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Pyrexia, myalgia, anorexia, dizziness, hypertension, night sweats, petechiae, pruritus, rhinitis, sinusitis, UTI |

### Serious Adverse Effects
- **Hematologic Toxicity:** Grade 3-4 cytopenias are highly common (thrombocytopenia 63%, neutropenia 60%). The median time to ANC nadir is 61-62 days, and platelet nadir is 49-53 days [US].
- **Secondary Malignancies:** MDS/AML occurred in 5.2% of relapsed/refractory patients in clinical trials [US].
- **Infections:** Serious infections occurred in 3% of patients within 3 months, including sepsis and pneumonia [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Platelet/Coagulation Interfereing Drugs:** Patients receiving medications that interfere with platelet function or coagulation (e.g., anticoagulants, NSAIDs) should be monitored more frequently for thrombocytopenia [US].
- **Formal Studies:** No formal drug interaction studies have been conducted [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not specified in source, but stated as "may cause fetal harm"]. Immunoglobulins cross the placenta. Women of childbearing potential must use effective contraception during treatment and for **12 months** after the final dose [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Due to the excretion of human IgG in milk and the potential for serious adverse reactions, breastfeeding is not recommended during treatment and for **6 months** after the final dose [US].
- **Fertility:** There is a potential risk of toxic effects on male and female gonads; males with female partners of reproductive potential should use contraception for **12 months** after treatment [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** In clinical studies, 38% of patients were ≥ 65 years. No overall differences in safety or effectiveness were observed between older and younger subjects, though greater sensitivity in some older individuals cannot be ruled out [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources. Eligibility for trials required "acceptable" renal and hepatic function [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** **Ibritumomab tiuxetan** is a murine IgG1 kappa monoclonal antibody directed against the CD20 antigen. It is covalently linked to the chelator tiuxetan, which binds the radioisotope Yttrium-90 (Y-90). The beta emission from Y-90 induces cellular damage via free radical formation in target and neighboring cells [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Administration results in sustained depletion of circulating B cells. Median recovery of B cells to normal ranges occurs by 9 months post-treatment [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** The mean effective half-life for Y-90 activity in blood is 30 hours. A median of 7.2% of the injected activity is excreted in the urine over 7 days [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Relapsed/Refractory NHL (Study 106-04):** A randomized trial comparing Zevalin to rituximab alone showed a significantly higher Overall Response Rate (ORR) for Zevalin (83% vs 55%) [US].
- **First-line Consolidation (FIT Study):** Patients with follicular NHL who responded to first-line chemotherapy were randomized to Zevalin or observation. Median progression-free survival (PFS) was 38 months for Zevalin vs 18 months for the control group [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store the kit at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Do not freeze.
- **Post-labeling:** Radiolabeled Y-90 Zevalin should be stored at 2-8°C and administered within 8 hours of preparation [US].
