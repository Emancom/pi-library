# Editorial Product Information: Ibalizumab

## Editorial Note
- Contributing Jurisdictions: United States (FDA).
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals based on available regulatory Product Information. 
- This document is an editorial synthesis and serves as a clinical reference; it is not a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. 
- **Note:** At the time of this editorial, only the United States (FDA) PI was provided for synthesis.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Ibalizumab-uiyk; a CD4 domain 2-directed humanized monoclonal antibody (IgG4 isotype).
- **Brand Name:** **Trogarzo** [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Injection: 200 mg/1.33 mL (150 mg/mL) solution in a single-dose vial [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion or IV push.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Theratechnologies Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **HIV-1 Infection:** Indicated for the treatment of human immunodeficiency virus type 1 (HIV-1) in combination with other antiretroviral(s). This is specifically for heavily treatment-experienced adults with multidrug-resistant (MDR) HIV-1 infection who are failing their current antiretroviral regimen [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
The treatment regimen consists of a single loading dose followed by maintenance doses every 14 days. 
- **Loading Dose:** 2,000 mg (administered once).
- **Maintenance Dose:** 800 mg every 2 weeks.

### Administration Instructions
**Trogarzo** must be administered by a trained medical professional. 
- **IV Infusion (Diluted):**
    - **Preparation:** Dilute the required number of vials (10 for loading, 4 for maintenance) in 250 mL of 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection.
    - **Rate:** Administer the loading dose over at least 30 minutes; maintenance doses over at least 15 minutes.
    - **Post-infusion:** Flush with 30 mL of 0.9% Sodium Chloride.
- **IV Push (Undiluted):**
    - **Preparation:** Administer the undiluted solution. Vials should stand at room temperature for 5 minutes before use.
    - **Rate:** Administer the loading dose over at least 90 seconds; maintenance doses over at least 30 seconds.
    - **Post-administration:** Flush with 2 to 5 mL of 0.9% Sodium Chloride.

### Observation and Monitoring
Patients must be observed for 1 hour following the completion of the initial loading dose. If no infusion-associated adverse reactions occur, the observation period for subsequent maintenance doses may be reduced to 15 minutes.

### Missed Doses
If a maintenance dose (800 mg) is missed by **3 days or longer** beyond the scheduled dosing day, a loading dose (2,000 mg) must be re-administered as soon as possible. Maintenance dosing (800 mg every 14 days) should then resume thereafter [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known prior hypersensitivity to **Trogarzo** or any components of the formulation [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Anaphylaxis and infusion-related reactions have been reported. If a clinically significant hypersensitivity reaction occurs, discontinue administration immediately and initiate appropriate therapy.
- **Immune Reconstitution Inflammatory Syndrome (IRIS):** As with other combination antiretroviral therapies, IRIS has been reported. This may involve inflammatory responses to indolent or residual opportunistic infections (e.g., progressive multifocal leukoencephalopathy).
- **Embryo-Fetal Toxicity:** Based on animal data, in utero exposure may cause reversible immunosuppression in infants (CD4+ T cell and B cell lymphocytopenia). Monitoring of exposed infants is required [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Most adverse reactions reported in clinical trials were mild to moderate in severity.
- **Common (5–10%):** Diarrhea (8%), dizziness (8%), nausea (5%), rash (5%).
- **Serious Adverse Effects:**
    - Severe rash.
    - Immune Reconstitution Inflammatory Syndrome (IRIS).
- **Laboratory Abnormalities (Grade 3 or 4):** Most frequent includes increased creatinine (10%), followed by increased bilirubin (5%), decreased leukocytes (5%), and decreased neutrophils (5%) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** No formal drug interaction studies have been conducted. Based on the mechanism of action and target-mediated drug disposition, drug-drug interactions are not expected [US].
- **Clinical Management:** No dose modifications are required when co-administered with other antiretrovirals.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Not categorized by letter]. There are no available data on use in pregnant women. Animal studies (monkeys) showed reversible lymphocyte count decreases in infants. 
    - **Clinical Consideration:** If used during pregnancy, immune phenotyping (CD4+ and B cell counts) is recommended for the infant. The safety of live vaccines in exposed infants is unknown.
- **Breastfeeding:** Mothers infected with HIV-1 should be instructed not to breastfeed to avoid the risk of postnatal HIV transmission. It is unknown if **Trogarzo** is excreted in human milk [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established in patients aged 0 to 18 years [US].
- **Elderly Use:** No specific studies have been conducted in geriatric populations [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** No formal studies were conducted. Renal impairment is not expected to impact pharmacokinetics as monoclonal antibodies are not eliminated via renal pathways [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** A CD4-directed post-attachment HIV-1 inhibitor. It binds to domain 2 of the CD4 receptor, interfering with the post-attachment steps required for HIV-1 entry into host cells. It does not interfere with MHC II-mediated immune functions or gp120 attachment to CD4.
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Exhibits non-linear pharmacokinetics. Following a 2,000 mg loading dose, steady-state levels are typically reached after the first 800 mg maintenance dose. The volume of distribution is approximately 4.8 L.
- **Microbiology:** Active against R5-tropic, X4-tropic, and dual-tropic HIV-1. There is no evidence of cross-resistance with other classes of antiretrovirals (NRTIs, NNRTIs, PIs, INSTIs, or fusion inhibitors) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Trial TMB-301:** A single-arm, multicenter trial in 40 heavily treatment-experienced adults with MDR HIV-1. 
    - **Primary Outcome:** 83% of subjects achieved a ≥ 0.5 log10 decrease in viral load after the 7-day functional monotherapy period. 
    - **Week 25 Outcomes:** 43% of subjects achieved HIV RNA < 50 copies/mL; 50% achieved < 200 copies/mL. Mean CD4+ T-cell count increased by 44 cells/mm³ [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vials:** Store under refrigeration at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F). Do not freeze. Protect from light.
- **Diluted Solution:** Once diluted, the solution should be administered immediately. If necessary, it can be stored at room temperature (20°C to 25°C) for up to 4 hours or refrigerated (2°C to 8°C) for up to 24 hours.
- **Undiluted Solution (IV Push):** Should be administered immediately after being drawn into a syringe [US].
