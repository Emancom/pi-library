## Editorial Note

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Ichthammol USP (100 mg in 1 g) [US].
- **Chemical Class:** Ammonium bituminosulfonate (Shale oil derivative).
- **Brand Name(s):** Walgreens Ichthammol Ointment [US].
- **ATC Code:** D05AA (Coal tars - closely related/associated class).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Topical Ointment, 10%.
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Walgreens Co. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- Indicated as a "drawing salve" for the removal of splinters, silvers, or similar foreign items from the skin [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Administration:**
    - Cleanse the affected area prior to application.
    - Apply the ointment to a gauze bandage once or twice daily.
    - Apply the bandage to the affected area.
    - Repeat as necessary.
- **Specific Instructions:** 
    - Do not rub or massage the affected area [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Ophthalmic Use:** Do not use in or near the eyes [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **External Use Only:** For topical application only.
- **Medical Consultation Required:** Patients should consult a physician before use if they have:
    - Deep puncture wounds [US].
    - Serious burns [US].
- **Treatment Cessation:** Use should be discontinued and a healthcare provider consulted if redness, irritation, swelling, or pain persists [US].
- **General Safety:** Keep out of reach of children.

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Frequency Not Specified:**
    - Localised skin irritation.
    - Redness.
    - Swelling or persistent pain at the application site [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Traditionally used as a "drawing salve." It is believed to soften the skin and exert a mild local irritant effect to facilitate the extraction of foreign bodies (splinters/silvers) [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Homeopathic properties listed as "helping to soothe skin" [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage Temperature:** Store at room temperature [US].
- **Special Handling:** Do not rub or massage into the skin upon application.
