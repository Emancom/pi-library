# Editorial Product Information: Ixabepilone

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical and regulatory data into a single, structured reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and should not be substituted for the official, legally approved PI in any specific jurisdiction when prescribing or administering this medication.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Ixabepilone (a semi-synthetic analog of epothilone B).
- **Chemical Class:** Microtubule inhibitor (epothilone).
- **Brand Name:** **IXEMPRA** [US].
- **ATC Code:** L01DC04 (derived from class).
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - **IXEMPRA** for injection: 15 mg single-dose vial (supplied with 8 mL diluent) [US].
    - **IXEMPRA** for injection: 45 mg single-dose vial (supplied with 23.5 mL diluent) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous infusion only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** R-Pharm US Operating, LLC [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Combination Therapy:** Indicated in combination with capecitabine for the treatment of metastatic or locally advanced breast cancer in patients resistant to treatment with an anthracycline and a taxane, or whose cancer is taxane-resistant and further anthracycline therapy is contraindicated [US].
- **Monotherapy:** Indicated as a single agent for the treatment of metastatic or locally advanced breast cancer in patients whose tumours are resistant or refractory to anthracyclines, taxanes, and capecitabine [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** The recommended dose is **40 mg/m²** administered intravenously over 3 hours every 3 weeks [US].
- **Dose Calculation:** For patients with a Body Surface Area (BSA) exceeding 2.2 m², the dose should be calculated using a maximum BSA of 2.2 m² [US].
- **Premedication:** All patients must be premedicated approximately 1 hour prior to infusion to prevent hypersensitivity reactions:
    - H1 antagonist (e.g., diphenhydramine 50 mg orally or equivalent).
    - H2 antagonist (e.g., ranitidine 150–300 mg orally or equivalent).
    - Patients with prior hypersensitivity require additional corticosteroid premedication (e.g., dexamethasone 20 mg IV 30 minutes before or orally 60 minutes before infusion) [US].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Use only the supplied diluent for reconstitution (results in 2 mg/mL).
    - Further dilute in 0.9% Sodium Chloride (pH adjusted to 6.0–9.0 with sodium bicarbonate), Lactated Ringer’s, or PLASMA-LYTE A [US].
    - Final concentration must be between **0.2 mg/mL and 0.6 mg/mL**.
    - Administer through an in-line filter (0.2 to 1.2 microns) using **DEHP-free** infusion containers and sets [US].
- **Re-treatment Criteria:** Neutrophils must be ≥1500 cells/mm³, platelets ≥100,000 cells/mm³, and non-haematologic toxicities resolved to Grade 1 or better before starting a new cycle [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- Baseline neutrophil count <1500 cells/mm³ or platelet count <100,000 cells/mm³ [US].
- History of severe hypersensitivity to drugs formulated with **Cremophor® EL** (polyoxyethylated castor oil) or its derivatives [US].
- **Combination Therapy Specific:** Contraindicated in patients with AST or ALT >2.5 x ULN or bilirubin >1 x ULN due to increased risk of toxicity and neutropenia-related death [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning: Toxicity in Patients with Hepatic Impairment:** [US] **IXEMPRA** in combination with capecitabine is contraindicated in patients with significant hepatic impairment (AST/ALT >2.5 x ULN or bilirubin >1 x ULN) due to an increased risk of neutropenia-related death.
- **Peripheral Neuropathy:** Primarily sensory; monitor closely. Withhold, reduce, or discontinue based on severity. Patients with diabetes or pre-existing neuropathy are at higher risk [US].
- **Myelosuppression:** Severe neutropenia is common. Frequent peripheral blood cell counts are required.
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Severe reactions, including anaphylaxis, can occur. Premedication is mandatory.
- **Cardiac Adverse Reactions:** Myocardial ischaemia and ventricular dysfunction have been reported (1.9% in combo therapy). Use with caution in patients with history of cardiac disease [US].
- **Alcohol Content:** The diluent contains dehydrated alcohol (ethanol), which may affect the central nervous system. Consider this for patients who should avoid alcohol or when driving/operating machinery immediately after infusion [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Frequency of Adverse Reactions
| Adverse Reaction | Combination with Capecitabine [US] | Monotherapy [US] |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| Peripheral Sensory Neuropathy (All Grades) | 65% | 62% |
| Fatigue/Asthenia | 60% | 56% |
| Nausea | 53% | 42% |
| Alopecia | 31% | 48% |
| Palmar-Plantar Erythrodysesthesia | 64% | 8% |
| Myalgia/Arthralgia | 39% | 49% |
| Stomatitis/Mucositis | 31% | 29% |

### Serious Adverse Effects
- **Haematologic:** Grade 4 neutropenia (>20%), febrile neutropenia, and anaemia.
- **Infections:** Sepsis, pneumonia, and neutropenic infection.
- **Vascular:** Thrombosis, embolism, and haemorrhage [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:**
    - **Strong CYP3A4 Inhibitors:** (e.g., ketoconazole) increase ixabepilone plasma concentrations. Avoid use; if unavoidable, reduce dose to 20 mg/m² [US].
    - **Strong CYP3A4 Inducers:** (e.g., rifampin, phenytoin) decrease concentrations. Avoid use; if unavoidable, gradually increase dose from 40 mg/m² to 60 mg/m² [US].
- **Capecitabine Interaction:** No clinically meaningful effect on the pharmacokinetics of capecitabine or vice versa when used in combination [US].
- **Grapefruit Juice:** Patients should avoid grapefruit juice as it may increase ixabepilone levels [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: No assigned letter category, but warnings state it can cause fetal harm]. Based on animal data, ixabepilone is embryo-fetal toxic and teratogenic.
- **Contraception:**
    - Females of reproductive potential: Use effective contraception during treatment and for **7 months** after the final dose [US].
    - Males with female partners: Use effective contraception during treatment and for **4 months** after the final dose [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** There are no data on presence in human milk. Advise patients not to breastfeed during treatment and for **2 weeks** after the final dose [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies suggest potential impairment of both male and female fertility [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness not established. Trials in patients aged 2–18 did not show efficacy [US].
- **Elderly Use (≥65 years):** Patients ≥65 years experienced a higher incidence of Grade 3/4 adverse reactions (stomatitis, diarrhoea, and neuropathy) compared to younger patients [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Mild to moderate renal impairment (CrCL >30 mL/min) does not meaningfully affect ixabepilone pharmacokinetics. No data for CrCL <30 mL/min [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** 
    - **Single Agent:** Reduce dose to 32 mg/m² for mild impairment (AST/ALT ≤10x ULN; Bilirubin ≤1.5x ULN). Use 20–30 mg/m² for moderate impairment. Avoid use if AST/ALT >10x ULN or Bilirubin >3x ULN [US].
    - **Combination Therapy:** Contraindicated if AST/ALT >2.5x ULN or Bilirubin >1x ULN [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Binds directly to $\beta$-tubulin subunits on microtubules, suppressing dynamic instability. This leads to mitotic arrest and apoptosis. It has low susceptibility to common resistance mechanisms like P-glycoprotein efflux [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Large volume of distribution (>1000 L); 67–77% protein bound.
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily via CYP3A4.
    - **Elimination:** Terminal half-life of ~52 hours. 65% excreted in feces, 21% in urine (mostly as metabolites) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Study 046 (Combination):** A randomized trial (n=752) compared ixabepilone + capecitabine to capecitabine alone. The combination significantly improved Median Progression-Free Survival (PFS) (5.7 months vs 4.1 months) and Objective Tumor Response Rate (34.7% vs 14.3%) [US].
- **Study 081 (Monotherapy):** A single-arm study in 126 patients resistant to anthracyclines, taxanes, and capecitabine showed an Objective Response Rate (ORR) of 12.4% [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vials:** Store in a refrigerator at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F). Retain in original package to protect from light [US].
- **Reconstituted Solution:** Stable in the vial (not syringe) for up to 1 hour at room temperature [US].
- **Diluted Infusion:** Stable at room temperature and room light for a maximum of 6 hours. Administration must be completed within this 6-hour window [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** [US] Rx-only.
- **Hazardous Drug:** Ixabepilone is a cytotoxic agent; follow applicable special handling and disposal procedures [US].
