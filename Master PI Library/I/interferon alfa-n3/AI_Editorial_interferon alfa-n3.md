# Editorial Product Information: Interferon alfa-n3

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) and labeling documentation from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Interferon alfa-n3 (human leukocyte derived).
- **Brand Name(s):** Alferon N Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** L03AB07 (Interferon alfa-n3).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Sterile aqueous injection for intralesional or subcutaneous administration.
    - Strength: 5 million IU per 1 mL vial (5,000,000 [arb'U]/mL).
    - Preserved multiple-dose vial [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Subcutaneous [US]; Note: Labeling for this concentration is historically associated with intralesional use.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** AIM ImmunoTech Inc. (formerly Hemispherx Biopharma, Inc.) [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Not specified in available sources.** (Note: While interferon alfa-n3 is historically indicated for the intralesional treatment of refractory or recurrent external condyloma acuminata, the provided labeling documents do not explicitly list the indications). [US]

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing:** The provided source indicates a standard dose volume of 0.05 mL, which contains 250,000 IU of interferon alfa-n3.
- **Administration Instructions:** 
    - For subcutaneous or intralesional use.
    - Do not shake the vial before use.
    - Administration must be performed using a sterile technique.
- **Titration and Missed Dose:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Not specified in available sources.** (Standard contraindications for alpha interferons typically include hypersensitivity to interferon alfa or any component of the formulation).

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None specified in provided sources [US].
- **General Warnings:**
    - **Human-Derived Product:** This product is derived from pooled human leukocytes. Although manufacturing includes viral inactivation steps, the risk of transmitting infectious agents cannot be totally eliminated.
    - **Hypersensitivity:** The product contains **Albumin (Human)** and was manufactured using **neomycin sulfate** (though neomycin is not detectable in the final product). Use with caution in patients with known sensitivities to these components.
- **Monitoring Requirements:** Not specified in available sources.

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Not specified in available sources.** (Commonly reported effects for alpha interferons include flu-like symptoms such as fever, fatigue, myalgia, and headache).

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Interferon alfa-n3 is a multisubtype interferon produced from human leukocytes. Alpha interferons bind to specific cell-surface receptors, initiating an intracellular signaling cascade that leads to antiviral, antiproliferative, and immunomodulatory effects.
- **Composition:** 
    - Active: Interferon alfa-n3.
    - Excipients: Phosphate buffered saline, Albumin (Human).
    - Preservative: 3.3 mg/mL phenol [US].
