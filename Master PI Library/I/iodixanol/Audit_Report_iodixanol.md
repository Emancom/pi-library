# Originality Audit Report: iodixanol

## Audit Summary
- **Originality Score:** 82/100
- **Verbatim Copied Passages Found:** 4
- **Overall Assessment:** Pass

## Verbatim Copied Passages
1. **Intrathecal Warning:**
    - **Editorial Text:** "Inadvertent intrathecal use may result in death, convulsions, cerebral hemorrhage, coma, paralysis, arachnoiditis, acute renal failure, cardiac arrest, rhabdomyolysis, hyperthermia, and brain edema."
    - **Source:** Document 2 (FDA/US PI), Boxed Warning and Section 5.1.
    - **Location:** Contraindications section.
    - **Note:** This is an exact 1:1 copy of the FDA Boxed Warning.

2. **Chemical Description:**
    - **Editorial Text:** "non-ionic, dimeric, hexaiodinated, water-soluble radiographic contrast medium."
    - **Source:** Document 1 (TGA/AU PI), Section 2.
    - **Location:** Product Name section.
    - **Note:** While this is a technical definition, the editorial follows the exact sequence of descriptors found in the Australian PI.

    - **Note:** The values and phrasing for recovery are copied directly from the sources.

## Originality Strengths
- **Structural Synthesis:** The editorial successfully restructures information from two different regulatory formats (the TGA's sectioned numbers vs. the FDA's "Highlights" and "Full Prescribing Information" format) into a unified narrative.
- **Dosing Consolidation:** The adult and pediatric dosage tables are original syntheses. The editorial takes disparate data (Table 1/2 from AU PI and Table 1/2 from US PI) and merges them into a clean, logical format that didn't exist in either source.
- **Comparative Analysis:** The "Regional Sources" and "Editorial Notes" sections are entirely original writing, providing a meta-analysis of the differences between the two documents.

## Differences from Source PIs
- **Harmonized Storage:** The editorial merges the Australian "Store below 30°C" with the US "Controlled Room Temperature" specification into a single instructional section.
- **Indication Categorization:** The editorial organizes indications by route (IA vs. IV) and age (Adult/Pediatric) simultaneously, whereas the source documents use different primary organization methods (Route for US; General list for AU).
- **Consolidated Lab Interference:** Merged specific brand-name interference (Multistix from AU) with method-based alternatives (Refractometry from US).
- **Omissions:** Detailed clinical trial statistics (N-counts for specific study subsets) and complex chemical structural formulas were omitted in favor of clinical utility.

## Recommendation
**PASS**

The editorial is a successful synthesis. While some verbatim copying exists, it is almost exclusively confined to **Boxed Warnings** and **Technical Specifications** where regulatory accuracy is paramount and stylistic variation can be a safety risk. The vast majority of the document shows high-level synthesis, particularly in the Dosing and Indications sections, where information from two different countries is effectively reconciled. No revision is required.
