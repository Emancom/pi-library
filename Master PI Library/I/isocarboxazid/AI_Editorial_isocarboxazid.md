# Editorial Product Information: Isocarboxazid

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating regulatory requirements and clinical guidance into a single, cohesive reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official PI or local regulatory requirements for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Isocarboxazid
- **Chemical Class:** Non-selective hydrazine monoamine oxidase inhibitor (MAOI)
- **Brand Name(s):** **Marplan** [US]
- **ATC Code:** N06AF01
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Oral tablet, 10 mg (peach-colored, round, scored) [US]
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Lifsa Drugs LLC [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Major Depressive Disorder (MDD):** Indicated for the treatment of depression. Due to its potential for serious adverse effects and dietary/drug interactions, **isocarboxazid** is not considered a first-line agent. It should be reserved for patients who have failed to respond satisfactorily to other antidepressant therapies [US].
- **Clinical Note:** Effectiveness has been established in outpatients with symptoms corresponding to DSM-IV major depressive disorder, often accompanied by symptoms of anxiety [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Adult Dosing
- **Initial Dose:** 10 mg twice daily [US].
- **Titration:** If tolerated, increase by 10 mg every 2 to 4 days.
- **Target Dose:** 40 mg daily (attained by the end of the first week of treatment) [US].
- **Maximum Dose:** 60 mg daily [US].
- **Dosing Frequency:** Total daily dose should be divided into 2 to 4 doses [US].
- **Maintenance/Discontinuation:** After achieving maximum clinical response, the dose should be reduced slowly over several weeks to the lowest effective maintenance level.

### Administration Instructions
- **Delayed Response:** Clinical benefit may not be observed for 3 to 6 weeks. If no response is seen after this period, continued administration is unlikely to be beneficial [US].
- **Surgical Consideration:** Discontinue at least 10 days prior to elective surgery to avoid interactions with anesthetic agents [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to **isocarboxazid** [US].
- **Cardiovascular/Cerebrovascular Disease:** Confirmed or suspected cerebrovascular defect, cardiovascular disease, or hypertension [US].
- **Pheochromocytoma:** Due to the risk of inhibited metabolism of pressor substances [US].
- **Liver/Renal Disease:** History of hepatic disease, abnormal liver function tests, or severe renal impairment [US].
- **History of Headache:** Patients with a history of frequent or severe headaches [US].
- **Concomitant Medications:**
    - Other MAOIs or dibenzazepine derivatives (e.g., TCAs).
    - SSRIs (e.g., fluoxetine, sertraline, paroxetine).
    - Sympathomimetics (e.g., amphetamines, ephedrine, levodopa, dopamine).
    - CNS depressants (e.g., narcotics, alcohol).
    - Meperidine, bupropion, buspirone, and dextromethorphan.
    - Antihypertensives, diuretics, and antihistamines [US].
- **Dietary:** Foods with high tyramine content or excessive caffeine [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warning: Suicidality and Antidepressant Drugs [US]
- Antidepressants increase the risk of suicidal thinking and behavior (suicidality) in children, adolescents, and young adults (ages 18–24) during short-term treatment of MDD and other psychiatric disorders.
- Monitoring for clinical worsening, suicidality, or unusual changes in behavior is required for all patients, especially during the initial months of therapy or at times of dose adjustments.
- **Marplan** is not approved for pediatric use [US].

### General Warnings
- **Hypertensive Crisis:** The most significant risk associated with **isocarboxazid** is the "cheese effect"—hypertensive crises resulting from ingestion of tyramine or sympathomimetics. These can be fatal and are characterized by occipital headache, neck stiffness, palpitations, and intracranial bleeding.
- **Second-Line Status:** Should be reserved for treatment-resistant cases due to the risk profile [US].
- **Hypotension:** Postural hypotension is common, especially in patients with pre-existing hypertension [US].
- **Seizure Threshold:** May lower the convulsive threshold; use with caution in patients with epilepsy [US].
- **Hepatotoxicity:** Low incidence reported; periodic liver chemistry tests are recommended. Discontinue at the first sign of jaundice or hepatic dysfunction [US].
- **Bipolar Disorder:** Patients should be screened for bipolar disorder before initiating therapy to avoid precipitating a mixed/manic episode [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are categorised by frequency based on short-term placebo-controlled trials [US].

| Frequency | Adverse Effect |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Dizziness (up to 29%), Headache (up to 15%) |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Nausea (6%), Dry mouth (9%), Orthostatic hypotension (4%), Sleep disturbance (5%), Insomnia (6%), Tremor (4%), Constipation (7%), Drowsiness (4%) |
| **Uncommon (<1%)** | Anxiety, forgetfulness, syncope, palpitations, urinary frequency, impotence |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Hypertensive crisis, intracranial hemorrhage, hepatotoxicity, SIADH (rare), and emergence of suicidality [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
### Pharmacodynamic Interactions
- **SSRIs:** Risk of fatal serotonin syndrome. Wait at least 14 days after stopping **isocarboxazid** before starting an SSRI. If switching from fluoxetine, wait 5 weeks before starting **isocarboxazid** [US].
- **Meperidine:** Contraindicated; risk of circulatory collapse, hyperpyrexia, and death [US].
- **Bupropion:** Contraindicated; allow 14 days between discontinuation of an MAOI and initiation of bupropion [US].
- **Buspirone:** Risk of elevated blood pressure; 10-day washout recommended [US].
- **Dextromethorphan:** Risk of psychosis or bizarre behavior [US].
- **Anesthetics:** Potentiation of hypotensive effects; cocaine or local anesthetics containing vasoconstrictors are contraindicated [US].

### Food-Drug Interactions (Tyramine)
Patients must strictly avoid:
- Aged cheeses, sour cream, Chianti wine, sherry, beer (including non-alcoholic), liqueurs.
- Pickled herring, anchovies, caviar, liver, canned figs, raisins, overripe bananas/avocados.
- Soy sauce, sauerkraut, fava bean pods, yeast extracts, and cured meats [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not Specified - Registry available]. Reproductive toxicity studies in animals are inadequate. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Excretion in human milk is unknown. Use with caution and only if clearly needed [US].
- **Fertility:** No data available on impairment of fertility in humans [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness not established in patients under 16 years of age. Not recommended for use in pediatric populations [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Pooled analyses suggest a reduced risk of suicidality in patients aged 65 and older compared to placebo. However, caution is advised due to the risk of orthostatic hypotension [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Use with caution to prevent drug accumulation; contraindicated in severe impairment [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Contraindicated in patients with a history of liver disease or abnormal liver function [US].
- **Diabetic Patients:** MAOIs may contribute to hypoglycemic episodes; use with caution [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Irreversible, non-selective inhibition of monoamine oxidase. This leads to increased levels of biogenic amines (epinephrine, norepinephrine, serotonin, and dopamine) in the CNS [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Specific pharmacokinetic parameters (absorption, distribution, metabolism, elimination) for **isocarboxazid** are not available in current official documentation [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Inhibition of MAO occurs in the brain, heart, and liver [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **MDD Studies:** Effectiveness was demonstrated in two 6-week, placebo-controlled trials of adult outpatients. Dosage was titrated up to 80 mg/day (mean dose 40 mg/day). **Isocarboxazid** showed a significantly greater reduction in Hamilton Depression Scale (HAM-D) scores compared to placebo [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F–86°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container [US].
