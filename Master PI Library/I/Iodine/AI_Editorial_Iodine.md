## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Povidone-iodine (USP) 10% (equivalent to 1% titratable/available iodine).
- **Chemical Class:** Iodophor (a complex of polyvinylpyrrolidone and iodine).
- **Brand Name(s):** Aplicare Povidone-Iodine [US].
- **ATC Code:** D08AG02 (Povidone-iodine).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):**
    - Topical Solution (Non-sterile), 10% w/v [US].
    - Topical Spray (Non-sterile), 10% w/v [US].
    - Topical Scrub (Non-sterile), 10% w/v [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical (External use only).
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Aplicare Products, LLC (a Medline Industries, LP company) [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Antiseptic Skin Preparation:** General antimicrobial skin cleansing.
- **Preoperative Preparation:** For single-use patient skin preparation prior to surgical procedures [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Adults and Paediatrics:** Apply locally to the affected skin area as needed [US].

### Administration Instructions
- For external use only.
- When used for preoperative preparation, the product is intended for single-use application only [US].
- **Surgical Scrub/Prep:** Application should be thorough, but clinicians must ensure the solution does not "pool" (see Section 5).

## 4. Contraindications
- **Ocular Exposure:** Do not use in or near the eyes [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Contraindicated in individuals with a known allergy or clinical sensitivity to iodine or any component of the formulation [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Avoid "Pooling":** It is critical to avoid the accumulation or "pooling" of the solution beneath the patient during surgical procedures. Prolonged exposure to wet solution may result in significant skin irritation or chemical burns [US].
- **Deep or Puncture Wounds:** Patients or clinicians should consult a physician prior to use on deep wounds, puncture wounds, or serious burns [US].
- **Infection/Irritation:** Discontinue use and seek medical advice if the condition worsens, if infection occurs, or if redness, irritation, swelling, or pain persists/increases [US].
- **Accidental Ingestion:** In case of oral ingestion, professional medical assistance or a Poison Control Center should be contacted immediately [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
The following adverse effects are documented based on clinical experience with topical povidone-iodine:
- **Frequency Not Specified:**
    - Skin irritation (associated with prolonged wet contact/pooling).
    - Localised redness.
    - Swelling or oedema at the site of application.
    - Localised pain or pruritus.
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Chemical skin burns (secondary to pooling and prolonged exposure) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions

- *Editorial Note:* Clinicians should generally avoid using povidone-iodine concurrently with other topical wound care products containing silver, hydrogen peroxide, or taurolidine, as these may diminish antimicrobial efficacy.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Povidone-iodine is an antiseptic complex. It slowly releases inorganic iodine, which exerts broad-spectrum microbicidal activity against bacteria, fungi, protozoa, and viruses via the oxidation of microbial proteins and nucleic acids.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Acts as a topical antiseptic for skin preparation [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not detailed in the source document. (Note: Systemic absorption of iodine can occur through intact skin and is increased in damaged skin or mucosal membranes).
