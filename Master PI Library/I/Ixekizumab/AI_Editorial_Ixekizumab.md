# Editorial Product Information: Ixekizumab

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided from the following jurisdiction: **Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, intended to consolidate clinical data into a concise, structured format.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for the official PI or prescribing information required for clinical practice in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory authorities for specific prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Ixekizumab (rch)
- **Chemical Class:** Humanised immunoglobulin G subclass 4 (IgG4) monoclonal antibody (mAb) targeting interleukin-17A (IL-17A).
- **Brand Name(s):** **Taltz** [Australian]
- **ATC Code:** L04AC13
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
  - Solution for injection: 80 mg in 1 mL single-dose prefilled pen (autoinjector). [Australian]
  - Solution for injection: 80 mg in 1 mL single-dose prefilled syringe. [Australian]
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Subcutaneous (SC) injection.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Eli Lilly Australia Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Plaque Psoriasis:** Treatment of moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis in adults who are candidates for phototherapy or systemic therapy. [Australian]
- **Psoriatic Arthritis:** Treatment of active psoriatic arthritis in adults who have shown an inadequate response or intolerance to previous disease-modifying antirheumatic drug (DMARD) therapy. It may be used as monotherapy or in combination with conventional DMARDs (e.g., methotrexate). [Australian]
- **Ankylosing Spondylitis (Radiographic Axial Spondyloarthritis):** Treatment of active ankylosing spondylitis in adults. [Australian]
- **Non-radiographic Axial Spondyloarthritis (nr-axSpA):** Treatment of active nr-axSpA in adults with objective signs of inflammation (elevated CRP and/or MRI evidence) who have responded inadequately to, or are intolerant of, non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs). [Australian]

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Plaque Psoriasis (Adults)
- **Initial Dose:** 160 mg (two 80 mg injections) at Week 0.
- **Maintenance Dose:** 80 mg every 2 weeks at Weeks 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, and 12, followed by 80 mg every 4 weeks thereafter. [Australian]

### Psoriatic Arthritis (Adults)
- **Initial Dose:** 160 mg (two 80 mg injections) at Week 0.
- **Maintenance Dose:** 80 mg every 4 weeks.
- **Note:** For patients with coexistent moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis, the dosing regimen for plaque psoriasis should be followed. [Australian]

### Axial Spondyloarthritis (Radiographic and Non-radiographic)
- **Recommended Dose:** 80 mg every 4 weeks. [Australian]

### Administration Instructions
- For subcutaneous use only.
- Common injection sites include the abdomen, thighs, or the back of the upper arm.
- Avoid injecting into areas affected by psoriasis.
- Following professional training, patients may self-inject.
- Do not use if the solution has been frozen. [Australian]

### Missed Dose
- Not specified in available source.

## 4. Contraindications
- Known serious hypersensitivity to **ixekizumab** or any of the excipients (sodium chloride, sodium citrate dihydrate, citric acid, polysorbate 80). [Australian]

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Infections:** **Ixekizumab** may increase infection risk. Common infections observed include upper respiratory tract infections, oral candidiasis, and tinea. Caution is advised in patients with chronic or clinically important active infections.
- **Tuberculosis (TB):** Patients must be evaluated for TB before starting treatment. Do not initiate in patients with active TB. Treat latent TB prior to starting **ixekizumab**.
- **Hypersensitivity:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, angioedema, and urticaria, have occurred. Discontinue immediately if a serious reaction occurs.
- **Inflammatory Bowel Disease (IBD):** New cases or exacerbations of Crohn’s disease and ulcerative colitis have been reported. Monitor patients with IBD closely.
- **Immunisations:** Complete all age-appropriate vaccinations prior to therapy. **Live vaccines must not be administered** during treatment; data on immune response to inactivated vaccines are limited. [Australian]

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Frequency Table (Clinical Trial Data)
| Frequency | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Injection site reactions (erythema, pain), upper respiratory tract infections (nasopharyngitis). |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Nausea, oropharyngeal pain, tinea infections, rhinitis (in axSpA), IBD (in axSpA). |
| **Uncommon (0.1–1%)** | Influenza, rhinitis, conjunctivitis, urticaria, oral candidiasis, dyshidrotic eczema. |
| **Rare (<0.1%)** | Anaphylaxis, oesophageal candidiasis, exfoliative dermatitis. |

- **Neutropenia:** Grade 3 (<1,000 cells/mm³) neutropenia was observed in 0.1% of psoriasis patients. It was generally transient and not associated with increased infection rates.
- **Immunogenicity:** Approximately 9–17% of psoriasis patients developed anti-drug antibodies (ADAs); roughly 1% had confirmed neutralising antibodies associated with reduced clinical response. [Australian]

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **CYP450 Substrates:** **Ixekizumab** does not have a clinically significant impact on the pharmacokinetics of drugs metabolised by CYP3A4, CYP2C9, CYP2C19, CYP1A2, or CYP2D6 (e.g., warfarin, omeprazole, midazolam).
- **Combination Therapy:** No interactions were observed with concomitant use of methotrexate, oral corticosteroids, NSAIDs, or conventional DMARDs.
- **Live Vaccines:** Avoid concomitant use. [Australian]

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [Australian Category C]. There are no adequate studies in pregnant women. Animal studies in monkeys showed no embryotoxicity or teratogenicity, though the drug crosses the placenta. Use only if potential benefit outweighs risk to the foetus.
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if **ixekizumab** is excreted in human milk. Animal data show low-level excretion in milk. A decision must be made whether to discontinue nursing or the drug.
- **Fertility:** Human data are not available. Animal studies showed no harmful effects on reproductive organs or menstrual cycles. [Australian]

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy in patients <18 years of age have not been evaluated. [Australian]
- **Elderly Use (≥65 years):** No dose adjustment required. Although no overall differences in safety/efficacy were observed compared to younger patients, data for patients ≥75 years are limited.
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** **Ixekizumab** has not been studied in these populations; no dosage recommendations can be made. [Australian]

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** **Ixekizumab** is a humanised IgG4 mAb that binds with high affinity (<3 pM) to the proinflammatory cytokine IL-17A, neutralising its activity. It does not bind to IL-17B, C, D, E, or F.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
  - **Absorption:** Mean peak concentrations reached in 4–7 days. Steady-state is achieved by Week 8 (Q2W dosing). Average bioavailability: 54–90%.
  - **Distribution:** Mean volume of distribution at steady-state: 7.11 L.
  - **Metabolism:** Expected to be degraded into small peptides and amino acids via catabolic pathways.
  - **Elimination:** Mean elimination half-life is 13 days in plaque psoriasis. Clearance increases with body weight. [Australian]

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Plaque Psoriasis (UNCOVER-1, 2, 3):** Treatment with **ixekizumab** (80 mg Q2W) resulted in superior PASI 75 and sPGA (0,1) response rates at Week 12 compared to placebo and etanercept. Maintenance of response (sPGA 0,1) was 71% at Week 60 with Q4W dosing.
- **Psoriatic Arthritis (SPIRIT-P1, P2):** Significant improvements in ACR20/50/70 and PASI 75 responses were seen at Week 24 compared to placebo in both biologic-naïve and TNF-inhibitor-experienced patients.
- **Axial Spondyloarthritis (COAST-V, W, X):** Significant improvements in ASAS40 response rates were achieved at Week 16 compared to placebo in radiographic and non-radiographic populations. [Australian]

## 12. Storage Conditions
- Store at 2°C to 8°C (Refrigerate).
- Do not freeze.
- Protect from light and do not shake.
- **Excursion:** May be stored out of the refrigerator at temperatures up to 30°C for a single period of up to 5 days. If not used within this window, the product must be discarded. [Australian]

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison/Schedule Classification:** Prescription Medicine – Schedule 4 [Australian].
