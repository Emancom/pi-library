# Editorial Product Information: **Iloperidone**

## Editorial Note
*   **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA). 
*   **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of **iloperidone** for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises clinical data and regulatory requirements to assist in safe prescribing and monitoring.
*   **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) or summary of product characteristics required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians must consult their local regulatory authority's approved documentation.

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredient:** **Iloperidone**
*   **Chemical Class:** Piperidinyl-benzisoxazole derivative; atypical antipsychotic.
*   **Brand Name(s):** **Fanapt** [US].
*   **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    *   **Oral Tablets:** 1 mg, 2 mg, 4 mg, 6 mg, 8 mg, 10 mg, and 12 mg [US].
*   **Route of Administration:** Oral.
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Vanda Pharmaceuticals Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Schizophrenia:** Treatment of schizophrenia in adults [US-approved].
*   **Bipolar I Disorder:** Acute treatment of manic or mixed episodes associated with Bipolar I disorder in adults [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
*   **Schizophrenia:** The recommended target dose is 12 mg to 24 mg per day, administered as 6 mg to 12 mg twice daily [US].
*   **Bipolar I Mania:** The recommended target dose is 24 mg per day, administered as 12 mg twice daily [US].
*   **Administration:** Tablets may be taken with or without food [US].

### Titration Schedule
Gradual titration is mandatory to minimize the risk of orthostatic hypotension [US]:
*   **Standard Titration (Schizophrenia):** Start at 1 mg twice daily. Increase to 2 mg, 4 mg, 6 mg, 8 mg, 10 mg, and 12 mg twice daily on consecutive days (Days 1–7) [US].
*   **Standard Titration (Bipolar Mania):** Start at 1 mg twice daily on Day 1. Increase to 3 mg (Day 2), 6 mg (Day 3), 9 mg (Day 4), and 12 mg twice daily (Day 5) [US].

### Dose Adjustments
*   **CYP2D6 Poor Metabolizers:** Reduce dose by 50%. A specific titration schedule (Pack C) is utilized for these patients [US].
*   **Reinitiation:** If a patient has been off **iloperidone** for more than 3 days, the initial titration schedule should be restarted [US].

## 4. Contraindications
*   **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to **iloperidone** or any excipients in the formulation. Reported reactions include anaphylaxis and angioedema [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warning [US]
*   **Increased Mortality in Elderly Patients with Dementia-Related Psychosis:** Antipsychotic drugs are associated with an increased risk of death in elderly patients with dementia-related psychosis. **Iloperidone** is not approved for this population.

### General Warnings
*   **QT Prolongation:** **Iloperidone** prolongs the QT interval (mean increase of ~19 msec when combined with metabolic inhibitors). Avoid use with other drugs known to prolong the QTc interval (e.g., Class IA or III antiarrhythmics, certain antibiotics) and in patients with congenital long QT syndrome or a history of cardiac arrhythmias [US].
*   **Neuroleptic Malignant Syndrome (NMS):** Potentially fatal; requires immediate discontinuation and intensive symptomatic treatment [US].
*   **Tardive Dyskinesia (TD):** Risk increases with duration of treatment and cumulative dose; consider discontinuation if signs appear [US].
*   **Metabolic Changes:** Atypical antipsychotics are associated with hyperglycemia/diabetes mellitus (monitor fasting glucose), dyslipidemia (monitor lipid profile), and significant weight gain (monitor weight) [US].
*   **Orthostatic Hypotension and Syncope:** Risk is highest during initial titration due to alpha-1 adrenergic antagonism. Monitor vital signs in vulnerable patients [US].
*   **Blood Dyscrasias:** Leukopenia, neutropenia, and agranulocytosis have been reported. Monitor CBC in patients with a history of low WBC/neutrophils; discontinue if absolute neutrophil count (ANC) is <1000/mm³ [US].
*   **Intraoperative Floppy Iris Syndrome (IFIS):** Observed in patients receiving alpha-1 blockers during cataract surgery [US].
*   **Priapism:** Severe cases may require surgical intervention [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are often dose-dependent.

### Common Adverse Reactions (Incidence ≥5% and 2x Placebo)
*   **Schizophrenia:** Dizziness, dry mouth, fatigue, nasal congestion, orthostatic hypotension, somnolence, tachycardia, and weight gain [US].
*   **Bipolar Mania:** Tachycardia, dizziness, dry mouth, increased hepatic enzymes, nasal congestion, weight gain, hypotension, and somnolence [US].

### Serious and Other Notable Effects
*   **Extrapyramidal Symptoms (EPS):** Rates in schizophrenia trials were 13.5% (10–16 mg/day) and 15.1% (20–24 mg/day) compared to 11.6% for placebo [US].
*   **Hyperprolactinemia:** May lead to galactorrhea, amenorrhea, and gynecomastia [US].
*   **Seizures:** Use with caution in patients with a history of seizures or conditions lowering the seizure threshold [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **Strong CYP2D6 Inhibitors (e.g., fluoxetine, paroxetine):** Increase **iloperidone** exposure. Reduce **iloperidone** dose by 50% [US].
*   **Strong CYP3A4 Inhibitors (e.g., ketoconazole, clarithromycin):** Increase **iloperidone** exposure. Reduce **iloperidone** dose by 50% [US].
*   **Combined CYP2D6 and CYP3A4 Inhibition:** Reduce dose by 50% [US].
*   **Centrally Acting Drugs and Alcohol:** Use caution due to additive CNS effects [US].
*   **Antihypertensive Agents:** Potential for additive hypotensive effects [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy:** [US]: Limited data in humans. Third-trimester exposure may cause neonatal extrapyramidal and/or withdrawal symptoms. Animal studies show increased intrauterine deaths and developmental delays at high doses. Use only if potential benefit justifies the risk.
*   **Breastfeeding:** [US]: **Iloperidone** is present in rat milk. Because of the potential for serious adverse reactions in infants, breastfeeding is not recommended during treatment.
*   **Fertility:** Animal studies indicate decreased fertility in both male and female rats [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness not established in patients <18 years of age [US].
*   **Elderly Use:** Higher risk of cerebrovascular adverse events and death in dementia-related psychosis. In general, clinical trials did not include enough subjects ≥65 to determine if they respond differently [US].
*   **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment is required for patients with creatinine clearance <30 mL/min [US].
*   **Hepatic Impairment:** No adjustment for mild impairment. Use caution and consider dose reduction in moderate impairment. Not recommended for patients with severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh Class C) [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
*   **Mechanism of Action:** The exact mechanism is unknown; efficacy is likely mediated through combined D2 and 5-HT2 antagonism [US].
*   **Pharmacodynamics:** High affinity for 5-HT2A, D2, and D3 receptors, and alpha-1 adrenoceptors. Moderate affinity for D4, 5-HT6, and 5-HT7. Low affinity for 5-HT1A and D1 [US].
*   **Pharmacokinetics:**
    *   **Absorption:** Well absorbed; peak plasma levels in 2–4 hours. Relative bioavailability is 96% [US].
    *   **Distribution:** Volume of distribution is 1340–2800 L; 92–97% protein bound [US].
    *   **Metabolism:** Primarily hepatic via CYP2D6 (hydroxylation) and CYP3A4 (O-demethylation). Major metabolites include P88 (active) and P95 [US].
    *   **Elimination:** Mean half-life is 18–33 hours depending on CYP2D6 metabolizer status [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   **Schizophrenia:** Supported by two short-term (4- and 6-week) placebo-controlled trials and one long-term randomized withdrawal trial. **Iloperidone** demonstrated superiority to placebo on BPRS and PANSS scores [US].
*   **Bipolar I Mania:** Supported by a 4-week placebo-controlled trial (n=392) where 24 mg/day was superior to placebo in changing the YMRS total score from baseline to Day 28 [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   **Temperature:** Store at controlled room temperature, 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F–86°F) [US].
*   **Handling:** Protect from light and moisture [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
*   **United States:** Classified as Rx-only [US].
*   **Other Regions:** Not specified in available sources.
