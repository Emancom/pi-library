# Editorial Product Information: Inebilizumab

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of inebilizumab for healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific PI. Clinicians must consult the local regulatory approved labelling before prescribing. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Inebilizumab-cdon [US].
- **Chemical Class:** CD19-directed humanized afucosylated IgG1 monoclonal antibody.
- **Brand Name:** **UPLIZNA**.
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** 100 mg/10 mL (10 mg/mL) clear to slightly opalescent, colorless to slightly yellow solution in a single-dose vial [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Horizon Therapeutics USA, Inc. (an Amgen Inc. company) [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Neuromyelitis Optica Spectrum Disorder (NMOSD):** For the treatment of adult patients who are anti-aquaporin-4 (AQP4) antibody positive [US-approved].
- **Immunoglobulin G4-Related Disease (IgG4-RD):** For the treatment of adult patients [US-approved].
- **Generalized Myasthenia Gravis (gMG):** For the treatment of adult patients who are anti-acetylcholine receptor (AChR) or anti-muscle specific tyrosine kinase (MuSK) antibody positive [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults)
The recommended dose for all approved indications is:
- **Initial Dose:** 300 mg as an intravenous infusion, followed two weeks later by a second 300 mg intravenous infusion.
- **Subsequent Doses:** 300 mg single intravenous infusion every 6 months (starting 6 months after the initial dose).

### Assessments Prior to Initiation
- **Hepatitis B Virus (HBV) Screening:** Perform HBsAg and anti-HBV tests before starting treatment.
- **Serum Immunoglobulins:** Perform quantitative testing for baseline levels.
- **Tuberculosis (TB) Screening:** Evaluate for active TB and test for latent infection.
- **Vaccinations:** Complete required immunizations at least 4 weeks prior to initiation.

### Premedication
Administer the following prior to every infusion to reduce the risk of reactions:
- **Corticosteroid:** e.g., methylprednisolone 80 mg to 125 mg IV (30 minutes prior).
- **Antihistamine:** e.g., diphenhydramine 25 mg to 50 mg orally (30–60 minutes prior).
- **Antipyretic:** e.g., acetaminophen 500 mg to 650 mg orally (30–60 minutes prior).

### Administration Instructions
- **Dilution:** Withdraw 30 mL (3 vials) and transfer to 250 mL of 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection. Mix by gentle inversion; do not shake.
- **Infusion Rate:** Titrate the infusion to completion over approximately 90 minutes. Use an infusion pump and a 0.2 or 0.22 micron in-line filter.
- **Monitoring:** Monitor the patient closely during and for at least one hour after the infusion is complete.

## 4. Contraindications
- History of life-threatening infusion reaction to inebilizumab [US].
- Active Hepatitis B infection [US].
- Active or untreated latent tuberculosis [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Infusion Reactions:** Can cause reactions including anaphylaxis. If a life-threatening reaction occurs, discontinue permanently.
- **Infections:** Serious, life-threatening, and fatal infections have occurred. Delay administration in patients with active infections.
- **HBV Reactivation:** Reactivation has been reported with B-cell depleting therapies. Monitor patients at risk.
- **Progressive Multifocal Leukoencephalopathy (PML):** Opportunistic JC virus infection of the brain. Monitor for new or worsening neurological signs.
- **Reduction in Immunoglobulins:** Progressive and prolonged hypogammaglobulinemia (IgG and IgM) can occur. Monitor levels and consider discontinuation if serious opportunistic or recurrent infections develop.
- **Fetal Risk:** Based on animal data, may cause fetal harm via B-cell depletion in the fetus [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Frequency by Indication
| Indication | Very Common (>10%) | Common (1–10%) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **NMOSD** | Urinary tract infection (UTI), arthralgia, nasopharyngitis, infusion reactions, headache | Upper respiratory tract infection, influenza, back pain |
| **IgG4-RD** | Lymphopenia, UTI, nasopharyngitis, upper respiratory tract infection | Pyrexia, neutropenia, myalgia, influenza, pneumonia |
| **gMG** | Headache, infusion reactions | Nasopharyngitis, cough, UTI |

### Serious Adverse Effects
- **Serious Infections:** Bacterial, fungal, and viral (new or reactivated).
- **Infusion-Related:** Anaphylaxis and severe infusion reactions.
- **Laboratory:** Severe lymphopenia and neutropenia.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Immunosuppressive/Immune-Modulating Therapies:** Concomitant use with other immunosuppressants (including systemic corticosteroids) may increase the risk of infection. Consider additive effects.
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Inebilizumab is not cleared via Cytochrome P450 enzymes; the risk of interaction with P450 substrates, inducers, or inhibitors is low [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: No category letter assigned]. Animal data indicate inebilizumab crosses the placenta and results in fetal B-cell depletion. Offspring of treated animals showed persistent immune dysfunction. 
- **Contraception:** Females of reproductive potential should use effective contraception during treatment and for 6 months after the last infusion [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Human IgG is excreted in milk. The risk to the infant (potential B-cell depletion) is unknown. Consider the clinical need for the mother and potential risks to the infant.
- **Fertility:** Animal studies showed reduced fertility in both males and females at all doses tested [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** In IgG4-RD trials, no overall differences in safety or efficacy were observed between patients ≥65 and younger adults. Data are limited for NMOSD and gMG in this population [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** No formal clinical studies have been conducted. PK analysis suggests gender, race, and age do not significantly affect clearance [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Binds to CD19, a surface antigen on pre-B and mature B lymphocytes. Following binding, it induces antibody-dependent cellular cytolysis (ADCC), leading to B-cell depletion.
- **Pharmacokinetics:** 
    - **Half-life:** Mean terminal half-life of 18 days.
    - **Metabolism:** Degraded by proteolytic enzymes.
    - **Elimination:** Systemic clearance is approximately 0.19 L/day. Clearance may be higher when B-cell counts are high (CD19-mediated clearance) and decreases as B-cells are depleted.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Reduces CD20+ B-cell counts (used as a proxy for CD19) in blood as early as 8 days post-infusion.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Study 1 (NMOSD):** A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial (n=213). In AQP4+ patients, inebilizumab reduced the risk of first adjudicated relapse by 77.3% compared to placebo (HR 0.227; p < 0.0001). No benefit was seen in AQP4-negative patients.
- **Study 2 (IgG4-RD):** A 52-week randomized trial (n=135). Inebilizumab reduced the risk of treated, adjudicated flare by 87% (HR 0.13; p < 0.0001). 
- **Study 3 (gMG):** A randomized, double-blind trial (n=238). At Week 26, patients showed a statistically significant improvement in the Myasthenia Gravis-Activities of Daily Living (MG-ADL) score compared to placebo (-4.2 vs -2.2 points; p < 0.0001).

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vials:** Store in a refrigerator between 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F).
- **Handling:** Keep in the original carton to protect from light. Do not freeze or shake. Store vials upright.
- **Diluted Solution:** Use immediately. If not used, it may be stored for up to 24 hours in the refrigerator (2°C to 8°C) or 4 hours at room temperature (20°C to 25°C).
