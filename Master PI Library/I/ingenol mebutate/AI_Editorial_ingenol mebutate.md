# Product Information: Picato (ingenol mebutate) Gel

## 1. Product Name and Active Ingredient
**Product Name:** Picato®  
**Active Ingredient:** Ingenol mebutate

Picato is a topical gel available in two strengths for site-specific application:
*   **0.015% Gel:** Each gram contains 150 micrograms of ingenol mebutate. (Tubes contain 70 mcg in 0.47 g of gel).
*   **0.05% Gel:** Each gram contains 500 micrograms of ingenol mebutate. (Tubes contain 235 mcg in 0.47 g of gel).

The drug substance is a purified diterpene ester extracted from the plant *Euphorbia peplus*.

## 2. Approved Indications
Picato gel is indicated for the topical treatment of solar (actinic) keratosis in adults. It is specifically formulated for use on non-hyperkeratotic, non-hypertrophic lesions.

## 3. Contraindications
Picato is contraindicated in patients with a documented history of severe hypersensitivity to ingenol mebutate or any of the excipients used in the gel formulation (including isopropyl alcohol, benzyl alcohol, and citric acid).

## 4. Warnings and Precautions

### Ophthalmic Exposure
Serious eye injuries have been reported following accidental exposure to Picato. Adverse reactions include severe eye pain, chemical conjunctivitis, corneal burns, eyelid edema, and ptosis [TGA/FDA]. 
*   **Avoid Periocular Use:** Do not apply the gel in the periocular area or near the eyes.
*   **Hand Hygiene:** Patients must wash their hands thoroughly with soap and water immediately after every application.
*   **Accidental Contact:** If the eyes are exposed, they should be flushed immediately with copious amounts of water, and the patient must seek urgent medical evaluation.

### Local Skin Responses (LSRs)
Severe local skin reactions are expected and are a marker of the drug's biological activity. These include erythema, flaking, scaling, crusting, swelling, and potential ulceration or vesiculation.
*   **Progression:** Reactions typically peak within one week of completing the treatment cycle.
*   **Resolution:** Face and scalp reactions generally resolve within 2 weeks; body reactions may take up to 4 weeks. 
*   **Healing Requirement:** Application should not commence until the skin has fully healed from any previous medicinal or surgical treatments.

### Risk of Malignancy
Healthcare providers should remain vigilant for the development of skin lesions within the treatment area. Atypical or suspicious lesions must be biopsied to rule out malignancy, specifically non-melanoma skin cancer [FDA].

### Hypersensitivity and Systemic Reactions
Though systemic absorption is minimal, anaphylaxis and severe allergic reactions have been reported. If a significant hypersensitivity reaction occurs, treatment must be discontinued immediately. Cases of Stevens-Johnson Syndrome have been identified in post-marketing surveillance [TGA].

## 5. Dosage and Administration
Picato is for **topical use only**. It is not for oral, ophthalmic, or mucosal use.

### Adult Dosing Schedule
*   **Face and Scalp:** Apply Picato gel 0.015% to the affected area (up to 25 cm²) once daily for **3 consecutive days**.
*   **Trunk and Extremities (Body):** Apply Picato gel 0.05% to the affected area (up to 25 cm²) once daily for **2 consecutive days**.

### Application Instructions
1.  **Preparation:** Do not apply immediately before or after showering, or within 2 hours of bedtime.
2.  **Application:** Squeeze the contents of one single-use tube onto a fingertip and spread evenly over the treatment area. 
3.  **Drying:** Allow the area to dry for 15 minutes.
4.  **Post-application Care:** Do not wash or touch the treated area for 6 hours. After this period, the area may be washed with mild soap and water.
5.  **Occlusion:** The treated area should not be covered with bandages or occlusive dressings.

## 6. Adverse Effects
The most common adverse reactions reported in clinical trials (>2%) include:
*   **Application Site Reactions:** Pain, pruritus, irritation, and localized infection.
*   **Systemic Reactions:** Headache and periorbital edema.

Post-marketing reports have identified additional risks, including application site scarring, pigmentation changes, herpes zoster reactivation, and severe allergic contact dermatitis.

## 7. Drug Interactions
Specific drug interaction studies have not been conducted. Given that ingenol mebutate does not reach quantifiable levels in the systemic circulation following topical application, interactions with systemically administered medications are considered highly unlikely [TGA/FDA].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
**Pregnancy (Category B3):** There are no adequate data on the use of Picato in pregnant women. Animal studies using intravenous administration showed some evidence of embryo-fetal toxicity at high doses. As a precautionary measure, use during pregnancy should be avoided unless the potential benefit justifies the risk to the fetus.

**Breastfeeding:** While systemic absorption is negligible, nursing mothers should ensure the infant does not come into physical contact with the treated area for at least 6 hours following application to prevent accidental transfer.

## 9. Pharmacokinetics
Pharmacokinetic profiles in humans remain uncharacterized because blood levels of ingenol mebutate and its metabolites fall below the lower limit of quantification (0.1 ng/mL) even when applied to large surface areas (100 cm²). The drug does not appear to induce or inhibit major human cytochrome P450 isoforms.

## 10. Mechanism of Action
The exact mechanism of action is not fully elucidated but is understood to be dual-phase:
1.  **Chemo-ablation:** Induction of rapid local cell death (necrosis) in the epidermal layers.
2.  **Immunostimulation:** Induction of a profound inflammatory response characterized by the release of proinflammatory cytokines and chemokines, leading to the infiltration of neutrophils and T-cells that target the lesion.

## 11. Storage Conditions
*   **Temperature:** Picato gel must be stored under refrigeration between **2°C and 8°C (36°F – 46°F)**.
*   **Disposal:** Tubes are for single use only. Any remaining gel in an opened tube must be discarded immediately after application. Protect from freezing and excessive heat.
