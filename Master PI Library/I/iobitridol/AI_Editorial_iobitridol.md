# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial was developed using official Product Information from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial synthesis of levocarnitine injection for use by healthcare professionals. It is designed to offer a clear, clinically accurate overview of the product's regulatory and clinical profile.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and serves as a clinical reference. It is not a substitute for the official Product Information approved in a specific jurisdiction, which should always be consulted for prescribing purposes.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP).
- **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule/Amino acid derivative [(R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt].
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection (Generic).
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency (Inborn Errors of Metabolism):** Indicated for the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that lead to secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD) on Dialysis:** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg administered as a slow bolus injection (2–3 minutes) or by infusion.
- **ESRD on Hemodialysis:** The recommended starting dose is 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight) administered as a slow bolus injection into the venous return line after each dialysis session [US].

### Indication-Specific Instructions
- **Metabolic Crisis:** A loading dose may be required, followed by equivalent doses every 3 to 4 hours (and never less than every 6 hours) over the first 24 hours. Subsequent daily doses typically range around 50 mg/kg, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilised [US].
- **ESRD Monitoring:** Initiation of therapy may be guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations below 40–50 µmol/L. Dose adjustments (e.g., reduction to 5 mg/kg) may be made as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough levels [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Inspect for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration.
- Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as it contains no preservatives [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### General Warnings
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients on dialysis. Some reactions occur within minutes of administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue immediately and consider the risks/benefits before re-administration [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Levocarnitine has been associated with seizures in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with a known history, an increase in seizure frequency or severity has been observed [US].

### Clinical Precautions
- **Monitoring:** It is recommended to obtain plasma carnitine concentrations before starting therapy. Ongoing monitoring (weekly/monthly) should include blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma free carnitine levels (target range: 35 to 60 µmol/L) [US].
- **Accumulation Risk:** While this document refers to the injection, it is noted that chronic high-dose oral administration in patients with severe renal impairment may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common Adverse Effects (ESRD/Dialysis Trials)
Based on placebo-controlled trials in chronic hemodialysis patients, the following events occurred at a frequency $\ge$ 5%:
- **Body as a Whole:** Abdominal pain (21%), headache (37%), injection site reaction (38%), pain (35%), fever (12%), chest pain (15%).
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (21%), hypotension (19%), tachycardia (9%).
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea (35%), vomiting (21%), nausea (12%).
- **Metabolic/Nutritional:** Hypercalcemia (15%), hypervolemia (12%).
- **Nervous System:** Dizziness (18%), paresthesia (12%).
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (27%), cough increase (18%), dyspnea (11%).

### Other Reported Effects
- **Gastrointestinal:** Gastritis, transient nausea/vomiting, and body odor have been observed [US].
- **Neurologic:** Seizures (postmarketing) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after any dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin anticoagulation [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not specifically lettered in provided PI]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. However, as animal studies are not always predictive of human response, it should be used in pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Specific studies on levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers are unavailable. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine concentrations in milk after exogenous administration. Benefits to the mother should be weighed against risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility was found in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders and ESRD is based on body weight (mg/kg). See Section 3 for specific age-related dosing logic [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** The drug is cleared via dialysis. In ESRD, specific post-dialysis dosing is required. High-dose oral therapy is cautioned in severe renal impairment due to metabolite accumulation [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters, which are quickly eliminated [US].

### Pharmacokinetics
- **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
- **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and $\gamma$-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
- **Elimination:** Following IV bolus (20 mg/kg), approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. The mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours, and the mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pharmacokinetic Study (ESRD):** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients, 20 mg/kg doses after dialysis increased mean trough levocarnitine levels from 21.3 µmol/L at baseline to 190 µmol/L after 8 weeks [US].
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** Studies of 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses three times weekly showed dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Protection:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable for up to 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) when mixed in PVC bags with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations from 0.5 mg/mL to 8.0 mg/mL [US].

- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources.
