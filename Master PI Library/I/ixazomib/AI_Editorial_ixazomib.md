# Editorial Product Information: Ixazomib

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA)
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of ixazomib product information, synthesised for healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This editorial synthesis is intended for informational purposes and does not replace the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory authorities for specific legal and prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Ixazomib (as ixazomib citrate)
- **Chemical Class:** Proteasome inhibitor
- **Brand Name:** **NINLARO** [US]
- **ATC Code:** L01XG03
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - Oral capsules: 4 mg, 3 mg, and 2.3 mg [US]
    - **4 mg:** Light orange, imprinted with "Takeda" and "4 mg"
    - **3 mg:** Light grey, imprinted with "Takeda" and "3 mg"
    - **2.3 mg:** Light pink, imprinted with "Takeda" and "2.3 mg"
- **Route of Administration:** Oral
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Takeda Pharmaceuticals America, Inc. [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Multiple Myeloma:** Indicated in combination with lenalidomide and dexamethasone for the treatment of patients with multiple myeloma who have received at least one prior therapy [US].
- **Limitations of Use:** Not recommended for use in the maintenance setting or in newly diagnosed multiple myeloma outside of controlled clinical trials [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** The recommended starting dose is 4 mg taken orally once weekly on Days 1, 8, and 15 of a 28-day cycle [US].
- **Combination Dosing:** Typically administered with lenalidomide (25 mg daily, Days 1–21) and dexamethasone (40 mg, Days 1, 8, 15, and 22) [US].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Take at least one hour before or at least two hours after food [US].
    - Swallow capsules whole with water; do not crush, chew, or open [US].
    - On days when both **NINLARO** and dexamethasone are scheduled, they should not be taken at the same time (dexamethasone should be taken with food) [US].
- **Missed Dose:** If a dose is delayed or missed, it should be taken only if the next scheduled dose is ≥72 hours away. Do not take a double dose to make up for a missed one [US].
- **Vomiting:** If vomiting occurs after a dose, do not repeat the dose; resume at the next scheduled time [US].
- **Pre-cycle Requirements:** Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) should be ≥1,000/mm³ and platelet count ≥75,000/mm³ prior to initiating a new cycle [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **United States:** None reported in available sources [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Thrombocytopenia:** Common; nadir typically occurs between Days 14–21 with recovery by the next cycle. Monitor platelet counts at least monthly; consider more frequent monitoring during the first three cycles [US].
- **Gastrointestinal Toxicities:** Diarrhea, constipation, nausea, and vomiting may require antidiarrheal or antiemetic support and dose modification [US].
- **Peripheral Neuropathy:** Monitor for symptoms (numbness, tingling, pain). The most common type is peripheral sensory neuropathy [US].
- **Peripheral Edema:** Monitor for fluid retention; investigate underlying causes as needed [US].
- **Cutaneous Reactions:** Rash occurred in 27% of patients. Serious reactions including Stevens-Johnson Syndrome (SJS) and Toxic Epidermal Necrolysis (TEN) have been reported; discontinue if suspected [US].
- **Thrombotic Microangiopathy (TMA):** Including fatal cases of TTP/HUS. Monitor for signs and discontinue if suspected [US].
- **Hepatotoxicity:** Drug-induced liver injury and cholestatic hepatitis have been reported (<1%). Monitor hepatic enzymes regularly [US].
- **Embryo-Fetal Toxicity:** Can cause fetal harm. Verify pregnancy status before initiation [US].
- **Increased Mortality (Maintenance Setting):** Increased deaths reported when used as maintenance therapy outside controlled trials; not recommended for this use [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Very Common (>10%) [US]
- **Hematologic:** Thrombocytopenia (85% all grades), neutropenia (74% all grades)
- **Gastrointestinal:** Diarrhea (52%), constipation (35%), nausea (32%), vomiting (26%)
- **Nervous System:** Peripheral neuropathy (32%)
- **General/Infections:** Peripheral edema (27%), upper respiratory tract infection (27%), back pain (27%), bronchitis (22%)
- **Skin:** Rash (27%)
- **Ocular:** Aggregate eye disorders (38%), specifically cataracts (15%) and conjunctivitis (9%)

### Serious Adverse Effects (<1%) [US]
- Acute febrile neutrophilic dermatosis (Sweet’s syndrome)
- Transverse myelitis
- Posterior reversible encephalopathy syndrome (PRES)
- Tumor lysis syndrome
- Thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura (TTP)

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Strong CYP3A Inducers:** Avoid concomitant use. Co-administration with rifampin decreased ixazomib Cmax by 54% and AUC by 74% [US].
- **Strong CYP3A Inhibitors:** No clinically meaningful effect (e.g., clarithromycin) [US].
- **Strong CYP1A2 Inhibitors:** No clinically meaningful effect expected [US].
- **Dexamethasone Effect:** Dexamethasone is a weak-to-moderate inducer of CYP3A4; consider the risk of reduced efficacy of oral contraceptives [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** NINLARO can cause fetal harm [US]. 
    - **Jurisdiction Category:** US Pregnancy letter categories are no longer used in current FDA labeling; however, it is noted to cause embryo-fetal toxicity in animal studies at exposures slightly higher than clinical doses [US].
    - **Contraception:** Females of reproductive potential must use effective non-hormonal contraception during treatment and for 90 days after the final dose. Males with female partners of reproductive potential should use effective contraception for 90 days after the final dose [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** No data on presence in human milk. Advise patients not to breastfeed during treatment and for 90 days after the final dose [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness not established in patients <18 years [US].
- **Elderly Use:** 55% of clinical trial patients were ≥65 years. No overall differences in safety or efficacy were observed, though greater sensitivity in some older individuals cannot be ruled out [US].
- **Renal Impairment:**
    - Mild/Moderate (CrCl ≥30 mL/min): No dose adjustment required [US].
    - **Severe/ESRD (CrCl <30 mL/min or requiring dialysis):** Reduce starting dose to **3 mg** [US].
    - Ixazomib is not dialyzable [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:**
    - Mild (Bilirubin ≤ULN and AST >ULN OR Bilirubin >1–1.5x ULN): No dose adjustment [US].
    - **Moderate/Severe (Bilirubin >1.5x ULN):** Reduce starting dose to **3 mg** [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Reversible proteasome inhibitor. It preferentially binds and inhibits the chymotrypsin-like activity of the beta 5 subunit of the 20S proteasome [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Median Tmax is 1 hour; absolute bioavailability is 58%. High-fat meals decrease AUC by 28% [US].
    - **Distribution:** 99% plasma protein bound. Volume of distribution is 543 L [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily cleared by multiple CYP enzymes (3A4, 1A2, 2B6) and non-CYP proteins [US].
    - **Elimination:** Terminal half-life is 9.5 days. 62% excreted in urine, 22% in feces [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Does not prolong the QTc interval at clinically relevant exposures [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **TOURMALINE-MM1 Study:** A randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial (N=722) in patients with relapsed/refractory multiple myeloma.
- **Outcomes:** The addition of ixazomib to lenalidomide and dexamethasone significantly improved progression-free survival (PFS).
    - **Median PFS:** 20.6 months (ixazomib arm) vs 14.7 months (placebo arm) [HR 0.74; p=0.012] [US].
    - **Overall Response Rate (ORR):** 78% (ixazomib arm) vs 72% (placebo arm) [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at room temperature, not exceeding 30°C (86°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Do not freeze. Store in the original packaging until use [US].
- **Hazardous Handling:** Ixazomib is a hazardous drug; follow applicable special handling and disposal procedures. Avoid direct contact with capsule contents [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources (US documents do not use the Australian SUSMP scheduling system).
