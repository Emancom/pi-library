## 3. Approved Indications
- First‑aid antiseptic to help prevent the risk of infection in minor cuts, scrapes, and burns. [US-approved]

## 4. Dosage and Administration
### General dosing (adult)
- Apply topically: apply freely and rub briskly with hands or a towel.

### Dosing by indication
- For use as a first‑aid antiseptic for minor cuts, scrapes, and burns. Specific quantity per application is not specified in available sources.

### Administration instructions
- For topical external use only.
- Rub briskly after application.
- Use in a well‑ventilated area; fumes may be harmful.
- Flammable: keep away from sparks, heat, and open flame.
- Do not get into eyes or mucous membranes.
- Do not apply to irritated skin.

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
- **For external use only.**
- **Flammable** — keep away from sparks, heat, and flame.
- Use in a well‑ventilated area; fumes may be harmful.
- Do not get into eyes or mucous membranes; avoid ocular contact.
- Do not apply to irritated skin.
- Ask a doctor before use for: deep wounds, animal bites, and serious burns.
- Stop use and seek medical advice if excessive skin irritation develops.
- Keep out of reach of children.
- In case of accidental ingestion, seek professional assistance or contact a Poison Control Center immediately.
- Internal ingestion may produce serious gastric disturbances.
- Product is not a substitute for products containing ethyl (grain) alcohol; does not contain ethyl/grain alcohol.

### Monitoring requirements
- Monitor treated skin for excessive irritation; discontinue use and seek medical advice if irritation occurs.
- Monitor for inadvertent ingestion and manage as a poisoning exposure.

### Specific clinical precautions
- Avoid use near flame or heat sources due to flammability.
- Exercise caution in poorly ventilated settings due to vapour exposure.
