# Editorial Product Information: Iloprost

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following regulatory jurisdiction: **Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration)**. 
- Information regarding other jurisdictions (e.g., US, European, Canadian, Japanese) was not available in the provided source material; where relevant, the absence of data from other regions is noted or the information is presented based on the available Australian documentation.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical and regulatory data into a concise reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not replace the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory documents for legal prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Iloprost (as iloprost trometamol).
- **Chemical Class:** Synthetic prostacyclin analogue.
- **Brand Name(s):** **Ventavis** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in provided source.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Nebuliser solution; 10 microgram/mL. Each 2 mL ampoule contains 20 micrograms of iloprost [Australian].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Inhalation via nebuliser.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Bayer Australia Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Primary Pulmonary Hypertension (PPH):** Treatment of patients in moderate or severe stages of the disease [Australian].
- **Secondary Pulmonary Hypertension:**
    - Due to connective tissue disease or drug-induced (moderate to severe stages) [Australian].
    - Due to chronic pulmonary thromboembolism where surgery is not an option (moderate to severe stages) [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults)
- **Initial Dose:** 2.5 micrograms (delivered at the mouthpiece).
- **Maintenance Dose:** If well tolerated, increase to 5.0 micrograms and maintain. If 5.0 micrograms is poorly tolerated, reduce to 2.5 micrograms.
- **Frequency:** 6 to 9 times daily during waking hours, adjusted to individual clinical need.
- **Inhalation Duration:** Approximately 4 to 10 minutes, depending on the nebuliser and dose.

### Administration Instructions
- Administer only via a suitable nebuliser (see *Use with Nebulisers* below).
- Avoid contact with skin and eyes; oral ingestion must be avoided.
- Use a new ampoule for each session; transfer the full content to the nebuliser chamber immediately before use.
- Use only a mouthpiece; facial masks are to be avoided to prevent accidental exposure.

### Use with Nebulisers
- Nebulisers should be CE certified and work with compressed air.
- Must deliver 2.5 or 5.0 micrograms at the mouthpiece within 4–10 minutes.
- **Mass Median Aerodynamic Diameter (MMAD):** 2 to 4 micrometers.
- **Respirable Range:** 1 to 5 micrometers.
- **Note:** The HaloLite system used in clinical trials is not currently available in Australia [Australian].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known allergy to iloprost or any excipients.
- **Haemorrhagic Risk:** Conditions where platelet inhibition may increase bleeding risk (e.g., active peptic ulcers, trauma, intracranial haemorrhage).
- **Cardiac Conditions:**
    - Severe coronary heart disease or unstable angina.
    - Myocardial infarction within the previous six months.
    - Decompensated cardiac failure (unless under strict medical supervision).
    - Severe arrhythmias.
    - Suspected pulmonary congestion.
- **Cerebrovascular Events:** Stroke or transient ischaemic attack within the previous three months.
- **Pulmonary Venous Disease:** Pulmonary hypertension due to venous occlusive disease.
- **Valvular Defects:** Congenital or acquired defects with myocardial dysfunction not related to pulmonary hypertension.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Syncope:** Physicians should be alert to conditions or drugs that increase syncope risk. Syncope may also indicate disease progression or insufficient therapeutic effect.
- **Hypotension:** Monitor vital signs during initiation. Do **not** initiate in patients with a systolic arterial pressure below 85 mmHg.
- **Bronchospasm:** Risk is higher in patients with bronchial hyperreactivity. Use with caution in patients with COPD or severe asthma.
- **Pulmonary Oedema:** If signs of pulmonary oedema occur, consider the possibility of associated pulmonary veno-occlusive disease and discontinue treatment.
- **Rebound Effect:** The risk of rebound following treatment interruption is not formally excluded; monitor critically ill patients closely if therapy is stopped.
- **Environment:** To minimise accidental exposure, ensure rooms are well-ventilated and consider nebulisers with filters or inhalation-triggered systems.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Antihypertensives/Vasodilators:** Iloprost may potentate the effects of these agents; dose adjustments may be required.
- **Anticoagulants/Antiplatelets:** Use with heparin, coumarins, aspirin, NSAIDs, or phosphodiesterase inhibitors (e.g., sildenafil, milrinone) may increase bleeding risk due to enhanced platelet inhibition.
- **Glucocorticoids:** Animal data suggest the vasodilatory effect of iloprost is attenuated by glucocorticoids, though the clinical significance in humans is unknown.
- **Digoxin/t-PA:** No pharmacokinetic interactions observed with digoxin or co-administered t-PA in humans.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** **Category B3** [Australian]. Women with pulmonary hypertension should avoid pregnancy due to life-threatening risks. There are no adequate data in pregnant women. Animal studies show increased skeletal anomalies and embryofetal resorption. Use in pregnancy only after a careful risk-benefit evaluation.
- **Breastfeeding:** Not recommended. Animal studies show excretion into milk; human data are unavailable.
- **Fertility:** No impairment observed in rat studies at doses significantly higher than clinical exposure.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Use is **not recommended** in children and adolescents under 18 years due to limited experience [Australian].
- **Elderly Use:** No specific dose adjustments are provided in the source; however, clinical data in this population are limited.
- **Renal Impairment:**
    - **CrCl >30 mL/min:** No dose adjustment required.
    - **CrCl ≤30 mL/min:** Not studied in clinical trials. Based on IV data, elimination is reduced in patients requiring dialysis. Exercise caution; follow the same dosing schedule as hepatic impairment.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Elimination is reduced in hepatic dysfunction.
    - **Child-Pugh Class B or C:** Use with caution.
    - **Initial Dosing:** 2.5 micrograms with 3–4 hour intervals (max 6 doses per day). Titrate cautiously based on tolerability.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** A synthetic analogue of prostacyclin ($PGI_2$). It mediates direct vasodilation of the pulmonary arterial bed, reducing pulmonary artery pressure and vascular resistance while improving cardiac output and mixed venous oxygen saturation.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Peak serum concentrations (100–200 pg/mL) occur at the end of inhalation.
    - **Distribution:** Half-life is 5 to 25 minutes. Protein binding is approximately 60% (predominantly albumin).
    - **Metabolism:** Extensively metabolised via $\beta$-oxidation of the carboxyl side chain. The primary metabolite, tetranor-iloprost, is pharmacologically inactive.
    - **Elimination:** Total clearance is ~20 mL/kg/min. Recovery of radioactivity is 68% in urine and 12% in faeces.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Phase II (Study A00794):** An open-label randomized study (n=63) showed significant improvement in NYHA class and quality of life at three months when iloprost was added to conventional therapy.
- **Phase III (Study A02997):** A double-blind, placebo-controlled trial (n=203) in NYHA Class III/IV patients. The primary responder endpoint (combined improvement in exercise capacity, NYHA class, and lack of clinical deterioration) was met by 16.8% of the iloprost group vs 4.9% for placebo (p=0.007).
- **Secondary Endpoints:** Showed a median 20m increase in 6-minute walk distance (p=0.032) and significantly improved Mahler dyspnoea index scores.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store below 30°C [Australian].
- **Shelf Life:** Refer to packaging or the Australian Register of Therapeutic Goods (ARTG) [Australian].
- **Incompatibilities:** Must not be mixed with other medicinal products.
