# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine Injection

## Editorial Note
- **Jurisdictions included in this editorial:** US (FDA). 
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial synthesis of the official Product Information for **levocarnitine** injection. It is intended as a clinical reference for healthcare professionals to assist in the safe and effective use of the medication.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) or Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory documents for full prescribing details and legal requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN/Generic name).
- **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection, USP [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Indicated for the acute and chronic management of patients with inborn errors of metabolism leading to secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD undergoing hemodialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Metabolic Disorders)
- **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion [US].
- **Severe Metabolic Crisis:** A loading dose is often followed by an equivalent dose divided over the subsequent 24 hours. Dosing frequency should be every 3 to 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours [US].
- **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses typically range around 50 mg/kg, though higher doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilized based on clinical requirement [US].

### Dosing in ESRD on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
- **Administration:** Administer as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].
- **Dose Adjustment:** Adjustments should be guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Reductions (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy if levels are adequate [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) when diluted in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s solution in concentrations between 0.5 mg/mL and 8 mg/mL (in PVC bags) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Known Hypersensitivity:** Not specified in available sources as a formal contraindication, but severe reactions require discontinuation (see Warnings) [US].
- **General:** None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients on dialysis. These may occur within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and initiate appropriate medical care [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure disorders. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Monitoring:** For patients with metabolic disorders, weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L) is recommended [US].
- **Renal Function:** The safety/efficacy of high-dose oral levocarnitine in patients with severe renal impairment or ESRD has not been established due to potential accumulation of toxic metabolites (TMA and TMAO) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are categorised by frequency where data is available from clinical trials in ESRD patients.

### Common to Very Common Adverse Effects (>5%) [US]
- **General:** Injection site reaction (33–59%), pain (30%), flu syndrome (25%), headache (22%), infection (15%), abdominal pain (9%), fever (7%).
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (20%), hypotension (14%), tachycardia (6%).
- **Gastrointestinal:** Diarrhea (16%), vomiting (15%), nausea (8%).
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (23%), cough increase (9%), rhinitis (9%), dyspnea (7%).
- **Neurological:** Dizziness (13%), paresthesia (5%).

### Serious and Other Notable Effects
- **Neurological:** Seizures (post-marketing) [US].
- **Gastrointestinal:** Gastritis, transient nausea/vomiting, and body odor have been reported [US].
- **Metabolic:** Hypercalcemia (9%), hyperkalemia (6%) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have occurred with concurrent use. **Clinical Management:** Monitor INR levels in patients on warfarin therapy upon initiation of levocarnitine or after dose adjustments [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not specified by letter, but reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. No adequate, well-controlled studies exist in pregnant women. Use only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine is a normal component of human milk. Supplementation has not been specifically studied in nursing mothers. In dairy cows, milk concentrations increase after exogenous administration. Weigh the benefits of maternal therapy against the risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impairment in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing is based on mg/kg body weight (see Section 3). 
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    - **Dialysis:** Specifically indicated for use (see Section 3).
    - **Metabolite Accumulation:** Chronic high doses of oral levocarnitine in patients with severe renal compromise may lead to accumulation of trimethylamine (TMA) and trimethylamine-N-oxide (TMAO) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring carrier molecule required for the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, facilitating energy production via beta-oxidation [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** It promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids in patients with specific organic acidopathies by forming acylcarnitine esters which are rapidly excreted [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Following IV administration, approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours. The distribution half-life is ~0.585 hours; the terminal elimination half-life is ~17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** Clinical trials (N=130) using doses of 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg demonstrated significant increases in pre-dialysis (trough) plasma levocarnitine concentrations within 12 to 24 weeks. Prior to treatment, mean levels were ~20 µmol/L; following 20 mg/kg dosing for 8 weeks, mean troughs reached ~190 µmol/L [US].
- **Bioequivalence:** Levocarnitine tablets were found to be bioequivalent to the oral solution in healthy volunteers [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Precautions:** Avoid excessive heat and protect from freezing [US].
- **Handling:** Single-dose vials; discard any unused portion as the formulation contains no preservative [US].

- **United States:** Rx-only [US].
- **Australia:** Not specified in available sources.
