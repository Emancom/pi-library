# Editorial Product Information: Iobenguane I 123

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the **United States (FDA)** for **AdreView** (iobenguane I 123 injection).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of iobenguane I 123 for healthcare professionals, consolidating regulatory information into a clinically relevant format.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official PI or local regulatory guidelines required for prescribing or administering this radiopharmaceutical in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Iobenguane I 123 (also known as I 123 meta-iodobenzylguanidine sulfate).
- **Chemical Class:** Radiolabelled guanidino-analogue of norepinephrine.
- **Brand Name:** **AdreView** [US].
- **ATC Code:** V09IX01.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Sterile, pyrogen-free solution for intravenous injection. Supplied in a 10 mL glass vial containing a total volume of 5 mL. The strength is 74 MBq/mL (2 mCi/mL) at the time of calibration [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Medi-Physics Inc. (GE Healthcare) [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Pheochromocytoma and Neuroblastoma:** Indicated as an adjunct to other diagnostic tests for the detection of primary or metastatic pheochromocytoma or neuroblastoma [US].
- **Congestive Heart Failure (CHF):** Indicated for the scintigraphic assessment of myocardial sympathetic innervation (measurement of the heart-to-mediastinum [H/M] ratio) in patients with NYHA class II or III heart failure and LVEF ≤ 35%. It is used to identify patients with lower one- and two-year mortality risks, indicated by an H/M ratio ≥ 1.6 [US].
- **Limitations of Use:** In CHF patients, it has not been established for selecting therapeutic interventions, monitoring response to therapy, or identifying patients at high risk of death [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Recommended Dose:** 370 MBq (10 mCi) for patients ≥ 16 years of age [US].

### Pediatric Dosing
- **Patients < 16 years and ≥ 70 kg:** 370 MBq (10 mCi) [US].
- **Patients < 16 years and < 70 kg:** The dose should be scaled according to body weight (ranging from 1 mCi for 3 kg to 9.9 mCi for 68 kg) as per established pediatric task group schedules [US].

### Administration Instructions
- **Thyroid Blockage:** Administer thyroid blockade (e.g., Potassium Iodide Oral Solution, Lugol’s Solution, or potassium perchlorate) at least one hour before the dose to patients at risk of thyroid accumulation of I 123 [US].
- **Hydration:** Encourage hydration and frequent voiding for 48 hours post-administration to minimize bladder radiation [US].
- **Injection:** Administer as a slow intravenous injection over 1 to 2 minutes. A subsequent flush of 0.9% sodium chloride may be used to ensure full delivery [US].
- **Imaging:** For oncology, begin whole-body planar imaging at 24 ± 6 hours. For CHF, begin anterior planar imaging of the chest at 4 hours (± 10 minutes) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to iobenguane or iobenguane sulfate [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions have been reported. Anaphylactic and hypersensitivity treatment measures must be available prior to administration [US].
- **Benzyl Alcohol Risk:** The product contains benzyl alcohol (10.3 mg/mL), which is associated with "gasping syndrome" in neonates and low birth-weight infants [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Patients with severe renal impairment may experience increased radiation exposure and decreased image quality [US].
- **Sympathetic Nervous System Conditions:** Conditions like Parkinson’s disease or multiple system atrophy may decrease cardiac uptake independent of heart disease, potentially causing imaging errors [US].
- **Hypertension:** Iobenguane may stimulate the release of norepinephrine, potentially causing transient hypertension. Monitor pulse and blood pressure for 30 minutes post-injection [US].
- **Thyroid Accumulation:** Failure to block thyroid iodine uptake increases the long-term risk of thyroid neoplasia [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Frequency < 1.3%:** Dizziness, rash, pruritus, flushing, headache, and injection site hemorrhage [US].
- **Clinical Trial Observations (CHF):** Adverse reactions occurring at a frequency > 1% included injection site reactions (hematoma and bruising) [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Serious hypersensitivity reactions have been reported in postmarketing experience [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
Drugs that inhibit the norepinephrine transporter or deplete norepinephrine stores can decrease iobenguane uptake, leading to false-negative results. 
- **Required Discontinuation:** The following should be discontinued for at least 5 biological half-lives prior to administration [US]:
    - Tricyclic antidepressants (e.g., amitriptyline, imipramine).
    - Antihypertensives that deplete stores or inhibit reuptake (e.g., reserpine, labetalol).
    - Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitors (SSRIs).
    - Sympathomimetic amines (e.g., phenylephrine, ephedrine).
    - Cocaine.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not explicitly assigned, but states "May cause fetal harm"]. Radioactive iodine products cross the placenta and can impair fetal thyroid function. Assess pregnancy status before administration [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** I 123 is present in human milk. Advise lactating women to interrupt breastfeeding and pump/discard milk for at least 6 days post-administration to minimize infant radiation exposure [US].
- **Fertility:** Long-term animal studies on fertility have not been conducted [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Pediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy established for neuroblastoma in patients aged 1 month to 16 years. Not established for patients < 1 month of age or for heart failure in any pediatric patient [US].
- **Geriatric Use:** No overall differences in safety/efficacy compared to younger patients, but increased sensitivity in some older individuals and decreased renal function should be considered for dose selection [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Iobenguane is cleared by glomerular filtration and is not dialyzable. Delayed clearance in severe impairment increases radiation dose [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Iobenguane is a norepinephrine (NE) analogue. It is taken up by the NE transporter into adrenergic nerve terminals and stored in presynaptic vesicles. It accumulates in adrenergically innervated tissues (adrenals, heart, etc.) and tumors derived from the neural crest [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Expected to have no pharmacodynamic effect at the diagnostic dose [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** 
    - **Distribution:** Rapidly cleared from blood; prolonged retention in the heart and adrenal medulla [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily excreted unaltered; < 10% as m-iodohippuric acid (MIHA) [US].
    - **Elimination:** 70–90% recovered in urine within 4 days [US]. Physical half-life of I 123 is 13.2 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Oncology:** A multinational trial of 251 subjects showed sensitivity between 0.77 and 0.80 and specificity between 0.69 and 0.77 for detecting neuroblastoma/pheochromocytoma [US].
- **Heart Failure:** Two trials (964 patients) demonstrated that patients with an H/M ratio ≥ 1.6 had significantly lower one- and two-year mortality rates (1.0% and 3.3% respectively) compared to those with lower ratios [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C–25°C (68°F–77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C–30°C [US].
- **Handling:** Store within the original lead container or equivalent radiation shielding. Does not contain a preservative [US].
- **Shelf Life:** Use within the expiration date and time stated on the label (expires 36 hours after calibration time) [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Prescription Only (Rx Only) [US].
