# Editorial Product Information: Imipenem, Cilastatin, and Relebactam

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of **Recarbrio** for use by healthcare professionals.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis intended for professional reference and is not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific PI when prescribing. At the time of this writing, only the United States PI was provided for synthesis.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** Imipenem (penem antibacterial), Cilastatin (renal dehydropeptidase inhibitor), and Relebactam (beta-lactamase inhibitor).
- **Brand Name:** **Recarbrio** [US].
- **ATC Code:** J01DH56 (Imipenem, cilastatin and relebactam).
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Supplied as a sterile powder for constitution in a single-dose vial containing 1.25 grams total: imipenem 500 mg (anhydrous equivalent), cilastatin 500 mg (free acid equivalent), and relebactam 250 mg (anhydrous equivalent) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Merck Sharp & Dohme LLC [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
**Recarbrio** is indicated in adult and pediatric patients (weighing at least 2 kg) for the treatment of infections caused by susceptible gram-negative microorganisms [US]:
- **Hospital-acquired Bacterial Pneumonia and Ventilator-associated Bacterial Pneumonia (HABP/VABP).**
- **Complicated Urinary Tract Infections (cUTI),** including pyelonephritis, in patients with limited or no alternative treatment options.
- **Complicated Intra-abdominal Infections (cIAI)** in patients with limited or no alternative treatment options.

*Note: Approval for cUTI and cIAI is based on limited clinical safety and efficacy data [US].*

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Adult Dosing (CLcr $\ge$ 90 mL/min)
- **Standard Dose:** 1.25 grams (imipenem 500 mg, cilastatin 500 mg, relebactam 250 mg) via IV infusion every 6 hours [US].
- **Duration:** 4 to 14 days, guided by infection severity and clinical response [US].
- **Infusion Duration:** 30 minutes [US].

### Pediatric Dosing (Weight $\ge$ 2 kg)
Dosing is based on age and weight [US]:
- **Birth to < 3 months ($\ge$ 2 kg):** 37.5 mg/kg every 8 hours (60-minute infusion).
- **3 months to < 18 years (< 30 kg):** 37.5 mg/kg every 6 hours (60-minute infusion).
- **3 months to < 18 years ($\ge$ 30 kg):** 1.25 grams every 6 hours (30-minute infusion).

### Administration Instructions
- **Constitution:** Must be constituted and further diluted using aseptic technique. Compatible diluents include 0.9% Sodium Chloride, 5% Dextrose, or 5% Dextrose with varying concentrations of Sodium Chloride [US].
- **Preparation for Reduced Doses:** For patients requiring dose reduction (e.g., renal impairment), a 100 mL bag is prepared, and a specific volume is withdrawn and discarded to achieve the required dose [US].
- **Compatibility:** Physically incompatible with propofol in D5W or NS [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Severe Hypersensitivity:** History of known severe hypersensitivity (e.g., anaphylaxis) to any component of **Recarbrio** [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious and occasionally fatal anaphylactic reactions have been reported with beta-lactams. Inquire about previous reactions to carbapenems, penicillins, or cephalosporins before initiation [US].
- **Seizures and CNS Adverse Reactions:** Reported with imipenem/cilastatin. Risks are higher when recommended dosages are exceeded, in patients with CNS disorders (e.g., brain lesions, history of seizures), and in those with compromised renal function [US].
- **Valproic Acid Interaction:** Concomitant use with carbapenems may significantly reduce valproic acid serum concentrations, increasing the risk of breakthrough seizures. Avoid this combination [US].
- **Clostridioides difficile-Associated Diarrhea (CDAD):** May range from mild diarrhea to fatal colitis. Evaluate if diarrhea occurs during or after treatment [US].
- **Development of Drug-Resistant Bacteria:** Use should be limited to infections proven or strongly suspected to be caused by susceptible bacteria to maintain effectiveness [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Frequency in Adult Patients
- **HABP/VABP ($\ge$ 5%):** AST increased (11.7%), ALT increased (9.8%), anemia (10.5%), diarrhea (7.9%), hypokalemia (7.9%), hyponatremia (6.4%) [US].
- **cUTI and cIAI ($\ge$ 2%):** Diarrhea, nausea, headache, vomiting, ALT/AST increased, phlebitis/infusion site reactions, pyrexia, and hypertension [US].

### Frequency in Pediatric Patients
- **All Indications (> 3%):** Vomiting (15%), diarrhea (9%), nausea (7%), headache (6%), phlebitis/infusion site reactions (5%), and rash (4%) [US].

### Serious Adverse Effects
- Seizures, hepatic failure, jaundice, and agranulocytosis (associated with the imipenem/cilastatin component) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Ganciclovir:** Reports of generalized seizures when used concomitantly with imipenem/cilastatin. Avoid use unless benefits outweigh risks [US].
- **Valproic Acid / Divalproex Sodium:** Carbapenems reduce serum valproic acid levels. Avoid concomitant use; if necessary, use supplemental anticonvulsants [US].
- **Probenecid:** While not significantly altering relebactam PK, it is an OAT3 inhibitor; however, clinical studies showed no significant drug-drug interaction with **Recarbrio** components [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: No category assigned under PLLR]. There are insufficient human data. Animal studies in monkeys with imipenem/cilastatin showed embryonic loss. Relebactam studies in mice showed increased incidences of cleft palate and skeletal malformations at 6 times the human exposure [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** No data on the presence of the drug in human milk or effects on the infant. Relebactam is excreted in the milk of lactating rats [US].
- **Fertility:** Relebactam did not impair fertility or reproductive performance in rats at exposures 7–8 times the MRHD [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Approved for patients $\ge$ 2 kg. Not recommended for those < 37 weeks post-menstrual age or < 30 kg with renal impairment [US].
- **Elderly Use:** No overall differences in safety or efficacy were observed, but greater sensitivity in some older individuals cannot be ruled out. Dose should be adjusted based on renal function [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Dose reduction is required for adults with CLcr < 90 mL/min and pediatrics ($\ge$ 30 kg) with eGFR < 90 mL/min/1.73m² [US].
  - **Adults CLcr 15–29 mL/min:** 0.5 g every 6 hours [US].
  - **Adults CLcr < 15 mL/min:** Not recommended unless hemodialysis is initiated within 48 hours [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not expected to affect clearance as the components are primarily renally excreted [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** 
    - **Imipenem:** Inhibits bacterial cell wall synthesis by binding to penicillin-binding proteins (PBPs).
    - **Cilastatin:** Inhibits the renal metabolism of imipenem by dehydropeptidase-I.
    - **Relebactam:** A diazabicyclooctane beta-lactamase inhibitor that protects imipenem from degradation by certain serine beta-lactamases (e.g., KPC, AmpC) [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Plasma protein binding is approximately 20% (imipenem), 40% (cilastatin), and 22% (relebactam) [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Imipenem is metabolized renally; relebactam is minimally metabolized [US].
    - **Elimination:** Primarily renal excretion. Mean half-life is approximately 1 hour for imipenem and 1.2 hours for relebactam [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **HABP/VABP (Trial 1):** Compared **Recarbrio** (n=266) to piperacillin/tazobactam (n=269). Day 28 all-cause mortality was 15.9% for **Recarbrio** vs 21.3% for the comparator [US].
- **cUTI/cIAI (Trials 2 & 3):** Supported efficacy in adults based on previous imipenem/cilastatin data and the contribution of relebactam established in vitro and in animal models [US].
- **Pediatric (Trial 4):** Open-label, active-controlled trial (n=85 receiving **Recarbrio**) showed comparable safety and efficacy across pediatric age groups from birth to < 18 years [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vials:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); keep in the carton to protect from light [US].
- **Constituted/Diluted Solution:** Maintains potency for 2 hours at room temperature (up to 30°C) or 24 hours under refrigeration (2°C to 8°C). Do not freeze [US].
