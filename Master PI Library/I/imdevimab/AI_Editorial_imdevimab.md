# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial is based on official Product Information from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Note:** At the time of this synthesis, only the United States Product Information was available for review. Information regarding other jurisdictions (e.g., Australian, European, Canadian) was not provided in the source material and is therefore marked as "Not specified in available sources" where applicable.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. It is a synthesis of clinical data and is not a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection [United States].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL) [United States].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous (IV).
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [United States].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** For the acute and chronic management of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [United States].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance haemodialysis [United States].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg. This may be administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or via infusion. 
    - In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose is often administered, followed by equivalent doses every 3 or 4 hours (and no less than every 6 hours) over the subsequent 24 hours.
    - Subsequent daily maintenance doses are typically 50 mg/kg, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilised.
- **ESRD Patients on Haemodialysis:** The recommended starting dose is 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight). 
    - Administration should occur as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
    - Dosage adjustments may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy, guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations.

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter or discolouration before administration.
- **Compatibility:** Stable and compatible when diluted in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations between 0.5 mg/mL and 8.0 mg/mL. Diluted solutions remain stable for up to 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags.

### Missed Dose Instructions
- Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known:** There are no listed contraindications in the available source [United States].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None.
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal oedema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. These may occur within minutes of administration. If a reaction occurs, the drug must be discontinued and appropriate medical intervention initiated.
- **Monitoring Requirements:** 
    - **Plasma Concentrations:** Monitoring of plasma carnitine levels is recommended (normal range 35 to 60 µmol/L). In metabolic disorders, monitoring should include blood chemistry and vital signs weekly or monthly.
    - **ESRD Monitoring:** Trough (pre-dialysis) levels should be monitored to guide downward dose adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) if levels exceed normal ranges.
- **Specific Clinical Precautions:** 
    - **Renal Insufficiency (Oral vs. IV):** While IV levocarnitine is used in ESRD, high doses of *oral* levocarnitine have not been evaluated in renal impairment and may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [United States].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Common (1–10% or higher):** 
    - **Gastrointestinal:** Nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain, diarrhoea, and gastritis.
    - **General:** Body odour (transient), headache, fever, and injection site reactions.
- **Cardiovascular (ESRD Population):** Hypertension (20%), hypotension (14%), tachycardia, and chest pain [United States].
- **Neurological:** Dizziness, paraesthesia, and insomnia.
- **Post-marketing Experience (Serious):** 
    - **Seizures:** Reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed.
    - **Hypersensitivity:** Anaphylaxis and respiratory distress.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalised Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** It is recommended to monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after any dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin anticoagulation [United States].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not assigned a lettered category in the current PI, but animal reproductive studies (rats/rabbits) at 3.8 times the human dose showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm.
    - **Recommendation:** Should be used during pregnancy only if clearly needed [United States].
- **Breastfeeding:** 
    - Levocarnitine is a normal component of human milk. Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied.
    - **Recommendation:** The benefits to the mother should be weighed against the potential risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant. Consideration may be given to discontinuing nursing or the drug.
- **Fertility:** Animal studies revealed no evidence of impaired fertility.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Use in children is supported by evidence for the treatment of inborn errors of metabolism. Dosage is based on body weight (see Section 3).
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in available sources regarding dose adjustments unique to the geriatric population.
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    - **IV Route:** Indicated for use in ESRD on dialysis. 
    - **Oral Route:** Safety and efficacy of high-dose oral levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function are not established due to metabolite accumulation.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In ESRD, it alleviates the metabolic consequences of carnitine loss due to dialysis and reduced renal synthesis.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not protein-bound.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites are trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and gamma-butyrobetaine (feces).
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. Terminal elimination half-life is approximately 17.4 hours.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week trial of 12 ESRD patients (20 mg/kg post-dialysis), mean pre-dialysis plasma carnitine increased from ~20 µmol/L to 190 ± 55 µmol/L after 8 weeks.
- **Dose-Ranging Studies:** Trials using 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses showed dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks.
- **Note:** While efficacy in increasing carnitine concentrations is proven, the impact on specific clinical outcomes in ESRD patients has not been definitively determined.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C.
- **Handling:** Avoid excessive heat. Protect from freezing.
- **Shelf Life:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as the formulation contains no preservatives.
