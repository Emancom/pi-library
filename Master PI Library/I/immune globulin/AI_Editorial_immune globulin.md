# Editorial Product Information: YIMMUGO (immune globulin intravenous, human - dira)

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial Product Information (PI) has been developed using official documentation from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of **YIMMUGO** for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises clinical data, regulatory warnings, and administration guidelines into a single reference.
- **Note:** This synthesis is an editorial resource and does not replace the official, legally binding Product Information required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Human Immunoglobulin G (IgG); Plasma derivative.
- **Brand Name:** **YIMMUGO** [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Solution for intravenous injection (10% IgG; 100 mg/mL). Available in single-dose vials of:
    - 5 g in 50 mL
    - 10 g in 100 mL
    - 20 g in 200 mL
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Manufactured by Biotest AG (Germany); Distributed by Kedrion Biopharma Inc [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Primary Humoral Immunodeficiency (PI):** Indicated for the treatment of PI in adults and paediatric patients 2 years of age and older. This includes, but is not limited to:
    - Common variable immunodeficiency (CVID)
    - Congenital agammaglobulinemia
    - X-linked agammaglobulinemia (XLA)
    - Wiskott-Aldrich syndrome
    - Severe combined immunodeficiencies (SCID) [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric $\ge$ 2 years)
- **Dose Range:** 300 mg/kg to 800 mg/kg (3–8 mL/kg) administered every 3 to 4 weeks.
- **Adjustment:** Dosing should be adjusted over time based on the individual patient’s clinical response and reaching desired trough IgG levels.

### Administration and Titration
**YIMMUGO** must be administered intravenously. The infusion rate must be titrated as follows:

| Infusion Number | Initial Rate (first 30 min) | Maintenance Rate (if tolerated) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **First Infusion** | 0.5 mg/kg/min (0.005 mL/kg/min) | Gradually increase every 30 min up to **3.0 mg/kg/min** (0.03 mL/kg/min) |
| **Subsequent Infusions** | 0.5 mg/kg/min (0.005 mL/kg/min) | Gradually increase up to **13 mg/kg/min** (0.13 mL/kg/min) |

### Special Considerations
- **Measles Exposure:** If a patient is exposed to measles, a one-time dose of 400 mg/kg may be administered within 6 days of exposure. For patients at high risk of future exposure currently receiving <530 mg/kg every 3–4 weeks, consider increasing the maintenance dose to at least 530 mg/kg [US].
- **Preparation:** Solution should be at room or body temperature. Inspect for clarity; do not use if turbid or discolored. Do not shake.
- **Mixing:** Do not mix with other intravenous medications or other IGIV products. Do not dilute.

## 4. Contraindications
- Patients with a history of anaphylactic or severe systemic reactions to human immune globulin.
- IgA-deficient patients who possess antibodies against IgA and have a history of hypersensitivity [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warning [US]
- **Thrombosis:** May occur with IGIV products. Risk factors include advanced age, prolonged immobilisation, hypercoagulable states, history of thrombosis, estrogen use, indwelling catheters, and cardiovascular risk factors. 
- **Renal Dysfunction and Acute Renal Failure:** May occur, particularly with products containing sucrose (Note: **YIMMUGO** is sucrose-free). Ensure adequate hydration before administration.
- **Management:** For at-risk patients, use the minimum dose and infusion rate practicable.

### General Warnings
- **Hypersensitivity:** Severe reactions, including anaphylaxis, can occur. Epinephrine must be available.
- **Hemolysis:** IGIV products can contain blood group antibodies (hemolysins). Monitor for clinical signs of hemolysis and falling haematocrit/hemoglobin levels.
- **Aseptic Meningitis Syndrome (AMS):** Reported infrequently, typically within hours to 2 days following treatment. More common with high doses (2 g/kg).
- **Transfusion-Related Acute Lung Injury (TRALI):** Characterised by severe respiratory distress and pulmonary edema, usually within 1–6 hours post-infusion.
- **Hyperproteinemia/Viscosity:** Can lead to pseudohyponatremia. It is vital to distinguish true hyponatremia from pseudohyponatremia to avoid inappropriate treatment.
- **Transmissible Infectious Agents:** As a human plasma product, it carries a theoretical risk of transmitting viruses or the Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease (CJD) agent.

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions (AR) are defined as those occurring during or within 72 hours of infusion.

### Frequency in Clinical Trials (PI)
| Adverse Reaction | Frequency (Patients) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Headache** | 19% |
| **Upper respiratory tract infections** | 12% |
| **Fatigue** | 8% |
| **Nausea** | 6% |
| **Increased blood pressure** | 6% |

### Serious and Postmarketing Adverse Effects
- **Serious:** Anaphylactic reaction and severe neutropenia (reported in trials) [US].
- **Postmarketing (Class Effect):** Apnea, ARDS, cardiac arrest, thromboembolism, coma, seizures, Stevens-Johnson syndrome, pancytopenia, and hepatic dysfunction.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Live Virus Vaccines:** Immunoglobulin administration may impair the efficacy of live attenuated vaccines (measles, mumps, rubella, varicella) for a period of up to 3 months or longer.
- **Loop Diuretics:** Concomitant use should be avoided as it may increase blood viscosity, potentially elevating the risk of thromboembolic events.
- **Laboratory Testing Interference:** Passive transfer of antibodies may result in false-positive serological tests or a positive Coombs’ test.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not assigned under current labeling rules]. No human data available. IgG is known to cross the placenta, primarily after 30 weeks of gestation. Use only if clearly needed.
- **Breastfeeding:** IgG is excreted in human milk. Benefits of breastfeeding should be weighed against the clinical need for the mother and potential risks to the infant.
- **Fertility:** Animal reproduction studies have not been conducted.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Evaluated in 18 patients (ages 2–16). Dosing requirements were similar to adults. Safety/efficacy in children <2 years has not been established.
- **Geriatric Use:** In patients >65 years, use the minimum infusion rate and dose practicable due to higher risks of thrombosis and renal insufficiency.
- **Renal Impairment:** Monitor renal function and urine output closely. In patients at risk of acute renal failure, use the minimum rate practicable.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specifically addressed in available sources beyond postmarketing reports of hepatic dysfunction as a class effect.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Provides a broad spectrum of opsonizing and neutralizing IgG antibodies against various pathogens and toxins. It may also exert immunomodulatory effects.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Restores abnormally low IgG levels to the normal range. Contains IgG subclasses in proportions similar to native human plasma.
- **Pharmacokinetics:** 
    - **Half-life:** Approximately 24.8 to 29.9 days in adults [US].
    - **Distribution:** Similar to normal plasma.
    - **Metabolism/Elimination:** IgG and IgG-complexes are degraded by cells of the reticuloendothelial system.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Trial 991 (NCT02810444):** An open-label, prospective trial of 67 patients with PI.
- **Primary Outcome:** The rate of serious bacterial infections (SBI).
- **Results:** The annualized rate of acute SBIs was 0.07 per person-year, meeting the success criteria of <1 SBI per person-year. The most common infections were bacterial pneumonia [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F). 
- **Room Temperature Excursion:** May be stored at room temperature (up to 25°C / 77°F) for a single period not exceeding 12 months. Do not return to the refrigerator once removed.
- **Handling:** Protect from light by keeping in the original carton. Do not freeze. Do not use if the solution has been frozen. 
- **Shelf Life:** Use promptly once the vial is entered.
