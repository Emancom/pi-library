# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial synthesis is derived from the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: US (FDA).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical and regulatory data into a single reference.
- **Note:** This synthesis is an editorial interpretation and does not replace the official, jurisdiction-specific PI required for prescribing or dispensing in any specific region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt. 
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative (carrier molecule for fatty acid transport).
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter Healthcare Corporation) [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - **Injection:** 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) single-dose vial [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Indicated for the acute and chronic management of patients with inborn metabolic errors resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    - The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg. 
    - Administration should be via slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion. 
    - **Acute Crisis:** A loading dose is often administered, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours. Dosing frequency should be every 3 to 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours [US].
    - **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses are typically 50 mg/kg, though higher doses up to 300 mg/kg have been administered based on clinical requirement [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:** 
    - **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight).
    - **Timing:** Administer as a 2 to 3-minute slow bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].
    - **Dose Adjustment:** Adjustments should be guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine levels. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected for particulate matter and discoloration prior to use [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations between 0.5 mg/mL and 8.0 mg/mL [US].
- Discard any unused portion as the formulation contains no preservatives [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known based on available source documentation [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in patients with ESRD on dialysis. Some reactions occur within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, therapy must be discontinued immediately and appropriate medical treatment initiated [US].
- **Seizures:** Levocarnitine (oral or intravenous) has been associated with reports of seizures. In patients with a pre-existing seizure disorder, an increase in the frequency or severity of seizures has been observed [US].
- **Monitoring:** 
    - **General:** Weekly and monthly monitoring should include blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations [US].
    - **Target Levels:** Plasma free carnitine concentrations should ideally be maintained between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].
- **Oral Formulation Caution:** Safety and efficacy of oral levocarnitine have not been evaluated in renal insufficiency. Chronic high doses of oral levocarnitine in ESRD or severe renal impairment may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Common Effects:** Transient nausea and vomiting have been observed. Other less frequent reactions include body odor and gastritis [US].
- **Clinical Trial Data (ESRD/Hemodialysis):** In double-blind, placebo-controlled trials, the following adverse events occurred at a frequency of ≥5% regardless of causality:
    - **Body as a Whole:** Abdominal pain, accidental injury, asthenia, chest pain, fever, flu syndrome, headache, infection, injection site reaction, and pain [US].
    - **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (18–21%), hypotension (3–19%), tachycardia, and palpitations [US].
    - **Digestive:** Diarrhea (9–35%), vomiting (9–21%), and nausea (5–12%) [US].
    - **Neurological:** Dizziness (10–18%), insomnia, and paresthesia [US].
    - **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (15–27%), cough, and dyspnea [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Anaphylaxis, bronchospasm, and laryngeal edema [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in the International Normalized Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin. Monitoring of INR is recommended upon initiation or dose adjustment of levocarnitine in patients receiving anticoagulant therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Category:** Not explicitly assigned a letter category in the current source, but reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm [US].
    - **Recommendation:** Should be used during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk concentrations of carnitine following exogenous administration. The risks of excess carnitine intake to the infant must be weighed against the benefit of supplementation to the mother [US].
- **Fertility:** Mutagenicity tests (Salmonella, etc.) were negative. Long-term carcinogenicity studies have not been performed [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders follows the 50 mg/kg weight-based recommendation [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically detailed in provided sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Specifically indicated for ESRD patients on dialysis (see Section 3). Caution is advised regarding the accumulation of metabolites if the **oral** form is used in this population [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production [US]. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In ESRD patients, levocarnitine injection increases plasma carnitine concentrations. It may alleviate metabolic abnormalities associated with the accumulation of toxic organic acids [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin. Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. Mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Efficacy:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients receiving 20 mg/kg IV three times weekly post-dialysis showed that trough levels increased from a baseline of ~21 µmol/L to ~190 µmol/L at 8 weeks. 
- **Dose-Ranging Studies:** Trials using 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses demonstrated dose-dependent increases in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [US].
- **Clinical Outcomes Note:** While IV levocarnitine increases carnitine concentrations in ESRD, its effects on specific clinical outcomes (e.g., malaise, muscle weakness) have not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store between 20°C and 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Protection:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat [US].
- **Vial Handling:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Prescription Only (Rx Only) [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources.
