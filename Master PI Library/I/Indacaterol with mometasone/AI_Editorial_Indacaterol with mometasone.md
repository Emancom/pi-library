# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical data into a single, structured reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official PI or local regulatory guidelines required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule/Amino acid derivative.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Sterile solution for injection: 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous (IV). 
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency (Inborn Errors):** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that lead to secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance hemodialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    - **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg administered as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) or via infusion [US].
    - **Initial Management:** In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose is often administered, followed by equivalent doses every 3 to 4 hours (and no less than every 6 hours) over the subsequent 24 hours [US].
    - **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses are typically 50 mg/kg, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilised based on clinical requirement [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - **Recommended Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
    - **Administration:** Administer as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter or discoloration prior to use [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations between 0.5 mg/mL and 8.0 mg/mL [US].

### Titration and Monitoring
- **Metabolic Disorders:** Monitoring should include blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L) [US].
- **ESRD:** Initiation may be prompted by trough (pre-dialysis) levels below 40–50 µmol/L. Downward dose adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be implemented as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough concentrations [US].

### Missed Dose
- Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known:** There are no listed contraindications in the current US provided documentation [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm have occurred, often within minutes of administration. These are most frequently reported in ESRD patients on dialysis. If a reaction occurs, discontinue immediately and consider the risks/benefits before re-administration [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Levocarnitine has been associated with reports of seizures in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In those with a history, there may be an increase in seizure frequency or severity [US].
- **Oral Formulation Caution:** The safety and efficacy of oral levocarnitine have not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency. High oral doses in patients with severely compromised renal function or ESRD may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites, trimethylamine (TMA) and trimethylamine-N-oxide (TMAO) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Very Common (>10%)
- **Body as a Whole:** Chest pain (12%), Flu syndrome (25%), Headache (22%), Injection site reaction (33%), Pain (30%).
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (20%), Hypotension (14%).
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea (16%), Vomiting (15%).
- **Nervous:** Dizziness (13%).
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (23%).

### Common (1–10%)
- **Digestive:** Abdominal pain (9%), Nausea (8%), Constipation (3%), Dyspepsia (5%).
- **Metabolic:** Hypercalcemia (9%), Hyperkalemia (6%), Hypervolemia (5%).
- **Musculoskeletal:** Leg cramps (4%).
- **Respiratory:** Cough increase (9%), Dyspnea (7%), Rhinitis (9%).
- **Special Senses:** Amblyopia (3%), Taste perversion (3%).

### Serious Adverse Effects
- **Anaphylaxis/Hypersensitivity:** [US]
- **Seizures:** Reported in both patient populations with and without prior history [US].
- **Body Odour:** Transient nausea, vomiting, and distinct body odour have been observed with carnitine supplementation [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** It is recommended to monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after any dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not explicitly assigned, but clinical text provided]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (based on surface area) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Specific studies in nursing mothers have not been conducted. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk carnitine levels after administration. Consider weighing the risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant against the benefits of supplementation for the mother; discontinuation of nursing or the drug may be necessary [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing recommendations for metabolic disorders and ESRD apply to paediatric patients; however, specific age-range limitations are not detailed in the provided source [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** IV levocarnitine is indicated for use in ESRD patients. However, high doses of the **oral** formulation should be avoided in severe renal impairment due to metabolite accumulation (TMA/TMAO) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring carrier molecule required for mammalian energy metabolism. it facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Carnitine promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids in patients with metabolic defects by forming acylcarnitine esters, which are rapidly excreted [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not protein-bound (including albumin). Follows a two-compartment model after IV bolus [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites are trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and [³H]-γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours. Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours; terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Trough Levels:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients receiving 20 mg/kg IV three times weekly showed that trough levels increased from a baseline of 21.3 ± 7.7 µmol/L to 190 ± 55 µmol/L after 8 weeks [US].
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** In a study comparing 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses, mean trough levels at 24 weeks were 148 ± 50, 240 ± 60, and 456 ± 162 µmol/L, respectively [US].
- **Clinical Outcomes:** While IV levocarnitine increases carnitine concentrations in ESRD patients, the impact on specific signs, symptoms, or clinical outcomes in this population has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Conditions:** Protect from freezing and avoid excessive heat [US].
- **Handling:** Single-dose vials; discard any unused portion as the formulation contains no preservatives [US].
