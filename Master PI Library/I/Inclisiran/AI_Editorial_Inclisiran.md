# Editorial Product Information: Inclisiran

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) document from the following jurisdiction: **Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of inclisiran for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises clinical, pharmacological, and regulatory data into a single reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI or local prescribing guidelines. Clinicians should consult the current official PI in their specific jurisdiction before prescribing.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Inclisiran (as inclisiran sodium).
- **Chemical Class:** Cholesterol-lowering double-stranded small interfering ribonucleic acid (siRNA) conjugated with triantennary N-acetylgalactosamine (GalNAc).
- **Brand Name:** **Leqvio** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** C10AX16.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Solution for injection in a pre-filled syringe. Each 1.5 mL syringe contains 284 mg of inclisiran (equivalent to 300 mg inclisiran sodium) [Australian].
- **Route of Administration:** Subcutaneous (SC) injection.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Novartis Pharmaceuticals Australia Pty Limited [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Hypercholesterolaemia and Mixed Dyslipidaemia:** Indicated as an adjunct to diet and exercise to reduce low-density lipoprotein cholesterol (LDL-C) in adults with heterozygous familial hypercholesterolaemia (HeFH), atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease (ASCVD), or those at high risk of a cardiovascular event [Australian].
- **Combination Therapy:** For use in combination with a statin, or a statin with other lipid-lowering therapies, in patients unable to reach LDL-C goals with maximally tolerated statin doses [Australian].
- **Monotherapy or Statin-Intolerant:** For use alone or in combination with other lipid-lowering therapies in patients who are statin-intolerant [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adults):** The recommended dose is 284 mg administered as a single subcutaneous injection.
- **Dosing Schedule:** Initial dose, followed by a second dose at 3 months, and then subsequent doses every 6 months thereafter [Australian].
- **Administration Instructions:** 
    - Must be administered by a healthcare professional.
    - Inject into the abdomen; alternative sites include the upper arm or thigh.
    - Avoid areas of active skin disease or injury (e.g., sunburn, rashes, inflammation, or infections).
    - Inspect visually for particulate matter; use only if the solution is clear and colourless to pale yellow.
- **Missed Dose Instructions:**
    - **Delayed by <3 months:** Administer the dose and continue the original dosing schedule.
    - **Delayed by >3 months:** Restart the dosing schedule (initial dose, 3 months later, then every 6 months).
- **Transitioning from PCSK9 Inhibitor Monoclonal Antibodies:** Administer the last dose of the monoclonal antibody, then wait until the next scheduled date for that therapy to administer the first dose of inclisiran [Australian].

## 4. Contraindications
- Hypersensitivity to the active substance or any of the excipients (water for injection, sodium hydroxide, and phosphoric acid) [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Cardiovascular Morbidity and Mortality:** The specific effect of inclisiran on cardiovascular outcomes has not yet been determined [Australian].
- **General Precautions:** Not recommended for use in patients with end-stage renal disease (CrCL < 15 mL/min) or severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh Class C) due to lack of study data [Australian].
- **Injection Site Inspection:** Do not inject into areas of active skin injury or inflammation.

## 6. Adverse Effects
The safety profile was evaluated in Phase III trials involving 3,655 patients, with the majority of adverse events being mild and unrelated to the drug.

| Frequency | Adverse Event |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common** (>10%) | Diabetes mellitus (11.6% vs 11.4% placebo) |
| **Common** (1–10%) | Injection site reactions (8.2%), Nasopharyngitis (7.6%), Arthralgia (5.0%), Back pain (4.5%), Urinary tract infection (4.4%), Bronchitis (4.3%), Diarrhea (3.9%), Cough (3.3%), Pain in extremity (3.3%), Dyspnea (3.2%), Dizziness (3.2%), Headache (3.2%), Angina pectoris (3.2%) |
| **Unknown** (Post-marketing) | Hypersensitivity (anaphylactic reaction, angioedema, rash, urticaria, pruritus) |

- **Injection Site Reactions:** These included pain, erythema, and rash. Most were mild to moderate, transient, and resolved without sequelae. Only 0.2% of patients discontinued treatment due to these reactions [Australian].
- **Immunogenicity:** Anti-drug antibodies (ADA) were detected in 4.9% of patients during 18 months of treatment, but no clinically significant differences in efficacy or safety were observed in ADA-positive patients [Australian].
- **Laboratory Values:** Asymptomatic elevations in serum hepatic transaminases (ALT/AST) between 1x and 3x the upper limit of normal (ULN) were more frequent with inclisiran than placebo, but did not progress to clinically relevant thresholds (>3x ULN) [Australian].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Inclisiran is not a substrate, inhibitor, or inducer of cytochrome P450 enzymes or common drug transporters (e.g., P-gp, BCRP, OATP1B1). Clinically significant interactions with other medications are not expected [Australian].
- **Statin Interactions:** No meaningful interactions were observed with atorvastatin, rosuvastatin, or other statins [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** **Category B1** [Australian]. There are no data on use in pregnant women. Animal studies showed no evidence of embryofetal harm at doses up to 39-fold the MRHD. As a precaution, avoid use during pregnancy.
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if inclisiran is excreted in human milk. Although animal data showed presence in rat milk, no systemic absorption occurred in suckling pups. A risk to the nursing child cannot be excluded; the benefit of breastfeeding vs. therapy for the mother must be weighed [Australian].
- **Fertility:** No human data available. Animal studies showed no effect on male or female fertility [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy in patients under 18 years of age have not been established [Australian].
- **Elderly Use:** No dose adjustment is required for patients aged 65 years and older [Australian].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment is necessary for mild, moderate, or severe renal impairment. However, use with caution in severe impairment due to limited experience. Not recommended in end-stage renal disease (CrCL < 15 mL/min) [Australian].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment is necessary for mild (Child-Pugh A) or moderate (Child-Pugh B) impairment. Not recommended for use in severe (Child-Pugh C) hepatic impairment [Australian].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Inclisiran utilizes the RNA interference (RNAi) mechanism. It is delivered to hepatocytes via GalNAc conjugation, where it directs the catalytic breakdown of mRNA for proprotein convertase subtilisin/kexin type 9 (PCSK9). This increases LDL receptor recycling and expression on the hepatocyte surface, enhancing LDL-C uptake and lowering circulating levels [Australian].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** LDL-C reduction is apparent within 14 days. Mean reductions of 49–51% are observed 30–60 days post-dose. No QTc prolongation was observed at supratherapeutic doses (3x MRHD) [Australian].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Peak plasma concentration (Cmax) reached in ~4 hours.
    - **Distribution:** 87% protein bound; high uptake selectivity for the liver.
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily metabolised by nucleases into shorter inactive nucleotides; not a CYP450 substrate.
    - **Elimination:** Terminal half-life is ~9 hours; 16% is cleared renally [Australian].

## 11. Clinical Trials
The efficacy of inclisiran was demonstrated in three Phase III trials (ORION-9, -10, and -11):
- **ORION-10 (ASCVD):** Inclisiran reduced LDL-C by 52% relative to placebo at Day 510. 84% of patients achieved LDL-C targets of <1.8 mmol/L [Australian].
- **ORION-11 (ASCVD/Risk Equivalent):** Reduced LDL-C by 50% relative to placebo at Day 510. 82% of ASCVD patients achieved targets [Australian].
- **ORION-9 (HeFH):** Reduced LDL-C by 48% relative to placebo at Day 510. Significant reductions were also noted in total cholesterol, Apo B, and Lp(a) [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store below 30°C.
- **Handling:** Do not freeze [Australian].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule:** **Schedule 4 (Prescription Medicine)** [Australian].
