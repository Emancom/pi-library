# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) document from the following jurisdiction: **US (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific Product Information required for prescribing. Clinicians should consult the local approved PI for their specific region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP). It is a carrier molecule required for the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane.
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Injection (solution): 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** For the acute and chronic management of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that lead to secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with end-stage renal disease who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg. This should be administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion [US].
    - **Acute Crisis:** A loading dose is often administered, followed by an equivalent dose divided over the subsequent 24 hours.
    - **Frequency:** Dosing should occur every 3 or 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours.
    - **Maximum Dose:** Doses up to 300 mg/kg have been administered in clinical practice [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:** 
    - **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
    - **Administration:** Administered as a slow 2 to 3 minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
    - **Adjustment:** Dose adjustments (e.g., down to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough (pre-dialysis) plasma carnitine levels.

### Monitoring
- **Metabolic Disorders:** Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations is recommended.
- **Target Range:** Plasma free carnitine concentrations should ideally be maintained between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Inspect parenterals visually for particulate matter or discoloration prior to use.
- **Compatibility:** Stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer's in concentrations from 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm have occurred, primarily in ESRD patients on dialysis. Some reactions manifest within minutes of intravenous administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue immediately and do not re-administer unless the benefits outweigh the risks; if re-administered, monitor closely for recurrence [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with a known seizure disorder, an increase in the frequency or severity of seizures may occur [US].
- **Renal Impairment (Oral vs. IV):** While the injection is indicated for ESRD, the safety and efficacy of **oral** levocarnitine have not been evaluated in renal insufficiency. Chronic high doses of oral levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions observed in dialysis patients (incidence ≥5% regardless of causality) include:

| Body System | Adverse Event (≥5%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Abdominal pain, accidental injury, asthenia, chest pain, fever, flu syndrome, headache, infection, injection site reaction, pain |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension, hypotension, tachycardia |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea, nausea, vomiting, dyspepsia, anorexia |
| **Metabolic** | Hypercalcemia, hypervolemia |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness, insomnia, paresthesia, depression |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis, cough increase, dyspnea, rhinitis |
| **Other** | Pruritus, leg cramps |

- **Neurologic:** Seizures (post-marketing report) [US].
- **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea and vomiting; less frequent reports of body odor and gastritis. Body odor may be more common with oral administration [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate that levocarnitine may increase the International Normalized Ratio (INR) when co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels in patients on warfarin therapy after initiating levocarnitine or following dose adjustments [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not assigned by letter, but stated as follows]: Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. There are no adequate/controlled studies in pregnant women. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine concentrations in milk following administration. Risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant should be weighed against the benefits of maternal supplementation [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy have been established for the management of inborn errors of metabolism (see Section 3 for dosing) [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in the available source; general caution regarding dose selection in the elderly is standard practice [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Indicated for use in ESRD patients on hemodialysis. Monitor trough levels to prevent excessive carnitine accumulation [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters, which are rapidly excreted.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (feces).
    - **Elimination:** Terminal elimination half-life is approximately 17.4 hours. About 76% of an intravenous dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In ESRD patients, intravenous administration increases plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Trough levels in dialysis patients often increase from ~20 µmol/L to nearly 190–240 µmol/L after 8–24 weeks of therapy (at 20 mg/kg doses) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** Efficacy was demonstrated in a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients and a larger dose-ranging study (n≈90). These trials showed that doses of 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg significantly increased trough levocarnitine concentrations. However, the impact of this increase on specific clinical outcomes in the dialysis population has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Precautions:** Avoid excessive heat and protect from freezing. 
- **Handling:** Vials are single-dose; discard any unused portion as the formulation contains no preservative [US].
