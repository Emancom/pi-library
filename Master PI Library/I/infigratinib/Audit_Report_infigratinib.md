# Originality Audit Report: infigratinib

## Audit Summary
- **Originality Score:** 42/100
- **Verbatim Copied Passages Found:** 8
- **Overall Assessment:** Fail

**Note on Sources:** Document 2 provided in the context is a PI for "Levocarnitine Injection" (Baxter), not infigratinib. Therefore, all "FDA" synthesis claims in the editorial are either hallucinations or based on the AI's internal training data rather than the provided files. This report audits the editorial against Document 1 (TGA Australia).

## Verbatim Copied Passages

**1. Approved Indications**
- **Editorial:** "...treatment of adult patients with previously treated, unresectable locally advanced or metastatic cholangiocarcinoma harboring a fibroblast growth factor receptor 2 (FGFR2) fusion or other rearrangement."
- **Source:** Document 1 (TGA), Section 4.1: "...treatment of adults with previously treated, unresectable locally advanced or metastatic cholangiocarcinoma with a fibroblast growth factor receptor 2 (FGFR2) fusion or other rearrangement."
- **Location:** Editorial Section 2.

**2. Ocular Disorders (Monitoring Schedule)**
- **Editorial:** "...required at baseline, at 1 month, at 3 months, and every 3 months thereafter."
- **Source:** Document 1 (TGA), Section 4.4: "...prior to initiation... at 1 month, at 3 months, and then every 3 months thereafter during treatment."
- **Location:** Editorial Section 4.

**3. Hyperphosphataemia (Incidence/Onset)**
- **Editorial:** "Reported in approximately 82% of patients, with a median onset of 8 days."
- **Source:** Document 1 (TGA), Section 4.4: "...reported in 82% of patients... The median time to onset of hyperphosphataemia was 8 days."
- **Location:** Editorial Section 4.

**4. Administration Instructions (Empty Stomach)**
- **Editorial:** "Take on an empty stomach, at least 1 hour before or 2 hours after a meal."
- **Source:** Document 1 (TGA), Section 4.2: "Hard capsules should be taken on an empty stomach at least 1 hour before or 2 hours after a meal."
- **Location:** Editorial Section 5.

**5. Dose Modifications (Renal Impairment)**
- **Editorial:** "...patients with mild to moderate renal impairment (CLcr 30 to 89 mL/min), the recommended dose is reduced to 100 mg once daily..."
- **Source:** Document 1 (TGA), Section 4.2: "...patients with mild to moderate renal impairment (creatinine clearance 30 to 89 mL/min...) is 100 mg once daily..."
- **Location:** Editorial Section 5.

**6. Adverse Effects (Serious Reactions)**
- **Editorial:** "Fatal sepsis occurred in 0.9% of trial participants."
- **Source:** Document 1 (TGA), Section 4.8: "Fatal adverse reactions occurred in 0.9% of patients and included sepsis."

**7. Drug Interactions (H2-Antagonists)**
- **Editorial:** "take infigratinib 2 hours before or 10 hours after the H2-antagonist."
- **Source:** Document 1 (TGA), Section 4.5: "TRUSELTIQ™ should be taken ≥2 hours before or 10 hours after dosing with H2-antagonists."
- **Location:** Editorial Section 7.

**8. Mechanism of Action**
- **Editorial:** "Infigratinib is a small-molecule kinase inhibitor that selectively targets Fibroblast Growth Factor Receptors (FGFR) 1, 2, and 3."
- **Source:** Document 1 (TGA), Section 5.1: "Infigratinib is a small molecule kinase inhibitor that targets FGFR1, FGFR2 and FGFR3..."
- **Location:** Editorial Section 10.

## Close Paraphrases
- **Dose Reductions for Toxicity:** The three-step reduction (100mg, 75mg, 50mg) follows the exact sequence in TGA Table 1.
- **Pharmacokinetics (Metabolism):** The editorial states "Predominantly metabolized via CYP3A4 (approximately 94% of total clearance)." The source (Section 5.2) states: "Infigratinib is predominantly metabolised by CYP3A4. The contribution of CYP3A4 in the total clearance of infigratinib is estimated to be 94%."
- **Excretion Statistics:** The list of percentages (77% feces, 3.4% unchanged, 7.2% urine) is taken directly from the "Excretion" subsection of TGA Section 5.2 with minimal syntactic changes.

## Originality Strengths
- **Organizational Structure:** The editorial successfully reorders the information into a more user-friendly "Quick Reference" format compared to the dense regulatory layout of the TGA document.
- **Editorial Note:** The introductory paragraph is original writing that sets a professional, synthesised tone (even if the subsequent content fails to maintain that level of synthesis).
- **Consolidation of Special Populations:** The grouping of Hepatic and Renal impairment into one logical "Special Populations" sub-header shows some structural independence.

## Differences from Source PIs
- **Source Discrepancy:** The editorial claims to incorporate FDA data from "Document 2," but Document 2 is actually a Levocarnitine PI. This suggests the AI "hallucinated" that the second document was an FDA Infigratinib PI or used outside knowledge.
- **Formatting:** The use of Markdown (bolding, bulleted lists) significantly improves the readability of the safety monitoring requirements.
- **Omission:** The editorial omits the highly specific "Grade 2/3" and "Grade 4" detailed management tables for CSR/RPED (Table 2 in TGA), opting for a simplified summary.

## Recommendation
**FAIL.** 

The editorial requires a significant rewrite to meet the "original synthesis" standard. While medical accuracy is paramount and certain figures (doses, percentages) cannot be changed, the surrounding descriptive text is currently a series of "copy-paste" extractions from the TGA document. 

**Required Changes:**
1.  **Rewrite the Mechanism of Action and Indications:** These sections are near-verbatim. Use active voice and different sentence structures (e.g., "By binding to FGFR 1-3, infigratinib prevents the cellular signaling that drives tumor growth...").
2.  **Paraphrase Safety Descriptions:** Instead of copying clinical trial results verbatim (e.g., the 0.9% sepsis rate), integrate them into more original sentences.
3.  **Correct Source Referencing:** Remove references to "Document 2" as an FDA source for Infigratinib, as the provided Document 2 is unrelated.
4.  **Synthesize Dosage Instructions:** Instead of copying the "Empty Stomach" sentence word-for-word, explain the rationale (link it to the 80-120% AUC increase mentioned in Pharmacokinetics).
