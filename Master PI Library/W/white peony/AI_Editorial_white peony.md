# Editorial Product Information: Octisalate and Titanium Dioxide (Parure Gold Skin Matte Foundation)

- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising available regulatory data into a single clinical reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official PI or local regulatory guidance required for prescribing or dispensing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** Ethylhexyl Salicylate (Octisalate) 3.5% and Titanium Dioxide 0.94% [US].
- **Chemical Class:** Organic (Octisalate) and Inorganic/Physical (Titanium Dioxide) UV filters.
- **Brand Name:** Parure Gold Skin Matte Foundation No-Transfer High Perfection 24H Care and Wear Infused with 24K Gold and White Peony with Sunscreen Broad Spectrum SPF 15 0C [US].
- **ATC Code:** D02BA (Sunscreens for topical use) – *Editorial assignment based on class*.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Topical emulsion (foundation). Strengths: Octisalate 35 mg/mL; Titanium Dioxide 9.4 mg/mL [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Topical.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Guerlain [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Sunburn Prevention:** Helps prevent sunburn [US].
- **Skin Health Maintenance:** Used as part of a sun protection regimen to decrease the risk of skin cancer and early skin aging caused by the sun [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Application:** Apply liberally 15 minutes before sun exposure [US].
- **Reapplication:** Must be reapplied at least every two hours [US].
- **Exposure Conditions:** If swimming or sweating, a water-resistant sunscreen should be used in conjunction with this product [US].

### Administration Instructions
- **Preparation:** Shake well before use [US].
- **Technique:** Apply to the face as a foundation for "24H care and wear" [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Skin Integrity:** Do not use on damaged or broken skin [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to Octisalate, Titanium Dioxide, or any of the inactive excipients listed in the product formulation [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### General Warnings
- **Route Restriction:** For external use only [US].
- **Ocular Exposure:** Keep out of eyes. In case of contact, rinse thoroughly with water to remove [US].
- **Adverse Reaction Protocol:** Discontinue use and consult a physician if a rash occurs [US].

### Sun Protection Measures
- Spending time in the sun increases the risk of skin cancer and premature skin aging. This product should be used as part of a comprehensive protection plan including:
    - Limiting time in the sun, particularly between 10:00 a.m. and 2:00 p.m. [US].
    - Wearing protective clothing (long-sleeved shirts, pants, hats, and sunglasses) [US].

### Specific Clinical Precautions
- **Accidental Ingestion:** Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, professional medical assistance or contact with a Poison Control Center is required immediately [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** 
    - **Octisalate:** An organic compound that absorbs UVB radiation.
    - **Titanium Dioxide:** A physical/mineral sunscreen that reflects and scatters UV radiation (primarily UVB and short-wave UVA).
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified in available source documentation for these topical active ingredients.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Provides a Broad Spectrum Sun Protection Factor (SPF) of 15 [US].
