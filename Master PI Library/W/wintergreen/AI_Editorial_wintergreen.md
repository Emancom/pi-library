# Editorial Product Information: Isopropyl Alcohol 70% with Wintergreen Oil

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) and labeling data provided by the following regulatory authority: **US (Food and Drug Administration)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising available regulatory data into a single, clinically focused reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific Product Information required for prescribing or dispensing in any specific region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Isopropyl Alcohol 70% (v/v) [US].
- **Chemical Class:** Secondary alcohol (Aliphatic alcohol).
- **Brand Name(s):** GNP Isopropyl Rubbing Alcohol 70% With Wintergreen Oil [US].
- **ATC Code:** D08AL03 (Isopropyl alcohol).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Topical liquid containing 700 mg of isopropyl alcohol per 1 mL (70% by volume) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** AmerisourceBergen [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **First Aid Antiseptic:** To help prevent the risk of infection in minor cuts, scrapes, and burns [US].
- **Topical Rub/Massage:** For use in rubbing, massage, and sponge baths [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Administration:** Apply the liquid freely to the affected area. Rub briskly using the hands or a towel [US].
- **Missed Dose:** Not applicable for this topical antiseptic as it is used on an as-needed basis.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to isopropyl alcohol or any of the inactive ingredients, including methyl salicylate (wintergreen oil) or FD&C color additives [US].
- **Specific Clinical Exclusions:** Do not apply to irritated skin [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **General Warnings:** For external use only [US].
- **Flammability:** This product is highly flammable. It must be kept away from sparks, heat, and open flames [US].
- **Inhalation Risk:** Use only in well-ventilated areas. Inhalation of fumes may be harmful [US].
- **Ocular/Mucous Membrane Exposure:** Avoid contact with eyes and mucous membranes [US].
- **Physician Consultation Required:** Patients should be advised to consult a healthcare professional before use for [US]:
    - Deep or puncture wounds.
    - Animal bites.
    - Serious burns.

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Dermatologic:** Excessive skin irritation may develop. If irritation occurs, discontinue use and consult a physician [US].
- **Gastrointestinal (Systemic Toxicity):** If taken internally, the product will produce serious gastric disturbances [US].
