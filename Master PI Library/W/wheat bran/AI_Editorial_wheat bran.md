# Editorial Product Information: Chicken Dewormer Powder (Peppers, Garlic, Capsicum, Oregano, Linseed Oil, and Wheat Bran)

## Editorial Note
- Contributing Jurisdictions: United States (FDA).
- Purpose: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of Chicken Dewormer Powder based on available regulatory filings. It is intended for use by healthcare professionals and veterinary specialists.
- Disclaimer: This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for official Product Information. In the United States, this product is classified as an unapproved drug and has not been evaluated by the FDA for safety or efficacy.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** A multi-component natural formulation consisting of:
    - Peppers (265 mg/1 g)
    - Garlic (130 mg/1 g)
    - Garlic Oil (100 mg/1 g)
    - Capsicum (292 mg/1 g)
    - Oregano (80 mg/1 g)
    - Linseed Oil (83 mg/1 g)
    - Wheat Bran (50 mg/1 g)
- **Brand Name:** Natural Chicken Dewormer [US]
- **ATC Code:** Not available.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Freeform yellow powder [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral (via feed).
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Pekomet Inc; Manufactured by Zhenjiang Zhongfuyou Biotechnology Co., Ltd. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Poultry Deworming:** Intended for the removal of parasites in chickens [US].
- **General Health Support:** Indicated to support poultry natural defense systems, gut health, skin and feather integrity, healthy circulation, and digestive function [US].
- **Nutritional Support:** Provides a physical barrier for overall wellness and enhances bioavailability of nutrients from feed [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult Poultry)
- **Standard Dose:** One tablespoon mixed into the feed for every four adult chickens [US].
- **Frequency:** The label provides conflicting instructions: "Feed once daily" in the general usage section, but "Repeat this twice a day for 10 consecutive days" in the specific directions for use [US]. Clinicians should exercise caution regarding this discrepancy.

### Paediatric Dosing (Chicks)
- **Dose:** Use half of the adult amount (approximately 1/2 tablespoon) for every four chicks [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Shake the product well before use.
- Mix thoroughly with feed using the provided tablespoon measure [US].
