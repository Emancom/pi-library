# Editorial Product Information: Homeopathic Sore Throat Formula (Multi-Ingredient)

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official product labeling documentation from the following jurisdiction:
    - **US (Food and Drug Administration)**
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing or dispensing in a specific jurisdiction. The US FDA has not evaluated this homeopathic product for safety or efficacy and is not aware of scientific evidence to support homeopathy as effective.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** 
    - **Echinacea angustifolia** (Purple Cone Flower) 6X HPUS
    - **Apis mellifera** (Honey Bee) 8X HPUS
    - **Atropa belladonna** (Deadly Nightshade) 8X HPUS
    - **Drosera rotundifolia** (Sundew) 8X HPUS
    - **Artemisia cina** (Wormseed) 8X HPUS
- **Chemical Class:** Homeopathic preparation.
- **Brand Names:** Botanic Choice Homeopathic Sore Throat Formula [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not available.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Oral tablet, 300 mg [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral (sublingual or buccal recommended per instructions to "dissolve under tongue").
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Distributed by Indiana Botanic Gardens, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- For the temporary relief of a sore throat in the absence of a fever [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Children >12 Years)
- **Initial Dosing:** 1–2 tablets, three times daily [US].
- **Maintenance Dosing:** Once symptoms appear to reduce, dose may be transitioned to once daily to maintain control [US].
- **Administration Instructions:** Tablets should be dissolved under the tongue or chewed [US].
- **Duration of Use:** If symptoms persist for more than 10 days, patients should be advised to consult a healthcare professional [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- Not specified in available sources.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **General Warnings:** Healthcare professional consultation is required if symptoms persist for more than 10 days [US].
- **Febrile Illness:** Indication is restricted to sore throat *without* fever [US].
- **Safety Seal:** Do not use if the outer safety seal is missing or broken [US].
- **Safety Precaution:** Keep out of reach of children [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
