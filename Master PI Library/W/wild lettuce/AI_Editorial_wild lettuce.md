# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA)
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of levocarnitine injection for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises clinical and regulatory data to support safe and effective prescribing and administration.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information (PI) or Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. At the time of preparation, only the US (FDA) source was provided for synthesis.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt. 
- **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule/Amino acid derivative.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection, USP (Baxter Healthcare Corporation) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL vial (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance hemodialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg. It may be administered as a slow bolus injection (2–3 minutes) or by infusion. 
    - *Loading Dose:* In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose is often administered, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours.
    - *Frequency:* Dosing should occur every 3 or 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours.
    - *Maintenance:* Subsequent daily doses are typically 50 mg/kg, though higher doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilised [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:** 
    - *Starting Dose:* 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight).
    - *Administration:* Administer as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus into the venous return line after each dialysis session.
    - *Adjustments:* Dosing should be guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Reductions (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on levels [US].

### Monitoring
- Plasma free carnitine concentrations should be maintained between 35 and 60 µmol/L. 
- Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma concentrations is recommended [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have occurred, typically in ESRD patients. Reactions can occur within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and evaluate risks/benefits before re-administration [US].
- **Neurologic Reactions (Seizures):** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Renal Impairment (Oral Caution):** While this PI concerns the injectable form, clinicians should note that chronic high-dose *oral* levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Clinical Trial Experience (ESRD/Dialysis)
Adverse events reported at a frequency ≥5% (regardless of causality) in dialysis patients include:
- **Body as a Whole:** Abdominal pain (9–21%), headache (22%), injection site reaction (33%), fever (7%), and asthenia (9%).
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (20%), hypotension (14%), tachycardia (6%), and chest pain (12%).
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea (16%), vomiting (15%), and nausea (8%).
- **Metabolic/Nutritional:** Hypercalcemia (9%), hyperkalemia (6%), and hypervolemia (5%).
- **Neurologic:** Dizziness (13%), insomnia (4%), and paresthesia (5%).
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (23%), cough increase (9%), and dyspnea (7%).

### Postmarketing Experience
- **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea and vomiting; body odor and gastritis occur less frequently.
- **Neurologic:** Seizures.
- **Hypersensitivity:** Anaphylaxis, bronchospasm [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after any dosage adjustment in patients receiving warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Category:** Not specified by letter, but reproductive studies in rats and rabbits (up to 3.8 times human dose) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm.
    - **Recommendation:** Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine is a normal component of human milk. While supplemental use in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied, studies in dairy cows show increased milk concentrations after exogenous administration. Weigh the benefits to the mother against the risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** No mutagenic effects were found in standard tests (Salmonella, yeast). Long-term animal carcinogenicity studies have not been conducted [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders is consistent with adult weight-based recommendations (50 mg/kg) [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically detailed in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** See "ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis" in Section 3 for specific dosing. Levocarnitine is easily removed by dialysis [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring carrier molecule required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane for beta-oxidation and energy production. In skeletal and cardiac muscle, fatty acids are the primary energy substrate.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In patients with inborn errors of metabolism, levocarnitine promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters, which are rapidly excreted.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (feces).
    - **Elimination:** Mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours. Approximately 76% of an intravenous dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Dialysis Studies:** In a 9-week study of ESRD patients (n=12), 20 mg/kg doses three times weekly post-dialysis increased mean pre-dialysis (trough) plasma carnitine levels from 20 µmol/L to 190 µmol/L at 8 weeks.
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** Studies of 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses confirmed a dose-dependent increase in trough concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks. While efficacy in increasing carnitine concentrations was proven, the specific effect on clinical outcomes in the ESRD population has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing. Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as it contains no preservatives.
- **Admixture Stability:** Compatible and stable for 24 hours in PVC bags at room temperature (25°C) when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer's at concentrations of 0.5 mg/mL to 8.0 mg/mL [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only (Prescription only) [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not applicable in the US jurisdiction; see regional requirements for Australia/UK/Canada.
