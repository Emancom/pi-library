# Editorial Product Information: Wild Yam (*Dioscorea villosa*)

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **US (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of **Woodsleep Wild Yam Cream** for healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI or clinical judgement required for prescribing or recommending this product in any specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** *Dioscorea villosa* (Wild Yam) root extract.
- **Chemical Class:** Herbal/Botanical extract (Saponin-containing).
- **Brand Name(s):** Woodsleep Wild Yam Cream [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Topical cream. Each 100 mg of cream contains 0.02 mg of *Dioscorea villosa* root extract [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical (Cutaneous).
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Shantou Woodsleep Biotechnology Co., Ltd. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
The following indications are approved [US]:
- Support for hormonal regulation.
- Relief of menopausal symptoms.
- Management of premenstrual syndrome (PMS) symptoms.
- Reduction of anxiety and mood swings to improve quality of life.
- Promotion of deep sleep and relief of fatigue and insomnia.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Adult (Topical):** Apply an appropriate amount of cream to the palms and gently massage into the skin until completely absorbed.
- **Application Sites:** Belly, chest, arms, and inner thighs [US].

### Dosing by Indication
- **Premenstrual Syndrome (PMS):** For menstruating women, it is recommended to initiate application one week prior to the expected onset of menstruation. Application should continue twice daily until the conclusion of the menstrual period [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Skin should be cleansed and dried prior to application.
- Hands should be washed before use to ensure hygiene and product efficacy [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to *Dioscorea villosa* or any of the excipients (e.g., glycerin, mineral oil, triethanolamine) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **General Precautions:** For external use only. Do not swallow [US].
- **Dermatological:** Discontinue use immediately if signs of skin irritation or rash occur [US].
- **Safety:** Keep the product out of the reach of children [US].
