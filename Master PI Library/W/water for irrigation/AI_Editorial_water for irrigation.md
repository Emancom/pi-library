# Editorial Product Information: Sterile Water for Irrigation

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals (specifically veterinary professionals, as per the source material).
- **Important Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for the official PI for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. The source document provided relates specifically to an unapproved veterinary drug marketed under applicable regulations.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active ingredient:** Sterile Water for Irrigation, USP (100% Water).
- **Brand name(s):** Alfa Veterinary Sterile Water for Irrigation [US].
- **ATC code:** V07AB (Solvents and diluting agents, including irrigating solutions).
- **Dosage form(s) and strength(s):** Sterile, non-pyrogenic, hypotonic solution in 100 mL, 250 mL, 500 mL, and 1000 mL containers [US].
- **Route(s) of administration:** For irrigation use only. **Strictly not for injection.**
- **Manufacturer/sponsor:** Laboratorios Alfa S.R.L. (Distributed by Vedco Inc.) [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Veterinary Irrigation:** Indicated for use as an irrigating fluid for the mechanical cleansing, rinsing, and washing of body cavities, tissues, wounds, and surgical sites [US].
- **Diluent/Vehicle:** Indicated as a vehicle or diluent for compatible veterinary drugs intended for irrigation [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult Animals):** The volume required depends on the capacity or surface area of the structure being irrigated and the nature of the specific procedure [US].
- **Typical Volumes:** Range from 50 mL to 1000 mL [US].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Administer slowly and avoid excessive pressure to minimize the risk of systemic fluid absorption [US].
    - Use aseptic technique throughout handling and administration [US].
    - Inspect the solution visually for clarity, particulate matter, and discoloration prior to use [US].
- **Additives:** If additives are introduced, use aseptic technique and mix thoroughly. Do not store solutions containing additives; use immediately [US].
- **Preparation:** Use the breakaway ring system to remove the outer cap and pour. Once opened, use the single-use container immediately and discard any unused portion [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Intravenous Administration:** This product is strictly contraindicated for injection or intravenous use [US].
- **Solute Requirement:** Contraindicated in clinical scenarios where a sterile, non-pyrogenic, but **isotonic** solution is required [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypotonicity and Hemolysis:** The solution is hypotonic and solute-free. Systemic absorption can lead to hemolysis. It is generally not recommended for use during major surgical procedures where significant absorption may occur [US].
- **Electrolyte Disturbances:** Large volume absorption can lead to severe electrolyte imbalances, including hyponatremia and hypoosmolality [US].
- **Contamination Risk:** Single-use only. To prevent pyrogen formation and contamination, discard any unused portions immediately after opening [US].
- **Inspection:** Do not use if the solution is cloudy, contains precipitates, or if the container/seals are compromised [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are primarily associated with systemic absorption of the hypotonic solution. Frequencies are not specified in the source material.

| Adverse Effect | Clinical Presentation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Hematologic** | Hemolysis due to hypotonicity [US]. |
| **Electrolyte** | Hyponatremia and hypoosmolality [US]. |
| **Neurological** | Tremors, seizures, lethargy, ataxia, and altered mentation (secondary to hyponatremia) [US]. |
| **Respiratory/Fluid** | Fluid overload leading to pulmonary edema or respiratory distress [US]. |
| **Systemic** | Water intoxication; in severe cases, coma or death [US]. |

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Incompatibilities:** Some additives may be chemically or physically incompatible with sterile water [US].
- **Known Incompatible Additives:** 
    - Strong oxidizing agents [US].
    - Alcohol-containing solutions [US].
    - Solutions with high electrolyte concentrations [US].
- **Management:** Always verify compatibility with a veterinarian or pharmacist before mixing. Inspect the final mixture for cloudiness or precipitation [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Safety has not been established in pregnant animals. Use only when the potential benefits clearly outweigh the potential risks. A documented risk-benefit assessment by a veterinarian is recommended [US].
- **Lactation:** Safety has not been established in lactating animals [US].
- **Fertility:** Data not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric/Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** While not explicitly detailed, the risk of fluid overload and electrolyte imbalance is logically higher in patients with renal impairment; care should be taken to minimize absorption during use [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Acts as a mechanical cleansing agent to remove debris, blood, and contaminants from surgical and traumatic sites [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Primarily intended for topical/local action. However, systemic absorption can occur through open wounds or body cavities, especially if used under pressure [US].
    - **Distribution/Metabolism/Elimination:** Once absorbed systemically, it follows the pharmacokinetics of free water.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** As a hypotonic, solute-free solution (0 mOsmol/L), it exerts osmotic pressure on cells, potentially leading to cell swelling and hemolysis if absorbed [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- No pivotal clinical trial data for this specific unapproved veterinary product was provided in the source documentation [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at a temperature not exceeding 30°C (86°F) [US].
- **Precautions:** Avoid excessive heat and protect the solution from freezing [US].
- **Shelf Life:** Not specified in the source; however, the product must not be used if the expiration date has passed [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as a Prescription Animal Drug (Rx-only). Caution: Federal law (USA) restricts this drug to use by or on the order of a licensed veterinarian. This product is marketed as an unapproved veterinary drug [US].
