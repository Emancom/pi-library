# Editorial Product Information: Water for Injection

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis incorporates information from the official Product Information (PI) documents of the following jurisdictions:
    - **Australian (TGA)**: Water for Injection (Phebra Pty Ltd)
    - **US (FDA)**: Water for Injection (Diluent Component for JYNNEOS, Bavarian Nordic A/S)
- **Purpose**: To provide a harmonised, clinically accurate overview of **Water for Injection** for healthcare professionals, consolidating regional variations in indications, storage, and regulatory status.
- **Note**: This document is an editorial synthesis and does not replace the official PI required for prescribing and administration in specific jurisdictions.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient**: Water for Injections (Hydrogen oxide; $H_2O$)
- **Chemical Class**: Solvent/Diluent
- **Brand Names**: 
    - **Phebra Water for Injection** [Australian]
    - **Diluent Component** (packaged with JYNNEOS vaccine) [US]
- **ATC Code**: V07AB
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s)**:
    - Sterile, clear, colourless, particle-free liquid.
    - pH range: 5.6–7.7 [Australian].
    - Available in 50 mL and 100 mL glass vials [Australian].
    - Available as a 0.65 mL single-dose vial [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration**:
    - Intravenous (IV), Intramuscular (IM), or Subcutaneous (SC) (following use as a diluent)
    - Irrigation [Australian]
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor**:
    - Phebra Pty Ltd [Australian]
    - Bavarian Nordic A/S [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Solvent/Diluent**: Used to dissolve or dilute medicinal substances or preparations for parenteral administration.
- **Vaccine Reconstitution**: Specifically indicated for the reconstitution of lyophilized antigen components (e.g., JYNNEOS vaccine) [US].
- **Irrigation**: Used as a sterile irrigation solution for washing or irrigating patients [Australian-approved; not specified in available US source].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing**: The volume used is determined by the specific requirements of the drug or substance being dissolved or diluted.
- **Administration Instructions**:
    - Use strict aseptic technique for all preparations.
    - Consult the primary Product Information of the solute to ensure compatibility, solubility, and the recommended maximum time between preparation and use.
    - **Crucial Safety Step**: Before parenteral administration, ensure the final solution is **isotonic** with blood to prevent hemolysis [Australian].
- **Special Presentation (JYNNEOS)**: For vaccine reconstitution, withdraw the entire contents of the diluent vial and transfer into the lyophilized antigen vial. Gently shake until completely dissolved [US].
- **Handling**: For single patient use on a single occasion only. Discard any remaining residue.

## 4. Contraindications
- Not specified in available sources [Australian]. 
- *Note: Standard contraindications for the primary drug being dissolved must be observed.*

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypotonicity Warning**: **Water for Injection** must not be injected intravenously without first being rendered isotonic by the addition of appropriate solutes. Injection of hypotonic water may result in local cell damage or hemolysis [Australian].
- **Compatibility**: Always verify the compatibility of all additives and drugs before mixing.
- **Visual Inspection**: Parenteral products should be inspected for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration. If observed, the solution must not be used [US].
- **Allergic Reactions**: While rare for the water itself, appropriate medical treatment must be available to manage potential anaphylaxis related to the primary drug/vaccine [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **General Profile**: No adverse reactions are known to be associated with **Water for Injection** when used as indicated to form an isotonic solution.
- **Serious Adverse Effects**:
    - **Hemolysis and Cell Damage**: May occur if the product is injected without the addition of solute (hypotonic injection) [Australian].
- **Post-reconstitution (Vaccine context)**: Common solicited reactions related to reconstituted products (e.g., JYNNEOS) include injection site pain, redness, swelling, and systemic effects like muscle pain, fatigue, and headache [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions**: No specific interactions for pure water are documented.
- **Management Note**: Interactions are secondary to the chemical compatibility of the dissolved medication. Consult the PI of the medicinal substance to be dissolved.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy**:
    - **Australian Category A**: Safe for use. It has been administered to a large number of pregnant women without evidence of increased malformations or harmful effects on the foetus.
    - **US (JYNNEOS context)**: Animal studies (rats/rabbits) using the vaccine reconstituted with this diluent showed no evidence of harm to the foetus; however, human data are insufficient to inform vaccine-associated risks.
- **Breastfeeding**: Can be administered to breastfeeding women [Australian].
- **Fertility**: No data available for water alone [Australian]; no effects on female fertility were reported in animal studies for the associated vaccine [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use**: No data available for standalone water [Australian]. For the US-specific vaccine context, safety and effectiveness are not established in individuals under 18 years of age [US].
- **Elderly Use**: No specific data available [Australian]. Clinical studies for the associated vaccine did not include sufficient numbers of subjects $\ge$ 65 years to determine if they respond differently than younger subjects [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment**: Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action**: Acts as a vehicle for the delivery of parenteral medications.
- **Pharmacokinetics**:
    - **Absorption/Distribution/Metabolism**: Water is naturally distributed throughout all body compartments. 
    - **Excretion**: Regulated primarily by the kidneys and through insensible losses (skin, lungs).
- **Pharmacodynamics**: Chemically inert when used as a diluent; however, it exerts a hypotonic osmotic effect if injected in pure form.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- Not applicable for **Water for Injection** as a standalone product. Clinical trial data provided in the sources relate to the efficacy and immunogenicity of the JYNNEOS vaccine when reconstituted with this diluent [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **General Storage**:
    - Store below 30°C [Australian].
    - Store at room temperature (20°C to 25°C) or refrigerated (2°C to 8°C) [US].
- **After Reconstitution**:
    - Once reconstituted (vaccine context), store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C for up to 12 hours. Do not freeze [US].
- **Shelf Life**: Refer to the expiry date on the packaging.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison/Schedule Classification**:
    - **Australian**: Not scheduled.
    - **US**: Rx-only.
- **Container Features**: Vial stoppers are not made with natural rubber latex [Australian, US].
