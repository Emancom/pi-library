# Editorial Product Information: FemiClear (Lemongrass, Willow Bark, and Olive Extract)

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (Drug Facts) from the following jurisdiction: United States (FDA). 
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. 
- **Important Clinical Note:** This product is classified as a homeopathic medicine. In the United States, it has not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) for safety or efficacy, and the FDA is not aware of scientific evidence to support homeopathy as effective [US].
- This document is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing or dispensing in any specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** Lemongrass (2X HPUS), Olive Extract/Oleuropein (3X HPUS), and Willow Bark Extract (3X HPUS).
- **Brand Name:** FemiClear Genital Herpes Symptoms (Multisymptom).
- **ATC Code:** Not assigned (Homeopathic).
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Topical ointment. Active concentrations are expressed in homeopathic dilutions (2X and 3X).
- **Route of Administration:** Topical.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Organicare Nature's Science, LLC [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- Symptomatic relief of itching, burning, tingling, and pain associated with **diagnosed** genital herpes [US].
- Note: This product is intended for the management of symptoms and is not a cure for the herpes simplex virus (HSV).

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Children $\ge$ 12 years)
- **Application:** Apply generously to the affected areas.
- **Frequency:** Use at a minimum of four times daily, then as needed.
- **Timing:** For optimal results, application should begin at the first sign of an outbreak [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Wash hands thoroughly both before and after applying the ointment.
- Apply only to the affected areas.
- Do not use in or near the eyes.

## 4. Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity or allergy to any active ingredient (Lemongrass, Olive Extract, Willow Bark) or any of the excipients (e.g., beeswax, oatmeal) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **External Use Only:** Not for oral or ophthalmic use.
- **Not a Cure:** FemiClear does not cure herpes or prevent future outbreaks [US].
- **Contagion Risk:** This product does not prevent the transmission of herpes or other viral infections. Viral infections remain contagious during treatment [US].
- **Barrier Contraceptive Interference:** This product may damage latex or polyurethane condoms and diaphragms, potentially leading to contraceptive failure [US].
- **Initial Diagnosis:** Patients should consult a healthcare professional if they are experiencing genital sores or blisters for the first time [US].
- **Infection Spread:** Do not share this product with others, as this may spread the virus [US].
- **Medical Consultation:** Users should stop use and consult a physician if symptoms worsen.

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **General:** Specific frequency data for adverse effects (e.g., very common, common) is not provided in the source documentation.
- **Dermatological:** Users are advised to monitor for worsening symptoms or irritation.
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Not specified in available sources, though hypersensitivity reactions are possible given the botanical components.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Mechanical Interactions:** Significant interaction with latex and polyurethane barrier contraceptives, which may be weakened or damaged by the ointment base [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Patients should consult a healthcare professional before use if pregnant [US]. No specific pregnancy category (e.g., US FDA letter system) is assigned in the source document.
- **Breastfeeding:** Patients should consult a healthcare professional before use if breastfeeding [US].
- **Fertility:** Data regarding the impact on human fertility is not available in the source documentation.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Approved for use in children aged 12 years and older at the adult dose. For children under 12 years of age, a doctor must be consulted prior to use [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** No dosage adjustments are provided for renal or hepatic impairment due to the topical, homeopathic nature of the product.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Marketed as an antiviral agent, healing agent, and pain reliever based on traditional homeopathic practice.
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified in available sources. Topical absorption is expected to be minimal.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Claims regarding symptom relief (itching, tingling, pain, and burning) are based on clinical and/or in vitro laboratory studies (non-human/non-animal) and traditional homeopathic practice [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- The source document reports that >90% of users reported less severe outbreaks based on data on file [US].
- Note: These claims have not been evaluated by the FDA for clinical medical evidence [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store between 68°F and 77°F (20°C to 25°C) [US].
- **Handling:** Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, medical help or Poison Control Center contact is required immediately.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
