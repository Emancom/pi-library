# Editorial Product Information: Sterile Water for Injection (Diluent)

## Editorial Note
- The jurisdictions and official Product Information (PI) documents contributing to this editorial are:
    - **Australian (TGA)**: Australian Product Information – ABRYSVO (Recombinant Respiratory Syncytial Virus Pre-Fusion F Protein) Vaccine.
    - **US (FDA)**: US Prescribing Information – WINREVAIR (sotatercept-csrk) for injection.
- **Purpose**: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals regarding the use of Sterile Water for Injection as a diluent, specifically in the context of reconstituting complex lyophilised biologic products. 
- **Disclaimer**: This is an editorial synthesis and clinical summary. It is not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific Product Information required for prescribing and administration in specific regions.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient**: Sterile Water for Injection (INN).
- **Chemical Class**: Solvent/Vehicle.
- **ATC Code**: V07AB.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s)**:
    - **Australian**: 0.5 mL pre-filled syringe or vial [as a component of the **Abrysvo** vaccine kit].
    - **US**: 1.0 mL or 1.3 mL pre-filled syringes [as a component of the **Winrevair** injection kit].
- **Route(s) of Administration**: Reconstitution for Intramuscular (IM) or Subcutaneous (SC) injection.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor**: 
    - **Australian**: Pfizer Australia Pty Ltd.
    - **US**: Merck Sharp & Dohme LLC.

## 2. Approved Indications
Sterile Water for Injection is indicated as a diluent for the reconstitution of specific lyophilised medicinal products:
- **Active Immunisation (RSV)**: Reconstitution of recombinant respiratory syncytial virus (RSV) pre-fusion F protein for use in pregnant women (24–36 weeks gestation) and individuals ≥60 years of age [Australian-approved].
- **Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension (PAH)**: Reconstitution of sotatercept-csrk for the treatment of adults with WHO Group 1 PAH to improve exercise capacity and functional class [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Reconstitution Instructions
- **Inspection**: The diluent should be a clear, colourless liquid. Inspect for particulate matter or discolouration prior to use.
- **Equilibration**: Allow the diluent and powder to reach room temperature (approximately 15 minutes) before preparation [US].
- **Mixing**: 
    - Use only the provided vial adapter and syringe.
    - Gently swirl the vial until the powder is completely dissolved. 
    - **Caution**: Do not shake or vigorously agitate the mixture [Australian, US].
- **Stability**: Once reconstituted, the solution should ideally be used immediately.
    - **Australian**: Must be used within 4 hours; do not freeze.
    - **US**: Must be used within 4 hours; discard unused portions.

### Administration Volume
- **Australian (Abrysvo)**: Withdraw 0.5 mL for a single intramuscular dose.
- **US (Winrevair)**: Volume is weight-based (0.3 mg/kg starting; 0.7 mg/kg target). Reconstitution with 1 mL or 1.3 mL of diluent provides a final drug concentration of 50 mg/mL.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity**: Known hypersensitivity to the active substance or any excipients of the product being reconstituted [Australian, US].
- **Direct Injection**: Sterile water for injection must never be injected intravenously without being rendered approximately isotonic by the addition of suitable solutes.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Preparation Errors**: Ensure the correct volume of diluent is used to reach the target concentration. In the US, incorrect volume calculation may lead to underdose or overdose.
- **Post-Reconstitution Appearance**: 
    - Reconstituted **Abrysvo** should be clear and colourless [Australian].
    - Reconstituted **Winrevair** should be clear to opalescent and colourless to slightly brownish-yellow, free of clumps or powder [US].
- **Injection Site Reactions**: Administration may be associated with vasovagal reactions (syncope) or local pain. Procedures should be in place to avoid injury from fainting [Australian].
- **Bleeding Risk**: Use with caution in patients with thrombocytopenia or coagulation disorders, as bleeding may occur following IM or SC administration [Australian, US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are generally associated with the active medicinal product rather than the diluent itself.
- **Very Common (>10%)**:
    - **Australian (Vaccine context)**: Vaccination site pain, headache, myalgia.
    - **US (PAH treatment context)**: Headache, epistaxis, telangiectasia, diarrhoea, dizziness, erythema, rash.
- **Rare (<0.1%)**:
    - **Australian**: Guillain-Barré syndrome (reported in individuals ≥60 years), lymphadenopathy.
    - **US**: Serious bleeding events (4-7%), severe thrombocytopenia.
- **Serious Adverse Effects**: Anaphylaxis has been reported rarely following administration of reconstituted biologics [Australian].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Coadministration**:
    - **Australian**: Reconstituted RSV vaccine can be administered concomitantly with seasonal influenza vaccine (standard or high-dose adjuvanted/unadjuvanted). Concomitant administration with dTpa showed non-inferiority for most components, though pertussis response was lower.
    - **US**: No formal drug interaction studies provided in the source for the reconstituted signaling inhibitor; however, monitoring of haemoglobin and platelets is required before each dose.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy**:
    - **Australian**: Category A. Data from >4,000 pregnant women indicates no malformative or feto/neonatal toxicity.
    - **US**: May cause fetal harm. Based on animal studies, Winrevair is associated with embryo-fetal mortality and structural variations. Effective contraception is required for 4 months after the final dose in the US jurisdiction.
- **Breastfeeding**:
    - **Australian**: Unknown if excreted in human milk; no adverse effects shown in breastfed newborns of vaccinated mothers.
    - **US**: Breastfeeding is not recommended during treatment and for 4 months after the final dose.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use**: Safety and efficacy have not been established in children <18 years of age [Australian, US].
- **Elderly Use**: 
    - **Australian**: Extensively studied in adults ≥60 years; efficacy maintained across two RSV seasons.
    - **US**: No overall differences in efficacy between patients <65 and ≥65 years, though bleeding events occurred more commonly in the older subgroup.
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment**: [US: No clinically significant PK differences in mild-to-moderate renal impairment; hepatic impairment not studied].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action**: Sterile Water for Injection acts as an inert vehicle for the dissolution and delivery of lyophilised drugs.
- **Pharmacokinetics**: Water is rapidly absorbed following IM or SC injection and follows standard physiological distribution and elimination pathways.
- **Pharmacodynamics**: The diluent itself possesses no pharmacodynamic activity.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Australian (RSV Vaccine)**: Pivotal Study C3671008 (pregnant women) and Study C3671013 (adults ≥60 years) demonstrated high efficacy in preventing RSV-associated lower respiratory tract disease.
- **US (PAH Treatment)**: The STELLAR trial showed a placebo-adjusted median increase of 41 metres in 6-minute walk distance (6MWD). The ZENITH trial demonstrated a 76% reduction in the risk of death or PAH-worsening events.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature**: Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C. Do not freeze [Australian, US].
- **Light Protection**: Keep in the original carton to protect from light.
- **Temperature Excursions**:
    - **Australian**: Unopened vials are stable for 5 days at 8°C to 30°C.
    - **US**: Unused kits may be out of the refrigerator (up to 25°C) for up to 24 hours.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule**:
    - **Australian**: S4 (Prescription Only Medicine).
    - **US**: Rx-only.
- **Additional Monitoring**: Reconstituted Abrysvo is subject to additional monitoring in Australia to allow quick identification of new safety information [Australian].
