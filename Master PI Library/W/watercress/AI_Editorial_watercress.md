# Editorial Product Information: Octinoxate, Octocrylene, Octisalate, and Avobenzone (360 by QUR Watercress Mint Lip Balm)

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial clinical overview intended for use by healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information/Labeling approved in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult the current local PI for prescribing and legal requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** 
    - Octinoxate (Octyl methoxycinnamate): 5% (50 mg/g)
    - Octocrylene: 3% (30 mg/g)
    - Octisalate: 2.2% (22 mg/g)
    - Avobenzone: 0.5% (5 mg/g)
- **Chemical Class:** Organic UV filters (sunscreen agents).
- **Brand Name:** 360 by QUR Watercress Mint Lip Balm [US].
- **ATC Code:** D02BA (Sunscreening agents for topical use) [Inferred; not explicitly stated in source].
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Topical stick; cumulative 10.7% w/w active sunscreen ingredients [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Topical.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Qur, Inc [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Sunburn Prevention:** Indicated to help prevent sunburn [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Children ≥6 months)
- **Application:** Apply liberally and evenly to the lips 15 minutes before sun exposure [US].
- **Reapplication:** Must be reapplied at least every two hours [US].
- **Water Exposure:** This product is not labeled as water-resistant; a water-resistant sunscreen should be used if the individual is swimming or sweating [US].

### Missed Dose Instructions
- Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Damaged Skin:** Do not use on damaged or broken skin [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warnings
- None [US].

### General Warnings
- **Route Restriction:** For external use only [US].
- **Ocular Exposure:** Keep out of eyes. In the event of contact, rinse with water to remove [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Discontinue use and consult a physician if a rash occurs [US].

### Specific Clinical Precautions
- **Skin Cancer/Skin Aging Alert:** Spending time in the sun increases the risk of skin cancer and early skin aging. This specific product has only been shown to help prevent sunburn, not to reduce the risk of skin cancer or early skin aging [US].
- **Ingestion:** In the event of accidental ingestion, seek medical assistance or contact a Poison Control Center immediately [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** A combination of chemical filters that absorb UV radiation. Octinoxate, Octocrylene, and Octisalate primarily provide UVB protection, while Avobenzone provides UVA protection.
