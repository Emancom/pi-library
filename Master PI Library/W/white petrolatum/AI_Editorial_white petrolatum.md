# Editorial Product Information: White Petrolatum

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) and labeling documentation from the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**. 
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing or dispensing in any specific jurisdiction. At the time of preparation, only United States regulatory documentation was available for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** White Petrolatum USP (99.8% w/w)
- **Chemical Class:** Mixture of semi-solid saturated hydrocarbons
- **Brand Name(s):** Cruex Petroleum Jelly [US]
- **ATC Code:** D02AC (Soft paraffin and fat products)
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Topical jelly containing 99.8g of white petrolatum per 100g [US]
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** MKJ Brands LLC [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
White petrolatum is indicated for the following uses:
- Temporary protection of minor cuts, scrapes, and burns [US].
- Relief and protection of chapped or cracked skin and lips [US].
- Protection of skin from the drying effects of wind and cold weather [US].
- Treatment and prevention of diaper rash [US].
- Provision of a barrier to seal out wetness [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing:** Apply topically to the affected area as needed [US].
- **Administration Instructions:** For external use only. When used for diaper rash, ensure the area is clean and dry before application to seal out moisture.

## 4. Contraindications
White petrolatum must **not** be used on:
- Deep or puncture wounds [US].
- Animal bites [US].
- Serious burns [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **General Warnings:** For external use only [US]. 
- **Ocular Exposure:** Avoid contact with eyes [US].
- **Clinical Monitoring:** Patients should be advised to discontinue use and consult a healthcare professional if the skin condition persists for more than 7 days or if symptoms clear up and recur within a few days [US].
- **Ingestion:** Keep out of reach of children. In case of accidental ingestion, immediate medical attention or contact with a Poison Control Center is required [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects

## 7. Drug Interactions

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** White petrolatum functions as a skin protectant and occlusive agent. It forms an industrial-strength barrier on the stratum corneum, reducing transepidermal water loss (TEWL) and protecting the skin from external irritants (e.g., wind, cold, moisture).
- **Pharmacokinetics:** 
    - **Absorption:** Negligible systemic absorption following topical application to intact skin.
    - **Distribution/Metabolism/Elimination:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Acts locally to provide emollient and protective effects.
