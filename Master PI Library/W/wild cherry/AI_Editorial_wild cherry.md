# Editorial Product Information: Pectin (Oral Demulcent)

## Editorial Note
- Contributing Jurisdictions: United States (FDA)
- Purpose: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of Pectin-based oral demulcent lozenges (including products marketed under the brand names **Lolleez** and **Sootheez**) for use by healthcare professionals. 
- This synthesis is based on official Product Information (Drug Facts) provided for the United States market. It is an editorial summary and does not replace the official PI or "Drug Facts" label required for prescribing or recommending in a specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Pectin (11.5 mg per lozenge/drop)
- **Chemical Class:** Polysaccharide (Galacturonic acid polymer)
- **Brand Names:** **Lolleez** (Pops), **Sootheez** (Drops/Lozenges) [US]
- **ATC Code:** R02AA20 (Other throat preparations)
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Organic Lozenge "Pops": 11.5 mg pectin per pop [US]
    - Organic Throat Drops: 11.5 mg pectin per drop [US]
- **Route of Administration:** Oral (Topical/Oromucosal)
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** BFY LLC [US]

*Note: This document also acknowledges the related product **Tumeez**, which contains **Calcium Carbonate** (200 mg) for use as an antacid; however, the primary focus of this editorial is the oral demulcent (Pectin).*

## 2. Approved Indications
- Temporary relief of minor discomfort and irritation of the throat [US].
- Temporary relief of sore mouth [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Children)
Dosage depends on the specific product formulation (pop vs. drop):

- **Pectin Organic Pops (**Lolleez**):**
    - **Adults and children 3 years of age and older:** Allow one pop to dissolve slowly in the mouth [US].
    - **Frequency:** May be repeated as needed or as directed by a healthcare professional [US].
- **Pectin Organic Drops (**Sootheez**):**
    - **Adults and children 10 years of age and older:** Allow one drop to dissolve slowly in the mouth [US].
    - **Frequency:** May be repeated as needed or as directed by a healthcare professional [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Products should be dissolved slowly in the mouth to ensure adequate contact time with the oropharyngeal mucosa.
- Do not chew or swallow the lozenge whole.

## 4. Contraindications
- Hypersensitivity to pectin or any of the inactive ingredients (e.g., honey, citric acid, various organic fruit concentrates) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### General Warnings
- **Sore Throat Warning:** If a sore throat is severe, persists for more than 2 days, or is accompanied/followed by fever, headache, rash, nausea, or vomiting, a physician should be consulted promptly, as these may be signs of a serious underlying condition [US].
- **Sore Mouth Warning:** If sore mouth symptoms do not improve within 7 days, the patient should consult a dentist or physician [US].

### Monitoring Requirements
- Clinical improvement of throat irritation should be assessed within 48 hours.

### Specific Clinical Precautions
- **Choking Hazard:** Lollipops/pops and drops represent a potential choking hazard, particularly in younger children. 
- **Stop Use and Ask a Doctor If:** 
    - Sore throat does not improve in 7 days [US].
    - Irritation, pain, or redness persists or worsens [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- Not specified in available sources. Pectin is generally recognized as safe (GRAS) for oral consumption at therapeutic doses.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- Not specified in available sources. 
- *Note for related Antacid (Calcium Carbonate):* Antacids may interact with certain prescription drugs. Patients should consult a pharmacist or doctor before use [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Breastfeeding:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Fertility:** Not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
### Paediatric Use
- **Pops (**Lolleez**):** Approved for children 3 years of age and older. For children under 3, a doctor should be consulted [US].
- **Drops (**Sootheez**):** Approved for children 10 years of age and older. For children under 10, a doctor should be consulted [US].

### Elderly Use
- Not specified in available sources; generally used according to adult dosing.

### Renal/Hepatic Impairment
- Not specified in available sources; systemic absorption of pectin is negligible.

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
- Pectin acts as an **oral demulcent**. It forms a protective film over the mucous membranes of the throat (pharynx), providing physical protection from irritation and soothing inflamed tissues.

### Pharmacokinetics
- **Absorption:** Not specified in available sources; pectin is a dietary fibre that typically undergoes minimal systemic absorption when used topically in the oropharynx.
- **Metabolism/Elimination:** Not specified in available sources.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- Store in a dry place [US].
- Maintain at temperatures between 65°F and 75°F (18°C–24°C) [US].
- Keep packaging (pouch, tin, or package) sealed when not in use [US].
