# Editorial Product Information: Phenylephrine HCl, Petrolatum, and Mineral Oil Hemorrhoidal Ointment

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (Drug Facts) provided by the **United States (FDA)**. 
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing or dispensing in any specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** 
    - **Phenylephrine Hydrochloride** (0.25% or 2.5 mg/1 g) [Chemical Class: Sympathomimetic amine / Vasoconstrictor]
    - **Petrolatum** (74.9% or 749 mg/1 g) [Chemical Class: Hydrocarbon mixture / Protectant]
    - **Mineral Oil** (14% or 140 mg/1 g) [Chemical Class: Liquid hydrocarbons / Protectant]
- **Brand Name:** DRS Pharmacy Hemorrhoidal Ointment [US]
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources (Suggested: C05AX03 / C05AA)
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Ointment containing Phenylephrine HCl 0.25%, Petrolatum 74.9%, and Mineral Oil 14% [US]
- **Route of Administration:** Topical (External and/or intrarectal) [US]
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** OL Pharma Tech, LLC (Drs Pharmacy) [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
This combination product is indicated for the following [US]:
- Symptomatic relief of local itching and discomfort associated with haemorrhoids.
- Temporary shrinking of haemorrhoidal tissue and relief of associated burning.
- Provision of a protective coating for the relief of anorectal discomfort.
- Protection of irritated or inflamed anorectal surfaces to facilitate less painful bowel movements.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Adult Dosing
- **Application Frequency:** Apply to the affected area up to 4 times daily.
- **Timing:** Ideally applied in the morning, at night, and after each bowel movement.

### Administration Instructions
- **Preparation:** When practical, cleanse the affected area by patting or blotting with an appropriate cleansing wipe. Dry gently with a tissue or soft cloth prior to application.
- **External Use:** Apply a small amount to the affected area.
- **Intrarectal Use:** The product is labeled for intrarectal use; however, instructions specify **not** to insert the product into the rectum using fingers or any mechanical device/applicator [US].
- **Tube Initial Use:** Puncture the foil seal using the top end of the cap.

## 4. Contraindications
No absolute contraindications are listed in the source document; however, patients with hypersensitivity to any component (including inactive ingredients like lanolin or parabens) should avoid use.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Route Restriction:** For external and/or intrarectal use only [US].
- **Cardiovascular/Endocrine Caution:** Patients should consult a physician before use if they have heart disease, high blood pressure, thyroid disease, or diabetes [US].
- **Urological Caution:** Patients with difficulty in urination due to an enlarged prostate gland should consult a physician before use [US].
- **General Precautions:**
    - Do not exceed the recommended daily dosage unless directed by a physician.
    - Discontinue use and consult a physician if bleeding occurs or if the condition worsens/does not improve within 7 days.

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **General:** Frequency data (e.g., common, rare) is not provided in the source documentation.
- **Serious Effects:**
    - Rectal bleeding (requires medical consultation).
    - Local irritation or hypersensitivity reactions (implied by the warning to stop use if condition worsens).
- **Systemic Effects:** Although topical, phenylephrine absorption may potentially lead to sympathomimetic effects (e.g., increased blood pressure or heart rate), particularly if used in excess of recommended doses.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Antihypertensives:** Patients should consult a healthcare professional if they are presently taking a prescription drug for high blood pressure [US].
- **Antidepressants:** Patients should consult a healthcare professional if they are taking medication for depression (e.g., Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors) [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Patients are advised to ask a health professional before use. No specific US FDA pregnancy category is assigned in the provided source.
- **Breastfeeding:** Patients are advised to ask a health professional before use.
- **Fertility:** Data not specified in available sources.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** For children under 12 years of age, a doctor must be consulted prior to use [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed; however, the cautions regarding cardiovascular disease and prostate enlargement are particularly relevant to this population.
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** 
    - **Phenylephrine HCl:** An alpha-1 adrenergic agonist that acts as a local vasoconstrictor to reduce the swelling (shrinking) of haemorrhoidal tissue.
    - **Petrolatum and Mineral Oil:** Act as emollients and protectants, forming a physical barrier on the skin/mucosa to prevent irritation and water loss.
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified in available sources for this topical formulation.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Localised vasoconstriction and mechanical protection.
