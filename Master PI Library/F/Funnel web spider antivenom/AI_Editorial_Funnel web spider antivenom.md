# Editorial Product Information: Funnel Web Spider Antivenom

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration)**.
- Data from other major jurisdictions (e.g., US, European, UK, Canadian, Japanese) were not provided in the source context and are not specified as having approved this specific rabbit-derived product.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. It is not a substitute for the official PI for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active ingredient:** Funnel web spider antivenom (rabbit). It is a purified immunoglobulin (predominantly IgG) derived from the plasma of rabbits immunised with the venom of the male Sydney funnel web spider (*Atrax robustus*).
- **Brand name(s):** **Funnel Web Spider Antivenom** [Australian].
- **ATC code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage form(s) and strength(s):** 
    - **Powder for injection:** Each vial contains 125 units (U) of antivenom (approximately 100 mg of rabbit immunoglobulin).
    - **Potency:** Standardised to neutralise 1.25 mg of funnel web spider venom per vial.
    - **Composition:** Contains ≤ 810 mg of rabbit plasma protein per vial. Excipients include glycine, sodium chloride, dibasic sodium phosphate, and monobasic sodium phosphate dihydrate [Australian].
- **Route(s) of administration:** Intravenous (IV) injection.
- **Manufacturer/sponsor:** Seqirus Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Systemic Envenoming:** Indicated for the treatment of patients exhibiting clinical manifestations of systemic envenoming following a bite by a funnel web spider [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric):** The recommended initial dose is **2 vials (250 units)**. The dosage is the same for both adults and children [Australian].
- **Titration and Repeat Dosing:** If the clinical effects of the venom are not completely reversed, the initial dose may be repeated after 15 minutes, provided it is safe to do so. Some cases may require additional subsequent doses [Australian].
- **Reconstitution Instructions:** 
    - Reconstitute each vial with 10 mL of Water for Injections. 
    - Gently swirl until fully dissolved; the vial may be inverted to aid dissolution. 
    - A clear to slightly opalescent, colourless solution is typically obtained within 10 minutes.
- **Administration:** Administer via slow intravenous injection. The reconstituted product must be used immediately [Australian].
- **Critical Preparation:** Adrenaline must be prepared and ready for use prior to administration to manage potential rapid-onset anaphylactic reactions. Treatment should occur in an intensive care unit or a setting with immediate access to resuscitation facilities [Australian].

## 4. Contraindications
- **No Absolute Contraindications:** There are no absolute contraindications to the use of this antivenom.
- **Clinical Threshold:** The product should only be administered when there is clear evidence of systemic envenoming with the potential for serious toxic effects [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None specified in available sources.
- **Animal-Derived Products:** As the product is prepared from animal (rabbit) plasma, the risk of transmitting infectious agents, including unknown pathogens, cannot be entirely excluded. Historically, there are no recorded cases of viral transmission with this product [Australian].
- **Hypersensitivity and Anaphylaxis:** Severe allergic reactions, including anaphylactic shock, may occur. Risk is higher in patients with atopy or those previously exposed to rabbit serum. Skin testing for sensitivity is unreliable and is not recommended [Australian].
- **First Aid Interaction:** Pressure bandaging and immobilisation (first aid) may delay the onset of symptoms. Removal of these bandages may precipitate rapid, severe systemic envenoming (e.g., severe pain, vomiting, dyspnoea, hypertension, pulmonary oedema, muscle fasciculations, or coma).
- **Monitoring:** Patients should be observed for signs of envenoming for at least four hours after the bite or following the removal of pressure bandages before deciding against antivenom administration [Australian].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions identified during post-approval use include:
- **Immune system disorders:**
    - **Common (≥1/100 to <1/10):** Allergic reactions, including anaphylactic shock and delayed serum sickness.
- **Skin and subcutaneous tissue disorders:**
    - **Common (≥1/100 to <1/10):** Urticaria, rash.
- **Serious Adverse Effects:**
    - **Serum Sickness:** A delayed reaction common with large volumes of foreign protein. Symptoms include fever, rash, arthralgia, lymphadenopathy, and albuminuria. Less commonly, it may lead to nephritis, neuropathy, or vasculitis. Onset typically occurs days to weeks after administration but may appear within 12 hours in previously sensitised patients [Australian].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic interactions:** No data available.
- **Incompatibilities:** Not assessed or identified at the time of registration [Australian].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Not specified in available sources (No information available). Clinical guidance suggests weighing the risks of untreated envenoming against the potential risks of antivenom [Australian].
- **Breastfeeding:** No data available. A risk-benefit assessment is required [Australian].
- **Fertility:** No data available [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** The dosage for children is identical to the adult dose [Australian].
- **Elderly Use:** No specific data available [Australian].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** No specific data available.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** The antivenom contains specific antibodies that neutralise toxic substances in the venom of *Atrax robustus*. 
- **Pharmacodynamics:** There is clinical evidence that the antivenom is also effective against venom from other funnel web spiders within the *Hadronyche* genus [Australian].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified in available sources.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- No pivotal trial data are described in the available source document [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store below 8°C (Refrigerate). Do not freeze.
- **Protection:** Protect the vial from light.
- **Post-Reconstitution:** Must be used immediately.
- **Latex Safety:** The vial and associated components do not contain natural rubber latex [Australian].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison/Schedule Classification:** 
    - **Prescription Only Medicine (S4)** [Australian].
