# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial: US (FDA).
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising available regulatory data into a single reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis intended for informational purposes and is not a substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (a carrier molecule for long-chain fatty acid transport).
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01 (Not explicitly stated in source, but standard for levocarnitine).
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:**
    - **Intravenous Injection:** 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) single-dose vials [US].
    - **Other Forms:** Levocarnitine is also available in oral tablet (330 mg) and oral solution (1 g/10 mL) formulations [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only (for the injectable formulation).
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Metabolic Disorders:** For the acute and chronic management of patients diagnosed with an inborn error of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:**
    - **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg administered as a slow bolus (over 2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion [US].
    - **Acute Crisis:** A loading dose may be given, followed by an equivalent dose divided over the subsequent 24 hours. Administration should occur every 3 to 4 hours, and never less frequently than every 6 hours [US].
    - **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses typically remain in the 50 mg/kg range, though higher doses (up to 300 mg/kg) have been used [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight) administered as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line after each dialysis session [US].
    - **Dose Adjustments:** Guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy if concentrations are supra-normal [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Inspect for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations between 0.5 mg/mL and 8.0 mg/mL [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **None Known:** There are no listed contraindications in the available source [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Some reactions occur within minutes of IV administration. Discontinue immediately if a reaction occurs [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure disorders. In patients with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity may occur [US].
- **Monitoring:** 
    - Obtain baseline plasma carnitine levels. 
    - Monitor weekly and monthly, including blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations [US].
    - Target plasma free carnitine concentration: 35 to 60 µmol/L [US].
- **Renal Impairment (Oral Dosing):** Chronic high-dose oral administration in patients with severely compromised renal function or ESRD may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are often confounded by the underlying pathology of the patient population.

### Very Common (>10%)
- **General:** Injection site reaction (up to 38%), Pain (up to 35%), Headache (up to 37%), Flu syndrome [US].
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (up to 21%), Hypotension (up to 19%) [US].
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea (up to 35%), Vomiting (up to 21%), Nausea [US].
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (up to 27%), Cough increase, Dyspnea [US].

### Common (1–10%)
- **General:** Abdominal pain, Fever, Chest pain, Accidental injury, Asthenia [US].
- **Cardiovascular:** Tachycardia, Palpitations, Arrhythmia, Atrial fibrillation [US].
- **Metabolic:** Hypercalcemia, Hyperkalemia, Hypervolemia, Peripheral edema, Weight changes [US].
- **Neurological:** Dizziness, Paresthesia, Insomnia, Depression [US].
- **Other:** Body odor, Gastritis, Anemia, Pruritus, Rash, Eye disorders, Urinary tract infection [US].

### Serious Adverse Effects
- **Hypersensitivity:** Anaphylaxis and laryngeal edema [US].
- **Neurological:** New onset or worsening of seizures [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when co-administered with levocarnitine. Frequent monitoring of INR is recommended after initiating treatment or following dose adjustments [US].
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:**
    - **US Category:** Not assigned a letter grade in the current PI, but reproductive studies in rats and rabbits (up to 3.8 times human dose) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine in milk. The risks of excess carnitine intake by the infant should be weighed against the benefits of maternal supplementation [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing is weight-based (50 mg/kg); see Section 3 for specific instructions. Efficacy and safety follow adult parameters for metabolic disorders [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in the available source [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    - Safety and efficacy of **oral** levocarnitine have not been evaluated in renal insufficiency. 
    - **IV** levocarnitine is indicated for ESRD patients on dialysis (see Section 3 for dosing) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into the mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not protein-bound [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites are trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Following a 20 mg/kg IV bolus, the mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours and the terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours. Approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients on hemodialysis receiving 20 mg/kg IV three times weekly post-dialysis, trough concentrations increased from a baseline of ~21 µmol/L to ~190 µmol/L by week 8 [US].
- **Dose-Ranging Studies:** Trials using 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg three times weekly showed a dose-proportional increase in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Precautions:** Avoid excessive heat; protect from freezing [US].
- **Handling:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as it contains no preservatives [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Prescription only (Rx only) [US].
- **Other Jurisdictions:** Not specified in available sources.
