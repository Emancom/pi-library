# Editorial Product Information: Flibanserin

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial synthesis: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising regulatory data into a single clinical reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI or prescribing information required for practice in a specific jurisdiction. Always consult the local approved PI for definitive prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Flibanserin (a non-hormonal multifunctional serotonin agonist antagonist).
- **Brand Names:** **Addyi** [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    - Film-coated tablets: 100 mg [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Sprout Pharmaceuticals, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Hypoactive Sexual Desire Disorder (HSDD):** Indicated for the treatment of premenopausal women with acquired, generalized HSDD. It is characterized by low sexual desire that causes marked distress or interpersonal difficulty and is not due to a co-existing medical or psychiatric condition, relationship problems, or the effects of a medication/drug substance [US].
- **Indication in Postmenopausal Women:** Indicated for women less than 65 years of age with acquired, generalized HSDD [US].

**Limitations of Use:**
- Not indicated for use in men or pediatric patients [US].
- Not indicated to enhance sexual performance [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing:** 100 mg once daily [US].
- **Timing:** Administer orally at bedtime. Dosing at bedtime is mandatory to mitigate risks of hypotension, syncope, and central nervous system (CNS) depression occurring during waking hours [US].
- **Discontinuation Rule:** Treatment should be discontinued after 8 weeks if the patient does not report an improvement in symptoms [US].
- **Missed Dose:** If a dose is missed at bedtime, the patient should be instructed to take the next dose at bedtime the following day. Do not double the dose [US].
- **Sequential Dosing with CYP3A4 Inhibitors:** 
    - If starting flibanserin after using a moderate or strong CYP3A4 inhibitor, wait 2 weeks after the last dose of the inhibitor [US].
    - If starting a moderate or strong CYP3A4 inhibitor after using flibanserin, wait 2 days after the last dose of flibanserin [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Alcohol Use:** Concomitant use with alcohol where flibanserin is taken within 2 hours of alcohol consumption [US].
- **CYP3A4 Inhibitors:** Concomitant use with moderate or strong cytochrome P450 3A4 (CYP3A4) inhibitors [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Contraindicated in patients with any degree of hepatic impairment [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to flibanserin or any of its excipients (reactions including anaphylaxis, angioedema, pruritus, and urticaria have been reported) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning [US]: Hypotension and Syncope in Certain Settings.**
    - Concomitant use with alcohol increases the risk of severe hypotension and syncope.
    - Concomitant use with moderate or strong CYP3A4 inhibitors increases flibanserin concentrations, significantly increasing the risk of hypotension and syncope.
    - Hepatic impairment significantly increases flibanserin levels, also increasing these risks.
- **Alcohol Interaction Management:** Counsel patients to wait at least 2 hours after consuming 1 or 2 standard drinks before taking flibanserin at bedtime. If 3 or more drinks are consumed, the bedtime dose must be skipped [US].
- **CNS Depression:** Flibanserin causes somnolence and sedation. Patients should avoid activities requiring full alertness (e.g., driving or operating machinery) for at least 6 hours after dosing and until they understand their individual response to the drug [US].
- **Hypotension and Syncope with Monotherapy:** Can occur even without co-ingestants. Patients experiencing pre-syncope should lie supine immediately [US].
- **Mammary Tumors:** Malignant mammary tumors were observed in female mice at 3–10 times the clinical exposure; clinical significance in humans is unknown [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
The majority of adverse reactions typically begin within the first 14 days of treatment [US].

| Adverse Reaction | Frequency (Premenopausal) | Frequency (Postmenopausal <65y) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dizziness** | 11.4% | 7.9% |
| **Somnolence** | 11.2% | 7.7% |
| **Nausea** | 10.4% | 6.6% |
| **Fatigue** | 9.2% | 3.0% |
| **Insomnia** | 4.9% | 5.7% |
| **Dry Mouth** | 2.4% | 2.4% |
| **Anxiety** | 1.8% | 2.6% |
| **Constipation** | 1.6% | 2.5% |

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Hypotension and syncope (particularly when combined with alcohol or CYP3A4 inhibitors), appendicitis (reported in 0.2% of flibanserin-treated patients vs 0% in placebo in trials) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Moderate/Strong CYP3A4 Inhibitors:** (e.g., ketoconazole, ritonavir, fluconazole, grapefruit juice) Significantly increase flibanserin exposure. **Contraindicated** [US].
- **Weak CYP3A4 Inhibitors:** (e.g., oral contraceptives, cimetidine) Multiple weak inhibitors may increase flibanserin levels and adverse effect risk [US].
- **Strong CYP2C19 Inhibitors:** (e.g., proton pump inhibitors, SSRIs) May increase flibanserin exposure, increasing risks of hypotension and CNS depression [US].
- **CYP3A4 Inducers:** (e.g., rifampin, St. John’s Wort) Substantially decrease flibanserin levels; concomitant use is not recommended [US].
- **Digoxin:** Flibanserin increases digoxin concentrations by inhibiting P-glycoprotein. Monitor digoxin levels closely [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not specified by letter [US]. 
    - **Data:** No studies in pregnant women. Animal data showed fetal toxicity only in the presence of maternal toxicity. Structural anomalies and fetal loss occurred at exposures >15 times human clinical doses [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Excreted in rat milk; human excretion unknown. Due to the potential for serious adverse reactions (e.g., sedation) in the infant, breastfeeding is not recommended [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies showed a slight increase in the duration of the estrus cycle but no adverse effects on fertility or early embryonic development [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Not indicated; safety and efficacy not established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not indicated for patients ≥65 years of age [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Flibanserin exposure increased 1.1 to 1.2-fold in mild to severe renal impairment. No dose adjustment is generally required, but caution is advised [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Exposure increased 4.5-fold. **Contraindicated** in all stages of hepatic impairment [US].
- **CYP2C19 Poor Metabolizers:** These patients (approx. 2-5% of Caucasians/Africans; 2-15% of Asians) have higher flibanserin exposures (similar to those seen with moderate CYP3A4 inhibitors) and are at increased risk of hypotension and syncope [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** The exact mechanism for treating HSDD is unknown. Flibanserin shows high affinity for serotonin receptors, acting as a 5-HT1A agonist and a 5-HT2A antagonist. It also possesses moderate antagonist activity at 5-HT2B, 5-HT2C, and dopamine D4 receptors [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** 33% absolute bioavailability. Tmax is 0.75 hours (fasted). Food increases AUC but delays Tmax [US].
    - **Distribution:** 98% bound to human serum albumin [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily metabolized by CYP3A4 and to a lesser extent by CYP2C19 [US].
    - **Elimination:** Mean terminal half-life is approximately 11 hours. Excretion is 44% in urine and 51% in feces [US].
- **Race/Ethnicity:** Flibanserin exposure is 1.4-fold higher in **Japanese** women compared to Caucasian women, though this is attributed to lower body weight rather than race [Japanese/US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Premenopausal HSDD:** Efficacy established in three 24-week trials (Studies 1, 2, 3). Flibanserin showed statistically significant improvements vs. placebo in the number of satisfying sexual events (SSEs) and sexual desire scores (FSFI-Desire domain) [US].
- **Postmenopausal HSDD (<65y):** Efficacy established in Study 6, showing significant improvement in SSEs and desire compared to placebo [US].
- **Driving Study:** A crossover study in 83 healthy women showed no adverse effect on driving performance 9 hours after a 100 mg dose [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage:** Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C–30°C (59°F–86°F) [US].
- **Shelf Life:** Not specified in available sources.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources.
