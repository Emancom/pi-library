# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial: **United States (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview of **levocarnitine injection** for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical and regulatory data into a single reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult the current approved PI in their specific region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); a carrier molecule involved in mammalian energy metabolism.
- **Chemical Class:** (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection, USP (Baxter Healthcare Corporation) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US-approved].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
- **General Dosing:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg.
- **Administration:** Administered as a slow bolus injection (over 2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion.
- **Loading Dose:** Often used in severe metabolic crisis, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours.
- **Frequency:** Doses should be administered every 3 or 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours.
- **Maximum Dose:** The highest dose reported in literature is 300 mg/kg [US].

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight.
- **Administration:** Administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line after each dialysis session.
- **Dose Adjustment:** Adjustments should be guided by pre-dialysis (trough) plasma levocarnitine levels. Downward titration (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy.

### General Instructions
- **Monitoring:** It is recommended to obtain plasma carnitine concentrations prior to therapy and monitor weekly/monthly. Target levels for free carnitine should be between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].
- **Visual Inspection:** Parenteral products should be inspected for particulate matter and discoloration before use.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Known Hypersensitivity:** Not specified as a formal contraindication in the provided source, but the source notes "None known" under the contraindications heading [US]. (See Warnings and Precautions for hypersensitivity management).

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None [US].
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have occurred, primarily in ESRD patients on dialysis. Some reactions occurred within minutes of IV administration. Discontinue immediately if a reaction occurs [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with a known seizure disorder, an increase in seizure frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Oral Formulation Caution:** The safety and efficacy of **oral** levocarnitine have not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency. High-dose oral administration in ESRD patients may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are often confounded by the underlying pathology (ESRD or metabolic errors).

### Very Common (>10%)
- **Body as a Whole:** Flu syndrome (25%), infection (15%), injection site reaction (33%), pain (30%), headache (22%), abdominal pain (21% at 10mg dose).
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (20%), hypotension (14%).
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea (16%), vomiting (15%).
- **Nervous System:** Dizziness (13%).
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (23%).

### Common (1–10%)
- **Cardiovascular:** Chest pain, palpitations, tachycardia, vascular disorder.
- **Digestive:** Nausea, dyspepsia, anorexia, melena.
- **Metabolic:** Hypercalcemia, hyperkalemia, hypervolemia, peripheral edema, weight changes.
- **Other:** Asthenia, back pain, fever, rhinitis, dyspnea, pruritus.

### Serious Adverse Effects
- **Anaphylaxis/Hypersensitivity:** Laryngeal edema and bronchospasm.
- **Neurological:** New onset or worsening of seizures.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalised Ratio (INR) have been observed when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** It is recommended to monitor INR levels after initiating levocarnitine or following dose adjustments in patients on warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not assigned in modern labeling, but stated as use "only if clearly needed"]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) revealed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. No adequate/controlled studies exist in pregnant women.
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk concentrations after administration. The benefits of maternal therapy must be weighed against potential risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies showed no impairment of fertility [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing is based on mg/kg (see Section 3). Specific paediatric safety profiles beyond weight-based dosing are not detailed [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** The drug is indicated for ESRD on dialysis. However, the **oral** form is cautioned against in severe renal impairment due to metabolite accumulation [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring carrier molecule required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In patients with metabolic errors or ESRD, levocarnitine helps clear accumulated acyl-CoA compounds by forming acylcarnitines, which are then excreted.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites are trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and [3H]-γ-butyrobetaine (feces).
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours (uncorrected for endogenous levels). Mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Efficacy:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients on hemodialysis receiving 20 mg/kg three times weekly showed a significant increase in plasma carnitine concentrations (trough levels increased from ~21 µmol/L to ~190 µmol/L over 8 weeks).
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** A study (N≈30 per group) comparing 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses confirmed dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Conditions:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat.
- **Stability:** Compatible and stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer's (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) [US].
- **Shelf Life:** Discard any unused portion of an opened vial as the formulation is preservative-free.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as Rx-only. NDC 36000-368-10 (10-vial carton) [US].
