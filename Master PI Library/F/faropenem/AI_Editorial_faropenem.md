# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial synthesis is derived from the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical and regulatory data into a single reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific Product Information required for prescribing within a particular region. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP).
- **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule/Amino acid derivative.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter Healthcare Corporation) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** For the acute and chronic management of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US-approved].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance hemodialysis [US-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    - The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion [US].
    - In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose is often administered, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours.
    - Dosing frequency should be every 3 or 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours [US].
    - Subsequent daily doses typically range around 50 mg/kg, though doses as high as 300 mg/kg have been utilised [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - The recommended starting dose is 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight) [US].
    - Administration should occur as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter or discoloration before use.
- **Compatibility:** Levocarnitine injection is stable for up to 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) when diluted in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s solution in PVC bags (concentrations ranging from 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) [US].

### Monitoring and Adjustment
- **Plasma Monitoring:** It is recommended to obtain plasma carnitine levels prior to therapy. Weekly and monthly monitoring is advised; the target free carnitine concentration is generally between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].
- **ESRD Dose Titration:** Downward dose adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough (pre-dialysis) levels [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Known Hypersensitivity:** No contraindications are currently listed in the available US source, though caution is required regarding hypersensitivity reactions.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. These may occur within minutes of injection. In the event of a severe reaction, the drug must be discontinued and appropriate therapy initiated [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with a known history, an increase in seizure frequency or severity may occur [US].
- **Renal Insufficiency (Oral Formulation Precaution):** While this PI describes the injectable form, clinicians should note that chronic high-dose *oral* levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites, trimethylamine (TMA) and trimethylamine-N-oxide (TMAO) [US].
- **Monitoring:** Regular monitoring of blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations is recommended [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are often confounded by the underlying pathology of metabolic disorders or ESRD.

### Common Adverse Effects (≥5% frequency in ESRD trials)
- **Body as a Whole:** Abdominal pain (9%), asthenia (9%), chest pain (12%), fever (7%), headache (22%), injection site reaction (33%), pain (30%) [US].
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (20%), hypotension (14%), tachycardia (6%) [US].
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea (16%), nausea (8%), vomiting (15%) [US].
- **Nervous System:** Dizziness (13%), paresthesia (5%), depression (5%) [US].
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (23%), cough increase (9%), dyspnea (7%), rhinitis (9%) [US].

### Serious and Post-Marketing Reactions
- **Neurologic:** Seizures [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, bronchospasm [US].
- **Other:** Transient nausea and vomiting; body odor and gastritis (less frequent) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalised Ratio (INR) have occurred when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after any dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not explicitly lettered, but reproductive studies in rats/rabbits (3.8x human dose) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. 
    - **Recommendation:** Levocarnitine should be used during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine is a normal component of human milk. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk concentrations following exogenous administration. The risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant must be weighed against the benefits of maternal supplementation [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility was found in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing recommendations for metabolic disorders apply to paediatric patients (see Section 3) [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Specific clinical data for patients aged 65 and over were not detailed in the available source to determine if they respond differently from younger subjects [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    - **IV Administration:** Specifically indicated for ESRD patients on dialysis.
    - **Oral Caution:** Safety and efficacy of the oral form have not been evaluated in renal insufficiency; see Section 5 regarding metabolite accumulation [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring carrier molecule required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane for beta-oxidation and subsequent energy production [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Levocarnitine clears accumulated toxic acyl-CoA compounds by forming acylcarnitine, which is rapidly excreted [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Following IV bolus (20 mg/kg), approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. The mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Efficacy:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients (20 mg/kg 3x weekly post-dialysis) demonstrated that trough concentrations increased from 21.3 µmol/L at baseline to 190 µmol/L at 8 weeks.
- **Dose-Ranging:** Studies comparing 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses in dialysis patients confirmed dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine levels over 12 and 24 weeks [US].
- **Outcome Note:** While efficacy in increasing carnitine concentrations is proven, the impact on specific clinical outcomes in the ESRD population has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Precautions:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat.
- **Handling:** Discard any unused portion of an opened vial as the formulation is preservative-free [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources (US context).
