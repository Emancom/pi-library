# Editorial Product Information: Follitropin delta

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided from the following jurisdiction: **Australia (Therapeutic Goods Administration)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical data and regulatory requirements. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific PI. Clinicians must refer to the current approved PI in their specific region for prescribing purposes.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Follitropin delta (rhu), a recombinant human follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH).
- **Chemical Class:** Gonadotropin. It is produced in a human cell line (PER.C6®) using recombinant DNA technology.
- **Brand Name:** **REKOVELLE** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** G03GA10.
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** Solution for injection in a pre-filled multidose pen. [Australian]:
    - 12 micrograms in 0.36 mL
    - 36 micrograms in 1.08 mL
    - 72 micrograms in 2.16 mL
- **Route of Administration:** Subcutaneous injection.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Ferring Pharmaceuticals [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Controlled Ovarian Stimulation (COS):** Indicated for the development of multiple follicles in women undergoing assisted reproductive technologies (ART), including in vitro fertilisation (IVF) or intracytoplasmic sperm injection (ICSI) cycles [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing Principles
- **Individualisation:** Dosage must be individualised for each patient based on serum anti-Müllerian hormone (AMH) concentration and body weight.
- **Measurement Units:** Dosing is in **micrograms**, not International Units (IU). The microgram dose for **REKOVELLE** is specific and cannot be applied to other gonadotropins.
- **Supervision:** Treatment must be initiated by a physician experienced in fertility treatment.

### First Treatment Cycle
- **Dose Calculation:** The daily dose is determined by the patient’s recent (within 12 months) AMH level (measured by Roche Elecsys® AMH Plus or Beckman Coulter Access AMH Advanced assays) and body weight (measured just prior to stimulation).
- **Dosing Algorithm [Australian]:**
    - **AMH < 15 pmol/L:** Fixed dose of 12 micrograms/day regardless of body weight.
    - **AMH ≥ 15 pmol/L:** Dose ranges from 0.19 to 0.10 micrograms/kg, decreasing as AMH concentration increases.
- **Maximum Daily Dose:** 12 micrograms for the first cycle.
- **Rounding:** Doses are rounded to the nearest 0.33 micrograms to match the pen scale.

### Subsequent Treatment Cycles
- **Adjustments:** The dose may be maintained or adjusted based on the previous cycle’s ovarian response. 
- **Maximum Daily Dose:** 24 micrograms for subsequent cycles.
- **Dose Titration:** 
    - **Hypo-response:** Increase dose by 25% or 50%.
    - **Hyper-response:** Decrease dose by 20% or 33%.
    - **Previous OHSS:** Decrease dose by 33%.

### Administration Instructions
- **Timing:** Initiate on day 2 or 3 of the menstrual cycle (GnRH antagonist protocol) or approximately 2 weeks after GnRH agonist down-regulation.
- **Site:** Subcutaneous injection, preferably in the abdominal wall.
- **Duration:** Continue until adequate follicular development (typically ≥3 follicles ≥17 mm) is achieved, usually by day 9 or 10.
- **Final Maturation:** Trigger with a single injection of 250 mcg hCG or 5,000 IU hCG.

## 4. Contraindications
- Hypersensitivity to the active substance or excipients.
- Tumours of the hypothalamus or pituitary gland.
- Ovarian enlargement or cysts not caused by polycystic ovarian syndrome (PCOS).
- Gynaecological haemorrhages of unknown aetiology.
- Ovarian, uterine, or mammary carcinoma.
- Pregnancy and lactation [Australian].
- **REKOVELLE** must not be used where an effective response cannot be obtained (e.g., primary ovarian failure, malformations of sexual organs, or uterine fibroids incompatible with pregnancy).

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Ovarian Hyperstimulation Syndrome (OHSS):** A significant risk of COS. Requires careful monitoring of follicular development via ultrasound and/or serum oestradiol. 
    - **Early OHSS:** Occurs within 9 days of triggering maturation.
    - **Late OHSS:** Occurs 10+ days after trigger, often associated with pregnancy.
    - **Management:** If response is excessive (>35 follicles ≥12 mm), withhold hCG and cancel the cycle.
- **Thromboembolic Events:** Risk is increased in women with history, severe obesity (BMI >30 kg/m²), or thrombophilia. Gonadotropin therapy further elevates this risk.
- **Ovarian Torsion:** Reported in ART cycles; may be associated with OHSS or previous surgery.
- **Multi-foetal Pregnancy and Birth:** Increased risk of multiple births; patients should be advised of maternal and perinatal risks.
- **Pregnancy Loss:** Incidence of miscarriage/abortion is higher in ART than natural conception.
- **Ectopic Pregnancy:** Increased risk in women with a history of tubal disease.
- **Monitoring:** Patients should be evaluated for hypothyroidism and hyperprolactinaemia before starting treatment.

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Frequency of Adverse Drug Reactions [Australian]
| Frequency | Adverse Event |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Headache, pelvic pain, nausea |
| **Common (1–10%)** | OHSS, pelvic discomfort, adnexa uteri pain, fatigue |
| **Uncommon (0.1–1%)** | Somnolence, dizziness, diarrhoea, vomiting, constipation, abdominal discomfort, vaginal haemorrhage, breast complaints (pain, swelling, tenderness) |

### Serious Adverse Effects
- Severe OHSS, which may rarely be complicated by ovarian torsion or thromboembolic events (e.g., pulmonary embolism, ischaemic stroke, myocardial infarction).

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Formal Studies:** No interaction studies have been performed.
- **GnRH Agonists:** Concomitant use for pituitary desensitisation may require a higher total dose of **REKOVELLE** to achieve adequate response.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Australian Category D:** Contraindicated. While no teratogenic risk is reported in clinical use with gonadotropins, there are no data on inadvertent exposure to **REKOVELLE**.
- **Breastfeeding:** Contraindicated [Australian]. It is unknown if follitropin delta is excreted in human milk.
- **Fertility:** Indicated for use in infertility.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** No relevant use in the paediatric population.
- **Elderly Use:** No relevant use in patients >65 years; safety and efficacy not established.
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Safety, efficacy, and pharmacokinetics have not been established in these populations.
- **Advanced Age (>40 years):** Limited clinical experience.

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
- Follitropin delta is a recombinant FSH. The primary effect of parenteral FSH is the development of multiple mature follicles.
- Unlike CHO-derived FSH, **REKOVELLE** contains both α2,3 and α2,6-linked sialic acid, reflecting its human cell line origin.

### Pharmacokinetics
- **Absorption:** Absolute bioavailability is approximately 64%. Peak concentration (Tmax) occurs at ~10 hours after daily dosing.
- **Distribution:** Steady-state volume of distribution is ~9 L.
- **Metabolism:** Expected to be eliminated via the kidneys; ~9% excreted unchanged.
- **Elimination:** Terminal half-life is ~28 hours after multiple doses. Clearance is 0.3 L/h.

### Pharmacodynamics
- Compared to follitropin alfa, **REKOVELLE** shows higher FSH exposure and higher ovarian response (oocytes retrieved, oestradiol levels) for the same IU dose in animal models. Clinical data suggests 10 mcg of **REKOVELLE** provides a similar response to 150 IU of follitropin alfa.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESTHER-1:** A randomised, assessor-blinded trial (n=1,326) showed **REKOVELLE** (individualised dose) was non-inferior to follitropin alfa (fixed 150 IU dose) regarding ongoing pregnancy (30.7% vs 31.6%) and implantation rates. It demonstrated a lower incidence of excessive ovarian response and OHSS preventive interventions in the **REKOVELLE** arm.
- **ESTHER-2:** Assessed immunogenicity across repeated cycles; anti-FSH antibody incidence was low (1.1%) and comparable to follitropin alfa, with no neutralising capacity or impact on response.
- **BEYOND Trial:** Compared GnRH agonist vs GnRH antagonist protocols. Oocyte yield was higher with GnRH agonist protocols, though stimulation duration was longer.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Unopened:** Store in a refrigerator (2°C – 8°C). Do not freeze. Protect from light.
- **Temperature Excursion:** May be stored at ≤25°C for up to 3 months once during shelf life; must be discarded if not used after this period.
- **Post-First Use:** Store at ≤25°C for a maximum of 28 days.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
