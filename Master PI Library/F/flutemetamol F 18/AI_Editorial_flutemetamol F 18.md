# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial is based on official Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdiction: United States (Food and Drug Administration).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial synthesis of the clinical properties, safety, and administration of levocarnitine injection for use by healthcare professionals.
- **Note:** This editorial synthesis is not a substitute for the official PI and should be used in conjunction with local prescribing guidelines.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP).
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection [United States].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [United States].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [United States].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Indicated for the acute and chronic management of patients with inborn metabolic errors resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [United States].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance dialysis [United States].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Administration
- Administer via slow intravenous bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion.
- Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter or discoloration prior to use.

### Metabolic Disorders
- **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg intravenously.
- **Loading Dose:** In cases of severe metabolic crisis, a loading dose may be given, followed by an equivalent dose split over the subsequent 24 hours.
- **Dosing Frequency:** Administer every 3 or 4 hours; intervals should never be less than every 6 hours.
- **Maintenance:** Subsequent daily doses are typically 50 mg/kg, though higher doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilised based on clinical requirement.
- **Monitoring:** Obtain baseline plasma carnitine levels. Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma carnitine (target free carnitine: 35 to 60 µmol/L) is recommended.

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight.
- **Timing:** Administer as a slow 2 to 3 minute bolus into the venous return line following each dialysis session.
- **Adjustments:** Dose initiation may be prompted by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levels <40 to 50 µmol/L. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be implemented as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough concentrations.

## 4. Contraindications
- None known at the time of publication [United States].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions may occur within minutes of administration. Discontinue immediately if a severe reaction occurs.
- **Seizure Risk:** Levocarnitine may increase the frequency or severity of seizures in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity.
- **Oral Formulation Restriction:** The safety and efficacy of **oral** levocarnitine have not been established in patients with renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in these patients may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [United States].

**Post-Marketing Experience:**
- **Neurologic:** Seizures.
- **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea and vomiting; body odor and gastritis (less frequent).
- **Immune:** Anaphylaxis and bronchospasm.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalised Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is used concomitantly with warfarin. Monitor INR levels at treatment initiation and following dose adjustments [United States].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [United States]: Category not specified by letter, but reproductive studies in rats and rabbits (up to 3.8 times human dose) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed.
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine is a normal component of human milk. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk concentrations following exogenous administration. Risks to the infant from excess carnitine intake must be weighed against maternal benefit. Discontinuation of nursing or the drug may be considered [United States].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [United States].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing is weight-based (50 mg/kg) as detailed in Section 3.
- **Geriatric Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Primarily indicated for ESRD; however, oral formulations should be avoided in this population due to metabolite accumulation [United States].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring carrier molecule essential for mammalian energy metabolism. it facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane for beta-oxidation and subsequent ATP production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not protein-bound.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and gamma-butyrobetaine (feces).
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. Terminal elimination half-life is approximately 17.4 hours.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Administration to ESRD patients on hemodialysis results in significant increases in plasma carnitine concentrations.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** Clinical data from a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients showed that 20 mg/kg doses three times weekly post-dialysis increased mean pre-dialysis trough levels from 21.3 µmol/L to 190 µmol/L after 8 weeks.
- **Dose-Ranging Data:** A study comparing 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses demonstrated dose-proportionate increases in trough carnitine levels over 12 and 24 weeks.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat.
- **Integrity:** Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as it contains no preservative.
- **Stability:** Compatible with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s for up to 24 hours at room temperature in PVC bags.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [United States].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not applicable for United States jurisdiction.
