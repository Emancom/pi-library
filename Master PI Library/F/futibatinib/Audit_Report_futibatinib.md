# Originality Audit Report: futibatinib

## Audit Summary
- **Originality Score:** 88/100  
- **Verbatim Copied Passages Found:** 2
- **Overall Assessment:** Pass

## Verbatim Copied Passages
1. **Ocular Monitoring Schedule**  
   - **Editorial Text:** "Perform a comprehensive ophthalmic exam, including optical coherence tomography (OCT) of the macula, before starting therapy, every 2 months for the first 6 months, and every 3 months thereafter."
   - **Source Match:** Document 2 (FDA), Section 5.1; Document 1 (TGA), Section 4.4.
   - **Location in Editorial:** Section 4, Ocular Toxicity.

2. **Phosphate Management Thresholds**  
   - **Editorial Text:** "...initiate phosphate-lowering therapy when levels reach ≥5.5 mg/dL. Dose adjustment or interruption is required for levels >7 mg/dL."
   - **Source Match:** Document 2 (FDA), Section 5.2; Document 1 (TGA), Table 2.
   - **Location in Editorial:** Section 4, Hyperphosphataemia and Soft Tissue Mineralization.

## Close Paraphrases
- **Embryofetal Toxicity:** The editorial states, "Female patients of reproductive potential and male patients with female partners of reproductive potential must use effective contraception during treatment and for one week following the final dose." This closely mirrors the "Contraception" subsections in FDA Section 8.3 and TGA Section 4.6.
- **Pharmacokinetics (Absorption):** The description of the high-fat/high-calorie meal effect ("reduces Cmax by 42% but does not significantly affect the total exposure (AUC)") uses the exact percentages and parameters from FDA Section 12.3, though rearranged into a sentence.
- **Adverse Effects List:** The list of "Very Common" reactions is a direct extraction of the ≥20% frequency data tables found in both documents, though the editorial correctly groups them by "Very Common" terminology (TGA style) while citing FDA for serious reaction rates.

## Originality Strengths
- **Introduction/Synthesis:** The editorial note at the beginning is entirely original and provides clear context for the harmonization of the two documents.
- **Contraindications:** The editorial successfully interprets the TGA's specific mention of lactose monohydrate as a hypersensitivity risk, contrasting it with the FDA’s "None" status.
- **Storage Conditions:** Section 11 is a high-quality synthesis that preserves the distinct requirements of both agencies (25°C for TGA vs. 20°C-25°C for FDA) without copying the exact formatting of the source labels.
- **Administration Instructions:** The instructions regarding missed doses and vomiting were rewritten into a clear, concise paragraph rather than the bulleted lists found in the source PIs.

## Differences from Source PIs
- **Merged Strengths:** The editorial identifies that the 16 mg tablet is currently unique to the US market [FDA], whereas the Australian document only lists the 4 mg strength.
- **Regulatory Clarification:** The editorial adds the distinction between "provisional approval" (Australia) and "accelerated approval" (USA), which is not explicitly contrasted in either individual source.
- **Structural Optimization:** The editorial moves "Excipients" into "Warnings and Precautions" to emphasize the clinical impact of lactose intolerance, whereas the source PIs list this under "Description" or "Qualitative Composition."
- **Selective Detail:** The editorial omits the highly specific clinical trial demographic breakdowns (e.g., specific percentages of Asian or White patients) in favor of a higher-level safety summary.

## Recommendation
**PASS**

The editorial is a high-quality, original synthesis. While two passages regarding monitoring schedules and clinical thresholds are verbatim, these are considered "regulatory boilerplate" or "fixed clinical parameters" where deviation in wording could lead to medical inaccuracy or non-compliance. The writer has successfully restructured the information, merged data points from two different jurisdictions, and maintained an independent "editorial voice" throughout the remainder of the document. No revision is required.
