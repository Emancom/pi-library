# Editorial Product Information: Follitropin alfa (Bemfola)

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) document from the following jurisdiction:
    - **Australian (TGA)**: BEMFOLA (follitropin alfa (rch)) Solution for Injection.
- **Purpose**: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical data and regulatory specifics into a single reference.
- **Disclaimer**: This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific Product Information required for prescribing. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority's approved PI for full prescribing details.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient**: Follitropin alfa (rch) (Recombinant human follicle stimulating hormone).
- **Chemical Class**: Glycoprotein hormone produced by recombinant DNA technology (Chinese Hamster Ovary cell line).
- **Brand Name(s)**: **Bemfola** [Australian].
- **ATC Code**: G03GA05.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s)**: Clear, colourless solution for injection in pre-filled, disposable pens:
    - 75 IU/0.125 mL (5.5 micrograms)
    - 150 IU/0.25 mL (11 micrograms)
    - 225 IU/0.375 mL (16.5 micrograms)
    - 300 IU/0.5 mL (22 micrograms)
    - 450 IU/0.75 mL (33 micrograms)
- **Route of Administration**: Subcutaneous (SC).
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor**: Gedeon Richter Australia Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
### Adult Women
- **Anovulatory Infertility**: Treatment of women (WHO Group II) who have not responded to clomiphene citrate or where clomiphene is contraindicated.
- **Assisted Reproductive Technologies (ART)**: Controlled ovarian hyperstimulation to induce the development of multiple follicles in patients undergoing procedures such as *in vitro* fertilisation (IVF).
- **Severe LH and FSH Deficiency**: Stimulation of follicular development in association with a luteinising hormone (LH) preparation. Clinical trial criteria for these patients included endogenous serum LH levels <1.2 IU/L.

### Adult Men
- **Spermatogenesis Stimulation**: Indicated in combination with human chorionic gonadotrophin (hCG) for men with hypogonadotrophic hypogonadism in whom hCG therapy alone has been ineffective.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Instructions
- **Supervision**: Therapy should be initiated by a physician experienced in treating fertility disorders.
- **Administration**: Subcutaneous injection. Rotate sites daily to prevent lipoatrophy.
- **Self-Administration**: Only for motivated, well-trained patients with access to expert advice.

### Women with Anovulatory Infertility
- **Initial Dose**: Typically 75–150 IU daily.
- **Titration**: Increase by 37.5 IU (minimum) up to 75 IU at 7 or 14-day intervals to achieve an adequate response.
- **Maximum Duration**: Abandon cycle if no adequate response is seen after 5 weeks.
- **Final Maturation**: Once optimal response is achieved, a single injection of 250 micrograms r-hCG or 5,000–10,000 IU u-hCG is given 24–48 hours after the last dose.

### Women Undergoing ART
- **Initial Dose**: 150–225 IU daily, starting on day 2 or 3 of the cycle.
- **Titration**: Adjust based on response (ultrasound/oestradiol); maximum dose usually not to exceed 450 IU daily.
- **Final Maturation**: Single injection of hCG (as above) 24–48 hours after the last dose.

### Women with Severe LH and FSH Deficiency
- **Regimen**: 75–150 IU FSH administered simultaneously with 75 IU lutropin alfa daily.
- **Titration**: Dose increases (if needed) should occur at 7–14 day intervals in increments of 37.5–75 IU.

### Men with Hypogonadotrophic Hypogonadism
- **Pre-treatment**: hCG alone until testosterone levels reach the eugonadal range (>9–10 nmol/L).
- **Combination Therapy**: **Bemfola** 150 IU three times a week plus hCG for a minimum of 4 months. Treatment may continue for 18–24 months to achieve spermatogenesis.

## 4. Contraindications
- Hypersensitivity to follitropin alfa or any excipients.
- Tumours of the hypothalamus or pituitary gland.
- **In Women**:
    - Pregnancy and lactation.
    - Ovarian enlargement or cysts of unknown aetiology.
    - Gynaecological haemorrhage of unknown aetiology.
    - Ovarian, uterine, or breast carcinoma.
    - Primary ovarian failure.
    - Malformations of sexual organs or fibroid tumours incompatible with pregnancy.
- **In Men**:
    - Primary testicular failure (elevated gonadotrophin levels).
    - Infertility disorders other than hypogonadotrophic hypogonadism.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Ovarian Hyperstimulation Syndrome (OHSS)**: A serious medical event involving marked ovarian enlargement, high sex steroids, and increased vascular permeability (fluid accumulation in peritoneal, pleural, or pericardial cavities). 
    - *Monitoring*: Regular ultrasound and oestradiol levels are mandatory. 
    - *Management*: If OHSS is suspected, withhold hCG. Advise patients to refrain from intercourse for at least 4 days or use barrier methods.
- **Thromboembolic Events**: Increased risk of thrombophlebitis, pulmonary embolism, or stroke, particularly in women with risk factors or in association with OHSS.
- **Multiple Pregnancies**: Higher incidence compared to natural conception; most are twins. Increased risk of maternal and perinatal complications.
- **Pregnancy Loss**: Higher rates of miscarriage or abortion in patients undergoing ovulation induction/ART compared to the general population.
- **Porphyria**: May increase the risk of acute attacks in patients with porphyria or a family history.
- **Other Evaluations**: Rule out hypothyroidism, adrenocortical deficiency, and hyperprolactinaemia before starting therapy.

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Treatment in General
| Frequency | Adverse Effect |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Very Common (>10%)** | Injection site reactions (pain, erythema, haematoma, swelling), headache, ovarian cysts (women). |
| **Common (1–10%)** | Mild/moderate OHSS, abdominal pain/distension/discomfort, nausea, vomiting, diarrhoea, dizziness. |
| **Uncommon (0.1–1%)** | Severe OHSS. |
| **Rare (<0.1%)** | Thromboembolism, ectopic pregnancy, adnexal torsion (complication of ovarian enlargement). |
| **Very Rare (<0.01%)** | Hypersensitivity/anaphylaxis, exacerbation of asthma. |

### Treatment in Men
- **Common (1–10%)**: Gynaecomastia, acne, weight gain.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Ovulation Stimulants**: Concomitant use with other agents (e.g., hCG, clomiphene) may potentiate the follicular response.
- **Pituitary Desensitisation**: Concurrent use of GnRH agonists or antagonists may necessitate higher doses of **Bemfola** to achieve an adequate response.
- **Clinically Significant Interactions**: None reported to date.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy**: **Contraindicated**. [Australian Category D]. Evidence of embryotoxicity and fetotoxicity in animal studies (dystocia, postimplantation loss at doses >5 IU/kg/day).
- **Breastfeeding**: **Contraindicated**. It is unknown if follitropin alfa is excreted in human milk. A decision must be made to either discontinue breastfeeding or the drug.
- **Fertility**: Indicated for use in infertility.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use**: Not indicated; should not be used in children.
- **Elderly Use**: Not indicated; should not be used in the elderly.
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment**: Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action**: Follitropin alfa is a recombinant human follicle stimulating hormone. In women, it stimulates ovarian follicular growth. In men, it stimulates spermatogenesis (when combined with hCG).
- **Pharmacokinetics**:
    - **Bioavailability**: Approximately 70% following SC administration.
    - **Distribution**: Initial half-life of ~2 hours; distributed to extracellular fluid.
    - **Metabolism/Elimination**: Terminal half-life of ~24 hours. One-eighth of the dose is excreted in the urine.
    - **Accumulation**: 3-fold accumulation occurs within 3–4 days of repeated dosing.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Bioequivalence**: Therapeutic equivalence between **Bemfola** and the reference product (Gonal-f) was demonstrated in a multi-national study involving women undergoing ART. The primary endpoint (number of oocytes retrieved) fell within the pre-defined equivalence range of ±2.9 oocytes.
- **Anovulatory Infertility**: Studies showed cumulative ovulation rates were comparable between follitropin alfa and urinary-derived FSH.
- **Male Hypogonadism**: Phase III studies demonstrated that 63% of men achieved a sperm density of ≥1.5 x 10⁶/mL within 18 months when treated with follitropin alfa and hCG.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Refrigeration**: Store at 2°C to 8°C. Do not freeze.
- **Temporary Storage**: Before opening and within shelf life, may be stored at or below 25°C for up to 3 months. Discard if not used after this period.
- **Protection**: Store in the original package to protect from light.
- **Post-Injection**: Pens are for single-patient use; discard immediately after use.
