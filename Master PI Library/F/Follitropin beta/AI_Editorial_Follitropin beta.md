# Editorial Product Information: Follitropin beta

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **Australian (Therapeutic Goods Administration)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical data into a concise, professional reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific PI. Clinicians must consult the local approved PI before prescribing or administering this medication.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Follitropin beta (recombinant human follicle-stimulating hormone [recFSH])
- **Chemical Class:** Gonadotropin
- **Brand Name(s):** **Puregon** [Australian]
- **ATC Code:** G03GA06
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Solution for injection in cartridges for use with a specific pen injector: 300 IU/0.36 mL, 600 IU/0.72 mL, and 900 IU/1.08 mL [Australian].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Subcutaneous injection.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Organon Pharma Pty Ltd [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Female Infertility:**
    - Treatment of anovulatory infertility (including polycystic ovarian disease) in women who have not responded to clomiphene citrate.
    - Controlled ovarian hyperstimulation (COH) to induce multiple follicular development in medically assisted reproduction programs (e.g., *in vitro* fertilisation [IVF], gamete intra-fallopian transfer [GIFT]).
- **Male Infertility:**
    - Treatment of deficient spermatogenesis resulting from hypogonadotrophic hypogonadism.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Female)
Dosage must be individualised based on ovarian response, monitored via ultrasound and serum oestradiol levels.

- **Anovulation/Follicle Ripening:**
    - **Initial Dose:** 50 IU daily for at least seven days.
    - **Titration:** If no response, increase the daily dose gradually until follicle growth or oestradiol levels indicate an adequate response (daily oestradiol ascent of 40–100% is optimal).
    - **Maintenance:** Maintain the effective dose until pre-ovulatory conditions (dominant follicle ≥18 mm; oestradiol 300–900 pg/mL) are met.
    - **Final Maturation:** Discontinue **Puregon** and administer human chorionic gonadotrophin (hCG) (5,000–10,000 IU) to induce ovulation.
- **Controlled Ovarian Hyperstimulation (ART):**
    - **Initial Dose:** 100–225 IU for the first four days.
    - **Titration:** Adjust based on response. Maintenance doses typically range from 75–300 IU daily.
    - **Final Maturation:** When ≥3 follicles measure 16–20 mm and oestradiol response is adequate, induce maturation with hCG (5,000–10,000 IU). Oocyte retrieval is performed 34–35 hours later.

### General Dosing (Male)
- **Standard Regimen:** 75 IU daily or 2–3 times per week.
- **Co-administration:** Must be combined with hCG (1,000–2,000 IU 2–3 times per week).
- **Duration:** Continue for at least three months before expecting spermatogenesis improvement.

### Administration Instructions
- Administer subcutaneously via a pen injector.
- Rotate injection sites to prevent lipoatrophy.
- Self-administration may be performed by patients or partners following thorough training.

## 4. Contraindications
- Hypersensitivity to follitropin beta or any excipients.
- Tumours of the ovary, breast, uterus, testes, pituitary gland, or hypothalamus.
- Pregnancy [Australian].
- Unexplained vaginal bleeding.
- Ovarian cysts or enlargement not related to polycystic ovarian disease (PCOD).
- Primary ovarian failure or primary testicular failure.
- Malformations of reproductive organs or uterine fibroids incompatible with pregnancy.
- Use in children or the elderly [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Ovarian Hyperstimulation Syndrome (OHSS):** A serious medical event distinct from uncomplicated ovarian enlargement. Risk increases with high doses and in patients with >18 follicles ≥11 mm. If OHSS is suspected, withhold hCG. Severe OHSS is life-threatening and requires hospitalisation and fluid/electrolyte management.
- **Multiple Births:** Increased risk of multiple gestations (mostly twins). In ART, risk is related to the number of embryos transferred.
- **Thromboembolism:** Gonadotropin therapy may increase the risk of venous or arterial thromboembolism, particularly in women with pre-existing risk factors (obesity, family history, thrombophilia).
- **Ovarian Torsion:** Reported following gonadotropin treatment; often associated with OHSS or previous pelvic surgery.
- **Antibiotic Traces:** May contain traces of streptomycin and/or neomycin; use caution in patients with known sensitivities.
- **Ectopic Pregnancy:** Higher incidence in infertile women undergoing ART; early ultrasound is recommended.
- **Monitoring:** Follicular development must be monitored via transvaginal ultrasound and serum oestradiol. For males, semen analysis is recommended 4–6 months into treatment.

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Female Patients
| Frequency | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Common** (1–10%) | Headache, abdominal distension, abdominal pain, OHSS, pelvic pain, injection site reactions (bruising, pain, redness, swelling). |
| **Uncommon** (0.1–1%) | Abdominal discomfort, constipation, diarrhoea, nausea, breast complaints (tenderness/pain), metrorrhagia, ovarian cysts, ovarian torsion, uterine enlargement, vaginal haemorrhage, generalised hypersensitivity. |

### Male Patients
| Frequency | Adverse Reaction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Common** (1–10%) | Headache, acne, rash, epididymal cyst, gynecomastia, injection site induration. |

- **Serious Effects:** Anaphylactic reactions have been reported in post-marketing surveillance (frequency unknown). Thromboembolic events may occur in rare instances.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Clomiphene citrate:** Concurrent use may enhance the follicular response.
- **GnRH agonists:** Pituitary desensitisation may necessitate higher doses of **Puregon** to achieve an adequate response.
- **Interactions with Laboratory Tests:** No known effects.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Australian Category B2:** Follitropin beta is not intended for use during pregnancy. While clinical data are insufficient to exclude teratogenicity if inadvertent exposure occurs, animal studies have not been performed.
- **Breastfeeding:** No data available regarding excretion in human milk. Due to the high molecular weight of the protein, it is unlikely to be excreted or absorbed by the infant's GI tract, but effects on milk production are unknown.
- **Fertility:** Indicated for the induction of ovulation and spermatogenesis.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** No relevant indication for use in children [Australian].
- **Elderly Use:** Not indicated; safety and efficacy not established in this population [Australian].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Recombinant FSH binds to FSH receptors in the ovaries (granulosa cells) and testes (Sertoli cells). In females, it is essential for follicular growth and steroidogenesis. In males, it stimulates the Sertoli cells to support spermatogenesis.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Peak plasma concentrations reached ~12 hours post-SC injection.
    - **Distribution:** Similar to endogenous FSH.
    - **Elimination:** Elimination half-life is approximately 40 hours (range 12–70 hours).
    - **Steady State:** Levels remain elevated for 24–48 hours; repeated administration leads to 1.5–2.5 times higher concentrations compared to a single dose.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Bioequivalence:** A study demonstrated that **Puregon** solution administered via a pen injector is bioequivalent to the freeze-dried cake formulation administered via a conventional syringe (after dose correction for syringe void volume) [Australian].
- **Pre-marketing Data:** Clinical trials involving 1,074 patients showed OHSS in 5.2% and ectopic pregnancy in 2.2% of subjects [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Pharmacy Storage:** Store at 2°C to 8°C (Refrigerate. Do not freeze). Protect from light.
- **Patient Storage:** Store at 2°C to 8°C until expiry. Alternatively, may be stored below 25°C for a single period of up to 3 months.
- **After First Use:** The cartridge may be stored below 25°C for a maximum of 28 days. Do not freeze.
