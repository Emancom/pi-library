# Editorial Product Information: Fostamatinib

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdiction: **US (FDA)**. 
- Information for other major jurisdictions (e.g., Australian, European, Canadian, Japanese) was not provided in the source material and is therefore not included in this harmonised overview. 
- **Purpose:** This document is an editorial synthesis intended for use by healthcare professionals and is not a substitute for the official PI used for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Fostamatinib disodium hexahydrate.
- **Chemical Class:** Tyrosine kinase inhibitor (Spleen Tyrosine Kinase [SYK] inhibitor).
- **Brand Name(s):** **TAVALISSE** [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):**
    - **Tablets:** 100 mg (orange, film-coated, round, biconvex, debossed with "100" and "R") [US].
    - **Tablets:** 150 mg (orange, film-coated, oval, biconvex, debossed with "150" and "R") [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Rigel Pharmaceuticals, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Chronic Immune Thrombocytopenia (ITP):** Indicated for the treatment of thrombocytopenia in adult patients with chronic ITP who have had an insufficient response to a previous treatment [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** 
    - **Starting Dose:** 100 mg taken orally twice daily [US].
    - **Dose Adjustment:** After 4 weeks, the dose may be increased to 150 mg twice daily if the platelet count has not reached at least 50 × 10⁹/L, to reduce the risk of bleeding [US].
    - **Maintenance:** Use the lowest dose necessary to maintain a platelet count of at least 50 × 10⁹/L [US].
- **Administration Instructions:** 
    - Tablets may be taken with or without food [US].
- **Titration and Modification:** 
    - Manage adverse reactions (e.g., hypertension, hepatotoxicity, diarrhea, neutropenia) via dose interruption, reduction, or permanent discontinuation [US].
    - **Dose Reduction Schedule:** Reductions proceed from 300 mg/day → 200 mg/day → 150 mg/day (once daily) → 100 mg/day (once daily). If further reduction is needed below 100 mg daily, the drug must be discontinued [US].
- **Discontinuation:** 
    - Discontinue therapy after 12 weeks if the platelet count does not increase sufficiently to avoid clinically significant bleeding [US].
- **Missed Dose Instructions:** 
    - Patients should take the next dose at the regularly scheduled time; do not double the dose [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **None:** There are no contraindications listed in the available US Product Information [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None [US].
- **Hypertension:** New or worsening hypertension, including hypertensive crisis (1%), may occur. Monitor blood pressure every 2 weeks until stable, then monthly [US].
- **Hepatotoxicity:** Elevations in ALT and AST are common. Monitor liver function tests (LFTs) monthly. If ALT/AST > 3 × ULN, manage with dose modification or interruption [US].
- **Diarrhea:** Occurs in approximately 31% of patients. Manage early with supportive measures (antidiarrheals, hydration). Severe diarrhea requires dose interruption or reduction [US].
- **Neutropenia:** Neutropenia occurred in 6% of patients; febrile neutropenia in 1%. Monitor absolute neutrophil count (ANC) monthly and for signs of infection [US].
- **Embryo-Fetal Toxicity:** Based on animal data, **fostamatinib** can cause fetal harm. Verify pregnancy status in females of reproductive potential before initiation [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Strong CYP3A4 Inhibitors:** (e.g., ketoconazole) Increase exposure to R406 (the active metabolite). Monitor for toxicity; dose reduction may be required [US].
- **Strong CYP3A4 Inducers:** (e.g., rifampin) Reduce exposure to R406 significantly. Concomitant use is **not recommended** [US].
- **CYP3A4 Substrates:** **Fostamatinib** may increase concentrations of CYP3A4 substrates (e.g., simvastatin). Monitor for substrate toxicities [US].
- **BCRP and P-gp Substrates:** **Fostamatinib** may increase concentrations of BCRP substrates (e.g., rosuvastatin) and P-gp substrates (e.g., digoxin). Monitor for toxicities [US].
- **UGT1A1 Inhibition:** R406 inhibits UGT1A1, which may lead to increased unconjugated bilirubin in the absence of other LFT abnormalities [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Category:** Not explicitly lettered in the provided US PI, but identified as a risk for fetal harm based on animal studies [US]. 
    - **Advice:** Advise pregnant women of the potential risk. Females of reproductive potential should use effective contraception during treatment and for 1 month after the final dose [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** 
    - There are no data on the presence in human milk. R406 was found in rodent milk at concentrations 5–10 times higher than maternal plasma. 
    - **Recommendation:** Advise women not to breastfeed during treatment and for 1 month after the final dose [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies showed reduced pregnancy rates and increased post-implantation loss in females, but no effect on male fertility [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established in patients <18 years. **Fostamatinib** is not recommended for pediatric use due to adverse effects on actively growing bones observed in nonclinical studies [US].
- **Elderly Use:** No overall differences in effectiveness were observed between patients ≥65 years and younger patients. However, geriatric patients experienced higher rates of serious adverse events (21% vs 9%) and hypertension (39% vs 23%) [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** No clinically significant PK changes were noted in patients with CLcr ≥30 to <50 mL/min or those on dialysis [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No clinically significant PK changes were noted in patients with Child-Pugh Class A, B, or C [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** **Fostamatinib** is a prodrug. Its active metabolite, R406, is a tyrosine kinase inhibitor that targets spleen tyrosine kinase (SYK). This inhibits Fc-activating receptor and B-cell receptor signal transduction, thereby reducing antibody-mediated destruction of platelets [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Absolute bioavailability of R406 is 55%. Median Tmax is 1.5 hours. High-fat meals increase AUC by 23% [US].
    - **Distribution:** R406 is 98.3% protein bound [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Metabolised in the gut by alkaline phosphatase to R406. R406 is further metabolised via CYP3A4 and UGT1A9 [US].
    - **Elimination:** Terminal half-life is approximately 15 hours. Primary excretion is fecal (80%) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **FIT-1 and FIT-2:** Two identical Phase 3, double-blind, placebo-controlled trials in 150 adults with persistent or chronic ITP. 
- **Efficacy:** A stable platelet response (≥50 × 10⁹/L on at least 4 of 6 visits between weeks 14–24) was achieved in 18% of the **fostamatinib** group vs 0% (FIT-1) and 16% vs 4% (FIT-2). 
- **Prior Treatment:** Among patients previously treated with TPO-RA (thrombopoietin receptor agonists), 17% achieved a stable response to **fostamatinib** [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage:** Store at room temperature, 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Keep the bottle tightly closed. Do not remove the desiccant canisters from the bottle [US].
