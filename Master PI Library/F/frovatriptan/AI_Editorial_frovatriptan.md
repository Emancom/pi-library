# Editorial Product Information: Frovatriptan

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial information is based on the Product Information (PI) from the following jurisdiction: United States (FDA).
- Purpose: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising regulatory data into a clinically relevant format.
- Note: This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI or prescribing information required in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory guidance before prescribing.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Frovatriptan succinate; a selective serotonin (5-HT$_{1B/1D}$) receptor subtype agonist (triptan).
- **Brand Name(s):** Frovatriptan Succinate [US].
- **ATC Code:** N02CC07.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Film-coated tablets: 2.5 mg [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Distributed by Leading Pharma, LLC; Manufactured by LIFEPharma FZE [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Acute Migraine Treatment:** Indicated for the acute treatment of migraine with or without aura in adults [US].
- **Limitations of Use [US]:**
    - Only for use after a clear diagnosis of migraine has been established.
    - Not indicated for the prophylactic therapy of migraine.
    - Not indicated for the treatment of cluster headache.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adults):** The recommended dose is a single 2.5 mg tablet taken orally with fluids [US].
- **Headache Recurrence:** If the migraine recurs after initial relief, a second tablet may be taken, provided there is an interval of at least 2 hours since the first dose [US].
- **Maximum Dose:** Do not exceed 3 tablets (7.5 mg) in any 24-hour period [US].
- **Non-Responders:** There is no evidence that a second dose is effective for the same headache if the patient did not respond to the first dose [US].
- **Frequency Cautions:** The safety of treating more than an average of 4 migraine attacks per 30-day period has not been established [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Ischemic Heart Disease:** History of coronary artery disease (CAD) (e.g., myocardial infarction, angina pectoris, silent ischemia) or coronary artery vasospasm, including Prinzmetal’s angina [US].
- **Arrhythmias:** Wolff-Parkinson-White syndrome or arrhythmias associated with other cardiac accessory conduction pathway disorders [US].
- **Cerebrovascular Disease:** History of stroke or transient ischemic attack (TIA) [US].
- **Peripheral Vascular Disease:** Including (but not limited to) ischemic bowel disease [US].
- **Uncontrolled Hypertension:** Significant elevation in blood pressure [US].
- **Specific Migraine Subtypes:** History of hemiplegic or basilar migraine [US].
- **Drug-Drug Combinations:** Recent use (within 24 hours) of another 5-HT$_1$ agonist or an ergotamine-containing/ergot-type medication (e.g., dihydroergotamine or methysergide) [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to frovatriptan succinate (angioedema and anaphylaxis have been observed) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Myocardial Ischemia and Infarction:** Serious cardiac adverse reactions, including acute myocardial infarction, have occurred within hours of administration. Cardiac evaluation is required for triptan-naïve patients with multiple cardiovascular risk factors (e.g., increased age, diabetes, hypertension, smoking, obesity, strong family history of CAD) [US].
- **Arrhythmias:** Life-threatening disturbances of cardiac rhythm (e.g., ventricular tachycardia/fibrillation) have been reported; discontinue if these occur [US].
- **Chest/Throat/Neck/Jaw Symptoms:** Sensations of pain, tightness, and pressure are common and usually non-cardiac, but patients at high cardiac risk should be evaluated [US].
- **Cerebrovascular Events:** Cerebral hemorrhage, subarachnoid hemorrhage, and stroke have been reported [US].
- **Vasospasm Reactions:** Non-coronary vasospasm may cause peripheral vascular ischemia, gastrointestinal ischemia/infarction (abdominal pain, bloody diarrhea), and Raynaud’s syndrome [US].
- **Medication Overuse Headache (MOH):** Overuse of acute migraine drugs (≥10 days/month) may exacerbate headaches. Detoxification may be necessary [US].
- **Serotonin Syndrome:** Potentially life-threatening, especially when co-administered with SSRIs, SNRIs, TCAs, or MAO inhibitors. Symptoms include mental status changes, autonomic instability, and neuromuscular aberrations [US].
- **Blood Pressure Increase:** Significant elevations in blood pressure and hypertensive crisis have been reported; monitor all patients [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions are generally mild to moderate and transient. Frequency data from US clinical trials (2.5 mg vs placebo):

| Adverse Reaction | Frovatriptan 2.5 mg (n=1554) | Placebo (n=838) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dizziness** | 8% | 5% |
| **Headache** | 4% | 3% |
| **Paresthesia** | 4% | 2% |
| **Fatigue** | 5% | 2% |
| **Flushing** | 4% | 2% |
| **Dry Mouth** | 3% | 1% |
| **Dyspepsia** | 2% | 1% |
| **Chest Pain** | 2% | 1% |

- **Other Common Reactions (≥1%):** Dysesthesia, hypoesthesia, vomiting, abdominal pain, diarrhea, insomnia, anxiety, sinusitis, rhinitis, abnormal vision, increased sweating, tinnitus, and palpitations [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Myocardial infarction, stroke, arrhythmias, serotonin syndrome, and severe hypersensitivity (anaphylaxis/angioedema) [US].
- **Postmarketing Experience:** Seizures have been reported [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Ergot-containing medicines:** May cause prolonged vasospastic reactions. Use within 24 hours of each other is contraindicated [US].
- **Other 5-HT$_1$ agonists:** Additive vasospastic effects. Use within 24 hours of each other is contraindicated [US].
- **SSRIs/SNRIs/TCAs/MAOIs:** Increased risk of Serotonin Syndrome [US].
- **Propranolol:** Increases the AUC of frovatriptan by 60% in males and 29% in females. No dose adjustment is explicitly required by the US PI, but clinicians should be aware of increased exposure [US].
- **Oral Contraceptives:** AUC and C$_{max}$ are approximately 30% higher in women taking oral contraceptives [US].
- **Ergotamine Tartrate:** Co-administration reduced the AUC and C$_{max}$ of frovatriptan by approximately 25% [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not specified by letter, but label notes potential for fetal harm based on animal data]. In animal studies, frovatriptan was associated with embryofetal lethality, fetal abnormalities, and decreased growth at doses higher than clinical levels. Use during pregnancy only if the potential benefit justifies the potential risk to the fetus [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** There are no data on the presence of frovatriptan in human milk. In rats, levels in milk were up to four times higher than in plasma. Caution should be exercised [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies in rats suggested a partial impairment of ovulation at high doses (≥130 times the MRHD), but no other fertility-related effects were noted [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness in patients under 18 years of age have not been established; use is not recommended [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Blood concentrations are 1.5 to 2 times higher in elderly patients compared to younger adults. However, no dosage adjustment is considered necessary [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dosage adjustment is necessary [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No adjustment for mild to moderate impairment. Use with caution in patients with severe hepatic impairment due to a predicted two-fold increase in AUC and lack of clinical experience [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Selective agonist for 5-HT$_{1B}$ and 5-HT$_{1D}$ receptors. It is thought to act by constricting intracranial blood vessels and inhibiting the release of pro-inflammatory neuropeptides from trigeminal sensory nerves [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics [US]:**
    - **Absorption:** Mean C$_{max}$ achieved in 2–4 hours. Bioavailability is 20% in males and 30% in females. Food delays T$_{max}$ by one hour but does not affect overall bioavailability.
    - **Distribution:** Low protein binding (~15%). Large volume of distribution (3.0–4.2 L/kg).
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily via CYP1A2. Several metabolites formed; desmethyl frovatriptan has lower affinity for 5-HT receptors.
    - **Elimination:** Mean terminal half-life is approximately **26 hours** (notably longer than other triptans). Renal clearance accounts for ~40–45% of total clearance.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Binds with high affinity to 5-HT$_{1B/1D}$ receptors [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Efficacy:** Demonstrated in four randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trials. In all trials, the percentage of patients achieving a headache response (reduction from moderate/severe pain to mild/no pain) at 2 hours was significantly higher with frovatriptan 2.5 mg (37%–46%) compared to placebo (21%–27%) [US].
- **Other Doses:** Lower doses (0.5 mg, 1 mg) were not effective at 2 hours. Higher doses (up to 40 mg) provided no additional benefit but increased adverse events [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20ºC to 25ºC (68ºF to 77ºF); excursions permitted to 15ºC to 30ºC (59ºF to 86ºF) [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from moisture [US].
