# Editorial Product Information: Finafloxacin

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising regulatory data into a single clinical reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory documents for specific legal and prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Finafloxacin, a fluoroquinolone antimicrobial.
- **Brand Name:** **XTORO** [United States].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources (commonly S02AA16 for otic anti-infectives).
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Sterile, preserved aqueous otic suspension containing 0.3% finafloxacin (3 mg/mL).
- **Route of Administration:** Topical otic use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Fonseca Biosciences, LLC [United States].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Acute Otitis Externa (AOE):** Indicated for the treatment of AOE, with or without the use of an otowick, caused by susceptible strains of *Pseudomonas aeruginosa* and *Staphylococcus aureus* [United States-approved].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric $\ge$ 1 year)
- **Standard Dose:** Instill four (4) drops into the affected ear(s) twice daily for seven days.
- **Dosing with Otowick:** If an otowick is required, the initial dose may be doubled to eight (8) drops. Subsequent doses should return to four (4) drops twice daily for the remainder of the seven-day course.

### Administration Instructions
- **Warming:** The bottle should be warmed by holding it in the hand for one to two minutes prior to use. This is intended to avoid dizziness that may occur following the instillation of a cold suspension.
- **Preparation:** Shake the bottle well before use.
- **Positioning:** The patient should lie with the affected ear upward. After instilling the drops, this position should be maintained for 60 seconds to ensure the medication penetrates the ear canal.
- **Hygiene:** To prevent contamination, avoid touching the bottle tip to the ear, fingers, or other surfaces.

## 4. Contraindications
- **United States:** None listed in the official PI. However, clinicians should note the warning regarding hypersensitivity (see Section 5).

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Overgrowth of Nonsusceptible Organisms:** Prolonged use of **XTORO** may result in the overgrowth of nonsusceptible organisms, including fungi and yeast. If superinfection occurs, the medication should be discontinued and alternative therapy initiated.
- **Allergic Reactions:** Hypersensitivity reactions may occur in patients with a history of allergy to finafloxacin, other quinolone antibiotics, or any excipients in the formulation (e.g., benzalkonium chloride). Discontinue use immediately if a rash or other allergic signs appear.

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions were evaluated in two Phase 3 clinical trials involving 618 patients. The following reactions occurred at an incidence of approximately 1%:
- **Ear and Labyrinth Disorders:** Ear pruritus (itching).
- **Gastrointestinal Disorders:** Nausea.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources. Given the low systemic absorption following topical otic administration, significant systemic drug-drug interactions are unlikely.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **United States (Risk Summary):** There are no adequate or well-controlled studies in pregnant women. Animal data (oral administration in rats and rabbits) demonstrated teratogenicity, including neural tube defects and skeletal anomalies, at exposures $\ge$ 1300 times the maximum human systemic exposure from otic use. **XTORO** should be used during pregnancy only if the potential benefit outweighs the potential risk to the fetus.
- **Breastfeeding:** Finafloxacin has been detected in the milk of nursing rats. While human systemic concentration after otic use is low, it is unknown if it is excreted in human breast milk. Exercise caution when administering to nursing mothers.
- **Fertility:** In rat studies, oral doses of 500 mg/kg/day resulted in male infertility (low sperm count and immobility). However, the NOAEL for fertility was 100 mg/kg/day, which is approximately 60,000 times the human systemic exposure from otic administration.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy have been established in patients aged 1 year and older. Safety and efficacy in infants under 1 year of age have not been established [United States].
- **Elderly Use:** No overall differences in safety or effectiveness have been observed between geriatric and younger patients.
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources; systemic exposure is negligible.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Finafloxacin is a fluoroquinolone that inhibits bacterial type II topoisomerase enzymes (DNA gyrase and topoisomerase IV). These enzymes are essential for bacterial DNA replication, transcription, repair, and recombination.
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Systemic absorption is minimal. In healthy subjects (4 drops BID for 7 days), plasma concentrations were mostly below the limit of quantitation (0.05 ng/mL). In AOE patients, the maximum observed plasma concentration was 0.234 ng/mL.
- **Microbiology:** Finafloxacin maintains activity against *Pseudomonas aeruginosa* and *Staphylococcus aureus*. It shows cross-resistance with other fluoroquinolones but generally not with other antibacterial classes.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Study Design:** Two randomised, multicenter, vehicle-controlled trials were conducted.
- **Outcomes:** **XTORO** was superior to vehicle for clinical cure (71% vs. 37-50%) and microbiological success.
- **Pain Relief:** The median time to cessation of ear pain was 3.5 days for **XTORO**-treated patients, compared to 5.3–6.8 days in the vehicle group.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at $2^\circ\text{C}$ to $25^\circ\text{C}$ ($36^\circ\text{F}$ to $77^\circ\text{F}$).
- **Handling:** Do not freeze. Keep the bottle tightly closed.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** 
    - **United States:** Prescription Only (Rx Only).
