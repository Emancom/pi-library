# Editorial Product Information: fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- At the time of writing, official PI documents for other jurisdictions (e.g., Australian, European, Canadian, Japanese) were not provided for comparison. Consequently, most clinical specifications are attributed to the **United States** regulatory framework.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. It is an editorial synthesis and must not be used as a substitute for the official, current PI in the jurisdiction of practice.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki; a HER2-directed antibody-drug conjugate (ADC).
- **Brand Name(s):** **ENHERTU** [US].
- **ATC Code:** L01FD04.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Lyophilized powder for injection in a single-dose vial (100 mg).
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Daiichi Sankyo Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
**ENHERTU** is indicated for the following populations [US-approved]:

- **HER2-Positive Early Breast Cancer:**
    - Neoadjuvant treatment of adults with Stage II or III disease (IHC 3+ or ISH+) followed by THP (taxane, trastuzumab, and pertuzumab).
    - Adjuvant treatment of adults with residual invasive disease following neoadjuvant trastuzumab/taxane-based therapy.
- **HER2-Positive Metastatic Breast Cancer:**
    - First-line treatment in combination with pertuzumab for unresectable or metastatic disease.
    - Monotherapy for patients who have received a prior anti-HER2-based regimen in the metastatic, neoadjuvant, or adjuvant setting and developed recurrence within six months.
- **HER2-Low and HER2-Ultralow Metastatic Breast Cancer:** Monotherapy for unresectable or metastatic disease that has progressed on endocrine therapy (for HR-positive) or prior chemotherapy.
- **HER2-Mutant NSCLC:** Monotherapy for adults with unresectable or metastatic non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) harboring activating HER2 (ERBB2) mutations.
- **HER2-Positive Gastric Cancer:** Monotherapy for locally advanced or metastatic gastric or GEJ adenocarcinoma following prior trastuzumab-based regimens.
- **HER2-Positive (IHC 3+) Solid Tumors:** Monotherapy for patients who have received prior systemic treatment and have no satisfactory alternative options.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
- **Breast Cancer, NSCLC, and Solid Tumors:** 5.4 mg/kg intravenously every 3 weeks.
- **Gastric Cancer:** 6.4 mg/kg intravenously every 3 weeks.

### Administration Instructions
- **Preparation:** Reconstitute with 5 mL Sterile Water for Injection to a concentration of 20 mg/mL. Dilute further in an infusion bag containing 100 mL of **5% Dextrose Injection**. 
- **CRITICAL:** Do NOT use Sodium Chloride (saline) for dilution as it is incompatible.
- **Filter Requirement:** Administer via an infusion set with a 0.20 or 0.22-micron in-line filter (PES or PS).
- **Infusion Rate:** Initial dose over 90 minutes. Subsequent doses over 30 minutes if tolerated. Do not administer as a bolus.

### Dose Modifications
- **Dose Reduction:** For 5.4 mg/kg starting dose, reductions are to 4.4 mg/kg and then 3.2 mg/kg. For 6.4 mg/kg, reductions are to 5.4 mg/kg and then 4.4 mg/kg.
- **Discontinuation:** Permanently discontinue for Grade 2 or higher ILD/pneumonitis, symptomatic congestive heart failure (CHF), or if the dose cannot be tolerated at the lowest reduction level.

## 4. Contraindications
- **United States:** None reported in the official PI.
- **Other Jurisdictions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warnings [US]
- **Interstitial Lung Disease (ILD) / Pneumonitis:** Severe, life-threatening, and fatal cases have occurred. Monitor for cough, dyspnea, and fever. Permanently discontinue for any symptomatic ILD (Grade 2+).
- **Embryo-Fetal Toxicity:** Exposure during pregnancy can cause fetal harm. Verify pregnancy status before initiation.

### General Warnings
- **Neutropenia:** Severe neutropenia, including febrile neutropenia, has been reported. Monitor complete blood counts (CBC) prior to each dose.
- **Left Ventricular Dysfunction (LVD):** Treatment may increase the risk of decreased Left Ventricular Ejection Fraction (LVEF). Assess LVEF at baseline and regularly during treatment.
- **Infusion Reactions:** Monitor for signs of hypersensitivity. Slow or interrupt infusion for mild reactions; permanently discontinue for severe reactions.

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Frequency and Severity
- **Very Common (>10%):** Nausea, fatigue, alopecia, vomiting, constipation, decreased appetite, musculoskeletal pain, diarrhea, and decreased blood counts (hemoglobin, white blood cells, neutrophils, platelets).
- **Serious Adverse Effects:**
    - **ILD/Pneumonitis:** Occurred in approximately 12% of patients at the 5.4 mg/kg dose [US].
    - **LVD:** LVEF decrease was reported in 4% to 11% of patients across various trials [US].
    - **Neutropenia:** Reported in 65% to 79% of patients depending on the indication [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** The topoisomerase inhibitor component (DXd) is a substrate of CYP3A4 and transporters (OATP1B1, P-gp). However, clinical studies with strong CYP3A4 inhibitors (e.g., itraconazole) or OATP inhibitors (e.g., ritonavir) showed no clinically significant impact on exposure [US].
- **Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Use with other myelosuppressive or cardiotoxic agents may increase the risk of adverse reactions.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not specified under new labeling, but risk of fetal harm is boxed]. Adverse outcomes include oligohydramnios and fetal death. Use effective contraception for 7 months (females) and 4 months (males) post-last dose.
- **Breastfeeding:** There are no data on presence in human milk. Advise against breastfeeding during treatment and for 7 months after the final dose due to potential serious adverse reactions in the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal data suggest potential impairment of male reproductive function and fertility [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy have not been established [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Patients $\geq 65$ years showed a higher incidence of Grade 3-4 adverse reactions (56% vs 49%) in monotherapy trials [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment for mild to moderate impairment (CLcr 30-89 mL/min). Data are insufficient for severe impairment [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment for mild (total bilirubin $\leq$ ULN with AST > ULN, or bilirubin 1-1.5x ULN) to moderate impairment. Not studied in severe hepatic impairment [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** A HER2-directed antibody-drug conjugate. The humanized IgG1 antibody binds to HER2 on tumor cells, is internalized, and undergoes lysosomal cleavage. This releases DXd, a DNA topoisomerase I inhibitor, which induces DNA damage and apoptosis. DXd is membrane-permeable, allowing for a "bystander effect" on neighboring cells.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Metabolism:** DXd is metabolized by CYP3A4. The antibody part is catabolized into small peptides.
    - **Elimination:** The median elimination half-life is approximately 5.4 to 5.7 days for the ADC [US].
    - **Excretion:** Not explicitly quantified in available sources.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **DESTINY-Breast03:** Demonstrated superior progression-free survival (PFS) compared to ado-trastuzumab emtansine in HER2-positive metastatic breast cancer (HR 0.28).
- **DESTINY-Breast04:** Showed efficacy in HER2-low metastatic breast cancer, significantly improving PFS and OS compared to physician's choice of chemotherapy.
- **DESTINY-Lung02:** Established the 5.4 mg/kg dose for HER2-mutant NSCLC based on objective response rates (ORR).
- **DESTINY-Gastric01:** Showed improved OS (12.5 months vs 8.4 months) in HER2-positive gastric cancer compared to irinotecan or paclitaxel.

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vials:** Store in a refrigerator at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) in the original carton to protect from light. **Do not freeze.**
- **Reconstituted Solution:** If not used immediately, store in the refrigerator (2°C to 8°C) for up to 24 hours.
- **Diluted Infusion:** Stable for up to 24 hours in the refrigerator or 4 hours at room temperature (including infusion time). Protect from light.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Classified as Rx-only. Hazardous drug—requires special handling and disposal procedures [US].
- **Other Jurisdictions:** Not specified in available sources.
