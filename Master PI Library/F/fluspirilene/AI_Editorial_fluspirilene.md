# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial:
    - United States (FDA): Levocarnitine Injection, solution (Baxter Healthcare Corporation)
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of levocarnitine injection for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises available regulatory data into a single clinical reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. At the time of this writing, only the United States (FDA) source documentation was provided for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); chemical class: (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Metabolic Disorders:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg.
- **ESRD on Hemodialysis:** The recommended starting dose is 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].

### Administration Instructions
- **Route:** Intravenous bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or infusion [US].
- **ESRD Patients:** Administer into the venous return line after each dialysis session [US].
- **Metabolic Crisis:** A loading dose is often administered, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours. Dosing frequency should be every 3 to 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours [US].
- **Parenteral Inspection:** Products should be inspected visually for particulate matter or discoloration before administration [US].

### Titration and Monitoring
- **Monitoring:** Weekly and monthly monitoring of plasma carnitine concentrations is recommended. Monitoring should include blood chemistries, vital signs, and overall clinical condition [US].
- **Target Concentration:** Plasma free carnitine levels should be maintained between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].
- **Dose Adjustments (ESRD):** Adjustments (e.g., down to 5 mg/kg) may be made as early as the third or fourth week of therapy, guided by pre-dialysis (trough) plasma concentrations [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients on dialysis. Some reactions occur within minutes of administration. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue the drug immediately and initiate appropriate medical treatment [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In those with a known seizure disorder, an increase in frequency or severity of seizures has been observed [US].
- **Monitoring Requirements:** In patients with ESRD, the effects of supplemental carnitine on clinical outcomes and signs of deficiency have not been fully determined; however, carnitine concentrations are significantly increased following therapy [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common Adverse Effects (≥5%)
In clinical trials of ESRD patients on hemodialysis, the following reactions were reported at a frequency ≥5% regardless of causality [US]:
- **Body as a Whole:** Abdominal pain (5–21%), headache (3–37%), infection (10–24%), injection site reaction (27–38%), pain (21–35%), fever (5–12%), flu syndrome (15–29%).
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (18–21%), hypotension (3–19%), tachycardia (5–9%), chest pain (6–15%).
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea (9–35%), vomiting (9–21%), nausea (5–12%).
- **Nervous System:** Dizziness (10–18%), paresthesia (3–12%).
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (15–27%), cough increase (10–18%), dyspnea (3–11%).

### Other Reported Effects
- **General:** Transient nausea and vomiting; less frequent reactions include body odor and gastritis [US].
- **Serious Effects:** Anaphylaxis and seizures [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have occurred when levocarnitine is used concomitantly with warfarin. **Recommendation:** Monitor INR levels after initiating levocarnitine or following dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not specified by letter, but indicated for use only if clearly needed]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) revealed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. No adequate, well-controlled studies exist in pregnant women [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine concentrations in milk following exogenous administration. Risks to the infant of excess carnitine must be weighed against the benefits of supplementation to the mother [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** See Dosage and Administration for metabolic disorders (weight-based dosing) [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Safety and efficacy of **oral** levocarnitine have not been evaluated in renal insufficiency. In ESRD patients on dialysis, chronic high doses of oral levocarnitine may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide), which are normally excreted in urine [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring carrier molecule required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Levocarnitine may promote the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids in patients with metabolic defects by forming acylcarnitine esters, which are rapidly excreted [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not protein-bound. Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours (uncorrected for endogenous levels). Mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Pharmacokinetics:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients receiving 20 mg/kg three times weekly after dialysis showed mean pre-dialysis (trough) levels increased from ~20 µmol/L at baseline to 190 ± 55 µmol/L after 8 weeks [US].
- **Dose-Ranging:** In studies (n≈30 per group) comparing 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg, trough concentrations increased in a dose-proportional manner over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing. Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as the formulation is preservative-free [US].
- **Stability:** Compatible and stable for 24 hours at room temperature in PVC bags when mixed with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources for other jurisdictions.
