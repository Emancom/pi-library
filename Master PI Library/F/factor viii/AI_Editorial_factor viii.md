# Unified Product Information: Human Coagulation Factor VIII / von Willebrand Factor Complex

## 1. Product Name and Active Ingredient
**Product Name:** Factor VIII / von Willebrand Factor (VWF) Complex (Human)
**Active Ingredients:**
*   **Human Coagulation Factor VIII (FVIII):** Measured as FVIII:C (coagulation activity).
*   **Human von Willebrand Factor (VWF):** Measured as VWF:RCo (ristocetin cofactor activity) or VWF:Ac (activity).

This product is a sterile, high-purity, lyophilized powder derived from human plasma. It contains both FVIII and VWF non-covalently bound in a stable complex. The ratio of VWF:RCo to FVIII:C varies by specific preparation, typically ranging from approximately 1:1 [FDA] to 2.4:1 [TGA].

## 2. Approved Indications
### von Willebrand Disease (VWD)
Indicated for adult and pediatric patients with VWD for:
*   On-demand treatment and control of spontaneous bleeding episodes.
*   Perioperative management of bleeding (prophylaxis for surgical procedures).
*   Routine prophylaxis to reduce the frequency of bleeding episodes.
*   *Note:* This is generally used when desmopressin (DDAVP) is ineffective, contraindicated, or insufficient [TGA].

### Hemophilia A
Indicated for adult and pediatric patients (specifically 12 years and older [FDA]) for:
*   Treatment and prophylaxis of non-surgical and surgical bleeding.
*   Routine prophylaxis to reduce the frequency of bleeding episodes.

## 3. Contraindications
This product is strictly contraindicated in individuals with a known history of:
*   Anaphylaxis or severe systemic reactions to human plasma-derived products.
*   Hypersensitivity to FVIII, VWF, or any excipients used in the formulation (e.g., sucrose, sodium citrate, calcium chloride).

## 4. Warnings and Precautions
### Hypersensitivity and Anaphylaxis
Allergic-type reactions may occur. Patients should be informed of early signs, including hives, generalized urticaria, chest tightness, wheezing, and hypotension. If symptoms occur, administration must be discontinued immediately.

### Thromboembolic Events
Continued treatment in VWD patients may lead to an excessive rise in FVIII:C levels, increasing the risk of thrombotic events. In surgical settings, FVIII:C levels should be monitored closely and should not exceed 250% [FDA]. Prophylaxis against venous thromboembolism should be considered for patients with known risk factors [TGA].

### Neutralizing Antibodies (Inhibitors)
Patients should be monitored for the development of neutralizing antibodies (inhibitors) to both FVIII and VWF. If expected plasma levels are not reached or bleeding is not controlled, a Bethesda assay or appropriate inhibitor test should be performed. Inhibitors to VWF may occur concomitantly with anaphylactic reactions.

### Pathogen Safety
As this product is derived from human plasma, the risk of transmitting infectious agents (e.g., HIV, HBV, HCV, HAV, and Parvovirus B19) cannot be totally excluded. Manufacturing involves dedicated virus inactivation steps (e.g., solvent detergent treatment and dry heat). Parvovirus B19 infection can be severe in pregnant women or immunocompromised patients [TGA]. Vaccination for Hepatitis A and B is recommended for regular recipients of plasma-derived products.

## 5. Dosage and Administration
### General Principles
Dosing should be individualized based on the patient’s weight, severity of the deficiency, location of the bleed, and clinical response. 1 IU of FVIII/VWF per kg body weight generally raises the plasma activity by approximately 2% (2 IU/dL).

**Dosing Formula:**
`Required IU = Body Weight (kg) x Desired Rise (%) x 0.5`

### VWD Population
*   **Minor Bleeding/Surgery:** Loading dose of 20–40 IU/kg (VWF:RCo) to achieve a therapeutic goal of >30% activity.
*   **Major Bleeding/Surgery:** Loading dose of 40–60 IU/kg (VWF:RCo) to achieve a therapeutic goal of >50% (bleeding) or 100% (pre-surgery peak).
*   **Prophylaxis:** 20–40 IU/kg two to three times weekly [FDA]. Pediatric patients under age 12 may require higher prophylactic doses (40–80 IU/kg [TGA]).

### Hemophilia A Population
*   **Minor Bleeds:** 10–20 IU/kg (FVIII:C) every 12–24 hours until resolved.
*   **Major/Life-Threatening:** Initial dose of 35–50 IU/kg (FVIII:C) [FDA] to achieve peak levels of 80–100%.
*   **Prophylaxis:** 20–40 IU/kg every 2 to 3 days.

### Administration
*   Administer via slow intravenous injection after reconstitution.
*   The recommended rate is 2–4 mL/min [FDA], not exceeding 6 mL/min [TGA].
*   The product must be used as soon as possible after reconstitution (within 4 hours [FDA] or 8 hours [TGA]).

## 6. Adverse Effects
*   **Very Common (≥1/10):** Headache [TGA].
*   **Common (≥1/100 to <1/10):** Hypersensitivity (urticaria, chest discomfort, dizziness), pyrexia (fever), FVIII inhibition in previously untreated patients.
*   **Uncommon/Rare:** Dysgeusia (taste perversion), abnormal liver function tests, thromboembolic events, and VWF inhibition.

## 7. Drug Interactions
No specific interaction studies have been conducted. The product should not be mixed with other medicinal products or administered in the same infusion set as other intravenous preparations.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
### Pregnancy
Safety during pregnancy has not been established in controlled clinical trials. Use only if the potential benefit justifies the risk. Practitioners should consider that delivery confers an increased risk of hemorrhage in patients with VWD or Hemophilia A.

### Breastfeeding
It is unknown whether the FVIII/VWF complex is excreted in human milk. A decision should be made whether to discontinue nursing or the drug, taking into account the clinical importance of the medication to the mother.

## 9. Pharmacokinetics
Pharmacokinetic parameters vary based on the specific product and VWD type:
*   **VWF:RCo Half-life:** Approximately 13.7 to 15.8 hours.
*   **FVIII:C Half-life:** Approximately 10.6 to 13.4 hours.
*   **Peak Plasma Levels:** Typically achieved within 15–30 minutes (VWF) and 30–60 minutes (FVIII) post-injection.

## 10. Mechanism of Action
The FVIII/VWF complex serves two primary roles in hemostasis:
1.  **VWF:** Acts as an adhesive protein, facilitating platelet adhesion to damaged vascular subendothelium and promoting platelet aggregation. It also serves as the essential carrier protein for FVIII, protecting it from rapid degradation.
2.  **FVIII:** Acts as a critical cofactor for Factor IXa in the intrinsic pathway of the coagulation cascade, leading to the activation of Factor X and the subsequent generation of thrombin and fibrin.

## 11. Storage Conditions
*   Store the lyophilized powder at 2°C to 8°C.
*   Do not freeze.
*   The product may be stored at room temperature (up to 25°C) for a single period of up to 6 months. Once removed from refrigeration, it should not be returned.
*   Protect from light by keeping the vial in its original carton.
*   **Reconstituted Solution:** Do not refrigerate after reconstitution. Use within 4 hours to minimize the risk of microbial contamination [FDA].
