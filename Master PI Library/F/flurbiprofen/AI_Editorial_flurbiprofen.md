# Editorial Product Information: Flurbiprofen Sodium (Ophthalmic)

## Editorial Note
*   This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following regulatory authority: **United States (FDA)**.
*   The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised, clinically accurate overview for healthcare professionals. 
*   This document is an editorial synthesis and must not be substituted for the official, jurisdiction-specific PI when prescribing or dispensing in a particular region.

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredient:** Flurbiprofen sodium (a phenylalkanoic acid derivative).
*   **Chemical Class:** Nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID).
*   **Brand Name(s):** Not specified in available sources (Generic: Flurbiprofen Sodium Ophthalmic Solution, USP).
*   **ATC Code:** S01BC04 (calculated based on class).
*   **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile ophthalmic solution, 0.03% (0.3 mg/mL) [US].
*   **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical ophthalmic.
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Manufactured by Bausch & Lomb Incorporated for Amici Pharma, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Inhibition of intraoperative miosis:** Indicated to maintain pupil dilation during ocular surgery by inhibiting the miotic response [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
*   **Pre-operative Administration:** A total of four (4) drops should be administered per surgical eye [US].
*   **Schedule:** Instill one (1) drop approximately every 30 minutes, beginning 2 hours prior to the start of surgery [US].

### Administration Instructions
*   To prevent contamination, patients and providers must avoid contacting the dropper tip with the eye or any surrounding surfaces [US].
*   **Cross-contamination prevention:** In cases of bilateral surgery, a separate bottle should be used for each eye to avoid the risk of cross-contamination [US].

### Overdosage
*   Acute systemic toxicity is unlikely with ophthalmic use. If accidental oral ingestion occurs, the patient should drink fluids for dilution [US].

## 4. Contraindications
*   **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to flurbiprofen sodium or any inactive component of the formulation (e.g., thimerosal, edetate disodium) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warnings
*   Not specified in available sources.

### General Warnings and Precautions
*   **Bleeding Risk:** Like other NSAIDs, flurbiprofen may interfere with thrombocyte aggregation, potentially increasing bleeding time. Reports indicate increased bleeding of ocular tissues (including hyphemas) when used in conjunction with ocular surgery [US]. Use with caution in patients with known bleeding tendencies or those receiving anticoagulants [US].
*   **Cross-Sensitivity:** There is a potential for cross-sensitivity to acetylsalicylic acid (aspirin) and other NSAIDs. Caution is advised when treating patients who have previously shown sensitivities to these medications [US].
*   **Delayed Healing:** Topical NSAIDs may slow or delay corneal healing. This effect may be exacerbated by the concomitant use of topical corticosteroids [US].

### Monitoring Requirements
*   Clinicians should monitor for signs of delayed wound healing and increased intraocular bleeding post-operatively.

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are generally localised to the site of administration.

*   **Common (>1%):**
    *   Transient burning and stinging upon instillation [US].
    *   Ocular irritation [US].
*   **Other Ocular Effects (Frequency not specified):**
    *   Ocular hyperemia [US].
    *   Fibrosis [US].
    *   Miosis and mydriasis [US].
    *   Hyphema (increased ocular bleeding) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **Acetylcholine Chloride and Carbachol:** While no pharmacological mechanism for interaction has been identified and clinical/animal studies showed no interference, there have been reports that these agents were ineffective when used in patients previously treated with flurbiprofen sodium [US].
*   **Topical Steroids:** Concomitant use may increase the risk of corneal healing problems [US].
*   **Anticoagulants/Antiplatelets:** Potential to increase the risk of ocular tissue bleeding during surgery [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy:** 
    *   **US Category:** Not assigned (Regulatory text states: Use only if potential benefit justifies potential risk to the fetus) [US].
    *   **Data:** Animal studies in rats at oral doses ≥0.4 mg/kg (approx. 300 times the human topical dose) showed embryocidal effects, delayed parturition, prolonged gestation, and retarded foetal growth [US]. There are no adequate, well-controlled studies in pregnant women.
*   **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if flurbiprofen is excreted in human milk. A clinical decision should be made to either discontinue nursing or discontinue the drug, considering the mother's clinical need [US].
*   **Fertility:** Long-term mutagenicity studies have not been performed [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness in paediatric patients have not been established [US].
*   **Elderly Use:** No overall differences in safety or efficacy have been observed between geriatric and younger patient populations [US].
*   **Renal Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
*   Flurbiprofen is an NSAID that inhibits the cyclo-oxygenase (COX) enzyme, which is essential for the biosynthesis of prostaglandins. In ocular surgery, prostaglandins mediate the miotic response by constricting the iris sphincter via non-cholinergic mechanisms. By inhibiting prostaglandin synthesis, flurbiprofen prevents intraoperative miosis [US].

### Pharmacokinetics
*   **Absorption/Distribution:** While intended for local action, prostaglandins are known mediators of intraocular inflammation (causing blood-aqueous barrier disruption and vasodilation) [US].
*   **Elimination:** Not specified in available sources.

### Pharmacodynamics
*   Clinical studies indicate flurbiprofen sodium has no significant effect on intraocular pressure (IOP) [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   Clinical studies have confirmed that flurbiprofen sodium ophthalmic solution effectively inhibits the miosis typically induced during cataract surgery [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   **Temperature:** Store at 15°C to 25°C (59°F to 77°F) [US].
*   **Integrity:** Do not use if the "Protective Seal" with the yellow symbol is not intact [US].
*   **Shelf Life:** Not specified in available sources.
