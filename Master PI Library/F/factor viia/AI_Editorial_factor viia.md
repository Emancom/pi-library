# Editorial Product Information: SEVENFACT (Coagulation Factor VIIa [recombinant]-jncw)

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial: US (FDA).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of **SEVENFACT** for use by healthcare professionals.
- **Note:** This synthesis is an editorial summary intended for informational purposes and is not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific PI required for prescribing.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Coagulation factor VIIa (recombinant)-jncw. 
- **Chemical Class:** Recombinant coagulation factor VIIa; glycoprotein (406 amino acids, ~50 kilodaltons) [US].
- **Brand Name(s):** **SEVENFACT** [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources (Commonly B02BD08 for recombinant Factor VIIa).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Lyophilized powder for injection in single-dose vials: 1 mg, 2 mg, and 5 mg [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous bolus injection [US].
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Laboratoire Français du Fractionnement et des Biotechnologies (LFB S.A.); Distributed by HEMA Biologics [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Hemophilia A or B with Inhibitors:** Treatment and control of bleeding episodes in adults and adolescents aged 12 years and older [US].
- **Limitations of Use:** **SEVENFACT** is not indicated for the treatment of patients with congenital Factor VII deficiency [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Adolescent ≥12 years)
Treatment should be initiated as soon as a bleeding event occurs. The dose and duration are determined by the severity and location of the bleed and the patient's clinical response.

### Dosing by Indication (Hemophilia A/B with Inhibitors) [US]
| Type of Bleeding | Recommended Dosing Regimen |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Mild to Moderate** (Joints, superficial muscle, soft tissue, mucous membranes) | 75 mcg/kg repeated every 3 hours until hemostasis is achieved **OR** a single initial dose of 225 mcg/kg. If hemostasis is not achieved within 9 hours, additional 75 mcg/kg doses may be given every 3 hours as needed. |
| **Severe** (Life or limb-threatening hemorrhage, intracranial, gastrointestinal, retroperitoneal, etc.) | 225 mcg/kg initially. If required, follow 6 hours later with 75 mcg/kg every 2 hours until hemostasis is achieved. |

### Administration Instructions
- **Reconstitution:** Bring vials and diluent (Sterile Water for Injection) to room temperature. Reconstitute using the provided vial adapter and pre-filled syringe (1 mg vial + 1.1 mL; 2 mg vial + 2.2 mL; 5 mg vial + 5.2 mL). Gently swirl until dissolved; do not shake.
- **Bolus Infusion:** Administer as a bolus intravenous infusion over 2 minutes or less.
- **Stability:** Reconstituted solution must be used within 4 hours. Do not freeze or store in a syringe.

## 4. Contraindications
- Known allergy to rabbits or rabbit proteins [US].
- History of severe hypersensitivity to **SEVENFACT** or any of its components [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warning: Thrombosis [US]:** Serious arterial and venous thrombotic events may occur. Risk is increased in patients with other thrombotic risk factors or those receiving concomitant prothrombin complex concentrates (aPCC/PCC). Monitor for signs of activation of the coagulation system.
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Anaphylaxis and infusion-related reactions (hives, rash, dyspnoea, hypotension) are possible. Patients with IgE-based hypersensitivity to casein may be at higher risk [US].
- **Neutralizing Antibodies:** While not reported in clinical trials for this population, neutralizing antibodies may occur. If hemostasis is not achieved, test for inhibitors [US].
- **Laboratory Monitoring:** Standard coagulation parameters (PT/INR, aPTT, FVII:C) do not necessarily correlate with clinical hemostatic efficacy [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions reported in clinical trials (incidence ≥1%) include:
- **Common (1–10%):** Headache, dizziness, infusion-site discomfort, infusion-site haematoma, infusion-related reaction, and fever [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Thromboembolic events (arterial and venous) are a primary safety concern [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Activated Prothrombin Complex Concentrates (aPCC/PCC):** Simultaneous use with FVIIa-containing products significantly increases the risk of serious thrombotic events. Avoid concomitant use [US].
- **Other Hemostatic Agents:** Clinical data regarding interaction with other procoagulants are limited.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not assigned under PLLR]. There are no adequate studies in pregnant women. Animal reproduction studies have not evaluated embryo-fetal toxicity. Use only if the potential benefit justifies the risk [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Presence in human milk is unknown. Consider developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding alongside the mother’s clinical need for the drug [US].
- **Fertility:** No adverse effects on mating index or fertility were observed in male rats at doses up to 13 times the human dose [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and efficacy established for patients ≥12 years of age. Efficacy was not demonstrated in a trial of children aged 6 months to <12 years [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical trials did not include sufficient numbers of patients >65 years. Increased thrombotic risk should be considered in patients >50 years with comorbidities [US].
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Recombinant Factor VIIa complexes with tissue factor (TF) to activate Factor X to Xa. This directly bypasses the need for Factors VIII and IX in the coagulation cascade to initiate the common pathway and form a hemostatic plug [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Shortens aPTT and PT and increases thrombin generation in a dose-dependent manner [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** 
  - **Half-life:** Approximately 2 hours [US].
  - **Clearance:** ~4.5 to 5.1 L/h [US].
  - **Excretion:** Biexponential decay; returns to baseline approximately 8 hours post-dose [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Study 1 (NCT02020369):** A global, multicentre, randomized, cross-over study of 27 patients with hemophilia A or B with inhibitors. Hemostatic efficacy at 12 hours was 82% for the 75 mcg/kg regimen and 91% for the 225 mcg/kg regimen for mild/moderate bleeds [US].
- **Pediatric Trial:** A trial in 25 patients (6 months to <12 years) failed to demonstrate hemostatic effectiveness in this younger demographic [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Unopened Kits:** Store at room temperature (2°C to 30°C / 36°F to 86°F). Protect from light. Do not freeze [US].
- **Reconstituted Solution:** Store at room temperature or refrigerated (2°C to 30°C / 36°F to 86°F) for up to 4 hours. Do not freeze or store in syringes [US].
