# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The information in this unified editorial was derived from the following source: US (FDA) Product Information for **Levocarnitine** Injection (Baxter Healthcare Corporation).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising clinical data into a concise, structured format.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing in a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory documents for specific legal and prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01 (Not explicitly specified in source; standard reference).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection: 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency in Metabolic Disorders:** For the acute and chronic management of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that lead to secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    - Recommended dose is 50 mg/kg.
    - Administration: Slow intravenous bolus (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion.
    - Severe Metabolic Crisis: A loading dose is often followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours.
    - Frequency: Every 3 to 4 hours (q3h or q4h); frequency should never be less than every 6 hours (q6h).
    - Maximum Dose: The highest administered dose reported is 300 mg/kg.
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - Recommended starting dose: 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight.
    - Administration: Slow intravenous bolus (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line after each dialysis session.
    - Initiation: May be prompted by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma concentrations below 40–50 µmol/L.

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be visually inspected for particulate matter or discoloration before use.
- **Stability:** Stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when diluted in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations of 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL [US].

### Titration and Monitoring
- **Metabolic Disorders:** Monitor plasma carnitine concentrations weekly or monthly. Target free carnitine levels are 35 to 60 µmol/L.
- **ESRD:** Dose adjustments (e.g., reduction to 5 mg/kg) may be made as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough plasma concentrations.

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known** [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm have been reported. These may occur within minutes of administration, particularly in ESRD patients on dialysis. If a reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately [US].
- **Seizure Risk:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity may occur [US].
- **Monitoring:** Periodic monitoring of blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations is recommended.
- **Oral Formulation Safety:** The safety and efficacy of oral levocarnitine have not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in ESRD patients may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common Adverse Effects (≥5%)
Based on placebo-controlled trials in ESRD patients on hemodialysis, the following events occurred at a frequency of ≥5%, regardless of causality:

| Body System | Adverse Event |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Chest pain, fever, flu syndrome, headache, infection, injection site reaction, pain |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension, hypotension, tachycardia |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea, abdominal pain, nausea, vomiting |
| **Metabolic** | Hypercalcemia, hypervolemia |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness, insomnia, paresthesia |
| **Respiratory** | Cough increase, dyspnea, pharyngitis, rhinitis |

### Serious/Specific Adverse Effects
- **Neurologic:** Seizures (both new-onset and exacerbation).
- **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea, vomiting, and gastritis.
- **Other:** Body odor (infrequent) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have been observed when used concomitantly with levocarnitine.
- **Management:** It is recommended to monitor INR levels after initiating levocarnitine or following dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Category:** Not explicitly assigned a letter category in the source, but animal data is provided.
    - **Data:** Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm.
    - **Recommendation:** Should be used during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** 
    - Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied.
    - Levocarnitine concentrations in milk increase in dairy cows following exogenous administration.
    - **Recommendation:** Weigh the risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant against the benefits of supplementation to the mother; consider discontinuing nursing or the drug [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing is weight-based (50 mg/kg) as described in metabolic disorders.
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    - Intravenous levocarnitine is specifically indicated for ESRD patients on dialysis. 
    - **Caution:** High-dose oral levocarnitine is cautioned against in severe renal impairment due to metabolite accumulation [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into cellular mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin. Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily faeces).
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. Mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours. Total body clearance is approximately 4.00 L/h [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Increases plasma carnitine concentrations. In ESRD, it addresses losses caused by dialysis and reduced meat/dairy intake.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pharmacokinetic Trials:** Studies in ESRD patients showed that 20 mg/kg doses three times weekly post-dialysis increased mean trough carnitine levels from ~20 µmol/L to 190 ± 55 µmol/L after 8 weeks.
- **Dose-Ranging:** Trough concentrations increased proportionally with doses of 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg over 12–24 weeks.
- **Note:** While levocarnitine increases carnitine concentrations in ESRD, the specific impact on clinical outcomes (e.g., muscle weakness, cardiomyopathy) has not been definitively determined in this population [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Conditions:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat.
- **Handling:** Single-dose vials; discard any unused portion as the formulation contains no preservative [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources for other jurisdictions.
