# Editorial Product Information: Fenfluramine

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** Australian (TGA). 
- **Purpose:** This document is a harmonised editorial synthesis of the Product Information (PI) for **fenfluramine**. It is designed as a high-level clinical reference for healthcare professionals.
- **Disclaimer:** This editorial is a synthesis of available regulatory data and does not replace the official Product Information mandated for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority’s current PI before prescribing.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Fenfluramine hydrochloride.
- **Chemical Class:** Serotonin releasing agent; amphetamine derivative.
- **Brand Name:** **Fintepla** [Australian].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available source.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** 2.2 mg/mL oral solution [Australian].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** UCB Pharma [Australian].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Dravet Syndrome (DS):** Indicated as add-on therapy for the treatment of seizures associated with Dravet syndrome in patients 2 years of age and older [Australian].
- **Lennox-Gastaut Syndrome (LGS):** Indicated as add-on therapy for the treatment of seizures associated with Lennox-Gastaut syndrome in patients 2 years of age and older [Australian].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric $\ge$ 2 years)
Treatment should be initiated and supervised by a physician experienced in epilepsy management. The starting dose is 0.1 mg/kg twice daily (BD). Titration may occur every 7 days based on clinical response and tolerability [Australian].

### Dosing Schedules by Concomitant Therapy [Australian]
| Concomitant Therapy | Starting Dose | Titration Increment | Maximum Daily Dose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Without Stiripentol** | 0.1 mg/kg BD | 0.2 mg/kg BD (Day 7); 0.35 mg/kg BD (Day 14) | 0.7 mg/kg/day (Max 26 mg/day) |
| **With Stiripentol** | 0.1 mg/kg BD | 0.2 mg/kg BD (Day 7) | 0.4 mg/kg/day (Max 17 mg/day) |

### Administration Instructions
- May be taken with or without food.
- Compatible with a ketogenic diet.
- Compatible with most enteral feeding tubes; flush the tube three times with water after administration.
- Use the provided 3 mL (green) or 6 mL (purple) graduated syringes for accurate dosing.

### Missed Dose and Discontinuation
- **Missed Dose:** Not specified in available source.
- **Discontinuation:** Dose must be decreased gradually to minimize the risk of increased seizure frequency and status epilepticus [Australian].

## 4. Contraindications
- Hypersensitivity to fenfluramine or any excipients.
- Aortic or mitral valvular heart disease.
- Pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH).
- Concomitant use of monoamine oxidase inhibitors (MAOIs), or use within 14 days of administration, due to the risk of serotonin syndrome [Australian].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warning: Valvular Heart Disease (VHD) and Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension (PAH) [Australian]
- Fenfluramine is associated with 5-HT2B receptor agonist activity, linked to VHD and PAH.
- **Monitoring:** Echocardiogram is required before treatment, every 6 months for the first two years, and annually thereafter. A final echocardiogram is required 6 months after the last dose.
- **Access:** Only available through a controlled access program to ensure cardiac monitoring and prevent off-label weight management use.

### General Precautions
- **Decreased Appetite and Weight Loss:** Highly prevalent; weight should be monitored. Risk is dose-related and may be additive with stiripentol.
- **Somnolence and Fatigue:** Common adverse effects; caution when used with other CNS depressants.
- **Suicidal Behaviour and Ideation:** Monitor for emerging signs of depression or suicidal thoughts.
- **Serotonin Syndrome:** Potentially life-threatening. Risk increases with other serotonergic agents (SSRIs, SNRIs, TCAs, triptans, MAOIs).
- **Glaucoma:** May cause mydriasis and precipitate angle-closure glaucoma. Discontinue if acute decrease in visual acuity occurs.
- **Increased Seizure Frequency:** As with all AEDs, a paradoxical increase in seizures may occur.

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Frequency in Dravet Syndrome (Active groups combined vs Placebo)
- **Very Common (>10%):** Decreased appetite (33.3%), diarrhoea (19.9%), pyrexia (15.7%), fatigue (12.5%), somnolence (13.0%), decreased blood glucose (11.6%), and abnormal echocardiogram (18.1% - note: predominantly trace/mild regurgitation considered physiologic) [Australian].
- **Common (1–10%):** Weight decreased (7.4%), upper respiratory tract infection (8.8%), lethargy (9.7%), tremor (6.5%), and status epilepticus (5.1%).

### Frequency in Lennox-Gastaut Syndrome
- **Very Common (>10%):** Decreased appetite (27.8%), fatigue (13.6%), somnolence (13.6%), diarrhoea (11.9%), and vomiting (10.8%) [Australian].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Stiripentol:** Increases fenfluramine exposure (AUC) by 130%. Dose reduction of fenfluramine is required.
- **Cannabidiol (CBD):** Increases fenfluramine AUC by 59%. No dose adjustment required.
- **Strong CYP1A2 or CYP2D6 Inhibitors (e.g., Fluvoxamine, Paroxetine):** Increase fenfluramine exposure. Dose reduction required (Max 20 mg/day without stiripentol; 17 mg/day with stiripentol).
- **Strong CYP1A2/CYP2B6/CYP3A Inducers (e.g., Rifampin):** Decrease fenfluramine exposure. Dose increase may be considered, not to exceed 52 mg/day.
- **Cyproheptadine:** Serotonin antagonist; may reduce fenfluramine efficacy.
- **MAOIs:** Contraindicated within 14 days due to serotonin syndrome risk.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Australian Category D. Limited data in humans. Animal studies showed embryofetal death and malformations at doses with maternal toxicity. Not recommended for women of childbearing potential not using contraception [Australian].
- **Breastfeeding:** Contraindicated. Risk to the breastfed child cannot be excluded; dexfenfluramine is known to be excreted in milk [Australian].
- **Fertility:** Animal data suggest potential effects on male and female fertility (decreased implantations, abnormal sperm) [Australian].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety/efficacy not established in children < 2 years.
- **Elderly:** No data available for patients $\ge$ 65 years.
- **Renal Impairment:** No adjustment for mild/moderate. For severe (eGFR 15–29 mL/min/1.73m²), max dose is 20 mg/day (17 mg/day with stiripentol) [Australian].
- **Hepatic Impairment:**
    - Mild (Child-Pugh A): Max 20 mg/day (13 mg with stiripentol).
    - Moderate (Child-Pugh B): Max 20 mg/day (Use with stiripentol not recommended).
    - Severe (Child-Pugh C): Max 17 mg/day (Use with stiripentol not recommended) [Australian].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Serotonin releasing agent that stimulates multiple 5-HT receptors (1D, 2A, 2C) via serotonin release. It also acts as a positive modulator of the sigma-1 receptor.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** $T_{max}$ 3–5 hours. Bioavailability 68–83%. No food effect.
    - **Distribution:** 50% protein bound. $V_d$ 11.9 L/kg.
    - **Metabolism:** >75% metabolised by CYP1A2, 2B6, and 2D6 to the active metabolite norfenfluramine.
    - **Elimination:** >90% renal excretion. Half-life 20 hours (fenfluramine) and 30 hours (norfenfluramine).

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Dravet Syndrome (Study 1, 2, 3):** Trials showed significant mean reductions in monthly convulsive seizure frequency (up to 64.8% reduction in the 0.7 mg/kg/day group vs placebo).
- **Lennox-Gastaut Syndrome (Study 4):** Showed a 26.5% median reduction in drop seizures for the 0.7 mg/kg/day group compared to 7.6% for placebo (p=0.0013) [Australian].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store below 30°C.
- **Restrictions:** Do not refrigerate or freeze.
- **Shelf Life:** 4 years. Discard 3 months after first opening the bottle [Australian].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Australia:** Poison Schedule S4 (Prescription Only Medicine). Subject to additional monitoring ($\blacktriangledown$).
