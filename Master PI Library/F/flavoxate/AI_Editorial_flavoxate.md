# Editorial Product Information: Flavoxate Hydrochloride

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial synthesis is derived from the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States** (Food and Drug Administration).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, consolidating regulatory information into a structured format for clinical reference. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory documents for definitive prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Flavoxate hydrochloride
- **Chemical Class:** Synthetic urinary tract spasmolytic (flavone derivative)
- **Brand Names:** Not specified in available sources [US generic document provided]
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 100 mg film-coated tablets [US]
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** PuraCap Laboratories LLC dba Blu Pharmaceuticals (Distributor); Epic Pharma, LLC (Manufacturer) [US]

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Symptomatic Relief:** Indicated for the management of dysuria, urgency, nocturia, suprapubic pain, frequency, and incontinence associated with:
    - Cystitis
    - Prostatitis
    - Urethritis
    - Urethrocystitis/Urethrotrigonitis
- **Clinical Note:** Flavoxate is intended for symptomatic relief and is not indicated for the definitive treatment of infections; however, it is compatible for co-administration with urinary tract anti-infectives [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **Adults and Paediatrics (>12 years):** 100 mg or 200 mg (one or two 100 mg tablets) administered three or four times daily [US].
- **Titration:** Upon improvement of symptoms, the dosage may be reduced [US].
- **Missed Dose:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Administration:** Oral tablets.

## 4. Contraindications
Flavoxate hydrochloride is contraindicated in patients with the following obstructive conditions [US]:
- Pyloric or duodenal obstruction
- Obstructive intestinal lesions or ileus
- Achalasia
- Gastrointestinal hemorrhage
- Obstructive uropathies of the lower urinary tract

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Glaucoma:** Use with caution in patients with suspected glaucoma [US].
- **CNS Effects:** Patients should be advised that the drug may cause drowsiness or blurred vision. Caution is required when operating motor vehicles, machinery, or performing tasks requiring alertness [US].
- **Monitoring:** Not specified in available sources.

## 6. Adverse Effects
Frequencies for adverse effects are not provided in the source documentation due to insufficient data for estimation [US].

- **Gastrointestinal:** Nausea, vomiting, dry mouth.
- **Central Nervous System:** Vertigo, headache, drowsiness, nervousness, and mental confusion (particularly in elderly patients).
- **Hematologic:** Leukopenia (one reported case, reversible upon discontinuation).
- **Cardiovascular:** Tachycardia, palpitation.
- **Allergic/Dermatologic:** Urticaria and other dermatoses, eosinophilia, hyperpyrexia.
- **Ophthalmic:** Increased ocular tension, blurred vision, disturbance in ocular accommodation.
- **Renal:** Dysuria.

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Management Notes:** While the source indicates compatibility with drugs used for urinary tract infections, specific interaction profiles with other medications are not detailed [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category B:** Reproduction studies in rats and rabbits at doses up to 34 times the human dose showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. There are no well-controlled studies in pregnant women. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** It is unknown if flavoxate is excreted in human milk. Exercise caution when administering to nursing women [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies revealed no evidence of impaired fertility [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established in children under 12 years of age. Use is not recommended in this age group [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Risk of mental confusion is noted specifically for the elderly population [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Flavoxate hydrochloride exerts a direct antispasmodic effect on the smooth muscle of the urinary tract [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Counteracts urinary tract muscle spasms.
- **Pharmacokinetics:** 
    - **Onset of action:** Approximately 55 minutes [US].
    - **Peak effect:** Observed at 112 minutes [US].
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 57% of the dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pharmacokinetic Study:** A study involving 11 healthy male subjects established the onset and peak effect times and renal excretion rates for the drug [US].
- **General Safety/Efficacy:** Safety and efficacy in children <12 years have not been demonstrated [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Prescription only (Rx only). Classified as a synthetic urinary tract spasmolytic.
