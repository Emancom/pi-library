# Editorial Product Information: Ferrous Sulfate

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **US (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising available regulatory data into a single clinical reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific PI or labelling required for prescribing or dispensing in any specific region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Ferrous sulfate (providing 15 mg of elemental ferrous cation per 1 mL) [US].
- **Brand Name(s):** OneVITE Pediatric, Fe-Vite, Brain Boost [US].
- **ATC Code:** B03AA07 (Ferrous sulfate).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Oral solution (drops); 15 mg elemental iron (as ferrous sulfate) per 1 mL [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Oral.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Akron Pharma [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Dietary Supplementation:** For the prevention or treatment of iron deficiency in infants and young children [US].
- **Jurisdictional Nuance:** In the United States, this specific product is marketed as a dietary supplement and has not been evaluated by the FDA to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Paediatric)
- **Infants through 12 months of age:** 1.0 mL daily [US].
- **Children 1 through 4 years of age:** 1.0 mL daily [US].

### Administration Instructions
- **Preparation:** Shake the bottle well before each use [US].
- **Measurement:** Use only the provided calibrated dropper to measure the 1.0 mL dose [US].
- **Method:** The solution may be administered directly into the mouth or mixed with formula, fruit juice, cereal, or other foods to increase palatability [US].
- **Hygiene:** Rinse and retain the dropper for subsequent dosing [US].

### Titration and Missed Doses
- Not specified in available sources.

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warning (Accidental Overdose)
- **[US]:** Accidental overdose of iron-containing products is a leading cause of fatal poisoning in children under 6 years of age. Keep this product out of the reach of children. In case of accidental overdose, contact a physician or poison control centre immediately.

### General Precautions
- **Storage Safety:** Must be kept out of reach of children due to toxicity risks [US].
- **Supervision:** Should be administered under the guidance of a healthcare professional [US].

### Monitoring Requirements
- Not specified in available sources.

## 6. Adverse Effects
Available data identifies the following adverse effects associated with oral ferrous sulfate solution:

| Frequency | Adverse Effect |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Common** | Temporary discoloration of teeth [US] |
| **Common** | Darkening of stool colour [US] |

- **Clinical Note:** Temporary tooth discoloration can be minimised by thorough brushing. Darkened stools are a benign side effect of oral iron therapy [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Ferrous sulfate serves as a source of elemental iron, an essential mineral required for the formation of haemoglobin and the transport of oxygen in the blood [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** One daily dose provides 100% of the Daily Value (DV) for infants and 150% of the DV for children aged 1–4 years [US].

- **Conditions:** Protect from freezing. After opening, store away from direct light. Refrigeration is not required [US].
