# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine Injection

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the **US (FDA)** for Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter Healthcare Corporation). At the time of compilation, documents from other jurisdictions (e.g., Australian, European, Canadian) were not provided for comparison.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview intended for use by healthcare professionals.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official PI required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority’s approved labelling.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP), a carrier molecule required for long-chain fatty acid transport across the inner mitochondrial membrane.
- **Chemical Name:** (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Sterile solution for intravenous use; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency:** For the acute and chronic management of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD undergoing hemodialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
- **Recommended Dose:** 50 mg/kg administered as a slow bolus (2–3 minutes) or by infusion [US].
- **Severe Crisis:** A loading dose is often followed by equivalent doses administered every 3 or 4 hours. Dosing should occur at least every 6 hours [US].
- **Titration:** Subsequent daily doses are typically 50 mg/kg, though higher doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilized as clinically required [US].
- **Monitoring:** It is recommended to obtain baseline plasma carnitine levels. Weekly and monthly monitoring should target a plasma free carnitine concentration between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
- **Administration Timing:** Administer as a slow bolus (2–3 minutes) into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].
- **Dose Adjustment:** Adjustments (e.g., downward to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy, guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations (normal range: 40–50 µmol/L) [US].

### General Instructions
- **Inspection:** Visually inspect for particulate matter or discoloration before use [US].
- **Missed Dose:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Known Hypersensitivity:** No contraindications are formally listed in the US PI, though hypersensitivity is a recorded risk (see Warnings) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None [US].
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm have been reported, primarily in dialysis patients. These may occur within minutes of administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and consider the risks/benefits before re-administration [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Levocarnitine (oral or IV) has been associated with seizures in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with a known history, an increase in seizure frequency or severity has been reported [US].
- **Oral Formulation Safety:** The safety and efficacy of oral levocarnitine have not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral use in ESRD patients may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects observed in clinical trials of ESRD patients on hemodialysis (incidence ≥5%) include:

| System Organ Class | Adverse Effect (Incidence ≥5%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Injection site reaction (33%), pain (30%), flu syndrome (25%), headache (22%), infection (15%), chest pain (12%), accidental injury (10%), fever (7%) |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension (20%), hypotension (14%), tachycardia (6%), palpitations (5%) |
| **Gastrointestinal** | Diarrhea (16%), vomiting (15%), nausea (8%), dyspepsia (5%), anorexia (5%) |
| **Metabolic/Nutritional** | Hypercalcemia (9%), hyperkalemia (6%), hypervolemia (5%), peripheral edema (5%), weight decrease (5%) |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis (23%), cough increase (9%), rhinitis (9%), dyspnea (7%) |
| **Neurological** | Dizziness (13%), insomnia (4%), paresthesia (5%) |

- **Other Noted Effects:** Transient nausea/vomiting, body odor, and gastritis have been observed [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Anaphylaxis and seizures [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after any dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not specified by letter]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at doses up to 3.8 times the human surface area dose showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Dairy cow data indicates levocarnitine concentrations in milk increase following exogenous administration. Clinicians must weigh the risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant against the benefits of maternal supplementation [US].
- **Fertility:** No impairment of fertility was observed in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders is weight-based (50 mg/kg). Refer to Section 3 [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically detailed in available sources beyond general adult dosing.
- **Renal Impairment:** Safely used in ESRD patients on hemodialysis (IV formulation). Note precautions regarding metabolite accumulation with oral formulations in this population [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not protein-bound. Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. Mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In ESRD patients, IV administration increases plasma levocarnitine concentrations and may alleviate metabolic abnormalities associated with carnitine deficiency [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Studies:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients receiving 20 mg/kg three times weekly after dialysis showed mean trough concentrations increased from 21.3 µmol/L to 190 µmol/L by week 8.
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** Studies using 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg showed dose-dependent increases in trough levocarnitine levels over 12 and 24 weeks.
- **Note:** While IV levocarnitine effectively increases carnitine concentrations, the specific impact on clinical outcomes (e.g., muscle weakness, cardiomyopathy) in the ESRD population has not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C–30°C [US].
- **Precautions:** Avoid excessive heat. Protect from freezing [US].
- **Shelf Life/Handling:** Single-dose vials; discard any unused portion as the formulation lacks preservatives. Compatible with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s for up to 24 hours at room temperature in PVC bags [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Rx-only. Poison schedule not applicable [US].
