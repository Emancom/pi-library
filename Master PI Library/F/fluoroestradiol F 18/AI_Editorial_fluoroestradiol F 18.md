# Editorial Product Information: Fluoroestradiol F 18

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information documents contributed to this editorial: US (FDA).
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising regulatory data into a single clinical reference.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis intended for informational purposes and is not a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing or administering this agent within a specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Fluoroestradiol F 18 (synthetic estrogen analog).
- **Brand Name(s):** **CERIANNA** [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Clear, colorless injection solution in a 50 mL multiple-dose vial. Activity concentration ranges from 148 MBq/mL to 3,700 MBq/mL (4 mCi/mL to 100 mCi/mL) at the end of synthesis [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** GE Healthcare Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Detection of Estrogen Receptor (ER)-Positive Lesions:** Indicated for use with positron emission tomography (PET) imaging as an adjunct to biopsy in patients with recurrent or metastatic breast cancer [US-approved].

**Limitations of Use:**
- Fluoroestradiol F 18 is not intended to replace tissue biopsy for confirming breast cancer recurrence or verifying ER status via pathology [US].
- This agent is not effective for imaging other receptors, including human epidermal growth factor receptor 2 (HER2) or the progesterone receptor (PR) [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** The recommended dose is 222 MBq (6 mCi), with an acceptable range of 111 MBq to 222 MBq (3 mCi to 6 mCi) [US].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Administer as a single intravenous injection over 1 to 2 minutes [US].
    - Use aseptic technique and radiation shielding (e.g., syringe shields) during preparation and administration [US].
    - Fluoroestradiol F 18 may be diluted with 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection, USP [US].
    - Follow the injection with an intravenous flush of 0.9% Sodium Chloride [US].
    - Total injection volume should be 10 mL or less [US].
- **Patient Preparation:**
    - **Hydration:** Patients should drink water to ensure adequate hydration before administration and continue frequent voiding for the first several hours post-injection to reduce radiation exposure to the bladder [US].
    - **Drug Interaction Assessment:** Confirm the discontinuation of ER-binding therapies for appropriate durations before imaging (see Section 7) [US].
- **Image Acquisition:**
    - Recommended start time is 80 minutes (range 20 to 80 minutes) after injection [US].
    - Patients should be positioned supine, preferably with arms above the head [US].
    - Typical scan duration is 20 to 30 minutes [US].
- **Missed Dose:** Not applicable for this single-dose diagnostic procedure.

## 4. Contraindications
- None [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Risk of Misdiagnosis:**
    - **Biopsy Priority:** Do not use PET imaging with this agent as a substitute for biopsy when a biopsy is clinically indicated.
    - **Discordant Results:** If PET imaging is negative but clinical characteristics or pathology suggest a potential benefit from hormonal therapy, clinical judgment/pathology should take precedence [US].
    - **Uptake Specificity:** Uptake is not specific to breast cancer; it can occur in other ER-positive tissues or tumors, such as those in the ovaries or uterus [US].
- **Radiation Risks:** As a radioactive diagnostic agent, fluoroestradiol F 18 contributes to a patient’s overall long-term cumulative radiation exposure, which is associated with an increased risk of cancer. Strict adherence to safe handling protocols is required to protect both patients and healthcare workers [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions were evaluated in clinical studies involving 1,207 patients. Reported reactions occurred at a frequency of **< 1%** (Rare) [US]:
- **General Disorders:** Injection-site pain.
- **Neurological/Gastrointestinal:** Dysgeusia (taste perversion).

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Drugs that bind to the estrogen receptor (ER) will compete with fluoroestradiol F 18, potentially leading to false-negative PET scans [US].
- **Management:** Discontinue ER-binding agents for at least five biological half-lives prior to administration [US]. Examples include:
    - **Tamoxifen (SERM):** Discontinue for 8 weeks [US].
    - **Fulvestrant (SERD):** Discontinue for 28 weeks [US].
    - **Elacestrant (SERD):** Discontinue for 11 days [US].
- **Note:** The concurrent use of aromatase inhibitors is permitted as they do not bind directly to the ER [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Category:** Not specified by letter category in available sources, but the US PI notes all radiopharmaceuticals have the potential to cause fetal harm. 
    - **Risk Summary:** There are no data regarding use in pregnant women. Animal reproduction studies have not been conducted. If pregnancy is suspected, a clinical assessment of the risk-benefit ratio is required, considering the radiation dose to the fetus [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** There are no data on the presence of the drug in human milk. To minimize radiation exposure to a breastfed infant, advise lactating women to avoid breastfeeding for **4 hours** after the administration of fluoroestradiol F 18 [US].
- **Fertility:** No animal studies have been performed to evaluate the impact on male or female fertility [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established in paediatric patients [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Clinical studies did not identify differences in pharmacokinetics or biodistribution in patients aged 65 years and older compared to younger adults [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources (though the drug is primarily metabolized in the liver) [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Fluoroestradiol F 18 is a synthetic estrogen analog that binds to the ER with high affinity ($K_d$ = 0.13 ± 0.02 nM). It allows for the non-invasive assessment of ER density and function in tumors [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** 95% bound to plasma proteins. It distributes primarily to the hepatobiliary system, intestines, kidneys, uterus, and bladder [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Rapidly metabolized in the liver. By 20 minutes post-injection, only 20% of circulating radioactivity remains as non-metabolized drug; by 2 hours, levels are < 5% of peak concentration [US].
    - **Elimination:** Excreted via both biliary and urinary pathways [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Uptake in tumors is directly proportional to ER expression as measured by in vitro assays [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Effectiveness:** The efficacy was primarily based on a study of 90 women (Study 1) with recurrent or metastatic breast cancer.
- **Outcomes:** PET imaging results were compared to biopsy (Allred score). In patients with a positive biopsy (Allred score $\ge$ 3), imaging was positive in 36 of 47 cases. Most false-negative scans (10 of 11) occurred in patients with lower biopsy scores (Allred 3–6). In patients with negative biopsies, imaging was negative in all 38 cases [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Storage Temperature:** Store at controlled room temperature, 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Store the vial upright inside its original shielded container. 
- **Shelf Life:** The product must be used within **12 hours** of the end of synthesis [US].
- **Disposal:** Dispose of unused radioactive materials in accordance with local and federal regulations [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** 
    - **United States:** Rx-only; Radioactive material (Requires specific licensure from the Nuclear Regulatory Commission or Agreement State) [US].
