# Editorial Product Information: Ferric Pyrophosphate Citrate

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **US (FDA)**. 
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview of ferric pyrophosphate citrate for healthcare professionals.
- This document is an editorial synthesis intended for informational purposes and is not a substitute for the official PI or summary of product characteristics (SmPC) required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. 

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Ferric pyrophosphate citrate (Iron (III)).
- **Chemical Class:** Mixed-ligand iron complex.
- **Brand Names:** **Triferic** [US].
- **ATC Code:** B03AC (Iron parenteral preparations - proposed/common category).
- **Dosage Forms and Strengths:**
    - **Solution:** 27.2 mg of iron (III) per 5 mL (5.44 mg/mL) in single-dose ampules [US].
    - **Powder for Solution:** 272 mg of iron (III) per packet [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Hemodialysis (via addition to hemodialysate) [US].
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Rockwell Medical, Inc. [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Maintenance of Hemoglobin:** Indicated for the replacement of iron to maintain hemoglobin levels in adult patients with hemodialysis-dependent chronic kidney disease (HDD-CKD) [US].
- **Limitations of Use:** 
    - Not intended for use in patients receiving peritoneal dialysis [US].
    - Not studied in patients receiving home hemodialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
The objective is to achieve a final iron (III) concentration of 2 micromolar (110 mcg/L) in the hemodialysate [US].

### Administration Instructions
- **Addition to Dialysate:** Ferric pyrophosphate citrate must be added only to the **bicarbonate concentrate**. It must **NOT** be added to acid concentrate mixtures [US].
- **Preparation of Dialysate:**
    - **Solution:** Add one 5 mL ampule to every 2.5 gallons (9.46 liters) of bicarbonate concentrate [US].
    - **Powder:** Add one packet (272 mg) to every 25 gallons (94.6 liters) of bicarbonate concentrate [US].
- **Frequency:** Administer during each dialysis procedure for the duration of maintenance hemodialysis therapy [US].
- **Stability:** Once added to the bicarbonate concentrate, the mixture should be used within 24 hours [US].
- **Inspection:** Solution ampules should be inspected for precipitation; the solution should appear slightly yellow-green [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None specified in available sources [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious and potentially fatal anaphylactic-type reactions have occurred with parenteral iron products. Symptoms include shock, hypotension, loss of consciousness, and collapse. Patients should be monitored during and after hemodialysis until clinically stable. Ensure immediate availability of resuscitative equipment and personnel [US].
- **Iron Laboratory Testing:** To avoid overestimation of serum iron and transferrin saturation (TSAT), blood samples for iron status should be drawn **pre-dialysis** [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common Adverse Reactions (Incidence >3% in Clinical Trials)
| System Organ Class | Adverse Reaction | Frequency (Triferic vs. Placebo) [US] |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vascular/Procedural** | Procedural hypotension | 21.6% vs 19.3% |
| **Musculoskeletal** | Muscle spasms | 9.6% vs 8.1% |
| **Nervous System** | Headache | 9.2% vs 5.4% |
| **General** | Peripheral edema | 6.8% vs 3.7% |
| **Infections** | Urinary tract infection | 4.5% vs 1.4% |
| **General** | Pyrexia | 4.5% vs 3.0% |
| **General** | Fatigue / Asthenia | 3.8%–4.1% vs 2.0%–3.0% |
| **Respiratory** | Dyspnea | 5.8% vs 4.4% |

### Serious Adverse Effects
- Arteriovenous (AV) fistula thrombosis (3.4%) and AV fistula site hemorrhage (3.4%) have been reported [US].
- Serious hypersensitivity/anaphylaxis (0.3% reported in trials) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic/Pharmacodynamic Interactions:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Notes:** In clinical trials, the majority of patients were receiving stable doses of erythropoiesis-stimulating agents (ESAs) concurrently [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** No human data available. Animal studies (rats/rabbits) showed adverse developmental outcomes only at maternally toxic doses significantly higher than theoretical human exposure. Use only if the potential benefit outweighs the risk to the fetus [US].
    - **Contraception:** Females of reproductive potential should use effective contraception during treatment and for at least 2 weeks following the final dose [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** No data exist regarding presence in human milk or effects on milk production. Consider the clinical need of the mother and potential adverse effects on the infant [US].
- **Fertility:** No adverse effects on fertility or reproduction were noted in animal studies at doses up to 40 mg/kg [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established in pediatric patients [US].
- **Elderly Use:** No overall differences in safety or efficacy were observed between patients ≥65 years of age and younger patients [US].
- **Renal Impairment:** Not specifically addressed; the drug is indicated specifically for use in patients with HDD-CKD during dialysis [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Ferric pyrophosphate citrate is added to the dialysate and transfers across the dialyzer membrane into the circulation. It binds directly to transferrin and is transported to erythroid precursor cells for incorporation into hemoglobin [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Apparent volume of distribution (Vz) ranges from 0.765 to 0.859 L [US].
    - **Elimination:** The half-life of serum iron is approximately 1.48 hours [US].
    - **Dialysis Conditions:** A reduction in blood and dialysate flow rates can decrease the cumulative iron delivered by approximately 33% [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pivotal Trials:** Two randomized, placebo-controlled trials in adult HDD-CKD patients (N=292 treated with Triferic).
- **Design:** Patients had Hb 9–12 g/dL, TSAT >20%, and ferritin >200 mcg/L. Triferic (110 mcg iron/L) was added to dialysate for up to 48 weeks [US].
- **Primary Outcome:** Triferic significantly maintained hemoglobin levels compared to placebo. Mean change in Hb from baseline to end-of-treatment was -0.03 to -0.08 g/dL in the Triferic group compared to -0.38 to -0.44 g/dL in the placebo group [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Ampules (Solution):** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F). **Protect from light**; keep ampules in the aluminum pouch until use [US].
- **Packets (Powder):** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Do not freeze** [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** 
    - **US:** Rx-only (Prescription Only).
