# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) document from the following jurisdiction: **US (FDA)**. 
- At the time of preparation, PI documents from other major jurisdictions (e.g., Australian, European, UK, Canadian, Japanese) were not provided for comparison. 
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for the official, current PI required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult local regulatory documents for legal prescribing requirements.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN/USP), a carrier molecule for long-chain fatty acid transport.
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Brand Names:** Not specified in available sources (generic provided as Levocarnitine Injection, USP).
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - **Injection:** 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) single-dose vial [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only [US].
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    - Recommended dose is 50 mg/kg [US].
    - Administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion [US].
    - A loading dose is often required in severe metabolic crises, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours [US].
    - Dosing frequency should be every 3 or 4 hours (q3h/q4h) and never less frequent than every 6 hours (q6h) [US].
    - Highest recorded dose: 300 mg/kg [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - Recommended starting dose: 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
    - Administration: Slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line after each dialysis session [US].
    - Adjustments: Dose reductions (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be made as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough carnitine levels [US].

### Monitoring and Adjustments
- **Plasma Carnitine Levels:** It is recommended to obtain levels prior to treatment and monitor weekly or monthly. Free carnitine levels should ideally be maintained between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].
- **Trough Levels (ESRD):** Initiation of therapy in ESRD may be prompted by pre-dialysis plasma concentrations below 40–50 µmol/L [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Known Hypersensitivity:** Not explicitly listed as a contraindication in the "Contraindications" section, but "None known" is stated [US]. However, serious reactions are detailed in Warnings (see Section 5).

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Boxed Warnings
- None specified in available sources [US].

### General Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients on dialysis. Reactions may occur within minutes of administration [US].
- **Neurologic Reactions (Seizures):** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Oral Formulation Cautions:** The safety of *oral* levocarnitine has not been evaluated in renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral administration in ESRD may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) normally excreted in urine [US].

### Monitoring Requirements
- **Warfarin Therapy:** Reports indicate an increase in International Normalized Ratio (INR) when used with warfarin. Monitor INR levels after initiation or dose adjustment [US].
- **Clinical Monitoring:** ESRD patients should be monitored for blood chemistry and vital signs alongside carnitine levels [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common and Very Common Adverse Effects
In clinical trials of ESRD patients (frequency ≥5% regardless of causality):
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (18–21%), hypotension (3–19%), tachycardia (5–9%) [US].
- **Gastrointestinal:** Diarrhea (9–35%), vomiting (9–21%), nausea (5–12%), abdominal pain (5–21%) [US].
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (15–27%), cough increase (10–18%), dyspnea (3–11%) [US].
- **Neurological:** Headache (3–37%), dizziness (10–18%), paresthesia (3–12%) [US].
- **Body as a Whole:** Injection site reaction (27–38%), chest pain (6–15%), fever (5–12%), infection (10–24%), asthenia (8–12%) [US].
- **Metabolic:** Hypercalcemia (6–15%), hypervolemia (3–12%) [US].

### Other Reported Effects
- **General:** Transient nausea and vomiting, body odor, and gastritis [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Anaphylaxis, bronchospasm, and laryngeal edema (hypersensitivity) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Pharmacodynamic interaction leading to increased INR. Recommended management: Frequent INR monitoring during levocarnitine initiation or titration [US].
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Levocarnitine is not protein-bound; therefore, it is unlikely to interact with drugs through protein-binding displacement [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not specified as a letter grade, but reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm [US].
    - **Recommendation:** Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** 
    - Levocarnitine is a normal component of human milk. Supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied [US].
    - In dairy cows, levocarnitine milk concentrations increase after administration. 
    - **Recommendation:** Weigh the benefits of maternal supplementation against potential risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant; discontinuation of nursing or the drug may be considered [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders is consistent with adult weight-based dosing (50 mg/kg) [US]. 
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:**
    - **IV Administration:** Indicated for ESRD on dialysis (10–20 mg/kg) [US].
    - **Oral Administration Caution:** Safety of oral formulations in renal insufficiency is not established; risk of toxic metabolite accumulation [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Facilitates the entry of long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also facilitates the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites are trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    - **Excretion:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours [US].
    - **Half-life:** Mean distribution half-life of 0.585 hours; terminal elimination half-life of 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pharmacokinetic Studies (ESRD):** In a 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients (20 mg/kg 3x/week post-dialysis), trough levels increased from a baseline of ~21 µmol/L to ~190 µmol/L by week 8 [US].
- **Dose-Ranging Studies:** Trials using 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg in ESRD patients showed dose-proportional increases in trough carnitine concentrations at 12 and 24 weeks [US].
- **Note on Outcomes:** While IV levocarnitine increases carnitine concentrations in ESRD, the impact on specific clinical outcomes in this population remains undetermined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C [US].
- **Conditions:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat. Discard any unused portion as the formulation is preservative-free [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable for up to 24 hours in PVC bags at room temperature when mixed with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s (0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources (N/A for US jurisdiction).
