# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following regulatory authority: **United States (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of Levocarnitine injection for use by healthcare professionals. It synthesises clinical and regulatory data into a concise, structured format.
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific PI. Clinicians should consult the local approved labelling when prescribing or administering this medication in their specific region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN).
- **Chemical Class:** Carrier molecule/Amino acid derivative [(R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt].
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection, USP (Baxter Healthcare Corporation) [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection; 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency (Inborn Errors of Metabolism):** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** For the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing maintenance dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult and Paediatric)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    - The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion [US].
    - In severe metabolic crises, a loading dose may be given, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours.
    - Frequency: Administer every 3 or 4 hours, and never less than every 6 hours [US].
    - Maximum administered dose reported is 300 mg/kg [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - Recommended starting dose: 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight) [US].
    - Administration: Slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line after each dialysis session [US].
    - Dose adjustments: Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be made as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter and discoloration before use [US].
- Discard any unused portion of a single-dose vial; the formulation contains no preservatives [US].

### Monitoring Requirements
- Obtain baseline plasma carnitine concentrations before initiating parenteral therapy.
- Weekly and monthly monitoring is recommended, including blood chemistry, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine level: 35 to 60 µmol/L) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known at the time of publication [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have occurred, primarily in ESRD patients. Reactions can occur within minutes of administration. Discontinue treatment immediately if severe reactions occur [US].
- **Seizures:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with pre-existing conditions, an increase in frequency or severity has been noted [US].
- **Metabolite Accumulation:** Chronic high-dose oral levocarnitine (not specifically the injection) in patients with severely compromised renal function may lead to the accumulation of potentially toxic metabolites, trimethylamine (TMA) and trimethylamine-N-oxide (TMAO) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
### Common Adverse Effects (≥5% in ESRD clinical trials)
Based on placebo-controlled trials in chronic hemodialysis patients [US]:

| Body System | Adverse Event (Regardless of Causality) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Injection site reaction (33%), Pain (30%), Flu syndrome (25%), Headache (22%), Abdominal pain (9%) |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension (20%), Hypotension (14%), Tachycardia (6%) |
| **Gastrointestinal** | Diarrhea (16%), Vomiting (15%), Nausea (8%) |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis (23%), Cough increase (9%), Dyspnea (7%) |
| **Neurological** | Dizziness (13%), Paresthesia (5%) |

### Other Reported Effects
- **Gastrointestinal:** Gastritis and transient nausea/vomiting [US].
- **General:** Body odor [US].
- **Postmarketing:** Seizures and hypersensitivity reactions (anaphylaxis) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalised Ratio (INR) have been observed when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** It is recommended to monitor INR levels after initiating levocarnitine or following dose adjustments in patients on warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** US Category: Not formally assigned (narrative provided). Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (surface area basis) revealed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk carnitine after exogenous administration. Weigh the risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant against maternal benefits. Consideration may be given to discontinuing nursing or the drug [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders is consistent with adult weight-based recommendations (50 mg/kg) [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically addressed in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** 
    - Used in ESRD patients undergoing dialysis [US].
    - Safety of oral levocarnitine has not been evaluated in renal insufficiency; chronic high doses may lead to metabolite accumulation [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** A naturally occurring substance required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane for beta-oxidation and energy production [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Levocarnitine clears accumulated acyl-CoA compounds (common in certain organic acidemias) by forming acylcarnitine, which is rapidly excreted [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin. Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites are trimethylamine N-oxide (TMAO) and γ-butyrobetaine [US].
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an intravenous dose is excreted in the urine over 24 hours. Mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Pharmacokinetics:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients on hemodialysis receiving 20 mg/kg three times weekly post-dialysis showed that trough (pre-dialysis) concentrations increased from a baseline of ~21 µmol/L to ~190 µmol/L after 8 weeks [US].
- **Efficacy Note:** While levocarnitine increases carnitine concentrations in ESRD patients, the effects on specific clinical outcomes or signs/symptoms of deficiency in this population have not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [US].
- **Protection:** Avoid excessive heat. Protect from freezing [US].
- **Stability (Admixture):** Compatible and stable for up to 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources for other regions.
