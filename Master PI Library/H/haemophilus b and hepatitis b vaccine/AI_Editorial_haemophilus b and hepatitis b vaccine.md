# Editorial Product Information: VAXELIS

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** United States (FDA). 
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of **VAXELIS** (Diphtheria and Tetanus Toxoids and Acellular Pertussis Adsorbed, Inactivated Poliovirus, Haemophilus b Conjugate [Meningococcal Protein Conjugate], and Hepatitis B [Recombinant] Vaccine) for use by healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis of the provided regulatory documentation and is not a substitute for the official Product Information (PI) required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** 
    - Diphtheria Toxoid
    - Tetanus Toxoid
    - Acellular Pertussis antigens: Pertussis Toxin (PT), Filamentous Hemagglutinin (FHA), Pertactin (PRN), and Fimbriae Types 2 and 3 (FIM).
    - Inactivated Poliovirus (IPV): Type 1 (Mahoney), Type 2 (MEF-1), and Type 3 (Saukett).
    - *Haemophilus influenzae* type b (Hib) capsular polysaccharide (PRP) conjugated to Neisseria meningitidis outer membrane protein complex (OMPC).
    - Hepatitis B surface antigen (HBsAg).
- **Brand Name:** **VAXELIS** [US].
- **ATC Code:** J07CA09 (Diphtheria-haemophilus influenzae B-hepatitis B-pertussis-poliomyelitis-tetanus).
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Injectable suspension supplied in 0.5 mL single-dose prefilled syringes. Each 0.5 mL dose contains:
    - 15 Lf Diphtheria toxoid
    - 5 Lf Tetanus toxoid
    - 20 mcg PT, 20 mcg FHA, 3 mcg PRN, 5 mcg FIM
    - 29 D-antigen units (DU) Type 1, 7 DU Type 2, 26 DU Type 3 IPV
    - 3 mcg Hib PRP conjugated to 50 mcg OMPC
    - 10 mcg HBsAg
    - 319 mcg Aluminum (adjuvant)
- **Route of Administration:** Intramuscular (IM) injection.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Manufactured by Sanofi Vaccines Canada Ltd. for MSP Vaccine Company [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Active Immunization:** Indicated for the prevention of diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis, poliomyelitis, hepatitis B, and invasive disease caused by *Haemophilus influenzae* type b.
- **Age Specification:** Approved for use in children from 6 weeks through 4 years of age (prior to the 5th birthday) [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **Vaccination Schedule:** Administered as a 3-dose primary series.
    - **Standard Timing:** 2, 4, and 6 months of age [US].
    - **Minimum Age:** The first dose may be administered as early as 6 weeks of age [US].
- **Booster Requirements:** A 3-dose series of **VAXELIS** does not constitute a complete primary pertussis series; an additional dose of a pertussis-containing vaccine (e.g., DTaP) is required to complete the series [US].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Shake the syringe well until a uniform, white, cloudy suspension is formed.
    - Inspect for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration.
    - Administer via IM injection. The preferred site for infants <1 year is the anterolateral aspect of the thigh.
    - **Prohibitions:** Do not administer intravenously, intradermally, or subcutaneously. Do not inject into the gluteal area. Do not mix with other vaccines in the same syringe.
- **Interchangeability:** **VAXELIS** may be used to complete the first three doses of the 5-dose DTaP series in infants who have received 1 or 2 doses of Pentacel or DAPTACEL. It may also complete the hepatitis B series in infants born to HBsAg-negative mothers [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** History of severe allergic reaction (e.g., anaphylaxis) following a previous dose of **VAXELIS**, any of its components, or any vaccine containing diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis, polio, hepatitis B, or Hib antigens [US].
- **Encephalopathy:** Occurrence of encephalopathy (e.g., coma, prolonged seizures) within 7 days of a previous pertussis-containing vaccine, not attributable to another cause [US].
- **Neurologic Disorders:** Progressive neurologic disorders, including infantile spasms or uncontrolled epilepsy; vaccination should be deferred until the condition is stabilized [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Prior Pertussis Vaccine Reactions:** Carefully weigh the risks and benefits if any of the following occurred within 48 hours of a previous pertussis-containing vaccine:
    - Fever ≥40.5°C (≥105°F) not due to another cause.
    - Collapse or shock-like state (hypotonic-hyporesponsive episode).
    - Persistent, inconsolable crying lasting ≥3 hours.
    - Seizures (with or without fever) occurring within 3 days [US].
- **Guillain-Barré Syndrome (GBS):** If GBS occurred within 6 weeks of a previous tetanus toxoid vaccine, the decision to give **VAXELIS** should be based on a careful assessment of benefits and risks [US].
- **Premature Infants:** Apnea has been observed following IM vaccination in some infants born prematurely. Consider the medical status and potential benefits/risks [US].
- **Altered Immunocompetence:** Immunocompromised persons may have a reduced immune response [US].
- **Laboratory Interference:** Hib vaccine-derived antigen (PRP) may be detected in urine for at least 30 days post-vaccination, potentially yielding a false-positive result in suspected Hib disease [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse reactions were solicited for 0–5 days following each dose.

| **Irritability** | ≥55% (Very Common) |
| **Crying** | ≥45% (Very Common) |
| **Injection site pain** | ≥44% (Very Common) |
| **Somnolence** | ≥40% (Very Common) |
| **Injection site erythema** | ≥25% (Very Common) |

- **Serious Adverse Events (SAEs):** In clinical trials, 2.0% of **VAXELIS** recipients reported an SAE within 30 days. Vaccine-related SAEs occurred in 0.1% of participants, primarily pyrexia.
- **Postmarketing Experience:** Voluntary reports include anaphylactic reactions, seizures (including febrile seizures), and extensive swelling of the injected limb [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Concomitant Vaccines:** Clinical trials evaluated **VAXELIS** administered with Rotavirus Vaccine (Live, Oral, Pentavalent) and Pneumococcal 13-valent Conjugate Vaccine. No clinically significant interference was noted, though the non-inferiority criterion for one pneumococcal serotype (6B) was not met [US].
- **Immunosuppressants:** May reduce the expected immune response to the vaccine [US].
- **Laboratory Tests:** Interference with urine antigen detection for Hib (see Section 5) [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Not approved for use in individuals 5 years of age and older. No human or animal data are available to assess risks during pregnancy [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** No data available on the presence of **VAXELIS** in human milk or its effects on milk production or the breastfed infant [US].
- **Fertility:** **VAXELIS** has not been evaluated for impairment of fertility [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness have not been established in infants younger than 6 weeks or in children/adolescents aged 5 through 17 years [US].
- **Elderly/Adult Use:** Not indicated for adults.
- **Renal/Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** 
    - **Diphtheria/Tetanus:** Induces neutralizing antibodies against specific toxins. Serum antitoxin levels ≥0.1 IU/mL are considered protective.
    - **Pertussis:** MoA for protection is not fully defined, but involves antibodies against PT, FHA, PRN, and FIM.
    - **Poliomyelitis:** Induces neutralizing antibodies against Poliovirus Types 1, 2, and 3.
    - **Hepatitis B:** Antibody concentrations ≥10 mIU/mL against HBsAg correlate with protection.
    - **Hib:** Anti-PRP antibody levels ≥0.15 mcg/mL are considered the minimal protective level; ≥1.0 mcg/mL predicts long-term protection [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pivotal Studies (005 and 006):** Controlled studies in the US compared **VAXELIS** (at 2, 4, 6 months) to separate administration of Pentacel and RECOMBIVAX HB. 
- **Immunogenicity:** **VAXELIS** was non-inferior to control vaccines for all antigens based on seroprotection rates and Geometric Mean Concentrations (GMC) following the 3rd dose, with the exception of FHA GMCs in certain lot consistency evaluations [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) [US].
- **Special Precautions:** Do not freeze. Discard if the vaccine has been exposed to freezing temperatures. Protect from light.
- **Shelf Life:** Do not use after the expiration date shown on the label.

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Poison Schedule/Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Latex:** The syringe plunger stopper and tip cap are not made with natural rubber latex [US].
- **Preservatives:** **VAXELIS** contains no preservatives [US].
