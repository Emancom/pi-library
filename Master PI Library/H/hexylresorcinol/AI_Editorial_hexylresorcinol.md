# Editorial Product Information: Hexylresorcinol

## Editorial Note
- Contributing Jurisdictions: US (FDA).
- Purpose: This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of hexylresorcinol based on official Product Information (PI) for healthcare professionals. 
- Note: This synthesis is an editorial summary and does not replace the official PI required for prescribing or dispensing in any specific jurisdiction. At the time of publication, only US-based product information was available for review.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Hexylresorcinol.
- **Chemical Class:** Substituted phenol (Antiseptic/Local Anaesthetic).
- **Brand Name(s):** Mucinex InstaSoothe Sore Throat Plus Pain Relief [US].
- **ATC Code:** R02AA12 (Antiseptics, others).
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Medicated lozenge containing 2 mg hexylresorcinol [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Oral (topical mucosal).
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Reckitt Benckiser LLC [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
Hexylresorcinol is indicated for the temporary relief of occasional minor irritation and pain associated with:
- Sore throat [US].
- Sore mouth [US].
- Canker sores [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing
Lozenges should be allowed to dissolve slowly in the mouth. Do not exceed the recommended dosage [US].

### Dosing by Age Group [US]
- **Adults and children 12 years of age and older:** Take 2 lozenges (one immediately after the other). This may be repeated every 2 hours as needed.
- **Children 6 to under 12 years of age:** Take 1 lozenge. This may be repeated every 2 hours as needed.
- **Children under 6 years of age:** Use is not recommended/contraindicated.

### Administration Instructions
- Dissolve slowly in the mouth; do not swallow whole or chew.
- May be directed by a doctor or dentist.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Paediatrics:** Use is contraindicated in children under 6 years of age [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to hexylresorcinol or any inactive ingredients (e.g., FD&C Red No. 40, FD&C Blue No. 1) [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Sore Throat Warning
If a sore throat is severe, persists for more than 2 days, or is accompanied/followed by fever, headache, rash, swelling, nausea, or vomiting, a physician should be consulted promptly [US].

### General Precautions
- **Duration of Use:** Treatment should be discontinued and a healthcare provider consulted if:
    - Sore mouth symptoms do not improve within 7 days.
    - Irritation, pain, or redness persists or worsens.
    - Swelling, rash, or fever develops [US].
- **Overdose:** In case of accidental overdose, immediate medical attention or contact with a Poison Control Centre is required [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Frequencies of adverse effects are not explicitly quantified in available OTC labelling sources.
- **Potential Effects:** Local irritation, redness, or allergic reactions (rash, swelling) [US].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Not specified in available sources; however, persistent or worsening symptoms require medical intervention.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Approved for children 6 years and older. Contraindicated in children under 6 years [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Hexylresorcinol acts as an oral pain reliever (local anaesthetic) and has antiseptic properties [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Provides local symptomatic relief of oral and pharyngeal pain.
