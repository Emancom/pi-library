# Editorial Product Information: Hydroquinone

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, synthesising available regulatory data into a clinically relevant format.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and does not serve as a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific Product Information required for prescribing. Practitioners should consult their local regulatory authority's current approved labeling.
- **Regulatory Status Note:** The specific product reviewed (Hydroquinone 4% Cream, Acella Pharmaceuticals) has not been found by the FDA to be safe and effective, and its labeling has not been approved by the FDA; it is marketed as an unapproved prescription drug [US].

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Hydroquinone (1,4-dihydroxybenzene).
- **Chemical Class:** Depigmenting agent; phenol derivative.
- **Brand Name(s):** Hydroquinone 4% Cream (Acella Pharmaceuticals) [US].
- **ATC Code:** D11AX11.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Topical cream containing 40 mg of hydroquinone per gram (4%) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical (External use only; not for ophthalmic or mucosal use).
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Acella Pharmaceuticals, LLC [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Melanin Hyperpigmentation:** Indicated for the gradual bleaching of hyperpigmented skin conditions, including [US]:
    - Chloasma
    - Melasma
    - Freckles
    - Senile lentigines
    - Other unwanted areas of melanin hyperpigmentation.

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing (Adult):** Apply to the affected areas and rub in well twice daily, typically once in the morning and once before bedtime [US].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Prior to first use, the foil seal must be completely removed.
    - Application should be limited to relatively small areas of the body at one time [US].
- **Treatment Duration:** If no clinical improvement is observed after 2 months of treatment, use of the product should be discontinued [US].
- **Missed Dose Instructions:** Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known prior history of sensitivity or allergic reaction to hydroquinone or any of the inactive ingredients in the formulation [US].
- **Pregnancy/Pediatrics:** Safety has not been established for use during pregnancy or in children aged 12 years and under [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Exogenous Ochronosis:** Hydroquinone may cause a gradual blue-black darkening of the skin known as exogenous ochronosis. If this occurs, treatment must be discontinued immediately. This condition is most frequently reported in Black patients but may also occur in Caucasian and Hispanic populations [US].
- **Sun Exposure:** This product does not contain sunscreen. Sun exposure to treated skin (even minimal) sustains melanocytic activity and causes repigmentation. Use of a broad-spectrum sunscreen (SPF 15 or greater) or protective clothing is mandatory during and after treatment [US].
- **Sensitivity Testing:** It is recommended to test for skin sensitivity before beginning therapeutic use. Apply a small amount to an unbroken patch of skin and assess within 24 hours. While minor redness is not a contraindication, the presence of itching, vesicle formation, or excessive inflammation precludes further treatment [US].
- **Ocular and Mucosal Avoidance:** Contact with eyes and mucous membranes must be avoided [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Common (Frequency Not Quantified):** 
    - Erythema
    - Stinging
    - Transient skin reddening
    - Mild burning sensation [US].
- **Uncommon/Rare:**
    - Dryness and fissuring of paranasal and infraorbital areas.
    - Localised contact dermatitis (hypersensitivity). If this occurs, discontinue medication and notify a physician [US].
- **Serious:** 
    - Exogenous ochronosis (see Section 5).

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Photosensitising Medications:** Patients should exercise caution when using concomitant medications known to be photosensitising, as this may exacerbate skin sensitivity [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category C:** Animal reproduction studies have not been conducted with topical hydroquinone. It is unknown if hydroquinone causes fetal harm or affects reproductive capacity. Use in pregnant women only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** It is not known whether topical hydroquinone is excreted in human milk. Exercise caution when administering to nursing women [US].
- **Fertility:** Animal studies have demonstrated some evidence of carcinogenicity and mutagenicity, but the impact on human fertility is unknown [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Safety and effectiveness in children below 12 years of age have not been established. Use is not recommended unless under the advice and supervision of a physician [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Produces reversible depigmentation of the skin by inhibiting the enzymatic oxidation of tyrosine to 3,4-dihydroxyphenylalanine (DOPA). It also suppresses other melanocyte metabolic processes [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption/Distribution:** Not specified in available sources.
    - **Metabolism/Elimination:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Exposure to ultraviolet light or sunlight will counteract the pharmacological effect, causing repigmentation of bleached areas [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- Pivotal trial data for the specific 4% cream provided in the source was not included. The source notes the product is "unapproved" by the FDA and has not been subjected to therapeutic equivalency testing [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Excursions:** Permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F and 86°F) [US].
- **Shelf Life:** Not specified in available sources.

- **United States:** Classified as Rx Only (Prescription). Note: Marketed as an unapproved drug [US].
