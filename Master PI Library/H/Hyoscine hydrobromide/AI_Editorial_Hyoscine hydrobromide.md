## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt.
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Sterile solution for injection: 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** For the acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** The recommended dose is 50 mg/kg. It should be administered as a slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or by infusion [US].
- **ESRD on Hemodialysis:** The recommended starting dose is 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight). Administration should occur as a slow bolus (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].

### Dosing by Indication
- **Severe Metabolic Crisis:** A loading dose is often administered, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours. Dosing intervals should be every 3 or 4 hours (q3h or q4h) and never less than every 6 hours (q6h) [US].
- **Maintenance in Metabolic Disorders:** Subsequent daily doses are typically 50 mg/kg, though higher doses up to 300 mg/kg have been utilised [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter and discoloration before use [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s (concentrations 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL) [US].

### Titration and Monitoring
- **Monitoring:** Weekly and monthly monitoring of blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations is recommended.
- **Target Levels:** Plasma free carnitine concentrations should ideally be maintained between 35 and 60 µmol/L [US].
- **Dose Adjustment (ESRD):** Adjustments may be made as early as the third or fourth week of therapy based on trough (pre-dialysis) levels. Downward adjustment (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) is indicated if levels are supranormal [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known** [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in patients with ESRD on dialysis. Some reactions occur within minutes of administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and consider the risks/benefits before re-administration [US].
- **Seizures:** Levocarnitine may cause seizures in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Renal Impairment (Oral Formulation Caution):** While this PI covers the injection, it notes that chronic high doses of *oral* levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [US].

| **Cardiovascular** | Cardiovascular disorder, hypertension, hypotension, tachycardia |

### Serious and Postmarketing Effects
- **Neurologic:** Seizures [US].
- **Gastrointestinal:** Transient nausea and vomiting; body odor and gastritis (less frequent) [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Anaphylaxis, bronchospasm, laryngeal edema [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have occurred when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after dose adjustments in patients on warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **US Category:** Not assigned a letter, but reproductive studies in rats and rabbits (up to 3.8 times human dose) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or foetal harm. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk concentrations after administration. Risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant must be weighed against maternal benefits [US].
- **Fertility:** No impairment of fertility was observed in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Dosing is based on mg/kg, similar to adults (see Section 3) [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Specifically indicated for ESRD patients on dialysis. High-dose oral therapy is cautioned in this group due to metabolite accumulation (see Section 5) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** In patients with inborn errors of metabolism or ESRD, levocarnitine promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine compounds, which are rapidly eliminated [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
    - **Elimination Half-life:** Mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].
    - **Excretion:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours (uncorrected for endogenous levels) [US].
    - **Bioavailability:** Absolute bioavailability of oral formulations is approximately 15–16% [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Pharmacokinetic Study (ESRD):** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients receiving 20 mg/kg three times weekly after dialysis showed mean pre-dialysis (trough) levels increased from ~20 µmol/L at baseline to 190 ± 55 µmol/L at 8 weeks [US].
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** In ESRD patients receiving 10, 20, or 40 mg/kg, trough concentrations increased proportionally with the dose over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C [US].
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing. Discard any unused portion of a single-dose vial as it contains no preservative [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **Legal Classification:** Rx-only [US].
- **Poison Schedule:** Not specified in available sources [Australian].
