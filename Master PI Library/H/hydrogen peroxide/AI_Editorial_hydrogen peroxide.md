# Editorial Product Information: Hydrogen Peroxide

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial synthesis is based on official Product Information (PI) provided by the **United States (FDA)**. At the time of this publication, PI documents from other jurisdictions (e.g., Australian, European, Canadian, Japanese) were not provided for comparison.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, consolidating clinical and regulatory data.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis and is not intended to replace official, jurisdiction-specific Product Information when prescribing or dispensing in a particular region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Hydrogen peroxide 3% (3 mL in 100 mL).
- **Chemical Class:** Inorganic peroxide; Oxidising agent.
- **Brand Names:** Wellness Basics [United States].
- **ATC Code:** D08AX01 (Antiseptics and disinfectants).
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Liquid solution, 3% USP [United States].
- **Route of Administration:** Topical; Oral (as a rinse/debriding agent).
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** P & L Development, LLC [United States].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **First Aid Antiseptic:** For the prevention of infection in minor cuts, scrapes, and burns [United States].
- **Oral Debriding Agent:** To assist in the removal of phlegm, mucus, or other secretions associated with occasional sore mouth [United States].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### First Aid Antiseptic
- **Adults and Children:** Clean the affected area. Apply a small amount of the product to the area 1 to 3 times daily.
- **Dressings:** The treated area may be covered with a sterile bandage. If a bandage is used, the area must be allowed to dry first [United States].

### Oral Debriding Agent (Oral Rinse)
- **Adults and Children 2 years and older:** Mix with an equal amount of water. Swish the solution in the mouth over the affected area for at least 1 minute, then expectorate (spit out). Do not swallow.
- **Frequency:** Use up to 4 times daily after meals and at bedtime, or as directed by a healthcare professional [United States].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Ocular Exposure:** Do not use in the eyes [United States].
- **Extensive Application:** Do not apply over large areas of the body [United States].
- **Prolonged Use:** Do not use for longer than one week [United States].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None specified in available sources.
- **Route Restriction:** For external use only [United States].
- **Medical Consultation Required:** Patients should consult a physician prior to use for deep puncture wounds, animal bites, or serious burns [United States].
- **Discontinuation Criteria:** Patients should stop use and consult a healthcare provider if:
    - Swelling, rash, or fever develops.
    - The condition persists or worsens.
    - Sore mouth symptoms do not improve within 7 days.
    - Irritation, pain, or redness persists or worsens [United States].
- **Handling:** Do not shake the bottle. Hold the bottle away from the face when opening [United States].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Frequency Data:** Specific frequency data (e.g., very common, common) is not provided in the source documentation.
- **Reported Effects:** Potential adverse reactions identified via "Stop Use" instructions include local irritation, redness, pain, rash, and swelling [United States].
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Development of fever or worsening of the underlying skin condition [United States].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:**
    - **Children under 12 years:** Use should be supervised by an adult [United States].
    - **Children under 2 years:** Consult a doctor or dentist prior to use for oral debridement [United States].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Hydrogen peroxide acts as an oxidising agent. When in contact with tissues containing the enzyme catalase, it releases oxygen, which provides a mechanical cleansing action (debridement) and exerts antiseptic effects [Editorial Note].
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Not specified in available sources.
