# Editorial Product Information: hypromellose + povidone

## 1. Product Identification
- Active ingredient (INN/generic name): **hypromellose + povidone**
- Chemical class: Ophthalmic lubricants (lubricant agents for ocular surface)
- ATC code: Not specified in available sources
- Route(s) of administration: Ophthalmic (eye)

## 2. Regulatory History
- Marketing start date: 05/30/2024 [US]
- Last revision/update of source PI: Revised: 6/2026 [US]
- Any withdrawal, suspension, or restriction history: Not specified in available sources

## 3. Approved Indications
- Temporary relief of discomfort due to minor irritations of the eye (e.g., exposure to wind or sun).
- Protection against further irritation of the eye.
- Relief of dryness of the eye.
- Jurisdictional note: Indications as stated in the available source are presented without region-specific restrictions; where an indication is region‑limited, regional attribution is not specified in the available sources.

## 4. Dosage and Administration
### General dosing
- Instill 1 or 2 drops in the affected eye(s) as needed. (Dose concentration in source: hypromellose 0.3% [3 mg/mL], povidone 0.5% [5 mg/mL].)
- Age-specific dosing (adult/paediatric): Not specified in available sources (direction "put 1 or 2 drops" given without age qualification).

### Dosing by indication
- No separate indication-specific dosing regimens provided beyond "1 or 2 drops as needed."

### Administration instructions
- For ophthalmic use only.
- Do not touch the tip of the container to any surface to avoid contamination.
- Replace cap after each use.
- Do not use the solution if it changes color or becomes cloudy.
- Tighten or snap on cap to seal after use.

## 6. Warnings and Precautions
- **For external use only.**
- Do not touch the tip of the container to any surface to avoid contamination; replace cap after each use.
- **Do not use if solution changes color or becomes cloudy.**
- **Stop use and ask a doctor if you:**
  - feel eye pain,
  - experience changes in vision,
  - have redness or irritation of the eyes that lasts,
  - note that the condition worsens or persists for more than 72 hours.
- **Keep out of reach of children.**
- **If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.**
- Monitoring requirements: Not specified in available sources beyond the instruction to seek medical evaluation for the signs/symptoms listed above.
- Specific clinical precautions: Not specified in available sources beyond the warnings and handling precautions above.
