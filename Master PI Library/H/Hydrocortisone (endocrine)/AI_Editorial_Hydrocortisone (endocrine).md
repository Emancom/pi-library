## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (USP).
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative; carrier molecule for fatty acid transport.
- **Brand Name(s):** Levocarnitine Injection (Baxter).
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** 
    - Sterile injection solution: 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) in single-dose vials [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
- **Metabolic Disorders:** 
    - Recommended dose: 50 mg/kg.
    - Administration: Slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) or infusion.
    - Initial Management: A loading dose is often administered in severe metabolic crisis, followed by equivalent doses over the subsequent 24 hours.
    - Frequency: Every 3 to 4 hours (q3h or q4h); frequency should never be less than every 6 hours (q6h).
    - Maximum Dose: The highest administered dose reported is 300 mg/kg [US].
- **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:**
    - Recommended starting dose: 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight).
    - Administration: Slow bolus injection (2 to 3 minutes) into the venous return line after each dialysis session.
    - Titration: Adjustments (e.g., downward to 5 mg/kg) may be made as early as the third or fourth week of therapy, guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Inspect visually for particulate matter or discoloration prior to administration.
- Discard any unused portion of the single-dose vial as it contains no preservatives.

### Monitoring
- Obtain baseline plasma carnitine levels before starting therapy.
- Monitor weekly and monthly, including blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine: 35 to 60 µmol/L) [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients on dialysis. Reactions can occur within minutes of IV administration. If a reaction occurs, discontinue immediately and initiate appropriate medical treatment [US].
- **Seizures:** Levocarnitine has been associated with seizures in patients with or without pre-existing seizure activity. In patients with known seizure disorders, an increase in seizure frequency or severity has been reported [US].
- **General (Oral vs. IV):** The safety/efficacy of *oral* levocarnitine has not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency. High doses of oral levocarnitine in ESRD patients may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects are often confounded by the underlying pathology of ESRD or metabolic disorders.

### Frequency ≥5% (ESRD Clinical Trials)
| Body System | Adverse Effect |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Chest pain, fever, flu syndrome, headache, infection, injection site reaction, pain, abdominal pain, asthenia |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension, hypotension, tachycardia |
| **Digestive** | Diarrhea, vomiting, nausea |
| **Metabolic** | Hypercalcemia, hypervolemia |
| **Nervous** | Dizziness, paresthesia |
| **Respiratory** | Cough increase, pharyngitis, dyspnea, rhinitis |

### Other Notable Effects
- **Common:** Body odor, gastritis [US].
- **Serious:** Anaphylaxis, seizures [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have occurred when used concomitantly with levocarnitine. 
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels in patients on warfarin therapy after initiating levocarnitine or following dose adjustments [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** 
    - **Category:** Not formally letter-categorized in the source, but reproductive studies in rats and rabbits (up to 3.8 times the human dose) revealed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. 
    - **Recommendation:** Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Levocarnitine supplementation in nursing mothers has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk carnitine after supplementation. 
    - **Recommendation:** Weigh the risks of excess carnitine intake in the child against the benefits to the mother. Consider discontinuing nursing or the drug [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal models [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Use is indicated for metabolic disorders; dosing is based on mg/kg (see Section 3) [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specifically detailed in provided source.
- **Renal Impairment:** Primarily indicated for ESRD on dialysis. Dose adjustments are based on trough carnitine levels (see Section 3) [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane for oxidation. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin.
    - **Metabolism:** Major metabolites are trimethylamine N-oxide (urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (feces).
    - **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. Terminal elimination half-life is approximately 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Study:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients receiving 20 mg/kg IV three times weekly after dialysis showed mean pre-dialysis trough levels increased from ~20 µmol/L at baseline to 190 ± 55 µmol/L after 8 weeks [US].
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** Studies of 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses showed a dose-proportional increase in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Conditions:** Protect from freezing. Avoid excessive heat.
- **Stability:** Compatible and stable for 24 hours in PVC bags when mixed with 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer's at concentrations of 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL [US].
