# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
- The information in this editorial has been synthesized from the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **US (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, consolidating clinical and regulatory data into a single reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis intended for professional reference and is not a substitute for the official, legally approved PI required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN/USP); (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium, inner salt. 
- **Chemical Class:** Amino acid derivative (naturally occurring carrier molecule).
- **Brand Names:** Levocarnitine Injection [US].
- **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Sterile solution for injection, 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Inborn Errors of Metabolism:** Acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism resulting in secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
- **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Metabolic Disorders
- **General Dose:** 50 mg/kg administered as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection or by infusion [US].
- **Loading Dose:** Often required in severe metabolic crises, followed by an equivalent dose over the subsequent 24 hours [US].
- **Frequency:** Administer every 3 or 4 hours; intervals should never be less than every 6 hours [US].
- **Maximum Dose:** The highest reported clinical dose administered is 300 mg/kg [US].

### ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis
- **Starting Dose:** 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg dry body weight [US].
- **Administration Timing:** Administer as a slow 2 to 3-minute bolus injection into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].
- **Dose Adjustment:** Guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine concentrations. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of therapy if concentrations are below normal (40–50 µmol/L) [US].

### Administration Instructions
- Parenteral products must be inspected visually for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration [US].
- **Compatibility:** Stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when mixed with Sodium Chloride 0.9% or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations between 0.5 mg/mL and 8 mg/mL [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **None known:** There are no listed contraindications in the available source [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
### Serious Warnings
- **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm have been reported, primarily in ESRD patients on dialysis. Reactions can occur within minutes of intravenous administration. If a severe reaction occurs, discontinue treatment immediately and initiate medical management [US].

### General Precautions
- **Renal Insufficiency (Oral Form):** While this editorial covers the injection, it is noted that chronic high doses of *oral* levocarnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function or ESRD may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine [TMA] and trimethylamine-N-oxide [TMAO]) [US].
- **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In those with a history of seizures, an increase in frequency or severity has been observed [US].
- **Monitoring:** For metabolic disorders, weekly and monthly monitoring is recommended, including blood chemistries, vital signs, and plasma carnitine concentrations (target free carnitine: 35–60 µmol/L) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse events are often confounded by the underlying pathology of ESRD or metabolic errors.

### Very Common (>10%) [US - ESRD Clinical Trials]
- **General:** Injection site reaction (27–38%), Pain (21–35%), Flu syndrome (15–29%), Headache (3–37%), Infection (10–24%), Chest pain (6–15%).
- **Cardiovascular:** Hypertension (18–21%), Hypotension (3–19%).
- **Digestive:** Diarrhea (9–35%), Vomiting (9–21%), Nausea (5–12%).
- **Metabolic:** Hypercalcemia (6–15%), Hypervolemia (3–12%).
- **Respiratory:** Pharyngitis (15–27%), Cough increase (10–18%), Dyspnea (3–11%).
- **Nervous:** Dizziness (10–18%), Paresthesia (3–12%).

### Common (1–10%) [US - ESRD Clinical Trials]
- **General:** Abdominal pain, Fever, Asthenia, Back pain.
- **Cardiovascular:** Tachycardia, Palpitations, Atrial fibrillation.
- **Digestive:** Gastritis, Anorexia, Constipation, Melena.
- **Nervous:** Anxiety, Depression, Insomnia.
- **Other:** Pruritus, Rash, Eye disorder, Taste perversion.

### Post-Marketing Experience
- **Neurologic:** Seizures [US].
- **General:** Body odor (reported with less frequency) [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Warfarin:** Reports of increased International Normalized Ratio (INR) have occurred when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin.
- **Management:** Monitor INR levels following the initiation of levocarnitine or after dose adjustments in patients receiving warfarin therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** [US: Category not specified]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits using doses up to 3.8 times the human dose (based on surface area) revealed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. However, no adequate, well-controlled studies exist in pregnant women. Use during pregnancy only if clearly needed [US].
- **Breastfeeding:** Safety has not been specifically studied. Studies in dairy cows show increased milk carnitine concentrations after exogenous administration. Weigh the risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant against the maternal benefit; consider discontinuing nursing or the drug [US].
- **Fertility:** No evidence of impaired fertility in animal studies [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Use in pediatric patients is acknowledged in dosing instructions for metabolic disorders (mg/kg basis), but specific age-related safety profiles are not detailed beyond general dosing [US].
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Primarily indicated for ESRD patients on dialysis. Monitoring for metabolite accumulation is required if switching to oral forms [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
### Mechanism of Action
Levocarnitine is a naturally occurring substance required for mammalian energy metabolism. It facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and subsequent energy production [US].

### Pharmacodynamics
In patients with metabolic errors, levocarnitine promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters, which are rapidly cleared [US].

### Pharmacokinetics
- **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin [US].
- **Metabolism:** Major metabolites include trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
- **Elimination:** Following a single IV bolus (20 mg/kg), approximately 76% of the dose is excreted in the urine within 24 hours. Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours; mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **ESRD Study:** A 9-week study of 12 ESRD patients on hemodialysis receiving 20 mg/kg three times weekly post-dialysis showed that trough levocarnitine concentrations increased from a baseline of 21.3 ± 7.7 µmol/L to 190 ± 55 µmol/L after 8 weeks [US].
- **Dose-Ranging Study:** Studies using 10, 20, and 40 mg/kg doses confirmed a dose-dependent increase in trough carnitine concentrations over 12 and 24 weeks [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
- **Handling:** Protect from freezing and excessive heat.
- **Preservation:** Formulation contains no preservative; discard any unused portion of a single-dose vial [US].

- **United States:** Classified as Rx-only (Prescription only).
- **Other Jurisdictions:** Not specified in available sources.
