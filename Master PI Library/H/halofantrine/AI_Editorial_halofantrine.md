# Editorial Product Information: Levocarnitine

## Editorial Note
*   This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following jurisdiction: **United States (FDA)**.
*   The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating clinical data into a concise, structured format.
*   **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis and not a substitute for the official Product Information required for prescribing within a specific jurisdiction. Clinicians should consult their local regulatory authority’s approved PI for full prescribing details.

## 1. Product Identification
*   **Active Ingredient:** Levocarnitine (INN); a carrier molecule involved in the mitochondrial transport of long-chain fatty acids.
*   **Chemical Class:** Small molecule, (R)-3-Carboxy-2-hydroxy-N,N,N-trimethyl-1-propanaminium.
*   **Brand Names:** **Levocarnitine Injection, USP** (Baxter) [US].
*   **ATC Code:** A16AA01.
*   **Dosage Forms and Strengths:** 
    *   Intravenous Injection: 1 g per 5 mL (200 mg/mL) single-dose vial [US].
*   **Route of Administration:** Intravenous use only.
*   **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Baxter Healthcare Corporation [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
*   **Secondary Carnitine Deficiency:** Indicated for both acute and chronic treatment of patients with inborn errors of metabolism that lead to secondary carnitine deficiency [US].
*   **End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD):** Indicated for the prevention and treatment of carnitine deficiency in patients with ESRD who are undergoing dialysis [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adult)
*   **Metabolic Disorders:** The standard recommended dose is 50 mg/kg. This may be administered as a slow bolus injection (2–3 minutes) or via infusion [US].
*   **ESRD Patients on Hemodialysis:** The recommended starting dose is 10 mg/kg to 20 mg/kg (dry body weight). This should be administered as a slow bolus injection (2–3 minutes) into the venous return line following each dialysis session [US].

### Indication-Specific Instructions
*   **Metabolic Crisis:** In severe cases, a loading dose is often administered, followed by an equivalent dose split over the subsequent 24 hours. Dosing frequency should be every 3 or 4 hours, and never less frequent than every 6 hours. Daily maintenance doses typically remain near 50 mg/kg, though doses up to 300 mg/kg have been used [US].
*   **ESRD Dose Adjustments:** Therapy initiation may be guided by trough (pre-dialysis) plasma levocarnitine levels falling below 40–50 µmol/L. Downward adjustments (e.g., to 5 mg/kg) may be initiated as early as the third or fourth week of treatment based on clinical response and plasma levels [US].

### Administration and Compatibility
*   **Compatibility:** Stable for 24 hours at room temperature (25°C) in PVC bags when diluted in 0.9% Sodium Chloride or Lactated Ringer’s at concentrations ranging from 0.5 mg/mL to 8 mg/mL [US].
*   **Visual Inspection:** Solutions must be inspected for particulate matter and discoloration before use. Vials are single-dose; discard any unused portion [US].

## 4. Contraindications
*   None known [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
*   **Boxed Warnings:** None [US].
*   **Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Serious reactions, including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, have been reported. These often occur within minutes of intravenous administration, particularly in ESRD patients. If a reaction occurs, discontinue the drug immediately and initiate medical management [US].
*   **Seizure Activity:** Seizures have been reported in patients with or without pre-existing seizure history. In patients with a known seizure disorder, there may be an increase in seizure frequency or severity [US].
*   **Oral Administration Cautions:** The safety/efficacy of *oral* levocarnitine has not been evaluated in patients with renal insufficiency. Chronic high-dose oral carnitine in patients with severely compromised renal function may lead to the accumulation of toxic metabolites (trimethylamine and trimethylamine-N-oxide) [US].
*   **Monitoring:** It is recommended to obtain baseline plasma carnitine levels and monitor weekly/monthly. Monitoring should include vital signs, blood chemistry, and plasma free carnitine concentrations (target range 35–60 µmol/L) [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
Adverse effects observed in clinical trials of ESRD patients on hemodialysis (incidence ≥5%):

| Body System | Adverse Reactions |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General** | Chest pain, headache, infection, injection site reaction, pain, fever, abdominal pain, asthenia |
| **Cardiovascular** | Hypertension, hypotension, tachycardia, arrhythmia, palpitations |
| **Gastrointestinal** | Diarrhea, vomiting, nausea, dyspepsia, anorexia |
| **Neurological** | Dizziness, paresthesia, insomnia, depression |
| **Respiratory** | Pharyngitis, cough, dyspnea, rhinitis |
| **Metabolic** | Hypercalcemia, hypervolemia, hyperkalemia, peripheral edema |
| **Musculoskeletal** | Leg cramps, myalgia (less frequent) |
| **Other** | Body odor (less frequent), gastritis, pruritus, rash, taste perversion |

**Serious Adverse Effects:** Anaphylaxis and other severe hypersensitivity reactions; seizures [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
*   **Warfarin:** Reports indicate an increase in the International Normalized Ratio (INR) when levocarnitine is co-administered with warfarin. Monitoring of INR is recommended upon initiation or dose adjustment of levocarnitine in patients on anticoagulant therapy [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
*   **Pregnancy:** [US Category: Not specifically lettered in source, but evaluated under previous standards]. Reproductive studies in rats and rabbits at 3.8 times the human dose (based on surface area) showed no evidence of impaired fertility or fetal harm. Clinical data in pregnant women are lacking; use only if clearly needed [US].
*   **Breastfeeding:** Studies in dairy cows show increased carnitine milk concentrations following administration. The risks of excess carnitine intake in the infant must be weighed against the maternal benefits. Discontinuation of nursing or the drug should be considered [US].
*   **Fertility:** Mutagenicity tests (Salmonella, S. cerevisiae, S. pombe) were negative. Long-term carcinogenicity studies have not been performed [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
*   **Paediatric Use:** Dosing for metabolic disorders and ESRD is based on mg/kg, similar to adults [US].
*   **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
*   **Renal Impairment:** Intravenous administration is indicated for ESRD patients. Caution is advised with *oral* formulations in this population due to metabolite accumulation [US].
*   **Hepatic Impairment:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
*   **Mechanism of Action:** Facilitates the transport of long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane, providing substrate for oxidation and energy production. It also promotes the excretion of excess organic or fatty acids by forming acylcarnitine esters [US].
*   **Pharmacokinetics:**
    *   **Distribution:** Not bound to plasma proteins or albumin. Mean distribution half-life is 0.585 hours [US].
    *   **Metabolism:** Major metabolites are trimethylamine N-oxide (primarily in urine) and γ-butyrobetaine (primarily in feces) [US].
    *   **Elimination:** Approximately 76% of an IV dose is excreted in urine within 24 hours. The mean terminal elimination half-life is 17.4 hours. Total body clearance is approximately 4.00 L/h [US].
*   **Pharmacodynamics:** In ESRD patients, IV administration increases plasma levocarnitine concentrations and corrects the acylcarnitine/carnitine ratio [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
*   **ESRD Studies:** In a 9-week study of ESRD patients (n=12), 20 mg/kg doses three times weekly post-dialysis increased mean pre-dialysis plasma concentrations from 20 µmol/L to 190 µmol/L by week 8. While plasma levels were increased, effects on clinical outcomes (malaise, weakness, arrhythmias) have not been definitively determined [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
*   **Temperature:** Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
*   **Conditions:** Avoid excessive heat and protect from freezing.
*   **Shelf Life:** Not specified in source, but discard any unused portion of opened vials as the formulation contains no preservative [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
*   **United States:** Rx-only.
