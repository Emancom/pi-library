# Editorial Product Information: Hypromellose and Povidone

## Editorial Note
- The following editorial product information has been synthesized from official Product Information (PI) source material provided by the United States (FDA). 
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, integrating available regulatory data into a single clinical reference.
- **Note:** This is an editorial synthesis intended for professional reference and is not a substitute for the official PI or summary of product characteristics (SmPC) required for prescribing or dispensing in any specific jurisdiction.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** Hypromellose (0.3%) and Povidone (0.5%).
- **Chemical Class:** Hypromellose (cellulose ether/polymer); Povidone (synthetic polymer).
- **Brand Name(s):** **Rohto Dual Light Relief** [US].
- **ATC Code:** S01XA20 (Artificial tears and other indifferent preparations) [Not specified in source; assigned by clinical convention].
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Ophthalmic liquid containing Hypromellose 3 mg/mL (0.3%) and Povidone 5 mg/mL (0.5%) [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Ophthalmic.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** The Mentholatum Company (US); Manufactured by Rohto Pharmaceutical Co., Ltd. (Japan) [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- For the temporary relief of ocular discomfort resulting from minor irritations of the eye [US].
- For the relief of eye discomfort due to exposure to environmental factors such as wind or sun [US].
- As a protectant against further irritation or for the relief of dryness of the eye [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Children)
- Instill 1 or 2 drops into the affected eye(s) as required [US].

### Administration Instructions
- To prevent contamination, ensure the tip of the container does not contact any surface [US].
- The cap should be replaced immediately after every use [US].
- Ensure the cap is tightly snapped to seal after administration [US].
- The solution should not be used if it becomes cloudy or changes colour [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- Not specified in available sources [US]. 
- *(Editorial Note: Standard clinical practice contraindicates use in patients with known hypersensitivity to any of the active or inactive ingredients.)*

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None [US].
- **General Warnings:** For external use only [US].
- **Monitoring and Discontinuation:** Patients should cease use and consult a healthcare professional if:
    - Eye pain occurs [US].
    - Changes in vision are experienced [US].
    - Redness or irritation of the eyes persists [US].
    - The clinical condition worsens or fails to improve within 72 hours [US].
