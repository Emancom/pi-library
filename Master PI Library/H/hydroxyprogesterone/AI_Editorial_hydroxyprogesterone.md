# Editorial Product Information: Hydroxyprogesterone Caproate

## Editorial Note
- The following jurisdictions' Product Information (PI) documents contributed to this editorial: **US (FDA)**.
- **Purpose:** This document provides a harmonised editorial overview of hydroxyprogesterone caproate for use by healthcare professionals. 
- **Disclaimer:** This is an editorial synthesis intended for reference and does not replace the official PI or local regulatory guidance required for prescribing in any specific jurisdiction. At the time of this writing, only the United States PI was available for synthesis.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Hydroxyprogesterone caproate (a caproate ester of the naturally occurring progestational hormone, hydroxyprogesterone).
- **Brand Names:** Available as a generic formulation [US].
- **ATC Code:** G03DA03
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Sterile, long-acting oil solution for injection. Available in 5 mL multiple-dose vials containing 250 mg/mL (1,250 mg per vial) [US].
- **Route of Administration:** Intramuscular (IM) injection only.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Distributed by AuroMedics Pharma LLC; Manufactured by Eugia Pharma Specialities Limited [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Advanced Adenocarcinoma of the Uterine Corpus:** Indicated for Stage III or IV disease [US].
- **Amenorrhea:** Management of primary and secondary amenorrhea in the absence of organic pathology [US].
- **Abnormal Uterine Bleeding (AUB):** Management of bleeding due to hormonal imbalance in the absence of organic pathology (e.g., submucous fibroids or uterine cancer) [US].
- **Diagnostic Tool:** Used as a test for endogenous estrogen production and for the production of secretory endometrium and desquamation ("Medical D and C") [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Administration
- Administer via deep intramuscular injection into the upper outer quadrant of the gluteal muscle.
- Due to its low viscosity, a small-gauge needle may be used. 
- Ensure the full dose is delivered due to the high concentration of the 250 mg potency.
- **Note:** Use of a wet needle/syringe may cause solution cloudiness but does not alter potency [US].

### Dosing by Indication [US]
- **Advanced Adenocarcinoma of the Uterine Corpus (Stage III or IV):** 
    - 1,000 mg or more, one or more times weekly (1 g to 7 g per week).
    - Discontinue upon relapse or after 12 weeks if no objective response occurs.
- **Amenorrhea and Abnormal Uterine Bleeding:**
    - **Cyclic Regimen:** 375 mg hydroxyprogesterone caproate administered at any time. 
    - Often used in conjunction with estradiol valerate (e.g., 20 mg estradiol on Day 1; 250 mg hydroxyprogesterone and 5 mg estradiol on Day 15 of a 28-day cycle).
    - Repeat every 4 weeks for 4 cycles.
- **Test for Endogenous Estrogen Production ("Medical D and C"):**
    - 250 mg as a single dose. May repeat once after 4 weeks for confirmation.
    - Bleeding 7 to 14 days after injection indicates presence of endogenous estrogen.

## 4. Contraindications
- Known or suspected carcinoma of the breast or other hormone-sensitive cancers, or a history of such conditions [US].
- Undiagnosed abnormal vaginal bleeding [US].
- Liver dysfunction or active liver disease [US].
- Missed abortion [US].
- Current or history of thrombotic or thromboembolic disorders [US].
- Use as a diagnostic test for pregnancy [US].
- Hypersensitivity to hydroxyprogesterone caproate or any component of the formulation [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Thrombotic and Thromboembolic Events:** Discontinue immediately if there is a sudden loss of vision (partial or complete), proptosis, diplopia, migraine, or if examination reveals papilledema or retinal vascular lesions [US].
- **Glucose Tolerance:** Progestins can decrease glucose tolerance. Diabetic patients must be monitored closely [US].
- **Fluid Retention:** Use with caution in patients with conditions aggravated by fluid retention, such as asthma, epilepsy, migraine, or cardiac/renal dysfunction [US].
- **Depression:** Observe patients with a history of psychic depression; discontinue if depression recurs to a serious degree [US].
- **Clinical Monitoring:** Pre-treatment physical examination must include breasts, pelvic organs, and a Papanicolaou (Pap) smear [US].
- **Allergic Reactions:** Hypersensitivity reactions have been reported; the drug is contraindicated in those with a history of progestin sensitivity [US].

## 6. Adverse Effects
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Arterial and venous thromboembolic events, including pulmonary embolism (potentially fatal), deep vein thrombosis (DVT), myocardial infarction, and stroke [US].
- **Neuro-ocular:** Retinal thrombosis and optic neuritis [US].
- **Gastrointestinal:** Nausea, vomiting, abdominal cramps, bloating, and cholestatic jaundice [US].
- **Injection Site:** Pain, soreness, swelling, itching, bruising, or abscess formation [US].
- **Dermatologic/Allergic:** Allergic rash, chloasma/melasma, itching, hirsutism, or loss of scalp hair [US].
- **Endocrine/Gynaecologic:** Breakthrough bleeding, spotting, changes in cervical mucus, breast tenderness, and post-treatment anovulation [US].
- **Systemic:** Edema, weight changes (increase or decrease), headache, migraine, dizziness, fatigue, and rise in blood pressure [US].
- **Respiratory:** Rare instances of coughing, dyspnea, and chest constriction following injection, particularly at higher doses [US].

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Laboratory Test Interference:** Progestins may affect the following tests [US]:
    - **Hepatic Function:** Increased sulfobromophthalein retention.
    - **Coagulation:** Increased prothrombin and Factors VII, VIII, IX, and X.
    - **Thyroid Function:** Increased Protein Bound Iodine (PBI) and butanol extractable PBI; decreased T3 uptake.
- **Metabolic Interactions:** *In vitro* data indicate metabolism is predominantly mediated by **CYP3A4** and **CYP3A5** [US].

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy:** Contraindicated for use as a diagnostic test for pregnancy. Note: This specific PI formulation is indicated for non-pregnant women [US]. 
- **Breastfeeding:** Progestins are detectable in breast milk. While many studies have found no adverse effects on infant health, growth, or development, clinicians should exercise caution [US].
- **Fertility:** Post-treatment anovulation and amenorrhea may occur [US].

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Elderly Use:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Renal Impairment:** Pharmacokinetics have not been formally evaluated. Fluid retention caused by the drug necessitates caution in patients with renal dysfunction [US].
- **Hepatic Impairment:** Not formally evaluated. However, because the drug is extensively metabolised by the liver, impairment may reduce elimination. Use is contraindicated in patients with liver dysfunction [US].

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** A potent, long-acting progestational steroid. It transforms proliferative endometrium into secretory endometrium, induces mammary gland duct development, and inhibits gonadotropic hormone release [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Exhibits slight estrogenic, androgenic, or corticoid effects, though these are not clinically reliable [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Peak serum levels occur 3 to 7 days after a 1,000 mg IM dose [US].
    - **Metabolism:** Primarily mediated by CYP3A4 and CYP3A5; metabolites include sulfated and glucuronidated products [US].
    - **Elimination:** Excreted in both urine and feces, predominantly as conjugated metabolites [US].

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Adenocarcinoma:** Clinical data indicate that doses of 1,000 mg or more weekly often induce regressive changes in advanced (Stage III or IV) adenocarcinoma of the uterine corpus. Better results are reported in histologically well-differentiated forms [US].
- **Limitations:** Data are insufficient regarding non-endometrioid adenocarcinoma of the uterine corpus [US].

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Temperature:** Store at controlled room temperature, 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [US].
- **Handling:** Low temperatures may cause crytallisation; these redissolve upon heating in boiling water. Store vial upright in its original box [US].
- **Light Protection:** Protect from light [US].
- **Shelf Life:** Discard any unused product 5 weeks after the first use (multi-dose vial) [US].

## 13. Regulatory Information by Jurisdiction
- **United States:** Prescription only (Rx only). NDC 55150-311-01 (single vial) and 55150-311-05 (carton of 5) [US].
- **Australian/European/Canadian:** Not specified in available sources.
