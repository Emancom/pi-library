## Verbatim Copied Passages

1.  **ECG Abnormalities (Section 4):**
    -   **Editorial Text:** "...is characterized primarily by tall, peaked T waves or elevations from previous tracings. There may also be lowering of the R wave and increased depth of the S wave, widening and even disappearance of the P wave, progressive widening of the QRS complex and prolongation of the PR interval and ST depression."
    -   **Source Match:** Document 1 (TGA), Page 4; Document 2 (FDA), Page 5.

2.  **Ocular Disorders (Section 4):**
    -   **Editorial Text:** "Hydrochlorothiazide, a sulfonamide can cause an idiosyncratic reaction resulting in choroidal effusion, acute myopia, or secondary angle-closure glaucoma."
    -   **Source Match:** Document 1 (TGA), Page 5.
    -   **Location:** Under "Metabolic and Ocular Effects."
    -   **Note:** Verbatim match to the TGA's specific wording for this idiosyncratic reaction.

## Close Paraphrases

1.  **Mechanism of Action (Section 10):** The description of amiloride's action ("reduces the electrical potential across the tubular membrane") is a very close paraphrase of the TGA/FDA description ("decreases the net negative potential of the tubular lumen").
2.  **Hyperkalaemia Risk (Section 4):** The statement "The risk is approximately 1–2% in patients without renal impairment or diabetes but increases significantly in the elderly..." closely follows the FDA Warning (Page 5) which states "the risk... is about 1 to 2 percent. This risk is higher in patients with renal impairment or diabetes..."
3.  **Pregnancy Category (Section 8):** The explanation of Category C effects follows the standard TGA definition used in Document 1 very closely.

## Originality Strengths
- **Harmonization of Units:** The editorial successfully merges Australian metric units (mmol/L) with US units (mg/dL) in the renal impairment and storage sections, creating a "unified" view that does not exist in either single source.
- **Section 11 (Storage):** This section successfully synthesizes two different regional requirements into a single instructional block.
- **Structural Synthesis:** The editorial reorganizes the information logically, particularly in the "Metabolic and Ocular Effects" and "Drug Interactions" sections, which are presented as clean bullet points rather than the dense prose of the source PIs.

## Differences from Source PIs
- **Merged Regulatory Guidance:** The editorial explicitly flags which agency (TGA or FDA) provides specific guidance (e.g., the specific TGA warning for ARDS and the FDA's note on initial therapy).
- **Consolidation of Adverse Effects:** The editorial groups adverse effects into "Common" and "Rare/Severe" and by organ system, whereas the source PIs use incidence-based tables (>1% vs <1%).
- **Omission of Clinical Trial Data:** The editorial omits the extensive epidemiological data regarding NMSC (Danish Cancer Registry) found in the TGA document, summarizing only the clinical takeaway.

## Recommendation
**NEEDS REVISION**

The document effectively synthesizes regional differences, but it fails the "original writing" requirement in highly technical sections. Specifically:
1.  **The ECG Abnormalities passage** must be rewritten in the author's own voice.
2.  **The Ocular Disorders description** should be rephrased to avoid the verbatim idiosyncratic reaction string.
3.  **The Mechanism of Action** should be simplified or reworded to avoid mirroring the "net negative potential" phrasing of the regulatory filings.

Once these verbatim technical blocks are rewritten, the document will pass as a genuine synthesis.
