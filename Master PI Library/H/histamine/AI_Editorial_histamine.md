## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredients:** This is a multi-ingredient homeopathic formulation. Active constituents include:
    - *Strychnos nux-vomica* seed (Nux vomica) 4X, 6X
    - *Atropa belladonna* (Belladonna) 6X
    - *Ephedra distachya* flowering twig (*Ephedra vulg.*) 6X
    - *Hydrastis canadensis* (Goldenseal) 6X
    - *Potassium dichromate* (*Kali bic.*) 6X
    - *Teucrium marum* 6X
    - **Histaminium hydrochlor** (Histamine) 12X
- **Brand Name:** SnoreStop Oral Spray [US]
- **ATC Code:** Not available for this homeopathic combination.
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** Oral spray solution. Each ingredient is prepared according to the Homeopathic Pharmacopoeia of the United States (HPUS). The 6X dilution represents one part per million.
- **Route of Administration:** Oral (Sublingual and Oropharyngeal).
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Green Pharmaceuticals [US].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Temporary relief of symptoms associated with non-apneic snoring.** [US-approved]
- **Note:** This product is specifically indicated for non-apneic snoring only and is not a treatment for obstructive sleep apnea (OSA).

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Children ≥12 years)
- **Standard Dose:** Four sprays total at bedtime (two sprays administered under the tongue and two sprays administered at the back of the throat).
- **Frequency:** May be used as needed up to 4 times per day.
- **Duration of Initial Treatment:** Patients should allow 3–5 days of consistent use for initial treatment effects.

### Administration Instructions
- **Priming:** The pump must be depressed until primed before the initial use.
- **Timing:** Administer at least 30 minutes apart from food, water, or other medications to prevent interference with homeopathic absorption.
- **Positioning:** Store and use the bottle upright.

### Missed Dose
- Not specified in available sources; however, as the product is used for symptomatic relief, missed doses may be taken when remembered, subject to the maximum daily frequency.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Paediatrics:** Use is contraindicated in children under 12 years of age [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to any of the active ingredients or excipients (e.g., potassium sorbate).

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Sleep Apnea Warning:** This product does not treat sleep apnea. Patients with suspected sleep apnea (e.g., excessive daytime sleepiness, witnessed gasping/apnea) must consult a health professional [US].
- **Duration of Use:** Patients should stop use and consult a physician if snoring symptoms do not improve within 7 days.
- **Administration Safety:** Use only if the safety seal is intact.
