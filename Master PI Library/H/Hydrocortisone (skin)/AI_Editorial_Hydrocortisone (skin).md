## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Hydrocortisone 0.5% [US]
- **Chemical Class:** Low-potency corticosteroid (Group I)
- **Brand Name(s):** Good Neighbor Pharmacy Hydrocortisone ½% With Aloe for Sensitive Skin [US]
- **ATC Code:** D07AA02
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Topical cream containing 0.5 g of hydrocortisone per 100 g (0.5%) [US]
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Distributed by AmerisourceBergen [US]; Manufactured by Sun Pharma Canada Inc. [US/Canadian manufacturer reference]

## 2. Approved Indications
**Hydrocortisone 0.5% cream** is indicated for the temporary relief of itching associated with minor skin irritations and rashes [US]:
- Eczema
- Insect bites
- Poison ivy, poison oak, or poison sumac
- Dermatitis resulting from soaps, detergents, cosmetics, or jewelry
- Seborrheic dermatitis
- Psoriasis
- External genital and anal itching [US]

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### General Dosing (Adults and Children ≥2 years)
- Apply to the affected area no more than 3 to 4 times daily [US].

### External Anal Itching
- **Adults:** When practical, cleanse the affected area with mild soap and warm water, rinse thoroughly, and gently dry by patting or blotting with toilet tissue or a soft cloth prior to application [US].
- **Children under 12 years:** Consult a physician prior to use [US].

### Administration Instructions
- For external use only [US].
- Do not exceed the recommended dosage without medical supervision [US].

### Missed Dose
- Not specified in available sources.

## 4. Contraindications
- Hypersensitivity to hydrocortisone or any inactive ingredients in the formulation [US].
- **Ocular use:** Do not use in or near the eyes [US].
- **Internal application:** Do not insert the product into the rectum using fingers or any mechanical applicator/device [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None [US].
- **Duration of Use:** Discontinue use and consult a physician if the condition persists for more than 7 days or if the condition clears up and recurs within a few days [US].
- **Medical Consultation Required:** Patients should consult a physician before use if they have:
    - Vaginal discharge [US]
    - Rectal bleeding [US]
    - Diaper rash [US]
- **General Precautions:** If the condition worsens during treatment, use should be discontinued immediately [US].
