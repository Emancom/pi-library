## 2. Approved Indications
- **Allergy Symptom Relief:** Indicated for the temporary relief of symptoms associated with pollen-related hayfever, including:
    - Runny nose and nasal congestion
    - Headache
    - Itchy, watery eyes
    - Sneezing
- **Management Assistance:** Intended to assist with the management of allergy symptoms through the use of titrated allergen exposures (homeopathic allergens) [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
### Adult and Paediatric (12 years and over)
- **Standard Dose:** 15 drops administered under the tongue every 3 to 4 hours [US].
- **Administration Instruction:** Drops should be held sublingually.

### Sensitive Individuals and Asthma Sufferers
- **Titration Schedule:** Take 1 to 3 drops and monitor for 3 hours. If tolerated, continue with the regular 15-drop dosage [US].
- **Discontinuation:** If allergy or asthma symptoms develop/worsen during administration, use must be discontinued [US].

## 4. Contraindications
- **Tamper Evidence:** Do not use if the tamper-evident seal is broken or missing [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Asthma Warning:** Patients suffering from asthma with severe allergies should consult a physician before use and follow specific titration instructions [US].
- **Symptom Persistence:** Users should stop use and consult a healthcare professional if symptoms do not improve within 5 days or if new symptoms appear [US].
- **Clinical Precautions:** Discontinue use immediately if asthma symptoms develop [US].
- **Alcohol Content:** Contains 20% alcohol [US].
