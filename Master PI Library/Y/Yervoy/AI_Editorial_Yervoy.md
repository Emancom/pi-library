# Editorial Product Information: Yervoy (Ipilimumab)

## Editorial Note
- **Contributing Jurisdictions:** Australian (TGA), US (FDA).
- **Purpose:** This unified editorial product information document harmonizes data from the Australian Therapeutic Goods Administration and the United States Food and Drug Administration. It is intended as a clinically accurate, structured reference for healthcare professionals.
- **Disclaimer:** This document is an editorial synthesis and does not substitute for the official Product Information approved in a specific jurisdiction for the purpose of prescribing.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Ipilimumab (rch) [Australian].
- **Chemical Class:** Recombinant, fully human monoclonal antibody (IgG1 kappa) targeting cytotoxic T-lymphocyte-associated antigen 4 (CTLA-4).
- **Brand Name:** **YERVOY**
- **ATC Code:** L01FX04
- **Dosage Form and Strength:** 5 mg/mL concentrate solution for infusion. Available as 50 mg/10 mL and 200 mg/40 mL single-dose vials.
- **Route of Administration:** Intravenous (IV) infusion.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Bristol-Myers Squibb (Australia/US).

## 2. Approved Indications
**YERVOY** is indicated for the treatment of various malignancies, primarily in combination with nivolumab:

### Melanoma
- **Unresectable or Metastatic Melanoma:** As monotherapy or in combination with nivolumab. [US: approved for adults and pediatric patients $\ge$ 12 years; Australian: approved for adults].
- **Adjuvant Treatment:** Adjuvant treatment of adult patients with cutaneous melanoma and pathologic involvement of regional lymph nodes (> 1 mm) who have undergone complete resection [US-approved; not explicitly listed in the primary Australian indication summary].

### Renal Cell Carcinoma (RCC)
- First-line treatment of adult patients with intermediate or poor-risk advanced RCC, in combination with nivolumab.

### Colorectal Cancer (CRC)
- Treatment of adults and pediatric patients $\ge$ 12 years with microsatellite instability-high (MSI-H) or mismatch repair deficient (dMMR) unresectable or metastatic CRC, in combination with nivolumab [US-approved; Australian approval specifies adult patients].

### Hepatocellular Carcinoma (HCC)
- **First-line:** Treatment of adult patients with unresectable or metastatic HCC, in combination with nivolumab [Australian-approved]. 
- **Previously Treated:** In combination with nivolumab for patients previously treated with sorafenib [US-approved].

### Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC)
- **First-line (PD-L1 $\ge$ 1%):** Metastatic NSCLC with no EGFR or ALK aberrations, in combination with nivolumab [US-approved].
- **First-line (Recurrent/Metastatic):** In combination with nivolumab and 2 cycles of platinum-doublet chemotherapy, in patients with no EGFR or ALK aberrations.

### Malignant Pleural Mesothelioma (MPM)
- First-line treatment of adult patients with unresectable MPM, in combination with nivolumab.

### Oesophageal Squamous Cell Carcinoma (OSCC/ESCC)
- First-line treatment of adult patients with unresectable advanced, recurrent, or metastatic ESCC with tumor cell PD-L1 expression $\ge$ 1%, in combination with nivolumab.

## 3. Dosage and Administration

### General Dosing (Adults)
Administration is by IV infusion over 30 minutes.

| Indication | Regimen | Duration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Melanoma (Unresectable/Metastatic)** | **Mono:** 3 mg/kg Q3W. **Combo:** 3 mg/kg + nivolumab 1 mg/kg Q3W. | Induction: 4 doses total. Following combo, nivolumab continues as monotherapy. |
| **Adjuvant Melanoma [US]** | 3 mg/kg Q3W. | 4 doses, then 3 mg/kg Q12W for up to 4 additional doses. |
| **RCC / CRC** | 1 mg/kg + nivolumab (3 mg/kg or 240 mg). | Q3W for 4 doses, followed by nivolumab monotherapy. |
| **HCC** | 3 mg/kg + nivolumab 1 mg/kg Q3W. | Up to 4 doses, followed by nivolumab monotherapy. |
| **NSCLC / MPM / OSCC** | 1 mg/kg Q6W + nivolumab (3 mg/kg Q2W or 360 mg Q3W). | Until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity (up to 2 years). |

### Administration Instructions
- **Preparation:** Allow vials to stand at room temperature for 5 minutes. Do not shake. Dilute with 0.9% NaCl or 5% Dextrose to a concentration of 1 mg/mL to 2 mg/mL (US) or 1 mg/mL to 4 mg/mL (Australian).
- **In-line Filter:** Must use a sterile, non-pyrogenic, low protein-binding in-line filter (pore size 0.2 $\mu$m or 1.2 $\mu$m).
- **Sequence:** When used in combination, administer nivolumab first, followed by **YERVOY**, then chemotherapy (if applicable) on the same day. Use separate bags/filters.

### Recommended Treatment Modifications
- **Dose Reduction:** Not recommended.
- **Withholding/Discontinuation:** Required for immune-related adverse reactions (irAEs). Generally, withhold for Grade 2 (mild-moderate) and permanently discontinue for Grade 3-4 (severe/life-threatening).

## 4. Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity:** Hypersensitivity to the active substance or any excipients [Australian].
- **US Position:** The US PI states "None."

## 5. Warnings and Precautions

### Boxed Warnings [Australian]
- **YERVOY** can cause severe and life-threatening immune-related adverse reactions (irAEs) involving any organ system.
- reactions are more frequent and severe when used in combination with nivolumab.
- Early diagnosis and appropriate management (corticosteroids) are essential.

### Immune-Mediated Adverse Reactions (IMARs)
- **Colitis/Enterocolitis:** Can lead to intestinal perforation. Monitor for diarrhea, abdominal pain, and blood in stool.
- **Hepatitis:** Severe elevations in transaminases and bilirubin. Fatalities have occurred. Monitor LFTs before each dose.
- **Dermatologic Reactions:** Rash and pruritus are common. Rare cases of Stevens-Johnson Syndrome (SJS) and Toxic Epidermal Necrolysis (TEN) have been reported.
- **Endocrinopathies:** Hypophysitis, adrenal insufficiency, thyroid dysfunction, and Type 1 Diabetes Mellitus. Symptoms may be non-specific (fatigue, headache).
- **Pneumonitis:** Monitor for cough, dyspnea, or radiographic changes.
- **Nephritis and Renal Dysfunction:** Monitor serum creatinine.

### Other Precautions
- **Infusion Reactions:** Discontinue for Grade 3-4 reactions.
- **Graft-versus-Host Disease (GVHD):** Increased risk of fatal GVHD in patients after allogeneic hematopoietic stem cell transplantation.
- **Embryo-Fetal Toxicity:** Can cause fetal harm. Advise of potential risk and use of effective contraception.

## 6. Adverse Effects

### Frequency Overview
- **Very Common (>10%):** Fatigue, rash, pruritus, diarrhea, nausea, vomiting, decreased appetite, pyrexia, headache, hypothyroidism, musculoskeletal pain, dyspnea, and cough.
- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Immune-related colitis, hepatitis, pneumonitis, endocrinopathies, and nephritis.

### Frequency Differences [Australian vs US]
Frequencies are generally consistent; however, the US PI emphasizes pediatric safety data for melanoma and CRC, noting the profile is similar to adults.

### Adverse Reactions in Combination Therapy
| Reaction | Combo (Melanoma 3mg/kg Ipi + 1mg/kg Nivo) | Ipi Monotherapy (3mg/kg) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Fatigue** | 62% | 51% |
| **Diarrhea** | 54% | 47% |
| **Rash** | 53% | 42% |
| **Colitis (Grade 3-4)** | 10% | 7% |

## 7. Drug Interactions
- **Pharmacokinetic Interactions:** Ipilimumab is a monoclonal antibody and is not metabolized by CYP450 enzymes. No clinically significant effect on the PK of co-administered CYP substrates (e.g., dacarbazine, paclitaxel/carboplatin) was observed.
- **Corticosteroids:** Use of systemic corticosteroids at baseline (before starting **YERVOY**) should be avoided due to potential interference with pharmacodynamic activity. They can be used after initiation to treat irAEs.
- **Anticoagulants:** Increased risk of gastrointestinal hemorrhage. Monitor closely.
- **Vemurafenib:** Concurrent administration with **YERVOY** (3 mg/kg) resulted in Grade 3 LFT elevations and is not recommended outside clinical trials.

## 8. Use in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
- **Pregnancy Category:**
    - **Australian:** Category C. Not recommended unless clinical benefit outweighs potential risk.
    - **US:** Embryo-fetal toxicity warning. Human IgG1 crosses the placental barrier. Contraception is advised for 3 months after the last dose.
- **Breastfeeding:** Ipilimumab has been detected in animal milk. It is unknown if it is secreted in human breast milk. Advise women not to breastfeed during treatment and for 3 months after the last dose.
- **Fertility:** No specific human studies; animal data are limited.

## 9. Use in Special Populations
- **Paediatric Use:**
    - **US:** Approved for melanoma and MSI-H/dMMR CRC in patients $\ge$ 12 years. Safety/efficacy not established under 12 years.
    - **Australian:** Safety and efficacy in children <18 years have not been established; use not recommended.
- **Elderly Use:** No overall differences in safety or efficacy between patients $\ge$ 65 and younger patients. No dose adjustment necessary.
- **Renal Impairment:** No dose adjustment required for mild to moderate renal dysfunction. Not studied in severe renal impairment.
- **Hepatic Impairment:** No dose adjustment for mild hepatic impairment. Use with caution in patients with transaminases $\ge$ 5 x ULN or bilirubin > 3 x ULN.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** CTLA-4 is a negative regulator of T-cell activity. Ipilimumab blocks the interaction of CTLA-4 with its ligands (CD80/CD86), augmenting T-cell activation and proliferation, thereby increasing anti-tumor immune response.
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Distribution:** Vss of 7.2 L.
    - **Elimination:** Terminal half-life ($\text{t}_{1/2}$) is approximately 15.4 to 20 days. Clearance is 12.1 to 16.8 mL/h.
    - **Accumulation:** Systemic accumulation is ~1.5 to 1.7-fold after Q3W dosing.

## 11. Clinical Trials
- **Melanoma (MDX010-20):** Phase 3 trial in previously treated patients. **YERVOY** 3 mg/kg showed significant improvement in overall survival (OS) compared to gp100 peptide vaccine (Median OS 10 months vs 6 months).
- **Melanoma (CheckMate-067):** Phase 3 trial. Nivolumab + **YERVOY** showed superior OS and PFS compared to **YERVOY** monotherapy.
- **RCC (CheckMate-214):** Intermediate/poor-risk patients showed superior OS with Nivolumab + **YERVOY** vs Sunitinib.
- **HCC (CheckMate-9DW):** First-line combo showed superior OS vs Lenvatinib or Sorafenib (23.7 vs 20.6 months).

## 12. Storage Conditions
- **Vials:** Store in a refrigerator ($2^\circ\text{C}$ to $8^\circ\text{C}$). Protect from light. Do not freeze or shake.
- **Diluted Solution:** Chemical and physical stability demonstrated for 24 hours between $2^\circ\text{C}$ and $25^\circ\text{C}$. To reduce microbiological hazard, use as soon as practicable.
