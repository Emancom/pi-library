# Editorial Product Information: Zinc Oxide 12% (Yerba Mate Mist SPF 30)

## Editorial Note
- This editorial synthesis is based on the official Product Information (PI) provided by the following regulatory authority: **US (FDA)**.
- The purpose of this document is to provide a harmonised editorial overview for healthcare professionals, incorporating available regulatory data into a single clinical reference.
- This document is an editorial synthesis and serves as a clinical guide; it is not a substitute for the official, jurisdiction-specific PI required for prescribing or dispensing in a particular region.

## 1. Product Identification
- **Active Ingredient:** Zinc Oxide 12% (120 mg/mL).
- **Chemical Class:** Inorganic/physical mineral UV filter.
- **Brand Name(s):** 100% Pure Yerba Mate Mist SPF 30 [US].
- **ATC Code:** D02AB (Zinc products) [Inferred].
- **Dosage Form(s) and Strength(s):** Aerosol spray/mist containing 12% Zinc Oxide [US].
- **Route(s) of Administration:** Topical.
- **Manufacturer/Sponsor:** Purity Cosmetics [US]; distributed in the European Union by Louis Vuitton Building [European address noted on packaging].

## 2. Approved Indications
- **Prevention of Sunburn:** Indicated to help prevent sunburn [US].
- **Photo-protection:** When used as directed with other sun protection measures, it decreases the risk of skin cancer and premature skin ageing caused by solar radiation [US].

## 3. Dosage and Administration
- **General Dosing:** Apply liberally to the skin 15 minutes before sun exposure [US].
- **Administration Instructions:**
    - Shake container well before use.
    - To ensure adequate protection, avoid spreading the product too thinly.
    - **Face application:** Do not spray directly onto the face. Spray the mist into the hands and then rub onto the face [US].
- **Reapplication Schedule:**
    - Reapply at least every 2 hours.
    - Reapply after 80 minutes of swimming or sweating.
    - Reapply immediately after towel drying [US].
- **Missed Dose:** Not applicable for this topical prophylactic product.

## 4. Contraindications
- **Damaged Skin:** Do not use on broken or damaged skin [US].
- **Hypersensitivity:** Known hypersensitivity to zinc oxide or any of the inactive ingredients listed in Section 10 [US].

## 5. Warnings and Precautions
- **Boxed Warnings:** None specified in available sources.
- **General Warnings:**
    - **For external use only.**
    - **Flammability/Pressure:** Contents are under pressure. Do not puncture or incinerate the container [US].
- **Ocular Exposure:** Keep out of eyes. If contact occurs, rinse thoroughly with water to remove [US].
- **Dermatological Precautions:** Discontinue use and consult a physician if a rash develops or persists [US].
- **Aspiration Risk:** Do not spray directly onto the face to avoid inhalation or ocular contact [US].
- **Monitoring Requirements:** Monitor for local skin reactions or signs of hypersensitivity.

## 6. Adverse Effects

- **Serious Adverse Effects:** Not specified in available sources.

## 10. Pharmacology
- **Mechanism of Action:** Zinc oxide is a physical (mineral) sunscreen that reflects and scatters UV radiation (UVA and UVB) to protect the skin from penetration [US].
- **Pharmacodynamics:** Provides broad-spectrum protection. The formulation includes *Ilex paraguariensis* (Yerba Mate) leaf extract, which is described as providing antioxidant properties to assist in protecting against free radicals [US].
- **Pharmacokinetics:**
    - **Absorption:** Topical zinc oxide generally remains on the skin surface with minimal systemic absorption.
    - **Metabolism/Elimination:** Not specified in available sources.
- **Inactive Ingredients:** Bentonite, butyloctyl salicylate, caprylhydroxamic acid, caprylyl glycol, safflower seed oil, cetearyl alcohol, coco-glucoside, glycerin, heptyl undecylenate, *Ilex paraguariensis* (Yerba Mate) leaf extract, jojoba ester, methyl dihydroabietate, and water [US].
